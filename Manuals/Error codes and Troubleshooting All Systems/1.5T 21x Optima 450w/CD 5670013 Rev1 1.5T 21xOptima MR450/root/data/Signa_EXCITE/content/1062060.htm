2  Overview

This procedure describes the replacement of the Gradient and RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor.

4  Procedure

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove Left Side Cover to access the SRI (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. There are two methods to access the RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor:

    1. Front Cover Removal (recommended):

      1. Remove Right Side Middle and Front covers (see Side Cover Removal and Installation).

      1. Remove Right Front cover (see Front Cover Removal and Installation).

    1. Front End Bell Removal

      1. Remove Split Bridge (see Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement).

      1. Remove Front End Bell (see Front End Bell Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor Housing from the Scan Room Interface (SRI).

    Illustration 1:  Air Flow Sensor Housing with Connector

    Illustration 2:  Air Flow Sensor Assembly Drawing

  1. Refer to the illustration below to locate the RF Coil Air Flow Sensor-Head Bracket at approximately the 1 o'clock position on the magnet enclosure.

    Illustration 3:  RF Coil Air Flow Sensor Head Bracket (Front End Bell Removed)

  1. Loosen the two (2) screws securing the Sensor-Head Bracket to the display plate.

    Illustration 4:  Screws Securing Bracket to Display Plate

  1. Remove the Sensor-Head Bracket from the display plate, and free the cable from the magnet enclosure.

  1. Carefully disassemble sensors from plastic blocks, discard failed sensor assembly, and reassemble new sensors into plastic blocks (the old plastic blocks must be reused as the sensor FRU does not contain plastic blocks).

    NOTE:Be careful not touch the exposed sensor elements.

  1. Route the new cable through the magnet enclosure, reattach the Sensor-Head Bracket under the two loosened screws, and retighten them.

    NOTE:Ensure the air flow sensor is perpendicular (90 degrees) to air flow and not in line (zero degrees) to air flow.

  1. Reconnect the RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor Housing to the SRI.

  1. Replace covers or End Bell in reverse order as listed in Step 2 and Step 3

  1. Remove Lock Out/Tag Out.

5  Finalization

  1. Ensure that sensors are operating correctly. Refer to Body Air Flow Functional Check for details.
