1  Procedure

1.1   Body Forward/Reverse Quadrature Scans

  1. To prepare the system for scan, set the following parameters:

    1. Click [New Pt] and then specify these settings:

      Illustration 1:  Scan I/O Page

      1. ID: geservice[Enter]

      1. Name: body f/r quad[Enter]

      1. Weight (lb): 111[Enter]

    1. Set patient protocols to Service.

      (Step Details)

    1. In the Patient Protocols section, click [Service] and then click [Other].

    1. Locate Protocol F and R quadrature, click the protocol name, and then click the arrow in between to add it to the Multi Protocol List or remove it.

    1. Click Accept to download the series.

  1. At the scanner, remove any surface or head coil from the bore.

  1. Place the Body TLT Sphere Phantom in the SPT Body Loader and place the Body Loader/TLT Phantom in the center of the cradle.

  1. Landmark on the center of the sphere. On the front of the magnet enclosure, press [LANDMARK] on the keypad, and then press [ADV TO SCAN].

  1. Start exam then select [Save Rx]. Complete the scan by selecting the [Scan] option.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reverse the black I and Q cables connecting to J2 and J3 at the hybrid splitter from the body coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Without changing the prescan settings, rescan the phantom using the same scan protocol.

  1. Wait until analysis is completed before restoring the system to the original configuration (i.e., return J2 and J3 to their original connection).

1.2   Forward/Reverse Quadrative Test Image Analysis

This analysis procedure applies only to body scans.

  1. Display each image so you can compare mean values.

  1. Use a square Region of Interest (ROI) of 4000 mm2 ± 50 mm2, located at the center of the image. Measure the mean pixel value (MPV1). In the viewer, this is shown as m=xxx. Jot down this value. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Restore the system to patient scanning condition.

  1. Do a test scan to ensure the system is running properly.
