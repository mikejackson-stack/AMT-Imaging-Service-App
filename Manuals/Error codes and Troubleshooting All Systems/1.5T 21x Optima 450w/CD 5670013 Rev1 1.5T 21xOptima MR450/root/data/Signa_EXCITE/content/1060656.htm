2.3  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

3  Procedure

  1. Move patient table away from the magnet. Manually press [Table Home] sensor on dock and drive LPCA towards rear of bridge.

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) . See LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove Trim Rings.

  1. Remove Bridge Cover.

  1. Remove Laser Cover.

    NOTE:Removal of the In Room Display is not required to remove the Front End Bell.

  1. Remove bridge assembly. See Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement).

    Illustration 1:  Front End Bell

  1. Remove the front Body Coil Bias cable(s) and the Body Coil ID cable.

  1. Remove the L-brackets from the vibration bracket.

    Illustration 2:  L-Bracket Removal

  1. Remove the screws holding the Front End Bell to the arm brackets.

    Illustration 3:  Front End Bell Screws

NOTICE
The Front End Bell is heavy. Avoid awkward body positioning. Use proper lifting techniques when moving the Front End bell.
 
 

  1. Monitor the slack in the Microphone and Bore Light Cables as the End Bell is removed. The cables should have enough slack to set the End Bell on the floor to the left of the front of the Magnet.

  1. Remove the End Bell.

  1. Reverse steps to install replacement Front End Bell. Confirm the gap between the Front End Bell and the RF Body Coil is 2.5 ±0.5 mm.

4  Finalization

  1. Ensure Microphone and Bore Lights work.

  1. Perform body scan (see Check Scan).
