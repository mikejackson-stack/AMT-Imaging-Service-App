1  Procedure

1.1   Replacing the P2 or P4 Patient Table Cable Take-Up for 32-Channel GEM Flat Table

  1. Undock the patient table and remove it from the Magnet Room.

  1. Remove both Lower Base Side Covers and fasten the Upper Bellows in the up position. For more information, refer to Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SECURELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar, then lower the table to ensure its weight is resting on the Safety Bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the bottom front cover, located on the front underside of the patient table, and the right or left table side cover (shown in Illustration 2) depending on which cable track is being removed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the gray TBL P3-J7 or P4-J7 cable from the Touch and Go Connector located in the upper left or right corner inside the table top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the release handle cover.

  1. Manually unlock the cradle and move it forward approximately 24 inches to clear the guide rails. For more information, refer to Removing and Replacing Cradle Assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise the back side of the cradle to gain access to the two screws on each side of the rear section. When the screws are removed, set the cradle down.

  1. Remove the three screws on the top of the rear section.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the upper screw for each cover mounting block and rotate out of the way.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift off the rear section.

  1. While wearing gloves, remove the left and right strap rails by sliding each one out from the rear of the cradle. Prop up the rear of the cradle to aid removal.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the cradle top slightly toward the rear of the table to free the cradle top from the front section, and lift the cradle top off the cradle.

  1. Remove the end rib and the shoulder screw.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully flex the cotton rod out of the rod cap, being careful not to lose any of the compression springs.

  1. Carefully disconnect the PA Coil cable connections.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws holding the PA Coil in the cradle, and carefully set the PA Coil aside.

  1. Remove the remaining end ribs and mid ribs over the P2 cable.

  1. Remove the cotton rod attachment, and rotate the cotton rod out of the way.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release the cradle.

  1. Move the cradle out from the table far enough to expose the bottom cover (front cable shield) beneath the P2 Connector.

  1. Remove the front cable shield.

  1. Detach the P port base before disconnecting the P-connector. Make note of the P port base orientation.

  1. Disconnect the two P port connectors from the cradle and remove all cable clamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the rear end of the cradle.

  1. Disconnect the two cable tracks from the bottom of the cradle.

  1. Carefully feed the cables and connectors through the holes located at the rear of the cradle, and remove the cradle from the table.

  1. Mark the existing cable where the cable track begins so that when the replacement cable is placed in the track, the proper amount of slack is available.

  1. Remove the two slotted brass screws from the cable take-up lead in the cable well.

  1. Pull the rear of the cable take-up, lay it out at the front of the table to expose the cover plate and screws, and remove both screws.

  1. Pull the cable take-up back into the cable well, and unlatch each cable track lid by prying up on the edge with a screwdriver. (Extra cable track lids are included as these cable track lids are somewhat fragile.)

  1. When all cable track lids are open, feed the cable through the hole and pull the cable being changed below the table.

  1. Open the cable take-up lids on the cable track below the table, and pull the cable being changed out of the cable track.

  1. Remove the four screws on the cable grommet and pull the cables to the side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any cable ties. If replacing the P2 Cable, disconnect the Table ID Cable from the bottom of the PA cable track. (Follow the top cable from the ODU P2 connector to the cable disconnect on the PA side of the table.)

  1. Remove the two ODU P connector screws, and carefully pull the ODU connector out the back of the table side dock connector.

  1. Measure the distance from the end connector to the cable track of the cable being replaced. Mark the new cable at that location so when the new cable is placed in the cable track, the same amount of slack is maintained.

  1. Replace the cable by following the above steps in reverse order.

    1. Connect the ODU cable.

    1. If replacing the P2 cable, connect the Table ID cable.

    1. Install the cable grommet.

    1. Feed the cable through the cradle hole.

    1. Close the cable take-up lids.

    1. Attach the cover plate.

    1. Attach the P port connectors and bases.

    1. Attach the front shield.

    1. Attach the end ribs, mid ribs, PA Coil, and connectors.

    1. Attach the cotton rods.

    1. Attach the strap rails.

    1. Attach the rear section.

    1. Attach the Touch and Go connectors.

    1. Attach the covers after performing Finalization checks.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Replacing 32-Channel GEM Flat Table PA Multiplexer Box Assembly

  1. Perform Replacing the P2 or P4 Patient Table Cable Take-Up for 32-Channel GEM Flat Table, Step 1 through Replacing the P2 or P4 Patient Table Cable Take-Up for 32-Channel GEM Flat Table, Step 30.

  1. If removing the P3 (PA Coil) cable track on the left side, disconnect the cables from the table Multiplexer found on the left side of the table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Unmount the Multiplexer from the Table.

  1. Feed the cable track down through the hole in the Table and set the cable track assembly aside.

  1. Reverse the steps above to install the new cable track.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Replacing 32-Channel GEM Flat Table P3 ODU to PA Multiplexer

  1. Undock the patient table and remove it from the Magnet Room.

  1. Remove the left Lower Base Side Cover, and fasten the Upper Bellows in the up position. For more information, refer to Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar, then lower the table to ensure its weight is resting on the Safety Bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the bottom front cover (located on the front underside of the patient table) and the left table side cover. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Disconnect the gray Touch and Go Cable from the connector located in the upper left corner inside the table top. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the Multiplexer. (See Illustration 15.)

  1. Remove the screws holding the bottom of the cable track.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws holding the top of the cable track.

  1. Disconnect the Table ID cable, located near the bottom of the cable track. (The Table ID cable routes to the ODU P2 connector.)

  1. Remove the four screws on the cable grommet and pull the cables to the side. (See Illustration 13.)

  1. Remove the two screws holding the ODU P3 connector to the table dock connector, and carefully slide the cable out the back of the dock connector.

  1. Reverse the steps above to install the new cable track.

    (Step Details)
