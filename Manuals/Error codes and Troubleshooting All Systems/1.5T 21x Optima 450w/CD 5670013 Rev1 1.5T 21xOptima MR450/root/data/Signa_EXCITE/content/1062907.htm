2  Overview

The Patient Table Up Limit Switch components are used to set the hydraulic cylinder upper limit. This procedure explains how to replace the Patient Table Up Limit Switch components.

The Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket for an electrical Table requires two persons and the timing varies depending on whether the Cradle is removed or not.

See each procedure for specific timing and number of required persons.

4  Procedure

4.1  Up Limit Switch Link Replacement

Required Persons: 1. Procedure Timing: 30 minutes

  1. Undock Patient Table and remove it from magnet room.

  1. Remove Right Upper Bellows and Right Lower Base Side Cover and Front Base Cover. See Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal for more information.

  1. Raise table to maximum height.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar.

    Illustration 1:  Table Lock Safety Bar

  1. Lower the Table and ensure that all of the Table top's weight is resting on the safety bar.

  1. Remove the two cotter pins holding the Link to the bell crank.

    Illustration 2:  Cotter Pins - Link to Bell Crank

  1. Slide the Link out and replace with the replacement Link.

  1. Fasten the link to the bell crank using the two cotter pins.

  1. If there are no additional replacements, Proceed to Finalization. Otherwise, proceed to Up Limit Switch Adjuster Block Replacement.

4.2  Up Limit Switch Adjuster Block Replacement

Required Persons: 1. Procedure Timing: 30 minutes

  1. Remove the Up Limit Switch Link. See Up Limit Switch Link Replacement.

  1. Remove the left Upper Bellows and left Lower Base Side Cover. See Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal for more information.

  1. Confirm that the Table top’s weight is resting on the Safety Bar.

  1. Mark the current location of the Adjuster Block on the Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket.

  1. Remove the two screws holding the Adjuster Block to the mounting bracket.

    Illustration 3:   Screws Holding Adjuster Block

  1. Install the new Adjuster Block to the mounting bracket using the two screws.

  1. Reinstall the Up Limit Switch Link.

  1. If there are no additional replacements, proceed to Finalization. Otherwise, proceed to Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket Replacement.

4.3  Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket Replacement

Required Persons: 2. Procedure Timing: 120 minutes

  1. Remove the Up Limit Switch Link. See Up Limit Switch Link Replacement.

  1. Mark the current location of the Adjuster Block on the mounting bracket and then remove the Adjuster Block. See Up Limit Switch Adjuster Block Replacement

  1. Release the Cradle and manually slide the Cradle forward and either have one person hold the Cradle end up so you have access to the Table Top Height Adjustment Screw or remove the Cradle completely. See Removing Cradle Assembly.

  1. Use a flat head screwdriver to remove the Cover Plug from the Table Top Height Adjustment Screw.

  1. Use needle-nosed pliers to remove the e-clip from the end of the Table Top Height Adjustment Screw.

    NOTE:Place a piece of tape over part of the e-clip before removing so that the e-clip does not become lost during removal. Remove tape after removing the e-clip.

  1. If the Cradle was not removed, slide Cradle back onto the Table.

  1. Confirm that the Table top’s weight is resting on the Safety Bar.

  1. While moving the “table down” rod to the rear of the Table, pull down on the hydraulic shaft. Stop when the hydraulic shaft is low enough to access the top of the Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket.

    Illustration 4:  Hydraulic Shaft

  1. Remove the two screws (at the top of the hydraulic cylinder) attaching the Up Limit Switch Mounting Bracket to the hydraulic cylinder.

    NOTE:The top can be rotated; this also changes the Table height. Make sure to return the top back to its original position.

  1. Measure where the Adjuster Block was located and transfer a mark onto the new mounting bracket at the same location. (This assumes that the hydraulic cylinder isn’t turned when removing the bracket.)

  1. Install the new mounting bracket.

  1. Carefully raise the Table. The hydraulic cylinder must fit through the hole in the table. Check that the hydraulic cylinder is completely within the hole and that the Table height adjustment is accessible.

  1. If the Cradle is still attached to the Table, release the Cradle, manually slide the Cradle forward, and have one person hold the end of the Cradle so you have access to the Table Top Height Adjustment Screw.

  1. Re-attach the e-clip and Cover Plug.

  1. If the Cradle was not removed, slide Cradle back onto the Table. Otherwise, reinstall the Cradle.

  1. Lower the Table onto the safety block.

  1. Install the Up Limit Switch Adjuster Block.

  1. Install the Up Limit Switch Link.

  1. Fully raise the cylinder.

  1. If necessary, adjust the Adjuster Block so that when the Table dock plunger is pressed in 2mm, the Adjuster Block just touches the link mechanism.

    Illustration 5:  Table Dock Pushbutton

5  Finalization

  1. Verify proper operation of the Table.

    1. Raise the Table Lock Safety Bar.

    1. Dock the Table.

    1. Verify proper up/down Table motion.

    1. When Table is in fully raised position, verify that Cradle and LPCA can be advanced normally into bore.

    1. Verify that raising Table to full height results in LPCA automatically driving out to engage Cradle.

    1. Verify that lowering the Table results in automatic retraction of LPCA into Bridge.

    1. Verify normal Cradle and LPCA motion from Home position to end of travel on Bridge using enclosure In Fast and Out Fast buttons at the Operator's Console.

  1. Replace the Upper and Lower Base Covers, and the Front Base Cover.
