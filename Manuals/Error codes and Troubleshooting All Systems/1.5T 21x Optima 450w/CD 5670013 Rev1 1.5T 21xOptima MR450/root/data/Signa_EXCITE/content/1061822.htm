2  Overview

The LPCA Quick Release Module is also referred to as the Quick Disconnect Assembly, Emergency Release Module, or Quick Disconnect Connector. The LPCA Quick Disconnect Modules allow for easy replacement of the coil connectors as they slide on and off their own track with minimal effort.

MR450w GEM and MR750w GEM only have a P1 port in the LPCA.

Electrical Hazard!
Strong voltages are present in or around the LPCA when energized.

4  Procedure

  1. At the Operator Control Panel (OCP), Drive the LPCA and Cradle through the magnet bore to within 1 foot (30 cm) from the end of the Rear Bridge.

    • This should provide enough room to work on the LPCA.

    • The LPCA will not move if the Patient Table is disconnected or not at the high limit prior to LPCA movement.

  1. At the foot of the table, twist or squeeze the Cradle Release Handle to detach the Cradle from the LPCA. Pull the Cradle by hand to the Home position so it is fully retracted onto the Patient Table.

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it away from the Magnet.

  1. Remove the four screws on the top of the LPCA cover.

    Illustration 1:  LPCA Cover Screws

  1. Remove the cover.

    NOTE:To remove the cover, slide it slightly forward and lift the front of the LPCA to clear the hardware and then slide back and up.

  1. Place the new cover on the LPCA Assembly starting at the rear and then the front. Take care to not pinch any of the wires in the rear of the LPCA.

  1. Attach the cover with the four screws.

  1. Grasp quick disconnect module in hand and pull and slide forward as shown below to remove it from plastic guide track and fixed rear connector assembly.

    Illustration 2:  Quick Disconnect Module Removal Path

  1. Slide new quick disconnect module onto guide track, slide to the rear, and firmly seat into rear fixed connector.

  1. Replace LPCA cover. Take care not to pinch any wires it the rear of the LPCA underneath the cover.

  1. Re-Docking the Patient Table will drive the LPCA to the Home Position and reattach to the Cradle/Patient Table. Dock the table by pressing the mechanical docking pedal. Tables equipped with P-connectors or GEM coil must next engage electrical dock connector by pressing the middle foot pedal shown below:
