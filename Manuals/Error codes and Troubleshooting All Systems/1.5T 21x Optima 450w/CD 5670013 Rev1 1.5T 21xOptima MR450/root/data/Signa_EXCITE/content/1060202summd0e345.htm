The dual fiber-optic loopback cable may be difficult to reinsert into the new XGA unit. Follow these steps if you are having difficulty connecting this cable.

NOTE:The removable Single Form-factor Plugable (SFP) Fiber-optic Transceiver Modules are static-sensitive devices that can be damaged by ESD during handling. Be sure to wear proper an anti-static grounding strap prior to handling this unit.

  1. Grasp the metal bar handle on the SFP module and remove the module from the XGA unit.

  2. Insert the dual fiber-optic cable into the SFP module by gripping either side of the hard plastic connector housing at the black section and then firmly pushing connector into the SFP module until the connector clicks into the module. Do not grip the molded plastic boot sections where the fiber-optic cables enter connector.

    NOTE:If the cable is inserted backwards, it will not go in all the way.

    Illustration 12:  Black Section on Dual Fiber-optic Loopback Cable

  3. Insert the SFP module with fiber-optic cable attached back into the XGA unit.
