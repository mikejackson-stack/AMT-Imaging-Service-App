2.2  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present throughout the System Cabinet when Power is applied.
Refer To OSHA lockout and tagout requirements.

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
The 4170 ICN weighs approximately 40 lbs. and requires two people to lift it.

NOTICE
Follow the procedure mentioned below for removing power to ICN chassis. Failure to properly power down ICN’s may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICN's be turned ON/OFF from the power button on the front. The ICN's have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

3  Procedure

  1. Locate the ICN's in the Power Gradient RF (PGR) system cabinet. The ICN's are located about 2/3 of the way up from the bottom of the cabinet, above the CAM Chassis, on the right hand side (see Illustration 1 and Illustration 2).

    Illustration 1:  ICN Location - 4100

    Illustration 2:  ICN Location - 4170

    NOTE:To open the PGR Cabinet, turn the key to the right. The cabinet handle pops out. Turn the handle bar to the left and the cabinet door opens.

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICN's be turned ON/OFF from the power button on the front. The ICN's have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

  1. Switch OFF the ICNs from the Common Service Desktop browser (see ICN On/Off Procedure).

  1. Perform LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  1. On the front of the ICN, undo the thumbscrew on each side of the ICN that will be removed/replaced (see Illustration 3 and Illustration 4).

    Illustration 3:  Thumbscrews on the Front of the ICN - 4100

    Illustration 4:  Thumbscrews on the Front of the ICN - 4170

  1. Once the thumbscrews are loose, slide the ICN until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails.

  1. Remove all connections from the rear of the ICN, noting locations for reinstallation (see Illustration 5 and Illustration 6). Please ensure that the Ethernet release tabs are completely pressed before the cables are removed. Remove both AC power plugs.

  1. Locate the two release tabs on the slide rails (see Illustration 8). Pressing each release tab toward the center of the ICN, pull the ICN forward until the rails release and the ICN is free. Place the ICN being replaced to the side in a safe area.

    Illustration 8:  ICN Slide Rails and Release Tabs

  1. Return slide rails into cabinet before inserting ICN.

  1. Insert new ICN into the PGR cabinet. Slide the ICN out until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails. Make sure release tabs are engaged.

  1. Connect all the cables back in a reverse operation.

  1. Press the release tabs. Push the ICN into the PGR Cabinet until the ICN is completely inside.

  1. Attach the thumbscrews to the front of the ICN to secure the ICN into the cabinet.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  2. Turn ICNs on per ICN On/Off Procedure.

    NOTE:For the first 3 or 4 minutes, LED's come on only at the rear of the ICN, next to the power cables. After 3-4 minutes, you will hear the ICN fans come on and then the LED at the front of the ICN starts flashing.

  3. Configure the new ICN (see VRE Reconfiguration).

  4. Perform a test scan to ensure the system is working properly.
