  Safety

WARNING
possible personal injury or equipment damage!
the module could fall when using the lift tool.
before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. this will help keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

CAUTION
Perform the proper Lockout/Tagout procedure for the type of replacement being performed. The cabinet should not have any power as you are assembling and using the hoist kit.

1  Procedure

  1. In the HEC, remove the support tube. The support tube is located on the right hand side of the cabinet and is secured with two mounting bolts.

  1. There are seven openings in the support tube to be used in multiple configurations (see Step ). Place support tube on top of cabinet in which an item is being replaced. To place the support tube correctly onto the cabinet, please follow the proper orientation (see Step ). Secure the support tube on top of the cabinet using a wrench to tighten the two bolts onto the top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the main lifting beam from the PGR Cabinet. The main lifting beam is located on the right side of the PGR cabinet and is secured with Velcro straps.

    (Step Details)

  1. The main lifting beam slides onto the top of the cabinet. You may have to move cables or hoses out of the way, but none of the cables or hoses need to be disconnected, especially for the PEN cabinet. There is a rear bracket for each position. The main lifting beam hook plate slides into the rear bracket (see Step ). (Check to ensure that the rear bracket is tight and secure.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the winch into the main lifting beam. Secure the winch in with the pin so it does not slide out. The winch is able to slide within the main lifting beam to allow proper alignment with the removed item or FRU lifting bracket (see Step ).

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Remove the winch by removing the pin from the main lifting beam and sliding out the winch. Place the winch (including hook and chains) into the hoist service kit box.

  1. Remove the main lifting beam. Return the lifting beam to the PGR cabinet. Insert the hook end of the beam into the cabinet edge first, then place the bottom part of the beam in. Secure it into the cabinet with the Velcro straps and pin at the lower end of the beam.

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew the support tube from the top of the cabinet. Return the support tube to the HEC and secure with the two bolts.
