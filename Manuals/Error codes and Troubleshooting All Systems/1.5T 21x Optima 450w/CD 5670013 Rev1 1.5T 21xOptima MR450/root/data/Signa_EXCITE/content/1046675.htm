2  Overview

System Gain Calibration is performed to determine the standard HEAD (transmit/receive coil) and BODY recon scale factors that will achieve a known level of image intensity when using the standard HEAD coil or BODY coil respectively with a known phantom solution and protocol. The desire is that when the same solution or tissue is scanned in either the standard HEAD or BODY coils with the same protocol, those tissues will have approximately the same image intensity level on a given system and between systems of like type.

This calibration accounts for differences in standard HEAD and BODY receive coil sensitivity, preamp gains, cable loss, receiver gain and other factors. The calibration must be done on standard HEAD and BODY at least once to calibrate those paths. The procedure/tool can calibrate both standard HEAD and BODY together, just HEAD alone, or BODY alone. System Gain Calibration consists of:

  • Head and Body Calibration

    NOTE:While these calibrations can not be run simultaneously, they can both be selected at the same time to run serially. Otherwise, run individual modes as listed in the next two options.

  • Body Calibration

  • Head Calibration

4  Procedure

4.1  Preliminary Setup

  1. Position the head sphere in the head loader and fasten the strap. Position the head loader on top of the head loader positioner in the head coil so the loader's black center line lies directly below the head coil's Superior/Inferior center marker.

    NOTE:3.0T spheres are pink. 1.5T spheres are light green.

    Illustration 1:  Positioning Head Sphere Loader

    NOTE:Make sure the top section of the split-top coil is properly oriented. Arrows on the NOTICE labels should be aligned for the correct orientation.

    Illustration 2:  Landmark Head Sphere

  1. Properly position the Head Sphere and Loader as previously noted. Use the Curved Cradle Insert to hold the head coil as shown below.

  1. Establish landmark on the center line of the Head Loader, but do not Advance to Scan.

  1. Run the cradle in so the display reads 1135 mm. (See illustration below.)

    Illustration 3:  Distance Between Head Coil Landmark and Centermark on Body Loader

  1. Place the body loader on the GEM Foam Phantom Positioner. Turn on the alignment light and then position the body loader on the cradle so that the alignment light is properly centered on the loader crosshair.

  1. Press [Advance to Scan].

  1. Start the System Gain Calibration Tool:

    1. From the Common Service Desktop, select [Calibration].

    1. Select [System Gain Calibration] from the calibration menu, then Click here to start this tool to start the procedure.

4.2  Head and Body Calibration

  1. Type 3 and press <Enter> on the screen shown below to select Both Head and Body Mode, and the calibration begins.

    NOTE:Head Gain will calibrate first. When completed, Body will calibrate. The following two illustrations apply to both Head and Body.

    Illustration 4:  Starting System Gain Calibration Tool

  1. The calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, Do you want to save the recon scale factor. Type Y and press <Enter> (on the screen shown below) to save the recon scale factor.

    NOTE:There will be (at least) two prompts: one for Head and one for Body.

    Illustration 5:  Calibration Output

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is completed.

4.3  Body Calibration

The system is dependent on landmarking the head coil, even for body mode only scans.

  1. Type 2 and press <Enter> to select Body Mode, and the calibration begins. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. When the test is complete, the calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, Do you want to save the BODY recon scale factor. Type Y and press <Enter> to save the calibration file. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is completed.

4.4  Head Calibration

  1. Type 1 and press <Enter> to begin the Head Mode. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. When the test is completed, the calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, Do you want to save the HEAD recon scale factor. Type Y and press <Enter> to save the calibration file. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is completed.

5  Finalization

  1. Remove all phantoms and service tools from the patient table used during the calibration.

  1. If you are performing this calibration during an installation, continue with the required calibrations. Otherwise, perform a SAVEINFO to save the new ReconScale values.
