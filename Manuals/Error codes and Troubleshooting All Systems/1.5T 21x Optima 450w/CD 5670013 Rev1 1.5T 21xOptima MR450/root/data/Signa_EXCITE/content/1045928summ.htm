3  Procedure

3.1   Service Filler Panels

Do not use foam Service Filler Panels for LVShim procedures.

3.2   Preparing for the LVShim Procedure

NOTE:3.0T LVShim phantoms have a pink solution; 1.5T phantoms have a green solution.

  1. Remove all coils from the cradle and phantoms from the bore.

NOTICE
Perform a Localizer scan to ensure the phantom is centered before performing the LVShim.
 
 

  1. Place the curved insert (5401439) into the patient table. Place the LVShim Phantom on the insert. See the following illustration.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark on the LVShim phantom

  1. Press the [Advance to Scan] button.

  1. Start the LVShim tool. The tool can be started in proprietary or non-proprietary mode.

    1. To start LVShim in proprietary mode:

      1. Insert the Restricted Service Key into the USB port of the Host Computer (if it is not already inserted).

      1. Click the Service Browser.

      1. Select the Calibration tab, and click Installation and Calibration to start the wizard.

      1. Select the appropriate shim (Gradient Shim) to be performed from the drop-down list. This launches the LVShim tool.

    1. To start LVShim in non-proprietary mode:

      1. Click the Calibration tab shown in the following illustration.

        (Step Details)

      1. Select LVShim from the Calibration menu, and click the link labeled Click here to start this tool.

      1. The LVShim tool launches. The following illustration shows an overview of this tool.

3.3   Gradshim LVShim Procedure

By default, Gradshim is performed in Auto mode, though Manual mode is also available.

3.3.1  Auto Mode

In Auto mode, gradient currents are automatically calculated after the [Scan] button is clicked to fine tune the homogeneity of the magnet. In this mode, the tool stops after the X, Y and Z harmonics are within specification. In Test mode, the image from the last scan is automatically reanalyzed. (See Step .)
