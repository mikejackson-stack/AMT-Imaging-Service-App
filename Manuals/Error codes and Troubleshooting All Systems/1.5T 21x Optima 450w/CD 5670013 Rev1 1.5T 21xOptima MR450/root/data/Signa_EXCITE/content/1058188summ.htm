Front Cover Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

1.1   Front Cover without In-Room Display Version

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

    (Step Details)

  1. Undock patient table, and position table away from the magnet.

  1. Remove the two (2) M6 screws that secure the front bridge cover (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Front Bridge Cover Screws

  1. Remove front trim ring by pulling it away from the enclosure. There are no bolts holding it into place (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove control panels (see Replacing Magnet Enclosure Control Panels).

  1. Using an M5 Allen wrench, remove the five (5) bolts which attach the front cover to the turtle bracket (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the five (5) bolts have been removed, pull the front cover slightly away from the magnet and identify the Conquest Voltage Regular Board on the upper right side. Carefully remove JP1 from the board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front cover; ensure the microphone cable connected to the lower right side of the cover is not pulled (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. To free the front cover from the microphone cord, you can either disconnect it from the rear pedestal (connector J9 on the remote intercom board), or detach it from the front cover (see Replacing the Microphone at the Magnet ).

  1. Reverse the steps to install replacement front cover.

1.2   Front Cover with In-Room Display version

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

    (Step Details)

  1. Undock patient table, and position table away from the magnet.

  1. Remove the alignment laser light cover by unscrewing two (2) screws. The cover is held in place by a popper on the lower left corner and snaps off (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two trim rings by pulling away from the enclosure (see Illustration 9). They are held in place by poppers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) M6 screws that secure the front bridge cover. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two control panel covers by unscrewing four (4) screws per panel (see Illustration 11). The covers are also held on by poppets and Velcro.

    (Step Details)

  1. To free each front cover, disconnect the two cables that connect the control panel to the magnet (see Replacing Magnet Enclosure Control Panels).

  1. Reverse the steps to install replacement front cover.

2  Finalization

  1. Whenever the in-room display or trackball control panels are disconnected and then reconnected, the host PC needs to be rebooted.

  1. After reboot, from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware, then Magnet Room, then In Room Display.

  1. The diagnostic will display the current status of the IRD and trackball connectivity. If status is not installed, recheck connections, reboot, and check the In Room Display diagnostic again.
