Driver Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
The module could fall when using The hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This will help keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

NOTICE
Prior to removing power from the Penetration (PEN) Cabinet, verify that ICNs are properly shut down. Perform LOTO to the PDM-RF Power on PDU to remove power from the PEN Cabinet and Driver Module. See Lockout/Tagout for RF Subsystems, Penetration Cabinet (PEN), and Magnet Room Electronics document for more details.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Driver Module

NOTICE
PDM - Recon Power can remain on.
 
 

  1. Perform the Lock Out / Tag Out procedure for the PEN Cabinet, LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove the four screws securing the Driver Module to the PEN Cabinet frame. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the 16 thumbs screws securing the penetration panel to the magnet room wall. See Illustration 2. This will allow access to the rear of the Driver Module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully pull the penetration panel away from the wall, leaving all cables attached to the panel.

  1. Remove the cables from the rear of the Driver Module. See .

    (Step Details)

  1. Push driver forward, a little, from the pen wall side.

  1. Set up the Hoist Service Tool according the following procedure, Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.

  1. Pull the Driver Module out from the cabinet until the slide rails lock into position.

  1. Attach lifting brackets to each side of the Driver Module. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the lifting bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets; attach the lifting bar to the hoist chain. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain.

  1. Press in the release tabs on each side of the Driver Module and pull it out further from the cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Unlatch the locking lever on each rail to remove the Driver Module from the PEN Cabinet slide rails. See Illustration 7. This will need to be done from the magnet room side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release the PEN Cabinet slide rails and slide them back into the cabinet.

  1. Remove both slide rails from the Drive Module. See Illustration 6.

  1. Lower the Driver Module onto the FRU packaging platform for removal.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Re-installing the Driver Module

  1. Re-attach both slide rails to the new Driver Module. See Illustration 6.

  1. Re-attach both lifting brackets to the new Driver Module. See Illustration 4.

  1. Re-attach the lifting bar (hoist kit) to the lifting brackets; re-attach the lifting bar to the hoist chain. See Illustration 5.

  1. Ratchet the winch to tighten the hoist chain and begin lifting the module.

  1. Slide out the PEN Cabinet slide rails a short distance to gauge how high to lift the module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Finish pulling out each PEN Cabinet slide rail until each locks into position.

  1. Carefully slide the Drive Module slide rails into the PEN Cabinet slide rails until they lock into position.

  1. Loosen the chain tension with the winch.

  1. Remove the lifting bar from the lifting brackets.

  1. Remove the lifting brackets from the Driver Module.

  1. Unlatch the locking lever on each rail Illustration 7. This will need to be done from the magnet room side.

  1. Finish pushing the Driver Module into the PEN Cabinet.

  1. Tighten the four (4) screws to secure the Driver Module to the PEN Cabinet frame. See Step .

  1. Attach the cables to the rear of the Driver Module. See Step .

  1. Tighten the 16 thumb screws to secure the penetration panel to the magnet room wall. See Illustration 2.

2  Finalization

  1. Reapply power to the system, remove all LOTO equipment.

  1. Perform the TR DD calibration. See TR Dynamic Disable Calibration document for details.

  1. Complete a Head and Body test scan to ensure the system is functional.
