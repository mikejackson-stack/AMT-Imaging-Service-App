4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Setup

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR (Signal-to-Noise) test using the 1.5T HD 8-Channel Foot Ankle Coil by Invivo (M3340CD).

  1. Insert the Curved Adapter Panel Assembly into the magnet end of the table as shown.

    Illustration 1:  Placement of Curved Adapter Panel Assembly

  1. Place the Foot Ankle Coil baseplate on the table as shown.

    Illustration 2:  Foot Ankle Coil Baseplate on Table

  1. Place the Small, Cylindrical Unified Phantom horizontally and the Cubical Unified Phantom vertically, and fix the phantom to the baseplate using the Velcro® as shown.

    Illustration 3:  Cubical Unified Phantom on Baseplate

  1. Cover the baseplate with the coil.

    Illustration 4:  Coil Over Baseplate

  1. Connect the Foot Ankle Coil to Port A of the system LPCA, and landmark the coil at the crossmark on the coil handle.

    Illustration 5:  Crossmark on Coil Handle

    1. Press [Advance to Scan].

    1. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool.

  1. After the test is completed, carefully remove the cubical then the cylindrical phantom from the baseplate to avoid damage to either phantom.
