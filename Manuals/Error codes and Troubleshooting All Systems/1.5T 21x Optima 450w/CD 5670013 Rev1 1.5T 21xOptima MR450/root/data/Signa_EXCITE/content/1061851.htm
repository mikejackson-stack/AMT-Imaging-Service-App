3.4  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

CAUTION
Dangerous Work Area!
Use of heavy equipment for coil removal and replacement presents hazards to all personnel in the area.

  • Set up restricted barriers and signs limiting access to the work area.

  • Notify all affected personnel in the area prior to starting the service activity. Review safety procedures and restricted areas for the job.

3.5  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Contact a trained, local Body Coil Tuning Expert prior to starting this procedure, as the coil will need to be tuned as part of the replacement finalization. If necessary, contact the Modality Operations Manager for a list of trained individuals.

4  Procedure

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet document.

  1. Remove Split Bridge. See Bridge Removal and Replacement document for details.

  1. Remove the Front End Bell. See Front End Bell Removal and Installation document for details.

    NOTE:Bore Temperature Sensor is attached to the Front End Bell.

  1. Disconnect the two air hoses at the rear end of the RF Body Coil.

  1. Disconnect the two drive cables (I and Q) at the rear end of the body coil. Disconnect the DC bias line at the rear, and disconnect the Hart ID cable and DC Bias line at the front of the body coil.

    NOTE:The rear end bell does not need to be removed for RF Body Coil replacement.

NOTICE
If there is no FRU case available, place the body coil on two pads, making sure they protect the drive cables of the body coil from damage. Make sure the body coil does not roll over.
 
 

  1. Fully remove the RF Body Coil as shown below, and place it inside the empty top half of the FRU case.

    Illustration 1:  Removing RF Body Coil

  1. Before installing the RF Body Coil, attach one of the foam strips at the back bottom edge of the body coil (6 o'clock position where the gradient coil does not have an acoustic barrier).

  1. Slide in the new RF Body Coil (shown below) with the blue drive cables face the rear end. Align the mounting pads on the body to the centering strips inside the gradient coil.

    Illustration 2:  Sliding in RF Body Coil

NOTICE
Do not use the air hose manifolds to install the RF Body Coil.
 
 

  1. Align the blue drive cables in the rear so the middle of the two drive cables are as close to the 6 o'clock position as visually possible. Make sure the blue drive cables and the DC bias cable are routed through the cutouts on the rear end bell as shown.

    Illustration 3:  Centering RF Body Coil

  1. Press the RF Body Coil so there is a 2.5 ±0.5 mm gap between the coil and the Rear End Bell.

NOTICE
For optimal performance of the system, the I and Q cables must be spaced a minimum of 75 mm (3 inches) from cables exiting the cable tracks from the LPCA and 30 mm (1.2 inches) from the Temperature Sensor Cable routed from the gradient coil as shown below.
 
 

  1. Reconnect the I and Q lines, the DD Bias lines, and the Hart ID cable.

    Illustration 4:  I and Q Line Spacing

  1. Reconnect the two air hoses at the rear end of the body coil.

  1. Attach the remaining yellow foam strip at the bottom front of the body coil (6 o'clock position where the gradient coil does not have an acoustic barrier).

  1. Replace the front end bell. Refer to Front End Bell Removal and Install. There should be a 2.5 ±0.5 mm gap between the front end bell and the RF Body Coil.

  1. Replace the split bridge. Refer to Bridge Removal and Replacement.

  1. Remove LOTO.

5  Finalization

  1. Have the previously contacted, properly trained Body Coil Tuning Expert tune the RF Body Coil.

  1. Run the TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Perform a DQA II (Gradient Cal/Iso Vector Z.

  1. Perform an EPI White Pixel Test - Full Mode.

  1. Run the LV Shim - Gradshim.

  1. Run the System Gain Calibration.

  1. Run an SPT Full Test Mode

  1. Run the Echo Planar Test (EPT).

  1. Perform a quick head and body scan to ensure the system is functioning properly.
