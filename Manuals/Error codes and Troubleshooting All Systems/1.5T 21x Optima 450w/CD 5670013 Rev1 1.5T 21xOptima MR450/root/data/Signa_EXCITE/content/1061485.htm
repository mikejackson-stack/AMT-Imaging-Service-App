2  Overview

This procedure describes the replacement of either LPCA cable tracks. The LPCA contains two independent tracks and both are FRU assemblies, meaning if one or more cables within a track fails, the entire track is replaced. Also, both tracks contain different connector types at both ends, therefore routing the cables when removing and replacing have unique requirements that are documented in this procedure.

As viewed from the front of the magnet, the P1 coil connectors track is on the left side of the rear pedestal and is designated by two FRUs noted below in Section 3.3, Replacement Parts. The A Port and other associated cables track is located on the right side of the rear pedestal.

4  Procedure

4.1  Remove Cable Track

When looking at the LPCA from the front, the left track contains the P1 and P2 coil connectors (see Table 1 for all cable types) and the right track contains the A connector (see Table 2 for all cable types). Each track contains cables that are different from each other, which translates into slight differences in how you remove and install a replacement cable track.

  1. At the Operator Control Panel (OPC), Drive the LPCA and Cradle through the magnet bore to within 1 foot (30 cm) from the end of the Rear Bridge.

    • This should provide enough room to work on the LPCA.

    • The LPCA will not move if the Patient Table is disconnected or not at the high limit prior to LPCA movement.

  1. At the foot of the table, twist or squeeze the Cradle Release Handle to detach the Cradle from the LPCA. Pull the Cradle by hand to the Home position so it is fully retracted onto the Patient Table.

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it away from the Magnet.

  1. Undock table and relocate away from magnet.

  1. Perform Lock-out Tag-out for rear pedestal and System Shutdown. For more information, see LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics) and System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

    Illustration 1:  LPCA Cover Screw Locations

  1. Remove both rear pedestal side covers.

  1. Remove the P1 and/or P2 Quick Release Modules. Use sufficient force to pull the module forward to slide it off its track.

    Illustration 2:  Remove the Quick Release Module

  1. To create sufficient space for removing and replacing P1/P2 cable connectors, do the following:

    1. At the rear of each cable mount bracket, remove two screws from the left side and three screws from the right side, and then set the cable connector aside.

      Illustration 3:  Cable Connector Screws

    1. Remove both bottom cable mount bracket screws, and then set the cable mount bracket aside.

    Illustration 4:  Cable Mount Bracket Screws

  1. Remove four slotted brass screws from the front and back of the Balun.

    Illustration 5:  Balun

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head brass screws that secure the cable track lead to the base of the LPCA.

    Illustration 6:  Cable Track Lead Screw Locations

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head screws that secure the cable track lead to the front of the rear pedestal bridge.

    Illustration 7:  Bridge Cable Track Screw Locations

  1. Disconnect all cable connections that feed from the front end of the cable track to points throughout the rear pedestal. See the table below.

    NOTE:Take note of cable routing and tie wrap locations.

    Table 1:  32 Channel - P-Port Cable Track connection points (No Reroute Box)

    LPCA EndPedestal End

    PED Load B, Blue Cable (at the back of the rear pedestal)

    PED-HUB-SLOT2-P1
    PED-HUB-SLOT_11 J1, on RFCB Slot 11 in RF Hub

  1. Feed loose end of cable connections up through the opening at the front of the rear pedestal Bridge and let them lay freely on the bridge.

    Illustration 8:  Cable Track Opening in Bridge

  1. Starting at the rear of the LPCA, pull cable connectors through cable well of bridge, under the LPCA, and out of the back of rear pedestal. Allow the cable track to hang freely.

  1. Starting from the front of the LPCA, pull the cable track (the side that includes the Baluns) through the opening on the LPCA in a forward direction as shown in Illustration 9.

    Illustration 9:  Removal Path of P1/P2 Cable Track

4.2  Removal of A-Port Connector Cable Track

When looking at the LPCA from the front, the right track contains the A Port connector, cables and various other functions including the limit switch, microphone cable, etc., and the left track contains the P1 and P2 coil connectors. Each track contains cables that are different from each other, which translates into slight differences in how you remove and install a replacement cable track.

  1. Drive the LPCA and Cradle into bore to within 1 foot (30cm) of end of bridge.

  1. At the foot of the table, twist or squeeze the Cradle Release Handle to detach the Cradle from the LPCA and then pull the cradle by hand to Home position so that it is fully retracted back onto table top.

  1. Undock table and relocate away from magnet.

  1. Perform Lock-out Tag-out for rear pedestal and System Shutdown. For more information, see LOTO for XFRD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics) and System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove both rear pedestal side covers.

  1. Remove the P1 and/or P2 Quick Release Modules. Use sufficient force to pull the module forward so it slides off its track. See Illustration 2.

  1. To create sufficient space for removing and replacing the A-Port Connector Cable Track, remove the two slotted brass screws from the bottom of the P2 cable mount bracket as shown in Illustration 4 and set it aside.

    NOTE:Do not remove the connector from the cable mount bracket.

  1. Disconnect the LED & Switch Harness connector.

    Illustration 10:  LED & Switch Harness

  1. Remove the Microphone cable from the rear of the Mic.

    Illustration 11:  Microphone Cable

  1. Remove four slotted brass screws from top of LPCA bulkhead plate. Be careful while maneuvering wires out of the way when removing screws.

    Illustration 12:  Top of Bulkhead Plate

  1. On the bottom of the bulkhead plate, remove the J1 connector first. Tilt the plate up to allow easier access to remove the retaining screws of J1.

  1. With the plate still tilted, remove the remaining connectors (J2, J4, and J5) from the bottom of the plate.

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head brass screws that secure the cable track lead to the base of the LPCA.

    Illustration 13:  Cable Track Lead Screws

  1. At the back of the LPCA, pull the cable track through the cable frame (opening) of the LPCA Assembly and let it hang loose.

  1. Remove the two (2) standard head screws that secure the cable track lead to the front of the rear pedestal bridge. See Illustration 7.

  1. Disconnect all cable connections that feed from the front end of the cable track to points throughout the rear pedestal. See the table below.

    Table 2:  32 Channel - A-Port Legacy Cable Track connection points

    LPCA EndOther End
    PED-LPCA J1PED-HUB-SLOT1 A
    PED-LPCA J5PED J2, Blue Cable (on the right side)
    PED-LPCA J2PED-HUB-SLOT_11 J6
    Rear MicrophoneRUN M3346 MAG-SRI-J31
    PED-LPCA J4PED-LOAD-A, at the back of the Rear Pedestal

  1. Feed loose end of cable connectors up through the opening at the front of the rear pedestal bridge and let it lay freely on bridge.

    NOTE:Maneuver the J6 connector so that it is level (straight) and angled in a diagonal to pass through the opening in the bridge.

  1. At the front of the rear pedestal bridge, pull the cable track through the cable well until the cable clears the underside of the LPCA.

4.3  Installation of New Cable Track

  1. Move replacement cable track into position.

  1. To ensure proper installation and reconnection of cables, follow the Removal procedures above (Section 4.1 or 4.2) in reverse order.

    NOTE:Wire tie the cables out of the way of the Quick Release Module and Slide Mounting. The Quick Release Module must slide freely without interference.

  1. Slide the Quick Release Module to ensure it is fully inserted and there is no interference from the cable installation.

  1. Apply two drops of Loctite to each screw then connect the four screws that secure track assembly to Bridge and LPCA.

    NOTE:Failure to apply two drops of proper thread locking compound to each cable chain screw before installation may result in screws working loose later and causing spike noise.

  1. Remove Lockout Tagout (For more information see LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics), restore power and start up the software (For more information see System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Replace LPCA cover.

  1. Re-Docking the Patient Table will drive the LPCA to the Home Position and reattach to the Cradle/Patient Table. Be sure to dock the electronics on the 32 channel systems by pressing the foot pedal shown below:

    Illustration 14:  Electronics Docking Pedal
