SRI Board Level Diagnostic

1  Diagnostic Link

Diagnostics >> Hardware Location >> Magnet Room >> SRI Datapaths

Illustration 1:  SRI Board Level Diagnostics

2  Purpose

The purpose of this diagnostic is to check that the SRI’s power-up and required connections are satisfied.

This diagnostic uses the system 1-wire bus to issue a hard reset of the SRI. After the SRI is reset, power-up tests of the RAM and FLASH on the board are performed. The SRI will also check that all required cables are detected and verify that the temperature and airflow sensors are not faulted. For this test to pass, the motor controller (MCPDB) in the PHPS must also be functional.

3  Components Tested

  • SRI

  • Bore and scan room air temperature sensor

  • Gradient coil temperature sensors

  • Airflow sensors

  • Motor controller interface

  • Cable connections tested:

    • position encoder

    • motor encoder

    • table interface box

    • longitudinal drive cable at the SRI

    • longitudinal drive cable at the PHPS

    • PHPS to SRI cable

    • gradient coil temperature

    • airflow sensor

    • bore/scan room temperature

    • bore light

    • alignment light

    • dock power cable

    • dock cable

    • home/end of travel/cradle attached switch

    • motor commutation feedback

4  Requirements

This diagnostic is available from the Common Service Desktop when required system components are available.

A complete system is required. No special hardware or configurations are necessary.

5  Block Diagrams

6  Test Sequence

  1. Select the SRI Board Level Diagnostics test.

  2. Click [Run].

  3. Ensure that the disagnostic passes by reviewing the status in the Test Results table.
