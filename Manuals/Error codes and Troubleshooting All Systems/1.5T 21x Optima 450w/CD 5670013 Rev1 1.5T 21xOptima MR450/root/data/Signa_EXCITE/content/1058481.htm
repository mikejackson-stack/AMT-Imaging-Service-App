Hard Disk Drive Replacement for HP8400

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hour30 mins

2  Overview

Because of the way the hard disk drives are partitioned, if one of the drives becomes corrupted and has no read/write capabilities, information saved on the system (since the most recent archive) will be lost. Use the HP Diagnostics Tool CD to identify the defective hard drive.

3.3  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages Exist Inside The Global operator console (Goc) when Power Is Applied.
Before Servicing Any Components Inside The Goc, Perform lock out/tag out.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

4  Procedure

  1. Please request the customer to archive all images to an external storage device (for example PACS, DVDs, etc.).

  1. Perform the Save Info procedure. For details, see Save and Restore Tool.

  1. Perform the LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace procedure before beginning the replacement.

  1. Remove the two screws that secure the left side panel.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by lifting up. Avoid straining the short ground lead that connects the cover to the GOC chassis. It can be disconnected at the middle junction if necessary.

    Illustration 2:  Left Side Panel Ground Lead

  1. Lift up on the latch (as shown), and remove the left side panel of the PC.

    Illustration 3:  Removing Left PC Panel

  1. Remove the power cable and the data cable (locations shown below) from the defective hard disk drive.

    Illustration 4:  Removing Power and Data Cables

  1. Remove the defective drive by pinching the green restraining tabs together as shown, then slowly pulling the drive out.

    Illustration 5:  Restraining Tabs on Disk Drive

  1. Confirm that the data cables from all three drives are connected to the correct SAS device locations as shown in the illustration and table below.

    Illustration 6:  SAS Device Locations

    Hard Disk Drive Connections:

    Drive SlotDrive IDSAS ID
    TopHDD2SAS2
    MiddleHDD1SAS1
    BottomHDD0SAS0

  1. Install the replacement hard drive by reversing Step 7 through Step 9.

5  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Perform the applicable Loading Host System Software procedure to load software and applications onto the operating system.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
