RF Hub Boards and Chassis Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Projectile Hazard
The RF Control Board and Power Distribution Board contain some ferrous material and can be attracted to the magnet.
Do not place RF control board or power distribution board on top of rear pedestal or near magnet (less than 2 ft away). Use caution when removing boards.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Follow all Lockout/Tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breakers in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU). See LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

-

3  Procedure

3.1   RF Switch Board

Multiple versions of the RF boards may have been used, but the location in the RF Hub Chassis and the replacement procedures are the same.

Illustration 2:  RF Switch Board Versions

3.1.1  Removing the RF Switch Board

  1. Remove the side covers from the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Turn off the power switch on the Power Distribution Board. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove cables on front of the RF Switch Board.

  1. Loosen the top and bottom screws on front of the RF Switch Board.

  1. Lift-up on the top and bottom release levers to eject the RF Switch Board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide out the RF Switch Board from the RF Chassis.

  1. Place the board in an ESD bag and remove it from the magnet room.

3.1.2  Installing the RF Switch Board

  1. Slide the new RF Switch Board into the RF Chassis. Make sure the board is in the track.

  1. Line up the mechanical lever groove with the chassis edge and lower the levers to insert the board. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the RF Switch Board to the RF Chassis.

  1. Attach cables to the front of the RF Switch Board.

3.4   Power Distribution Board

3.4.1  Removing the Power Distribution Board

  1. Remove the side covers from the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Turn off the power switch on the Power Distribution Board. See Illustration 1.

  1. Turn off the power switch on the Scan Room Power Supply.

  1. Remove the cables on front of the Power Distribution Board.

  1. Loosen the top and bottom screws on front of the Power Distribution Board.

  1. Lift-up on the top and bottom release levers to eject the Power Distribution Board.

  1. Slide out the Power Distribution Board from the RF Chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the terminator (shunt connector) on Slot J3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the board in an ESD bag and remove it from the magnet room.

3.4.2  Installing the Power Distribution Board

  1. Slide the new Power Distribution Board into the RF Chassis.

  1. Line up the mechanical lever groove with the chassis edge and lower the levers to insert the board. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the Power Distribution Board to the RF Chassis.

  1. Attach the cables to the front of the Power Distribution Board.

  1. Attach the previously used terminator on Slot J3.

  1. Turn on the Scan Room Power Supply.
