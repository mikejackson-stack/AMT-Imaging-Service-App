3  Procedure

3.1  Removing the Hydraulic Rotary Pump

  1. Undock Patient Table and move it out of magnet room.

  1. Remove Lower Base Side Covers. Refer to the Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Cover Removal procedure.

  1. Raise Patient Table to full up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Set Caster Brake to keep Table stationary.

    Illustration 1:  Caster Brake

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar.

    NOTE:Lower Table so its weight is resting on the lock bar.

    Illustration 2:  Lock Safety Bar

  1. Remove Front Base Cover from the Table.

  1. To remove the Table Frame Connector assembly (32 channel only), do the following:

    Illustration 3:  Table Frame Connector Assembly

    1. Remove the e-clip from the bottom of each Rocker Shaft that holds the Rocker Clamp in place. If desired, remove the e-clip from the upper part of the Rocker Shaft above the Rocker Clamp.

    1. Slide the Rocker Shafts up to remove.

    1. Mark the location of cable track screws (for repositioning later) and then loosen the cable track screws.

      Illustration 4:  Cable Track Screws

    1. Slide the Table Frame Connector assembly forward off its track. If you do not have enough cable slack to slide off its track, remove the cable track screws completely.

    1. Move the entire assembly aside to reveal the Rotary Pump assembly below.

  1. On the left side of the pump, use a temporary clamp for the transparent oil hose to prevent oil from draining the reservoir.

  1. Put a towel under the area, and then remove plastic clamp on transparent hose on pump and remove hose from pump. Position end of hose in upright position to prevent oil from leaking.

    Illustration 5:  Brass Fitting and Clamp

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL!
ROTARY PUMP CONTAINS STEEL. THE PUMP'S PRIMARY SUBSTANCE IS STEEL AND POSES EXTREMELY DANGEROUS PROJECTILE HAZARD IF WORKING IN MAGNET ROOM.
ENSURE YOU ARE PERFORMING PROCEDURE OUTSIDE OF MAGNET ROOM.

  1. On the right side of the pump, use an 11/16 open-end wrench to disconnect the manifold nut from the connector fitting.

    NOTE:Use a towel as plug to stop flow of fluid.

    Illustration 6:  Manifold Pipe Attached to Elbow Fitting on Side of Rotary Pump

  1. Remove three brass screws from the lower front Table base. After screw removal, the pump assembly hangs freely and should allow sufficient movement. Use care in holding the Rotary Pump, the brass plate, and the Guide Coupling in place as those components (as a unit) will fall straight down.

    Illustration 7:  Front Table Base

    NOTE:Place absorbent towels underneath work area.

3.2  Installing the Hydraulic Rotary Pump

  1. Apply Loctite 242 to the three brass screws and reattach the pump brass plate to the front Table base. See Illustration 7.

  1. Reattach the manifold pipe to the right side of the pump.

  1. Move the hydraulic oil hose into position at the left side of pump, remove the temporary clamp, and reconnect the hydraulic oil hose and secure with fittings. Position permanent clamp close to hex of brass fitting and away from Down Rod so the permanent clamp and the Down Rod do not touch.

    Illustration 8:  Hydraulic Oil Hose, Fittings, Clamp

  1. To reassemble Table Frame Connector assembly, do the following:

    1. Slide assembly back onto its track.

    1. Tighten the cable track screws in their original position.

    1. Replace rocker shaft in Rocker Clamp making sure the rocker shaft slides through the Rod Link Connector on each side.

    1. Replace the e-clip on the bottom (and top if removed) of each shaft on each side of frame.

    1. Dock the Table, hold open the down valve and run the rotary pump for 30 seconds.

    1. Raise the Table. Raise the Table Lock Safety Bar to its upright position.

    1. Replace front Table cover.

  1. If necessary, add hydraulic oil to reservoir. Level should be 6.5 to 12.5 mm above “FULL LINE” when Table is at lowest position.

    Illustration 9:  Hydraulic Fluid Reservoir

  1. To ensure that there is no air deposited in the Hydraulic cylinder from the Hydraulic lines, perform the Patient Table Hydraulics Bleeding procedure. Check fittings for leaks.

  1. Replace the Upper and Lower Base covers.

4  Finalization

In a service browser, run the Coil Datapath Diagnostic tool. Verify the connector works properly.
