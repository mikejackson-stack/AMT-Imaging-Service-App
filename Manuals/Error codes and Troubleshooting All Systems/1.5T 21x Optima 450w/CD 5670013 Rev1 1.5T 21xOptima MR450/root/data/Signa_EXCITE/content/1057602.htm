3  Procedure

3.1  Cradle Release Block Position Adjustment

  1. Undock Patient Table and remove from magnet room.

  1. Remove the Patient Table bottom front cover.

    Illustration 1:  GEM Bottom Front Cover

  1. Loosen four (4) hex socket head screws securing the release block assembly.

    Illustration 2:  Bottom View of Cradle Release Block Assembly

  1. Adjust the housing's position so as to minimize the distance between the latch and the latch housing. Cycle (press) the latch several times, to ensure that the latch never sticks in the “down” position.

    Illustration 3:  Cradle Release Latch - Right Position

    Illustration 4:  Cradle Release Latch - Wrong Position

3.2  Cradle Release Block Retraction Adjustment

  1. Dock Patient Table.

  1. Remove Upper and Lower Base Covers. Refer to Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Cover Removal.

  1. Loosen the lock nut, then tighten or loosen the cable adjustment screw in the pivot block so that the cradle release block is flush or just below the level of the table top.

    Illustration 5:  Cradle Release Block Retraction Adjustment

  1. Re-tighten lock nut.

    NOTE:If the adjustment screw does not provide enough range for adjustment, coarse adjustment may be made to the cable length where the cable end is clamped into actuator bracket. Fine adjustment will be required after performing a coarse adjustment.

  1. Replace Upper and Lower Base Covers as well as Patient Table bottom front cover.

4  Finalization

Dock table to magnet and ensure table/cradle functions. Ensure that the table undocks and LPCA is released when the undock pedal is depressed.
