RF Field Safety

General Electric Company policy specifically prohibits service personnel from using any part of their body for a phantom, or dummy, load while testing the power amplifier or related components in the RF subsystem. This restriction protects service personnel from inadvertent overexposure to the heating effects of high energy RF fields, which could occur while servicing with certain safety features bypassed or otherwise disabled.
