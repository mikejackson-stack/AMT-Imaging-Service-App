3  Procedure

3.1  Removing the XGD Fan Box Assembly

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove cables J13 and J14 from the XGA to expose the fan. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Remove cables from XGA

  1. Remove cable J2 from the fan. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  XGD Fan Box Assembly

  1. Loosen the two (2) screw securing the fan to the cabinet. See Illustration 2.

  1. Pull out the fan from the cabinet.

3.2  Re-installing the XGD Fan Box Assembly

  1. Slide in the new fan until it is fully seated in the cabinet.

  1. Tighten the two (2) screws to secure the fan to the cabinet.

  1. Re-attach cable J2 to the fan.

  1. Re-attach cables J13 and J14 to the XGA.

4  Finalization

Restore power using the LOTO for the PGR/PDU/Gradient Subsystems. See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystemdocument for details. Check and ensure the Fan is operating appropriately.
