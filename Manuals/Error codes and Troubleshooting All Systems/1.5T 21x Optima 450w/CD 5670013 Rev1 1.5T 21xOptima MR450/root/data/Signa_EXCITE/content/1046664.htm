LCD Monitor Screen Adjustments

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
10 min5 mins0 min

2  Overview

The intent of this section is to adjust the monitor to a default condition and proceed with calibrations.

  • See LCD Monitor Input Priority Setting (for VP231 wb and VP2330 wb)

  • See Calibrate ViewSonic Monitor (for VP231 wb and VP 2330 wb)

  • See Calibrate NEC Monitor (for LCD2490WUXi and LCD2490WUXi2)

3  Procedure

3.1  LCD Monitor Input Priority Setting (for VP231 wb and VP2330 wb)

  1. Set up the Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) monitor input priority according to the instructions below.

    Illustration 1:  LCD Monitor Input Priority Setting

    NOTE:In case NO D-sub displays with a black window and the image does not display on the monitor, press the <Ctrl> + <Alt> + <Backspace> keys simultaneously.

  1. Continue to the next section.

3.2  Calibrate ViewSonic Monitor (for VP231 wb and VP 2330 wb)

  1. Refer to the illustration below for control locations and functions.

    Illustration 2:  ViewSonic VP231wb and VP2330wb OnView Controls

  1. Find the model number in the upper right corner on the front of the monitor and record it.

    Illustration 3:  Locating Monitor Model Number

  1. To display the Main Menu, press the [1] button shown below.

    Illustration 4:  Main Menu

    NOTE:All OnView menus and adjustment screens disappear automatically after about 30 seconds.

  1. Modify the Red-Green-Blue (RGB) setting by navigating to Color Adjust -> User Color.

    • If this is a VP2330wb, continue to Step 5.

    • If this is a VP231wb, skip to Step 6.

  1. (For VP2330wb) Numeric values are used for adjustments. Calibrate the monitor to match the settings in the table below.

    NOTE:Contrast/Brightness and Color Adjust are different menu selections.

    Table 1:  Required Settings

    Contrast/Brightness
    Contrast60
    Brightness50
    Color Adjust
    Hue50
    Saturation50
    Color Adjust6500K

  1. (For VP231wb) A green slider (shown below) is used for adjustments instead of numerical values. To calibrate the monitor, count the major tick marks (total of 14) to determine the proper slider location.

    • The slider is set at 9.75. Calibrate the monitor to match the settings in the table that follows.

    • Contrast/Brightness and Color Adjust are different menu selections.

    Illustration 5:  On-Screen Adjustments

3.3  Calibrate NEC Monitor (for LCD2490WUXi and LCD2490WUXi2)

  1. See the two illustrations that follow for control locations and functions.

    Illustration 6:  NEC On/View Controls

    Illustration 7:  Control Panel

    NameDescription
    1 Ambi-Bright SensorDetects the level of ambient lighting allowing the monitor to make adjustments to various settings resulting in a more comfortable viewing experience.

    Do not cover this sensor.

    2 PowerTurns the monitor on and off.
    3 LEDIndicates the power is on.

    Can be changed between blue and green in the Advanced On-Screen Manager (OSM) Control menu.

    4 Input/SelectEnters the OSM Control menu and OSM sub menus.

    Changes the input source when not in the OSM Control menu.

    5 ExitExits the OSM Control menu and sub menu.
    6 Left/Right*Navigates to the left or right through the OSM Control menu.
    7 Up/Down*Navigates up or down through the OSM Control menu.
    8 Reset/Rotate OSMResets the OSM back to factory settings.

    Pressing this button when the OSM is not displaying rotates the OSM Control menu between portrait and landscape mode.

    9 Key GuideDisplays on the screen when the OSM Control menu is accessed.

    The Key Guide rotates when the OSM Control menu is rotated.

    * The Left/Right and Up/Down buttons functionality is interchangeable depending on the orientation (landscape/portrait) of the OSM.

4  Finalization

  1. To save adjustments, press the [1] button.

  1. To exit the menu, press the [1] button again.
