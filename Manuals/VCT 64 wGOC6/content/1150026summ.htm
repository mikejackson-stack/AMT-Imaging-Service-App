Auto Alignment Light Setting

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Enable procedure

  1. Open Unix shell.

  1. Type cd /usr/g/GEfirm , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type setAutoAlignmentLight , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type 1 , press [Enter] key.

  1. Restart application or reboot the system.

2.2   Disable procedure

  1. Open Unix shell.

  1. Type cd /usr/g/GEfirm , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type setAutoAlignmentLight , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type 0 , press [Enter] key.

  1. Restart application or reboot the system.
