• OS drive resides in slot 1 (bottom) -- label white SCSI connector tag as “Channel 1”.

  • Image disk 1 resides in slot 2 -- label white SCSI connector tag as “Channel 2”.

  • Image disk 2 resides in slot 3 -- label white SCSI connector tag as “Channel 2”.

  • (For PET Systems only) PET Image Database Drive resides in slot 4 (top-most) -- this 4th disk drive will be on SCSI Channel 2 as Image disk 1 and 2. It will no longer be connected to SCSI Channel 1.
