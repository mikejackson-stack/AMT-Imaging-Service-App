Gantry Cover Tilt Sensor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Preparation

  1. Move Table to the home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and front cover.

1.2   Front Cover tilt sensor

  1. On the back of the front cover disconnect touch strip cable from the tilt sensor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 2 nuts (9/32 inch socket) that secure touch sensor pad to the front cover.

  1. Install new touch pad and tighten the 2 nuts to the following values.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Rear Cover tilt sensor

  1. On the back of the back cover disconnect touch strip cable from the tilt sensor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 4 nuts (9/32 inch socket) that secure touch sensor pad to the cover.

  1. Install new touch pad and tighten the 4 nuts to the following values.

    (Step Details)

1.4   Gantry reassembly

  1. Using an ohm meter, check the tilt sensor by connecting the ohm meter to the two lines from the sensor at the connector. The tilt sensor should read 20K ohms prior to pressing on the sensor and then approximately 20 ohms when the sensor is pressed. Check the torque on the nuts if this check fails.

  1. Reconnect the cable.

  1. Install the gantry front cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

2  Finalization

  1. From the front cover control panel, press and hold the gantry forward tilt button and then press on the tilt sensor while still tilting gantry. Verify the gantry tilt stops.
