2  Overview

This procedure is used to check the tube temperature value.

Last revised: October 14, 2009

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

4  Procedure

  1. Click [DAILY PREP].

  1. Click [TUBE WARM-UP].

  1. Click [ACCEPT & RUN TUBE WARMUP] in Warning window.

    NOTE: If Attention window is displayed, click [OK].

  1. Click [PAUSE] in Scan list pane.

  1. Click [CANCEL] in Scan list pane.

NOTICE
Do not start X-ray exposures. Starting tube warm-up will result in additional tube cooling wait times.
 

  1. Click [CURRENT MESSAGE] from right monitor.

  1. Click [VIEW LOG].

  1. Click [LAST PAGE].

  1. Read the last entry of the following statement:

    Tube temperature after Cold Tube Warmup is xxx.xx degrees Celsius.

    or

    Tube Temperature after Quality Control Scan is xxx.xx degrees Celsius.

    where xxx.xx is the tube temperature value.

  1. Repeat steps 2 through 5 and steps 8 through 9, as necessary to generate updates.

  1. Click [CLOSE] in Message Log Report window.

  1. Click [QUIT] in Daily Preparation window.

5  Finalization

No finalization steps.
