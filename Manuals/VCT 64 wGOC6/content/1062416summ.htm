3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove VeRB Computer

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove console's Keyboard Tabletop and set aside. This will allow better access to the console's computer rack.

  1. Remove the cable connections at the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis being replaced.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis.

  1. Remove the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws and washers from the front of the VeRB Computer chassis, holding the chassis to the Operator Console rack.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the VeRB Computer chassis forward (out the front of console) and set aside.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Install Replacement VeRB Computer

  1. Slide the replacement VeRB Computer into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Replace the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws and washers to secure the VeRB Computer chassis to the Operator Console rack. Torque to 4.6 N-m.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis and verify that its power supply switch is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear cable connection(s).

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

  1. Do not allow Applications to start. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

3.5   Verify Replacement VeRB Computer Operation

  1. Verify that the VeRB Computer has powered up and is not displaying or sounding any POST Errors.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the VeRB computer, perform the System Configuration procedure.

    1. Open a Terminal window and log on as Root.

    1. Type: reconfig[Enter]

    1. Click[Accept]

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the System Configuration procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers and Keyboard Tabletop assembly.
