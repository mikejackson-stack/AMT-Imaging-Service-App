1  Safety

WARNING
POTENTIAL FOR ELECTRIC SHOCK
VOLTAGES MAY BE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING COMPONENTS.

WARNING
POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY (PINCH, CRUSH, ENTANGLEMENT OR IMPACT INJURIES ARE POSSIBLE.)
GANTRY HAS ROTATING ASSEMBLY
USE THE GANTRY ROTATIONAL LOCK, TO PREVENT UNCOMMANDED MOTION OF THE GANTRY ROTATING ASSEMBLY.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

NOTICE
The heat exchanger may take on excessive amounts of coolant due to thermal expansion. If the tube is not allowed to cool prior to tube change, the system will not scan and the heat exchanger will require replacement. Wait at least 1 hour after the last scan before disconnecting the tube from the heat exchanger.
 

NOTE:Prior to removal of the failed tube, check for insert failure by manually slowly rotating the gantry through a full 720 degrees of rotation both clockwise and counterclockwise. If the sound of moving debris or broken glass is heard, replace tank and heat exchanger at the same time as tube.

3  Procedure

3.1   Tube Removal

  1. Move the table to its home position.

  1. Remove the right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Remove M12 bolts holding right front gantry cover mounting bracket (See Illustration 1) with 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank is in 3 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Cut Ty-wraps on Inverter and Auxiliary Box clamps and remove High Voltage cable from clamps.

  1. Loosen High Voltage cable locking ring with spanner wrench.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable candlestick and place a cover over candlestick.

  1. With Dry Wipes, soak up the oil in the High Voltage Tank receptacle.

  1. Place a cap or paper towel in top of receptacle to prevent residual oil spillage and dirt from contaminating receptacle.

  1. Remove Failsafe C-spacers from left and right sides of Tube with a 5mm Allen Wrench ( See Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Rotate gantry such that X-Ray Tube is in 3 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully cut Ty-wraps and disconnect thermal sensor cable from Heat Exchanger Power Interface Board J7 connector.

  1. Remove dial-mounting bracket with a flat blade screw driver (See Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully cut Ty-wraps and disconnect anode stator cable from Auxiliary Box J1 connector.

  1. Remove clamp holding anode stator cable to Auxiliary Box.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Z-Alignment adjuster block assembly with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Pump-to-Tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align quick-disconnect pin with slot and disconnect Pump from Tube.

  1. Remove Heat Exchanger-to-Tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align quick-disconnect pin with slot and disconnect Heat Exchanger from Tube.

  1. Insert lifting post into gantry frame and attach boom and hoist.

  1. Disconnect Smart ID cable from Tube Smart ID board and cut Ty-wrap holding cable to Tube lifting bracket.

  1. Connect hoist hook to Tube lifting point and remove all slack in chain.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two bottom M12 mounting bolts with 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two upper M12 mounting bolts with 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cover from new Tube crate, flip it over and place it on floor below suspended old tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place old Tube on crate cover for shipping (See Illustration 4) and remove hoist hook from Tube lifting point.

    Illustration 4:  Hoisting Tube

  1. Secure old Tube to Tube crate cover using adjustable straps.

3.2   Lexan Window Check

WARNING

This step is only for removing plastic window from Collimator input (Tube-side) port. Do not remove plastic window from Collimator output (patient-side) port.

  1. Before installing new Tube, check Collimator Tube-side port on interposer plate.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Tube Installation

  1. Allow new Tube to warm to room temperature before installation.

  1. Place new Tube crate on floor near gantry where it can be accessed by hoist.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Tube crate center section and attach to Tube crate cover holding old Tube.

  1. Remove adjustable straps from new Tube.

  1. Attach hoist hook to Tube lifting point and lift new Tube free from Tube crate.

    (Step Details)

  1. With Tube suspended, remove protective cover and visually inspect copper filter (See Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Illustration 7:  Overheated copper filter

    Carefully swing Tube into position on gantry.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using new pre-assembled mounting hardware shipped with Tube, align Tube mounting through-holes with Collimator frame mounting points.

    (Step Details)

  1. Hand turn four M12 mounting bolts into Tube holes a few turns.

    (Step Details)

  1. Detach hoist hook from Tube lifting point.

  1. Disengage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Rotate Tube to 12:00 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Move Tube until it settles into its centered position on interposer plate.

  1. Visually inspect that Tube position is close to centered in Z-direction.

  1. Tighten each M12 mounting bolt on Tube assembly in a cross pattern configuration, with a 10mm hex bit socket torque wrench and 12 inch extension, to the following torque value.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place original Tube crate base on top of crated old Tube and secure its locking hardware.

  1. Move Tube crate away from gantry to provide easier access.

  1. Remove hoist/boom/post assembly from gantry.

  1. Connect Smart ID cable to Smart ID board and Ty-wrap cable to Tube lifting bracket.

  1. Connect Heat Exchanger hose male quick-disconnect to Tube female quick-disconnect.

  1. Connect Pump hose female quick-disconnect to Tube male quick-disconnect.

  1. Secure Heat Exchanger-to-Tube and Pump-to-Tube quick-disconnect safety mechanisms with hardware shipped with Tube.

  1. Tighten each safety mechanism M6 bolt, with a 5mm hex bit socket torque wrench, to the following torque value.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach Z-Alignment adjuster block assembly with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

  1. Connect anode stator cable to Auxiliary Box J1 connector.

  1. Place clamp around anode stator cable exposed shield and attach clamp to Auxiliary Box with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

  1. Attach dial-mounting bracket to Tube and tighten each captive screw with a flat blade bit torque driver to 0.45 N-m or 4.0 lbf-in.

  1. Connect thermal sensor cable to Heat Exchanger Power Interface Board J7 connector and appropriately secure with Ty-wrap(s).

  1. Attach 2 Failsafe C-spacers to right and left sides of Tube with a 5mm Allen Wrench (See Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 3 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Replace High Voltage Tank well O-ring with new O-ring shipped with Tube.

  1. Perform Securing HV Cable.

  1. Install right gantry front cover bracket (See Illustration 1).

  1. Manually rotate gantry, at least one full revolution, and verify that no rubbing occurs.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.
