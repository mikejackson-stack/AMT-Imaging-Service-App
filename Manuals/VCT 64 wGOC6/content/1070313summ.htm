Filament Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Remove any objects from scan window area which may interfere with the rotating gantry.

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For VCT type X-ray Generation Systems.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI], or [X-RAY GENERATION], [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI].

  1. Click [FILAMENT CAL].

  1. Verify that [DUCTILITY WARM UP], [LARGE FILAMENT CAL], and [SMALL FILAMENT CAL] buttons are highlighted as selections.

  1. Click [SCAN].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [CANCEL] in Generator Filament Calibration window.

  1. Click [DISMISS] in Generator Tool - JEDI window.
