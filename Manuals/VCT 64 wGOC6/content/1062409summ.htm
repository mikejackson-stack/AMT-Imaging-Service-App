LCD Monitor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Access to the Operator Console Tabletop is required. Make sure adequate space is available on the console.

-

-

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

Consideration should be given to the age of the LCD Monitor being replaced. If LCD Monitor is several years old, the back lighting of the display will have degraded. Replacing one LCD Monitor may result in unequal display quality when comparing the newly installed replacement LCD Monitor to that of the other display. Consider replacing both LCD Monitors if these conditions apply. Also Note: If a NEC 1990 SXi monitor is being replaced with a EIZO S1921-X due to unavailability of the older NEC LCD Monitor, consider replacing both NEC LCD Monitors to maintain equal display quality.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove Old LCD Monitor

  1. Disconnect the Power Cord and Video Cable from the LCD Monitor being replaced.

  1. Remove the LCD Monitor and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement LCD Monitor

  1. Place the replacement LCD monitor on the Operator Console.

  1. Plug in Video Cable in the SVGA connector on the LCD Monitor and secure the two (2) retaining screws.

  1. Reconnect the Power Cord to the LCD Monitor

    (Step Details)

3.4   Verify LCD Monitor Replacement

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Make sure the Power Switch on the Monitor is turned on. (NEC - Front and Side panels of the LCD Monitor, EIZO - Front panel of LCD Monitor)

  1. Visually verify that the LCD Monitor powers up and receives a video signal.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the LCD Monitor it is necessary to perform LCD Video Monitor Setup.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the LCD Monitor Setup Procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.
