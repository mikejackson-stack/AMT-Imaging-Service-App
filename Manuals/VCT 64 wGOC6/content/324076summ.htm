DCB and DIFB Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Card Access

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Stop the rotor of X-ray tube in case of Liquid Bearing Tube before HVDC off. Refer to Liquid Bearing Tube Rotor stop procedure for details.

  1. Disable Axial drive and HVDC on the service switch panel.

  1. Position the gantry for easy access to the DIFB chassis required and lock gantry rotation.

  1. The DCB and DIFB cards are located behind the DAS on either side of the 180 degree weights. See Illustration 2 - VCT DAS Side View.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disable gantry 120 VAC from the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the top cover for easier access to the DIFB chassis.

  1. Remove/loosen the cover screws on the top of the DIFB chassis. See Illustration 3 - DCB/DIFB Chassis Top View.

    (Step Details)

  1. Grab the cover on both sides using your thumbs to press in on the cover pin release tabs and lift the cover straight up and off the chassis. The tabs push a spring-loaded pin in the chassis frame. The cover is kept in line with the chassis by side channels in the chassis.

2.2   Card Removal

  1. If removing the DCB, first disconnect the 2 fiber optic connections from the front side of the DAS plate.

  1. Remove the DAS board (DCB or DIFB) as required.

    1. Grab the board by the metal tab and pull away from backplane connector to remove board.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the board from the chassis.

2.3   Card Installation

  1. Insert the new card and push in on the card tab with your finger to seat the card into the backplane connector. You may need to shift the card up/down in the card guide slightly to align the connector. Gentle pressure is all that is needed to seat the connector in the backplane once the connector is aligned.

    (Step Details)

  1. If installing a DCB, reinstall the fiber optic cable connection.

  1. Turn on the Gantry 120 VAC service switch and press the drives reset button on the service switch panel.

  1. Run the Flash download tool to make sure the card replaced is running the latest firmware. A console pop up message may also indicate that this is required after turning the DAS back on. Watch the LED's to make sure status is consistent after a reset is completed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the Gantry 120 VAC service switch.

  1. Install the DIFB card cage cover making sure the cover base tabs are in the slots at the base of the chassis.

  1. Install the 4 captive cover screws and Torque to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the gantry top cover.

  1. Turn on the Gantry 120 VAC, HVDC and Axial Drive service switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

3  Finalization

  1. Run the System scanning test from the Functional Checks procedure list.
