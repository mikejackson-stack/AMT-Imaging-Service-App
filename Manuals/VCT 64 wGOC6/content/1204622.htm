4  Procedure

4.1  PMT Removal

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button on desktop display and select [Shutdown].

    1. If Applications are down, open a Terminal Window. Type: halt , then press <Enter>.

  1. When halt command has finished, power Off the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Apply LOTO for console. For procedures, see Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE.

  1. Disconnect cables on the rear of the PMT assembly. Make certain these cables are clearly labeled.

    Illustration 2:  PMT Connections

    ItemDescriptionDestination
    1AC Power OutletNot Used
    2AC Power InletRear Bulkhead - J5
    3DVD USB InputNIO64HD Computer - USB 5 (System Board)
    4External USB (PMT Front Panel USB)NIO64HD Computer - USB 7 (Expansion Card)

  1. Remove the original PMT from GOC6.6 console and set aside.

4.2  PMT Install

  1. Place replacement PMT on GOC6.6 VCT console.

  1. Reconnect all cables removed earlier.

  1. Remove LOTO on console. For procedures, see Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE.

4.3  Functional Checks, Alignments and Setups

  1. Power On console.

  1. Confirm that the power switch on the rear of the PMT assembly is turned on.

  1. Confirm that the green Power Status LED on the PMT front panel is lit.

    Illustration 3:  PMT Power Status LED
