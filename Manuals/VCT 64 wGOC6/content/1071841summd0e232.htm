NOTE:GOC6 Series Console Specific: Will not be displayed on older GOC consoles. This selection only appears after a new hard drive has been installed in the HSDA of a GOC6 Series console and ample time (2 minutes) has been given for the HSDA to recognize the installation of a new drive. Non-destructive. NOTE: New Hard Drive will be rebuilt and included into the existing logical disk volume. Depending on size of data needed to rebuilt on new drive, this process can take 30 minutes or more to complete. System scanning is not permitted during this time.
