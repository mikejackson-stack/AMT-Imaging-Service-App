Tilt Potentiometer Replacement (New)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD.
THIS SERVICE PROCEDURE REQUIRES THE GANTRY TO BE TILTED ALL THE WAY BACK IN ITS MINUS 30 DEGREE POSiTION AND PLACED ON ITS MECHANICAL STOPS.

WARNING
Potential for Shock
Voltage is present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation

  1. If the gantry is not at zero tilt, use the gantry control panel to tilt the gantry close to zero tilt.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the table up to bore level and all the way back.

  1. Stop the rotor of X-ray tube in case of Liquid Bearing Tube before HVDC off. Refer to Liquid Bearing Tube Rotor stop procedure for details.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Gantry left side, top, front and rear covers.

  1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC service switch, and tilt the gantry all the way back to the tilt stop blocks using Tilt Service Switch with Tilt enable.

  1. Turn off the gantry 120VAC service switch.

3.2   Tilt Feedback Assembly Removal

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the cable of the potentiometer to the gantry frame.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable connector of potentiometer from TGPG.

  1. Press the tilt belt in middle of the belt span, and make sure of the degree of the tightened belt.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen two (2) hex screws holding the tilt feedback assembly to the gantry frame, to remove tension from the belt, and remove the belt.

  1. Remove the two (2) hex screws, and remove the tilt feedback assembly from gantry.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Tilt Feedback Assembly Installation

  1. Set the potentiometer cable along the left edge of the mounting bracket, and fix the cable to the bracket using tie-wraps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Put the tilt feedback assembly in place, and install the two hex screws loosely to hold the tilt feedback assembly in place.

  1. Position the belt on and around the tilt motor pulley and the small pulley of potentiometer.

  1. Move the tilt feedback assembly position to maintain a proper degree of tension (the same tension as before) for tilt belt.

  1. Tighten the two (2) hex screws to the following torque values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the potentiometer cable to the TGPU.

  1. Using tie-wraps, secure the Tilt Pot cable to the gantry.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 1.5 mm hex key, loosen the two hex screws on the front of the tilt pot pulley (do NOT remove the screws). This allows the tilt indicator disk to freely rotate for alignment.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Tilt Alignment and Characterization

  1. Clear all tools from the gantry.

  1. Using the Service Switches, turn ON 120 VAC, Service Mode and Tilt Enable.

  1. Push down on the Tilt Service Switch to tilt the gantry backwards (BWD) to the mechanical stops at -31° degrees.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the Tilt Indicator Disk to -31°.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 1.5 mm hex key, tighten the two hex screws on the front of the tilt pot pulley. Refer to Tilt Feedback Assembly Installation, Step 8.

  1. Perform Tilt Characterization. Check for possible interference.

    1. Go to Service Desktop - Calibration - Mechanical Calibration - [Characterize Tilt]

    1. Follow on screen instructions.

      (Step Details)

  1. Tilt the gantry back to home position and confirm the Tilt Indicator Disk lines up at zero degrees.

  1. Install Gantry Front and Rear cover.

  1. At Service Switch Panel, turn OFF Tilt Enable and Service Mode. Turn ON Axial Enable and HVDC.

  1. Re-install Left and Right Side covers.

4  Finalization

  1. Run the System scanning test from the Functional Checks procedure list.

  1. Run the Quality Assurance test from the Functional Checks procedure list.

  1. Save System State.
