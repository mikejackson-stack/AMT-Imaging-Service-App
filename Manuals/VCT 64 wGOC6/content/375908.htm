4  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the IMS and Cradle to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove the following Table covers:

    • Cradle

    • Top Cover (Right/Left)

    • Rear Top Cover

    • Rail Cover

    Illustration 1:  Table Covers

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

    Illustration 2:  Table Rails

  1. Grease the cradle linear guide:

    1. Move the cradle carriage forward until the grease nipple of the cradle linear guide is visible.

    1. Attach the grease gun to the grease nipple of the cradle linear guide, and inject the grease until new grease starts to leak.

    1. Move the cradle carriage (In limit ↔ Out limit) a few times by hand.

      Illustration 3:  Grease Nipple for Cradle Linear Guide

  1. Grease the IMS linear guide:

    1. Move the IMS forward until the rear grease nipple of the IMS linear guide is visible.

    1. Attach the grease gun to the grease nipple of the IMS linear guide, and inject the grease until new grease starts to leak.

    1. Attach the grease gun to the front grease nipple of the IMS linear guide, and inject the grease until new grease starts to leak.

    1. Move the IMS (In limit ↔ Out limit) a few times by hand.

      Illustration 4:  Grease Nipples for IMS Linear Guide

  1. Wipe off any excess grease.

5  Finalization

  1. Re-install the cradle and Table covers.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Verify that the cradle and IMS movement are smooth and normal.
