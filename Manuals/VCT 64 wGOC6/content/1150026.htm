2  Overview

The “Auto Alignment Light” is a function that automatically turns on the Gantry Alignment Light by pressing Table foot switch when the gantry is at home position.

If the gantry is not at home position, neither the alignment light turns on nor gantry rotates.

This function is supported by the following products:

  • Optima CT660 system with 11HW34.16 or later software

  • VCT FREEdom (13HW31.x or later)

Last revised:December 17, 2013

4  Procedure

4.1  Enable procedure

  1. Open Unix shell.

  1. Type cd /usr/g/GEfirm , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type setAutoAlignmentLight , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type 1 , press [Enter] key.

  1. Restart application or reboot the system.

4.2  Disable procedure

  1. Open Unix shell.

  1. Type cd /usr/g/GEfirm , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type setAutoAlignmentLight , press [Enter] key.

  1. Type 0 , press [Enter] key.

  1. Restart application or reboot the system.

    NOTE:This setting is saved/restored by System State.
