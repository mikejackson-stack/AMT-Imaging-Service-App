Console Rear Cooling Fan Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

DANGER
CRUSH HAZARD
WITH CONSOLE REAR COVER REMOVED AND POWER APPLIED TO OPERATOR CONSOLE, THE OUTSIDE OF THE COOLING FANS BLADES ARE EXPOSED.
TAKE CARE TO KEEP FINGERS AND OTHER OBJECTS CLEAR OF SPINNING FANS.

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Access to both the front and rear of the Operator Console is required. Move the console away from walls and remove obstacles in room.

-

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from the rear of the console.

3.2   Remove Existing Fan

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure..

  1. Disconnect power to the failed Fan. Firmly grasp the power connector with one hand and pull power connector from fan. (See illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) 4mm socket cap screws, nuts, and washers that hold the Fan to the mounting bracket. Save the screws and the fan guard.

  1. Remove Fan and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement Fan

  1. Install the replacement fan using the four (4) 4mm socket cap screws, nuts, washers and fan guard removed from the previous steps. Torque to 1.0 N-m.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reattach the power connector on the replaced fan.

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

DANGER: CRUSH HAZARD.With console rear cover removed and power applied to Operator Console, the outside of the cooling fan blades are exposed. KEEP FINGERS AND OTHER OBJECTS CLEAR OF SPINNING FANS.

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

3.5   Verify Replacement Fan Operation

  1. Visually verify the Operator Console powers up and that all components (Computers, Monitors, Peripheral Towers, etc.) have power.

  1. Verify that the fan is blowing air in the proper direction.

    1. Air should be blowing OUT of the console cabinet.

    1. If the air is blowing IN (into console cabinet), you must physically reverse the fan.

      (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Remove console power.

  1. Install console Rear Cover and restore console power.
