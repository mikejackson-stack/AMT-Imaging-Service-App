3.4  Safety

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE Z820, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION.

 

NOTICE
IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

4  Procedure

4.1  Z820 Computer Removal

  1. Shutdown system. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Console:

    • If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button on desktop display and select [Shutdown].

    • If Applications are down, open a Terminal Window. Type: halt , then press [ENTER].

    • When halt command has finished, power Off the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Apply LOTO. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE procedure.

  1. Remove Keyboard Tabletop, Front and Rear covers from GOC6.6 console.

    Refer to Replacement → Console (GOC6.6) → Console Cover Removal and Installation.

  1. Remove the Z820 computer from the GOC6.6 console chassis.

    Refer to Replacement → Console (GOC6.6) → GOC6.6 VCT Host Computer (Z820) Replacement

4.2  System or Image Hard Drive Removal

  1. Open the Z820 computer side access panel.

    Illustration 1:  Z820 Side Access Panel Removal

  1. Locate System or Image Hard Drive FRUs by referencing the Illustrations below.

    Illustration 2:  Z820 Airflow Guide and Expansion Card Support

  1. Remove the original Hard Drive assembly from the computer’s drive bay.

4.3  System or Image Hard Drive Preparation

  1. Remove the 2.5 to 3.5 inch Form Factor Adapter Bracket with Hard Drive from the Hard Drive carrier rails. See Illustration 3.

  1. Remove the 2.5 to 3.5 inch Form Factor Adapter Bracket from Hard Drive and set Hard Drive aside. See Illustration 3.

  1. Install replacement Hard Drive into the original 2.5 to 3.5 inch Form Factor Adapter Bracket removed from original Hard Drive.

  1. Install replacement Hard Drive with Form Factor Adapter Bracket into original Hard Drive carrier rails.

    Illustration 3:  Hard Drive Form Factor Brackets and Carrier Rail Assembly

4.4  System or Image Hard Drive Install

  1. Install the replacement Hard Drive assembly into the computer drive bay.

  1. Close Side Access Panel.

4.5  Restore the Console

  1. Fully slide the Z820 computer back into the console chassis, until mounting brackets are flush against the console chassis.

  1. Remove and store the Service Platform.

  1. Install and torque the four (4) 3mm socket cap screws that mount the Z820 computer in the GOC6.6 console.

    NOTE:Torque each the for (4) 3mm socket cap mounting screws to:
    2.3 N-m1.7 lb-ft20.4 lb-in23.5 Kg-cm

  1. Reconnect all cables removed earlier to the Z820 computer.

  1. Remove LOTO on console.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform a Load From Cold (LFC).

    Refer to Software (GOC6.6) Software Installation Procedures (LFC)(13HW31.x) Load From Cold procedure

    NOTE:When a hard disk drive, which was previously used in a RAID volume, is reused ads system or image disk, clearing the configuration information in the hard disk drive is required.

    Refer to Z820 RAID Disk Clear Configuration Procedure.

  1. Install console covers and Keyboard Tabletop assembly.

    Refer to Replacement → Console (GOC6.6) → Console Cover Removal and Installation Procedure.

  1. Perform the Functional Checks → System Scanning Test instructions from the procedure list.
