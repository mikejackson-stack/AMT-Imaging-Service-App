3  Procedure

3.1   Remove the Old Slip Ring

  1. Remove Gantry side, top and rear covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Slip Ring safety covers.

NOTE: (For CT/PET Discovery System) The CT Gantry tilt hardware is removed when combined with a PET system, so the Gantry tilt function does not apply. Disregard requests for Gantry tilt in this procedure.

  1. Tilt Gantry forward to +30 degrees. This helps with positioning the new ring as it lessens the weight to hold while installing the ring and checking alignments.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltage present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Completely shut down system power. (A1, Lockout/Tagout)

  1. Remove rear cover mounting brackets, both sides. Three (3) 12mm bolts on each mount.

  1. Remove brush block, see Slip Ring Brush Block Replacement for details. A new brush block comes with the ring.

  1. Remove all wiring tie-wraps on the Ring inside diameter to allow removal of the ring and 6 mounting brackets.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables from Slip Ring.

  1. Disconnect cables and remove Receiver antenna bracket with antenna assembly.

NOTE:Follow this procedure EXACTLY. Do not take shortcuts. Both axial and radial runout as well as Gantry balance are at stake.

  1. Rotate Gantry so the Tube is in 12 o'clock position. This puts the signal interface board on the backside of the ring at 12 o'clock and the transmitter at 4 o'clock when looking from the back of the gantry.

  1. Engage axial rotating lock to prevent Gantry rotation.

  1. Mark Slip Ring, Slip Ring cast mounting brackets, and rotating casting with numbers 1 through 6.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Install New Slip Ring

  1. Open new Slip Ring box. Put on the gloves.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Gantry, remove all twelve (12) 12 mm bolts securing the cast mounting brackets to the rotating casting.

    (Step Details)

DANGER
SEVERE INJURY CAN RESULT.
THE SLIP RING AND CAST MOUNTING BRACKETS WEIGH ABOUT 150 lb (68 kg).
Three (3) PEOPLE MINIMUM ARE NEEDED TO REMOVE THIS ASSEMBLY.

NOTE:Remember there are three (3) mounts with pins, so pull ring straight off. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to separate the brackets from the rotating casting if necessary. The pins are primarily for positioning, not for holding entire weight of the ring.

  1. After removing the last bolts, carefully remove Slip Ring and cast mounting bracket assembly.

  1. Place the old ring, brass side down, on top of foam cover.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Inspect all interfaces for burrs, debris, or other imperfections. These items can result in runout failures, requiring a repeat of this entire procedure.

  1. Transfer each of the cast mounting brackets one at a time. Ensure these brackets are installed in the same position from old to new Slip Ring.

    1. Place cast mounting bracket number 1 on the new Slip Ring aligning the 6 mm pin in the slot.

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the 6 mm bolt in the 6.2 mm hole and align over Slip ring insert.

    1. Gently pull or push the cast mounting bracket radially out (away from isocenter). Use the pin slot as the stop and the 6.2 mm hole for alignment.

    1. Tighten the 6 mm bolt until the lock washer starts to compress. Just tighten (snug) enough so that bolts are engaged.

    1. Install the other three (3) 6 mm bolts and hand tighten until they are seated on the casting.

      (Step Details)

  1. Repeat for all six (6) cast mounting brackets.

  1. Align new slip ring and cast mounting brackets with the rotating casting.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release axial rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque the 12 mm bolts on each mounting bracket (bracket to gantry) as follows:

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the filters, wiring harnesses and ground clamps to the slip ring.

3.3   Radial Runout Check and Adjustment

  1. Mount and adjust the Dial Indicator so that the plunger tip rides on the blue edge next to the RF transmission ring.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the Gantry by hand and measure the Radial Runout. Radial runout should not exceed .0319 inches (32 mils, 0.81 mm).

  1. If the Radial Runout is out of specification perform the following steps.

    1. Identify the “High” and “Low” physical locations on the slip ring.

    1. With the gantry tilted forward + 30 degrees, place the “High” location at 12 o’clock.

    1. Loosen-do not remove-the four (4) 12 mm bolts on each of the six (6) cast mounting brackets.

    1. Physically lift/push/pull the ring to release binding tension. (At + 30 degree tilt, gravity effects are minimized)

  1. If any adjustments were made, re-torque bolts per the following table. Tightening sequences must be followed for desired results. (See Illustration 5)

    (Step Details)

3.4   Axial Runout Adjustment

  1. Adjust the Dial Indicator and place the plunger tip directly on the brass surface of ring 12 (outer most ring).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the Gantry by hand and measure the Axial Runout. Axial runout should not exceed .0327 inches (33 mils, 0.83 mm).

  1. If the Axial Runout is out of specification perform the following steps. This procedure will first loosen and retighten the mounts in case a mount was not seated properly and then shim a mount if necessary.

    1. Identify the “High” and “Low” physical locations on the slip ring.

    1. With the Gantry tilted forward + 30 degrees, place the “Low” location at 6 o’clock.

    1. Loosen - do not remove - the four (4) 12mm bolts on each of the six (6) cast mounting brackets.

    1. Physically lift/push/pull the ring to release binding tension. (At + 30 degree tilt, gravity effects are minimized)

  1. If any adjustments were made, re-torque bolts per the following table. Tightening sequences must be followed for desired results. (See Illustration 5)

    (Step Details)

  1. Recheck the axial runout and perform the following if still out of specification (should not exceed .0327 inches (33 mils, 0.83 mm)).

    1. Inspect the one (1) or two (2) closest cast mounting brackets for proper seating at both the slip ring and rotating casting interfaces.

      (Step Details)

    1. Correct any “High” gaps greater than .005 inches (0.127 mm) at any interface edge.

    1. Recheck both Radial and Axial runout.

    1. Using standard notebook paper (0.003 inches (0.076 mm) thick nominal) make shims for one (1) or two (2) mounting locations on either side of the “Low” location.

      (Step Details)

    1. Loosen the four (4) 6 mm bolts at the cast mounting bracket to slip ring interface (back side of the ring).

    1. Remove the 2 outer diameter bolts and slide shim between the slip ring and cast mounting bracket to block the two (2) outside diameter holes.

    1. Carefully puncture, remove, trim and reinstall shim.

    1. Install the 6mm bolts and snug all 4 bolts. Since torque can not be applied with the slip ring installed, tighten the bolts 1/4 turn past snug.

  1. Repeat this procedure as needed.

    (Step Details)

3.5   Finish Mechanical Installation

  1. Route and secure all cabling to the Slip Ring as shown in the following illustrations. (5 connections)

    (Step Details)

  1. Install Antenna/Receiver assembly. Reference Dual Channel Fiber Rx Replacement.

  1. Install brush block assembly. Reference Slip Ring Brush Block Replacement

  1. Install slip ring safety covers.

  1. Install the Cover mount brackets.

  1. Install gantry covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power to system.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a [Gantry Rotation safety check] from the [Functional Checks] folder of the service manual.

  1. Perform a Gantry Balance Check.

  1. Acquire an axial scan: (120kV/80mA, 0.5 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire one helical scan: (120kV/40mA, 30 sec. Scan)

  1. Verify NO increase in LSCOM errors.

  1. Verify NO increase in corrected or uncorrected FEC errors.

  1. Perform the Quality Assurance Test.
