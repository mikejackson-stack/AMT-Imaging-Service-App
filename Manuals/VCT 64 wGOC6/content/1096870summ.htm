Align the Table to the Gantry

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Before you start, turn on the laser and check that the beam is still on the mark placed on the wall. If not, reset the laser.

-

-

If the mark is not present, use a measuring tape and place a 102 mm (4 in.) piece of masking tape on the cradle at the 1000 mm and on the laser line.

-

-

Table base to cradle alignment location is 1005 mm from the center of cradle to the floor.

-

-

Table cradle must be level in all directions (centered within the lines on a Johnson Professional level).

-

-

All table adjusters should be preset to 20 mm (3/4 in.) down from the table base to make adjustment easier. Based on floor levelness and your experience, a different preset height may work better. One thread must be showing above all locking rings when leveled.

-

-

Table cannot be higher than 1005 mm from floor to cradle.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Leveling the Table

NOTICE
Avoid leaning on the cradle during this procedure.

DO NOT pin the gantry during this alignment process.

This procedure is for systems mounted on 102 mm (4 in.) concrete floors only!

 

NOTE:If the floor covering was not properly removed with the glue cleaned off, or the levelers were not centered over the floor cutouts, the leveler may become trapped against the edge of the floor covering, causing the table to become unlevel. If this happens, move the table and enlarge the 102 mm (4 in.) floor cutout for the table. Glue removal is important and aids in moving the table to its final location in accordance with the floor levelness specification.

  1. Have the table side panels removed and a ratchet, 1-1/8 in. socket, and a 2 ft. level ready to use.

  1. Turn on the laser’s “I” beam (vertical beams) by pressing the ON button 2 times.

    (Step Details)

  1. The table on the dollies should be resting on the floor, and the laser beam visible on the cradle. The laser light should now shine down the cradle onto the rear vertical target. Moving the table on the dollies by raising and lowering makes it easier to center the table right-to-left.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the table so that the base is roughly centered over the scan center line, the front edge of the table base is on the 26.5 in. (± 0.25 in.) line, and the table is resting on the floor. Check that the leveling feet are centered in the cutout circles.

  1. Carefully move the table so that the cradle front center line and the back target are aligned. You may need to raise the table to move it. When aligned, lower the table to the floor.

  1. If not already done, measure 1000 mm from the front of the cradle, and place a piece of tape under the laser center line. Carefully mark a line along the laser line when the table is centered.

  1. The laser beam should now connect the cradle front centerlines, the 1000 mm cradle center line, onto the rear alignment tool vertical center, and finally onto the alignment centering mark placed on the wall, when properly centered along the center line. Use the centering alignment line on the wall to be sure the laser is still centered. If the alignment line on the wall is NOT on the original mark, readjust the laser and repeat the above steps (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Potential for Injury.
In the ship position, the table tips easily!
DO NOT lean on the table! The shipping bracket should still be in place.

  1. The table should be completely on the floor and resting on all 4 levelers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise or lower the table as needed using the front and rear levelers and level the cradle horizontally in the front and back locations (refer to Illustration 2):

    1. First, horizontally across the cradle at the front of the cradle.

    1. Second, horizontally across the cradle at the 1000 mm mark on the cradle.

    1. When the cradle is roughly level in both locations, leave the level on the cradle horizontally.

  1. Level the cradle front to back using a 4 ft.level placed in the center of the cradle. These two leveling actions often conflict and a few iterations may be required.

    (Step Details)

  1. When completed, turn off the laser tool.

    (Step Details)

2.2   Cradle/Table Parallel Check

CAUTION
Do not lean on the table during this procedure.

  1. Confirm that there is a centerline on the table at 1000 mm measured from the black dot on the front of the table.

  1. If the line is not present, turn on the laser and using masking tape from the installation support kit, add the centerline.

  1. This line should be centered with the line on the front of the cradle and the rear table line.

  1. This reference line will also be used to determine x-ray on table alignment. Do not remove.

3  Finalization

  1. Re-check gantry bubble levels.

  1. Re-check that each of the eight adjuster is loaded by attempting to turn it.

CAUTION
Eye protection is required when using a hammer and chisel.

  1. Tighten the lock rings at all locations with the spanner, where possible. Use a hammer and chisel to tighten the lock rings only where you cannot use the spanner.

  1. At least one full round of thread must be visible above the lock ring at all adjuster locations.

    (Step Details)
