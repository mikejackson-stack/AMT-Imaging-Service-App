3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove the Old Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader

NOTE:If GOC6 Console is equipped with PS/2 Type Keyboard or Mouse, see Section 4.4

  1. Remove the USB cable connection from the Console’s Upper Bulkhead Panel for the Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader being replaced.

  1. Remove the Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader and set aside.

3.3   Install the Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader

  1. Place the replacement Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball on the Operator Console.

  1. Plug in Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader cable in the USB connector at the Rear Bulkhead Panel. Refer to Illustration 2 to confirm correct connections.

    (Step Details)

3.4   GOC6 Console Equipped with PS/2 Type Keyboards and/or Mouse

  1. Remove Rear Console Cover to allow access to the Host Computer.

  1. Disconnect either the Keyboard or Mouse from its respective PS/2 Port on the rear of the Host Computer.

  1. Connect the replacement Keyboard or Mouse.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pass the PS2 keyboard or Mouse cable along the underside of the Upper Rear Bulkhead panel at the top of the console cabinet, along with the Monitor Video and DVD Peripheral Tower USB cables. Affix a tie wrap to secure the Keyboard or Mouse cable to the Upper Rear Bulkhead Panel.

  1. Re-install Rear Console Cover.

3.5   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

3.6   Verify Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader Operation

  1. Visually verify Keyboard, Mouse, Trackball or Barcode Reader works as expected with the CT Applications.

  1. No other verifications required.

    (Step Details)
