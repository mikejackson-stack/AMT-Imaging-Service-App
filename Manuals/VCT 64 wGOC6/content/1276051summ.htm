2  Procedure

2.1   For LightSpeed VCT with 11HW12.5 and 12HW14.6

Application software should be in shutdown.

  1. Open a Unix shell, and type the following.

    1. cd/usr/g/config <ENTER>

    1. cp prep_flags.cfg.vct prep_flags.cfg.vct.org <ENTER>

    1. chmod +w prep_flags.cfg.vct <ENTER>

    1. nedit prep_flags.cfg.vct <ENTER>

  1. Change the value of the following line to 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [File], and click [SAVE], then select [Exit] to close the window.

2.2   For LightSpeed VCT with 13HW31.8 and Optima CT660 with 12HW28.8

Application software should be in shutdown.

NOTE:In case of Optima CT660 with 12Hw28.8, the file name is prep_flags.cfg.cj64.

  1. Open a Unix shell, and type the following.

    1. cd/usr/g/config <ENTER>

    1. cp prep_flags.cfg.vct prep_flags.cfg.org <ENTER>

    1. chmod +w prep_flags.cfg.vct <ENTER>

    1. xedit prep_flags.cfg.vct <ENTER>

  1. Change the value of the following line to 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [SAVE], and select [Quit] to close the window.
