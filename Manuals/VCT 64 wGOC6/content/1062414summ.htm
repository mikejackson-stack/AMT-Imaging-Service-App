3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove Old SCIM Assembly

  1. Remove the HD-50 cable connection from the back of the SCIM assembly being replaced.

  1. Disconnect the keyboard USB cable from the upper bulkhead panel and pull the keyboard free from the SCIM plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the SCIM assembly and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement SCIM

  1. Place the replacement SCIM assembly on the Operator Console.

  1. Reattach the keyboard to the SCIM plate, remembering to pass the keyboard USB cable through the opening in the SCIM plate.

  1. Reconnect the SCIM HD-50 cable the rear SCIM assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the keyboard USB cable at the upper bulkhead panel.

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

3.5   Verify Replacement SCIM Operation

  1. Verify the keyboard is functional.

  1. Verify that all controls on the SCIM assembly are mechanically working (i.e. Pushbuttons and Volume controls move freely).

  1. No other verifications required.
