Band Test

1  Safety

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

Gantry covers and scan window installed.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Perform Center Phantom.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

  1. Select [NEW PATIENT].

  1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: Band Test

  1. Select Service from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [MANUFACTURING], [IMGSER 20CMQA VCT 64].

  1. Select Series 3, Series Description: QA3 Heli/0.51/120kV/335mA/.4s, by clicking [NEXT SERIES] twice.

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Click [END EXAM].

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

  1. Select ImageWorks desktop.

  1. Select scanned examination from List/Select browser.

  1. Click [VIEWER] and visually review all images for band artifacts.

  1. For images depicting bands, perform the following steps.

    1. Select [SERVICE] desktop and move Service desktop to left monitor.

    1. Select [IMAGE QUALITY] from Service desktop.

    1. Select [IMAGE ANALYSIS] and move Image Analysis window to left monitor.

    1. Select image from right monitor List/Select browser.

    1. Click [MANUAL] radio button on Image Analysis window.

    1. Click [MANUAL] selection button, [BAND].

    1. Click [ACCEPT].

    1. Follow instructions in Image Analysis window lower right pane, to size Band ROI and analyze.

    1. From Report window, if Avg. Ref. Band Factor is out of specification range +/-2.8, perform appropriate corrective action.

    1. Click [DISMISS] in Report window.

    1. Click [QUIT] in Image Analysis window.
