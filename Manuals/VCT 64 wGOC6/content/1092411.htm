4  Procedure

4.1  Rotor Control Board Box Removal

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry such that Rotor Control Board Box is in 2 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Cut Ty-wraps on Rotor Control Board Box and Mounting Bracket clamps ( See items 1 and 2 in Illustration 1) and remove High Voltage cable from clamps.

    Illustration 1:  Rotor Control Board Box

  1. Disconnect front cables from Rotor Control Board Box connectors ( J1, J2 and J13 ).

  1. Remove four M6 mounting bolts and washers using a wrench with a 5mm hex bit and 12 inch extension.

    NOTE:If bolts are supplied with new Rotor Control Board Box, throw away old bolts and washers.

    NOTE:If bolts are not supplied with new Rotor Control Board Box, reuse existing bolts and washers.

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 1 (See Illustration 2)

    Illustration 2:  Rotor Control Board Box Removal

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 2 (See Illustration 2)

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 3 (See Illustration 2)

  1. Lifting the High Voltage cable slightly, remove Rotor Control Board Box by lifting up from side 1 (See Illustration 2) and pulling out at an angle towards gantry right side.

    NOTE:Carefully angle Rotor Control Board Box back clamp under High Voltage cable. Avoid pinching fingers and damaging High Voltage cable.

4.2  Rotor Control Board Box Installation

  1. Install new Rotor Control Board Box into mounting bracket.

    NOTE:Carefully angle Rotor Control Board Box back clamp under High Voltage cable. Avoid pinching fingers and damaging High Voltage cable.

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 1 (See Illustration 2)

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 4 (See Illustration 2)

  1. Slide the Rotor Control Board Box towards side 3 (See Illustration 2)

  1. With a 5mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension, fasten new Rotor Control Board Box with M6 mounting bolts and washers.

  1. Torque each M6 mounting bolt to the following specification.

    5.8 ft.-lbs7.9 N-m70.0 in-lbs 80.8 kg-cm

  1. Connect and secure front cables to Rotor Control Board Box connectors ( J1, J2 and J13 ).

  1. Route High Voltage cable to Rotor Control Board Box and Mounting Bracket clamps and secure with Ty-wraps (See Illustration 1).

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Restore power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

4.3  Setup, Checks, Alignments, and Calibrations

  1. Gantry Rotation Safety Check

  1. Gantry Balance

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Install gantry front cover, gantry top covers, gantry left side cover and scan window.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install right side gantry cover.
