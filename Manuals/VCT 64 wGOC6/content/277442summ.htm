Heater Diagnostic Test (Pro, RT, VCT)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. If desired, you may check any or all of the following:

    1. PPC kV control board alive and running: S0-S7 LEDs are lit successively or a combination of them blink.

    1. Heater board alive and running: DS1 and DS2 LEDs are lit successively.

    1. Heater DC supply present: DS3 LED is lit.

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL].

  1. Launch the [Heater Diagnostic] tool.

  1. Run the test. A scan sequence will proceed.

  1. If a problem occurs, check the error code.
