Cradle Motor and Cradle Drive Belt Tension Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

1  Procedure

  1. Turn the Tension meter on.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Move the cradle and IMS to out limit position.

  1. Remove power from the table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table component and covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Hold the tension meter sensor on the middle of the upper side of belt, and lightly strum the top of the cradle motor belt in the middle of the belt span to make it vibrate. This is the narrower of the two cradle belts. Measure the frequency and procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Compare measurement value to the cradle motor belt frequency spec of (Between 311 and 355 HZ - optimal range).

  1. If frequency is less than 280HZ, proceed to the following sub-steps:

    1. Loosen 3 bolts shown below.

      (Step Details)

    1. Adjust belt using flat head screwdriver to slide the motor bracket and then tighten the three bolts securely while keeping tension on the belt.

      (Step Details)

    1. Continue to adjust belt and measure frequency until it is within spec.

  1. Hold the tension meter sensor on the middle of the upper side of belt, and lightly strum the top of the cradle drive belt in the middle of the belt span to make it vibrate. This is the wider of the two cradle belts. Measure the frequency and procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Compare measurement value to the cradle drive belt frequency spec of (Between 222 and 246 HZ - optimal range).

  1. If frequency is within spec, reassemble all the covers and verify that all table functions are operating normally. If frequency is less than 185HZ, proceed to the following sub-steps:

    1. Loosen 2 bolts on the cradle drive belt tensioner to remove tension on the belt. The bolts are located on the left side of the IMS.

      (Step Details)

    1. Adjust belt using flat head screwdriver to set the tension on the cradle drive belt. Tighten the two bolts securely while keeping force applied to the screwdriver.

      (Step Details)

    1. Continue to adjust belt and measure frequency until it is within spec.

  1. Reassemble all covers.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the table from the service Switch Panel in gantry.

  1. Move the cradle and IMS in and out completely 10 cycles, and verify that table movement is operating normally.
