LCD Video Monitor Overview

Last Revised on:20 November, 2014

This procedure covers the following topics:

  • Section 1 - General Monitor Information (cleaning, warm-up time, specifications, image persistence)

  • Section 2 - Monitor Controls and Informational Messages

1  General Monitor Information

1.1  Warm-Up Time

For optimum performance, allow 20 minutes for warm-up.

1.2  Environmental Considerations

1.2.1  EIZO LCD Monitor

  1. Operating

    • Temperature: 5 - 35 °C (41 - 95 °F)

    • Humidity: 20 - 80% (no condensation)

    • Pressure: 700 - 1,060 hPa

  2. Storage

    • Temperature: -20 to +60 °C (-4 - 140 °F)

    • Humidity: 10 - 80% (no condensation)

    • Pressure: 200 - 1,060 hPa

1.2.2  Bigtide LCD Monitor

  1. Operating

    • Temperature: 5 to 40 °C

    • Humidity: 30 - 80% (no condensation)

    • Pressure: 674 - 1,040 hPa

  2. Storage

    • Temperature: Temperature: -20 to +60 °C (-4 to 140 °F)

    • 10 - 85% (no condensation)

    • Pressure: 674 - 1,040 hPa

1.3  Weight

1.3.1  EIZO LCD Monitor

With stand: 5.9 kg (13 lbs)

1.3.2  Bigtide LCD Monitor

With stand: 7.6 kg (16.7 lbs)
