3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from the rear of the console.

3.2   Remove Console Power Switch Panel Assembly from Chassis

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Disconnect Console Power Switch cable from the Power Distribution Box, Connector J23.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Ethernet Port and USB cables from the rear of the Console Power Switch Panel Assembly (Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen and remove the two (2) 4mm socket cap mounting screws holding the console Power Switch Assembly to the console chassis and remove the entire Console Power Switch Assembly with its wiring harness from console.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Install Replacement Console Power Switch

  1. 1. Disconnect the two (2) Fast ON Connectors on the rear of the Power Switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Failed Power Switch by compressing the spring clips on the backside and pressing the Switch out the front of the Power Switch Panel.

  1. Install the replacement Power Switch by pressing the switch into the Power Switch Panel from the front.

  1. Reconnect the two (2) Fast On Connectors removed earlier.

3.4   Install Replacement Console Power Switch Cable

  1. Disconnect the two (2) Fast ON Connectors on the rear of the Power Switch.

  1. Disconnect the Power Switch Panel Ground wire by loosing the Hex Nut and removing the Ground Wire ring lug from the ground stud. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Cut any Ty-Raps® holding the cable to the Power Switch Panel Assembly.

  1. Install the replacement Power Switch Cable by reconnecting the two (2) Fast On Connectors to the Power Switch. Refasten the Ground Wire to the Ground Stud on the Power Switch Panel Assembly using the hex nut removed earlier. Torque to 1.3 N-m.

  1. Replace any Ty-Raps® removed earlier.

3.5   Reinstall Console Power Switch Assembly on Chassis

  1. Place and hold the repaired Console Power Switch Panel Assembly in upper left compartment of console.

  1. Install the Power Switch Panel Assembly using the two (2) 4mm socket cap mounting screws removed earlier. Torque to 2.9 N-m.

  1. Replace the Ethernet and USB cables back in the rear of the Console Power Switch Panel Assembly.

  1. Reconnect Console Power Switch Cable to the Power Distribution Box, Connector J23.

  1. Check that the two (2) Circuit Breakers (CB1 & 2) on the Power Distribution Box are set ON.

3.6   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

3.7   Verify Replacement Power Switch and/or Cable Operation

  1. Verify that power is restored to all the console components:

    1. Internal Computer modules (i.e. Host, DIG, HSDA ... etc.)

    1. External Peripherals (i.e. Monitors, Peripheral Tower ... etc)

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove console power.

4  Finalization

  1. Install console Front Cover.

  1. Restore console power.
