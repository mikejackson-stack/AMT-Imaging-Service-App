2  Procedure

  1. Select [REPLACEMENT] from Service desktop.

  1. Select [NEW TUBE CONFIG].

  1. Select appropriate tube failure code from [TUBE UNIT FAILURE CODE] pull-down menu.

  1. Enter tube insert serial number in [INSERT SERIAL NUMBER] edit box.

  1. Enter tube housing serial number in [HOUSING SERIAL NUMBER] edit box.

  1. Click [OK] in Change Tube Information window.

  1. Click [OK] in Message window.

  1. Click [SHUTDOWN] from desktop selection area.

  1. Select [RESTART] in Attention window.

  1. Click [OK] in Attention window.
