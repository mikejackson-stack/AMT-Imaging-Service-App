2  Overview

This procedure describes the steps necessary to replace from FRP*¹ IMS covers to RIM*² IMS covers.

*1 FRP : Fiver Reinforced Plastics

*2 RIM : Reaction Injection Modling

Last Revised:December 19, 2014

4  Procedure

4.1  Preparation

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

4.2  Replacement Procedure

  1. Remove the following Table covers:

    • IMS Side Covers (Left/Right)

    • IMS Rear Cover

  1. Mark the position of IMS cover bracket on both sides for installation of new bracket.

    Illustration 1:  IMS Cover Bracket Position

  1. Disconnect the cable connectors of the tape switches and touch sensors.

  1. Remove six (6) screws holding the both sides of the IMS cover brackets.

    Illustration 2:  Six (6) screws of the IMS Cover Bracket

  1. Remove the IMS cover bracket with the IMS upper cover.

    Illustration 3:  IMS Cover Bracket and IMS Upper Cover Removal

  1. Remove the IMS cover brackets (24L and 24R), and two dummy connectors from the removed IMS cover bracket.

    Illustration 4:  IMS Cover Bracket (24L and 24R) and Dummy Connectors

  1. Transfer the IMS cover brackets (24L and 24R) to the new IMS cover brackets (33L and 33R), and temporarily tighten the two screws on the left and right sides of the brackets.

    Illustration 5:  IMS Cover Bracket 24L and 24R Attachment

  1. Transfer the dummy connectors to the new IMS cover brackets (33L and 33R).

    Illustration 6:  Dummy Connector Attachment

  1. Install the IMS cover bracket (33L and 33R) to fit the mark on the table frame.

    Illustration 7:  IMS Cover Bracket (33L and 33R) Installation

  1. Attach the IMS cover bracket 33 to the brackets (33L and 33R).

    Illustration 8:  IMS Cover Bracket 33 Attachment

  1. Remove the IMS cover bracket (14L and 14R).

    Illustration 9:  IMS Cover Brackets (14L and 14R) Removal

  1. Install the IMS cover brackets (31L and 31R) to the table frame using plate nuts, attach the dummy connector to the bracket 31R.

    Illustration 10:  IMS Cover Brackets (31L and 31R) Installation

  1. Install the IMS finger guard to the left side of the table.

    Illustration 11:  IMS Finger Guard Installation

  1. Instal the new IMS Side Covers (Left/Right).

  1. Connect the cable (⑩) to J120 and touch sensors on IMS side covers (J123 and J124).

    Illustration 12:  Sensor Cable Connector

  1. Tie-wrap the sensor cable to the tie-wrap base on the pump bracket, to remove excess slack in the cable

    Illustration 13:  Sensor Cable and Tie-wrap Base

  1. Attach the IMS dummy plug label to the J120 connector of the 5120581-X cable using a tie-wrap (⑪).

    Illustration 14:  IMS Dummy Plug Label Attachment

  1. Move the IMS cover brackets (24L and 24R) to set the position of IMS side covers as shown in illustration blow, and tighten the four screws on the left and right side IMS cover brackets.

    Illustration 15:  Position of IMS Side Covers

  1. Install the new RIM IMS rear cover.

    1. Slide the IMS rear cover hook into position

    1. Tighten the two screws.

      Illustration 16:  IMS Rear Cover Installation
