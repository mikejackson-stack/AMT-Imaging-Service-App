2  Overview

This procedure describes the steps necessary to upgrade the gantry with the Integrated Peripheral controller assembly and cabling. The IPC is a new version of the Gantry Options Board (GOB) for use on all VCT platforms.

Last Revised on:April 22, 2009

4  Procedure

4.1  Preparation for Upgrade

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Make sure all parts have been delivered in the kit per the contents list to avoid partial install issues. Reference Finalization, Step 3 for parts list.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Remove the Gantry left side, top, front and rear covers. Connect the cover E-stop circuit to the terminators on the gantry.

  1. Remove all Gantry Base Covers. Cover on the bottom of the tilting assembly can stay in place. The side base covers can be discarded as they will be replaced with new side covers with cutouts for the interface panels.

  1. Remove the gantry lower cantrells (latches) for the rear cover to avoid potential damage during gantry tilt steps.

  1. Tilt the gantry all the way back for easier access to gantry base from the rear using the following steps.

    1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC service switch and press the table drives enable at the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

    2. Turn on service mode switch S4 (up position)

    3. Turn on the tilt enable switch S9 (on position)

    4. Use the tilt switch S10 to tilt the gantry all the way back to the tilt stop blocks. Check clearances on both sides when getting close to full back to make sure there are no obstructions that need to be cleared.

    5. Turn off the gantry 120VAC service switch.

  1. Shut down the console and perform LOTO for the gantry per safety procedures.

  1. Open the attached PDF file for easy reference in the next sections. This drawing shows the overview of the parts being installed.

    Media File 1:  Options panel upgrade kit drawing

4.2  Ethernet switch assembly installation

  1. From the supplied 1” wide velcro strips cut the following:

    • Cut 2 strips (hook and loop pair) 100mm (4 inch) long for the Ethernet switch

    • Cut 1 strip (hook and loop pair) 75mm (3 inch) long for the power supply

  1. Attach the hook side of the velcro strips to the Ethernet switch and power supply and mount to new plate as shown in Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    With the loop side of the velcro attached to the hook side remove the protective strip over the adhesive and mount the Ethernet switch and power supply to the mount plate such that each part is centered between the hole and cutout in the plate for each of the two components. The port side of the Ethernet switch should line up with the edge of the plate.

    Illustration 1:  Velcro installation

    Illustration 2:  Component locations

  1. Route the two velcro straps around the plate, through the cutout and around the Ethernet switch and power supply individually. The end of the strap folded back on itself should be on the front side of the each part. Reference Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Velcro Strap Routing

  1. Place a mark on the cable bundle above and or below the top cable tie plate for the gantry cable harness for reference when positioning the harness on the new plate. Clip the cable ties holding the gantry cable harness to the current cable tie brackets. Reference Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Gantry cable harness

  1. Remove the E-stop terminator plate assembly from the gantry (2 screws, 5 mm hex wrench). Reference Illustration 4.

  1. Remove the 2 cable tie brackets (3 and 5 mm hex wrenches) from the gantry leg and discard. Reference Illustration 4.

  1. Mount the new bracket assembly with Ethernet switch and power supply to the gantry leg using the supplied M6 and M4 screws (2 each, 3 and 5 mm hex wrenches), lock washer and washers and using loctite 242.

    Table 1:  M4 Screw Torque values

    7.9 N-m70 lb-in5.8 lb-ft81 kg-cm

    Illustration 5:  New bracket assembly

  1. Install the E-stop terminator assembly (reference Illustration 5) onto the new bracket (5 mm hex wrench) and torque to:

    Table 3:  M6 Screw Torque values

    7.9 N-m70 lb-in5.8 lb-ft81 kg-cm

  1. Position the gantry cable harness in the new bracket using the marks placed on the cable bundle to position the bundle then use cable ties to strap the bundle in place. Reference Illustration 5.

  1. Connect the power line from the Ethernet power supply to the switch and bundle the extra length of power line to the gantry harness. Reference Illustration 6.

4.3  Emergency Stop/Service Outlet Plate and cabling upgrade

  1. Remove the E-stop button and outlet plate assembly. (4 screws using 3 mm hex wrench)

    Illustration 7:  E-Stop and outlet box modification

  1. Remove 2 screws (3 mm hex wrench) holding outlet box to the gantry leg.

  1. Place power cable adapter plate behind outlet box and reinstall on gantry leg using the 2 M4 screws removed above (3 mm hex wrench) and hand tighten.

  1. Install E-Stop and service outlet plate into box using the 4 M4 screws previously removed. (3 mm hex wrench)

    Table 4:  M4 screw torque

4.4  IPC Panel and power box installation

4.4.1  Gantry right side (service switch side)

  1. Assemble right panel to right side mount bracket using M4 lock washers and nuts for the 2 studs from the panel and hand tighten.

    NOTE:may need to shift the pre-mounted ground wire to fit the panel into the bracket.

    Illustration 9:  Parts

  1. Mount the Power upgrade box to the gantry frame as follows.

    1. Position the box as shown in Illustration 10. The plastic cable clamps are for the Ethernet, fiber, and power lines.

      Illustration 10:  Power box mounting

    1. The rear plate mount point will be fastened by velcro as follows:

      • Cut a piece of velcro (from the pieces left over after installing the ethernet switch assembly), approximately 1 inch or 2.5 cm square.

      • Remove the covers over the adhesive and place the velcro between the bracket and the gantry to hold the bracket in place. This mount location does not affect the structure but having velcro here will ensure there are no vibrations that will cause a noise from the bracket if it is not attached.

  1. Install the interface panel mount plate on top of the power assembly bracket, seen in Illustration 10. Center the screws in the slot. Adjustment to base covers will be done later. Mount to the frame with 2 M4x12 screws with lock and flat washers (3 mm hex wrench). Torque to:

    Table 5:  M4 Screw torque

    2.3 N-m20 lb-in1.7 lb-ft23 kg-cm

  1. Put Hydraulic lines from the tilt piston into the supplied clamps. Route the power cables, Ethernet and fiber cabling as shown in Illustration 11.

    Illustration 11:  Cable routing

  1. Plug in the AC power cord to the power box and install the AC lines from the new harness into the interface panel as shown in Illustration 11. Note the AC power connection to the Interface Panel Outlets for color and terminal locations (Blue to N terminal, Brown to L terminal, and Green to the Gnd). The other end of the cables will be routed to their destinations later.

4.4.2  Gantry left side (TGPU side)

  1. Assemble left interface panel to left side mount bracket using M4 lock washers and nuts for the 2 studs from the panel and hand tighten. Reference Illustration 12 and Illustration 13.

    NOTE:May need to shift the pre-mounted ground wire to fit the panel into the bracket.

    Illustration 12:  Parts

    Illustration 13:  IPC and Interface panel mounted

  1. Remove the M6 screw from the GTFR assembly mounting. (5 mm hex wrench)

  1. Position the IPC assembly over top of the hydraulic lines and the IPC mount panel between the hydraulic cable clamp and GTFR base plate as shown in Illustration 13 and continue with the following steps.

    1. If the assembly does not fit due to interference with the support gusset shown in Illustration 14 then the alternate IPC assembly base plate will need to be used. Perform Step 4 to change to the alternate IPC assembly base plate and do NOT continue with this step.

      Illustration 14:  Gantry mount hole locations

    1. Install an M4 screw (3 mm hex wrench) with lock and flat washer through the IPC mount plate into the rear hole by the gantry gusset and torque to the values shown in Table 6.

      Table 6:  M4 screw torque

      2.3 N-m20 lb-in1.7 lb-ft23 kg-cm

  1. Perform this step ONLY if you need to change to the alternate IPC base plate as identified in Step 3.

    1. Remove the cable and clear cover (4 mm wrench for cover screws) from the IPC board assembly.

    1. Remove the 6 standoffs holding the IPC board to the base plate and install it on the alternate base place supplied with the kit using the 6 standoffs.

      Illustration 15:  Example alternate base plate

    1. Using a 5 mm hex driver or small wrench, remove the Cardiac and Pulmonary cables from the base plate and install in the same locations on the alternate plate. Make sure to keep all hardware in same order as removed.

    1. Reconnect the cable and reinstall the plastic cover onto the IPC board.

    1. Position the IPC assembly over top of the hydraulic lines and the IPC mount panel between the hydraulic cable clamp and GTFR base plate as shown in Illustration 13.

    1. Perform the following steps to use velcro to fasten the IPC assembly rear mount to the gantry to avoid any vibrational noise during system use.

      • Cut a piece of velcro (from the pieces left over after installing the ethernet switch assembly), approximately 1 inch or 2.5 cm square.

      • Remove the covers over the adhesive and place the velcro between the bracket and the gantry to hold the bracket in place. This mount location does not affect the structure but having velcro here will ensure there are no vibrations that will cause a noise from the bracket if it is not attached.

  1. Reinstall the M6 screw (5 mm hex wrench) with lock and flat washers. Torque to:

    Table 7:  M6 Screw Torque values

    7.9 N-m70 lb-in5.8 lb-ft81 kg-cm

  1. Position the interface panel assembly on top of the IPC base plate with 2 M4 screws (3 mm hex wrench) with flat and lock washers and hand tighten.

  1. Install the cardiac lemo feed through plug to the port with the heart symbol port on the panel. Position such that the nut on the outside of the panel is threaded down flush with the threads. tighten the rear nut to secure the feed through. Repeat for the Respiratory feed through for the lung symbol port. Reference Illustration 16 and Illustration 17.

4.5  Cable installation and routing

  1. Connect the E-Stop cables for front and rear covers to the terminator plugs on the gantry frame.

  1. Remove LOTO to allow the gantry to power up. Apply power to the gantry. Turn on the 120 VAC service switch and press the table drive reset button.

  1. Using the Tilt switch (S10), tilt the gantry forward to have easy access to the cable tray along the front base of the gantry.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Voltages present in gantry
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES from the safety section of the service documentation . this procedure includes work directly in the gantry power pan.

  1. Turn off the 120 VAC service switch and disable power to gantry using LOTO.

  1. Layout the remaining cabling in front of the gantry. Look at the labels on the ends to identify which end goes on which side of the gantry. Specific position will be covered in the following steps.

    NOTE:When feeding cables to the left and right sides of the gantry in later steps, feeding the cables under the cable clamps will save time instead of removing and reinstalling the clamps if space allows.

    Illustration 18:  Gantry cable tray

    NOTE:Open the following cable harness drawing as a reference when installing the cable harness. The drawing shows the cables and connection locations as well as harness location with respect to the gantry cable clamps shown above.

    Media File 2:  Cable Harness drawing

  1. For the cable harness, the 2 ground wires should be positioned in the left/right center of the gantry to feed straight out to the ground bar under the foot/switch. This is the starting position to then route the rest of the harness following the existing gantry cable pathways to the respective locations.

  1. Route the cables to their defined locations shown in Media File 2 with the following guidelines.

    1. Install the TGPU power tap off cable and Install cable clamp behind TGPU to hold power harness. Reference Illustration 19.

      Illustration 19:  TGPU power tap off cable

    1. When installing the new Ethernet cable to the TGPU use a cable tie to tie back the existing Ethernet cable end at the TGPU and then plug in the new Ethernet connection. Use this same cable tie idea when connecting the new Ethernet cable at the gantry input (rear base of gantry by power input).

      Illustration 20:  Ethernet connection

    1. Connect the power cable lugs to the outlet as shown in Illustration 21. Note the AC power connection to the Interface Panel Outlets for color and terminal locations (Blue to N terminal, Brown to L terminal, and Green to the Gnd).

      Illustration 21:  Power connections example

    1. For the three (3) Ethernet cables going to the Ethernet switch, route them under the velcro strap holding the switch to keep them in place.

  1. Starting at the center of the gantry cable path, cable tie the new cables to the existing gantry cable bundles working from to each side to the gantry. Remove existing cable ties and install new cable ties at each cable tie bracket on the gantry frame.

  1. The gantry to injector cable (5169456) included in the kit runs from the Sub-D connector by the IPC panel to the Injector.

  1. Inspect the cable runs and finish installing any cable ties to keep cable bundles together.

4.7  Power on checks

  1. Before turning on power, turn off CB4 on the new power upgrade box.

  1. Remove LOTO and power on the gantry.

  1. Now turn on CB4 on the new power upgrade box.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Voltages present in gantry
USE PROPER safety PROCEDURES when testing with power present.

  1. Using a digital voltmeter, check each of the 4 outlets, 2 on each interface panel to make sure 120VAC is seen.

    If no power is seen shut down gantry power. Perform LOTO to remove power to gantry. Recheck power cabling connections for the new power box.

    Restore power and repeat this step.

  1. While watching the gantry cabling, tilt the gantry forward and backward using the service switch panel Tilt switch S10. (Service Mode S4 is up and Tilt Enable S9 is ON) to make sure there is no cable interference with tilt function and there are no cables being pulled or rubbed during tilt function.

  1. Leave the gantry in a zero tilt position. Turn Tilt Enable S9 to Off and put the Service Mode switch in the down position.

  1. Turn off the gantry using the 120 VAC service switch.

4.8  Reassembly gantry

  1. Install the gantry base covers including the 2 new side base covers with the cutouts for the interface panels.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

    NOTE:When putting on the rear base covers you may need to adjust the IPC panels such that the base cover can be fully installed. Adjust panels in or out as necessary.

  1. Install the gantry lower rear cantrells (latches) for the gantry rear cover attachment.

  1. Install the gantry front and rear covers, scan window, then the top and left side covers.

  1. Turn on the 120 VAC, Axial drive and HVDC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

  1. Add the upgrade rating plate to the gantry left leg near the other rating plates.

5  Finalization

  1. Run the System scanning test from the Functional Checks procedure list.

  1. Plug in any options the customer has to the interface panels as applicable for your site. Perform any defined functional checks for the options plugged into the interface panels.
