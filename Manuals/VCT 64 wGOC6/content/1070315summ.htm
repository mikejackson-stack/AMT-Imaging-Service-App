3  Procedure

3.1   Data Acquisition

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Remove scan window.

  1. Select [CALIBRATION] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [ISO ALIGNMENT].

  1. If Attention window is displayed when tool is launched, CAREFULLY READ and click [ACCEPT].

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the [ACCEPT] button is clicked, another Attention window will be displayed. CAREFULLY READ and click [OK].

  1. Click [SCAN] to execute air scans.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach a 1/8-inch diameter round metal pin to gantry end of cradle, or to phantom holder.

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

  1. Move table toward gantry until axial internal alignment lights are aligned with metal pin's mid-point.

  1. Adjust phantom holder side knob until metal pin is 35mm to right of sagittal internal alignment lights.

  1. Move table elevation until coronal internal alignment lights are aligned with metal pin.

  1. Move table up 35mm above scan plane.

  1. Click [SCAN] to execute pin scans.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [CALCULATE].

  1. If no adjustments are required, click [DISMISS] and skip to section 5.

3.2   Mechanical Alignment

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Manually rotate gantry until X-Ray tube reaches 2 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Mount dial indicator gauge on Z-Axis mounting bracket, located on tube assembly front face, with gauge's probe resting perpendicular to tube casting's front face (Item 1 in Illustration 1)

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount dial indicator gauge on X-Axis mounting bracket, located on tube assembly left face, with gauge's probe resting perpendicular to tube casting's left face (Item 2 in Illustration 1)

  1. Set both dial indicators to zero.

  1. Loosen four M12 mounting bolts on tube assembly about 1/4 to 1/2 turns with a 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

  1. Turn X-axis adjustment screw (Item 4 in Illustration 1), located on tube assembly left side, in direction specified by ISO Alignment tool, until dial indicator shows ISO-Alignment calculated value.

    (Step Details)

  1. If Z-axis dial indicator shows that tube moved left or right, turn Z-axis adjustment screw (Item 3 in Illustration 1) to position tube back to its original left-right position.

  1. Tighten four M12 mounting bolts on tube assembly in a cross pattern configuration, with a 10mm hex bit socket torque wrench and 12 inch extension, to the following torque values:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Click [RESTART] from ISO Alignment tool and repeat Section 4.1, Steps 4 - 15 until no adjustments are required.

  1. Perform Z-Alignment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove dial indicators.

  1. Verify dial indicator mounting-bracket screws are tight.
