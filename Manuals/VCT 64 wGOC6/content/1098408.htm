STEP 3A: Verify Site Readiness

Labor:1 person; 0.25 man-hour
Tools:LOTO Kit
Site-Readiness Checklist
NOTICE

  • All sites must have a main disconnect with Lockout/Tagout capability.

  • Non GE-supplied breakers must have/provide under-voltage protection. For more information, refer to the Pre-Installation Manual for this system.

  • A LICENSED ELECTRICIAN shall install and make connections inside the main disconnect.

 

  1. Lock-out and tag-out the A1 breaker.

  2. Complete inspection to verify site readiness.

  3. Visually inspect the site for damages.
