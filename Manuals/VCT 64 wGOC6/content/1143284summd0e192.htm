Type: [root@hostname] nmap -n darcrmm verbrmm darcarray darcarray2 and press [Enter].

All computers physically configured on the console in question should respond to nmap command (See example below). Note: LightSpeed VCT will not have a “darcarray2” and may not have a “verbrmm” in command output.
