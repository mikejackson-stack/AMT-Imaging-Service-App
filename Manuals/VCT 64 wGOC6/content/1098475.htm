STEP 7: Move Gantry Into Final Position

Labor:2 people; 0.5 man-hour
Tools:Ratchet and Socket Set
Install Support Kit
Gantry Adjuster Tool, P/N 2107863
Spanner Wrench, P/N 2110003
PPE

1. With the dollies on masonite board, position the gantry over the floor cutouts. Remove the shipping bolts and place the leveling pads beneath the adjusters.

2. Route the gantry-end of Run #52 through the rear entry cable box and under the gantry frame (PDU to Gantry LVAC Power: P/N 2343528 or 2343528-2).

3. Evenly lower the gantry until it is just off the floor and rotate it into position such that the leveling pads are centered in the floor cutouts. Position accuracy is important, because any deviation in position of the gantry pads is magnified for the table pads.

4. Manually turn the gantry levelers until there is 20 mm between the bottom of the leveling pad and the gantry frame.

5. Lower the gantry until it rests on the floor with all four leveling pads centered inside the floor cutouts.
