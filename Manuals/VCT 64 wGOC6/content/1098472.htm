STEP 6: Transport Gantry to Scan Room

Labor:2 people; 1 man-hour
Tools:Protective Floor Coverings
PPE

  1. Remove all transportation packaging except for the dollies.

  2. Use protective floor covering (masonite board) over all final floor surfaces.

  3. Supervise and assist the delivery team to transport the gantry to the scan room.

  4. Visually inspect the gantry for condensation and damages.
