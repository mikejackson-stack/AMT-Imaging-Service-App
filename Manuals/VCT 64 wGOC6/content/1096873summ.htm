Drilling the Gantry and Table Anchor Holes

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
POTENTIAL FOR PATIENT INJURY.
IMPROPERLY SECURED TABLE MAY TIP, DISLODGING PATIENT.
PROPER ANCHORING IS KEY TO MAINTAINING PATIENT SAFETY DURING SYSTEM OPERATION.

1  Procedure

1.1   Drilling the Anchor Holes

NOTE:The gantry rear cover should still be removed and the table should still be on the dolly.

  1. Make sure that all table and gantry levelers (four each) are firmly on the concrete floor.

NOTICE
Potential for Equipment Damage from Dust

To prevent damage due to the dust created during drilling, you must cover all electronic assemblies in the table base prior to drilling.

 

  1. Locate the hammer drill and ½ in. X 12 in. drill bit. The ½ in. bit is used to drill all eight (8) table and gantry anchor holes. You must use the drilling bushing to drill all table and gantry holes. All primary holes can be drilled with the gantry front covers installed.

  1. Use a piece of tape to mark the drill bit depth of 190 mm (7-1/2 in.) from the tip of the ½ in.masonry drill bit.

  1. Use the ½ in. bit to drill all eight (8) anchor holes to a depth of 190 mm (7-1/2 in.), as measured from the top of the drill bushing. Review Illustration 1 and Illustration 2 prior to drilling.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place appropriate protection to prevent damage and dust contamination to electronic assemblies.

  1. Place the drill bushing inside each adjuster, to keep the hole vertical and centered within the adjuster.

    (Step Details)

  1. Drill the holes perpendicular to the floor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Recheck the depth of all holes by inserting an anchor backward into the hole. You should have 20 mm (3/4 in.) or less showing. Re-drill if needed.

  1. When finished drilling and clearing the anchor holes, vacuum the debris from the inside of each of the holes and from the surrounding (floor) area.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Alternate Table and Gantry Anchor Holes

If you cannot use one of the adjuster anchor holes due to structural interference, such as reinforcement bars in the concrete, you must use one of the alternate anchor locations, as shown in Step . You must also move the respective leveler(s) and pad(s) to the new alternate location(s) and re-drill.
