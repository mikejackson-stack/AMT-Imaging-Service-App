4  Procedure

4.1  ORP Assembly Removal

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry such that ORP assembly is in 2 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Disconnect four front cables and one rear cable from ORP Assembly.

  1. Detach and remove cable clamp from top of ORP Assembly with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

  1. Move cable bundle away from ORP Assembly in order to avoid damage during ORP Assembly removal / install process.

  1. Remove three M6 mounting screws from front of gantry with a 5mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension (See Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  ORP Assembly Mounting Screws

  1. Remove ORP Assembly from gantry.

4.2  ORP Assembly Installation

  1. Position ORP Assembly on to gantry and fasten with three M6 mounting screws.

  1. Tighten each M6 mounting screw with a 5mm hex bit socket torque wrench and 12 inch extension, to the following torque values.

    Table 1:  M6 Mounting Screws Torque Specifications

    5.8 ft.-lbs7.9 N-m 70 in-lbs80.8 kg-cm

  1. Connect four front cables and one rear cable to ORP Assembly.

  1. Attach cable clamp to ORP Assembly top cover bracket and install harness into cable clamp.

  1. Secure cable clamp to ORP Assembly top cover bracket with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

  1. Disengage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Restore power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. From Console Unix Shell prompt, type touch /usr/g/fw/orpFtp and press [ENTER].

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

4.3  Setup, Checks, Alignments, and Calibrations

  1. FLASH Download

  1. Gantry Rotation Safety Check

  1. Gantry Balance Procedure

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Install gantry front cover, gantry top covers, gantry left side cover and scan window.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install gantry right side cover.
