Tube Oil Cooling System Heat Exchanger Compensation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for Burns. Severe personal injury can occur via burns from contact with hot oil. Do not service while tube and oil are hot (See required condition, below).

CAUTION
Large Amounts of air in tube heat exchanger can cause IQ artifacts or scan aborts.

CAUTION
Never open Oil compensation tools valve while it is connected to gantry.

CAUTION
Potential for personal injury. An energized gantry presents mechanical (rotational) and electrical hazards.

CAUTION
Apparatus can be used on oil-based systems only. Use of this apparatus on water-based cooled systems (blue hoses) will cause unknown system damage.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For Performix Pro VCT 100 X-ray Tube.

-

No X-Ray exposures have been made for AT LEAST one (1) hour. Tube casing and oil hoses are cool to touch.

3  Procedure

3.1   Apparatus Preparation

  1. With apparatus valve in open state, push in piston until it stops, forcing air out of apparatus.

  1. Attach hose onto apparatus valve spout.

  1. Insert open end of hose into a filled Transformer Oil container.

  1. Tip apparatus so that piston handle is up and slowly pull apparatus piston back as far as possible to fill cylinder with oil (See Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. If there is air present in cylinder, complete the following steps to bleed air from cylinder (See Illustration 2).

    1. Lay apparatus flat. Air will rise to valve area by sight glass.

    1. Push piston all the way in, forcing out all air and liquid, then slowly pull piston back.

    (Step Details)

  1. With cylinder full and air free, close valve tightly.

  1. Hold apparatus above oil container, and slowly pull hose off end of apparatus, drain oil into container. Wipe any residual oil from apparatus with dry wipes.

3.2   Preliminary Step

NOTICE
If this procedure execution is NOT due to another Service Procedure, complete the following preparation steps.
 

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [ AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry so tube rests at 3 o'clock or 4 o'clock positions.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Remove tube oil pump to tube quick disconnect safety mechanism bolts with a 5mm hex bit socket drive and uncouple quick disconnects.

3.3   Tube Heat Exchanger Bellows Compensation

  1. Connect apparatus male quick disconnect to tube oil pump female quick disconnect (See Illustration 3).

  1. Connect apparatus female quick disconnect to tube male quick disconnect (See Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Keeping handle higher than rest of apparatus, push down piston's handle until it stops.

    (Step Details)

  1. With bellows full of oil, note volume of oil remaining in apparatus cylinder.

  1. Slowly pull back on apparatus handle so that 30 units of oil are removed from heat exchanger bellows.

    (Step Details)

  1. Uncouple apparatus male quick disconnect from tube oil pump female quick disconnect.

  1. Uncouple apparatus female quick disconnect from tube male quick disconnect.

  1. Connect tube oil pump female quick disconnect to tube male quick disconnect.

  1. Empty any oil in apparatus syringe, clean apparatus and prepare for air shipment/storage.

4  Finalization

  1. If procedure execution was due to another service procedure, return to associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform the following steps:

    1. Restore tube oil pump to tube quick disconnect safety mechanism and torque M6 bolts with a 5mm hex bit torque drive to the following values.

      (Step Details)

    1. Disengage rotational lock.

    1. Install gantry front cover, gantry top covers, gantry left side cover, and scan window.

    1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [ AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

    1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

    1. Install gantry right side cover.
