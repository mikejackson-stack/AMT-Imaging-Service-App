GOC6.6 VCT Host Computer (Z820) FDIP Card Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE Z820, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE Scanner.

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Z820 Computer Removal

  1. Open the Z820 computer side access panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Expansion Card Support.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the existing FDIP card with new one.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Expansion Card Support and close the Side Access Panel.

3.2   Restore the Console

  1. Fully slide the Z820 computer back into the console chassis, until mounting brackets are flush against the console chassis.

  1. Remove and store the Service Platform.

  1. Install and torque the four (4) 3mm socket cap screws that mount the Z820 computer in the GOC6.6 console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect all cables removed earlier to the Z820 computer.

  1. Remove LOTO on console.

4  Finalization

  1. Install console covers and Keyboard Tabletop assembly. Refer to Replacement → Console (GOC6.6) → Console Cover Removal and Installation Procedure.

  1. Perform the Functional Checks → System Scanning Test instructions from the procedure list.
