High Voltage Termination Plug Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for Personal Injury.
Potential for Personal Injury. An Energized Gantry Presents Mechanical (Rotational) and Electrical Hazards.
Prior to continuing this procedure, turn OFF all 3 service switches on the Service Switch Panel (120VAC, Axial Drive Enable, HVDC Enable)

1  Procedure

  1. Open gantry covers and turn OFF Axial and HVDC.

  1. Rotate tube to 12:00.

  1. Turn 120VAC switch OFF.

  1. Cut the Tie Wraps securing the HV cable and remove the HV Candlestick from tank receptacle.

  1. Check HV tank well.

NOTICE
O-ring must be in place for an oil seal. Inspect candlestick and well for burn marks and oil presence. Dry spots on the candlestick may indicate that there is not enough oil in the receptacle. Lack of oil can cause arcing at the interface, which would be reported by the system as spits.
 

  1. Insert oil into the HV tank well to avoid air gaps when plug is inserted.

  1. Install the HV termination Plug tool. Oil should overflow slightly with tool inserted.

  1. Wipe up surplus oil before tightening HV Termination Plug retaining ring. The metal washer must be in place for the proper fit of the Termination Plug.

  1. Tighten the retaining ring until the tool is snug in the well to make a good oil seal when tank is upside down.

  1. Secure the HV cable end with Tie Wraps to keep it from being damaged during manual tube positioning.
