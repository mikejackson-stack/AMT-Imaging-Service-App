Gantry Cover Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Muscle Strain
Covers are large and heavy
Use two (2) people to lift and change covers as described by this procedure.

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service-Gantry.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT scanner.

-

-

The Gantry side, top and scan window procedures must have already been used.

-

-

Table should be at it's lowest position to allow front cover to be moved away from gantry.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Front Cover Replacement

  1. Remove the current gantry side covers, top covers, scan window, and front cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Connect E-stop cable to terminal as shown in the cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the Gantry Display Panel from the front cover by removing the five (5) captive screws. Refer to Replacements > Gantry > Display > Display Assembly Replacement and Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two Control Panels from the front cover by pressing on each ball stud for the panel until the panel is released. See Replacements > Gantry > Display > Operator Control Panel Replacement and Illustration 1 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Breathing Light Assembly from the front cover by removing the four (4) screws with a 3-mm hex wrench. See Replacements > Gantry > Display > Breathing Light Assembly Replacement and Illustration 1 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Tilt Sensor from the front cover by removing the two (2) nuts with a 9/32-inch socket wrench. See Replacements > Gantry > Display > Gantry Cover Tilt Sensor Replacement and Illustration 1 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) E-stop assemblies by removing the four (4) 4-mm hex screws for each assembly. Refer to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tilt the front cover to its horizontal position over the top of the table.

  1. Turn gantry power ON and move the table up to the gantry cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cover dollies from the front cover and allow cover to rest on the table.

  1. With two (2) people, lift the cover off the table and set aside.

  1. With two (2) people, place the new cover on the table and mount the cover dollies onto the new front cover.

  1. Lower the table to its original position and turn gantry power OFF.

  1. Rotate front cover to vertical and mount the Display Panel, Control Panels, Breathing Light Assembly, E-stop assemblies, and Tilt Sensor using the opposite method from which they were removed.

  1. Install the new front cover on the gantry.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply VCT Front Gantry Cover Label to gantry cover at the same position it is on the existing front cover. See Illustration 2 for reference.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Rear Cover Replacement

  1. Remove the current gantry rear cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the X-Ray Display Panel from the rear cover by removing the four (4) screw and washer combinations attaching the panel. Refer to Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two Control Panels from the rear cover by pressing on each ball stud for the panel until the panel is released. See Replacements > Gantry > Display > Operator Control Panel Replacement and Illustration 3 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Tilt Sensor from the rear cover by removing the four (4) nuts with a 9/32-inch socket. See Replacements > Gantry > Display > Gantry Cover Tilt Sensor Replacementand Illustration 3 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Breathing Light and Microphone assemblies with the Tilt Sensor panel. Refer to Illustration 3 to note position of these components.

  1. Remove the two (2) E-stop assemblies by removing the four (4) 4-mm hex screws. Refer to Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. If desired, turn gantry power ON and move the table up to waist-height, for easier handling with the dollies.

    (Step Details)

  1. With two (2) people, move the cover alongside table and tilt it over to lay on the table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the dollies.

  1. Using two (2) people, set the old cover aside and place the new cover on the table.

  1. Attach dollies to the new cover.

  1. With two (2) people, tilt the cover back up onto the dollies.

  1. If table was moved up for cover swap, return to original position and turn gantry power OFF.

  1. Mount the Display Panel, Control Panels, E-stop assemblies, and Tilt Sensor Panel using the opposite method from which they were removed.

  1. With two (2) people, return the cover to the back of the gantry, and install the new rear cover on the gantry.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Turn ON 120 VAC switch from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. When AC power is restored to the system, prior to enabling axial drive or HVDC, remember to rotate the gantry by hand to ensure there is no interference between covers and rotating components.

  1. Turn ON Axial Drive and HVDC switches from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remember when power is restored, observe Gantry Display, Gantry Control panels, and Breathing Lights during power on. Verify Power On Self Test (POST) completes normally and all cover indicators were illuminated during the POST. Verify that all Gantry control panels are functional with no ERR indicators. Also verify that the Gantry to Operator intercom is functional.

  1. From the front cover control panel, press and hold the gantry forward tilt button and then press on the tilt sensor while still tilting gantry. Verify the gantry tilt stops.

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.

  1. Verify all covers, especially side covers are properly secured.

  1. Ensure there is no interference during all tilt range.
