Lexan Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
This procedure involves handling lead. Wear your lead PPE and follow all lead-handling safety procedures.
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Lexan Removal

  1. Locate the interposer plate. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Before removing the interposer plate, outline its position with a marker as shown in Illustration 2. This facilitates returning the collimator to its original position later.

    (Step Details)

  1. On VCT systems, remove 3 screws from the interposer plate as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. After removing the interposer plate screws, unscrew the alignment adjuster until the plate can be removed.

  1. Drive the collimator filters to the home position. See Illustration 4. This reduces the chance of contamination and potential image quality problems.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws from the lead aperture as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. With a flat blade screwdriver, carefully pry out the lead aperture.

  1. Clean any debris away from the filter and the lead aperture.

  1. Remove Lexan from the lead aperture and clean the underside of any debris. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Reassemble Collimator

  1. Using a single drop of Loctite 242 on each aperture screw, re-attach the lead aperture.

  1. Re-attach the Interposer plate, re-aligning it to the previously marked position. See Illustration 2
