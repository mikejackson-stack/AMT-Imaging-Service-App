Heat Exchanger Filter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Potential for Shock.
Voltage may be present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

1  Procedure

  1. Remove side, top, and front gantry covers.

  1. Turn OFF service switches on the Service Switch Panel (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120VAC).

  1. Rotate gantry so that Heat Exchanger is at the 2 O'Clock position (tube at or just below 3 o'clock).

  1. Remove the screws holding the filter in place. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove filter by lifting straight up and out.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install filter. Be sure the lower edge of the filter engages the tabs on the heat exchanger frame.

  1. Replace the screws holding the filter.

  1. Switch ON service switches on the Service Switch Panel (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120VAC).

  1. Replace all covers on the gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
