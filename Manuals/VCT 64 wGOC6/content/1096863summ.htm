Install Operator Console, Peripherals, Hard Drives, etc

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Unpack the Console

If console is construction site packed, vacuum the dust and debris before removing packaging.

  1. Remove all items from the console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all packing materials and discard.

  1. Place the step-board under the front edge of the skid and step on it to raise the front edge of the skid as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two front cushions from the bottom of the skid (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Seismic Brackets from each side of the console (see Illustration 4). Save the brackets for use later if you need to mount the console to the floor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift up on the strap on the front of the step-board (Illustration 2) to lower the skid. Remove the step-board.

  1. Ensure the console stabilizers are in line with the notched portion at the front of the skid. This allows enough clearance to smoothly roll the console down the ramps (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the console to the installation location.

  1. Attach the two pads to the console stabilizers (see Illustration 6). (If not already attached)

    (Step Details)

  1. Screw the pads all the way into the legs

1.2   Remove Console Covers

  1. Loosen the two captive screws at the bottom of console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the bottom of the cover outward and upward until you can lift it free of the console at the top.

1.3   Adjust Table Top Height and Position Console

  1. The console normally arrives with the bottom hole on the monitor top aligned with the fourth bolt from the bottom (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. For optimum operator comfort, align the top hole of the keyboard table top with the third or fourth hole from top of cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert washers on front bolts.

  1. Install the console side covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. After all necessary cables to the console have been run, move the console into its final position, maintaining a 15 cm (6 in.) minimum distance between the console and the wall.

1.4   Attach Keyboard Table Top

  1. Locate the spring-loaded latches at either end of the underside of the keyboard table top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the latches inward (toward center of table top).

  1. Position table on the brackets so that the latches align with the holes.

  1. Release the latches, making certain that the pins fully engage the bracket holes (pins should protrude beyond outside edge of bracket).
