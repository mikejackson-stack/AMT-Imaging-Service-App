1  Ceiling Mounted Component Bolts

These instructions refer to any GE-supplied ceiling-mounted object.

  1. Acquire and place an appropriately-sized ladder under the ceiling-mounted component.

  2. Remove any ceiling covers and ceiling tiles to access the pedestal base.

  3. Verify that the mounting plate and pedestal base are securely bolted.

    • If the mounting plate is loose, inform the customer to have their contractor check and repair the structural integrity.

    • If the pedestal base is tight, confirm that the bolts that were marked at install have not moved.

    • If the bolts were not marked at install, mark the bolt and plate using a permanent marker.

    • If any loose hardware is detected, use a torque wrench and re-secure. Add a new visual inspection mark.

  4. Inspect the arm assembly and confirm that the assembly is tight.

  5. If the arm assembly is loose, refer to the installation manual and tighten.

  6. Check the component end and confirm that the mounting hardware is tight. Tighten any loose hardware.

  7. Reinstall all removed covers and ceiling tiles and store the ladder.

3.2  Cardiac Scan Setup for Functional Check

  1. On the setup screen, enter the following selections:

    • New Patient: GE Test

    • On the Protocol Selection screen, select theUser tab (see Illustration 1).

    • On the User tab, select [Chest].

  2. On the scan monitor (on the dark blue bar) select: Gating On

  3. Look for the following diagnostics to display (see Illustration 2):

    • Heart rate on the gantry display board

    • Cardiac pulses shown on the screen

    • Gating BPM displayed on the screen

    • ECG trace highlighted on the screen
