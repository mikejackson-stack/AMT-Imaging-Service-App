Proportional Valve Assy Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
crash hazard !
the table must be supported using the support tool as described below when changing the proportional valve assembly.

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table components and covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the Table Support Tool as follows:

    1. Perform this step if applicable:

      (Step Details)

    WARNING
    crash hazard!
    The Table is very heavy and can move if The Global PET/CT Table transporter is not secured!
    The transporter must be secured from moving before attaching the table support tool.

    1. Perform this step if applicable:

      (Step Details)

    1. The adjuster of the support tool A should be partially extended, about 3 cm, before supporting the Table.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach the support tool A to the left top frame, and turn the set screw to CCW to fix the support tool A.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach the other support tool A to the right top frame, and turn the set (hexagon) screw to CCW to fix the support tool A.

    1. Attach the support tool B to the support tool A using 4 screws.

    1. Extend the adjusters by turning them so that the adjusters touch the floor, and lower the Table onto the support. Make sure that there is no oil on the floor and the support tool is installed perpendicular to the floor.

      (Step Details)

NOTICE
Confirm the following before removing power from Table:

  • The floor under the support tool is not slippery

  • The support tool is installed perpendicular to floor.

  • The support tool is firmly fixed to the table top frame by set screws.

  • The support tool is securely holding the full weight of the Table.

 

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Cover the Inverter and the Power Supply to prevent oils contamination.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the valve cable to the Table frame.

  1. Disconnect the cable connectors of the proportional valve.

  1. Put the oil pan under the left proportional valve, and unscrew 4 screws, then remove the left proportional valve.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Properly position the O-ring on the manifold. If not, a oil can be leaked out from the joint.
 

  1. Fit new O-rings into the manifold. (If the new O-rings are not contained in the replacement kit, reuse the existing O-rings.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Proportional Valve in place, and tighten the 4 screws.

  1. Route the new valve cable along the old cable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat Step 10 though Step 12 for the other side valve.

  1. Connect the valve cable connectors.

  1. Fasten the valve cable to the Table frame with tie-wraps.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position, and verify that the new proportional valves are securely installed.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Remove the Table Support Tool from the Table.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Raise and lower the Table repeatedly to remove air from the hydraulic system.

  1. Perform Elevation Inching Adjustment.

  1. Using the Gantry control keys, raise and lower the Table repeatedly, and verify that the valve cable does not catch or pull excessively.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-install the Table components and covers.
