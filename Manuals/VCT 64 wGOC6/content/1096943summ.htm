Center Phantom

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Prepare the QA Phantom

NOTE:The QA phantom is shipped water-filled.

  1. Locate the multi-language sticker packet in the QA phantom shipping box.

  1. Attach the sticker with the customer's language to the face of the phantom hanger bracket.

  1. Check for bubbles.

  1. Purge and refill if necessary.

1.2   Center Phantom

  1. Locate the QA phantom and mount it and the phantom holder on the table.

  1. Use the adjustment knobs on the phantom holder to level the phantom front-to-back and sideto- side with a bubble level.

  1. Select [SCANNER UTILITIES] on the left monitor.

  1. Select [CENTER PHANTOM].

  1. Follow the on-screen procedures.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [QUIT] when the phantom is within specification.

    (Step Details)
