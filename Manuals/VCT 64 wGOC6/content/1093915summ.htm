Heat Exchanger Power Supply Replacement

1  Safety

DANGER
electrocution hazard
voltage present in gantry
use proper lockout/tagout procedures from the safety section of the service documentation when stated during procedure. This procedure includes work directly in the gantry power pan.

WARNING

NOTICE

3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation for Replacement

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the gantry top and left side covers, scan window and front cover. Connect the cover E-stop circuit to the terminators on the gantry.

3.2   Removal of old Power Supply Assembly

  1. Rotate the gantry so that the power supply is in the 6:00 position. Refer to Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Gantry Position

  1. Loosen inner screws on the right side wires of power supply; insert a flat head screwdriver down through the top slot and remove wires from the assembly. Refer to Illustration 2. Take care not to completely remove the screws.

    Illustration 2:  Power Supply Wires

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) screws on the bottom of the power supply with a 3mm hex wrench. Refer to Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Mounting points of Power Supply

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide power supply to the right side to access the left side wires and remove using the same method as the right side wires. Refer to Illustration 2 and the corresponding note for details.

3.3   Installation of new Power Supply Assembly

  1. Set new power supply on bracket and slide to the right so that the left side cable connections can be accessed with a screwdriver. Connect the left side wires by inserting cables into the power supply between the head of the screw and the board. Tighten the screw using a flat head screwdriver. Refer toIllustration 5 (left) for wire position.

WARNING
equipment damage possible
loose components can come off if not secured properly
Failure to follow the instructions in this procedure as defined can cause a component to become loose later.

  1. Slide power supply into correct position and bolt onto the gantry at the same four (4) points as the old supply. Refer to Illustration 3. Torque front two (2) bolts according to Table 1. Reinstall spacer/washer if removed earlier.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a screwdriver to reconnect the right side power supply wires to the power supply. Refer to Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Wire connections to power supply

3.4   Reassembly of Gantry

  1. Install the gantry front cover, scan window, top and left side covers.

  1. Turn on the Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

4  Finalization

  1. Re-connect cables to the gantry display and controls panels.

  1. Replace gantry right side cover.

  1. Run the System Scanning Test.
