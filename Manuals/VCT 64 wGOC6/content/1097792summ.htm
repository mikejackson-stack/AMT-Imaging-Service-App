1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

CAUTION
POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
HOST COMPUTER CHASSIS CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 30 POUNDS.
DEPENDING ON YOUR HEIGHT AND ACCESS TO EQUIPMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION, LIFTING ASSISTANCE MAY BE REQUIRED. USE YOUR JUDGMENT TO DETERMINE IF LIFT ASSIST OR A SECOND PERSON IS REQUIRED TO ASSIST IN REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

3  Procedure

3.1   Powering Off (Shutting Down) the Operator Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power Off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch.

  1. Make sure to follow all Lockout/Tagout requirements while performing this procedure. Refer to Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Removing the HP Z400 Host Computer from Operator Console

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console covers.

  1. Remove the cable connections to the HP Z400 Host Computer chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the Host Computer chassis.

  1. Loosen the two (2) cap screws on the retaining bracket at the lower right front of the Host Computer chassis, which holds the Host Computer in place in the Operator Console right side compartment. See Illustration 1

  1. Pivot the Retaining Bracket away from the Host Computer chassis.

    Illustration 1:  HP Z400 Console Mounting

  1. Slide the Host Computer forward (out the front of console) and set aside.

3.3   Access Panel Removal

  1. If necessary, unlock the access panel using the keys located in the rear of the workstation. (See Illustration 2

  1. Once unlocked, pull up on the handle and lift off the access panel cover.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Replacing HP Z400 Host Computer Lower Level FRUs

3.4.1  SCSI Card Replacement

  1. Follow the steps in HP Z400 SCSI Card Replacement for replacing the (Optional) SCSI Card in the HP Z400 Host Computer.

  1. Return to this procedure when finished and continue with Section 4.5.

3.4.2  Future FRU's

  1. Reserved for future FRU additions

  1. Return to this procedure when finished and continue with Section 4.5.

3.5   Reinstalling the HP Z400 Host Computer in Operator Console

  1. Slide the HP Z400 Host Computer into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Hand tighten the two (2) cap screws on the retaining bracket to secure the Host Computer chassis to the Operator Console right side compartment.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the Host Computer chassis.

  1. Replace the rear cable connections.

3.6   Powering-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify that the Host Computer front panel Power LED is illuminated. If not, press the Host Computer front panel power switch to apply power to the Host Computer.

4  Finalization

  1. Refer to System Scanning Tests in the Functional Checks chapter of this manual to confirm proper operation.

  1. Check that all customer functions are operational. (Auto voice, filming, network, archive).

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
