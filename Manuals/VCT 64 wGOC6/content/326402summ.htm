Rotating 48VDC PS and Fuse Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

1  Procedure

1.1   Power Supply Assembly Removal

  1. Remove gantry side covers and disable Axial drive and HVDC from the Service Switch Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the gantry to position the 48V power supply on the right side of the gantry above the service switch panel and lock gantry in position with rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Turn off the gantry 120 VAC switch on the service panel. Power down the console and use LOTO procedures to make sure gantry power is removed.

  1. Remove the gantry top covers and front cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Power supply output cables on the front and rear of the supply as shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3. Tywraps for the front cable support need to be removed as shown. Make note of any other tywraps removed during supply removal for reinstallation later. Also remove cable clamps attaching the output cables to the power supply assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the AC input cables from the terminal strip on the front of the power supply.

  1. Remove the 8 mounting screws attaching the power supply assembly to the cast frame and slide the supply out the front of the gantry.

1.2   Power Supply Assembly Installation

  1. Slide the new power supply assembly in from the front of the gantry.

  1. Install the 8 - M4 screws to attach the supply to the cast frame.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power to the system and gantry per LOTO procedures.

2  Finalization

  1. Check power supply output levels per 48 Volt Power Supply Checks.

  1. Perform a Gantry Rotation Safety Check.

  1. Check Gantry Balance to verify gantry is still balanced.

  1. Run DASTools[Auto Test] to verify that no noise is introduced by the new supply.

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install the gantry front, top and left side covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.
