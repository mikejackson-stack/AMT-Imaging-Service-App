2  Overview

This document provides the necessary steps to verify system calibration for different scan modes.

Last Revised:November 9, 2009

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

CAUTION
Remove any objects from scan window area which may interfere with the rotating gantry.

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.

3.2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For VCT type X-ray Generation Systems.

4  Procedure

4.1  Data Acquisition

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [KV & MA (X-RAY)].

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set following parameter values.

    ParameterValue
    X-Ray Test TypeManual
    kV SelectionValue from Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits
    mAValue from Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits
    min ISD3
    # Exposures1
    Exp Time (sec)1.00
    Filter TypeClosed
    ApertureClosed
    Focal SpotValue from Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits

  1. In Gantry Params pane, set following parameter values.

    ParameterValue
    GantryDisabled

  1. Click [RUN].

  1. Press [START] on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Verify that the following conditions are met:

    1. The kV and mA X-ray tool “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” is within limits defined in Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits for prescribed scan protocol.

    1. The kV and mA X-ray tool “mA Average Value” is within limits defined in Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits for prescribed scan protocol.

    1. The kV and mA X-ray tool “mA @ 5 msec Average Value” is within limits defined in Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits for prescribed scan protocol.

    1. The kV and mA X-ray tool “Exposure Duration Average Value” is within limits defined in Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits for prescribed scan protocol.

      NOTE:If any of these conditions are not met, perform appropriate corrective action and retest.

  1. Record results in appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. Repeat Step 4 through Step 9 for all protocols defined in Scan Protocol Parameters and Limits.

  1. Click [DISMISS] in kV and mA Test window.

  1. For failed results, isolate root cause by running the following procedures in order shown below and perform appropriate corrective action.

    1. Meter Verification

    1. High Voltage Tank Feedback Resistor Verification

    1. Filament Calibration

  1. Store data form with system history records.
