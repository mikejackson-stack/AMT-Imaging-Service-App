Autovoice/Intercom Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Patient Speaker

To adjust the volume of the patient speaker in the table:

  1. Adjust the left-most volume thumb wheel on the SCIM while speaking into the console microphone. (Press the bar on the SCIM to talk; release the bar to listen.)

  1. The assistant should be able to clearly hear the operator.

1.2   Operator Console Speaker

To adjust the console speaker volume:

  1. Have an assistant speak into the gantry microphone.

  1. Adjust the SCIM console volume knob until you can clearly hear the assistant.

1.3   Autovoice Volume

  1. On the Scan Desktop, select [PROTOCOL MANAGEMENT].

  1. Select [AUTO VOICE RECORD].

  1. Click the [3.4] button, to the right of FF2. Inspiration.

  1. Click the [PLAY] button, to play the Inspiration AutoVoice message.

  1. Adjust the center volume thumb wheel while Autovoice is playing, to set the volume for the gantry speaker.

  1. Repeat steps 4 and 5 as necessary to achieve satisfactory volume.

  1. Select [DONE], then select [QUIT].
