Focal Spot Transfer Function

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hourNot Applicable

2  Overview

The Focal Spot Transfer function translates system level inputs (e.g., kVp, mA, focal spot size and position) to cathode voltage values for normal imaging and Gemstone Spectral Imaging.

Last Revised:July 29, 2009

4  Procedure

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Select [CALIBRATION] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [TRANSFER FUNCTION CALIBRATION].

  1. Verify that [TRANSFER FUNCTION CAL] and [PINCH MODE CAL] buttons are highlighted as selections.

  1. Click [OK] and follow screen instructions.

    NOTE:If tube is cold, a tube warm up is performed.

  1. Press [START] button on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. When completed, click[DISMISS].

5  Finalization

  1. Filament Calibration

  1. Save System State.
