STEP 2: Mark Equipment Anchoring Locations

Labor:1 person; 1 man-hour
Tools:Site-Readiness Checklist
Floor Template
Chalk Line
Masking Tape
Measuring Tape
Center Punch and Hammer
China marker, wax marking pencil, or equivalent

1. Using the GE site drawing, mark ISO and the table centerline on the floor.

2. Position the table template on the floor first, and then the gantry template on top. Align the templates to each other at the two centerlines, and tape the templates together.

3. Align the templates to the ISO and table centerline marks on the floor. Tape the templates to the floor.

4. Verify location with the customer.

5. Use the center punch to mark the 8 anchor locations. Mark the ends of the table centerline.
