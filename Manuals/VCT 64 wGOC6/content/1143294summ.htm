Microphone Cover Installation/Replacement Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow all required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner

3  Procedure

3.1   System Preparation

  1. Move table cradle all the way out of gantry.

  1. Remove gantry side and top covers.

  1. Turn OFF [120VAC], [HVDC ENABLE] and [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] switches on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove gantry front cover.

  1. Locate Breathing Light Assembly (inside front cover, top middle) and identify the part number. If you have an older version, 2200386-3, proceed to Microphone Cover Installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-3. Otherwise, for 2200386-4 versions, proceed to Microphone Cover installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-4 .

    (Step Details)

3.1.1  Microphone Cover Installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-3

  1. Ensure the microphone and the wires (Item 1 and 2) are aligned to the side to make a good seal with the Microphone Cover; refer to Illustration 3. It may be necessary to cut the tie-wrap (Item 3) and install a new one after making the proper adjustment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three screws and washers highlighted in Illustration 4 and discard. New hardware is included in the kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the hole locations from the previous step and Illustration 5, install the microphone cover with new hardware: Two M3 screws and washers and one M4 screw and washer. Ensure the cover makes a good seal with the breathing light assembly mounting plate (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Gantry Back Cover and repeat Steps 1-3 in Microphone Cover Installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-3 to install the other microphone cover.

  1. Proceed to Post-Installation Inspections.

3.1.2  Microphone Cover installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-4

  1. Ensure the microphone and the wires (Item 1) are aligned to the side to make a good seal with the Microphone Cover; refer to Illustration 7. It may be necessary to cut the tie-wrap and install a new one after making the proper adjustment.

  1. Remove the three screws and washers (Item 2 in Illustration 7) and discard. New hardware is provided in the kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the microphone cover with new hardware: Two M3 screws and washers and one M4 screw and washers. Ensure the cover makes a good seal with the breathing light assembly mounting plate, see Illustration 8 and Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Gantry Back Cover and repeat Steps 1-3 in Microphone Cover installation on Breathing Light Assembly 2200386-4 to install the other microphone cover.

  1. Proceed to Post-Installation Inspections.

3.2   Post-Installation Inspections

  1. Inspect for any cables and wires that could stick up off of the rotating gantry and interfere with the microphone cover. If any cables seem to be sticking up, similar to Illustration 10, cut the tie-wrap, adjust the cables to fit into the cable routing and install new tie-wrap.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-install the Gantry front and back covers and manually rotate the gantry and inspect for any contact between the cover and the rotating gantry.

  1. Turn ON [120VAC], [HVDC ENABLE], and [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] switches on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Open Axial Functionality (CSD - Diagnostics) and perform an axial rotation test at 2 sec/rev. While system gantry is rotating, listen for any noise indicating contact between microphone cover and rotating surface.

  1. Perform an axial rotation test at a higher speed (e.g., 0.5 sec/rev). During axial rotation test, activate the speaker on the SCIM and listen to the audio coming from the gantry. Ensure background noise has decreased (may need to adjust for better seal between microphone cover and mounting plate).
