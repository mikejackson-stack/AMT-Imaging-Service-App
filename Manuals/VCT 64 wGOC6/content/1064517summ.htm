Reset TnT

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For VCT type X-ray Generation Systems.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

Confirm [120 VAC], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch Panel are turned on.

-

-

Confirm Gantry Controls Table Collision Sensor Indicator has been reset.

-

-

Confirm Scan Hardware has been successfully reset.

-

-

Confirm no flashing indicators on Gantry Controls or Display.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI], or [X-RAY GENERATION], [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI].

  1. Click [RESET TNT DATA]

  1. Select data areas to be re-initialized.

    1. For Tube Replacement, select (highlight) [TUBE TNT DATA].

    1. For all other procedures, select (highlight) [TUBE TNT DATA] and [GENERATOR TNT DATA].

  1. Click [RESET].

  1. Click [OK] in Attention window.

  1. Click [CANCEL] in Reset TnT Data window.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [DISMISS] in Generator Tool - JEDI window.

3  Finalization

  1. Filament Calibration

  1. Save System State.
