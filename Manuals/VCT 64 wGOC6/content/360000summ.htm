SCSI Tower Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console front switch, when instructed to “Power Down”.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the SCSI cable, terminator and power cables.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the replacement SCSI tower, cables and terminator.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON the power to the Console and SCSI tower.

    (Step Details)

  1. As the console boots, you will see the SCSI tower devices info appear in the SCSI inventory as the console boots.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform a Reconfig.

  1. At application level complete a save and restore for the MOD. This operation should occur without errors.

  1. Read the customer option DVD, as a test of the DVD drive. The drive should operate without errors.

  1. Restore system for customer operation.

2  Finalization

  1. Refer to Table 1 in “GOC5 (GRE) Module and FRU Replacement Guide” for retest procedures.
