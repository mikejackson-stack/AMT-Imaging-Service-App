VCT Detector Plenum Upgrade Procedure

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for Shock
Voltage may be present
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document GUI for appropriate procedures.

1  Procedure

  1. Use the VCT Detector Air Plenum Removal and Installation procedure to remove the old plenum.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the black routing harness that runs over top of the plenum between the low and high channel routing boards. This will be replaced with a new harness supplied with the kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cable from the 48V power supply fuse board to the original plenum. This is the cable going to the back of the CFC and DHC supplying power to each unit. Install new supplied power cable along the same route as the old cable.

  1. Remove the DCB (Backplane J3) to CFC (J10) RS232 communication cable. Install the new supplied communication cable along the same route as the old cable.

  1. Install the new routing board harness over top of the plenum. SeeIllustration 4 andIllustration 5 for cable routing and clamping.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the new routing harness to the routing boards on each side of the Detector rails. See Illustration 6 for connector positioning since the labeling is hard to see with the detector in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install new plenum assembly per VCT Detector Air Plenum Removal and Installation . Do NOT connect cables to the fan or heater controllers yet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cable covers from the front of the Fan and Heater controllers if installed.

  1. For the Heater Controller (DHC) cable routing perform the following steps. SeeIllustration 7 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. For the CFC cable routing perform the following steps. SeeIllustration 8 for reference.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the new cabling per cable labels to the new plenum. All connections are on the front of the fan and heater controllers. Apply cable ties (ty-wraps) as necessary. Refer to Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure all cabling is secure. Use more cable ties if necessary to keep the cabling restrained.

2  Finalization

  1. Power on the gantry and let the detector warm up. This make take up to 45 minutes depending on how long the gantry power was off. You can still perform the gantry balance check while the detector is warming up.

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand with Axial Drive disabled to make sure all cabling is secure and can not move to hit the stationary frame.

  1. Perform a gantry balance check and rebalance if necessary.

  1. Perform a generic service scan using the QA phantom to make sure the system scans as expected.
