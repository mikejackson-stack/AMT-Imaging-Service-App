2  Overview

The System Option Licenses are tied to the Host Computer Ethernet (eth0) MAC address. The NIC card can be exchanged, if one is reasonably certain that the Host Computer failure is not related to the NIC card. This will allow for the reuse of System Option Licenses, otherwise new Licenses must be obtained.

This procedure describes and illustrates the steps necessary to exchange (transfer) the NIC Card from an existing HP Z400 Host Computer to a new HP Z400 Host Computer.

NOTE:This procedure is used in conjunction with the GOC6.5 Host Computer Replacement Procedure.

3.2  Safety

CAUTION
POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
HOST COMPUTER CHASSIS CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 30 POUNDS. DEPENDING ON YOUR HEIGHT AND ACCESS TO EQUIPMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION, LIFTING ASSISTANCE MAY BE REQUIRED.
USE YOUR JUDGMENT TO DETERMINE IF LIFT ASSIST OR A SECOND PERSON MAY BE REQUIRED TO ASSIST IN REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HOST COMPUTER, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 

NOTICE
IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

4  Procedure

  1. Open the original Host Computer Side Cover.

    NOTE:The System Option Licenses are tied to the Host Computer Ethernet (eth0) MAC Address. The NIC cards (Slot 3) can be exchanged, if one is reasonably certain that the Host Computer failure is not related to the NIC card. This will allow for the reuse of System Option Licenses, otherwise new Licenses must be obtained.

  1. Unlatch the Retention Clamp that holds the Expansion Cards in place.

    Illustration 1:  Expansion Card Retention Clamp Removal

  1. Remove the NIC from Slot 3, set aside.

    Illustration 2:  NIC Card (Slot 3) Removal

  1. Open the replacement Host Computer Side Cover.

  1. Unlatch the Retention Clamp that holds the Expansion Cards in place.

  1. Remove the NIC Card from Slot 3 and install the original Host Computer NIC Card set aside earlier.

  1. Install the NIC Card removed from the replacement Host Computer into the old Host Computer.

  1. Re-latch the Expansion Card Retention Clamps in both Host Computers

  1. Replace the Side Covers on the Host computers.

    NOTE:IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS COVER MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE COVERS AND RETENTION CLAMPS ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
