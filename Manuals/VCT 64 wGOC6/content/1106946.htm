2  Overview

This procedure applies to CT and PET/CT systems with the Integrated Peripheral Controller (IPC) board or the Gantry Options Board (GOB). The IVY 3150-B is a replacement for the IVY 3150-A.

3  Procedure

  1. Connect AC (IEC) power cable to the back of the Cardiac Monitor.

3.2  Enabling the IVY 3150 Cardiac Monitor

  1. At the console select:

    1. Common Service Desktop.

    1. Configuration tab.

    1. [Install Gating Monitor].

  1. From the menu select:

    1. [IVY3150].

    1. [Accept].

      NOTE:This automatically sets up the monitor at IP address 10.44.22.21.
