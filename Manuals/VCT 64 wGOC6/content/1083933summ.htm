3  Procedure

3.1   Gantry Preparation

NOTICE
This procedure uses many screw, lock and flat washer combinations. Make sure the order of installation has the lock washer positioned between the flat washer and head of the screw.
 

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial drive, HVDC and 120VAC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all Gantry base covers. (The cover on the bottom of the tilting assembly can stay in place.)

  1. Tilt the gantry all the way back for easier access to gantry base from the rear using the following steps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Shut down the console and perform LOTO for the gantry per safety procedures.

3.2   Power Box Removal

  1. Unplug the AC power cord from the power upgrade box.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen cable clamps and remove the hydraulic lines, power cables, Ethernet, and fiber cabling (3 mm hex wrench).

  1. Place a mark on the IPC panel mount plate next to each of the two screws to use for reference when reinstalling the plate later.

  1. Remove the IPC panel mount plate that is on top of the power assembly bracket (3 mm hex wrench).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the AC power box plate mount screw that is behind the cable clamps by the gantry power pan (3 mm hex wrench).

    (Step Details)

  1. Prepare to tilt the gantry forward by removing LOTO and restore power to gantry by the 120 VAC service switch.

    1. Use the tilt switch S10 to tilt the gantry forward for easy access to front side of gantry power pan. Check clearances on both sides when tilting to make sure there are no obstructions that need to be cleared.

    1. Turn off gantry 120 VAC service switch and reinstall LOTO to ensure no power to gantry.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Voltages present in gantry
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES from the safety section of the service documentation . this procedure includes work directly in the gantry power pan.

  1. From the front side of the gantry, remove the plastic cover from the top of the power pan by 2 - M4's (3 mm hex wrench).

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut cable ties as necessary for the AC power line from the power pan to the AC power box and remove the Power box from the gantry frame from the back of the gantry.

3.3   Connecting Power Lines to Power Pan

This section defines the connection between the power box and the power pan.

  1. Set the new power box in place and run the AC power lines to the front for connection to the gantry power pan.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the 120 VAC line filter, connect the lugs as shown in Table 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the plastic cover over the power pan with the 2 - M4's (3 mm hex wrench). Snug the screws being careful not to crack the plastic cover.

  1. Install cable ties where they were removed to keep cable bundles together.

3.4   Power Box Installation

  1. Remove LOTO to allow power to the gantry for tilt operation.

  1. Tilt the gantry all the way back for easier access to gantry base from the rear using the following steps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount the Power box to the gantry frame as follows.

    1. Position the box as shown in Illustration 7. The plastic cable clamps are for the hydraulics, Ethernet, fiber, and power lines.

      (Step Details)

    1. Install the AC power box assembly to the frame with M4 screws with lock and flat washers (3 mm hex wrench). First tighten the screw in the back between the gantry power pan and gantry leg.

      (Step Details)

    1. Reinstall the IPC mount panel on top of the AC power box base plate using the marks made previously to position the IPC panel. Torque all 3 mount screws to:

      (Step Details)

  1. Route the hydraulic lines, power cables, Ethernet and fiber cabling as shown in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug in the AC power cord to the power upgrade box and interface panel as shown in Illustration 8.

3.5   Power On Checks

  1. Before turning on power, turn off CB4 on the new power box.

  1. Remove LOTO and power on the gantry using the 120 VAC service switch.

  1. Turn on CB4 on the new power box.

DANGER
Electrocution hazard
Voltages present in gantry
USE PROPER safety PROCEDURES when testing with power present.

  1. Using a digital voltmeter, check each of the 4 outlets, 2 on each interface panel to make sure 120VAC is seen.

    (Step Details)

  1. While watching the gantry cabling, tilt the gantry forward and backward using the service switch panel Tilt switch S10. (Service Mode S4 is up and Tilt Enable S9 is ON) to make sure there is no cable interference with tilt function and there are no cables being pulled or rubbed during tilt function.

  1. Leave the gantry in a zero tilt position. Turn Tilt Enable S9 to Off and put the Service Mode switch in the down position.

  1. Turn off the gantry using the 120 VAC service switch.

3.6   Gantry Reassembly

  1. Install the gantry base covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on the 120 VAC, Axial drive and HVDC service switches from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Run the System Scanning Test from the Functional Checks procedure list.

  1. Plug in any options the customer has to the interface panels as applicable for your site. Perform any defined functional checks for the options plugged into the interface panels.
