Install Table Footswitch Assembly

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. After table positioning is completed and the anchors are installed, install the footswitch assembly as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pop off foot pedal screw cover tabs.

  1. Remove foot switch covers.

  1. Remove 3 Phillips screws that secure the assembly cover.

  1. Remove the footswitch assembly cover.

  1. Using two (2) M6 bolts, attach the footswitch assembly to the table base.

    Illustration 2:  Attach Footswitch

  1. Level the footswitch assembly using the three (3) level adjusters. Two are on the gantry side and one is in the middle. Use a 9 in. level to check the levelness in all directions.

    Illustration 3:  Level Footswitch
