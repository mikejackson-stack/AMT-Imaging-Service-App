Mechanical-Electrical Transition

  1. Installation site cleaned and all trash properly disposed.

  2. Return of dollies and lean carts arranged for and pick-up made.

  3. Mechanical installation section of the GE Form e4879 completed.

  4. Seismic mounting installed, if required in your area.

    5. NOTE:If the electrical installation will be performed by a different person/team than the mechanical installation, the mechanical installer shall also complete the following steps. If the same person/team is performing the electrical installation (for example, Field Engineers doing to complete install), the following steps can be performed at a later time according to the Electrical Work Flow.

  5. FE Service cabinet moved to the location shown on the site print, and service items placed inside.

  6. All covers installed and aligned

  7. All options installed on the table and gantry

  8. GE Healthcare personnel notified that the mechanical installation is completed
