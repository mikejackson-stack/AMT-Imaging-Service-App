GSCB Power Module Replacement

3  Procedure

3.1   GSCB Power Module Removal

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button on desktop display and select [Shutdown].

    1. If Applications are down, open a Terminal Window. Type: halt , then press <Enter>.

    (Step Details)

  1. When halt command has finished, power Off the console at the front panel switch.

  1. Apply LOTO for console. For procedures, see Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE.

  1. Remove the console rear chassis cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the GSCB Power Module in the upper left corner of the GOC6.6 console looking from the rear of the console.

    Illustration 2:  GSCB Power Module Location - Console Rear View

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the four (4) USB cables from the Upper Bulkhead panel, these cables will interfere with the power module removal. Make certain the cables are clearly labelled.

    Illustration 3:  USB Cables - Upper Bulkhead panel

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the AC and DC Power cables from the GSCB Power Module.

    Illustration 4:  GSCB Power Module Connections

    ItemDescriptionDestination
    1GSCB DC Power OutGSCB - Black DB-9 (Male) Power Cable
    2GCSB AC Power InConsole Power Distribution Box - J15

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew the Phillips captive thumb screw to unfasten the power module.

    Illustration 5:  GSCB Power Module Fastening Removal

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the GSCB Power Module out the back side of the console and set aside.

    Illustration 6:  GSCB Power Module Removal

    (Step Details)

3.2   GSCB Power Module Install

  1. Slide the replacement GSCB Power Module into console and fasten the captive Phillips screw.

  1. Reconnect all cables removed earlier, including the four (4) USB cables removed from the Upper Bulkhead panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove LOTO on console. For procedures, see Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Functional Checks, Alignments and Setups

  1. Power On console.

  1. Confirm proper operation of the GSCB. Reference GSCB and ICOM Functional Checks procedure.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Install console rear cover. Refer to Replacement → Console (GOC6.6) → Console Cover Removal and Installation Procedure.

  1. Perform the Functional Checks → System Scanning Test instructions from the procedure list.

  1. Perform the Functional Checks → Quality Assurance Test instructions from the procedure list.
