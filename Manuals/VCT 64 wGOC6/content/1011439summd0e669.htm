NOTE:The PMT DVD-RAM Drive Status indicator light will be lit GREEN during the saving process. The save process is not complete until the PMT DVD-RAM Drive Status indicator light is no longer lit. Failure to take this into account could cause a system crash and result in the data not being saved properly.

NOTE:Be sure to close other application processes before saving scanfiles. Failure to do so could cause a system crash and result in the data not being saved properly.

NOTE:The saving process time can take from a minute for an Axial scanfile up to 2 hours for a Cardiac Helical Scan.
