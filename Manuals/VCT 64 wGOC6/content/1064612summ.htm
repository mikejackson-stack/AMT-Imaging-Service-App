Laser Light Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
POSSIBLE EYE INJURY.
LASER BEAMS CAN CAUSE PERMANENT EYE DAMAGE.
WHEN OPERATING THE ALIGNMENT LIGHTS, DO NOT STARE AT THE LASER BEAMS.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and front cover.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen laser clamp with a 5 mm hex wrench holding the black laser in place as shown in Illustration 1. If additional clearance is needed, loosen 2 mounting screws (NOT the alignment screws). Remove mounting bracket if necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the new laser into the mounting bracket and tighten the clamp screw using a 5 mm hex wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque the Mounting and alignment screws (if touched) to the following:

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Alignment Light Adjustment to check and adjust the new laser.

  1. Install the gantry top covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.
