3  Procedure

3.1   Removing The TGPU

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover.

NOTICE
Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Perform all required LOTO activities.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove gantry left side cover. If further access is required, remove top and rear covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 8 thumbscrews in the TGPU cover, then remove the cover.

  1. Disconnect all cabling into the TGPU.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cabling assembly plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the eight (8) screws attaching the TGPU board.

  1. Remove the defective TGPU.

3.2   Install TGPU

  1. Put the new TGPU board in place.

  1. Secure the board by replacing the eight (8) screws (Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the cable assembly plate.

  1. Carefully install all of the removed cables, making sure that no pins are bent during engagement.

  1. Remove LOTO, and power up everything except the gantry.

  1. Before powering ON the 120 VAC gantry power, open a shell (terminal) window on the console and type the following

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel and press the E-stop reset.

  1. Execute Flash download query and update. Refer to the [Flash download] procedure if necessary.

  1. Perform the power supply adjustment procedure in the Finalization section of the TGPU Power Supply Replacement.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC service switches.

  1. Replace the TGPU cover.

  1. Replace gantry covers except for the right side cover that allows access to Service Switch Panel.

  1. Turn on the 120 VAC, HVDC and Axial Drive service switches.

  1. Replace gantry right side access cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform the System Scanning Test from the Functional Checks folder.
