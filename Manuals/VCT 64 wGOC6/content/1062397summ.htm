High Voltage Tank Replacement

3  Procedure

3.1   High Voltage Tank Removal

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry such that EMC Box cover, between Inverter and High Voltage Tank, can be easily removed. (See Item 1 in Illustration 1)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove EMC Box cover between Inverter and High Voltage Tank with a 3mm Allen Wrench ( See Item 1 in Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank lower mounting bolts are accessible for removal (See Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two High Voltage Tank lower M12 mounting bolts with 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank is in 2:30 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Cut Ty-wraps on Inverter and Auxiliary Box clamps and remove High Voltage cable from clamps.

  1. Loosen High Voltage cable locking ring with spanner wrench.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable candlestick and place a cover over candlestick.

  1. With Dry Wipes, soak up the oil in the High Voltage Tank receptacle.

  1. Place a cap or paper towel in top of receptacle to prevent residual oil spillage and dirt from contaminating receptacle.

  1. Remove Inverter EMC Box cover bracket with a 3mm Allen Wrench (See Item 1 in Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove EMC Box (white nylon) top cover on High Voltage Tank, with a flat blade screw driver, to expose High Voltage cable terminals (See Item 2 in Illustration 3).

  1. Detach High Voltage Inverter/Tank connections from EMC Box terminals with a 13mm hex socket drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect J12 connector and remove cable clamp from High Voltage Tank with a 3mm Allen Wrench (See Item 1 in Illustration 4)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect J4 connector and remove cable clamp from High Voltage Tank with a 3mm Allen Wrench (See Item 2 in Illustration 4)

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert lifting post into gantry frame and attach boom and hoist.

  1. Connect hoist hook to High Voltage Tank lifting point.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two High Voltage Tank upper M12 mounting bolts with 10mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using hoist, carefully lift and swing High Voltage Tank free of gantry and lower to floor towards gantry right side.

  1. Detach hoist hook from High Voltage Tank lifting point.

3.2   High Voltage Tank Installation

  1. Place new High Voltage Tank crate near gantry front-right side and open top of crate.

  1. Attach hoist hook to new High Voltage Tank lifting point.

  1. Using hoist, carefully lift new High Voltage Tank out of crate, swing to front of gantry and position over gantry two guide pins.

  1. Fasten High Voltage Tank to gantry by inserting four M12 mounting bolts and washers with a 10mm hex bit socket torque wrench and 12 inch extension (See Illustration 2)

  1. Tighten each M12 mounting bolt to the following preload torque value.

    (Step Details)

  1. Detach hoist hook from High Voltage Tank lifting point.

  1. Attach hoist hook to old High Voltage Tank lifting point.

  1. Lift old High Voltage Tank into crate, detach hoist hook from lifting point, and prepare for storage/shipping.

  1. Remove hoist/boom/post assembly from gantry.

  1. Tighten each M12 mounting nut to the following pre-torque value.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route and connect J12 connector to High Voltage Tank (See Item 1 in Illustration 4)

  1. Apply Loctite 242 to 3mm screw and attach cable clamp to High Voltage Tank with a 3mm Allen Wrench. Torque to same values as step 22 below.

  1. Route and connect J4 connector to High Voltage Tank. (See Item 2 in Illustration 4)

  1. Attach cable clamp to High Voltage Tank with a 3mm Allen Wrench.

  1. Route and attach High Voltage Inverter/Tank cable to High Voltage Tank EMC Box terminals.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten mounting nuts with a 13mm hex bit socket torque wrench, to the following torque values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install EMC Box (white nylon) top cover on High Voltage Tank and tighten each captive screw with a flat blade bit torque driver to 0.45 N-m or 4.0 lbf-in.

  1. Install Inverter EMC Box cover bracket and fasten with four M4 washers and screws from Section 4.1.

  1. Tighten each M4 screw with a 3mm hex bit socket torque wrench, to the following torque values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install EMC Box cover between Inverter and High Voltage Tank and fasten with four M4 washers and screws from Section 4.1.

  1. Tighten each M4 screw with a 3mm hex bit socket torque wrench, to the following torque values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Securing HV Cable.

  1. Restore power to system. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.
