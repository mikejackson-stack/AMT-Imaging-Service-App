Rear Bulkhead Panel Connector Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Access to both the front and rear of the Operator Console is required. Move the console away from walls and remove obstacles in room.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from the rear of the console.

3.2   Removing Defective Connector on Rear Bulkhead Panel

  1. Disconnect cables connected to the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Uninstall Rear Bulkhead Panel by removing the four (4) 4mm socket cap mounting screws holding the panel to the console chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the Rear Bulkhead Panel from the console chassis.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Fiber Optic Connector on Rear Bulkhead Panel

  1. Disconnect Dual Fiber from the back side of the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Fiber Optic connector from the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by compressing the spring retention clips on the sides of the Fiber Optic connector, and push the connector free from the panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Fiber Optic connector on the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by pressing the new connector into the panel from the back side until the spring retention clips on the side of the Fiber Optic connector snaps into place.

  1. Reconnect the internal Fiber Optic cable to the new connector.

3.4   Ethernet Connector on Rear Bulkhead Panel

  1. Disconnect the Ethernet cable from the back side of the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Ethernet connector from the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by releasing the spring clip holding the connector in the panel, and push the connector free from the panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Ethernet connector on the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by pressing the new connector into the panel from the backside until the spring retention clip on the side of the Ethernet connector snaps into place.

  1. Reconnect the internal Ethernet cable to the new connector.

3.5   Gantry (DB-25) Connector on Rear Bulkhead Panel

  1. Disconnect TGP (DB-25) cable from the backside of the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

  1. Remove the Gantry (DB-25) connector from the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by removing the two (2) hex standoffs and nuts holding the DB-25 connector to the panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new DB-25 connector on the Rear Bulkhead Panel, by holding the new connector on the panel from the back side and install the Hex Head Standoff and nuts removed earlier.

  1. Reconnect the internal Gantry (DB-25) cable to the new connector.

3.6   Reinstall Rear Bulkhead Panel

  1. Install the Rear Bulkhead Panel on the console chassis using the four (4) 4mm socket cap mounting screws removed earlier. Torque to 2.9 N-m.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the removed external cables to the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

    (Step Details)

3.7   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Apply power to the console using the operator console power switch.

3.8   Verify Replacement Connector

  1. Test the replacement Rear Bulkhead Panel connector by verifying that communications and signal paths have been restored. Verify that the system boots properly and that gantry and table are reset.

  1. No other verification necessary.
