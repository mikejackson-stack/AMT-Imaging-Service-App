Backplane Replacement Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Gantry Preparation

  1. Move table to home position, fully out and down.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Stop the rotor of X-ray tube in case of Liquid Bearing Tube before HVDC off. Refer to Liquid Bearing Tube Rotor stop procedure for details.

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive and HVDC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Position the detector at 12 o'clock and lock gantry rotation.

  1. Turn OFF the 120 VAC switch on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and front and rear covers.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Detector Removal

  1. Cover the Tube Collimator port to protect it against dropped tools or screws. (Cloth or any other available item)

  1. Remove the Air Plenum as shown in Detector Air Plenum Removal/Installation.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the VCT Detector Replacement Procedure remove the digital detector from the gantry using sections 4.4 and 4.5.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Backplane Removal

  1. Remove all cabling from the backplane to be replaced. Both backplane procedures are basically the same. The low channel backplane will be used for examples in this procedure as it has the addition of the fiber optic connections to the DCB.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the DIFB cover from the chassis behind the backplane to be replaced. Disengage the DIFBs from the backplane connectors (including the DCB if removing the low channel backplane). See VCT DCB and DIFB Replacement for details.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the backplane cover shield. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. After removing the backplane cover shield, remove the EMC connector shields from all Sub-D connectors and set them with the backplane cable gasket. See Illustration 3 for shield example.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 9 screws around the edge of the backplane.

  1. Lift off the backplane and place it in a static bag.

3.4   Backplane Installation

  1. Position new backplane on the DAS plate.

  1. Install 9 screws and torque to the value shown in Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the EMC shields onto the Sub-D connectors so the fingers are angled away from the backplane as shown in Illustration 3.

  1. Position the backplane cover shield and torque screws to the value shown in Table 2. Reference Illustration 2 for the backplane shield.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the supplied backplane barcode sticker on the backplane shield over top of the old sticker to identify the new backplane on the system.

  1. Engage the DIFB’s (and DCB if low channel backplane) into the backplane.

  1. Install the DIFB chassis cover.

  1. Install the 4 captive DIFB cover screws and torque to the value shown in Table 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the 2 M6 DIFB cover screws and torque to the value shown in Table 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the DAS fiber optic cables and Sub-D connectors as applicable. (Inspect pins of sub-D cables before installing.). Using proper torque will avoid breaking cable jackscrews. Torque Sub-D connectors to the value shown in Table 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the DAS backplane cable gasket on the connector as shown in Illustration 1 and carefully insert the connector into the backplane. When properly lined up, the connector should easily slide onto the backplane pins.

  1. Torque the backplane connector to the value shown in Table 6.

    (Step Details)

3.5   Detector Installation

  1. Install the Detector as shown in VCT Detector Replacement Procedure using section 4.6.

  1. Install the Air Plenum as shown in Detector Air Plenum Removal/Installation.

  1. Finish installing the DAS/Detector harnessing as shown in VCT Detector Replacement Procedure section 4.8.

  1. Make sure the DCB and DIFB boards are installed into the backplane connectors and that no board is sticking out from the others when looking across all the boards from the side.

3.6   Gantry Reassembly and Testing

  1. Make sure the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel are OFF.

  1. Install the gantry front cover, rear cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. From the DASTools interface run the following rotating tests.

    1. Select and Run [mA Ratio Test] (creates/updates the bad channel map)

    1. Select and Run the [Auto Test] from DASTools.

  1. If all the checks pass continue with the next section. If anything fails, troubleshoot per the appropriately failed test.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

3.7   System Calibration

  1. The detector requires up to 45 minutes to reach operating temperature prior to starting the calibration process. Failure to wait will cause artifacts later when the detector reaches operating temperature. The time to warm up starts from the time the gantry power was turned on. Check the Common Service Desktop to make sure the detector temperature is at 38 degrees C +/- 1.5 prior to starting calibrations. The operator message area will post a message when the detector is back to normal temperature.

  1. Perform Collimator calibration using [Scanner Utilities] - [Collimator Cal].

  1. Perform Full Spectral calibration using [Scanner Utilities] - [Detailed Cal].

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform FastCal using [Daily Prep] - [Fastcal]. (Required after every detailed calibration)

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a [Quality Assurance Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.

  1. Perform a [Save State] to save new calibration data.
