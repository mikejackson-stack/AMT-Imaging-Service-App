3  Procedure

3.1   Gantry Setup for replacement

  1. Drive the table to its full back position and position it full up if not already there.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off 120 VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC Service Switches.

  1. Remove the Gantry left side, top, rear and front covers. The front cover can stay connected to the gantry cables, just needs to be moved away from the gantry toward the table. Connect the rear cover E-stop cable to the terminator on the gantry.

  1. Set up Field ESD strap connecting it to a gantry ground point. Wear the strap when replacing any of the gantry balance circuit boards in the following sections.

3.2   Part replacement

Perform the section below for the part you are replacing. This covers 3 parts, Sensor buffer board, and each of the two balance sensors.

3.2.3  Side balance sensor (between gantry leg and tilting assembly)

  1. The gantry side sensor is located on the side of the gantry leg as shown below. The heater filter is removed for clearer view.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Gantry heater assembly using the Gantry Heater Replacement procedure and remove the filter as well. This is necessary to gain access for tightening the screws that restrain the new sensor board.

  1. Tilt the gantry forward to about 20 degrees as shown on the gantry display for easy access to the side sensor board through the heater port using the following steps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the access through the heater/filter port, disconnect the molex connector for the sensor board.

  1. Remove the 2 M4 screws holding the cover on the gantry leg.

  1. Remove the 4 M4 Screws holding the sensor board to the gantry leg (3 mm hex wrench).

  1. Replace board and torque the 4 M4 screws to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace cover making sure the wires are in the cutout and not pinched by the cover. Torque M4 screws to the settings in the step above.

  1. Reconnect the molex connector to the sensor.

  1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC service switch and press the table drives enable at the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Return the gantry to a zero tilt position using switch S10. Then turn Tilt Enable S9 to Off and put the Service Mode switch in the down position.

  1. Turn the 120 VAC service switch off.

  1. Reinstall the gantry heater assembly using the Gantry Heater Replacement procedure. Do not power on the gantry.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Gantry Service balance from the Common Service Desktop Calibrations Tab. This procedure will have you install the gantry balance trial weights to create a new sensitivity matrix. Reference the Gantry Balance Procedure if needed.
