GOC6.6 VCT Host Computer (Z820) DVD Drive Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE Z820, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION.

 

NOTICE
IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   Z820 Computer Removal

  1. Shutdown system. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply LOTO. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE procedure.

  1. Remove Keyboard Tabletop, Front, Rear and Top covers from NIO64 console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Z820 computer from the NIO64 console chassis.

    (Step Details)

3.2   DVD Drive Replacement

  1. Open the Z820 computer side access panel.

  1. Remove the Expansion Card Support and the Airflow Guide.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cables for DVD drive.

  1. Pull the drive release latch, and pull out DVD drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the existing DVD drive with new one.

  1. Reinstall all removed components, and reconnect any cables that have been disconnected.

3.3   Restore the Console

  1. Fully slide the Z820 computer back into the console chassis, until mounting brackets are flush against the console chassis.

  1. Remove and store the Service Platform.

  1. Install and torque the four (4) 3mm socket cap screws that mount the Z820 computer in the GOC6.6 console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect all cables removed earlier to the Z820 computer.

  1. Remove LOTO on console.

4  Finalization

  1. Install console covers and Keyboard Tabletop assembly.

    (Step Details)
