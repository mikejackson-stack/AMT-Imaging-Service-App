Stationary Base Cover Removal and Reinstall

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Illustration 1 shows the gantry base covers. There are two side covers, two rear covers and a single front cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Start with the rear covers (Shown as items 6 and 7). Using an 8 mm hex wrench twist the 3 twist locks counter-clockwise to release the locks and pull each cover out from the back of the gantry.

  1. Remove the side covers (shown as items 4 and 5) if needed by removing the one screw on the side (3 mm hex wrench) and 2 screws the interface panel (2.5 mm hex wrench) on the back side of the gantry, then slide the side cover away from the gantry to the side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the base front cover (shown as item 3) if necessary by removing two screws on each end holding the cover mount bracket to the gantry base rail to release the cover from the brackets. Lift the cover up and over the foot switch cover and away from the gantry.

  1. To reinstall the gantry base covers, reverse the above steps, putting on the covers in the order of front, sides then rear covers.
