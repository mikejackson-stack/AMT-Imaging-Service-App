HP xw8200 DVD Optical Drive Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable has been disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in “On” or “Standby” mode.
You must disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the workstation.

CAUTION
Optical Drive damage could result during removal or installation.
Ensure that the optical drive tray is closed and drive is secure when moving the workstation.
Failure to do so can damage the drive.

NOTICE
Turn off the workstation before disconnecting any cables.
 

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HP COMPUTER, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Power cord must be disconnected from HP Computer and the computer’s PSU drained of all power. Press and hold HP computer’s power switch on the computer’s front panel for approximately 45 seconds after disconnecting the power cord.

3  Procedure

3.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Remove/disengage any security devices that prohibit opening the workstation.

  1. Disconnect all peripheral device cables from the workstation.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Optical Drive Removal

  1. Remove access panel cover per HP xw8200 Lower Level FRU Replacement.

  1. Lift up on the three tabs located on the front bezel (Item 1 in Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the front bezel away from the chassis and remove the bezel (Item 2 in Illustration 1).

  1. Disconnect the power, drive, and audio cables from the drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the green drive lock release lever and gently slide the drive out of the chassis (Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

3.3   Optical DVD Installation

  1. Lift the green drive lock release lever while sliding the optical drive into the bay.

  1. When the optical drive is partially inserted, release the drivelock release lever and slide the drive completely into the bay until the drive is secured.

  1. Connect the power, drive, and audio (if required) cables to the drive and workstation.

    (Step Details)

  1. Return to respective Lower Level FRU Replacement procedure.

    (Step Details)
