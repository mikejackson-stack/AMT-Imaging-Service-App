Anchor the System

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
POTENTIAL FOR PATIENT INJURY.
IMPROPERLY SECURED TABLE MAY TIP, DISLODGING PATIENT.
PROPER ANCHORING IS KEY TO MAINTAINING PATIENT SAFETY DURING SYSTEM OPERATION.

1  Procedure

1.1   Installing the Anchors

  1. Recommended: Use “Hilti Kwik-Bolt II” anchors P/N 2106573 (½ in. dia. x 8 in. long) as shipped with the VCT/CT750 HD system for this procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Assemble the anchors before you install them. (refer to Illustration 1).

    1. Remove the nut and washer from the anchor.

    1. Add a ¼ in. thick washer (PN 2105873) under the regular anchor washer.

    1. Reassemble the anchor washer and nut and position nut so the top is flush with the threads of the anchor.

  1. Use the anchor seating tool to hammer anchors into the holes.

  1. Adjust all eight (8) anchor bolts until tight.

  1. No more than 21 mm (1 in.) should show above the nut.

  1. Do not cut off the anchor.

  1. There should be at least one full round of thread showing above the lock ring.

  1. Complete this section on GE Form e4879.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Alignment Recheck

NOTE:Alignment is critical. Recheck carefully.

  1. Turn on the alignment tool and recheck alignments. The table alignment must be the same as in the Cradle/Table Parallel Check. If re-leveling is required, repeat this procedure. Using the bubble levels, make adjustments to maintain the required alignment.

  1. Measure distance from the table center white line perpendicular to the floor. This distance must be 1005mm± 2mm. Ensure the table is still level.

  1. Once alignment is verified, torque all mounting bolts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mark the laser locations at 1000mm.

  1. Remove the laser tools.

  1. Reinstall the table rails.

    (Step Details)
