Do not service or physically troubleshoot left side of gantry while system is energized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

3  Procedure

3.1   Launching Tool

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING TOOL]

    (Step Details)

  1. Select Sub-system tab. (See Illustration 1)

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [EXIT] to close tool.

3.2   Tool Functionality

  1. When tool is launched, hardware/system conditions are queried in a continuous mode. Display is updated every second and is in Continuously Update mode.

  1. Click [STOP] releases Continuously Update mode.

  1. Click [UPDATE ONCE] causes values to be updated once.

  1. Display Color Definitions

    1. Green: Data in normal expected range.

    1. Yellow: Data exceed upper or lower warning limits.

    1. Red: Data exceed upper or lower specification limits.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [HELP] to be routed to systems service manual.
