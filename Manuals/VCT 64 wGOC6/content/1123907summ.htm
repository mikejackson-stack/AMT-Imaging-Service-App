HSDA RAID Controller Firmware Upgrade

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

NOTICE
FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Access to front of the Operator Console is required. Remove obstacles in room.

-

-

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

In order to support the combination of both Hard Drive FRUs (5308760 / 5308760-2) in the same HSDA, the HSDA RAID Controller must be running with firmware revision “371E”.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   HSDA RAID Controller Firmware Check

  1. Shutdown CT Applications.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open a terminal window on the system.

    (Step Details)

  1. In Terminal Window, start a secure shell session on the DIG or DIG2 computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter “lsscsi” command in shell session.

    (Step Details)

  1. The HSDA’s RAID Controller Firmware Revision will be display. See following Illustration.

    (Step Details)

  1. Close the Terminal Window.

    (Step Details)

3.2   HSDA’s Firmware Upgrade (Flashing) Procedure

  1. Remove GOC6 or 6.5 Console’s front cover.

  1. Install GOC6 & 6.5 HSDA Firmware Update Application Disk (5413299) in Host PC’s DVD Drive.

  1. Shutdown CT Applications (if not already shutdown).

  1. Open a terminal window on the system.

    (Step Details)

  1. Log on a root.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount the GOC6 & 6.5 HSDA Firmware Update Application Disk.

    (Step Details)

  1. Change directory to mounted CD.

    (Step Details)

  1. Execute the Firmware Update script.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check success of firmware flash process.

    (Step Details)

  1. Unmount the GOC6 & 6.5 HSDA Firmware Update Application Disk and removed CD from Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Close Terminal Window.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Configuration “reconfig” to recreate Disk Array and Scan Database.

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front Cover.
