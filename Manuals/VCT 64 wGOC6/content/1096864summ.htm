2  Procedure

2.1   Gantry Preparation

Locate and install any required floor protection now.

2.1.1  Access Greater than 28 in.

  1. Remove all the transportation packaging from the gantry, except for the dollies.

2.1.2  Access Less than 28 in.

Measure from the wall or object protruding from the wall to the gantry side cover. The gantry left side cover must be installed for this measurement. When finished, the gantry cannot be closer than 14 in. to the wall or object protruding from the wall.

  1. Remove all the transportation packaging from the gantry, except for the dollies.

  1. Remove the blue dolly from the left side of the gantry and install the limited access dolly so that the gantry can be positioned closer to the left side wall.

    1. Remove the three (3) M14 hex bolts that secure the gantry to the dolly.

    1. Replace the removed dolly with the shipped black gantry-positioning dolly, and reinstall the three (3) M14 hex bolts.

    1. Raise the gantry so that it is once again off of the floor.

      (Step Details)

2.2   Gantry Installation

  1. Position the gantry over the floor cutouts appropriately.

    1. Locate the four (4) leveling pads, and position each beneath its associated adjuster. A ½ in., ratchet and 1-½ in. socket are required to loosen the gantry shipping bolts.

    1. Use the dollies to evenly lower the gantry, until it is just off of the floor (approximately ¾ in. or 20 mm). Use a ½ in. ratchet to raise and lower the dollies.

    1. Carefully rotate the gantry into the correct position over cutouts in the floor.

    1. Use floor protection as appropriate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the locking rings with the spanner tool. Turn gantry levelers by hand until each leveler is down 1/2 in.(13 mm) from the bottom of the gantry frame.

  1. Leave the locking rings loose so you can fine-tune the leveling pads to compensate for slight variations in the floor surface.

  1. Position the gantry so that the adjusters are centered over their respective holes marked earlier into the floor cutouts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a ½ in. ratchet, gently lower the gantry until it rests on the floor over the marked areas. When finished, the gantry should now be resting on all four leveling pads.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
