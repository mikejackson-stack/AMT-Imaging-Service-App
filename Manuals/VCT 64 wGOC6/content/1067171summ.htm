Tube Oil Cooling System Air Detection Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For Performix Pro VCT 100 and Performix HD X-ray tubes.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

No X-Ray exposures or gantry rotations have been made for AT LEAST 30 minutes.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Phantom Setup

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Attach phantom holder to gantry end of cradle.

  1. Mount phantom to phantom holder.

  1. Place bubble level on top of phantom, positioned along Z-axis. Adjust phantom holder back knob until phantom is level front to back.

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

  1. Move table into gantry until axial internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom's axial reference mark.

  1. Adjust phantom holder side knob until sagittal internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom’s sagittal reference mark.

  1. Elevate table until coronal internal alignment lights are aligned with the phantom’s coronal reference mark.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

3.2   Acquire Images

  1. Click [NEW PATIENT].

    (Step Details)

  1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: Air Detection Test

  1. Select Service tab from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [MANUFACTURING], [GRDR 20CMQA VCT 64].

  1. Modify the prescription by completing the following steps:

    NOTE:All image groups are modified with the same values.

    1. Click [SCAN TYPE].

    1. Select [0.4] for Rotation Time and click [OK].

    1. Click [THICK SPEED].

    1. Select [40.0] for Detector Coverage (mm), [2.5 16i] for Axial Thickness (mm) & Number of Images Per Rotation and click [OK].

    1. Click [NO. OF IMAGES].

    1. Enter 16 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [START LOC].

    1. Enter S42.5 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [END LOC.].

    1. Enter S80 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [SFOV].

    1. Select [LARGE BODY].

    1. Click [MA].

    1. Enter 250 for Manual mA and click [OK].

    1. Click [PREP GROUP (SEC)].

    1. Enter 15 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [DFOV].

    1. Enter 25 and press [ENTER].

    1. Click [RECON OPTION].

    1. Enter 70 for Windows Width, 15 for Windows Level and click [OK].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [MOVE TO SCAN] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Click, [END EXAM].

  1. If a PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

3.3   Image Review

  1. Select ImageWorks desktop.

  1. Select scanned examination from List/Select browser.

  1. Select [VIEWER] and review all images for blotchy artifacts. (See Illustration 1, Illustration 2, and Illustration 3). If an artifact is encountered, perform Tube Oil Cooling System Air Removal.

    (Step Details)
