Slip Ring Transmitter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
Potential for shock.
Voltage may be present. Potential for injury if covers removed and power is left ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

WARNING
Potential for shock.
Voltage may be present. Potential for injury if covers removed and power is left ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

  1. Remove gantry left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) main power switches (HVDC, 120VAC, and Axial Drive) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Shut down the console and perform the Lockout Tagout steps to lock out power to the gantry.

  1. Remove the left side cover and top covers, then scan window and gantry rear cover.

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand to gain access to the slip ring transmitter module. The transmitter is on the back side of the slip ring behind the detector assembly.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Be careful to not contaminate the slip ring or signal tracks.
 

  1. Disconnect the power input, signal output and fiber optic cables. Reference Illustration 2 showing ring assembly off gantry for identification of connections and mounting screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 2.5 mm allen wrench or bit, remove the four screws holding the transmitter to the slip ring and remove the transmitter.

  1. Remove the 2 screws holding the Coupler (see Illustration 2) and slide the coupler away from the connections.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new transmitter using the 4 mount screws. Turn the screws until they are fully seated onto the transmitter but not tight, then turn 1/4 turn more to apply sufficient torque

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new coupler by sliding it along the back surface of the slip ring onto the connections and mount using the 2 mount screws using the same process as the previous step for the transmitter.

  1. Install the power, signal and fiber optic connections to the transmitter. Reference Illustration 2 for connection locations.

  1. If you are replacing both transmitter and receiver through the use of the XFP to SFP+ upgrade kit, use the Slipring Receiver Replacement instructions before powering on the gantry. When done with both replacements use the Receiver replacement procedure to finish and perform final checks.

  1. Remove the A1 Lockout/Tagout. Power up the console.

  1. Install the gantry rear, top and left side covers. Install scan window.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure proper data transmission.

  1. Check the RTS Dip stats for the scan just run and make sure there are no errors shown.
