Table 4:  Controller Status Indicators

LEDNameColorStatus
1Ctrl StatusGreen / AmberGreen Indicates that the controller is active and operating normally.
Amber Indicates the controller is being initialized or has failed. The controller is not ready.
2C_DirtyAmberON Indicates that data is currently cached in memory or is supported by the BBU during a power loss.
3Temp.AmberON Indicates that one of the preset temperature thresholds is violated.
4 *BBU LinkGreenON Indicates that the BBU is present.
5Hst BusyGreenFLASHING Indicates there is active traffic through the Host Port. (SAS Controller in DIG)

6Drv BusyGreenFLASHING Indicates there is active traffic on the drive channels.
