High Voltage Tank Feedback Resistor Verification

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Potential for electric shock when voltages are present. Use proper protection equipment to avoid shock.

CAUTION
No personnel or patients should be in the area of the gantry while scanning without adequate radiation safety precautions being utilized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For VCT type X-ray Generation Systems.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by GE Service trained personnel.

-

-

X-ray tube should be cold.

-

-

Digital Multimeter VDC Mode must have 10 Mohm input impedance.

-

-

Digital Multimeter must have a mA scale capability of 199.9 mA

-

-

Digital Multimeter must have 3-1/2 digits display capability

-

-

Digital Multimeter must be capable of displaying at least 2 decimal places.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Gantry Preparation

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 3 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock. See Equipment Service - Lockout-Tagout-PPE from Safety section.

  1. Insert lifting post into gantry frame and attach boom and hoist.

  1. Suspend High Voltage Bleeder from chain.

  1. Cut Ty-wraps on Inverter and Auxiliary Box clamps and remove High Voltage cable from clamps.

  1. Loosen High Voltage cable locking ring with spanner wrench.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable terminal stick from High Voltage Tank receptacle.

  1. With Dry Wipes, soak up oil in High Voltage Tank receptacle and in each High Voltage Bleeder receptacles.

  1. Open syringe package from piston side (avoid any contact with other tip).

    (Step Details)

  1. Fill up syringe with 18 ml oil from oil can.

  1. Hold the syringe at an angle to the receptacle as shown in Illustration 2 and pump 18 ml oil on to the receptacle wall.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat steps 14 and 15 for each High Voltage Bleeder receptacles.

  1. Discard the syringe properly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install High Voltage Bleeder between X-ray Tube and High Voltage Tank. (See Illustration 3 or Illustration 4)

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

3.2   Generator Internal kV Bleeder Test

3.2.1  Digital Multimeter Method - Recommended

  1. Attach Banana-to-BNC Connectors to both ends of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Plug one RG-58 Banana-to-BNC connector into High Voltage Bleeder's X3/A and X3/B plugs.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug other RG-58 Banana plug connector into Digital Multimeter Volts and Common receptacles.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Turn on and configure Digital Multimeter.

    1. Set to [VDC].

    1. Press [RANGE] button until display depicts 00.00.

    1. Set Digital Multimeter in a place where it can be easily viewed from console.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSITCS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [KV & MA (X-RAY)].

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. In Gantry Params pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [RUN].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes and observe last stable volts reading just prior to turn off on Digital Multimeter.

  1. Obtain Measured kV value by ( -10kV/V ) * ( Digital Multimeter value).

  1. Record “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” from kV and mA X-ray tool.

  1. Verify that the following conditions are met:

    1. Measured kV is within 3% of kV Selection.

    1. “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” is within 2% of kV Selection.

    1. Measured kV is within 2% of “Cathode/Total kV Average Value”.

      (Step Details)

  1. Record results in appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 100.

  1. Repeat Steps 10-15.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 120.

  1. Repeat Steps 10-15.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 140.

  1. Repeat Steps 10-15.

3.2.2  Oscilloscope Method - Alternate

  1. Plug oscilloscope into one of the gantry's service outlets.

  1. Disconnect all probes from oscilloscope.

  1. Turn on oscilloscope and warm up for a minimum of 20 minutes.

  1. Perform oscilloscope Signal Path Compensation.

    NOTE:The menu selections are based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on other oscilloscopes.

    1. Press [UTILITY].

    1. Select CAL from [SYSTEM].

    1. Press [SIGNAL PATH PASS].

    1. Press [OK COMPENSATE SIGNAL PATHS].

      (Step Details)

    1. Press [MENU OFF].

  1. Connect 10X probe to oscilloscope Channel 1 plug.

  1. Perform oscilloscope Probe Compensation.

    NOTE:Connectors and menu selection are based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on other oscilloscopes.

    1. Attach probe tip and reference lead to [PROBE COMP] connectors.

    1. Press [AUTOSET].

    1. Check shape of displayed waveform for a flat top square wave.

      (Step Details)

  1. Attach Banana-to-BNC Connector to one end of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Plug Banana plugs into High Voltage Bleeder's X3/A and X3/B plugs.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach Banana-to-Clips Connector to other end of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Signal to red clip and oscilloscope 10X Probe Common to black clip.

  1. Turn on [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Configure oscilloscope.

    NOTE:The menu selections are based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on other oscilloscopes.

    1. Set Horizontal[SCALE] to 200ms.

    1. Press [CH 1].

    1. Set Vertical[SCALE] to 2 V / division.

    1. Set Vertical[POSITION] to 2 minor ticks (0.4 of a division) from top of screen.

    1. Press Trigger[MENU], [SOURCE], [CH. 1].

    1. Press Trigger[MENU], [COUPLING], [DC].

    1. Press Trigger[MENU], [SLOPE], [NEG].

    1. Set Trigger[MENU], [LEVEL] to -3.00V.

    1. Press Trigger[MENU], [MODE & HOLD], [NORMAL].

    1. Set Horizontal[DELAY] to OFF state.

    NOTE:This sets Horizontal Trigger to 10%.

    1. Press [MEASURE], [GATING], [BETWEEN V BAR CURSORS].

    1. Press [MEASURE], [SELECT MEASUREMENT FOR CH. 1], [MEAN].

      (Step Details)

    1. Press [MENU OFF].

      (Step Details)

  1. Perform oscilloscope Offset Value.

    NOTE:This is oscilloscope-offset value that will be subtracted from true measurement values in later steps.

    NOTE:Do NOT change the vertical scale on oscilloscope after measuring offset value.

    NOTE:The menu selection is based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on other oscilloscopes.

    1. Press Trigger[FORCE TRIG].

    1. Record oscilloscope Channel 1 mean value in volts.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [KV & MA (X-RAY)].

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. In Gantry Params pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [RUN].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Record “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” from kV and mA X-ray tool.

  1. On oscilloscope, use [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place left cursor to just inside of waveform front edge.

  1. Press [SELECT] and use [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place right cursor to just inside of waveform back edge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record Channel 1 mean value in volts, including minus sign if there is one.

  1. Obtain Measured kV value by (-10kV/V) * (Channel 1 Mean value - Oscilloscope Offset value).

  1. Verify that following conditions are met:

    1. Measured kV is within 3% of kV Selection.

    1. “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” is within 2% of kV Selection.

    1. Measured kV is within 2% of “Cathode/Total kV Average Value”.

      (Step Details)

  1. Record results in appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 100.

  1. Repeat steps 18-26.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 120.

  1. Repeat steps 18-26.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set kV Selection to 140.

  1. Repeat steps 18-26.

3.3   Generator Internal Scan Timer

  1. If Section 4.2.1 was performed, complete the following steps:

    1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

    1. Plug oscilloscope into one of the gantry's service outlets.

    1. Turn on oscilloscope and connect 10X probe to oscilloscope Channel 1.

    1. Perform oscilloscope Probe Compensation per Section 4.2.2 Step 6.

    1. Detach Banana-to-BNC Connector (one connected to Digital Multimeter) from RG-58 Cable.

    1. Attach Clips-to-BNC Connector to open end of RG-58 Cable.

    1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Signal to red clip and oscilloscope 10X Probe Common to black clip.

    1. Turn on [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

    1. Configure oscilloscope per Section 4.2.2 Step 12.

  1. In kV and mA Test (X-ray Functional Test) pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. In Gantry Params pane, set following parameter values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [RUN].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. On oscilloscope, adjust Select - Coarse knob to place cursor to just right of waveform left edge.

  1. Press [SELECT] and adjust Select - Coarse knob to place cursor to just left of waveform right edge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record Measured Scan Time value in seconds as the time between the two cursors.

  1. Verify that Measured Scan Time is within 4% of Exp Time (sec).

  1. Record results in appropriate GE e4879 Installation form, GE e4879 Certified Component Verification form, or GE Data Report Planned Maintenance Schedule form.

  1. Click [DISMISS].

4  Finalization

  1. Turn off [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Disconnect oscilloscope from High Voltage Bleeder.

  1. Turn off and unplug oscilloscope. Prepare for storage.

  1. Turn off [120 VAC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Disconnect the High Voltage Bleeder from system and prepare bleeder components for storage.

  1. Perform Securing HV Cable.

  1. Install gantry front cover, gantry top covers, and gantry left side cover.

  1. Install scan window.

  1. Turn on [120 VAC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install gantry right side cover.

  1. Store data form with system history records.
