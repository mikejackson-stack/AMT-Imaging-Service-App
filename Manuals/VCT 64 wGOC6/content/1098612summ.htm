3  Procedure

3.1   Environmental Coupon Installation

3.1.1  Gantry Preparation

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial Drive and 120VAC from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Lock gantry in position with Rotational Lock.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover.

  1. Locate the “Stationary 48V Power Supply” on the left side of the Gantry. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

3.1.2  Unpacking the Coupon

NOTE:The Coupon is enclosed in a protective plastic shipping material. Within this shipping material is a plastic bag containing the Coupon.

Illustration 2:  Coupon in Shipping Material

Illustration 3:  Shipping Material Removed

  1. Without damaging the shipping material, remove the plastic bag from the shipping container. Store this material in the Service cabinet, as it will be used to return the Coupon upon its removal at a later date.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
COUPON CONTAMINATION
DO NOT TOUCH THE METAL STRIPS
DEBRIS, DIRT, OR FINGER PRINTS WILL ADVERSELY AFFECT THE COUPON’S ACCURACY

CAUTION
NITRILE GLOVES MUST BE WORN WHEN HANDLING THE COUPON

  1. Remove the Coupon from the plastic bag.

  1. While still wearing gloves, record the requested data on the Coupon.

    (Step Details)

3.1.3  Coupon Installation

  1. Attach the Velcro strip contained in this kit to the upper edge of the power supply.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Coupon such that the metal strips are at the lower edge of the power supply, then attach the Coupon to the Velcro strip.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the tie wrap contained in this kit, attach the lower right corner of the “Coupon” to the power supply cable. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. The installation is complete.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove gloves and proceed to the Finalization section.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Environmental Coupon Removal

3.2.1  Gantry Preparation

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and disable Axial Drive and 120VAC from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Lock gantry in position with Rotational Lock.

  1. Remove the Gantry left side cover.

  1. Locate the Coupon attached to the power supply. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
COUPON CONTAMINATION
DO NOT TOUCH THE METAL STRIPS
DEBRIS, DIRT, OR FINGER PRINTS WILL ADVERSELY AFFECT THE COUPON’S ACCURACY

CAUTION
NITRILE GLOVES MUST BE WORN WHEN HANDLING THE COUPON

  1. Cut the tie wrap securing the Coupon and remove the Coupon from the Velcro strip. The Velcro need not be removed from the power supply.

  1. While still wearing gloves, record the date the Coupon was removed from the scanner in the “Date Out” field of coupon.

    (Step Details)

3.2.2  Return of Coupon

  1. While still wearing gloves, place the Coupon into the original plastic bag. Then place the plastic bag into the original shipping container.

  1. Remove gloves and address the package as indicated below and send the package to the Vendor using the Postal services in your area.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the Finalization section.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Install the Gantry left side cover.

  1. Remove Rotational Lock from gantry.

  1. Restore power to the Gantry and Axial Drive by turning on the 120VAC and Axial Drive Service switches.

  1. Install the Gantry right side cover.

  1. Press Table Drive reset on the Gantry controls.

  1. Perform an axial scan and confirm the scan is successful.
