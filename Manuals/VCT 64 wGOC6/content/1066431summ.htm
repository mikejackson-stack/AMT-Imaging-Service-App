IMS Belt Tension Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

1  Procedure

  1. Turn the Tension meter on.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Move the cradle and IMS to out limit position.

  1. Remove power from the table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable connector of the tape switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the pump upper cover as follows:

    1. To make re-installation easier, mark the position of pump upper cover on both sides by outlining the edge with a maker or pencil.

    1. Disconnect the cable connectors of the tape switches and touch sensors.

    1. Remove six (6) screws holding the both sides of the pump upper cover, and remove it.

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove IMS motor cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the clean damper is mounted on the IMS Motor Shaft, it will need to be removed to access the IMS Drive Belt. Remove the clean damper by loosening the two setscrews on the backside of the clean damper. Note the orientation of the setscrews on the shaft for replacement.

    (Step Details)

  1. Hold the tension meter sensor half way on the middle of the upper side of belt, and lightly strum the top of the IMS belt in the middle of the belt span to make it vibrate. Measure the frequency and procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Compare measurement value to the IMS belt frequency spec of (Between 100 and 140 HZ).

  1. If frequency is out of spec, proceed to the following sub steps:

    1. Loosen 2 screws on IMS motor bracket. Tighten belt by using flat head screwdriver to push motor bracket assembly. Tighten 2 screws securely while keeping tension on the belt.

      (Step Details)

    1. Continue to adjust belt and measure frequency until it is within spec.

  1. If you removed the clean damper earlier in the procedure, please re-install it on the IMS Motor Shaft. Please refer back to step Step 9 for proper installation.

  1. IMS belt adjustments are complete at this time. IMS covers can be re-installed at this point.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the table from the service Switch Panel in gantry.

  1. Move the cradle and IMS in and out completely 10 cycles, and verify that table movement is operating normally.

  1. Remove power from the table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Re-install the Table covers.
