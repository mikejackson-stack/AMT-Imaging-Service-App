STEP 8: Level the Gantry

Labor:2 people; 0.5 man-hour
Tools:Standard Install Tool Kit
Gantry Adjuster Tool, P/N 2107863
Spanner Wrench, P/N 2110003
PPE
NOTE:For best results, use the gantry dollies to raise the gantry while making rough adjustments to the levelers. Make fine-tuned adjustments with no load on the dollies.

1. Loosen all adjuster lock rings.

 

2. Level the left side front-to-back, making height adjustments to the levelers with the gantry load on the dollies.

3. Level the right side front-to-back making height adjustments to the levelers with the gantry load on the dollies.

4. Level the gantry side-to-side making height adjustments on the side that is higher. Turn each leveler on that side equally.

5. With no load on the dollies systematically make fine-tuned adjustments to the levelers until both bubble levels are centered left-to-right and front-to-back.

6. All four leveling pads should be carrying a portion of the gantry weight, and the bubble should be completely inside the inner circle on both bubble levels.
