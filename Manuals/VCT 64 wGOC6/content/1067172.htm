4  Procedure

4.1  Phantom Setup

  1. Move table to longitudinal home position.

  1. Attach phantom holder to gantry end of cradle.

  1. Mount phantom to phantom holder.

  1. Place bubble level on top of phantom, positioned along Z-axis. Adjust phantom holder back knob until phantom is level front to back.

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

  1. Move table into gantry until axial internal alignment lights are aligned with phantom's axial reference mark.

  1. Click [SCANNER UTILITIES].

  1. Click [CENTER PHANTOM].

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Adjust phantom position if results are out of specification range.

    NOTE:Phantom center specification is ± 0.5mm.

  1. Repeat steps [9] - [11] until phantom is centered.

  1. Click [DONE] in Phantom Centering window.

  1. Click [QUIT] in Scanner Utilities window.

  1. Set Internal Landmark.

4.2  Acquire Scans

  1. Click [NEW PATIENT].

  1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

    1. Patient ID: Service

    1. Patient Name: Streak Test

  1. Select Service tab from Protocol Selection, Anatomical Selector.

  1. Select [IMAGE QUALITY], [44.5 STREAK TEST].

    NOTE:Make sure the prescription is a single group axial protocol.

  1. Click [CONFIRM].

  1. Click [CONTINUE] in Attention window.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Click [END EXAM].

  1. If PPS Response window is displayed, click [COMPLETE].

4.3  Image Review

  1. Select ImageWorks desktop.

  1. Select scanned examination from List/Select browser.

  1. Click [VIEWER] and review all images for streak artifacts.

  1. Perform Streak Analysis on images with streak artifacts.

4.4  Streak Analysis

  1. Select [SERVICE] desktop and move Service desktop to left monitor.

  1. Select [IMAGE QUALITY] from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [IMAGE ANALYSIS] and move Image Analysis window to left monitor.

  1. Select image from right monitor List/Select browser.

  1. Click [MANUAL] radio button on Image Analysis window.

  1. Click [MANUAL] selection button, [STREAKS].

  1. Click [ACCEPT]. The image is displayed in the Image Analysis window with a Streak ROI.

  1. Follow instructions in Image Analysis window lower right pane to size Streak ROI and analyze.

  1. From Report window, if Measured Streak Factor is out of specification range +/-4.0, perform Image Quality Troubleshooting in accordance with prescribed procedures.

  1. Click [DISMISS] in Report window.

  1. Click [QUIT] in Image Analysis window.
