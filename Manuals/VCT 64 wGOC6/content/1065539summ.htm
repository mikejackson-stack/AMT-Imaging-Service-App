PET Gantry Service Position

1  Safety

NOTICE
Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

System shutdown and power removed.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Moving PET Gantry to Service Position

  1. Remove covers as needed. If needed, refer to covers remove/install procedure.

  1. Loosen the PET Gantry Interlock bolt (2) below the micro switch, then pull the Spring Latch (3) on the side of the PET Gantry Interlock so that the block (1) drops down. Repeat this for the interlock on the other side of the gantry. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Verify that no cables or covers are caught when the PET Gantry is being moved.
 

  1. Use an electric drill (2) and flexible drive shaft (1) to separate the PET Gantry from the CT Gantry. Begin drilling slowly to verify the drill is turning the correct way and no cables interfere with the movement of the PET Gantry. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Stop drilling when the entire PET Gantry is in contact with the rubber stops.

3.2   Moving PET Gantry Back to Scan Position

  1. Raise Service Latch up in the Trailer to disengage the position lock.

  1. Use the electric drill to move the PET Gantry back towards the CT gantry.

  1. Stop when the PET Gantry hits the mechanical stops at the CT Gantry.

  1. Pull the Spring Latch out, lift up the PET Gantry Interlock, and tighten the bolt at the end with a hex wrench securing the PET Gantry into place.
