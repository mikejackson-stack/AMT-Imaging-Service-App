2  Overview

This document provides the necessary steps to replace and configure the Smart Cathode Onboard Rotation Processor Unit (SCORP) for imaging.

4  Procedure

4.1  SCORP Module Removal

  1. Move table to home longitudinal position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove scan window

  1. Remove gantry left side, top, and front covers.

  1. Rotate gantry by hand to gain access to SCORP module, located between gantry 107 balance weights and DAS Detector assembly.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Remove all cables and connections to SCORP module (See Illustration 1).

    NOTE:Remove cable restraining clap screw with 3mm hex bit driver.

    Illustration 1:  SCORP Cabling

  1. Remove three detector thermocouple wires

  1. With a 5mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension, remove three M6 SCORP mounting screws from front of gantry (See Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  SCORP Mounting Screws

  1. Remove SCORP module from gantry.

  1. With a 5mm hex bit socket drive, remove four M6 countersink screws that connect mounting bracket to SCORP module.

  1. Remove mounting bracket from SCORP module.

4.2  SCORP Module Installation

  1. With a 5mm hex bit socket drive, install mounting bracket to new SCORP module with four M6 countersink screws.

    NOTE:Apply Loctite 242 to each screw before inserting screw.

  1. Torque each M6 countersink screw to the following specification.

    5.8 ft.-lbs 7.9 N-m 70 in-lbs80.8 kg-cm

  1. Install new SCORP module on to gantry.

  1. With a 5mm hex bit socket drive and 12 inch extension, fasten new SCORP module to gantry with three M6 mounting screws.

  1. Torque each M6 mounting screw to the following specification

    5.8 ft.-lbs 7.9 N-m 70 in-lbs80.8 kg-cm

  1. Reconnect three detector thermocouple wires.

  1. Reconnect all wire connections to SCORP module.

    NOTE:Make sure all cables are secured in their clamps and properly Ty-wrap.

    NOTE:Fasten cable restraining clap screw with 3mm hex bit driver.

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Turn ON [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch Panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

4.3  Setup, Checks, Alignments, and Calibrations

  1. Gantry Rotation Safety Check

  1. Gantry Balance Procedure

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Install gantry left side, top, and front covers.

  1. Install scan window.

  1. Turn ON [HVDC ENABLE], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch Panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install right side gantry cover.

  1. Flash Download
