Hydraulic High Pressure Hose Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
CRASH HAZARD !
THE TABLE MUST BE SUPPORTED USING THE SUPPORT TOOL AS DESCRIBED BELOW WHEN CHANGING THE HIGH PRESSURE HOSE.

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table components and covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the Table Support Tool as follows:

    1. Perform this step if applicable:

      (Step Details)

    WARNING
    crash hazard!
    The Table is very heavy and can move if The Global PET/CT Table transporter is not secured!
    The transporter must be secured from moving before attaching the table support tool.

    1. Perform this step if applicable:

      (Step Details)

    1. The adjuster of the support tool A should be partially extended, about 3 cm, before supporting the Table.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach the support tool A to the left top frame, and turn the set screw to CCW to fix the support tool A.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach the other support tool A to the right top frame, and turn the set (hexagon) screw to CCW to fix the support tool A.

    1. Attach the support tool B to the support tool A using 4 screws.

    1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

    1. Extend the adjusters by turning them so that the adjusters touch the floor, and lower the Table onto the support. Make sure that there is no oil on the floor and the support tool is installed perpendicular to the floor.

      (Step Details)

NOTICE
Confirm the following before removing power from Table:

  • The floor under the support tool is not slippery

  • The support tool is installed perpendicular to floor.

  • The support tool is firmly fixed to the table top frame by set screws.

  • The support tool is securely holding the full weight of the Table.

 

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the oil hoses.

  1. Put the oil pan on the Table base.

  1. Disconnect the high-pressure hose from the hydraulic pump. A oil may spill out from the high-pressure hose. Drain oil in the high-pressure hose.

  1. Disconnect the high-pressure hose from the hydraulic cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the high-pressure hose, one end to the cylinder, and the other end to the pump, then tie-wrap the hose in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position, and verify that the new high-pressure hose is securely connected, and a oil is not leaked out from the joint.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Remove the Table Support Tool from the Table.

  1. Table pump lubrication:

    1. Mark the position of pump upper cover on both side for re-installation.

    1. Disconnect the cable connectors of the tape switches and touch sensors.

    1. Remove six (6) screws holding the both sides of the pump upper cover, and remove the pump upper cover.

      (Step Details)

    1. Pry a lid up using a flat-head screw driver.

      (Step Details)

    1. Pour approximately 100cc of the oil into the pump.

    1. Power up the Table from the Gantry Service Switch.

    1. Raise the Table as high as possible.

    1. Turn off the Table from the Gantry Service Switch.

    1. Repeat step Step 16.d through Step 16.h until Table height reaches mechanical Up-limit.

    1. Check the level of the oil by using a stick such as tie-wrap, specification is shown in illustration below.

      (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Secure any loose hoses with tie-wraps.

  1. Raise and lower the Table repeatedly to verify that the hoses do not catch or pull excessively.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-install the Table covers.
