4  Procedure

4.1  Tube Rotor Stop Procedure

  1. Start “Rotor Control Tool” under “Diagnostic” tab from CSD menu.

  1. Check “Stop” radio button and press “Accept” button. Then press “confirm” to rotate Gantry.

    Illustration 1:  Window - Rotor Control Tool

  1. Confirm that the Tool Status shows “Succeeded” and press “Dismiss” button to close the tool.

    Illustration 2:  Window - Tool Status
