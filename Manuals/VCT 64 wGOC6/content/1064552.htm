4  Procedure

4.1  Disassembly

  1. If the battery is still working, save the JEDI runtime parameters by clicking the [SAVE RUNTIME PARAMETERS] button of the [GENERATOR TOOL] found in the [DIAGNOSTICS] tab of the Common Service Desktop.

WARNING
pOTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
Voltage may be present
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper Lockout / Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all the three switches (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120 VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Position the inverter assembly at 2 o'clock.

NOTICE
Do not drop screws into inverter. If you do, they must be removed or damage to the inverter will occur.
 

  1. Remove largest inverter cover.

  1. Use an electrostatic Discharge (ESD) protection bracelet and follow all ESD procedures.

  1. Gently grasp the NV Ram battery on the PPC board (see Illustration 1) between your thumb and forefinger. Pull it directly away from its support on the board.

    Illustration 1:  NV RAM Battery on PPC Board

    NOTE:This NV RAM contains a lithium carbon monoflouride (LiCFx) battery, used to provide the battery backup for the static ram and the time device. Return the former component to your local GEMS representative for recycling.

4.2  Reassembly

  1. Install the new battery on the PPC board in the same location from which it was removed. Ensure that it is properly installed.

  1. Replace inverter cover.

  1. Turn ON all the three switches (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120 VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Replace gantry covers.

5  Finalization

  1. Reapply the power.

  1. If the battery was working before the replacement, restore the JEDI runtime parameters that were saved prior to removing the PPC board battery. (This can be done by clicking the [RESTORE RUNTIME PARAMETERS] button on the main[GENERATOR TOOL] screen.) Otherwise, initialize the JEDI database and run Filament Calibration.

  1. Start some exposures in different modes.

  1. Verify that the system works correctly.
