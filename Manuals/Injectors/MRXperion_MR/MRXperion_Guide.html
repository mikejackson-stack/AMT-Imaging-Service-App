Error Messages & Troubleshooting

ErrorCauseAction
HEAD COMM FAULTCommunication between head and Certegra lostCheck fiber optic cable at both ends. Clean connectors with IPA swab. Power off head and Certegra. Power on Certegra first, then head.
SYRINGE ERRORSyringe not detected or incorrect typeRemove syringe, wipe sensor window and syringe bayonet with dry cloth. Reseat until audible click. Try sealed new syringe. Inspect sensor LED (should illuminate).
PRESSURE FAULTInjection pressure exceeded limitAssess IV site for infiltration. Reduce flow rate. Verify catheter gauge. Check tubing for kink.
AIR IN LINEAir detected in fluid pathPrime system completely. Remove patient tubing, hold end up, advance plunger slowly until air-free.
MOTOR FAULTDrive motor errorPower cycle system. Run self-test from Certegra service menu. Inspect drive mechanism for obstruction.
LOW FLUIDSyringe nearly emptyReplace syringe before next injection. Prime before use.
TEMP ALARMSystem temperature out of specCheck ambient temp (15-30°C). Verify warmer function if installed.
