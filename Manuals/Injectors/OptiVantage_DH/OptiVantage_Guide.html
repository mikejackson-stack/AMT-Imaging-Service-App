Troubleshooting Tips

Tip: The OptiVantage DH has a "Purge" function on the console that is useful for priming lines and clearing air bubbles. Always purge both sides before connecting to patient.
Tip: If "Syringe Not Detected" persists after cleaning sensors and trying a new syringe, inspect the latch mechanism on the syringe cradle. A worn or bent latch can prevent the syringe from seating fully against the sensor.
Warning: The OptiVantage DH is a CT-only injector. It is NOT MR-conditional. Never bring into the MRI scan room.
Tip: For recurring high-pressure aborts in the same patient population, consider evaluating your IV catheter gauge. Studies show that 22G catheters frequently cause pressure limit events at flow rates above 3 ml/sec.
