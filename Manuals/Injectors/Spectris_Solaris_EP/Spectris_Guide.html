Fiber Optic Installation Tips

RF Shield Routing: Fiber optic cables must pass through the RF shield via approved EMC waveguide filters or EMC penetration panels. Do NOT drill new holes without RF filter. Verify with RF shield integrity test after installation.
Minimum Bend Radius: Fiber optic cables must not be bent below their minimum bend radius (~30mm for standard fiber). Tight bends at the penetration panel are the #1 cause of intermittent comm errors (code 1806).
Battery Safety: Do not operate the head with a failed or uncharged battery. Battery voltage below spec can cause erratic motor behavior and mid-injection aborts.

Troubleshooting Tips & Tricks

Tip: Error 1806 (comm lost) — before blaming the fiber cable, first check the bulkhead connectors on the RF penetration panel. These connectors are often dirty or slightly loose and are cleaned/tightened in 5 minutes vs. replacing a fiber cable.
Tip: Error 1811 recurring — if IV pressure is not the cause, the most common fix is replacing the syringe sensor assembly in the head. This is a PM consumable item.
Tip: If the head battery won't hold a charge past 50%, replace the battery. A partially charged battery will cause random mid-injection aborts as the motor draws peak current.
Tip: When re-greasing the plastic gears (step 5i), use only MR-safe, non-ferrous lubricant. Standard petroleum-based greases may contain ferrous particles that attract to the magnet over time.
