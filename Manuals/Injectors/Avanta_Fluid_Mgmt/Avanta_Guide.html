Troubleshooting Guide

IssueLikely CauseAction
Fill cycle failsBottle spike connection not sealedReseat contrast bottle until spike fully engages. Check spike tip for damage.
Air in system after bottle changeAir admitted during changeoverRun extended prime cycle. Check all connections. Verify bottle fully pierced by spike.
Incorrect volume deliveredFlow calibration driftRun flow calibration from service menu. Verify pump output at 1 and 5 ml/sec.
Inventory software alarmBottle below minimum thresholdReplace contrast bottle. Update inventory in software.
Pump motor noiseWorn pump head bearingsInspect pump head. Replace if bearing noise audible during operation.
Backflow detectedFailed check valveTest each check valve individually. Replace failed valve. Do not operate with backflow.
Warmer not reaching temperatureHeater element or sensor faultVerify setpoint (37°C). Check heater element continuity. Inspect temperature sensor.

#TaskAcceptance

2Check valves — verify one-way function each valveNo backflow in either direction when tested
3Bottle spike and connection port inspectionNo cracking, wear, or deformation
4Pump head O-rings and seals inspectionNo compression set or extrusion; replace if degraded
5Flow calibration — A-head (contrast) and B-head (saline)Delivered rate within ±5% of set rate at 1 and 5 ml/sec
6Full fill cycle test — time cycle, verify no errorsCycle completes without fault; time within spec
7Auto-prime function — verify clears all airFluid exits tubing end air-free within 30 sec
8Pressure alarm testAlarm activates at set threshold; injection halts
9Air detection test with test air bubbleAir detector triggers; system halts injection
10Warming system calibration (if equipped)Setpoint 37°C; reaches temp within 30 min
11Inventory tracking accuracySoftware shows correct bottle level after known volume used
12Clean all surfaces and bottle rackNo contrast residue; rack secure
13Emergency stop testE-stop halts all pump and fill activity immediately
14Service report to HTM/BiomedicalSigned, dated, serial number, all readings documented
