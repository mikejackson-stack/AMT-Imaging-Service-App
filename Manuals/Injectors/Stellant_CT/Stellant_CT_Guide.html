Error Codes — User Recoverable

User Recoverable errors: follow on-screen instructions to clear. If error persists, contact Bayer service.
CodeDescriptionFirst Response
867Invalid syringe / syringe not recognizedRemove and reinstall syringe. Inspect syringe sensor optics and latch. Clean sensor window. Try a known-good syringe. Check flex ring connector.
2115Syringe sensor fault — syringe position not detectedVerify syringe fully seated. Inspect sensor assembly and optics board. Check syringe well for contrast contamination blocking optics.
2212Communication error between display and injector headCheck ISI cable connections at both ends. Verify cable seating at the head cable connector. Power cycle system. Inspect head cable for damage.
3016Pressure limit exceeded — injection abortedCheck IV line for kinks or clamps. Verify 20G+ needle. Reduce flow rate or pressure limit. Check for occlusion in line. Consider diluting contrast.
Invalid SyringeSyringe brand/size not accepted by protocolVerify syringe is the correct OEM type for the protocol configured. Check for expired disposables. Clear with correct syringe or update protocol.
Low Contrast / Low SalineInsufficient volume for programmed protocolFill or replace syringe. Reprogram protocol with available volume.
Pressure Limit ReachedInjection paused at set pressure limitAssess IV site. Check for occlusion. Reduce flow rate. Restart injection if IV patent.

Error Codes — Critical (Requires Power Cycle)

Critical errors require recording the code and rebooting. If error returns after reboot, do not operate — contact Bayer service.
CodeDescriptionAction
Head Cable FaultHead cable open/short circuit detectedInspect head cable and connectors. Check for damage or pinching. Swap head cable if available. Do not operate with faulty cable.
Motor FaultDrive motor over-current or encoder failureCheck for mechanical obstruction. Verify drive belt and motor coupling. If fault persists, motor or driver board replacement required.
Power Supply FaultDC rail voltage out of specCheck wall power quality. Inspect power supply voltages. Replace power supply if rails fail spec.
Flex Ring FailureSyringe flex ring connector faultInspect flex ring on injector head. Clean contacts. Replace flex ring assembly.
Board Comm FailureInternal PCB communication failurePower cycle. If persists, internal board fault — requires depot service or board swap.

Troubleshooting Tips & Tricks

Tip: Most "syringe not recognized" errors (code 867/2115) are caused by contrast residue on the optic sensors. Clean sensors with a dry lint-free cloth before replacing any boards.
Tip: If the injector head won't power on but the workstation is fine — first check the head cable connector at the head (it can vibrate loose on the pedestal mount).
Tip: Before calling Bayer service for error code 2212 (comm error), always swap the ISI cable end-to-end. These cables fail much more commonly than the boards themselves.
Warning: The Stellant is NOT MR-conditional. Never bring it into the MRI scan room. Use the Spectris Solaris EP for MRI-guided injections.
Warning: When replacing the flex ring, power the system completely off first. Connecting/disconnecting the flex ring under power can cause board damage.
