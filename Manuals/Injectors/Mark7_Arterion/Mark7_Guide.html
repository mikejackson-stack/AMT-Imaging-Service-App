System Overview

The Mark 7 Arterion is a single-head injector primarily used in angiography suites and CT for high-pressure, high-flow-rate contrast delivery. It is common in interventional radiology (IR) and cardiac cath labs.

ComponentDescription
Injector HeadSingle syringe with high-pressure piston drive; pivot-mounted on ceiling or floor stand.
Control ConsoleTouch-screen or button-panel programming unit at control room.
Patient ModuleRemote arm/inject handswitch or foot pedal.
High-Pressure LineRated for angiographic pressures up to 1200 psi.

Key Differences: Angio vs. CT Use

PM Tips (Annual / Semi-Annual)

TaskNotes
Inspect head cableCheck for insulation damage, secure connector at both ends
Inspect syringe drive and pistonNo excessive wear; piston moves smoothly through full travel
Lubricate drive mechanismPer OEM specification; do not over-grease
Test pressure limit accuracyVerify at multiple flow rates; calibrate if out of spec
Volume accuracy test±1 ml accuracy at multiple volumes
Flow rate accuracy testTime delivery at multiple set rates
Inspect mounting hardwareCeiling/floor mount hardware; check all bolts torque
Inspect handswitch / foot pedalArm/inject commands functional; cable intact
Electrical safetyPer IEC 60601 and facility requirements
Tip: For angiography use, always confirm the high-pressure extension tubing is rated for the maximum programmed pressure. Standard IV tubing is NOT safe at angiographic pressures.
Tip: The Mark 7 Arterion shares many diagnostic approaches with the Stellant — inspect the same components (head cable, syringe sensors, motor drive) using the same techniques.
Warning: This is a CT/Angio injector. NOT MR-conditional. Do not bring into the MRI scan room.
