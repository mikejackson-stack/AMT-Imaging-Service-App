2  Overview

The system rotating side 48V power supply does not require adjustment. The power supply can be checked for ripple and voltage level.

Last UpdatedJanuary 28, 2008

4  Procedure

  1. Ensure Axial drive is disabled via the service switch panel prior to checking the power supply.

    NOTE:The power supply on this system does not require any adjustment. The power supply levels and ripple can be checked to confirm basic operation. The power supply and fuse board is located (low channel end, opposite end from 107 weight stack) on the same end of the detector as the plenum fan controller.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT.
Do NOT measure the 48 volts at the power supply output terminals. The output is 48V at up to 90Amps and a slip of the meter lead could RESULT IN PERSONAL INJURY AND/OR COMPONENT DAMAGE.

  1. The test points are located on the Fuse board on the front end by the cable connections. Use this point to check the power supply level and ripple. If power supply does not meet these specifications a replacement will be required.
