Heater Diagnostic Test

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Timing Not Applicable5 mins Timing Not Applicable

2  Overview

The objective of this procedure is to drive the heater inverter on the tube filament in order to identify a faulty HV tank or tube.

Last RevisedJuly 15, 2008

3  Procedure

  1. If desired, you may check any or all of the following:

    1. PPC kV control board alive and running: S0-S7 LEDs are lit successively or a combination of them blink.

    1. SC Drive board is alive and running:

      1. DS3, DS4, DS7, and DS10 are activated (green).

      1. DS1, DS2, DS5, DS6, DS8, and DS9 LEDs are lit successfully.

      1. DS11 and DS12 are not activated (red).

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL] .

  1. Launch the [Heater Diagnostic] tool.

  1. Run the test. A scan sequence will proceed.

  1. If a problem occurs, check the error code.

4  Finalization

No finalization required.
