Tube Oil Cooling System Air Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR BURNS.
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR VIA BURNS FROM CONTACT WITH HOT OIL.
Do not service while tube and oil are hot (See required condition, below).

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

For Performix Pro VCT 100 X-ray Tube and Performix HD X-ray Tube

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

No X-Ray exposures have been made for AT LEAST one (1) hour. Tube casing and oil hoses are cool to the touch.

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Apparatus Preparation

  1. Remove reservoir cap and place funnel into tank. Place rags around funnel to capture any spilled oil. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Fill reservoir with approximately 1 US gallon of transformer oil ( Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

3.2   Apparatus Air Purge

Prior to starting air removal procedure, air must be removed from large blue hose.
 
 

  1. Connect apparatus female and male disconnects ( Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug in apparatus power supply ( Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON apparatus pump by actuating rocker switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. When all air is out of large blue hose (~ 30 seconds), shut OFF apparatus pump and uncouple apparatus quick-disconnects.

3.3   Preliminary Setup

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY
AN ENERGIZED GANTRY PRESENTS MECHANICAL (ROTATIONAL) AND ELECTRICAL HAZARDS.
exercise utmost caution.

  1. Move table away from gantry and remove any objects from Detector Field of view.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers, and gantry front cover.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch of Service Switch.

  1. Rotate gantry so that tube is between 1 o'clock and 2 o'clock positions.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch of Service Switch.

  1. Remove tube oil pump to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts at location A and uncouple quick-disconnect. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Remove tube heat exchanger to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts at location B and uncouple quick-disconnect. (See Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

3.4   Tube Air Removal

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY
GANTRY OIL PUMP SHOULD NOT BE ENERGIZED DURING AIR REMOVAL PROCESS.
exercise utmost caution

  1. Unplug J52 pump power connection so system pump does not run.

  1. Connect apparatus female disconnect to tube male disconnect at location A. (See Illustration 7.)

  1. Connect apparatus male disconnect to tube female disconnect at location B. (See Illustration 7.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

  1. Tilt gantry forward 30 degrees so that top of gantry is tilted towards table.

  1. Plug in power supply for apparatus.

  1. Ensure apparatus reservoir is at least 1/2 full.

  1. Turn ON apparatus pump by actuating rocker switch.

  1. Slowly rotate tube to 3 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

    (Step Details)

PERSONAL INJURY OR DAMAGE TO UNSECURED HOSES
DURING GANTRY ROTATION
BE CAREFUL OF PINCH POINTS

Keep pump running for duration of Steps 10 through 22. Pump is a high pressure / low flow type so it will take time to remove the air.

During all air removal steps, raising yellow return line allowing air in system to rise to highest point for easy removal. In general, try to keep it pointed upward as much as possible.

 
 

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise from 3 o'clock position to 6 o'clock position to allow air to rise to back of the tube. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise from 6 o'clock position to 9 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube counter-clockwise from 9 o'clock position to 6 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube counter-clockwise from 6 o'clock position to 3 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube counter-clockwise from 3 o'clock position to 12 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube counter-clockwise from 12 o'clock position to 9 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube counter-clockwise from 9 o'clock position to 6 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise from 6 o'clock position to 9 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise from 9 o'clock position to 12 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise from 12 o'clock position to 3 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes, raising return line periodically.

  1. Slowly rotate tube clockwise the tube from 3 o'clock position to 6 o'clock position. Hold for 1-2 minutes and raising the return line periodically.

  1. Repeat steps [10] – [21] a minimum of 2 more times until air is no longer being visibly removed.

  1. Turn OFF apparatus pump by actuating rocker switch.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Uncouple apparatus female disconnect from tube male disconnect at location A. (See Illustration 7.)

  1. Uncouple apparatus male disconnect from tube female disconnect at location B. (See Illustration 7.)

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

3.5   Pump and Heat Exchanger Air Removal

  1. Tilt gantry back to 0 degrees.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Cut Ty-Wraps holding pump hose in heat exchanger channel at location E. (See Illustration 8.)

    (Step Details)

BE CAREFUL NOT TO CUT THE HOSE.
 
 

  1. Connect apparatus male disconnect to tube oil pump female disconnect at location C. (See Illustration 9.)

  1. Connect apparatus female disconnect to heat exchanger male disconnect at location D. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] onService Switch panel. Wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

  1. Perform Steps [8] - [24] of Tube Air Removal.

  1. Uncouple apparatus male disconnect from tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Uncouple apparatus female disconnect from heat exchanger male disconnect.

  1. Remove apparatus from system and connect system components to each other.

  1. Plug J52 pump power connection.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch Panel for only 30 seconds, then turn OFF.

    (Step Details)

Make sure the [120 VAC] switch of the Service Switch Panel is turned OFF before unplugging the J52 pump power connector.
 
 

  1. Unplug the J52 pump power connection.

3.6   System Air Removal

  1. Uncouple quick-disconnect between tube oil pump and tube.

  1. Connect apparatus male disconnect to tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Connect apparatus female disconnect to tube male disconnect.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel. Wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

  1. Tilt gantry forward 30 degrees so that top of gantry is tilted towards the table.

  1. Perform Steps [8] - [24] of Tube Air Removal.

  1. Uncouple apparatus male disconnect from the tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Uncouple apparatus female disconnect from tube male disconnect.

  1. Connect tube oil pump female disconnect to tube male disconnect.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

  1. Tilt the gantry back to 0 degrees.

4  Finalization

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Plug J52 pump power connection.

  1. Heat Exchanger Compensation

  1. Tube Oil Cooling Flow Rate Measurement

  1. Restore tube oil pump to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism. Torque M6 bolts to the following values.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ty-Wrap hoses to tube heat exchanger.

  1. If another Service Procedure required procedure execution, perform the following steps.

    1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [HVDC ENABLE] switches.

    1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

    1. Return to associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform the following steps.

    1. Install gantry front, top, and left side covers.

    1. Install scan window.

    1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

    1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

    1. Install gantry right-side cover.

  1. Unplug air removal apparatus power supply.

  1. Clean air removal apparatus of any excess oil and prepare for air shipment/storage.
