Cam Drive Motor and Flex Coupling Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 
 

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry (located in the Safety tab of this publication).

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required. See sections on Gantry Cover Removal under Replacement > Gantry> Enclosure.

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) gantry service switches [Axial Drive] , [HVDC] , [120 VAC] ).

  1. Position the gantry with the X-ray tube at 6 o'clock.

  1. Remove the three (3) red plugs from the Collimator front surface (on side from which motor will be removed).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the motor, while using a flashlight aimed through the plug holes to locate the hex-head screw so it can be loosened.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 3/32 Hex key, loosen the flexible coupling hex-head screw on the cam side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the motor drive connector at the motor and cut the tie wrap.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) hex socket M4 screws holding the motor to the Motor Mount Plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the cam drive motor and flex coupling slightly and disconnect the grommet from the bottom of the assembly, then remove the assembly.

  1. Using a 3/32 hex key, loosen the hex-head screw and remove the flex coupling.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the flex coupling. Place a small drop of Loctite 242 on hex screw threads. Torque to 0.15 N-m (13 lb-in).

  1. Install the new motor and tighten the four hex socket screws to 0.3 N-m (26 lb-in).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Install the Auxiliary Box and Torque all fasteners, per Auxiliary Box Replacement procedure. Release Gantry Rotational Lock and proceed.

  1. Tighten the flexible motor coupling screw. Torque to 0.15 N-m (13 lb-in).

  1. Connect the motor drive connector at the motor and replace the tie wrap.

  1. Install the three (3) plugs.
