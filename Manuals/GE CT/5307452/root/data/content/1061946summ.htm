Gantry Rotation Safety Check

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CRUSH HAZARD
EQUIPMENT COULD EJECT FROM GANTRY.
Ensure that no personnel are in the same room/area as the gantry prior to and during thE scanS.

1  Procedure

  1. Make sure the Axial Drive Enable switch (S2) and HVDC swich (S3) on the service switch panel are off.

  1. Manually rotate the gantry through a full rotation listening for an contact between stationary and rotating parts

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on the HVDC and Axial Drive service switches.

  1. Start the Axial Functional diagnostic.

    1. Open the Common Service Desktop

    1. Select the [Diagnostics] Icon

    1. Select the [Data Acquisition] Folder and then [Axial Functional] menu item to start the diagnostic.

Do NOT be present in the scan room while the gantry is rotating. Listen from the scan room door.
 
 

  1. Select a rotation speed of 2 seconds and run the test. This step should be run for a minimum of 30 seconds.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat Step 5 for rotation speeds of 1.0 and 0.5 seconds.

  1. Wait 3 minutes after the gantry stops rotating before continuing to allow for axial drive cooling.

  1. Rotate the gantry at 0.35 seconds/revolution for 60 seconds (to give the gantry time to get up to speed).

    (Step Details)

  1. If no issues arise, gantry rotation capability is safe for use.
