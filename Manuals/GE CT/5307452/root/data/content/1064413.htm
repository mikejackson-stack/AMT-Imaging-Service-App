2  Overview

This document provides the necessary steps to verify the health of the Generator internal kV bleeder and the internal scan timer.

There are two methods for verifying the health of the Generator internal kV bleeder. The first, and recommended, method uses a digital multimeter as the readout instrument. The alternative uses an oscilloscope with a 10X probe.

4  Procedure

4.1  Gantry Preparation

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 3 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Assemble lifting post, boom, and chain hoist and insert into gantry post socket.

  1. Suspend High Voltage Bleeder from hoist/boom.

  1. Carefully cut Ty-wrap securing High Voltage cable between High Voltage Tank and X-ray Tube.

  1. Loosen High Voltage cable's locking ring with a spanner wrench.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable terminal stick out of High Voltage Tank receptacle.

  1. Install High Voltage Bleeder between X-ray Tube and High Voltage Tank. (See Illustration 1.)

    NOTE:Ensure that you attach a ground cable from the High Voltage Bleeder to the High Voltage Tank. Incorrect High Voltage Bleeder setup can result in damage to the High Voltage Subsystem.

    Verify that the key on the Bleeder Kit High Voltage cable is aligned with the High Voltage tank receptacle slot.

    Make sure each well of the High Voltage Bleeder (X1 and X2) and the well of the High Voltage Tank are filled with Transformer Oil.

    Illustration 1:  High Voltage Bleeder Connection Block Diagram

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is successfully reset.

4.2  Generator Internal kV Bleeder Test

4.2.1  Digital Multimeter Method - Recommended

  1. Attach Banana-to-BNC Connectors to both ends of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Plug one RG-58 Banana-to-BNC connector into High Voltage Bleeder's X3/A and X3/B plugs.

    NOTE:Ensure the polarity is correct (Banana plug Common Pin to High Voltage Bleeder x3/B).

  1. Plug other RG-58 Banana plug connector into Digital Multimeter Volts and Common receptacles.

    NOTE:Ensure the polarity is correct (Banana plug Common Pin to Digital Multimeter Common).

  1. Turn ON [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Turn ON and configure Digital Multimeter.

    1. Set to [VDC] .

    1. Press [RANGE] button until display depicts 00.00.

    1. Set Digital Multimeter in a place where it can be easily viewed from console.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSITCS] tab from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [KV & MA (X-RAY)] from [DIAGNOSITCS] tab.

  1. In [KV & MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set the following parameter values:

    ParameterValue
    X-Ray Test TypeManual
    kV Selection80
    mA50
    min ISD3
    # Exposures1
    Exp Time (sec)2.00
    Filter TypeAir
    AperatureClosed
    Focal SpotLarge

  1. In [GANTRY PARAMS] pane, set following parameter values:

    ParameterValue
    GantryDisabled

  1. Press [RUN] button and observe last stable volts reading just prior to turn off on Digital Multimeter.

  1. Obtain Measured kV value by ( -10kV/V ) * ( Step 10 value) and record on GEe4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Record [KV & MA X_RAY GEN] tool “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Verify that the following conditions are met:

    1. Measured kV value ( Step 11) is within 3% of [KV SELELCTION] value.

    1. Cathode/Total kV Average value ( Step 12) is within 3% of the [KV SELECTION] value.

    1. Measured kV value ( Step 11) is within 2% of the Cathode/Total kV Average value ( Step 12).

      NOTE:If any of these conditions are not met, replace High Voltage Tank and retest.

  1. Record all results on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (S-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 100.

  1. Repeat Steps 10- 14.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (S-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 120.

  1. Repeat Steps 10- 14.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (S-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 140.

  1. Repeat Steps 10- 14.

4.2.2  Oscilloscope Method - Alternate

  1. Plug oscilloscope into one of the gantry's service outlets.

  1. Disconnect all probes from oscilloscope.

  1. Turn ON oscilloscope and warm up for a minimum of 20 minutes.

  1. Perform oscilloscope Signal Path Compensation.

    NOTE:The menu selections are based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on similar oscilloscopes.

    1. Press [UTILITY] button.

    1. Press [SYSTEM] , [CAL] screen button.

    1. Press [SIGNAL PATH PASS] screen button.

    1. Press [OK COMPENSATE SIGNAL PATHS] screen button.

      NOTE:“Calibration in progress, please wait.” is displayed during compensation.

      NOTE:Compensation can take up to 10 minutes.

      NOTE:When completed, “Signal path compensation has successfully completed. Push MENU OFF to remove this message” is displayed.

    1. Press [MENU OFF] screen button.

  1. Connect 10X probe to oscilloscope Channel 1 plug.

  1. Perform oscilloscope Probe Compensation:

    1. Attach probe tip and reference lead to [PROBE COMP] connectors.

    1. Press [AUTOSET] button.

    1. Check shape of displayed waveform for a flat top square wave.

      NOTE:If waveform is not a flat top square wave, adjust probe Low Frequency Compensation and repeat (a) - (c).

  1. Attach Banana-to-BNC Connector to one end of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Plug Banana plugs into High Voltage Bleeder's X3/A and X3/B plugs.

    NOTE:Ensure the polarity is correct (Banana plug Common Pin to High Voltage Bleeder X3/B).

  1. Attach Banana-to-Clips Connector to other end of RG-58 Cable.

  1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Signal to red clip.

  1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Common to black clip.

  1. Turn ON [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Configure oscilloscope.

    NOTE:The menu selections are based on a TDS3034B oscilloscope and might be different on similar oscilloscopes.

    1. Set [HORIZONTAL] , [SCALE] to 400ms.

    1. Press [CH 1] .

    1. Set [CHANNEL 1] , [VERTICAL] , [SCALE] to 2 V / division.

    1. Set [CHANNEL 1] , [VERTICAL] , [POSITION] to 2 minor ticks (0.4 of a division) from top of screen.

    1. Press [TRIGGER] , [MENU] , [SOURCE] , [CH. 1] screen buttons.

    1. Press [TRIGGER] , [MENU] , [COUPLING] , [DC] screen buttons.

    1. Press [TRIGGER] , [MENU] , [SLOPE] , [NEG] screen buttons.

    1. Set [TRIGGER] , [MENU] , [LEVEL] screen button to -3.00V.

    1. Press [TRIGGER] , [MENU] , [MODE & HOLD] , [NORMAL] screen buttons.

    1. Set [HORIZONTAL] , [DELAY] to OFF state.

    NOTE:This sets Horizontal Trigger to 10%.

    1. Press [MEASURE] , [GATING] , [BETWEEN V BAR CURSORS] screen buttons.

    1. Press [MEASURE] , [SELECT MEASUREMENT FOR CH. 1] , [MEAN] screen buttons.

    1. Press [MENU OFF] .

      NOTE:Pulse mean is displayed on right hand side of screen.

  1. Perform oscilloscope Offset Value.

    NOTE:This is oscilloscope-offset value that will be subtracted from true measurement values in later steps.

    NOTE:Do NOT change the vertical scale on oscilloscope after measuring offset value.

    1. Press [TRIGGER] , [FORCE TRIG] .

    1. Record oscilloscope Channel 1 mean value in volts.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] tab from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [KV & MA (X-RAY)] from [DIAGNOSTICS] tab.

  1. In [KV & MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set the following parameter values:

    ParameterValue
    X-Ray Test TypeManual
    kV Selection80
    mA50
    min ISD3
    # Exposures1
    Exp Time (sec)1.00
    Filter TypeAir
    AperatureClosed
    Focal SpotLarge

  1. In [GANTRY PARAMS] pane, set following parameter values:

    ParameterValue
    GantryDisabled

  1. Press [RUN] button and complete scan.

  1. Record [KV & MA X-RAY GEN] tool “Cathode/Total kV Average Value” on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. On oscilloscope, use [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place left cursor to just inside of waveform front edge.

  1. Press [SELECT] button and use [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place right cursor to just inside of waveform back.

  1. Record Channel 1 mean value in volts, including minus sign if there is one.

  1. Obtain Measured kV value by (-10kV/V) * (Step 23 value - Step 14 value) and record on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Verify that following conditions are met:

    1. Measured kV value ( Step 24) is within 3% of [KV SELCTION] value.

    1. Cathode/Total kV Average value ( Step 20) is within 3% of the [KV SELECTION] value.

    1. Measured kV value ( Step 24) is within 2% of the Cathode/Total kV Average value. (See Step 20)

      NOTE:If any of these conditions are not met, replace High Voltage Tank and retest.

  1. Record all results on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 100.

  1. Repeat steps 19-26.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 120.

  1. Repeat steps 19-26.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set [KV SELECTION] to 140.

  1. Repeat steps 19-26.

4.3  Generator Internal Scan Timer

  1. If Section 4.2.1 was performed, complete the following steps:

    1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

    1. Plug oscilloscope into one of the gantry's service outlets.

    1. Turn ON oscilloscope and connect 10X probe to oscilloscope Channel 1.

    1. Perform oscilloscope Probe Compensation per Oscilloscope Method - Alternate, Step 6.

    1. Detach Banana-to-BNC Connector (one connected to Digital Multimeter) from RG-58 Cable.

    1. Attach Clips-to-BNC Connector to open end of RG-58 Cable.

    1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Signal to red clip.

    1. Connect oscilloscope 10X Probe Common to black clip.

    1. Turn ON [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

    1. Configure oscilloscope per Oscilloscope Method - Alternate, Step 13.

  1. In [KV AND MA TEST (X-RAY FUNCTIONAL TEST)] pane, set the following parameter values.

    ParameterValue
    X-Ray Test TypeManual
    kV Selection100
    mA40
    min ISD3
    # Exposures1
    Exp Time (sec)1.00
    Filter TypeClose
    ApertureClosed
    Focal SpotLarge

  1. In [GANTRY PARAMS] pane, set the following parameter values.

    ParameterValue
    GantryDisabled

  1. Press [RUN] button and complete scan.

  1. On the oscilloscope, use the [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place the left cursor to just inside of waveform front edge.

  1. Press [SELECT] button and use the [SELECT - COARSE] knob to place the right cursor to just inside of waveform back edge.

  1. Record the Measured Scan Time value (Dt) in seconds as the time between the two cursors.

  1. Verify that the Measured Scan Time value ( Step 7) is within 4% of the [EXP TIME (SEC)] value.

  1. Record the results on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Click [DISMISS] from [KV & MA X-RAY GEN] .

5  Finalization

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Disconnect oscilloscope from the High Voltage Bleeder.

  1. Turn off and unplug oscilloscope. Prepare for storage.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Disconnect the High Voltage Bleeder from system and prepare bleeder components for storage.

  1. Perform Securing HV Cable.

  1. Install gantry front, top, and left side covers.

  1. Install scan window.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] button on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install gantry right side cover.

  1. Store GE e4879 Component Replacement Form with system history records.
