2  Procedure

  1. On the console select the [DIAGNOSTICS] tab in the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Launch the [COLLIMATOR APERTURE TEST] . Refer to Illustration 1 for the layout of the tool interface.

  1. Select the desired combinations of focal spot size and slice collimation. The default is all ON or selected. If none of the buttons are selectable, then Collimator Calibration may not have been performed or the files are not available. Click Confirm.

  1. The [START SCAN] button on the SCIM will begin flashing. Press the [START SCAN] button to begin running the collimator aperture tests.

  1. The tool will let you know if it passes. An example of the test results is shown in Illustration 2. The various cam positions are recorded and can be used to evaluate the beam aperture during scanning.

  1. If the tests do not pass then perform Collimator Calibration. If the tests still do not pass then troubleshoot or replace the collimator.
