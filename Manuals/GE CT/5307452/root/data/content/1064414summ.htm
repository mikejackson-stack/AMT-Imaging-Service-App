Direction  5307452-8EN,  Rev#  4

Meter Verification

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

For CT750 HD Generator System

-

-

Required procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

Scanner should be fully operational.

-

-

X-Ray tube should be cold to avoid damage during power cycling.

-

-

Digital Multimeter VDC Mode must have 10Mohm input impedance.

-

-

Digital Multimeter must have a mA scale capability of 199.9mA.

-

-

Digital Multimeter must have 3-1/2 digits display capability.

-

-

Digital Multimeter must be capable of displaying at least 2 decimal places.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation

  1. Move table to home longitudinal position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 3:00 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

3.2   mA Meter Verification

  1. Insert Smart Cathode Drive to Multimeter Cable audio plug to High Voltage Tank audio receptacle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect Smart Cathode Drive to Multimeter Cable RED plug into Digital Multimeter mA receptacle

  1. Connect Smart Cathode Drive to Multimeter Cable BLACK plug into Digital Multimeter Common receptacle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on Digital Multimeter and select mA DC setting.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] tab from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] or [X-RAY GENERATION] folder then [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] .

  1. Select [METER VERIFICATION] from [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] window.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [ACCEPT] from [METER VERIFICATION] and walk to Digital Multimeter.

  1. Observe highest mA reading attained from Digital Multimeter, to one decimal place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter measured mA value in [MA LOW SCALE DVM] edit box and press [ENTER] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify [MA LOW SCALE DELTA] is within [MA LOW SCALE LIMIT] , and [MA HIGH SCALE DELTA] is within [MA HIGH SCALE LIMIT] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Record results on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove Smart Cathode Drive to Multimeter Cable audio plug from High Voltage Tank audio receptacle.

3.3   kV Meter Verification

  1. Insert Digital Multimeter RED lead banana plug to High Voltage Tank BLUE receptacle.

  1. Insert Digital Multimeter BLACK lead banana plug to High Voltage Tank BLACK receptacle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect Digital Multimeter Cable RED plug into Digital Multimeter VDC receptacle.

  1. Connect Digital Multimeter Cable BLACK plug into Digital Multimeter Common receptacle.

  1. Turn on Digital Multimeter and select V DC setting.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until can hardware is reset.

  1. Click [ACCEPT] from [METER VERIFICATION] and walk to Digital Multimeter.

  1. Observe highest volts reading attained on Digital Multimeter.

  1. Calculate measured kV value by multiplying Digital Multimeter value by -20.

  1. Enter measured kV value in [KV] edit box and press [ENTER] .

  1. Record results on GE e4879 Component Replacement Form.

  1. Click [CANCEL] from [METER VERIFICATION] .

  1. Click [DISMISS] from [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] .

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove Digital Multimeter RED lead banana plug from High Voltage Tank BLUE receptacle.

  1. Remove Digital Multimeter BLACK lead banana plug from High Voltage Tank BLACK receptacle.

4  Finalization

  1. Release rotational lock.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Switch Panel.

  1. Press [ESTOP RESET] on Service Switch panel and wait until scan hardware is reset.

  1. Install gantry right side cover.

  1. Store GE e4879 Component Replacement Form with system history records.
