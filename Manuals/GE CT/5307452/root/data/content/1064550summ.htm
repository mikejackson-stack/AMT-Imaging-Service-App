Heat Exchanger Pump Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

1  Procedure

1.1   Heat Exchange Pump Removal

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move the table to its home position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn OFF all three switches (HVDC ENABLE, AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, and 120 VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove scan window.

  1. Remove gantry left side, top, and front covers.

  1. Rotate the gantry so the tube is at the 2 o'clock position.

  1. Remove the SCORP by following the procedure for SCORP Replacement.

  1. Remove the 4 M6 screws ( Illustration 1) that mount the pump to the 107 weight stack bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect Heat Exchanger Pump 120 VAC connector J52. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power interface board cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect pump from heat exchanger at quick disconnect. Line the slot up with the pin and push. The pump should easily disengage (See Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect oil hose from X-Ray tube.

  1. Remove the two bolts from the safety mechanism and disconnect the oil hose.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the hose and temperature sensor wiring aside. Remove pump and hose from gantry.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Heat Exchanger Pump Installation

  1. Position the pump and connect pump to Heat Exchanger at quick disconnect. Make sure you align the pin and slot, then connect. See Illustration 4. Once connected, rotate the collar so the pin and slot are not aligned.

  1. Route the hose to the tube. It routes underneath the Heat Exchanger shroud, but between the shroud and the clear plastic power interface board cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the pump hose to the tube. Secure the quick disconnect using the safety locking mechanism. The torque spec. for the quick disconnect safety mechanisms is 9.9 N-m (7.3 lb-ft, 88 lb-in, 101 kg-cm), however, if the locking mechanism rotates in relation to the quick disconnect, tighten slowly until it does not do so.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Power Interface board cover.

  1. Connect pump 120VAC connector.

  1. Install the SCORP by completing the SCORP Replacement procedure.

  1. Install the 4 M6 Heat Exchanger pump mounting screws. If you just replaced the heat exchanger, it may require some force to realign the pump. Apply final torque to the four M6 screws:

    (Step Details)

  1. Install gantry right side cover
