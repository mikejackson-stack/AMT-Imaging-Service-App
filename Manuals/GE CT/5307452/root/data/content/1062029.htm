4  Procedure

4.1  Slip Ring Receiver Adjustment Procedure

This document describes the steps necessary to properly adjust the position of the Receiver antenna above the emitter traces on the rotating slip ring. Accurate placement of the antenna is important to achieve the optimal data transfer performance and to avoid contact between the ring and the stationary antenna. The optimal position of the antenna above the emitter traces on the ring is 1.41 mm height (.060 inches), and centered. Under normal circumstances, the position of the antenna should never need adjustment. Only adjust if there are indications of interference. Under no circumstances should the antenna be allowed to contact the rotating components.

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover.

  1. Disable gantry Axial drive, HVDC and 120 VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

  1. Perform appropriate LOTO procedures to lock out power to the gantry.

  1. Remove mylar scan window and gantry top and rear covers. Connect the E-stop cable removed from the back cover to the gantry terminator in preparation for later steps.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Remove the top slip ring cover to access the receiver.

  1. Pull out the gap tool from the gantry frame storage location.

    Illustration 1:  Gap Tool Location

  1. Loosen the gap adjustment screws for the receiver.

    Illustration 2:  Receiver

  1. The antenna should now be loose above the emitter. Insert the adjustment tool between the antenna and the emitter traces on the ring. The tool should fit snugly. The desired result is to space the antenna above the emitter, at a height of 1.41 mm (.060 inches) above the emitter.

    Illustration 3:  Gap Tool Use

  1. Press slightly on the top of the antenna as the adjustment screws are tightened.

  1. Carefully slide the adjustment tool from between the antenna and the emitter.

  1. Loosen the alignment screws and adjust the RF Receiver shoe until the gold pins line up with the center line of the RF antenna on the slip ring on each side of the receiver shoe.

    Illustration 4:  Receiver Shoe Alignment

  1. While holding the position of the antenna, tighten the axial alignment screws.

Potential for Ring Damage. Do not rotate the gantry with the adjustment tool installed. Damage to the delicate PCB traces will result. There is no method to repair ring boards in the field - a ring replacement would be necessary.
 
 

  1. Inspect the air-gap between the antenna and the ring by rotating the gantry in small increments of about 3 feet (1 meter). Look for clearances between the emitter and the antenna. While rotating the ring, check that the emitter trace is aligned with the antenna. During rotation, no parts of the antenna should contact the emitter surface.

    • The stationary and rotating components must never touch, even with the gantry tilted.

    • The run-out of the platter slip ring traces should not exceed 0.83 mm axially, and 0.81 mm radially.

    • Especially check clearances near the emitter solder and PCB connections.

  1. Install the top slip ring cover before restoring any power.

  1. Restore power to the system by removing LOTO.

  1. Enable gantry 120 VAC, HVDC and Axial Drive to prepare for testing slip ring transmission path.

4.2  Slip Ring DIP stats

  1. Verify proper operation by running verification scans. Verification procedure should consist of:

    1. Run 5 stationary and 5 rotational scans with x-ray. The technique is not important. It is important to exit the exam, because this triggers the “DIP Stats” update.

    1. From the Common Service Desktop, Diagnostics tab, select RTS viewer

    1. Choose the DIP selection and view each scans DIP stats. There should be no FEC Failures or Sync Errors.

  1. If there are transmission issues disable Axial Drive and HVDC from the service switch panel, then check the power to the transmitter and receiver and alignments.

    1. Transmitter power supply measurements

      • The Transmitter Power comes from the rotating 48V power supply by way of the SCORP. A 48V feed goes to the SCORP and then to the transmitter.

      • Measure the DC voltage on the wires at the SCORP connector leading to the Transmitter. The specification is +48 volts DC ± 5.0 volts.

    2. Receiver power supply measurements

      • Locate the Receiver Power supply next to the GFI breaker behind the Service Switch Panel.

      • Measure the DC voltage on the wires leading to the Receiver. The specification is +12 volts DC, ± 2.0 volts. Use indicator LEDs to ensure proper operation.

5  Finalization

  1. Turn off Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120 VAC service switches from the service switch panel.

  1. Replace the gantry covers (except right side cover). Re-install the mylar window.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Turn on 120 VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC service switches from the service switch panel. Enable table drives and install the gantry right side cover.

  1. Perform System scanning test from the Functional Checks section to verify scanner operation.
