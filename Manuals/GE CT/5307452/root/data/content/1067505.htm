Discovery CT750 HD Service Methods - Adv

Gantry Encoder Inspection

Last Revision22 October 2008

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand and inspect the rotational encoder wheel for signs of wear.

  2. If the rubber is worn or shows sign of excessive wear, replace it.

  3. On the first PM, use a permanent marker to add the gantry installation date on the encoder.

  4. Replace the encoder at the end of two years.

  5. Use a permanent marker to record the replacement date on the new encoder.
