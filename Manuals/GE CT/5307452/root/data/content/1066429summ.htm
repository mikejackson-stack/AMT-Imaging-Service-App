IMS Bearing Tension Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Disengage the bearing blocks from the IMS base by unscrewing 2 screws, refer to IMS Bearing Block Replacement.

  1. Move the IMS bearing away from the Gantry by rotating the IMS shaft.

    Illustration 1:  Bearing Block Disengagement

  1. Remove a screw holding the IMS driving cover (RR) to the Table.

    Illustration 2:  Screw Removal

  1. Set eye-bolts to Table frame and the bearing block.

    Illustration 3:  Support Screw Setting

  1. Set the spring balance to the eye-bolts.

    Illustration 4:  Spring Balance Attachment

  1. Make sure of the bearing block tension as follows:

    Hold the clean damper FIRMLY to avoid injury due to reverse rotation of the clean damper by a tension of the spring balance.

    1. Hold and rotate the clean_damper by hand.

    1. Keep the IMS shaft rotating in manner of above step to move the bearing block in the IN direction.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify that this value is 52 kg ± 2 kg.

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove two eye-bolts from the bearing block and the Table frame.

  1. Engage the bearing block to the IMS base, refer to IMS Bearing Block Replacement.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the service Switch Panel.

  1. Move the cradle and IMS in and out completely 10 cycles and verify that table movement is operating normally.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-install the Table covers.
