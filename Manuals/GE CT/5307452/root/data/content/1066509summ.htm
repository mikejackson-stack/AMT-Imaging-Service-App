Cradle Lock Assy Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Lock Assy cable connector.

  1. Unscrew 3 screws, and remove the Lock Assy from the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Lock Assy into place, and tighten the 3 screws.

  1. Push the cylinder head to release the cradle lock, and verify that the gap between the lock block and the cradle lock is 1.5mm ± 0.25.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release the cylinder head, and verify that the cradle locking function works well.

  1. Connect the Lock Assy cable connector.

2  Finalization

  1. Re-install the Table covers.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Verify that the cradle IN/OUT function is operating normally.
