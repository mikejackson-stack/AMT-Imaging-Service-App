4  Procedure

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Disable Axial Drive and HVDC on the service switch panel.

  1. Position the DAS/Detector in the 12 o’clock position.

  1. Shut down power to the rotating assembly using the 120 VAC service switch on the service switch panel and ensure power is off to the gantry.

  1. Remove the gantry left side, top and front covers.

  1. Access to controller side screws may be blocked by the gantry balance weights, remove the plenum using the Plenum removal and installation instructions and work on the plenum off the gantry.

  1. Remove any cable connections still left on the controller being replaced.

    Illustration 1:  plenum with cable covers

  1. Remove screws from both sides (3 each side) of the controller assembly and slide the assembly to the front to remove it from the plenum casting.

  1. Slide the new assembly into the Mount Channels.

  1. Reinstall the 6 controller chassis screws.

  1. Torque the 6 controller chassis screws to:

    4 N-m2.9 lb-ft35 lb-in40.4 Kg-cm

  1. Reinstall the plenum on the detector using the Plenum removal and installation instructions, including all cabling and cable safety covers.

  1. Install the gantry front, top and left side covers.

    Refer to Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn power back on to gantry, release rotational lock and retest for original failure mode.

  1. Allow the detector to warm up prior to customer use. This will take from 15 to 45 minutes depending on how long the Detector power was off.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform a [Fastcal] to account for any minor thermal variations introduced by new controller.
