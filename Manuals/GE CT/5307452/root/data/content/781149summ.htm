Gantry Stationary (CFC) Fan Control Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Electrocution hazard
High Voltages present.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   CFC Module Removal

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and rear cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform all required LOTO activities to ensure no power to the gantry.

  1. The CFC module is located on the left side of the gantry between the stationary 48VDC power supply and TGPU. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. The CFC module has 4 cables on the top. Disconnect all 4 cables before removing the module. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the 2 thumbscrews, open the cover on the CFC module. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Inside, remove the Molex connector from J2.

  1. The CFC module is secured to a gantry mounting plate with 6mm hex-head screws on each side. Remove all screws with a 5 mm hex wrench. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the module toward the back of the gantry to remove.

3.2   CFC Module Replacement

  1. Install the new control module using the 6 mm (5 mm hex wrench) screws.

  1. Reconnect all cabling removed, reference section above.

  1. Install CFC front cover after installing the molex internal connection.

  1. Install the gantry rear cover and top covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the LOTO and power up the system.

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the gantry left and right side covers.

4  Finalization

  1. Monitor the operator message area until the system indicates the detector temperature has returned to normal.

  1. Perform a [Fastcal] from the [Daily Prep] button on the Operator console.

  1. Perform a [Quality Assurance Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.
