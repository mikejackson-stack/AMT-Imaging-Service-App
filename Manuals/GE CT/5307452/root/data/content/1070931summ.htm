New Tube Configure

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

For Performix Pro VCT 100 X-ray Tube and Performix HD X-ray Tube.

-

-

Recommend procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. On the console, select [REPLACEMENT] from the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Select [NEW TUBE CONFIG] .

  1. Select the appropriate tube failure code from the [TUBE UNIT FAILURE CODE] pull-down menu.

  1. Enter the tube insert serial number in the [INSERT SERIAL NUMBER] edit box.

  1. Enter the tube housing serial number in the [HOUSING SERIAL NUMBER] edit box.

  1. Click [OK] . A pop-up window is displayed asking to shut down and restart the system.

  1. Click [OK] from the pop-up window.

  1. From the desktop selection area, click the [SHUTDOWN] icon button. A pop-up attention box appears.

  1. From the pop-up attention box, select the [RESTART] radio button.

  1. Click [OK] . The system reboots.
