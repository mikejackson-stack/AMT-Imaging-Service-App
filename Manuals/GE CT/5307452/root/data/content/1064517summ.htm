Reset TnT

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Recommend procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

Confirm [120 VAC], [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE], and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on the Service Switch Panel are turned ON.

-

-

Confirm Gantry Controls Table Collision Sensor Indicator has been reset.

-

-

Confirm Scan Hardware has been successfully reset.

-

-

Confirm no flashing indicators on Gantry Controls or Display.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. On the console, select DIAGNOSTIC from the Service Desktop.

  1. Select the GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI or X-RAY GENERATION folder, then [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] .

  1. Click [RESET TNT DATA]

  1. Verify that [TUBE TNT DATA] and [GENERATOR TNT DATA] are selected (highlighted).

  1. Click [RESET] . An Attention pop-up window is displayed.

  1. Click [OK] on the Attention pop-up window.

  1. Click [CANCEL] when the process is completed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [DISMISS] from GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI.
