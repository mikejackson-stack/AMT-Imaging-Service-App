Slipring Receiver Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove the Receiver

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn off the 3 service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the service switch panel.

  1. Perform all required LOTO activities to remove power from the gantry.

  1. Remove the left, side, and top covers, as well as the scan window and rear cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top slipring cover to gain access to the receiver.

  1. Disconnect the fiber optic and power supply cables.

  1. Remove the 2 mount screws holding the receiver plate assembly to the stationary frame to remove the receiver assembly. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

1.2   Install New Receiver and Adjust

  1. Install the new receiver assembly using the mount screws.

  1. The Receiver Gap Tool is a long black plastic strip stored next to the brush block mount. Remove the tool from the stationary frame below the receiver ( Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the plastic gap tool between the receiver shoe and the slipring antenna ( Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the two mount screws adjust the receiver shoe such that the gap tool is lightly held (not tight) between the shoe and the slipring antenna. The gap tool should be able to be removed without using force.

  1. Torque the mount screws to the settings in Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the alignment screws and adjust the RF Receiver shoe until the gold pins line up with the centerline of the RF antenna on the slipring on each side of the receiver shoe. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque the alignment screws to the settings below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove LOTO and turn on power. Turn on the 120VAC service switch and slowly rotate the slip ring watching the gap between the shoe and the slip ring.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the gantry is ready for rotation.

2  Finalization

  1. Turn on the 120VAC, HVDC and Axial drive service switches and enable Table drives.

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure proper data transmission.

  1. Check the RTS Dip stats for the scan just run and make sure there are no errors shown.

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC service switches.

  1. Install the slipring top cover, gantry rear cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC, HVDC and Axial Drive service switches.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.
