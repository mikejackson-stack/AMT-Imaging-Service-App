2  Procedure

  1. Save the generator runtime parameters. (See Save Restore Generator Runtime Parameters.)

  1. On the console, select [DIAGNOSTIC] from the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Select the [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] or [X-RAY GENERATION] folder, then select [GENERATOR TOOL – JEDI] .

  1. Select [INITIALIZE DATABASE] .

  1. Click [OK] on the Attention pop-up window. The generator database is reset.

  1. Click [OK] when the process is completed.

  1. Click [DISMISS] .
