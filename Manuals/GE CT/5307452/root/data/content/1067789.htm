2  Overview

This test is a verification of the relationship of the gantry tilt to the table level in the Y plane.

Last Revised:July 25, 2008

3.2  Safety

Shock Hazard
Shock or equipment damage may occur if removing covers with power applied.
Follow cover procedures to make sure Table and or Gantry Power is properly disabled when removing or installing covers.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

4  Procedure

  1. From the [Install] folder of the service manual, select the [Installation Manual] [Book 2 Electrical Calibration] .

  1. Perform the [Tilt Alignment Tool Procedure] From the [Image Quality] chapter.

  1. Remove gantry base and table base covers as applicable for adjustments required by the alignment procedure.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Table → (Cover Removal Procedures).

5  Finalization

  1. Replace Table and Gantry covers removed in prior steps.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedures).

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Table → (Cover Removal Procedures).

  1. Finalization is complete.
