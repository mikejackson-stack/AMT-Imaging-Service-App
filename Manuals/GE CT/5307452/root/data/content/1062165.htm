2  Overview

The Focal Spot Transfer function translates system level inputs (e.g., kVp, mA, focal spot size and position) to cathode voltage values.

Last Revised:30 July 2008

4  Procedure

  1. Move the table away from the gantry and remove any objects from the Detector Field of view.

  1. On the console, select [SCANNER UTILITIES] from the bottom of the Scan Monitor.

  1. Select [TRANSFER FUNCTION] from the Scanner Utilities window.

  1. Click [OK] on the Attention pop-up window.

  1. Press [START] button on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. When completed, select [QUIT] from the Scanner Utilities window.
