Intercom Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the control cable.

  1. Remove the cover assembly by removing 2 nuts and washers from one side and loosening the 2 on the other side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 4 screws that secure the intercom board to the cover.

  1. Install the new intercom board in reverse order. Verify that J2 jumper is in auto position.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform the Intercom board check and adjustment defined by Intercom Adjustment.

  1. Make sure cover is tightened if removed for adjustments.
