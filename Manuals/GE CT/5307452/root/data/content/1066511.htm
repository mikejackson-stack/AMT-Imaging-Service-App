4  Procedure

  1. Move the Cradle to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove a cradle grip by unscrewing 2 bolts.

    Illustration 1:  Cradle Grip Removal

  1. Slide a cradle rear cover away from the Gantry, and remove it.

    Illustration 2:  Cradle Rear Cover Removal

  1. Remove a support bar by unscrewing a bolt.

    Illustration 3:  Support Bar Removal

  1. Unscrew 6 bolts, and remove the cradle.

    Illustration 4:  Cradle Removal

The cradle is being installed with any shims to adjust the cradle position. So, when removing the cradle, write down the numbers of shims set under the cradle mounting bolt(s).
 
 

  1. Install the new cradle as follows:

    1. Remove two attachment rails from the defective cradle, and install them to the new cradle.

      Illustration 5:  Attachment Rails

    1. At the rear of the cradle align cradle to the cradle carriage.

    1. Install the 6 cradle screws. Do not tighten yet.

    1. Verify that the clearance between both side guide rollers and the cradle is approximately same.

      Illustration 6:  Cradle Centering

    1. Tighten the 6 cradle screws (Torque: 27.9Nm (285kgf-cm)).

    1. Install the support bar (1 bolt).

    1. Install the cradle rear cover, this cover has tabs on inside that must fit onto the edge at the back of the cradle provided for this cover.

      Illustration 7:  Cradle Rear Cover Installation

    1. Install the cradle grip (2 bolts).

5  Finalization

  1. Verify that the Table moves in/out normally.
