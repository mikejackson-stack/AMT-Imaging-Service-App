Console Monitor Cleaning

Last Revision30 July 2008

1  Overview

Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) screens are easily susceptible to damage and scratches. Do not touch the display surface, and make sure you clean the screen correctly.

Not all types of cleaning solutions are acceptable for LCD screens. Using alcohol or ammonia - based cleaners may cause permanent damage to the LCD. Over time, using these types of cleaners could cause the surface of the screen to yellow. It can also make the screen brittle and eventually cause the screen surface to crack.

DO NOT USE THE FOLLOWING CLEANERS:

  • Acetone

  • Ethyl alcohol

  • Ethyl acid

  • Ammonia

  • Methyl chloride

 
 

The following types of cleaners are acceptable:

  • Water

  • Vinegar (mixed with water) (Use 1 part vinegar to 4 parts water.)

  • Isopropyl Alcohol

2  Procedure

NOTE:Do not spray any liquids on the LCD screen directly. (You want to avoid dripping the solution directly into your computer or laptop.)

Do NOT use paper towels. These can scratch the LCD screen.

  1. Turn off the monitor.

  2. Apply the solution you are using on your cloth first, NOT directly on the monitor screen.

  3. Stroke the cloth across the display in one direction, moving from the top of the display to the bottom.

  4. Use a vacuum to clean dust from vents, as needed.
