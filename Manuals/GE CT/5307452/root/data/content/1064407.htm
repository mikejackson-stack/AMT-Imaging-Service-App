2  Overview

This document provides the necessary steps to check for the proper flow rate in the tube oil cooling system.

Unusual image artifacts, tube over-temperature errors, or noisy tube oil pump are symptoms of flow related problems. Low flow rate is an indicator of possible air in the cooling system or a faulty tube oil pump.

4  Procedure

4.1  Air Removal Apparatus Preparation

  1. Remove the reservoir cap and place funnel into tank. Place rags around funnel to capture any spilled oil.

    Illustration 1:  Air Removal Apparatus Preparation

  1. Fill reservoir with approximately 1 US gallon of transformer oil.

    NOTE:At a minimum, make sure the reservoir is 1/2 full prior to starting the air removal procedure.

    NOTE:The reservoir volume is approximately 5 liters (1.3 gallons).

    Illustration 2:  Fill Reservoir with Transformer Oil

4.2  Flow Meter Apparatus Air Purge

Prior to starting the flow rate measurement procedure, air must be removed from the flow meter apparatus.
 
 

  1. Connect the flow meter apparatus female quick-disconnect to the air removal apparatus male quick-disconnect.

  1. Connect the flow meter apparatus male quick-disconnect to the air removal apparatus female quick-disconnect.

  1. Orient the hoses such that the flow meter outlet hose is at the highest point.

  1. Plug in the air removal apparatus power supply.

    NOTE:The power supply as shown is set up for 115V operation. However, it is a universal power supply and will work internationally with the correct interface adapter.

  1. Turn the air removal apparatus power ON to the pump by actuating the rocker switch.

  1. When all air is out of the flow meter apparatus, shut OFF the apparatus pump and uncouple the quick-disconnects.

4.3  Preliminary Setup

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY
AN ENERGIZED GANTRY PRESENTS MECHANICAL (ROTATIONAL) AND ELECTRICAL HAZARDS.

If this procedure execution is NOT due to another Service Procedure, complete the following preparation steps.
 
 

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [ AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on the Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry so that the tube is at 12 o'clock position.

  1. Engage Gantry Rotational Lock.

  1. Remove tube oil pump to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts and uncouple the quick-disconnect.

4.4  Flow Rate Measurement

  1. Connect the flow rate apparatus male disconnect to the tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Connect the flow rate apparatus female disconnect to the tube male disconnect. (See Illustration 3.)

    Illustration 3:  Connect the Flow Rate Apparatus

  1. Turn ON the [120 VAC] switch of the Service Switch.

  1. Measure the oil flow rate, using the black line on the white indicator and aligning it with the left hand scale of the flow meter ( Illustration 4).

    1. If the flow is equal to or more than 4.0 gpm, the flow is adequate.

    1. If the flow is 0 gpm, check the electrical connections on the tube oil pump and verify that the pump is operating.

    1. If the flow is less than 4.0 gpm but greater than 0.5 gpm, there is most likely air in the tube oil cooling system. Perform Tube Oil Cooling System Air Removal.

    Illustration 4:  Measure the Flow Rate

  1. Turn OFF the [120 VAC] switch of the Service Switch.

  1. Uncouple the flow meter apparatus male disconnect from the tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Uncouple the flow meter apparatus female disconnect from the tube male disconnect.

  1. Connect the tube oil pump female disconnect to the tube male disconnect.

5  Finalization

  1. Clean flow meter apparatus of any excess oil and prepare for air shipment/storage.

  1. If another Service Procedure required procedure execution, return to the associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform the following steps

    1. Restore tube oil pump to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism. Torque M6 bolts to the following values:

      5.8 ft.-lbs 7.9 N-m 70 in-lbs80.8 kg-cm
      NOTE:If safety mechanism rotates in relation to the quick-disconnect, tighten slowly until it stops rotating.

      NOTE:Do not over-tighten M6 bolts. Doing so can cause quick-disconnects to leak.

    1. Disengage gantry rotational lock.

    1. Install gantry front cover, top covers, left side cover, and scan window.

    1. Turn ON the [120 VAC] and [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] switches.

    1. Press the [ESTOP RESET] button of the Service Switch panel. Wait until the scanning hardware is successfully reset.

    1. Install gantry right side cover.
