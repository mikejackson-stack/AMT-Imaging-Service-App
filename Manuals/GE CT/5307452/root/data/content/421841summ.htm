Detector Routing Board and Thermostat Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

This procedure references the detector replacement procedure. Remove all gantry covers per that procedure.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Gantry Preparation

  1. Use the Detector Replacement Procedure including gantry cover removal steps to pull the detector away from the gantry for easy access to the routing boards.

3.2   Routing board/Thermistor replacement

The Low channel Routing board is on the 48V Power supply side of the detector on the end of the detector rail. High channel routing board is on the end by the 107 weight stack.

  1. Remove the 4 screws holding the cover to the routing board standoffs.

  1. Remove the 4 studs to remove the board from the detector. Then remove the 2 screws of the thermostat under the board and remove the assembly.

3.3   Routing board/Thermistor installation

  1. Position the thermostat and install the 2 screws to fasten the thermostat to the detector rail.

  1. Position the board and install the 4 stud to fasten the board to the studs on the detector rail.

  1. Install the cover.

  1. Finish reconnecting all cabling and then reinstall the Detector per the Detector Replacement Procedure Installation process including cover installation steps.

4  Finalization

  1. Allow the detector to warm up while monitoring the detector temps to make sure all 3 zones are coming up to temperature using DAStools Aux channel test to report current rail temperatures. The detector may take up to 45 minutes to come up to temperature. Perform any tests required to confirm original problem has been resolved.

  1. Perform IQ checks per the Detector Replacement procedure. If any IQ issues are identified perform a full Detailed calibration and Fastcal.
