Tube Temperature Verification

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Timing Not Applicable5 mins Timing Not Applicable

2  Overview

This procedure is used to check the tube temperature value.

Last revised: 26 June 2008

3  Procedure

  1. On the console, select [DAILY PREP] from the bottom of the Scan Monitor.

  1. Select [TUBE WARM-UP] .

  1. Click [ACCEPT & RUN TUBE WARMUP] on the Attention pop-up window.

  1. Click [PAUSE] from the Cold Warmup Scan list pane.

  1. Click [CANCEL] from the Cold Warmup Scan list pane.

Do not start X-ray exposures. Starting tube warm-up will result in additional tube cooling wait times.
 
 

  1. Click the [CURRENT MESSAGE] area of the Display/Image Monitor.

  1. Click [VIEW LOG] from the Current Message area.

  1. Click [LAST PAGE] from the Current Message area.

  1. Read the last entry of the following statement:

    Tube temperature after Cold Tube Warmup is xxx.xx degrees Celsius.

    where xxx.xx is the tube temperature value.

  1. Repeat steps 2 through 5 and steps 8 through 9, as necessary to generate updates.

  1. Click [CLOSE] from the Current Message area and click [QUIT] from the Daily Preparation window when tube temperature shows 200.00 Celsius or less.

4  Finalization

No finalization required.
