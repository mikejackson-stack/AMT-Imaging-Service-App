3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove Old LCD Monitor

  1. Disconnect the Power Cord and Video Cable from the LCD Monitor being replaced.

  1. Remove the LCD Monitor and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement LCD Monitor

  1. Place the replacement LCD monitor on the Operator Console.

  1. Plug in Video Cable in the analog SVGA connector on the LCD Monitor and secure the two (2) retaining screws.

  1. Reconnect the Power Cord to the LCD Monitor

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure the Power Switch on the side of the LCD Monitor is turned on.

3.4   Verify LCD Monitor Replacement

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify that the LCD Monitor powers up and receives a video signal.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the LCD Monitor it is necessary to perform LCD Video Monitor Setup.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the LCD Monitor Setup Procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Refer to to confirm proper operation.
