4  Procedure

Potential For Equipment Damage. DO NOT lift the Collimator by the Motors.
 
 

  1. Move table to its home position.

  1. Remove gantry covers as described under the section Replacement > Gantry > Enclosure

  1. Remove the tube by following the procedure in Tube Replacement.

  1. Disconnect connectors J8 and J9 from collimator.

  1. Remove collimator by removing the two (2) mounting bolts from the top, two (2) bolts from the bottom, and 2 bolts from the rear of the collimator.

  1. Install new collimator by replacing the six (6) bolts removed in above step.

    1. Apply Pre-load torque to M14 Mounting bolts:

      Table 1:  Pre-Load Torque

      36 lb-ft 53 N-m 465 lb-in540 kg-cm

    1. Apply Final torque to M14 Mounting bolts:

      Table 2:  Final Torque

      78 lb-ft 106 N-m 937 lb-in 1081 kg-cm

  1. Secure the tube back into place following the procedure Tube Replacement

  1. Reassemble gantry covers.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform the following procedures in this order:

    • FLASH Download

    • Gantry Rotation Safety Check

    • Gantry Balance Check

    • Z-Alignment

    • Cold ISO Alignment

    • Hot ISO Alignment

    • (For Advance Class procedure) HHS Scans (Adv)

    • Collimator Calibration

    • Detailed Calibration

    • Fast Calibration

    • System Scanning Test

    • Quality Assurance Test

    • System State Save Restore
