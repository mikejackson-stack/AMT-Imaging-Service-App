Meter Verification (PM)

Last Revision24 October 2008

This procedure verifies that the internal kV and mA meters are functional. If the meters are not functional, the inverter cannot be used in the system.

  1. Rotate the gantry so that the HP inverter assembly is in the 2 o’clock position.

  2. Turn off all three service switches on the Gantry Service Panel (Axial drive enable, HVD enable, 120 VAC)

  3. Remove the inverter cover nearest the cradle.

  4. Attach a protective ESD wrist strap.

    NOTE:Failure to use protective ESD equipment may result in destroyed/damaged equipment.

  5. Remove jumper TP17.

  6. Using a digital multi-meter (DMM/DVM) as a dc ammeter, connect the red signal lead to TP19 and the black common lead to TP15.

  7. Restore the 120 VAC power and press <Drives_Reset> on the Gantry Service Panel.

  8. On the Service Desktop, launch the Generator Tool.

  9. Wait for the gantry display to reset. (If there are no flashing lights on the gantry display, it is reset.)

  10. On the Service Desktop, launch the Meter Verification tool.

  11. Verify the mA meter:

    1. Using the tool, enter a delay time to allow you to walk to the meter.

    2. Run the test and walk to the meter.

    3. Record the ending mA reading on the multi-meter to one decimal place. If this accuracy cannot be obtained, change the range on the meter and redo the test.

    4. Event the value in the box for mA Low Scale. It appears in the mA High Scale box automatically. The difference between the two measurements should appear in the delta column.

    5. Verify that the delta is within the limits (on the right).

    6. Remove the DVM leads from TP15 and TP19.

    7. Reconnect the jumper.

  12. Verify the kV meter:

    1. Turn off the 120 VAC gantry switch.

    2. Switch the multi-meter to DV volt meter.

    3. Connect the red signal lead to KV_N (TP6) and the black common lead to KV_GND (TP8).

    4. Turn on the 120 VAC power and press <Drives_Reset> on the Gantry Service Panel. Wait for the rest to finish.

    5. Run the test and walk to the meter.

    6. Record the ending voltage on the meter to one decimal place. If this accuracy cannot be obtained, change the range on the meter and redo the test.

    7. Multiply by 10.

    8. Enter this calculation in the box for kV.

    9. Verify that all three deltas are within the limits.

      NOTE:kV may not have a limit. The system saves the data.

  13. Turn off the 120 V switch on the Gantry Service Panel.

  14. Remove the test equipment.

  15. Reinstall the inverter cover.

1  Required Conditions

  • Scanner should be fully operational.

  • X-ray tube should be cold to avoid damage during power cycling.

  • Digital Multimeter must meet the following conditions:

    • VDC Mode must have 10 Mohm input impedance

    • Have an mA scale capability of 199.9 mA

    • Have a display capability of 3-1/2 digits

    • Have a display capability of at least 2 decimal places

2  Gantry Preparations

  1. Turn OFF all three switches on the gantry service switch panel (Axial Drive Enable, HVDC, 120 VAC).

  2. Rotate the gantry so that the HV Tank assembly is in the 3 o'clock position.

  3. Engage the rotational lock.
