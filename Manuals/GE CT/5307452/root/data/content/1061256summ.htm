Slipring Brush Block Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Perform Lockout Tagout for system power (A1 disconnect) prior to replacing this component.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and rear cover.

  1. Remove Rear Gantry cover support bracket that is over top of the Brush Block assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Slip Ring safety covers.

  1. Remove brush block safety cover (4 screws with 4 mm hex wrench)

  1. Disconnect all connections to Brush block (see Illustration 2) .

    (Step Details)

Do not touch brushes with your fingers. Transfer of carbon dust can occur which can then be spread to other components touched.
 
 

  1. Remove four (4) 6 mm cap screws that secure brush block assembly to gantry.

  1. Carefully remove brush block.

    (Step Details)

  1. Clean the slip ring prior to installing the new brush block and brushes. Use the PM Procedure for cleaning the slip ring.

  1. Install the new brush block and secure with the four (4) 6 mm cap screws. Do not tighten yet.

  1. Push the brush block against the position adjustment set screws in the mounting bracket.

  1. Using a flashlight, look into the top and bottom High Voltage brush locations on the inner HV ring (inside edge of slip ring furthest from brush block mounts). Verify the brush block position is adjusted so the brushes ride in the center of their tracks. Adjust the brush-block setscrews if necessary to center the brush on the slip ring track.

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque the four (4) 6 mm cap screws to:

    (Step Details)

Brush Contamination Possible. Do not touch brushes with your fingers. The skin oils will contaminate the brush and reduce usable life and potentially create future failures.
 
 

  1. Install the new brushes supplied with the new brush block as torque as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reassemble gantry.

    1. Install power lines to brush block per cable labels.

    1. Install brush block cover and slip ring covers.

    1. Install gantry cover mount over top of brush block.

    1. Install Gantry covers and restore power to system.

4  Finalization

  1. Restore power to the system. Verify proper operation by running verification scans. Verification procedure should consist of:

    1. From the Common Service desktop, Diagnostics tab, run the Axial Functional Test, rotating the gantry for 600 seconds to set the new brushes. Exit axial functional when done.

    1. Run 5 stationary and 5 rotational scans with x-ray. The technique is not important. It is important to exit the exam, because this triggers the “DIP Stats” update.

    1. From the Common Service Desktop, Diagnostics tab, select RTS viewer

    1. Choose the DIP selection and view each scans DIP stats. There should be no FEC Failures or Sync Errors. See below for example portion of stats.

      (Step Details)

    1. Select the TGP and ORP low speed stats and verify zero disconnects, see below for example portion of ORP stats.

      (Step Details)

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual.
