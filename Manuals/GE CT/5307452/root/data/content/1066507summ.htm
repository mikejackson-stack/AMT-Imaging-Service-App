Cradle Drive Belt Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table component and covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen 2 bolts to remove tension from the Cradle Drive Belt 2, and remove the belt from the small pulley of the clutch and the pulley of the cradle drive roller.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the new Cradle Drive Belt on and around the small pulley of the clutch and the pulley of the cradle drive roller.

  1. Adjust the belt tension according to Cradle Motor and Cradle Drive Belt Tension Adjustment.

2  Finalization

  1. Re-install the cradle and Table covers.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Move the cradle In/Out completely 10 cycles, and verify that the Table movement is operating normally.
