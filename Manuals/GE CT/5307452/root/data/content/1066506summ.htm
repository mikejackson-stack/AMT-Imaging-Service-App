Cradle Absolute Encoder Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table components and covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the Encoder cable to the Table frame.

  1. Disconnect the Encoder cable connector.

  1. Attach an adhesive tape to the wire drum, in order to avoid loosening the wire.

  1. Attach an adhesive tape to the wire hook on the cradle carriage.

  1. Loosen the adjusting nut, to remove tension from the cradle wire.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen 2 set screws on the wire drum, then remove the wire drum from the Encoder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew 3 screws, and remove the Encoder from the Table.

  1. Install the new Encoder in place, and tighten the 3 screws.

  1. Re-install the wire drum to the Encoder, and tighten the 2 set screws, to fasten the wire drum.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the adjusting nut in the CW direction until the distance D is 1 ± 0.5 mm, and tighten the set nut.

  1. Take off the adhesive tapes from the wire drum and the wire hook.

  1. Re-connect the Encoder cable connector, and fasten the cable to the Table frame with tie-wraps.

  1. Move the cradle manually IN and OUT, and verify that the cradle movement is smooth.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Perform Cradle Characterization.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-intall the cradle and Table covers.
