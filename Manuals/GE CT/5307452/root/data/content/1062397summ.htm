1  Safety

VOLTAGES MAY BE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING COMPONENTS.

GANTRY HAS ROTATING ASSEMBLY
USE THE GANTRY ROTATIONAL LOCK, TO PREVENT UNCOMMANDED MOTION OF THE GANTRY ROTATING ASSEMBLY.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

3  Procedure

3.1   High Voltage Tank Removal

  1. Move table to its home longitudinal position.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove scan window

  1. Remove gantry left side, top, and front covers.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 2 o'clock position.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove two lower M12 tank-mounting bolts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry such that High Voltage Tank assembly is in 2:30 o'clock position.

  1. Turn OFF [120 VAC] switch on Service Switch panel.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Cut Ty-Raps on inverter and auxiliary box clamps.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable candlestick.

  1. Place a cover over the candlestick.

  1. Place a cap or paper towel in the top of receptacle to prevent oil spillage and dirt from contaminating receptacle.

  1. Remove four M3 screws holding top cover on High Voltage Tank to expose wire connections.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect wire connections from the High Voltage Tank ( Illustration 2) as follows:

    1. High Voltage Tank measure cable (from the inverter)

    1. High Voltage inverter/tank cable (from the inverter)

    1. 48VDC Supply

    1. Communication Cable to SCORP and DAS

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect hoist hook to tank lifting point. Remove all slack in chain and apply light tension.

  1. Remove upper two M12 tank-mounting bolts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two front M12 mounting nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using hoist, carefully lift tank and swing it free from gantry.

3.2   High Voltage Tank Installation

  1. Lift new High Voltage tank using gantry hoist.

  1. Slide new High Voltage tank into place from front of gantry, over the 2 mounting studs.

  1. Fasten new M12 mounting bolts, washers, and nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply the following “preload” torque to the M12 mounting bolts and nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the hoist hook from the tank.

  1. Apply the following final torque to the M12 mounting bolts and nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the wire connections to the High Voltage Tank ( Illustration 2).

  1. Install the top cover on the High Voltage Tank with the M3 screws ( Illustration 1).

  1. Apply final torque to the M3 screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure High Voltage cable. (see Securing HV Cable).

CAN OCCUR DUE TO INCORRECTLY SEALED AND SECURED HIGH VOLTAGE CABLES.
