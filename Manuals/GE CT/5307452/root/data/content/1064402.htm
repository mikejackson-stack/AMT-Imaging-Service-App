Tube Warmup

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Timing not applicable5 minutesTiming not applicable

2  Overview

Tube warm-up heats the tube to a safe operating point for patient scanning.

Last Revised:June 24, 2008

3  Procedure

  1. Move the table away from the gantry and remove any objects from the Detector Field of view.

  1. Install gantry covers and scan windows.

  1. On the console, select [DAILY PREP] from the bottom of the scan monitor.

  1. Select [TUBE WARMUP] from Daily Preparation window.

  1. Click [ACCEPT & RUN TUBE WARMUP] on the Attention pop-up window.

  1. Press the [START] button on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. When completed, select [QUIT] from Daily Preparation window.

4  Finalization

No finalization required.
