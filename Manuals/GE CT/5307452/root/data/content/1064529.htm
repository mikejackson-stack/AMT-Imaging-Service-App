Discovery CT750 HD Service Methods - Adv

PM Inspection

Last Revision23 July 2008

1  Preliminary Requirement

Complete a tube warm-up.

(To complete a tube warm-up: On the Exam Rx Desktop, select [DAILY PREP] and then [TUBE WARMUP] . Follow the steps on the screen until all scans are completed. This brings the tube to a safe operating point.)

2  Overview

2.1  X-ray On Light Inspection

During the Functional IQ Test, verify that the following X-ray On indicators light up:

  • SCIM

  • Gantry Front

  • Gantry Rear (To avoid walking into the room during a scan, you may want to use a mirror to check this X-ray indicator.)

  • Room Warning Light

Record your verification on the report form.

2.2  Scan Control Pushbutton Check

During the Functional IQ Test, verify that the following scan control pushbuttons are operational:

  • Start scan (and keypad lights).

  • Stop scan (and keypad lights).

  • Pause scan.

  • Advance to scan.

  • Stop table.

Record your verification on the report form.

2.3  Functional IQ Test

This series provides a functional IQ image test with Pass or Fail results.

Review the images and verify that they meet the specifications listed in this document, including means and standard deviation measurements. Keep a record of failures that occur during the image series.

Use the following scan parameters during the image series acquisition:

QA3  Helios  0.516  120kV  135 mA  1 sec
NOTE:Consider any image series scan that does not meet specifications as failing.

Each time you install the phantom on the phantom holder, make sure you use a bubble level and the Z-axis knob on the phantom holder to level the phantom.

4  Acquiring the 20 cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the phantom holder on the head-end of the table.

  2. Mount the 20 cm QA phantom on the phantom holder.

  3. Center, align, and level the 20 cm QA phantom.

    • Level the phantom using the bubble level and the Z-Axis knob on the phantom holder.

    • Align the black line on the phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Press landmark on the phantom line.

    1. On the exam Rx desktop, select [New Patient] .

    2. Enter the following information:

      • Patient ID: Service

      • Patient Name: QA3 Helical IQ

    3. From the Protocol Selection display, select the Service Tab under the anatomical selector.

    4. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click [Manufacturing] .

    5. On the Service Category Protocol List click [45.7] .

    6. Click [Next Series]

      Selecting-> QA3 Heli-0.516/120kv/135mA/1sec is displayed in the Series Description:

    7. Click [Confirm Scan] .

    8. End exam.

5  QA PM Functional IQ Verification - QA3 Helical Series

NOTE:During the Image Analysis process, you need to switch between the Image Works screen (to select the image series to analyze) and the Image Analysis screen (to run a particular Auto 1x analysis test). Use ALT+F3 to send a window to the back.

If you select the Service Desktop and quickly select Image-Works screen, the Service Desktop screen pops-up on the Image-works screen.

  1. Select the Image Works screen.

    1. Select/highlight the exam number from step h.

    2. Select/highlight the Series 1.

  2. Launch theImage Analysis Tool.

    1. Select Service Desktop.

    2. Drag the Service Desktop to the left -hand monitor.

    3. SelectImage Quality .

    4. Select Image Analysis.

    5. Drag the Image Analysis tool to the left-hand monitor.

    6. Select Auto 1x.

    7. Select Auto 1x.

    8. Select ImagSer 20Qa.

    9. On the pull-down menu, select qa3 helical Heli/0.516/120kv/135mA/1sec.

    10. Select [Auto] .

    11. Click [Accept] .

  3. Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Per Image Table.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator window of the report display indicates PASS.

    2. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in the table for the first 20 cm QA Image Series.
