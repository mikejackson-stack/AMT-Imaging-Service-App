3.3  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
THE VeRB COMPUTER CHASSIS CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 40 POUNDS.
DEPENDING ON YOUR HEIGHT AND ACCESS TO EQUIPMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION, LIFTING ASSISTANCE MAY BE REQUIRED. USE YOUR JUDGMENT TO DETERMINE IF LIFT ASSIST OR A SECOND PERSON MAY BE REQUIRED TO ASSIST IN REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 
 

IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 
 

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

4  Procedure

4.1  Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    • If Applications are running, click the Shut Down icon and select Shut Down.

    • If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell using the Toolchest. Type: {ctuser@hostname} halt. Press <Enter>.

    The Operator Console monitor will display a ‘System Halted’ message when it is acceptable to power off the Operator Console.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:  Console Power Switch

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

4.2  Remove VeRB Computer

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the cable connections at the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis.

    NOTE:Verify that all cables are labeled and clearly marked. If necessary, add a label for clarity.

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis.

  1. Remove the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws and washers from the front of the VeRB Computer chassis, holding the chassis to the Operator Console rack.

    Illustration 3:  VeRB Computer Mounting

  1. Slide the VeRB Computer chassis forward (out the front of console) and set aside. See procedure.

4.3  Remove GPU Card from VeRB Computer

  1. At the rear of the VeRB Computer Chassis, release the Chassis Cover Lock using a Phillips screwdriver. Back out lock screw at least two (2) full turns.

    Illustration 4:  VeRB Computer Chassis Cover Lock

  1. Slide the Chassis Cover towards the front of the chassis and remove.

    NOTE:You may need to tap on back upper edge of cover to release cover before sliding forward.

    Illustration 5:  VeRB Computer Cover Removal

  1. Remove the Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp from VeRB Computer Chassis.

    Illustration 6:  VeRB Computer Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp Removal

  1. Disconnect the two (2) Power Supply Connectors at the rear of the GPU Card.

  1. Remove the two (2) Phillips Screws holding the GPU Card in the VeRB Computer chassis and carefully remove the card from the Systemboard.

    Illustration 7:  VeRB Computer GPU Card Location

4.4  Install Replacement GPU Card in VeRB Computer

  1. Carefully install the replacement GPU Card in the same location (slot) in the VeRB Computer. Make sure all cables are clear of the GPU Card and the card seats firmly in the Systemboard.

    Illustration 8:  VeRB Computer GPU Card Slot Location

  1. Reinstall the two (2) Phillips Screws to secure the GPU Card to the chassis.

  1. Reconnect the two (2) Power Supply Connectors to the rear of the GPU Card.

  1. Reinstall the Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp in the VeRB Computer Chassis.

    Illustration 9:  VeRB Computer Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp Placement

    NOTE:The Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp must be installed as illustrated above. The Clamp should be centered over the Cooling Fan with slotted end over expansion cards.

  1. Reinstall the VeRB Computer Chassis Cover and lock the cover in place.

    NOTE:IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.

4.5  Re-Install the VeRB Computer

  1. Slide the replacement VeRB Computer into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Replace the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws and washers to secure the VeRB Computer chassis to the Operator Console rack. Torque to 4.6 N-m.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the VeRB Computer chassis and verify that its power supply switch is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear cable connection(s).

    Illustration 10:  VeRB Cable Connections and Destinations

    For cabling details, refer to GOC6 Console Schematic 5212920SCH for a scalable PDF version of the schematic.)

4.6  Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the VeRB Computer front panel Power LED is illuminated. If not, press the VeRB’s front panel power switch to apply power to the VeRB Computer.

    Illustration 11:  VeRB Front Panel Indicators and Switches

4.7  Verify Replacement VeRB Computer GPU Card Operation

  1. Verify that the VeRB Computer has powered up and is not displaying or sounding any POST Errors.

    NOTE:See the VeRB Troubleshooting procedure if any errors appear.

  1. No other verifications steps required.

5  Finalization

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
