2  Overview

The Filament Calibration tool runs an automated scan sequence and populates the JEDI database accordingly.

Last revised:22 August 2008

4  Procedure

  1. Move the table away from the gantry and remove any objects from the Detector Field of View.

  1. On the console, select [DIAGNOSTIC] tab from the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Select [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI] or [X-RAY GENERATION] folder then [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI] window.

  1. Select [FILAMENT CAL] from the [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI] window.

  1. Verify that [DUCTILITY WARM UP] , [LARGE FILAMENT CAL] , and [SMALL FILAMENT CAL] buttons are highlighted as selections.

  1. Click [SCAN] to start the filament calibration. The software automatically warms the tube and runs the scans to calibrate the filament. If filament calibration fails, run the program again.

    NOTE: If the Filament Calibration fails persistently, perform Initialize Generator Database and repeat this procedure again.

  1. Click [CANCEL] from [FILAMENT CAL] .

  1. Click [DISMISS] from [GENERATOR TOOL - JEDI] .
