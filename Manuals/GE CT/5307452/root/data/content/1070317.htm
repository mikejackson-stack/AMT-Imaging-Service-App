2  Overview

This procedure provides steps for HOT ISO Alignment.

Last Revised:October 23, 2008

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

3.2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

4  Procedure

  1. Select the [CALIBRATION] tab in the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Select [HOT ISO ALIGNMENT] . The Hot ISO Screen appears as shown in Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Hot ISO Screen

  1. Remove any objects from the detector FOV. (Cradle, phantoms, Mylar window, etc.)

  1. Click the first Scan button to execute the Heat Soak and Seasoning scan.

  1. Press [START] on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Click the second Scan button to execute the Air Scans (small spot and large spot).

  1. Press [START] on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Place an 1/8 in. screwdriver on the phantom holder (should be pointing into the Z direction). Turn ON the laser alignment lights. Advance the adjust the table to position the metal shaft 35 mm up and 35 mm to the right of ISO center as marked by the laser alignment lights. Make sure the shaft remains perpendicular to the scan plane.

  1. Click the third Scan button to execute Pin Scans (small spot and large spot).

  1. Press [START] on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. Click the [Calculate] button to calculate correction factor.

    NOTE:No movement of the tube is required. This is a software correction done automatically.
