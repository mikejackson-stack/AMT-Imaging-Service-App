Rotor Diagnostic Test

2  Overview

The purpose of this test is to drive the rotation inverter in high speed and low speed modes on the tube in order to identify a defective rotation board or a wrong connection between rotation board and tube.

Last Revised18 July 2008

3  Procedure

  1. If desired, you may check any or all of the following:

    1. PPC kV control board alive and running: S0-S7 LEDs are lit successively or a combination of them blink

    1. Cabling between rotation board and tube checked

    1. Rotation board alive and running: DS5 LED blinking for rotation V2 (or DS4 LED blinking for rotation V3)

    1. Rotation board DC supply present: DS7 neon is lit

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL] from the [DIAGNOSTICS] tab in the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Launch the [Rotor Diagnostic] tool. Refer to the Illustration for the tool interface. ADD GRAPHIC HERE

  1. Run the test. A rotor sequence will proceed.

  1. If a problem occurs, check the error log.
