Inverter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR ELECTRIC SHOCK
VOLTAGES MAY BE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING COMPONENTS.

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY (PINCH, CRUSH, ENTANGLEMENT OR IMPACT INJURIES ARE POSSIBLE.)
GANTRY HAS ROTATING ASSEMBLY
USE THE GANTRY ROTATIONAL LOCK, TO PREVENT UNCOMMANDED MOTION OF THE GANTRY ROTATING ASSEMBLY.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

3  Procedure

3.1   Inverter Removal

  1. Save Generator Runtime Parameters (see Save Restore Generator Runtime Parameters).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Auxiliary Box according to Auxiliary Box Replacement procedure.

  1. Remove High Voltage cable clamps from front of Inverter.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Inverter covers.

  1. Disconnect wire connections inside the Inverter assembly as follows.

    1. High Voltage inverter/tank cable. (Make sure to remove washer and nut.)

    1. HVDC wire coming from choke assembly. (Make sure to remove washers and nuts.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect wire connections from the front of Inverter assembly as follows:

    1. DC Bus from Auxiliary Box (J6)

    1. Low Voltage Power Supply (J1)

    1. Smart Tube ID (J5)

    1. Auxiliary Box CAN (J13)

    1. System CAN (J3)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Internal CAN and RTL inverter/tank cable clamp from bottom of Inverter assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert lifting post into gantry frame and attach boom and hoist.

  1. Connect hoist hook to Inverter lifting point. Remove all slack in chain and apply light tension.

  1. Carefully remove four (4) long M12 bolts and washers that hold Inverter chassis to gantry.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully swing inverter free of gantry.

3.2   Inverter Installation

  1. Using hoist, attach new inverter to gantry. (As you lower inverter, it will fall into place.)

  1. Remove inverter covers.

  1. Fasten new inverter with new M12 mounting bolts and washers, as well as blue Loctite (242).

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque each M12 mounting bolt to the following specification:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove boom/post/hoist assembly.

  1. Reconnect wire connections inside Inverter assembly as follows:

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace inverter covers.

  1. Reconnect wire connections from the front of the Inverter assembly as follows:

    1. DC Bus from Auxiliary Box (J6)

    1. Low Voltage Power Supply (J1)

    1. Smart Tube ID (J5)

    1. Auxiliary Box CAN (J13)

    1. System CAN (J3)

  1. Reconnect Internal CAN and RTL inverter/tank cable and cable clamp to bottom of Inverter assembly.

  1. Install High Voltage cable clamps to front of Inverter.

  1. Install Auxiliary Box according to Auxiliary Box Replacement.
