Gantry Rear Cover Removal and Re-Install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Side and Top covers and scan window have been removed.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Removal

  1. Assemble the rear cover dolly.

    1. Tighten the two (2) shoulder bolts to the rear cover.

      (Step Details)

    Pinch Hazard
    Uncontrolled cover movement
    Make sure the wing nuts are tightened on the cover dollies prior to releasing cover from gantry mounts

    1. Fit side dolly through the shoulder bolts and secure assembly with two (2) wing nuts. See Illustration 1

    1. Repeat steps a and b for the other side dolly.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT. POTENTIAL FOR INJURY IF COVERS REMOVED AND POWER IS LEFT “ON”.
DISABLE ALL SERVICE SWITCHES PRIOR TO REMOVING REAR COVERS.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Disconnect cables on the right side of the rear cover.

  1. Remove rear cover.

    1. Disengage upper and lower cantrell brackets on both sides of the rear cover.

      1. Using steady but firm pressure, lift each of the lower cantrell brackets from their associated retainers. See Illustration 2.

        (Step Details)

      1. Disengage the locking mechanism on the upper cantrell brackets by using your thumb to slide the trigger (red lever) back. This will release the locking mechanism and allow the cantrell to be rotated upwards with steady and firm pressure.

    1. Disengage the rubber retaining straps on both sides.

    1. Disengage the cam latch just above the rubber straps.

    1. Move cover away from gantry as needed.

    1. To keep the E-Stop loop connected, connect cable J1 to the terminator located on the cantrell arm or on the gantry frame below the cantrell.

      (Step Details)

2.2   Installation

  1. Position cover in back of gantry.

  1. Attach the rear cover

    1. Align the studs on both sides of the rear cover with the receivers located on the gantry frame.

    1. Insert the stud on one side into its associated receiver and attach the rubber retaining straps. Then insert the stud on the other side into its associated receiver and attach its rubber retaining straps.

      (Step Details)

    1. Engage the cam latch just above the rubber straps on the cover mount bracket.

  1. Reattach upper and lower cantrell brackets on both sides.

    1. Remove upper cantrell brackets from service position and rotate them into position over their associated retaining pins. Press down firmly on the bracket and snap it into place. The locking mechanism on each upper bracket should lock the bracket securely into place. Perform this action on both sides.

    1. Remove lower cantrell brackets from service position and rotate them into position over their associated retaining pins. Press down firmly on the bracket and snap it into place.

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove dolly, disassemble and store safely away.

  1. Reattach cables to cover.

3  Finalization

  1. Continue with other cover installation procedures as necessary. When AC power is restored to the system, prior to enabling axial drive or HVDC, remember to rotate the gantry by hand to ensure there is no interference between covers and rotating components.

  1. When power is restored, Observe Gantry Display and Gantry Control panels during power on. Verify Power On Self Test (POST) completes normally and all cover indicators were illuminated during the POST. Verify that all Gantry control panels are functional with no ERR indicators. Also verify that the Gantry to Operator intercom is functional.
