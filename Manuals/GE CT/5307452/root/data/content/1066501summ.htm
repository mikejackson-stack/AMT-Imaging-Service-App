24V Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table Cover:

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the Power Supply cables to the Table frame.

  1. Disconnect the following cable connectors:

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew 4 screws, and take the Power Supply with the bracket out the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect all cable connectors, and put the new Power Supply on the floor.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Measure the voltage on the Test Point on the GTCB, and check that it is within +22.8VDC ~ +25.2VDC. If it falls outside these limits, adjust it by turning the potentiometer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC)

  1. Screw the new Power Supply with the bracket into the base of the Table.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Verify that the Table up/down and in/out functions are operating normally.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-install the Table covers.
