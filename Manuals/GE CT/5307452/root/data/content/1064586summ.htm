Axial Dynamic Braking Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive and HVDC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Rotate the gantry to position the tube at about the 6 o'clock position such that you can look in from the front of the gantry and have easy access to the back of the dynamic brake assembly.

  1. Turn OFF the 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Turn off HVDC. Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before servicing components.

  1. Perform all required LOTO activities to remove all power to the gantry.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and rear cover.

  1. Remove the gantry tilting assembly bottom cover.

  1. Remove the left side tilting safety panel next to the dynamic brake module by removing the screws using a 5 mm hex wrench.

  1. Remove the safety panel mount bracket from the dynamic brake module by removing the two screws. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen four (4) captive cover screws located on the side metal cover of the dynamic brake assembly to access the one cable connection inside the assembly. Reference Illustration 1.

  1. Remove all cable connections from the assembly and any brackets holding cables to the assembly

  1. Remove the brake assembly as follows.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the Axial Dynamic braking module in reverse order, having the second person hold the assembly while installing the two screws. Then install the third screw from the front of the gantry.

  1. Torque the three mounting screws to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure all cable connections are connected including the one internal to the module. Reference Illustration 1.

  1. Install the safety cover mount bracket to the dynamic brake.

  1. Install the gantry tilting assembly base cover and the tilting safety cover next to the braking module.

  1. Install the gantry rear cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove LOTO and restore power to system.

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.
