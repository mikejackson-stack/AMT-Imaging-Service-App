Heat Exchanger Compensation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR BURNS.
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR VIA BURNS FROM CONTACT WITH HOT OIL.
DO NOT SERVICE WHILE TUBE AND OIL IS HOT (SEE REQUIRED CONDITION BELOW).

LARGE AMOUNTS OF AIR IN THE TUBE HEAT EXCHANGER CAN CAUSE IQ ARTIFACTS OR SCAN ABORTS.

NEVER OPEN THE OIL COMPENSATION TOOL'S VALVE WHILE IT IS CONNECTED TO THE GANTRY.

2  Required Conditions

3  Procedure

3.1   Apparatus Preparation

  1. With apparatus valve in open state, push in piston until it stops, forcing air out of apparatus.

  1. Attach hose onto apparatus valve spout.

  1. Insert open end of hose into a filled Transformer Oil container.

  1. Slowly pull apparatus piston back as far as you can to fill cylinder with oil

    (Step Details)

NOTE:The piston handle may need to be twisted slightly while pulling it back.

  1. Tip apparatus so that piston handle is up, forcing any air to rise to top of cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. If there is air present in cylinder, complete the following steps to bleed air from cylinder.

    1. Lay apparatus flat. The air will rise to valve area by the sight glass.

    1. Push piston all the way in again, forcing out all air and liquid, then slowly pull piston back.

    (Step Details)

  1. With cylinder full and air free, close valve tightly.

  1. Hold apparatus above oil container, and slowly pull hose off end of apparatus, drain oil into container. Wipe any residual oil from apparatus with dry wipes.

3.2   Preliminary Step

Potential for Personal Injury
An energized gantry presents mechanical (rotational) and electrical hazards.

If this procedure execution is NOT due to another Service Procedure, complete the following preparation steps.
 
 

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry so tube rests at 3 o'clock or 4 o'clock positions.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Remove tube oil pump to tube quick-disconnect safety mechanism bolts and uncouple the quick-disconnect.

3.3   Tube Heat Exchanger Bellows Compensation

Apparatus can be used on oil-based systems only.
Use of this apparatus on water-based cooled systems (blue hoses) will cause unknown system damage.

  1. Connect apparatus male disconnect to tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Connect apparatus female disconnect to tube male disconnect. The handle should angle upward.

    (Step Details)

Never open the apparatus valve while it is connected to the gantry.

  1. Keeping handle higher than rest of apparatus, push down piston's handle until it stops. This fills the heat exchanger and bellows with oil, while keeping any small traces of air at the top of the cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. With bellows full of oil, note volume of oil remaining in apparatus cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slowly pull back on apparatus handle so that 30 units of oil are removed from heat exchanger bellows. Use bottom of piston (where oil and piston meet) as measurement reference line.

    (Step Details)

  1. Uncouple apparatus male disconnect from tube oil pump female disconnect.

  1. Uncouple apparatus female disconnect from tube male disconnect.

  1. Connect tube oil pump female disconnect to tube male disconnect.

4  Finalization

  1. Empty apparatus syringe, clean apparatus and prepare for air shipment/storage.

  1. If procedure execution was due to another service procedure, return to associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform the following steps:

    1. Restore tube oil pump to tube quick disconnect safety mechanism. Torque M6 bolts to the following values:

      (Step Details)

    1. Disengage gantry rotational lock.

    1. Install gantry front cover, top covers, left side cover, and scan window.

    1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] , and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

    1. Press [E-STOP RESET] on Service Switch panel. Wait until scanning hardware is successfully reset.

    1. Install gantry right side cover.
