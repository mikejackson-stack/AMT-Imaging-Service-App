3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove the Old Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball

  1. Remove the USB cable connection from the Console’s Upper Bulkhead Panel for the Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball being replaced.

  1. Remove the Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball and set aside.

3.3   Install the Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball

  1. Place the replacement Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball on the Operator Console.

  1. Plug in Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball cable in the USB connector at the Rear Bulkhead Panel.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

3.5   Verify Replacement Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball Operation

  1. Visually verify Keyboard, Mouse, or Trackball work as expected with the CT Applications.

  1. No other verifications required.

    (Step Details)
