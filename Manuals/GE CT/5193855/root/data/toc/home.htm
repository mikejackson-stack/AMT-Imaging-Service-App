5193855 - LS 5.X RT Limited Access Service Information (Adv)

The information contained in this publication applies to ALL LightSpeed 5.X RT systems (both Limited Access Compliant and non-Limited Access Compliant). Non-Limited Access Compliant systems can not be upgraded to a Limited Access Compliant system. Direction 5113141-800 (Class A) and Direction 5113142-800 (Class C) for LightSpeed 5.X RT non-Limited Access Compliant systems are now obsolete.
