Axial Encoder Assembly Replacement (Limited Access)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

1  Procedure

  1. Remove all covers except front cover.

  1. Rotate gantry by hand until space is available to access the encoder.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Tilt gantry 30° to the back.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Perform all required LOTO actions.

  1. Remove encoder from the back of the gantry.

  1. Disconnect the Encoder DB-9 pin connect from the gantry harness. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully cut tie-wraps as necessary.

  1. Using the 8 mm hex key, remove the shoulder screw on which the encoder assembly pivots.

  1. Install in reverse order.

  1. Reset the C-Pulse (see Resetting the C-Pulse).

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Axial Encoder Check, located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Perform Axial Control Functional, located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
