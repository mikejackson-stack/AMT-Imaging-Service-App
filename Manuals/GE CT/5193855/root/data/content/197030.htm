2  Overview

Verify that the internal mA meter are functional. If the meter is not functional, the inverter cannot be used in the system. A summary of the steps to be performed follows:

  • Connect meter to test points and a load resistor for the tube current

  • Perform x-ray exposures

  • Take measurements

3.2  Safety

X-ray Exposure.
The procedure described in this document requires performing x-ray exposures, unlike the Meter Verification procedure for Pro16, which has a function to verify the internal mA and kV meters without performing x-ray exposures.

4  Procedure

  1. Perform warm-up scans, if the x-ray tube is cold.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover if it's not already removed.

  1. Turn off all three service switches on the Service Switch Panel (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120VAC).

  1. Rotate the gantry such that the power unit is in the 2 O'clock position.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock. Ensure that gantry rotation is locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

  1. Remove the Inverter cover. If there are two covers on the inverter, remove the larger of the two, nearest the cradle.

  1. Remove the fan unit from the power unit. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 1:  Power Unit

    Illustration 2:  Power Unit, Fan Removed

  1. Attach a protective ESD wrist strap.

Potential for Equipment Damage. Failure to use protective ESD equipment may result in destroyed/damaged equipment.
 
 

  1. Connect a multi-meter across the load resister on the kV Measure board. See Illustration 3. Use the extender probe for connection.

    Illustration 3:  Multi-meter Connection

    NOTE:You do not have to measure the resistance of the load resistor and cables & probes of the multi-meter to calculate mA afterwards.

  1. Set the multi-meter to the DC voltage measurement mode (mV).

  1. Turn ON the 120VAC and HVDC ENABLE on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Wait about 2 minutes for the gantry display to reset. No flashing lights on the gantry display should indicate this.

  1. Clear the E-Stop condition by pressing the DRIVES RESET button on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. On the Service Desktop, select [DIAGNOSTICS] , then [X-RAY GENERATION] , then [KV & MA (X-RAY)] .

  1. Verify the following scan parameters:

    • X-Ray test type = manual

    • Gantry = Disabled

    • Exposure time = 2s

    • No of scans = 1

    • Focal Spot = Large

    • kV = 120kV

    • mA = 50mA

  1. Press RUN and take scan.

  1. Measure the output.

  1. Repeat the measurement for 200mA.

  1. The difference between mA measured (= [measured voltage (mV)] / 5 (ohms)) and the displayed mA on console should be within ±2% of the requested mA.

  1. Remove the multi-meter leads from the load resistor.

  1. Verify mA by measuring on test points:

    1. Set the multi-meter to the DC voltage measurement mode (mV).

    1. Connect red signal lead from meter to MA_N (TP20) and black common lead to MA_GND (TP19) on the CT Interface board. Reference HV Tank Feedback Resistor Verification (LS16, Ultra & Plus).

    1. Take the scan again (mA = 50mA) and walk to meter.

    1. Record the ending voltage on meter to one decimal place. If this accuracy cannot be obtained, change range on meter and redo scan.

    1. Multiply the reading by 100. (mA measured = measured voltage (mV) x 100) Verify that the difference between this value and the displayed mA on console is within ±2% of the requested mA.

    1. Repeat the measurement for 200mA.

5  Finalization

  1. Replace inverter and gantry covers if necessary.

  1. All the measurements should be within the limits.

  1. Record the results on the HHS data sheet.
