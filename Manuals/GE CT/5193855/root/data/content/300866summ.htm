MDAS -5 Volt Analog Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING COMPONENTS.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
NEVER SERVICE THE GANTRY WITH THE AXIAL DRIVE ENABLED.

1  Procedure

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

  1. Remove gantry right and left side covers.

  1. Remove gantry top covers.

  1. Tilt gantry back 30 degrees.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches on the Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry to access DAS power supplies.

  1. Disconnect the rotating power harness as required. Save all hardware for reuse.

Burn Hazard.
Power Supply may be hot to touch.
Use caution to prevent possible burn injury from hot surface contact.

  1. Remove the power supply’s clear plastic cover by removing four (4) each: 4mm cap screws, flat and lock washers.

  1. Disconnect the AC and DC connectors from the power supply.

  1. Remove the three (3) 10 mm cap screws that are used to mount the power supply to the detector plate. [Two screws are located at one end of the power supply assembly, and the third is at the other end.] Remove the power supply assembly from the gantry.

  1. Mount the new power supply using the three (3) 10 mm cap screws, flat and lock washers. Set torque on the screws to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the AC and DC connectors.

  1. Attach the plastic cover to the assembly, using 4mm cap screws, flat and lock washers. Set torque on the screws to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the rotating harness cables as before.

2  Finalization

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT.
FAILURE TO USE AN INSULATED VOLTAGE ADJUSTMENT “TWEAKER” CAN RESULT IN PERSONAL INJURY AND/OR COMPONENT DAMAGE.

  1. Adjust supply output, if necessary.
