4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the 12 screws in the TGP/MSUB cover.

  1. Remove the cover.

  1. Disconnect all cabling into the TGP.

  1. Remove the eight (8) screws in the TGP.

  1. Remove the defective TGP.

  1. Install the new TGP in the reverse order.

  1. Execute Flash download query and update. In case the TGP has a firmware later than OC, it may fail. In this case, execute the following NOTE:

    NOTE:Before powering ON the 120VAC Gantry power, open a shell and type the following to perform the FLASH download procedure so that TGP firmware is downloaded from the OC.

    cd /usr/g/fw [Enter]

    touch tgpFtp [Enter]
