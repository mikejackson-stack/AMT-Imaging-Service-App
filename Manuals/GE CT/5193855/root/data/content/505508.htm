2  Overview

This procedure troubleshoots issues with the Autovoice board.

Last Revised on:12 September 2007

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.

4  Procedure

NOTE:On all HP xw8xxx Host PCs, ensure that integrated audio is enabled in the PC's BIOS. If integrated audio is disabled in BIOS, Autovoice will not function.

  1. On desktop screen:

    1. Open Autovoice Volume and bring the audio volume to 100% for Right and Left.

    1. Select [Accept] then [Save and Quit] .

  1. Make sure the SCIM volume is all the way up to 9.

  1. Try autovoice again.

    1. If no success, remove console front cover and check if Intercom Box (ICOM) power LED is ON.

    1. If ICOM LED is OFF, check power cable connection.

    1. If power remains off, order ICOM box.

    1. If ICOM LED is ON, press Reset button on the Intercom Box (ICOM).

    1. Press POT button on ICOM until the LED is on for P_MAX (Patient Maximum).

    1. Press and hold Volume Up button for 5 seconds.

  1. Try autovoice again.

    1. If no success, open a Unix Shell.

    1. Type audioSetting<Enter>.

      1. In pop-up window, bring Volume, PCM, and Mic values to 100%.

      1. Select [Accept] then [Save and Quit] .

  1. Try autovoice again.

    1. If no success, does volume up and down work on SCIM?

    1. If NO: Order SCIM.

    1. If YES: Order ICOM box.

5  Finalization

  1. Reinstall all removed covers.
