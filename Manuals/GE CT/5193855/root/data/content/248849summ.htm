ISO Alignment Procedure (RT)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Overview

The geometry of ISO alignment is shown in Illustration 1. The following computational software are required for ISO alignment:

  1. Centroid calculation program as used by CT/I.

  1. ISO and CBF computation.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Procedure

  1. Launch the [ISO Alignment] tool from the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Perform Tube Installation Certification, if needed. Refer to SmarTube™ Setup.

  1. Execute Air scan (small spot).

  1. Execute Air scan (large spot).

  1. Place the 1/8 inch screw driver on the phantom holder (should be pointing into the Z direction).

  1. Execute pin scan (small spot).

  1. Execute pin scan (large spot).

  1. CALCULATE ISO center alignment.

  1. Mount dial indicators on the POR and ISO dial mounting brackets used for tube alignments.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the 4 M-12 bolts on the tube assembly about 1/2 turn.

  1. Adjust the tube UP / DOWN as indicated by the calculation. The gold adjustment bolt is located near the top of the tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Snug the four (4) M12 bolts and verify dial gauge still reads the correct adjustment value.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat steps 1 through 6.

  1. If the adjustments are within limit proceed to the next step, otherwise go to step 9.

  1. Tighten the M12 tube mounting bolts to the Pre-load torque specification. Refer to Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply final torque on all four M12 mounting bolts. Refer to Table 2.

    (Step Details)
