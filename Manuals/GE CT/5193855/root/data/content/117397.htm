3  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove rear gantry cover (see Gantry Rear Cover Removal and Re-install).

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel and follow Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove J1 and J2 cables.

  1. Remove hex screws holding the receiver to the antenna.

    Illustration 1:  Slipring Receiver

  1. Replace receiver only with a 5.x RT receiver. Receiver should be labeled as specific to RT (or Stargate) systems.

  1. Verify antenna gap and alignment adjustments, (see S-A HSDCD Slip Ring Adjustments)

4  Finalization

  1. Hardware Reset

  1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kV/40mA, 1000mm table movement)

  1. Acquire 100 axials: (120kV/80mA, 0.5 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire 1 helical: (120kV/40mA, 30 sec. Scan),

  1. Acquire 10 axial scans: (120kV/400mA, 4 sec. Scan)

  1. Verify NO increase in corrected or uncorrected FEC errors. To check HSDCD Statistics: Select Log Viewing, System Browser, Run Time Statistics, DIP Statistics.
