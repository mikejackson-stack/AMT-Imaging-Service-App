PM Schedule - LightSpeed 5.x

These schedules are for LightSpeed 5.x only, as approved by CT Engineering. LightSpeed 5.x PMs are based on gantries doing 2 million revolutions per year.

NOTE:Last Revised: 25 January, 2007

NOTE:The PM is based on (4) service cycles per year at 500k revolution intervals.

  • Schedule A - PM cycle begins when Rev Counter shows xxx500k revs.

  • Schedule B - When Rev Counter increments another xxx500k revs.

  • Schedule C - When Rev Counter increments another xxx500k revs.

  • Schedule D - When Rev Counter increments another xxx500k revs.

NOTE:PM time without camera (includes time allocation for Gantry cover handling):

  • Schedule A - 2.0 hours

  • Schedule B - 2.0 hours

  • Schedule C - 2.0 hours

  • Schedule D - 3.2 hours

NOTE:** This step applies only to systems with filters on the DAS duct fan assembly.
