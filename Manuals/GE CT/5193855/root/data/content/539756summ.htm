1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR BURNS.
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR VIA BURNS FROM CONTACT WITH HOT OIL.
Do not service while tube and oil are hot (See required condition, below).

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

No X-Ray exposures have been made for AT LEAST one (1) hour. Tube casing and oil hoses are cool to the touch.

3.3   Procedure

  1. Remove the reservoir cap and place funnel into tank. Place rags around funnel to capture any spilled oil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Fill reservoir with approximately 1 US gallon of transformer oil, part number T0552G.

    (Step Details)

Prior to starting the air removal procedure, air must be removed from the large blue hose. The objective of the following steps is to clear the air from the large blue hose, prior to pumping oil into the tube oil circuit.
 
 

  1. Connect the female and male disconnects as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug in the power supply.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON the power to the pump by actuating the rocker switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. When all air is out of the large blue hose (~ 30 seconds), shut OFF the pump, and disconnect the quick disconnects.

Potential for Personal Injury.
An Energized Gantry Presents Mechanical (Rotational) and Electrical Hazards.
Prior to continuing this procedure, turn OFF all 3 service switches on the Service Switch Panel (120 VAC, Axial Drive Enable, HVDC Enable).

  1. Remove the bolts that hold the quick disconnect collars in place around the disconnects, at locations A and B (already removed at location B). Do this with the gantry rotated into a convenient position to do the work, tube at 1-2 o’clock.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the bracket that holds the tube hose in a coiled position and straighten the hose.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the quick disconnects at locations A and B.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Air removal will be done on the components separately: first the tube, and then the pump and heat exchanger together.

  1. Connect the female disconnect from the air removal kit to the tube male disconnect at location A (see Illustration 7).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the male disconnect from the air removal kit to the tube female disconnect at location B (see Illustration 7).

  1. Plug in the power supply for the air removal kit.

  1. Ensure the air removal kit reservoir is at least 1/2 full.

  1. Turn ON the air removal kit pump using the rocker on/off switch. Keep the pump running for the duration of Step 15 through Step 17.

  1. While periodically raising the clear oil return line, allow the air removal pump to run for 1-2 minutes.

    (Step Details)

  1. With the gantry unlocked, gently rotate the gantry so that the tube is in approximately the 4-5 o’clock position. Hold this position for 1-2 minutes. Again periodically raise the return line to allow air to escape.

  1. Rotate the gantry so that the tube is in approximately the 7-8 o’clock position. Hold this position for 1-2 minutes, raising the return line periodically.

Potential for personal injury or equipment damage.
Gantry rotation creates potentially hazardous pinch points.
When rotating the gantry, be careful to avoid pinch points that may cause personal injury or damage to the unsecured hoses.

  1. Repeat Step 16 and Step 17 a minimum of three (3) times. However, if air continues to be removed, keep repeating until air is no longer being visibly removed.

  1. Turn OFF power to the air removal pump.

  1. Remove the air removal quick disconnects from the tube.

  1. Cut the tie wraps holding the pump hose in the heat exchanger channel, shown in E. Be careful not to cut the hose.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure the air removal kit reservoir is at least 1/2 full.

  1. Connect the air removal kit to the disconnects of the oil pump and heat exchanger as shown in C and D.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat Step 14 through Step 18, applied to the heat exchanger.

  1. When air removal process is complete, the heat exchanger bellows is filled to capacity. Therefore, the coolant circuit must be compensated according to the Heat Exchanger Compensation (VCT, Pro) procedure.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform the Hercules Coolant Flow Rate Measurement procedure.
