MDAS 5 Volt Logic Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING COMPONENTS.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
NEVER SERVICE THE GANTRY WITH THE AXIAL DRIVE ENABLED.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Remove +5 volt analog power supply.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

  1. Remove gantry right and left side covers.

  1. Remove gantry top covers.

  1. Tilt gantry back 30 degrees.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches on the Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry to access DAS power supplies.

Burn Hazard.
Power Supply may be hot to touch.
Use caution to prevent possible burn injury from hot surface contact.

  1. Disconnect the rotating power harness as required. Save all hardware for reuse.

  1. Remove the + 5 vdc analog power supply.

  1. Disconnect the AC and DC leads at the power supply terminal strips.

  1. Remove the three (3) 4 mm cap screws, flat and lock washers securing the power supply to the detector plate.

  1. Remove the four (4) 4 mm countersunk screws.

  1. Install mounting plate and four (4) 4 mm countersunk screws onto new power supply. Torque to 1.4 N-m (12.4 lbs-in).

  1. Install the new power supply using three (3) 4 mm cap screws, flat and lock washers. Torque to 2.3 N-m (20.4 lbs-in).

  1. Reconnect the AC and DC leads to the power supply terminal strip.

  1. Install the + 5 vdc analog power supply.

  1. Secure the rotating power harness as before.

4  Finalization

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT.
FAILURE TO USE AN INSULATED VOLTAGE ADJUSTMENT “TWEAKER” CAN RESULT IN PERSONAL INJURY AND/OR COMPONENT DAMAGE.

  1. Adjust supply output, if necessary.
