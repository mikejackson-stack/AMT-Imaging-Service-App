4  Procedure

  1. Remove side, top, and back gantry covers.

    NOTE:The top covers have three fans, making them heavier than the covers on previous CT scanners.

    The top cover has a guide rail on each side to aid in cover removal.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

    NOTE:The power supply is located behind the TGPu assembly ( Illustration 1.) The Phillips-head screws holding the power supply in place are located on the side of the assembly.

    Illustration 1:  TGPU Power Supply Location

  1. Power down the console and Lockout/Tagout.

  1. Remove the gantry front cover.

  1. Remove the 4 screws that hold the power supply in place ( Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:  Power Supply Screw Locations

  1. Disconnect the leads and replace the power supply.

  1. Remove the defective power supply.

  1. Carefully lower the new power supply into place and install the Phillips-head screws.

  1. Reconnect the power leads.

    NOTE:All leads are labeled.

  1. Reconnect any cables that may have been removed.

5  Finalization

  1. Remove the A1 LOTO.

  1. Turn ON the gantry service switches and power up the console.

  1. With a DVM, check the voltage on the supply.

  1. Install all removed gantry covers.

    NOTE:Reconnect all top cover fan connections. Ensure that no cables are left in the rotating path. Return dollies to storage areas.

  1. Retest the system.
