Table Base Cover Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the right Base Cover before the left.

  1. Turn each of the four captive Dzus fasteners one-quarter turn CCW, to release the cover.

  1. Turn each of the four captive Dzus fasteners one-quarter turn CW, to fasten the cover.
