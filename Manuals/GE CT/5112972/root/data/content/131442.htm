CTPM-1320 (Clean Tube Radiator Filter)

2  Overview

 Procedure Effectivity:

Assembly:GANTRY
Subassembly:X-Ray Tube
Purpose:Clean or Replace the Tube Radiator Filter
Last Revised:October 2003
NOTE:This procedure is important to maintain the cooling capacity for the tube radiator.

4  Procedure

  1. Turn OFF all power (Gantry Rotation, 550VDC Enable, and 120VAC Power) at the Gantry Service Switch.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the radiator filter.

    NOTE:There are two filters on the Performix Gemini style of tube. Vacuum both filters on this style tube.
