CTPM-0401 (Replace MOV on Transformer)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTE:These MOV's should be replaced annually due to the difficulty in troubleshooting them for an open circuit failure and due to their breakdown over time. They are easier to replace than to test. However, anyone interested in testing them for circuit loading rather than open circuit may follow this procedure.

  1. To test for shorted MOV's, do the following:

    1. Turn off the x-ray drives; remove the XFMR cover.

    1. Perform an exposure from Gencal at 80 kV/300 mA/4 sec. Note the mA reading on the Gantry display.

    1. Turn off the x-ray drives.

    1. Referring to Illustration 1 for location of MOV's, disconnect the red MOV'S from mA+ and mA- terminals.

      (Step Details)

    1. Perform a second exposure from GENCAL (reset first) at 80 kV/300 mA/4 sec. Note mA reading on the Gantry. If reading differs from previous exposure by more than 3mA, then the MOV'S are partially shorted.

  1. To replace the defective MOV's, do the following:

    1. Refer to Illustration 1 for location.

    1. Turn off the x-ray drives.

    1. Remove the XFMR cover.

    1. Remove red MOV'S from mA+ and mA- terminals.

    1. Install the new set.

    1. Power up and perform exposure from Gencal at 80 kV/300 mA/4 sec. Note the mA reading on Gantry. It should be within 3mA of the exposure taken with MOV'S disconnected.

  1. Replace the covers and return to normal operation.
