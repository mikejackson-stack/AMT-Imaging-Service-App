CTPM-0708 (Check I.S., Cradle, Elevation Belt)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove Table Covers

  1. Remove the table covers to access Table Gantry Generator Control (TGGC).

1.2   Reset TGGC

  1. Reset TGGC by pushing reset on the CPU board. Place the table in home position.

1.3   I/S Pot Check

  1. Locate TP13 (IS POS) and TP21 (AGND) on the A4 board in the TGGC card cage. With a DVM, connect the “+” lead to TP13 and the “-” lead to TP21. The meter should measure -0.5VDC +/- 0.05 volts.

1.4   Cradle Pot Check

  1. Connect the “+” meter lead to A4TP14 (Long POS) and the “-” lead on A4TP21. The meter should measure -0.2VDC +/- 0.05 volts.

1.5   Elevation Pot Check

  1. Raise the table to maximum elevation using the “slow up” button on the table control panel. Connect “+” meter lead on A4TP12 (Elev POS) and the “-” lead on A4TP21. The meter should measure -4.61VDC +/- 0.05 volts. Remove the meter.

1.6   I.S. Drive Belt Check

  1. Locate the I.S. pot drive belt and inspect it for splitting or cracking. Press on it and check for ¼-inch deflection.

1.7   Elevation Drive Belt Check

  1. Locate the Elevation pot drive belt and inspect it for splitting or cracking. Press on it and check for ¼-inch deflection.
