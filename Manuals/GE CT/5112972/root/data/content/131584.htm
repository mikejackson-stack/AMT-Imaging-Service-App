3  Procedure

NOTE:A tube warm-up is performed automatically.

  1. Run Auto mA Cal using the following sequence:

    1. Touch [Utilities] .

    1. Touch [1,2,3] .

    1. Touch [CT Tools] .

    1. Touch [Generator Charact] .

    1. Touch [.Auto mA Cal] .

  1. Press Start Scan when illuminated; the program will now automatically run the following:

    • Ductility warm-up

    • Auto mA Cal

  1. The system will record the final filament currents on the plasma.
