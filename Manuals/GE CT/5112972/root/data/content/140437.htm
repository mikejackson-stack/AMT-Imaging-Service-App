4  Procedure

  1. Inspect Xenon fill bag (see Illustration 1). It should not be damaged. Check the seams, check for holes, etc.

    Illustration 1:  Bag Inspection

  1. Inspect patient hoses (see Illustration 2). They should be free of damage.

    Illustration 2:  Hose Inspection

  1. Open top of unit under Xenon fill bag. Inspect all hoses or loose clamps that could cause a leak.
