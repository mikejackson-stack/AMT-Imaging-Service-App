4  Procedure

  1. Inspect the operation of the three fans at the top of the PDU and of the servo fan, and verify normal and quiet operation.

    NOTE:Replace the fans that have developed audible noise or that have stopped functioning.

    • For a noisy or failed fan in the Allen Bradley Servo Amp (46-297910P1), the entire amplifier will need to be replaced.

    • For a noisy or failed fan in the West Amp Servo Amp (46-296136P1), order and replace the fan (46-229909P1).
