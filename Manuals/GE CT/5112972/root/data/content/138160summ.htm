CTPM-1506 (Inspect Transformer Lamps)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. If any of the six green indicator lamps on the transformer assembly are out, a transient may have opened F1-F6, or the lamp is open. Replace the fuse and/or lamp as required. Refer to Illustration 1 for the location of the lamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. These lights may be manually inspected, as the system can continue to function without transient protection.
