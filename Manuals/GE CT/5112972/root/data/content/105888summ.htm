CTPM-0204 (Vacuum Console Interior)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Locate the console on/off power switch (see Illustration 1) and turn the console off.

  1. Remove the front lower console panel.

  1. Swing open the hinged circuit boards to gain access to the console interior.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Remove the console top rear cover.

  1. Vacuum both the monitor area and the console interior using care not to disturb wiring or bump any adjustments.

  1. Re-install the console panels and turn the console power back on.

  1. Re-enable the alpha lock on the keyboard.
