4  Procedure

4.1  Table Upper Assembly

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT.
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

NOTE:For best access, fully elevate the table.

  1. Remove both the top table side covers.

    1. Locate two fasteners on bottom of surface of cover, and turn ccw 1/4 of a turn; fasteners are not captive.

    1. Lift Cover up to disengage pins and control panel, and then outward.

    1. If needed, jump the side cover tape switch connection by removing the jumper plug from its storage position on the bracket and plug it into the Tape Switch Harness Connector.

      NOTE:Note that the jumper plug must be removed from the harness connector and returned to its storage position before the side cover can be refitted.

  1. Remove the cradle drive cover.

    1. Loosen and keep the two thumb screws.

    1. Unthread the two captive knurled screws holding cover in place, and slide the front of the cover, off the pins.

  1. Install and tighten the calibration pin in the home position to hold the carriage in place.

  1. Remove the cradle.

    1. Verify that the cradle/carriage is at home position and latched before removing the cradle.

      NOTE:Failure to latch the cradle/carriage could cause damage to the encoder assembly.

    1. Remove and keep the six plug buttons and six screws.

    1. Lift the cradle upward to remove from the table.

4.2  Inspect Cradle for Adhesive Tape / Verify Operation

  1. Inspect the underside of cradle for adhesive tape.

  1. Remove the adhesive with a cloth and alcohol.

  1. Clean cradle catch pan / clean cradle drive cover.

4.3  Inspect Rails and Carriage

  1. Remove rail covers.

  1. Inspect rails for peeling or debris. Clean if necessary, trying to preserve some grease. Spread grease over areas that have been cleaned.

  1. Inspect the cam followers and v-rollers for side play or excessive wear.

    • Replace the upper cam followers and/or v-rollers as needed.

    • Replace the carriage assembly with lower rollers as needed.

  1. Re-install the rail covers.

4.4  Check Cradle Latch

  1. Inspect latch and adjust if needed. (See Illustration 1).

    1. Position carriage at "home" position with calibration pin; tighten the pin.

    1. Adjust the position of the Latch Assembly in its slots, so that the distance between the carriage latch block and the forward edge of the latch bar opening is .050 +/- .005; tighten the four screws.

    1. Adjust the set screw in the latch clevis block, so that the outer edge of the latch bar overlaps the outer edge of the carriage latch block by .050 +/- .005; tighten the lock nut.

    1. Adjust the position of the solenoid bracket in slots, so that the clearance between the outer edge of the carriage latch block is .050 +/- .005, with solenoid plunger fully bottomed; tighten two screws.

    1. Adjust the position of the spring bracket, so that the spring is extended .125 +/- .010 beyond its free length with the latch bar against the set screw; tighten the two screws.

    Illustration 1:  Inspecting the Cradle Latch

4.5  Check Cradle Drive Release Solenoid

  1. Inspect cradle drive release mechanism. (See Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Inspecting the Cradle Drive Release Mechanism

  1. Adjust/replace as necessary.

4.6  Check Cradle Encoder Assembly

  1. Remove encoder assembly cover.

  1. Inspect the assembly for the following:

    • Loose set screws

    • Worn/old belt

    • Wire cable engagement in spool

    • Kinks or frays in wire cable

    • Debris in assembly

DO NOT attempt to replace or repair the wire cable or encoder. Replace the entire encoder assembly.
 
 

  1. Reinstall encoder assembly cover.

4.7  Reinstall the Cradle

  1. Position the rear end of the cradle over the carriage and align the screw holes. Install one bolt, but do not tighten.

  1. Looking through the gantry bore at the end of the table, position the cradle laterally on the drive rollers so that the same gap exists between the sloped sides of the cradle and the guide rollers.

  1. Tighten six screws to 13ft-lbs (1.8m-kg) and replace the plug buttons.

  1. Make sure that the cradle/carriage is not resting against the latch.

  1. Return the calibration pin to its storage position.

4.8  Check Limit Switches

  1. Check the limit switches for proper operating condition.

    1. Longitudinal Limit Switch: Verify that it actuates when the cradle/carriage is at its maximum travel position, as determined by the calibration pin.

    1. Home Position Limit Switch: Verify that it actuates when the carriage is 0.50 in +/- .03 out of home position, as determined by the calibration pin.

  1. Adjust as necessary or replace switches that are defective. Adjust the position of the switches in the bracket, if needed, by using their corresponding two nuts.

  1. Verify the cradle operation. Make sure that the calibration pin is in its storage position.

  1. Reinstall the cradle drive cover.

  1. Reinstall the table side covers.

    1. To refit the side cover, move the cover inward toward table making sure the control panel is aligned with its opening. When pin begins to engage table, move the floating control panel until it pops through opening making sure that the rubber panel is not caught on the edges of the opening.

    1. Install the two fasteners and turn cw 1/4 of a turn.

    1. Test Tape Switch to insure that PDU Contactor is opened when tape switch is pressed.
