4  Procedure

  1. Turn power off before moving drives.

  1. Push optical disk filter grill latch to the side and remove filter ( Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  OPTICAL & MAGNETIC DISK DRIVES

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the filter.

  1. Re-install the filter and the filter grill.

  1. Turn on both the drives and verify that the fans operate.
