CTPM-5201 (3M Laser Camera Maintenance)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

Do not use alcohol pads on suction cups or rollers.
 
 

  1. Clean the suction cups. Refer to Illustration 1.

    1. Lift the optics module. There is a latch release on the right side of the module.

    1. Use a lint free cloth dampened with water and clean the suction cups.

    (Step Details)

  1. Clean the transport plate with alcohol pads from cleaning kit. Allow the transport plate to dry.

  1. Clean the transport plate.

    NOTE:If the camera is docked to a non-3M processor (i.e., Kodak), there will be a docking unit to clean. If there is no docking unit skip to next step.

    1. Open the docking unit cover with the two knobs located on either side.

    1. Clean the docking unit stainless steel transport plate using alcohol pads found in the 3M cleaning kit.

    1. Allow plate to air dry. Close the docking unit cover.

  1. Close the optics module.

  1. Run cleaning film and inspect magazines. Place the docking unit lever in the magazine position.

    1. Press the load button to access the supply magazine.

    1. Press the unload button to access the receive magazine.

    The supply magazine may have films in it. Take it to a darkroom to remove the un-used films. Do not use a red safe light with infrared film.
     
     

    1. Inspect supply and receive magazines for damage. Repair kit #46-266975P183.

    1. Peel the protective paper from the cleaning film, which is found in the 3M cleaning kit. Place it in the supply magazine with the adhesive side up.

    1. Place the supply magazine back into the camera.

    1. Press the Density Test button on the local panel as shown in Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. When the ready light comes on, the cleaning film is in the receive magazine.

    1. Press the receive unload button and remove the cleaning film from the receive magazine.

    1. Check to see that the adhesive side of the film is still tacky. If it is not, repeat the previous five steps with a new cleaning film until it comes out tacky. If the camera is docked to a processor, put the docking unit lever back into the processor position.

Potential for equipment damage.

Using vacuum hose near live electronic equipment may cause equipment damage due to static.

Make sure equipment is turned off prior to vacuuming near electronic equipment.

 
 

  1. Clean filter and vacuum interior.

    1. Open both magazine drawers and remove the two front cover screws on each side.

    1. Slide the front cover to the right to remove it.

    1. Vacuum the interior as needed.

    1. Replace the front cover and vacuum the filter on the bottom of the cover (filter number 46-266975P78).

  1. Clean the exterior panels. Use an alcohol pad and a lint-free cloth to clean any dirt on the exterior panels.
