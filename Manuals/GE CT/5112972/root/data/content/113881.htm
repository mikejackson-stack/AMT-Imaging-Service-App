4  Procedure

4.1  Transtector

  1. Verify that the three red LED's on the transtector are lit.

4.2  Power TEK Suppression Filter

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
POWER IS ON! UNIT MAY BE WIRED BEFORE MAIN DISCONNECT, SO DO NOT ASSUME POWER IS OFF IF MAIN 480V BREAKER IS OFF.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

  1. Using a clamp-on ammeter, measure the AC current through each loop (See Illustration 1). Compare to original reading documented from installation. If no record of previous documentation can be found, start one now.

  1. If any readings are grossly out of range from previous readings, schedule replacement of appropriate module.

    Illustration 1:  Power Tek and Transtector
