CTPM-0302 (Inspect Stop Ends)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove both the Gantry side covers.

  1. Turn off the scan drive switch.

  1. Rotate Gantry CCW until the right side E-hook activates ( Illustration 1).

  1. Continue rotating. Verify that the hook stops the Gantry rotation.

  1. Rotate Gantry CW until the left side E-hook activates.

  1. Continue rotating and verify that the hook stops Gantry rotation.

  1. Return the system to normal operation.
