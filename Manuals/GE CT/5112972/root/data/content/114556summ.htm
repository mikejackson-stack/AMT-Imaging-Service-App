CTPM-0805 (Clean Coarse Filter)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Bring system down - cancel to “R", then type release DZ0<CR>.

  1. Turn off the spindle power on drive.

  1. Open the front cover.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Remove and clean the coarse filter from its compartment in the front cover. Use the vacuum cleaner.

  1. Check the condition of the foam seal around the blower.

  1. Re-install the filter in the same direction of air flow and reassemble the system.
