NOTE:The brushes will be “worn in” and will need to be put back in their original holder with the original orientation.

Measure the length of each brush. Record the length of the shortest brush and the date inspected on the motor. The brushes should be replaced when the length of the brush is less than 6.5mm (1/4 inch). If replacement is required, replace all brushes at the same time. Special attention should be paid to the copper braid on the brush. If this is frayed or broken, replace the brush. Failure to do so will result in damage to the brush holder and the motor will have to be replaced.
