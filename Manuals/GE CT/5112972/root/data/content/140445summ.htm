CTPM-5103 (Clean Metal, Rubber Rollers)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Cleaning the Metal Roller Set

Refer to Illustration 1 for the location of the first, second, and third metal roller sets.

  1. Remove the send and receive magazines from the autoloader.

  1. Dampen a section of soft lint-free cloth with water and remove any dust or debris from each roller.

    (Step Details)

  1. Dry the roller completely with a dry section of cloth. Use isopropyl alcohol to remove grease and other tough stains.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Cleaning the Rubber Roller Set

The fourth rubber roller set feeds the exposed film into the receive magazine. Refer to Illustration 2 for the exact location of the fourth rollers.

  1. Remove the send and receive magazines from the autoloader.

  1. Dampen a section of soft lint-free cloth with water and remove any dust or debris from each roller.

    (Step Details)

  1. Dry the roller completely with a dry section of cloth. Use a mild liquid detergent solution to remove grease and other tough stains.
