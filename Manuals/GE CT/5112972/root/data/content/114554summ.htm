CTPM-0804 (Inspect Foam Deterioration)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Bring down the system - cancel to “R" level then type release DZ0<CR>. You should get the “!" prompt.

  1. Turn off spindle power and wait for pack to stop spinning.

  1. Open disk pack compartment and inspect the condition of the foam seals.

  1. If the compartment has a glass window on top, inspect its rubber seal and clips that hold it in place.

  1. If any repair, replacement, or cleaning needs to be done, first remove the disk pack.

  1. If any work is performed, after re-installing the disk pack, disable servo on the drive logic board, power-up the spindle, and purge the chamber for three minutes.
