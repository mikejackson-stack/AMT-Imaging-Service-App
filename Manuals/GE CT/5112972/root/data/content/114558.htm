4  Procedure

  1. Bring down the system. Cancel to “R", then type release DZ0<CR>.

  1. Power down disk drive spindle. Turn off the power.

  1. Remove the front cover.

  1. Loosen the two mounting screws for the discharge brush so the brush hangs down. Do not bend the brush or it will not make the proper connection. (See Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Cleaning the Static Discharge Brush

  1. Clean the brush with alcohol and wipe dry.

  1. Re-tighten the brush mounting screws.

  1. Restore the DC power and replace the front cover.
