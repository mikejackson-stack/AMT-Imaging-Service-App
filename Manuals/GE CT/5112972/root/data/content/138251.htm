4  Procedure

  1. Check the following five fans:

    1. Rear Scan chassis Fan (1)

    1. Fans underneath the Scan chassis (2)

    1. Fans on top of ST-CTC AP (2)

  1. Repair or replace any fans that make unusual noise.

  1. Clean the dirty fans with a cloth and water.
