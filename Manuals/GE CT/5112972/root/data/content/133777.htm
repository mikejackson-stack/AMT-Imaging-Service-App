4  Procedure

  1. Display the Generator Characterization menu

  1. Toggle the softkey [Monitor Enable] to ON, to display the scan time in the message log.

  1. In the OBC, connect a scope to the kV board, as follows:

    • Channel 1, Exposure Command (EXCM, TP22). Scope ground to (LGND, TP6) [TP3]. 2v/div.

    • Channel 2, Total kV (KVTB, TP30) [TP11]. Scope ground to (AGND, TP39) [SIG, TP12]. 1v/div.

    • Set the Scope Time base to 200msec, positive trigger.

  1. Use DDC to take a stationary, 1.0 sec, 0mm, 100kV, 40mA, Large Focal Spot scan.

  1. Record the measured scan time from the oscilloscope and the displayed scan time from the message log. Spec limits are as follows:

    NOTE:Scope Exposure Duration = 0.96 to 1.04 s. Displayed Exposure Duration = 0.99 to 1.02 s.

  1. Display the Generator Characterization menu.

  1. Toggle the softkey [Monitor Enable] OFF to stop the scan time display in the message log.

    NOTE:Failure to turn the [Monitor Enable] OFF results in the system message log filling with exposure information.

  1. Disconnect the scope from the kV board.

  1. Replace the OBC cover.
