CTPM-0103 (Check Argus Bad Block Log)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Bring down system by releasing DA0.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For S140 CPU) Mount DGC diags, Ades tape (46-237845G2) and set proper tape density on drive.

  1. (For MV 7800 CPU) Mount MV Adex tape (46-266498G2) and set proper density on tape drive.

  1. Boot ADES Tape by typing B22<Enter>, then continue per Illustration 1. (Menus may vary slightly).

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot and run 6239_FMTR per Illustration 2 and Illustration 3. (Menus may vary slightly).

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the error log is empty, i.e. there should be no cylinder numbers listed.

  1. If blocks are found, service time will be required to re-map these errors or re-format this disk, depending upon the information found under the H/S status word. It should not be performed during the PM time.

  1. Boot system back up from DA0 and check for normal operation.
