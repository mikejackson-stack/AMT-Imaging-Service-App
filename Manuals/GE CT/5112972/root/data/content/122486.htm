4  Procedure

  1. Be sure that the console power is turned off.

  1. Remove the pedestal front door.

  1. Swing out the panel with security board to get access to the front panel of the console power modules.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Clean / vacuum the console interior.
