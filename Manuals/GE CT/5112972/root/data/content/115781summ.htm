CTPM-1002 (Check X-Ray Lights (HHS))

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Queue up axials.

    (Step Details)

  1. Look for any observable delays in light turn-on time during scanning, or any light flickering or dropouts during x-ray on time.

  1. Make sure each of the four lights goes on and off for the duration of the axial scan.

  1. Queue up short scout (20mm).
