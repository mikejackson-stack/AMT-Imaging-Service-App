CTPM-0304 (Verify Heater Operation)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the right side cover of Gantry and turn off scan drive switch.

  1. Rotate Gantry as needed to view the highlight detector heater ( Illustration 1).

  1. Observe that the “Heater On" led cycles on periodically ( Illustration 1). This indicates that power is applied to the heater elements.

  1. After verification, return system to normal operation.
