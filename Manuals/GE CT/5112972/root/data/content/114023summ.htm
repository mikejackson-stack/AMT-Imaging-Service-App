CTPM-0706 (Inspect Encoder Cable)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the table side covers and I.S. end cover to gain viewing access to the encoder cables and idlers. (See Illustration 1).

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

  1. Inspect the entire path of the I.S. and cradle encoder cables for fraying, bends, or other damage.

  1. Move the table in and out of the Gantry and ensure that the cables and idlers move smoothly.

  1. Replace the covers.
