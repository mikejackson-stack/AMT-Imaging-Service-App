4  Procedure

4.1  Air Inlets and Exhaust Ports

  1. Verify that all air inlets and exhaust ports are clean and clear of dirt and obstructions.

    NOTE:It is important to leave the VME slot cover plates and slot air restriction baffle plates in all empty VME rack slots. This helps direct the cooling airflow across the active VME boards.

4.2  VME Rack Exhaust

  1. Make sure to allow 6-8" clearance from the left of the computer and any room walls, or other equipment, to allow breathing space.

4.3  SCSI Tray

  1. Clean the exhaust holes in the tray chassis.
