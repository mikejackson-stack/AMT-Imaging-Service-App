CTPM-0712 (Inspect, Clean Table Covers)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Inspect all the table covers for cracks and damage, especially around cover mounting hardware.

  1. Inspect the covers for loose or missing hardware.

  1. Clean the table covers and cradle with a clean cloth and appropriate cleaning solution.
