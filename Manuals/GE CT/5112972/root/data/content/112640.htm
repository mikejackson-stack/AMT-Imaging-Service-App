4  Procedure

  1. Bring system down in an orderly fashion. From “R" level run “DOWN" <CR>

  1. Turn off x-ray drives, data processing switches on REM box.

  1. Turn off main A1 panel breaker and open up the breaker panel.

  1. Locate the system ground wire. Using the smallest clamp-on ammeter that will close around the wire, measure AC current. It should be 0 mA.

  1. Remove the cover between the table and gantry. Remove the bottom grill on the PDU and the front cover (see Illustration 1 and Illustration 2).

  1. Leave A1 panel open and turn power back on. Turn on data processing and x-ray drives at the rem panel.

  1. At the console, turn alpha lock back on the keyboard.

  1. Boot system up to “M" level.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

  1. Using the Fluke or T232 Clamp-On:

    1. At the Table/Gantry ( Illustration 1), you should have three #10 wires and one #4 wire. One #10 goes to computer. One goes to PDU, and one connects to the base of the Gantry. Currents should be less than 100 mA in each of the #10 wires. Current through the #4 should be less than 40 mA. Verify and record the results.

      Illustration 1:  Table/Gantry Reference Ground Point

    1. At the PDU, you should have two #14 wires at the bottom left front ground bus bar. Current through these should be less than 100 mA. Verify and record. (See Illustration 2).

      Illustration 2:  PDU Ground Point

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

  1. Using the T231 Clamp-On:

    1. Verify and record that the currents are close to these typical currents. (See Illustration 2).

      • Neut/Grnd = 75 mA

      • T1 Neut = 20 amp

    1. At the computer cabinet, verify current through all grounds to computer, mag tape, disk, gaids, and all optional equipment is less that 50 mA.

    1. At the AP, verify current through #8 ground is less than 50 mA.

    1. At the camera, verify current through #8 ground is less than 50 mA.

    1. At each console, verify current through each #8 ground is less than 50 mA.

    1. Return to the incoming A1 power panel and clamp on to the incoming ground.

    1. With system now at “M" level, verify that the current reading is not 0 mA but is less than 250 mA. This verifies that the wire is connected at the other end.

    1. Perform a 120 kV, 200 mA, 2 second scan (warm up tube if necessary) and verify current reading at A1 panel ground is less than 400 mA (350 mA is typical).

  1. Remove all test equipment, close all covers, and verify normal system operation. Save all recorded readings for reference.
