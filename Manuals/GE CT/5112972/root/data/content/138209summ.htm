CTPM-1702 (Lower Assembly PM)

1  Procedure

1.1   Preparation

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT.
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Elevate the Table to Max Height or near Max Height.

  1. Tilt the Gantry to Zero Degrees +/- 3 degrees.

  1. Retract the cradle to the home and latched position.

  1. Have a Multi-Meter, screw drivers, and a damp cloth handy.

1.2   Inspect ETC fan

  1. Remove the table base covers.

  1. Inspect the ETC fan for proper operation / condition. Verify fan is turning and generating air flow. Verify Fan is not generating excess noise indicating bearing failure. Replace the fan if needed.

  1. Turn off 24 hour power to the Table (fan power).

  1. Clean the fan with damp cloth (water).

1.3   Check Elevation Encoder Assembly

  1. Inspect the Elevation Encoder Assembly for the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the metal cover from the ETC Board. Loosen the Captive Screw to tilt the ETC Board flat and level.

  1. Return the power to the Table and re-boot the scanner.

  1. Verify that as you lower the Table the rollers of the two Elevation Cam Limit Switches will follow the features of the Cam (Located near Elevation Encoder). Then raise the Table to near maximum height.

  1. Position the Table Elevation at or very near to the Elevation "C" Pulse; use the Up and Down buttons to jog the Table Elevation to this Position. Table Up or Down Slow moves at 1 inch / 5 seconds. Hence it should be easy to jog to the "C" Pulse with-in +/- 0.2 inch. Use the Green Elevation "C" Pulse LED (on ETC Board) to verify proper positioning.

  1. Measure and record this "C" Pulse Table Height. Use a Straight Edge across the Cradle and measure to the floor. At this point the Gantry Display should be tracking / displaying the Table Height, if not you did not cross the Elevation "C" Pulse yet or have a hardware problem.
