3.2  Safety

Possible eye damage.
Laser can cause eye damage.
Do not look directly into the laser lights. Inspect windows at an angle.

4  Procedure

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT.
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Inspect the scan and the laser windows for scratches or cracks. Make sure the scan window has no creases.

  1. Replace the damaged windows.
