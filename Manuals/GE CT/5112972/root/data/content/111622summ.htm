CTPM-0316 (Inspect Take-Up)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove side covers on Gantry and disable scan drive switch.

  1. Open front and rear covers of Gantry.

  1. Remove left side cable take-up cover as viewed from the front of the Gantry. This will be our viewing port for the take-up.

  1. Shine a flashlight into the take-up. Rotate the Gantry by hand as necessary to inspect the entire range of travel from E-hook to E-hook. Be careful not to trip the E-stops by hitting the hooks too hard.

  1. Inspect for debris from take-up rollers, worn cables, loose hardware, broken spring steel, or missing clamps.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Remove any debris with a vacuum cleaner and correct any problems.

  1. Re-assemble the system and verify operation.
