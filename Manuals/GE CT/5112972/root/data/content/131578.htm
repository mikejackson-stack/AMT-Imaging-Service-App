4  Procedure

4.1  Installing the HV Divider

  1. Switch off "X-Ray and Drives" power on the REM box. On systems with Gantry side controls, press the [E-Stop] button.

  1. Inside the Gantry:

    1. Switch off the 550v Enable.

    1. Switch off the Axial Drive.

    1. Rotate the tube to the 3 o'clock position.

    1. Pin the Gantry.

    1. Switch off the 120 vac Gantry power.

    1. Install HV Divider between the Tube and Tanks. See Illustration 1.

      Illustration 1:  Installing the HV Divider

      NOTE:A table or tube hoist for the HV Divider will be necessary to make the cables reach.

  1. Add ground wire (minimum size of AWG 12) from Tube Ground to Bleeder Ground. See Illustration 1.

    1. Switch on the 120vac Gantry power.

    1. Switch on the 550v enable.

  1. Switch on "X-Ray and Drives" power on the REM box. On systems with Gantry side controls press the “on” button.

  1. Reset the hardware.

4.2  Instrumentation Setup

NOTE:If possible, try to plug the scope into the table 120 vac outlet, using an extension cord if necessary. This reduces noise seen on scope waveforms.

  1. Using an oscilloscope* with 10X probes, do the following:

    • Connect channel one to the anode output of the divider and the scope ground to the bleeder ground.

    • Connect channel two to the cathode output of the divider and the scope ground to bleeder control.

      NOTE:To minimize bleeder-induced ripple on the KV waveform, it is recommended that a 30-feet Belden shielded twister cable be connected between the scope probes and the bleeder. See the Tools and Test Equipment section at the beginning.

    • Trigger channel one, positive, DC couple, trigger mode normal.

    • Channel one and two, 10v/div, time base 200ms.

    • Invert Channel two.

  1. Go to the Generator Characterization menu and toggle the softkey [Monitor Enable] ON. Now, kV and mA readings will be displayed in the message log.

    NOTE:Verify which type of kV Board is in the OBC. Names of components on the 46-321064G1-D kV Board are shown in this procedure without brackets. Names of components on the 46-321198G1 kV Board are shown in this procedure in [brackets].

4.3  Cathode Calibration

  1. In DDC take a 1 second, static, 0mm exposure at 100kV/50mA using a large focal spot.

  1. The Computer Displayed reading spec for the Cathode kV and Anode kV is 50 +/- 0.5 kV.

  1. Compare the scope reading from the average Cathode kV and the displayed Cathode kV in the message log.

    NOTE:If scope cursors are used to window the trace, the Left Vertical Cursor must be positioned to the Right of the Rising Edge of the waveform and the Right Vertical Cursor must be positioned to the Left of the Falling Edge of the Waveform.

  1. Adjust the Cathode pot on the kV Board until the scope reading for the Cathode kV and the displayed reading for the Cathode kV in the message log are +/- 0.5kV of each other.

    • The pot labeled CA [CAKV, R316] on the kV board will adjust the scope reading.

    • CCW will decrease the scope kV.

    • CW will increase the scope kV.

    • 1/2 turn is approximately 0.5 kV.

  1. Record readings in FORM 4879 (Data Record).

4.4  Anode Calibration

  1. In DDC take a 1 second, static, 0mm, exposure at 100kV/50mA using a large focal spot.

  1. Compare the scope reading for the average Anode kV and the displayed Anode kV in the message log.

    NOTE:If the scope cursors are used to window the trace, the Left Vertical Cursor must be positioned to the Right of the Rising Edge of the waveform and the Right Vertical Cursor must be positioned to the Left of the Falling Edge of the Waveform.

  1. Adjust the Anode Pot on the kV Board until the scope reading for the Anode kV and the displayed reading for the Anode kV in the message log are +/- 0.5kV of each other.

    • The pot labeled AN [ANKV, R318] on the kV board will adjust the scope reading.

    • CCW will decrease the scope kV.

    • CW will increase the scope kV.

    • 1/2 turn is approximately 0.5 kV.

  1. Record readings in FORM 4879 (Data Record).
