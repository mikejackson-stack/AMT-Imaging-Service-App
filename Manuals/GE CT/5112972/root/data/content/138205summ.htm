CTPM-1607 (General Cleaning)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Clean Plasma / Touch Screen

NOTE:Turn the console power off.

NOTE:Do not use any type of abrasive pad, scouring powder or solvent, such as alcohol.

  1. Clean the touch screen with a clean cloth lightly moistened with a mild detergent solution.

1.2   Clean Display Monitor and Console Exterior

  1. With a clean cloth lightly moistened with a mild detergent solution clean the exterior of the console, including the keyboard area.

  1. Wipe the face of the display monitor. Do not apply liquid or any other cleaner directly to the monitor.

1.3   Clean Table Covers and Cradle

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT.
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Clean table covers and cradle with a cloth and alcohol. Be sure to remove any adhesive from underneath the cradle.

  1. Be sure to wipe off contrast from the cradle edge extrusion.

1.4   Clean Gantry Covers Including Top

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT.
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Use a cloth and a mild detergent (such as "Goop" ) or alcohol to clean the covers.

  1. Turn the console back on.
