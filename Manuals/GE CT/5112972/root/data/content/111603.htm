4  Procedure

  1. Remove the side covers of Gantry and disable the scan drive switch.

  1. Open the front cover.

  1. Inspect inside of Gantry, especially tube, DAS, and mylar window, for signs of oil leakage from the tube wells.

  1. Wipe-off any oil. If oil is found, the tube wells will need to be checked. Refer to procedure in the Generator section, CTPM-0411 (Change Oil In Tube Wells).
