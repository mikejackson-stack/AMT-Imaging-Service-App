CTPM-0104 (Check ACE Fan)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Locate Ace drive chassis fan on the back of the Ace drawer, or HTD drawer in the computer cabinet.

  1. Using a flashlight, or by feeling for the proper airflow, check that the power supply fan is operating normally.

  1. Check that the lateral fans on the drawer are operating normally.

  1. Fans should also be free of dirt and should operate quietly.
