4  Procedure

  1. This test adds resistance in the overload sensing circuit to force a generator overload at normal techniques.

  1. Turn generator off at REM box. Open transformer cover.

  1. Observe that there are two orange leads with push-on terminals at XG2A18TP-mA- and two similar brown leads at XG2A18TP-mA+. Pull J1 at XG2A14A1 mA sensing circuit board, and using an ohmmeter, identify and mark the orange lead at TP-mA- and the brown lead at TP-mA+ which connect to XG2A14A1J1-21 and J1-22, respectively.

  1. Reconnect J1 at the mA sensing board and the brown lead at TP-mA+. Connect a 20 ohm, 5%, 5 watt resistor in series with the orange lead at TP-mA-. Procure locally or order part #46-266990G1.

  1. Turn the generator on and perform a test exposure using GENCAL.

  1. Attempt to make a series of 80 kV, 1 sec. exposures at 170 and 300 mA (see first NOTE below). A generator overload shall not occur at 170 mA, and shall occur at 300 mA (see second NOTE below).

    NOTE:These exposures are most easily performed from Gencal program in CTDS.

    1. Enter CTDS.

    2. Select GC (CR).

    3. Perform Function 7 (reset TGGC and download).

    4. Perform Function 2 (verify/adjust).

    5. Select Corxtab table and manual mode by editing.

    6. Select new technique - change time to 4 seconds.

    7. Select return to retain previous values when finished.

    8. Select “R" to retry scan if required.

    9. Select the “Cancel" key to exit.

    NOTE:Use MGTEST FETCH FAULT STATUS (option 22) and verify a 20F2 fault is reported. If Gantry is left in service mode (for error display), just verify that the display shows 242 error on left side of display.

  1. When the generator overload occurs, verify that a subsequent exposure is prevented and that the generator overload fault is reported at the operators console.

  1. Reset the overload using Gencal Function 7 and verify that a 170 mA exposure is then permitted.

  1. Turn the x-ray generator off and relocate the 20 ohm resistor to the identified brown lead at XG2A18TP-mA+, then repeat steps above to test the circuits using the brown lead. When finished, remove the resistor and reconnect the brown lead at TP-mA+.

  1. Label both the orange lead and brown lead that were used with the resistor for future reference.

  1. Restore power and verify proper exposures occur at 170 and 300 mA.
