4  Procedure

  1. Remove the right side Gantry cover.

  1. Disable scan drive at switch inside Gantry.

  1. Manually position the Gantry to where the x-ray tube is accessible (3 O'Clock).

  1. Turn off the “X-Ray and Drives" switch at the REM box.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Inspect the heat exchanger (radiator) behind the tube cooling fans for dust and dirt build-up ( Illustration 1). If excessive, remove fans and vacuum, then re-install fans.

  1. Turn on the “X-Ray and Drives" switch at the REM box and check the two tube fans for normal operation.

  1. Return the system to normal operation.
