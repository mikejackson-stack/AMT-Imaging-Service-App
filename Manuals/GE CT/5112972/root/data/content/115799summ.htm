CTPM-1007 (General Cleaning Procedures)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Clean Plasma Screen

  1. Clean plasma screen with a methanol damped cotton cloth.

1.2   Clean Display Monitor and Console Exterior

  1. Use a lightly damp (not wet) cloth to wipe the face of the display monitor. Do not apply liquid or any other cleaner directly to the monitor.

  1. Using a damp cloth, clean the console exterior.

1.3   Clean Table Covers and Cradle

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Clean the table covers and the cradle with a cloth and alcohol. Be sure to remove any adhesive from underneath the cradle.

  1. Be sure to wipe off contrast from the cradle edge extrusion.

1.4   Clean Gantry Covers Including Top

BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY BE PRESENT
UNPROTECTED CONTACT WITH BLOOD-BORNE PATHOGENS MAY CAUSE DISEASE TRANSMISSION.
Use proper procedures and PPE to avoid blood-borne pathogens.

  1. Use a cloth and a mild detergent (such as "Goop") or alcohol to clean the covers.
