Direction  5112972-800,  Rev#  2

CTPM-0110 (Check AP Fans, Clean Filters)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

1  Procedure

1.1   FLOATING POINT AP:

  1. Remove the bottom cabinet grill and air filter.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the filter.

  1. With a flashlight, inspect the blower in the bottom of the cabinet. Ensure quiet operation and that air is being drawn into the bottom front of the cabinet.

  1. Vacuum the foam filters on the front of the AP chassis and the fan screen on the rear of the chassis. Check that the cooling fan in the rear of the chassis is operating properly.

  1. Swing out the power supply on the left side of the chassis and check the fan.

1.2   STAR AP (STCT):

  1. Remove the grill and the filter in the front of the cabinet.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the filter and ensure that the blower is operating properly.

  1. Verify that the power supply fan located at the rear of the cabinet is operational. Use a TY wrap inserted into the blades since this fan will spin even if it is not powered on due to the air flow from the cabinet blower.

1.3   STAR AP (STCTC):

  1. Open the rear cabinet door and locate the two cooling fans on the top side of the AP chassis.

  1. Ensure that these are operating properly.

  1. Verify that the power supply fan is also operational.
