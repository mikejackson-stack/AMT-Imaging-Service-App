4  Procedure

  1. Change the oscilloscope to add ch. 1 and ch. 2 to read total kV from the HV divider.

    Channel one and two, 20v/div, time base 200ms, trigger ch. one, positive.

  1. In DDC take a 1 second, static, 0mm, exposure at 100kV/50mA, using a large focal spot.

  1. Record the scope reading and avg. kV as displayed in the message log in FORM 4879 (Data Record).

  1. Go to the Generator Characterization menu and toggle the softkey [Monitor Enable] OFF.

  1. Now kV and mA readings will not be displayed in the message log.
