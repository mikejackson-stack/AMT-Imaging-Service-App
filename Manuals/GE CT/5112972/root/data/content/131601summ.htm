2  Procedure

  1. Display the Generator Installation and Verification menu.

  1. Select [mA Meter Check] .

  1. Select [Accept] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the displayed Anode kV, Cathode kV and Total kV values in the FORM 4879 (Data Record) “Circuit OFF” and “Circuit ON” table.

  1. Select [Dismiss] .

  1. Remove the external HV Divider and restore the normal system connections.

  1. Make sure you ty–wrapped the High Voltage cables into place.

  1. Wrap paper towels around tube locking rings to absorb excess oil.

  1. Restore the OBC cover (to retain OBC boards during rotation.)
