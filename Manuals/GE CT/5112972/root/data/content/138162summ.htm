Direction  5112972-800,  Rev#  2

CTPM-1507 (CRPDU PM Checks)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Inspect Servo Amp Fan Operation

Refer to Illustration 1 for location of the Servo Amp Fan.

  1. Open the top cover.

  1. Inspect the operation of Servo Amp fan in the top of the CRPDU and verify normal and quiet operation.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Check MOV LEDs on the Control Board

Refer to Illustration 1 for location of MOVs and LEDs.

  1. Open the front cover.

  1. Check the green LEDs just above the MOVs on the right bottom side of the control board. Normal operation is shown by the green LEDs turned on. A LED turned off indicates a blown fuse due to a MOV shortage.

1.3   Clean Air Vent Screen on the Bottom of the Chassis

Refer to Illustration 1 for location of the air vent screen.

  1. Make sure to power the system down.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the dust and dirt from the air vent on the bottom of the chassis. There is no need to remove the Plexiglas cover.

  1. Replace the covers.

  1. Turn the system power back on.
