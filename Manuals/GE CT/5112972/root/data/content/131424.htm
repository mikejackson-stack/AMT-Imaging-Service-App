4  Procedure

  1. Position table to lowest elevation.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
use proper lockout/tagout procedures before servicing equipment.

  1. Turn off the facility power to PDU - use tag and lockout procedures.

  1. Remove and set aside both the gantry side covers.

  1. Turn off all the three switches on the status control box on the right side of Gantry.

  1. Open the top cover and engage the prop rod.

  1. Remove and set aside the Gantry scan window.

  1. Open the front cover.

  1. Check the ventilation holes and fans on the following covers and chassis (see Illustration 1) :

    • Filament Power Assembly

    • Cathode Inverter

    • DAS

    • Anode Inverter

    • DAS Power Supply

    • OBC

    • STC

    • Gantry top cover

    • Axial motor filter assembly

    Illustration 1:  Checking Ventilation Holes and Fans in the Covers and Chassis

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the ventilation holes as needed.

  1. Clean the fans with a cloth and alcohol.
