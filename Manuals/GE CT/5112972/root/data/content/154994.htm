CT Planned Maintenance Procedures

PM Schedule - HSA, HSA-RP

These schedules are for HSA, HSA-RP products only, as approved by CT Engineering. HSA PMs are based on gantry revolutions. PMs will be done every 250k gantry revolutions.

NOTE:Total time in hours:

  • Schedule S - 5.8 hours

  • Schedule T - 5.7 hours

  • Schedule U - 5.8 hours

  • Schedule V - 4.8 hours

NOTE:* This product can use the ETC OR the Schleifring style of slip ring. Please use the appropriate PM procedure.
