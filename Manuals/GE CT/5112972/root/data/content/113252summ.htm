CTPM-0410 (Transformer Clean Up)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Turn off x-ray drives on Rem box.

  1. Remove top cover of high voltage transformer.

  1. Clean top of XFMR using alcohol or some other degreaser such as Fantastik, 409, etc. Remove any excess oil, dirt, etc.

  1. Locate the mA+, mA- terminals and the brass metal spark gaps attached to these terminals.

  1. Verify that a piece of film or something of equivalent thickness can be slid between the point of the brass spark gap and the transformer. Bend them as necessary to achieve this.

  1. Locate the ground stud on the XFMR. Verify that there is a wire with a thin metal grounding rod attached to this stud.

  1. Replace cover and clean outside surfaces.
