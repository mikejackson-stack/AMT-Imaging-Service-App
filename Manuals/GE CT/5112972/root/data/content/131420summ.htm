CTPM-1311 (Check Drive Belt for Wear)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Position the table to the lowest elevation.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
use proper lockout/tagout procedures before servicing equipment.

  1. Turn off the facility power to PDU - use tag and lockout procedures.

  1. Remove and set aside both the gantry side covers.

  1. Turn off all the three switches on the status control box on right side of the Gantry.

  1. Open the top cover and engage the prop rod.

  1. Remove and set aside the Gantry scan window.

  1. Open the front cover.

  1. Inspect the condition of drive belt. Check for cracks, wear, or fraying. Make sure to check the entire drive belt.

  1. Replace the drive belt, if necessary.
