4  Procedure

NOTE:This procedure applies to old style Gantrys with exposed bearings.

  1. Remove the side covers on Gantry and disable the scan drive switch.

  1. Open the front and rear covers of Gantry.

  1. Inspect the eight bearing assemblies. See Illustration 1. Each consists of two thrust bearings and one rubber coated roller. Inspect for cracked rubber coating or broken bearings. Part number for radial bearing: 46-237184G1.

  1. Reassemble the system and verify operation.
