4  Procedure

4.1  Trackball

The Trackball and the Mouse require little maintenance. Verify that their movement is smooth. If it is not smooth proceed to clean them.

  1. Turn the console power off.

  1. Turn the trackball upside down and remove the three screws in the bottom of the case. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Trackball

  1. While holding the top and bottom halves of the case together, turn the trackball right side up and remove the cover.

  1. Lift the ball out and clean it using a mild detergent.

  1. Using rubbing alcohol on a cotton swab clean the roller surfaces that come in contact with the ball.

  1. Put the rubber ball back into the trackball. Replace the cover and slide it back clockwise until it locks into place.

  1. Clean quarterly.

4.2  Mouse

  1. Turn the console power off.

  1. Turn the mouse upside down and remove the mouse track ball cover by sliding it counter clockwise. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Mouse

  1. Turn the mouse right side up and let the rubber ball fall out into your hand. Clean it using a mild detergent.

  1. Using rubbing alcohol on a cotton swab clean the roller surfaces that come in contact with the ball.

  1. Put the rubber ball back into the mouse. Replace the cover and slide it back clockwise until it locks into place.

  1. Clean quarterly.
