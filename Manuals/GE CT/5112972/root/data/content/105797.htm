4  Procedure

  1. Use 91% Isopropyl Alcohol, cotton swab, and/or lint free cloth to clean the following:

    1. Clean the head assembly. Use cotton swab to remove debris from the head channels (see Illustration 1, item a).

    1. Clean the head tape guides and tape cleaner surface with lint free cloth (see Illustration 1, items b and c)

    1. Clean vacuum chamber and air holes with end of resistor or similar cleaning object (see Illustration 1, item d).

    Do not enlarge holes !
     
     

    1. Clean Capstan with dry lint free cloth. Do not use alcohol. It may damage the Capstan rubber surface (see Illustration 1, item e).

    For more detailed information, refer to Direction 46–018076, DG 6026 & Kennedy 9300 Magnetic Tape Drives General Service Manual.
