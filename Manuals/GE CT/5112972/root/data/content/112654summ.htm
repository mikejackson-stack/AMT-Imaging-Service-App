CTPM-0409 (Change Oil In Transformer Wells)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Turn x-ray drives off.

  1. Remove XFMR cover. Using cable spanner wrench, remove anode and cathode cables. If necessary, gently pry them out with a thin blade screwdriver if they won't come out easily. Do not pull on them.

  1. Ground the three pins on each cable momentarily to the metal transformer.

  1. Remove oil from the SO cathode and SO anode wells.

  1. Inspect the candlesticks and wells for any carbon arcing.

  1. Refill the wells with 13 cc of insulating oil part #T0552G.

  1. Replace anode and cathode cables, making sure to key them properly. Re-tighten and re-assemble.

  1. Make an exposure and verify normal operation.
