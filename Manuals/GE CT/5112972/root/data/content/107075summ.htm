CTPM-0206 (Check For Screen Burns)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Turn the console power off.

  1. Turn the room lights on. The display monitor image screen should be a uniform gray color. Screen burn will be especially noticeable in the area where the gray scale is displayed.

  1. Also check the alphanumeric monitor for absence of screen burn.

  1. Turn the power back on and re-enable the keyboard alpha lock.
