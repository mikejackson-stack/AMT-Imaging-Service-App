4  Procedure

NOTE:The poly filter has a useful life of about 400k slices/exposures. Next, the filter starts to develop hairline cracks, which will eventually separate and cause image artifacts. Replacing the filter proactively every 400k slices or four years will eliminate the images artifacts during customer use that are caused by the filter cracking. The inspection takes five minutes to actually replace the Collimator Filter; Align and Calibrate takes approximately two hours.

  1. Note date of last Filter or Collimator change. (See sticker on collimator, site log, or last PM sheet for date.)

  1. Check and log slices and date at this PM. Subtract this count from the count logged at the last Filter or Collimator change.

  1. If greater than 400k slices or greater than four years, order Filter and replace at next PM or Tube change, whichever comes first (i.e. within the next 30 days).
