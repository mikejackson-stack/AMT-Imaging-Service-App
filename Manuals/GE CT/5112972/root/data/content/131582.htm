4  Procedure

  1. Beginning from the Top Level Plasma, execute the following sequence of softkeys:

    1. Touch [Utilities] .

    2. Touch [1, 2, 3] .

    3. Touch [CT Tools] .

    4. Touch [Generator Charact] .

    5. Touch [Read Metering] .

    6. On the Plasma, Touch [Run] .

  1. Record the displayed Anode kV, Cathode kV and Total kV values on FORM 4879 (Data Record) for "Circuit OFF" and "Circuit ON".

  1. Remove the external HV Divider and connect the system normally.

  1. Verify the High Voltage cables are ty-wrapped in place.

  1. Re-apply the paper toweling around tube locking rings to absorb excess oils.

  1. Re-install the OBC Cover (to retain OBC boards during rotation.)

No finalization required.
