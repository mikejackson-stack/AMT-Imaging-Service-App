CTPM-1431 (Verify Exposure Time Accuracy (HSS))

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Timing Not Applicable15 mins Timing Not Applicable

2  Overview

 Procedure Effectivity:

Assembly:HIGH VOLTAGE
Subassembly:X-Ray Generation
Purpose:Verify Exposure Time Accuracy (HSS)
Last Revised:December 1996

3  Procedure

  1. Turn on [Monitor Enable] to read the scan times.

    1. Select [End Exam] to halt any exam in progress.

    1. Select [System Integration] .

    1. Select [Generator Calibration] .

    1. Select [Start] to open the Generator Installation and Verification menu.

    1. Select [Bleeder Setup] (DDC) to open the Diagnostic Data Collection menu .

    1. Select [Monitor Enable] to display the scan times in the message log.

      NOTE:Do NOT Dismiss, or close the DDC window, because it turns OFF the monitor enable function.

  1. Toggle to the Exam Rx desktop.

  1. Select the Service Protocol Exposure Time Accuracy, OR manually select the scan parameters in Table 1.

    Table 1:  Exposure Time Accuracy Scan Parameters

    Scan TypekVmASFOVThicknessScan TimeStart Loc.End Loc.Pitch
    Scout12040 - - - I10S10 -
    Scout12040 - - - I75S75 -
    Scout12040 - - - I300S300 -
    Axial 12040Large10mm0.6sec S0 - -
    Axial 12040Large10mm1.0sec S0 - -
    Axial 12040Large10mm4.0sec S0 - -
    Helical12040Large10mm  I145S1451:1

  1. Complete the scans and record the scan times displayed in the message log on FORM 4879 (Data Record).

  1. When complete, toggle to the Service Desktop, and [Dismiss] the DDC window to turn OFF the Monitor Enable function.

    NOTE:Failure to turn OFF Monitor Enable fills the message log with kV, mA and scan times.

4  Finalization

No finalization required.
