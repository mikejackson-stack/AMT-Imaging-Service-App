CTPM-1703 (Vacuum Inside Table)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Turn the Table power off.

  1. Remove the table base covers and side panels.

  1. Inspect covers for damage (cracks, chips), and replace or repair, if necessary.

Potential for equipment damage.

Using vacuum hose near live electronic equipment may cause equipment damage due to static.

Make sure equipment is turned off prior to vacuuming near electronic equipment.

 
 

  1. Vacuum the inside of the Table.

  1. Clean the covers with a clean cloth and alcohol.

  1. Restore the covers and the side panels.

  1. Turn the Table power on.
