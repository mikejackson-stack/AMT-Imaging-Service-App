4  Procedure

  1. Display the Generator Installation and Verification menu.

  1. Select [Bleeder Setup] (DDC).

  1. Verify/Set–up the following DDC parameters:

    • STATIC X–RAY ON

    • 1 SECOND

    • 1 SCAN

    • FOCAL SPOT LARGE

    • 100 KV

    • 50 MA

    • MONITOR ENABLE

  1. Change the oscilloscope to add ch.1 and ch.2, to read total kV from the HV divider.

  1. Channel one and two, 20v/div, time base 200ms, trigger ch. one, positive.

  1. Select [ACCEPT RX] .

  1. Record the scope reading, and the Avg. kV displayed in the message log, in FORM 4879 (Data Record).

  1. Display the Generator Characterization menu.

  1. Toggle the softkey [Monitor Enable] to OFF, so the message log no longer displays kV and mA readings.
