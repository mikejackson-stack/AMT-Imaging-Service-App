CTPM-0305 (Check DAS Supply Fans, Filter)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   C-DAS

NOTE:Older style C-DAS systems do not have a fan or blower.

  1. Remove the Gantry right side cover and disable the scan drive switch.

  1. Rotate the Gantry as needed to view the DAS power supplies.

  1. Check for rotation and quiet operation of the DAS cover fan.

1.2   E-DAS

NOTE:Newer style E-DAS does not have a fan.

  1. Remove the right side cover from Gantry and turn off the scan drive switch. Unplug the side cover fan cable (some covers do not have fans). Manually rotate Gantry so that the lower part of E-DAS is at 3 O'Clock position.

  1. From the right side facing the E-DAS, slide filter out and inspect/clean. Also, check by using a Ty-wrap that the fan is running. It is under the filter. (See Illustration 1).

  1. Return the filter to its normal position. Re-install the side cover fan cable. Turn on the loop contactor switch. Re-install the side cover.
