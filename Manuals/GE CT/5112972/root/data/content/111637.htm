4  Procedure

  1. Remove table covers to access TGGC. Remove Gantry right side cover.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

  1. To check tilt pot, tilt Gantry until shipping bolt holes on right side of Gantry line up. This is by definition 0 degrees tilt.

  1. Locate TP17 (Tilt POS) and TP21 (AGND) on the A4 board in the TGGC. With a DVM, connect the “+" meter lead to TP17 and the “-" lead to TP21. The meter should read -2.5VDC +/- 0.009VDC.

  1. To check axial pot, disable scan drive switch.

  1. Rotate Gantry manually to 0 degrees (tube at bottom of Gantry). Make your measurement when axial encoder C pulse is observed on led on Gantry board. Be sure to be in CCW area, not CW area for the measurement. This is identified by the white area on the take-up, not the gray area.

  1. Locate TP18 (Axial POS) and TP21 (AGND) on the A4 board in the TGGC.

  1. With a DVM, connect the “+" meter lead to TP18 and the “-" lead to TP21. The meter should read -1.25VDC +/- 0.009VDC.

  1. Any adjustment will require a re-characterization of tilt or axial.

  1. Return system to normal operation.
