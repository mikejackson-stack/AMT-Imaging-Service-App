4  Procedure

Laser can cause eye damage.
Do not look directly into the laser behind the alignment light windows.
Inspect the windows at an angle.

NOTE:Refer to Illustration 1 for procedure steps.

  1. Clean mylar window in Gantry.

  1. Check mylar window for scratches. Replace if necessary.

  1. Clean alignment light windows in Gantry.

  1. Inspect alignment light windows for scratches, and cracks. Replace if necessary.
