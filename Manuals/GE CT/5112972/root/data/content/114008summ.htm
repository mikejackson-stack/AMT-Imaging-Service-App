CTPM-0701 (Check I.S. and Cradle Release)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Drive the I.S. and cradle off home position and towards the Gantry approximately two feet.

  1. Grasp the sides of the I.S. with both hands and pull it away quickly from the Gantry. The I.S. should break free and the cradle should unlatch. “Cradle Unlatched" light on Gantry should light.

  1. Reset table lock switch on the table control pad by pushing down and up on either yellow knob.

  1. Make sure the I.S. and cradle are not at home position. Once again grasp the sides of the I.S. with both hands. This time quickly push the I.S. towards the Gantry. It should again break free and “Cradle Unlatched" should light.

  1. Again reset the table locks.
