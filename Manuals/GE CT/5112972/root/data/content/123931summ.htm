CTPM-1203 (Clean Filter Screen)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. (For Computer chassis air intake filter screen) Insert the brush underneath the computer chassis from the front-right side until the bristles are just under the chassis to clean the front part of the intake screen.

  1. Rotate the brush handle in a counter clockwise direction while using your other hand to pull the brush to the left - rolling the dust onto brush.

  1. When the brush reaches the left side of the cabinet, remove the brush and clean off the lint.

  1. Reinsert the brush under the front-right side again, but twice as far in and repeat steps two and three to clean the rear portion of the intake screen.

  1. Continue until the entire bottom intake screen has been cleaned in this manner.

  1. Using a sweeping motion, remove any dust on the floor, underneath, or around the chassis to prevent it from being drawn into the intake screen.
