3.3  Safety

The Argus drive is very sensitive and should not be bumped or jarred when removing and reinstalling the front cover.
 
 

4  Procedure

  1. Bring system down in an orderly fashion. Cancel to “R" and release DA0.

  1. Power off the Argus at the front panel.

  1. Remove front cover by depressing cover clips on each side.

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum filter and make sure it is positioned correctly. (Filter is part #46-251291P430).
