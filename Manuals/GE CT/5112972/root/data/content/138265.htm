4  Procedure

  1. Inspect fans for operation, noise, and obstructions in the following:

    • Fans in the Fan Tray Assembly.

    • Fan at rear of HSD Tray.

  1. Repair or replace any fans that make unusual noise.

  1. Clean dirty fans with a cloth and water.
