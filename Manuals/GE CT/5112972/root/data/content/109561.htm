4  Procedure

  1. Remove both the Gantry side covers and turn off the scan drive switch.

  1. Open the front and rear Gantry covers.

  1. Inspect the condition of main drive belt. Look for thinning of the belt, fraying near edges, or any visible steel cords. Any signs of these anomalies warrants belt replacement.

  1. Verify the correct main belt tension by measuring 3/4" on compressed spring. (See Illustration 1).

  1. Inspect the take up belt (See Illustration 2). Look for thinning of the belt, fraying, or any visible steel cords. Any signs of these anomalies warrants belt replacement.

  1. Verify the correct take-up belt tension by measuring 1/8" as shown on Illustration 2.

  1. Inspect the condition of over speed belt (See Illustration 2). Look for thinning of belt, fraying of belt.

  1. Re-assemble the Gantry and check for normal operation.
