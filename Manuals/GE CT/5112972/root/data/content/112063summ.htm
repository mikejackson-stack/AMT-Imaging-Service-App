1  Procedure

  1. Turn the x-ray drives off at REM box. Verify whether the red “Off" lamp lights up.

  1. Remove the front cover of XG1. Verify whether red lamp A11DS1 is on. (See Illustration 1).

  1. Turn the x-ray drives on. Verify green “On" lights on REM.

  1. Verify whether red lamp A7DS1 is on. (See Illustration 1).
