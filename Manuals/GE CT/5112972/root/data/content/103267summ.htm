CTPM-0106 (Check Bay Fans, Filters)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE IS PRESENT DURING PROCEDURE.
Make sure to keep body parts away from exposed circuits while performing this procedure.

1  Procedure

1.1   CPU FANS

  1. Remove the front plastic chassis covers on the computer. Remove the FCC hardened chassis covers (gold covers) if applicable. There will be a main chassis and an expansion chassis for S140. There will be a main chassis only for MV 7800.

  1. Using a flashlight, check that all fans in the computer are operating normally. Four fans are located on the left side of each chassis.

1.2   BLOWERS AND FILTERS

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Older style cabinets ( Illustration 1): Remove the two bottom cabinet grills and remove the filters. Vacuum the filters. With a flashlight, inspect the cabinet blowers in the bottom of the cabinets. Insure quiet operation and that air is being drawn in the bottom front of the cabinet.

  1. Newer style cabinets: Locate the left side cabinet grill and swing it open. Remove the large cabinet air filter and vacuum it.
