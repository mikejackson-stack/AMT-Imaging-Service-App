NOTE:This procedure is performed to verify whether kV and mA accuracies are met over the range of selectable technics.

CTPM-1407 (Auto mA Calibration)

4  Procedure

  1. Beginning from the end of Auto mA Cal, do the following:

    • Touch [Backup] (to get to the Genrator Charact menu)

    • Touch [HHS Scans]

  1. Press Start Scan when it illuminates. The system will now perform the technics called out in HHS Data Record Document.

  1. Allow the system to complete tube calibration scans. If the system cannot complete the Auto mA cal because of HV aborts, perform heat soak and seasoning.

  1. Record the Computer displayed kV and mA at 5ms, Anode mA, Cathode kV, and Total kV in HHS Data Record Document for small focal spot and large focal spots.
