CTPM-1307 (Inspect Axial Motor Brushes)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Precautions / Prerequisites

NOTE:If, during an inspection, an original brush is reinserted into the motor, make sure the brush is reinserted in the same location and orientation.

  1. Position the table to the lowest elevation.

  1. Gantry tilt at about zero degrees.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
use proper lockout/tagout procedures before servicing equipment.

  1. Turn off facility power to PDU.

  1. Remove and set aside both the gantry side covers.

  1. Turn off the SCAN DRIVES SAFETY switch (right side of gantry) and the X-RAY/DRIVES switch (on REM panel). For HSA, turn off all the three switches on the status control box on the right side of the gantry.

  1. Open the top cover and engage the prop rod.

  1. Open the front cover.

  1. For HSA, remove and set aside the gantry scan window.

1.2   Inspection

  1. Manually rotate the rotating base to a position of axial motor accessibility if necessary.

  1. Loosen the brush cap. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. Vacuum at each brush/cap opening to remove any excess brush wear material and any material that may have fallen on to the gantry base or the surrounding area.

NOTE:The brushes at approximately the 6 o’ clock position will most likely expel the majority of the brush wear material.

  1. Remove all eight brushes from the motor, one at a time.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert each brush into its holder; all brushes should move freely in their holders. The brushes will be “worn in” so make sure that the brushes are put back In their original holder with the original orientation. When installing the caps, make sure each cap butts-up to the brush spring head (the brush spring head should lay flat in the cap cavity.)

    (Step Details)
