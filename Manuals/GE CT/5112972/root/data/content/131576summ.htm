CTPM-1401 (mA Meter Verification (HHS))

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Inside the Gantry, do the following:

    1. Switch off the 550v enable.

    1. Switch off the Axial Drive.

    1. Rotate the Anode Tank to the 2 o'clock position.

    1. Pin the Gantry.

    1. Remove the OBC cover (if not previously done).

  1. Using a DVM as a DC mA meter, (Set meter range to one decimal place.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Anode HV Tank Measurement board. If a wire from the harness is present on TP5 (see Illustration 2) Measurement Board, the following test lead is not required. If no wire is present, install the test lead as follows:

    1. Use a jumper with a 68 ohm 5 watt resistor in series.

    1. Connect one end to TP11 (ACAL2) on the mA board.

    1. Connect the other end to TP5 on the Anode Tank Measurement board.

      (Step Details)

  1. Under Generator Characterization Menu, select [Read Metering] .

  1. On the display, enter a time delay in seconds, this delay is the time required for you to walk from the console to the DVM and record the reading. The test will not begin until this time delay has expired. Once the test begins the meter circuit will be enabled for only four (4) seconds. For HSA, on the plasma, select [Run] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the test lead from the anode tank (if used).

  1. Repeat the test for the Cathode side as follows:

    1. Using a DVM as a DC mA meter, (set meter range to one decimal place.)

    1. Connect its red lead to TP9 (CCAL1) on the mA Board.

    1. Connect its black lead to TP14 (CCAL2) on the mA Board.

    1. (See Illustration 1, for OBC Board Slot Assignments).

  1. Locate the Cathode HV Tank Measurement board. If a wire from the harness is present on TP5, the following test lead is not required. If no wire is present, install a test lead as follows:

    1. Using a jumper with a 68 ohm 5 watt resistor in series.

    1. Connect one end to TP14 (CCAL2) on the mA board.

    1. Connect the other end to TP5 on the Cathode Tank measurement board.

      (Step Details)

  1. Record the displayed Cathode mA values on FORM 4879 (Data Record) for "Circuit OFF" and "Circuit ON". A guideline for "Circuit ON" values is 140mA to 200mA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the test equipment.

  1. Switch on the 550v enable.

2  Finalization
