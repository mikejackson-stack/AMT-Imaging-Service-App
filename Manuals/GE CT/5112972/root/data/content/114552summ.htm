Direction  5112972-800,  Rev#  2

CTPM-0803 (Replace Absolute Filter)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Boot down the system - cancel to “R" level then type release DZ0<CR>. You should get the “!" prompt.

  1. Power down the spindle. When it stops, turn off the DC power switch.

  1. Remove the front cover (See Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen, then unfasten the filter holding strap (See Illustration 1).

  1. Remove the filter.

  1. Install new filter with the air flow arrow pointing up. Be very careful not to damage the filter or the filter edge seal when installing it.

  1. Tighten the holding strap snugly.

  1. Write the date on the new filter.

  1. Disable the servo by flipping the switch on the edge of the drive logic board (See Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Power-up DC power and spindle and carefully check for air leaks around the top and bottom of filter.

  1. Examine old filter input side. It should be either white like the output side or slightly gray from dirt. If it is excessively gray with dirt, then the frequency of changing the filter should be increased to four per year instead of two per year.

  1. Power-down the spindle to reset fault led.

  1. Re-enable the servo switch.

  1. Replace the covers and return to normal operation.
