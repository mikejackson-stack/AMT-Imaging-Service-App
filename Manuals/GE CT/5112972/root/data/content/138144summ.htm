CTPM-1501 (Inspect Filter)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

POTENTIAL FOR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.

USING VACUUM HOSE NEAR LIVE ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT MAY CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE DUE TO STATIC.

MAKE SURE EQUIPMENT IS TURNED OFF PRIOR TO VACUUMING NEAR ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT.

 
 

  1. For the first year, inspect the PDU filter every six months for accumulation of lint and dirt. If it is dirty, clean with vacuum or compressed air, and continue to inspect and clean it semi-annually.

  1. If the filter has not accumulated lint and dirt after the first six months of inspection, change the inspection schedule to yearly.
