CT Planned Maintenance Procedures

PM Schedule - LightSpeed QXi

These schedules are for LightSpeed QX/i product only, as approved by CT Engineering. The Planned Maintenance is based on (4) service cycles per year at 500k revolution intervals or 3 months, whichever comes first.

NOTE:Last Revised: June 7, 2007

  • Schedule A - PM cycle begins when Rev Counter shows xxx500k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule B - Next 500k Rev. When Rev Counter shows xxx1000k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule C - When Rev Counter increments another 500k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule D - When Rev Counter increments another 500k revs or 3 months.

NOTE:PM time without camera:

  • Schedule A - 4.2 hours

  • Schedule B - 4.3 hour

  • Schedule C - 6.2 hour

  • Schedule D - 4.3 hour

NOTE:* This PM procedure applies only to systems with filters on the DAS duct fan assembly.

** This product can use the ETC OR the Schleifring style of slip ring. Use the appropriate PM procedure.
