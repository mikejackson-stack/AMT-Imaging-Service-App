4  Procedure

  1. Drive cradle along full length of travel and inspect bottom for cracks, tape, and residue. Remove any tape that is found. (See Illustration 1).

  1. Check the edges and top of table for contaminates such as barium, contrast, etc. Clean to prevent artifacts.

  1. Verify that cradle and I.S. drive smoothly along full length of travel.
