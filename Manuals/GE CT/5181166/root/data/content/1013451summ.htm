2  Procedure

2.1   Equipped Fan Inverter Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedure.

  1. Remove side top and front gantry covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Cut any ty-wraps holding the cables to the Fan Inverter.

  1. Disconnect the power cable connector of the Fan Inverter.

  1. Unscrew the 2 screws, and remove the Fan Inverter from the Power Unit.

    (Step Details)

2.2   Equipped Fan Inverter Installation

  1. Place the new Fan Inverter on the Power Unit.

  1. Connect the power cable connector of the Fan Inverter.

  1. Fasten the cables to the Fan Inverter using tie-wraps.

3  Finalization

  1. Verify that the Fan is operating normally.
