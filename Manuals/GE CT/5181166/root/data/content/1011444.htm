References to Illustrations, Drawings, and Pictures

Visual objects such as illustrations, digital pictures, figures, drawings and graphics are placed into a document to support the steps of the procedure. Visual objects can be an approximate representation of the device, it's function; or they may provide spatial orientation information. Use of visual objects, as a procedural guide only, is an invalid use of the document. You must read and understand the steps outlined to properly execute the procedure. If any conflict between the visual object and the written step-by-step procedure is found, seek Field Leadership expertise to clarify the procedural intent.
