ABS Encoder Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 
 

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cradle (Refer to Cradle)

  1. Move the cradle carriage to the mechanical IN limit position by hand.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the following cover and component from the Table:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable connector leading from the encoder.

  1. Remove a screw, and remove the encoder wire from the wire hook located on the underside of the cradle carriage.

  1. Remove the encoder assy from the encoder holder by unscrewing its 2screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new encoder assembly by referring to Step 8 through Step 6.

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.
