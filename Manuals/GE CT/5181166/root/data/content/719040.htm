3  Procedure

3.1  Bottom Cover

Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 
 

  1. Move the cradle OUT to its fully retracted position (farthest from Gantry).

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    NOTE:If the Table up/down movement is inoperative, use the service power cable to raise the Table (refer to Enforced Table Elevation).

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Unscrew the two screws and remove the rear bottom cover.

  1. Remove the four screw covers from foot pedals.

  1. Unscrew the four Allen screws and remove the four foot pedals.

  1. Remove the four screw caps from the front bottom cover.

  1. Unscrew the four screws and remove the front bottom cover.

    Illustration 1:  Table Front and Bottom Covers

3.2  Top Cover / Middle Side Cover

Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 
 

  1. Move the cradle OUT to its fully retracted position (farthest from Gantry).

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    NOTE:If the Table up/down movement is inoperative, use the service power cable to raise the Table (refer to Enforced Table Elevation).

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “HVDC” , “120VAC” and “Axial Drive” and switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the top cover(left or right) bottom holding screws. The Illustration 2 shows the screw locations.

  1. Slide the top cover (left or right) free from the Table until the touch sensor switch connectors are visible.

  1. Disconnect the touch sensor switch cable connectors.

  1. Remove the top cover (left or right).

  1. Remove the Middle Side cover (left or right) bottom holding screws. The Illustration 2 shows the screw locations.

  1. Slide the Middle Side cover (left or right) free from the Table until the touch sensor switch connector is visible.

  1. Disconnect the touch sensor switch cable connector.

  1. Remove the Middle Side cover (left or right).

    Illustration 2:  Table Top Covers / Middle Side Covers
