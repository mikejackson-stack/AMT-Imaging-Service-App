1.2   Sensor Test

The sensors are Piezoelectric devices that send a signal to the Balance Sensor Buffer board proportional to the amplitude of the vibration sensed.

  1. Make sure the sensor boards are torqued to 20.4 in-lbs (2.3 N-m) to the gantry base. A loose sensor board will give bad results.

  1. Connect a voltmeter between TP3 or TP4 (Sensor 1 or 2) reference to TP5 ground on the Balance Sensor Buffer Bd. With the cover off the sensor under test, blow or “lightly” tap on the sensor. You will see a variation in voltage signal amplitude coincident with the tap or air flow across the sensor in the range of 0mV to a few hundred millivolts offset from ambient.

  1. If the prior step did not produce any change in readings, the sensors can be isolated and tested in the same way by disconnecting the sensor from the buffer board and connecting the voltmeter to the 2 wires from the sensor under test. There is no bias voltage required by the sensors.
