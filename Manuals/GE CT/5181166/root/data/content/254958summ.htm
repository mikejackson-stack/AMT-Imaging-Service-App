Shorted Inverter Test (LS16, Ultra, Plus)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Generator input line in an acceptable range (670VDC +/-10%).

-

-

PPC kV control board alive and running: DS17 - DS24 Leds are lit successively or a combination of them blink.

-

-

Inverter gate_cmd board DC supply present: DS300 neon is lit.

-

-

Inverter quad snubber board DC supply present: DS1-DS2 led are lit.

-

-

2  Procedure

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Remove gantry side and front covers.

  1. Disconnect the HV Tank primary cables from the inverter. Take care not to disconnect at the same time the plate cable connected to the quad snubber board which is tightened with the HV Tank primary cables.

  1. Install the short circuit cable (2217152) included in the first aid spare. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. The parallel inductor and serial inductor must be connected.

  1. Tighten the Azimuth lock pin.

  1. Restore power to the gantry.

  1. Enable the 120VAC and HVDC using the Service Switch Panel. Press ESTOP DRIVES RESET also.

  1. Verify that the DS1-DS2 leds on inverter quad snubbers board is lit.

  1. Verify that the DS300 neon on inverter gate_cmd board is lit.

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL] .

  1. Launch the [SHORTED INVERTER DIAGNOSTIC] tool.

  1. Run the diagnostic. Verify:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper lockout / tagout procedure.

  1. Remove the short circuit cable, reconnect the HV Tank primary cables.

3  Finalization

  1. If this test fails, and the “Inverter Gate Command Test” passed, your system probably has an inverter problem.

  1. Refer to the following troubleshooting:

    1. Note 1: Ilp and Ilr currents not detected.

      (Step Details)

    1. Check the -15V (Led DS2) on PPC kV control board (see block diagram).

      (Step Details)

    1. Power off the Generator. Wait until all neons are off.

    1. Check that the currents transformers (capacitor set) to gate_cmd board cable is correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Check that the inverter inductors are correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Check that HV Tank is correctly connected to the capacitors set. If yes:

    1. Check that the gate_cmd to HV Tank cable and HV Tank to PPC kV control cables are correctly connected.

      (Step Details)

    1. Reconnect all the cables.

  1. Note 2: Ilp current not detected.

    1. Power off the Generator. Wait until all neons are off.

    1. Check that the parallel inductor is correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Check that the parallel inductor impedance is 0 Ohms. If no: replace inverter. If yes:

    1. Check that inverter capacitors (capacitors set) are not broken. If yes, replace the capacitor set. Else:

    1. Disconnect the currents transformers to gate_cmd board cable. Check that the parallel current transformer impedance is 0. If no: replace the capacitor set. Else:

    1. Check that the gate_cmd to HV Tank cable and HV Tank to PPC kV control cables are correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Disconnect the HV Tank to PPC kV control cable. Check that the impedance between pin20 and pin21 of J2 of HV Tank is 3,3Ohms. If no replace the inverter. If yes: replace PPC kV control board.

    1. Reconnect all the cables.

  1. Note 3: Ilr current not detected.

    1. Power off the Generator. Wait until all neons are off.

    1. Check that the inductors are correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Disconnect the currents transformers to gate_cmd board cable. Check that the serial current transformer impedance is 0. If no: replace capacitor set. Else:

    1. Check that inverter capacitors (capacitors set) are not broken. If yes, replace the capacitor set. Else:

    1. Check that the gate_cmd to HV Tank cable and HV Tank to PPC kV control cables are correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Disconnect the HV Tank to PPC kV control cable. Check that the impedance between pin20 and pin21 of J2 of HV Tank is 5 Ohms. If no replace inverter. If yes: replace PPC kV control board.

    1. Reconnect all the cables.

  1. Note 4: Ilr current resonant frequency is lower than expected.

    1. Power off the Generator. Wait until all neon’s are off.

    1. Check that the inductors are correctly connected. If yes:

    1. Check that inverter capacitors (capacitors set) are not broken. If yes: replace the capacitor set. Else:

    1. Disconnect the currents transformers to gate_cmd board cable. Check that the parallel current transformer impedance is 0. If no: replace the capacitor set. Else: replace PPC kV control board.

    1. Reconnect all the cables.
