ORP Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

NOTE:Some steps may require service in blind areas.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

Gantry rotated to a position that offers the best service access, before turning off gantry 120/ 208 VAC.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Control Panel.

  1. Position ORP at the 2 o'clock position.

  1. Put on the grounding wrist strap.

  1. Remove cabling from front of the ORP Assembly.

  1. Remove the two (2) large screws to remove the ORP Assembly.

  1. Remove the two (2) cover screws and remove cover.

  1. Remove seven (7) screws connecting the ORP board to the ORP Assembly.

  1. Remove the defective ORP board.

  1. Install the new ORP board in reverse order.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO and Power-Up system.

  1. On the console, run Flash Download.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
