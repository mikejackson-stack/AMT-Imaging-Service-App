2  Procedure

2.1  Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 48cm Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, and center the 48cm Phantom.

    • Align phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Level phantom using bubble level and the Z Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    • Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] utility in the left head [SCANNER UTILITIES] selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Set up the system to scan a four scan, 16 image, 48cm Phantom image series.

    MANUAL SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    Refer to Table 1 and setup an Axial scan with the parameters as shown.

    AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT] .

    2. Type the following entries in the two listed Patient Information fields:

      Patient ID: Service

      Name: 48cm Phantom Image Series

    3. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab under Anatomical Selector.

    4. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING] .

    5. On the Service Category Protocol List, click on the [40.10 IMAGE SERIES 48CM] selection for 4/8 slice system; click on the [45.7 IMAGE SERIES 48CM] selection for 16 slice system.

    6. On the ExamRx protocol parameter display, select the 1st Series for 4/8 slice system(Series Description: QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s); select the 3rd Series for 16 slice system(Series Description: 48 16x1.25/120kV/200mA/2s

    7. Set internal Landmark.

  1. Acquire a four-scan, 16 image, 48 cm Phantom image series.

2.2  Brightness Uniformity and Noise

  1. Image Performance Verification:

    Refer to “Auto 1x Image Analysis Tool - Overview” and “Auto-1x Image Analysis Sub-Tests: Descriptions” in Image Analysis Tools, for details on Auto 1x Image Analysis tools.

  1. Specifications:

    Each Row (2A, 1A, 1B, and 2B) of the series must pass 48cm Brightness Uniformity and Noise (for the first series scan parameters) specifications:

    AvXo - AvXc:< ± 8.5
    AvSDo:< 70.0

  1. Recommended Failure Recovery:

    1. Perform [DETAILED CAL] .

    1. Perform [AUTO CT# ADJUST] .

    1. Repeat this procedure to verify 48cm Phantom Image Performance.
