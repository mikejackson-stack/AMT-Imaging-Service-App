4  Procedure

Understand and follow the Safety Guidelines Manual.
 
 

  1. Move the cradle to the OUT mechanical limit position by hand.

  1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    NOTE:If the Table up/down movement is inoperative, use the service power cable to raise the Table (refer to Enforced Table Elevation) .

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off “120VAC”, “Axial Drive” and “HVDC” switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the following cover from the Table:

    • Rear Bottom Cover (Refer to Table Cover Removal)

    • -

  1. Remove 4 screws, and slide the power driver support toward the Gantry.

    Illustration 1:  Location of Filter

  1. Remove the Stepping Motor Driver (refer to Stepping Motor Driver Replacement)

  1. Remove a plastic cover by unscrewing its 4 screws.

    Illustration 2:  Plastic Cover Removal

  1. Mark and remove the wires from the Filter.

  1. Remove the Filter by unscrewing its 2 screws.

    Illustration 3:  Filter Removal

  1. Install the new Filter by referring to Step 9 through Step 5.

  1. Restore the Table to original configuration.

5  Finalization

No finalization required.
