1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR ELECTRIC SHOCK.
LETHAL VOLTAGES MAY BE PRESENT IN THE GANTRY.
Follow proper Electrical Lock-out/Tag-out procedures.

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY (PINCH, CRUSH, ENTANGLEMENT OR IMPACT INJURIES ARE POSSIBLE).
GANTRY HAS ROTATING ASSEMBLY.
Use the Gantry’s Rotational Lock, to prevent uncommanded motion of the Gantry’s rotating assembly.

Potential HV Cable Damage.

Do not sharply bend HV cables, or damage the cables. The minimum allowed radius of bent HV cables is 6.35 cm (2.5 in.).

 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

3.3   Old Tube Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove and set aside gantry side, top and front covers.

  1. Remove the M12 screws holding support bracket for the right front gantry cover and set the assembly aside. It may be necessary to tilt the gantry back to remove the third bolt (not normally installed).

    (Step Details)

If the gantry must be tilted, remember to tilt the gantry back to zero degrees.
 
 

  1. Turn off the AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, and 120VAC switches.

  1. Turn off facility power to PDU and lockout/tagout according to lockout/tagout procedure.

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the x-ray tube reaches the 2 o’clock position.

  1. Remove the two tube mounting bolts and washers which are located at right side of tube (from front side of gantry). Continue to rotate the gantry unti the x-ray tube reaches about 3 o’clock position.

    (Step Details)

CRUSH POINT.
IF THE GANTRY IS NOT SECURELY LOCKED, UPON REMOVAL OF THE X-RAY TUBE, THE GANTRY WILL VIOLENTLY ROTATE DUE TO WEIGHT IMBALANCE.
Ensure the gantry is securely locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

Make sure the tube is at 90 degrees so the tube hangs at the correct engagement angle for removal and installation.
 
 

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock, and check that the gantry is securely locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
IF THE HOIST HOOK'S LOCK NUT IS LOOSE, THEN THE X-RAY TUBE MIGHT DROP FROM THE HOIST, WITH THE RESULTING IN SERIOUS INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL.
before using the hoist, check to make certain that the hook's lock nut is not loose. If it is loose, do not use the hoist. instead, order a new hoist.

  1. Insert the lifting post, boom and chain hoist.

  1. Disconnect the 12 pin tube I.D. system cable, from the top of the tube unit.

  1. Disconnect the 4 pin mate-n-lock pump and fan power system cable.

  1. Remove the anode and the cathode cable:

    (Step Details)

Crush Hazard.
Remove the mounting bars in the following (lower/upper) order to lessen the risk of injury to your hand. Keep one hand under the bolt and pressure plate while unfastening it. This is to prevent them from falling into the fan that is attached to the tube.

  1. Remove the other 2 mounting bolts and washers.

  1. Carefully swing the tube clear of the gantry.

    (Step Details)

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
TUBE CAN SEPARATE FROM GANTRY.
DO NOT REMOVE FACTORY INSTALLED MOUNTING PLATE.

  1. If the replacement tube has a mounting plate attached (see Illustration 6), DO NOT REMOVE IT. Skip Step 15 and Step 16.

    (Step Details)

Potential for IQ Artifacts.

When removing the mounting plate from the tube, be careful with the Copper Filter. It should be free of debris, scratches and dust. Particles create artifacts in the image by affecting the attenuation properties of the copper filter.

 
 

  1. If the replacement tube does NOT have a mounting plate attached, remove the mounting plate by removing the four M10 x 18 hex head screws.

  1. If new tube has no mounting plate attached, inspect the copper filter. The copper filter should be clean, dent and scratch free:

    (Step Details)

The following tools are required for this inspection procedure:

  • Phillips #2 Screwdriver

  • Flat blade Screwdriver

  • Bright Flashlight

  1. Inspect the Bowtie Filter. It is possible that the Bowtie Filter is contaminated and the Primary Copper Filter is not contaminated.

    1. Remove the Collimator Control Board Sheet Metal Cover. See Illustration 8.

    VOLTAGE IS PRESENT.

    Do not force the filter if it feels stuck. Damage to the limit switch can result.

    Do not move the filter more than necessary for inspection. The filter can fall off the drive screw.

     
     

    1. With power removed from the gantry, use a flat blade screwdriver and position the bowtie filter assembly so it is visible through the input port or tube side of the collimator assembly.

      (Step Details)

    1. Using a flashlight, inspect the bowtie filter for contamination. Reference Illustration 10.

3.4   New Tube Installation

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
TUBE CAN SEPARATE FROM GANTRY, IF NOT TORQUED CORRECTLY.
Always use proper torque on all fasteners.

  1. Allow the tube unit to warm to room temperature before you install it.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
TUBE CAN SEPARATE FROM GANTRY.
Do not remove factory installed mounting plate.

  1. If the new tube has a factory installed mounting plate, DO NOT REMOVE IT. Skip to Step 4.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
IF YOU TURN THE TORQUE WRENCH MORE THAN 90º (1/4 TURN) WHILE APPLYING FINAL TORQUE, THE MOUNTING THREADS ARE STRIPPED.
Do not use this tube.

  1. Attach the mounting plate from the old tube using new M10 x 18mm bolts provided with the tube. Do NOT use Loctite.

    (Step Details)

Potential for IQ artifacts.

When attaching the mounting plate on the tube, be careful with the Copper Filter. It should be free of debris, scratches and dust. Particles create artifacts in the image. Use a lint free wipe to clean, if needed.

 
 

  1. Re-check facility power and make sure it is off.

SEVERE INJURY TO PATIENT COULD OCCUR.
USING THE WRONG BOLTS COULD PLACE STRESS ON BOLTS, CAUSING TUBE TO SEPARATE FROM GANTRY.
Use proper bolts.

    1. Position the tube on the gantry: The “crosses” on the mounting plate and on the collimator should fit in perfectly when the tube is aligned properly.

      (Step Details)

    1. Loosen the gantry rotation lock, and rotate the tube to about 2 o’clock position, then Engage gantry rotational lock again.

    1. Fasten the mounting plate to rotation structure with another two mounting bolts which are located at the lower position of tube, and set pre-load torque to:

      (Step Details)

    1. Set final torque to all four bolts to:

      (Step Details)

  1. Attach the tube I.D. cable to the 12 pin mate-N-Lock connector on top of the tube.

  1. Attach the tube pump and fan power cable to the 4 pin mate-N-Lock connector.

  1. Remove the plastic cap plug from each cable receptacle on the tube unit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lightly wet the new rubber quad ring with transformer oil (917).

  1. Return the quad ring to its slot at the top of the receptacle retaining ring.

  1. Pour transformer oil (917) into the receptacle to a depth of 10 mm (0.375 in).

Incorrectly routed or secured HV cables will result in damage to the HV cables and/or other parts of the gantry.
 
 

  1. Be sure to route the HV cable as shown in Illustration 12.

  1. Align the cable terminal orienting key with the notch in the receptacle.

Do not over tighten the locking ring. Over tightening can deform the cable plug sealing surfaces, break the oil seal between receptacle and housing, twist the receptacle, and disrupt internal wiring.
 
 

  1. Slowly insert the cable, to engage the connector pins, and seat the cable in the well.

    (Step Details)

IF YOU GET OIL ON YOUR HANDS, WASH THEM NOW.
 
 

  1. Carefully wipe up all excess oil.

  1. Secure HV Cables using large tie-wraps, as shown in Illustration 12.

  1. Disconnect hoist from tube and boom.

  1. Remove the post and boom from the gantry. Reference Illustration 4.

  1. Check for oil leaks:

  1. Turn on gantry 120 VAC, HVDC ENABLE and AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE on the service switch panel. Wait at least 10 minutes to warm up the filament.

    1. Tie-wraps and cables are in place.
