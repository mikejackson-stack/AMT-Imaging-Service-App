4  Procedure

  1. Run [NEW TUBE CONFIG] program.

  1. Enter the failure codes and new values.

  1. Exit.

    • The console will reboot after performing this operation.

    • The system will set the tube temperature to hot, after configuring a new tube. The cooling delay is approximately 90 minutes.
