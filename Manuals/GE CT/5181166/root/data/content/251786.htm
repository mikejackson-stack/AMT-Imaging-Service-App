4  Procedure

4.1  PI Filter Board Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove side top and front gantry covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the power unit reaches the 3 o’clock position.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

  1. Remove the PI Filter covers (top and side).

  1. Disconnect the 4 cables from the PI Filter board.

  1. Unscrew the 6 screws, and remove the PI Filter board from the Power Unit.

    Illustration 1:  PI Filter Board

4.2  PI Filter Board Installation

  1. Ensure that Lockout/Tagout procedure has been applied, and that gantry power is removed.

  1. Install new PI Filter board and re-connect all wires.

  1. Replace PI Filter covers.

5  Finalization

  1. Run System Scanning Test verify that the system is operational.
