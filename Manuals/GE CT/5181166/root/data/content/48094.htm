2  Overview

Use the System Scanning Test to verify hardware functionality. Review images for visible artifacts, and review the message log for unacceptable errors.

Last Revised:September 2004

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

Avoid Detector Damage. Never scan above 50 mA without first placing a phantom in the field of view. Levels in excess of 50 mA can cause temporary radiation damage to the detector that lasts several hours. If you acquire image series cals with a radiation damaged detector, the cals may cause artifacts in subsequent image series scans.
 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 
 

3.2  Required Conditions
