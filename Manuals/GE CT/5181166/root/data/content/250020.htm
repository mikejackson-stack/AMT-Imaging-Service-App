4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off the three power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Disconnect J1 and left control cables.

  1. Loosen the five screws (For BrightSpeed, four screws) that fasten the start panel to the cover.

  1. Replace the control panel and reconnect J1 and left control cables.

Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Reassemble the gantry.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform a functional scan to confirm proper operation.
