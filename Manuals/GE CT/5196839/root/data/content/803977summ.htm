Gantry Rotation Interference Touch Strip Replace

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Disconnect touch strip cable.

  1. Remove gantry rotation touch strip.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install new gantry rotation touch strip.

4  Finalization

  1. Reinstall removed gantry covers.

  1. Ensure that all top cover connections are reconnected.

  1. Ensure that no cables are left in the rotating path.

  1. Return dollies to storage area.
