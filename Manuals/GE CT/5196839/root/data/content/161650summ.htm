Gantry Rotation Safety Check

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CRUSH HAZARD
EQUIPMENT COULD EJECT FROM GANTRY.
Ensure that no personnel are in the same room/area as the gantry prior to and during this scan.

1  Procedure

  1. Launch Diagnostic Data Collection (DDC) Utility:

    1. Open the Common Service Desktop

    1. Click on the [Diagnostics] Icon

    1. Click on the [Data Acquisition] Folder

    1. Click on the [Diagnostic Data Collection] Menu Item

  1. Prepare System for DDC Scan. Refer to Illustration 1.

    1. Select [Rotating X-Ray Off]

    1. Type: 120 in Scan Time (sec) field.

    1. Type: 0 in the Gantry Velocity (sec/rev) field and press <Enter>. The system will then default to the fastest allowable service mode revolution time.

    1. No of Scans = 5. This will result in 10 minutes of rotation.

    1. Verify other settings, as shown.

    1. Click on [Accept RX] button, to send scan request

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the Scan button on the Console, to start each DDC Scan. When complete, the DDC Utility dismisses the Scan List GUI and the Real Time Information window.

  1. If no issues arise, gantry rotation capability is safe for use.
