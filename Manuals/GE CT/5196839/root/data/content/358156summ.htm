Direction  5196839-800,  Rev#  6

IMS Cable Bear Assy Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table Covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two screws holding the cable duct cover, and remove the cable duct cover from the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Touch Sensor Assy R (it is necessary to disconnect the cable connector), and put it on the Table frame.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect four cable connectors and a GND wire on the cable duct, and remove any clamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect two cable connectors and a GND wire from the GTCN board, and remove any clamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two support brackets (holding the IMS cable guide to the IMS frame) by unscrewing 2 (x2) screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any tie-wraps holding the IMS cable to the Table frame, and remove any clamps, then disconnect the following cable connectors:

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the IMS so that the cable outlet is behind the rail support.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove a screw holding the front duct cover to the under Table frame and remove the IMS cable bear assembly from the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new IMS Cable Bear Assy by referring to Step 12through Step 7.

  1. Verify that the top surface of the support bracket is parallel with the top surface of the Table frame.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the cable connector of the IMS motor does not interfere with IMS/Cradle movement.

  1. Verify that the cables in the cable guide have no deflection and are not twisted.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the cables on the Table frame have no deflection.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Re-install the touch sensor assy R, all removed Table trays and covers.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Verify that the IMS moves IN and OUT normally.
