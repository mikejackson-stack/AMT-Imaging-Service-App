48cm Phantom Img Series Verif (LS 16, Ultra, Plus)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 48cm Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, and center the 48cm Phantom.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the system to scan a single, 64 image, 48cm Phantom series.

    (Step Details)

  1. Acquire a single, 64 image, 4 scan, image series of the 48 cm Phantom.

1.2   Image Performance Verification

1.2.1  Series Means (AvXo-AvXc and AvSDo) and Center Smudge

  1. Launch the Image Analysis Tool.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the Service Desktop.

    1. From the Service Desktop upper navigational bar, click on the [IMAGE QUALITY] icon.

    1. In the left navigational area directory, click on [IMAGE ANALYSIS] .

      (Step Details)

  1. Using the [AUTO 1X] Tool on the Image Analysis screen, generate a Series Means and Center Smudge report for the 1st series of the 48cm Phantom acquisition.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select the 48cm Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series.

    1. On the Image Analysis Tool window, click on the [AUTO 1X] diamond shaped button.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 48CM] test from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right and select [16X1.25/120KV/400MA/2S] from the test pull-down menu, and continue to slide cursor to the right to select [AUTO] .

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

  1. (For LS 16) Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Series Means (AvXo-AvXc, and AvSDc) data and the Center Smudge data for each row in Table 2.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1.

    1. Record the Series Means (AvXo-AvXc, and AvSDc) data and the Center Smudge data for each row in Table 2.

      (Step Details)

  1. (For LS Ultra, Plus) Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Series Means (AvXo-AvXc, and AvSDc) data and the Center Smudge data for each row in Table 3.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1.

    1. Record the Series Means (AvXo-AvXc, and AvSDc) data and the Center Smudge data for each row in Table 3.

      (Step Details)

1.2.2  Band and Streak Artifact

  1. Review the 48cm Phantom images acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series for Bands and Streaks using the Image Works Desktop.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop.

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, select the 48cm Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series.

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, click on the [VIEWER] button.

    1. While paging through the 64 images in the series, analyze images for any Band or Streak artifacts. Adjust Window/Level using the center mouse button, as required.

    1. Note any image that appears to have a Band or Streak artifact.

  1. Verify any images noted above ( Step 1.e) meet 48cm Phantom Band or Streak Image Performance requirements.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the Service Desktop.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select an image of the 48cm Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series that appeared to have a Band or Streak while reviewing the series in Image Works.

    1. On the Image Analysis Tool window, click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 48CM] test from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right and select [16X1.25/120KV/400MA/2S] from the test pull-down menu, and continue to slide cursor to the right to select [BAND] or [STREAK] .

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

      (Step Details)

    1. Position and size the Band or Streak ROI using the left cursor button.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click on [ACCEPT MODIFICATION] twice, to generate a report.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Repeat steps b through g, for each image noted in Step 1.e.

      (Step Details)
