2  Overview

Verify that the CAM A/B Motor is functional.

Last Revised:August 2004

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

Before beginning this procedure, please read Equipment Service - Gantry, located in the Safety tab of this publication.
 
 

3.2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

For this procedure, refer to the Rotating Controller Interface Bus (RCIB) Functional Interconnect.

-

-

For this procedure, refer to the X-Ray Collimation and Filtration Functional Interconnect.

-

-

4  Procedure

  1. Ohm CAM A (or CAM B) motor windings.

  1. Swap CAM drive harness with other CAM.

  1. Perform CAM A/B Encoder Check

    NOTE:If the Manual Encoder test passes but failures occur under motor control, refer to Cam Drive Motor and Flex Coupling Replacement. The set screw on the shaft internal to the collimator might be loose.

5  Finalization

No finalization required.
