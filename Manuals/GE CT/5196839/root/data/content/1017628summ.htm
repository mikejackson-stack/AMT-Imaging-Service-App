Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

3  Procedure

  1. Prepare Table for Scanning:

    1. Using the Gantry Control Panel buttons, move the Table out of the Gantry until the Table stops longitudinal.

    1. Attach phantom holder to Gantry side of Table.

    1. Mount 20cm QA Phantom on to phantom holder.

    1. On the Gantry Control Panel, press the [ALIGNMENT LIGHTS] button.

    1. Using the Gantry Control Panel buttons, move the Table into the Gantry until the axial internal alignment lights are lined up with the phantom’s axial reference mark.

    1. Level phantom using bubble level and the Z-Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    1. Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] procedure in the left head SCANNER UTILITIES selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

    1. Using the Gantry Control Panel buttons, move the Table until the internal alignment lights are lined up with the water part of phantom. The Scan below should use the water part of the QA phantom.

  1. Wait 30 minutes without scanning to let the tube cool down.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up and complete HotTube QA20cm exam process.

    1. Click the [EXAM RX] icon button located in the Desktop / Status area.

    1. At the lower left corner of the Scan monitor, click the [NEW PATIENT] button.

    1. From the Patient Information section, type Service in the “Patient ID” edit box and HotTube QA in the “Patient Name” edit box.

    1. From Protocol Selection Anatomical Selector section, click the Service tab.

    1. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING] .

    1. On the Service Category Protocol List, click on the [45.16 HotTube QA20cm] selection.

      (Step Details)

    1. On the Gantry Control Panel, click the [ALIGNMENT LIGHTS] button. Make sure the scan will be performed at water part of QA phantom.

    1. On the Gantry Control Panel, click the [INTERNAL LANDMARK] button.

    1. On the Series 1 Prescription screen, click the [CONFIRM] button and complete the scan with no errors logged.

    1. Click the [NEXT SERIES] button on the prescription screen.

    1. On the Series 2 Prescription screen, click the [CONFIRM] button and complete the scan with no errors logged.

    1. Press the [END EXAM] button on prescription screen. The “PPS Response” box might be displayed or prescription screen disappears. If the “PPS Response” box is displayed, press the COMPLETE button on “PPS Response” box. The “PPS Response” box and the prescription screen disappears.

  1. Center Smudge Index HotTube QA20cm Exam Analysis:

    1. Click the [IMAGEWORKS] icon button located in the Desktop / Status area.

    1. From the ImageWorks browser, select the exam with the HotTube QA name and the first 2 series from the exam list.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click the [UNIX – LEFT SIDE] button located on the ImageWorks Desktop Toolbar.

    1. At the terminal window prompt, type run_RT16smt and press [ENTER] . Once the program has finished executing, the analysis results are displayed.

    1. Review the Row Average Smudge Index results for Series 1, Series 2.

      (Step Details)

  1. Close the Terminal window by typing exit at the prompt and pressing <Enter>.

  1. If center smudge check fails, run air ratio calibration, run Detailed Calibration as needed.
