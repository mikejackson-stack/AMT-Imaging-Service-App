Elevation Characterization Procedures

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Elevation Characterization

  1. Move the cradle and IMS table to the OUT-limit position.

  1. Remove the front base cover, right base cover, and top right cover.

  1. Set the service switch (MODE_SEL) to the SERVICE to enter the service mode.

  1. Confirm that the “ELEV” LED on the GTCB board in the table is ON. If it is NOT ON, press the “Motion Target” button until “ELEV” LED is ON.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press and hold the service switch (ACTION) to the IN/UP position to move the table to the mechanical UP-limit position.

  1. Confirm that the “POT_ADJ UP” LED (Green) is ON. If it is NOT ON, perform Height Potentiometer Adjustment. Refer to Height Potentiometer Adjustment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the two “Characterize” Buttons at the same time to start characterization.

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm that the “UP/IN” LED is ON. See Illustration 1.

  1. Using the Service Switches, move the table at 980 +/- 0.5mm of the distance between characterization points (the bottom of the table base, not floor, to the front cutout of the IMS frame as shown).

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the two “Characterize” Buttons at the same time. See Illustration 3.

  1. Confirm that the “DOWN/OUT” LED is ON. See Illustration 1.

  1. Using the Service Switch (ACTION), move the table at 700 +/- 0.5mm of the distance between characterization points (the bottom of the table base, not floor, to the front cutout of the IMS frame as shown). See Illustration 4.

  1. Press the two “Characterize” Buttons at the same time to start characterization. See Illustration 3

  1. Confirm that both of “UP/IN” LED and “DOWN/OUT” LED are ON. See Illustration 1.

  1. Using the Service Switch, move the table to the mechanical down-limit position.

  1. Press the two “Characterize” Buttons at the same time. See Illustration 3.

  1. 5 - 10 seconds later (to write characterization data to the flash memory), confirm that both of LED's (UP/IN and DOWN/OUT) are OFF. See Illustration 1.

  1. If both of LED's blink during procedures, it means that characterization fails. Retry characterization.

1.2   Height Potentiometer Adjustment

When the Height Potentiometer (elevation potentiometer) is replaced or it must be adjusted again, perform this procedure.

  1. Using the Service Switches, move the table to the mechanical UP-limit position.

  1. Confirm that the “POT_ADJ UP” LED (Green) is ON. If it is NOT ON, adjust the height potentiometer.

    1. Remove the left base cover, left IMS side cover, front left side cover, rear left side cover, and front left leg plate.

    1. Loosen the two POT bracket bolts to release belt tension.

    1. Rotate the gear of the height POT until “POT_ADJ UP” LED is ON.

    1. While applying proper tension to the POT belt, secure the two POT bracket bolts.

    1. Confirm that the “POT_ADJ UP” LED is ON.

      (Step Details)

  1. Using the Service Switches, move the table to the mechanical DOWN-limit position.

  1. Confirm that the “POT_ADJ DOWN” LED (Yellow) is ON. If it is NOT ON, adjust the height potentiometer again.

  1. When finished with characterization set the service switch (MODE_SEL) to the NORMAL position for customer use.

2  Finalization

  1. Install the covers in the reverse order of removal.
