Inverter Assy Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Perform this step depending on the following Table type:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the Front Base Cover.

  1. Cut any tie-wraps, and disconnect motor wires (U, V, W) from the front of the Inverter Assy.

  1. Disconnect the following cable connectors:

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen a screw, and slide the Inverter Assy toward the Gantry, then remove it from the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new Inverter Assy in place, and tighten the screw.

  1. Re-connect all wires and cable connectors, and fix the cables using tie-wraps.

2  Finalization

  1. Re-install the Table covers.

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Verify that the Table Up/Down function is operating normally.
