2  Procedure

2.1  Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 20cm QA Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, and center the 20cm QA Phantom.

    • Align black line on phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Level phantom using bubble level and the Z Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    • Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] procedure in the left head [SCANNER UTILITIES] selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Set up the system to scan three 20cm QA Phantom image series with a Recon of the 1st series.

    AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    a. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT] .
    b. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information following fields:

    * Patient ID: Service

    * Name: 20cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Acquire the 4th 20cm QA Phantom image series (Series Description: QA3 Sm 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

2.3  Failure Recommended Actions

2.3.1  1st Image Series (4 Image MTF Average) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    The 4 Image MTF Average must pass specifications:

    4 Image MTF Average: 0.65 to 1.00

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.2  2nd Image Series (Visible Lines) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    The Largest 5 line pair patterns (coded F, E, D, C, and B) must be visible for each of the four images in this series.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    NOTE:The most common failure for this test is that the phantom has air bubbles that are obscuring the line pair patterns.

    1. Carefully inspect the 20cm QA Phantom for air bubbles. If required, refill the phantom with water, to eliminate all air bubbles.

    2. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.3  3rd Image Series (Visible Holes) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    The number of visible holes in this image series must pass specifications as follows:

    • Contrast Factor = 2.0 to 3.99: Minimum of 2 holes must be visible (10mm and 7.5mm).

    • Contrast Factor = 4.0 to 7.99: Minimum of 3 holes must be visible (10mm, 7.5mm, and 5mm).

    • Contrast Factor = 8.0 to 12.0: Minimum of 4 holes must be visible (10mm, 7.5mm, 5mm, and 3mm).

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.4  4th Image Series Failure Recovery

  1. Series Means:

    Specifications:

    Both Rows (2A1A, 1B2B) of the series must pass QA#3 Series Means specifications:

    • AvXc: < +/- 3.0.

    • AvXo - AvXc: < +/- 3.0.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.5  QA#3 (AvSDc)

  1. Specifications:

    Both Rows (2A1A, 1B2B) of the series must pass QA#3 AvSDc specifications:

    • AvSDc (< 5K Scans): 2.90 to 3.50.

    • AvSDc (> 5K Scans): 2.80 to 3.60.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.6  Center Artifact or Center Smudge

  1. Specifications:

    All eight images of the series must pass 20cm QA#3 Center Artifact and Center Smudge specifications:

    • Center Artifact factor: +/- 3.5.

    • Center Smudge factor: +/- 2.2.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

2.3.7  Ring, Band, or Streak Artifact Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    • Ring Artifact: Ring Factor < +/- 4.8.

    • Band Artifact: Band Factor +/- 2.8 from 0cm to 8.5cm < +/- 2.8 > 8.5cm.

    • Streak Artifact: Streak Factor < +/- 4.0.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Troubleshoot Ring, Band or Streak artifact failures as described in the Service Manual.

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 20cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series and 20cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.
