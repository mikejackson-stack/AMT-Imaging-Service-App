4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive Enable Gantry Service Switch.

  1. Carefully remove the Gantry (Mylar) Scan Window.

  1. Remove the rear cover.

  1. Remove the axial drive pulley cover (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Axial Drive Pulley Cover

  1. Turn ON the Axial Drive Enable.

  1. Under Axial Diagnostics on the console, initiate a 0.5s rotation for 10 seconds.

  1. Allow the gantry to spin up.

  1. Listen for the noise and, using a flashlight, observe the drive belt tracking. Some Z-axis shifting of the belt is acceptable. The measure of an unacceptable position is the presence of noise and the observation that the belt is contacting the extreme edges of the drive pulley during operation.

POTENTIAL PHYSICAL INJURY
THE SPINNING GANTRY PRESENTS A SIGNIFICANT MECHANICAL HAZARD.
KEEP YOUR HANDS, FEET, LOOSE CLOTHING AND HAIR OUT OF THE GANTRY AREA DURING ROTATION.

  1. Allow the gantry to come to a complete stop.

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive Enable switch.

  1. Loosen, but leave snug, the three (3) bolts securing the motor mount bracket to the stationary frame (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Axial Motor Mount Bracket Bolts

  1. Work the axial drive assembly in the proper direction to correct the skew and then tighten the three (3) mounting bolts to 66 +/- 3 N-m (48.7 +/- 2.2 lb.-ft.). See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Axial Motor Position Adjustment

  1. Repeat the procedure from Step 7 until the noise is eliminated and/or the belt tracks in the center of the drive pulley with minimal Z-axis motion.

  1. Allow the gantry to come to a complete stop.

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive Enable switch.

  1. If removed previously, reinstall the axial drive pulley cover. Bolt torque should be 8 N-m (71 inch-lbs, 6 lb.-ft.).

  1. One at a time, remove the three bolts securing the motor. Clean each of the bolt threads, apply Loctite 271 (p/n: 46-170684P2) and reinstall the bolts with a torque of 66 +/- 3 N-m (48.7 +/- 2.2 lb.-ft.).

  1. Reinstall the rear and top covers. Carefully reinstall the Mylar window.

  1. Turn ON the Axial Drive Enable switch.

  1. Reinstall the side covers.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform a series of test scans to ensure proper system operation.
