Rotor Diagnostic Test (Pro, RT, VCT)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. If desired, you may check any or all of the following:

    1. PPC kV control board alive and running: S0-S7 Leds are lit successively or a combination of them blink

    1. cabling between rotation board and tube checked

    1. rotation board alive and running: DS5 Led blinking

    1. rotation board DC supply present: DS7 neon is lit

  1. Launch the [GENERATOR TOOL] . See tools section for more information.

  1. Launch the [Rotor Diagnostic] tool.

  1. Run the test. A rotor sequence will proceed.

  1. If a problem occurs, check the error code.
