5196839 - LS RT16 and Xtra Ltd Access Service Info (Adv), Rev 6

The information contained in this publication applies to ALL LightSpeed RT16 and Xtra systems (both Limited Access Compliant and non-Limited Access Compliant). Non-Limited Access Compliant systems can not be upgraded to a Limited Access Compliant system. Direction 5173296-800 (Class A) and Direction 5174162-800 (Class C) for LightSpeed RT16 and Xtra non-Limited Access Compliant systems are now obsolete.
