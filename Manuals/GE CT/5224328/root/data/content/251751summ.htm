HEMIT Tank Replacement (LS16, Ultra, Plus)

1  Safety

HV Cable Damage!

Do not sharply bend HV cables, or damage the cables. The minimum allowed radius of bent HV cables is 6.35 cm (2.5 in.).

3  Procedure

3.1   HEMIT Tank Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove the side, top, and front gantry covers.

  1. Turn OFF all the three switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove the two M12 screws from the right front gantry cover mounting bracket, remove and set aside the bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the HEMIT Tank reaches the 9 o’clock position.

  1. Cut any tie-wraps.

  1. Use the spanner wrench to remove the high voltage cable connector from the tank on the power unit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any tie-wraps.

  1. Disconnect the 3 cable connector from the HEMIT Tank.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the HEMIT Tank reaches the 3 o’clock position.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Remove the HEMIT Tank from the gantry:

    SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
    IF THE HOIST HOOK'S LOCK NUT IS LOOSE, THEN THE HEMIT TANK MIGHT DROP FROM THE HOIST, WITH THE RESULTING IN SERIOUS INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL.
    before using the hoist, check to make certain that the hook's lock nut is not loose. If it is loose, do not use the hoist. instead, order a new hoist.

    1. Attach the hoist to the boom arm in the gantry.

    1. Attach the hoist lifting chain to the lifting bracket on the HEMIT Tank bottom.

    1. Remove slack from the hoist chain.

    1. Remove the 4 bolts that fasten the HEMIT Tank to the rotating base.

    1. Use the hoist to lower the HEMIT Tank to the floor.

      (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that Lockout/Tagout procedure has been applied, and that gantry power is removed.

  1. Ensure that gantry rotational lock is engaged and gantry does not rotate by attempting to rotate gantry by hand.
