Direction  5224328-800,  Rev#  5

Home Flag Sensor Board Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

Make sure LOTO procedures are followed for the gantry.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Preparation

  1. Move the table to the home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive and HVDC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Rotate the gantry to place the 48V Power supply behind the service switch panel. This places the tube at about the 10 o'clock position for easier access to the sensor board.

  1. Turn OFF the 120 VAC switch on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers and front cover.

1.2   Replace Sensor Board or Sensor board assembly

  1. Locate the Home Flag Sensor board ( Illustration 1) near the encoder assembly (Gantry 11 o'clock location).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the harness from the sensor board.

  1. If replacing the Sensor board assembly (with bracket) follow this step.

    (Step Details)

  1. If replacing the Sensor board only, follow this step.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace Sensor board or Board Assembly installing the screws previously removed, tightening 1/4 turn past seated.

  1. Reconnect cable to the sensor board.

1.3   Restore Gantry

  1. Slowly rotate gantry by hand and adjust flag position to pass through the center of the opto-sensor on the sensor board. The home flag is next to the 48V Power supply.

  1. Make sure that the flag is centered in the opto-sensor on the sensor board ( Illustration 3) and does not make contact with it. Looking at the side view along the side of the 48V PS as shown in Illustration 4 can help.

    (Step Details)

  1. If necessary, adjust by loosening the screws and slightly shifting the board. (reposition gantry for easy access along side of the tube as done previously)

  1. Perform the Reset the C-Pulse procedure.

  1. Install the gantry front cover, top covers and left side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a [System Scanning Test] from the [Functional Checks] menu of the service manual to ensure system operation.
