MDAS +5 Volt Analog Power Supply Replacement

2  Overview

Replace faulty power supply and adjust voltage as necessary:

  • Remove gantry side and top covers.

  • Tilt gantry back 30 degrees and rotate to access DAS power supplies.

  • Disable gantry 120 vac power.

  • Remove safety shields and disconnect power supply harnesses.

  • Replace failed power supply.

  • Adjust voltages as necessary.

    NOTE:The +5 vdc DAS power supply is located behind the DAS left chassis.

4  Procedure

  1. Position the table to its lowest position.

  1. Remove gantry right and left side covers.

  1. Remove gantry top covers.

  1. Tilt gantry back 30 degrees.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable, HVDC, and 120VAC switches on the Service Switch panel.

  1. Rotate gantry to access DAS power supplies.

  1. Disconnect the rotating power harness as required. Save all hardware for reuse.

  1. Remove the plastic cover by removing four (4) each: 4mm cap screws, flat and lock washers.

Burn Hazard.
Power Supply may be hot to touch.
Use caution to prevent possible burn injury from hot surface contact.

  1. Disconnect the AC and DC connectors.

  1. Remove the three (3) 10mm cap screws that secure the power supply to the detector plate. [Two screws are located at one end of the power supply assembly, and the third is at the other end.] Lift the power supply assembly out of the gantry.

  1. Install the power supply assembly to the detector plate, using the three (3) 10 mm cap screws, flat and lock washers. Set torque on the screws to:

    28.3 lb.-ft.38.4 N-m339.6 lb.-in391.4 kg-cm

  1. Reconnect the supply output and supply power.

  1. Attach the plastic cover to the assembly, using 4mm cap screws, flat and lock washers. Set torque on the screws to:

    0.5 lb.-ft.0.7 N-m6.2 lb.-in7.2 kg-cm

  1. Secure the rotating harness cables as before.
