Emergency Off - Powerware 9155-10 GE UPS Unit

Last Revised13 October 2008

1  Verify UPS Shuts Off

  1. Press any room System Emergency Off (SEO) button.

  2. Make sure that the UPS applies no backup power to the CT system. The power to the CT table, console, and gantry should turn OFF and stay OFF.

2  Power Up the UPS

After you have verified that the UPS shuts off and no power is going to the CT system, use the following steps to power up the UPS:

  1. Verify that the system is ready to receive power and that all persons are clear of any potentially energized equipment.

  2. Set the Manual Bypass switch to theService position.

  3. Verify that the Load and Auxiliary circuit breakers on the UPS are in the ON position and that the Battery breaker is OFF.

  4. Verify that the Line On light on the Main Disconnect & UPS Control Panel (A1) is illuminated, indicating mains power is available.

  5. Verify that the Key switch in the A1 panel is in the ON position, and none of the room SEO buttons have been latched open.

  6. Momentarily press the Start button on the Main Disconnect & UPS Control Panel.

  7. Verify that the Contactor On and UPS On lights on the Main Disconnect & UPS Control Panel are illuminated.

  8. Re-set the UPS battery breaker to the ON position.

  9. Verify that both the UPS and CT scanner begin to power up.

    NOTE:After approximately 30 seconds, the UPS should indicate electronic bypass mode. The UPS should display a flashing green Inverter On indicator and a steady yellow Bypass On indicator.

  10. When the yellow Bypass indicator is on steadily, move the Manual Bypass switch to the UPS position. After a few seconds, the green Inverter On indicator should change to a steady On, and the Bypass indicator should turn Off.

    NOTE:If the UPS fails to power up as indicated, refer to: Powerware 9155-10GE UPS 10 kVA User's Guide located at .

3  Finalization

Once the power is applied to the system and the system is booted up, perform 50 axial scans at 120 kV, 50 mA to check system functionality. The system should scan and image without any errors.
