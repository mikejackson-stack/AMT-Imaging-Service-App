3  Procedure

3.1  Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 48cm Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, and center the 48cm Phantom.

    • Align phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Level phantom using bubble level and the Z Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    • Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] procedure in the left head [SCANNER UTILITIES] selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Set up the system to scan a single, 64 image, 48cm Phantom series.

3.2.2  Band and Streak Artifact

  1. Review the 48cm Phantom images acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series for Bands and Streaks using the Image Works Desktop.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop.

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, select the 48cm Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series.

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, click on the [VIEWER] button.

    1. While paging through the 64 images in the series, analyze images for any Band or Streak artifacts. Adjust Window/Level using the center mouse button, as required.

    1. Note any image that appears to have a Band or Streak artifact.

  1. Verify any images noted above ( Step 1.e) meet 48cm Phantom Band or Streak Image Performance requirements.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the Service Desktop.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select an image of the 48cm Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series that appeared to have a Band or Streak while reviewing the series in Image Works.

    1. On the Image Analysis Tool window, click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 48CM] test from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right and select [16X1.25/120KV/400MA/2S] from the test pull-down menu, and continue to slide cursor to the right to select [BAND] or [STREAK] .

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

      NOTE:If required, adjust Window/Level using the center mouse button.

    1. Position and size the Band or Streak ROI using the left cursor button.

      NOTE:To ensure specifications are properly applied, do not adjust the Reference ROI's for the Band and Streak artifact tests. The Reference ROI's are adjustable after the 1st Accept Modification click.

    1. Click on [ACCEPT MODIFICATION] twice, to generate a report.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Repeat steps b through g, for each image noted in Step 1.e.

3.3  Failure Recommended Actions

3.3.1  Series Means (AvXo-AvXc or AvSDo) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    Each Row of the series must pass 48cm Series Means (for the first series scan parameters) specifications:

    • AvXo - AvXc: < +/- 8.5

    • AvSDo: < 50.0

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 48cm Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series and Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.

3.3.2  Center Smudge Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    80% the Rows must pass Center Smudge specifications when re-scanning: Center Smudge: Smudge Factor < +/- 14.0

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. If one image of one Row fails (Row fails once):

      • Re-scan the 48cm Phantom acquiring two series of 64 images using the same protocol settings as listed in Table 1.

      • Analyze the two 64 re-scanned image series using the Image Analysis [IMGSER 48CM] -> [16X1.25/120KV/400MA] -> [AUTO] tool.

      • Verify that all the 128 re-scanned images do not fail Series Means (AvXo - AvXc and AvSDo) or Center Smudge.

    1. If more than one image of one Row fails (Row fails more than once) or multiple rows are failing:

      • Repeat Detailed Cal for the 48cm Phantom.

      • Re-scan the 48cm Phantom acquiring three series of 64 images using the same protocol settings as listed in Table 1.

      • Analyze the three re-scanned image series using the Image Analysis [IMGSER 48CM] -> [16X1.25/120KV/400MA] -> [AUTO] tool.

      • Verify that all the 192 re-scanned images do not fail Series Means (AvXo - AvXc and AvSDo) or Center Smudge.

3.3.3  Band or Streak Artifact Failure Recovery

  1. Specification:

    • Band Artifact: Band Factor < +/- 8.0

    • Streak Artifact: Streak Factor < +/- 4.0

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Troubleshoot Band or Streak artifact failures, as described in the System Service Manual.

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 48cm Phantom.

    1. Repeat Acquiring the 48cm Phantom Image Series and Image Performance Verification, to verify Image Performance.
