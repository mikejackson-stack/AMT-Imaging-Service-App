DAS-16 Chassis and Backplane Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

Ensure that you are properly grounded by using the appropriate ESD wrist strap and cord connected to a good ground point in the Gantry.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Chassis Removal (Left, Center or Right)

  1. Move the table cradle to the home position, and lower the table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover, and turn OFF all three (3) service switches. Remove the rest of the gantry covers.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Position the bad chassis at 6 O’clock. Shut off system power using appropriate Lockout Tagout procedures.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the DAS air plenum, per DAS Air Plenum Removal and Installation.

  1. Put on wrist strap and use good ESD precautions.

  1. Using Detector Handling precautions (verify use of ESD strap, grounding and Nitrile gloves), disconnect Detector flexes from the appropriate backplane. See MDAS/GDAS 16 DDIF Cleaning Procedure for details.

  1. Disconnect the appropriate power cables:

  1. Disconnect the appropriate data cables:

NOTE:Use the DAS shields provided with the gantry balance kit to protect against particle contamination during balance weight removal and installation.

NOTE:For systems with a GDAS, there may be a shield plate between the right DAS chassis and Jedi HV tank. See GDAS EMI Shielding for a description of this shield. This shield plate needs to be removed to remove the DAS right chassis and reinstalled when installing the new chassis.

  1. Remove the gantry balance trim weights and threaded rod nuts on the front of the detector plate. This is for left and right chassis replacement only.

  1. Loosen Center Chassis top mounting bolt for left or right chassis removal.

  1. Remove four (4) large 12mm cap screws holding the chassis to the Detector Plate, while holding on to the chassis. When the last cap screw is removed, carefully slide the chassis away from under the Center chassis and remove from the Gantry. Place chassis on an ESD pad (to protect converter cards).

    (Step Details)

1.2   Chassis Installation (Left, Center or Right)

Ensure that you are properly grounded by using the appropriate ESD wrist strap and cord connected to a good ground point in the Gantry.
 
 

  1. Carefully place the DAS Chassis in position. Use special care:

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure by using four (4) large 12mm Cap screws, with flat and lock washers. Torque each chassis mounting screw to 49 ft.-lbs (66.4 N-m).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the appropriate power cables:

  1. Connect Data Cables

  1. Transfer the Converter boards from the replaced chassis to the new chassis.

    1. Confirm ESD practices

    1. Remove Chassis board cover

    1. Remove 1 board at a time and transfer each board from the original chassis to the new chassis in the same board slot location.

  1. Install Detector flexes with your Nitrile glove protected fingers.

    1. Confirm ESD and detector flex handling practices. Use ESD wrist strap and Nitrile gloves.

    1. Interposers and Dust Shields are shipped with each new chassis.

  1. Install chassis board cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install threaded rod lock nuts, gantry balance trim weights and torque to 66 N-m (48 lbs-ft).

  1. Install plenum, per “Plenum Installation,” in DAS Air Plenum Removal and Installation.

  1. Return weights to their original sequence and position (if applicable).

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Restore power.

  1. See Retest Matrix for tests after replacing a DAS Chassis.

  1. Install / Close all covers.

2  Finalization

  1. DAS Detector Integration

    1. Perform X-Ray Verification.

    1. Perform Pop and Noise Microphonics Test (16-Slice).

  1. Perform FastCal.

  1. Perform Image Quality Test Procedures.

  1. Perform Saving System State.
