3  Procedure

3.1   Setup

  1. Install all gantry covers and scan window.

  1. Move table to its home longitudinal position.

  1. Attach phantom holder to gantry end of cradle.

  1. Mount phantom onto phantom holder.

  1. Place bubble level on top of phantom, positioned along the Z-axis. Adjust phantom holder back knob until phantom is level front to back.

3.2   Calibration

  1. Select [SCANNER UTILITIES] from the bottom of Scan Monitor.

  1. Select [DETAILED CALIBRATION] from Scanner Utilities window. Gantry Balance is performed.

  1. Verify all [FOCAL SPOT] buttons are selected (highlighted).

    (Step Details)

  1. Press [CONFIRM] in Detailed Calibration window. Attention message box is displayed.

  1. Click [OK] in Attention message box.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Air Calibrations are performed.

  1. Set up and center phantom in scan plane.

    NOTE:Setup and centering must be completed and calibration started in less than 30 minutes, otherwise you need to start over.

    1. Turn on laser alignment lights.

    1. Move table into gantry until axial internal alignment lights are lined up with phantom's axial reference mark.

    1. Adjust phantom holder side knob until sagittal internal alignment lights are lined up with phantom’s sagittal reference mark.

    1. Elevate table until coronal internal alignment lights are lined up with phantom’s coronal reference mark.

    1. Set Internal Landmark.

    1. Move table out of gantry 80mm.

    NOTE:Axial internal alignment lights should be in the water area of the phantom.

    1. Elevate table 40mm.

    1. Press [CONFIRM] .

    1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Phantom Centering scan is performed.

    1. Adjust phantom position if results are out of specification range.

      (Step Details)

    1. Repeat steps [h] – [j] until phantom is centered.

    1. Once phantom is centered, press [CONTINUE] .

    1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Water Phantom Calibrations (Small) are performed.

  1. Elevate table 40mm and center phantom.

    1. Press [CONFIRM] .

    1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Phantom Centering scan is performed.

    1. Adjust phantom position if results are out of specification range.

      (Step Details)

    1. Repeat steps [a] – [c] until phantom is centered.

    1. Once phantom is centered, press [CONTINUE] .

    1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Water Phantom Calibrations (Large) are performed.

  1. When Water Phantom Calibrations are completed, an Attention box is displayed.

  1. Click [OK] in Attention message box.

  1. Press [QUIT] in Scanner Utilities window.

4  Finalization

  1. If procedure execution was due to another Service Procedure, return to associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform the following steps.
