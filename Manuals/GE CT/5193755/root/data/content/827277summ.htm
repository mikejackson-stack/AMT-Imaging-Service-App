3  Procedure

3.1   Removing The TGPu

Potential for shock.
Voltage may be present.
No service on the left side while energized. Follow appropriate LOTO procedures.

  1. Remove all gantry covers except for the front cover.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the 12 screws in the TGPU covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cover.

  1. Disconnect all cabling into the TGPU.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the eight (8) screws attaching the TGPU board.

  1. Remove the defective TGPU.

3.2   Install TGPu

  1. Put the new TGPu board in place.

  1. Secure the board by replacing the eight (8) screws.

  1. Carefully install all of the removed cables, making sure that no pins are bent during engagement.

  1. Replace the cover.

  1. Replace the twelve (12) screws in the TGPU cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Execute Flash download query and update. TGPu firmware older than OC may fail. In this case, execute the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn ON all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, and 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

4  Finalization

  1. Reinstall covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
