MDAS General Cleaning

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Failure to use the ESD nozzle and proper ESD grounding will generate a static charge of DAS components up to 12,000 volts.
 
 

2  Procedure

  1. Take a DC Noise baseline scan using DASTools Manual test.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn Off DAS Power Supplies

  1. Remove the Air Plenum (Fan Cover)

  1. Remove front chassis cover to gain access to boards.

  1. Clean one chassis at a time. Manually rotate the Gantry so that the DAS is at the 90 degree position. Clean the Right Chassis (or top chassis) first.

When using compressed air, do not invert the can as this will cause liquid to come out. Testing has shown that this can cause a large static charge to develop where the spray hits.
 
 

  1. Remove all the circuit cards from the chassis in sequence.

    (Step Details)

3  Finalization

  1. Complete a Fastcal.

  1. Verify Image Quality by scanning the 48cm phantom using the Service Protocol: "Image Series 48cm" and image specifications.
