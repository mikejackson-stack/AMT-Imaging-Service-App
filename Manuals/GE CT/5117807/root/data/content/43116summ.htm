Gantry Tilt Regulatory Covers Removal and Re-Install

1  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

While it is possible to remove these covers without removing the front cover first, removal of the front cover does make the process easier and eliminates the need to tilt the gantry to gain access for the right side. For front cover removal, see Gantry Front Cover Removal and Re-install

3  Procedure

3.1   Right Side Tilt Regulatory Cover Removal

  1. Remove gantry side covers (see Gantry Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
NEVER SERVICE THE GANTRY WITH THE AXIAL DRIVE ENABLED.

  1. Turn off axial drive enable. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tilt gantry backward to around -5.0 degrees.

  1. Disconnect the lower right front cover brace from the gantry front cover.

  1. Using a 6 mm ball-end hex wrench, remove four screws securing the cover to the gantry frame.

  1. Tilt gantry forward to around +18.5 degrees.

  1. Referring to Illustration 2, carefully slide the panel out of position through the rear of the gantry.

  1. Reconnect the right lower gantry front cover latch

    (Step Details)

3.2   Right Side Tilt Regulatory Cover Installation

  1. Verify axial drive is turned off.

  1. Tilt gantry forward to around +18.5 degrees.

  1. Referring to Illustration 3, carefully slide panel into position from the rear of the gantry.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tilt gantry backward to around -5.0 degrees.

  1. Disconnect right lower gantry front cover latch.

  1. Loosely fasten two front screws, two lock washers, and two washers to the holes at the front edge of the panel (see Illustration 3).

  1. Loosely fasten two rear screws, two lock washers, and washers to the new holes at the rear of the panel (see Illustration 3).

  1. Assure that the clearance indicated in Illustration 3 (1 cm) is met before final tightening of the four screws.

  1. Tighten screws.

  1. Reconnect right lower gantry front cover latch.

3.3   Left Side Tilt Regulatory Cover Removal

  1. Verify axial drive is turned off.

  1. Tilt gantry to 0 degrees.

  1. As shown in Illustration 4, remove the four screws, four lock washers, and four washers fastening the cover to the gantry HEMRC cover and the front cover angle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cover by sliding it through the rear of the gantry.

3.4   Left Side Tilt Regulatory Cover Installation

  1. Simply reverse the installation procedure to reinstall the cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Re-install gantry front and side covers.

  1. Turn on axial drive enable.
