Keyboard Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Remove the existing keyboard cable from the left-hand port on the top of the console front bulkhead.

2  Finalization

No finalization required.
