HP Host Computer Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 20.5kg (45 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Order a replacement Option CD or DVD (as applicable), when ordering the HP Computer.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Power OFF (Shut Down) the Console

  1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button.

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console front switch, when instructed to “Power Down”.

  1. Remove the rear Operator Console cover.

  1. Visually verify proper labeling of each cable and disconnect all HP Host Computer rear cables.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front Operator Console cover.

  1. Remove and retain the four (4) front mounting screws.

Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 20.5kg (45 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

  1. Slide out the HP Host Computer module.

3.2   Optional Hard Drive Swap

The HP OC System Disk Drive and/or Image Disks Drives can be swapped, if needed. Follow the Optional Hard Drive Swap procedure. Then return to these instructions.

3.3   Install the New HP Host Computer

  1. If present, remove the top and bottom covers from the new HP Host computer.

    (Step Details)

Potential for injury.
The HP Computer module weighs approximately 20.5kg (45 lbs).
Use care, when removing the HP module.

  1. Slide in the new HP Host Computer module.

  1. Replace the four (4) front mounting screws.

  1. Reconnect all HP Host Computer rear cables.

  1. At the rear of the HP Host Computer, verify the power switch is in the ON position.

  1. Apply power to the Operator Console by turning ON the front switch.

3.4   Decision Tree

Two scenarios might be seen when replacing an entire HP Host Computer:

  1. If the HP Host Computer was fully swapped in as a FRU, then do a full LFC and call the OLC for replacement customer options CD or DVD (as applicable).

  1. If the HP Host Computer was swapped in, but the site's three original Disk Drives were reinstalled, the console should boot up to Applications. You need to call OLC for replacement customer options CD or DVD (as applicable).

3.5   Retro Recon Test

The Reconstruction (not Scan) portion of the system can be tested at this point.

  1. Click on the [RETRO RECON] selection on the left Monitor.

  1. Click the [RAT GOLD] series.

  1. Click [SELECT SERIES].

  1. Click on [CONFIRM].

  1. Verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Click on [QUIT].

4  Finalization

  1. Take some scans, verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Check that all customer functions are operational. (Auto voice, filming, network, archive.)

  1. Install the front and rear Operator Console covers.
