4  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the Table Base Covers, the right Side Rail Covers and the right Side Panels.

  1. To remove the Actuator Cover, remove both clips that fasten the spring pin to the actuator cover.

  1. Slide out the pin, releasing the spring.

  1. Remove the two screws that fasten the cover hinge to the U-Bracket.

  1. Remove the Actuator Cover from the Table.

  1. To install the Actuator Cover, center the cover in the rear leg opening.

  1. Tighten the hinge screws.

  1. Seat the spring in the notch on the spring pin.

  1. Reassemble the Table paneling.
