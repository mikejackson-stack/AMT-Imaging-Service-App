2  Overview

This procedure explains how to remove and re-install the CT Gantry top covers.

4  Procedure

4.1  Top Cover Removal

Potential for Shock.
Voltage may be present. Potential for injury if covers removed and power is left ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover (see Gantry Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) main power switches (HVDC, 120VAC, and Axial Drive) on the Service Switch Panel (SSP). See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Service Switch Panel

  1. Disconnect the power cable that connects the fans on the top cover to the gantry.

    NOTE:The cooling fans are now mounted on the top covers.

  1. Take the end of the top cover nearest to the side cover and tilt upwards.

  1. Slide the cover down to disengage the tab from the mounting bracket. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Top Cover Tabs and Bracket

  1. Lift the cover clear and repeat the above steps for the other cover.

4.2  Top Cover Installation

The top cover consists of two (2) pieces. Install the front and rear gantry covers, if not already installed (see Gantry Front Cover Removal and Re-install) and (see Gantry Rear Cover Removal and Re-install).

  1. Take one of the top covers and align the tabs on the cover with its associated bracket. Lift and slide the cover into place.

  1. Take the other top cover and align the tabs on the cover with its associated bracket. Lift and slide the cover into place.

  1. Connect the cable from the fans to the gantry.

Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn on the three (3) power switches.

  1. Ensure fans work properly.

  1. Re-install the gantry side covers.
