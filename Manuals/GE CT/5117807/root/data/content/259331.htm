3  Procedure

  1. Remove rear cover (see Gantry Rear Cover Removal and Re-install) and right side cover (see Gantry Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Using the hex socket, remove 2 cap screws from the back side of supply located on the rear right fan assembly.

  1. Remove safety plate.

  1. Disconnect wiring.

  1. Replace the slipring power supply assembly.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
