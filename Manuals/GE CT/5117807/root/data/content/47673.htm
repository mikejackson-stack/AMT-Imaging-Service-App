3.4  Safety

SEVERE INJURY HAZARD
DO NOT REMOVE THE HYDRAULIC CYLINDERS AT ANY OTHER TILT POSITION THAN GANTRY TILTED ALL THE WAY BACK.
Since it is likely that a catastrophic failure has occurred, the safest position to perform this procedure is with the gantry tilted all the way back.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

4  Procedure

SEVERE INJURY HAZARD
DO NOT REMOVE THE HYDRAULIC CYLINDERS AT ANY OTHER TILT POSITION THAN GANTRY TILTED ALL THE WAY BACK.
Since it is likely that a catastrophic failure has occurred, the safest position to perform this procedure is with the gantry tilted all the way back.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGES PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Shutdown system software and remove all system power at the main disconnect (A1) panel.

    1. Perform proper Lockout/Tagout power control procedures.

    1. Install the yellow tilt brackets.

  1. Disconnect all rear external gantry power pan connection and move cables aside. Label wires for later assembly. Take care not to damage the fiber optic cabling.

  1. Remove the rear screws securing the gantry power pan to the gantry base frame.

  1. Loosen the front screws securing the gantry power pan. You do not need to remove the front screw as the power pan is slotted.

  1. Perform Step 4 and Step 5 for the Hydraulic Tilt pump assembly.

  1. Carefully identify all tie-wraps and fasteners that secure the hydraulic lines to the gantry frame. Write down the routing of these lines.

  1. Remove only the fasteners identified in Step 7. Be careful not to puncture the hydraulic lines.

  1. Loosen four (4) hex socket caps at the top of the left hydraulic cylinder and three (3) screws at the bottom of the left hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Remove the left hydraulic cylinder and its lines clear of the gantry base.

  1. Carefully install the new left hydraulic cylinder in place. Make sure the lines can be properly routed along the gantry frame.

  1. Remove the right hydraulic cylinder and its lines clear of the gantry base.

  1. Remove the hydraulic pump assembly from the gantry base with the lines and cylinders.

  1. Install the new hydraulic pump assembly. Make sure the lines are routed correctly.

  1. Install the right hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Secure all hydraulic lines as identified in Step 7. Make sure to leave about 1 inch slack on both left and right cylinder to prevent hose damage during forward tilt.

  1. Assemble the tilt pan wiring connections. Secure the pan to the gantry frame.

  1. Set the torque on the M12 screws for the upper cylinder brackets as listed in the following table.

    Table 1:  Torque values

    66 N-m49 lb-ft584 lb-in673 kg-cm

  1. Set the torque on the M6 screws for the lower cylinder brackets to as listed in the following table.

    Table 2:  Torque values

    7.9 N-m5.8 lb-ft70 lb-in80.5 kg-cm

  1. Restore main disconnect power.

  1. Set the tilt relay board to manual and tilt the gantry forward in 5 degree increments. Check both sides for possible obstructions or hose damage.

  1. Refer to Hydraulic Tilt Motor Speed Adjustment.

  1. Exercise the tilt function several full cycles to purge the air from the hydraulic system.

  1. When the tilt pump is warm to the touch, make final speed adjustments.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

  1. Refer to “Clean-up and Personal Hygiene,” located in Equipment Service - Chemicals and Materials, for proper disposal of contaminated materials.

5  Finalization

  1. Verify full range of tilt operation.
