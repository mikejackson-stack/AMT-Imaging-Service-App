3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to Power OFF the Operator Console:

    1. If Applications are up, click on the [Shut Down] button and select [Shutdown].

      (Step Details)

    1. If Applications are down, open a Unix Shell. Type: halt.

      (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the console at the front panel switch.

3.2   Remove the Old DARC

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console covers.

  1. Visually verify proper labeling of each cable and disconnect all rear DARC Node cables. If necessary add a label for clarity.

NOTE:Refer to the schematic located on the rear console cover for cable connection details.

NOTE:For a scalable PDF version of the GOC3 schematic diagram, click on the following PDF icon:

  1. Remove and retain the four (4) front mounting screws and slide out the DARC Node.

3.3   Install the New DARC

  1. Verify the replacement DARC Node is correct for the Site DAS Type.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the replacement DARC Node into the Operator Console.

  1. Replace the four (4) front mounting screws on the front of the DARC Node.

  1. Verify the power switch located at the rear of the DARC Node is in the ON position.

All cable connector screw-locks must be finger-tightened, only. Do NOT use any tool to tighten cable screw-locks.
 
 

  1. Replace all rear cable connections to the DARC Node.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Console

  1. Power [ON] the Operator Console at the front switch.

  1. At the DARC Node verify the front panel green LED labeled POWER is on. If not press the front panel power switch (far-left switch) to apply power to the DARC Node.

  1. At the IG Node verify the front panel green LED labeled PWR is on. If not press the front panel power switch (top-most switch) to apply power to the IG Node.

  1. At the SDDA verify the front panel green LED labeled I/O is on. If not press the front panel power switch to apply power to the SDDA.

  1. Stop Applications from auto-starting at the 5 second PINK Box.

3.5   Configure New DARC

Check to see if your software set is labeled EXACTLY as listed below. If it is, follow the instructions in Software Present in List. If it is not, follow the instructions listed in Software NOT Present in List.

  • LightSpeed Pro16 (60) HP60_5.1M3 P/N 2401815 (M3 Software or later)

  • LightSpeed Pro16 (80) H5.0M3, P/N 2400466 (M3 Software or later)

  • LightSpeed Pro16 (100) H5.0M3.1, P/N 2401267 or 2405153 (M3.1 Software or later)

  • LightSpeed RT H5.2M3, P/N 2396455 (M3 Software or later)

  • HiSpeed QX/i HSQXIM4 (Linux Pegasus/GOC3), P/N 2401245

  • LightSpeed 4.X H4.2M4 (Linux Pegasus/GOC3), P/N 5111370

  • LightSpeed 3.X H3.3M4 (Linux Pegasus/GOC3), P/N 5111368

3.5.1  Software Present in List

  1. Refer to the appropriate LFC Procedures Chapter in GRE Software Installation Procedures (2378261-100), “LFC - CD-ROM OS Installation on DARC” section:

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to the appropriate LFC Procedures Chapter in GRE Software Installation Procedures (2378261-100), “LFC - CD-ROM DARC Application Installation on HP Host Computer” section:

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to Verify Communications, below.

3.5.2  Software NOT Present in List

  1. Perform a COMPLETE software load from cold for your system, as defined in the appropriate LFC procedures in GRE Software Installation Procedures (2378261-100).

  1. Proceed to Verify Communications, below.

3.5.3  Verify Communications

Now that the OS and DARC Application software loads are complete, verify communications as follows:

  1. Open a Unix Shell.

  1. Type: ping darc

    (Step Details)

  1. Type: rsh darc

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify the DARC Node DIP board type (see table, below).

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify the DARC Node DIP Board Diagnostics pass.

    (Step Details)

  1. Type: ping ig1

    (Step Details)

  1. Type: rsh ig1

    (Step Details)

  1. Type: exit

3.6   testHighSpeed in SDDA

  1. At the ctuser@darc prompt type: cd /usr/g/bin

  1. Type: ls test*(testHighSpeed is listed)

  1. Type: testHighSpeed

    (Step Details)

  1. Type: exit

3.7   Start Up Applications

As ctuser, type: st

3.8   Retro Recon Test

The Reconstruction (not Scan) portion of the system can be tested at this point.

  1. Click on the [RETRO RECON] selection on the left Monitor.

  1. Click the [RAT GOLD] series.

  1. Click [SELECT SERIES].

  1. Click on [CONFIRM].

  1. Verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Click on [QUIT].

4  Finalization

  1. Take some scans (e.g., scout, axial & helical).

    Verify the image(s) reconstruct and are displayed.

  1. Install the front and rear Operator Console covers.
