35 cm QA Phantom Image Series Verification

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 35cm QA Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, & center the 35cm QA Phantom.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the system to scan three 35cm QA Phantom image series with a Recon of the 1st series.

    (Step Details)

  1. Acquire the 1st 35cm QA Phantom image series and one Recon series (MTF and Contrast, Visible Lines).

  1. Acquire the 3rd 35cm QA Phantom image series (Series Description: QA2 Ho 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

  1. Acquire the 4th 35cm QA Phantom image series (Series Description: QA3 Sm 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

1.2   35cm QA Phantom Image Series Image Performance Verification

1.2.1  1st QA Phantom Image Series (4 Image MTF Average and Contrast Scale) Image Performance Verification

  1. Launch the Image Analysis Tool.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the Service Desktop.

    1. From the Service Desktop upper navigational bar, click on [IMAGE QUALITY TESTS].

    1. In the left navigational area directory, click on [IMAGE ANALYSIS].

      (Step Details)

  1. Using the Auto 1x Tool on the Image Analysis screen, generate a QA#1 MTF and QA#1 Contrast Scale report for the 1st series of the 35cm QA Phantom acquisition.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select the 1st series of the 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series.

    1. On the Image Analysis Tool window, click on the [AUTO 1X] diamond shaped button.

    1. Click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 35CMQA] test from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right to select [QA1 MTF 2X10/120KV/260MA/1S] from the test pull-down menu.

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

  1. Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Per Image MTF, 4 Image MTF Average, and Per Image Contrast Scale data. .

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1 for the 1st 35cm QA Image Series.

    1. Record Per Image MTF and Per Image Contrast Scale data for each image in Table 2.

    1. Record the 4 Image MTF Average in Table 2.

      (Step Details)

1.2.2  2nd QA Phantom Image Series (4 Bone Retro Image Visible Lines) Image Perf. Verification

  1. Using the Auto 1x Tool on the Image Analysis screen, generate a Per Image QA#1 Lines report for the 2nd series of the 35cm QA Phantom acquisition.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select the 1st image of the 2nd series of the 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series.

    1. Click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 35CMQA] test from the pull-down menu, and slide cursor to the right to select [QA1 LI 2X10/120KV/260MA/1S].

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button. The tool displays a Visible Lines pop-up window.

    1. On the Visible Lines pop-up window, click on the [VISIBLE LINE] button.

      (Step Details)

    1. From the Visible Line pull-down menu, select the letter ( [A] through [F]) that matches the smallest line pair pattern that you can discern in the Image View Port.

    1. On the Visible Lines pop-up window, click on the [OK] button.

  1. Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Per Image QA#1 Visible Lines data for the 1st image in Table 3.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1 for the 2nd 35cm QA Image Series.

    1. Record the Per Image QA#1 Visible Lines data for the 1st image in Table 3.

  1. Repeat Steps 1 and 2, for each of the remaining three images in the 2nd 35cm QA Image Series

    (Step Details)

1.2.3  3rd QA Phantom Image Series (4 Image Visible Holes) Image Performance Verification

  1. Using the Auto 1x Tool on the Image Analysis screen, generate a Per Image QA#2 Holes report for the 3rd series of the 35cm QA Phantom acquisition.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select the 1st image of the 3rd series of the 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series.

    1. Click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select [IMGSER 35CMQA] from the pull-down menu, and slide cursor to the right to select [QA2 HO 2X10/120KV/260MA/1S].

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button. The tool displays a Visible Hole pop-up window.

    1. On the Visible Hole pop-up window, click on the Visible Hole button.

      (Step Details)

    1. From the Visible Hole pull-down menu, select the number ( [1] through [5]) that matches the smallest hole that you can discern in the pattern centered in the image displayed in the Image View Port.

    1. On the Visible Hole pop-up window, click on the [OK] button.

  1. Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the Per Image QA#2 Visible Holes data for the 1st image in Table 4.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Verify the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1 for the 3rd 35cm QA Image Series.

    1. Record the Per Image QA#2 Visible Holes data for the 1st image in Table 4.

  1. Repeat Steps 1 and 2 for the 3rd, 5th, and 7th images in the 3rd 35cm QA Image Series.

    (Step Details)

1.2.4  4th QA Phantom Image Series (QA#3 Small) Image Performance Verification

  1. Using the Auto 1x Tool on the Image Analysis screen, generate a QA#3 Small report for the 4th series of the 35cm QA Phantom acquisition.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select the 4th series of the 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series.

    1. Click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select [IMGSER 35CMQA] from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right to select [QA3 SM 2X10/120KV/260MA/1S] from the test pull-down menu, and continue to slide cursor to the right to select [AUTO].

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

  1. Review the Image Analysis Report data and record the QA3 Small data for the 4th 35cm QA Image Series in Table 6.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Verify that the scan parameter values displayed in the report match those in Table 1 for the 4th 35cm QA Image Series.

    1. Record the QA3 Small data (Row 2A1A and Row 2B1B Series Means AvXc and AvXo - AvXc), QA3 (AvSDc), and the Center Smudge Row data in Table 6.

      (Step Details)

  1. Review the 4th 35cm QA Phantom images acquired in Section 1 for Rings, Bands, and Streaks using the Image Works Desktop.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the [IMAGE WORKS DESKTOP].

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, select the 4th 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series.

    1. From the Image Works Browser window, click on the [VIEWER] button.

    1. While paging through the eight images in the series, analyze images for any Ring, Band, or Streak artifacts. Adjust Window/Level using the center mouse button, as required.

    1. Note any image that appears to have a Ring, Band, or Streak artifact.

  1. Verify any images noted above (step 3e) meet 35cm QA Phantom Ring, Band, or Streak Image Performance requirements.

    1. From the Global Control Palette, click on the Service Desktop.

    1. From the Service Browser window, select an image of the 4th 35cm QA Phantom series acquired in section 1 that appeared to have a Ring, Band, or Streak while reviewing the series in Image Works.

    1. On the Image Analysis Tool window, click on the [AUTO 1X] rectangular button, select the [IMGSER 35CMQA] test from the pull-down menu, slide cursor to the right to select [QA3 SM 2X10/120KV/260MA/1S] from the test pull-down menu, and continue to slide cursor to the right to select [RING], [BAND], or [STREAK].

    1. Click on the [ACCEPT] button.

      (Step Details)

    1. Position and size the Ring, Band, or Streak ROI, using the left cursor button.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click on [ACCEPT MODIFICATION] twice to generate a report.

    1. Verify the Overall Test Pass/Fail Indicator Window of the Report display indicates PASS.

    1. Repeat steps b through g for each image noted in Step 3e.

1.3   Failure Recommended Actions

1.3.1  1st Image Series (4 Image MTF Average) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications

    (Step Details)

  1. Recommended Recovery

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

1.3.2  2nd Image Series (Visible Lines) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    (Step Details)

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    NOTE:The most common failure for this test is that the phantom has air bubbles that are obscuring the line pair patterns.

    1. Carefully inspect the 35cm QA Phantom for air bubbles. If required, refill the phantom with water, to eliminate all air bubbles.

    1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.
