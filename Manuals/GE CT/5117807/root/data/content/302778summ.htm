Tilt Potentiometer Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right and left gantry side covers.

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive and HVDC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel (leave the 120VAC switch ON).

  1. Loosen the two (2) hex screws that mount the Tilt Potentiometer to the gantry assembly.

  1. Remove the belt from the 32-tooth pulley on the Tilt Pot Assembly.

  1. Remove connectors from the Tilt Pot Assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two (2) hex screws that secure the Tilt Pot.

  1. Remove the Tilt Pot Assembly.

  1. Reverse the above steps to install the new Tilt Pot Assembly.

  1. Perform Tilt Characterization.

2  Finalization

No finalization required.
