1  Procedure

1.1   Detailed Replacement Procedure

  1. Remove all gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove slipring safety covers.

  1. Tilt Gantry forward to +30 degrees.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Completely shutdown system power. (A1, Lockout/Tagout)

  1. Remove rear cover mounting brackets, both sides. Three (3) 12mm bolts on each mount.

  1. Remove brush block and ty-rap the brush block to the stationary member out of harms way.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all wiring ty-raps on the ring inside diameter.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables from slipring.

  1. Remove antenna bracket with antenna assembly.

Follow this procedure exactly. Do not take short cuts. Both axial and radial runout as well as gantry balance are at stake.
 
 

  1. Rotate gantry so signal PCB is in 12 o'clock position. This puts the tube at 12 o’clock also.

  1. Engage axial rotating lock to prevent gantry rotation.

  1. Mark slipring, slipring cast mounting brackets, and rotating casting with numbers 1 through 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open new slipring box.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the existing Transmitter module that came mounted on the slipring with the 5.X RT specific transmitter module also shipped with the slipring. (The same slipring is used on multiple products; only the transmitter changed for this system.)

  1. On the gantry, remove all twelve (12) 12mm bolts securing the cast mounting brackets to the rotating casting.

    (Step Details)

  1. After removing last bolts, carefully remove slipring and cast mounting bracket assembly.

SEVERE INJURY CAN RESULT.
THE SLIPRING AND CAST MOUNTING BRACKETS WEIGH ABOUT 150 LB (68 KG).
3 PEOPLE MINIMUM ARE NEEDED TO REMOVE THIS ASSEMBLY.

Remember there are 3 mounts with pins, so pull ring straight off. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to separate the brackets from the rotating casting if necessary.
 
 

  1. Place the old ring, brass side down, on top of foam cover.

    (Step Details)

Inspect all interfaces for burrs, debris, or other imperfections. These items can result in runout failures, requiring a repeat of this entire procedure.
 
 

  1. Transfer each of the cast mounting brackets one at a time. Ensure these brackets are installed in the same position from old to new slipring.

    1. Place cast mounting bracket number 1 on the new slipring aligning 6mm pin in slot.

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the 6mm bolt in the 6.2mm hole and align over slipring insert.

    1. Gently pull or push the cast mounting bracket radially out (away from ISO). Use the pin slot as the stop and the 6.2mm hole for alignment.

    1. Tighten the 6mm bolt until the lock washer starts to compress. Just tighten (snug) enough so that bolts are engaged.

    1. Then install the other three 6mm bolts and snug until they are engaged.

      (Step Details)

  1. Repeat Step 18 for all six (6) cast mounting brackets.

  1. Align new slipring and cast mounting brackets with the rotating casting.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release axial rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

  1. Final torquing. Reference Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Start with bracket location # 1 and torque the two (2) 12 mm bolts to 68 N-m (49 ft-lbs).

  1. Repeat Step 22 for cast mounting bracket # 2 through # 6 in order.

    (Step Details)

  1. Start with bracket location # 1 and torque the four (4) 6mm bolts in order 1, 2, 3, 4. Reference Illustration 2. Torque to 6 N-m (53 in-lbs or 4.45 ft-lbs).

  1. Repeat Step 25 for location # 2 through # 6 in order.

  1. Connect the filters, wiring harnesses and ground clamps to the slipring.

  1. Mount and adjust the Dial Indicator so that the plunger tip rides on the Blue edge of the HSDCD ring.

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand and measure the Radial Runout.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the Dial Indicator and place the plunger tip directly on the brass surface of ring 12.

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand and measure the Axial Runout.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure all rotating harnesses with ty-raps as observed at start of this procedure ( Illustration 1).

  1. Install Antenna/Receiver assembly. Reference “HSDCD Slipring Receiver Adjustment Procedure,” located in S-A HSDCD Slip Ring Adjustments.

  1. Install brush block assembly (see Slipring Brush Block Replacement).

  1. Install slipring safety covers.

  1. Restore power to system.

1.2   Radial Runout Adjustment

POTENTIAL FOR DEATH OR SEVERE INJURY
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT ON SLIPRING
Properly shut down the system, and follow Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Identify the “High” and “Low” physical locations on the slipring.

  1. With the gantry tilted forward + 30 degrees, place the “High” location at 12 o’ clock.

  1. Loosen-do not remove-the four (4) 6 mm bolts at the cast mounting bracket to slipring interface.

  1. Loosen-do not remove-the four (4) 12 mm bolts on each of the six (6) cast mounting brackets.

  1. Physically lift/push/pull the ring to release binding tension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-torque bolts per Detailed Replacement Procedure, Step 21 through Detailed Replacement Procedure, Step 25. Tightening sequences must be followed for desired results.

1.3   Axial Runout Adjustment

POTENTIAL FOR DEATH OR SEVERE INJURY
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT ON SLIPRING
Properly shut down the system, and follow Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Identify the “High” and “Low” physical locations on the slipring.

  1. With the gantry tilted forward + 30 degrees, place the “Low” location at 6 o’ clock.

  1. Loosen-do not remove-the four (4) 6 mm bolts at the cast mounting bracket to slipring interface.

  1. Loosen-do not remove-the four (4) 12 mm bolts on each of the six (6) cast mounting brackets.

  1. Physically lift/push/pull the ring to release binding tension.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-torque bolts per Detailed Replacement Procedure, Step 21 through Detailed Replacement Procedure, Step 25. Tightening sequences must be followed for desired results.

If readings are still out of specification continue with Step 7.
 
 

  1. Inspect the one (1) or two (2) closest cast mounting brackets for proper seating at both the slipring and rotating casting interfaces.

    (Step Details)

  1. Correct any “High” gaps greater than .005 inches (0.127 mm) at any interface edge.

  1. Recheck both Radial and Axial runout.

  1. Using standard notebook paper (0.003 inches (0.076 mm) thick nominal) make shims for one (1) or two (2) mounting locations on either side of the “Low” location.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) 6 mm bolts at the cast mounting bracket to slipring interface.

  1. Slide shim between the slipring and cast mounting bracket to block the two (2) outside diameter holes.

  1. Carefully puncture, remove, trim and reinstall shim.

  1. Install the 6 mm bolts and torque per Detailed Replacement Procedure, Step 25.

  1. Repeat this procedure as needed.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Hardware Reset using console gantry reset (Hardwire).

  1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kV/40mA, 1000mm table movement)

  1. Acquire 100 axials: (120kV/80mA, 0.5 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire 1 helical: (120kV/40mA, 30 sec. Scan)

  1. Acquire 10 axial scans: (120kV/400mA, 4 sec. Scan)

  1. Verify NO increase in LSCOM errors. To check LSCOM Statistics: Select Log Viewing, System Browser, Run Time Statistics, RCOM/SCOM Statistics.

  1. Verify NO increase in corrected or uncorrected FEC errors. To check HSDCD Statistics: Select Log Viewing, System Browser, Run Time Statistics, DIP Statistics.
