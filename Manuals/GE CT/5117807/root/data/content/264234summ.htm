MDAS Air Plenum Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Plenum Removal

  1. Remove gantry covers, shut OFF power, and lock the gantry in position (see Accessing the MDAS Assembly).

  1. Disconnect Fan AC power at Connector J22.

  1. Set air baffles on either end of the duct to the “Removal Position” (see Illustration 1):

    1. Loosen knurl nuts

    1. Slide baffle into position

    1. Hand tighten knurl nuts

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four (4) mounting bolts (2 at each end). See Illustration 1.

  1. Carefully slide the plenum towards the front, without catching wires or Detector flexes.

  1. Set the plenum aside, on a clean surface.

  1. Continue with replacement procedures.

1.2   Plenum Installation

  1. Ensure that baffles are secured in “REMOVAL POSITION”.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift plenum into place, and align guide holes with guide pins.

  1. Slide plenum onto guide pins.

  1. Fasten plenum securely into place, using the four (4) M6 mounting bolts and four lock washers. Torque to 7.9 N-m (69.9 in-lbs).

  1. Return baffles to the “OPERATING POSITION”.

  1. Connect Fan AC power at Connector J22.

  1. Remove rotational lock, restore power and reassemble gantry covers.

2  Finalization

No finalization required.
