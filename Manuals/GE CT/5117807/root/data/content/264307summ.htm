MDAS Elastomers Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Ensure that you are properly grounded by using the appropriate ESD wrist strap and cord connected to a good ground point in the Gantry.
 
 

2  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers, shut OFF power, and lock the gantry in position (see Accessing the MDAS Assembly).

  1. Remove the MDAS air plenum (see MDAS Air Plenum Removal & Installation).

  1. Put on wrist strap and use good ESD precautions. Wear Finger Cots, and follow good Detector Handling practices.

  1. Remove appropriate Flex housing outer cover by removing the housing Cover clamps.

    (Step Details)

Elastomers may become dislodged. Elastomers can potentially FALL OUT of housings, while flexes are being removed. Exercise caution.
 
 

  1. Place an anti-static Detector Flex Boot on each exposed flex.

  1. Carefully remove the questionable elastomer from its seating.

  1. Insert the new elastomer so that the gold contact lines face outward.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove anti-static boots from flexes, and ensure that flex ends are clean. Clean with approved Alcohol pads, where required.

  1. Replace flex covers (B-Side first), and torque clamps to 13 in-lb (1.47 N-m).

  1. When complete, replace MDAS air plenum.

  1. Remove rotational lock, restore power, and reassemble gantry covers.

3  Finalization

No finalization required.
