1.1   Standard Procedure

1.2   Alternate Procedure

NOTE:All voltages are measured at the tilt potentiometer (see Step 2.c during the characterization activity.

  1. Calculate voltages.

    1. Read the three scribe line degree positions and the voltage value printed on the rating plate. Make note of these (write them down).

    1. Do the math:

      (Step Details)

  1. Set the Tilt Pot

    1. Tilt gantry to align, as precisely as possible, the center (Zero Scribe Mark) scribe lines (see Illustration 2).

      (Step Details)

    1. For coarse adjustment, loosen tilt pot mounting bracket and relieve tension on the timing belt.

    1. Connect DVM minus lead to CCW (center post) of the potentiometer and DVM plus to S (closest outside post) of the potentiometer.

    1. Rotate small pulley until the DVM reads the same value as that printed on the tilt rating plate (Middle Characterization Voltage).

    1. Restore belt tension with the tilt pot-mounting bracket and secure (refer to Tilt Pot and Belt Adjustment located inTilt Pot Assembly Adjustments - Angle, Limit procedure for details on how to properly tension the tilt belt).

    1. For final adjustment, loosen tilt pot clamps and rotate the body of the pot until the DVM reads the same as the value on the tilt rating plate (Middle Characterization Voltage).

    1. Tighten pot clamps and verify DVM reading.

    1. Leave the DVM connected for the remainder of the procedure.

  1. Perform Tilt Characterization

    1. Launch the characterization process on the console (see

    1. Ignore gantry display readings during the process.

    1. Enter the three angles in their respective places.

    1. Press [CONTINUE] .

    1. When the console asks you to drive the gantry to the value entered for the top characterization line, drive the gantry backward until your Volt Meter reads the value you calculated for the TCL voltage.

    1. Press [OK] .

    1. When the console asks you to drive the gantry to the middle characterization line, drive the gantry forward until your Volt Meter reads the value printed on the tilt rating plate (MCL voltage).

    1. Press [OK] .

    1. When the console asks you to drive the gantry to the Bottom Characterization Line, drive the gantry forward until your Volt Meter reads the value you calculated for the BCL voltage.

    1. Press [OK] .

    1. Click [SAVE AND CONTINUE] .
