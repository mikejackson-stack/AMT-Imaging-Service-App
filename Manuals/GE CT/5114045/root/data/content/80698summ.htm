Install New Tube

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD.
POTENTIALLY LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT IN THE GANTRY.
Follow electrical lockout/tagout procedures.

POTENTIAL FOR PHYSICAL INJURY.
POSSIBLE UN-COMMANDED GANTRY ROTATION.
Engage Gantry Rotational lock.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
TUBE CAN SEPARATE FROM GANTRY, IF NOT TORQUED CORRECTLY.
always use proper torque on all fasteners.

1  Procedure

  1. Allow the tube unit to warm to room temperature before you install it.

SEVERE INJURY POSSIBLE.
TUBE CAN SEPARATE FROM GANTRY
Do not remove factory installed mounting plates.

  1. Mounting plates are permanently installed at factory. DO NOT REMOVE IT.

  1. Re-check facility power and make sure it is off.

SEVERE INJURY TO PATIENT COULD OCCUR.
USING THE WRONG BOLTS COULD PLACE STRESS ON BOLTS, CAUSING TUBE TO SEPARATE FROM GANTRY.
Use proper bolts, per system type.

  1. Use the hoist to lift the new tube unit:

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the tube I.D. cable to the 12 pin mate-N-Lock connector on top of the tube.

  1. Attach the tube pump and fan power cable to the 4 pin mate-N-Lock connector.

  1. Fasten the grounding strap to the 1/4-20 ground stud on top of the tube unit.

  1. Remove the plastic cap plug from each cable receptacle on the tube unit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lightly wet the new rubber quad ring with transformer oil (917).

  1. Return the quad ring to its slot at the top of the receptacle retaining ring.

  1. Pour transformer oil (917) into the receptacle to a depth of 10 mm (0.375 in).

Incorrectly routed or secured HV cables will result in damage to the HV cables and/or other parts of the gantry.
 
 

  1. Be sure to route the HV cable as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align the cable terminal orienting key with the notch in the receptacle.

Do not over tighten the locking ring. Over tightening can deform the cable plug sealing surfaces, break the oil seal between receptacle and housing, twist the receptacle, and disrupt internal wiring.
 
 

  1. Slowly insert the cable, to engage the connector pins, and seat the cable in the well.

    (Step Details)

IF YOU GET OIL ON YOUR HANDS, WASH THEM NOW
 
 

  1. Carefully wipe up all excess oil.

  1. Secure HV Cables using Large Ty-Raps, as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Disconnect hoist from tube and boom.

  1. Remove the post and boom from the gantry (see Illustration 3) .

    (Step Details)

  1. Check for oil leaks:

    (Step Details)

  1. If oil leaks are found, tighten the locking ring slowly until there is no leakage, paying attention to not over-tighten.

  1. Install the right gantry front cover bracket. Reference Illustration 3.

  1. Restore system power at the main disconnect panel.

  1. Turn on gantry 120 VAC, HVDC POWER or 550 VDC Enable, and AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE service switches. Wait at least 10 minutes to warm up the filament.
