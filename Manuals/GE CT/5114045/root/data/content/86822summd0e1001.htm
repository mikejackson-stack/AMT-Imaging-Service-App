  • In DD analysis, ratio the hot scan to the corresponding cold scans from the air, head, body bowtie type scans. The ratio is approximately 1.0, with some slight slope to the curve. Expand the scale as necessary, and look for a bump or spike (up or down) on the curve greater than about.001 high. Check both the 8x1.25 and 8x2.50 ratios.
