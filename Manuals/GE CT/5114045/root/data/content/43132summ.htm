Gantry Top Covers Removal and Re-Install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read Equipment Service - Gantry, located in the Safety tab of this publication.

1  Procedure

1.1   Top Cover Removal

Potential for Shock.
Voltage may be present. Potential for injury if covers removed and power is left ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) power switches on the STC backplane: AX DR, HVDC, and 120VAC. See Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the end of the top cover nearest to the side cover and tilt upwards.

  1. Slide the cover down to disengage the tab from the mounting bracket. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the cover clear and repeat the above steps for the other cover.

1.2   Top Cover Installation

The top cover consists of two (2) pieces. Install the front and rear gantry covers, if not already installed.

  1. Take one of the top covers and align the tabs on the cover with its associated bracket. Lift and slide the cover into place. Position the cover to fully engage the fan interlock switch.

  1. Take the other top cover and align the tabs on the cover with its associated bracket. Lift and slide the cover into place, while being sure to engage the fan interlock switch.

Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn on the three (3) power switches.

  1. Ensure fans work properly.

  1. Re-install the gantry side covers.
