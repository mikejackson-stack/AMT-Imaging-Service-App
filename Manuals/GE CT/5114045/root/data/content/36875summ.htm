Longitudinal Limit Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Cradle Drive Cover to access the Cal pin (located in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the wires from the limit switch terminals.

  1. Remove the nut that fastens the switch in place.

  1. Remove the defective switch from its bracket.

  1. Install the replacement switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the position of the switch in the bracket:

    1. Loosen the two nuts that fasten the bracket in place

    1. Move the Cradle/carriage assembly to the maximum travel position, and fasten into position with the Cal pin.

    1. Move the bracket until the switch actuates with the carriage in the maximum travel position.

  1. Return the Cal pin to its storage position (in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
