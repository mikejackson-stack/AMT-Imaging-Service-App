3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the Cradle Drive Cover to access the Cal pin (located in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Install the Cal pin at the home position, then remove the Cradle Assembly (see Cradle Assembly Replacement).

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the two lock nuts that fasten the Longitudinal Encoder Assembly Cover in place.

  1. Remove the two screws that fasten the Front End Cover in place.

  1. Remove the pot assembly:

    1. Loosen the clamp that fastens the pot sprocket to the pot shaft.

    1. Disconnect the Pot Assembly from the table harness, at J17.

    1. Loosen the two servo clamps.

    1. Slide the defective Pot Assembly downward, and off the sprocket.

    1. Take care not to lose the plastic spacer on the pot shaft.

  1. After you install the replacement Pot Assembly:

    1. Make sure the sprocket comes in contact with the plastic spacer.

    1. Attach a DVM to terminals #2 and #1 (GND) of the pot.

    1. Turn the pot shaft with a small screwdriver, until the DVM displays 0.80 ±0.01 VDC.

    1. Maintain the voltage display, while you tighten the pot clamp.

  1. Return the Cal pin to its storage position (in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Characterize Longitudinal.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
