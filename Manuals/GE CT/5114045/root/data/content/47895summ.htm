Control, Scan Start Panel Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Remove front cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Disconnect J1 and left control cables.

  1. Loosen 5 screws that fasten the start panel to the cover.

  1. Replace control panel and reconnect J1 and left control cables.

  1. Reassemble gantry.
