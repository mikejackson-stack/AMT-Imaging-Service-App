4  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    • Side covers.

    • Top covers.

    • Front cover.

  1. Turn ON all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Position HEMRC at 6 o’clock.

  1. Remove (2) 4 mm cap screws and washers.

    Cover under the HEMRC Assembly closest to ISO Center.

  1. Using a DVM measure both sides of the resistors to chassis ground. Verify zero (0) volts.

  1. Remove electrical connection from defective resistor.

    • Write down wiring connections and hardware arrangement.

    • Do not drop the hardware into the HEMRC assembly

  1. For both resistors perform the following:

    • Remove 6 mm lock nut and star lock washer.

    • Remove 6 mm tensioning nut.

    • Loosen, do not remove, the (2) 10 mm nuts to separate the resistor mounting covers.

    • Slide the covers apart. Note the original position.

    • Carefully retract long bolt to pivot the failed resistor up out of the assembly.

    • Catch the insulating washers.

  1. Replace failed resistor.

  1. Loosely install the tensioning nut on both long bolts.

  1. Carefully position resistors and insulating washers while you slide the mounting covers together. Make sure the covers engage properly.

  1. Snug the tensioning bolts finger tight to hold the mounting covers together.

  1. Position the mounting covers as identified in Step 9. Torque both 6 mm nuts to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Rotate the resistors so that the connectors are parallel to the HEMRC assembly to prevent electrical arcing or shorts.

  1. Torque the tensioning nut to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Install the star lock washer and 6 mm lock nut. Torque to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Replace electrical connections to resistor.

    • Careful not to drop hardware into HEMRC assembly.

    • Orient hardware as identified in step 8.

  1. Replace HEMRC resistor cover. Torque (2) 4 mm cap screws and washers to 5.9 Nm

  1. Apply power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Turn ON HVDC and 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify no smoke or arching.

  1. Assemble gantry covers.
