3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Cradle Drive Assembly, and set it on a work surface (see Cradle Assembly Replacement).

  1. Remove the belt from the pulleys:

    1. Loosen the four screws that fasten the motor in place.

    1. Slide the motor toward the drive roller, to remove tension from the belt.

    1. Remove the belt.

  1. After you install the new belt:

    1. Slide the motor away from the drive roller, until the belt deflects 0.050/0.060 inches with 4 to 6 ounces applied at its mid-span.

    1. Tighten the four screws, to fasten the motor, with tension on the belt.

  1. Replace the Cradle Drive Assembly.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
