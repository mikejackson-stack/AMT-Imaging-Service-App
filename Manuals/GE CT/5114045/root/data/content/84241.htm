2  Theory

2.1  Why You Got It

The system monitors the Anode mA and adjusts both anode and cathode in a closed loop mode. If the one or both sides drift between 5% to 10%, an “mA out of tolerance” error will be reported to the error log. If only one side drifts 5% to 10% above or below the other side, then the “mA imbalance” error will be reported. Error conditions make it possible for both errors to be reported for a failure. Examine the Error Log and Result Screen to determine which side is at fault. If the Anode side is low, the Cathode will be driven to maximum while the system tries to raise the Anode mA. If the Cathode mA side is low, the Anode mA will usually be within specification.

3  Troubleshooting Summary

  1. Verify error in log and note all pertinent details.

  2. Run kV & mA Diags to verify mA out of tolerance as the primary error

  3. Verify mA meter verification

  4. Verify Focal Spot Relay

  5. Troubleshoot Filament Drive error

  6. Verify 36vdc Filament Power Supply

  7. Verify OBC Power Supply

  8. Call the OLC (tentative)

  9. Software .char files

  10. Escalate

    • Tips and tricks

    • Zone Support

4  Troubleshooting Details

  1. Verify error in log and note all pertinent details.

    Verify that mA out of tolerance is the primary error. Tube spits, KV Overcurrents, KV shoot-throughs, large KV errors, or Open_Filament errors will cause mA out of tolerance errors.

  2. Verify mA meter verification.

    • This is most true if anode and cathode mA differ. The tube is a series device, and current is constant in a series device. If mA differs from anode to cathode suspect a measurement problem.

    • Use the mA Meter Check procedure.

      FAIL: mA Meter Verification.

      • Repair / Replace as needed.

      • Verify System Operation.

      • YOU ARE DONE.

      PASS: mA Meter Verification. Proceed to Step 3.

  3. Verify Focal Spot Relay (on the OBC Backplane) - Refer to Filament Diagnostic.

    FAIL: Focal Spot Relay.

    • Repair / Replace as needed.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Focal Spot Relay ok, then proceed to Step 4.

  4. Troubleshoot Filament Drive errors - Refer to Errors 183124, 214335 - X-Ray Gen: Filament Inverter.

    FAIL: Filament Drive.

    • Repair / Replace as needed.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Filament Drive ok, then proceed to Step 5.

  5. Verify 36vdc Filament Power Supply - Use a scope to verify the 36vdc Filament Power Supply. Spec is no load +36vdc, +2vdc (-)5vdc with no more than 500mv of ripple mA Loop.

    FAIL: Replace 36vdc Filament Power Supply.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: 36vdc Filament Power Supply ok, then proceed to Step 6.

  6. OBC Power Supply - Use a scope to verify the OBC Power Supply, no more than 500mv of ripple mA Loop.

    FAIL: Replace OBC Power Supply.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: OBC Power Supply ok, then proceed to Step 7.

  7. Software - Load from Cold. Reference Software Load Procedures ("Load From Cold" manual).

    PASS: Error goes away.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    FAIL: Error still there, then proceed to Step 8.

  8. Last resort actions.

    1. Tips and tricks.

      • Most times mA out of Tolerance is a secondary error. If you have gotten this far in this flow, look for other primary errors.

      • Tube mA is the result of the proper filament heating. Troubleshoot filament function to troubleshoot mA. Refer to the documents listen in the beginning of this section.

      • Determine if the error is with the small filament or large filament only. Or if with both filaments. Large or small filament split after the OBC back panel.

    2. Zone Support Engineers and / or OnLine Center Engineers live to help Field Engineer with any questions that you might have. They would love to hear from you.

5  Examples of Failing Result Screens

Reference schematic (Filament Select and MA LOOP).

To Rule out if the tank is bad connect your DVM at test point TP17 on the measurement board. with a DVM you should have -0.462 volts for a selection of 100KV/50ma/5 second test exposure if you have a bad tank you will probably not get anything out, However if it is a bad connection between the measurement board and the OBC/ma Control board or a bad circuit on the ma control board you will get a reading of greater than -0.5 volts until the test aborts with an error. Also, the diagnostic screen will show that the Anode ma is too low and the Cathode ma almost double what it should be.
