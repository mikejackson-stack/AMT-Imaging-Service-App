OBC Backplane Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock.

    (Step Details)

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Remove 4 mm cap screws (including washers) at the front of the mounting bracket.

  1. Remove mounting bracket.

  1. Remove 4 mm cap screws that secure AC plastic shield.

  1. Remove plastic shield.

  1. Disconnect input/output (IO) wiring from power supply strips.

  1. Cut ty-rap mounts as necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all power connections at OBC Power I/F board.

  1. Disconnect J11, J16, and J17 connectors from backplane.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut any necessary ty-raps.

  1. Remove all circuit boards.

  1. Remove (4) M12 cap screws securing chassis to rotating base casting.

  1. Carefully lift chassis off gantry.

  1. Place OBC on flat table back plane facing up.

  1. Remove (12) 4 mm cap screws securing backplane to the chassis.

This includes the flat and lock washer on each of the screws. These washers are important to the grounding integrity of the backplane.
 
 

  1. Remove backplane.

  1. Install new backplane. Torque (12) 4 mm cap screws and washers to 2.3 N-m (20.35 in-lbs).

  1. Install Chassis on gantry. Apply Loctite 242, and torque (4) M12 cap screws to 66.4 N-m (49 ft-lbs).

    (Step Details)

  1. Complete re-assembly.

  1. Torque 4 mm cap screws (dry) to 2.3 N-m (20.35 in-lbs).

  1. Torque 6 mm cap screws (dry) to 7.9 N-m (5.8 ft-lbs).

  1. Torque OBC Cover to 2 N-m (17.7 in-lbs).

2  Finalization

  1. Check empty chassis power supplies (see OBC Power Supplies Checks and Adjustments).

  1. Verify mA meter (see mA Meter Check).

  1. Verify kV meter (see kV Meter Check).

  1. Perform Auto mA Cal, located in KV Gain Pots Adjustment procedure.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
