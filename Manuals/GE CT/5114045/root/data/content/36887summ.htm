Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the Table Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install) and Table Side Covers (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the ground strap connection from the z-channel.

  1. Each panel has two flat-head screws that fasten the Pivot Tube to its bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. When you install the Side Panel, make sure the pivot points move without interference. If the pivot points cannot move freely:

    1. Loosen the two screws that fasten the upper mounting bracket in place.

    1. Slide the bracket in its slots, until the side panel pivot points move freely.

  1. Reassemble the Table and replace the covers.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Emergency Stop check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
