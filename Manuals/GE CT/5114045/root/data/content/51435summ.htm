mA Meter Check

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY.
GANTRY ROTATES AT SPEEDS UP TO 120RPM.
NEVER PUT ANY BODY PART INTO THE GANTRY WITHOUT FIRST DISABLING THE AXIAL DRIVE AND VERIFYING (CHECK TWICE) THAT IT IS DISABLED. ENSURE THAT THE DRIVE STATUS LED IS NOT LIT. DO NOT SERVICE THE GANTRY IF THIS LED IS ON.

1  Procedure

  1. Inside the Gantry:

    POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY.
    GANTRY ROTATES AT SPEEDS UP TO 120RPM.
    NEVER PUT ANY BODY PART INTO THE GANTRY WITHOUT FIRST DISABLING THE AXIAL DRIVE AND VERIFYING (CHECK TWICE) THAT IT IS DISABLED. ENSURE THAT THE DRIVE STATUS LED IS NOT LIT. DO NOT SERVICE THE GANTRY IF THIS LED IS ON.

    1. Switch OFF the HVDC ENABLE on STC backplane.

      (Step Details)

    1. Switch OFF the AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE on STC backplane.

    1. Rotate the Anode tank to the 2 o’clock position.

    1. Engage the gantry rotational lock.

  1. Select [SERVICE DESKTOP] .

  1. Select [REPLACEMENT] .

  1. Select [HV SUBSYSTEM CHANGE] .

  1. Select [GENERATOR CHARACTERIZATION] .

  1. Select [READ METERING] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a DVM as an mA meter; connect it to the hardware on the anode side:

    1. Connect the black lead to TP8 (ACAL1) on the mA board.

    1. Connect the red lead to TP11 (ACAL2) on the mA board.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Display, select the [ACCEPT] button.

  1. Record the displayed, and measured, Anode mA values for “Circuit OFF” and “Circuit ON”.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the test equipment from the Anode side, if used.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a DVM as an mA meter:

    1. Connect the black lead to TP9 (CCAL1) on the mA board.

    1. Connect the red lead to TP14 (CCAL2) on the mA board.

  1. On the Display, Select the [ACCEPT] button.

  1. Record the displayed and measured Cathode mA values for “Circuit OFF” and “Circuit ON”.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the test equipment from the Cathode side if used.

    (Step Details)
