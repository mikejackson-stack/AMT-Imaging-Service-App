Tilt Relay Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

Wear a grounded wrist strap when you handle a circuit board.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry right cover and Gantry base covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Tilt gantry back 20 degrees.

Wear a grounded wrist strap when you handle a circuit board.
 
 

  1. Use a short #2 phillips screwdriver to loosen the 4 screws that fasten plastic cover to the Tilt Relay Board Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove J2 and J3 connectors.

  1. Loosen 4 screws that secure the 4 cables to the relay board.

  1. Loosen 8 screws to release the Tilt Relay board.

  1. Install new Tilt Relay board and reassemble gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Verify full range of tilt operation.
