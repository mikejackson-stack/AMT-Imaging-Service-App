LightSpeed 4.X Service Information (Advanced)

Gantry Display Test

  1. Turn ON the gantry 120 VAC Enable Switch on the STC backplane.

  2. Make sure the gantry display goes through the power-up self-test. The display continues to cycle through its self-test until it completes the hardware reset and download.

  3. TURN ON “X-RAY DRIVES” power by pressing the RESET button on the gantry control panel.
