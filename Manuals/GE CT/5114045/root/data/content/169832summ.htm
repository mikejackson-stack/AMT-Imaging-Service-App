DAS-16 Interposer Replacement

1  Procedure

1.1   Interposer Replacement Precautions

Failure to follow correct procedures can result in bent or broken Interposer pins. This can result in intermittent artifacts, noise, or DAS Chassis/Detector replacement. Never use screwdrivers, pocket knives etc, to service the DDIF.
 
 

  1. Damage sustained by using wrong tool and rough handling.

    1. Bent pins - Result of screwdriver as a removal tool.

    1. Broken pin - Result of forcing the Flex onto the Interposer.

      (Step Details)

    1. Loose pins - Result of forcing the Interposer to seat in the backplane BGA connector.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Broken Interposer Pin Removal

If you are reading this you are in desperate trouble. A male pin is broken off in a female socket. You have two choices;

  1. Replace the DAS Chassis if pin is in the backplane BGA connector or replace the Detector if pin is in a flex lead.

  1. Attempt to remove the broken pin.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Dust Shield Retainer Clip Replacement

The replacement of this part must be performed with great care. Severe damage to the backplane bga connector can result requiring DAS chassis replacement.
 
 

  1. Removal Procedure

    (Step Details)

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Gantry covers are removed and Lockout/Tagout power control performed. See Accessing the MDAS Assembly.

  1. Failed retainer clip has been identified. Flex lead and Dust Shield have been removed.

  1. Remove flex lead and dust shield from left and right sides of the failed retainer clip.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position Gantry with failed retainer clip at the 2 O’clock position.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using small sharp side cutters, grip the right side of the damaged retainer clip. Squeeze the clip until it cracks and fragments into smaller pieces.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the same process as in the previous step on the left side of the failed retainer clip.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove from the backplane, all fragments of the destroyed retainer clip.

1.4   Installation Procedure

  1. Observe the new retainer clip has snap lock features on two sides. These snap locks secure to the "A" and "B" backplane BGA connectors.

  1. With your Nitrile Glove protected fingers, push the retainer clip into place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Assemble the DDIF, unlock the gantry.

  1. Restore gantry power.

    (Step Details)
