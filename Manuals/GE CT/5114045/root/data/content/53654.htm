4  Procedure

Use proper lockout/tagout procedures.

  1. Remove gantry side covers and gantry top covers.

  1. Turn OFF Axial Drive and HVDC on the STC backplane.

  1. Rotate gantry until Collimator/Detector Power assembly reaches the 2 o’clock position.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Remove four (4) nuts on Filament Power assembly safety cover and remove cover.

  1. Collimator P.S. output checks:

    • Connect positive voltmeter lead to +OUT terminal on power supply.

    • Connect negative voltmeter lead to -OUT terminal on power supply.

    • Turn voltage adjustment pot to adjust the output voltage to 24 ± 1 VDC.

  1. Reassemble Gantry.
