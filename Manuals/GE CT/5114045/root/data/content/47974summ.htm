OBC Circuit Boards Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

1  Procedure

1.1   Accessing the OBC Chassis

  1. Remove the right gantry side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove right top gantry cover.

  1. Position OBC at the 2 o’clock position

  1. Put on grounding wrist strap.

  1. Loosen the 8 captive screws, and remove the OBC Front Cover.

1.2   ARTESYN-III Board Procedure

  1. Unplug serial and ethernet cables.

  1. Loosen top and bottom fasteners with a Phillips #2 screwdriver.

  1. Pull Artesyn board out using black plastic tabs located on top and bottom of the board.

  1. Place the board in an Anti-Static bag.

  1. Install the new board.

  1. Install OBC Cover. Torque to 2 N-m (17.7 in-lbs).

1.3   Gentry I/O, HEMRC, KV, MA, and LSCOM Boards Procedure

  1. Pull two latches to release the board.

  1. Place the board in an Anti-Static bag.

  1. Install the new board. Secure the newly installed board using the two latches.

  1. Install OBC Cover. Torque to 2 N-m (17.7 in-lbs).

2  Finalization

  1. For Artesyn CPU Board, perform successful hardware reset and System Scanning Test.

  1. For LSCOM, perform the following:

    1. Manual check for TAXI errors. Open a Unix shell:

      (Step Details)

    1. Hardware Reset using console gantry reset (Hardwire).

    1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kV/40mA., 1000mm table movement)

    1. Acquire 100 axials: (120kV/80mA., 0.5 sec. Scan)

    1. Acquire 1 helical: (120kV/40mA., 30 sec. Scan)

    1. Acquire 10 axial scans: (120kV/400mA., 4 sec. Scan)

    1. Verify NO increase in Link errors.

    1. To check LSCOM Statistics:

      (Step Details)

  1. For Gentry I/O Board, perform the following:

    1. Verify mA meter (see mA Meter Check).

    1. Verify kV meter (see kV Meter Check).

    1. kV and mA verification (see KV Gain Pots Adjustment).

    1. Exposure time accuracy (see Exposure Time Accuracy).

    1. System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).

    1. X-Ray Functional Test (see X-Ray Functional Test).

  1. For mA Board, perform the following:

    1. Perform mA Meter Check.

    1. Perform kV Meter Check.

    1. Perform Auto mA Calibration located in KV Gain Pots Adjustment procedure.

    1. Perform Exposure Time Accuracy.

    1. Perform System Scanning Test.

  1. For kV Board, perform the following:

    1. Perform kV Meter Check.

    1. Perform mA Meter Check.

    1. Perform KV Gain Pots Adjustment.

    1. Perform System Scanning Test.

  1. For HEMRC Control Board, perform the following:

    1. Select kV & mA under Troubleshoot; refer to information in section “SW & HW Tools Available for Troubleshooting” located in kV Troubleshooting Theory.

    1. Perform System Scanning Test.
