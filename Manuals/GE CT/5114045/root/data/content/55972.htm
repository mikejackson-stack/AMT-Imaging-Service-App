2  Overview

The Linux Workstation (HP xw8000) is a FRU 1, and has no field serviceable parts within its chassis. The procedure for replacing this computer is a four step process:

  • Save system state to DVD, and shut down system.

  • Disconnect and remove the problem computer from the Global Console.

  • Connect replacement computer into the system.

  • Load software (see the Software Installation Procedures manual for details) and restart system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Remove Defective Computer

Save System State

Before replacing the Linux workstation, make certain to Save System State to DVD (if possible). For details, refer to Saving System State.

 
 

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY OR DEATH BY ELECTRIC SHOCK.
POTENTIALLY LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT IN OPERATOR'S CONSOLE.
To avoid risk of electric shock, follow proper LOCKOUT/TAGOUT procedures.

  1. Tag and lockout system power.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Remove the console’s front cover. (See Front Cover Removal Procedure (in Global Console Covers Removal and Installation).)

  1. Loosen the two captive screws and use the strap to pull out the console tray.

    Illustration 1:  Console Slide Out Tray (GC-Linux)

    Illustration 2:  Console Tray in Its Extended Position (GC-Linux)

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the back of the computer.

  1. Remove the velcro straps from the computer, and lift the computer off of the tray.

    Illustration 3:  Removing Computer from Tray (GC-Linux)

4.2  Install Replacement Computer in Console

Potential for Data Loss

It may be necessary to swap hard drives between the old and new workstations, to retain the customer’s image data. If such action is necessary, perform the “Optional Hard Drive Swap” ( Optional Hard Drive Swap) before proceeding.

 
 

  1. Place computer on computer tray - with front of computer at right.

  1. Replace velcro straps.

  1. Reconnect cables to rear of computer. Ty-rap as necessary.

4.3  Install Computer Software & Restart

  1. Restore power to the console and install software onto the replacement computer. Refer to the Software Installation Procedures manual for detailed instructions.

  1. Slide the console tray into place and secure with the two captive screws.

  1. Install console’s front cover.

    1. Lift the cover and engage the top edge of the cover with the top lip of the console.

    1. Slowly swing the cover downward until in place.

    1. Tighten two captive screws at bottom of console to secure cover.

4.4  Optional Hard Drive Swap

Perform this procedure ONLY IF NECESSARY to retain customer image data. There are no FRUs within the HP xw8000 computer, and no other components should be removed. Disturbing any other components within the HP chassis could result in equipment damage, which will void any warranty.

4.4.1  Remove the Side Cover & Front Bezel

With ALL cables removed from both the old and new workstations, perform the following steps:

To prevent damage to components within the HP xw8000 chassis, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

 
 

  1. Unlock the cover on the side of the old workstation.

    The cover keys are attached to the back panel of the system.

  1. Pull out on the latch to release the cover.

  1. Tilt the cover open, then lift it off.

    Illustration 4:  Opening the Side Cover

  1. Remove the front bezel, by gently lifting the three plastic tabs on the left side of the bezel.

    Illustration 5:  Front Bezel Tabs

  1. Rotate the bezel off the workstation chassis.

  1. Repeat the above procedure, to open the new workstation.

4.4.2  Remove the Hard Drives

Do NOT mix IDE & SCSI drives.

Your workstation has either IDE or SCSI hard drives (both types can not be installed in the same system). Do NOT attempt to interchange IDE and SCSI drives. You can connect either:

  • One to five SCSI drives using the integrated SCSI controller, OR

  • One or two IDE drives using the integrated IDE controller

 
 

  1. Place the workstation on its side with the system board facing up.

  1. Remove the power and data (IDE or SCSI) cables from the drive.

    Illustration 6:  Hard Drive Power Cable

    Illustration 7:  Hard Drive Data Cable (SCSI)

  1. Place your fingers on the colored release clips on the sides of the drive, and squeeze inward. Pull gently just enough to release the drive rail latches.

    Illustration 8:  Hard Drive Rails

The drive rails are not screwed on to the drive. Do not hold the drive by the rails when it is not installed in a drive bay, because you could drop and damage the drive.
 
 

  1. Grasp the hard drive with your hand and pull out.

  1. Place the hard drive (with rails attached) on a static mat.

  1. Repeat the above procedures on the new workstation, to extract its hard-drive.

4.4.3  Install the Hard Drives

Hold onto the hard drive - NOT the rails - when handling the drive.
 
 

  1. Insert the old drive (with the image data) into the bay of the new workstation. Load the drives in the order shown in Illustration 9 (IDE) or Illustration 10 (SCSI):

    1. For IDE drives, insert the first drive into bay 2 (second from bottom), and the second drive into bay 3 (third from bottom). Press down gently until the drive snaps into place.

      Illustration 9:  IDE Drive Loading Order

    1. For SCSI drives 1 through 4, insert the drive into the next available bay, loading the drives in the order shown below. Press down gently until the drive snaps into place.

      Illustration 10:  SCSI Drive Loading Order

    1. For SCSI drive 5:

      • Remove the three drive screws from the storage in the system chassis ( Illustration 10).

      • Insert the drive.

      • Install the screws, attaching the drive directly to the bottom floor of the workstation. No drive rails are used.

      Illustration 11:  Installing a fifth SCSI drive

  1. Connect the drive cables:

    1. Connect the power cable to the hard drive (see Illustration 6).

    1. For IDE drives, connect the IDE cable to the drive.

      If your workstations (both old and new) have IDE hard drives, the system has two IDE cables. Connect the primary IDE connector to the hard drives. The secondary IDE connector is for optical drives. Make sure you connect them properly.

    1. For SCSI drives, connect the SCSI cable to the drive. Do NOT change the jumper settings on your old drive.

  1. Repeat Step 1 and Step 2, to place the NEW drive into the OLD workstation.

    NOTE:The old workstation must have the new drive installed, when it is returned.

4.4.4  Replace the Side Cover and Front Bezel

  1. Ensure that all internal cables are properly connected and safely routed in the new workstation.

  1. Replace the front bezel:

    1. Align the plastic quarter rounds on the inside of the bezel with the sheet metal sockets on the right side of the chassis.

      Illustration 12:  Bezel Alignment

    1. Rotate the bezel into position and verify that the plastic tabs on the left side of the bezel click into position on the workstation chassis. The bezel should be flush against the workstation.

  1. Lower the cover onto the chassis (aligning the guide rail on the bottom inside edge of the cover with the bottom edge of the workstation chassis).

    Illustration 13:  Aligning and Closing the Cover

  1. Close the cover.

  1. Lock the cover using the key provided.

  1. Repeat the above procedure, to close and lock the old workstation, with the new (empty) drive installed.
