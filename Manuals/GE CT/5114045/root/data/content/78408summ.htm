Tube Fan and Pump Relay Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

1  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock.

  1. Remove (2) 4 mm cap screws that secure safety cover over tube fan/pump relay (see Illustration 1) .

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the (4) wires from the relay.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove (2) 4 mm cap screws and washers.

  1. Install new relay and safety cover. Torque 4 mm cap screws to 2.3 Nm.

  1. Restore power.

  1. Verify no errors in system log and tube fan is operating.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Plane of Rotation (POR) Alignment.

  1. Perform Beam on Window (BOW) Alignment.

  1. Perform ISO Alignment Procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform CBF/SAG Alignment Process.
