PEG-IG Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

Wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed electronic parts on an anti-static surface.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Gain access to the ICE box (see Access to the SRU’s Interior).

  1. Disconnect the serial cable from the faceplate of the Pegasus IG board.

  1. Remove the four (4) 2mm Allen head screws that hold the PEG-IG circuit board in place.

Wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed electronic parts on an anti-static surface.
 
 

  1. Remove the old PEG-IG board from the card cage and place it into a static bag.

  1. Remove the new PEG-IG board from its static bag, and inspect the board.

    1. Verify that it has only two (2) jumpers installed as follows (see Illustration 1):

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify that the board’s connectors are free from foreign objects and that no pins are bent.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the four (4) allen head screws and two (2) nylon flat washers on the PEG-IG board faceplate. The washers should be used for the mounting holes on the “tabs” at the left of the faceplate.

  1. Place the new circuit board into the card cage and tighten the four (4) allen head screws to properly secure the board.

  1. Reattach the serial cable to the serial port on the PEG-IG board’s faceplate.

  1. Reassemble the console, reversing steps used earlier. Be certain to reattach any cables removed for access.

  1. Reapply power, bring the system back up, and run the appropriate diagnostic tests.
