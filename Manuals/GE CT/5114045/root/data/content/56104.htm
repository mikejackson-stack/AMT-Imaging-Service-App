2  Procedure

  1. Place computer on computer tray.

  1. Reconnect cables.

  1. Reinstall recon box, making certain to reattach all cables.

  1. Slide tray in, and secure with captive screws.

  1. Reinstall console’s front cover, using a Phillips screwdriver. Refer to “Front Cover,” in Global Console Covers Removal and Installation, for details.

  1. Turn on system power.

  1. Turn on console power switch to start up computer.

3  Finalization

No finalization required.
