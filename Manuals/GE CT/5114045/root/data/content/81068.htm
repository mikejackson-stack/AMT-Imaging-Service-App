4  Procedure

4.1  Accessing the Software

  1. Select [SERVICE DESKTOP] .

  1. Select [REPLACEMENT] .

  1. Select [HOT ISO ALIGNMENT] .

    Illustration 1:   HOT ISO Screen

4.2  SmarTube™ Setup

Perform Tube Installation Certification, if needed. Refer to SmarTube™ Setup.

4.3  Adjustment Procedure

  1. Execute the Heat Soak and Seasoning scan.

  1. Execute Air Scan (small spot).

  1. Execute Air Scan (large spot).

  1. Place the 1/8 inch screw driver on the phantom holder (should be pointing into the Z direction).

  1. Execute Pin Scan (small spot).

  1. Execute Pin Scan (Large spot)

  1. Calculate correction factor with Calculate button.

    NOTE:No movement of the tube is required. This is a software correction done automatically.
