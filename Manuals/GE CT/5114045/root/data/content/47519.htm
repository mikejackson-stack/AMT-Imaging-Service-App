4  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Remove front cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Loosen two screws to remove 2 cables.

  1. Loosen 4 hex screws that fasten the emergency stop button to the cover.

  1. Replace the emergency stop button and reconnect cables.

  1. Reassemble gantry.
