3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

    NOTE:Pay attention to the location of the wires on the defective supply, before you remove them. Restore the wires to their original configuration on the replacement supply.

  1. Remove the Table Drive Power Supply:

    1. Locate the Quad Output PS (lower power supply).

    1. Disconnect the power input and out wires from the terminals.

    1. Locate and remove the 2 screws that fasten the Quad Output PS to the right side of the Power Assembly main bracket.

    1. Slide the defective power supply out of the assembly.

  1. Install the replacement Quad Output PS.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Confirm full cradle travel and Gantry tilt capability.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
