Home Position Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the right Table Side CoversTable Side Covers Removal and Re-install and Cradle Drive Cover, to access the Cal pin (located in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Install and tighten the Cal pin to about one-half inch from the home position, to hold the carriage in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the wires from the Home Position switch terminals.

  1. Remove the nut that fastens the switch in place.

  1. Remove the defective switch from its bracket.

  1. Install the replacement switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the position of the switch in the bracket:

    1. Loosen the two nuts that fasten the bracket in place.

    1. Move the bracket until the switch actuates when the carriage reaches 0.50 ±0.03 inches from the Home Position.

  1. Return the Cal pin to its storage position (in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
