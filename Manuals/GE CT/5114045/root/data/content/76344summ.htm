High Voltage Tank (Cathode) Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

1  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove the 3 M12 cap screws that will release the support bracket near the STC assembly Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate gantry until the Cathode HV transformer tank reaches the 3 o’clock position.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Ensure gantry rotational lock is fully engaged to prevent unexpected gantry motion.

  1. Use the spanner wrench to remove the high voltage cable connector from the high voltage transformer tank.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cables J1, J2 and J6 from the measurement PWB.

Observe the position of the cables and ty-raps for later installation. It is critical to prevent damage to the system during normal gantry rotation (14 G’s of force are felt at 0.5 second rotational speed).
 
 

  1. Remove four screws fastening the cover to the inverter assembly

    (Step Details)

  1. Measure voltage on the two large capacitors to verify 0 volts.

  1. Disconnect J1 connector from the bottom of the inverter assembly.

  1. Disconnect J6 connector from gate driver PWB.

  1. Carefully disconnect four fiber optic cables from gate driver board, making note of where the Ty-Rap is for routing the cable back in it's original position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify HVDC rail or 120 VAC is not present.

  1. Disconnect HVDC cable from capacitor PWB.

  1. Cut Ty-raps from side plate of inverter.

  1. Remove all cables from the Inverter by removing cable restraint at the top of the inverter.

  1. Remove two (2) inverter output leads from H.V. Transformer Tank locations P1 and P2.

  1. Remove the four (4) 3/8 bolts from the inverter baseplate, that fasten the inverter assembly to the H.V. Transformer Tank.

  1. Remove the inverter assembly from the gantry:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) bolts that fasten transformer tank to the rotating base.

  1. Use the hoist to lower the transformer tank to the floor.

  1. Install the new transformer tank.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount the inverter to the HV tank. Torque the four (4) 3/8 inch bolts to 20 ft-lbs.

  1. Remove the host and boom.

  1. Reassemble all cabling and secure with ty-raps as observed in Step 9 through Step 19.

Ensure cables are properly secured. It is critical to prevent damage to the system during normal gantry rotation (14 G's of force are felt at 0.5 second rotational speed).
 
 

  1. Before you install the HV Cable Connector, add 20 cc of dielectric oil to the HV Connector well in the HV Transformer Tank.

  1. Align the cable terminal orienting key with the notch in the receptacle.

Do not over-tighten the locking ring. Over tightening can deform the cable plug sealing surfaces, break the oil seal between receptacle and housing, twist the receptacle, and disrupt internal wiring.
 
 

  1. Slowly insert the cable, to engage the connector pins, and seat the cable in the well.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully wipe up all excess oil.

  1. Secure HV Cables using Large Ty-Raps as shown in Figure 9-68, on page 906.

  1. Reassemble Gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform KV Gain Pots Adjustment.

  1. Perform kV Meter Check.

  1. Perform mA Meter Check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
