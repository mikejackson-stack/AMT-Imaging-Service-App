CTPM-2003 (Check Fans, Vacuum Interior)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Turn off the power.

  1. Verify that system fans and power supply fans are working.

Potential for equipment damage.

Using vacuum hose near live electronic equipment may cause equipment damage due to static.

Make sure equipment is turned off prior to vacuuming near electronic equipment.

 
 

  1. Clean/vacuum interior, and verify that all air inlets and exhaust ports are clean and clear of dirt and obstructions. For H2 and H3 Consoles, refer to Illustration 1 for console front view and Illustration 2 for console rear view. No console illustrations are shown for GOC1 and GOC2 Consoles.
