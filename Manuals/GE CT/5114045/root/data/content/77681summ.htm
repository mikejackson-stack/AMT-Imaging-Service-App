Direction  5114045-800,  Rev#  10

Filament Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Position HEMRC at 1 o’clock to clear fan obstruction.

  1. Remove HEMRC cover.

  1. Disconnect all connectors from HEMRC Interface board.

  1. Disconnect HVDC Power Cable from HVDC+ and HVDC- lugs (3/8” brass nuts).

  1. Disconnect HVDC cable ground bracket, cut choke ty-raps and set cable aside.

  1. Remove (6) 4 mm cap screws and washers and remove HEMRC Interface board.

  1. Cut ty-raps to Cathode High Voltage Cable.

  1. Position cable behind HEMRC assembly.

  1. Remove the J15 DB 9 connector on the left side of the T1 transformer.

  1. Remove J3/J9 CAN communication cable shield ground bracket. (7 mm nuts and washer).

  1. Remove (2) 10 mm nuts and washers securing HEMRC Filter mounting bracket. Set aside bracket.

  1. Remove (4) 10 mm nuts and washers to remove top-tier mounting bracket and remove bracket.

  1. Remove wires at Allen Bradley amplifier R, S.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove wires X1, X3 and H1, H4 from T1 transformer.

  1. Disconnect connections to HEMRC Drive. DC+, DC-.

  1. Disconnect FWB+ (Orange) and FWB- (Purple) at the Full wave Diode bridge on the HEMRC Filter bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect stator cable shield ground bracket and ground wire from the HEMRC filter board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect Stator black T1 wire (U), the white T2V wire (V), and the green T3W wire (W).

  1. Set HEMRC Filter bracket aside

  1. Disconnect SCR1-1 SCR1-2, SCR1-3 wires.

  1. Disconnect Filament power supply wires from fuse next to T1 transformer. Feed them through the grommet into the bottom-tier.

  1. Remove the T1 transformer.

    1. Remove (4) 6 mm cap screws.

    1. Remove (4) 9/16” standoffs.

  1. Remove the (4) 7 mm nuts securing the Allen Bradley amplifier.

  1. Remove the amplifier and set aside.

  1. Free the chopper harness from the quick release wire guides.

  1. Carefully thread the chopper power harness through the mid-tier plate to the white J7 connector. Do not attempt to force the connector through the hole. It will not fit.

  1. Remove remaining (2) 10 mm nuts and washers for mid-tier plate.

  1. Remove the (2) 10 mm nuts on the bottom center support brace.

  1. Carefully lift and flip the mid-tier plate and rest it on the HV tank next to the HEMRC assembly.

  1. Remove the (3) 4 mm cap screws from both sides rear air diverter on bottom plate.

Possible cuts to body parts.
This is sharp spring steel.
Use caution when working around this component.

  1. Remove the (4) 10 mm nuts and washers using the box wrench. (2 are beneath the transformer.)

  1. Remove filament power supply.

1.2   Installation

  1. Install new filament power supply. Torque (4) 10 mm nuts and washers to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Install spring steel air diverters. Torque 3 per side, 4 mm cap screws to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Install mid-tier plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully work chopper power harness back out to mid-tier.

  1. Secure chopper harness in quick clip wire guides.

  1. Install T1 transformer. Torque 9/16” standoffs and (4) 6mm cap screws and washers to 5.9 Nm.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install Allen Bradley amplifier. Torque (4) 7 mm nuts to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Connect the SCR1-1, SCR1-2, SCR1-3 and Filament power supply fuse wires.

  1. Connect the FWB+ (Orange) and FWB- (Purple) wires to the Full wave Diode bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Stator cable shield ground bracket to the HEMRC Filter Bracket. Torque to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Connect the DC+, DC- lead from the filter board and Chopper harness to the amplifier.

  1. Connect the Stator black T1 wire (U), the white T2V wire (V), and the green T3W wire (W).

  1. Connect the T1 transformer X1, X3 and H1, H4 wires. (X3 and H4 is toward the rear).

  1. Connect the T1 transformer R and S wires to the Allen Bradley amp.

  1. Install the top-tier bracket. Torque the (4) 10 mm nuts and washers to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Install the J15 DB 9 connector. Make sure to tighten the finger screws.

  1. Secure the HV cable to the rear of the HEMRC assembly.

  1. Install the HEMRC Interface board. Torque (6) 4 mm cap screws and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Connect all HEMRC connectors.

  1. Secure the J3/J9 CAN communications cable shield ground. Torque (2) 7 mm nuts and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Route HVDC cable loop under top-tier bracket. Secure HVDC cable shield ground. Torque (2) 7 mm nuts and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Secure HVDC choke to left side top-tier support bracket with 2 ty-raps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install HEMRC cover. Tighten 1/4 turn past seated finger tight.

  1. Apply power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Turn ON HVDC and 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify no smoke or arching.

  1. Perform hardware reset.

  1. Assemble gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform kV Meter Check.

  1. Perform mA Meter Check.

  1. Perform Auto mA Calibration located in KV Gain Pots Adjustment .

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
