DAS Control Board (DCB) Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Board Removal

Follow ALL Electro-Static Discharge procedures. Always use ESD Wrist Strap and grounding cords. The use of a ground Monitor is suggested.
 
 

  1. Move the table cradle to the home position, and lower the table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover, and turn OFF all three (3) service switches on the STC backplane. Remove the rest of the gantry covers.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position the DAS at 12 O’clock.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock (see “Rotational Locking Pin,” Equipment Service - Gantry).

  1. Put on wrist strap and use ESD precautions.

  1. Remove the right DAS chassis cover (see Illustration 1):

    1. Disconnect the fiber-optic cable from the Transmit (TX) port.

    1. Loosen the six (6) captive screws that hold the cover in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Eject the DCB, using the red ejector tabs (identical to converter card removal; see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide DCB out of chassis and place into anti-static bag.

1.2   Board Installation

Follow ALL Electro-Static Discharge procedures. Always use ESD Wrist Strap and grounding cords. The use of a ground Monitor is suggested.
 
 

  1. Remove new DAS Control Board from anti-static bag.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify proper Jumper configuration:

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert new board into chassis:

    1. Align DCB edges to card guides in Chassis.

    1. Slide the board into the chassis until red ejector tabs align with the card guides (see Illustration 2).

    1. Fold the Red tabs over to push board into place. See Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

  1. Turn the Gantry 120V power ON (STC backplane switch), and verify DCB Power LED is illuminated.

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Turn on the Axial and 550VDC switches, on the STC backplane.

  1. Perform Flash Download. Ensure successful completion.

2  Finalization

  1. Verify proper functionality:

    1. Run at least10 passes of Scan Data Path Diagnostic.

    1. Take 10 I/Q scans of the 48cm phantom.

    1. Verify fault or reason to replace the board now passes.

  1. Reassemble gantry covers.
