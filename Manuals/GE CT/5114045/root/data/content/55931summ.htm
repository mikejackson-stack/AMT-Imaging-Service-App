Global Console Covers Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Side Covers

1.1.1  Removal Procedure

  1. Loosen the two captive screws at the bottom of the side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the bottom of the cover up and out.

  1. Lift cover off of cover hangers (see Illustration 2, below)

    (Step Details)

1.1.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Engage side cover on cover hangers.

  1. Swing cover into position.

  1. Tighten the two captive screws at the bottom of the side cover.

1.2   Front Cover

1.2.1  Removal Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Loosen two captive screws at bottom of console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate bottom of cover outward and upward until it may be lifted free of the console at the top.

1.2.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Engage top of cover with top front of console.

  1. Swing cover down, into place.

  1. Tighten two captive screws at bottom of console.

1.3   Rear Cover

  1. Loosen two captive screws at top of rear cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tilt cover backward, so top moves away from console.

  1. Lift cover away, so that tabs disengage from bottom lip of opening.

  1. To reinstall, reverse the previous steps.
