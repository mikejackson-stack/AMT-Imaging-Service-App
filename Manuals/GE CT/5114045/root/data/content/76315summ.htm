Transformer Tank Measurement Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry

1  Procedure

  1. Position table to lowest elevation.

  1. Turn OFF facility power to PDU.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures.

  1. Remove, and set aside, both gantry side covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches on the status control box on right side of Gantry.

  1. Lift top cover, and engage prop rod.

  1. Remove Scan window.

  1. Open front cover.

  1. Rotate Measurement Board to 3 o'clock position.

  1. Unplug connectors J1, J2, J5, and J6.

  1. Remove 6 screws and washers that fasten measurement board to High Voltage Supply.

  1. Carefully pry measurement board off High Voltage Tank.

  1. Replace Measurement Board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reassemble Gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform KV Gain Pots Adjustment.

  1. Perform kV Meter Check.

  1. Perform mA Meter Check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
