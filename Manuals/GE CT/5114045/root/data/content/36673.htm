3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ), the right Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install), and the Actuator Cover (see Actuator Cover Removal and Re-install).

  1. Remove the two nuts that fasten the Magnet Rod to its plate at the upper end of the actuator.

  1. Slide the Magnet Rod out of the actuator.

  1. Replace the Magnet Rod, and adjust the Upper and Lower Actuator switches.

    NOTE:Because magnet strengths vary, follow the Actuator Limit Switch Replacement procedure to adjust both Actuator Limit Switches.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
