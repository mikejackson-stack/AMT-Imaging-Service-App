3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn each of four captive Dzus fasteners one-quarter turn CCW, to release the cover.

  1. Turn each of four captive Dzus fasteners one-quarter turn CW, to fasten the cover.

    The screwdriver slot in the fastener appears horizontal when successfully engaged.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Emergency Stop check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
