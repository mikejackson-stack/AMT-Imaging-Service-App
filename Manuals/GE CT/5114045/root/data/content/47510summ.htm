STC Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove plastic safety cover.

  1. Disconnect 4 cables that connect to power supply

  1. Remove 4 hex screws.

  1. Remove STC Power Supply.

  1. Install new Power Supply.

2  Finalization

  1. Refer to adjustment procedures, “STC Power Supplies” (see STC Power Supplies Checks).

  1. Peform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
