Install Replacement Octane2 in H3 Console

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Place computer on computer tray.

  1. Reconnect cables.

  1. Install console’s front cover.

    1. Lift the cover and engage the bottom edge of the cover with the bottom lip of the console.

    1. Slowly lean the cover inward until in place.

  1. Turn on system power.

  1. Turn on console power switch to start-up computer.
