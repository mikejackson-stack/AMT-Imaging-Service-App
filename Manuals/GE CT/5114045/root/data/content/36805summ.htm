Direction  5114045-800,  Rev#  10

Home Latch Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the right Table Side Covers (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install) and Cradle Drive Cover, to access the Cal pin (located in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Install and tighten the Cal pin in the home position, to hold the carriage in place.

  1. Remove the Cradle Assembly (see Cradle Assembly Replacement) and the right side Rail Cover.

  1. Disconnect the harness at the terminal strip.

  1. Remove the four screws that fasten the Latch Assembly to the z-channel.

  1. Remove the defective Latch assembly.

  1. Install the replacement Latch Assembly, but do not tighten the four screws.

  1. Adjust the position of the entire Latch Assembly in its slots:

    1. Adjust the Latch assembly until the distance between the carriage latch block and the forward edge of the latch bar opening equals 0.050 ±0.005 inches.

    1. Maintain this distance while you tighten the four screws.

    1. Adjust the set screw in the latch clevis block, until the outer edge of the latch bar overlaps the outer edge of the carriage latch block by 0.050 ±0.00 inches.

    1. Maintain this distance while you tighten the jam nut.

  1. Make sure the solenoid plunger bottoms out:

    1. Adjust the position of the solenoid bracket, until the clearance between the outer edge of the latch bar and the outer edge of the carriage latch block equals 0.050 ±0.005 inches.

    1. Maintain this distance while you tighten the two screws.

  1. Adjust the position of the spring bracket, until the spring has 0.125 inches pre-load, when the latch bar rests against the set screw.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Cradle (see Cradle Assembly Replacement).

  1. Return the Cal pin to its storage position (in the bottom of the right z-channel, beneath the Cradle Drive Cover).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Verify Cradle latches and unlatches.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
