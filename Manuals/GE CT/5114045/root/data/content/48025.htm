4  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and gantry top covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock.

  1. Remove (2) 4 mm cap screws that secure safety cover over tube fan/pump and relay.

  1. Disconnect cable J15 on OBC backplane.

  1. Cut the 3 mounting ty-raps.

  1. Disconnect the black and white wires from terminal 3 and 4. The terminal 3 wire is white and the terminal 4 wire is black.

  1. Remove harness.

  1. Remove J15 connector.

  1. Remove ambient thermistor assembly.

Make sure new thermistor is mounted in the same position as the original.
 
 

  1. Install new ambient thermistor assembly.
