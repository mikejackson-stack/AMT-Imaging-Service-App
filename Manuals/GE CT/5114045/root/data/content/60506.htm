3  Procedure

3.1  Objective

  1. Verify that the CAM A/B Encoder is functional.

    REFERENCES

    • Functional Interconnects - Rotating Controller Interface Bus (RCIB) - 2305060

    • Functional Interconnects - X-Ray Collimation and Filtration - 2267744

3.2  Procedure Summary

  1. Check the power supply to AMP.

  1. Swap cables with the other AMP.

  1. Select [SERVICE DESKTOP] .

  1. Select [DIAGNOSTICS] .

  1. Select [X-RAY GENERATION] .

  1. Select [COLLIMATOR AND FILTRATION] .

  1. Exercise the CAM A/B encoders and verify no test failures. Reference Illustration 1.
