4  Procedure

4.1  Removal

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    • Side covers.

    • Top covers.

    • Front cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Position HEMRC at 1 o’clock to clear fan obstruction.

  1. Remove HEMRC cover.

  1. Disconnect all connectors from HEMRC Interface board.

  1. Disconnect HVDC Power Cable from HVDC+ and HVDC- lugs (3/8” brass nuts).

  1. Disconnect HVDC cable ground bracket, cut choke ty-raps and set cable aside.

  1. Remove (6) 4 mm cap screws and washers and remove HEMRC Interface board.

  1. Remove J3/J9 CAN communication cable shield ground bracket. (7 mm nuts and washer).

  1. Remove (4) 10 mm nuts and washers to remove top-tier mounting bracket and remove bracket.

  1. Remove wires X1, X3 and H1, H4 from T1 transformer.

  1. Remove the T1 transformer.

    1. Remove (4) 6 mm cap screws.

    1. Remove (4) 9/16” standoffs.

4.2  Installation

  1. Install T1 transformer. Torque 9/16” standoffs and (4) 6mm cap screws and washers to 5.9 Nm.

    • Remember the top spacer plate.

    • X1 and X3 terminals are tank side.

  1. Connect the T1 transformer X1, X3 and H1, H4 wires. (X3 and H4 is toward the rear).

  1. Install the top-tier bracket. Torque the (4) 10 mm nuts and washers to 5.9 Nm.

  1. Install the HEMRC Interface board. Torque (6) 4 mm cap screws and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Connect all HEMRC connectors.

  1. Secure the J3/J9 CAN communications cable shield ground. Torque (2) 7 mm nuts and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Route HVDC cable loop under top-tier bracket. Secure HVDC cable shield ground. Torque (2) 7 mm nuts and washers to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Secure HVDC choke to left side top-tier support bracket with 2 ty-raps.

    This should be flat against the bracket to not interfere with the cover.

  1. Install HEMRC cover. Tighten 1/4 turn past seated finger tight.

  1. Apply power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Turn ON HVDC and 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify no smoke or arching.

  1. Perform hardware reset.

  1. Assemble gantry.
