3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ) and left Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. Remove the elevation encoder belt from the sprockets:

    1. Loosen the two screws that fasten the Elevation Encoder Assembly to the base.

    1. Slide the encoder assembly toward the large sprocket, to remove tension from the encoder belt.

    1. Remove the belt.

  1. After you install the new belt:

    1. Align the splice on the belt with the mark on the large sprocket.

    1. Slide the Elevation Encoder Assembly away from the large sprocket, until the belt deflects 0.250 inches with 32 - 35 oz. applied at its mid-span.

    1. Tighten both screws, to fasten the encoder assembly in place, with tension on the belt.

  1. Loosen the coupler screw, and adjust the Encoder Table Elevation C-pulse (see Elevation Encoder Replacement).

  1. Loosen and adjust the switch cam (see Interference Matrix Switch Replacement).

  1. Characterize the elevation axis (see Elevation Characterization).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. If necessary, turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
