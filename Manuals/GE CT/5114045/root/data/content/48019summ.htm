OBC Thermistor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and gantry top covers.

  1. Tilt Gantry forward to 30 Degrees. (For easier access.)

  1. Turn OFF Axial Enable and HVDC on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock. (OBC boards will be removed.)

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

Use proper ESD procedures when handling circuit boards.
 
 

  1. Remove OBC cover and all circuit boards.

  1. Disconnect all OBC Power I/F connections.

  1. Remove the (4) 4 mm cap screws and washers securing the OBC Power I/F board.

  1. Remove the (3) 4 mm cap screws securing the CPU mounting bracket.

  1. Disconnect the thermistor harness from internal backplane J9.

  1. Cut ty-raps and carefully unscrew the thermistor from the bracket.

  1. Install new thermistor and secure with very small ty-raps.

  1. Install internal J9 backplane connector and mount CPU bracket with (3) 4 mm cap screws finger tight.

  1. Carefully insert LSCOM board and seat in backplane connectors. This will align the CPU bracket.

  1. Tighten the (3) 4 mm cap screws for the CPU bracket.

  1. Remove the LSCOM board.

  1. Install the OBC Power I/F board. Tighten (4) 4mm cap screws and washers.

  1. Complete OBC assembly.

  1. Apply power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Turn ON HVDC and 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify no smoke or arching.

  1. Perform hardware reset.

  1. Assemble gantry.
