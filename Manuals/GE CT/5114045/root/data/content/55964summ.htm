Keyboard Table Top Replacement - Global Consoles

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Locate the latches at either end of the underside of the keyboard table top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull latches inward (toward center of table top) until pins have cleared the bracket holes.

Potential for injury.
The protruding brackets may be a potential source of injury, while working around the console.
Protective “boots” may be made out of bubble wrap or other suitable material, and placed over the ends of the exposed brackets. An example is shown in the illustration below.

  1. Lift table top off of brackets, and set aside.

    (Step Details)

  1. To reinstall the table top, reverse the previous steps, making certain to properly align the latch pins with the appropriate bracket holes.
