4  Procedure

4.1  Laser Diode Amp

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and gantry rear cover.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen laser clamp nut.

  1. Attempt to remove the laser from clamp using the 5mm hex key. If additional clearance is needed, loosen 2 mounting screws NOT alignment screws (see Illustration 1). Remove mounting bracket if necessary.

    Illustration 1:  Laser Mount Assembly

4.2  Coronal Laser Assembly

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove right and rear covers.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen 2 hex screws holding laser assembly in place.

  1. Replace the defective laser assembly.

  1. Reconnect electrical connection to new laser assembly.

  1. Align laser light (see Alignment Light Adjustment).

  1. Reassemble gantry.

4.3  Internal Laser Assembly

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove right and rear covers.

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen 2 screws holding laser assembly in place.

  1. Replace the defective laser assembly.

  1. Reconnect electrical connection to new laser assembly.

  1. Align laser light (see Alignment Light Adjustment).

  1. Reassemble gantry.

4.4  Sagittal/External Laser Mount

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove right and rear covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Disconnect the electrical connection to the defective laser assembly

  1. Loosen 4 hex screws holding laser assembly in place.

  1. Replace the defective laser assembly.

  1. Reconnect electrical connection to new laser assembly.

  1. Align laser light (see Alignment Light Adjustment).

  1. Reassemble gantry.
