4  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Use the quarter inch ratchet to loosen but not remove the 2 lower nuts and washers.

    NOTE:Be careful not to lose the nuts and washers. Also notice that the flat washer is installed first, then the lock washer and then the nut.

  1. Remove 2 upper nuts and washers.

  1. Disconnect control cable.

  1. Remove 4 screws that secure intercom board.

  1. Reinstall intercom board in reverse order. Verify that J2 jumper is in auto position.

  1. In Step 3 and Step 4, be sure to torque to 9 inch pounds for rotational force integrity.

  1. Play canned AUTOVOICE messages.
