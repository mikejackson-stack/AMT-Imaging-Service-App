4  Procedure

  1. Verify that you have all the parts/tools required.

  1. Turn off 120VAC Gantry Power.

  1. Disconnect Detector Cable for failed thermistor probe. There are 3 thermistors and 2 connectors, one on each side of the Detector Plate assembly.

  1. Remove Thermistor wires/pins from the connector. Note on paper their location within the connector. Use pin extractor tool.

    Illustration 1:  DMB or Low and Center Channel side connector

    Illustration 2:  High Channel side connector

  1. Verify correct old Thermistor wires, then cut the old Thermistor cable close to where the Thermistor cable and Detector Heater cables are joined in the shrink-wrap sleeve. Do NOT cut the Detector Heater wires or the shrink-wrap. Leave the old wire in place.

  1. Unscrew the Thermistor wire “Hold-down clamps” as needed.

    Illustration 3:  Remove old thermistor. Low, Center, High Channels left to right.

  1. Unscrew and remove the Thermistor using a 9mm open-end wrench.

Make sure no dirt or debris gets into the detector through the open hole.
 
 

  1. Make sure the new Thermistor is clean before installing (No lint, dirt, debris, etc.)

  1. Screw in the new Thermistor until snug. Use finger pressure on wrench. Do not over-tighten. The metal Thermistor housing is only a shell. The wire should be free so it does not get twisted.

  1. Route the wire along the detector side just like the old wire, against the inner radius of the Heater element.

  1. Secure the wire with the “Hold-down” wire clamps. Make sure the wire is properly positioned at the Thermistor so it doesn't interfere with the DAS Fan Air Plenum, and that the wire isn't too tight, creating a sharp bend at the detector. It should also not be so loose as to get caught or rub on anything during rotation.

  1. Route the wire along side the shrink-wrapped cable going to connector. Ty-rap the new cable to the outside of the shrink-wrapped cable.

  1. Cut the bad harness near the shrink-wrap at the connector end.

  1. Install the pins of the new thermistor wire into the connector as previously marked.

  1. Coil the excess harness and secure with ty-raps. Make sure it does not interfere with anything during gantry rotation.

  1. Re-install the connector and restore power. Perform a hardware reset.

  1. Allow the detector to warm-up 15 minutes before testing.

  1. Test the system. Take several scans for a couple of hours so that you are satisfied that the problem has been corrected.

5  Finalization

  1. Wait at least one (1) hour with power ON for the Detector to warm up.

  1. Verify detector temperature is 36 degrees Celsius.

  1. Perform FastCal.

  1. Perform Image Quality Test Procedures. Take several scans for a couple of hours so that you are satisfied that the problem has been corrected.
