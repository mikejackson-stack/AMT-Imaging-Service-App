Table Side Cover Tape Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Table Side Covers (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Remove the two shoulder screws that fasten the connector to the bracket in the Side Cover.

  1. Remove the terminals from the connector.

  1. Cut the Ty-Raps that fasten the Tape Switch wire to the four brackets.

  1. Remove the Tape Switch.

  1. When you install the replacement Tape Switch:

    1. Orient the shorter wires toward the connector bracket, when you route the wires through the holes in the cover.

    1. Slide the tape switch into the channel.

    1. Do not remove the protective strip from the adhesive.

    1. Replace the side cover.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

  1. Test the Tape Switch operation.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Emergency Stop check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
