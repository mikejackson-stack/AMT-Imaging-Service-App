Breathing Light Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry side covers and top covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove the following gantry cover as needed:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 2 cables.

  1. Loosen 4 screws that fasten the breathing light assembly to the cover.

  1. Replace the breathing light assembly panel and reconnect cable.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Verify patient can be heard at the console.

  1. Test using Breath Display button on gantry control panel.
