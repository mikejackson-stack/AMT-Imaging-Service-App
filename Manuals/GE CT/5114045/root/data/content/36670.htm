3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ).

  1. If you plan to replace or adjust the Upper Limit Switch, remove the Actuator Cover (see Actuator Cover Removal and Re-install).

  1. Remove the wires from the switch.

  1. To remove the Upper Limit Switch:

    1. Loosen the screw that fastens the switch in place.

    1. Slide the switch up, along the actuator until free.

    1. Remove the wires from the terminals

  1. To remove the Lower Limit Switch:

    1. Remove the screw and two washers from the switch.

    1. The nut plate is trapped, but may slide down the actuator.

    1. Remove the wires from the terminals

  1. To install either limit switch:

    1. Slide the switch into place on the actuator.

    1. Connect the wires to the W and C terminals.

    1. Do not tighten the screw until you adjust the switch position.

  1. To adjust the Upper Limit Switch:

    1. Locate the calibration plate, on the right side of the base frame.

    1. Loosen the two Cal plate screws.

    1. Move the plate to its horizontal position, and tighten the screws.

    1. Restore power, and elevate the table until the distance between the bottom of the Cal plate, at the UPPER LIMIT SWITCH position, and the center mark on the upper rear leg pivot pin equals 33.62 ±0.03 inches.

    1. Remove power, and attach a continuity device to the W and C terminals.

    1. Loosen the clamping screw on the upper limit switch.

    1. Slide the switch upward until at least 1 of the magnet tubes appears below the switch.

    1. Slowly slide the switch downward until the switch opens, then tighten the screw.

  1. To adjust the Lower Limit Switch:

    1. Locate the calibration bar on the right rear corner of the base frame.

    1. Remove the Cal bar from its storage position.

    1. Reposition the bar in the storage hole, to make it parallel to the rear surface of the base frame, then tighten the screw.

    1. Restore power, and elevate the table until the distance between the bottom outer edge of the Cal bar and the center mark on the upper rear leg pivot pin equals 14.53 ±0.03 inches.

    1. Remove power, and attach a continuity device to the W and C terminals.

    1. Loosen the clamping screw on the switch, and slide the switch downward as far as possible.

    1. Slowly slide the switch upward, until the switch opens, then tighten the screw.

  1. Return the calibration plate and bar to their storage positions.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
