Axial Encoder Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

NOTE:Some steps may require service in blind areas.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

Gantry rotated to a position that offers the best service access, before turning off gantry 120/ 208 VAC.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tilt gantry back to 30 degrees.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF all 3 gantry service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Disconnect the Encoder DB 9 pin connect from the gantry harness.

  1. Carefully cut tie-wraps as necessary.

  1. Using an 8 mm hex key, remove the shoulder screw on which the encoder assembly pivots.

  1. Install in reverse order.

  1. Perform “Resetting the C-Pulse” (see Resetting the C-Pulse).

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Axial Encoder Check, located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Perform Axial Control Functional, located in Axial Motion Checks.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
