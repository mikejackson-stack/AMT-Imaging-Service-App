Gas Spring Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install )and the right Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. The gas springs keep the Actuator under tension, even when you raise the table to maximum height. To remove tension from the Actuator:

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen and remove the lock nut from the upper Actuator mounting pin.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the E-Rings from the top and bottom Gas Spring mounting pins.

  1. Remove the pins from their mounting blocks.

  1. Remove both Gas Springs.

POSSIBLE EXPLOSION HAZARD
COMPACTING A PRESSURIZED GAS SPRING MAY CAUSE IT TO EXPLODE.
DISPOSE OF GAS SPRINGS PROPERLY. Do not throw defective gas springs in the trash. Return both gas springs to: GEMS Recycling Center, Ace Warehouse BLDG. 11, 2200 E. College Ave. Cudahy, WI, 53110

  1. When you install the replacement Gas Springs:

    1. Position the Gas Spring pressure chambers UP, or closest to the front table leg.

    1. Take care to install the E-ring correctly.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Clean excess oil from springs, and exercise table elevation full range with maximum load.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
