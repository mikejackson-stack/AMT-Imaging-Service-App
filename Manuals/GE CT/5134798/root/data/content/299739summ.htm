LCD Video Monitor Setup - NEC 1980SXi

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1  Monitor Connections and Powering Up

  1. Connect the 15-pin Sub-D connector cable(s) between the Operator Console’s HOST and each monitor’s blue connector INPUT 2 D-SUB. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the AC in line cord from the Operator Console to each monitor's rear side using the GE supplied console power cord. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply power to each monitor by setting the Vacation Switch on the left side of the monitor to the ON position. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the front panel far right button to apply power to each monitor. Verify the green LED is illuminated. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

1.2  Monitor Color Temperature and Gamma/Brightness Setup

1.2.1  Tools 2 Factory Preset

FACTORY PRESET: Selecting Factory Preset allows you to reset all On Screen Manager (OSM) control settings back to the factory settings. During this procedure, the RESET button must be held down for several seconds to take effect.

  1. Press the > button. The initial On Screen Manager (OSM) Menu appears. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the > button five (5) times to highlight the Tools 2 Menu. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the SELECT/1↔2 button to enter the Tools 2 Menu. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the < button one (1) time to highlight the FACTORY PRESET symbol. See Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the SELECT/1↔2 button to enter the FACTORY PRESET Menu.

  1. Press and hold the RESET button for three (3) seconds to perform the FACTORY PRESET. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the EXIT button. The initial OSM Menu appears. See Illustration 5.

  1. Press the front panel far right button (see Illustration 4) to remove power. Verify the green LED is not illuminated.

1.2.2  Color Temperature Setup

  1. Press the front panel far right button to apply power to each monitor. Verify the green LED is illuminated. See Illustration 4.

  1. Press the > button to display the initial OSM Menu. See Illustration 5.

  1. Press the > button three (3) times to highlight the RGB Menu.

  1. Press the SELECT/1↔2 button to enter the RGB Menu. The [N] may be highlighted initially. See Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the > button two (2) times to highlight setting 1. Verify it is 9300K. See Illustration 11.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For setting 1 at 9300K) Press EXIT twice and continue with Step 8.

  1. (For setting 1 NOT at 9300K) If necessary, press the SELECT/1↔2 button to enter inside the setting 1 color temperature menu. Change the setting to 9300K as follows:

    1. Press the – or + buttons as necessary to set the TEMPERATURE to 9300K. See Illustration 12.

      (Step Details)

    1. Press the EXIT button to close the Temperature Adjust Menu.

    1. Press the EXIT button to close the Selection 1 Menu.

    1. Press the EXIT button to close the OSM Menu.

NOTE:The monitor must be powered OFF and then ON again to access the Advanced Setup menu in the next section.

  1. Press the front panel far right button to remove power. Verify the green LED is not illuminated. See Illustration 4.

1.2.3  Gamma and Brightness Setup

  1. Press and hold down the SELECT/1↔2 button and, while holding this button, apply power to the monitor by holding the front panel far right button.

  1. Release the SELECT/1↔2 button and verify the green LED is illuminated.

  1. The Advanced Menu should be displayed. See Illustration 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the > button four (4) times to highlight selection 5, GAMMA SELECTION.

NOTE:First time out of the box monitors will not follow the SELECT/1↔2 step. Instead, press the + button three (3) times to scroll down the Menu and then continue with the > button once to highlight CUSTOM VALUE.

  1. Press the SELECT/1↔2 button to enter inside the GAMMA SELECTION Menu.

  1. Press the > button once to highlight CUSTOM VALUE. See Illustration 14.

    (Step Details)

  1. If necessary, press the – or + buttons to set the CUSTOM VALUE to 2.6 (K). See Illustration 15.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the EXIT button.

  1. Press < button four (4) times to highlight Selection 1, BRIGHTNESS.

  1. (For Brightness setting at 220) Continue with Step 12.

  1. (For Brightness setting NOT at 220)

    1. Press the SELECT/1↔2 button to select BRIGHTNESS.

    1. Press the – or + buttons to set the BRIGHTNESS to 220. See Illustration 16.

      (Step Details)

    1. Press the EXIT button to close the BRIGHTNESS menu.

  1. Press the EXIT button to close the Advanced Menu.

NOTE:The monitor must be powered OFF and then ON again to exit the Advanced Setup menu mode and enable the OSM menu mode.

  1. Press the front panel far right button to remove power. Verify the green LED is not illuminated.

  1. Press the front panel far right button to apply power to each monitor. Verify the green LED is illuminated.

1.3  Monitor Positioning

1.3.1  Raise and Lower Monitor Screen

  1. Place your hands on the side of the monitor.

  1. Lift or lower the monitor to the desired height. See Illustration 17.

    (Step Details)

1.3.2  Tilt and Swivel

  1. Grasp both sides of the monitor screen with your hands.

  1. Adjust the monitor tilt and swivel as desired. See Illustration 18.

    (Step Details)

1.3.3  Remove Monitor Stand for Mounting (Optional)

Use this section only if the customer wants the monitor alternately mounted:

  1. Disconnect all cables.

  1. Place your hands on each side of the monitor and lift up to the highest position.

  1. Place monitor face down on a nonabrasive surface. (Place the screen on a 2 inch platform so that the stand is parallel with the surface.) See to Illustration 19.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the stand cover by sliding the top/bottom pieces off the stand. Remove the 4 screws connecting the monitor to the stand and lift off the stand assembly. The monitor is now ready for mounting in an alternate manner. See Illustration 20.

    (Step Details)

Please use the attached screws (4 pieces) when mounting. To fulfill the safety requirements the monitor must be mounted to an arm which guaranties the necessary stability under consideration of the weight of the monitor. The LCD monitor shall only be used with an approved arm (e.g. GS mark).
 
 

NOTE:Use only VESA-compatible alternative mounting method.

  1. Reverse this process to reattach the stand.

2  Finalization

No finalization required.
