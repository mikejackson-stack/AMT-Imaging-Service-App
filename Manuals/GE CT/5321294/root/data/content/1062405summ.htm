ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
HOST COMPUTER CHASSIS CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 40 POUNDS.
DEPENDING ON YOUR HEIGHT AND ACCESS TO EQUIPMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION, LIFTING ASSISTANCE MAY BE REQUIRED. USE YOUR JUDGMENT TO DETERMINE IF LIFT ASSIST OR A SECOND PERSON MAY BE REQUIRED TO ASSIST IN REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch ( Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove the Old Host Computer

  1. Remove the Front and Rear Operator Console Covers per the prescribed cover removal procedure..

  1. Remove the cable connections to the Host Computer chassis being replaced.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the Host Computer chassis

  1. Remove the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws ( Illustration 3) from the front of the Host Computer chassis, holding the chassis to the Operator Console right side compartment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Host Computer chassis forward (out the front of console) and set aside. Refer to the procedure.

3.3   Exchange NIC Card (eth0) Host Computer

  1. Follow the Host Computer NIC Exchange-HP8400 procedure. Then return to these instructions.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Install the Replacement Host Computer

  1. Slide the replacement Host Computer into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Replace the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws to secure the Host Computer chassis to the Operator Console right side compartment. Torque to 4.6 N-m.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the Host Computer chassis and verify that its power switch on the Host Computer’s Power Supply is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear cable connections.

    (Step Details)

  1. For cabling details, refer to GOC6 Console Schematic 5212920SCH for a scalable PDF version of the schematic.

3.5   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the Host Computer front panel Power LED is illuminated. If not, press the Host Computer’s front panel power switch to apply power to the Host Computer.

    (Step Details)

3.6   Verify Replacement Host Computer Operation

  1. Verify that the Host Computer has powered up and is not displaying or sounding any POST Errors.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the Host computer it is necessary to perform software Load from Cold (LFC). Refer to the GOC6 Load From Cold.

    (Step Details)

  1. After completing the software LFC, make certain that you perform a final Save System State.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the LFC procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
