HSDA HDD Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 
 

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove Old HDD

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the failed Hard Disk Drive (HDD) Tray from the HSDA Assembly following the procedure below. To remove a HDD Tray from the HSDA Assembly, unlock the HDD Tray Door, press the HDD Tray Door Release Button, and pull up and out on the HDD tray.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Install Replacement HDD

  1. Slide the replacement HDD Tray into the HSDA Assembly open bay.

  1. Make sure the HDD Tray is securely inserted and HDD Tray Door is closed and locked.

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the HSDA Assembly LCD Panel Power LED is illuminated.

    (Step Details)

  1. Do not allow Applications to start. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

3.5   Verify Replacement HDD Operation

  1. Verify that the replacement HSDA HDD has powered up and is not displaying any Errors. The HDD Status LED on right side of HDD Tray should be illuminated Green.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the HSDA Assembly with its new HDD, perform the System Configuration procedure.

  1. Do not allow Applications to start.

    1. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

    1. Open a Terminal window and log on as Root.

    1. Type: reconfig [Enter]

    1. On the System Tab, select [Yes] on the Rebuild Scan Disk Array and click [Accept] .

      (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the System Configuration procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front Cover.
