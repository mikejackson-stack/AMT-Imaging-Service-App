HSDA Controller Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 
 

IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 
 

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 
 

UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES SHALL THE SYSTEM BE USED TO ACQUIRE DATA DURING THIS PROCESS! FAILURE TO FOLLOW THIS WARNING MAY RESULT IS CORRUPT OR LOST PATIENT DATA.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove Old HSDA Controller Module

  1. Remove the Front and Rear Operator Console Covers per the prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. At the rear of the Operator Console remove the SAS and Network Cables from the HSDA.

  1. Loosen and remove the two (2) Phillips screws holding the Controller Module Retention Clamps in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the two (2) Retention Clamps down and away from the Controller Module to extract the module from the HSDA Assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the Controller Module and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement HSDA Controller Module

  1. Slide the replacement Controller Module into the HSDA Assembly Controller Module bay.

  1. Make sure the Controller Module is securely inserted and replace the two (2) Phillips screws in the Retention Clamps.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the SAS and Network cables to the HSDA Controller Module.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout/Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the HSDA Assembly LCD Panel Power LED is illuminated.

    (Step Details)

  1. Do not allow Applications to start. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

3.5   Verify Replacement HSDA Controller Module Operation

  1. Verify that the HSDA with replacement Controller Module has powered up and is not displaying any Errors.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the HSDA Assembly with its new Controller Module it is necessary to perform the System Configuration procedure.

  1. Do not allow Applications to start.

    1. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

    1. Open a Terminal window and log on as Root.

    1. Type: reconfig<Enter>

    1. On the System Tab, select [Yes] on the Recreate Scan Disk Array and click [Accept] .

      (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the System Configuration procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
