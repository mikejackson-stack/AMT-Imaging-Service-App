MOD Peripheral Tower Replacement

1  Safety

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Access to the MOD Peripheral Tower is required. Make sure adequate space is available on console table top.

-

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Remove Old MOD Peripheral Tower

  1. Remove the SCSI cable connection from the back of the MOD Peripheral Tower chassis being replaced.

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the MOD Peripheral Tower chassis.

  1. Remove the MOD Peripheral Tower and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement MOD Peripheral Tower

  1. Place the replacement MOD Peripheral Tower chassis on the Operator Console.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the MOD Peripheral Tower chassis. Verify that the power switch on the MOD Peripheral Tower Power Supply is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear SCSI cable connections.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the MOD Peripheral Tower front panel Power LED is illuminated.

    (Step Details)

3.5   Verify Replacement MOD Peripheral Tower Operation

  1. Visually verify the Peripheral Tower front panel Power LED is illuminated and that each of the MOD Drive Access LEDs illuminates when media is inserted and the tray is closed.

  1. No other verifications required.
