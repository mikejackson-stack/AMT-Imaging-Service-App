Power Distribution Box Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CRUSH HAZARD
POWER DISTRIBUTION BOX IS LOCATED IN A TIGHT SERVICE ACCESS LOCATION.
CAUTION NEEDS TO BE TAKEN TO PREVENT POSSIBLE PINCH POINTS WHILE REMOVING THE ASSEMBLY.

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Access to both the front and rear of the Operator Console is required. Move the console away from walls and remove obstacles in room.

-

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove Power Distribution Box Assembly

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Disconnect all power cords connected to the Power Distribution Box, both internal and external to console chassis.

  1. Disconnect the Console Power Switch Assembly wiring harness from the Power Distribution Box.

  1. Disconnect the SysLite Cable from J24 on the side of the Power Distribution Box ( Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Accessory Ground Cable(s) from Rear Bulkhead Panel of console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three (3) 7mm Hex mounting nuts holding the Power Distribution Box to the console chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Power Distribution Box towards the front of the console and remove the entire Power Distribution Box assembly from console.

3.3   Install Replacement Power Distribution Box Assembly

  1. Slide replacement Power Distribution Box in left compartment of console.

  1. Install the Power Distribution Box to the console chassis using the three (3) 7mm Hex mounting nuts removed earlier.

  1. Connect the Accessory Ground cable(s) to rear of console.

  1. Connect the SYSLITE Cable to the side of the Power Distribution Box.

  1. Connect the Console Power Switch Assembly wiring harness to the Power Distribution Box.

  1. Connect all power cords connected to the Power Distribution Box, both internal and external to console chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check that all three (3) Circuit Breakers on the Power Distribution Box are set ON.

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

3.5   Verify Replacement Power Distribution Box Operation

  1. Visually verify the Operator Console powers up and that all components (Computers, Monitors, Peripheral Towers, etc.) have power.

    (Step Details)

  1. No other verifications required.

4  Finalization

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
