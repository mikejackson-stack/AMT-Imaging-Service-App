4  Procedure

4.1  Front Cover

4.1.1  Removal Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Loosen two captive screws at bottom of console. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Front Cover Removal - Global Consoles

  1. Rotate bottom of cover outward and upward until it may be lifted free of the console at the top.

4.1.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Engage top of cover with top front of console.

  1. Swing cover down into place, position left or right as required to align mounting points.

  1. Tighten two captive screws at bottom of console.

4.2  Side Covers

4.2.1  Removal Procedure

  1. Loosen the two captive screws at the bottom of the side cover. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Side Cover Removal - Global Console

  1. Rotate the bottom of the cover up and out.

  1. Lift cover off of cover hangers. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Side Cover Hanger - Global Console

4.2.2  Installation Procedure

  1. Engage side cover on cover hangers.

  1. Swing cover into position.

  1. Tighten the two captive screws at the bottom of the side cover.

4.3  Rear Cover

  1. Loosen two captive screws at top of rear cover.

    See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Rear Cover - Global Console GOC6

  1. Tilt cover backward, so top moves away from console.

  1. Lift cover away, so that tabs disengage from bottom lip of opening.

  1. To reinstall, reverse the previous steps.

4.4  Keyboard Table Top Removal

  1. Locate the spring-loaded latches at either end of the underside of the keyboard table top.

  1. Pull the latches inward (toward center of table top), then lift Keyboard Table Top up and away from console.

    Illustration 5:  Keyboard Table Top Release Handles

4.5  Keyboard Table Top Installation

  1. Position table on the brackets so the latches align with the holes.

  1. Pull and release the latches, so the pins fully engage into the bracket holes (pins should protrude beyond outside edge of bracket).
