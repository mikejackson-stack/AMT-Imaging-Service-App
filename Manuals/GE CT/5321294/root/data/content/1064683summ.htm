1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
THE DIG COMPUTER CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 50 POUNDS. IF HARD DISK DRIVES (HDD) ARE NOT FIRST REMOVED FROM HSDA.
REMOVE ALL HDDS FROM HSDA PRIOR TO REMOVING THE HSDA ASSEMBLY. DEPENDING ON YOUR HEIGHT AND ACCESS TO EQUIPMENT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION, LIFTING ASSISTANCE MAY BE REQUIRED. USE YOUR JUDGMENT TO DETERMINE IF LIFT ASSIST OR A SECOND PERSON MAY BE REQUIRED TO ASSIST IN REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION.

TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 
 

IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 
 

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove DIG Computer

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the cable connections at the rear of the DIG Computer chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cord at the rear of the DIG Computer chassis.

  1. Remove the four (4) 5mm socket cap screws and washers from the front of the DIG Computer chassis, holding the chassis to the Operator Console rack.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the DIG Computer chassis forward (out the front of console) and set aside. See .

3.3   Remove GPU Card from DIG Computer

  1. At the rear of the DIG Computer Chassis, release the Chassis Cover Lock using a Phillips screwdriver. Back out lock screw at least two (2) full turns.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Chassis Cover towards the front of the chassis and remove.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp from DIG Computer Chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the two (2) Power Supply Connectors at the rear of the GPU Card.

  1. Remove the two (2) Phillips Screws holding the GPU Card in the DIG Computer chassis and carefully remove the card from the Systemboard.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Install Replacement GPU Card in DIG Computer

  1. Carefully install the replacement GPU Card in the same location (slot) in the DIG Computer. Make sure all cables are clear of the GPU Card and the card seats firmly in the Systemboard.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the two (2) Phillips Screws to secure the GPU Card to the chassis.

  1. Reconnect the two (2) Power Supply Connectors to the rear of the GPU Card.

  1. Reinstall the Expansion Card Hold-down Clamp in the DIG Computer Chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the DIG Computer Chassis Cover and lock the cover in place.

3.5   Re-Install DIG Computer

IMPORTANT: IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS COVERS MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE COVERS ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.

  1. Slide the replacement DIG Computer into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Replace the four (4) 5mm socket cap screws and washers to secure the DIG Computer chassis to the Operator Console rack. Torque to 4.6 N-m.

  1. Mount the power cord to the rear of the DIG Computer chassis and verify that its power supply switch is turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear cable connection(s).

    (Step Details)

3.6   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console’s front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the DIG Computer front panel Power LED is illuminated. If not, press the DIG front panel power switch to apply power to the DIG Computer.

    (Step Details)

3.7   Verify Replacement DIG Computer GPU Card Operation

  1. Verify that the DIG Computer has powered up.

  1. Verify that the DIG Computer is not displaying or sounding any POST Errors.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
