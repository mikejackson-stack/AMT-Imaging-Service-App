HSDA Chassis Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

POTENTIAL FOR INJURY TO SERVICE PERSONNEL
THE HSDA CAN WEIGH IN EXCESS OF 50 POUNDS. IF HARD DISK DRIVES (HDD) ARE NOT FIRST REMOVED FROM HSDA.
REMOVE ALL HDDS FROM HSDA PRIOR TO REMOVING THE HSDA ASSEMBLY.

TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

 
 

IN ORDER TO MEET EMC REQUIREMENTS, THE CHASSIS MODULES MUST BE PROPERLY INSTALLED AFTER SERVICING. CHECK THAT THE MODULES ARE MOUNTED FIRMLY IN PLACE AND LOCKED DOWN WHERE APPLICABLE.
 
 

FOLLOW ALL REQUIRED SAFETY AND PPE PROCEDURES CUSTOMARY FOR YOUR ORGANIZATION WHEN WORKING ON THIS PRODUCT.

3  Procedure

3.1   Power-Off (Shut Down) the Console

  1. Select one of the following methods to power off the Operator Console:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power OFF the Operator Console at the front panel switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform prescribed Lockout/Tagout procedure. For added protection, disconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console.

3.2   Remove Old HSDA Chassis

  1. Remove the front and rear Operator Console Covers per prescribed cover removal procedure.

  1. Remove the cable connections at the rear of the HSDA chassis being replaced.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power two (2) cords from the PSU Modules at the rear of the HSDA chassis.

  1. Carefully remove the eight (8) Hard Disk Drive (HDD) Trays from the HSDA chassis following the procedure below, needed to reduce weight of assembly. (Place removed HDD Trays on a Static Mat while removed from HSDA Chassis.)

    1. To remove an HDD Tray from the HSDA Assembly, unlock the HDD Tray Door, press the HDD Tray Door Release Button, and pull up and out on the HDD tray.

      (Step Details)

    1. Repeat of the other seven (7) HDD Trays in the top two (2) rows of the HSDA Assembly.

  1. Remove the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws from the front of the HSDA chassis, holding the chassis to the Operator Console rack.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the HSDA chassis forward (out the front of console) and set aside.

3.3   Install Replacement HSDA Chassis

  1. Slide the replacement HSDA Chassis into the Operator Console from the front.

  1. Replace the four (4) 5mm socket cap mounting screws to secure the HSDA Chassis to the Operator Console rack. Torque to 4.6 N-m.

  1. Mount the two (2) power cords to the rear of the HSDA Chassis and verify that its power supply switches are turned to the ON position.

  1. Replace the rear cable connection(s).

    (Step Details)

  1. For cabling details, refer to GOC6 Console Schematic 5212920SCH for a scalable PDF version of the schematic.

  1. Re-install all eight (8) HDD Trays back into the new HSDA Chassis, populating the top two (2) rows of the chassis.

  1. Make sure all HDD Trays are securely inserted and HDD Tray Doors are closed and locked.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Power-On the Operator Console

  1. Reconnect the Twist-N-Lock Main Power Cable from rear of console and remove Lockout Tagout protection applied earlier.

  1. Power ON the Operator Console at the console front panel switch.

  1. Visually verify the HSDA Assembly LCD Panel Power LED is illuminated.

    (Step Details)

  1. Do not allow Applications to start. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

3.5   Verify Replacement HSDA Chassis Operation

  1. Verify that the HSDA Assembly has powered up and is not displaying any Errors.

    (Step Details)

  1. In order to assure proper operation of the HSDA Assembly, it is necessary to perform the System Configuration procedure.

  1. Do not allow Applications to start.

    1. During boot-up, cancel Application Startup when the CT Software Auto-start pop-up window appears.

    1. Open a Terminal window and log on as Root.

    1. Type: reconfig [Enter]

    1. On the System Tab, select [Yes] on Regenerate Scan Disk Array and click [Accept] .

      (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. After completing the System Configuration procedure, perform a complete shutdown of the Operator Console and restart the system.

  1. Refer to System Scanning Test to confirm proper operation.

  1. Reinstall Console Front and Rear Covers.
