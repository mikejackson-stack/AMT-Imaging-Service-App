2  Overview

This document provides necessary steps to detailed calibrate the system.

Last Revised:15 September 2008

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Recommend procedure completion by trained personnel.

-

-

4  Procedure

  1. Move table away from gantry and remove any objects from Detector Field of view.

  1. Install all gantry covers and scan window.

  1. Select [SCANNER UTILITIES] from bottom of Scan Monitor.

  1. Select [COLLIMATOR CALIBRATION] from Scanner Utilities window.

  1. Click [OK] on Attention pop-up window.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes. Collimator Calibrations are performed.

  1. When calibrations are completed, an Attention box is displayed.

  1. Click [OK] in Attention message box.

  1. Press [QUIT] in Scanner Utilities window.

5  Finalization

  1. If procedure execution was due to another Service Procedure, return to associated Service Procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another Service Procedure, perform Detailed Calibration.
