Touch Sensor Positioning

1  Procedure

  1. Inspect the side touch sensor for position as follows:

    1. Verify that the position of the both side touch plates are stuck out from the bottom surface of the Table top cover more than 2mm (see illustration below).

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the following Table covers:

      (Step Details)

    1. Push up the side touch plate to activate the touch sensor, and measure the operating distance (D’) shown in illustration blow, then verify that the operating distance (D’) is 1mm ~ 2mm.

      (Step Details)

    1. If the operating distance (D’) is out of tolerance, adjust the sensor position by two set screws (Torque: 2.6Nm / 1.92ft.-lbs).

      (Step Details)

    1. Check other side touch sensors as in step Step 1.c. and step Step 1.d.

  1. For GT1700: Inspect the rear touch sensor for position as follows:

    1. Remove the following Table Covers:

      (Step Details)

    1. Push up the rear touch plate to activate the touch sensor, and measure the operating distance (D’) shown in illustration below, then verify that the operating distance (D’) is 1mm ~ 2mm.

      (Step Details)

    1. If the operating distance (D’) is out of tolerance, adjust the sensor position by two set screws (Torque: 0.56Nm / 0.41ft.-lbs).

      (Step Details)

    1. Check other rear touch sensors as in step Step 2.b and step Step 2.c.

  1. For GT2000 and Global PET/CT Table: Inspect the rear touch sensor for position as follows:

    1. Remove the following Table covers:

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the top plate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Push up the rear touch plate to activate the touch sensor, and measure the operating distance (D’) shown in illustration below, then verify that the operating distance (D’) is 1mm ~ 2mm.

      (Step Details)

    1. If the operating distance (D’) is out of tolerance, adjust the sensor position by two set screws (Torque: 0.56Nm / 0.41ft.-lbs).

      (Step Details)

    1. Check other rear touch sensors as in step Step 3.c and step Step 3.d.

  1. Re-intall the Table covers.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the service Switch Panel in Gantry.

  1. Move the cradle In/Out repeatedly, and raise and lower the Table repeatedly to verify that the Table movement is operating normally.

  1. Push up the side touch plate, and verify that the touch sensor is activated without being hidden behind the Table top cover.
