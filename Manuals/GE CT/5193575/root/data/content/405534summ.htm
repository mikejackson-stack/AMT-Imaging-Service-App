1  Procedure

1.1   Mono-Polar High Voltage Tank (MPHVT) Removal

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Remove gantry side, top, and front covers.

  1. Turn OFF all three switches (AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, 120 VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Rotate the gantry such that HVT assembly is in the 2 o'clock position, and that you can just barely reach the lower M12 tank mounting bolts. This position will ease removal and installation.

  1. Lock the gantry in position, using the rotational lock. Ensure that gantry rotation is locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

  1. Remove HV cable candlestick. You will need to cut the three tie-wraps on the inverter and the aux box clamps.

  1. Place a cover over the candlestick.

  1. Place a cap or paper towel in the top of the receptacle to prevent oil spillage and dirt from contaminating the receptacle.

  1. Remove EMC Box cover between Inverter and HV Tank.

  1. Remove the EMC Box cover bracket on the Inverter holding the EMC Box cover. (You need to remove four-4M 8 mm cap screws and four-4M flat washers. Save them to remount the bracket.)

  1. Remove the top of the EMC Box on the HV Tank (white nylon cover) to expose the wire connections. (You need to remove 2 bolts and save them to reattach the top of the box.)

  1. Disconnect the wire connections from the HV Tank. This includes:

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the hoist hook to the tank lifting point. Remove all slack in the chain and apply light tension.

  1. Remove the four 4M mounting screws, starting with the bottom then the top. The new tank should come with mounting hardware. If new screws are provided with the tank, throw these screws away.

  1. Using the hoist, carefully lift the tank and swing it free from the gantry.

1.2   Mono-Polar High Voltage Tank Installation

NOTE:Follow proper torque specifications (see Torque Wrench Information) for all fasteners.

  1. Lift new tank using gantry hoist.

  1. Set the tank on the two guide pins located on the tank bracket.

  1. Use NEW M12 mounting bolts and washers. (If new bolts are not supplied, clean all dried Loctite from existing bolts.) Do not use Loctite on the new bolts.

  1. Apply “preload” torque:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the hoist hook from the tank.

  1. Apply the final torque to the M12 mounting bolts:

    (Step Details)

An incorrectly sealed and secured HV cable can result in damage to the tank, tube, or other parts of the gantry.
 
 

  1. Secure the HV cable. Reference procedure: Securing HV Cable.

  1. Reattach the wiring:

    (Step Details)

  1. Reattach the top of the EMC Box (white nylon cover) to the HV Tank using the two bolts from before.

  1. Reattach the EMC Box cover bracket to the Inverter using the four- 4M 8mm cap screws and 4M flat washers. Add Loctite 242 to the screws as required, and torque them to 2.3 N-m.

  1. Reattach the EMC cover between the Inverter and the HV Tank. Add Loctite 242 to the bolts as required, and torque them to 2.3 N-m.

2  Finalization

  1. Complete a balance check (see Gantry Balance Procedure).

  1. Run Filament Calibration.
