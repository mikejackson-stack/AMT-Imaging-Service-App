GTCB Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Perform this step if applicable:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the Front Base Cover.

  1. Disconnect all cable connector from the GTCB Assy.

  1. Loosen 2 screws, and slide GTCB Assy with the bracket toward the Gantry, then remove it from the Table.

  1. Remove the existing GTCB Assy from the bracket, and attach the new GTCB Assy to the bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new GTCB Assy with bracket in place, and tighten the 2 screws.

  1. Re-connect all cable connectors to the GTCB Assy.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Perform the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC), and re-install the Table Covers.
