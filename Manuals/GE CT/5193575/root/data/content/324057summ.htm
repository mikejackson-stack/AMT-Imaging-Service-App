VCT DAS Fan, Fan Plate Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Procedure Steps

  1. Disable Axial Drive on the service switch panel.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove gantry side, top and front covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the DAS/Detector at 12 o’clock and lock gantry rotation.

  1. Shut down power to the rotating assembly using the breaker behind the service switch panel. See Illustration 1-Rotating assembly power breaker.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Removal

  1. Disconnect the fan control lines and loosen captive screws holding the fan plate to the Air plenum. See Illustration 2-VCT Plenum front view. See Illustration 3 for 2006 Style plenum changes. Remove the single cable connection on the fan plate for the 2006 style plenum.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift off the fan plate. The entire plate or individual fan can now be replaced.

1.3   Installation

  1. If replacing a single fan, Install new fan onto the plate and torque nuts to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the fan plate assembly. There are guide pins on each end of the plenum casting.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect fan control lines.

  1. Torque fan plate screws to:

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power to gantry rotational assembly and check to make sure all 5 fans are running. Use a piece of paper or similar object to make sure fans are pulling air into the plenum. A fan that is not running will still have the fan blades moving as air is pushed back out of the plenum through that fan port.
