3.2   Specifications

The Gantry Tilt offset shall not exceed 0.1 mm. Desired offset is 0.0 mm as measured/calculated. Failure to meet this specification will result in Stair Step Artifacts during Cardiac studies and Reformat reconstructions.

3.3   Recommended Recovery

The most common failure for this test is that the table is not level. It is also common that both the table and gantry must be adjusted to meet the required specifications. Proceed to Recovery Procedure. (An Example Follows.)

  • Initial measurement and calculation showed +0.4 mm tilt offset.

    Found table was low in front.

    Re-leveled table.

  • A second measurement and calculation showed +0.2 mm tilt offset.

    Table was now level, so adjusted the gantry. Gantry was tilted forward.

    Adjusted both rear gantry levelers 1 full turn counterclockwise.

  • A third measurement and calculation showed 0.0 mm tilt offset.

3.4   Recovery Procedure

  1. Using a level (preferably a 4-foot level), verify table is level in the Z plane (front-to-back). If not, adjust the rear table leveler pads as follows to achieve this.

    1. A positive offset calculation means the table is low in front (close to gantry) and high in back.

    1. A negative offset calculation means the table is high in front (close to gantry) and low in back.

    1. Loosen all four (4) table mounting bolts.

    1. Adjust the two rear table levelers equally until the bubble in the level is perfectly centered. Turn levelers counterclockwise to lower the table.

    1. Torque all four (4) table mounting bolts when completed.

    1. Perform Gantry Tilt Verification procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the table is level, then the gantry is at fault. Adjust the gantry as follows.

    1. Loosen all four (4) gantry mounting bolts.

    1. Verify the bubble in level is completely within the center circle. See Illustration 3.

      (Step Details)

    1. Gantry Bubble Levels Adjust rear gantry levelers equally to correct tilt offset by turning levelers counterclockwise to lower the gantry (tilt backward).

    1. Each 0.1 mm offset correction requires 1 mm of gantry movement or 1/2 turn of both the levelers equally.

    1. Torque all four (4) gantry mounting bolts when completed.

    1. Perform Gantry Tilt Verification procedure.
