Noise and Uniformity

Last Revised:15 November 2005

1  Term Definitions

Xc 

Mean CT number for the specified center coordinates of the phantom image.

AvXc 

Average Mean CT number for the center of the phantom image: Average the mean CT value for all specified center coordinates of all slices in an exam.

Xo 

Mean CT number for the outside of the phantom image: Average the mean CT value for all specified outside coordinates of one slice.

AvXo 

Average outside mean CT number for the number of slices in an exam.

AvSDc 

Average image noise about the center image coordinate (measured as the standard deviation) of all slices in an exam.

AvSDo 

Average image noise (standard deviation) for the outside of a phantom: Average of all outside coordinates of all the slices in an exam.

2  Procedure

  1. Procedure Click [IMAGE WORKS] Desktop, [BROWSER] .

  2. Click Exam, Series 1.

  3. Highlight Image 9 through 17, then click [VIEWER] .

  4. Click [Format] and select single image view format.

  5. Click [Grid] .

  6. Click [Measure] and select [ROI box] .

  7. Highlight the ROI box and use the left mouse button to resize the ROI box to a 9.95mm x 9.95mm square.

  8. With ROI box 1 highlighted, use keyboard commands in the next step to copy and paste.

    • <Ctrl> + <c> = copy

    • <Ctrl> + <v> = paste

  9. To position ROI Box Cursors 2 and 3 as shown below, 75 mm from ISO, highlight ROI Box and left-click the mouse in the center of the box to drag to the correct position.

  10. Click [Format] and select the 9-on-1 image view format. See illustration, below.

  11. Highlight Box 1 and type prop a in the accelerator bar in the lower left. Press <Enter>.

  12. Highlight Box 2 and type prop a in the accelerator bar in the lower left. Press <Enter>.

  13. Highlight Box 3 and type prop a in the accelerator bar in the lower left. Press <Enter>.

  14. Record and calculate the ROIs’ averages in Table 1.

    1. The mean CT number of the center ROI should equal 0 ±3. (Xc)

    2. The standard deviation of the center ROI should equal 4.3 ±0.4. (SDc)

    3. The average of outer ROI boxes 2 and 3 per image. (AvXo ROI 2 & 3 and AvSDo ROI 2 & 3)

    4. The difference of the AvXo - AvXc should be 0 ±3.

    5. The average of all nine image parameters must not exceed the specifications listed in Table 1.

3  Failure Recovery

3.1  Series Means Specifications

The average of all images of the series must pass QA#3 Series Means specifications:

  • AvXc: 0 ±3.0

  • AvXo - AvXc: 0 ±3.0

3.2  QA#3 (AvSDc) Specifications

The average of all images of the series must pass QA#3 AvSDc specifications:

AvSDc: 4.3 ±0.4

3.3  Recommended Recovery

  1. Perform Auto CT # Adjust.

  2. Repeat Detailed Calibrations.

  3. Rescan and then repeat the test to verify Image Performance.
