2  Overview

The purpose of the Z-Alignment procedure is to align the X-Ray Tube with the Detector and Collimator.

Last Revised:23 October 2008

4  Procedure

4.1  Preparation

If this procedure execution is not due to another service procedure, complete the following preparation steps:

  1. Move table to home position.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Remove scan window, gantry left side cover, gantry top covers and gantry front cover.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

4.2  Data Acquisition

  1. Select [CALIBRATION] tab from Service Desktop.

  1. Select [Z ALIGN] .

  1. Click [MOVE] from Z-Align tool.

  1. Verify [DESIRED TUBE POSITION] from Tube Position window is 0.

  1. Click [OK] from Tube Position window if [CURRENT TUBE POSITION] is not 0.

  1. Click [DISMISS] from Tube Position window.

  1. Click [SCAN] from Z-Align tool.

  1. Press [START] on SCIM when it flashes.

    NOTE:If one or more tube spits occur, repeat steps 7 and 8.

  1. Click [CALCULATE] from Z-Align tool.

  1. If Z-Align tool displays both messages : “Z-Axis Tube Alignment PASSES Specification” and “Detector Skew PASSES Specification,” skip Mechanical Alignment.

    NOTE:If “Z-Axis Tube Alignment PASSES Specification” and “Detector Skew FAILS Specification,” check collimator and detector functionalities, perform appropriate corrective action, and retest.

4.3  Mechanical Alignment

  1. Turn OFF [HVDC ENABLE] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [120 VAC] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Manually rotate gantry until X-Ray tube reaches 2 o'clock position.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Mount dial indicator gauge on Z-axis mounting bracket, located on tube assembly front face, with gauge's probe resting perpendicular to tube casting's front face ( Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Dial Indicator Mounting Location

  1. Set dial of gauge to zero (0).

  1. Loosen the four M-12 mounting bolts on tube assembly about 1/4 to 1/2 turns.

  1. Turn Z-axis adjustment screw, located on tube assembly front face, in direction specified by Z-Align tool, until dial indicator shows Z-Align calculated value.

    EXAMPLE: “Z-Align Adjust 5.000 mm 200.000 mils (move towards gantry).”

    NOTE:1 mil is equal to 0.001 inches.

    Turn adjustment screw counterclockwise for “move towards gantry.”

    Turn adjustment screw clockwise for “move away from gantry.”

  1. Tighten the four M12 mounting bolts on tube assembly in a cross pattern configuration to the following torque values:

    49 ft-lbs66 N-m590 in-lbs680 kg-cm
    NOTE:Ensure the dial indicator value does not change during the tightening procedure.

  1. Disengage rotational lock.

  1. Turn ON [120 VAC] , [AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE] and [HVDC ENABLE] switches on Service Switch panel.

  1. Click [RESTART] from Z-Align tool and repeat Section 4.2, Steps 3 - 9 to re-check adjustment.

  1. Repeat section 4.3 until Z-Align tool displays both messages: “Z-axis Tube Alignment PASSES Specification” and “Detector Skew PASSES Specification.”

    NOTE:If “Z-axis Tube Alignment PASSES Specification” but “Detector Skew FAILS Specification,” check collimator and detector functionalities, perform appropriate corrective action, and retest.

5  Finalization

  1. If procedure execution was due to another service procedure, return to associated service procedure.

  1. If procedure execution was not due to another service procedure, perform Cold ISO Alignment.
