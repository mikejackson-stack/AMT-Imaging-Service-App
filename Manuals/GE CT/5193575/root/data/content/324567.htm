Fast Calibration

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Timing not applicable10 minutesTiming not applicable

2  Overview

Fast Calibration ensures consistent image quality and radiation exposure by correcting for channel gain differences in the detector, detector drift correction resulting from radiation damage, imperfection in the bowtie filter, and removal of bowtie shape from the data.

Last Revised:23 July 2008

3  Procedure

  1. Move the table away from the gantry and remove any objects from the Detector Field of view.

  1. Install gantry covers and scan window.

  1. On the console, select [DAILY PREP] from the bottom of the scan window.

  1. Select [FAST CALIBRATION] from the Daily Preparation window.

  1. Press the [START] button on the SCIM when it flashes.

  1. When completed, select [QUIT] from Daily Preparation window.

4  Finalization

No finalization required.
