Gantry Top Cover Removal and Re-Install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT. POTENTIAL FOR INJURY IF COVERS REMOVED AND POWER IS LEFT ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

3  Procedure

3.1   Top Cover Removal

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT. POTENTIAL FOR INJURY IF COVERS REMOVED AND POWER IS LEFT ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) main power switches (HVDC, 120VAC, and Axial Drive) on the Service Switch Panel (SSP).

  1. Disconnect the power cable on the upper right that connect to the fans on the gantry top cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the end of the top cover nearest to the side cover and tilt upwards.

  1. There are guides on each side of the cover that keeps the cover from sliding side to side as the cover is removed. Slide the cover down the ridges on the gantry front cover until the cover is about chest height.

  1. Grip the cover near the center on each side and lift the cover from the gantry.

  1. Place the cover on the floor, out of the traffic path.

3.2   Top Cover Installation

  1. With the cover in the vertical position, alignment tab pointing upward, grip the cover in the center and lift to about chest height.

  1. Align the guide tabs so that the top cover is centered in the gantry opening. Slowly slide the cover up to the top of the gantry. Carefully push the cover up the ramp on the cover bracket until the top is seated in place.

  1. Lower the top cover into the slotted bracket at each side of the front cover

  1. Connect the cable from the fans to the gantry. Repeat this procedure for the other side’s top cover.

Always turn ON the 120 VAC before the HVDC. Turning ON HVDC power before 120 VAC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn on the three (3) power switches.

  1. Ensure fans work properly.

  1. Re-install the gantry side covers.
