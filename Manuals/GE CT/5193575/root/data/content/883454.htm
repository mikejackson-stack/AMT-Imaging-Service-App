4  Procedure

4.1  Fan Visual Operation Check (Power On Check)

  1. Check that the fan in the rear of the SCSI tower is operational.

  1. Remove console front cover and check that console back chassis fans are operational. (Power On Check.)

  1. Check that the fan in the rear of the computer is operational.

4.2  System Power-Down

  1. Turn OFF Power on front of console.

  1. Turn OFF power panel breaker.

  1. LOCK OUT/TAG OUT!

4.3  General Console Cleaning and Inspection

  1. Use a vacuum cleaner with a HEPA filter and soft brush to carefully clean the following components.

    NOTE:If the amount of dust is thick, remove this dust from screens and fans by hand.

    1. Console Fans and Air Intake Screen (Filters, if present)

    1. Modules Fans and Air Intake Screens

    1. Console Interior

    1. SCSI Tower Fan and Air Intake Screens

    1. Exhaust Ports

  1. Verify that the console is clean and clear of dirt and obstructions.

  1. Visually inspect the following items for wear:

    1. Check for loose or worn cables. All recon and computer cables should be tight to the touch.

    1. Inspect the desktop, keyboard top, and their supports for damage and tightness.

    1. Check EMC gaskets.

    1. Keyboard

    1. Trackball

    1. SCIM

    1. Mouse

      NOTE:If you need to replace an item, refer to the IPL for the part numbers.

      A second mouse is shipped with the system.

4.4  System Power-Up

  1. Power-up the console.

  1. Remove the LOCK OUT/TAG OUT device.

  1. Check that the mouse and keyboard cables are snug.

  1. Turn ON the power panel breaker.

  1. Turn ON the console front power switch.

  1. Boot the console to application level.

  1. Check all console error logs for component problems.

5  Finalization

Conduct monitor adjustments as required.
