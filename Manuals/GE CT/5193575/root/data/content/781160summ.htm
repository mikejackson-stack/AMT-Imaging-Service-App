Gantry Fans CFC Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Move the table to its home position.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120VAC. Turning OFF 120VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) main power switches (HVDC, 120VAC, and Axial Drive) on the Service Switch Panel (SSP).

NOTE:The gantry cover fans CFC is located between the stationary 48 VDC supply and the TGPU.

  1. Perform all required LOTO activities.

  1. Remove the four (4) hex head screws that hold the supply to the mounting plate.

  1. Remove all cable connections.

  1. Replace the CFC.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove the A1 LOTO.

  1. Turn ON the gantry service switches and power up the console.

  1. Install all removed gantry covers.

  1. Reconnect all top cover fan connections.

  1. Ensure that no cables are left in the rotating path.

  1. Return dollies to storage areas.

  1. Retest the system by scanning.
