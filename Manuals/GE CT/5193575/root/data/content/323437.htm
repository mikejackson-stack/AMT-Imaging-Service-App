3.3  Safety

ESD Hazard with the air plenum removed since the Detector FRM cards are exposed. Use proper ESD grounding when working in this area.
 
 

4  Procedure

4.1  Initial Steps if covers and power have not already been removed

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove right side gantry cover.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

  1. Disable Axial Drive and HVDC on the service switch panel.

  1. Position the Detector at 12 o'clock and lock gantry rotation.

  1. Turn off the 120 VAC service switch from the service switch panel.

  1. Remove the gantry front cover.

4.2  Removal Procedures

  1. Disconnect the 48V Power supply output cable to be able to move the cable bundle out of the way for easier plenum removal. See Illustration 1.

    NOTE:With the 2006 Style plenum, the 48V cable routing allows easy plenum removal without needing to remove the 48V cable. Only remove cable if you think it is necessary.

    Illustration 1:  48V Power Supply Cable

  1. If another person is available to help, skip to step 5 to remove plenum with fan plate in place.

  1. Remove the fan cabling (5 fan control lines) and loosen the 12 captive screws holding the fan plate to the Air plenum. See Illustration 2 for VCT Detector front view.

    Illustration 2:  VCT Detector Front View (2005 Style Plenum)

    Illustration 3:  2006 Style Plenum with cable covers

  1. Remove the fan plate.

  1. From the CFC disconnect J10 (left side) and J9 (on back side).

  1. From the DHC disconnect all cables except J1 (remember J4 on the back side) including cable clamp(s). Newer plenums will have all the cables on the front for easy access.

  1. Loosen the 8 captive plenum screws, 3 bottom, 3 top, 1 on each side. Newer plenums may have only 2 screws on the bottom.

  1. Pull up on safety pin small knob and rotate ¼ turn to keep the safety pin disengaged. (on both sides of plenum) See Illustration 4.

    You may need to pull the pin up and shift plenum forward slightly if pin does not release properly. Plenum is held in place by the two safety pins when all screws are released.

    Newer version plenums will have this pin on the side.

    Illustration 4:  Plenum Safety Pin

  1. Use Ribs as handles to lift plenum off detector. Be careful around thermistors. Lift plenum straight away from the gantry to avoid bending the plenum alignment pins on either side of plenum. (see Illustration 5)

    The filter assembly is attached to the rear of the plenum. The back side of the filter is an EMC screen that can be damaged if poked or hit. Make sure the plenum is set on a flat clean surface and not on top of any small objects that can poke the EMC screen.

    Illustration 5:  Air Plenum

  1. Perform the work for which the plenum was removed.

4.3  Plenum Installation

  1. Install the air plenum housing being careful not to hit the EMC mesh on the backside of the plenum during installation. Plenum alignment pins on either end of detector casting can poke holes in the EMC mesh. (see Illustration 6) Make sure no cabling is caught between the detector casting and the air plenum.

    Illustration 6:  Alignment pins for plenum

  1. Torque plenum top and side M8 screws to the value shown in Table 1.

    Table 1:  Plenum top and side M8 screw torque

    19.2 N-m 14.2 lb-ft170 lb-in196 Kg-cm

  1. Torque plenum bottom M6 screws (into light seal plate) to the value shown in Table 2.

    Table 2:  Plenum bottom M6 screw torque

    8 N-m 6 lb-ft 71 lb-in 82 Kg-cm

  1. Install the fan plate if removed and torque the 12 screws to Table 3.

    Table 3:  Fan plate torque

    8 N-m 6 lb-ft 71 lb-in 82 Kg-cm

  1. Reconnect fan control lines and all plenum cabling. Remember the connections on the rear of the older Fan Control and Heater Control assemblies. Install ty-wraps for any removed.

  1. For the newer (2006 style) plenum with the cable cover shields on the front of each controller covering the cables, reinstall the cable covers and torque screws to:

    2.4 N-m1.8 lb-ft21.2 lb-in24.5 Kg-cm

  1. Continue with the procedure that required plenum removal/installation.

    NOTE:When power is restored to the gantry, check to make sure all 5 plenum fans are running. Use a piece of paper or similar object to make sure fans are pulling air into the plenum. A fan that is not running will still have the fan blades moving as air is pushed back out of the plenum through that fan port. Check fan connections and pin sockets if a fan is not running.
