4  Procedure

  1. Click on IMAGE WORKS Desktop, BROWSER. Wait for recon to complete.

  1. Select Exam, Series 1, Image 1.

  1. Click [Viewer] .

  1. Click [Format] and select single image format view.

  1. Click the [Grid] button, a setup grid for positioning ROI Box cursors.

  1. Click [User Prefs] , then click Grid Prefs [Customize…]

    • Matrix Lines = On

    • Line Style = Dotted

    • Grid Spacing = 20

    • Tick Spacing = 10

    • Tick length = 5

  1. Click [OK] , and then [Save As Defaults] .

  1. Create an ROI box cursor of 10.20mm x 10.20mm dimension:

    1. Position the mouse cursor in the Accelerator Command Bar.

    1. Type: rect 244 244 267 244 267 267 244 267<Enter>

      NOTE:The command above denotes rectangular coordinates. Do not attempt to resize the ROI box cursor. If you do accidentally change the size dimensions, correct the error by clicking on [ROI Box] and using the left mouse button to resize the cursor to a 10.20 x 10.20 square.

    1. With the ROI box highlighted, use keyboard commands in the next step to copy and paste:

      • <Ctrl> + <c> = copy

      • <Ctrl> + <v> = paste

  1. Position ROI box cursors as shown in Illustration 1. Highlight ROI Box and left-click the mouse in the center of the box to drag to the correct position.

    Illustration 1:  Positioning ROI Boxes, Single Image Format

  1. Click [Format] and select the 4-on-1 image view format. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Propagating ROI Boxes, 4-on-1 Image Format

  1. Highlight Box 1 and type prop a in the accelerator bar in the lower left. Press <Enter>.

  1. Highlight Box 2 and type prop a in the accelerator bar in the lower left. Press <Enter>.

  1. Record the mean and STD values in Table 1. All Images must pass MEAN.
