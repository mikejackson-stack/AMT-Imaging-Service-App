VCT Detector Replacement Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

3  Procedure

3.1   System Preparation

  1. At the console start the [FRDM Wizard] from the Common Service Desktop Replacement tab. Illustration 1 shows the main screen.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the [Replace Whole Detector] tab and then [Start Process] . This will set up the system software to prepare for a Detector replacement. The physical replacement can now be performed.

  1. Move the table full back out of the gantry but leave it up to use as a work table.

  1. Remove the right side gantry cover and disable Axial Drive and HVDC from the service switch panel.

  1. Position the detector at 12 o'clock and lock gantry rotation.

  1. Shut down all gantry power from the service switch panel.

  1. Remove all gantry covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cover the Tube Collimator port to protect it against dropped tools or screws. (Cloth or any other available item)

3.2   FRU container handling

  1. Open crate and pull out the aluminum detector box. Crate handling instructions are defined on a sheet included on the inside top cover of the crate.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Balance Weight and Air Plenum Removal

  1. Remove the gantry balance weights over top of the detector casting. Keep them in order to put them back in the exact same order when done.

  1. Remove the Detector Air Plenum as shown in Detector Air Plenum Removal/Installation.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Digital Detector Removal

  1. Disconnect cabling over the top of the digital detector and lay the harness out of the way.

    1. Release cable clamps and clamp plates as necessary to release cabling from the digital detector casting but screw them back in place to keep track of them.

    1. All cabling and fiber optic cables will need to be loose from the detector casting.

    1. The black harness goes to routing boards on either side of the detector. It is easier to disconnect them once the detector is slid forward away from the backplane.

    1. Loosen the cabling over the fixed block behind the balance weight threaded rods to help cable movement when installing lift hook.

  1. Make sure ESD strap is in use prior to continuing.

  1. Disconnect the Digital Modules from the backplane by disconnecting digital screws. See Illustration 2.

    1. Completely loosen the digital cable thumbscrews for all 57 digital cables.

    1. Do NOT release the wedge lock clamps. Wedge lock clamps must remain activated to keep the FRDM’s (Digital Modules) in place.

    1. Completely loosen both digital cable screws for a single module at the same time to avoid pulling the backplane connector at an angle. Digital cable screws should pull away from backplane freely when completely disconnected.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull both digital screws of each module together away from backplane (about 3.8”, 8mm) to release the digital connector from the backplane for all 57 modules. See Illustration 3.

    1. Module 57 allows a side view to look and confirm that the digital cable is being released from the backplane when pulling out on the digital cable screws.

    1. Check all digital cables again to verify that they are released.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the ORP J2 connector so the cable and ferite do not catch on the detector during removal.

3.5   Releasing Digital Detector from DAS Plate

  1. Install the gantry lift fixture with detector extension arm and the chain hoist.

  1. Remove the detector top mount screw and discard only the screw, keep the large washer (new mount screw supplied). See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the top cover support out of the way.

    1. Use a marker to draw a line on the top cover mount joint such that it can easily be put back in place later.

    1. Remove the bottom screws and loosen the top screws of the top cover mount to pivot it up and out of the way.

POSSIBLE FALLING OBJECT
DETECTOR MAY FALL OFF HOIST IF THE DETECTOR LIFT HOOK IS NOT PROPERLY INSTALLED
Make sure the slide plate is pinned in the “down” position prior to moving detector

  1. Attach the lift hook to the Detector top mount screw hole. The rod is inserted into the top mounting hole and the slide fits down into the casting to lock the lift hook in place. Make sure the safety pin is installed to keep the slide portion of the hook captive in the detector top casting.

    1. Lift the slide plate up to allow the lift pin to be inserted into the detector top mount hole.

    1. Slide the lift pin completely into the top mount hole.

    1. Make sure the slide plate is down behind the front edge of the detector casting.

    1. Insert the safety pin into the hole on either side of the slide plate to lock the plate in the down position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the hoist to the lift hook and take up slack on the chain.

  1. Remove the 4 detector end plate screws (2 on each end) and discard the screws keeping the washers.

  1. Remove 2 detector rail nuts and discard. New nuts are in the supplied mounting kit.

  1. Attach the alignment pin nose cones on each side of the detector to protect the threaded ends of the alignment pins during removal and installation. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the M5 hex screw (4mm hex wrench) from the rear of the gantry (keep and reuse this screw). See Illustration 4.

  1. Slide the digital detector away from the backplane to access the routing boards on either side of the detector.

  1. Disconnect the black harness from both sides of the detector.

  1. Slowly slide the digital detector away from gantry watching for any cable or other component obstructions.

  1. Lower the digital detector into the FRU crate and mount using the supplied bolts.

  1. Using the Aero-duster, clean out all backplane digital connectors to make sure no particles are in the connectors prior to installing the new detector.

3.6   Digital Detector Installation

  1. Remove the top mount screw from the new detector in the crate to allow installation of the lift hook.

    (Step Details)

  1. Transfer any needed cable clamps from the old detector to the new detector in the same location for ease of reference later.

  1. Switch the lift hook to the new detector. Attach the lift to the new digital detector.

  1. Lift the new detector out of the crate.

  1. On the back side on each end of the detector, rotate the adjustment bushings clockwise as looking from the back of the detector to make sure they are not sticking out prior to detector installation. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the top center mount screw adjustment bushing (on the DAS plate) clockwise so it is not sticking out of the plate. This bushing and the side bushings will be readjusted in an upcoming step just prior to putting the screws in.

  1. Carefully slide the detector onto the alignment pins. Connecting the cables to the routing boards on each side of the detector as you slide it on will make that task easier.

  1. Remove the nose cone alignment pin guides and install the new detector rail nuts (with lock washers). See Illustration 8 for an overview of the mounting order.

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque detector rail nuts in a 2-step process using the torque settings shown in Table 1 and Table 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the rear screw and torque to the values shown in Table 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert a long 12 mm hex bit (don't use a ratchet handle), supplied with the system, into the holes for the end plate screws to access the spacers. Turn clockwise until the spacers are tight using your hands only, no wrench (bushing will be against the DAS plate). See Illustration 9.

    1. The adjuster bushings are just to take up any space between the detector end plates and the DAS plate.

    1. Using more than hand force will cause the detector casting to bow when torquing the last screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new detector end plate screws (2 per side) making sure the large washers are on the screws. They must have the large washer required for proper seating.

  1. Torque the 4 detector end plate screws first to the initial setting and then all 4 to the final settings shown Table 4 and Table 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert a long 12 mm hex bit (no wrench) into the top mount hole to access the spacer. Turn counter clockwise until the spacer is tight using your hands only, no wrench (bushing will be against the detector casting).

    1. This is just to take up any space between the Detector casting and the DAS plate.

    1. Using more than hand force will cause the detector casting to bow when torquing the screw.

  1. Install the new top mount screw with large washer and torque first to the initial setting and then to the final setting shown in Table 6 and Table 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the protective cover from the detector by removing the 3 screws. Put the cover on the old detector in the using the same 3 screws.

  1. Carefully install and finger tighten both digital cable thumb screws using the process below to help avoid cross threading. The screws will go in easy when properly aligned.

    1. Pull the digital cable screws away from the backplane initially and then in toward backplane to help initial connector alignment. Use a flashlight if necessary to see if the connector is aligned to the backplane socket. A “click” can be heard when you set down the screws indicating the screws have hit the backplane threaded socket.

    1. Rotate the screws counter clockwise to hear a clicking sound made by the threads of the screw and threads of the socket. This will insure that the screws are lined up to the connector prior to threading them in.

    1. Start both screws into the sockets. Thread both screws in together so the cable edge connector is pulled into the backplane straight. Stop and back out the screws if any squeak is heard as this indicates cross threading. Try rotating the screws counter clockwise until you hear a second “click”. This can help if the screw was previously cross threaded to avoid the crossed threads.

    1. May need to carefully pull the digital cable screws to one side to help alignment if needed while performing steps a-c above.

  1. When done seating all digital screws, look across the digital screws from the side to see if any screws are sticking out. This is a quick indication of a forgotten screw or potentially cross threaded screw that needs to be backed out and reinserted

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure all digital cable screws are finger tight first, then torque to the values shown in Table 8.

    (Step Details)

3.7   Air Plenum Installation

  1. Install the Detector Air Plenum as shown in Detector Air Plenum Removal/Installation.

  1. Install the gantry balance weights in the same order as removed. Torque as defined in Gantry Balance Procedure.

3.8   Harnessing

  1. On the Low Channel side (by 48V Power Supply) route the cables listed below together and route between studs in position as shown in Illustration 11 and Illustration 12.

    1. 4 DAS Power and CFC/DHCB Power (5 cables)

    1. Generator, CFC, 2 Fiber Optic, Routing board interconnect (5 cables)

    1. 4 DAS Power cables

    1. Generator, CFC, Routing board interconnect (3 cables) (NOTE: CFC cable moves to B with 2006 style plenum)

    1. RCIB, CFC/DHCB Power, 2 Fiber Optic (4 cables)

    (Step Details)

  1. On the High Channel side (ORP side) route the harness as shown in Illustration 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure the front cover display is connected to allow the follow on testing. Don’t need to put covers on until ready for calibrations.

  1. Turn on the gantry 120VAC at the service switch panel.

  1. Release the gantry rotational lock.

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand looking for any potential loose cabling or connectors. Review the cable routing to make sure it is consistent with the definitions in this section.

  1. Enable HVDC and gantry Axial drive from the service switch panel.

3.9   Testing

  1. From the FRDM Process Tool currently open at the console select [Minicheck] and acknowledge the pop up window if the gantry is ready to rotate.

    (Step Details)

  1. After exiting the alignment plot, the DASTools interface will appear. Run “mA Ratio Test” to update the bad channel map for the new detector.

  1. From the DASTools interface run the following rotating tests.

    1. Select and Run [mA Ratio Test] (creates/updates the bad channel map)

    1. Select and Run the [Auto Test] from DASTools.

  1. If all the checks pass continue with the next section. If anything fails, troubleshoot per the appropriately failed test.

  1. Perform any desired quick checks if problem was not a digital issue that was already verified by the mini check tests.

  1. Perform a tube alignment check per the “Z align” instructions. Adjust the tube if necessary.

  1. Perform a tube iso alignment check per the “Iso Alignment Procedure”. Adjust the tube if necessary.

  1. Perform a gantry balance check and rebalance if needed.

3.10   Gantry Reassembly

  1. Install all gantry covers remembering to enable Axial Drive just before installing gantry right side cover. Gantry covers MUST be in place prior to running calibrations.

3.11   System Calibration

  1. The detector requires up to 45 minutes to reach operating temperature prior to starting the calibration process. Failure to wait will cause artifacts later when the detector reaches operating temperature. The time to warm up starts from the time the gantry power was turned on. Check the Common Service Desktop to make sure the detector temperature is at 38 degrees C +/- 1.5 prior to starting calibrations.

  1. From the FRDM Process Tool currently open at the console select [Prep for Calibrations] . The Scanner Utilities window will open.

  1. Perform Collimator calibration using [Scanner Utilities] - [Collimator Cal] .

  1. Perform Full Detailed calibration using [Scanner Utilities] - [Detailed Cal] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform FastCal using [Daily Prep] – [Fastcal] . (Required after every detailed calibration)

3.12   Image Quality Testing

  1. Perform the following Image Quality verification checks.

    1. Position the QA phantom and level it. Landmark on the line provided and perform phantom centering using the [Scanner Utilities] [Phantom-centering] .

    1. Run the ImgSer 20cmQA VDAS64 series (protocol 45.7) scans by selecting them from the [Service] [Manufacturing] list of protocols. Run all series.

    1. Position the 35cm Poly phantom and level it. Landmark on the center of the phantom and perform phantom centering using the [Scanner Utilities] [Phantom-centering] .

    1. Run the ImgSer 35cm VDAS64 image series (protocol 45.8) scans by selecting them from the [Service] [Manufacturing] list of protocols. Run the first 3 series.

    1. Raise the table by 65mm to vertically offset the 35cm Poly phantom.

    1. Run the 4th series of the ImgSer 35cm VDAS64scans (protocol 45.8).

    1. Using the [Image Analysis] tool, analyze each scan. When using the [Image Analysis] tool, run the analysis in order of the series scanned from the image analysis menu for the phantom used.

      (Step Details)

    1. For the offset 35cm poly scan, look for streaks, bands or rings, use the [Image Analysis] manual mode to analyze and artifacts seen. See Table 9 for artifact specifications.

      (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. From the FRDM Process Tool currently open at the console select [Update Detector Configuration] when the system is ready for customer operation. This will read and update the new detector configuration and perform any background cleanup of temporary software files created by the tool during the replacement process.

  1. Tools Return Policy: Re-package all provided service tools, in the original packages, and place into the FRU shipping container. Failure to return these items will result in your Service Contract being billed the significant cost of these tools.

    (Step Details)
