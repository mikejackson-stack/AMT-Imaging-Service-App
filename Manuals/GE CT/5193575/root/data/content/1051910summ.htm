2  Procedure

2.1   Gantry preparation

  1. Remove gantry right side cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Drive, HVDC and 120 VAC switches on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the gantry left side cover, top covers, scan window and rear cover. Connect the rear cover E-Stop cable per instructions in cover removal to allow gantry rotation later in the procedure.

2.2   Part Verification

  1. To unlock the tensioner assembly, loosen the two M12 screws using a 10mm hex key. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the elongated hex screw using a 6mm hex key and a 12 inch extension. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Inspect the spring and spring holder. See Illustration 3. If the spring is green color (p/n 46-297391) and the spring holder is 41mm long (p/n 2258510) these are the old parts. Replace the old parts with the higher tension spring and spring holder. Go to Step 4 for replacement. If the spring is blue color (p/n 5191844) and the spring holder is 39.5mm long (p/n 5191734) these are the correct parts. Re-install correct spring and spring holder and proceed to Step 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove green spring, 41mm long spring holder, and washer.

  1. Install the new blue spring, new 39.5mm long spring holder, and the washer removed in Step 4. The correct spring and spring holder are in part #5192330. This spring and spring holder provide higher belt tension.

  1. Tighten the elongated hex screw until the washer no longer spins freely. Verify that the washer spins with some resistance. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully rotate the gantry by hand two revolutions (clockwise as viewed from the front). Ensure that the belt is tracking properly, insure that the belt teeth are seated properly in the gear teeth, insure that the washer still spins with some resistance.

  1. Tighten the (2) M12 screws to 66N-m (50 Ft-lb) to lock the tensioner assembly. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

2.3   Setup Microphone and Belt Frequency Tool

  1. Perform procedure in Setup Microphone and Belt Frequency Tool (Adv).

2.4   Tension Calculation

  1. Turn on gantry 120 VAC service switch from the service switch panel to allow gantry to rotate easily. Leave the Axial drive switch OFF.

  1. Position tube at 0 degrees (tube at top most position) while rotating by hand in the clockwise direction.

    (Step Details)

  1. With the microphone connected to the laptop/PC and the Belt Frequency Tool running as setup in Setup Microphone and Belt Frequency Tool, go to the gantry axial drive belt upper span near the spring tensioner assembly. Position the microphone in the middle of the upper span of the belt. Position the microphone so that it is approximately 1” away from the front of the belt without touching the belt. Position the laptop/PC so that you can see the belt tension program running.

  1. Lightly strum the belt (like a guitar string) while holding the microphone 1” away from the middle section of the belt. Time it so that you strum the belt immediately after a new “Acquiring data” line is written by the program. The belt frequency will be reported by the program.

    (Step Details)

  1. Take measurements with the tube positioned at 0, 90, 180, 270 degrees and record. Measurements at each tube position must be within 85 – 102 Hz which corresponds to 450 – 650 N belt tension. Average the four readings. The average must be within 89 – 98 Hz which corresponds to 500 – 600 N belt tension.

  1. If readings are not within spec, go to Adjusting Belt Tension and adjust belt tension. If readings are within spec, go to Finalization section in this procedure.

2.5   Adjusting Belt Tension

  1. To unlock the tensioner assembly, loosen the two M12 screws using a 10mm hex key. See Illustration 1.

  1. Adjust the elongated hex screw using a 6mm hex key and a 12 inch extension. Adjust in ¼ turn increments (clockwise tightens the belt). See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Torque the two M12 screws to lock the tensioner assembly per Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the gantry clockwise two turns to even out the belt tension.

  1. Measure the belt frequency again with the tube at 0, 90, 180, 270 degrees. Continue to adjust belt and take readings until measurements are within spec.

3  Finalization

  1. From the Common Service Desktop, Diagnostics Menu, start the Axial Functional Diagnostic.

  1. Select open loop rotation, 0.35 second gantry speed.

  1. Run the test a few times and verify that deceleration noise is acceptable.

  1. Verify that belt tension measurement is still 89 – 98 Hz.

  1. To stop the program running on your laptop/PC, hit <Enter> a couple of times

  1. Install the gantry rear, top and right side covers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable 120 VAC HVDC and Axial Drive service switches from the service switch panel. Press the table drives enable button on the lower right corner of the service switch panel.

  1. Install the gantry right side cover.
