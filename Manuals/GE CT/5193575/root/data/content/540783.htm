Band or Streak Artifact Failure Recovery

1  Specifications

  • Band Artifact: Band Factor < +/-8.0

  • Streak Artifact: Streak Factor < +/- 4.0

2  Recommended Recovery

  • Troubleshoot band or Streak artifact failures, as described in the System Service Manual.

  • Perform Detailed Calibrations and perform all Image Series testing.
