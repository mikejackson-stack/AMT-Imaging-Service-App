Dual Channel Tx Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT. POTENTIAL FOR INJURY IF COVERS REMOVED AND POWER IS LEFT ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT. POTENTIAL FOR INJURY IF COVERS REMOVED AND POWER IS LEFT ON.
Always remove the right side cover first, and turn OFF power at the service switches.

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF the three (3) main power switches (HVDC, 120VAC, and Axial Drive) on the Service Switch Panel (SSP).

  1. Rotate the gantry by hand to gain access to the slipring transmitter module.

    (Step Details)

Be careful to not contaminate the slipring or signal tracks.
 
 

  1. Remove the power and fiber optic cables.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a flat blade screwdriver, remove the two screws holding the transmitter.

  1. Replace transmitter.

  1. Reverse the above steps to install new transmitter

  1. Remove the A1 Lockout/Tagout. Turn ON the gantry service switches, and power up the console.

  1. With a DVM, check the voltage on the supply.

  1. Install all removed gantry covers. Be sure to reconnect all top cover fan connections. Check that no cables are left in the rotating path. Return dollies to storage areas.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Complete a hardware reset.

  1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kvp/40ma, 1000mm table movement)

  1. Acquire 100 axials: ( 120 kvp / 80ma, 0.5 sec.scans)

  1. Acquire 10 helical: (120kvp / 40ma, 30 sec. Scans)

  1. Verify No increase in corrected or uncorrected FEC errors.
