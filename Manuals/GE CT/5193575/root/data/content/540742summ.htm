Acquiring the 35cm Phantom Image Series - VCT

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

The details in this section apply to LightSpeed VCT (64-slice) scanners ONLY. For equivalent information for the Pro32 (32-slice), please refer to Acquiring the 35cm Phantom Image Series - Pro32.
 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

3  Procedure

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 35 cm Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, & center the 35 cm Phantom.

    1. Align phantom using the internal laser lights.

    1. Level phantom using bubble level and the Z-Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    1. Center phantom using the Center Phantom procedure in the left head Scanner Utilities selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

      (Step Details)

  1. Perform the Auto Scan Protocol Setup:

    1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [New Patient] .

    1. Type the following entries in the two listed Patient Information fields:

      (Step Details)

    1. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service tab.

    1. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [Image Quality] .

    1. On the Service Category Protocol List, click on [QA IMGSER 35CM VDAS64] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the internal Landmark.

  1. Execute the protocol and review all images for rings and bands using [Image Works] .
