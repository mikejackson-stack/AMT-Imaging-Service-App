NOTE:If reading is ‘overload’, do not strum so hard. If reading is ‘no trigger’, strum a little harder. Moving the microphone a bit closer or a bit farther can also help in these situations. Continue until you get at least 3 consecutive measurements that are within 5 HZ of each other.

Adjust Belt Tension in small increments. If the frequency is high, loosen the belt. If the frequency is low, tighten the belt.
