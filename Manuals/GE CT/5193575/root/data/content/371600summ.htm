Removal / Installation of Table Covers

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Top Cover Removal / Installation

  1. Top Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Slide the top cover toward the Gantry, to release the inside hook of the cover, and remove the cover from the Table.

      (Step Details)

  1. Top Cover Installation:

    1. Insert the inside hook into the gap between the bracket and top frame, and slide the top cover away from the Gantry.

    1. Screw the top cover to the Table top frame.

      (Step Details)

1.2   Front Top Cover Removal / Installation

  1. Front Top Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Slide the front top cover toward the Gantry, and remove it.

      (Step Details)

  1. Front Top Cover Installation:

    1. Attach the front top cover to the ball catch, and tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

    1. -

1.3   Rear Top Cover Removal / Installation

  1. Rear Top Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove the four screws holding both of the top covers.

    1. Slide the top covers toward the Gantry, and remove the top covers.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the following screws (see Illustration 6).

      (Step Details)

    1. Slide the rear top cover away from the Gantry, and remove it.

      (Step Details)

  1. Rear Top Cover Installation:

    1. Fit the rear top cover into the Table frame, and tighten the five screws.

    1. Re-install both of the top covers.

1.4   IMS Rear Cover Removal / Installation

  1. IMS Rear Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Open the IMS rear cover, and pull it out of the hook.

      (Step Details)

  1. IMS Rear Cover Installation:

    1. Set the hook inside the IMS rear cover to the Table bracket, and tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

    1. -

1.5   IMS Side Cover Removal / Installation

1.5.1  For GT2000

  1. IMS Side Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove two caps and two screws.

    1. Slide the IMS side cover free from the Table until the connectors of the touch sensor switches are visible.

    1. Remove the IMS side cover from the Table.

      (Step Details)

  1. IMS Side Cover Installation:

    1. Connect the connectors of the touch sensor switches.

    1. Attach the IMS side cover to the ball catch, and tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

1.5.2  For GT1700

  1. IMS Side Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove the IMS Rear Cover.

    1. Remove two caps and two screws.

    1. Disconnect the cable connector of the touch sensor.

    1. Remove a screw that fasten the touch sensor to the IMS Side Cover, and remove the touch sensor.

    1. Remove a screw (located at rear of the IMS Side Cover), and loosen a screw (located at front of the IMS Side Cover), then remove the IMS Side Cover from the Table.

      (Step Details)

  1. IMS Side Cover Installation:

    1. Attach the touch sensor to the new IMS Side Cover.

    1. Re-connect the cable connector of the touch sensor.

    1. Attach the IMS Side Cover to the Table, and tighten the two screws.

    1. Install the IMS Rear Cover.

1.6   Front Base Cover Removal / Installation

  1. Front Base Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Set the front base cover up, and remove the cover by lifting it upward to release the hook.

      (Step Details)

  1. Front Base Cover Installation:

    1. Verify that the both side covers are installed.

    1. Set the hook inside the front base cover to the Table bracket, and tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

1.7   Side Base Cover Removal / Installation

1.7.1  For 5154867/5155337 Side Base Cover

  1. Side Base Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove the front base cover.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Open the side base cover, and slide it toward the Gantry to release the hook.

      (Step Details)

  1. Side Base Cover Installation:

    1. Set the hook inside the side base cover to the Table bracket, and tighten the two screws.

    1. Re-install the front base cover.

1.7.2  For 5127459/5127460 Side Base Cover

  1. Side Base Cover Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove the front base cover.

    1. Remove two screws.

    1. Open the side base cover, and slide it toward the Gantry to release the ball catch.

      (Step Details)

  1. Side Base Cover Installation:

    1. Attach the side base cover to the ball catch, and tighten the two screws.

    1. Re-install the front base cover.

1.8   Side Plate Removal / Installation

  1. Side Plates Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

    1. Remove the IMS side cover (left).

    1. Remove two upper support brackets of the side plates by unscrewing 2 (x2) screws, and put the side plates on the floor.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the side base cover (left).

    1. Remove two screws, and remove the front and rear side plates from the Table base.

  1. Side Plates Installation:

    1. Remove the cover plate from the Table.

      (Step Details)

    1. Power up the Table using the Table circuit breaker, and lower the Table to the down limit position, then remove power from the Table by turning off the Table circuit breaker.

    1. Attach the cover plate to the Table top frame, and tighten two screws so that there is a clearance of 0.5 millimeter between the cover plate and the rear leg.

      (Step Details)

    1. Power up the Table using the Table circuit breaker, and raise and lower the Table repeatedly to verify that the cover plate does not interfere with the rear leg plate (Fig. A) and the rear leg (Fig. B). Especially pay attention to the points circled below.

      (Step Details)

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position, and remove power from the Table by turning off the Table circuit breaker.

    1. Attach the upper support bracket of the rear side plate to the Table top frame, and hand tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach a spacer, a bush, and a shaft to the lower side of the rear side plate, and screw the side plate to the Table base frame.

      (Step Details)

    1. Push up the upper support bracket of the rear side plate against the Table top frame, and pull the rear side plate away from the rear leg plate, then tighten the 2 screws of the upper support bracket of the rear side plate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect the GND cable to the Table frame.

      (Step Details)

    1. Power up the Table using the Table circuit breaker, and lower and raise the Table repeatedly to verify that the rear side plate does not interfere with the leg plate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position, and remove power from the Table by turning off the Table circuit breaker.

    1. Attach the upper bracket of the front side plate to the Table top frame, and hand tighten the two screws.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach a spacer, a bush, and a shaft to the lower side of the front side plate, and screw the side plate to the Table base frame.

      (Step Details)

    1. Push up the upper support bracket of the front side plate against the Table top frame, and pull the upper support bracket way from the cover plate, then tighten the 2 screws of the upper support bracket of the front side plate.

    1. Connect the GND cable to the Table frame.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify that the front side plate does not interfere with the cover plate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Power up the Table using the Table circuit breaker, and lower and raise the Table repeatedly to verify that the front side plate does not interfere with the leg plate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Repeat Step 2.a through Step 2.q for the other cover plate and side plates.

    1. Remove power from the Table by turning off the Table circuit breaker, and re-install the front base cover, the side base cover, the IMS side covers, and the IMS rear cover.

1.9   Cover Plate Removal / Installation

  1. Cover Plate Removal:

    1. Raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

    1. Remove the IMS side cover (left or Right).

    1. Remove the cover plate by unscrewing 2 screws.

  1. Cover Plate Installation:

    1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel, and raise the Table to its highest position.

    1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

    1. Remove the following Table covers:

      (Step Details)

    1. Power up the Table from the Service Switch Panel, and lower the Table to down limit position, then turn off all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

    1. Install the Cover Plate according to Side Plate Removal / Installation, Step 2.
