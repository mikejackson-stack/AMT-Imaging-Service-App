3  Procedure

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive and the HVDC switches located on the Service Switch Panel.

NOTE:The c-pulse and scribed line on the Encoder gear are Factory set. Do NOT loosen and screws on the Encoder gear.

  1. Rotate the gantry until the opto-sensor is in the center of the home plate. See Illustration 1 for example of Good and Wrong sensor locations on the Home Plate.

  1. The table below will identify the LED number.

If the c-pulse does not flash or light up, verify that the home plate is in the center of the opto-sensor, and lift as well as rotate the encoder gear assembly one tooth at a time in each direction until the c-pulse lights up (see Illustration 2 ). If the c-pulse is not lined up with the center of the home plate, images may appear slightly tilted or rotated.
