IMS Drive Belt Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the Table to maximum height.

  1. Move the Cradle and IMS to OUT limit position.

  1. Remove power from Table by turning off 120VAC, Axial Drive and HVDC switches on Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the following Table covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable connector of the tape switch.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the pump upper cover as follows:

    1. To make re-installation easier, mark the position of pump upper cover on both sides by outlining the edge with a maker or pencil.

    1. Disconnect the cable connectors of the tape switches and touch sensors.

    1. Remove six (6) screws holding the both sides of the pump upper cover, and remove it.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove IMS motor cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the clean damper is mounted on the IMS motor shaft, remove it.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mark the motor bracket position for re-installation.

  1. Loosen 2 screws to remove tension from the belt, and remove it.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the new belt on and around the motor pulley and the shaft pulley.

  1. Align the surface of the bracket with the mark, and tighten the 2 screws, to fasten the motor, with tension on the belt.

  1. Adjust the belt tension according to IMS Belt Tension Adjustment.

  1. If the clean damper is removed, re-install it to the IMS motor shaft.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Table from the service Switch Panel.

  1. Use the Gantry control keys to move the IMS in and out.

    (Step Details)
