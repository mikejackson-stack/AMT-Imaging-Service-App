4  Procedure

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled. Use the gantry rotation lock to lock out gantry rotation. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Remove gantry side, top and front covers.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

  1. Disable Axial Drive and HVDC on the service switch panel.

  1. Shut down power to the rotating assembly using the breaker behind the service switch panel. See Illustration 1 for Rotating Assembly Power Breaker.

    Illustration 1:  Rotating assembly power breaker

  1. Position the DAS in the 12 to 3 o’clock area for easy access and lock gantry rotation.

  1. Remove connections from all sides of the heater controller or fan controller assembly, whichever is being replaced. There are connections on the back side of both assemblies. See Illustration 2 for location of controller assemblies.

    NOTE:The following pictures show the older (2005 style) plenum. The new (2006 style) plenum and controllers have all sub-D connections that are all on the front.

    Illustration 2:  Fan Controller (CFC) and Heater Controller (DHC) 2005 style

    Illustration 3:  2006 style plenum with cable covers

  1. Remove screws from both sides (3 each side) of the board assembly and slide the assembly to the front to remove it from the plenum casting. See Illustration 4 for mount channel example shown with the Fan Control assembly.

    Illustration 4:  Mount Channels

  1. Slide the new assembly into the Mount Channels.

  1. Reverse the removal steps to reassemble the system.

    NOTE:For the Fan control board of the older (2005 style) plenum: Make sure the fan control lines plugged into their respectively numbered locations. Physical fans are numbered 1-5 left to right as looking at the front of the gantry but control board connections are 1-5 right to left.

  1. Torque the 6 assembly screws to:

    4 N-m2.9 lb-ft35 lb-in40.4 Kg-cm

  1. For the newer (2006 style) plenum with the cable cover shields on the front of each controller covering the cables, reinstall the cable covers and torque screws to:

    2.4 N-m1.8 lb-ft21.2 lb-in24.5 Kg-cm

  1. Turn power back onto gantry, release rotational lock and retest for original failure mode.

  1. Allow the detector to warm up prior to customer use. This will take from 15 to 45 minutes depending on how long the Das/Detector power was off.

5  Finalization

No finalization required.
