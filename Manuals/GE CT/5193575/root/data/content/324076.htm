4  Procedure

4.1  Card Access

  1. Remove gantry right side and top covers.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

  1. Disable Axial drive and HVDC on the service switch panel.

  1. Power off the rotating assembly using the Breaker located behind the service switch panel. See Illustration 2 - Rotating assembly power breaker.

    Illustration 2:  Rotating Assembly Power Breaker

  1. The DCB and DIFB cards are located behind the DAS on either side of the 180 degree weights. See Illustration 3 - VCT DAS Side View.

    Illustration 3:  VCT DAS Side View

  1. Rotate the gantry to position the desired DIFB chassis to the 2 o’clock position for easy access.

  1. Remove/loosen the cover screws on the top of the DIFB chassis. See Illustration 4 - DCB/DIFB Chassis Top View.

    Illustration 4:  DCB/DIFB Chassis Top View

  1. Grab the cover on both sides using your thumbs to press in on the cover pin release tabs and lift the cover straight up and off the chassis. The tabs push a spring-loaded pin in the chassis frame. The cover is kept in line with the chassis by side channels in the chassis.

4.2  Card Removal

  1. If removing the DCB, first disconnect the 2 fiber optic connections from the front side of the DAS plate.

  1. Position the card ejection tool in front of the board to be removed. Refer to Illustration 5. Engage the tool with the card and the bottom rail pins making sure the tool is in line with the card. Pull back to eject the card from the backplane connector.

    NOTE:Use of the ejector tool is optional. Alternate method is to grab the card tab and pull board toward slipring.

    NOTE:VDAS64 systems have 29 DIFB boards, HDAS64 systems have 15 DIFB boards (odd numbered boards only)

    Illustration 5:  Ejection Tool Use

  1. Remove the ejection tool, the card can now be slid out of the chassis.

4.3  Card Installation

  1. Do NOT use the ejection tool to install the new board as excessive force can damage the connectors. Insert the new card and push in on the card tab with your finger to seat the card into the backplane connector. You may need to shift the card up/down in the card guide slightly to align the connector. Gentle pressure is all that is needed to seat the connector in the backplane once the connector is aligned.

  1. Install the DIFB card cage cover making sure the cover base tabs are in the slots at the base of the chassis.

  1. Install the 4 captive cover screws and Torque to:

    2.75 N-m2 lb-ft24 lb-in28 Kg-cm

    Install the 2 M6 cover screws and Torque to:

    7.9 N-m5.8 lb-ft70 lb-in80.5 Kg-cm

  1. Turn on power to the gantry via the breaker as shown in Illustration 2 - Rotating assembly power breaker.

  1. Run the Flash download tool to make sure the card replaced is running the latest firmware. A console pop up message may also indicate that this is required after turning the DAS back on.

    NOTE:The LED's of the new board may not flash in the same pattern as the existing boards until the Flash Download is performed.

5  Finalization

  1. Rerun the failing test or imaging protocol to verify system operation.
