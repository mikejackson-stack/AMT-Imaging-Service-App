3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

The details in this section apply to LightSpeed VCT (64-Slice) scanners ONLY. For equivalent information for the Pro32 (32-Slice), please refer to Acquiring the 20cm QA Phantom Image Series - Pro32.
 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

3.2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

4  Procedure

  1. Set up the system to scan the centered QA Phantom.

    1. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [New Patient] .

    1. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information fields:

      • Patient ID:Service

      • Name:      20cm QA Phantom Image Series

    1. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab.

    1. On the Service tab, select [IMAGE QUALITY] , [QA Phantom ImgSer VDAS64] . (Manually create this protocol if needed.

    Table 1:  QA 20cm Protocol (Series Description: Service QA Image Series) - VCT

    Scan Type:Helical Full 0.4 sec
    Start Location:S0.000
    End Location:S80.000
    No. of Images:17
    Thick Speed:5.0
    20.62
    0.516:1
    Interval (mm):5.000
    Gantry Tilt:S0.0
    SFOV:Small Body
    kV:120
    mA:335
    Total Exposure Time:2.4
    DFOV:22.7
    Recon Type:Std

  1. Click [Show Recon 2] and verify the following:

    NOTE:Recon 2 will not start until the software recognizes that there are no more scans.

    Table 2:  QA 20cm Protocol Recon 2 Settings - VCT

    Recon Enabled:Y
    Recon Start Location:S0.000
    Recon End Location:S80.000
    No. of Images:17
    Thick (mm):5.0
    0.516:1
    Interval (mm):5.000
    DFOV (cm):22.7
    Recon Type:Bone

  1. Position phantom reference marks to internal alignment lights using gantry controls. Do not change phantom centering.

  1. Set internal landmark.

  1. Perform scanning.

  1. Exit the exam.
