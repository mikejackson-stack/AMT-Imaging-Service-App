LightSpeed 7.X Service Information - Adv

High Contrast Spacial Resolution

Last Revised:14 November 2005

1  Procedure

  1. Click on [Erase All] to clear all ROI Boxes.

  2. Click on [Format] and select single image view format.

  3. Select [Image Number 1] , the longest line visible. See Illustration 1.

  4. Click [Measure] and select [ROI Box] .

  5. Highlight the ROI box and use the left mouse button to resize the ROI box to fit into the 1.6 mm (large line pair) volume. (Make the ROI box a square box approximately 9.75mm x 9.75mm.)

Illustration 1:  High Contrast Spatial Resolution Section

The standard deviation should equal 37 ±4.

NOTE:The optional patterns will give successively lower values and are not specified at this time.

2  Line Pair Visualization

  1. Click on [Browser] .

  2. Select Exam, Series 2, Image 1, then click on [Viewer] .

  3. Click on [Format] and select single image view format.

  4. You should see all five 0.6mm bars in images reconstructed with the Bone algorithm.

3  High Contrast Spatial Resolution Failure Recovery

3.1  Specifications

The largest 5-line pair patterns (coded 1.6mm, 1.3mm, 0.8mm, 0.6mm, and 0.4mm) must be visible for the first four images in this series.

3.2  Recommended Recovery

NOTE:The most common failure for this test is that the phantom has air bubbles that are obscuring the line pair patterns.

  1. Carefully inspect the 20cm QA phantom for air bubbles. If required, refill the phantom with water to eliminate all air bubbles.

  2. Repeat the test to verify Image Performance.
