Elevation Characterization - H2 Table

2  Overview

This procedure is used to calibrate the table elevation.

Last Revised on:May 17, 2006

3  Procedure

  1. Remove table right base cover (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install) and right side cover (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Locate the small L-shaped measurement block, stored on top of the bigger measurement block.

    1. Remove the small L-shaped measurement block from its storage position.

    1. Install it in its characterization position, at the right rear of the table.

    1. Move the measurement plate, on the right side of table, to the out position.

    1. Tighten the mounting screws for both plates.

  1. If you are not on the Service Desktop, click on the [SERVICE DESKTOP] icon.

  1. Click on the [CALIBRATION] icon.

  1. Select [MECHANICAL CHARACTERIZATION] .

  1. Select [TABLE ELEVATION] .

  1. Follow the Elevation Characterization instructions displayed on the screen. Important - Take care to measure the exact elevation distances. Incorrect measurement results in Elevation Characterization failure.

    Refer to Illustration 1. Measure the elevation distance as vertically as possible. Measure between the bottom surface of each measurement block and the center of the rear leg upper pivot pin.

  1. Return the measurement plates to the storage position.
