4  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove gantry right side cover and gantry top covers .

  1. Tilt Gantry backward to 30 Degrees. (For easier access.)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Disconnect Power Cord from Fan.

  1. Remove (4) 4 mm cap screws that fasten Fan and Grill in place.

  1. Transfer grill to new fan, if necessary, and install new Fan.

  1. Reassemble Gantry.

  1. Turn ON 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify Fan is operational.

  1. Assemble gantry.
