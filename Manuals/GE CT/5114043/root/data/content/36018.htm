LightSpeed 3.X Service Methods (Advanced)

Setting Date and Time

If the timezone is wrong, you must run reconfig on the OC to select the correct one.

  1. On the Service Desktop, select [Utilities] then [Shutdown Applications] .

  2. Open a Shell and become <root> in the OC window.

  3. Enter: su

    Enter the super user (root) password, default password is #bigguy)

  4. Enter: setdate

    You will now be presented with a series of date questions. Enter time specific values.

    The month is ? <MM>

    The day is ? <DD>

    The hour is ? <HH>

    The minute is ? <mm>

    The year is ? <YYYY>

    MM is month (01-12), DD is day (01-31), HH is hour (00-23), mm is minutes (0-59), YYYY is the year. Verify the OC is set to both the desired time and date.

  5. Close the shell by typing: exit

  6. Type: st to restart application software.
