STC, OBC CPU Bd - Troubleshooting Configurations

This procedure provides expected self test (Power-Up) results for various troubleshooting configurations

1  Normal Power-up Sequence

LEDs on STC & OBC Artesyn boards are ON as indicated by an ’X’ in Table 1.

Table 1:  Normal Power-Up Sequence for STC and OBC Artesyn Boards

 SM1234
Step 1XXXXXX
Step 2X XXX 
Step 3X XX X
Step 4X(Dim) XXX
Step 5X     
Step 6 - POST CompleteXX    

Ping command to STC and OBCR will PASS.

2  Slipring Bypassed

OBC and STC self tests should pass as per Table 1.

Ping command to STC and OBCR will PASS.

3  Artesyn & LSCOMM Boards Installed in OBC & STC Chassis

All other boards are removed from the STC and OBC (i.e., HEMRC, kV, mA, Gentry, Axial)

OBC and STC self tests should pass as per Table 1.

Ping command to STC and OBCR will PASS.

4  STC with Coaxial Cable Removed from Artesyn Board

OBC self tests should pass as per Table 1.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” as indicated by an “X” in the table.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” and blinking as indicated by a “Y” in Table 2.

Table 2:  Failed STC Power-Up Sequence for STC Artesyn Board with Coax Removed

 SM1234
Step 1XXXXXX
Step 2X XXX 
Step 3X XX X
Step 4X(Dim) XXX
Step 5X     
Step 6X     
Step 7X Y  Y
Step 8X Y Y 

Ping command to STC and OBCR will FAIL.

5  STC with LSCOMM Removed

OBC self tests should pass as per Table 1 and OBC LSCOMM has DS6 (DISCONNECT) LED lit.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” as indicated by an “X” in the table.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” and blinking as indicated by a “Y” in Table 3.

Table 3:  Failed STC Power-Up Sequence for STC Artesyn Board with LSCOMM Removed

 SM1234
Step 1XXXXXX
Step 2X XXX 
Step 3X XX X
Step 4X     
Step 5X  YY 
Step 6X  YYY

Ping command to OBCR will FAIL.

Ping command to STC will PASS.

6  OBC with LSCOMM Removed

STC self tests should pass as per Table 1 and STC LSCOMM has DS6 (DISCONNECT) LED lit.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” as indicated by an “X” in the table.

LEDS on STC Artesyn board are “ON” and blinking as indicated by a “Y” in Table 4.

Table 4:  Failed OBC Power-Up Sequence for OBC Artesyn Board with LSCOMM Removed

 SM1234
Step 1XXXXXX
Step 2X XXX 
Step 3X XX X
Step 4X     
Step 5X  YY 
Step 6X  YYY

Ping command to OBCR will FAIL.

Ping command to STC will PASS.

7  OBC with All Boards Removed (EXCEPT Artesyn & LSCOMM)

ALL CABLES removed from OBC backplane EXCEPT J16 (SLIPRING) and J4, J6, J8 (POWER).

OBC and STC self tests should pass as per Table 1.

Ping command to STC and OBCR will PASS.

8  OBC with All Boards Removed (EXCEPT Artesyn & LSCOMM)

ALL CABLES removed from OBC backplane EXCEPT J16 (SLIPRING) AND J4 (+5v & LGND).

Power connections J8 (24v & PGND) and J6 (+/- 15v & SGND) removed.

OBC and STC self tests should pass as per Table 1.

Ping command to STC and OBCR will PASS.

9  OBC with J4 Removed (+5v & LGND)

Artesyn Board Dark (FAILS to power-up)

Ping command to OBCR will FAIL.

Ping command to STC will PASS.
