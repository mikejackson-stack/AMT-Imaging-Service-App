mA Control Board Theory

The 2154834 mA Board is managed by the Cathode mA. This change is required for compatibility with the Performix X-Ray Tube.

Illustration 1:  2154834, HEMRC mA Board

1  mA Board LEDs

DS1: (GRN) CLOOPmA Loop is Closed
DS2: (GRN) INVENInverter is Enabled
DS3: (RED) ANOAnode OverCurrent Fault Detected in mA Monitoring
DS5: (RED) CAOCathode OverCurrent Fault Detected in mA Monitoring
DS6: (RED) FIL FLTFilament Inverter Fault Detected (includes Inverter Fault Filament Undercurrent, Filament Overcurrent Open Filament and Shorted Filament)
DS7: (RED) INV FLTInverter Fault Detected
DS8: (RED) FIL UCFilament Undercurrent Fault Detected
DS9: (RED) OFILOpen Filament Fault Detected
DS10: (RED) SH FILShorted Filament Fault Detected
DS11: (RED) FIL OCFilament Overcurrent Fault Detected
DS12: (RED) IFLT Filament Inverter Fault Detected (same as DS5 except on Inverter section of board)
DS13: (GRN) SMSP Small Focal Spot is Selected
DS14: (GRN) INV ON Inverter is On
