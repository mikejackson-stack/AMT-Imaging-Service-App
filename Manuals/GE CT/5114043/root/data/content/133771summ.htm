CTPM-1429 (Rise and Fall Times)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTE:Determine the type of kV board in the OBC. This procedure lists the names of components on the 46-321064G1-D kV board without brackets. This procedure lists the names of components on the 46-321198G1 and 2138293 kV board in [brackets].

  1. In the OBC, connect a scope to the KV board.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the Scope Time base to 200 usec. Positive or Negative trigger as required.

  1. Select [Protocol Name] .

  1. Select [Load] .

  1. Select [Rise Fall (DDC)] .

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Accept Rx] .

  1. Select [Pause] after the start of scan, to prevent the scope from displaying the fall time.

  1. After you record the rise time, select [Resume] to initiate the fall time scan.

  1. Record the delay between the rise/fall of the EXCM signal, and the 75% threshold crossing of the selected kV (on FORM 4879 (Data Record)). Do not include the waveform overshoot.

    (Step Details)
