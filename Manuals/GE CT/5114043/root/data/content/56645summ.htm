SCIM/Keyboard Replacement - H3 Console

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   SCIM/Keyboard Removal

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system, then turn off console power.

  1. Remove the console front cover. See Front Cover Removal and Installation - H3 Console.

  1. Using caution, carefully remove the existing keyboard cable from the rear of the computer. Tie-wrap existing keyboard cable to other cables located along the back wall of the console.

    (Step Details)

  1. Dress all cables to form a neat service loop to prevent the computer cables from pulling out, then insert the computer tray.

1.2   SCIM/Keyboard Installation

CONNECTING THE KEYBOARD, TRACKBALL, AND MOUSE

  1. Route the keyboard cables under the SCIM, as shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route the mouse and keyboard cable (Run 5) through the console desktop raceway and through the 1" hole on the top of the console. Connect the mouse and keyboard cables directly to the back of the Octane computer ( Illustration 1). Use supplied tie-wraps to dress both cables and form a service loop.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route the trackball cable (Run 6) through the console desktop raceway, and connect it directly to the SCIM.

    (Step Details)

  1. The SCIM cable is run under the monitor table top and connected to the console back bulkhead J19.

  1. Select and install the proper overlay for your system: (1) with Tilt or (2) without tilt.

  1. The keyboard should attach to the SCIM using the supplied Velcro strip and fit snugly against the SCIM when finished, as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check all cable connections and install front cover. Turn on console power and check that the console boots without errors. At the application level, complete functional checks of the keyboard and SCIM. Set voice controls and listening volumes to appropriate levels.

    (Step Details)
