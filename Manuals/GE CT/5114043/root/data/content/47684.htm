4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF all three (3) gantry service switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC).

  1. Remove 4 slotted screws to loosen the dual-switch bodies.

    Reference Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Gantry Tilt Pot Assembly 2178677

  1. Disconnect outside switch. (Black and white wires in top and bottom locations.)

  1. Disconnect inside switch. (Yellow and red wires in top and bottom locations.)

  1. Disconnect molex connector to replace harness.

  1. Cut ty-raps as necessary.

  1. Install new switch assembly and adjust per “Tilt Limit/Interference Adjustments” located in Tilt Pot Assembly Adjustments - Angle, Limit procedure.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

5  Finalization

  1. Verify full range of tilt operation.
