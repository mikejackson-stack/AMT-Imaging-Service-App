4  Procedure

  1. Remove the right gantry cover, and turn off the HVDC and 120VAC service switches on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove both top gantry covers (see Gantry Top Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Rotate gantry until the fuse box assembly is within reach.

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive switch on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove connectors J1, J2 and J3. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  DAS fuse box assembly, with DHCB attached

  1. Remove 2 top allen screws (one front, one back). See Illustration 1.

  1. Drop unit down, and swing out on lower pivot pins.

  1. Slip pivot pins on new DHCB assembly into their slots, and rotate assembly into place.

  1. Replace two allen screws on top of unit.

  1. Reconnect J1, J2 and J3.

  1. Return all three service switches to their ON positions, and reassemble the gantry covers.
