Direction  5114043-800,  Rev#  9

Table Drive Power Supply

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ).

  1. Remove the power supply hold down bracket.

  1. Remove the screws attaching the outlet cover and move the cover aside to gain access to the power supplies.

  1. The Table Drive PS is the upper power supply. Disconnect the wires from both ends of the power supply.

  1. Remove the upper two attaching screws from the right side of the Power Assembly main bracket attaching the Table Drive PS, and slide the power supply out.

  1. To install the Table Drive PS, reverse above steps, making sure to connect the wires correctly.

  1. Refit the Base Covers.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Exercise elevation function.
