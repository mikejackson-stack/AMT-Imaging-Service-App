3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install) and the left Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. Locate the table harness at the rear of the ETC mounting panel:

    1. Disconnect the J15 Encoder cable from the table harness.

    1. Cut the Ty-Raps, to free the cable.

  1. Locate the Elevation Encoder Assembly:

    1. Loosen the screw on the encoder-side of the flexible coupling.

    1. Loosen the two set screws that fasten the thumb wheel to the encoder shaft.

  1. Remove the encoder assembly:

    1. Locate the three servo clamps that fasten the encoder to the mounting block.

    1. Turn each servo clamp 1/2 turn ccw.

    1. Pull the encoder away from the block.

    1. Slide the thumb wheel and the spacer off the shaft.

    1. Remove the encoder from the table.

  1. Install the replacement encoder assembly:

    1. Place the spacer on the Encoder shaft.

    1. Insert the shaft through the block and thumb wheel.

    1. Firmly seat the encoder in the block.

    1. Let the Encoder cable hang down (±45 degrees) while you tighten the three servo clamps.

    1. Press the thumb-wheel against the spacer and the Encoder, while you tighten the two set screws in the thumb-wheel.

  1. C-Pulse Adjustment:

    1. Locate the Calibration plate, on the right side of the base frame.

    1. Loosen the two screws on the Cal plate, move the plate to its horizontal position, then tighten the screws.

    1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

    1. Restore table power, and elevate the table until the distance between the bottom of the Cal plate, at the C-pulse position, and the center mark on the upper rear leg pivot pin equals 27.52 ± 0.01 inches.

    1. Turn the thumb-wheel to rotate the encoder shaft and light the C-Pulse LED on the ETC PWB.

    1. Tighten the flexible coupler screw to clamp the Encoder shaft in the C-Pulse position.

    1. Verify the C-Pulse LED remains lit.

    1. Return the Cal plate to its storage position.

  1. Characterize the elevation axis (see Elevation Characterization).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
