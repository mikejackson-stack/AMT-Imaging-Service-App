Elevation Actuator Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

POTENTIAL CRUSH HAZARD TO YOURSELF OR OTHERS.
IF THE GAS SPRINGS CANNOT SUPPORT THE WEIGHT OF THE TABLE, THE TABLE COULD FALL.
Use the Table Gas Spring Block Service Tool PN# 2144261, or Place a support bar between the z-channel and floor, (Approximately 3.0 feet in length (91.5Cm), and/or enlist the aid of a second person to watch the table.

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
BE SURE TO FOLLOW PROPER LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURES.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ), the right Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install), and the Actuator Cover (see Actuator Cover Removal and Re-install).

  1. Remove the Actuator Cover U-bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Gas Spring Block Service tool as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the motor wires and ground strap.

  1. Cut the Ty-Raps, and remove the clamp that fastens the limit switch wires to the Actuator.

  1. The gas springs keep the Actuator under tension, even when you raise the table to maximum height. To remove tension from the Actuator:

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen and remove the lock nut from the upper Actuator mounting pin.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pay attention to the positions of the two bumper washers and spacer when you remove them.

  1. Retract the Actuator with the power supply, or the hex drive, but do not let the Actuator fall when the rod end clears the upper mounting block.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove, and keep, the E-ring from the lower mounting pin.

  1. Slide the pin out.

  1. Lift and remove the Actuator from the table.

  1. When you install the Actuator:

    1. Coat the lower pin with a thin layer of Molykote grease.

    1. Take care to install the E-ring correctly.

    1. Place one bumper washer on either side of the rod end of the Upper Mounting Pin.

      (Step Details)

    1. Torque the lock nut to 40 ft-lbs.

  1. Connect the motor wires to the corresponding terminals.

  1. Connect the wires to the corresponding W and C terminals of each switch.

  1. Ty-Rap the limit switch harness in place.

  1. Remove the Gas Spring Block Service Tool or wood block or both.

  1. Adjust the Upper and Lower Actuator Limit Switches (see Actuator Limit Switch Replacement).

  1. Reassemble the Actuator Cover (see Actuator Cover Removal and Re-install).

  1. Characterize the elevation axis (see Elevation Characterization).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. If necessary, turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
