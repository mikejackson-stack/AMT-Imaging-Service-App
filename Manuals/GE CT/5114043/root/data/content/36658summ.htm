Actuator Cover Removal and Re-install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install), the right Table Side Covers (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install), and right Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. Locate the Actuator cover:

    1. Remove both of the clips that fasten the spring pin to the actuator cover.

    1. Slide out the pin, to release the spring.

    1. Remove the two screws that fasten the cover hinge to the U-bracket.

    1. Remove the Actuator cover from the table.

  1. When you install the actuator cover:

    1. Center the cover in the rear leg opening.

    1. Tighten the hinge screws

    1. Seat the spring in the notch on the spring pin.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.
