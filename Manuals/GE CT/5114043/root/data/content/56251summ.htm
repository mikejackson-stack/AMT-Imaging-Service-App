RIP Board Replacement

1  Safety

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

The connectors for the PMCs are very fragile, and must be treated gently. Be especially careful when removing or installing a PMC, so that you do not damage the connectors.
 
 

The DIP board is a static sensitive device. Good ESD practices should be followed.
 
 

NOTE:P/N 2197234-2 has been replaced by P/N 2339284. The new replacement part requires a COMPLETE LFC to properly flash it.

3  Procedure

3.1   RIP Board Removal

  1. Gain access to the ICE box (see Access to the SRU's Interior).

The DIP board is a static sensitive device. Good ESD practices should be followed.
 
 

  1. Remove the cables connected to RIP board.

  1. Disconnect the optical cable from the port optical connector labeled RX.

  1. Loosen the thumb screws (knurl nuts) so that the RIP card can be removed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Gently but firmly, grasp the RIP board by it handles and pull it loose and towards you.

    (Step Details)

  1. Immediately place the RIP board in a anti-static bag or onto a static free work surface.

3.2   RIP Board Flash Procedure

Once the RIP Board has been replaced, the flash ROM on it must be programmed to work in the LightSpeed system. This procedure is normally executed as a part of the Config/Reconfig procedure (during or after a Load From Cold).

NOTE:P/N 2197234-2 has been replaced by P/N 2339284. Depending on the board type, a LFC is required or only a pflash. Use the instruction below to determine the action required when replacing the RIP board.

  1. Turn on the console and stop the system at unix level - click on Cancel when the window pops up indicating that you have 5 seconds to stop applications.

  1. Open a unix shell

  1. Type: showprods | grep Helios_MVME <Enter>

  1. If the Prompt comes back with the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. If required (see NOTE, above), rerform a complete software “Load from Cold.” Refer to the appropriate Software Installation Procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Continue with the procedure.

3.3   DIP Replacement, RIP Reinstallation

  1. To remove the DIP from the RIP Board, do the following: (It’s not necessary to remove any standoffs from the RIP to remove the DIP Board.)

    1. On the bottom of the RIP board assembly, remove the two (2) screws nearest the face plate that attach the DIP to the RIP. These screws thread into the DIP board’s edge connectors. See Illustration 3, A.

    1. On the top side of the RIP board, remove the two (2) screws nearest the edge connector retaining the DIP. It’s not necessary to remove the stand-offs. See Illustration 3, B.

    1. Gently pull the DIP and RIP board apart, where they are attached by the PCI/PMC edge connector. See Illustration 3, C.

    1. Lift the DIP board out. Illustration 3, D. Place into a anti-static bag immediately.

    (Step Details)

  1. To install a DIP board, do the following:

    1. Rotate the DIP into position and gently but firmly press down on the PCI/PMC edge connector to seat. See Illustration 3, D then C.

    1. Re-install all of the removed screws. See Illustration 3, B then A.

  1. Gently but firmly, re-install the RIP board assembly into its VME card cage slot and secure with thumb screws.

  1. Attach cables to RIP, SCSI and DIP boards and reassemble console.

  1. Remove lockouts and power up console.
