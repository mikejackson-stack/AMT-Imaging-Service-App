2  Procedure

  1. Shutdown system and remove power.

  1. Remove the console's front cover. Pull out platform upon which the computer rests. Release its tie-down strap, if present.

  1. Remove the PCI module (refer to Octane PCI Card Module Replacement).

Wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed module on an antistatic surface.
 
 

NOTE:The power supply is grounded while its power cord is plugged in. Just have power off to the console.

  1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the two captive screws at the base of the power supply module.

  1. Grasp the handle, pull it out, then unplug the power cord.

    Illustration 1:  Octane Power Supply

  1. Reverse these steps to reinstall.
