2  Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

POTENTIAL FOR ELECTRICAL SHOCK.
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Follow proper lockout/tagout procedures.

  1. Tag and lockout system power.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Remove the console’s front cover, using a Phillips head screwdriver (see “Front Cover,” in Global Console Covers Removal and Installation).

  1. Loosen two captive screws and pull out computer tray.

  1. Remove the recon box:

    1. Disconnect the cables attached to the front of the recon box

    1. Remove the brackets that hold the recon box in place

    1. Move the recon box out of the way, setting it on a clean and stable surface

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the back of the computer.

  1. Lift the computer out and place on a stable and clean work surface.

No finalization required.
