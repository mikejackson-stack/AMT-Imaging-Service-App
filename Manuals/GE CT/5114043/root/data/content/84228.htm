2  Theory

2.1  Why You Got It

The mA Filament Control Board monitors the voltage at the output of the filament inverter. When the improper voltage is detected, it is reported as an open filament. Both large and small filament use the same board circuitry and by K78 on the OBC backplane. K78 is switched by filament circuitry on the mA Control board.

Every time the cathode HV cable is removed from the tube well a Filament Open Error is logged.

2.2  Reference Documents

Functional Interconnect drawings, mA Loop diagram.

2.3  FRUs Involved

  • Tube

  • Cathode HV cable

  • Cathode Tank (contains the filament transformer)

  • Wiring from tank to OBC backplane

  • Filament select relay

  • mA Control Board

  • Harness

  • 38vdc Power Supply

3  Troubleshooting Summary

  1. 38vdc Power Supply.

  2. Ohm Filament and HV Cable.

  3. Filament Drive Verification.

  4. Filament Command Verification.

  5. Verify filament select relay K78 on the backplane of the OBC.

  6. Transformer & Measurement Board verification

  7. Escalate to Level 2 Support.

4  Troubleshooting Details

  1. 38vdc Power Supply - Verify 38vdc at the mA Filament Control Bd.

    FAIL: No 38vdc at the mA Filament Control Bd.

    • Repair

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: 38vdc present at the mA Filament Control Bd. ok, then proceed to Step 2.

  2. Ohm Filament and HV Cable - Use the X-Ray Gen: Ohm Filament procedure.

    FAIL: Filament did not ohm ok

    • Repair / Replace per Job Card.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Filament ohm OK, then proceed to Step 3.

  3. Filament Drive Verification - Use the Error 183124, 214335 - X-Ray Gen: Filament Inverter procedure.

    FAIL: Filament did not ohm OK

    • Repair / Replace per Job Card.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Filament ohm ok, then proceed to Step 4.

  4. Filament Command Verification - Use the Error 183124, 214335 - X-Ray Gen: Filament Inverter procedure.

    FAIL: Filament Command not proper

    • Repair / Replace per Job Card.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Filament Command ok, then proceed to Step 5.

  5. Verify mA Select Relay K78 - Select a 5 amp filament command from diagnostics. Switch between small spot and large spot. Verify that the voltage on the Measurement changes also.

    FAIL: Focal Spot Select relay not working.

    • Repair / Replace Filament Relay.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Focal spot ok, then proceed to Step 6.

  6. Transformer & Measurement BD verification - Use the Error 183101, 183296, 183186 - X-Ray Gen: mA Tolerance procedure.

    FAIL: Filament transformer not working.

    • Repair / Replace per Job Card.

    • Verify System Operation.

    • YOU ARE DONE.

    PASS: Filament transformer ok, then proceed to Step 7.

  7. Last Resort, Do in this order:

    1. Replace the tube.

    2. Replace the cathode tank.

    3. Replace the mA Filament Control Bd.

    4. K78 Relay

    5. Call Zone Support and/or Online Support
