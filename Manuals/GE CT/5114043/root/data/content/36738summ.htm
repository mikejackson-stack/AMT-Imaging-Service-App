Cradle Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Drive the Cradle/carriage to the latched, home position, before you try to remove the assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove, and keep, the six plug buttons that cover the cradle bolt holes.

  1. Loosen and remove the six screws located beneath the plug buttons.

  1. Lift the Cradle upward, to remove the assembly from the table.

  1. To install the Cradle:

    1. Position the rear end of the Cradle over the carriage.

    1. Align the holes in the cradle to the threads in the carriage.

    1. Start, but do not tighten, all six screws.

    1. Look through the gantry bore at the end of table.

    1. Laterally position the Cradle on the drive rollers, until the same gap exists between the sloped sides of the Cradle and the guide rollers.

    1. Tighten the six screws to 13 ft-lbs.

    1. Replace the plug buttons.

  1. Make sure the cradle/carriage does not rest against the latch.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.

  1. Repeat with maximum weight.
