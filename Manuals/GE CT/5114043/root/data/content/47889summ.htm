Display Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Remove front cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove 4 cables.

  1. Loosen 6 screws that fasten the display assembly to the cover.

  1. Replace the display assembly panel and reconnect cables.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Verify self test and counts change for table up/down, in/out and gantry tilt.
