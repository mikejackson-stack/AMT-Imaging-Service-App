X-Ray Gen: Ohm Filament

Estimated Completion Time: 30 minutes

1  Objective of Procedure

The objective of this procedure is to check the continuity of the HVDC cables and the X-Ray Tube small and large filaments. The HVDC Cables can fail generally in an open condition. There are three (3) conductors, labeled “L” for large, “S” for small, and “C” for common. The X-Ray Tube filaments can fail in either open or shorted conditions. You will take resistance measurements through the cables to measure the resistance of the filaments.

2  Procedure Summary

  1. Measure resistance of all HVDC conductors to frame ground.

  2. Measure resistance of the small and large filament through the Cathode HVDC cable.

  3. Measure resistance of the of the Cathode HVDC cable.

3  Procedure Details

  1. Remove power to the gantry using proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  2. Remove the Cathode HVDC cable from the Cathode HV Tank.

  3. Connect the common lead of the DVM to frame ground.

  4. Connect the positive lead of the DVM to “S”, “L”, “C” pins of the HVDC cable each in turn.

    The readings (from step 4) should all be OPEN.

  5. Connect the common lead of the DVM to the “C” pin of the HVDC cable candlestick.

  6. Connect the positive lead of the DVM to the “S” and “L pins of the HVDC cable candlestick each in turn.

  7. Reverse polarity of the test leads and repeat steps 5 and 6.

  8. Compare readings to the table below.

    If a reading of OPEN or high resistance was measured in steps 5 through 7, proceed with the following steps.

  9. Remove the cathode HVDC cable at the X-Ray Tube.

  10. Connect the DVM leads across the HVDC cable, “C” to “C”, “S” to “S”, “L” to “L” each in turn.

    If a reading of OPEN or high resistance is measured in step 9, replace the Cathode HVDC cable.

  11. Inspect the X-Ray Tube Cathode well and verify;

    • No arcing or cracks.

    • Contact springs present in each pin receptacle.

  12. HVDC Cable pins are tight, if applicable, or the pin gap is present.

    Tighten HVDC pin to 18 inch pounds or spread the pin gap using tool, part number 46-208572P44.

  13. Insert HVDC cable back into X-Ray Tube. Repeat steps “5, 6, 7 and 8”.

If readings are still abnormal, replace the X-Ray Tube, as the filaments are damaged.

Failures in steps 1 through 4 are a result of a damaged HVDC cable or filament to case short. Replace appropriate part as determined in this procedure.

RESISTANCE READING THROUGH THE HVDC CABLE.

Performix Tube Cathode

L to C Approximately 0.5 OhmsC to L Approximately 0.9 Ohms
S to C Approx 2.1 OhmsC to S Approximately 0.5 Ohms
L to S Approx 0.7 OhmsS to L Approximately 2.3 Ohms
NOTE:The resistance measurements of the filament are polarity sensitive. The first test point is always referenced as the positive lead of the DVM. There is a diode like effect due to the construction of the filament and focal cup interaction.

At no time should you read any continuity to ground. Readings are approximates, variability in meter leads and measurement methods should be taken into consideration.

END OF PROCEDURE
