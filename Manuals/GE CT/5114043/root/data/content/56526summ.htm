Front Cover Removal and Installation - H3 Console

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. While holding the cover in place with your hand, use a 4mm “Allen” wrench to rotate the pull-rings counterclockwise. Slowly lean the cover forward and lift out of place with both hands. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Installation Procedure

  1. Lift the cover and engage the bottom edge of the cover with the bottom lip of the console.

  1. Slowly lean the cover inward until in place.

  1. While holding the cover in place, use a 4mm “Allen” wrench to rotate each pull-ring clockwise.
