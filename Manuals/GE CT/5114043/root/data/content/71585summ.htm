Remove Defective Octane2 - H3 Console

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. From the service desktop, shut down the system.

  1. Tag and lockout system power.

  1. Turn off console power.

  1. Remove the console’s front cover. Use a 4mm “Allen” wrench to rotate each pull-ring counterclockwise. See H3 Console Front Cover Removal and Installation for details.

  1. Pull out computer tray.

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the back of the computer.

  1. Lift the computer out and place on a stable and clean work surface.
