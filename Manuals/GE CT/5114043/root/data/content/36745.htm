3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the right Table Side Covers (see Table Side Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Latch the carriage, and remove the Cradle Assembly (see Cradle Assembly Replacement).

  1. Loosen the two captive screws, and remove the Cradle Drive Cover.

    1. Disconnect the ground strap from the Z-Channel.

    1. Unplug the J21 wire connector from the left side.

    1. Unplug the J18 wire connectors from the right side.

      NOTE:An unlatched Cradle/carriage assembly could quickly move toward the gantry and damage the longitudinal encoder assembly.

  1. Move the carriage toward the gantry:

    1. Hold the carriage in place with one hand.

    1. Manually unlatch the carriage with the other hand.

    1. Slowly move the carriage toward the gantry, until it meets the bumper stop.

  1. Remove the Cradle Drive Assembly:

    1. Tilt the front roller of the Cradle Drive downward.

    1. Lift the entire assembly up, then backward, and then down.

    1. Gently remove the assembly from the bottom of the table.

      NOTE:Take care not to disturb the Longitudinal Encoder cable.

  1. After you reassemble the Cradle Drive:

    1. Center the Cradle between the guide rollers.

    1. Torque the six screws to 13 ft-lbs.

      (see Cradle Assembly Replacement)

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Characterize Longitudinal.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
