3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove the left Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ).

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Servo Amp Assembly.

  1. Remove the two screws that fasten the interference switches to the bracket.

    NOTE:Pay attention to the location of the wires on the defective switch, before you remove them. Restore the wires to their original configuration on the replacement switch.

  1. Detach the wires from the terminals of the defective switch.

  1. Remove the defective switch and install the replacement switch.

  1. Fasten the switches to the bracket, but do NOT tighten the screws.

  1. Adjust the switch-to-cam clearance:

    1. Position the switches until the roller lever comes in contact with the switch housing.

    1. Adjust the switch position until a 0.035/0.045 inch gap exists between the roller and the outside cam surface.

    1. Maintain this distance while you tighten the two screws.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

  1. Adjust the actuation point:

    1. Locate the Cal plate, on the right side of the base frame.

    1. Loosen the two Cal plate screws, move the plate to the horizontal position, then tighten the Cal plate screws.

    1. Elevate the table until the distance between the bottom of the Cal plate (at the INT. MTX SWITCH S2 position) and the center mark on the upper rear leg pivot pin equals 26.03 ±0.03 inches.

  1. Adjust the cam:

    1. Loosen the cam clamping screw.

    1. Rotate the cam until switch S2 (closest to center of the table) rides on the outside surface of the cam.

    1. Turn the cam CW (seen from the left side of the table) until S2 actuates.

    1. Tighten the cam clamping screw, and verify the setting.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

4  Finalization

  1. Check Characterization Limits (for tilt and table elevation interference).

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
