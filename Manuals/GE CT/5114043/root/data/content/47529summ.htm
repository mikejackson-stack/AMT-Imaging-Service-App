STC AC Filter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Remove plastic safety cover.

  1. Disconnect 4 cables that connect to power supply

  1. Remove 3 hex screws.

  1. Remove STC Power Supply.

  1. Use socket wrench to remove 4 nuts on top and bottom of AC Filter.

  1. Use socket wrench to remove 2 nuts on top and bottom of AC Filter.

  1. Remove STC AC Filter.

  1. Install new STC AC Filter.
