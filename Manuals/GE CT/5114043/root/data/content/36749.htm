3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the left Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install).

  1. Loosen the captive screws (or remove the four screws) that fasten the servo amp cover in place.

  1. Remove the servo amp cover and set it aside.

  1. Locate the Elevation/Tilt Amplifier, toward the inside of the table.

    1. Detach all of the wire connectors.

    1. Remove the seven screws that fasten the Amp to its mounting bracket.

  1. Remove and replace the defective Elevation/Tilt Amplifier.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
