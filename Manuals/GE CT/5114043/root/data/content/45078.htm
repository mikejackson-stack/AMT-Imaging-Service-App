3  Procedure

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off the Axial Drive and the HVDC switches located on the STC backplane.

NOTE:The c-pulse and scribed line on the Encoder gear are Factory set. Do NOT loosen and screws on the Encoder gear.

  1. Rotate the gantry until the opto-sensor is in the center of the home plate. See Illustration 1 for example of Good and Wrong sensor locations on the Home Plate.

    Illustration 1:  Home Plate Passing Through Opto-Sensor Window

  1. Verify that the DS3 LED (Home Flag) on the axial board lights up. Notice that when the gantry is rotated, LEDs DS7 and DS6 flash (A and B pulses). The DS3 LED should only light up when the home plate is in the opto-sensor window. Once the DS3 LED is on, rotate the gantry ± 1 degree. Verify that the DS2 LED lights up. Notice that the DS2 LED (c-pulse) might not stay on but rather just flash. The key is to stop rotating the gantry as soon as the DS2 LED flashes. If the DS2 LED flashes or lights up, you have coincided with the c-pulse, and are finished if the opto-sensor is still in the center of the Home Plate, otherwise continue with the next step.

  1. If the c-pulse does not flash or light up, verify that the home plate is in the center of the opto-sensor, and lift as well as rotate the encoder gear assembly one tooth at a time in each direction until the c-pulse lights up (see Illustration 2 ). If the c-pulse is not lined up with the center of the home plate, images may appear slightly tilted or rotated.
