3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the Base Covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ), and the left Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. Locate the table harness at the rear of the ETC mounting panel:

    1. Disconnect the J15 Encoder cable from the table harness.

    1. Cut the Ty-Raps, to free the cable.

  1. Remove the two screws that fasten the Elevation Encoder Assembly to the table base.

  1. Slide the Assembly toward the large sprocket, to relieve belt tension.

  1. Remove the belt from the encoder assembly.

  1. Remove the defective Encoder Assembly from the table.

  1. Install the replacement Elevation Encoder Assembly:

    1. Replace the belt

    1. Slide the Elevation Encoder Assembly away from the large sprocket, until the belt deflects 0.250" with 32 - 35 oz. applied at its mid-span.

    1. Tighten both screws, to fasten the encoder assembly in place, with tension on the belt.

  1. Adjust the encoder C-Pulse position (see Elevation Encoder Replacement).

  1. Adjust the Table Interference Matrix Switch (see Interference Matrix Switch Replacement).

  1. Characterize the elevation axis (see Elevation Characterization).

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
