Axial Dynamic Braking Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove right and left side covers. Rear cover removal is optional.

  1. Tilt gantry forward to allow sufficient clearance for assembly cover removal.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane. Remove all system power at the Main Disconnect panel and use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

SEVERE INJURY OR DEATH CAN RESULT.
LETHAL VOLTAGES PRESENT WITHIN THIS ASSEMBLY.
ALWAYS REMOVE POWER BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE ANY COVERS.

  1. Loosen 4 slot binder screws located on the side metal cover.

  1. Carefully remove side metal cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws and chopper load resistor cover as necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace failed components.

  1. Replace covers and reassemble gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform one (1) 0.5 sec scan. Verify gantry stops in less than 10 seconds.

  1. Perform Axial Control Functional (located in Axial Motion Checks).
