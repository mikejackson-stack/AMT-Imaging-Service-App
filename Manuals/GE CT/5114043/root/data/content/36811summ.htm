Leg Tape Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the right Side Panels (see Table Side Panels Removal and Re-install).

  1. Disconnect the Tape Switch from the harness.

  1. Remove the Tape Switch and the adhesive from the rear leg.

  1. Thoroughly clean the mounting surface with alcohol.

  1. Remove the protective strip from the adhesive, and press the switch firmly in place.

  1. Replace the Side Panels.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.

  1. Test the Tape Switch for proper operation.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Emergency Stop check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
