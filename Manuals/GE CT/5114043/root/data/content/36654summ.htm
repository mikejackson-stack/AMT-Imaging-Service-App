AC-DC Power Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn off the Table breaker in the PDU, to remove power from the entire table.

  1. Remove the left base covers (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ).

  1. Locate the Power Assembly:

    1. Remove the screws from the outlet cover.

    1. Move the cover aside, to gain access to the power switches.

    1. Loosen the nut that fastens the defective power switch to the plate.

    1. Remove the switch from the plate

      (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the screw terminals, and remove the wires from the defective switch.

  1. Transfer the wires to the same location on the replacement switch.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

  1. Turn on the Table breaker in the PDU to restore power.
