4  Procedure

4.1  Initial Steps

  1. Remove right side cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

4.2  ARTESYN-3 Board Procedure

  1. Unplug serial and ethernet cables.

  1. Loosen top and bottom fasteners with a Phillips #2 screwdriver.

  1. Pull Artesyn board out using black plastic tabs located on top and bottom of the board.

  1. Install new Artesyn board and reassemble gantry.

4.3  LSCOM & AXIAL Boards Procedure

  1. Turn OFF all the 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Pull two latches to release the board.

  1. Remove the defective board.

  1. Install the new board. Secure the newly installed board using the two latches.

5  Finalization

  1. For Artesyn CPU Board, perform System Scanning Test.

  1. For LSCOM, perform the following:

    1. Manual check for TAXI errors. Open a Unix shell:

      • type: nbsClient stc

      • type: s sr0

      Verify no increase in Link Errors

      • Enter: <CTRL + C> [to exit],

      • type: nbsClient obcr

      • type: s sr0

      Verify no increase in Link Errors.

      Enter: <CTRL + C> [to exit],

    1. Hardware Reset using console gantry reset (Hardwire).

    1. Acquire 10 scouts: (120kV/40mA., 1000mm table movement)

    1. Acquire 100 axials: (120kV/80mA., 0.5 sec. Scan)

    1. Acquire 1 helical: (120kV/40mA., 30 sec. Scan)

    1. Acquire 10 axial scans: (120kV/400mA., 4 sec. Scan)

    1. Verify NO increase in Link errors.

    1. To check LSCOM Statistics: Select Log Viewing, System Browser, Run Time Statistics, RCOM/SCOM Statistics.

  1. For Axial Board, perform the following:

    • Exposure time accuracy (see Exposure Time Accuracy)

    • System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test)
