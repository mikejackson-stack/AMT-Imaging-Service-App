STC Backplane Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the following gantry covers. (Front cover removal not needed for this procedure.)

    (Step Details)

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Completely shutdown power. (A1, Lockout/Tagout)

  1. Disconnect all control harness cabling.

  1. Remove STC.

  1. Unscrew ty-rap that secures cabling with hex key.

  1. Remove circuit boards.

  1. Remove hex screws from base assembly and 3 screws behind the tilt riser.

  1. Carefully pivot STC backwards and slide assembly out.

  1. Remove 3 hex screws that secure chassis to backplane.

  1. Remove chassis.

  1. Install new STC Backplane.

  1. Install in reverse order.

2  Finalization

  1. Check empty chassis power supplies (all slots).

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test)

  1. Safety Component Replacement Verification: Test Axial Enable by exercising alignment lights. They won’t come on if axial functions are inoperable.
