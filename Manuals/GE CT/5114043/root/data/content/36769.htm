2.3  Safety

Prevent permanent damage to the static-sensitive boards. Attach the anti-static wrist strap to your wrist and to a bare metal grounding point on the table before you continue.
 
 

3  Procedure

  1. Remove table base cover (see Table Base Covers Removal and Re-install ).

  1. Power off the table by flipping the 3 switches opposite the ETC assembly.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to loosen the 2 screws that fasten cover over the ETC Board.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to remove the screw on the floor of the table control area that allows the assembly to pivot.

  1. Pivot the assembly.

  1. DIsconnect all connections to the Interface Board.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to remove 4 copper colored screws that secure Interface Board.

  1. Use a hex key to remove 3 screws that fix Interface Board above ETC Board.

  1. Lift off Interface Board.

  1. Install the new board, replace screws and reconnect cabling.

  1. Pivot ETC assembly and secure in operational position.

  1. Restore power to table.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Flash Download to load applications firmware (see Flash Download Tool).

  1. Verify hardware reset, gantry display and motion controls are fully functional.

  1. Install ETC assembly cover.

  1. Reassemble table
