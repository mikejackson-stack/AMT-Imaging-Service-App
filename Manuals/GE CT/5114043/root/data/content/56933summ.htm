Octane PCI Card Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Shutdown system and remove power.

  1. Remove the console’s front cover. Pull out the platform upon which the computer rests. Release its tie-down strap, if present.

Wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed electronic parts on an anti-static surface.
 
 

  1. Remove any cables from the PCI module.

  1. Loosen the two captive screws (see Illustration 1).

  1. Pull out the release lever along the bottom of the module (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide out the module, taking care not to allow the compression connector to touch anything. Cap the compression connector, once the module is resting on an antistatic surface.

  1. (For Octane1 only) To replace a PCI card, refer to Octane PCI Card Replacement.

  1. To re-install, reverse the previous steps.
