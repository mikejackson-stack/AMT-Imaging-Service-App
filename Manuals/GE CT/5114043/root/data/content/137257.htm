LightSpeed 3.X Service Methods (Advanced)

PM Schedule - LS Plus, Ultra, 16; HS QXi

These schedules are for LightSpeed Plus (LS2.X, LS3.X), LightSpeed Ultra (LS3.X), LightSpeed Ultra 16 (LS4.X) and HiSpeed QX/i products only, as approved by CT Engineering. The Planned Maintenance is based on (4) service cycles per year at 500k revolution intervals or 3 months, whichever comes first.

NOTE:Last Revised: June 7, 2007

  • Schedule A - PM cycle begins when Rev Counter shows xxx500k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule B - Next 500k Rev. When Rev Counter shows xxx1000k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule C - When Rev Counter increments another 500k revs or 3 months.

  • Schedule D - When Rev Counter increments another 500k revs or 3 months.

NOTE:PM time without camera (includes time allocation for Gantry cover handling):

  • Schedule A - 1.75 hours

  • Schedule B - 2.1 hour

  • Schedule C - 4.2 hour

  • Schedule D - 2.1 hour

NOTE:* This product can use the GRE OR other Consoles. Use the appropriate PM procedure.

** This product can use the ETC OR the Schleifring style of slip ring. Use the appropriate PM procedure.

*** This PM procedure applies only to systems with filters on the DAS duct fan assembly.
