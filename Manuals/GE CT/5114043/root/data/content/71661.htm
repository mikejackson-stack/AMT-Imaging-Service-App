LightSpeed 3.X Service Methods (Advanced)

SRU Replacements - Global Consoles

The modules listed below provide instructions for replacing components within the Scan Reconstruction Unit, on the Global Operator Consoles (GOC1 and GOC2). To gain access to the SRU's interior, for component replacement, please refer to Access to the SRU's Interior.

  • To replace the RIP and/or DIP boards, also refer to RIP Board Replacement.

  • To replace the Pegasus Image Generator (PEG-IG) board, also refer to PEG-IG Board Replacement.

  • To replace the VME Power Supply, also refer to VME Power Supply Replacement
