SCR Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTE:Before beginning this procedure, please read the safety information in Equipment Service - Gantry.

1  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Remove power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Position HEMRC at 1 o’clock to clear fan obstruction.

  1. Remove HEMRC cover.

  1. Using a DVM measure both sides of all fuses to chassis ground. Verify zero (0) volts.

  1. Note and record position of three (3) wires (Red, Pur and Wht) attached to SCR.

  1. Note and record position of two (2) wires (Blk and Wht) attached to SCR.

  1. Note and record position of SCR terminals.

  1. Remove leads identified in Step 8 and Step 9.

  1. Remove two (2) 4 mm cap screws.

  1. Clean SCR mounting surface on resistor mounting panel, using a dry tissue to remove thermal compound.

  1. Prepare new SCR by coating the mounting surface with thermal compound (46-170212P1).

  1. Mount SCR in position recorded in Step 10. Torque (2) 4 mm cap screws to 1.7 Nm.

  1. Replace leads removed in Step 8 and Step 9.

  1. Install HEMRC cover. Tighten 1/4 turn past seated finger tight.

  1. Apply power at main disconnect (A1) panel. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Turn ON HVDC and 120 VAC on the STC backplane. Verify no smoke or arching.

  1. Perform hardware reset.

  1. Assemble gantry.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
