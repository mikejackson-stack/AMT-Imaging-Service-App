4  Procedure

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    • Side covers.

    • Top covers.

    • Front cover.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 2 o’clock (see Illustration 1) .

    Illustration 1:  OBC Backplane Filament Rela

  1. Carefully remove the Filament relay retaining clip on the OBC backplane.

    Observe how the clip is mounted for later assembly.

  1. Remove Filament Relay.

  1. Install new relay and make sure retaining clip is properly installed on the Filament Relay.

  1. Restore power.

  1. Reassemble gantry.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform Auto mA Calibration located in KV Gain Pots Adjustment procedure.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
