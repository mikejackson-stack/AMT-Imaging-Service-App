4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry left side cover.

  1. Remove 2 hex screws that secure assembly to gantry.

  1. Disconnect power from thermostat.

  1. Remove thermostat.

  1. Install new thermostat.

    NOTE:When installing new thermostat, synchronize settings from old thermostat with new thermostat.
