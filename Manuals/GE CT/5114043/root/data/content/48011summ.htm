OBC Power Interface Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove all gantry covers. (Front cover removal is unnecessary for this procedure)

    (Step Details)

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components.

  1. Completely shutdown power. (A1, Lockout/Tagout)

  1. Position OBC at 3 o'clock.

  1. Use to remove cap screws that secures AC plastic shield.

  1. Remove plastic shield.

  1. Remove fuse from fuse holder.

  1. Install new fuse.

  1. Reassemble assembly in reverse order.

  1. Restore power and verify per adjustment procedure.
