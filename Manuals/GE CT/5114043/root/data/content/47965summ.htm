Cooling Fan (Left-Right) Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right and left covers.

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Unplug the cable connecting the fan to the rest of the assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) 4mm cap screws.

  1. Remove the hex nut that fastens capacitor to the assembly.

  1. Replace fan and capacitor.

  1. Turn ON all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Carefully depress fan cover switch and verify fan operation.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reassemble gantry.
