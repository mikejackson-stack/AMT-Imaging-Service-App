3  Procedure

  1. Locate the Feed-Through connector near the SCA-LAN PWB, on the table ETC PWB.

  1. Rotate the knurled rings to remove, and install the BNC Feed-Through Connector.

  1. Use care to route the cables and BNC connectors out of harms way.
