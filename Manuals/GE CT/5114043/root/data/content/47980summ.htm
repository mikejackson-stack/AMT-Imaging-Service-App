OBC Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Move table to its lowest elevation.

  1. Remove the following gantry covers:

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn OFF all 3 switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120 VAC) on the STC backplane.

  1. Position OBC at 3 o’clock.

  1. Remove the 4 mm cap screw and washer securing the front mounting bracket.

  1. Remove mounting bracket.

  1. Remove the 4 mm cap screws and washers that secure AC plastic shield.

  1. Remove plastic shield.

  1. Disconnect input/output (IO) wiring from power supply strips.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove (2) 6 mm cap screws and washers on rear gold mounting bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove power supply and gold bracket from OBC assembly.

  1. Remove (3) Phillips screws that secure mounting bracket to the power supply.

  1. Remove mounting bracket.

  1. Reassemble assembly in reverse order.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Check empty chassis power supplies (see OBC Power Supplies Checks and Adjustments).

  1. Perform System Scanning Test (see System Scanning Test).
