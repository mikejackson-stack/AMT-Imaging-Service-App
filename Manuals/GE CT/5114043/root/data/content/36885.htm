3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Turn each of two Dzus fasteners one-quarter turn CCW, and remove, to release the cover.

  1. Tip the bottom of the cover outward slightly, then lift the cover upward to clear the four pins in the z-channel.

  1. If you plan to use any of the table drives:

    1. Remove the Tape Switch Jumper Plug from its storage position.

    1. Plug the Tape Switch Jumper into the harness connector.

    NOTE:Use the Jumper Plug to simulate the presence of a Side Cover Tape Switch, when you remove the corresponding cover from the table.

  1. When you install the Side Cover:

    1. Return the jumper plug to its storage position.

    1. The screwdriver slot in the fastener aligns with the Side Cover when you successfully engage the fastener.

  1. Test the Side Cover Tape Switch operation.

  1. Reassemble the Table, and replace the covers.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform Emergency Stop check.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
