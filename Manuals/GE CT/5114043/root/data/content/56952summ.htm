Octane PCI Card Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Lie the PCI module on its right side and loosen the two screws that hold the left side access cover. Then lift and remove this cover.

PCI cards are extremely sensitive to static electricity.
 
 

  1. The PCI card resides in a slot. Unscrew the board from the front panel. You can also expand the I/O door. See Illustration 1. The I/O door expands open if necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. To re-install, reverse the previous steps.
