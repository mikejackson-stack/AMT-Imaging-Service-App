2  Preliminary Requirements

2.1  Safety

Potential for personal injury.
Components may be hot.
Wait five minutes after the power is off before you continue. Let it cool.

Wear a grounded ESD wrist strap. Place removed electronic parts on or in an antistatic surface.
 
 

3  Procedure

  1. Shutdown the system and remove power.

  1. Remove the console's front cover. Pull out the platform upon which the computer rests. Release its tie-down strap (if present).

  1. Remove the locking bar (if applicable).

  1. Loosen the captive screw in the sliding handles on the top and bottom.

  1. Pull both handles at the same time until they are fully extended.

    Illustration 1:  Removing the System Module

  1. Grasp the module handle with your left hand and place your right hand against the top of the computer's back. Pull the module out without allowing the delicate connectors on its back edge to touch anything.

  1. Place the system module on an antistatic surface with the component side up.

  1. Place a cap on each compression connector.

  1. To replace the system module, perform the above steps in reverse order.

  1. You may need to set/reset boot environment variables. See Replacement & Verification of H3 Console Hardware and “Boot Environment Variables,” in Boot PROM - Boot-up - Devices (IRIX OS), for details.
