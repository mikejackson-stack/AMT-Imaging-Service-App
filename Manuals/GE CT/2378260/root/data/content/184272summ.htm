1.1   SCIM, Keyboard, Trackball and Mouse Installation

  1. Route the keyboard cable under the SCIM, as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

Potential for equipment damage.

Never connect a mouse or keyboard with the host computer powered “ON”. Doing so can destroy components within the host computer.

 
 

  1. The keyboard and mouse connect to the ports on the top of the console rear bulkhead. The keyboard connects to the port on the right, the mouse connects to the left port ( Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the SCIM cable to the SCIM as shown in Illustration 3 (note the cable routing).

    (Step Details)

  1. Select and install the proper overlay for your system: (1) with Tilt or (2) without tilt.

    (Step Details)

  1. The keyboard should attach to the SCIM using the supplied Velcro strip and fit snugly against the SCIM when finished, as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Connecting the SCSI Tower

  1. Connect the SCSI cable to the rear of the SCSI tower.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the SCSI terminator to the rear of the SCSI tower. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the power cable to the rear of the SCSI tower.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Connecting the Monitor

  1. With the monitor and computer switched off, connect the video signal cable to the monitor’s video input (see Figure 7).

Equipment Damage Possible.

Do not touch the video signal cable connector pins as this might bend them. When connecting the video signal cable, check the alignment of the HD15 connector. Do not force the connector in the wrong way, or the pins might bend.

 
 

  1. Connect the power cord to the monitor, then connect it to console power panel outlet J1 or J7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect console component power cords as listed in Table 1. (“J numbers” increment from top to bottom and left to right.

1.5   Installing a DASM (Hardware Option)

If you have a digital DASM to install, complete the following steps to connect it to the computer.

  1. Turn the DASM over so you are looking at the bottom.

  1. Set the DASM SCSI ID to 1.

  1. Set the DASM termination to ON.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the RS232/RS422 switch to the RS232 position for a digital DASM.

  1. Refer to Illustration 10. Attach the SCSI cable supplied with DASM kit to the back of the DASM.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the free end of the SCSI cable to the rear of the Host Computer (see Illustration 10).

  1. Plug DASM power cord into J10 (or any open slot).

  1. Attach remaining cable(s) from camera to DASM and install the DASM to the right of the slide-out tray.

1.6   Modem Option

1.6.1  Modem Installation

If you have a modem to install, do it now.

  1. Place the modem on top of the outlet box and attach it with velcro to the power supply.

  1. Hook up the power, phone, and serial line, as shown in drawing 2378862. The drawing is located in the back cover of the console (For the pdf version of the schematics, click on the pdf icon in GRE Operator Console Interconnect, Step 2).

    (Step Details)

1.6.2  Modem Connection Requirements

  1. Power Cable and Supply: Connect to any open AC jack in the console.

  1. Phone Line: Connect to the Hospital Phone line.

  1. Serial Cable: Connect to the HP rear Serial Port.

    (Step Details)
