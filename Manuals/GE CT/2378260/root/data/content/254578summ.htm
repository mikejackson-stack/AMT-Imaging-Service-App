Upgrading ETC and Artesyn Boards in Table

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

1  Procedure

NOTE:It may be necessary to tilt the gantry in order to remove the table base covers.

  1. Remove table base cover.

POTENTIAL FOR SHOCK
VOLTAGE MAY BE PRESENT.
Use proper lockout/tagout procedures before replacing components. See safety menu of Service Document gui for appropriate procedures.

  1. Power down the console. Use proper Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to loosen the two (2) screws that fasten the cover over the ETC Board.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to remove the screw on the floor of the table control area that allows the assembly to pivot.

  1. Pivot the assembly.

Prevent permanent damage to the static-sensitive boards. Attach the anti-static wrist strap to your wrist and to a bare metal grounding point on the table before you continue.
 
 

  1. Disconnect all connections to the Interface Board.

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to remove 4 copper colored screws that secure Interface Board.

  1. Use a hex key to remove the three (3) screws that fix Interface Board above ETC Board.

  1. Lift off Interface Board.

  1. Disconnect all cables to ETC and Artesyn.

  1. Use a hex key to remove the eight (8) screws that hold the ETC Board. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a hex key to remove the one (1) screw that holds the Artesyn Board.

  1. Remove the existing ETC and Artesyn Boards as one, place on a static mat on cradle and then separate the two boards.

  1. Locate the new ETC and Artesyn Boards.

  1. Remove face plate and discard.

  1. Set/verify ETC Board settings:

    (Step Details)

  1. Set/verify Artesyn Board settings (DIP switches and jumpers) per Illustration 2, Table 1, and Table 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reassemble the new ETC and Artesyn Boards as one.

  1. Return all Table power switches to their "ON" position and check that no wires are damaged or pinched.

  1. Install all removed table covers.
