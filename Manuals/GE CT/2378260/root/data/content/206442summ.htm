1.1   PDU Quick Inspection

  1. Follow appropriate CT Safety precautions when working in the PDU.

  1. Open the top of the PDU and locate the 120 VAC switch on the rear of the PDU.

  1. Check the wirers on switch 1-1 (see Illustration 1.

1.2   PDU Repair Procedure

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
Use Proper Lock-Out/Tag-out procedures before beginning work in the PDU.

  1. Before you start, Shut down Console & system and LOTO at A1.

  1. Remove the PDU top and front covers. Locate connector J2 on the bottom panel of the PDU (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the extra wire shown at left in Illustration 2.

  1. Cut orange wire J2 –33 ½ “ above the connector. Strip the end of the wire with the label.

  1. Remove the push on connector from SW1 –1 ( there should be two (2) orange and one (1) purple wire.

  1. Connect your DVM to the orange wire on J2 -33, set to Ohm and locate the correct orange wire going to switch SW1. When you find the correct orange wire cut the wire on the switch end and tape up both using electrical tape.

  1. Locate connector J7 on the PDU control board (see Illustration 3)

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut purple wire J7 –47 (A6 P7- 47) ½ “ above the connector. Strip the end of the wire with the label.

  1. The push on connector from SW1 –1 should be removed, locate the (1) purple wire and cut this wire.

  1. Connect your DVM to the purple wire on J7 –47 (A6 P7-47), set meter to Ohm and locate the purple wire going to switch SW1. When you find the correct purple wire cut the wire on the switch end and tape up both using electrical tape.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the one remaining wire to SW1 pin 1. Recheck all changes install PDU front cover and close PDU top.

  1. Remove LOTO and power on system.

  1. Remove the gantry right side cover and check that the Gantry 120 VAC switch is functional. When turned off, the gantry fans and 120 VAC indicator lights should be off. If not additional troubleshooting is requires. Refer to the gantry base power schematics.

  1. Turn on all service switches. Replace all removed gantry covers

  1. Complete a test scan to verify scanner operation.
