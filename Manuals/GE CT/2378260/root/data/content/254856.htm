4  Procedure

4.1  Determine Required Parts

There are three configurations of the H1 (1.X) gantry. Refer to the following items to determine which parts are required for this upgrade, based on the gantry configuration.

  1. (For Systems with an O2 computer.) Locate these three (3) cables in the Connectivity Kit, 2402589:

    1. Console “Y” cable, 5112263

    1. Console adapter cable, 2295231

    1. Gantry adapter cable, 2295232

  1. (For Systems without an O2 computer.) Locate these two (2) cables in the Connectivity Kit, 2402589:

    1. Console “Y” cable, 5112263

    1. Gantry adapter cable, 2295232

  1. There will be extra cables and adapters in the box, after completing this upgrade. Only use these additional cables if required for your system upgrade. Return all unused components to the Recycle Center, for proper disposal.

  1. The gantry “Y” cable has a “long” end, with three (3) cables that are not used for this upgrade. Coil these unused cables in the bottom of the gantry.

  1. There are two (2) different gantry “Y” cables and three (3) console “Y” cables/adaptors included in this connectivity kit. Cables required for configurations 1 and 2 are detailed in this procedure.

  1. The following illustrations ( Illustration 1, Illustration 2, Illustration 3 and Illustration 4) show the cable connections as they should appear when the upgrade is complete.

    Illustration 1:  Gantry “Y” Cable, 2295232

    Illustration 2:  Console Adapter Cable, 2295231

    Illustration 3:  Console “Y” Cable, 5112263

    Illustration 4:  Correctly Wired S-DAS Console

  1. Depending on system configuration, the gantry A1A5 panel should look similar to that shown in Illustration 5 and Illustration 6.

    Illustration 5:  Gantry Connections

    Illustration 6:  A1A5 Panel Connections

  1. Depending on system configuration, the console back panel should look similar to that shown in Illustration 7 or Illustration 8.

    Illustration 7:  Console Back Panel (with O2 Computer)

    Illustration 8:  Console Back Panel (without O2 Computer)

  1. Remove gantry base cover, and compare the gantry/console configuration to those shown in the previous illustrations. Locate the GRE Connectivity Kit. Locate and use the appropriate adapter cables for your system upgrade.

4.2  Procedure Details

Choose and follow the procedure appropriate to your particular system configuration, as listed below. Then, complete the installation, using the common procedure detailed in Additional Connections and Checks.

  • H1 1.X Gantry Configuration One (32 Pin Cable) for systems with or without O2 and with a 32 pin console J20 cable, 2139577.

  • H1 1.X Gantry Configuration Two (25 Pin Cable) for systems with or without O2 and with a 25 pin console J20 cable, 5112263.

4.2.1  H1 1.X Gantry Configuration One (32 Pin Cable)

Follow this procedure ONLY if your configuration uses the 32 pin console J20 cable, p/n 2139577.

  1. Refer to Illustration 9.

    Illustration 9:  Configuration One

  1. Locate the following three (3) cables in the Connectivity Kit:

    • 5112263 - Console “Y”

    • 2295231 - Console adapter

    • 2295232 - Gantry adapter

  1. Connect console adapter, 2295231, to the existing J20 cable. See Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the console “Y” adapter, 5112263, to the cables J20 and J18 - 2191452 (existing console cables). Then connect the console “Y” adapter J20 end to console J20 connector. See Illustration 3

  1. At the gantry base, connect the gantry adapter cable, 2295232, to the J1 cable, 2139577, from the console. Then connect the short end of the adapter cable to the gantry A5J1 connector. Coil the long end with three cables in the gantry base. Connect the thin gray cable from the console J18 to gantry A5J4. See Illustration 1.

  1. Check all cable connections and install all gantry base covers.

4.2.2  H1 1.X Gantry Configuration Two (25 Pin Cable)

Follow this procedure ONLY if your configuration uses the 25 pin console J20 cable, p/n 5112263.

  1. Refer to Illustration 10.

    Illustration 10:  Configuration Two

  1. Locate the following two (2) cables in the Connectivity Kit:

    • 5112263 - Console “Y”

    • 2295232 - Gantry Adapter

  1. Connect the console “Y” adapter to cables J20 - 5112263 and J18 - 2191452 (existing console cables). Then connect the console “Y” adapter J20 end to console J20 connector. See Illustration 3.

  1. At the gantry base, connect the gantry adapter cable, 2295232, to the J1 - 5112263 cable from the console. Then connect the short end of the adapter cable to the gantry A5J1 connector. Coil the long end with three cables in the gantry base. Connect the thin gray cable from the console J18 to gantry A5J4. See Illustration 1.

  1. Check all cable connections and install all gantry base covers.
