5167277-800 - FX/Xtream SW & Upgrade Procedures

Rear Cover Removal Procedure

Table 1:  

Last Revised:19 May 2005

The rear console cover is replaced with on that is approximately 5” deeper to accommodate the “PathFinder” fans that provide improved cooling to the console.

  1. Loosen two captive screws at the top of the rear cover. Refer to Illustration 1.

  2. Tilt cover backward so that top moves away from console.

  3. Lift cover away so that tabs disengage from bottom lip of opening.

  4. Discard the cover; it will be replaced with the rear cover in your “PathFinder” upgrade kit toward the end of the “PathFinder” software and hardware installation.
