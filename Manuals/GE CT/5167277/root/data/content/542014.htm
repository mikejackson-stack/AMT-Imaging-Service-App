5167277-800 - FX/Xtream SW & Upgrade Procedures

Axial Motor Control Rework (HP60 Only)

Last Revised:10 February 2006
This procedure should be performed ONLY for sites that meet the following:

  • Systems with software version 04MW44.9 or 05MW14.5, or systems upgrading to 06MWxx.x SW that have not already performed this AMC rework.

  • HP60 systems

For sites that meet these conditions, this procedure may be performed during the INITIAL LFC process.

 
 

1  Modifications of Wire Connections

  1. Remove Gantry right side cover.

  2. Turn OFF the gantry service switches (Axial Drive and 120VAC) and perform Lockout/Tagout procedures.

  3. Remove the support bar from the front cover.

  4. Remove the side mesh cover as follows:

    1. Remove two (2) screws holding the front side of the side mesh cover to the tilting frame.

    2. Turn ON the 120VAC gantry service switch, and tilt the gantry to FWD 30 degrees position, then turn OFF the 120VAC service switch.

    3. Remove two (2) screws holding the rear side of the side mesh cover to the tilting frame, then remove the side mesh cover.

  5. Remove the top cover from the motor.

  6. Change the wire connections from ‘Low Voltage‘ (current connection) to ‘High Voltage‘ (new connection).

    Refer to the following illustrations:

  7. Reinstall the top cover of the motor, the side mesh cover, and the support bar.
