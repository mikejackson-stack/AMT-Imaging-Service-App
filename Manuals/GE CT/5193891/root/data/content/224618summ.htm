3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off all three (3) switches (Axial Drive, HVDC, 120VAC) on the Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the 12 screws in the TGP/MSUB cover.

  1. Remove the cover.

  1. Disconnect all cabling into the TGP.

  1. Remove the eight (8) screws in the TGP.

  1. Remove the defective TGP.

  1. Install the new TGP in the reverse order.
