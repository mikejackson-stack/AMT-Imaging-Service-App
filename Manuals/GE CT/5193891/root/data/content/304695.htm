3  Procedure

  1. Raise the table to maximum height.

  1. Remove Table Base Covers and Table Side Rail Covers.

  1. Remove the two screws from the panel being removed.

    NOTE:Pay attention to the orientation of the ground strap terminal, before you remove it. Orient the terminal in the same direction when you replace it.

  1. Remove the Ground Strap from the back side of the panel.

  1. To reassemble the table, replace the Base and Side Rail Covers.

No finalization required.
