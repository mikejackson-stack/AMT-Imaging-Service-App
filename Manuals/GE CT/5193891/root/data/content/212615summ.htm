Tube Replacement - Performix Pro (MCS_7079)

1  Safety

POTENTIAL FOR ELECTRIC SHOCK.
LETHAL VOLTAGES MAY BE PRESENT IN THE GANTRY.
Follow proper Electrical Lock-out/Tag-out procedures.

POTENTIAL FOR PERSONAL INJURY (PINCH, CRUSH, ENTANGLEMENT OR IMPACT INJURIES ARE POSSIBLE).
GANTRY HAS ROTATING ASSEMBLY.
Use the Gantry’s Rotational Lock, to prevent uncommanded motion of the Gantry’s rotating assembly.

The heat exchanger may take on excessive amounts of coolant due to thermal expansion if the tube is not allowed to cool prior to tube change. If this occurs, the system will not scan and the heat exchanger will require replacement. Therefore, you should wait at least 1 hour after the last scan before disconnecting the tube from the heat exchanger.
 
 

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.

3  Procedure

3.1   Remove Old Tube

  1. Before performing this procedure, familiarize yourself with the Performix Pro Tube Crate information and Securing HV Cable (Pro 16) procedure.

  1. Remove and set aside gantry side, top, and front covers.

  1. Remove the M12 bolts holding mounting bracket for the right front gantry cover and set the assembly aside. It may be necessary to tilt the gantry back to remove the third bolt (not normally installed).

    (Step Details)

If the gantry must be tilted, remember to tilt the gantry back to zero degrees.
 
 

  1. Turn off the AXIAL DRIVE ENABLE, HVDC ENABLE, and 120VAC switches on the service switch panel.

120 / 208 VAC Gantry power must not be on with any of the coolant / heat exchanger hoses disconnected. With Gantry power on and the cooling loop incomplete, the pump will run uncontrollably and become damaged.
 
 

  1. Turn off facility power to PDU and lockout/tagout according to lockout/tagout procedure.

  1. Position gantry so that the tube is positioned between 11 and 12 o'clock, and the HV Tank is at the 3 o'clock position.

NOTE:The HV cable's candlestick has a rubber sealing ring that must not be lost.

  1. Free the HV cable.

    1. Carefully cut all tie-wraps securing HV cable. Note HV cable routing.

    1. Loosen the cable's locking ring.

    1. Pull cable terminal stick out of its receptacle on HV tank. Place sheath/cover from new tube crate over HV Cable's candlestick.

    1. Place a cap or enough paper towel in the newly created hole on the HV tank receptacle to prevent oil from leaking when you rotate the gantry.

  1. Disconnect anode stator cable from X-Ray tube.

    1. Remove 3 pin connector from aux box. Twist connector and cut tie-wraps holding cable to the HV cable.

    1. Disconnect the clamp holding the cable to aux box. Keep the clamp, as you will need it for the new tube.

  1. Rotate the Gantry until the X-Ray tube reaches the 3 o'clock position.

CRUSH POINT.
IF THE GANTRY IS NOT SECURELY LOCKED, UPON REMOVAL OF THE X-RAY TUBE, THE GANTRY WILL VIOLENTLY ROTATE DUE TO WEIGHT IMBALANCE.
Ensure the gantry is securely locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

  1. Engage rotational lock.

  1. Measure, along top of the tube housing, the distance from the edge of the ISO adjustment block to the rear edge of the tube housing. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the measurement. This will help put the new tube in roughly the same place, making alignments easier.

  1. Remove Power I/F board cover.

  1. Disconnect thermal sensor from Power I/F board at J7. Cut any tie-wraps holding the wires to the gantry.

  1. Remove dial mounting bracket. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Quickly disconnect the pump from the tube at the quick disconnect (QD). Align the pin with the slot on the QD and disconnect.

The coolant in the heat exchanger is a Propylene Glycol and Water mixture. This mixture is conductive. For proper handling in the event of a spill, consult the MSDS.
 
 

  1. Quickly disconnect X-Ray tube from heat exchanger at quick disconnect.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place rubber caps over the tube's coolant connectors. The caps are contained in the new tube's crate.

  1. Insert the lifting post, boom, and chain hoist. Refer to Illustration 1 for location.

  1. Disconnect Smart ID cable from Smart ID module (on tube).

  1. Connect hoist to tube lifting point. If using a sling, connect sling to lifting hooks.

    1. Ensure that the sling/hoist joint is centered directly above the tube.

    1. Remove slack in hoist/chain/sling but do not over tighten. The tube should not move when the mounting bolts are removed.

  1. Remove the two bottom M12 mounting bolts, and then remove the upper bolts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Discard the mounting bolts and washers. The new tube comes with new mounting bolts.

Be careful not to damage the fragile copper filter on the tube’s window.
 
 

  1. Place the tube in the crate for shipping. For more information, see Performix Pro Tube Crate.

  1. Remove the adjuster screw for use with the new tube.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Install New Tube

Do not turn 120 / 208 VAC Gantry power on with any of the coolant/ heat exchanger hoses disconnected. With Gantry power on and the cooling loop incomplete, the pump will run uncontrollably and become damaged.
 
 

  1. Identify the proper bolt kit for your system, from the replacement tube crate.

  1. Allow the tube unit to warm to room temperature before you install it.

Crush Point.
The tube lies on its side in the crate and will flip 90 degrees when it is raised.
Ensure that you place the crate so that neither you nor the gantry is hit by the tube as it is raised out of the crate.

Use care when hoisting the tube to avoid equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Place new tube near gantry so that you can attach the hoist to the hook(s) and use the hoist to lift the tube into place. For more information, see Performix Pro Tube Crate.

  1. With tube suspended, visually inspect the copper filter. The primary copper filter is easily examined, since it is removed with the tube. The copper filter should be clean, as well as dent and scratch free:

    (Step Details)

  1. Make a mark on the tube housing where the edge of the ISO adjustment block should be, according to your measurement in the removal process. See Illustration 2.

  1. Attach adjuster screw from old tube to new tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully swing the tube into place.

POTENTIAL FOR PATIENT INJURY.
X-RAY TUBE IS ON ROTATING ASSEMBLY.
Select proper bolt kit from tube crate, based on system type.

When attaching the tube, be careful with the Copper Filter. It should be free of debris, scratches and dust. The reason is particles create artifacts in the image by affecting the attenuation properties of the copper filter.
