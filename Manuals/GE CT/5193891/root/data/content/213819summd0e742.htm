If you cannot follow the quick tube change procedure for any reason, perform the retest steps below:

  1. It may be necessary to compensate the Heat Exchanger. This ensures that the amount of oil in the Heat Exchanger is correct. See Heat Exchanger Compensation(Oil-based heat exchangers only).

  2. Reset Tube TnT Data (in JEDI Generator Tool (Pro16))

  3. Plane of Rotation (POR)

  4. Beam on Window (BOW) Alignment

  5. CBF/SAG Alignment Process

  6. ISO Alignment Procedure

  7. Meter Verification (Pro 16)

  8. HV Tank Feedback Resistor Verification (Pro 16) (bleeder test)

  9. Filament Calibration

  10. HHS Scans

    Tube Usage Information is found on the Common Service Desktop following path:

    Error Logs>Tube Usage>Details.

  11. Gantry Balance Procedure (Pro16)

  12. Hot ISO Alignment

  13. New Tube Config

  14. Collimator Calibration

  15. Z-Slope

  16. DAS Gain Calibration

  17. Detailed Cals

  18. N Number Check

  19. Image Series

  20. Gantry Rotation Safety Check

  21. Save System State
