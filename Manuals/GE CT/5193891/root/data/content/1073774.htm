Equipment Service - Non-standard Parts

Last Revised17 October 2008
Receiving non-standard parts in the field
 
 

If you receive a non-standard part, for which there is no approved, documented process for correction, return the part as Failed on Arrival (FOA) and order a replacement part immediately. Any unapproved field modification of a part creates a product that is deemed adulterated per the FDA and is not acceptable for use.
