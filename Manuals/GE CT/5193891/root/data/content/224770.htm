LightSpeed 5.X Pro16 Limited Access Service Information (Adv)

GDAS-16 EMI Shielding

The GDAS-16 subsystem requires extra EMI shielding on the Jedi HV tank and a shield plate between the inverter tank and the GDAS-16 right side chassis/DAS Plate. This shielding can be seen in Illustration 1.

Illustration 1:  GDAS EMI shielding

The EMI shielding on the HV Tank comes with the Tank FRU. The shield plate between the tank and the DAS plate/right side chassis must be removed to remove/install the right side GDAS-16 chassis or the HV tank.
