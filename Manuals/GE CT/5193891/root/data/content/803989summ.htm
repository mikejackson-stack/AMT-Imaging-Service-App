MSUB Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

Shock Hazard
Voltage Present
No service on left side while energized.

Follow ALL required safety and PPE procedures customary for your organization, when working on this product.
 
 

This procedure is not limited-access compatible.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Only trained service personnel should service the GE CT Scanner.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    (Step Details)

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn off the three (3) main power switches (Axial Drive, HVDC and 120VAC) on the gantry’s Service Switch Panel.

  1. Remove the 12 screws in the TGP/MSUB cover.

  1. Remove the cover. Install the static wristband and attach the other end to gantry ground.

  1. Remove the cabling from the MSUB board.

  1. Remove the two (2) spacers that hold the MSUB to the mounting bracket.

  1. Remove the MSUB board.

  1. Reverse the previous steps to install the new MSUB board.

4  Finalization

  1. Flash download may be required. Complete procedure if prompted.

  1. Perform System Scanning Test.
