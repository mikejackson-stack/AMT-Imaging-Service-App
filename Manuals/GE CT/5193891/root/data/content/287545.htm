3  Procedure

3.1  Acquiring the 35cm QA Phantom Image Series

  1. Mount the Phantom Holder on the head-end of the table.

  1. Mount the 35cm QA Phantom on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Align, level, & center the 35cm QA Phantom.

    • Align black line on phantom using the internal laser lights.

    • Level phantom using bubble level and the Z Axis knob on the Phantom Holder.

    • Center phantom using the [CENTER PHANTOM] procedure in the left head [SCANNER UTILITIES] selection and the X and Y Axis knobs on the Phantom Holder.

  1. Set up the system to scan three 35cm QA Phantom image series with a Recon of the 1st series.

    AUTO SCAN PROTOCOL SETUP

    a. On the Exam Rx desktop, select [NEW PATIENT] .
    b. Type the following entries in the listed Patient Information following fields:
    • Patient ID:Service
    • Name:35cm QA Phantom Image Series
    c. From the Protocol Selection display, click on the Service Tab under Anatomical Selector.
    d. On the Service Protocol Selection window, click on [MANUFACTURING] .
    e. On the Service Category Protocol List, click on the [45.9 IMGSER 35CMQA MDAS_16_16] selection.
    f. On the ExamRx protocol parameter display, select the 1st Series (Series Description: QA1 MTF 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).
    g. Set internal Landmark.

  1. Acquire the 1st 35cm QA Phantom image series and one Recon series (MTF and Contrast, Visible Lines).

  1. Acquire the 3rd 35cm QA Phantom image series (Series Description: QA2 Ho 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

  1. Acquire the 4th 35cm QA Phantom image series (Series Description: QA3 Sm 2x10/120kV/260mA/1s).

3.3  Failure Recommended Actions

3.3.1  1st Image Series (4 Image MTF Average) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications

    The 4 Image MTF Average must pass specifications:

    4 Image MTF Average: 0.65 to 1.00

  1. Recommended Recovery

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

3.3.2  2nd Image Series (Visible Lines) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    The Largest 5 line pair patterns (coded F, E, D, C, and B) must be visible for each of the four images in this series.

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    NOTE:The most common failure for this test is that the phantom has air bubbles that are obscuring the line pair patterns.

    1. Carefully inspect the 35cm QA Phantom for air bubbles. If required, refill the phantom with water, to eliminate all air bubbles.

    1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

3.3.3  3rd Image Series (Visible Holes) Failure Recovery

  1. Specifications:

    The number of visible holes in this image series must pass specifications as follows:

    • Contrast Factor = 2.0 to 3.99:

      Minimum of 2 holes must be visible (10mm & 7.5mm)

    • Contrast Factor = 4.0 to 7.99:

      Minimum of 3 holes must be visible (10mm, 7.5mm & 5mm)

    • Contrast Factor = 8.0 to 12.0:

      Minimum of 4 holes must be visible (10mm, 7.5mm, 5mm, & 3mm)

  1. Recommended Recovery:

    1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

    1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

3.3.4  4th Image Series Failure Recovery

  1. Series Means:

    1. Specifications:

      Both Rows (2A1A, 1B2B) of the series must pass QA#3 Series Means specifications:

      • AvXc: < +/- 3.0

      • AvXo - AvXc: < +/- 3.0

    1. Recommended Recovery:

      1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

  1. QA#3 (AvSDc):

    1. Specifications:

      Both Rows (2A1A, 1B2B) of the series must pass QA#3 AvSDc specifications:

      • AvSDc (< 5K Scans): 2.90 to 3.50

      • AvSDc (> 5K Scans): 2.80 to 3.60

    1. Recommended Recovery:

      1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

  1. Center Artifact or Center Smudge:

    1. Specifications:

      All eight images of the series must pass 35cm QA#3 Center Artifact and Center Smudge specifications:

      • Center Artifact Factor: +/- 3.5

      • Center Smudge Factor: +/- 2.2

    1. Recommended Recovery:

      1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Auto CT# Adjust for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.

  1. Ring, Band, or Streak Artifact Failure Recovery:

    1. Specifications:

      • Ring Artifact: Ring Factor < +/- 4.8

      • Band Artifact: Band Factor +/- 2.8 from 0cm to 8.5cm

                                                                < +/- 2.8 > 8.5cm

      • Streak Artifact: Streak Factor < +/- 4.0

    1. Recommended Recovery:

      1. Troubleshoot Ring, Band or Streak artifact failures as described in the Service Manual.

      1. Repeat Detailed Cal for the 35cm QA Phantom.

      1. Repeat Sections 1 & 2, to verify Image Performance.
