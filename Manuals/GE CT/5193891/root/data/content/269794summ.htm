Inspect Bowtie Filter

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the Collimator Control Board Sheet Metal Cover. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

Do Not Attempt to Move the Filter with Power ON.
 
 

Do not force the filter if it feels stuck. Damage to the limit switch can result.
 
 

Do not move the filter more than necessary for inspection. The filter can fall off the drive screw.
 
 

  1. With power removed from the gantry, turn the position adjuster to position the bowtie filter assembly so it is visible through the input port or tube side of the collimator assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a flashlight, inspect the bowtie filter for contamination. Reference Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)
