Direction  5193891-800,  Rev#  5

Heat Exchanger Purging (RT, Pro 16 80kW)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   GANTRY PREP

  1. Remove side and top gantry covers.

  1. Turn off all 3 service switched on the Service Switch Board (120 VAC, Axial Drive Enable, HVDC Enable).

  1. Remove front Gantry Cover. Cable disconnection is optional based on amount of space desired for procedure.

  1. Position Gantry such that quick disconnect on blue hose (between tube and Heat Exchanger) is easily accessible.

  1. Loosen Jam Nuts at bottom of quick disconnect bolts and remove the bolts. Do NOT disconnect the hose at this point.

  1. Rotate the gantry such that the tube is in between the 5 and 6 o’clock positions and place absorbent pads in base of cover under the hose.

    (Step Details)

Pinch Point
The following work to be performed could create inadvertent gantry rotation causing injury
Engage gantry rotational lock to prevent gantry rotation.

  1. Engage gantry rotational lock. Ensure that the gantry is locked by attempting to rotate the gantry by hand.

1.2   PURGE BOTTLE PREP

  1. Unpack the purge kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the quick disconnects on the purge kit together.

  1. Make sure the cap is tight.

  1. Turn the bottle upside down until all air in the hoses is removed. Verify that all air is removed from hoses by noting lack of bubbles flowing into the bottle.

  1. With the bottle still inverted, disconnect the hoses from each other.

  1. Return the bottle to the upright position.

1.3   ATTACH PURGE KIT TO GANTRY

  1. Disconnect the hose between the heat exchanger and the tube.

  1. Connect the purge kit between the tube and the heat exchanger and set it on absorbent pads on the floor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the cap on the bottle 1 complete turn to allow for venting during the purge process

1.4   PURGE PROCEDURE

  1. Turn on the Heat Exchanger pump by turning on the Gantry 120 VAC switch on the service switch board.

  1. Watch for the following:

    1. LEAKS – if any leaks are detected, turn off the 120VAC switch and repair the leak before continuing.

    1. FLUID COLOR CHANGE – If there is any air in the system, the fluid in the purge kit will become a milky color. If there is no air in the system, the fluid will remain a clear lime green color.

    1. FLUID LEVEL – During purging the fluid level in the bottle may rise and fall approximately 5cm. If the fluid level rises to the top of the bottle, remove enough fluid from the bottle to ensure that the bottle does not overfill.

  1. Once the fluid is a clear color (not milky colored) AND the fluid level in the bottle has stabilized, turn off the gantry 120VAC to remove power from the heat exchanger pump

NOTE:This will usually happen either immediately (indicating no air was present in system before purging), or after some period of time (fluid turns milky color then becomes clear again after approximately 10-15 minutes)

  1. Tighten the cap on the purge bottle

  1. Elevate the purge bottle so that it is higher than the hose connections.

  1. At this height above the hose connections, hold the purge bottle upside-down so that the fluid can drain into the heat exchanger’s accumulator.

  1. Hold purge bottle in this inverted position for approximately 15-45 seconds.

  1. Return purge bottle to upright position on the floor.

  1. Disconnect the purge kit from the heat exchanger and tube.

  1. Reconnect the tube to the heat exchanger

  1. Disengage gantry rotational lock.

  1. Rotate the gantry such that the quick disconnects for the hose are easily accessible.

  1. Tighten the safety bolts on quick disconnect. The torque specification for the safety bolts is 3N-m (2.2 lb-ft, 26 lb-in, 30 kg-cm)

  1. Snug the jam nuts.

  1. Safely restore the system for customer use.

2  Finalization

  1. Verify functionality of the Scanner before returning it to the customer.
