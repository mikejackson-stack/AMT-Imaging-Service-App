2  Overview

The GDAS power supplies are contained in two chassis, each with an internal fan tray that has two fans each. The Fan is the FRU. The Fan replacement method is the same for both power supplies.

4  Procedure

  1. Remove gantry covers as required.

    Refer to Parts Replacement → Gantry → Enclosure → (Cover Removal Procedure).

ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
use proper lockout / tagout procedures before replacing the components.

Always turn OFF the HVDC before the 120 VAC. Turning OFF 120 VAC power before HVDC power can result in equipment damage.
 
 

  1. Turn OFF the Axial Enable and HVDC switches on the service switch panel.

  1. Rotate the gantry to access the DAS power supply on the left side (same side as TGP) then turn off the 120 VAC service switch.

CRUSH HAZARD.
UNEXPECTED GANTRY ROTATION CAN CAUSE SERIOUS INJURY.
Never service the gantry with the axial drive enabled.

  1. Lock the gantry rotation.

  1. Disconnect the cables to the supply to allow the top cover to be removed.

  1. Loosen the captive screws of the cover and remove the top cover of the power supply.

  1. Loosen the 3 captive screws holding the fan tray assembly (1 on top and 2 on the side). These are all combination heads of Torx and medium straight screwdriver. The Fan tray bottom screw on the side of the Digital power supply can be hard to get to. A 4mm hex wrench will fit this T30 Torx head to allow easier removal and tightening. Disconnect the Fan tray power plug and pull out the fan tray.

    Illustration 1:  GDAS Power Supply connectors

  1. Remove the failed fan and install the new fan.

The connector thumb screws of the GDAS power supply and backplane connector harnesses WILL BREAK very easily if using a large diameter handle screwdriver. A gentle hand torque using a small diameter handle screwdriver is recommended.
 
 

  1. Replace the tray into the power supply. Reconnect the molex connector to the fan tray and tighten the 3 screws to hold the fan tray in place.

  1. Reverse the previous steps to put the power supply cover back in place and reconnect all cabling.
