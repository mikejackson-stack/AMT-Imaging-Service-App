SCIM Diagnostics

There are two software diagnostic tests that can be run to help isolate SCIM and keyboard failures.

Illustration 1:  SCIM (with Keyboard)

TEST 1 

From the Service Desktop (with the service key inserted), select: [DIAGNOSTIC] , [SYSTEM CONTROL] , [HOST DIAGNOSTIC] , [KEYBOARD] .

TEST 2 

From the Service Desktop (with the service key inserted), select: [DIAGNOSTIC] , [OPERATOR I/O] , [HARDKEY/KEYBOARD] , [RUN] .
