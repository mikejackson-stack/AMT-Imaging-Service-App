Gantry Top Covers & Fan Removal (Limited Access)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEff

Front and Rear Gantry Covers installed

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Remove the Top Cover Fan Assemblies as follows:

    1. Reach beneath the Right Top Cover and unscrew the captive thumbscrews that fasten the Fan Assembly to the Top Cover.

    1. Fasten the Fan Assembly Tools into the threaded holes in the cover. See Illustration 1.

      (Step Details)

    1. On the Right Top Cover, disconnect the two fan assembly cables and the Rear Cover cable.

    1. Remove the Fan Assembly. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the cover to clear the bracket and Gantry assemblies.

  1. Repeat Step 1 through 4 to remove Left Top Cover.
