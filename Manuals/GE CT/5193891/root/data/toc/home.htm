5193891-800 - LS 5.X Pro16 Limited Access Service Info (Adv), Rev 5

The information contained in this publication applies to ALL LightSpeed 5.X Pro16 (100kW) systems (both Limited Access Compliant and non-Limited Access Compliant). Non-Limited Access Compliant systems can not be upgraded to a Limited Access Compliant system. Direction 5113143-800 (Class A) and Direction 5113144-800 (Class C) for LightSpeed 5.X Pro16 non-Limited Access Compliant systems are now obsolete.
