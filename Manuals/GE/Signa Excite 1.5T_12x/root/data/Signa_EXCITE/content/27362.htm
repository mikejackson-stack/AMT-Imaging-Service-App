Oxygen Monitor Safety

NOTE:Since March 1, 1994, the oxygen monitor is no longer delivered with GE MR systems. Oxygen monitors are still available as an accessory from GE Medical Systems through Diagnostics Imaging Accessories (DIA).

The oxygen monitor sensor element should be replaced every six to twelve months (beginning in 1994, a new five- year oxygen sensor element became available). They are shipped in transparent airtight packages, and should be inspected upon receipt. The cells contain a strong caustic potassium (KOH) solution. If the cell membrane is punctured, do not open the package.
