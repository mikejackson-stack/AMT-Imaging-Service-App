Front End Bell Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Ensure that the RF Tube feet are extended, then slowly move the front end bell into place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use an 8 mm wrench to install the 24 M10 bolts that secure the front end bell to the magnet. Use a star pattern when tightening the bolts. The front enclosure should touch the aluminum ring when finished.

  1. Install the eight nuts that secure the front end bell to the RF coil (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the two bias lines at the front of the magnet.

  1. Connect the four coolant lines located inside of the front end bell.

  1. Install the two plates located on top of the coolant feed throughs (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the water fittings for the supply and return lines (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Route the temperature sensor cable through the front end bell.

  1. Install the cosmetic cover over the RF Coil/front end bell junction.

  1. Install the cosmetic ring. See Illustration 2.

  1. At the SCC Cabinet, refill the coolant fluid that was drained during the removal process.

  1. Replace the bridge. See Bridge Replacement.

  1. Remove the lock out and tag out from the RF Cabinet and the SCC Cabinet.

  1. Flip the toggle switch on the SCC cabinet back to the upper position so the Magnet Monitor will be able to dial out.
