2  Procedure

  1. Properly power down the GOC.

  1. Remove the three screws in the rear that secure the right side panel.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the right side panel by sliding in backwards.

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the WIM.

  1. Remove all cables that are connected to the WIM. Verify proper labeling.

    Illustration 2:  Removing the Cables

  1. Remove the WIM by rotating the black handles on the right side counter-clockwise.

  1. Slide the WIM to the right.

    Illustration 3:  Sliding the WIM

  1. Install replacement WIM by reversing the preceeding steps.

3  Finalization

No finalization steps.
