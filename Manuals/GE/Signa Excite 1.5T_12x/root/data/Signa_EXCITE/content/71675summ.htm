T2 Uniformity Image Analysis

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Select [Image Quality], under [Cal/Checks] on the Service Desktop, then click on [Start].

  1. For TwinSpeed, highlight the same GradMode as was used in T2 Uniformity Checks Body Scans, then click on [OK].

  1. A NEMA Image Quality window will appear on the desktop, as shown in Illustration 1. At the Select Test prompt, type 4<Enter> for the T2 Uniformity Check.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter the image exam, series, and the first of the four image numbers.

  1. Analysis then begins. The final values are displayed on the screen as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the Mean T2, Standard Deviation, and Relative Deviation in Data Sheet (T2 Uniformity Data Sheet).

  1. Repeat Step 3 through 6 above, for analysis of the remaining series.

  1. Type 6 then press <Enter> to exit the NEMA IQ Analysis Menu.

2  Finalization
