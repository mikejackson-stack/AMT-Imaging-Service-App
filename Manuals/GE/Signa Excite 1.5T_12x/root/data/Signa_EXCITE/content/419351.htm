3  Procedure

  1. Apply the Brake on the three lock casters, one at a time. See Illustration 1. Refer to Patient Transport Theory for additional information.

    Illustration 1:  Caster in Braked Position

  1. While holding the handle of the table, push and pull the table and observe any play in the casters. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Push and Pull Direction

  1. If there is movement in any of the braked casters, it must be replaced.

  1. Refer to the Castor Replacement procedure, Patient Table Casters.

  1. If needed, replace casters using Total Lock Caster, 2330261 and Directional Lock Caster, 2330261-2.
