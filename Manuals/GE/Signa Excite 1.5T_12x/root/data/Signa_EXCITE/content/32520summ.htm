Ground Leakage Current Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
possible FATAL PERSONAL INJURY!
LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT WITHIN THE Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) EVEN WHEN ALL PDU BREAKERS ARE OFF.
CHECK THAT POWER AT THE MAIN DISCONNECT IS OFF, LOCKED out AND TAGGED out BEFORE PROCEEDING.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Ground Resistance Checks are completed and passed.

-

-

All cable installation complete.

-

-

Facility disconnect off.

-

-

Power Distribution Unit (PDU) secondary power circuit breakers off.

-

-

Main Disconnect CB1 PDU is on.

-

-

All subsystem cabinet circuit breakers and subsystem power switches are On.

-

-

No hard-wired signal cables connected to any subsystem cabinets. Fiber-optic cables may be connected and not impact this check.

3  Procedure

  1. Perform Phoenix PDU LOTO.

  1. Dress in the appropriate personal protection clothing.

  1. With a DVM, make sure all energy has dissipated and the Main Disconnect Panel and PDU indicator lights are Off and power switches inactive.

  1. Connect one clamp-type lead to the EXTERNAL jack on the top of the Dale Safety Analyzer. Connect the other clamp-type lead to the CHASSIS jack on the top of the Dale 600/600E. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Dale Safety Analyzer, set the main selector switch to Leakage mA - External

  1. Connect the CHASSIS clamp-type lead to the PDU Ground Bus Bar.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Only connect one ground cable to the EXTERNAL clamp-type lead. If a second ground cable exists, leave it disconnected during the test.
 
 

  1. At PDU Bus Bar, disconnect ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet being tested.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on facility Disconnect.

  1. Turn on the CB1 Main Circuit Breaker to power the PDU.

  1. When mode sequencing is complete (all relays stop clicking on standard PDU), turn on Circuit Breaker for the subsystem cabinet under test.

NOTE:500 mV corresponds to 500 mA of leakage current.

  1. Measure the leakage current for the cabinet ground. See the illustration below for the ground connections to the PDU in the cabinet you are measuring.

    1. If the cabinet being tested has only one ground connection at the PDU Ground Bus Bar, verify that less than 500 mV (500 mA) is measured.

      (Step Details)

    1. If the cabinet being tested has two ground connections at the PDU Ground Bus Bar, verify that less than 5,000 mV (5,000 mA) is measured. If more than 5000 mA is measured, a serious leakage current problem exists that must be corrected. Use the appropriate procedures to troubleshoot the system.

  1. Turn off Circuit Breaker for the subsystem cabinet under test.

  1. Turn off and LOTO the CB1 Main Circuit Breaker for the PDU .

  1. Turn off facility Disconnect.

NOTICE
Some subsystem cabinets have one ground connection at the PDU Bus Bar, while other subsystem cabinets have two. If two ground connections exist, ensure that both are reconnected in the following step.
 
 

  1. At PDU Ground Bus Bar, reconnect ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet being tested.

  1. Repeat Step 1 through Step 15, turning on appropriate circuit breakers for each remaining subsystem cabinet being tested.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore the system:

    1. Remove leads from Dale Safety Analyzer.

    1. Turn on all PDU secondary power circuit breakers (CB1 through CB17).

    1. Close the PDU Cabinet.

4  Finalization

  1. Ensure the system is ready for patient scanning.

  1. Return to Grounding and Leakage Current Checks or PAC Assembly Leakage Current Test.
