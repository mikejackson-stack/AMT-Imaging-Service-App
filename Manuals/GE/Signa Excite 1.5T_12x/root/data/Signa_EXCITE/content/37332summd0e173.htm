NOTE:If you are performing Rough LVshim, you may have to use [Manual Prescan] to find the center frequency. Use [Center Freq Coarse (CFL)] to center on the frequency peak as necessary. Be sure to save the center frequency value before exiting manual prescan. Select the Frequency menu at the top of Manual Prescan window and select [Save Frequency]. You should also write down the center frequency value for future reference.
