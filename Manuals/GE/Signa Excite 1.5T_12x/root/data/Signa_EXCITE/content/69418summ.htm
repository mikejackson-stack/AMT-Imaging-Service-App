Bore Light Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove right rear enclosure panel. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn latch holding light source assembly ¼ turn to the right and pull assembly out. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull off plug on halogen light and heat sink. Pull halogen light and heat sink off plate. Halogen light and heat sink will need to be rocked back and forth to accomplish this. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull heat sink off of halogen light bulb.

  1. Slide heat sink onto new halogen light bulb.

  1. Press light/heat sink assembly in place on plate.

  1. Feed light assembly into hole in magnet foot and turn latch ¼ turn counter clockwise to close.

  1. Put enclosure cover back in place.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 or MR1 A20 CB4.

  1. Restore power to operator button panel and display assemblies by switching on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

    (Step Details)
