NEC 1880sx/1980SXi Monitor Installation

1  Procedure

1.1   Installation of the1880SX/1980SXi in a Fixed Site Environment

  1. Installation of the1880SX/1980SXi in a Fixed Site Environment

  1. Remove the new LCD monitor from its box and its packing and set the NEC 1880SX LCD color monitor on the workspace desktop.

  1. Some items will not be used. These can be disposed of locally.

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the monitor and connect the Video and power cables.

  1. Use the restraint clips provided. Route these cables neatly to the back of the monitor base and the Operators Workspace. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Safety Lockout of the Operators Workspace and restore power to the system.

  1. Verify that main LCD monitor power is ON. If not, press the power button on the front panel and the side panel to turn the main LCD monitor ON. See Illustration 2 for side view.

  1. The monitor should be positioned no closer than 16 inches and no further away then 28 inches from your eyes. The optimal distance is 24 inches for either of the monitors.

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot up Signa.

  1. Password: adw2.0<Enter>

  1. Install the proper Gamma Correction patch per the MR Signa Host Monitor Gamma Correction Software CD instructions found in the front section of the CD jewel case.

1.2   Installation or Replacement of an 1880SX/1980SXi in a Mobile Environment

This section pertains to mobile systems only. Replacement of an 1880SX/1980SXi LCD is performed, by removing the four screws that hold the LCD panel to its base. This procedure will only work either for the 1880SX or 1980SXi.

  1. Remove the replacement LCD panel from its shipping box.

  1. Place the new LCD Monitor, face down on a flat surface where it will not get scratched or damaged.

  1. Remove the 4 screws that hold the LCD panel to its base.

  1. Remove the failed 1880SX/1980SXi monitor from its base in the same manner.

  1. Using a Phillips screwdriver, mount the new LCD panel to the existing mobile base using the 4 screws.

  1. Attach the failed LCD Panel to the new base or dispose of the both items according to normal procedures.
