Replace Solenoid Valve

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Ensure that you have the Solenoid Valve Replacement Kit 2317288-4. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four Phillips head screws to remove the bracket that houses the solenoid valve. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the crescent wrench, remove the top nut. See Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the blue Solenoid. See Illustration 4

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove lock washer from base of stud. See Illustration 5

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the spanner nut on the solenoid. See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the stud. See Illustration 7

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four (4) nuts. See Illustration 8

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top plate of the solenoid valve. See Illustration 9

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the rubber diaphragm assembly from the top plate and discard. See Illustration 10

    (Step Details)

  1. In the top plate is a silver cylinder. Push the silver cylinder out and replace the O-ring. See Illustration 11

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the new rubber diaphragm from the Solenoid Valve Kit and place the small spring on the round base the rubber diaphragm. See Illustration 12

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the small metal stud from the kit and place on the spring of the rubber diaphragm till spring snaps on to the bottom ridge of the stud. See Illustration 13

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the larger conical shaped spring and place over the top of stud till it snaps on the first ridge of the stud. See Illustration 14

    (Step Details)

  1. In the lower plate of the solenoid valve, remove the two (2) O-rings and replace with rings from kit. See Illustration 15

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the rubber diaphragm assembly in the top plate. See Illustration 16

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the top plate on the lower plate of the solenoid valve. Make sure the rubber diaphragm lines up with the correct holes.

  1. Replace the four nuts and screws. See Illustration 17

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the larger metal stud with threading from the kit and slide over the stud from the rubber diaphragm. See Illustration 18

    (Step Details)

  1. Take the spanner nut form the kit and slide the nut in place to secure stud. See Illustration 19

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the locking washer on top of the spanner nut. See Illustration 20

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the blue solenoid. See Illustration 21

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the locking washer and nut on top of the blue solenoid and secure. See Illustration 21

  1. Replace the solenoid valve assembly
