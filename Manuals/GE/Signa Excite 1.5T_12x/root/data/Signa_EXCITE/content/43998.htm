3.2  On-Screen Controls

This section describes the adjustments available for the OSM controls.

  1. Brightness and Contrast

    1. Brightness: Adjusts the overall image and background screen brightness.

    1. Contrast: Adjusts the image brightness in relation to the background.

    1. Auto Adjust Contrast: Adjusts the image displayed for non-standard video inputs.

  1. Auto Adjust

    Automatically adjust the Image Position or the H. Size setting.

  1. Position Controls

    1. “H” Position: Controls Horizontal Image Position within the display area of the LCD.

    1. “V” Position: Controls Vertical Image Position within the display area of the LCD.

      Auto: Automatically sets the horizontal and vertical image position within the display area of the LCD.

  1. Image Adjust Controls

    1. “H” Size: Adjusts the horizontal size by increasing or decreasing this setting.

    1. Fine: Improves focus, clarity, and image stability by increasing or decreasing the Fine setting.

    1. Auto Adjust Coarse: Automatically adjusts the Coarse setting.

  1. AccuColor® Control System

    1. Five color presets select the desired color setting. This setting must be set to selection 1 - 9300. If a setting is adjusted, the name of the setting will change to Custom.

    1. R, B, G: Increases or decreases the color depending upon which is selected. The change in color will appear on screen and the increase or decrease will be shown by the color bars.

  1. OSM H POS./OSM V POS.

    You can choose where the OSM control image appears on the screen. Selecting OSM Location allows you to manually adjust the position.

  1. OSM Lock Out

    This control completely locks out access to all OSM control functions. When attempting to activate controls while in Lock Out mode, a message screen will appear indicating that the controls are locked out.

    • To enter the Lock Out mode, simultaneously press <PROCEED> and the button; the LOCK OUT window will appear.

    • To activate the Lock Out function, simultaneously press and hold down the <PROCEED> and the button; the OSM window will disappear within seconds and the LOCK OUT function will be activated.

    • To de-activate the Lock Out function, simultaneously press <PROCEED> and the button.

  1. ALL RESET

    This allows you to reset all OSM control settings back to the factory settings. Highlighting the setting and then pressing the <RESET> button can reset individual settings.

  1. Information

    Indicates the current display resolution, frequency setting, and type of Sync signal of the monitor.

    NOTE:Mode Change should only be used if the monitor does not recognize a resolution. This shouldn’t ever be the case with our system. You can change to the appropriate resolution by selecting the <Mode information> and selecting (increase or decrease) the corresponding option.

    NOTE:If the or the button is pressed while TYPE is highlighted then the Sync Type is switched between Separate Sync and Sync on Green.
