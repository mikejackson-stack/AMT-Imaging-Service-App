Replacing Speaker

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Speaker Removal

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove three (3) screws holding speaker/microphone in place. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Drop speaker/microphone assembly down and remove two (2) push on speaker wire connections. See Illustration 2.

  1. Remove two (2) nuts holding speaker to mounting plate. See Illustration 2.

  1. Lift speaker off of mounting plate.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Speaker Installation

  1. Secure new speaker to mounting plate with two (2) nuts.

  1. Plug in speaker wires.

  1. Secure speaker/microphone assembly to rear end bell using three (3) screws removed earlier.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 or MR1 A20 CB4.

  1. Restore power to enclosure assemblies by switching on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

    (Step Details)
