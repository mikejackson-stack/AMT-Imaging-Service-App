NOTE:Reflex 200 and 400 boards have slots on the front for ribbon cables. Reflex 200B, 400B and 800 do not have these slots but instead communicate through the PIM module that is mounted on the rear of the Midplane. Replacement Reflex 200, 400 and 200B boards must be configured before installation. See 2333495 Signa Reflex AP Option for MGD for Reflex 200 and 400 board configuration information. See 2376837 ReFlex AP Option Installation for EXCITE II for Reflex 200B configuration information.

Reflex 400B/800 master and slave boards have separate FRU part numbers so, as long as the correct replacement board is ordered, No configuration of these boards is necessary.
