1. First, if you do not see the CDROM on your desktop, click on the [Start Here] icon on the desktop.

  2. The CDROM should now appear on your desktop.

  3. Right-click on the CDROM icon and select [Eject]. This will eject the CD if the CD was “mounted.” If this does not work try pressing the eject button on the CDROM drive. If this still does not work try the Terminal method described below.
