Magnet Ramp Port Panel (PP1 A6)

Signa Advantage 1.5T, 1.0T, & 0.5T and Signa Advantage 1.5T Upgrade Penetration Panels include a removable panel in the lower section. Unfastening captive thumb nuts allows quick removal for feed through of Oxford or GE Magnet ramping cables. This port is designed to accept all cable combinations, according to magnet type, as listed in Table 1.
