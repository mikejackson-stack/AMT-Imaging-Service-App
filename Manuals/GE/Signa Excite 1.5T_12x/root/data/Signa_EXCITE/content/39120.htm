Abort and Exit

If for any reason you want to abort the scan, click the [Abort], then press the <Stop Scan> key on the keyboard to stop the scan. After ten to 20 seconds, the grayed [Scan] button will become active and you can continue.

Click the [Exit] button to exit from Grafidy 3. It will take a while to exit because the tool is writing out the log file
