3  Procedure

  1. For systems using the Quad Head Coil, skip to Step 2. For systems with the new Split Top Head Coil position SNR Sphere in the head loader as sown in Illustration 1. Fasten the strap to the top of the head loader to secure the SNR Sphere inside the loader. Position the head loader on top of the Head TLT loader holder within the head coil so the loader is centered and the loader axial mark is at the center of the head coil window. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. For systems with the quad head coil position DQA phantom in the head coil. Turn on the alignment lights and align so the center of the DQA phantom matches the axial beam. Then place the head TLT sphere in the head loader and fasten the strap to the top of the head loader to secure the sphere. Remove the DQA phantom and replace with the head loader/sphere centering it using the axial alignment light.

  1. Place the SPT Body Loader and Body Sphere after the head coil. Make sure to place these on the nesting plate. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark on the head coil. As the calibration runs, the table will move to the body coil scan by itself.

  1. On the Service Desktop, start the service browser if it's not already running.

  1. Start the System Gain Calibration Tool as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the tool starts, type 2<Enter> for Auto Mode. See Illustration 5.

  1. Select both head and body, type 3<Enter>. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. When testing is complete, you will be asked “Do you want to save the recon scale factor”. Press the <Enter> key since “y” is the default. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Calibration is complete.

  1. Select option 4 to Quit, then press <Enter> to exit.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove phantoms and Service Tools used during the calibration.

  1. Reboot the system.

  1. Do a test scan with the system.
