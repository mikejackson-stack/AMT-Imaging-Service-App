PDU Module In Gradient Cab Emergency Off And Stop Circuits

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Perform System Shutdown, and verify that the PC is off.

  1. Press one of the EMERGENCY STOP buttons:

    (Step Details)

  1. At the Magnet Enclosure, verify that all LED’s and lights on the magnet display are off. Verify that the AC Power on the front of the RF amp is off. See Illustration 1. Verify that all LED’s on the Gradient (either ACGD or HFD) power supply module are off. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press EMO Reset button on the PDU. The mentioned LED’s should now illuminate.

  1. Repeat steps 2 through 4 for each of the other EMERGENCY STOP buttons.

  1. Press one of the following EMERGENCY OFF buttons:

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the breaker internal to the PDU trips.

  1. Reset the Input circuit at PDU by turning it OFF, then back ON.

  1. Repeat steps 6 through 8 for other EMERGENCY OFF buttons.
