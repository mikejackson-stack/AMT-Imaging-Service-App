3  Procedure

3.1   SRI-II upgrade from SRI-I

  1. Remove existing SRI-I. Verify that all cables going to the SRI are properly labeled.

  1. Verify switch settings on the SRI-II for LCC magnet configuration. Switches should be set as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Installation for LCC Magnets

  1. Install replacement SRI-II in the location of the SRI-I. See Illustration 2 for proper orientation of the SRI-II. Use the provided Velcro to attach the SRI-II to the base plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. The cable from SRI-II to the Magnet Display must be replaced. Remove the right and left side covers from the magnet. These covers are Velcro-ed to the magnet. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front Gumby cover that is secured by two front screws. See Illustration 3.

  1. Remove the magnet front-right blue cover. See Illustration 4. After removing the screws, slide the blue cover to the right.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully, using a small screw driver, pry the magnet display away from the front cover. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable that runs from the SRI to the magnet display.

  1. Totally remove the cable that runs between the SRI-II and the magnet display by undoing the cable ties. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the replacement cable that arrived with the SRI-II between magnet display and SRI-II.

  1. Put covers back on to the magnet as they were removed.

3.3   Installation for S2 through S5 Magnets

  1. Pull the magnet front cover forward, as the SRI is located on the top-right section of the magnet enclosure.

  1. Remove the existing SRI

  1. Install the replacement SRI using the supplied bracket.

  1. needed the slot that seats the SRI-II (when front enclosure is moved back into operating position) can be increased due to the increased width of the new SRI-II.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Reinstall all covers on the magnet.

  1. Setup a test scan to insure proper operation.
