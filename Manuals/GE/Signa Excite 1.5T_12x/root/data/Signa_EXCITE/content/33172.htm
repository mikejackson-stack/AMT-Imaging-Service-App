3.2  Safety

WARNING
Eye contact may cause minor irritation.
The TLT Sphere contains Dimethyl Silicone fluid.
DO NOT INGEST. If a leak or spill occurs wipe up or absorb in inert material and place in container for disposal. Incineration or landfill is the recommended disposal method.

NOTICE
There is the potential to damage the equipment when procedures are not followed. Completely remove the quad head coil from the cradle before performing any body scans. Failure to do so may damage the head coil T/R network.

4  Procedure

4.1  Body Coil Calibration

  1. Remove quad head coil (if present) from cradle.

  1. Press <Alignment Lights> button to illuminate the alignment lights.

  1. Position the Body TLT Sphere and prop it up with a pad per . Move the cradle to position the landmark on the center of the sphere.

  1. At the keypad on the front magnet enclosure, press <Landmark>, then <Advance to Scan>.

  1. At the operator work space, prepare the system for a System Gain Cal, Body scan using the "Service Protocols" procedure located on the service methods CD-ROM

  1. Click on [Auto Prescan]. After auto prescan is complete, verify that R1 = 9 and R2 = 14. If not, perform a manual prescan and set R1 = 9 and R2 = 14. Record the R1, R2, TG, and system frequency (AX) values on the .

  1. Click on [Scan].

4.2  Body Scan Analysis

  1. At the Operator Workstation, go to the Service Desktop, click on[ Cal/Checks] and select System Gain Calibration from the tools list, then click on [START]. Continue per .

  1. To change the Recon Scale Factor, right click on [Operations] and select [Display CVs].

  1. Enter CV name cfcgain (must be lower case) followed by <Enter>.

  1. Type the new Recon Scale Factor (which is sometimes referred to as the coil gain factor), followed by <Enter> and [Accept].

  1. Right click on [Research Operations] and select Download, then click on [Auto Presca].

    NOTE:The Download followed by Auto Prescan is needed for the system to recognize the modified Recon Scale Factor for the next scan.

  1. After Auto Prescan is complete, verify that R1 = 9 and R2 = 14. If not, perform manual prescan and set R1 = 9 and R2 = 14. Record the R1, R2, TG, and system frequency (AX) values in .

  1. Click on [Scan]. After the scan is completed return to System Gain Calibration and press Enter to continue (place cursor within the "System Gain Cal" window to activate input in this window). The tool executes using the scan just performed and displays more values (see ).

    NOTE:If the Recon Scale Factor value was changed, the Signa software must be restarted for the new values to be recognized for all future body scans, however, if you are immediately performing the Head System Gain Calibration, do not restart the Signa software at this time.

  1. To restart Signa software, right click on SGI desktop wallpaper and select [System Shutdown] from the Service Tools menu, or click System Shutdown in the Service Desktop Manager area. Due to a bug in the software you must wait 15 seconds before logging back in. Failure to wait 15 seconds will cause a “Host Monitor timeout in 50 secs” error. At the login screen, double click on the Signa icon and type the password adw2.0 <Enter> to restart Signa software.

5  Finalization

  1. If no other section of System Gain is to be completed, Insure the system is ready for patient scanning by doing a verification scan.
