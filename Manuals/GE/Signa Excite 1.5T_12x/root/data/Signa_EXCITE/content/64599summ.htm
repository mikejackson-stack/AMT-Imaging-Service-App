CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
ENCODER CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, REMOVE ENCODER AND MOUNTING BRACKET FROM MAGNET ROOM TO INSTALL NEW ENCODER.

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove Old Encoder

  1. Switch off circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

  1. Lock out and tag out MR1 A14 CB6 using appropriate procedure. (Refer to .)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP1 (+) and TP14 (–) is 0 Vdc.

  1. Remove rear pedestal right side cover (as viewed from rear of magnet).

  1. Disconnect encoder cable from J3 of Magnet I/F #1 (MG3 A1).

  1. Loosen set screw on shaft coupling (see Illustration 2).

  1. Remove screw from encoder mounting bracket, and remove encoder and mounting bracket from rear pedestal. Remove encoder and mounting bracket from magnet room (see Illustration 2.

  1. Remove four screws that hold encoder to mounting bracket.

1.2   Install Replacement Encoder

  1. Install new encoder onto mounting bracket with four screws.

  1. Install bracket and encoder assembly onto rear pedestal.

  1. Tighten set screw on shaft coupling.

  1. Connect encoder cable to J3 of Magnet I/F #1.

  1. Replace rear pedestal right side cover.

  1. Remove lock out and tag out items. Switch on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

  1. Adjust encoder voltage. Refer to procedure for .

  1. Starting with cradle at Home position, drive table into bore until longitudinal position display reads 10 mm.

  1. Place tape marker on cradle and adjacent spot on table.

  1. Drive table into bore until longitudinal position display reads 1010 mm.

  1. Measure distance between marks and verify that distance traveled is 1000 mm +1 mm.
