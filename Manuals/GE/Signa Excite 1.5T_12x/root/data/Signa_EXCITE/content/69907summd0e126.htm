For RF/Pen cabinet, Switch on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

For RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet: Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A20 CB4 at rear of RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet on System Support Module (SSM).

For SRF Cabinets, place the breaker in the rear of the cabinet in the ON position.
