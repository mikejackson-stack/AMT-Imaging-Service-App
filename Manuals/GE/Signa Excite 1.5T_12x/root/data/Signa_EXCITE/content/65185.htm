2  Procedure

  1. Properly power down the GOC.

  1. Remove the three screws in the rear that secure the right side panel.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the right side panel by sliding in backwards.

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the HUB.

  1. Remove all cables that are connected to the HUB. Verify proper labeling.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Cables

  1. Loosen the strap that secures the HUB and carefully remove it.

  1. Install the replacement HUB by reversing the steps.

No finalization steps.
