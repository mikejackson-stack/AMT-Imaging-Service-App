2  Overview

This procedure is intended to determine the GE coil name used during a scan if the Customer has re-named a coil.

4  Procedure

  1. Make sure Exam is loaded on the hard drive. If needed, dump the Exam back on to the system hard drive.

  1. Open a Cshell

  1. In the Cshell type: readHead<Enter>. A tool will appear.

  1. Enter the proper Exam number and series number.

  1. Select option g<Enter>

  1. Select option y<Enter>

  1. Go to the second page and scroll until the coil name is displayed. See Illustration 1.
