Isolation Box Troubleshooting and Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Fatal Electrical Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present in the rear pedestal during normal operations.
Perform lock out tag out as described in the first step of this document.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Systems Cabinet, Amplifier and SSM are locked out at the PDU per the procedure at the end of this document.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Troubleshooting the Isolation Box Assemblies

  1. Lock out and/or tag out Systems Cabinet, Amplifier and SSM:

    1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

    1. Flip the breaker switches for the “System Cabinet”, “Amplifier” and “SSM” to the OFF position.

    1. Close the cover over the breaker switches and install your lock and tag.

    1. Verify the power is off by checking the fans and the LEDs on the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and the SSM.

  1. Remove the side covers on the rear pedestal.

  1. Disconnect the I or Q cables and the Hybrid cable from the suspected Isolation Box Assembly. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using non-ferrous screwdriver, remove suspected Isolation Box Assembly. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. If there is a 90-degree connector attached to one of the outputs, be sure to remove this from the failed Isolation Box Assembly prior to continuing with the troubleshooting. This 90-degree connector will need to be saved to be used with the new Isolation Box Assembly.

  1. With the DMM set to measure resistance, check that the values are as expected. See Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. If the values are not as expected, proceed to the Replacing the Isolation Box Assembly.

3.2   Replacing the Isolation Box Assembly

  1. Ensure that the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and SSM have been powered down, locked out and tagged out:

    1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

    1. Flip the breaker switches for the “System Cabinet”, “Amplifier” and “SSM” to the OFF position.

    1. Close the cover over the breaker switches and install your lock and tag.

    1. Verify the power is off by checking the fans and the LEDs on the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and the SSM.

  1. Remove the side covers on the rear pedestal.

  1. Disconnect the I or Q cable and the Hybrid cable from the suspected Isolation Box Assembly. See Illustration 1.

  1. If there is a 90-degree connector attached to one of the outputs, be sure to remove this from the failed Isolation Box Assembly and reinstall on the new one.

  1. If it has not already been removed from the Hybrid Splitter, using a non-ferrous screwdriver, remove the two small screws attaching the failed Isolation Box Assembly to the hybrid. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the failed Isolation Box Assembly.

  1. Screw the new Isolation Box Assembly to the Body Hybrid.

  1. Connect the cable from the Hybrid to the Isolation Box. Connect the I or Q cable to the other side of the Isolation Box.

  1. Install the side covers on the rear pedestal.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove lock out and/or tag out per procedure at the end of this document:

    1. Notify affected personnel that equipment will be re-energized and LOTO devices are being removed.

    1. Remove Lock-Out Tag-Out equipment from PDU and flip the breaker switches for the “Systems Cabinet”, “Amplifier” and “SSM” to the ON position.

    1. Reset the Emergency Off switch.

    1. Reset the TPS. The TPS should reset in the normal amount of time (about 1 minute and 20 seconds).

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure system operation.
