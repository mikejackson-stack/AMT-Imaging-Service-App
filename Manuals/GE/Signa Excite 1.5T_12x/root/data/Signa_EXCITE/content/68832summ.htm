Bridge Restoration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Slide the rear pedestal back to its original position so it properly mates to the front bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the four M10 hex nuts that hold the front bridge and rear bridge together (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Level the bridge by adjusting the vertical location of the front bridge support.

  1. Reconnect the cable track cup that holds the bridge cables.

  1. Re-clamp the belt using four screws holding the two belt clamp plates. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Push down the tension arm to reapply tension to carriage drive belt. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the carriage cover and reinstall four screws in carriage cover.

  1. Install covers on the rear pedestal. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the corner magnet covers. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Switch on the circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 located at the rear of the RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on the MEPS module.

    (Step Details)
