Restoration After Coil Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Refer to MR SYSTEM LOCKOUT PROCEDURE.

  1. Locate the subsection referring to the PDU used in the MR system.

  1. Restore power to the MRI system.

  1. Verify the gradient chiller is running, check the coolant level, and replenish coolant as necessary.

  1. View the fittings through the transparent window on the front end-bell and confirm that none of the fittings are leaking coolant.

  1. Check the gradient coolant valves behind the magnet and confirm that these are also not leaking.

  1. Reinstall the bridge into the bore.

    1. Slide the long section of the bridge back into the bore.

    1. Secure the front bridge support to the end-bell using the two (2) M10 bolts.

    1. Tight the locknuts on the M10 bolts.

  1. If the system has a split bridge, move the rear pedestal until the two parts of the bridge are engaged. Secure with the four (4) M10 nuts and an adjustable wrench.

  1. Replace front Aesthetic ring (no tools needed).

  1. Replace front bridge cover and secure using two (2) M6 Phillips screws.

  1. Confirm that LEDs are illuminated on the front of the RFI and SSM and confirm that the fans in the front of the RF amplifiers are rotating.

NOTICE
THE SSM WILL NOT FAULT IF IT IS CALIBRATED WITH A DISCONNECTION OR POOR CONNECTION TO ONE OF THE 4 MAGNET ROOM DYNAMIC DISABLE BIAS LINES (J72, J73, J75, AND J76 ON THE MAGNET ROOM SIDE OF THE PENETRATION PANEL). IMAGE SNR DEGRADATION WILL BE NOTICED BUT THE SSM MAY NOT FAULT. MAKE CERTAIN THE FOUR BIAS LINE CONNECTIONS FROM THE PEN PANEL TO THE RF COIL ARE CONNECTED BEFORE PERFORMING THIS CALIBRATION.
 
 

  1. Press and release the CAL switch on the front of the SSM (see Illustration 1). This samples the open circuit fault detect signals and adjusts the threshold accordingly for the Dynamic Disable Circuits: J42 (Body 1), J43 (Body 2), and J44 (Direct Drive). When the CAL switch is pressed the LEDs labeled TEST, DRV FLT, and PS FLT will illuminate (this does not indicate a fault).

    (Step Details)

  1. View the front panel LEDs. Ensure the Bank 1, LED #10 is OFF (see Illustration 2). This LED indicates the adjustment process was successful.

    (Step Details)

  1. Flip the toggle switch on the SCC cabinet back to the upper position so the Magnet Monitor will be able to dial out.

2  Finalization

  1. Run TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Verify that the system is functional by completing one head and body scan.

  1. Dispose of excess gradient coolant drained earlier from the gradient coil as per local ordinances and regulations.
