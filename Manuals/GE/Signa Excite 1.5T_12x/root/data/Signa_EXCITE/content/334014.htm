Replacement of A or B connector on LPCA

2  Overview

Procedure for replacement of the A or B connector in the LPCA.

NOTE:The process for changing either the A or B connector is the same, however the connectors are physically different.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

3.2  Safety

WARNING
Electrical Hazard!
Strong voltages are present in or around the LPCA when energized.
Remove power from the lPCA at the pdu as directed later in this procedure, then lockout and tagout the pdu.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal of Cable take-up

  1. Run LPCA to the rear of the magnet and remove the cover to the LPCA.

  1. For sites with RFS cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled PHPS/SSM on the PDU. Or for sites with RF cabinet and Systems cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled SSM.

  1. Remove the cabling for the connector to be replaced. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Cable Removal

  1. Remove the LED outputs from the connector. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  LED Removal

  1. Loosen the two Phillips screws that secure the connector to the LPCA. See Illustration 3. Remove the connector.

    Illustration 3:  Connector Removal

4.2  Installation of New Cable take-up

  1. Place replacement connector in position.

  1. Connect all cables from connector to LPCA, including LED cables.

  1. Re-install cover on LPCA.

  1. Remove Lock-out Tag-out and restore power.

5  Finalization

  1. Run MCR2 tool to verify cabling.
