GOC Power Distribution Supply Replacement

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Procedure

  1. Properly power down the GOC.

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by removing the 4 screws in the rear. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the power cord from the rear of the GOC.

  1. Remove the two screws and ground cable from the rear of the GOC Power Distribution Supply. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Screws and Cable

  1. Remove the two front screws from the GOC Power Distribution Supply.

  1. Remove the four screws from the door hinge.

  1. Carefully slide the GOC Power Distribution Supply out of the GOC.

  1. Insert the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply. Reverse the preceeding steps to properly install the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply.

No finalization steps.
