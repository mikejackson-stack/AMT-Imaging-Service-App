NEC 1850X Monitor Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Shutdown Signa and perform a safety lockout/tagout of the Operator Workspace.

  1. Remove the new LCD monitor from its box and its packing and set the NEC 1850X LCD color monitor on the workspace desktop and remove its rear cover. Apply light pressure to side of cover to release the plastic snap hooks. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Video and power cables. Route these cables neatly to the back of the Operators Workspace. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-attach the cover to the back of the LCD. Apply light pressure on the right side of the small cover to lock the cover in place. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Safety Lockout of the Operators Workspace and restore power to the system.

  1. Verify that main LCD monitor power is ON. If not, press the power button on the front panel and the side panel to turn the main LCD monitor ON. See Illustration 4 for side view.

  1. 5. The monitor should be positioned no closer than 16 inches and no further away then 28 inches from your eyes. The optimal distance is 24 inches for either of the monitors.

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot up Signa.
