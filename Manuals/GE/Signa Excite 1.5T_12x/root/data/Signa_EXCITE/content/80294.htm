Gradient Coil Replacement in a Mobile

Additional Requirements for Gradient coil Replacement in a Mobile include:

  • Total of 4 Field Engineers vs. 3 for fixed site

  • Forklift with side-to-side fork motion ability

  • Experienced forklift driver (precise movement of the forklift is required)

  • 4 x 8 sheet of plywood cut to fit on the floor behind the magnet

Defective Gradient Removal Procedure, however the Gradient coil is removed from the rear of the magnet vs. from the front for a fixed site.
