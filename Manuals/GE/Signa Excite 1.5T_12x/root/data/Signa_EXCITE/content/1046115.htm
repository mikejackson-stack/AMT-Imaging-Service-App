3  Procedure

  1. Disconnect any coil that is connected to the LPCA, and drive the cradle into the bore.

  1. Rotate the handle toward you (shown in Illustration 1) and verify that the cradle releases from the LPCA.

    • If the cradle releases, this check is completed and NO further action is needed.

    • If the cradle does not release, proceed to the next step.

    Illustration 1:  Cradle Release

  1. Roll the cradle into the magnet approximately 1.5 feet (0.5 meters), and remove it from the table and turn it upside down.

  1. Locate the cradle emergency release adjustment shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Cradle Release Adjustment Location

  1. Untie the Kevlar cord and loosen the hex-head set screw (shown in Illustration 3) and either:

    • Tighten the cradle release mechanism if cradle does not release when handle is rotated, or

    • Loosen the cradle release mechanism if cradle releases without handle rotation.

    Illustration 3:  Cradle Release Adjustment

  1. Tie unused section of Kevlar cord to the cradle release mechanism.

  1. Repeat Step 1 and Step 2.
