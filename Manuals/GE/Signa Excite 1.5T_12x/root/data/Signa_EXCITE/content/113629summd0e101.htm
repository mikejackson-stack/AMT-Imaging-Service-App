Pilot and Production sites are shipped using the TR in the Driver Module. There will be a cover over the SSM TR switch if this is applicable at a site. Use this step to disable TR errors in this case.

NOTE:Property Damage! Prevent coil and associated switch damage, by Removing all phantoms and hardware (i.e., Head Coil, surface Coil...) from the magnet bore.

  1. Verify that the system is idle and all coils have been removed from magnet bore.

  2. Remove the front cover of the RF Accessories Cabinet.

  3. On the front of the Driver Module, disable T/R error reporting by setting the TR switch to the DIS (disable faults) position. See Illustration 1.

Illustration 1:  Driver Module Switch

NOTE:HV, DD and MC should remain set to Enable.
