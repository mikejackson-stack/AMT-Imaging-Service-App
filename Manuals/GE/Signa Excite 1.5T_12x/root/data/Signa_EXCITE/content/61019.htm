Gradient Switch Functional Check

2  Preliminary Requirements

2.1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Disable any software running in the background on the laptop, such as Personal Digital Assistant (PDA) syncing or “Hotlink” programs, that take exclusive control of the COM 1 port and will prevent HyperTerminal from using this port. Failure to do this will result in COM 1 port availability errors when attempting to configure the port from HyperTerminal.

-

-

Laptop is configured for Communications with the Gradient Switch.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Lock Out and Tag out the Gradient Switch Cabinet.

  1. After ensuring the power is OFF, remove the protective plastic panel on the rear of the Gradient Switch module.

  1. Connect one end of the Serial Port Cable to the J6 Debug Terminal on the rear of the Gradient Switch and the other end to the 9 pin Sub-D COM1 serial port on the rear of the laptop.

    NOTE:The serial port cable (P/N 2283292) ships with the Gradient Switch Cabinet and is secured in the bottom of the cabinet.

  1. Remove the Lock out/ Tag out.

  1. Power on the Gradient Switch cabinet, Default is WB and the 3 WB LEDs are lit. PS LED is lit.

  1. Double click the GradSwitch icon on the laptop to open the Hyper Terminal window to begin a session.

  1. Type: <File/Properties/Settings/ASCII Setup/> and press <Enter>.

  1. Choose [Send Line Ends With Line Feeds] and [Echo Typed Characters Locally], and confirm by clicking [OK].

  1. Type in all capital letters <GSC_SW 1 1 1> and press <Enter>.

    1. WB is still on

    1. The Terminal response display will be: <GSC_STAT 1 1 1>.

  1. Type : <GSC_SW 2 2 2> and press <Enter>.

    1. The Switch will change to ZM status (the three green LEDs' ZM will be ON).

    1. The Terminal response display will be: <GSC STAT 2 2 2>

    1. If an error exists, it will be displayed on the Terminal display screen.

  1. End the Hyper Terminal session on the laptop.

4  Finalization

  1. Lock out and tag out the Gradient Switch Cabinet.

  1. Disconnect the serial cable from the J6 Debug Terminal.

  1. Replace the protective plastic panel.

  1. Remove Lock out /Tag out.

  1. Perform a good-bye scan.
