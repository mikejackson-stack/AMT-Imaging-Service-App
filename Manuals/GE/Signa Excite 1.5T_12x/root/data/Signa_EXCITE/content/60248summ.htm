SRFD2 Amplifier Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
AVOID SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH BY ELECTROCUTION.
REMOVE POWER FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE THE SRFD2 MODULE. VERIFY THAT THE RFI/AMP AND SSM BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU ARE OFF. LOCK AND TAG OUT THE PLEXIGLAS COVER ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY.
THE SRFD2 MODULE WEIGHS 200 LB (91 KG).
THE UNIVERSAL HOIST KIT IS REQUIRED TO REMOVE/REPLACE THE MODULE FROM THE CABINET.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY. HEAVY OBJECT.
THE HOIST KIT WEIGHS 73 LB (33 KG).
ASSISTANCE TO LIFT THIS PRODUCT, MECHANICAL (DOLLIES) OR ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL IS REQUIRED.

WARNING
CABINET TIP HAZARD! POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THE RF CABINET WILL TIP OVER IF THE RF AMPLIFIER IS EXTENDED WITHOUT ANTI-TIP LEGS BEING INSTALLED.
CONFIRM THAT THE ALUMINUM ANTI-TIP LEGS STORED IN THE REAR OF THE CABINET ARE INSTALLED ON EITHER SIDE OF THE BASE OF THE CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE AN RF AMPLIFIER.

1  Procedure

1.1   SRFD2 Amplifier Module Removal

  1. Perform SRFD2 Cabinet Power Lockout and Tagout.

  1. Using the screws included with the anti-tip legs, secure the aluminum anti-tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the SRFD2 Cabinet; screw down the leveling pads at the front of the anti-tip legs so they touch the floor. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect wires from L1, L2 and L3 of LOAD AC connectors and the grounding wire on the rear of the AC Panel. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J14 and J18 connections on the rear of SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four screws securing the amplifier to the cabinet. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY. HEAVY OBJECT.
THE HOIST KIT WEIGHS 73 LB (33 KG).
ASSISTANCE TO LIFT THIS PRODUCT, MECHANICAL (DOLLIES) OR ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL IS REQUIRED.

  1. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections, and place them on a flat surface for assembly. (See Illustration 5 and Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten them together with the 4.75 in. x 1/2 in. caps crews and hex nuts.

  1. Use crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws.

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the rail assembly. (See Illustration 7.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front and rear covers of the SRFD2 Cabinet.

  1. For RFS, remove the front cover and the top blank as shown in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Steel Rail and elevating bracket assembly at the top of the SRFD2 Cabinet, and center the Rail Assembly on the top of the RF Amplifier. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. (See Illustration 10.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
The hoist assembly could fall!
The locking pin keeps the hoist in the rail. Failure to use the locking pin as designed may mean the hoist and anything being lifted by the hoist will fall to the floor endangering person and property.
Do not lift anything with the hoist without the hoist locking pin in place.

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail, and insert the holding (locking) pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail as shown in Illustration 11. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top from the FRU case by releasing the butterfly clamp on each side. (The top is placed in front of the SRFD2 Cabinet for the failed module to be placed on, becoming the bottom of the case for returning the failed module.) See Illustration 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the proper Lift Brackets (P/N 46-317508P1), attach to the side of the module, then attach the Hoist Assembly to the lift brackets. (See Illustration 14.) Reuse the screws from the front of the cabinet to secure the panels.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four (4) screws from each sliding rail. Note that one screw is not accessible when the rails are fully extended. (SeeIllustration 15 and Illustration 16.)

    1. Pull the module out of the cabinet (as shown in Illustration 15) to the point that only three screw heads on the side of the module are in view and the hole in the rail provides access to the screw. Remove the screws from all rails.

      (Step Details)

    1. Pull the module out to point where rails are fully extended, and remove the three (3) remaining screws from each rail. (See Illustration 16.)

      (Step Details)

  1. Push the slides all the way into the cabinet, turn the module so the fans are placed properly on the FRU case, and lower the module.

  1. Remove the Hoist Brackets from the module.

1.2   SRFD2 Amplifier Installation

  1. Place the new module that is on the FRU case base in front of the cabinet by sliding the base between the anti-tip legs, and remove sidewalls by releasing the butterfly clamp. (See Illustration 17.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the shipping strap.

  1. Attach the hoist assembly to the hoist brackets.

  1. Attach the hoist brackets to the SRFD2 module. (See Illustration 18.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the hoist, raise the module to align with the rails.

  1. Once the module is at the correct height to attach the rails, slide the rails completely out, and secure the three (3) screws that are accessible. (See Illustration 19.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the hoist brackets from the module, and return them to the Hoist Kit.

  1. Push the module in to gain access to the fourth screw on each rail, and secure the rail by replacing the screw. (See Illustration 20.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide module completely into cabinet, and insert four (4) screws into the front of the amplifier to secure it to the cabinet. (See Illustration 21.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT THE POSSIBLE ELECTRIC SHOCK FROM AN EXPOSED POWER CABLE,
DO NOT FEED THE SRFD2 PIGTAIL/POWER CABLE BEHIND THE SLIDING RAILS. THIS MAY CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE CABLE POTENTIALLY EXPOSING CABLES. SEE Step 9 AND Illustration 22 FOR THE CORRECT CABLE DRESSING.

  1. Dress the pigtail cable to provide a service loop (40.15 in./1020 mm) for the SRFD2 Module, and attach the cable to the upper horizontal side strut in the cabinet using tie-wraps. (See Illustration 22.) It is important that the cable service loop is placed behind the AC Panel and behind the Module. Do not place the cable on the side of the cabinet behind the sliding rails. This will cause the cable to become damaged and create a hazard.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J14 and J18 connections on the rear of the SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 23.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the L1, L2 and L3 of LOAD AC connectors and the grounding wire on the rear of the AC Panel. (See Illustration 24.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the plastic cover on the AC Panel by securing the four (4) screws. (See Illustration 25.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the four (4) screws to secure the AC Panel to the rear of the cabinet and reconnect J1. (See Illustration 26.)

    (Step Details)

  1. At the rear of the SRFD2 Module, put the CB1 breaker in the ON position or lever up. (See Illustration 27 for location of breaker.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Universal Hoist from the cabinet.

  1. Close the rear cabinet door on the SRFD2 Module.

  1. Remove LOTO from the Circuit Breaker cover on the front of the PDU Cabinet, and put the RFI/AMP and the SSM Breakers in the ON position.

  1. Confirm that LEDs are illuminated on the front of the SRFD2 Module and SSM, and ensure the fans at the rear of the SRFD2 Module are rotating.

1.3   Returning Failed Module

Prepare the failed module by performing the following steps:

  1. Secure the module with the shipping strap, wrap the pigtail power cord and tuck on the side of the module inside the shipping strap. (See Illustration 28.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the sidewalls onto the base and secure. (The black stripe on the outside of the box should align with the black stripe on the base where the module resides.) See Illustration 29.

    (Step Details)

  1. Return the 16-inch Hoist Rail to the FRU case. (See Illustration 30.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the top on the box, keep the black stripe aligned and secure. (See Illustration 29.)

  1. Return SRFD2 Module for root cause analysis by vendor.
