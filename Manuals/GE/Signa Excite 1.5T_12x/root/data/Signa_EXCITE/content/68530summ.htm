Replacing Control Panels

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

    (Step Details)

  1. Each control panel has (2) two latches that are accessed with a 3mm allen wrench. Insert allen wrench into one hole in control panel and turn counter clockwise ½ turn. Insert allen wrench into the other hole in control panel and turn counter clockwise ½ turn. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift control panel out and remove SCSI type II connectors. There are (5) connectors on the middle control panel and (1) one each on the left and right control panel. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install replacement Control Panel by pushing on SCSI connectors. Great care needs to be taken when installing center Control Panel connectors so they do not get crossed. See Illustration 2.

  1. Reverse Step 1 using 3mm allen wrench.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 or MR1 A20 CB4.

  1. Restore power to operator button panel and display assemblies by switching on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

    (Step Details)
