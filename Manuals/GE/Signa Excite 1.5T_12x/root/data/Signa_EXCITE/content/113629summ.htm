  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD !!
Fatal electrical voltages through out The MNS Cabinet.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THER MNS RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

WARNING
ELECTRICAL/RF BURN HAZARD.
RF BURNS AS A RESULT OF DISCONNECTING HELIAZ CABLES WHILE THE SYSTEM IS SCANNING.
PREVENT POSSIBLE RF BURNS WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM J3 OR J4 ON THE RF AMP BY VERIFYING THAT THE SYSTEM IS NOT MANUALLY PRESCANNING OR SCANNING. VERIFY THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE AND THE RF ENABLE SWITCH IS IN THE "OFF" POSITION.

NOTE:Property damage! Prevent coil and associated switch damage, by removing all phantoms and hardware (i.e., head coil, surface coil...) from the magnet bore.

NOTE:Possible equipment damage. Do not operate the MNS RF Amplifier with an improper load connected to its output. Doing so may permanently damage the amplifier.

    1. Verify that the system is idle and all coils have been removed from magnet bore.

    1. Remove the front cover of the RF Accessories Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the SSM, disable T/R error reporting by setting the TR switch to the DIS (disable faults) position.

1.2   MNS 4KW (CPC)- MAXIMUM POWER CALIBRATION

This section describes how to perform Head Maximum RF Power Calibration.

  1. Setting up the RF Coupler. In this section, you will assemble the Wattmeter Sensor with the correct Sensor Elements.

    1. Insert the 50KW Element into the Reflected Sensor Port. Make sure the arrow on the element points in the direction of the arrow (from Input to Output) on the Sensor. Lock Element in place using the locking tab on the sensor. (See Illustration 2)

      (Step Details)

    1. Insert the 5000W Element into the Forward Sensor Port. Make sure the arrow on the element points in the opposite direction of the arrow (from Input to Output) on the Sensor. Lock Element in place using the locking tab on the sensor. (See Illustration 2)

    1. Insert the 50KW Element into the Reflected Sensor Port. Make sure the arrow on the element points in the direction of the arrow (from Input to Output) on the Sensor. Lock Element in place using the locking tab on the sensor. (See Illustration 2). The reflected port is not used during this procedure. Placing the 50kW element into the reflected port is just a safety measure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Wattmeter and Dummy Load Setup. In this section, you will connect the Wattmeter and Dummy Load to the RF Output port of the MNS RF Amplifier.

    1. Verify scanner is in “idle”, turn off RF Enable and disconnect J14 from the back of the MNS RF Amplifier.

      (Step Details)

    1. Disconnect the RF Output cable, (“RF Output”) from the back of the MNS RF Amplifier.

    1. Connect the Input of the RF Wattmeter Sensor to the RF Output port at the back of the MNS RF Amplifier. (See Illustration 3).

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect the Output of the RF Coupler to the 35KW Dummy Load using the N Adapter in the kit. (See Illustration 4).

      (Step Details)

    1. Switch RF Enable to ON.

1.3   Setting Up The Wattmeter to Measure Forward Maximum Power

This section configures the Wattmeter to read Peak RF Power. A description of the Wattmeter is given here.

  1. Press the ON button on the Wattmeter. (If coupler and sensor is not correctly attached to the amplifier you will get a “No Sensor” indication)

  1. Select the up arrow button under Scale.

  1. Type: 5000 to set the range.

  1. Select Enter button

  1. Check the setting, by selecting the arrow button under Scale once again.

  1. Press <Enter> to exit this mode.

  1. Check the setting, by selecting the arrow button under Scale once again.

  1. Press <Enter> to exit this mode.

  1. Select the arrow button under Fwd Units make sure the scale reads “W” (or “kW “on some watt meters). If the scale reads dBm, select the arrow button under Fwd Units again, to toggle the scale to read “W” (Watts or kW kiloWatts) on the TOP, Forward Power scale. Ignore the BOTTOM, reflected Power scale value. It is not used during Maximum Power Calibration.

  1. Select the <Shift> button.

  1. Select the arrow button under Setup.

  1. Verify it reads “43”. If not, type the number “43” followed by the enter button. (Illustration 5 shows what should be displayed at this time).

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the arrow button under Type, change unit of measurement to Peak. (Illustration 5 shows what should be displayed at this time).

  1. The meter is now configured to read RF Peak Power for the 4KW MNS Maximum Output calibration.

1.5   Adjusting the MNS RF Amplifier Gain

This section adjusts the MNS RF Amplifier gain to output 4000W at TG of 200 by using The Gain Potentiometer on the back of the CPC amplifier.

  1. Locate the Gain potentiometer on the back of the amplifier.

  1. Go back to the scanner and select [Manual Prescan].

  1. Gradually increase Transmit Gain (TG) to 200. Note: If a trip occurs, allow the system to recover and set the TG to the value just prior to the trip.

  1. Go to the back of the MNS RF Amplifier. The Wattmeter should be indicating the power level being produced.

  1. 6. Slowly adjust the potentiometer until the wattmeter just reads 4000W. (The Wattmeter does have some fluctuation so you will need to finely adjust the amplifier until it seems to be averaging a power level just above 4000W).

  1. When 4000 Watts is achieved, stop [Manual Prescan].

  1. Verify scanner is in “idle”, turn off RF Enable.

  1. Disconnect the RF Watt Sensor from the RF Output Connection.

  1. Connect the Original Output Cable to the MNS RF Amplifier, and tighten the connector.
