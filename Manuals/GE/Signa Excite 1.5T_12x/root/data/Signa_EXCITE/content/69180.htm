2  Overview

Procedures for replacing alignment lights, and alignment of the laser lights. There are two (2) laser lights in the top middle assembly and one (1) each in the side assemblies.

3  Procedure

3.1  Laser Light Removal

  1. Perform Lock Out / Tag Out Process (refer to MR System Lock Out Procedures.).

  1. Remove middle control panel to allow access to power supply board. Refer to Control Panel Replacement.

  1. Pry cover off of defective laser assembly with a screwdriver. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  ALIGNMENT LIGHT LOCATIONS

  1. Remove two screws, and let laser light assembly hang free. See Illustration 2.

  1. Remove screw clamping laser in place. See Illustration 2.

  1. Using a needle nose pliers, pull retaining clip off laser/housing assembly. See Illustration 2.

  1. Slide laser light out of housing. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  LASER ALIGNMENT LIGHT ASSEMBLY

  1. Follow laser light leads to the power supply board and pull connector off.

3.2  Laser Light Replacement

  1. Plug new laser light into power supply board.

  1. Slide housing onto laser and install retaining clip. See Illustration 2.

  1. Install clamp and slotted screw onto cover plate. See Illustration 2.

  1. Install cover plate to end bell using two furnished screws. See Illustration 2.

  1. Replace center control panel.

  1. Refer to the corresponding Lockout/Tagout power restoration process.

  1. Turn on laser alignment lights and check alignment of replaced laser. If laser is not aligned, use Illustration 3 for alignment specifications. Refer to Laser Light Alignmentfor procedure on how to properly align the laser lights.
