PHOENIX PDU with Plastic breaker Cover- Sub-System Lockout

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
POSSIBLE SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH
Electrical Shock Hazard
REMOVE POWER FROM THE RF/PDU CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE ANY COMPONENTS FROM THE SYSTEM

1  Procedure

1.1   PHOENIX PDU with Plastic breaker Cover- Sub-System Lockout

  1. Power Down the computer

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the SRF or ACGD cabinet.

  1. Open Plexi-glass plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers and switch off the appropriate sub-system breakers. See Illustration Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a digital multimerter and verify that AC voltage is not present at the device that correspond to the breaker in Step 2.

  1. Close the plexi-glass plastic cover and lock and tag it out. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

1.2   PHOENIX PDU with Plastic breaker Cover- Sub-System Lockout. (SRF and ACGD)- Restoring Power

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being re-applied.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the SRF or ACGD cabinet.

  1. Remove the lock and the tag.

  1. Open plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers and switch on the appropriate sub-system breakers. See Illustration 2.

  1. Flip the circuit breaker for the Operators Workspace PC, ON.

  1. Flip the circuit breaker for the Operators Workspace Host, ON

  1. At this time refer to illustration Illustration 3 and power on any other appropriate circuit breakers, you may have turned off.

    (Step Details)

  1. Power up the PC computer

  1. Power up the Host computer

  1. Boot Signa and log into scanning level.

2  Finalization

  1. Insure all subsystems have powered up successfully

  1. Do a test phantom scan to insure proper scanning
