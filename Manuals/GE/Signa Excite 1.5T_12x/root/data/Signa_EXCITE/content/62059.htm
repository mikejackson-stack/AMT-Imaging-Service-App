2  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport, and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove upper and lower side covers (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  REMOVAL OF PATIENT TABLE COVERS

  1. Remove small cover below flipper assembly (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  INTERLOCK CABLE AND MAIN CRADLE LATCH CABLE ROUTING

  1. Remove cradle (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  PATIENT TRANSPORT CRADLE REMOVAL

  1. Loosen set screw at interlock lever.

  1. Unfasten cable end from interlock lever.

  1. Pull cable out of pin stop on flipper bar (see Illustration 4).

  1. Insert new cable through head of pin stop so that swaged end fits into hole in pin stop (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  INTERLOCK CABLE AND MAIN CRADLE LATCH CABLE ROUTING

  1. Route cable through cable casing and insert through hole in interlock lever.

  1. Loop cable twice through hole in interlock lever and secure with set screw. Tighten securely to prevent cable from slipping (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  INTERLOCK LEVER

  1. Adjust for indicated gap in down valve linkage when cradle is not in position. Use threaded cable adjuster for fine adjustments. Tighten lock nut after adjustment is completed, then cut off excess length of cable (see Illustration 6).

    Illustration 6:  DOWN VALVE LINKAGE ADJUSTMENT

  1. Replace cradle and upper and lower side covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Illustration 1).
