2  Overview

Procedure for replacement of the PAC assembly on a fixed site system.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages are Present around the Magnet Enclosure and RearPedestal electronics.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal of PAC

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

    For RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet: Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A20 CB4 at rear of RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet on System Support Module (SSM).

    (Refer to .)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

    For RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet, verify that voltage between MR1 A20 TP4 (+) (SRI+24V) and TP13 (–) (SRI RTN) is 0 Vdc.

  1. The PAC can be located under either the right or left side of the magnet enclosure. Identify which side the PAC is on by locating the patient leads, the PAC will be on that side.

  1. Remove the lower enclosure trim covers to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 1:  ACCESS TO MAGNET ENCLOSURE FRONT VIEW

    Illustration 2:  ACCESS TO MAGNET ENCLOSURE REAR VIEW

  1. Turn latches in holding tray in place. Slide tray out far enough to gain access to the PAC cables. See Illustration 3.

  1. Disconnect the six (6) cable attached to the PAC. See Illustration 3.

  1. Remove four (4) screws holding PAC to tray. See Illustration 3.

  1. Lift PAC off tray and remove.

    Illustration 3:   PAC/SRI TRAY

4.2  Installation of New PAC

  1. Place new PAC on tray.

  1. Install four (4) screws. See Illustration 3.

  1. Connect six (6) cables. See Illustration 3.

  1. Slide tray under magnet and secure latches.

  1. Replace enclosure trim panels.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 or MR1 A20 CB4.

  1. Restore power to operator button panel and display assemblies by switching on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

    RF/Pen II and RF/PDU Cabinet: Switch on circuit breaker MR1 A20 CB4 on the SSM at rear of RF/Pen II Cabinet or RF/PDU.
