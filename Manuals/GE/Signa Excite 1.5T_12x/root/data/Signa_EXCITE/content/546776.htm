2  Overview

The Longitudinal Drive is located in the rear pedestal. Two people are required to replace the Longitudinal Drive Motor due to the its strong attraction to the magnet.

3.3  Safety

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage!
Strong Magnetic Field present.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements:

  • Movement of ferrous material in the Magnet Room must follow the GE service procedure for that device. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to and from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss.

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
LONGITUDINAL DRIVE MOTOR CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, REMOVE LONGITUDINAL DRIVE MOTOR AND MOUNTING BRACKET FROM MAGNET ROOM TO INSTALL NEW LONGITUDINAL DRIVE MOTOR. (See Illustration 2 for proper process to remove motor from Magnet Room.) TWO PEOPLE ARE REQUIRED AT ALL TIMES FOR THIS PROCEDURE.

4  Procedure

  1. (For Signa EXCITE 1.5T 11.x Systems - Direction 2333500) Perform LOTO on the appropriate PDU:

    • For Teal, refer to TEAL Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

    • For Phoenix, refer to PHOENIX Power Distribution Unit (ACGD and GRFD-OpenSpeed) or PHOENIX PDU With Plastic Breaker Cover-Subsystem Lockout.

    • For Standard, refer to Standard Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

    • For Compact, refer to Compact Power Distribution Unit (PDU).

  1. (For Signa EXCITE HD 1.5T & 3.0T, 12.x Systems - Directions 5133315 & 5133330) Perform LOTO on the appropriate PDU: For Phoenix, refer to PHOENIX Power Distribution Unit (ACGD and GRFD-OpenSpeed) or PHOENIX PDU With Plastic Breaker Cover-Subsystem Lockout.

  1. (For Signa EXCITE 3.0T, 11.x Systems - Direction 2389160) Perform LOTO on the appropriate PDU:

    • PHOENIX Power Distribution Unit (ACGD and GRFD-OpenSpeed), PHOENIX PDU With Plastic Breaker Cover-Subsystem Lockout, Lockout / Tagout for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room or Lockout / Tagout for Gradient Amplifier / PDU.

    • For any other PDU, refer to Lockout / Tagout for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room or Lockout / Tagout for Gradient Amplifier / PDU.

  1. Remove the rear pedestal left side cover (as viewed from rear of magnet).

  1. Disconnect wires from the terminals on the motor.

  1. Loosen the two lower set screws (shown below) on motor shaft coupling.

  1. While one person holds onto the motor firmly with both hands, the other person should loosen and eventually remove the two long motor mounting screws shown in Illustration 1. (When removing the two long motor mounting screws from the bracket, the aluminum spacers will become free as well.)

  1. Twist the motor / bracket assembly to free the inner bracket. Insert a screwdriver in the Separation Location shown in Illustration 1and pry apart.

  1. With the last screw removed, carefully remove the motor from Magnet Room by placing the motor on the floor, against the wall farthest from the magnet.

    1. Slide the motor to the front of the Magnet Room as shown in the illustration below.

    1. Retain the two long screws and the aluminum spacers for the replacement motor.

      Illustration 2:  Removal of Motor from Magnet Room

  1. With the motor outside of the Magnet Room, remove the four mounting screws (shown below) from the motor mounting bracket. (These screws go through the mount and secure the motor by pulling it tight to the mount.)

    Illustration 3:  Motor and Bracket Separation

  1. Install the replacement motor on to the bracket, and reinstall the four mounting screws on the bracket as shown above.

  1. Carefully bring the motor and bracket assembly into the Magnet Room along the same path used to remove the assembly. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. To reinstall the motor and bracket assembly in the Rear Pedestal, ensure that a second person holds onto the motor firmly with both hands, while the first person reconnects the motor and bracket assembly to the clutch assembly with the two long screws and the aluminum spacers.

  1. Draw the four screws of the bracket up evenly, tightening the motor in its mount.

  1. Tighten the screw on the coupling to finish the motor installation.

  1. Reattach the electrical cable to the motor.

  1. Remove LOTO.

  1. Restore system power.
