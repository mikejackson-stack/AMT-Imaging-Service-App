3  Procedure

3.1   Set Up Grafidy Kit for First Axis Calibration

  1. Remove the Head Coil and holder from the cradle.

  1. Select the coils from the kit that matches the system type.

  1. Place the coil baseplate on the cradle, and configure the Grafidy coil/samples and collars appropriately for the first axis on which Grafidy will be performed. (See the Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug the Extremity/Linear Adapter into the Quad Head Coil Carriage Assembly, and connect a two-foot coaxial cable from Extremity/Linear Adapter BNC to J4 on the Pin Diode Box.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the long coaxial cable from J1 on the Pin Diode Box to a service coaxial feed-through on the Penetration Panel (Exam Room side).

    1. Route the cable through the bore of the magnet, exiting at the rear.

    1. Connect the other end of the cable to the Pin Diode Drive Test Box. (See Illustration 2.)

      (Step Details)

  1. Landmark on the centerline of the Grafidy coil baseplate, not the coils. On the front of the magnet enclosure, press LANDMARK and MOVE TO SCAN on the keypad.

  1. Verify that the switch on the Pin Diode Drive Test Box is in the Remote position.

  1. Connect a BNC cable from the TRIGGER INPUT (called PATCH PANEL INPUT on some older boxes) connector on the Pin Diode Drive Test Box to J12 (DAB out 6) on the FP I/O Board on the front of the RRF chassis.

  1. Plug in the power cord for the Pin Diode Drive Test Box.

  1. Place the power switch for the Pin Diode Drive Test Box in the On position (referred to as 1).

    (Step Details)

3.2   Prescan Checks

  1. Center the laser crosshairs on top of the coils and landmark the system, and Advance to Scan to 0 mm.

  1. Begin a new patient scan and enter geservice as the Patient ID.

  1. Enter a Patient Weight of 111 lbs.

  1. Be sure that the Patient Protocols selection is [SERVICE].

  1. Click the [Other] button and choose [Grafidy].

  1. Click [Accept].

  1. Click [Save Series].

  1. Right-click [Research Operations], and select [Setup Parms] (may result in a few second pause).

  1. Once open, enter the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Done] to close the frames window.

  1. To verify research operations, select [Display CVs] (control variables) and set the mode to 1 (default is 0). This will make prescan much faster.

  1. Click the [Accept] button.

  1. Research operations: Select [Download].

  1. Select [Manual Prescan] to begin the Center Frequency Coarse Check.

  1. Select [Window] and [Two Windows] from the Window menu. On the double window two peaks will display that should be approximately the same amplitude within 50%. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Done].

  1. Select <Auto Prescan>.

  1. If Auto Prescan fails, adjust the rotary step attenuator to 30 db (from 20 db). Occasionally, it may need to be adjusted to 10 db instead (from 20 db). Auto Prescan again until it is successful.

  1. Continue to Grafidy 3 Auto Mode and Manual Mode or Jump Back to Main Menu.
