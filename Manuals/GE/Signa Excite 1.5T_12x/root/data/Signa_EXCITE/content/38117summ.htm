Respiratory Compensation Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage. Completely remove the Quad Head Coil from the cradle before performing any body scans. Failure to do so may damage the head coil T/R network.
 
 

NOTICE
Never vent the bellows. Always connect the bellows to the PAC Assembly before stretching it. Failure to connect bellows to PAC Assembly first causes inaccurate waveforms and, therefore, inaccurate results.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. At the Operator Workspace, select the scan icon in the desktop control panel.

  1. If necessary, exit out of any previous exams by selecting [End Exam].

  1. Click on [New Pt] and enter the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Head Coil (if present) from the cradle. At keypad magnet front enclosure, press LANDMARK. Landmark near where the center of the head coil would be.

  1. Connect the respiratory bellows to the PAC Assembly.

  1. Make sure the table is at the zero (0) degree position.

  1. Press ADV TO SCAN.

  1. The message window must be clear befor continuing. If the window is not clear, click on the message bar area, and [Clear], then [Close].

  1. Prepare a scan using Respiratory Compensation protocol.

  1. Click on [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Select [Respiratory] from the Gating Controls Menu. The respiratory wavefrom appears below the BPM waveform on the LCD Panel Display.

    (Step Details)

  1. There is no need to start the scan.

  1. Take the respiratory bellows in both hands and gently pull on either end.

  1. Pull the bellows by hand duplicating the motion of typical regular breathing. If the bellows and respiratory compensation software is working properly the LED's should follow the actions of the bellows. (Verify that respiratory Bar Graph on PAC assembly is registering rhythmic change of bellows). If it does not, proceed troubleshoot the bellows (for tears or holes), connections and then the PAC assembly is self for problems.

2  Finalization

  1. Restore the system to patient scanning condition.

  1. Do a check scan to insure proper operation.
