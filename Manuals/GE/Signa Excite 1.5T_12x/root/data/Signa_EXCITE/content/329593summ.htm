Replacement of Cable Take-up

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal of Cable Take-Up

  1. Run LPCA to the rear of the magnet and remove the cover to the LPCA.

  1. Position the LPCA as shown in Illustration 1 so there is access to the screws that secure the black cable take-up assembly to the LPCA and Bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. For sites with RFS cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled PHPS/SSM on the PDU. For sites with RF cabinet and Systems cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled SSM.

  1. Remove all cabling at the LPCA for the cable take-up to be replaced.

  1. Remove all cabling at the rear pedestal (16 channel switch, etc) for the cable take-up to be replaced.

  1. Remove the two Phillips head screws that secure the cable take-up to the LPCA. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the two Phillips head screws that secure the cable take-up to the Bridge. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the cable take-up assembly.

1.2   Installation of New Cable take-up

  1. Place replacement cable take-up in position.

  1. Connect all cables to the LPCA and Rear Pedestal.

    (Step Details)

  1. . Apply two drops of LockTite to each screw then connect the four screws that secure take-up assembly to Bridge and LPCA.

  1. Remove Lock-out Tag-out and restore power.

  1. Move LPCA to the rear of the magnet and install LPCA cover.

2  Finalization

  1. Run MCR2 tool to verify cabling.
