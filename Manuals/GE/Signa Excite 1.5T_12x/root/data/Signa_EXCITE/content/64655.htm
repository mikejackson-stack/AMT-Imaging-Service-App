2  Overview

The Home Limit Switch Board (MG3 A1 A1) is located in the rear pedestal inside of the Magnet I/F #1 (MG3 A1). See Illustration 1.

Illustration 1:  LOCATION OF MAGNET I/F #1

3  Procedure

  1. Switch off MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

  1. Lock out and tag out MR1 A14 CB6 using appropriate procedure. (Refer to .)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP1 (+) and TP14 (–) is 0 Vdc.

  1. Remove rear pedestal right side cover (as viewed from rear of magnet).

  1. Remove screws from cover of Magnet I/F #1.

  1. Disconnect sensor plug from J2 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Disconnect harness from J3 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Replace failed board.

  1. Connect harness to J3 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Connect sensor plug from J2 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Install screws onto cover of Magnet I/F #1.

  1. Install rear pedestal right side cover.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet on MEPS module.

  1. Switch on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6.

  1. Verify that cradle returns to Home position, and that longitudinal drive motor has stopped trying to drive the cradle.

No finalization steps.
