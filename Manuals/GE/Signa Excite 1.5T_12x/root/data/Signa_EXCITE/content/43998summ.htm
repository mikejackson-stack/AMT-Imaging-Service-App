1.2   On-Screen Controls

This section describes the adjustments available for the OSM controls.

  1. Brightness and Contrast

    1. Brightness: Adjusts the overall image and background screen brightness.

    1. Contrast: Adjusts the image brightness in relation to the background.

    1. Auto Adjust Contrast: Adjusts the image displayed for non-standard video inputs.

  1. Auto Adjust

    (Step Details)

  1. Position Controls

    1. “H” Position: Controls Horizontal Image Position within the display area of the LCD.

    1. “V” Position: Controls Vertical Image Position within the display area of the LCD.

      (Step Details)

  1. Image Adjust Controls

    1. “H” Size: Adjusts the horizontal size by increasing or decreasing this setting.

    1. Fine: Improves focus, clarity, and image stability by increasing or decreasing the Fine setting.

    1. Auto Adjust Coarse: Automatically adjusts the Coarse setting.

  1. AccuColor® Control System

    1. Five color presets select the desired color setting. This setting must be set to selection 1 - 9300. If a setting is adjusted, the name of the setting will change to Custom.

    1. R, B, G: Increases or decreases the color depending upon which is selected. The change in color will appear on screen and the increase or decrease will be shown by the color bars.
