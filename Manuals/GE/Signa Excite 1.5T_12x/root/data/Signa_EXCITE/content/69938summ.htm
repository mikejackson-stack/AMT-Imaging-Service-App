Front End Bell Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on magnet enclosure power supply (MEPS) module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove bridge. See Bridge Removal.

  1. Remove Control Panel.

  1. Remove front enclosure covers. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift open (2) two latches connecting front end bell and rf tube. See Illustration 2.

  1. Remove M6 allen cap screw and phenolic spacer. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove (2) two 17mm nuts holding front end bell on. See Illustration 2.

  1. Remove front end bell by pulling top out past studs, then lifting vertically to clear end bell inserts, then pulling out the rest of the way.

  1. Reverse procedure to reinstall new endbell. Be sure to follow gap specifications in Step 11.

  1. All enclosure component seams and gaps visible to the operator and patient shall be less than 0.25 inches (6.35 mm). Exceptions may be made in areas where esthetic visual enhancement of the component gap is required by industrial design.

    (Step Details)
