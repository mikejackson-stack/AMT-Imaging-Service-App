3  Procedure

3.1  Introduction

The Universal Lift Hoist is assembled from parts stored in the Universal Lift Hoist Kit. Lift attachments specially designed for the 1.5T SRF Cabinet are found in the outer most compartments of the lift kit. The lift brackets are found in the RF amplifier FRU container

3.2  Assembly Procedural Steps

DANGER
cabinet tip hazard!
possible personal injury!
make sure that the aluminum anti tip legs are stored in the rear of the cabinet are installed on either side of the base of the cabinet before attempting to remove an rf amplifier.

  1. Using the screws included with the anti tip legs, secure the aluminum anti tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the SRF Cabinet. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Installing Anti Tip Legs on Sides of Cabinet

  1. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections and place them on a flat surface for assembly. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Steel Rail Sections, Hex Bolts, and Hex Nuts

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten then together with 4.75” X 1/2” caps crews and hex nuts

  1. Use the crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the rail assembly. See Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Hoist Top Rail Parts with Elevating Brackets

  1. Remove the front and rear covers of the RF/PDU or SRF Cabinet

  1. Place the Steel Rail and elevating bracket assembly at the top of the RF/PDU or SRF Cabinet. Center the Rail Assembly on the top of the RF Amplifier. See Illustration 4

    Illustration 4:  Hoist Parts Rear View Assembled

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. See Illustration 5

    Illustration 5:  Hoist Assembly

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail. Insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 6

    Illustration 6:  Placement of Hoist Assembly Into Front End of Steel Rail Assembly

  1. Select the proper lift brackets for the Analogic RF Amplifier and attach to side of amplifier, then attach the Hoist Assembly to the lift bracket. See Illustration 7

    Illustration 7:  Attaching Lift Bracket To Hoist Assembly

  1. 11. The Universal Lift Hoist is now ready for operation. Illustration 8 shows the Universal Lift Hoist being used to remove a RF Amplifier Module from an RF/PDU Cabinet. Although the location of the RF components in the SRF Cabinet is different, the removal and installation process is the same as for the RF/PDU Cabinet.

    Illustration 8:  Using the Universal Lift Hoist To Remove The Upper RF AMP
