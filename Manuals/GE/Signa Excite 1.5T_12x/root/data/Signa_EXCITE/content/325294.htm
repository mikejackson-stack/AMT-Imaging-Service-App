3  Procedure

3.1  HO Shim Supply Power Down and Lockout/Tagout Procedure

  1. Prepare for the shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Press the power switch at the back of the HO Shim Supply

  1. Remove the power cord from the plug it is plugged into

  1. Using DMM, measure voltage at plug to ensure no power present

  1. Lock out the power plug.

3.2  HO Shim Filter Replacement

  1. Disconnect the HO Shim output cable from equipment and magnet room sides of filter, SeeIllustration 1

    Illustration 1:  HO Shim Filter Replacement

  1. Remove nuts from HO Shim filter (located on magnet room side).

  1. Pull original filter out from equipment room side

  1. Install new filter references steps 1-3.

  1. Proceed to finalization.

3.3  HO Shim Supply Replacement (RRI Supply Only)

NOTE:HO Shim Filter Replacement only applies to the RRI supply as the weight requires the Hoist Kit when only 1 FE is present. Illustration X-X is the RRI Supply

  1. Using the screws included with the anti-tip legs, secure the aluminum anti-tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the Cabinet. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:   HO Shim Supply Replacement (RRI Supply Only)

  1. Remove the front cover and the top bracket from the cabinet.

    Illustration 3:  Installing Anti Tip Legs on Sides of cabinet

  1. Open the rear cabinet door

  1. Remove the four (4) screws that attach the fan module to the TAC cabinet. Set the fan module in the cabinet.

  1. Locate and open the Universal Lift Hoist Kit.

NOTE:The Universal Lift Hoist is assembled from parts stored in the Universal Lift Hoist Kit.

  1. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections and place them on a flat surface for assembly. See Illustration 3

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten them together with 4.75” X 1/2” caps crews and hex nuts

    Illustration 4:  Steel rail sections, Hex Bolts and Nuts

  1. Use the crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws.

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the rail assembly. See Illustration 5

    Illustration 5:  Hoist Top rail parts with elevating brackets

  1. Center the Steel Rail and elevating bracket assembly at the top of TAC Cabinet, so the hoist assembly will face the front of the cabinet. See Illustration 6.

  1. Tighten the bolts on the Steel Rail to secure it to the top of the cabinet.

    Illustration 6:  Hoist Installed on TAC Cabinet

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. See Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Hoist Assembly

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail. Insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 8.

  1. Attach the hoist side brackets to the resistive shim supply. See Illustration 10

  1. Using the above steps as reference, attach the side brackets to the new supply and hoist it up into the cabinet

  1. Install the four (4) bolts that attach the resistive shim supply to the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Attach the cables removed in step 15.

  1. Remove the hoist assembly from front rail.

  1. Loosen the bolts that secure the Steel Rail to the top of the cabinet.

3.4  HO Shim Supply Replacement (NAVSupply Only)

NOTE:This section is only for the replacement of the HO Shim Supply (NAV version). This supply is light enough to replace without the use of the Hoist Kit. See Illustration X-X for NAV supply.

  1. Remove all cables that attach to the back of the supply. See Illustration xx below for details.

    Illustration 12:  HO Shim Supply Replacement (NAVSupply Only

    Illustration 13:  HO Shim Supply Replacement (NAVSupply Only) _ 2

  1. Remove 4 screws that attach the shim supply to the cabinet

  1. Carefully slide out and remove the shim supply.

  1. Install the new supply and secure with 4 screws

  1. Attach all cables/connectors removed in Step 1

  1. Proceed to Finalization, Step 1

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from power cable to shim supply.

  1. Plug in shim supply power cable and turn on power switch at back of supply

  1. Reset TPS

  1. If NAV supply is used, proceed to HO Shim Supply Functional Checks and run shim coil continuity check.

  1. If RRI supply is used, proceed to HO Shim Functional Check and check out at least 1 FOV.

  1. Run a Head or Body scan to make sure system is ok.
