Replacing BRM-D (Short) RF Body Coil

1  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

CAUTION
Dangerous Work Area!
Use of heavy equipment for coil removal and replacement presents hazards to all personnel in the area.

  • Set up restricted barriers and signs limiting access to the work area.

  • Notify all affected personnel in the area prior to starting the service activity. Review safety procedures and restricted areas for the job.

3  Procedure

3.1   Unpacking BRM-D (Short) RF Body Coil

  1. A new Cardiac RF body coil is packed in a shipping container (trunk) that is strapped to a pallet. Unstrap the shipping container from the pallet and remove the RF body coil from the trunk. (The process is reversed when preparing the defective RF body coil for return.)

  1. See Illustration 1 through Illustration 3 to remove the replacement coil.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Replacing BRM-D (Short) RF Body Coil

  1. Remove the RF Body coil from the CRM without using the RF tracks, which are too thick to use. Use pieces of smooth, stiff material (sheets of film work well) to easily slide the RF coil out of the Gradient Coil. See Illustration 4 through Illustration 14 for removing and replacing the BRM-D RF Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Complete remaining installation steps per Gradient Coil Installation Procedure.

  1. Return the failed RF coil to the shipping crate, reversing the steps used to remove the replacement coil in Unpacking BRM-D (Short) RF Body Coil.

4  Finalization

  1. Install all removed components.

  1. Clean up the site in preparation for patient scans.

  1. Perform TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.
