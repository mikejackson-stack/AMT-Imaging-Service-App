NOTICE
To prevent possible damage to chip capacitors under the RF Body Coil blanket, keep RF Body Coil straight during removal (avoid excessive up/down or tilting sideways).

Further caution must be taken for a CX magnet with an old BRM mounting that has Al plates (2213168, 2213167) with gussets. These gussets extend 10 mm into the RF tube pass, and may damage the coil. If necessary, replace the old Al brackets with the new brackets (2213168-3, 2213167-3) without a gusset. (See Gradient Coil Replacement Preparation.)
