Temperature Sensor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
STRONG MAGNETIC FIELD. PERSONAL INJURY MAY OCCUR!
FERROUS MATERIALS CAN BECOME DANGEROUS PROJECTILES IN THE PRESENCE OF THE MAGNETIC FIELD PRODUCED BY THE SIGNA MAGNET.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

(For 11.x:) Be sure that the Temperature Sensor Cable Kit has all the required contents listed in Temperature Sensor Cable Kit.

-

(For 12.x and 14.x BRM:) Temperature sensor cable replacement will require additional tie-wraps to replace those cut during removal. Two-part epoxy will be required to secure the new sensors to the BRM end bell. The following parts are available through GPO stock: 46-282264P1 Epoxy, 46-320187P1 Nozzle, and 2298033 Applicator.

3  Procedure

3.1   BRM Installations Only

This procedure is for BRM installations only. For TRM enclosures, see TRM Installations.

  1. Remove the front split bridge. (See Illustration 1 for location and Bridge Removal for the removal procedure.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Each control panel has one latch accessed with a 3 mm Allen wrench. Insert Allen wrench into one hole in the control panel, and turn counterclockwise 1/2 turn.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift control panel out, and remove SCSI Type II connectors. (There are five connectors on the middle control panel and one each on the left and right control panel. See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front enclosure covers. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift to open the two latches connecting the front end bell and RF tube. (See Illustration 5.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two 17 mm nuts holding the front end bell on. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Remove the front end bell by pulling the top out past the studs, lifting vertically to clear the end bell inserts, then pulling the top out the rest of the way.

  1. Locate the bore temperature sensor on the front end bell. (See Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the tape that covers the sensor.

  1. Remove the sensor by breaking the epoxy. (A non open-flame heat gun may be used to soften the epoxy for easier removal.)

  1. Pull the sensor out by the cable from the felt lining. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Thread the new cable through the felt lining.

  1. Insert the sensor elements into the end bell holes, and apply the 5-minute epoxy around the sensor elements.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use tie-wraps to secure the new cable to the cable mounts.

  1. Seal the felt liner.

  1. Reattach the front end bell.

  1. Reconnect the SCSI cables to the control panels.

  1. Reinsert the control panels into the enclosure.

3.2   TRM Installations

  1. Remove the front split bridge. (See Illustration 7 for location.) For TRM 11.x and 12.x, see 16 Channel Bridge Replacement. For TRM 14.x, see Bridge Removal and Replacement.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pry the cover strap from the surface of the end bell and RF tube. Removal of the strap will reveal the existing temperature sensor cable. (See Illustration 8.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the right side bore light end cap to expose the temperature sensor installed adjacent to the RF body coil, adjacent to the bore light. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the sensor and cable from the end bell groove and RF body coil. (See Illustration 9.)

  1. Install the replacement temperature sensor cable, and reinstall the end cap and cover strap.
