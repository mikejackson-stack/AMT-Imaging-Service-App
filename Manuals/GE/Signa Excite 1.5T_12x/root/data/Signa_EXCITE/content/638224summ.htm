Check CAB Filter and Condenser

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE CAB BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES.
PERFORM LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements. DO THIS BY SECURING THE CAB POWER CABLE.

1  Procedure

1.1   CHECK CAB AIR FILTER

  1. Disconnect CAB power cable from the CAB-MDP.

  1. LOTO the CAB power cable.

  1. Remove the filter access panel by removing the 4 screws holding the filter access panel in place. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the nut on the filter assembly. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the filter assembly cover.

  1. Remove and check the air filter. Wash the filter or use a brush to clean the air filter if necessary. If the filter is damaged, order a replacement air filter. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-install the air filter.

  1. Re-install the air filter assembly cover.

  1. Re-install the air filter access panel.

1.2   CHECK CAB CONDENSER COILS

  1. Check the CAB condenser coils for dirt or damage. The CAB condenser coils can be observed by looking through the rear louvre. Use a flashlight if necessary. See Illustration 4

    (Step Details)

  1. If the condenser is dirty, flush with water and let dry, or vacuum the dust.
