3  Procedure

3.1  Unpacking CRM RF Body Coil

  1. A new Cardiac RF body coil is packed in a shipping container (trunk) that is strapped to a pallet. Unstrap the shipping container from the pallet, and remove the RF body coil from the trunk. (The process is reversed when preparing the defective RF body coil for return.)

  1. SeeIllustration 1 through Illustration 3 to remove the replacement coil.

    Illustration 1:  Tilt RF Coil Case and Roll Off Pallet

    Illustration 2:  Move RF Coil Case to Final Location and Set Case Horizontally

    Illustration 3:  Open RF Coil Case and Remove New Coil

3.2  Replacing CRM RF Body Coil

  1. Remove the RF Body coil from the CRM without using the RF tracks, which are too thick to use. Use pieces of smooth, stiff material (sheets of film work well) to easily slide the RF coil out of the Gradient Coil. SeeIllustration 4 throughIllustration 9 for removing and replacing the CRM RF Body Coil.

    Illustration 5:  Loosening CRM RF Body Coil Detail

    NOTE:To prevent possible damage to chip capacitors under the RF Body Coil blanket, keep the RF Body Coil straight during removal. Avoid excessive up/down or tilting sideways.

    Illustration 6:   Removing CRM RF Body Coil

    Illustration 7:  Installing CRM RF Body Coil

    Illustration 8:  Securing CRM RF Body Coil Overview

    Illustration 9:  Securing CRM RF Body Coil Detail

  1. Complete remaining installation steps per Gradient Coil Installation Procedure.

  1. Return the failed RF Coil to the shipping crate, reversing the steps used to remove the replacement coil in Unpacking CRM RF Body Coil.

4  Finalization

  1. Install all removed components.

  1. Clean up the site in preparation for patient scans.

  1. PerformTR Dynamic Disable Calibration.
