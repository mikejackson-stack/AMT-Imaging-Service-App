Installing Dynamic Disable Switch Board

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
POSSIBLE +500 (1.0T) OR +1000 (1.5T) VOLT POTENTIAL TO THE DYNAMIC DISABLE SWITCH BOARD.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE RF SYSTEM CONTROLLER MODULE.

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
POSSIBLE +500 to 1000 VOLT POTENTIAL for 3.0T Systems TO THE DYNAMIC DISABLE SWITCH BOARD.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE RF Driver MODULE.

1  Procedure

  1. Turn Driver Module Power off with MR1A14CB6.

  1. Shut down power to the System cabinet by switching OFF the System cabinet breaker at the PDU.

  1. Lock out Tag out front plate of PDU. (Refer to OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.)

  1. Verify that power to the system Cabinet is off by checking the fans and LEDs on the MGD chassis, the RRF chassis, and the Driver module.

  1. Reinstall Dynamic Disable Switch (DDS) Board into Body Coil Assembly. Tighten screw hand tight.

  1. Install cover over DDS Board.

  1. Reassemble removed magnet enclosure pieses.

  1. Reconnect J72, J73, J75 and J76 to the Penetration Panel (magnet enclosure side).

  1. Remove lockout and tag from ACGD/PDU Cabinet.

  1. Run TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Turn System cabinet Power on and Driver module.

2  Finalization

  1. Check body and head SNR. Refer to procedure for Signal To Noise Check.
