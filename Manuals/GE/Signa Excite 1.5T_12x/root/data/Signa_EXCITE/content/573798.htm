2  Overview

Download time can be up to 10 minutes on a replacement GP3 module. If the software loaded on the Signa system does not match the firmware loaded on the GP3 Board received as a replacement, there will be a one-time lengthy download of current firmware to the GP3. During this download the IPG will read “GP Load”. Please be patient.

3.3  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
POSSIBLE 800 VOLTS ON CAPACITORS.
WAIT AT LEAST 5 MINUTES AFTER UNIT HAS BEEN TURNED OFF BEFORE SERVICING. TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal Procedure

  1. Power down the Gradient Cabinet by placing the Gradient 208V Control Power Breaker and the Gradient 420 V Power Breaker in the OFF position on the Front Panel of the PDU. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Breaker Locations

  1. Lock out the breaker panel and tag it.

  1. Wait at least 5 minutes after the Gradient Cabinet has been turned off in order for the power on the capacitors to dissipate.

  1. After power to the Gradient Cabinet Power has been removed, take a Digital Multi-meter and set it to its highest AC voltage range.

  1. On the terminal block in the rear of the cabinet of the Gradient Amplifier-PS, measure between the ground connection and the incoming power connection at the black wire (see Illustration 2). Repeat this measurement between the ground connection and the red wire, and between the ground connection and the white wire. A measurement of 0 VAC indicates that the 420V incoming power has been successfully removed.

    Illustration 2:  Verifying Removal of Power

  1. Disconnect all cables from the rear of the GP3 module. Make sure the power cord is removed, see Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Location of Power Cord on the Rear of the GP3

  1. Remove four (4) Phillips head screws from the outer corners of the GP3 front panel. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  GP3 Assembly Showing Screw Locations

  1. Remove the module completely from the cabinet.

4.2  Replacement

  1. Remove the replacement GP3 from shipping box and place in cabinet in the GP3 location.

  1. Connect all cables at the rear of the module.

  1. Make sure the Power Cord at the rear of the module is connected. See Illustration 3.

  1. Place the four (4) Phillips head screws in the outer corners of the GP3 front panel and secure GP3 in the cabinet. See Illustration 4.
