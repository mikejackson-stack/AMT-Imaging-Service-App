Coil Filter Panel Connections

The coil filter panel with a Dynamic Disable and Inductive Drive Filter (PP1 A11) is standard with Signa Advantage 1.5T, 1.0T, and 0.5T systems. (See Illustration 1). Upgraded systems will be brought to this configuration. Systems with Multicoil option have a Multicoil Bias Filter (PP1-A16), a Multicoil Switching Filter (PP1-A14), and a Multicoil Receive Select Switch (PP1-A14). (See Illustration 2 for 1.5T systems with Multicoil option; see Illustration 3 for 0.5T systems with Multicoil option). Signa Advantage 1.5T systems with Spectroscopy option have a Spectro Receiver 20db Gain Block (PP1 A13). (See Illustration 2).
