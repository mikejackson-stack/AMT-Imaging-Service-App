Secondary Cradle Latch Actuating Cable

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport, and move from exam room (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove upper and lower side covers (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cradle (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Unfasten cable end from end of push rod (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull cable out of pin housing (see Illustration 5).

  1. Insert new cable through cable casing (see Illustration 5).

  1. Route cable through cable casing (see Illustration 5).

  1. Loop cable twice through hole in push rod and secure with set screw. Tighten securely to prevent cable from slipping (see Illustration 5).

  1. Adjust cable so that when push rod is pushed in 3/8 inch (9.5 mm); pin stop is flush with pin housing. Use threaded cable adjuster for fine adjustment. Tighten lock nut after adjustment is complete, then cut off excess length of cable (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Return patient transport to scan room.

  1. Dock patient transport.

  1. Verify that release pin is flush to 1/16 inch (1.6 mm) below flush when table is docked.

  1. Replace cradle and upper and lower side covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers, and position toward front of table (see Illustration 2).
