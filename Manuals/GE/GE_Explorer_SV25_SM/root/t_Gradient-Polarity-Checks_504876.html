About this task

Reversal of the polarity of a single gradient causes the right/left or top/bottom reversal of the images from two axes, and reversal of the offsets for one axis. Incorrect polarity of all of the gradients causes the images from all planes to be upside down and backward, and causes the system to scan offset images in the opposite direction from those commanded for all axes.

Note:

The values mentioned in this procedure are related to the physical x, y, and z gradient amplifiers, respectively. The values for setting x-, y-, and z-gradient to obtain 1 gauss/cm, respectfully, are in the system configuration file.

Last Update09/28/2004

Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, select the SCAN icon
  2. Click on New Pt to enable setting of a new landmark.
  3. the following:

    Id: geserviceEnter

    Name: gradient polarityEnter

    Weight (Lb.): 111Enter

  12. For TwinSpeed, select GradMode=WHOLE.
  13. Click on Save Series
  14. In the RX MANAGER window, click on Prepare to Scan.
  15. Click on Scan (system Auto Prescans first).
  16. When the axial image displays, verify that the A appears in the lower-middle of the image. The image should have the GE logo in the upper right-hand corner (see Figure 3).
    Note:

    Left/right reversal is caused by incorrect polarity of the X gradient. Top/bottom reversal is caused by improper Y gradient polarity.

  17. For TwinSpeed, repeat scans for GradMode=ZOOM.
  18. In the RX MANAGER window, click on End Exam to exit.

Finalization

  1. Put away any phantoms and any other tools used for the test.
  2. Return to Geometry Verification Start Page or Go back to Software Configuration Checks
