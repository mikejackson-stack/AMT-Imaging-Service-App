Topic ID: id_SL112186-1074912

Overview

Procedure

This document describes how to check the maximum volume and minimum volume of the Patient’s voice, in the magnet bore. Also, describes how to check the maximum volume of Operator's voice.

  • Operator Volume- controls the speaker volume in the Bore, (Operator’s voice).

  • Patient Volume- controls the volume of the speaker in the keyboard, (Patient’s voice).

Topic ID: id_SL112189-1074912

Maximum Keyboard Speaker Volume Check (Patient’s Voice)

Procedure

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control at maximum, by rotating the volume wheel UP with your finger, (Clockwise).
  2. One-person talks into the patient microphone in the magnet bore. The second person check that the desired maximum volume can be heard at the keyboard speaker.
Topic ID: id_SL112192-1074912

Minimum Keyboard Speaker Volume Check (Patient’s Voice)

About this task

This section checks the minimum volume of the Patient’s voice, in the magnet bore. The Operator benefits from this adjustment.

Procedure

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control at minimum, by rotating the volume wheel down with your finger. (counterclockwise).
  2. One person should listen at the keyboard speaker (Patient’s Voice). The other person talks into the patient microphone in the magnet bore. The person at the keyboard checks that the desired minimum volume can be heard at the keyboard speaker.
  3. Finally, adjust the patient volume control so that the appropriate voice can be heard from the keyboard speaker.
Topic ID: id_SL112195-1074912

Maximum Patient Speaker Volume (Operator’s Voice)

Procedure

  1. At the Operator Workspace Keyboard, set the patient volume control at maximum by rotating the Operators volume wheel UP with your finger, (Clockwise).
  2. One person listens at the patient speaker. The other person talks into the keyboard microphone. Confirm that the desired maximum volume can be heard at the patient speaker.
  3. Finally, adjust the operator volume control so that the appropriate voice can be heard from the patient speaker.
