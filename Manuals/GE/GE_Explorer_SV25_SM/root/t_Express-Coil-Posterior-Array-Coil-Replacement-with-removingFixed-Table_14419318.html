Observe Anti-Static Precautions.

About this task

This instruction provides procedure to disassemble and assembly cradle from tabletop on the SV fixed table.

Topic ID: id_SL3092355-1097918

Remove Fixed Table

Procedure

  1. System Power must be turned OFF. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker .
  2. Notice
    Wear the static electricity prevention wrist band when accessing the cables below cradle not to damage the electronics module such as Mega SW.
    Remove FRP bottom covers of Fixed Table.
    1. Remove the Rubber cap and then screws of the FRP bottom cover on the near the Gantry end of the Table.
    2. Similarly remove the cap and screws of the FRP bottom cover on the rear end of the Table.
    3. Remove the right Bottom FRP Cover. Similarly remove the left Bottom FRP cover on the other side
    4. Now remove the Dock FRP Cover Locking screws and then the Dock FRP cover carefully.
    5. Keep both Bottom FRP Covers and Dock FRP covers aside safely.
  3. Disconnect cables.
    1. Disconnect the Sensor cable and Actuator Power supply cable from the Power supply extension cable from Control box.
    2. Disconnect the Earthing cables from the Dock Frame.
    3. Disconnect the Control Board Cable-2 (TEB Cable) Assembly from the connector box.
    4. Disconnect the Control Board Cable (TEB Cable) Assembly from the connector box.
  4. Remove the screws and nuts connecting the Table and the Dock frame.
  5. Loosen four Leveling pads.
  6. Move the Table.
Topic ID: id_SL2983910-1097918

Cradle Disassembly Procedure:

Procedure

  1. Press White Delrin in one hand & pull the cradle out by holding at front cradle section groove.
  2. Press the White delirin & simultaneously pull cradle out about 20mm from tabletop.
  3. Pull cradle out about 450mm from tabletop.
  4. On the other end, cradle will be out of tabletop side guide.
  5. Lift the cradle at the handle by 1 hand and use the other hand to insert the foam
    Note:

    Foam is stored in Cart during installation.

  6. Notice
    Wear the static electricity prevention wrist band when accessing the cables below cradle not to damage the electronics module such as Mega SW.
    Access to the Connectors below the Cradle.
  7. If there is no cable clamp: Remove RF & DC connector from the socket mounted on the cradle:
  8. Remove two M3 screw with the help of flat screwdriver and remove cable track fixed on the cradle.
  9. If there is cable clamp: Remove RF & DC connector from the socket mounted on the cradle:
  10. Remove two M3 screw with the help of flat screwdriver and remove cable track and Clamp Top fixed on the cradle:
  11. Now cradle is independent of Table top.
  12. Lift the cradle by both hand and place cradle on flat surface.
Topic ID: id_SL2908775-1097918

Cradle Assembly procedure:

About this task

Procedure

  1. Check that the pad is still on the table
  2. Lift the new cradle and put it back onto the Table. One person needs to sustain the cradle head end.
  3. Notice
    Wear the static electricity prevention wrist band when accessing the cables below cradle not to damage the electronics module such as Mega SW.
    Replacement procedure for Revised RF Cable, DC Cable and DC Cable clamp assy.

    Get below mentioned FRU part as per replacement requirement

  4. Assemble DC cable Clamp bottom to Cable track end link.
  5. Fasten these parts to cradle section with M3 screw.
  6. Connect RF Cable and DC cable to cradle board, ensure both the cable are placed flat one over the other.
  7. Snap the Cable track along with RF Cable and DC cable to Cable track end link.
  8. Assemble DC cable Clamp top to DC cable Clamp bottom with M3 screw.
  9. Notice
    DO not use cable tie to hold cable assy, cable tie is replaced by Clamp assy
    Remove PAD and restore the Cradle position.
  10. Push back the cradle into the table top.
  11. Push back the cradle until it locks into table top. Now cradle is secured into the tabletop.
  12. In case if cradle is interfering or not locking with table top Side guide, for adjustment refer to Cradle Guide Rail Latch Adjustment .
  13. Restore the Table.
  14. Restore Foam to Cart.
  15. Paste PA coil identification label , refer to PA coil identification label Replacement procedure

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Turn On the System Power. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker.
  2. Perform Express Coil MCQA Test .
