About this task

This document covers PHOENIX Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (ACGD and GRFD- OpenSpeed)- Lockout and PHOENIX Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (ACGD and GRFD- OpenSpeed) - Restoring Power. Prior to performing work on the System Cabinet electronics (except PDU) and electronics in Magnet Room, the steps in this Lock Out Tag Out Procedure must be performed by a LOTO Authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all steps in this procedure will ensure a safe environment when working on these parts.

Power Down the computer subsystems and perform LOTO for GOC

Procedure

  1. From Common Service Desktop, click 'Utility / VRE / Soft Power Off' to turn off the VRE.
    Note:

    It is not necessary to perform Software Power OFF for VRE from HDE14.0_M4 software.

  2. At the background of desktop, right click and select 'Service Tool / System Shutdown' to shutdown the system.
  3. Select OK to confirm the shutdown.
  4. Wait for the system to indicate on the monitor that it is safe to power off the computer before proceeding. (This usually takes about 90 seconds before this message is seen.)
  5. Open plastic Cover. Remove power to the Operators Workspace by shutting off the GOC breakers on the front of the PDU. See Figure 3.
  6. Lock and Tag the Breaker Panel on the PDU.
Topic ID: id_SL28617-550055

HDe 1.5T System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker - Lockout

Procedure

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, press the Power Off button.
  2. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green, OFF (O) position.
  3. Lock Out Tag Out the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker.
    1. Press on the left-pointing arrow on the end of the handle to expose the location where the lock and tag can be placed.
    2. Lock and tag out the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker.
Topic ID: id_SL28620-550055

HDe 1.5T System Cabinet PDU - Restoring Power

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being re-applied.
  2. Remove the Lock and the Tag from the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker.
  3. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the Red, 1 ONposition.
  4. Flip the circuit breaker for the Operators GOC ON.
  5. Open plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers and power on any other appropriate circuit breakers, you may have turned off.
  6. Boot Signa and log into scanning level
Topic ID: id_14976103

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Insure all subsystems have powered up successfully
  2. Do a test phantom scan to insure proper scanning
