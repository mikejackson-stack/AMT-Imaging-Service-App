About this task

The 3.0T 32-channel body array coil (PN 5391469) consists of anterior coil (PN 5391468) and posterior coil (PN 5392802). It is recommended that you perform MCQA scans using the full mode when both anterior and posterior coil are available.

This module provides setup instructions for:

  • Full Mode MCQA Setup

  • Anterior Only Mode MCQA Setup

  • Posterior Only Mode MCQA Setup

The 3.0T body array coil MCQA test uses the 3.0T phantoms, which are pink.

Note:

Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

Full Mode MCQA Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the 3.0T body array posterior coil on the table.
  2. Place the body array lower phantom positioner on the side of the posterior coil. Confirm that the edge of the phantom position aligns with the edge of the table and is in contact with the posterior coil.
  3. Connect the posterior coil to the P2 connector.
  4. Place the lower body array phantom positioner on the posterior coil. Center the lower phantom positioner L/R on the posterior coil. Place the unified phantoms inside the lower positioner. Place the upper body array phantom positioner. Make sure the side with the wider edge foam points towards the LPCA and magnet side.
  5. Place the anterior coil on top of the upper phantom positioner. Be sure the anterior is centered directly over the posterior coil. Fasten the straps of the anterior and posterior coil. Plug the anterior coil in connector P1 on the LPCA.
  6. Landmark at the center cross-hair of the anterior coil and advance to scan.
  7. Run the MCQA Tool.

    Confirm that the correct coil ID is displayed in the Current Coil field: 3.0T_DV_32chBodyArray_Coil_by_GE_Unified. Enter the coil serial number and press Start.

Anterior Only Mode MCQA Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the body array phantom positioner (lower) on the table.
  2. Center the phantom positioner left/right on the patient table. Confirm that the edge of the phantom positioner (upper) aligns with the edge of the table and touches the phantom positioner lower.
  3. Place the phantoms inside the bottom positioner. Make sure the side with the wider edge foam points towards the LPCA and magnet side.
  4. Place the body array phantom positioner (upper) on the phantoms.
  5. Place the body array anterior above the phantom positioner (upper) and route the anterior coil cable along the edge of the table.
  6. Plug the connector into P1.
  7. Landmark at the center cross-hair of the anterior coil Figure 8 and advance to scan.
  8. Run MCQA Tool . Confirm that the correct coil ID is displayed in the Current Coil field: 3.0T_DV_32chBodyArray_AnteriorCoil_by_GE_Unified. Enter the coil serial number, and press Start.

Posterior Only Mode MCQA Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the 3.0T body array posterior coil with the nest pad on the table.
  2. Place the body array phantom positioner (lower) on the side of the posterior coil. Ensure that the edge of the phantom positioner aligns with the edge of the table and is in contact with the posterior coil as shown.
  3. Connect the posterior coil to P2 connector on the LPCA.
  4. Place the lower phantom positioner on the posterior coil.
  5. Center the phantom positioner L/R on the posterior coil.
  6. Place the unified phantoms inside the bottom positioner.
  7. Place the upper phantom positioner. Confirm that the side with the wider edge foam points towards the LPCA and magnet side.
  8. Landmark at the center of the phantom positioner. Press Advance to scan.
  9. Run MCQA Tool. Confirm that the correct coil ID is selected in the Current Coil field: 3.0T_DV_32chBodyArray_PosteriorCoil_by_GE_Unified. Enter the coil serial number and press Start.
Topic ID: id_14929940

Finalization

Finalization

After the test is complete, remove the phantom positioners, the phantoms, and the coil. Return them to their storage area.
