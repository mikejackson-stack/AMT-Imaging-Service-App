About this task

The Milan 16-Port 10/100 Fast Gigabit Ethernet Workgroup Switch (shown in Figure 3) is used to receive and transfer data between the host computer and the Systems cabinet and is part of the VRE subsystem.

Topic ID: id_SL13547702-537775

Removal/Replacement of Network Switch

Procedure

  1. Locate the Gigabit (network) switch in the system cabinet. Depending on the configuration of your system, the Gigabit switch can be located either in the middle or bottom of the MGD chassis. It can be identified by locating the group of RJ45 jacks on the front of the chassis.
  2. Remove the RJ45 connectors from the front of the switch, taking note of the order and placement of the terminal wires that they plugged in to.
  3. Using a Phillips screw driver, unscrew the four screws on the front of the switch so you can pull the switch forward to remove it from the cabinet.
  4. Remove the power plug from the outlet at the back of the switch.
  5. If a returnable FRU, securely repack the defective switch into its packaging and send it back to its respective destination.
Topic ID: id_s4-4-537775

Reinstallation and Power up Procedure

Procedure

  1. Reconnect all connectors in their original correct RJ45 jacks.
  2. Plug in the switch power connection.
  3. Verify that the power switch is in the ON position (see Figure 4).
  4. Secure the switch into its original location in the system cabinet by tightening its four screws.
  5. From the Common Service Browser, go to the Utilities tab, and then the VRE folder. Then click Power On.
Topic ID: id_14959431

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Reset TPS.
  2. If reset is successful, this unit is working. Otherwise, run all tests under MGD Host Datapath to troubleshoot.
