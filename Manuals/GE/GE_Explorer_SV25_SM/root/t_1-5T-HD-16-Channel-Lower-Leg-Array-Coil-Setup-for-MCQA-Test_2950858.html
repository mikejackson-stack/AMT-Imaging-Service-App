About this task

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HD Lower Leg Coil by GE/USAI (M3335LY).

Note:

Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

Topic ID: id_SL2927473-585344

Legacy Phantom Setup

Procedure

  1. Note:

    If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

    Position the base in the cradle, connect the preamp box to the cables and system, and position the leg segments on the base as shown in Figure 1.
  2. Place a phantom in each leg segment as shown in Figure 2.
  3. Secure the anterior leg and foot coils over the phantoms and advance coil towards the bore. See Figure 3.
  4. Landmark on the coil crosshairs as shown in Figure 4.
  5. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Test.

Unified Phantom Setup

Topic ID: id_SL2950927-585344

Mode: Lower Leg LEFT

Procedure

  1. Place the base of the coil on the patient table and position the left leg segments on the base as shown in Figure 5.
  2. Place the large and small cylindrical unified phantoms in the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Figure 6.
  3. Place the large and small cylindrical unified phantoms in the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Figure 7.
  4. Place the cubicle unified phantom in the foot part of the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Figure 8.
  5. Cover the phantoms with the anterior portion of the coil, as shown in Figure 9, using the straps provided.
  6. While covering the foot part, make sure that the arrow marks are aligned with each other as shown in Figure 10.
  7. Connect both the cables of the preamp box to the coil, as shown in Figure 11, and then connect the cables of preamp box to B port of system LPCA.
  8. Connect the coil to the respective port of system LPCA.
  9. Landmark the coil on the marks provided, as shown in Figure 12, and press advance to scan.
  10. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Test. For Lower Leg Array left mode, the following phantom sets are listed from the dropdown menu as shown in Figure 13.
  11. Select Unified Phantom Set – Single connector – Left Mode from the dropdown menu.
  12. Proceed with the test.
Topic ID: id_SL2950950-585344

Mode: Lower Leg RIGHT

Procedure

  1. Place the base of the coil on the patient table and position the left leg segments on the base as shown in Figure 14.
  2. Place the small cylindrical unified phantom in the right leg portion of the coil as shown in Figure 15.
  3. Place the cubicle unified phantom in the foot part of the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Figure 16.
  4. Cover the phantoms with the anterior portion of the coil, as shown in Figure 17, using the straps provided.
  5. While covering the foot part, make sure that the arrow marks are aligned with each other as shown in Figure 18.
  6. Connect both the cables of the preamp box to the coil, as shown in Figure 19, and then connect the cables of preamp box to B port of system LPCA.
  7. Connect the coil to the respective port of system LPCA.
  8. Landmark the coil on the marks provided, as shown in Figure 20, and press advance to scan.
  9. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Test with the Unified Phantom Set.
  10. For Lower Leg Array Right mode, the following phantom sets are listed from the dropdown menu, as shown in Figure 21.
  11. Select Unified Phantom Set – Single connector – Right Mode from the dropdown menu.
  12. Proceed with the test.
