Remove Cable from Coil

  1. Remove the coil from the magnet room before you begin this procedure.

  2. Wear an ESD wrist strap to avoid electrostatic discharge.

  3. Remove the screws that attach the covers to the unit.

  4. Remove the covers.
  5. Disconnect the RF cables and the DC wires from the feed boards.
  6. Remove the two screws from the strain relief clamp.

  7. Remove the cable from the coil.

Topic ID: id_SL5086146-1061491

Install New Cable in Coil

Note:

(For RRx receive chain systems) Both the A-port and P-port cable assemblies are compatible with the RRx receive chain. You can use either assembly to complete this procedure.

  1. Attach the cable to the coil with the cable clamp.

  2. Connect the DC wires and RF cables Figure 2.
