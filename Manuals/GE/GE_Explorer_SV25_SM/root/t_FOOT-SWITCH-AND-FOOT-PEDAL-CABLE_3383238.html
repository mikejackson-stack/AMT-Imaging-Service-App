About this task

While servicing the table it may be required to disconnect the table from Gantry and required to take out from the magnet room. This instruction provides procedure to take out the table out from the Magnet Room

Procedure

  1. Turn the system power OFF. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker.
  2. Remove FRP bottom covers of Fixed Table.
    1. Remove the Rubber cap and then screws of the FRP bottom cover on the near the Gantry end of the Table.
    2. Similarly remove the cap and screws of the FRP bottom cover on the rear end of the Table.
    3. Remove the right Bottom FRP Cover. Similarly remove the left Bottom FRP cover on the other side
    4. Now remove the Dock FRP Cover Locking screws and then the Dock FRP cover carefully.
    5. Keep both Bottom FRP Covers and Dock FRP covers aside safely.
  3. Disconnect the Foot switch Cable from the Control box.
  4. On the backside of the Dock FRP cover, there are eight screws holding the four Foot switches.
  5. Remove the screws of the foot switch, which needs to be replaced.
  6. Push out the foot switch from the Dock FRP cover.
  7. Open the foot switch with the help of a screwdriver.
  8. Rotate the clamp and remove the cable from the clamp.
  9. Disconnect the Cables from the Foot switch.
  10. Now the foot switch is free from the Table. Take the new Foot switch and Open the foot switch with the help of a screw drive.
  11. Connect the foot switch cables to the foot switch.
  12. Remove the right side screw of the cable clamp in the foot switch, and loosen the other one.
  13. Rotate the clamp and rout the cable through the clamp.
  14. Tighten the both cable clamp screws.
  15. Assemble the foot switch housing to Foot switch cover with the help of screwdriver.
  16. Place the switch on the FRP Cover, and insert the footswitch screw from the backside of the Dock FRP cover.
  17. Pull the cable from the backside of the Dock FRP Cover and rout cable.
  18. Now connect the foot switch cable to the control box, placed in the Gantry.
  19. Fix the Table FRP covers back to the Table. Fix the FRP bottom covers to the table.
