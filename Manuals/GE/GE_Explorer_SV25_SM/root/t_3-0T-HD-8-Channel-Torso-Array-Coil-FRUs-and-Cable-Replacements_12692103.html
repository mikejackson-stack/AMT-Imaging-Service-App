About this task

This document contains the procedure for replacing the cable assembly on the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Torso Coil by GE/USAI, catalog M3335LN.

Topic ID: id_SL3240665-619972

Cable Removal

Procedure

  1. Notice
    To be performed by authorized Service Engineer only.
    Note:

    This procedure applies to either anterior or posterior.

    Remove the coil center cover by removing all the screws around its perimeter.
  2. Check the connection of the cable assembly in the coil.
  3. Cut off the cable tie.
  4. Disconnect the PCX plug PCB (with 4 PCX connectors) on the cable end.
  5. Using a flat screwdriver and tweezers, disconnect the DC plug on the cable end from the center board.
  6. Remove the center frame bridge that holds the cable assembly.
  7. Remove the cable clamp (with two screws) that holds down the cable assembly.
  8. Unseat the cable from the cradle of the bottom cover.
Topic ID: id_SL3240678-619972

Cable Installation

Procedure

  1. Note:

    The new cable has two cable ends with the PCX connectors on the board. The longer one is for the anterior package (without Velcro belt). The short one is for the posterior package (with Velcro belt mounted). The installation procedure is the same for both cables.

    Seat the bottom cover (with cable cradle) in the proper position.
  2. Put the new cable assembly into the coil.
  3. Ensure the cable strain relief matches the notch of the cradle, and ensure the cable connector end sits level and upright. (The side with “Do not loop” label of the in-cable box should face up. If it does not face down, rotate the strain relief.)
  4. Connect the DC plug to the corresponding receptacles on the center board.
  5. Connect the PCX plugs board to the corresponding receptacles on the center board (see Figure 2).
  6. Fasten the RF cable and DC wires together on the tie mount using one cable tie (see Figure 2).
  7. Put on the cable clamp holding the cable. Tighten the cable clamp with the two screws.
  8. Put on the center frame bridge that holds the cable assembly (see Figure 5).
  9. Close and screw the coil center cover onto the coil. Scan the coil to ensure proper assembly.
