Procedure

  1. Click the (Service Tools) icon to open the Service Desktop Manager.
  2. Click in the lower part of the Service Desktop Manager if the Common Service Desktop is not already visible.
    Note:

    It takes a few seconds for the Common Service Desktop to appear. If the Common Service Desktop never appears, then confirm that it isn’t already open or hasn’t been dragged partially off the screen or isn’t hidden behind another open window.

  3. Click the (Image Quality) tab at the top of the Common Service Desktop.
  4. Select MultiCoil Receive Chain Tool from the menu on the left side of the CSD.
  5. Under MultiCoil Receive Chain Tool on the right side of the CSD, select Click here to start this tool. The MultiCoil Receive Chain Test window opens as shown in Figure 1.
  6. Identify the receive cable on the Head Coil. This cable is usually marked with a blue tape on the Head Quick Disconnect Box, but it can be confirmed by the following:
    1. Insert the MCR H/W into the MultiCoil (lower) connector on the carriage cover and attach one of the cables from the head coil (with the DC blocker bullet) to the connector with the blue band.
    2. From the Documentation menu on the tool’s Graphical User Interface, select Appendix and then Verify Head Receive Line. See Figure 3.
    3. Follow the instructions on the flowchart and click on the Download button at the bottom of the screen. This will automatically download the protocol to the scan desktop. See Figure 4.
      Note:

      DO NOT INCREASE THE TG HIGHER THAN THE VALUE OF 120 SHOWN IN Figure 4. INCREASING THE TG MUCH ABOVE 120 CAN DAMAGE THE PREAMP BOARD ON THE CHANNEL UNDER TEST. THIS WOULD RESULT IN REDUCED SNR ON THE CHANNEL

    4. Go to the scan desktop. When the download is complete, select Manual Prescan. If the selected cable is the receive line, then you should get signal on receiver 1. If there is no signal, repeat the step with the other cable from the head coil.
    5. Once the correct cable has been identified as the receive cable, close the displayed flowchart document by clicking on the Close button. See Figure 4.
  7. Select the appropriate test option.
  8. Click Continue.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
