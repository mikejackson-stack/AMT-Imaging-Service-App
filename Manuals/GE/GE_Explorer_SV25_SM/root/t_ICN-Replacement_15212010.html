Notice
Under NO circumstances should the ICNs be turned on or off from the power button on the front. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.

Procedure

  1. Notice
    If an ICN in the stack (not at the top of the rack) requires replacement, all the ICNs above it must be uncabled and removed before the failed ICN can be removed. If the ICNs are not individually supported, attempting to remove any one ICN in the stack can result in damage to any ICN above it.
    Locate the ICNs in the system cabinet. Depending on the configuration of the system, the ICNs can be located either below the MGD chassis or at the bottom of the cabinet.
  2. Notice
    Under NO circumstances should the ICNs be turned on or off from the power button on the front. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
    Switch off the ICNs from the service browser.

    If there is an ICN in the rack, switch off all of the ICNs before removing any from the rack.

  3. Perform LOTO on the Systems Cabinet. See the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.
  4. Remove the power cables, Infiniband, and all three Ethernet switch connections from the rear of the ICNs. Refer to the appropriate illustration below for the applicable ICN.
    Note:

    Make a note of the Infiniband and Ethernet connection ports for easier installation.

  5. Remove the existing CX4-CX4 Infiniband cable(s) from the cabinet by disconnecting them from the Infiniband board on the CAM/MGD chassis. Do not discard the CX4-CX4 cable. Save one for use with the other service cable kit cables (for future P1/P2 port loopback testing for the CAM/MGD INFINIBAND board).
  6. Remove the screws securing the ICNs at the front. Refer to the appropriate ICN illustration below.
  7. Slide the ICNs out from the front of the cabinet.
  8. (For SUN 4100, SUN 4170 and SUN 4170M2 ICNs only) The bracket on the front of the ICN must be removed (unsnapped) and installed on the replacement ICN.
    1. To remove the right side bracket, push downward and rotate to a 45 degree angle.
    2. To remove the left side bracket, slide the bracket upward.
    3. Install the brackets on the new ICN by performing the above steps in reverse order.
  9. Warning
    possible personal injury!
    The 4170/4170M2 ICN is heavy and weighs approximately 40 lb.
    do not attempt to lift the icn unless two people are available.
    Replace the old ICN with the new one and reconnect the Ethernet and AC power cable(s) first.
  10. Note:

    The DC ICN 5127452 is exempted from the following Disk Wipe requirement.

    Perform the Disk Wipe procedure using the latest revision of the Disk Management Tool Service Manual, PN 5500610–1EN, available from the online documentation library.
  11. Connect each end of the Infiniband cable with the appropriate connector to the ports on the ICN Infiniband card and CAM/MGD chassis Infiniband card. Consult the appropriate system diagrams, if assistance is needed. Refer to either 5133310 1.5T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams or 5133320 3.0T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams
  12. Configure the new ICN by referring to Configuring the ICN - VRE.

Finalization

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure the system is working properly.
  2. Perform a SaveInfo.
