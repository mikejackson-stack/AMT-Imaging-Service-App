About this task

This document contains Dual Drive RF Amp replacement procedure. This unit is in the ISC cabinet (shown below). The Dual Drive RF Amp unit is about 335 lbs (152kg). The unit weighs enough that the use of a hoist kit is required. This document details the correct way to use a hoist kit to remove the Dual Drive RF Amp unit and replace with the field replaceable unit (FRU) safely.

Procedure

  1. CAUTION
    Power must be off at least 5 minutes prior to removal of RF Amp.
    Remove left front cover per following steps.
    1. Open left front cover and remove a screw to disconnect GND cable.
    2. Unlock the cover.
    3. Lift up and remove the cover.
    Note: If the service area is narrow, do the same for right front cover.
  2. Perform the Hoist Setup procedure to set up and secure the hoist.
  3. Disconnect connector and remove Water Leak sensor tray for RF Amp by removing 2 screws.
  4. Drain coolant. Perform Draining Operation before replacing RF Amp, SSSA, or SSSPS.
  5. Remove bracket above the RF Amp by removing two screws.
  6. Note: Prior to the next step, prepare to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is connected. Do not allow leakage to fall on white leak sensor strips located in cabinet in located in front and under RF amplifier.
    Disconnect the cables on the front of the Dual Drive RF Amp, and disconnect the coolant lines.
  7. Carefully move the cables and lines to the side of the cabinet, allowing the surrounding area to be free from any obstruction of movement for when the amplifier will be pulled out.
  8. Unscrew the six attachment screws.
  9. Using the two front handles, carefully slide out the Dual Drive RF Amp unit.
  10. Attach the lifting brackets to both sides of the Dual Drive RF Amp. The lifting brackets can be found in the Dual Drive RF Amp FRU crate. The attachment screws are captive screws and remain with the lifting brackets.
  11. DANGER
    Potential serious injury.
    Do not place any part of body under the replacement part to avoid the crush caused by accidental fall of the object.
    Also, do not flip the angle of lifting chain over 15 degree to mitigate the risk of accident.
    Perform the following steps.
    1. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket.
    2. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    3. Press the release tabs in on each side out the RF Amp.
  12. Remove the top of the wooden crate and turn it on its base.
  13. Ratchet the winch to lower the Dual Drive RF Amp into the package.
  14. Remove the hook from the lifting bracket.
  15. Unscrew the lifting brackets from the sides of the Dual Drive RF Amp.
  16. Roll the removed Dual Drive RF Amp to the side.
  17. Remove the center sections of the FRU crate.
  18. Bring the FRU to the front of the cabinet.
  19. Attach the slide rails to the FRU.
  20. Attach the lifting brackets to the sides of the new amplifier.
  21. Install Dual Drive RF Amp by the reverse order of the removal.
  22. Connect the all of the cables and coolant lines to the Dual Drive RF Amp.
  23. Disassemble the Hoist kit.
  24. Restore ISC.

Finalization

  1. Notice
    The RF Amp may be damaged if power is on without coolant inside. To avoid this situation, set the RF Amp Breaker to OFF Position before power ON for new FRU.
    Set the RF Amp Breaker to OFF Position.
  2. Restore the power. Refer to Removing LOTO - ISC.
  3. Wait till pressure meter (black meter) of CCU moves for about 40 seconds.
  4. Turn the RF Amp Breaker ON
  5. Perform RF Amplifier and UPM Calibration .
  6. Perform Dual drive quadrature tool.
  7. Perform check scan.
  8. After attaching the center sections of the FRU package to the removed FRU unit base, attach the top of the FRU crate to prepare the FRU for return.
    Note: The Dual Drive RF Amp has two center sections.
