Handling of Ferrous Material

Notice
In the event that a ferrous object becomes attached to the magnet, contact your MR Regional Support Engineer for directions before attempting removal.
Notice
Report all injuries (regardless of severity) within 24 hours to your immediate supervisor, Environmental Health and Safety, and the complaint handling system per General Electric policy. Refer to training course, GEHC-QUAL-69211915, Service Record Completeness Training for Field Engineers, for guidance on filing a complaint.
Topic ID: id_SL4960040-1162773

Overview

This procedure describes the handling of ferrous objects in the magnet room when various conditions exist. The GE Healthcare MR Pre-Installation Manual (PIM) describes the minimum Magnet Room size, primarily for the serviceability areas necessary to service devices by the Field Engineer. There are several components that are ferrous within the Magnet Room. The Blower Box Motor Assembly is an example of a Field Replaceable Unit (FRU) that is ferrous. All ferrous material handling must comply with magnet safety precautions discussed in MR Magnet and PET/MR Safety Requirements.

There are three categories of room size to be identified before moving ferrous material within the Magnet Room. Correspond the site's room size to one of the situations, and follow the guidelines described in the next section:

  1. Room meets or exceeds the minimum (min.) room size, and ample space exists outside of the 200 Gauss field. (See Room Size Meets Minimum Requirements with Clear Service Path Outside 200 Gauss.)

  2. Room meets the minimum room size, but has no clear service path outside of the 200 Gauss field. (See Room Size Meets Minimum Requirements without Clear Service Path Outside 200 Gauss.)

  3. Room is less than the minimum room size, and there is no alternate exit except past the magnet. (See Room Size Less Than Minimum Requirements with No Alternate Exit.)

Magnet Room Conditions

DANGER
possible personal injury or equipment damage!
some FRU's contain ferrous material. any field strength greater than 200 gauss will forcibly attract these units to the magnet.
to prevent injury or equipment damage, stay outside of the 200 gauss field when moving ferrous materials.
Warning
possible personal injury or equipment damage
the radiation survey meter is ferrous and will be attracted to the magnet.
to prevent injury or equipment damage, do not bring the meter into the magnet room WHEN THE MAGNET IS RAMPED.

For ALL room sizes: When in doubt, check it out. If there is any uncertainty of the magnetic fringe field, room dimensions, or safety concerns when moving a large ferrous device past the magnet, contact the On Line Center (OLC) for assistance.

Room Size Meets Minimum Requirements with Clear Service Path Outside 200 Gauss

Magnet Room meets the minimum room size requirements, and a clear path exists outside of the 200 Gauss magnetic field.

  1. When servicing any equipment within the Magnet Room, it is critically important for the Service Engineer to consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical to avoid high flux-density fields. Refer to the “Highly Ferrous Material Pre-Work Checklist” in MR Magnet and PET/MR Safety Requirements.

  2. Safety Requirement:

    • Movement of ferrous material within the Magnet Room must follow the GE service procedure for that device. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to/from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss.

    • Two MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the area of magnetic fields.

  3. When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure there are no trip hazards, obstacles (furniture, cables, clutter), slippery surfaces, or any other items even partially restricting the path that would force deviation from the planned route.

    Notice
    A clear Service Path includes the absence of any General Electric or customer equipment. This includes items as minor as door stops, electrical plugs that may lead the FE to move into a higher gauss field. No deviation is acceptable.
    If there are portable obstacles in a path, temporarily remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed.
    It is required to walk the paths prior to beginning service to ensure there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

Room Size Meets Minimum Requirements without Clear Service Path Outside 200 Gauss

There is no service path available outside the 200 Gauss field, but the Magnet Room meets the requirements for a minimum room size.

  1. Confirm the room meets the minimum room size defined in the PIM.

  2. When servicing any equipment within the Magnet Room, it is critically important for the Service Engineer to consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical to avoid high flux-density fields.

  3. Safety Requirement:

    • Movement of ferrous material within the Magnet Room must follow the GE service procedure for that device. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to/from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss.

    • Two MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the area of magnetic fields.

    Notice
    A clear Service Path includes the absence of any General Electric or customer equipment. This includes items as minor as door stops, electrical plugs that may lead the FE to move into a higher gauss field. No deviation is acceptable.

  4. When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure there are no trip hazards, obstacles (furniture, cables, clutter), slippery surfaces, or any other items even partially restricting the path that would force deviation from the planned route.

    • If there are portable obstacles in a path, temporarily remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed.

    • It is required to walk the paths prior to beginning service to ensure there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

    CAUTION
    Potential Personal Injury!
    Objects may have edges sharp enough to scratch or cut.
    Wear proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) when moving the object from the Magnet Room to avoid any possible injuries.

  5. Move any large, ferrous object using two MR safety trained personnel by placing it against the wall and floor at the service side of the magnet as shown below.

    Observe the following:

    1. No material, other than what is described in the Service Methods manual, is to be used to remove a FRU. Examples: Dollies or other devices that may be ferrous.

    2. Caution must be taken to prevent the device from moving closer to the magnet or being lifted above the floor during transportation.

    3. Never put yourself between the magnet and the ferrous object at any time.

    4. Always plan and use identified path.

    5. Phantom carts should not be stored in a minimum-sized room, or they must be removed from the room during servicing the equipment to allow for adequate minimum service area.

Room Size Less Than Minimum Requirements with No Alternate Exit

Room size less than the minimum with no other exit point requires a magnet ramp down. (Ramp down may not be necessary if the room has a second exit so ferrous objects do not need to be moved past the magnet.)

DANGER
possible personal injury!
Do NOT attempt to move the ferrous device when this condition exists and the Magnet is ramped.
