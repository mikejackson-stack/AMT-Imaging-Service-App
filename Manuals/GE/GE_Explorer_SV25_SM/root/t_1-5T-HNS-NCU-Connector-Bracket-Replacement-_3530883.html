About this task

This procedure applies to Revision 3, 1.5T Head, Neck, Spine (HNS) Coils only (refer to rating plate for revision number). It details the replacement of the NCU connector bracket on the posterior portion of the HNU.

Procedure

  1. Notice
    A properly grounded wrist strap must be worn by the Field Engineer (FE) performing this procedure.
    Run the HNS Coil MCQA Tool for the Posterior Head-Face and Posterior Head-Chest Horseshoe TL configurations to verify the coil is functioning properly prior to rework. Refer to 1.5T HNS Coil MCQA Setup to set up the coil.
  2. Remove the bottom cover of Head-Neck unit. Set bottom cover and the brass screws aside.
  3. Unplug NCU Connector RF and DC Cables from the CH11 Feedboard.
    1. Gently pull up on the RF Connector Retainer, then remove it by pulling back on the small vertical tab. Set the retainer aside.
    2. Unplug the DC Connector by pulling on the white connector housing.
  4. Using a 1/8 in. slotted screwdriver, remove the two external screws from the NCU Cover, and set them aside.
  5. Remove the NCU Connector cover and inner threaded retainer. Set these items aside.
  6. Remove the two brass screws from the NCU Connector, and set them aside.
  7. Remove the NCU Connector by carefully pulling the cables through the posterior former wall, and set the connector aside.
  8. Using a 6 in. long slotted screwdriver, remove the two mounting screws from the NCU Bracket. Discard the old bracket and the brass mounting screws.
  9. Install the new bracket with the brass screws included in FRU Kit using the 6 in. long slotted screwdriver.
  10. Reinsert the NCU Connector into the housing, and install using the two brass screws removed earlier. Tighten the screws until the connector is fully seated, then tighten an additional one-eighth turn.
  11. Reinstall the inner threaded retainer, making sure to orient it correctly.
  12. While holding the inner threaded retainer, reattach the NCU cover. Tighten the screws until the cover is fully seated, then tighten an additional one-eighth turn.
  13. Reattach the RF and DC cables to the CH11 Feedboard.
  14. Reinstall the lower cover of the HNS unit using the brass screws removed earlier.

Finalization

Verify the coil functionality by running the HNS Coil MCQA Tool for the Posterior Head-Face and Posterior Head-Chest Horseshoe TL Configurations. Refer to 1.5T Head Neck Spine (HNS) Coil MCQA Setup to set up the coil.
