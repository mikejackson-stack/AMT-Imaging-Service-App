Table 4. Safety
DANGER
STRONG MAGNETIC FIELD!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the SIGNA magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
Mechanical latch contain ferrous material.
Holding this assembly too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract it to the magnet. To prevent possible bodily injury or material damage, use two people to promptly remove assembly from magnet room.
Warning
Personal injury and equipment damage!
Strong Magnetic Field
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.
Safety Requirements:
  • The service path must allow for the highly ferrous devices be moved against the wall along the ground until outside of the 200 Gauss zone at the front of the magnet when it may be lifted and removed from the room.
When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

Procedure

  1. Make sure there is nothing on the cradle. No load on cradle is very important for this replacement; please double-check before next step.
  2. Remove three screws on base cover and put Table base cover aside.
  3. Remove Motor cover and side covers.
    1. Remove five screws and slide out the Motor Cover.
    2. Remove two rubber caps, remove six screws, and remove side cover. Do the same for the other side.
  4. Move the Table to down limit if possible for better serviceability.
    Note: If the Table cannot move down, refer to note in Step 5.
  5. (For Telescopic Cover) Remove six screws which fix the upper telescopic cover to the table frame.
    Note: If the Table cannot move down, perform the following steps

    1. Remove the four screws from upper telescopic cover except two screws at diagonal position.

    2. Hold the upper telescopic cover by two person at the diagonal position and remove the remaining two screws.

    3. Slowly move down the telescopic cover in parallel by two person.

  6. If Table is down, move the Table to up limit.
  7. Perform Lockout / Tagout for ISC.
  8. Install screw rod for the safety operation.
  9. Warning
    FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
    Mechanical Latch contains ferrous material.
    Holding this assembly too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract it to the magnet. To prevent possible bodily injury or material damage, use two people to promptly remove mounting bracket assembly from magnet room. Stay as far away from the magnet as possible when exiting the magnet room.
    CAUTION
    Scratch your hand!
    Wear a pair of gloves to protect your hand.
    Notice
    Mechanical Latch itself is not a FRU part because it a ferrous object and cannot be removed from Assembly from top access. Never remove it from the assembly.
    Remove Mechanical Latch per following steps.
    1. Disconnect the cable.
    2. Remove four M6 screws and washers.
    3. Keep pushing manual unlatch button to unlatch the lock. At the same time, remove the Latch assy by sliding back.
    4. Remove it through the bottom opening between EHA and scissors.
      Note: The weight of Latch assy is 2.5 kg.
  10. Notice
    Make sure the Lock is in open condition as shown in Figure 6, otherwise the assy can’t be installed correctly.
    Check the new Lock condition. If it’s in lock condition, push the manual unlatch button to change it to open condition.
  11. Make sure Patient Table is at the top position (the height difference between the Table top and Bridge top is within 1 mm). If not, move up the Table by screw rod.
  12. Restore Mechanical Latch.
    1. Put the Mechanical Latch assembly on the base and push it ahead until it is locked to the hook make the hook on the carriage.
    2. Loosely install four screws and washers.
    3. Keep pushing the assembly ahead until it can’t be moved and hold it . Check and make sure there is no gap between part 1 and part 2 and tighten the four screws.
    4. Restore the cable.
  13. Make sure that all cables are connected.
  14. Remove safety screw rod from the Table.
  15. Turn the system power ON. Refer to Removing LOTO - ISC
  16. Check the following items by referring to Figure 9. Use Table pedal to move the table up and down to make the table at up-limit position or not at the up-limit position.
    1. When the table is at up-limit position, check that the conditions in Item 1 are satisfied.
    2. When the table is not at up-limit position, check that the conditions in Item 2 are satisfied.
  17. Restore Covers in reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Move table from down-limit position to up-limit position two times. Make sure the table can work normally.
  2. Perform check scan.
