Table 1. Safety
Warning
Strong magnetic field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring any ferrous tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Strong magnetic field
The cart for the magnet enclosure package includes ferrous material, which can become a dangerous projectile in the presence of the magnetic field that the magnet produces.
Do not bring the cart into the magnet room after the magnet is ramped up.
Note: Protective film is attached on the magnet enclosure from the forward production (Q3, 2016). It will be applied for both pre-attached enclosures on the magnet and the enclosures shipped for installation.

The purpose of protective film is to protect the enclosure surface from the scratch during transportation and system installation.

If the protective film is attached to the cover, please keep the following items in mind.

  • The film will be removed just before customer turnover. Please do not remove the film until then.
  • When fixing the cover with screws, peel off the film only for the fixing location.

Procedure

  1. Install Side cover center beam brackets (5534644) with four stainless steel hexagon M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws and four large flat M10 Stainless Steel Washers (2001-M10-10) for both left and right side.
  2. Install Cover Lower Latch Assembly 2 (5620618 and 5620618-2) at four locations using two Flat M6 Stainless Steel Washers (2000-M6-10) and two Stainless Steel Hexagon Nuts (3000-M6C-33) at each location.
    Note: 5620618 and 5620618-2 are symmetrical parts.
    AInstall Cover Lower Latch Assembly 2
    BThis stud is not used
    CFix the cover with a screw and washer

    Start at the front right bottom corner and proceed to the other three corners.

  3. Install the side cover right-hand upper rear with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 x 50 Socket Head Screws (1000-M10C050-33).
    Note: The magnet enclosure will be installed after magnet shimming; it is a task of Installation and Calibration wizard (ICW).
  4. Install the side cover right-hand upper front with one M10 x 50 Socket Head Screw ( 1000-M10C050-33).
  5. Install the side cover left-hand upper rear with three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  6. Install the side cover left-hand upper front with three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  7. Notice
    Before installing the center top cover left-hand, make sure the thee cables (M3341, M3386, and M3387) are connected to SRI and PAC.
    Install the center top cover left-hand with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  8. Install access cover left-hand side with two M6-1 x 12MM Phillips Pan Head Screws (1007-M6C012-31).
  9. Rear bridge side covers by fixing three M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and three M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30) for both covers.
  10. Connect the M3350 cable to the connector at the bracket of right bridge side cover.
  11. Install rear bridge bottom cover with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  12. Install rear center bridge cover two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  13. Install duct bar with four M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33).
  14. Install rear cable cover with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33).
  15. Note: From Q3 2016, only one side cover dolly is shipped with the system because of VCP purpose. Use one dolly for both lower side covers.
    Install the lower side cover assembly (right) with two M10 x 25 Socket Head Cap Screws (1000-M10C025-33) and two M10 Large Flat Washers (2001-M10-30).
  16. Notice
    Before installing the lower side cover assembly (left), make sure the long coil cables (M1356 through M1365) are connected to the DPP.
    Do the same procedure in Figure 16 for the lower side cover assembly (left).
  17. Install the side nameplate cover for both side using poppers.
  18. If not yet done, install the alternate language laser light warning label over the English label at the location shown below for non-English country.
