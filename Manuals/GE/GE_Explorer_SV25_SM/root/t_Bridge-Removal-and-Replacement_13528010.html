About this task

This procedure explains how to replace the patient transport split bridge. Plan on about 30 minutes to remove the bridge. Double the time if reinstalling the same bridge components. Plan on at least 75 minutes if installing a new bridge that requires subcomponent assembly. (See Replacing Bridge Part within Bridge Assembly.)

Removing Front Bridge

Procedure

  1. Position the LPCA to the far rear of the enclosure, and undock the patient table.
  2. Detach the cradle from the LPCA.
  3. Perform LOTO on the PDU for the PEN panel, patient blower, and magnet room. See the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.
  4. Release the drive belt tension by flipping the lever under the rear pedestal.
  5. Remove the LPCA cover.
  6. Count and make a note of the number of notches above the belt clamp.
  7. Unscrew the two Phillips screws that hold the belt clamp plate in place on each of the two belt clamps.
  8. Pull the drive belt free from the rear pedestal. Carefully unthread the drive belt from the rear pulley.
    Note:

    At the rear pedestal, route the drive belt over rear pulleys only. Do not route the drive belt over crossbars or other objects in the rear pedestal. Doing so can overstress the drive system.

  9. At the rear of the split bridge, use a 17 mm wrench to remove the four M10 nuts and washers that attach the rear pedestal to the split bridge.
  10. Using a 5 mm Allen wrench, remove the two M6 screws holding the front bridge cover in place and remove the cover.
  11. Using a 17 mm wrench, remove the two M10 Bridge Bracket bolts. (When these bolts are removed the height adjuster and spacer will no longer be secured.)
    Note:

    If reusing this bridge, do not change the location of the height adjustment for the threaded nylon nuts. If replacing this bridge with a new bridge, adjust the lower nuts so the edges of the bridge adjacent to the end bell are flush to 1 mm below the flat surfaces of the end bell.

  12. Slide the bridge out from magnet, making sure the drive belt comes along with the bridge.

Replacing Bridge Part within Bridge Assembly

Procedure

  1. Lay the bridge on its top and use a 5 mm Allen wrench to remove the four M6 Allen bolts that secure the bridge mounting bracket.
  2. Remove the belt from the bridge.
  3. Warning
    FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
    THE PULLEY ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
    TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, move the bridge out of the magnetic field before continuing.
    Flip the bridge so the top is facing up. Using a regular screwdriver, unscrew the two bolts.
    Note:

    When these bolts are removed, the plate on the opposite side will fall out.

  4. Remove the pulley as shown.
Topic ID: id_SL1555020-487326

Bridge Replacement/Installation

Procedure

  1. If necessary, reinstall (or install new) pulley ASM and drive belt onto the new bridge by reversing the steps in Replacing Bridge Part within Bridge Assembly.
  2. To reinstall the bridge, perform the steps above in reverse order.
