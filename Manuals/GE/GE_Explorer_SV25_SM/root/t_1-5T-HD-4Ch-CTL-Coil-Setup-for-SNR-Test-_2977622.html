Legacy Phantom Setup

Procedure

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Figure 1.
  2. Place a phantom between Posterior and Anterior parts of the coil as shown in Figure 2.
  3. Landmark on the coil crosshairs as shown in Figure 3.
  4. Perform Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool.
Topic ID: id_SL2977569-571630

Unified Phantom Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and latch it to the table.
  2. Place the TL unified phantoms on the centre of the TL section of the coil as shown inFigure 4.
  3. Align the phantoms to the centre in S/I and R/L directions as shown in Figure 5 and Figure 6.
  4. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom on the CT section and fix the C-Spine Bridge to the hooks provided as shown in Figure 7.
  5. Adjust the large cylindrical unified phantom such that it is in complete contact with the TL unified phantom (Figure 8).
  6. Adjust TL unified phantoms such that both the phantoms are in complete contact with each other as shown in Figure 9.
  7. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.
  8. Landmark the coil at the cross mark line on the C-Spine Bridge (Figure 10) and advance to scan.
  9. Perform Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool.
