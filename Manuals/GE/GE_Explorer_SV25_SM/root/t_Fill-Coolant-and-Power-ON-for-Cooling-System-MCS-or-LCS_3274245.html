Topic ID: id_SL1588515-1084943

Fill Coolant to the Reservoir Tank for LCS/MCS

Procedure

  1. Remove Signal Cable from rear panel.
  2. Remove cap of the reservoir.
    1. For MCS, remove top and side cover, then access to the cap of the reservoir.
    2. For 8kW/4kW LCS, open front cover and access to the cap of the reservoir.
  3. Fill the reservoir. Check the float in the reservoir sight tube on the front of the unit to see the coolant level in the tank as you are filling. Fill the coolant so that the float reaches near the upper limit. Then, close the cap of the reservoir.

Power ON for MCS or 8kW/4kW LCS

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Coolant level may drop during start-up because of the air bubble remained in the hose. Check the coolant level and fill the reservoir as described inFigure 6 or Figure 7 until the coolant level becomes stable. Do not allow tank to empty before running the LCS/MCS as this may damage the pump.
    Remove top cover of 8kW LCS or MCS to check the LED of Reverse Phase Repay.
  2. Verify that the breaker inside of 8kW LCS or MCS ON.
  3. For 4kW LCS, turn the switch ON located at front of 4kW LCS.
  4. Turn the AC Distribution Breaker at System Cabinet PDU Panel ON.
  5. Turn the System Cooling Unit Breaker at System Cabinet PDU Panel ON.
  6. Push the EMO Reset SW ON. Now, LCS/MCS will start running.
  7. Notice
    Regarding MCS, if power is supplied from System to MCS, the fans on the top continue to work even though the breaker of MCS is tripped and the pump stops working. Please always check red LED of Reverse Phase Relay when you turn the System Cooling Unit power on.
    Check that LED on Reverse Phase Repay is NOT lit while MCS/LCS is running. If it is lit, breaker will be tripped after 5 minutes. It is necessary to swap the cable when the LED is lit. See Note.
    Note:

    If the LED is lit or breaker is tripped, perform the following.

    1. Turn the System Cooling Unit Breaker at System Cabinet OFF. Perform LOTO.

    2. Open LCS/MCS Top cover.

    3. Swap the two cables (Red and Orange).

    4. Turn the Breaker in the System Cooling Unit ON.

    5. Turn System Cooling Unit Breaker at System Cabinet ON and check that the LCS/MCS is working without any fail.

      If the Breaker is tripped, check the following.

      1. Power cable connection at the system cabinet terminal.

      2. Input voltage to the LCS/MCS may be low.

  8. Perform Priming Pump by switching the breaker inside 8KW LCS or MCS rapidly ON-and-OFF for several times.
  9. Run the LCS/MCS about 10 seconds and turn OFF the breaker at System Cabinet. Check that there is no leakage of Coolant.
  10. Turn the System Cooling Unit Breaker at System Cabinet ON.
  11. Notice
    Coolant level may drop during start-up because of the air bubble remained in the hose. Check the coolant level and fill the reservoir as described in Figure 6 or Figure 7 until the coolant level becomes stable. Do not allow tank to empty before running the chiller as this may damage the pump.
    Re-fill the reservoir again. Check the float in the reservoir sight tube on the front of the unit to see the coolant level in the tank as you are filling. Close the cap of the reservoir.
  12. Observe that there is no leakage.
  13. Check the Cabinet Monitor and leakage LED is not lit for each sensor.
    Note:

    There are three Leak sensor routes.

    • Leak Sensor 2 (5332131, 5334211, 5332992)

    • Leak Sensor 3 (5332993)

    If the Leak Sensor2 detects the leakage, LK2 LED is lit and error message is displayed on Host and Emergency Stop will work.

    If the Leak Sensor3 detects the leakage, LK3 LED is lit and error message is displayed.

  14. For LCS, read the water flow meters and adjust the water flow with valve to satisfy the specification.
  15. Restore the water filler cover on top of the unit to access the reservoir.
  16. Connect the signal Cable to MCS, 8kW LCS, or 4kW LCS.
  17. According to the labels information, Install labels.
