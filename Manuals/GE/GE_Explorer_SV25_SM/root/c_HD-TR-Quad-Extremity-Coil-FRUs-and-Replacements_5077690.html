HD T/R Quad Extremity Coil FRUs and Replacements

Topic ID: id_SL5080645-1061486

Personnel Requirements

Required Persons Procedure Timing

Topic ID: id_SL5080646-1061486

Overview

This document contains the FRUs, additional accessories, and spare kit components for the HD T/R Quad Extremity Coil by Invivo. It also contains replacement procedures for the FRUs. The coil replacement procedures are the same for all the following:

  • (For curved table)

    Catalog M3335ME 1.5T coil

  • (For flat table)

    Catalog M7000FT 1.5T coil

  • (For curved table)

    Catalog M3335LP 3.0T coil

  • (For flat table)

    Catalog M7000GT 3.0T coil

Tools and Test Equipment

(For cable replacement) 1 flat-head screwdriver for #6 size screws

(For mechanical components) 1 flat-head screwdriver for #4, 6, and 10 size screws

(For testing cable functionality) Digital multimeter

(For mechanical components) Loctite 222 (12-month shelf life), part 46-170683P1 (one 10 cc bottle)

Topic ID: id_SL5080660-1061486

Cable Check and Replacement

Notice
To be performed by authorized Service Engineers only.
Topic ID: id_SL5080661-1061486

Cable Continuity Check

  1. Select the OHMMETER function on the digital multimeter (DMM).

  2. Using the DMM, verify continuity of the cable by performing the following operations on the system connector:

    1. Continuity check from A1 to A2 pins

    2. Continuity check from M9 to M10 pins

  3. Remove six brass screws from the underside of the cable connect housing, and unplug the cable from PCB.

  4. Access the reference points on the exposed interconnect PCB.

    Perform continuity checks on the following reference points from the interconnect PCB to the system connector block.

    From To
    MC-1C1
    REF LOADH3
    TXF2
    PA +10 VDCM3
    MC BIASJ1
    COIL IDM7
    COIL ID RTNM8

    • Flex the cable while checking to test for intermittent open and short conditions in the cable.

    • If the cable fails any of the tests, replace the cable assembly by contacting GE Customer Service.

    • If the cable passes the tests, plug the interconnect PCB back into the preamp PCB and replace underside with the six brass screws.

Topic ID: id_SL5080665-1061486

Cable Replacement Procedure

  1. Remove the bottom screws of the extended housing.

  2. Carefully remove the top cover and place to the side.

  3. Unseat the cable from the cradle and carefully remove the small PCB with 20-pin connector from the large PCB.

  4. Set the cable assembly aside.

  5. Replace it with the new cable assembly, fully mating the 20-pin connector and socket.

  6. Seat the cable assembly into the cradle, and replace the cover.

  7. Ensure that the cable end sits level and upright.

  8. Hand tighten the screws, and scan the coil to ensure proper assembly.

Mechanical Parts Replacement

Topic ID: id_SL5080696-1061486

Mechanical Hardware Check

  1. Lift the coil locks on both sides of the coil, and slide the coil from side to side.

    The coil must slide freely back and forth on the base with no binding or grinding.

  2. Lock the coil in the center of the base. Check the bottom of the base to ensure the four rubber feet are tight.

  3. Make sure that the coil latch is secure and that the base lock is fastened.

Topic ID: id_SL5080701-1061486

Draw Latch Replacement

  1. Remove #6-32 x 0.438 FHMS brass screws, quantity 2 (item #2) from the housing and replace the old draw latch with the new draw latch.

  2. Use Loctite 222 on the brass screws. Insert them into the draw latch and hand tighten.

  3. Hand tighten the brass screws.

  4. Test the draw latch to ensure proper assembly.

Topic ID: id_SL5080706-1061486

Handle and Cover: Coil Lock Replacement

  1. Mate the new handle and cover pieces together, and fasten with #4-40 x 0.375 PHMS brass screws.

  2. With both handle locks in the upright position, pry one side of the handle lock from the cradle on the side of the housing.

  3. Use Loctite 222 on the brass screws. Replace the lock assembly with the new one, and secure with the screws.

  4. Slide the old lock assembly from the T-bracket (item #6) and replace it with the new lock assembly.

  5. Place one end of the lock assembly into the cradle of the housing and maneuver the other side into the cradle.

  6. Test the lock mechanism to ensure proper assembly.

Topic ID: id_SL5080712-1061486

T-Bracket Replacement

  1. Remove the coil lock as instructed in Handle and Cover: Coil Lock Replacement and replace the T-bracket.

  2. Replace the coil lock assembly as instructed in Handle and Cover: Coil Lock Replacement.

  3. Test the lock mechanism to ensure proper assembly.

Topic ID: id_SL5080724-1061486

Rubber Bumper Replacement

  1. Remove #6-32 x 0.50 PHMS brass screw (item #9).

  2. Replace the rubber bumper and tighten the brass screw.

  3. Use Loctite 222 on the PHMS screws, replace the baseplate rail with the new one, and secure with the screws.

  4. Cover the #6 PHMS screws with the new skid pads from the kit.
