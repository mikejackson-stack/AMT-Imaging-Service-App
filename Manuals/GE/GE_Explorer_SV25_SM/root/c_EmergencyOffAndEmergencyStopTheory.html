Emergency Off (E-OFF) and Emergency Stop (E-Stop)

Emergency off (E-OFF)

Emergency Off (E-OFF) button removes all electrical power from ALL components except the static magnetic field and some devices, see Table 1. Including any power sources from hospital’s uninterrupted power supply (UPS) equipment. The Emergency Off button not only stops a scan in a patient emergency, but also in the event of a serious equipment fault or hazards such as fire/water in the vicinity of the MR equipment.

Table 1. No impact device when press E-OFF button
Magnet Rundown UnitMR Touch/Magnetic Resonance Elastography Resoundant Acoustic Driver(option)
Pneumatic Patient AlertOxygen Monitor(option)
Magnet MonitorWireless Gating PAT Charging Station (only if your system have a wireless gating)
Warning
Personal injury and equipment damage
The Emergency OFF button doesn't turn off the magnetic field. To avoid personal injury or equipment damage, do not bring any ferromagnetic equipment into the magnet room. Assume that equipment is magnetic unless it is clearly labeled otherwise.
Note: Pressing the E-OFF button may cause damaged of the MR system and data lose.

Locations of E-OFF button

One E-OFF button is located on the MR system Main Disconnect Panel (if provided), two Remote E-OFF pushbuttons are supplied with the system. One pushbutton is for installing in the magnet room. The second pushbutton is optional and can be installed in the equipment or operator room.

Emergency Stop (E-Stop)

Emergency Stop (E-Stop) buttons remove the power of Patient Handling subsystem, Gradient subsystem, and RF subsystem.

Locations of E-Stop buttons

The Emergency Stop (E-Stop) buttons locate on keyboard and magnet enclosure front cover.
