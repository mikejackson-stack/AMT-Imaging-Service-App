The 1.5T Coil MCQA will utilize the 1.5T phantoms, which are green in color. The 3.0T Coil MCQA will utilize the 3.0T phantoms, which are pink in color.----

Procedure

  1. Place the Flat Filler Panel (shown below) on the table.
  2. Place the PV Coil on the Flat Filler Panel and connect the coil connector to P2 Port.
  3. Place the two TL Unified Phantoms on the Table, centering them right to left and making sure the TL Unified Phantom touches the edge of edge of the PV Coil the as shown.
  4. Perform the following steps.
    1. Place the PA Phantom Positioner on top of the TL Unified Phantoms, fitting the alignment block on the PV Coil into the alignment notch on the PA Phantom Positioner.
    2. Flip the PV Upper unit onto the PA Phantom Positioner.
  5. Perform the following steps.
    1. Move the cradle and adjust the PV Coil position in so that the alignment light hits in the middle of the latch (shown below) of the PV Coil.
    2. Adjust the position of two TL Unified Phantom and PA Phantom Positioner per Step 3 and Step 4
    Note:

    Confirm that whole coil is left/right centered to the cradle.

  6. Fasten with Velcro® straps of PV upper unit as shown.
  7. Press Landmark and press Advance to Scan.
  8. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool.
    Note:

    Select “3.0T_GEM_PV_Upper” for Current Coil field.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
