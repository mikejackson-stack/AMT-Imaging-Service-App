Notice
Possible equipment damage to RF amplifier connected with an improper load to its output. Do not operate the RF amplifier with an improper load connected to its output. Doing so may permanently damage the amplifier.

Procedure

  1. Disabling T/R Drive error condition:
    1. Verify the system is idle and all coils have been removed from the magnet bore.
    2. Remove the front cover of the RF accessories cabinet.
    3. On the front of the driver module, set the T/R switch to Disable. This will disable T/R fault reporting.
      Note: HV, DD, and MC should remain set to Enable.
    4. Disconnect T/R bias cable from the RF amplifier (J11 on AMT amplifier or J14 on CPC amplifier).
  2. Set up the measurement system according to one of the three diagrams shown below.
  3. Power Attenuator Method:
    1. Using an RG214 cable, connect the RF output of the amplifier to the input of a high-power attenuator.
    2. Connect additional attenuation to the output of the high power attenuator until a total of 65-70 dB of attenuation is attained.
    3. Using a BNC cable, connect the output of the attenuators to the input of the oscilloscope.
    4. Set the oscilloscope to full bandwidth (>150 MHz) and DC 50 Ohm input impedance.
  4. Coupler Method:
    1. Connect the RF output of the amplifier to the input of power coupler.
    2. Connect the output of the power coupler to a suitable 50 Ohm dummy load.
    3. Connect additional attenuation to the coupled output of the power coupler until at total of 65-70 dB of attenuation is attained.
    4. Connect the output of the attenuators to the input of the oscilloscope.
    5. Set the oscilloscope to full bandwidth (>150 MHz) and DC 50 Ohm input impedance.
  5. Power Meter Method: Set up according to the directions for MNS Max RF Power Meter (Watt Meter) Setup..
  6. Set up the scan parameters as shown in the table below.

  7. Calibrate the amplifier:
    1. For CPC 4kW calibration:

      1. The manual prescan should already be running. Measure the peak envelope power using the oscilloscope (power attenuator method or coupler method) or power meter method.

      2. For ease of calculation an Excel tool is available. Enter the measurement data into the appropriate calculator.

      3. If the gain adjustment is positive, turn the amplifier gain potentiometer adjustment clockwise. The amplifier gain potentiometer is located at the back of the CPC amplifier.

      4. You may adjust the gain of the CPC amplifier while the scan continues to run.

      5. Increase the TG to 200. When 4kW is measured at TG=200, you have successfully calibrated the amplifier. If the amp trips before achieving 4kW:

        Note: To know what peak-to-peak value you should be looking for, refer to Figure 5.

        • Back off the gain pot about 1/8 turn.

        • Allow the amplifier time to recover.

        • Restart manual prescan.

        • Select Scan TR.

        • Repeat this until you have set the amp operating at its peak output at a TG of 200.

        Note: It is normal for the CPC display on the front of the amp to be fluctuating between +/-10% of actual power due to slight differences in oscilloscopes, attenuators, and amplifiers.

      6. Reset TPS.

    2. For AMT 8kW calibration:

      1. Remove the front cover of the AMT amplifier (this will take about 8 minutes as there are 50+ screws to remove). When the covers are removed, you will see two holes for gross (6.0dB) and fine (0.5 dB) adjustments as pictured in Figure 3 and Figure 4.

        Note: It is possible that these two holes will be covered with an external screw. If this is the case, remove the screw so that the amplifier looks like the one in Figure 3.

      2. The manual prescan should already be running. Measure the peak envelope power using the oscilloscope (power attenuator method or coupler method) or power meter method.

      3. Enter the measurement data into the appropriate calculator.

      4. STOP or PAUSE the manual prescan before adjusting the gain controls on the AMT amplifier.

      5. If the calculator specifies an adjustment for the 6.0 dB control, insert a long Philips screwdriver, size #0 or #00, in the 6.0 dB adjuster and turn the control clockwise the specified number of clicks.

      6. Restart the manual prescan and measure the power. Use the calculator provided to recalculate the remaining gain adjustment.

      7. STOP or PAUSE the manual prescan before adjusting the gain controls on the AMT amplifier.

      8. Insert a Philips screwdriver in the 0.5 dB adjustment control and turn the specified number of turns. *** IF the gain of the system jumps by about 8 dB when adjusting the 0.5 dB control that indicates that it has been rotated past its zero point as per Figure 4. This is easy to fix:

        • If you were DECREASING gain (clockwise turns), turn 4 more clockwise clicks on the 0.5 dB control and 1 clockwise click on the 6.0 dB control.

        • If you were INCREASING gain (counterclockwise turns), turn 4 more clicks counterclockwise on the 0.5 dB control and 1 counterclockwise click on the 6.0 dB control

      9. Repeat steps 7.b.vi, 7.b.vii, and 7.b.viii as necessary to achieve the correct gain.

      10. After the calculated gain adjustment is almost zero, set the TG to 200 and verify full power output without peak power faults. ***You may need to add one additional clockwise click of the 0.5 dB control if the system is sensitive to faults at the 8 kW peak power level.

      11. When 8 kW is measured on the oscilloscope at TG=200, you have correctly calibrated the amplifier.

        Note: To know what peak-to-peak value you should be looking for, refer to Figure 5.

      12. Put the front cover back on the AMT amplifier.

        Reset the TPS.
