2  Overview

This document contains the procedures for Front Cover removal and installation. The front covers consist of two halves and contain the Emergency Stop and Control Panels for the system.

Instructions for replacing the Light Strips are also included.

4  Procedure

4.1  Preliminary

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Undock the patient table and position the table away from the magnet.

4.2  Front Cover Removal

The type and order of the Side Cover removal depends on the components accessed. For Left or Right Front Cover removal, remove the corresponding Side Middle and Side Front Covers first.

  1. Remove the Side Covers (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).

    Illustration 1:  Front Cover Screw Locations

    NOTE:Remove screws in the order listed. Screw locations shown above are approximate and may be hidden under unremoved components.

  1. Remove the Left and Right Trim Rings.

  1. Remove the Bridge Cover (4 screws).

  1. Remove the screw holding both Front Covers together and the bottom screw of each Front Cover from it’s bottom mounting.

  1. Remove the 2-paired Covers (4 screws) on the back of the In-Room Display (IRD) to access a Front Cover screw behind the Display. (see Replace Operator Display, Control Panels, and Estop Button.)

  1. Remove the screw located up on top by the Top Cover (above the IRD) and the screw located below the IRD.

  1. Disassemble the Operator Control Panel and disconnect the cables (see Replace Operator Display, Control Panels, and Estop Button).

  1. Remove the 2 screws located behind the OCP.

  1. Remove the 2 screws holding the Left or Right Front Cover to the bracket mounted on the magnet and set the covers aside.

    NOTE:Use care when moving the Front Covers

    • Both Left and Right Covers have the Vertical Light Strips attached. Disconnect the power cord from each light before moving or replacing (see Vertical Light Strip Replacement.

    • The Left Front cover has the Ambient Room Temperature Sensor attached. Be careful of the sensor cable when setting cover aside. When replacing the Left Front Cover, remove and reattach the Ambient Room Temperature Sensor (see Ambient Room and Bore temperature Sensors Replacement).

  1. Reverse the instructions when replacing or reattaching the Front Covers. Ensure proper fit.

4.3  Vertical Light Strip Replacement

For Left or Right Light Strip removal, remove the corresponding Side Middle and Side Front Covers.

  1. Ensure the LOTO is still in place and the patient table is undocked (see Preliminary).

  1. Remove the corresponding Side Covers (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. Disconnect the power cord to the corresponding Light Strip.

  1. Remove the Light Strip by removing the screws or poppets holding the Lights to the Cover.

  1. Replace and reconnect the Light Strip by reversing the above instructions. Ensure proper fit.

5  Finalization

  1. Whenever the In-Room Display or Trackball Control Panels are disconnected and then reconnected, a TPS Reset must be completed. (This should not be necessary if the host computer is rebooted.)

  1. Check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary.

    • For the In-Room Display - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then In Room Display.

    • For the Control Panel - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then SRI Functional Tests.
