3.0T Breast Coil System Cable Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Please observe anti-static precautions.
 
 

  1. Put on the ESD Grounding Wrist Strap and ground.

  1. Place coil bottom side up. SeeIllustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all screws (total of 18). See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the coil cover.

  1. Remove cable ties (2), see Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all RF cables (8). See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect DC Cables (4). See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two screws from cable clamp and remove the clamp from coil. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Cable Assembly. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the new cable assembly by sliding the strain relief of the cable into the cutout of the housing, with its round section towards the bottom. Press the strain relief down firmly, so the edge of the housing fits into the groove of stain relief nicely.

  1. Reinstall the cable clamp. See Illustration 5.

  1. Install all channel connections (8 RF coaxial and 4 DC connectors) by matching the designators on the cable with the designators on the PCB. Tie cables J5, J6, J7, J8, and P5 to the left cable tie holder on the PCB. Tie cables J13, J14, J15, J16, and P6 to the right cable tie holder. Route the cables as shown in Illustration 7 so no cables touch the rectangular shield. Cables P3 and P4 should not be tied.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the coil cover by replacing and securing all screws (total of 18). See Illustration 1.
