Grounding and Leakage Current Checks

There are three procedures associated with checking the grounding and the leakage currents on a MR system. Each procedure requires different tools and test equipment and should be performed in the sequence below.

  • First, perform the Ground Resistance Check

  • Next, complete the Ground Leakage Current Test

  • Finally, perform the PAC Assembly Leakage Current Test
