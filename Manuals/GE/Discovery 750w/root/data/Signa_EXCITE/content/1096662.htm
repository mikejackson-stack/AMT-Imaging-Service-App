3  Procedure

NOTE:The Uniformity Test can be executed in 2 separate modes: Elliptical and Quadrature.

The Quadrature mode uses the standard RF setting. Amplitudes of Channel 1 and Channel 2 are equal, and the phase difference between Channel 1 and cChannel 2 is 90 degrees.

The Elliptical mode uses the elliptical settings of 3 dB difference between Channel 1 and Channel 2 and a phase difference of -30 degrees.

  1. At the scanner, remove any surface or head coil from the bore.

  1. For GEM or flat tables, place the Elliptical Phantom in the center of the cradle. Use pads to support the phantom and test fixture. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Elliptical Phantom in Center of Cradle

  1. Set the angle of the phantom to 0 degrees.

    NOTE:Allow the phantom solution to settle for 5 minutes prior to scanning. This will prevent flow-related artifacts, which may affect the test results.

  1. Landmark on the center of the phantom as shown. Press [Landmark] and [Advance to Scan] on the keypad on the front of the magnet enclosure.

    Illustration 2:  Landmark for Elliptical Phantom

  1. Install the USB service key.

NOTICE
Do NOT re-Landmark between scans.
 
 

  1. In a command window, type the following:

    cd /usr/g/service/mclass/uniformity

    touch .uniformity_enable

    run_uniformity_tool

    The Uniformity Tool screen (shown below) displays with the scan status and results.

    NOTE:All buttons are inhibited during a scan. Only the [Abort Scan] button is available during a scan, but inhibited when Scan is idle.

    Illustration 3:  Uniformity Tool Screen and Status

  1. From the Dual Drive mode drop-down menu, select Quadrature Mode or Elliptical Mode.

  1. Select Four Scans mode from the Data acquisition mode drop-down menu.

  1. Click [Scan and Analysis].

  1. Click [Yes] when asked, Have you landmarked?

    Illustration 4:  Landmark Confirmation

  1. Rotate the phantom to horizontal as instructed, wait 5 minutes for it to settle, then select [Continue] to start the scan.

    Illustration 5:  Horizontal Position Confirmation

    NOTE:The data gathering information in Step 12 can be used to assist with centering the body coil.

  1. From the Utilities section of the Common Service Desktop, select <Log into AGP/SCP>, then Click here to start this tool.

    1. In the SCP Terminal window, type: printPower.

    1. Record the Body Peak Power (FW) value in Table 1.

      NOTE:The Body Peak Power value is required to reposition the coil. It is not needed to check Uniformity. This value must be collected while the system is pulsing RF.

  1. After the first scan completes, confirm that the dialog (shown in Illustration 5) displays requesting the user to reposition the phantom.

    NOTE:The illustration below shows the various phantom positions as viewed from the table end.

    Illustration 6:  Phantom Positions

  1. When asked to position the phantom, rotate it counterclockwise to the 45-degree plane, and wait 5 minutes for the phantom to settle. Select [Continue] to start the scan.

    Illustration 7:  45-Degree Position

  1. Repeat Step 12.

  1. After the second scan completes, confirm that the dialog displays requesting the user to reposition the phantom.

  1. When asked to position the phantom, rotate phantom counterclockwise to a 90-degree plane, and wait 5 minutes for the phantom to settle. Select [Continue] to start the scan.

    Illustration 8:  90-Degree Position

  1. Repeat Step 12.

  1. After the third scan completes, confirm that the dialog displays requesting the user to reposition the phantom.

  1. When asked to position the phantom, rotate it counterclockwise to a 135-degree plane, and wait 5 minutes for the phantom to settle. Select [Continue] to start the scan.

    Illustration 9:  135-Degree Position

  1. Repeat Step 12.

  1. Scanner should stop after scanning in the 135-degree position, and automatically process and display results as shown below.

    NOTE:There are different specifications for Quadrature Mode and Elliptical Mode.

    Illustration 10:  Plot for Scan Results

  1. The screen (shown in Illustration 10) indicates if the Uniformity test passed or failed.

  1. Record the TG and Uniformity (U) displayed values in the table below.

    NOTE:Be aware of the following:

    • The Body Peak Power or RF Amplifier Peak Forward (FW) Power values will vary somewhat during a scan, but a general reading can be determined within a few seconds. Be consistent throughout this procedure in the method used to record this value.

    • TG Values can be obtained from the Uniformity Tool screen shown in Illustration 3.
