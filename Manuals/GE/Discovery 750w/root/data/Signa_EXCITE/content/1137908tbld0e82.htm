High phase difference between Channel 1 and Channel 2
  1. It is critical that channel 1 and 2 transmission lines are never cut, re-terminated, or otherwise altered in length on site. Confirm properly labeled transmission line cables of equal length and type are used throughout entire RF chain including: DTX Exciter to RF Amp, RF Amp to SPW, SPW to DTRSW, and DTRSW to Body Coil.
  2. If all the cables are correct, go to Section 3
Low Body SNR

Perform System Performance Test (SPT) for Body and Head SNR only. Troubleshoot any SNR or TG failures per routine processes.
