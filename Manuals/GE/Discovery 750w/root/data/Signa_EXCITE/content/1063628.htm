2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 3.0T Shoulder Array by GE/USAI, catalog M3335LR.

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, refer to Auto Coil Installation.

4  Procedure

  1. Insert the Curved Adapter Panel Assembly into the magnet end of the table as shown.

    Illustration 1:  Placement of Curved Adapter Panel Assembly

  1. Position the phantom positioner and the coil as shown. Connect the cable.

    Illustration 2:  Phantom Positioner, Coil, and Cable Connection

  1. Insert the phantom into the coil, then guide the coil onto the phantom positioner pins (holes in coil frame will accept positioner pins) as shown below.

    Illustration 3:  Phantom in Coil on Positioner

  1. Landmark on coil crosshairs as shown.

    Illustration 4:  Landmarking on Coil

  1. Proceed to the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool procedure.
