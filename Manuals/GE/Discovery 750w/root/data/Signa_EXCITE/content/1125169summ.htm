RF Body Coil Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

CAUTION
Dangerous Work Area!
Use of heavy equipment for coil removal and replacement presents hazards to all personnel in the area.

  • Set up restricted barriers and signs limiting access to the work area.

  • Notify all effected personnel in the area prior to starting the service activity. Review safety procedures and restricted areas for the job.

CAUTION
The body coil is heavy.
Weight is approximately 95lbs (45kg) and requires three people in order to replace the coil.

1  Procedure

  1. Read the Hart ID information of the RF Body Coil. Look for the “BoardRevision” information (8 or 16).

    (Step Details)

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO FOR XRFD Amp and PEN Cabinet document.

  1. At outside of Magnet Room, open FRU Box and set FRU Body Coil as follows.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check configuration of dielectric material for FRU Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Bridge Cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Split Bridge. See Bridge Removal and Replacement document for details.

  1. Remove the Front End Bell. See Front End Bell Removal and Install document for details.

  1. Disconnect the two (2) air hoses at the rear end of the body coil.

  1. Disconnect the two (2) drive cables (I&Q) at the rear end of the body coil. Disconnect the DC bias line at the rear, and disconnect the Hart ID cable of the body coil.

  1. Remove the Rear End Bell. See Rear End Bell Removal and Install document for detail.

  1. Remove yellow foam which seals between the acoustical barriers and body coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove bolts fixing the RF Body Coil as following steps.

    1. For front side, remove four M6 bolts and washers from the bases of RF Coil Centering Assembly.

    1. For rear side, remove four M6 bolts and washers from bottom of RF Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Bore Temperature Sensor (shown below) and tape to the magnet enclosure so it is out of the way.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slightly pull out the Body Coil as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove RF Coil Centering Assemblies.

    (Step Details)

  1. Fully remove the coil (as shown below) and place it inside the empty bottom half of the FRU case.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check configuration of dielectric material for defective Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the following Data Analysis Tool (pdf) and follow instruction in the tool to setup dielectric material for FRU Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Transfer the front RF Coil Centering Assemblies (Front) which were used for defective RF Body Coil to the new RF Body Coil.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Prior to each Body Coil Install, inspect airflow gaskets( 5404530 and 5404530-2). Gaskets should be bonded along entire length.

Illustration 13:  Airflow Gaskets

 
 

  1. Slide in the new RF body coil (shown below).

    (Step Details)

  1. Fix the RF Body Coil by bolts and washers by reverse order.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Do not skip RF Body Coil Isolation procedure. It will have big influence to Body Coil Tuning.
 
 

  1. Perform RF Body Coil Isolation.

  1. Reconnect the Bore Temperature Sensor to the coil .

    (Step Details)

  1. Install yellow foam as previously installed. (front: 2/3 of the diameter with the top 1/3 open, rear: full diameter around body coil)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the front end bell. Refer to Front End Bell Removal and Install. There should be a 2.0 to 2.5 mm gap between the front end bell and the RF body coil.

  1. Replace the rear end bell. Refer to Rear End Bell Removal and Install. There should be a 2.0 to 2.5 mm gap between the rear end bell and the RF body coil.

  1. Reconnect the two air hoses at the rear end of the body coil.

  1. Reconnect two cables.

  1. Replace the split bridge. Refer to Bridge Removal and Replacement.

  1. Remove LOTO and perform TPS Reset.

  1. Perform RF Body Coil Tuning.

  1. Restore Defective Body Coil in FRU Box as it was.

    (Step Details)
