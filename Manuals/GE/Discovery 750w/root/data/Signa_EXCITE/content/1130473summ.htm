DTRSW Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
When removing and or replacing the DTRSW on the side of the magnet, ensure that the module is as far away from the magnet as possible when moving it.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the FRU part of DTRSW

  1. Turn off the Scan Room Power Supply (SRPS) at the Penetration Cabinet.

  1. Remove Right Side Center Cover and Right Side Rear Cover. Refer to Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect cables connected to the DTRSW. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the defective part.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents setup by the strong magnetic field interacting with the DTRSW can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the DTRSW on the side of the magnet, always do so by keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it .

  1. While carrying the module out of the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. If you are replacing 3Tw Dual Drive Transmit Receive Switch, remove “Preamp Combiner” and install it on the new 3Tw Dual Drive Transmit Receive Switch.

1.2   Installing the FRU of DTRSW

  1. While carrying the module into the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents setup by the strong magnetic field interacting with the DTRSW can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the DTRSW on the side of the magnet, always do so by keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it .

  1. Secure the module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach all cables that were previously removed. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Replace side cover.

  1. Perform TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Perform Dual Drive Quadrature Tool.

  1. Perform SaveINFO to save new calibration values.

  1. Complete a body and head test scan to make sure the system is functional.
