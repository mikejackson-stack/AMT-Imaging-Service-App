Surgical Table Dock Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
Exposure to Pathogens!
Potential pathogen fluids may be present on the table. If fluids are present, notify hospital personnel for cleanup and disposal.
Wear nitrate gloves and safety glasses while servicing the OR-Compatible Table.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Access to the Operating Room is required to align the replaced table dock to the Operating Room Maquet Surgical Table.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Dock Assembly Removal

  1. Undock the OR-Compatible Table and move it out of the Magnet Room.

  1. Set the brakes on the casters to prevent the table from moving.

  1. Move the cradle forward approximately one foot to allow access to the dock. Refer to Removing Cradle Assembly for details.

  1. Remove the Rear Bottom Cover by removing the four Phillips-head screws shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the left and right Upper Rear Side Covers. (There are seven Phillips-head screws on each cover.)

  1. Remove the left and right Rear Side Bumpers, secured with 3/8-inch hex nuts and brass washers, and the Rear Bottom Cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the Upper Table Dock to the bezel by wrapping duct tape around the end of the table and under the Table Dock Bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three Allen-head screws from the Upper Table Dock via the access holes on each side of the table.

    (Step Details)

  1. While supporting the dock assembly with one hand, remove the strips of tape securing the dock to the table bezel.

  1. Lower the dock assembly and remove it from the table as shown below.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Replacement Dock Assembly Installation

  1. Position the replacement dock assembly into the end of the table.

  1. Secure the dock with the two-inch wide duct tape.

  1. Reinstall the six Allen-head screws (three on each side) to mount the dock to the table. Do not tighten the screws at this time.

  1. Move the cradle back to the Home position to prepare to move the table.

  1. Move the OR-Compatible Table into the Operating Room.

  1. Make sure the table is set to the highest position by pumping the Up pedal on the table.

  1. Carefully dock the OR-Compatible Table to the Maquet Surgical Table.

  1. Verify that the OR-Compatible Table is close enough to the Maquet Surgical Table to allow for undocking of the patient transport. The two retaining pins must be below the patient transport as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure the two tables are parallel with each other.

  1. With the tables docked, tighten the dock assembly's six Allen-head screws.

  1. Undock and re-dock the tables to ensure proper operation and alignment. Make adjustments to the dock mounting position as necessary.

  1. Move the OR-Compatible Table from the Operating Room to the service area.

  1. Reinstall the Rear Side Bumpers.

  1. Reinstall the Upper Rear Side Covers.

  1. Reinstall the Rear Bottom Cover.
