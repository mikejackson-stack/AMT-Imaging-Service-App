Laser Light Alignment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
POISON HAZARD!
1.5T AND 1.0T PHANTOMS CONTAIN NICKEL, A SUSPECT CARCINOGEN.
DO NOT INGEST. DISPOSE OF AS A HAZARDOUS WASTE ACCORDING TO STATE AND FEDERAL REGULATIONS.

WARNING
POISON HAZARD!
3.0T PHANTOMS CONTAIN DIMETHYL SILICONE FLUID. DO NOT INGEST.
IF LEAK OR SPILL OCCURS, WIPE UP OR ABSORB IN INERT MATERIAL AND PLACE IN A CONTAINER FOR DISPOSAL. INCINERATION OR LANDFILL IS THE RECOMMENDED DISPOSAL METHOD.

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the lens cover for the laser lights.

  1. Turn the laser lights on.

NOTE:For systems that do not have Coronal alignment lights, proceed to Step 4.

  1. If adjustment is needed, loosen the flat-head screw, and move the joystick until the laser light is properly aligned. (See Illustration 1 for location of the screw and joystick.)

    (Step Details)

  1. As of August 1, 2008, the alignment light configuration changed so that only one alignment light (shown in Illustration 3) is used for Axial and Sagittal planes. Refer to Step 8 if adjustments are required.

    1. For a dual laser light configuration, adjust both joysticks.

    1. For a single laser light configuration, only adjust the light on the left side.

      (Step Details)

WARNING
Magnetic Field!
When running a Plumb Line, a nonmagnetic device or tool must be used.
do not use a magnetized device.

  1. Using a nonmagnetic device and string, run a plumb line down from the Sagittal and Axial (top) alignment light. Verify that both are aiming straight down. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. When adjusting the Sagittal alignment light:

    1. Make sure the plumb line runs down the side of the laser light cover as shown in Illustration 5.

    1. Verify that the string is properly aligned with the beam, then adjust the Sagittal alignment light. (This light runs parallel to the magnet bore.)

    (Step Details)

  1. When adjusting the Axial alignment light:

    1. Make sure the plumb line runs down the side of the laser light cover as shown in Illustration 6

    1. Verify that the string is properly aligned with the beam, then adjust the Axial alignment light. (This light runs perpendicular to the magnet bore.)

    (Step Details)

  1. If adjustments are required to the Axial or Sagittal alignment light, remove the panel on the upper display by carefully prying it off, and use the joysticks to adjust the alignment light. (See Illustration 7 and Illustration 8.)

    (Step Details)
