After approximately one minute, the user will be asked to confirm the peak power value output from the amplifier. The peak power should be within the limits specified in Body and Head Maximum Power Setup and Calibration, in the Scan section. If the power read from the power meter is within these limits, click [Yes]. Otherwise, click [No] and re-check calibration of RF amplifier.

The tool reports the steps as they progress. The Status field on the UPM Tool interface should say PASS or FAIL within about three minutes. If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting.
