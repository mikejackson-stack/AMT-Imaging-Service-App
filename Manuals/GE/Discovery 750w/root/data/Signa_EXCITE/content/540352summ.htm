Fiber Repeater Diagnostics

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Proper Lock Out / Tag Out as stated in Step 1 must be performed.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Follow the procedures in LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room to lock out and tag out the penetration panel.

  1. You will need to access the jumper on the Fiber Optic Repeater board. This board is on the pen panel and has a clear plastic cover mounted in front of it. Remove the four (4) bolts that attach the cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set loopback jumper in the Fiber Optic Repeater Board (PP1A17A1) located on the equipment room side of Pen Panel, to Position "B" (Loopback). See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. To start the Repeater diagnostics, on the Service Browser's Diagnostics tab, click Fiber Repeater Diagnostics. Select and run both tests. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Return loopback jumper in the Fiber Optic Repeater Board back to position “A” (see Illustration 2).

  1. Replace the clear cover removed in Step 2.

  1. If the Fiber Optic Repeater Board needs to be replaced, see Fiber Optic Repeater Board Replacement.

  1. Do a test scan before customer turn over.
