Rear End Bell Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove the front portion of the split bridge (see Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement).

  1. Remove Rear Cable Cover and Rear Trim Rings

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Rear Cable Cover and Rear Trim Rings

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the nuts and washers holding the Rear Pedestal to the magnet and move the Rear Pedestal away from the magnet.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
The Rear End Bell is heavy. Avoid awkward body positioning. Use proper lifting techniques when handling the Rear End Bell.
 
 

  1. Remove the five screws holding the Rear End Bell to the Arm Brackets.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Several cables are hidden behind the Rear End Bell. Be careful when removing the end bell so as not to inadvertently tug on any cables.
 
 

  1. Remove the Two Blower Hoses from the RF Body Coil.

  1. Disconnect RF drive I & Q cables from the RF body coil.

  1. Disconnect the speaker wires at the top of the Rear End Bell.

  1. Remove the Patient Air Hose from the Rear End Bell

  1. Remove the Rear End Bell.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the two Bore Light Cables.

  1. Reverse steps to install the Rear End Bell. Confirm that the gap between the Rear End Bell and the RF Body Coil is 2.5 ±0.5 mm. Ensure Proper fit of covers.
