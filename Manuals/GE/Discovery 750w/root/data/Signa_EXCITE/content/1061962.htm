System Power On

  1. Confirm that LOTO for Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) is applied.

    DANGER
    ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
    HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
    USE PROPER LOCK-OUT / TAG-OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining the MDP.

    Illustration 1:  General Electric MDP

    Illustration 2:  TEAL MDP

  2. Confirm that the MDP voltage selection is correct. (The MDP is shipped with the default voltage setting of 480 VAC.)

    1. Confirm that the power transformer connections are properly configured.

      Table 1:  Power Transformer Connections

      Incoming VoltageGE Transformer TerminalTEAL Transformer Terminal
      380 VACH3H4
      400 VACH3H5
      420 VACH3H5
      480 VACH4H6

    2. If the power connections are not configured correctly, have the proper personnel correct the wiring connections. (Refer to Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) Service Manual for additional details.)

      Illustration 3:  GE MDP Control Power Transformer (H4 Input Wiring Shown)

      Illustration 4:  TEAL MDP Control Power Transformer

  3. Confirm that the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC) voltage selection is correct.

    Illustration 5:  HEC

    1. Confirm that the switch is in the proper position.

      Table 2:  HEC Voltage Switch Positions

      Incoming VoltageHEC Voltage Switch Position
      480 VACUp
      420 VAC
      400 VAC
      380 VACDown
      NOTE:The HEC can operate at 480 VAC or 380 VAC.

    2. If the switch is not in the correct position, correct the setting.

      Illustration 6:  HEC Voltage Selection Switch

  4. Confirm the incoming power wiring to the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) located in the PGR Cabinet.

    Illustration 7:  PGR Cabinet

    1. Remove the small cover plate.

      Illustration 8:  Pre-DVapps Input Voltage Select Cover (79 kVA PDU)

      Illustration 9:  Input Volage Select Cover (73 kVA PDU)

    2. Confirm that the three input voltage selection terminal blocks are correctly wired. If the power connections are not correctly wired, have the proper personnel correct the wiring. Verify that the connections are properly configured before proceeding. (Refer to the Teal PDU Installation and Maintenance manual for additional details.)

      Illustration 10:  Input Voltage Selection - Set at 480 VAC

    NOTE:The following step only applies to pre-DVapps applications. The DVapps PDU 5343114 is not adjustable.

  5. Confirm the overload and short circuit trip settings for the PDU input circuit breaker.

    1. Remove the small cover plate under the main circuit breaker. Lift open the transparent plastic cover on the lower portion of the circuit breaker to access the DIP switches controlling the circuit breaker operation.

    2. The following illustration shows the position of the DIP switches for each input voltage selection. Verify that the switches under the letter “L” are configured correctly.

      Illustration 11:  Circuit Breaker DIP Switch Settings

      NOTE:If the DIP switches are not correctly set, have the proper personnel correct the DIP switch settings according to the Teal PDU Installation and Maintenance manual.

  6. Notify field service and other installation personnel, who are working at the installation site, that the MDP LOTO will be removed so the MDP power can be turned on.

    NOTE:Before powering on the HEC, check all plumbing fittings and valves.

    • After applying power to the pumps via the HEC, verify there are no leaks. Check the back of the XRM gradient coil hose fittings for leaks.

    • The XRM cooling hoses can leak if a hose is pushed into the connector before the blue lock ring is installed. Install the blue lock ring, then push the hose into the XRM until it clicks into place, indicating that the hose connection is secure.

    The blue coolant fluid can stain floors.

  7. Restore power to the MDP, HEC, then PDU (in this order).

  8. Switch on the PDU's main input circuit breaker.

  9. Press the EMO RESET button on the PDU front control panel.

    NOTE:In the following step, turn on the Gradient 420V Power CB Breaker last.

  10. Except for the Gradient Cabinet power modules, switch all cabinet circuit breakers to ON and GRADIENT 420V POWER CB last. (The Gradient Cabinet power module circuit breakers should remain OFF until the room humidity stabilizes under 60 percent.)

  11. Confirm that the Magnet Monitor power is connected to the customer source.

Leak Sensor Functionality Check

WARNING
Electrocution!
could result if touching the leak sensor strip when PDU is powered on.
do not touch the leak sensor strip.

This check ensures that the Leak Sensors are functioning appropriately.

  1. Power on the Gradient 48V Switch in the PDU. Ensure that all other circuit breakers are Off.

  2. Ensure that the AUX board power is On.

  3. Put a small drop of water on the leak sensor strip, but avoid touching the leak sensor strip while performing this task.

    Illustration 12:  Leak Sensor Strip Location

  4. The PDU Main Circuit Breaker should trip (power down) within two (2) minutes of putting the drop of water on the leak strip. This ensures that the Leak Sensor is functioning properly. If it is not functioning properly, refer to Leak Detection Sensor Theory and Troubleshooting.

  5. Before powering on the PDU, ensure that the Leak Sensor strip is clean and dry.
