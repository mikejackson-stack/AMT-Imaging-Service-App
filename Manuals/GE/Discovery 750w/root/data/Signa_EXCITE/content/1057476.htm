2  Overview

PAC Checks is one of the final steps in the installation portion of the Installation and Calibration Wizard, though it can be done at any time during the installation. During PAC Checks, there are three tasks that must be completed and checked off:

  • Peripheral Pulse Probe Check

  • ECG Leads Checks

  • Respiratory Compensation Checks

3  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click the Calibration tab.

    If in Install or Upgrade mode, select [Install and Calibration Wizard] and Click here to start this tool.

  1. PAC Checks is one of the final steps after Install In Spec as shown below.

    Illustration 1:  PAC Checks

  1. Perform Peripheral Pulse Probe Check. When completed, check the box next to Is Peripheral Pulse Probe Check completed?

  1. Perform the ECG Leads Checks ordered by the customer. When completed, check the box next to Is ECG Leads checked?

  1. Perform Respiratory Compensation Checks. When completed, check the box next to Is Respiratory Checks Done?

  1. Click [Submit].

4  Finalization

  1. Return to the Installation and Calibration Wizard for information on how to advance to the next calibration and links to other calibration procedures.
