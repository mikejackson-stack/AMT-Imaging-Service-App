3  Procedure

3.1  Arm Board Hinge Replacement

  1. Undock the Patient Table and remove table from the scan room to perform work.

  1. Move the arm board on the table into the up position by squeezing the lever on the underside and lifting up. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 1:  Lifting Up Arm Board

  1. Remove the upper side covers nearest the hinge to be removed. (See Touch and Go Strip and Side Bumper Replacement.)

  1. Using the short tip Allen wrench (to access inner screw), remove two screws attaching the hinge to the arm board. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 2:  Two Upper Hinge Screws

  1. Using the short tip Allen wrench (to access inner screw), remove three screws attaching the hinge to the table. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 3:  Three Lower Hinge Screws

  1. Using a flathead screwdriver, remove the two screws from the lever mechanism cover under the arm board. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 4:  Lever Mechanism Screws

  1. Loosen the setscrew securing the cable to the lever mechanism and remove the cable to allow the hinge mechanism to be removed completely. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 5:  Lever Mechanism Cable

  1. Insert the new assembly through the arm board and thread the cable into the sheath and wrap as shown making sure that it is tight enough to actuate.

  1. Reattach the arm board hinge to the table with three lower hinge screws. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 6:  Three Lower Hinge Screws

  1. After applying a small amount of Loctite 242 to each upper hinge screw, reattach the arm board hinge to the attaching the arm board with the two upper hinge screws. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 7:  Two Upper Hinge Screws

  1. Check the functionality of the lever and make sure that the arm board can be moved up and down.

  1. Install the upper side covers nearest the hinge. (See Touch and Go Strip and Side Bumper Replacement.)

  1. Return table to scan room.

3.2  Arm Board Stop Shoulder Screw Replacement

  1. Undock the Patient Table and remove it from the scan room to perform work.

  1. Unscrew the Upper Left Rear Cover. This section removal requires unscrewing the section from both the bellows and the Table. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 8:  Table Covers

NOTICE
Do not position cover in a manner that places stress on the plastic.
 
 

  1. Once unfastened, the Upper Left Rear Cover can be placed out of the way by sliding around handle. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 9:  Upper Cover Removal

  1. Locate the bumper shoulder screw and replace as needed. (Shown below.) Apply Loctite when reinstalling.

    Illustration 10:  Bumper Shoulder Screw

  1. Reverse the previous steps for reassembly.

4  Finalization

Dock Table and verify proper operation from Operator Console.
