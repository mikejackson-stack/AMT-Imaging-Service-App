Auto Coil Install

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Plug the coil into appropriate A- and/or P-Ports. The system automatically detects the new coil and a dialog box describes that the coil has not yet been configured for the system.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Yes] to configure the new coil. A confirmation dialog box appears indicating that the coil was successfully configured.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click the OK button and prescribe a new patient for changes to take effect.

  1. If the required information was not found on the system, and the coil could not be configured, a dialog box advises you to take a suggested course of action.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the system is unable to configure the coil and no media is available that contains the coil data, click the [Cancel] button.
