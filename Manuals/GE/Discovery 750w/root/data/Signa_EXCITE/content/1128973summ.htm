3.2   Hardware Setup

  1. Perform the following sub-steps to set up any Body coil scan on scan window to Setup Driver module BIAS mode.

    1. Dock the Patient Table.

    1. At Magnet Control Panel, press [Alignment Light], [Landmark], and [Advance to Scan] button sequentially.

    1. At the Operator Console, set up any Body coil scan on scan window to setup Driver module BIAS mode. The following steps are example of Body coil scan setup.

      1. Click New Patient Icon.

      1. Enter “geservice” for Patient ID and “111” Lbs for Weight. Then, click [Start Exam] at bottom right.

      1. In the Protocol Selector, Select “Service” from Protocol Library. And, select “snr_check”and “SNR Body Axial, FuncChk:Sys”. Then, Click [>] (right arrow) icon. Protocol is transferred in right field (Multi Protocol Basket).

      1. Click [Accept] for Protocol Selector.

      1. Click [Save Rx] to download body coil scan parameter.

      (Step Details)

    1. At Magnet Control Panel, press [In] button so that Cradle move to In-limit position.

    1. Unlatch the LPCA from the cradle.

    1. Twist the Cradle Release Handle at the rear of the cradle and pull the cradle out to the home position.

    1. Undock the patient Table.

  1. Remove Bridge Cover, Right Side Center Cover, and Right Side Rear Cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place Body Loader with phantom on to the left-right center of the bridge and push it to the Magnet Center (The front edge of body loader is 990mm (39”) away from the Front edge of the Bridge).

    (Step Details)

3.3   S-Parameter measurement

3.3.1  Operation Check

  1. Check the operation by following methods.

    1. From NWA-Setup drop-down menu, select External Tools, and select Rough Tuning.lnk.

      (Step Details)

    1. Check that plots are shown as follows. If not, check bias cables.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove one Bias cable at the Network analyzer Bias T(Port1). Confirm that there should be a significant change in the plots for fS11.

    1. Remove the other Bias cable at the Network analyzer Bias T(Port2). Confirm that there should be a significant change in the plots for fS22.

    1. Restore two bias cable to the Network analyzer Bias T (Port1 and Port2).

3.3.2  Confirmation Measurement

  1. From NWA-Setup drop-down menu, select External Tools, and select Confirmation Measurement.lnk. Write down fS11, fS22, fS21, and MagS21 values.

    (Step Details)

3.4   Data Analysis

  1. Open the following Data Analysis Tool (pdf) and follow instruction in the tool.

    (Step Details)

3.5   Tuning

This section describes how to adjust fS11, fS22, or fS21 according to the result of Data Analysis Tool so that all of measured value are within specification

  1. From NWA-Setup drop-down menu, select External Tools, and select Rough Tuning.lnk.

    (Step Details)

  1. Read fS11, fS22, or fS21/MagS21 value as shown in Illustration 19.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn VC1, VC2, or VC3 using stick to tune fS11, fS22, or fS21 respectively so that fS11, fS22, fS21 are as close as Target Frequency displayed in Data Analysis Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once adjustment is completed, go to Confirmation Measurement to confirm fS11, fS22, fS21, and MagS21.

    (Step Details)

3.6   Further Adjustment

3.6.1  Adjustment by Dielectric Material

Under the following two conditions, adjustment of dielectric material is needed.

  • fS11, fS22, or fS21 < 127.6 MHz at the first measurement of S-Parameter measurement.

  • fS11, fS22, or fS21 > 128.2 MHz at the first measurement of S-Parameter measurement.

  • VC1, VC2, or VC3 reached the end of rotation and it is navigated to perform “Adjustment by Dielectric Material” by Data Analysis tool.

  1. Perform “End Exam” from pull down menu of scan window (End / End Exam).

NOTICE
Lockout/Tagout for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet ust be performed before Body Coil Removal. Refer toLOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet.
 
 

  1. Remove Body Coil Refer to RF Body Coil Replacement for Body Coil Removal.

  1. Change Dielectric Material according to the site condition.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Prior to each Body Coil Install, inspect airflow gaskets( 5404530 and 5404530-2). Gaskets should be bonded along entire length.

Illustration 24:  Airflow Gaskets

 
 

  1. After changing the dielectric material, install Body Coil by reverse order of removal. Refer to RF Body Coil Replacement.

  1. Verify that LOTO is removed and TPS Reset was done.

  1. Set VC1, VC2, and VC3 to the center of rotation by turning VC to CCW until it reaches the end and then turning VC to CW by 23 turns.

  1. Return to Hardware Setup.

3.6.2  Adjustment of MagS21

  1. If MagS21 is failed even though other values are within the specification, perform Body Coil Isolation. If failed after Body Coil Isolation and Body Coil Tuning, replace Body Coil.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform “End Exam” from pull down menu of scan window (End / End Exam).

  1. Restore Cables, phantom, and enclosure.

  1. Run the TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Run the System Gain Calibration.

  1. Run the DD Quadrature Calibration .

  1. Run the Echo Planar Test (EPT).

  1. Perform a quick head and body scan to ensure the system is functioning properly.
