2.1   Unified Phantom Procedure

  1. Place the baseplate of the wrist coil on the patient table as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the base plate riser to the base plate as shown in Illustration 2. Base plate and the Base plate riser in the locked position are shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach 3.0T HD 8-Channel Wrist Array to the baseplate assembly using the knobs is as shown in Illustration 4. Slide the locked coil to center and align properly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the cubical unified phantom in the coil by lifting the upper portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 5. Close the coil and lock it as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to port A of the LPCA and landmark the coil at the marks shown in Illustration 7 and advance to scan.
