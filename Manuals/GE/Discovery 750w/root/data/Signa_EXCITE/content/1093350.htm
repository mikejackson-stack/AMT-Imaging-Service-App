3  Procedure

NOTICE
Only coils manufactured and/or distributed by General Electric should have a shock sensor sticker, and each coil should have only one shock sensor installed. (Look at the rating plate on the coil for this information.) Coils not purchased through General Electric (E-Cats) are not included, and should not have a shock sensor installed.
 
 

  1. Check if the site has any coils listed in either table below.

    1. Look for physical damage to the coil that could affect its functional effectiveness. Refer to the coil operator manual for more details if necessary. If there is physical damage, such as a cracked or broken piece off the coil, proceed directly to Step 6.

    1. Check the coil for a shock sensor sticker:

    • If the coil does NOT have a shock sensor sticker, follow the Coil Shock Sensor Installation procedure to install a shock sensor to the coil.

    • If the shock sensor sticker is present on the coil, continue to Step 2.

  1. Check if the shock sensor was triggered (center tube turns red as shown in illustration on the right side).

    Illustration 1:  Shock Sensor

    • If the shock sensor was triggered (center tube is red) or contaminated in any way, continue to Step 3.

    • If the shock sensor was not triggered (center tube is white as shown in illustration on the left side above), continue to Finalization, Step 1.

    NOTE:Any red or pink color in the center tube indicates a triggered shock sensor as shown below; tube may not be completely red.

    Illustration 2:  Triggered Shock Sensor

  1. Remove the triggered shock sensor from the coil and dispose of it according to local procedures.

  1. Verbally educate the customer on safe use of coils and implications on the warranty for mishandling of coils.

  1. Requalify the coil by running the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.

    • If the coil passes the MCQA test, apply a new shock sensor sticker following Coil Shock Sensor Installation, and continue to Finalization, Step 1.

    • If the coil fails the MCQA test, continue to Step 6.

NOTICE
Remove the shock sensor before returning the damaged coil.
 
 

  1. Replace the coil according to replacement procedures. Fill in the Coil Data Collection Sheet, indicating what failed on the coil, and include a copy inside the shipping container with the damaged coil.

    1. Charge the customer for repair/replacement of the damaged coil per local procedures.

    1. Verbally educate the customer on safe use of coils and implications on the warranty for mishandling of coils.

4  Finalization

  1. Make sure the above procedure is completed for all coils at the customer site listed in Table 1 or Table 2. Return the coils for use to the customer.

  1. Reuse the original packaging of the coil shock sensors for transportation.

  1. If running low, order a new box of shock sensor stickers through SSP Purchasing a couple weeks ahead of the next PM to allow time for delivery.
