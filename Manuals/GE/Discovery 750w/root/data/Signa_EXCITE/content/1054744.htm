2  Overview

This procedure describes how to replace the MNS Exciter located in the 3.0T PEN Cabinet.

3.3  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
Fatal voltages throughout the cabinet when energized.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removing the MNS Exciter

  1. Perform the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove the thumbscrews that secure the MNS Exciter to the PEN cabinet (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  MNS Exciter Cabinet

    ACAN Fiber-Optic Board (CFB)
    BNB Exciter Board
    CMNS BB Exciter Board
    DThumbscrews - Secures module to cabinet frame

  1. Disconnect all the cables to the MNS Exciter. Be sure to note cable locations.

  1. Carefully slide the MNS Exciter out of the PEN cabinet (see Illustration 1).

4.2  Installing the MNS Exciter

  1. Install the new MNS Exciter.

  1. Connect the Exciter cables appropriately.

  1. Replace the thumbscrews to secure the Exciter to the PEN cabinet.

NOTICE
Whenever the PEN cabinet LOTO is removed, the Exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.
 
 

  1. Remove the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

5  Finalization

After installation procedure is complete, follow the MNS Amplifier Calibration procedure to calibrate the 8kW AMT amplifier for 3.0T.
