4  Environmental Requirements

Storage

This equipment must be stored under the following conditions:

  • Ambient temperature range of +5°C to +65°C

  • Relative humidity range of 10% to 100%, non-condensing

  • Atmospheric Pressure Range: 500 hPa to 1060 hPa

Dimensions: 18 in W X 15 in. L X 11 in. H (46 cm x 38 cm x 28 cm)

Weight: 20 lbs. (9.09.kg)

To store the coil and baseplate, a storage space of 55 cm/21.5 in (width) x 45 cm/17.6 in (depth) x 35 cm/13.7 in (height) is required.
