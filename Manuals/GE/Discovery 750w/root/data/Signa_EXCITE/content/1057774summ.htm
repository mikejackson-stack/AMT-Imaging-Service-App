High Speed Stability (HSS) Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   HSS Using Test Port (Preferred Method)

This is the preferred method for running HSS, as the test output is more consistent.

  1. Disable TR Driver faults by moving SW2 on the Driver Module to TR Disable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disable RF Enable on the Exciter.

  1. Connect the TR Filter to the RF Amplifier as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable RF Enable on the Exciter.

  1. Proceed to HSS Setup and Scan to execute the HSS procedure.

1.2   HSS Using Head Port

  1. Disable TR Driver faults by moving SW2 on the Driver Module to TR Disable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert 40 dB of attenuator in between RF Input (J14) and the normal RF Input cable:

    1. Disconnect the RF Input cable from J14 of the RF Amplifier.

    1. Connect the in-line 10 db Step Attenuator to J14 and the RF Input cable just removed.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Verify that 40 dB of attenuation is in series with the RF Input to prevent damage to the coil. Using the Test Port is recommended as the RF output signal has better stability than the Head output.
 
 

1.3   HSS Setup and Scan

NOTICE
Sample motion can affect HSS output. To ensure the vial fits snugly into the coil housing, wrap a piece of tape completely around the vial before placing it into the holder.
 
 

  1. Insert the 0.25 m NiCl2 Sample Vial into the coil.

    1. Ensure no solution is trapped in the top section of the vial before inserting it into the coil.

    1. If solution is trapped in the vial top, correct by flicking the vial with your finger to send the solution to the bottom of the vial.

    1. Insert the coil into the GEM foam positioner. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Place the GEM foam phantom positioner at the head end of cradle.

    1. Connect the p-port adapter to the head carriage and connect HSS coil to the adapter. (See Illustration 6.)

    1. Landmark on the center of the slots in the foam positioner. (Do not try to landmark on the HSS coil).

      (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Coil Adapter to the LPCA:

    (Step Details)

  1. At the MR Service Desktop, select the Image Quality icon, and start the HSS Tool by clicking on the Click here to start this tool link.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify the following on the HSS GUI:

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Scan] on the HSS GUI. (The prescan and scan will be done automatically. No user setup is required.)

    (Step Details)

  1. After the scan is finished, the HSS Viewer will automatically display. See HSS Results.

2  Finalization

  1. Restore all connections to the RF Amplifier.

  1. Remove all HSS hardware (coil, test cables, etc.) from the bore.

  1. Position the TR Driver switch on the Driver Module to TR Enable.

  1. HSS creates extensive locked raw files that must be removed to restore locked raw file capacity. Follow the steps in Step .

    (Step Details)

  1. Under the DISK heading, highlight the HSS files, and click [Delete].

  1. Perform a Check Scan to verify the system is operating normally.
