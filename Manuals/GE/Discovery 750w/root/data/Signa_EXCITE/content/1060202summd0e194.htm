NOTE:If replacing the X-axis XGA unit, you may need to remove the PGR Cabinet door and lean it to the side of the cabinet. Secure the free end of the green/yellow ground wire detached from the upper part of left door with tape to the PGR Cabinet frame so that it is not free to inadvertently contact any of the incoming L1, L2, or L3 AC mains power connections in the terminal strip in the upper left part of the PGR Cabinet.
