3  Procedure

3.1  Body Coil RL Alignment

  1. Find Step Gauge which is placed at front mounting bracket and remove it.

    Illustration 1:  Step Gauge Location

NOTE:Body Coil shape may not be perfect circle and it might be slightly ellipse. So, there are two specs for RL alignment and AP alignment.

  1. At front end, check the gaps between Body Coil and Inner Gradient Coil at 2 horizontal measurement locations (90 deg and 270 deg) using Step Gauge.

    Spec: Differential of Gap (90deg) and Gap (270 deg) ≤ 0.5mm (1 Step)

    Illustration 2:  GAP Check 1

  1. If the gaps does not satisfy the specification in step2, adjust RL position using RF Body Coil support to meet specification of Step2.

    Illustration 3:  Adjustment of RF Body Coil support

  1. Repeat Step2 and Step3 for Rear end.

3.2  Body Coil AP Alignment

  1. At front end, check the gaps between Body Coil and Inner Gradient Coil at 3 vertical measurement locations (0 deg 120 deg, and 240 deg) using Step Gauge.

    Spec:

    • Differential of Gap (0deg), Gap (120 deg), and Gap (240 deg) ≤ 0.5mm (1 Step)

    • Gap (0deg) ≤ Gap (120 deg) and Gap (240 deg)

    Illustration 4:  GAP Check 2

  1. If the gaps does not satisfy the specification in step1, adjust AP position using RF Body Coil support to meet specification of Step1.

    Illustration 5:  Adjustment of RF Body Coil support

  1. Repeat Step1 and Step2 for Rear end.

3.3  Final Check

  1. Check RL position and AP position of Front End and Rear End again per Body Coil RL Alignment and Body Coil AP Alignment and adjust the position if necessary until the specifications are satisfied.

  1. Restore Step Gauge at front mounting bracket.

  1. If you are doing RF Coil Replacement Procedure, return to the original procedure. Changing RF coil isolation/alignment will affect coil tuning
