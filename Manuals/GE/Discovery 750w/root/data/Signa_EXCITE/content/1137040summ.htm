Exciter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
Fatal voltages throughout the cabinet when energized.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Exciter

  1. Perform the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove the thumbscrews that secure the Exciter to the PEN cabinet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all the cables to the Exciter. Be sure to note cable locations.

  1. Carefully slide the Exciter out of the PEN cabinet (see Illustration 1).

1.2   Installing the Exciter

  1. Install the new Exciter.

  1. Connect the Exciter cables appropriately.

  1. Replace the thumbscrews to secure the Exciter to the PEN cabinet.

NOTICE
Whenever the PEN cabinet LOTO is removed, the Exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.
 
 

  1. Remove the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

2  Finalization

  1. Perform Body and Head Maximum Power Setup and Calibration .

  1. Perform Universal Power Monitor - Head and Body Setup and Calibration .

  1. Perform UPM - Body and Head Functional Check.

  1. Perform DD Quadrature calibration.

  1. Perform a quick head and body scan to ensure the system is functioning properly.
