LED Power Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution hazard.
Dangerous and or fatal voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow loto steps as given in the first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels.

1  Procedure

  1. Perform the Lock Out / Tag Out procedure for the PEN Cabinet (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

    Illustration 1:  LED Power Supply

  1. Confirm that the CB1 rocker switch is turned OFF (in the lower position).

  1. Remove the power plug (J1).

  1. Remove the 9-pin output cable (J2).

  1. Loosen and remove the two thumbscrews that secure the power supply in the PEN cabinet.

  1. Carefully slide out and remove the unit from the PEN cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully slide the new LED Power Supply into the rack in the PEN cabinet.

  1. Attach the two thumbscrews and hand-tighten securely.

  1. Attach the 9-pin output cable (J2).

  1. Attach the power plug (J1).

  1. Restore the power to the PEN cabinet (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Turn ON the CB1 rocker switch.
