3.0T HD 8-Channel Torso Array Coil FRUs and Cable Replacements

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Cable Removal

NOTICE
To be performed by Authorized Service Engineer Only
 
 

NOTE:The procedure is for anterior or posterior.

  1. Remove coil center cover by removing all screws around its perimeter (see Illustration 1). Check the connection of the cable assembly in the coil (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut off the cable tie.

  1. Disconnect the PCX plugs PCB (with 4 PCX Connectors) on the cable end (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect DC plug on the cable end from the center board using flat screwdriver and tweezers (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the center frame bridge that holds the cable assembly (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cable clamp (w/ two screws) that holds down the cable assembly (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Unseat cable from cradle of the bottom cover.

1.2   Cable Installation

NOTE:The new cable has two cable ends with the PCX connectors on the board. The longer one is for anterior package (without Velcro belt). The short one is for posterior package (with Velcro belt mounted). The installing procedure is same for both.

  1. Seat the bottom cover (with cable cradle) on the proper position. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Put the new cable assembly (see Illustration 8) into the coil and make sure the cable strain relief matches the notch of the cradle (see Illustration 9) and ensure cable connector end sits level and up right. (The side with “do not loop” label of the in-cable box should face up. If it doesn’t face down, rotate the strain relief.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the center board (see Illustration 10).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the PCX plugs board to the corresponding receptacles on the center board (see to Illustration 2 ).

  1. Fasten the RF cable and DC wires together on the tie mount using one cable tie (see to Illustration 2 ).

  1. Put on the cable clamp holding the cable. Tighten the cable clamp with the two screws (see Illustration 11)

    (Step Details)

  1. Put on the center frame bridge that holds the cable assembly (see to Illustration 5 ).

  1. Close and screw the coil center cover onto the coil. Scan coil to ensure proper assembly (see Illustration 12).

    (Step Details)
