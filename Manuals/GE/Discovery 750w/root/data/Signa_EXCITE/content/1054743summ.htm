1  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT WITHIN THE power distribution unit (PDU) EVEN IF ALL PDU BREAKERS ARE OFF.
Disconnect and Lockout/tagout (LOTO) the power from the system at the facility's Main Disconnect.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Remove any twists and kinks from the cabling. When routing cables, avoid circular loop bundles. Use “figure eight” coils of less than six feet in diameter.

3  Procedure

3.1   System Power Shutdown

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT WITHIN THE PDU EVEN IF ALL PDU BREAKERS ARE OFF.
Disconnect and LoTO power from the system at the facility's Main Disconnect.

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire PGR Cabinet, perform a system shutdown of the system software. The Volume Recon Engine (VRE) System that resides in the cabinet can become corrupted if it loses power while software applications are active.
 
 

  1. To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect the power from the PDU before performing the following procedures.

  1. Notify ALL personnel, who are working on the system, that the system is being shut down.

  1. Shut down the system software before removing power.

  1. For PDU (in PGR Cabinet with plastic safety cover):

    1. Open the front left door of the PGR Cabinet.

    1. Open the plastic cover and flip the operator's workspace host circuit breakers to the OFF position. This allows control upon power up, when this procedure is completed.

    1. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counterclockwise to the green OFF (O) position.

    1. Perform LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem, which will disconnect the INPUT circuit breaker.

3.8   Installing LPCA Cover (5183855-2)

The MNS T/R Module should be removed from the system when spectroscopy is not in use. The presence of the module during a non-MNS scan may cause image quality degradation.

  1. With the LPCA cover off, place the two LPCA rails (P/N 5267384) on the top of the MNS LPCA cover so the notches are facing up. When viewing from the patient end, the right LPCA rail should have the notch farthest away and the left rail closest.

  1. Attach the rails to the cover by fastening the screws from the underside. (Screw heads should be hidden underneath the cover.)

  1. Install the LPCA cover onto the system and secure with four screws.

3.11   Restoring System Power

  1. Notify ALL personnel, who are working on the system, that the system is being powered up.

NOTICE
Whenever the PEN Cabinet LOTO is removed, the Exciter Board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.
 
 

  1. Remove LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Open the plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers, and switch ON the appropriate subsystem breakers.

  1. Place any individual circuit breakers that were turned off to the ON position.

  1. At the Common Service Desktop Manager, select the [Utilities] tab.

  1. Select the [Config File Manager] (screen shown below), and ensure the following parameters match the definitions as stated:

    1. Spectroscopy RF Amplifier = yes to configure the Multi-Nuclear Spectro RF Amplifier (amp to ready).

    1. Broadband Transceiver = yes to configure the MNS Broadband Transceiver (frequency change).

    1. Power Monitor Type = 4.

    1. 3T Spectro RF AMP Type = 0.

      (Step Details)

  1. Click [Save Changes] before exiting.

  1. Click [Quit] to exit the Config File Manager.

  1. Perform Verification of SaveInfo Data.

3.15   MNS Exciter Calibration

To guarantee a linear output from the MNS exciter at a frequency other than 31P and 13C, do a manual calibration using a command in the AGP window. This calibration uses the loopback path only. No RF is sent to the broadband amp. The system only needs to be configured for MNS with the standard MNS hardware (exciter, cabling, etc). It is not necessary to connect an MNS TR module during this calibration.
