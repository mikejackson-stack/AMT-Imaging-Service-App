4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the lower section of the coil on the patient table as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Coil Placement

  1. Align the alignment mark on the coil to zero position on the scale as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Alignment Marks

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom into the positioner as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Phantom Placed into Positioner

  1. Place this phantom-positioner setup on the lower part of the coil as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Phantom Positioner Placement

  1. Ensure that the phantom positioner is aligned with the posterior coil section as indicated by the marks on the positioner and the coil (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  Positioner Aligned with Posterior Coil Section

  1. Carefully align the connector pins on the lower part of the coil with the upper part of the coil and latch both the coil sections into place as in Illustration 6. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)

    Illustration 6:  Coil Sections in Place

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA and landmark the coil at the cross mark available near the handle of the coil (see Illustration 7). Advance to scan.

    Illustration 7:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Proceed to the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool procedure to test the coil.
