Replacement of PAC and SRI-4

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages are Present around the Magnet Enclosure and Rear Pedestal electronics.
Remove Power to The Magnet Enclosure and perform LOck-out/tag-out (LOTO).

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THIS PROCEDURE REQUIRES MOVEMENT OF FERROUS MATERIAL IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO THE MAGNET.
TWO FIELD ENGINEERS ARE REQUIRED AT ALL TIMES.

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage!
Strong Magnetic Field is present.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high-flux density fields.

Safety Requirements:

  • The service path must allow for the part to be moved along the ground a distance equal to that of the distance between the magnet and the foot-end of the patient table prior to being lifted vertically.

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal of PAC

  1. Perform LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Refer to the illustration below for location of the PAC (behind the left side, middle cover).

    Illustration 1:  PAC Location

  1. Disconnect all cables from the I/O panel except the white PPG Cable, which is permanently attached.

    1. Remove the left side covers, referring to Side Cover Removal and Installation, to access the PAC.

    1. Carefully pass the PPG Cable through the opening in the left side, middle cover. (Avoid damage to the cable while removing the cover.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables attached to the side of the PAC.

  1. Remove the screws securing the PAC and PAC interface to the electronic rack.

1.2   Installation of New PAC

1.2.1  PAC-2BV

  1. Place the PAC in the proper location and install it in reverse order from how it was removed.

  1. Attach all previously removed cables to the replacement PAC.

  1. Replace all previously removed covers and enclosure trim panels.

  1. Check the alignment of the PAC with the enclosure in all three planes. Refer to the illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove LOTO and restore power to the system.

1.3   Removal of SRI

Before removing the SRI, the PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Turn off power to the PDU by following LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Refer toIllustration 2 to locate the SRI, under the left side of the magnet enclosure toward the rear of the magnet.

  1. Refer to Side Cover Removal and Installation to remove the left side covers and expose the SRI.

  1. Remove the temperature sensor box attached to the SRI. (It will be reinstalled on the replacement SRI.)

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the SRI.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four screws securing the SRI to the electronics rack; unstrap and remove the SRI.

  1. Remove the straps from the SRI. (They will be used when installing the new SRI.)

1.4   Installation of New SRI

  1. Place the new SRI in the proper location, and install in the reverse order of how it was removed.

  1. Mount the removed temperature sensor box onto the replacement SRI.

  1. Reconnect the cables and replace the enclosure panels.

  1. Remove LOTO and restore power to the system.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a TPS Reset.

  1. Perform a Check Scan.
