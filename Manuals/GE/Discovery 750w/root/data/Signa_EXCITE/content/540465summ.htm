Fiber Optic Repeater Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Proper Lock Out / Tag Out as stated in Fiber Optic Repeater Board Removal, Step 1 must be performed.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Fiber Optic Repeater Board Removal

  1. Follow the procedures in LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room to lock out and tag out the penetration panel.

  1. The Repeater Board is located on the Pen Panel. Using a ¼” wrench, remove the four screws that secure the Protective Cover (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the power harness (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the fiber optic lines from connectors J1-J5 on one side and J7-J11 on the other (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the Fiber Optic Repeater board from the chassis.

3.2   Fiber Optic Repeater Board Installation

  1. Connect the fiber optic lines from the STIF, SRI, and PAC boards to the Fiber Optic Repeater board as indicated in Table 1 (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect power harness to Repeater Board (see Illustration 2).

  1. Install protective cover (see Illustration 1).

4  Finalization

  1. Remove the LOTO tags from the system and restore all power.

  1. Perform Fiber Optic Repeater Loopback Test.
