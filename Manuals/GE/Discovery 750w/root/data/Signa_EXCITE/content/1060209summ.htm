ICW Finalization

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click on the Calibration tab, then click on [Install and Calibration Wizard] ⇒ Click here to start this tool. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Call the On Line Center to perform checkout of the Magnet Monitor. Be prepared to provide the Cryogen ID. (The customer provides the Cryogen ID, usually at the same time as they provide the System ID. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Magnet Monitor Checked out with OLC?

  1. Check the pressure reading on the Magnet Monitor. The pressure reading should be between 3.9 psi and 4.1 psi. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Magnet Pressure Control Functional Check done?

  1. Initialize Planned Maintenance Initialization. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Planned Maintenance Initialization done?

  1. Perform a Verification of SaveInfo Data. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Save Info done?

NOTICE
For a full list of all the components that should have ICD cards with the installation/upgrade being performed, refer to HDv Items to be entered in GIB.
 
 

  1. Enter Product Locator information into Global Installed Base. Collect all Product Locator Cards from equipment, then either:

    1. If not able to access the GE Intranet, complete the information on the cards and send via regular mail.

    1. If able to connect to GE Intranet:

      1. Use web link to GIB: http://gib.gehealthcare.com/.

      1. Log on using SSO # and password.

      1. Select [Global Installed Base Entries].

      1. Scroll down and enter the customer's System ID and Country.

      1. Click [Submit].

      1. Click [New Entries]:

        (Step Details)

      1. For Add (A): Enter A.

      1. Click [Submit].

        (Step Details)

      1. For items that the new components replace, remove their association from the system. Locate the items in the list of items associated with the system on GIB, and type D for Delete and press [Enter].

  1. Click [Submit].

2  Finalization

  1. Dispatch your hours for the installation.

  1. If there are remaining steps in the Installation Calibration Wizard, return to the ICW procedure for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.

  1. If all the steps of the Installation Calibration Wizard are complete and have passed, close both the Installation Calibration Wizard and the Common Service Desktop. The next time the Installation Calibration Wizard is accessed from the Common Service Desktop ⇒ Calibration tab, the wizard will be in Maintenance Mode.
