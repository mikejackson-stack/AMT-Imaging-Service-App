2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test using the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Breast Array (M3335LA).

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Connecting the Coil

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and insert the phantom positioner into the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Placement of Phantom Positioner

  1. The position of the spherical phantoms from the side of the coil is as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Spherical Phantoms - Side View

  1. Place the phantoms on the phantom positioner as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Phantom Placement

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark on the center of the bridge between both the chambers as shown in Illustration 4 and advance to scan.

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
