12267798 - Safe mode startup of the XGD (XGA-SB X axis) failed.
  • Check EMO reset and perform a TPS Reset.
  • In the case where all XGAs report 'Safe mode startup' one thing common to all has been affected. Check condition of 48 V Gradient Power PS2 by looking through vent holes above the Magnet Power Supply hubble connector on the bottom, right front of the PDU. Both green LEDs should be ON. The upper green LED is for PS1, while the lower green LED is for PS2.
  • In the case where one message is reported for a single axis (not all axes), the SFP fiber-optic module in the XGA or XGA control board may have failed. See information in Section 4.3 or Illustration 15 for the process to troubleshoot a defective SFP module.
