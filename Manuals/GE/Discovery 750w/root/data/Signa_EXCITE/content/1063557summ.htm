1.1.3  LVShim PM Mode

The LVShim - Gradshim procedure is performed in LVShim PM mode. The objective is to fine tune the homogeneity of the magnet by using gradients only. The Gradshim process is performed with a sampling diameter of 22 cm DSV and the same bandwidth as Fine Shim, which depends on the quality (good or bad) of the fine tuning. Once the scan passes and all harmonics are within specification, the magnetic field is reanalyzed in Test mode automatically at 45 DSV for customer acceptance.

1.7.2  Test GE Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU)

NOTICE
If the MRU fails the test, arrange for IMMEDIATE remedial service. Failure to have an operating MRU will prevent bringing down the magnetic field in the event of an emergency.
 
 

NOTICE
Avoid a quench of the magnet! Do NOT attempt any tests or maintenance on the MRU module unless thoroughly familiar with the process. No PM step will require pressing the [Emergency Rundown] button in the center of the MRU module.
 
 

  1. Preliminary Requirements

    1. Required Persons: 1

    1. Preliminary Timing (mins): 15

    1. Tools and Test Equipment: None required

    1. Consumables and Replacement Parts: None required

    1. Required Conditions: None

  1. MRU Testing Procedure

    1. Verify that green LED on the front panel (shown below) is illuminated, indicating the battery charger has power.

      (Step Details)

    1. Press the TEST BATTERY switch. The green LED labeled BATTERY will illuminate if the battery is adequate.

      1. If the LED illuminates, proceed to Step 2.g.

      1. If the LED does not illuminate, proceed to Step 2.c.

    NOTICE
    Avoid a quench of magnet! Do NOT use an ohmmeter on quench heater circuits or in switch heater circuits on an energized magnet.
     
     

    1. Open the front cover and check for 20.7 V between TP-1 and TP-2 on the circuit board.

      1. If voltage is 20.7 V or greater, the comparator circuit or LED driver circuit requires adjustment or repair.

      1. If voltage is below 20.7 V, proceed with next three steps.

    1. Check for an open battery fuse F2. If blown, replace with a 2.5 amp fuse.

    1. Visually inspect the battery for signs of leakage.

    1. Remove fuse F2, and check the battery voltage. If battery replacement is required, order three 6 V Gel Cell Batteries P/N 46-294231P5.

    1. Position the TEST HEATER switch to A, and verify that green LED labeled HEATER illuminates.

      1. If the LED illuminates, proceed to Step 2.j.

      1. If the LED does not illuminate, proceed to Step 2.h.

    1. Press the TEST HEATER LED switch to verify LED is working. If LED is OK, but does not illuminate when the TEST HEATER switch is pressed, a serious circuit failure has been detected.

    1. Contact your MAC Team Leader, MR RSE, or the NSC 24-hour Magnet Support Line at 1-800-321-7937 for correct troubleshooting procedure.

    1. Position the TEST HEATER switch to B, and verify that green LED labeled HEATER illuminates.

      1. If the LED illuminates, proceed to Step 2.m.

      1. If LED does not illuminate, proceed to Step 2.k.

    1. Press the TEST HEATER LED switch to verify LED is working. If LED is OK, but does not illuminate when the TEST HEATER switch is pressed, a serious circuit failure has been detected.

    1. Contact your MAC Team Leader, MR RSE, or the NSC 24-hour Magnet Support Line at 1-800-321-7937 for the correct troubleshooting procedure.

    1. Replace the front cover.

1.9   Secondary PEN Wall - Clean Body Coil Air Filter

Preliminary Requirements

  • Required Persons: 1

  • Preliminary Timing (mins): Not applicable

  • Tools and Test Equipment: Screwdriver and/or Pliers

  • Consumables and Replacement Parts: None required

  • Required Conditions: None

  • Safety: No safety considerations

  1. Using a screwdriver or pliers, open the filter clamp and remove the body coil filter shown below.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Do not use any chemical cleaning sprays or fluids. Clean the filter with water only.
 
 

  1. Rinse off the air filter with cool, low-pressure water. Allow gravity to flush dirt out of the air filter by applying water to the clean side of the filter, up and down the length of the pleats.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Use of any other drying method (such as compressed air, dryer heater or heat gun) could damage the filter.

  1. After rinsing the filter, gently shake off the excess water and allow the filter to dry naturally.

NOTICE
Failure to install the filter properly could lead to an improper seal, resulting in equipment damage. Follow filter OEM installation instructions.
 
 

NOTE:Do not apply filter oil prior to reinstalling the filter.

  1. Inspect the filter for damage.

    1. If the filter is damaged, discard it; order and install a new one.

    1. If the filter is not damaged, reinstall it in the same position prior to removal. Make sure the filter clamps are secure. (Clamps are reusable.)
