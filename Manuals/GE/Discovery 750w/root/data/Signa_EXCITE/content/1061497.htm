3  Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table and remove it from the Magnet Room.

  1. Raise the patient table to highest elevation with the Up foot pedal.

  1. Remove the right Upper and Lower Side Covers from the patient table according to Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the table Lock Safety Bar, then lower the table to ensure weight is resting on the safety bar.

    Illustration 1:  Table Lock Safety Bar

  1. Hold the Down foot pedal down, while pumping the Up foot pedal at the same time. (This can also be accomplished by propping the Down valve in the open position with a flat-blade screwdriver placed between the Down valve and the cylinder block as shown.)

    Illustration 2:  Location of Down Valve and Cylinder Block

  1. After all the air bubbles pass though the clear tubes (back to the reservoir), stop pumping and release the Down valve.

  1. Remove the screwdriver to close the Down valve.

  1. Purge the lift cylinder by pumping the table up to the top.

  1. Open the bleed screw a few turns at the cylinder top until oil flows steadily from the screw after the air bubbles purge.

  1. Tighten the bleed screw to just snug.

  1. With the table at its highest elevation, raise the Lock Safety Bar.

  1. Lower the table to its lowest position using the Down foot pedal.

  1. Fill table reservoir with Hydraulic Oil, P/N 46-230006P2, so the fluid is 1/4 to 1/2 in. (6.5 to 12.5 mm) above the Full Line when the table is at its lowest position.

    Illustration 3:  Hydraulic Fluid Reservoir on Table

4  Finalization

  1. Replace the side covers on the patient table.

  1. Maker sure to redock the table.
