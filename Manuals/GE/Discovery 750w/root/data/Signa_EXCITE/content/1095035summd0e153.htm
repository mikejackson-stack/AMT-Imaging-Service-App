NOTE:Use care when moving the Front Covers

  • Both Left and Right Covers have the Vertical Light Strips attached. Disconnect the power cord from each light before moving or replacing (see Vertical Light Strip Replacement.

  • The Left Front cover has the Ambient Room Temperature Sensor attached. Be careful of the sensor cable when setting cover aside. When replacing the Left Front Cover, remove and reattach the Ambient Room Temperature Sensor (see Ambient Room and Bore temperature Sensors Replacement).
