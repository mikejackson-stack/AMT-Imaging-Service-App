Transient Detection Module (TDM) Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
When removing and or replacing the Transient Detection Module (TDM) on the side of the magnet, ensure that the module is as far away from the magnet as possible when moving it.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the TDM

  1. Perform LOTO for Penetration Cabinet before the TDM unit is replaced.

  1. Turn off the Scan Room Power Supply (SRPS) at the Penetration Cabinet.

  1. Remove Left Side Center Cover and Left Side Rear Cover. Refer to Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables connected to the TDM. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) screws holding the TDM to the magnet. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents setup by the strong magnetic field interacting with the Transient Detection Module (TDM) can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the Transient Detection Module (TDM) on the side of the magnet, always do so by keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it .

  1. While carrying the module out of the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

1.2   Installing the TDM

  1. While carrying the module into the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents setup by the strong magnetic field interacting with the Transient Detection Module (TDM) can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the Transient Detection Module (TDM) on the side of the magnet, always do so by keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it .

  1. Secure the module to the magnet by tightening the four (4) screws provided.

  1. Attach all cables that were previously removed. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Replace side cover.

  1. Restore the power using the LOTO for the PEN Cabinet.

  1. Complete a test scan to make sure the system is functional.

  1. Run TNS Datapath Diagnostics to confirm there are no remaining issues.
