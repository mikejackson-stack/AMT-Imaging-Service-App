Universal Power Monitor - MNS Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
Dangerous voltage levels throughout the UPM Cabinet
MAKE SURE That the RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

1  Procedure

1.1   MNS AMPLIFER FORWARD POWER CALIBRATION

  1. Inhibit T/R Fault with switches on the Driver Module on RF System cabinets, Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:This tool must see a Landmark only. It is not necessary to have a Head coil in place, or any other scan parameter entered than what is described below. Simply Landmark on the cradle where the Head coil would be.

  1. To establish a Landmark, Start a [New Exam].

  1. Enter the following parameters:

    1. Patient ID: geservice.

    1. Weight: 111.

  1. Press Align button on.

  1. Move the cradle to the Home position and then drive it into the bore.

  1. Press Landmark button when the align light is approximately where the center of the head coil would be.

  1. From the Common Service Desktop Select:

    1. Select Calibration Tab

    1. Select UPM Tests

    1. Select hyper Link to start UPM tool. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

  1. Select the Nuclei [3IP].

  1. Select the RF Chain you are going to calibrate [MNS].

  1. Select Power Monitor type. [Forward].

  1. Select: [Calibrate] Button

  1. Select [Start] button.

  1. Status should indicate Pass/Fail. If Failure occurs, see If a UPM failure Occurs

1.2   If a UPM failure Occurs

  1. Verify proper calibration equipment setup.

  1. Check cabling between UPM and Amplifier.

  1. Check mgd_stage File: cd /w/config. Remove the “p” if necessary. (p=UPM emulated).

  1. Perform Maximum RF power calibration procedure. Repeat UPM calibration.

  1. Check UPM for power.

  1. Check error log for details of UPM failure.

  1. Perform Body UPM calibration. If it fails, replace detector boards. If it passes continue to troubleshoot problem with Body mode. Problem is most likely with the PA section of the RF amplifier and cabling back to the UPM.

  1. If detector boards are replaced, you must perform UPM calibration to put the new boards into service.

2  Finalization

  1. Select [Quit] to exit the tool.

  1. [End Exam].

  1. Upon completion of BOTH Forward and Reflected calibration: [Reset TPS].

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment from the MNS Amplifier.

  1. Re-attach the MNS Heliax Cables.

  1. Re-enable T/R Drive, see Illustration 1.
