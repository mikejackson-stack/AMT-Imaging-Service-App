ConditionReferenceEffectivity

3  Procedure

3.1   Replacement of RF Body or Head Transmit Cable with Removing Side Cover

3.1.1  Removal of RF Body or Head Transmit Cable

  1. The power in the cabinet needs to be off. Perform Lockout / Tagout for PGR PDU / Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Using an adjustable wrench, disconnect the failed RF Body or Head Transmit Cable from the front of the RF Amplifier, as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove right side cover to access to the RF transmit cables.

    (Step Details)

  1. From the right side opening, withdraw the defective cable and label the cable as “defective”.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the Service Platform in front of the PGR Cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using an adjustable wrench, remove body and head transmission lines from the plate at the top of the PGR Cabinet (shown in Illustration 5), and place out of the way.

  1. Remove plate shown in Illustration 5 by loosening 4 thumb screws.

  1. Disconnect the defective cable from Bulkhead IF Connector.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut the tie wraps from lower position and remove the failed cable.

    (Step Details)

3.1.2  Installation of New RF Body Transmit Cable

  1. Connect RF Transmit cables to the bottom of cabinet IF plate, restore cabinet IF on the top of the cabinet, and restore Tx Cables to the top of the cabinet.

  1. Remove Service Platform.

  1. Fix the RF cable by tie wraps with the other RF cables at three locations.

  1. Route the cable with 90 degree connector into cabinet from the right side opening to RF Amp front panel.

  1. Reinstall the right side cover.

  1. Restore RF Amp cable.

  1. Restore the power using Lockout / Tagout of PGR PDU / Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Go to Finalization ( Finalization, Step 1).

3.2   Replacement of RF Body or Head Transmit Cable without Removing Side Cover

3.2.1  Removal of RF Body or Head Transmit Cable

  1. The power in the cabinet needs to be off. Perform Lockout / Tagout for PGR PDU / Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Before disconnecting any coolant lines on the front of the Dual Drive RF Amp unit, shut down the Power electronics Pump (PE) at the Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) by pressing ▲ and F3 simultaneously. Then switch the PE circuit breaker to OFF.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cables on the front of the Dual Drive RF Amp, and disconnect the coolant lines.

    (Step Details)

  1. Label the failed cable as “defective”.

  1. Carefully move the cables and lines to the side of the cabinet, allowing the surrounding area to be free from any obstruction of movement for when the amplifier will be pulled out. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew the six attachment screws (shown below).

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the two front handles, carefully slide out the Dual Drive RF Amp unit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the Service Platform in front of the PGR Cabinet over the RF Amp.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using an adjustable wrench, remove body and head transmission lines from the plate at the top of the PGR Cabinet (shown in Illustration 12), and place out of the way.

  1. Remove plate shown in Illustration 12 by loosening 4 thumb screws.

  1. Disconnect the defective cable from Bulkhead IF Connector.

    (Step Details)

  1. Label the failed cable end as “defective”, then coil and secure with tie wrap at the rear of the cabinet.

    (Step Details)

3.2.2  Installation of New RF Body Transmit Cable

  1. From the top of PGR Cabinet, route end of cable with 90 degree connector into the cabinet so that it passes though the Cabinet rear opening area to the bottom.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect RF Transmit cables to the bottom of cabinet IF plate, restore cabinet IF on the top of the cabinet, and restore Tx Cables to the top of the cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Service Platform.

  1. Fix the newly routed cable to the rear of Cabinet by tie wrap.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route the cable with 90 degree connector under the RF Amp to the front.

    (Step Details)

  1. Coil excess of failed cable with “defective” label into a loop, secure, and position under RF Amp.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Dual Drive RF Amp into the cabinet completely and fix it with 6 screws.

  1. Reconnect the remaining cables and coolant lines to the Dual Drive RF Amp.

  1. Restore CAM Chassis.

  1. Restore the power using Lockout / Tagout of PGR PDU / Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Go to Finalization ( Finalization, Step 1).

4  Finalization

  1. Perform DD Quadrature calibration.

  1. Perform head and body scan.
