GEM MCQA Setup - PA Lower

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTE:Prior to executing MCQA on 3.0T PA, perform Dual Drive Quadrature Calibration (see Dual Drive Quadrature Calibration Tool).

  1. Place the Flat Filler Panel (shown below) closest to the bore on the GEM table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the two TL Unified Phantoms on the GEM table, centering them in the right to left direction, ensuring that the bottom edge of the second phantom (farthest from bore) coincides with the bottom edge of the PA Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark at the bottom edge of the flat filler panel closest to the bore, and press [Advance to Scan].

    (Step Details)

  1. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool. Ensure that the correct coil is selected in the Current Coil field: 1.5T_GEM_PA2 (shown below) or 3.0T_GEM_PA2 for a 3.0T system.

    (Step Details)
