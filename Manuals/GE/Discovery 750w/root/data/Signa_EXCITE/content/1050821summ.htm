DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

  1. Time method for dissipation of stored energy - 5 minutes.

  1. Visual inspection of Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) power switch.

  1. Multimeter set to read voltage for verification of RF portion of PGR cabinet and PEN cabinet.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) used to ensure Disconnections

Personal lock and tag for each FE at the site working on the de-energized equipment.

1.3   Applying LOTO for the RF Subsystem

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Visually confirm that power is on. Look at the RF Amplifier LEDs to visually confirm that the AC POWER is on (see illustration below).

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the circuit breaker at the bottom of the RF amplifier to the OFF position.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the PDM-RF (or RF+MNS) circuit breaker to the OFF (DOWN) position. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breakers, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment. (Shown above.)

  1. Confirm that the power is OFF. Look at the RF Amplifier LEDs to see that all LEDs are dark to visually confirm that the power is OFF.

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Visually confirm that power is on. Look at the SRPS or PHPS LEDs to visually confirm that the power is on (see illustration below).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the PDM-RF (or PEN Cabinet) circuit breaker to the OFF (DOWN) position. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breakers, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment. (Shown above.)

  1. Confirm that the power is OFF. Look at the SRPS or PHPS LEDs to visually confirm that the power is OFF.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that all energy has been dissipated. Measure the voltage at the Power Distribution Module incoming AC connector and confirm 0 volts. (See illustration below.)
