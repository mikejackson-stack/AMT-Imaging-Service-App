Universal Power Monitor - Head and Body Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
Dangerous voltages throughout the cabinet.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THE RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

WARNING
ELECTRICAL/RF BURN HAZARD.
RF BURNS Can Occur AS A RESULT OF DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES WHILE THE SYSTEM IS SCANNING.
PREVENT POSSIBLE RF BURNS WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM J3, J4, or J22 ON THE RF AMP BY VERIFYING THAT THE SYSTEM IS NOT MANUALLY PRESCANNING OR SCANNING. VERIFY THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE AND THE RF ENABLE SWITCH IS IN THE “OFF” POSITION.

NOTICE
Property Damage! Prevent coil and associated switch damage by removing all phantoms and hardware (i.e., head coil, surface coil) from the magnet bore unless otherwise instructed.
 
 

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage. Do not operate the RF amplifier with an improper load connected to its output unless otherwise instructed. Doing so may permanently damage the amplifier.
 
 

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage. Do not connect/disconnect wattmeter sensor while meter is ON. Always turn meter OFF prior to plugging/unplugging sensor.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Body Mode

When calibrating channel 1, remove the RF input for channel 2 or J21 on the RF Amp. When calibrating channel 2, remove the RF input for channel 1 or J14 on the RF Amp. After performing UPM calibration for Body Channel 1 or Body Channel 2, proceed to UPM Body and Head Functional Checks for the channel being calibrated.

1.1.1  Body Forward Power Calibration

Refer to Body and Head Maximum Power Setup and Calibration, in the section Setting up the RF Coupler For Body Calibration, for instructions on connecting the RF Coupler with the correct Forward Power Sensor. The RF Coupler is needed in order to verify the RF power output before UPM calibration.

  1. Inhibit the TR faults, and disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection.

  1. Verify that switches SW2 and SW3 are in the TR DISABLE and DD DISABLE positions, respectively.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    WARNING
    Electric shock risk!
    pay close attention to the connections to the dummy load. Failure to connect the dummy load correctly could result in electric shock.

    1. Remove the Body Heliax cable from BODY RF OUT / J4 for channel 1 calibration or J22 for channel 2 calibration.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect the 35 kW RF Dummy Load to BODY RF OUT / J4 for channel 1 calibration or J22 for channel 2 calibration.

      (Step Details)

    1. Plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

      (Step Details)

1.1.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Body Forward Power

This tool must see a Landmark only. It is not necessary to have a Body Phantom in place or any other scan parameter entered other than what is described below. Simply Landmark on the cradle where the Body Phantom would be.

  1. Remove the Head coil, and verify that it is not connected.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Start the CSD, then click on the Service browser tab.

  1. If necessary, start the UPM Tool:

    1. Proprietary tool (available to GE Field Service only):

      1. Verify that the service key is installed.

      1. From the Calibration menu, select Calibration Wizard, and select Click here to start this tool.

      1. After the Calibration Wizard starts, select RF/UPM Calibration & Functional Check (Body) from the menu.

      1. Review and confirm the pre- and post-calibration dependencies.

      1. To start the procedure, select Click here to start the tool.

        (Step Details)

    1. Non-proprietary service tools:

      1. To start the procedure from the Common Service Desktop (CSD), select the Calibration tab.

        (Step Details)

      1. From the Calibration menu, select UPM Tool, then select Click here to start this tool.

        (Step Details)

    (Step Details)

  1. In the UPM Tool, set Nuclei to 1H.

  1. Set Task to Calibrate UPM.

  1. Set RF Chain to Body Ch 1 or Body Ch 2 (depending on the channel being calibrated).

  1. Set Power Type to Forward.

  1. Select [Start] and verify that the hardware setup works, then select [Yes].

    (Step Details)

1.1.3  Body Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position (see Illustration 2).

  1. Disconnect the RF Dummy Load from BODY RF OUT / J4 for channel 1 or J22 for channel 2 on the front of the RF Amplifier (see Illustration 3). Leave J4 or J22 open and unconnected.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Return to the UPM Tool interface.

  1. Set Nuclei to 1H.

  1. Set RF Chain to Body Ch 1 or Body Ch 2 is selected.

  1. Set Power Type to Reflected.

  1. Verify that Task is set to Calibrate UPM.

  1. Select [Start] and verify that the hardware setup works, then select [Yes].

  1. The tool reports the steps as they progress. The Status field on the UPM Tool interface should say PASS or FAIL within about three minutes.

    (Step Details)

  1. After completing the UPM Calibration, reset TPS and then perform the Functional Check procedure for the applicable RF output (Body channel 1 or Body channel 2), as follows:

    1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the Down position.

    1. Reconnect the RF Dummy Load from BODY RF OUT / J4 for channel 1 or J22 for channel 2 on the front of the RF Amplifier.

    1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the Up position.

    1. Return to the UPM Tool interface.

    1. Change Task to UPM Functional Check and click [Start].

      (Step Details)

  1. After completing the UPM Functional Check, proceed to the next logical step in the RF/UPM calibration (either Body Channel 2 or Head).

1.2   Head Mode

Refer to Body and Head Maximum Power Setup and Calibration, in the section Setting up the RF Coupler (for Head mode), for instructions on connecting the RF Coupler with the correct Forward Power Sensor. The RF Coupler is needed in order to verify the RF power output before UPM calibration.

1.2.1  Head Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the TR faults, and disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection.

  1. Move switches SW2 and SW3 to the TR DISABLE and DD DISABLE positions, respectively (see Step ).

  1. Move the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter on top of the Penetration Cabinet to the DISABLE (DOWN) position (see Illustration 2).

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Head Heliax cable from HEAD RF OUT / J3.

    1. Connect the 35 kW RF Dummy Load to HEAD RF OUT / J3.

      (Step Details)

    1. Plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

      (Step Details)

1.2.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Head Forward Power

The Head Coil must be installed in order to run the UPM Tool in Head mode.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter on top of the Penetration Cabinet by moving it to the ENABLE (UP) position.

  1. With head coil attached to the LPCA, landmark on the head coil and advance to scan.

  1. Start the Common Service Desktop (CSD), and select the Calibration tab.

  1. Start the RF/UPM Cal & Functional Check (Head) Tool:

    1. Proprietary tool (available to GE Field Service only):

      1. Verify that the service key is installed.

      1. From the Calibration menu, select Calibration Wizard, and select Click here to start this tool.

      1. After the Calibration Wizard starts, select RF/UPM Calibration & Functional Check (Head) from the menu.

      1. Review and confirm the pre- and post-calibration dependencies.

      1. To start the procedure, select Click here to start the tool.

        (Step Details)

    1. Non-proprietary service tools:

      1. From the Common Service Desktop (CSD), select the Calibration tab.

      1. From the Calibration menu, select UPM Tool, then select Click here to start this tool.

  1. In the UPM Tool, set Nuclei to 1H.

  1. Set Task to Calibrate UPM.

  1. Set RF Chain to Head.

  1. Set Power Type to Forward.

  1. Select [Start] and verify that the hardware setup works, then select [Yes].

    (Step Details)

1.2.3  Head Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Before proceeding, disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the front of the RF Amplifier, disconnect the RF Dummy Load from HEAD RF OUT / J3. Leave J3 open and unconnected.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Return to the UPM Tool interface.

  1. Set Nuclei to 1H.

  1. Set Task to Calibrate UPM.

  1. Set RF Chain to Head.

  1. Set Power Type to Reflected.

  1. Select [Start] and verify that the hardware setup works, then select [Yes].

    (Step Details)

  1. After completing the UPM Calibration, reset TPS and then perform the Functional Check procedure for the applicable RF output as follows:

    1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the Down position.

    1. Reconnect the RF Dummy Load from HEAD RF OUT / J3 on the front of the RF Amplifier.

    1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the Up position.

    1. Return to the UPM Tool interface.

    1. Change Task to UPM Functional Check and click [Start].

      (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment.

  1. Reattach all cables that were removed during the procedure.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Confirm that switch SW2 is set to TR ENABLE and switch SW3 is set to DD ENABLE for TR drive fault detection. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Reset TPS and perform a test scan to verify that the system is operating properly
