3  Procedure

  1. Remove the cable from the coil.

    1. Remove the 10 screws holding the cover on the unit as shown in Illustration 1.

      Illustration 1:  Remove The 10 Screws

    1. Remove the cable with connector A.

      1. Disconnect the RF cables with label D1- L, D2- L, D3- L, D4- L and the DC wires from the boards as shown in Illustration 2.

        Illustration 2:  Disconnect The RF Cables With Label D1- L, D2- L, D3- L, D4- L And The DC Wires

      1. Disconnect the RF cables with label C1- L, C2- L, C3- L and C4- L from the board as shown in Illustration 3.

        Illustration 3:  Disconnect the RF Cables With Label C1- L, C2- L, C3- L and C4- L

      1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable with connector A as shown in Illustration 4.

        Illustration 4:  Remove The Two Screws That Hold Down The Cable With Connector

      1. Remove the cable with connector A from the coil

    1. Remove the cable with connector B.

      B side cable removes using the same steps as described in Step 1.b.

  1. Insert the new cable to the coil

    1. Insert The New Cable With Connector A.

      1. Attach the cable with the cable clamp to the coil. See Illustration 5.

        Illustration 5:  Attach The Cable With The Cable Clamp To The Coil

      1. Connect the RF cables with label C1- L, C2- L, C3- L and C4- L to the board as shown below (note: the cables are marked for proper placement) and reattach locks with screws. See Illustration 6.

        Illustration 6:  Connect The RF Cables With Label C1- L, C2- L, C3- L and C4- L

      1. Connect the RF cables with label D1- L, D2- L, D3- L, D4- L and the DC wires to the boards as shown below (note: the cables are marked for proper placement) and reattach locks with screws. See Illustration 7.

        Illustration 7:  Connect The RF Cables With Label D1- L, D2- L, D3- L, D4- L and the DC Lines

    1. Insert The New Cable With Connector B by following the same instructions for the A side, Step 2.a.

    1. Replace the two covers and the 10 retaining screws, see Illustration 1.

No finalization steps.
