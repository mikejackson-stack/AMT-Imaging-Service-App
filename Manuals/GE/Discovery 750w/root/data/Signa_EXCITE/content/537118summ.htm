Alignment Light Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Press Alignment button on magnet enclosure's control panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check whether Axial Alignment light at the top is aligned with the marks on the Bridge as shown (see Illustration 2). If there are no marks in the cradle, verify the Axial alignment light is plumb (that is, pointing straight down).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Some systems may not have the Coronal alignment lights. Skip the next step if the system does not contain these lights.

  1. The two laser lights should shine across the magnet entry be aligned with each other and the marks (see Illustration 4). There should be only one laser line seen (the one from laser source on the left side aligned with the one from right side laser beam).

    (Step Details)

  1. If the lights are not aligned as described in Step 2 (that is, the axial line does not match up to the marks on the bridge or the plumb line), then the top (axial) Laser source at the magnet entry has to be adjusted. If the lights are not aligned as described in Step 3 (that is, you see two lines), then the Laser sources on left and right hand side of the magnet entry has to be adjusted, Refer to Laser Light Alignment Procedures to align.

2  Finalization

  1. Make two marks with a pen in the locations as shown in Illustration 2). These marks will be the reference for future Alignment Light Checks.
