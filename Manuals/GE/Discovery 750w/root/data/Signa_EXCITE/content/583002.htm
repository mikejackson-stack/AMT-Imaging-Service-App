3  Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, see Auto Coil Installation.

  1. Insert the Curved Adapter Panel Assembly in the magnet end of the table as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Placement of Curved Adapter Panel Assembly

  1. Attach the base plate with 3.0T HD 8 Channel High Res Brain Array Coil to the cradle in the same position on the cradle as the standard head coil. Note the serial number of the coil.

  1. Slide the coil back to permit access to the head holder as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Coil Slid Back to Access Head Holder

  1. Place the phantom ring on the head holder. (The edge of the ring that goes toward the magnet has edges that overhang the head holder to secure the ring in position.)

  1. Place the Head TLT Sphere on the phantom ring as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Head TLT Sphere Placed on Phantom Ring

  1. Slide the coil all the way forward (away from the magnet) on the baseplate, and set the locks as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Sphere Aligned with Coil

  1. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil, and Advance to Scan.

  1. Click here to go to the MCQA test.
