Hydraulic Fluid Level Check and Fill

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock Patient Table and relocate outside of magnet room.

  1. Remove Upper Bellows. (For details, see Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.)

  1. Drop table into FULL DOWN position by depressing far right pedal located at rear of patient table. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Patient Transport Rear Pedals

  1. View hydraulic fluid reservoir located at rear of patient table and determine if fluid needs to be added. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pump table top to fully raised position by depressing second pedal from right. (The LPCA reattaches with cradle automatically.)

  1. Add fluid as necessary based on observation from Step 4. See Illustration 2.

  1. Replace Upper Bellows.
