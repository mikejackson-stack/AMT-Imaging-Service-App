Emergency Stop Bypass Troubleshooting

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

CAUTION
ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD!
CONTACT WITH CONNECTORS LEADING TO AN ENERGIZED POWER SUPPLY CAN CAUSE ELECTRICAL SHOCK.
FOLLOW LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE WHILE PERFORMING SERVICES TO ELECTRICAL CONNECTION COMPONENTS.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Power to the PGR Cabinet is turned off and LOTO procedures implemented.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   System Emergency Stop Bypass Test

This test is performed to determine the condition of the emergency stop circuitry in the PGR Cabinet or the Magnet Enclosure.

  1. Verify system has been shut down and LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Disconnect Cable E3002 from Connector J103 (shown below) located on the connector panel on the top of the PGR Cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect 15-pin Sub-D female Jumper to J103 on the PGR Cabinet connector panel as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power to the PGR Cabinet and remove LOTO.

  1. Press the green [EMO Reset] button on the front of Power Distribution Unit (PDU) to reset the E-Stop circuit.

  1. Verify operation of the E-Stop circuit, and confirm that the RF Amplifier and Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) powers on. (Refer to Illustration 5 for a flowchart of troubleshooting activities.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Auxiliary Board Bypass Test

This test is performed to determine condition of the emergency stop circuitry in the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Verify system is shut down and LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Disconnect Cable E3017 from Connector J9 (shown below) of the Auxiliary PWB Enclosure Assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the 15-Pin Sub-D male Jumper to Cable E3017 (shown below) from Connector J9 that was disconnected from the Auxiliary PWB Board in the above step.

    (Step Details)

  1. Restore power to the PGR Cabinet and remove LOTO

  1. Press the green [EMO Reset] button on the front of the PDU to reset the E-Stop circuit.

  1. Verify operation of the E-Stop circuit, and confirm that the RF Amplifier and PHPS powers on. (Refer to Illustration 5 for a flowchart of troubleshooting activities.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Resistance Test

This test is performed to determine condition of the emergency stop circuit.

  1. Using a multimeter and check the resistance between Pins 7 and 15 of Cable E3002 (disconnected from J103 on the connector panel located on the top of the PGR Cabinet). Resistance should read 105k ohms or less.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a multimeter, verify the resistance between Pins 1 and 20 of Cable M3302 (disconnected from connector J40 on the PEN panel). Resistance should read 105k ohms or less. If reading is out of range, proceed to the next step.

  1. Using the block diagram below as a guide, start at J40 of the PEN panel and work through the emergency stop circuit to each emergency stop enclosure, looking for the open circuit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a multimeter, check the continuity of emergency stop paths between the J9, J11, and J40 connectors on the PHPS. (Refer to the complete E-Stop diagrams located in the Power section of System Diagrams > Interactive Block Diagrams on the Service Methods DVD for relevant connector pins.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a multimeter, check the continuity of the emergency stop connections throughout the PHPS.

    (Step Details)
