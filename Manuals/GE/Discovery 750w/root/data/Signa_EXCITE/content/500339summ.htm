ICN Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present throughout the System Cabinet when Power is applied.
Refer To OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.

WARNING
possible personal injury!
The 4170/4170M2 ICN is heavy and weighs approximately 40 lb.
do not attempt to lift the icn unless two people are available.

NOTICE
Follow the procedure mentioned below for removing power to ICN chassis. Failure to properly power down ICNs may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICNs be turned On / Off from the power button on the front. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

1  Procedure

NOTICE
If an ICN in the stack (not at the top of the rack) requires replacement, all the ICNs above it must be uncabled and removed before the failed ICN can be removed. If the ICNs are not individually supported, attempting to remove any one ICN in the stack can result in damage to any ICN above it.
 
 

  1. Locate the ICNs in the system cabinet. Depending on the configuration of the system, the ICNs can be located either below the MGD Chassis or at the bottom of the cabinet. See below.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICNs be turned On / Off from the power button on the front. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

  1. Switch Off the ICNs from the service browser. (Refer to ICN On/Off Procedure .) If there is an ICN in the rack, switch off ALL the ICNs before removing any from the rack.

  1. Perform LOTO for Systems Cabinet or RF Systems Cabinet (RFS).

  1. Remove the power cables, Infiniband and all three Ethernet Switch connections from the rear of the ICNs. Refer to the appropriate illustration below for the applicable ICN.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws securing the ICNs at the front. Refer to the appropriate ICN illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the ICNs out from the front of the cabinet.

  1. (For 5127452-3, 5337894-3, 5337894-4, 5911000-4, and 5911000-5 ICNs only) The bracket on the front of the ICN must be removed (unsnapped) and installed on the replacement ICN.

    1. To remove right side bracket, push downward and rotate to a 45 degree angle to remove as shown below.

      (Step Details)

    1. To remove left side bracket, slide bracket upward as shown below.

      (Step Details)

    1. Install the brackets on the new ICN by performing the above steps in reverse order.

WARNING
possible personal injury!
The 4170/4170M2 ICN is heavy and weighs approximately 40 lb.
do not attempt to lift the icn unless two people are available.

  1. Replace the old ICN with the new one and reconnect all the cables in reverse order. Consult the appropriate system diagrams, if assistance is needed. Refer to either 5133310 1.5T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams or 5133320 3.0T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams .

  1. Configure the new ICN by referring to ICN Reconfiguration.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure the system is working properly.
