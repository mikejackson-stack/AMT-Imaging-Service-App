Power Strip Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

PDU must be powered off.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Turn off power to the PDU by following LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTICE
The next step requires the use of the XPS Lifting Bracket (FRU).
 
 

  1. Remove the XPS 'Z' unit from the PGR Cabinet by following XPS Replacement.

    Illustration 1:   XPS 'Z' Unit

  1. Unhook the power strip cable plug from the top of the PDU near the back (shown below).

    Illustration 2:  PDU Panel

  1. Remove the tie wraps.

  1. Remove the power cable from the back of the PDU.

  1. Unhook the power strip and remove it from the PGR Cabinet.

    Illustration 3:  Power Strip in PGR Cabinet

  1. Install the new power strip and reroute the cable as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the power strip cable to the cabinet with the use of tie wraps at the top and along the side rails of the cabinet. (Tie wrap the power strip cable to the cabinet separately; avoid joining with any existing cables.)

  1. Plug the end of the power strip cable into the outlet labeled, RECON, on top of the PDU near the back (shown in Illustration 2).

  1. Restore the XPS 'Z' unit into the PGR cabinet by following XPS Replacement.

  1. Remove LOTO from PGR PDU/Gradient subsystem.
