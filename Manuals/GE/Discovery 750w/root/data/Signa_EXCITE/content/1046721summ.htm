2  Procedure

2.1   Functional Test

2.1.1  Hardware Setup

  1. Install the T/R module onto the top of the low profile carriage assembly (LPCA) cover.

  1. Connect the cables to the A connector on the LPCA.

  1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

  1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Inner DC block between the QA probe cable and the quick disconnect module.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the QD module to the T/R module.

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

2.1.2  Scanner Setup

  1. Click the [Worklist] icon, then click the [Add Pt Record] icon.

  1. Type MNS Setup (Last Name / First Name) and press <Enter> for Patient Name.

  1. Type geservice and press <Enter> for Patient ID.

  1. Type 111 and press <Enter> for Patient Weight.

  1. Click [Show All Protocols]. From the Protocol Selector library, select Service then double click on MNS_Setup.

  1. Add the Func Test for the nucleus under test to the Multi Protocol Basket. Click [Accept].

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during the Hardware Setup (see Hardware Setup).

  1. Click [Start Exam].

  1. Click [Save Rx].

  1. To set the following control variables (CVs) , click the down arrow next to the Scan option. Select [Research], then [Display CVs].

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the down arrow next to the [Scan] button, then click [Research] and then [Download].

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the down arrow next to the [Scan] button and click [Spectro Prescan].

  1. Slide [R1] and [R2] to the maximum positions.

  1. Set [TG] (Transmit Gain) to 0 (zero).

  1. Set the top display mode to [Magnitude].

  1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a peak in the top display and a free induction decay (FID) in the bottom display as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the center frequency to center the peak in the Magnitude window using [Delta Frequency DX ]. After each adjustment click [Apply].

  1. Record the Magnitude peak height. The peak height at TG = 0 should exceed the minimum acceptable value in Table 1.

  1. Use the ZOOM Mode functions to measure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Done] when complete.

2.2   Signal to Noise Test

If the Functional Test procedure Functional Test has successfully completed and you now want to begin the Signal to Noise Test, select [SNR Test] from the MNS-Setup area and select [Setup]. Then proceed to Scanner Setup, Step 9.

2.2.1  Hardware Setup

  1. Install the T/R module onto the top of the LPCA cover.

  1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

  1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place.

  1. Connect Inner DC Block to BNC connector. See Illustration 3.

  1. Connect the QA probe cable to the quick disconnect module.

  1. Connect the QD module to the T/R module.

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

2.2.2  Scanner Setup

  1. Click the [Worklist] icon, then click the [Add Pt Record] icon.

  1. Type MNS Setup (Last Name / First Name) and click on <Enter> for Patient Name.

  1. Type geservice then click on <Enter> for Patient ID.

  1. Type 111 and click on <Enter> for Patient Weight.

  1. Click [Show All Protocols]. From the Protocol Selector library choose Service. From the list select MNS_Setup.

  1. Add the [SNR Test] for the nucleus under test to the Multi Protocol Basket. Click [Accept].

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during Hardware Setup.

  1. Click [Start Exam].

  1. Click [Save Rx].

  1. To set the following CVs, click the down arrow next to the Scan option. Select [Research], then [Display CVs].

    1. flip_rf1 = 2, click <Enter>.

    1. pw_rf1 = 40, click <Enter>.

    1. ia_rf1 = 32767, click <Enter>.

    1. xmtaddSCAN = 200, click <Enter>.

    1. Click [Accept].

  1. Select the down arrow next to the [Scan] button, then click [Research] and then [Download].

  1. Select the dwon arrow next to the [Scan] button and then clcik [Spectro Prescan]

  1. Slide [R1] and [R2] to the maximum position.

  1. Set [TG](Transmit Gain) to 0 (zero).

  1. Set the top display mode to [Magnitude].

  1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a peak in the top display and a free induction decay (FID) in the bottom display.

  1. Adjust the center frequency to center the peak in the Magnitude window.

  1. Change the display mode in the top window to [Pure Absorp]. The shape of the peak changes with some of the signal dipping below zero. The distortion varies depending on the QA coil and scanner.

  1. Use the [Zero Order Phase] control to adjust the shape of the peak until it is fully positive and symmetric (see Illustration 7). The Zero Order Phase control was used to correct the spectrum until all signals were positive and the peak shape was symmetric.

    (Step Details)

  1. Increase [TG] by units of 10 until the peak decreases and becomes slightly negative, about 180.

  1. Decrease [TG] slowly until the peak is split half-positive and half-negative (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Record this null value for [TG].

  1. Subtract 60 units from the null [TG] value and set [TG] to this value. Now the peak is positive again and at maximum amplitude.

  1. Click [Stop] and then click [Done].

2.3   Noise Test

If the Functional Test procedure, Functional Test, has successfully completed and you now want to begin the Noise Test, select [SNR Test] from the MNS Setup area and select [Setup]. Then proceed to Scanner Setup, Step 9.

2.3.1  Hardware Setup

  1. Install the TR module onto the top of the LPCA cover.

  1. Connect the cables to the A connector on the LPCA.

  1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

  1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place.

  1. Connect Inner DC Block between the QA probe cable and the quick disconnect module. See Illustration 3.

  1. Connect the QD module to the TR module.

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

2.3.2  Scanner Setup

  1. Click the [Worklist] icon, then click the [Add Pt Record] icon.

  1. Type MNS Setup (Last Name / First Name) and click on <Enter> for Patient Name.

  1. Type geservice then click on <Enter> for Patient ID.

  1. Type 111 and click on <Enter> for Patient Weight.

  1. Click [Show All Protocols]. From the Protocol Selector library choose Service. From the list select MNS_Setup.

  1. Add the Noise Test for the nucleus under test to the Multi Protocol Basket. Click on [Accept].

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during Hardware Setup.

  1. Click [Start Exam].

  1. Click [Save Rx].

  1. To set CV ia_rf1 = 0, click the down arrow next to the Scan option. Select [Research]and then [Display CVs]. Change the value for the CV and click [Accept].

  1. Select the down arrow next to the [Scan] button, then click [Research] and the [Download].

  1. Select the dwon arrow next the [Scan] button, then click [Spectro Prescan].

  1. Slide [R1] and [R2] to the maximum position.

  1. Set [TG] to 0 (zero).

  1. Turn the RF ENABLE Switch on the MNS Exciter OFF by flipping it down (see Illustration 13).

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the top display mode to [Power].

  1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a band of noise across the power spectrum. See Illustration 14. There should be no distinct broad peaks across the spectrum. The noise intensity should taper off near the edges.

    (Step Details)

  1. The tops of the noise should be about even across the window without any peaks. Peaks in the power spectrum may indicate a source of noise in the scan room.

  1. Click [Stop] and then click [Done].

2.3.3  Scanning and Analysis

  1. Click [Scan] to collect the Noise data.

  1. Switch to the [Tools] Workspace and open a Command Window.

  1. Type sage then click <Enter> to initiate the SAGE spectroscopy analysis tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on, in order, [File], [Load], and [Rawdata] to open the Raw Load data dialog window.

    (Step Details)

  1. When the scan is finished click [Update List] to refresh the list of P files to choose from (see Illustration 16).

  1. The upper-most P file is the latest. Select this file and click [Load].

  1. In the main SAGE panel, click in order [Macros], [Func Tests], and [SNR Test…].

  1. Move the windows around if necessary.

  1. Set up the SNR Test window as listed in Table 3 for nucleus under test.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Run] on the SNR Test window. The noise is calculated for the active data set and reported in the command window where SAGE was started.

  1. Record the n=32 value for Noise. Compare to the maximum acceptable.

    (Step Details)

  1. In the spectrum window, select Chan then click on [Power].

  1. The spectrum should be flat across the window with tapering to zero at each edge as shown in Table 1. Broad peaks in this window suggest unwanted noise in the scan room. Use this test as a tool for tracking down noise sources if found (see Illustration 19). There should be no distinct broad peaks across the spectrum. The noise intensity should taper off near the edges.

    (Step Details)

  1. To zoom out and see the full spectrum, click [Zoom], then right click in the window and select [Unzoom]. Right click again and click [Quit] to exit the zoom control.

  1. Turn the RF ENABLE Switch on the MNS Exciter ON by flipping it upward (see Illustration 13).

3  Finalization

  1. Remove any hardware used during system testing.

  1. Do a test scan of the system to ensure proper operation.
