Fiber Optic Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Using the GE Service Browser, select Diagnostics > System Function > Peripheral Communications > Fiber Optic Diagnostics to navigate to the PSE (and PSEIO) Fiber Optic Test screen (shown below).

    Illustration 1:  PSE (and PSEIO) Fiber Optic Test

  1. Click [Force ON] to turn on all fibers.

  1. Visually inspect the PAC out connector:

    1. The PAC Out (J10) connector of the PSE board should be bright.

    1. The PSEIO connector should be bright.

    1. If using a light meter, the output should be > -17 dBm.

  1. Click [Force OFF] to turn off all fibers. Confirm that all fibers (PAC Out and PSEIO connectors) are dark.

  1. If any of the results fail, replace the PSE board.

2  Finalization

  1. Conclude the testing by clicking [STOP].

  1. Verify all removed fiber optic cables are reinstalled correctly.

  1. Click [Exit] to quit the tool.
