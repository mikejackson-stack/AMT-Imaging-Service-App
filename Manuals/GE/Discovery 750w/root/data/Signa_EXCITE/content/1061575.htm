2  Overview

This document describes the removal and installation of the Rear and Front Microphones. The Rear Microphone is located inside the LPCA and the Front Microphone is attached to the Front End Bell. Both microphones connect to the SRI.

4  Procedure

4.1  Replacing Rear Microphone

Before replacing a microphone, plug the replacement into the SRI (J31) to verify that the replacement microphone will fix the problem.

  1. Drive the LPCA and Cradle into bore to within 1 foot (30cm) of end of bridge.

  1. At the foot of the table, twist or squeeze the Cradle Release Handle to detach the Cradle from the LPCA and then pull the cradle by hand to Home position so that it is fully retracted back onto table top.

  1. Follow all Lock Out/Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. (See LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics.)

  1. Remove the LPCA Cover (four screws).

    Illustration 1:  Remove the LPCA Cover

  1. Pull the microphone and housing out of the LPCA.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Microphone and Housing from LPCA

  1. Remove the microphone from its housing by gently pulling microphone to the rear. (See Illustration 3.)

    Illustration 3:  Removing Microphone from Housing

  1. To remove and install rear microphone, refer to LPCA Cable Tracks Replacement to replace the Cable Track.

    NOTE: Individual cables cannot be ordered or replaced. Order the entire cable track.

  1. Remove the Left Side Middle Cover (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. Install the Microphone into the housing.

  1. Install the Microphone and housing into the LPCA.

NOTE: Take care that the receive cables are tucked in well, so as to not nick the exposed insulation on the cable track wires.

  1. Install the LPCA cover and four screws.

5  Finalization

  1. Run a quick check to ensure the microphone is functioning properly.

  2. Run the Coil Datapath Diagnostic (CDD).
