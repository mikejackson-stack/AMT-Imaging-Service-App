Rear Cover Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the Rear Pedestal Covers (five screws, two at the top of each cover and one holding both covers at the lower back).

  1. Remove the two Rear Cable Cover screws and slide the cable cover straight up to disengage from the upper hooks.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the appropriate Side Covers for the Left or Right Rear Cover (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. Remove Rear Trim Rings.

  1. Remove the appropriate Rear Cover by removing the screws shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reverse the removal procedure to install the Rear Cover. Ensure proper fit.
