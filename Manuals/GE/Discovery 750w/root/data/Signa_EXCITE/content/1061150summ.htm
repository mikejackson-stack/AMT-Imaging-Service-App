Patient Table Hydraulic Cylinder Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Hydraulic Cylinder Removal

  1. Undock the Patient Table and remove it from the Magnet Room.

  1. Raise the Patient Table to the full up position.

  1. Fasten the Upper Bellows in the up position, and remove the Lower Base Side Cover as explained in Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the table Lock Safety Bar, then lower the table to ensure weight is resting on the safety bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release the cradle and manually slide the cradle forward and prop it up (as shown below) for access to the table top height adjustment screw, or remove the cradle completely. For more information, refer to Removing Cradle Assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a flat-blade screwdriver to remove the cover plug from the table top height adjustment screw.

  1. Place a piece of tape over part of the E-clip, and use needle-nose pliers to remove the clip from the end of the adjustment screw.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the cradle was not removed, slide the cradle back onto the table.

  1. If a Table Interface Panel (TIP) is present at the lower front of the table chassis, remove the right and left slotted screws from the TIP.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the tie wrap and black cushion tape from the hydraulic line shown above.

NOTICE
If a TIP is present, avoid stripping the lightweight aluminum screws when disconnecting the black cable connectors from the TIP.
 
 

  1. If a TIP is present on right side of table chassis, remove all front side cable connectors from the TIP, and secure them out of the way to fully expose the base of the hydraulic cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. While moving the Table Down Rod to the rear of the table, pull down on the hydraulic shaft until it is fully retracted.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Up Sensor Bracket.

  1. Place sufficient absorbent towels underneath the work area.

  1. Disconnect the black hydraulic lines from the base of hydraulic cylinder using two 3/4 inch open-end wrenches, then fasten the lines up and to the side to avoid excess spillage. To prevent oil from draining from the reservoir, clamp the clear tube, remove the black clamp, then remove the clear tube.

  1. Remove the E-clip from the end of the cylinder mounting pin, located at base of the cylinder assembly.

  1. Line up the cutout on the caster linkage with the pin, and slide the cylinder mounting pin out from the base of the hydraulic cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the hydraulic cylinder from the base of the table chassis by angling the cylinder top toward the rear while lifting the base up and out. Be careful of linkage attached to the down valve.

  1. Remove the Up sensor collar by pressing the pin out.

  1. Remove the screw and washer from the cylinder.

1.2   Hydraulic Cylinder Replacement

  1. Replace the collar and pin on the hydraulic cylinder so it is flush at both ends.

  1. Replace the washer and screw on the new cylinder.

  1. Prepare the replacement cylinder for installation by pressing the cylinder to its fully retracted position. (It may be necessary to move the linkage to fit the actuation pin of the Down value into the groove of the linkage.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully angle the bottom of the hydraulic cylinder into the base of the table to avoid bending the actuation pin on the Down valve.

  1. Insert the cylinder mounting pin to secure the base of the hydraulic cylinder to the frame of the table and reattach the E-clip.

  1. Reattach the Up Sensor Bracket.

  1. Reconnect the black hydraulic lines to the base of the hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Hold the Down valve open and exercise the Up Pump foot pedal to bleed the hydraulic system by shunting air to the hydraulic reservoir.

  1. If the cradle is still attached to the table, release the cradle and manually slide it forward to access the table top adjustment screw hole.

  1. Align the upper cylinder shaft with collar (hole) in the bottom of the table, then slowly pump up the cylinder until the table raises slightly; guide the Up Sensor Bracket into position.

    (Step Details)

  1. When top of cylinder reaches table top, reattach the E-clip.

  1. Ensure that Lock Safety Bar is in the vertical (up) position.

  1. Partially open the bleed screw on the hydraulic cylinder until no air bubbles are visible.

  1. Open the Down valve and lower the table to its lowest position.

  1. When the table is fully up, bleed the cylinder again by partially opening the bleed screw until no air bubbles are visible.

1.3   Reassembly

NOTICE
When reconnecting the large J1/J2 and J4/J5 black cable connectors, their lightweight aluminum screws can be easily stripped. Do not over torque when replacing the cable connectors on the TIP, and avoid overtightening the screws shown below.
 
 

  1. If a TIP is present, reconnect all cable connectors to the front side of the TIP as shown in Illustration 4, and replace both TIP screws on each side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Upper bellows in the down position, and replace both lower base side and front base covers.

  1. Replace the cover plug on table top, then replace the cradle assembly.

1.4   Final Calibration and Adjustment

  1. Perform Table Top Height Adjustment.

  1. If necessary, add hydraulic oil to the reservoir. (Level should be 6.5 to 12.5 mm above the Full Line when the table is at its lowest position.)

  1. Inspect the Down Valve Linkage Adjustment according to the illustration below. Obtain the desired tolerance between the down valve linkage and bracket by adjusting the two set screws on the bracket.

    (Step Details)
