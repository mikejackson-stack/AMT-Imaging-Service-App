  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present throughout the System Cabinet when Power is applied.
Refer To OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
ICN modules weigh approximately 40 lbs. and require two people to lift them.

NOTICE
Follow the procedure mentioned below for removing power to ICN chassis. Failure to properly power down ICN’s may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICN's be turned ON/OFF from the power button on the front. The ICN's have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Replacing ICN 4170 with ICN 4100

1.1.1  Removing ICN 4170

  1. Locate the ICN 4170 in the Power Gradient RF (PGR) system cabinet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Power down the host.

  1. Perform LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  1. Loosen the thumbscrews on each side of the ICN (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide out the ICN until the stop is reached at the end of the side rails.

  1. Remove all the connections at the back of the ICN.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
The 4170 ICN weighs approximately 40 lbs. and requires two people to lift it.

  1. Locate the two release tabs on the slide rails (see Illustration 4). Press both release tabs toward the center of the ICN while pulling the ICN forward until the rails release and the ICN is free. Place the ICN to the side in a safe area.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the network switch and slide it down to access mounting screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate and remove the four mounting screws for each side-mount bracket (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

1.1.2  Installing ICN 4100

  1. Attach slide rails to mounting brackets using 3 screws each.

  1. Locate placement for mounting bracket in the cabinet. Mount brackets for installation of ICN 4100 units (see Illustration 7). Make sure to orient the brackets with the slot on the bottom (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach trays to mounting brackets as shown below.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
The 4100 ICN weighs approximately 40 lbs. and requires two people to lift it.

  1. Insert ICN 4100 units into the PGR cabinet. Slide the ICNs out until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails. Make sure the release tabs engage and the ICNs are locked into the rails.

  1. Connect all cables listed in Table 1. Illustration 10 shows the orientation of cables when looking at the rear of the ICN. Green indicates ports that should have cable inputs; red indicates ports that should NOT have cable inputs.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the release tabs and push the ICNs into the PGR cabinet until the ICNs are completely inside.

  1. Tighten the thumbscrews on the front of the ICNs to secure.

1.1.3  Initializing ICN 4100

  1. Remove LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  1. Power on the host.

    (Step Details)

  1. Upon boot up, log in at root and invoke Guided Install.

  1. Configure the new ICN (see VRE Reconfiguration).

1.2   Replacing ICN 4100 with ICN 4170

1.2.1  Removing ICN 4100

  1. Locate the ICN 4100 in the Power Gradient RF (PGR) system cabinet (see Illustration 12).

    (Step Details)

  1. Power down the host.

  1. Perform LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  1. Loosen the thumbscrews on each side of the ICN(s). (See Illustration 13.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide out the ICN(s) until the stop is reached at the end of the side rails.

  1. Remove all the connections at the back of the ICN(s).

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
The 4100 ICN weighs approximately 40 lbs. and requires two people to lift it.

  1. Locate the two release tabs on the slide rails (see Illustration 15). Press both release tabs toward the center of the ICN while pulling the ICN forward until the rails release and the ICN is free. Place the ICN to the side in a safe area.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the ICN trays from the ICN mounting brackets.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three screws securing each mounting bracket for the ICN.

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  Installing ICN 4170

  1. Loosen the mounting screws for the network switch and slide the switch down to give access to the mounting location for the slide rails for the ICN 4170.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the slide rails for the ICN 4170 module to the mounting rails in the PGR cabinet using four screws each.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Possible personal injury!
The 4170 ICN weighs approximately 40 lbs. and requires two people to lift it.

  1. Insert the ICN 4170 unit into the PGR cabinet. Slide the ICNs out until the stop is reached at the end of the slide rails. Make sure the release tabs engage and the ICNs are locked into the rails.

  1. Connect all cables listed in Table 2. Illustration 20 shows the orientation of cables when looking at the rear of the ICN. Green indicates ports that should have cable inputs; red indicates ports that should NOT have cable inputs.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the rail release tabs and push the ICN module into the PGR cabinet until the ICN is completely inside.

  1. Tighten the thumbscrews on the front of the ICN to secure it to the cabinet mounting rails.

  1. Move the network switch back into its position and secure it with its mounting screws.

1.2.3  Initializing ICN 4170

  1. Remove LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment.

  1. Power on the host.

    (Step Details)

  1. Upon boot up, log in at root and invoke Guided Install.

  1. Configure the new ICN (see VRE Reconfiguration.

  1. Perform Loading Host System Software.

    (Step Details)
