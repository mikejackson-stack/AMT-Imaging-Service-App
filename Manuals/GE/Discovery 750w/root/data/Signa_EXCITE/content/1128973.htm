2  Overview

Body Coil Tuning is performed by adjusting Variable Capacitors (VC1, VC2, VC3) to tune fS11, fS22, fS21, and MagS21 so that these parameters are within specification. If it is difficult to adjust parameters by Variable Capacitors, replacement of dielectric material might be required that is described in Section 4.6.1.

4.4  Data Analysis

  1. Open the following Data Analysis Tool (pdf) and follow instruction in the tool.

    NOTE:

    • Adobe Acrobat 9 Reader or higher is required. Please ask IT Help Desk if needed.

    • Click on the screen of pdf once to activate the tool.

    Media File 1:  Data Analysis Tool

    Follow the navigation in the Tool.

    NOTE:This tool analyzes the followings;

    1. Tool Analyzes if fS11 and fS22 are within 127.6 MHz ~ 128.2 MHz for first measurement. If fS11, or fS22 is out of 127.6 MHz ~ 128.2 MHz, navigate to Sec 4.6.1 adjustment by dielectric material.

      • fS11 or fS22 > 128.2 MHz: Go to Adjustment by Dielectric Material (Increase Dielectric Material by one step).

      • fS11 or fS22 < 127.6 MHz: Go to Adjustment by Dielectric Material (Decrease Dielectric Material by one step).

    2. If all of measured values satisfy specification by PASS indication, tool shows the message “All Pass. End of Tuning”. This is end of Body Coil Tuning. Go to Finalization, Step 2.

    3. If any measured values are out of specification and all of VC do not reach the end of rotation, the tool will navigate to adjust body coil by VC1, VC2, or VC3 described in Tuning.

    4. If any measured value is out of specification and VC reached the end of rotation, this tool judge whether it is possible to tune by VC or dielectric material replacement is required. If tuning by VC1, VC2, VC3 is possible, tool will navigate to adjust body coil by VC1, VC2, or VC3 described in Tuning. If replacement of dielectric material is required, tool will navigate to Adjustment by Dielectric Material.

4.5  Tuning

This section describes how to adjust fS11, fS22, or fS21 according to the result of Data Analysis Tool so that all of measured value are within specification

  1. From NWA-Setup drop-down menu, select External Tools, and select Rough Tuning.lnk.

    Illustration 18:  Rough Tuning

  1. Read fS11, fS22, or fS21/MagS21 value as shown in Illustration 19.

    Illustration 19:  Rough Tuning Plots

    NOTE:To show single waveform window in a display, double click on the desired window to expand. To restore to the four windows mode, double click again on the window.

  1. Turn VC1, VC2, or VC3 using stick to tune fS11, fS22, or fS21 respectively so that fS11, fS22, fS21 are as close as Target Frequency displayed in Data Analysis Tool.

    NOTE:1 CW Turn of VC1 => -10 kHz to -20 kHz of fS11, 1 CW Turn of VC2 => -10 kHz to -20 kHz of fS22

    NOTE:1 CW Turn of VC3 => +15 kHz to +40 kHz of fS21. fS11 and fS22 will be slightly decreased by tuning VC3 to CW direction. Please re-confirm that fS11 and fS22 values after VC3 adjustment.

    Illustration 20:  Tuning

  1. Once adjustment is completed, go to Confirmation Measurement to confirm fS11, fS22, fS21, and MagS21.

    Tips for Fine Tuning: If fS11, fS22, and fS21 are within Target Frequency +/- 150KHz but some results are still out of specification, use “Confirmation Measurement.lnk” described in Confirmation Measurement to check Frequency in stead of “Rough Tuning.lnk”.

    The difference of “Rough Tuning Mode” and “Confirmation Measurement Mode” is as follows.

    • Rough Tuning Mode: Real time measurement. But, the measured value is not stable enough for fine tuning.

    • Confirmation Measurement Mode: Accurate measurement value is shown by single sweep. But, it takes time to show result.

4.6  Further Adjustment

4.6.1  Adjustment by Dielectric Material

Under the following two conditions, adjustment of dielectric material is needed.

  • fS11, fS22, or fS21 < 127.6 MHz at the first measurement of S-Parameter measurement.

  • fS11, fS22, or fS21 > 128.2 MHz at the first measurement of S-Parameter measurement.

  • VC1, VC2, or VC3 reached the end of rotation and it is navigated to perform “Adjustment by Dielectric Material” by Data Analysis tool.

  1. Perform “End Exam” from pull down menu of scan window (End / End Exam).

NOTICE
Lockout/Tagout for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet ust be performed before Body Coil Removal. Refer toLOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet.
 
 

  1. Remove Body Coil Refer to RF Body Coil Replacement for Body Coil Removal.

  1. Change Dielectric Material according to the site condition.

    • fS11 or fS22 > 128.2 MHz: Increase Dielectric Material by one step. Refer to Illustration 21.

    • VC reached the end of rotation and fS11 or fS22 >128.05 MHz, or fS21 > (fS11 + fS22)/2 + 0.05 MHz: Increase Dielectric Material by one step. Refer to Illustration 21.

      Illustration 21:  Increase Dielectric Material by one step

    • fS11or fS22 < 127.6 MHz: Decrease Dielectric Material by one step. Refer to Illustration 22.

    • VC reached the end of rotation and fS11 or fS22 < 127.75 MHz, or fS21 < (fS11 + fS22)/2 - 0.05 MHz: Decrease Dielectric Material by one step. Refer to Illustration 22.

      Illustration 22:  Decrease Dielectric Material by one step

    NOTE:If there is no Body Coil FRU case available, place the body coil on two pads, making sure they protect the drive cables of body coil from damage. Make sure the body coil does not roll over.

    NOTE:If 16 LOWER SHIFT pieces are attached on Body Coil, no additional material needed for replacement.

    NOTE:FRU of Body Coil includes dielectric materials for adjustment and no need to order “Dielectric Material FRU Kit”. If dielectric material is required after XRMw replacement, order “Dielectric Material FRU Kit (5423650).

    NOTE:When replacing dielectric material to “Standard x 8 pieces” or “Standard x 8 pieces + Lower Shift x8 pieces”, the order is important. Follow the illustration below.

    Illustration 23:  Dielectric Material Order

NOTICE
Prior to each Body Coil Install, inspect airflow gaskets( 5404530 and 5404530-2). Gaskets should be bonded along entire length.

Illustration 24:  Airflow Gaskets

 
 

  1. After changing the dielectric material, install Body Coil by reverse order of removal. Refer to RF Body Coil Replacement.

  1. Verify that LOTO is removed and TPS Reset was done.

  1. Set VC1, VC2, and VC3 to the center of rotation by turning VC to CCW until it reaches the end and then turning VC to CW by 23 turns.

  1. Return to Hardware Setup.

4.6.2  Adjustment of MagS21

  1. If MagS21 is failed even though other values are within the specification, perform Body Coil Isolation. If failed after Body Coil Isolation and Body Coil Tuning, replace Body Coil.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform “End Exam” from pull down menu of scan window (End / End Exam).

  1. Restore Cables, phantom, and enclosure.

  1. Run the TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Run the System Gain Calibration.

  1. Run the DD Quadrature Calibration .

  1. Run the Echo Planar Test (EPT).

  1. Perform a quick head and body scan to ensure the system is functioning properly.
