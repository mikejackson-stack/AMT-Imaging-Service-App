4  Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements: Store the coil in an air-conditioned Scan or Equipment Room. A storage space of 55 cm x 45 cm x 40 cm is required.

Dimensions: 19 in. (W) x 12 in. (L) x 12 in. (H), 48.26 cm x 30.48 cm x 35.56 cm

Weight: 15 lb. (6.81 kg)

This coil should be transported and stored under the following conditions:

  1. Ambient temperature range of +5°C to +65°C.

  2. Relative humidity range of 10% to 100%, noncondensing (transporting).

  3. Atmospheric pressure range of 500 hPa to 1,060 hPa.
