Removing and Replacing Cradle Assembly

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Cradle from the Patient Table

  1. Undock the Patient Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the same time, press the Cradle latch block and push in the Cradle release button at front of Cradle.

    (Step Details)

  1. While holding down the latch block and pressing the Cradle release button, slide Cradle forward enough so that the Cradle covers the top area of the Cradle latch block. Release the latch block and Cradle release button.

CAUTION
PINCHED FINGERS
FORWARD MOVEMENT OF CRADLE CAN PINCH FINGER WHILE HOLDING DOWN CRADLE LATCH BLOCK.
MOVE CRADLE FORWARD SLOWLY AND DELIBERATELY UNTIL BOTTOM EDGE OF CRADLE SUFFICIENTLY COVERS TOP OF CRADLE LATCH BLOCK WHERE YOU CAN REMOVE YOUR FINGER FROM BLOCK.

  1. Push in right actuator on the Table front base.

    (Step Details)

  1. Do one of the following:

    1. If you do not need to fully remove the Cradle, follow these steps.

      1. Slide the Cradle forward far enough to clear the Cradle guide rail latches at the rear of the Table.

      1. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the front of the Cradle.

        (Step Details)

    1. If you need to fully remove the Cradle, follow these steps.

      1. Remove the Cradle cover and then the PA Coil or PA Filler. For details, refer to 1.5T & 3.0T GEM Replacements and follow the steps for PA Coil removal.

        (Step Details)

      1. Remove the mid ribs over the P2 cable.

        (Step Details)

      1. Disconnect Cotton Rod attachment.

        (Step Details)

      1. Release the Cradle.

      1. Move Cradle out from Table far enough to expose the bottom cover (Front Cable Shield) beneath the P2 Connector.

      1. Detach P-port base before disconnecting the P-connector. Make note of how the P-port base is oriented.

        (Step Details)

      1. Remove the Front Cable Shield.

      1. Disconnect the two P port connectors from the Cradle and remove all cable clamps.

      1. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the rear end of the Cradle.

      1. Disconnect the two cable tracks from the bottom of the Cradle.

      1. Carefully feed the cables and connectors through the holes located at the rear of the Cradle and remove the Cradle from the Table.

    1. To replace the Cradle, perform these steps:

      1. Position the Cradle on the Table and carefully feed the cables and connectors through the holes located at the rear of the Cradle.

      1. Reinstall the cable tracks at the bottom of the Cradle.

      1. Position cabling as shown below.

        (Step Details)

      1. Replace all cable clamps and reconnect the two P port connectors on the Cradle.

      1. Replace the Front Cable Shield.

      1. Replace the mid ribs over the P2 cable.

      1. Reinstall the PA Coil or PA Filler. For details, refer to 1.5T & 3.0T GEM Replacements and follow the steps for PA Coil installation.

      1. Replace the Cradle cover.

1.2   Replacing Rear Cradle Components

  1. Remove the Cradle release cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release the Cradle and lift the Rear Section cover of the Cradle. If needed, refer to Removing the Cradle from the Patient Table, Step 5.a.

  1. Remove the screws on the side of the Rear Section and lower Cradle back onto the Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three screws holding the Rear Section cover to the Cradle.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the upper screw for each cover mounting block and rotate out of the way.

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to the Cradle assembly drawing mark numbers to replace any of the rear subcomponents listed in the Replacement Parts list.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Replacing Front Cradle Components

  1. Release the Cradle and slide the Cradle off the Table approximately 6 inches (100 mm) to expose the Cradle front.

  1. Refer to the Cradle assembly drawing to replace any of the front subcomponents listed in the Replacement Parts list.

1.4   Replacing Center Cradle Components

  1. Release the Cradle and remove the Cradle cover by referring to 1.5T & 3.0T GEM Replacements and following the steps for PA Coil removal until the Cradle cover is removed. If needed for the part you're replacing, continue with removal of the PA Coil or PA Filler. Otherwise do not remove the PA Coil or PA Filler.

  1. Refer to the Cradle assembly drawing to replace any of the center subcomponents listed in the Replacement Parts list.

  1. When replacing the cotton rod (22 or 23) or rod cap (13), make sure the correct adjustments are made.

    NOTE: When installing cotton rods, make sure the jam nuts on cotton rods are tightened.

    1. For the left cotton rod (mark number 22), thread into adjustment block (9) until the latch (27) is flush with the side wall when the rod cap (13) is pressed flush with the front face of the Front Section (3). Note: This step is easier to perform if the cradle is elevated in the rear so you can see the side of the cradle (use a non-magnetic material such as a pad or wood).

    1. For the right cotton rod (23), the release bar (24) should be flush against the Front Section (3) when the handle bar (30) is in the normal (starting) position. Ensure that there is no slack in the cotton rod.

  1. If the PA Coil was removed, replace the PA Coil following the applicable reinstallation instructions in 1.5T & 3.0T GEM Replacements.

1.5   Replacing P Port Covers

  1. Each P port cover (front and rear) is attached by a rubber tether. Remove P port cover by gently tugging its tether to pull it out.

  1. Replace with new P Port Cover, gently pushing its rubber tether into place.

2  Finalization

  1. Dock the Table.

  1. Confirm that the Cradle release functions properly.

  1. Confirm that the LPCA connects to the Cradle and that the Cradle can move into the bore.

  1. If the Cradle includes a PA Coil, perform an MCQA scan. See 1.5T and 3.0T GEM MCQA Setup - PA Lower.
