2  Procedure

2.1   XGD Gradient Master Control (GMC) Board

2.1.1  Removing the XGD GMC

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove cables J7, J8, J9 and J10/J11.

  1. Loosen the top and bottom screws securing the XGD GMC.

  1. Using your thumbs, push in and then gently pull the extractor tab located on the top and bottom of the XGD GMC. The lever action of the ejectors disengages the XGD GMC from the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Slide the XGD GMC all the way out of the XGD Cronus Chassis.

2.1.2  Re-installing the XGD GMC

  1. Use the ejectors to insert the new XGD GMC into the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the XGD GMC.

  1. Re-attach cables J7, J8, J9 and J10/J11.

2.5   XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

2.5.1  Removing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Rotate both thumb screws to the UNLOCK position.

  1. Pull out the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly from the XGD Cronus Chassis using the handle provided (top of fan).

2.5.2  Re-installing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

  1. Insert the new XGD Cronus Fan Assembly fully into the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Rotate both thumb screws to the LOCK position.
