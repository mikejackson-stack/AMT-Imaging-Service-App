GEM Introduction

This document describes the 3.0T GEM (Geometry Embracing Method) high-density coils made by General Electric Healthcare, which are receive-only coils. The 3.0T GEM Catalog Number is M7000AM. The coils included for this catalog number are: 3.0T GEM Head Neck Unit (HNU), 3.0T GEM Anterior Array (AA), and 3.0T GEM Posterior Array (PA). The Peripheral Vascular (PV) Coil is optional for the GEM system, and the Catalog Number is M7000AL.

The performance of any modifications and/or repairs that have not been evaluated and approved by GEHC Coils Engineering may cause the device to be adulterated within the meaning of section 501(h) of the Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act, 21 U.S.C. § 351(h) and is strictly prohibited.

4  Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements: All coils should be stored in the scanner room.

For HNU Coil

  • Dimensions: 495 mm (L) x 388 mm (W) x 354 mm (H)

  • Weight: 19.4 lbs. (8.8 kg)

For AA Coil

  • Dimensions as stored on the patient positioner: 556 mm (L) x 673 mm (W) x 280 mm (H)

  • Weight: 7.9 lbs. (3.6 kg)

For PA Coil and PA Filler

  • Dimensions: 1288 mm (L) x 414 mm (W) x 100 mm (H)

    NOTE:Length includes the system cable.

  • Weight: 14.9 lbs. (6.8 kg)

For PV Coil

  • Dimensions in folded position for storage: 697 mm (L) x 642 mm (W) x 248 mm (H)

  • Weight: 20.2 lbs. (9.2 kg)
