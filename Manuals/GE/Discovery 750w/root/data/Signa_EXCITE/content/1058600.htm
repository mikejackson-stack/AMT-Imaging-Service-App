2  Overview

This procedure covers the replacement of the Longitudinal drive Motor and Encoder. The Longitudinal Drive Motor and Encoder are both located on the same Rear Pedestal bracket assembly.

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage
Strong Magnetic Field
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements

  • The service path must allow for the longitudinal drive to be moved against the wall along the ground until outside of the 200 Gauss zone at the front of the magnet when it may be lifted and removed from the room.

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

CAUTION
In order to safely service items in the rear pedestal, per the Preinstallation Manual, the minimum distance between the back of the magnet enclosures and the rear wall must be: 66 inches (1676 mm) for MR750 and MR450 and 53 inches (1346 mm) for MR450w. If site is operating under a room size deviation and space is not provided, follow proper procedure to remove the rear pedestal from the room to service it.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removing Drive Motor/Encoder Bracket Assembly

  1. Drive the LPCA to within 1 foot (30cm) of end of rear pedestal.

  1. Perform Lock Out/Tag Out per the procedure for the LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room) .

  1. Remove the right Rear Pedestal cover by removing two screws on the right side and one rear screw. Lift up the cover out of the bottom support brackets.

    Illustration 1:  Rear Pedestal Screw Locations

  1. Remove the cover from the LPCA by removing the four screws.

    Illustration 2:  LPCA Cover Screw Locations

  1. Loosen the two socket screws on drive belt tensioner and then loosen tensioner bolt until the belt becomes loose.

    Illustration 3:  Drive Belt Tensioner

    NOTE:On exposed LPCA count number of belt teeth visible from rear position belt clamp. See Illustration 4. When reinstalling the belt the same number of belt teeth must be visible.

  1. Loosen the two nuts of the rear position belt clamp and pull the belt out of the LPCA.

    Illustration 4:  Belt Clamp

  1. Remove the bridge rear hood by removing the four screws.

    Illustration 5:  Rear Pedestal Rear Hood

  1. Disconnect the Drive Motor commutator cable from the Table Interface Module (TIM) at J3, and disconnect J1 and J2 at the motor assembly.

    Illustration 6:  Cable Path from Table Interface Module to Drive Motor/Encoder

  1. Disconnect the drive motor encoder cable from the TIM at J2.

  1. Pull both cables through the grommet opening in the RF Attenuator bracket.

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
MOUNTING BRACKET, ENCODER, AND DRIVE MOTOR CONTAIN FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, use two people to PROMPTLY REMOVE MOUNTING BRACKET ASSEMBLY FROM MAGNET ROOM. stay as far away from the magnet as possible when exiting the magnet room.

  1. Loosen (do not remove) the four (4) M10 head bolts, two (2) on each side from the motor mounting bracket assembly using a 17mm socket wrench.

    NOTE:Have one person hold the assembly while loosening or reinstalling it to prevent cross threading.

  1. Carefully remove the two (2) M10 bolts and lock-washers on one side while holding the bracket assembly in place, then remove the bolts and lock-washers on the other side. The assembly drops straight down from the bottom of the bridge. Handle with care per the above warning.

  1. Carefully maneuver the drive belt from the rear of the LPCA through the bracket assembly.

4.2  Installing Drive Motor Assembly

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
MOUNTING BRACKET, ENCODER, AND DRIVE MOTOR CONTAIN FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. To prevent possible bodily injury or material damage, the mounting bracket assembly must be handled by two people simultaneously.

  1. Rethread the belt through the motor. Hold the bracket assembly in place while replacing the two (2) M10 bolts and lock washers on each side.

  1. Feed the drive belt up through the bridge and into the LPCA under the belt clamp. Position the belt so that the same number of belt teeth are exposed as was noted during disassembly. Tighten the two M4 hex nuts.

  1. Tighten the Tensioner Bolt clockwise until the Drive Belt Tensioner socket screws bottom out in the slots. Tighten the tensioner socket screws. See Illustration 3.

  1. Replace the bridge rear hood by angling the hood onto the bridge and securing it with four brass M4 screws.

  1. Feed the Drive Motor Commutator and Encoder cable through the RF Attenuator bracket grommet opening. See Illustration 6

  1. Reattach the Drive Motor commutator cable to J3 and the Encoder cable to J2 on the Table Interface Module (TIF) . See Illustration 6

    NOTE:Position the cables to minimize interference with the RF attenuator heat sink natural convection cooling.

  1. Replace the LPCA cover with the four (4) brass M6 pan head screws using a medium slotted screwdriver.

  1. Remove Lock Out Tag Out.

  1. Drive the LPCA to the front of the magnet HOME position.

  1. Replace the Rear Pedestal side covers.

5  Finalization

Perform Cradle Motion Test.
