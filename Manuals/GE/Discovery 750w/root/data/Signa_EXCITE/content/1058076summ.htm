GOC Audio Assembly Replacement - New GOC

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
possible electric shock could cause bodily harm.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, Shut down the Host computer and perform Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) BEFORE STARTING THIS PROCEDURE.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Host computer and GOC Audio Assembly must be powered off.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Shut down the system software and power down the PDU per LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove two screws (shown below) that secure the right side panel, and lift the panel off to expose the GOC Audio Assembly (AA).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the GOCAA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify proper labeling before removing the cables connected to the GOCAA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four screws securing the Audio Box (shown in Illustration 3), and remove it from the GOC frame.

  1. Install the replacement Audio Box, resecure the four screws and reconnect all cables.

  1. Reattach the GOC right side panel.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO to PGR/PDU and apply power to the PDU.

  1. Perform Intercom Adjustment for the newly installed GOC Audio Box.

  1. Reboot the host computer.

  1. Perform a scan.
