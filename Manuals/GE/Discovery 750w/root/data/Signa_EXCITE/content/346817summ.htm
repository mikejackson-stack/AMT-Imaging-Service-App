  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

NOTICE
The Dale Safety Analyzer may only be taken within 20 inches (51 cm) of the PAC Remote Interface Assembly at the Magnet. Getting closer to the Magnet will affect the Dale Safety Analyzer, thereby altering the tool’s ability to take an accurate measurement.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Procedure Using the OLD Dale 600/600E Safety Analyzer

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

  1. Print out and record data in the supplied Data Sheet:

NOTICE
The Dale Safety Analyzer may only be taken within 20 inches (51 cm) of the PAC Remote Interface Assembly at the Magnet. Getting closer to the Magnet will affect the Dale Safety Analyzer, thereby altering the tool’s ability to take an accurate measurement.
 
 

  1. Plug the Analyzer into a power source in the magnet room, which allows access to the PACII Remote Interface Assembly while staying at least 20 inches (51 cm) from the magnet.

  1. At Physiological Acquisition Controller (PAC-II) Remote Interface Assembly, connect the Patient Lead cable and the Cardiac Lead Set at the ECG connector. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect cardiac leads to Dale 600/600E (46-328406G1). See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the [Neutral] Button in the CLOSED position.

  1. Place the [Polarity] switch in the NORMAL position.

  1. On the Dale 600/600E set the main selector switch to LEAKAGE - LEAD-GND.

  1. Move the LEAD switch through the positions shown in Table 1. Verify leakage current displayed on meter is less than 50 micro amps and record the values in the Data Sheet.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Dale 600/600E set the main selector switch to LEAKAGE - LEAD-LEAD.

  1. Move LEAD switch through the positions shown in Table 1. Verify leakage current displayed on meter is less than 50 micro amps and record the values in the Data Sheet.

  1. On the Dale 600/600E set the main selector switch to LEAKAGE - LEAD-ISO.

  1. Make sure that the leads are not touching the floor.

  1. Move the LEAD switch to ALL LEADS.

  1. Press button [ISO TEST] and record the meter reading in Data Sheet. This reading must be less than 50 micro amps.

  1. Repeat Step 6 - Step 14 with the POLARITY switch in the REVERSED position.

1.2   Procedure Using the NEW Dale 601/601E Safety Analyzer

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

  1. Print out and record data in the supplied Data Sheet:

NOTICE
The Dale Safety Analyzer may only be taken within 20 inches (51 cm) of the PAC Remote Interface Assembly at the Magnet. Getting closer to the Magnet will affect the Dale Safety Analyzer, thereby altering the tool’s ability to take an accurate measurement.
 
 

  1. Plug the Analyzer into a power source in the magnet room, which allows access to the PACII Remote Interface Assembly while staying at least 20 inches (51 cm) from the magnet.

  1. At Physiological Acquisition Controller (PAC-II) Remote Interface Assembly, connect the Patient Lead cable and the Cardiac Lead Set at the ECG connector. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the Test Load selector switch is set at 601 for IEC601.1. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the ECG leads to Dale 601/601E. See Illustration 5. Not all systems will have the Cardiac (C) lead.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the [L2 ] (Neutral) Switch in the CLOSED position. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Be sure to pause in the OFF (Middle) position when switching polarity from normal to reverse

  1. Place the [Outlet] (Polarity) switch in the NORMAL position.

  1. On the Dale 601/601E set the Function Switch to PATIENT LEAD LEAKAGE.

  1. Move Lead switch through the lead positions shown in Table 2. Verify leakage current displayed on meter is less than 50 micro amps and record the values in the Data Sheet.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Dale 601/601E set the main selector switch to PATIENT AUX CURRENT.

  1. Move Lead switch through the lead positions shown in Table 2. Verify that the current displayed on meter is less than 50 micro amps and record the values in the Data Sheet.

  1. On the Dale 601/601E set the main selector switch to M.A.P. (Mains on Applied Parts sink current).

  1. Make sure that the leads are not touching the floor.

  1. Move the Lead switch to measure All Leads, which is the 6th position all the way to right on the switch.

  1. On the LIFT/Ground/MAP Switch, press and hold M.A.P. Verify that the current displayed on meter is less than 50 micro amps and record the values in the Data Sheet.

NOTE:Be sure to pause in the OFF (Middle) position when switching polarity from normal to reverse.

  1. Repeat Step 8 - Step 15 with the polarity switch in the REVERSED position.

2  Finalization

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

NOTICE
The Dale Safety Analyzer may only be taken within 20 inches (51 cm) of the PAC Remote Interface Assembly at the Magnet. Getting closer to the Magnet will affect the Dale Safety Analyzer, thereby altering the tool’s ability to take an accurate measurement.

  1. Remove the Patient Lead cable from the ECG Connector.
