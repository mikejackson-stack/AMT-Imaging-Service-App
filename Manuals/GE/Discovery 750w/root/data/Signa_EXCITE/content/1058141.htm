2  Overview

The electrical power to the Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) and Power Distribution Unit (PDU), and thus all electronics powered by these items, can be removed by locking out/tagging out (LOTO) the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) . Prior to servicing and/or maintenance work on the MDP, the steps in this Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) procedure must be performed by a LOTO-authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all the steps in this procedure ensures a safe environment and avoids equipment damage when working on these parts.

4  Procedure

4.1  Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy:

  • Internal to the MDP: Instantaneous

  • Auxiliary equipment in other cabinets: 5 minutes

4.2  Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnection(s)

A personal lock and tag per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

4.3  Applying LOTO

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the Operator Workspace, shut down the Host PC. See “Shutting Down the Host PC” in System Startup and Shutdown.

4.4  Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for reenergizing the equipment.

    Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the MDP.

  1. On the PDU, press the EMO RESET button (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Reset the PGR PDU

  1. Power up the Host PC. See System Startup and Shutdown.
