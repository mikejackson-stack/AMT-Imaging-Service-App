HOShim Calibration Reference Map Creation and Procedures

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
Poison Hazard
3.0T phantoms contain Dimethyl Silicone fluid.
Do not ingest. Eye contact may cause minor irritation. If a leak or spill occurs wipe up or absorb in inert material and place in container for disposal. incineration or landfill is the recommended disposal method.

WARNING
Poison Hazard
Signa 1.5T and 1.0T system phantoms contain nickel, a suspected carcinogen.
Do not ingest. Dispose of as a hazardous waste according to state and federal regulations.

3  Procedure

3.1   Field Map Creation

NOTICE
Notice: After loading, landmarking, and Advance To Scan, the Phantom must be allowed to settle for 15 minutes prior to scanning.
 
 

  1. At the operator workstation, select Service Desktop and prepare the system by performing a [TPS Reset].

  1. Load and landmark the LVShim phantom (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the operator workstation, select the scan icon in the desktop control panel and set up High Order Shim calibration scan as follows.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set Patient Protocols to Service

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Save Series].

  1. Execute scan by clicking <Scan>.

  1. When the image is displayed in the Autoview window, select [Display ] in the desktop control panel. To display the image, select Exam, Series, and Image, and click [Viewer]. Do the following to verify that the phantom is centered in the Z axis:

    1. Click [User Prefs].

    1. In the User Preferences pop-up window, find Grid Prefs and click [Customize…].

    1. Change Grid Spacing (mm) to 150, click [OK], and then click [Apply].

    1. Select the grid symbol button to turn on the grid. See Illustration 2 for an example of an image with the alignment grid.

      (Step Details)

    1. Adjust Window (W) and Level (L) settings until the edges of the phantom image can be clearly seen.

    1. Verify that the inner phantom sphere is centered within the 150-mm grid in the S/I direction, as shown in Illustration 2. If the phantom is not centered, move the phantom in or out of the bore and right to left as necessary (based on the grid markings), then landmark again and re-scan.

3.2   Running the Makereference Script

  1. On TRM Systems, this section will take 45 minutes to complete. On the 3T/94 CRM, this section will take 90 minutes to complete.

  1. Start the HOShim tool.

    1. On the service Desktop, open the Service Browser if it is not already running.

    1. Select the Calibration Tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once you have started the HOShim Reference Map, the following GUI will appear (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Start Scan]. Another notice will appear that states, “Scanning. Please wait Until Scan Done!”. Click OK.

  1. The system will prescan, and then scan. There are multiple scans in this sequence. There is no user interaction during this time. For estimated length see Step 1.

  1. After the scan is done, the lower portion of the screen will indicate “Reference Map Creation in Progress”.

  1. Once the scan is complete click [Quit].

  1. Verify HOShim reference maps were created correctly (seeHOShim Verification).

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a SaveInfo to backup calibration files. (See Verifying Save Restore Media to verify your SaveInfo.)

  1. If completed, put away all phantoms and tools.
