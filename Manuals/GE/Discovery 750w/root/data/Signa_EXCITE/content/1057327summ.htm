3  Procedure

3.1   CFB Removal

  1. Remove the front cables to the CFB (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:If you need better access to the CFB, unscrew the two thumbscrews on the front of the CFB panel. Carefully slide the shelf forward, being watchful of surrounding cords, to better conduct the CFB replacement (see Illustration 3).

Illustration 3:  CFB Panel Thumbscrews

  1. Using a ¼” wrench, remove the four screws that secure the protective cover (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the fiber optic lines from connectors J1-J7 (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the CFB from the PEN Cabinet.

3.2   Fiber Optic Repeater Board Installation

  1. Connect the fiber optic lines from the RF Hub, Scan Room Interface (SRI) , and Physiological Acquisition Controller (PAC) boards to the CFB as indicated in Table 1(see Illustration 5). The latch on the fiber-optic connector connects facing up.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect cables to the front of the CFB (see Illustration 2).

  1. Install protective cover (see Illustration 4).

4  Finalization

  1. Remove the LOTO tags from the system and restore all power.

  1. Perform CFB Functional Diagnostics.
