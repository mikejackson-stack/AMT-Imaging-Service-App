3.0T HD 8-Channel Wrist Coil FRUs and Replacements

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsProcedure Timing
130 minutes

2  Procedure Overview

This document contains the replacement procedures (see Section 4)and the list of field replaceable units (FRUs) (see Section 5) for the 3.0T HD 8CH Wrist Coil by Invivo, catalog M7000GW.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

  • #2 Phillips screwdriver

  • ESD Grounding Wrist Strap (for post replacement)

3.2  Replacement Parts

See Section 5 for FRU list and part numbers.

4  Procedure

4.1  Coil Cable

4.1.1  Cable Continuity Check

NOTICE
To be performed by an authorized Service Engineer only.
 
 

Cable continuity checks are found in 3.0T Wrist Coil Troubleshooting.

4.1.2  Cable Replacement Procedure

NOTICE
To be performed by an authorized Service Engineer only.
 
 

  1. The four coil connector mounting screws are located at the end of the coil. Remove the four coil connector mounting screws (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Remove Four Screws

  2. Remove the coil connector by grasping the coil connector housing and pulling away from the coil in the inferior direction Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Disconnect Cable

4.2  Post Replacement

CAUTION
Electrostatic Damage (ESD
Removing the coil cable and top cover exposes ESD sensitive components.
Use all applicable ESD prevention procedures (including a ESD Grounding Wrist Strap) when handling the coil.

  1. Remove the six cable assembly screws and the cable assembly, then remove the four cover screws and the top cover.

    Illustration 3:  Cable and Cover Removal

  2. Turn the Top Cover over and remove the six top post block screws, then remove the top post block assembly. Replace the parts as shown below as needed.

    Illustration 4:  Top Post Block Disassembly

  3. Remove the four side post block assembly screws (see Illustration 3). Replace the parts as shown below as needed.

    Illustration 5:  Top Post Block Disassembly

  4. Reassemble the coil using the steps above.

4.3  Mechanical Hardware Check

  1. Exercise the locking mechanism on the top of the coil housing by moving the latch handle back and forth to unlock/lock the coil. The lock must not loosen or unlock during the course of an examination.

    Refer to the 3.0T HD 8–Channel Wrist Array Coil Operator’s Manual, Section: Locking the Coil. (Invivo part number: 4535-303-0503)

  2. Exercise placing the baseplate riser onto the baseplate aligned with the knob location and slide the baseplate riser into the locked position.

    Refer to the 3.0T HD 8–Channel Wrist Array Coil Operator’s ManualSection: Patient Positioning. (Invivo part number: 4535-303-0503)
