3.0T HD 8-Channel Cardiac Coil FRUs and Replacements

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Replace The Coil Cable

  1. Remove anterior coil cover.

  1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly.

  1. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the anterior coil cable end from the anterior interface board (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the old anterior cable assembly (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Fix the anterior cable section of a new coil cable assembly on the anterior section of the coil and reattach the cable clamp with the two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Close the anterior coil cover.

  1. Remove posterior coil cover.

  1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the posterior cable end from the posterior interface board (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the old posterior cable assembly (see Illustration 4).

  1. Fix the posterior section of a new coil cable assembly on the posterior section of the coil and reattach the cable clamp with two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board (see Illustration 4 and Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Close the posterior coil cover.

1.2   Replace The Anterior Coil Assembly

  1. Remove anterior coil cover.

  1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly (see Illustration 3).

  1. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the anterior coil cable end from the anterior interface board (see Illustration 1).

  1. Remove the old anterior coil assembly.

  1. Fix the anterior coil cable on a new anterior coil assembly and reattach the cable clamp with the two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board (see Illustration 3).

1.3   Replace The Posterior Coil Assembly

  1. Remove posterior coil cover.

  1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly (see Illustration 4).

  1. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the posterior coil cable end from the posterior interface board (see Illustration 4).

  1. Remove the old posterior coil assembly.

  1. Fix the posterior coil cable on a new posterior coil assembly and reattach the cable clamp with the two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board (see Illustration 4).

  1. Close the posterior coil cover.
