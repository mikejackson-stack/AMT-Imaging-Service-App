Intercom Adjustment For GOC WIM

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Accessing the Intercom Board

  1. Remove the 3 securing screws from the rear of the right side GOC cover. Slide the cover to the rear and remove.

  1. Access the intercom board by turning the black knobs one quarter turn clockwise.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Maximum Keyboard Speaker Volume R3 or R80 - (Patient’s Voice)

This section adjusts the maximum volume of the Patient’s voice, in the magnet bore. The Operator benefits from this adjustment.

  • Operator Volume- controls the speaker volume in the Bore, (Operator’s voice).

  • Patient Volume- controls the volume of the speaker in the keyboard, (Patient’s voice). Refer to Illustration 2.

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control, (Middle control) at maximum, by rotating the volume wheel UP with your finger, (Clockwise).

    (Step Details)

  1. One-person talks into the patient microphone in the magnet bore. The second person adjusts R80 on the Intercom Board for the desired maximum volume at the keyboard speaker. This is an iterative process. Repeat the voice test until the maximum desired level is achieved.

  1. Depending on the version of Intercom board, adjust R80 clockwise to increase maximum volume at the patient speaker. Refer to Illustration 3 to determine which version of Intercom board you have.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Minimum Keyboard Speaker Volume- R10 or R9 (Patient’s Voice)

This section adjusts the minimum volume of the Patient’s voice, in the magnet bore. The Operator benefits from this adjustment.

NOTE:On the Console Intercom Board, R97 (keyboard speaker minimum volume) is factory set at approximately 5.5 turns counter clockwise.

Turning R97 counter clockwise increases the minimum volume.

While turning the potentiometer, you can hear a faint clicking sound when it reaches either the maximum or minimum limit.

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control, (Middle control) at minimum, by rotating the volume wheel down with your finger. (counterclockwise).

  1. One person should listen at the keyboard speaker (Patient’s Voice). The other person talks into the patient microphone in the magnet bore. The person at the keyboard should make minor adjustments to R97 on the Intercom Board for the desired minimum volume at the keyboard speaker. This is an iterative process. Make a slight adjustment to the potentiometer, test the volume and repeat the voice test until the desired Minimum Volume level has been achieved.

  1. Adjust R97 counterclockwise to increase minimum volume at the keyboard speaker.

1.4   Patient Speaker Volume (Speaker in Bore)

  1. At the Operator Workspace Keyboard, set the patient volume control at minimum by rotating the Operators volume wheel Down with your finger, (Counterclockwise). Refer to Illustration 4. This control is to the far left.

    (Step Details)

  1. One person listens at the patient speaker. The other person talks into the keyboard microphone and adjusts R81 on the Intercom Board for the desired minimum volume at the patient speaker. This is an iterative process and requiring repeated voice testing and potentiometer adjustment. Repeat the process until the desired minimum level is achieved.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Close the Workspace Interface Module. Be sure that the module is completely sealed.

  1. Perform a head and body scan to ensure system functionality.
