1  Procedure

1.1   Setup Scan Software

NOTE:This procedure should be run for 48 cm field of view (FOV) on the Body Coil, 28 cm FOV on the Body Coil, and 24 cm FOV on the Head Coil.

  1. Click [New Pt].

    1. Patient ID:geservice.

    1. Weight:111 lbs.

  1. Click [Show All Protocols].

  1. Click [GE] and then [Other] protocols.

  1. Select "hos GE" and the series that corresponds to the FOV being tested, either 48 cm, 28 cm, or 24 cm.

  1. Click [Accept].

1.2   Begin Scan

  1. Place the correct phantom in the coil to be tested.

    NOTE:3.0T phantoms have pink solution. 1.5T phantoms have light green solution.

    1. For the 48 cm FOV Field Map: Setup the Body TLT Sphere Phantom on the patient cradle. See Illustration 1.

    1. For the 28 cm FOV Field Map: Setup the Body TLT Sphere Phantom on the patient cradle. See Illustration 1.

    1. For the 24 cm FOV Field Map: Place the head TLT phantom on the patient cradle. See Illustration 2.

  1. Landmark on appropriate location.

  1. Click [Save] and [Start Exam], [Save Rx], [Scan]. The Shim User Interface should appear after the scan stops.

  1. Position the region of interest (ROI) (use an oval shape) within the center of the spherical phantom image covering approximately 80% of the volume in all three planes. To move the ROI, click and drag with the left mouse button inside the ROI. To change the size of the ROI, click and drag with the left mouse button outside the ROI.

  1. Click [Advanced], then [Calculate Shim] buttons. The user should see the “Advanced” screen (Illustration 4) that contains the specific shim information for each shim channel. See Illustration 3 and Illustration 4.

  1. Record data in Table 1, 2, or 3.

  1. If the Advanced Shim User Interface screen is open, click [Download]. Then in the Basic Shim User Interface screen (Illustration 5) click [Quit].

  1. Click [Scan] again. Select Add images to same series when the pop up appears. Do not pre-scan between iterations.

  1. Repeat Setup Scan Software, Step 1 through Step 8 for remaining reference maps. Test all reference maps for a system.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Achieving Convergence

  1. Convergence is achieved when one of the following two conditions is met:

    1. The difference in Current RMS between two consecutive runs is less than 1 Hz (See Illustration 3).

    1. The Advanced Interface (Illustration 4) is asking for less than 30mA (0.03A) of Shim Current Delta.

      (Step Details)

  1. Once convergence is achieved, the following steps are repeated for each channel until all shim channels are functionally tested. Only test one reference map/setup.

    1. Go to the “Advanced” screen and disable all channels except for the one to be tested by clicking on the button next to each channel. This process starts testing the ZY channel, refer to Shim Channel Sequence and Delta Shim Current Data, for the sequence for the remaining channels.

    1. Enter a 100mA offset by typing 0.1 in the current delta field for the channel being tested and select [Done] from the basic user screen to download and exit the interface.

    1. Click [Scan] to run the shim scan with the current offset. The user interface appears at the end of the scan.

    1. Click [Advanced] to open the “Advanced” screen again. Make sure only the ZY channel is enabled (may need to deselect the other channels again, channels stay disabled if they are deselected prior to using [Calculate Shim] but not after).

    1. Click [Calculate Shim] and look at the change requested for the ZY channel. The requested current change should be opposite in polarity from the offset entered in step Step 2.b and should be about 70mA in amplitude signifying at least a 70% requested correction of the shim offset. Typically for most channels you see much better than this. Record the delta current requested in the supplied data sheets: .

    1. Click [Done] to download the current delta and exit the interface.

    1. Click [Scan] and repeat Step 2.a through Step 2.f until at least 90% of the shim offset entered in Step 1 is recovered. Add the delta currents from each iteration and record them in the data sheets . The total recovered current should be between 0.090 and 0.100 amps See the example in the last row of the table.

      (Step Details)

    1. Repeat the sub-steps in this step for each of the other high order shim volumes.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a Check Scan to ensure the scanner is ready for patient scanning.

  1. Put away any phantoms or tools used.
