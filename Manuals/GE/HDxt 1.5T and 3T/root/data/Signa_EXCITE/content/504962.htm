2  Overview

PAC Checks is listed as one of the final steps of the Installation Portion of the Installation Calibration Wizard, , though it can be done at any time during the install. During PAC Checks, there are three tasks that must be completed and checked off:

  • Peripheral Pulse Probe Check.

  • ECG Leads Check.

  • Respiratory Compensation Check.

3  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click on the Calibration Tab.

    If you are in Install or Upgrade mode, click on [Install and Calibration Wizard] ⇒ [Click here to start this tool].

  1. PAC Checks is one of the final steps after Install In Spec. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  PAC Checks

  1. Check the Peripheral Pulse Probe . When this is complete, check the box next to “Is Peripheral Pulse Probe Check completed?”

  1. Check the ECG Leads that have been ordered by the customer. When this is complete, check the box next to “Is ECG Leads checked”

  1. Perform Respiratory Compensation Check . When this is complete, check the box next to “Is Respiratory Checks Done?”

  1. Click [Submit].

4  Finalization

  1. 1. Return to the ICW procedure for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.
