3  Procedure

  1. This alternate proprietary procedure is available for GE use, and to sites with a valid Advanced Service Package Limited License.

    1. Click on [New Pt], and press <Enter>.

    1. Id: geservice

    1. Name: gradient calibration

    1. Weight (lb.): 111

    1. Set Patient Protocols to [Service]

    1. In the Protocol field, type o.32.1 (o=Other, 1=series number).

    1. For TwinSpeed, select GradMode=Select Mode to match selection made when opening Grad Cal too.

    1. [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click on [Scan] (the system auto prescans first).

  1. Perform a Calibration Image Analysis..

  1. For TwinSpeed, repeat this section for both GradModes.
