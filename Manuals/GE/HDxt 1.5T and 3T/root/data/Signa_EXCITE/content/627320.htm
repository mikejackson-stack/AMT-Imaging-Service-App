1.5T SRFD2 RF Module Fan Replacement

1  Overview

This procedure describes the process for replacing fans on the outside rear of the SRFD2 RF Amplifier. The process for changing fans on older SRFD2 RF Amplifiers is much different than the newer units. Older amplifiers can be identified by the serial number printed on the manufacturer rating plate affixed to the rear of the amplifier shown in Illustration 1.

NOTE:The serial number is printed on the second line of the rating plate as YRFW-####: YR is the last two digits of the year it was built, FW is the two-digit fiscal week, and #### is the four-digit unit number.

Illustration 1:  SRFD2 Serial Number Rating Plate

Choose the appropriate procedure:

  • For units where the YR code is 02 or less and the #### code is 0001 to 0030 (letter “P” may appear after this number), follow the procedure in Step (for early SRFD2 Amplifiers).

  • To replace fans in all other units, follow the procedure described in Step (for SRFD2 Amplifiers with serial numbers 0031 or higher).

2  Tools and Procedure for SRFD2 with Serial Numbers 0001 to 0030

The following three tools are required for fan replacement:

  • No. 2 Phillips Screwdriver shown in Illustration 2

  • Slot or Flat-Blade Screwdriver shown in Illustration 3

  • Needle Nose Pliers shown in Illustration 4

Illustration 2:  Phillips Screwdriver

Illustration 3:  Slot Screwdriver

Illustration 4:  Needle Nose Pliers

Proceed to Fan Replacement for Early SRFD2 Amplifiers Only for fan replacement instructions.

3  Tools and Procedure for SRFD2 with Serial Numbers 0031 or Greater

The following four tools are required for fan replacement (first three shown in Illustration 5):

  • 7/32 in. Socket with 1/4 in. (~0.635 cm) Drive

  • 3 in. long, 1/4 in. diameter (~0.635 cm) Drive Extension

  • 1/4 in. (~0.635 cm) Drive Handle

  • Needle Nose Pliers shown in Illustration 4

Illustration 5:  Socket with Drive Extension and Handle

Proceed to Fan Replacement for Later SRFD2 Amplifiers Only for fan replacement instructions.
