Gradient Coil Replacement Coil Installation

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
4 minumum6 hours

2  Overview

After having the fork lift truck remove the failed coil and cradle from the cart, the replacement body coil, on its cradle, must be loaded and taken to the scan room.

3.2  Safety

CAUTION
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY AND/OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE!
NOT HAVING THE CRADLE ANCHORED TO THE CART COULD CAUSE THE CRADLE TO TIP WHEN THE COIL MOVES ONTO IT.
THE THREE CRADLE ALIGNMENT/LOCK BOLTS MUST BE IN PLACE BEFORE THE COIL IS ROLLED ONTO THE CART.

3.3  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

4  Procedure

4.1  For Mobiles

The most difficult part of this procedure is getting the replacement coil correctly aligned so that it rolls easily and completely into the bore. A small carpenter's level is a useful tool to have at this point.
