2.3  Safety

NOTICE
Please refer to the cable FRU list above in Replacement Parts

  • If the cable FRU is with the Coil ID Board, then the Coil ID Board must be removed as well.

  • If the cable FRU is without the Coil ID Board, then do not remove the Coil ID Board.

 
 

NOTICE
Observe Anti-Static Precautions. Use a wrist strap and ESD workstation (ESD mat connected to ground and place to connect ESD wrist strap).
 
 

3  Procedure

3.1  Cable Removal

  1. Put an ESD wrist strap on and ensure it is grounded.

  1. Place posterior coil patient side down.

  1. Remove the straps.

  1. Remove the 8 screws from the cover at the pelvic end of the coil. There are two screws located under the strap. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Back of Posterior

  1. Unscrew the cable clamp. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Screws at Cable Clamp

  1. Locate and disconnect all RF cables coming from the cable trap. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Disconnect Indicated RF Cables

  1. Locate and remove the DC wire bundle coming from the cable trap. See Illustration 3.

  1. Remove the two screws holding the cable trap PCB to the coil. See Illustration 3.

  1. Remove the cable from the coil. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Posterior Cable Removal from Coil

3.2  Cable Replacement

  1. Place strain relief in cutout (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  Strain Relief in Cutout

  1. Align cable trap PCB from the new cable with the screw posts.

  1. Secure the cable trap PCB in place with the previously removed nylon screws.

  1. Secure cable clamp over cable until tight with the previously removed brass screws. See Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Secured Cable Clamp

  1. Attach the DC wire bundle to J1. See Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  DC Wire to J1

  1. Attach the Clear cable at E1 Out.

  1. Attach the Yellow cable to E5 Out.

  1. Attach the Red cable to E3 Out.

  1. Attach the Orange cable to E4 Out.

  1. Attach the Black cable to E6 Out.

  1. Attach the Brown cable to E2 Out.

  1. Replace the cover.

4  Finalization

If available, perform Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool to ensure that the coil still operates properly, otherwise perform the Surface Coils SNR Test.
