SPT Safety Features

After you have started SPT, you may stop testing by selecting Interrupt [SPT] or [Quit SPT]. If you elect to cancel the test at any time during the run, all valid test results created in the current run are assembled into a result file. See Troubleshooting, Utilities for SPT, for additional information about utilities available with SPT for clearing conditions that may be present after an untimely abort.
