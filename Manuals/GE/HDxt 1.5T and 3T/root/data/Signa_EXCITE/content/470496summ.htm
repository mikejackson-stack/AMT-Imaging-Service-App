Gradient Cabinet Fan Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
High voltage from two sources.
Remove Power from both sources by placing the following breakers in the OFF Position, Gradient 208V Control Power Breaker, the 420 V Gradient Power Breaker and Gradient Fan Breaker. Lock and Tag the Breaker Panel.

WARNING
Personal injury may occur.
The fan motor/blower assembly has sharp edges.
wear protective gloves to avoid injury when removing the fan assembly From the box.

WARNING
Lifting Hazard.
Heavy object, The Fan Motor/Blower weighs 48 lbs.
The procedure requires two people to Safely remove the fan motor/blower from the cabinet.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Manpower Requirements: 2 (two) people are needed to lift fan. Total Fan Assembly Weight: 114 lbs. Total Fan Motor/Blower Weight: 48 lbs.

3  Procedure

3.1   Preliminary Set Up Procedure

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
High voltage from two sources.
Remove Power from both sources by placing the following breakers in the OFF Position, Gradient 208V Control Power Breaker, the 420 V Gradient Power Breaker and Gradient Fan Breaker. Lock and Tag the Breaker Panel.

  1. Power down the Gradient Cabinet by placing the following breakers in the OFF position:

    (Step Details)

  1. Lock out the Breaker panel and tag it.

  1. Wait 5 minutes after the power has been removed from the Gradient cabinet to insure that the capacitors have had time to discharge.

  1. Take a Digital Multimeter and set it to its highest AC voltage range.

  1. Verify that all energy has been dissipated. On the terminal block in the rear of the cabinet of the Gradient-PS, measure between the ground connection and the incoming power connection at the black wire, see Illustration 2. Repeat this measurement between the ground connection and the red wire, and between the ground connection and the white wire. A measurement of 0 VAC indicates that the 420V incoming power has been successfully turned off.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Removal Procedure Steps

  1. Locate the Fan Enclosure at the top of the Gradient cabinet.

  1. Remove two (2) phillips-head screws from each fan assembly side panel.

  1. Either tilt or remove the side covers to provide access to the top grill for removal.

  1. Remove the Fan Enclosure Top Grill.

  1. Locate the cable that contains the power wires leading directly to the Fan Motor. This cable is terminated with a cannon connector. See Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the cannon connector from the inside of the Fan Enclosure. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 4 screws, using small phillips screwdriver, holding the connector in place. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the fan cannon connector from inside the fan motor/blower assembly. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cut Ty-wraps, see Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the six (6) 11/32” or 9mm hex nuts that secure the Fan Motor Mounting Channel to the Fan Enclosure. See Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Lifting Hazard.
Heavy object, The Fan Motor/Blower weighs 48 lbs.
The procedure requires two people to Safely remove the fan motor/blower from the cabinet.

WARNING
Personal injury may occur.
The fan motor/blower assembly has sharp edges.
wear protective gloves to avoid injury when removing the fan assembly From the box.

  1. Use two people to grasp the Fan Motor Mounting bracket and lift it up and out of the Fan Enclosure.

3.3   Installing Replacement Fan Assembly

WARNING
Lifting Hazard.
Heavy object, The Fan Motor/Blower weighs 48 lbs.
The procedure requires two people to Safely remove the fan motor/blower from the cabinet.

WARNING
Personal injury may occur.
The fan motor/blower assembly has sharp edges.
wear protective gloves to avoid injury when removing the fan assembly From the box.

  1. Use two people to grasp the Fan Motor Mounting bracket and lift it up and into the Fan Enclosure on top of the Gradient Cabinet.

  1. Align the fan assembly with the Mounting Channel screw holes.

  1. Replace the six (6) 11/32” or 9mm hex nuts and secure the Fan Motor Assembly to the Fan Enclosure. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route the Fan Assembly power wires along the assembly and tie wrap into position. See Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the fan cannon connector inside the fan motor/blower assembly location. See Illustration 11.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the 4 screws, using small phillips screwdriver and secure the connector in place. See Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. In the Gradient Cabinet, locate the cable that contains the power wires for the Fan Motor. Connect at the Cannon Connector.

  1. Replace the Fan Enclosure Top Grill.

  1. Replace the side covers to the Fan Module.

  1. Replace two (2) phillips-head screws at each fan assembly side panel and secure.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from PDU Breaker panel.

  1. Power up the Gradient cabinet by placing the Gradient Breakers of the PDU in the ON position.

  1. Verify the fan operates. The fan should create enough suction to hold the rear cabinet door closed or hold a piece of paper over the top of the fan module to make sure the paper is pushed straight up.
