System Intercom

Intercom Board Version 2117167-4:

Some customers may want more or less volume from the Intercom system. This document explains how to configure the jumpers on Intercom board version 2117167-4, to increase or decrease Base Amplification of the board.

NOTE:There are jumpers on this board that can be moved to change the “default” amplifier output for both transmit (Microphone sensitivity) and Receive (Speaker volume) settings. It is recommended that the jumpers remain in the default position. However, if the customer wants more or less volume than the current volume levels provide, reset the jumpers accordingly, and then proceed to fine tuning using the potentiometers as described in the next few paragraphs. Refer to Table 1.

Base Amplification Choices:

  • Jumpers JP5 and JP6 adjust the Base Amplification for the Patient Speaker located in the KEYBOARD. If the Operator wishes to hear the patient better, move the Jumper on JP5 to JP6, and readjust the potentiometers for Minimum and Maximum volume levels.

  • Jumpers JP7 and JP8 adjust the Base Amplification for the Operators Speaker, located within the MAGNET BORE. If the Patients are complaining that they cannot hear the Operator, move the Jumper on JP7 to JP8, and readjust the potentiometers for Minimum and Maximum volume levels.
