Ladder-Platform 2319156 Set-Up

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential Scratches/Cuts
The Ladder-Platform may have edges sharp enough to scratch or cut skin.
Wear leather gloves while assembling, adjusting or disassembling the Ladder-Platform to protect against scratches/cuts and to keep your hands clean.

1  Procedure

  1. Position the ladder components in an open area about 6 feet (1800 mm) square.

  1. Lay the platform section on the floor with its underside facing up as in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert one ladder leg in each positioning notch as in Illustration 1.

  1. Securely latch both ladder latches of each ladder leg to the platform. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust both telescoping sections of each ladder leg for the platform height required. Pinning the telescoping sections fully retracted yields a 40 inch (1016 mm) platform height, while fully extended yields a 50 inch (1270 mm) platform height. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully flip the assembled platform and ladder legs upright.

  1. Insert the long safety rail section in the pipe sleeves on the platform's long edge. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the short safety rail section in the pipe sleeves on the platform short edge that will not be needed as a ladder. See Illustration 4.
