Dock Pedal Stroke Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Tables Manufactured Before April 2002 (Signa Lightweight Patient Transport Table ONLY)

  1. Remove all outer and inner trim covers from sides of Patient Transport.

  1. Tighten or loosen the stroke adjustment screw as required so when the dock pedal is fully depressed during the docking operation, the hook cam mechanism just goes over-center. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Tables Manufactured After April 2002 (Signa Lightweight, Signa Oncology, and Signa OR-Compatible Patient Transport Tables)

  1. Remove the hatch at the rear of the table for adjusting the undock cable. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the Screw (Counter clock wise) until the table undocks easily when the pedal is pushed. (This will tighten the cable pulling the lever down). See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. When finished, tighten the lock nut and reattach the cover. See Illustration 3.
