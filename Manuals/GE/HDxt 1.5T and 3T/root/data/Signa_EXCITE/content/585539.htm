5  Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements: Store the coil in an air-conditioned scan room or equipment room. To store the coil, a storage space of 55 cm x 45 cm x 35 cm is required.

The equipment should be transported and stored under the following conditions:

  • Ambient temperature range of +5°C to +65°C.

  • Relative humidity range of 10% to 100%, noncondensing.

  • Atmospheric pressure range of 500 hPa to 1060 hPa.

Dimensions: 18 in. (W) x 15 in. (L) x 11 in (H), 46 cm x 38 cm x 28 cm

Weight: 20 lbs (9.09 kg)
