Emergency Stop and Off Check EXCITE

Last Update08/18/2004

1  Overview

  • The EMERGENCY STOP function of the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) for the Gradient cabinet, RF cabinet, System cabinet and magnet enclosure

The PDU EMERGENCY STOP buttons are located on the Operator Workspace Keyboard and on the Magnet Enclosure front cover (two buttons, left and right).

An EMERGENCY OFF button is located on the System Main Disconnect Panel. The other EMERGENCY OFF buttons are located by the customer.

Illustration 1:  PDU Emergency Stop Button Locations

2  System Shutdown

  1. Shut down the PC by clicking on the [Start] button, selecting [Shut Down] and confirming the operation.

  2. Shut down the LINUX PC by going to the Service Desktop, clicking on [System Shutdown], and confirming the operation.

3  Emergency Stop

Illustration 2:  Emergency Stop Locations

  1. Press one of the EMERGENCY STOP buttons as shown in Illustration 2.

  2. Verify the power to the Gradient subsystem and RF subsystem is shut off by checking that the cabinet cooling fans have stopped. Also, verify that the Display LEDs on the Magnet Enclosure are not illuminated.

NOTE:Activation of the Emergency Stop Button for ACGD will remove the high voltage power from the gradient driver, so there will be no gradient power output to the body coil. However, be aware that the low voltage power will stay enabled in the ACGD cabinet after the Emergency Stop Button is pressed. This low voltage power remains on so the ACGD will be ready for scanning when full power is restored.

  1. On the PDU, press the EMO Reset Button to return power to all effected cabinets. See Illustration 3 for ACGD PDU.

  2. Repeat steps 1 through 3 for each of the other EMERGENCY STOP buttons.

Illustration 3:  ACGD OR RF-PDU

4  Emergency Off

NOTE:The AC power for the Shield/Cryo-Cooler compressor and the Magnet Monitor are not controlled by the Emergency Off button.

  1. Press one of the EMERGENCY OFF buttons (the location of buttons is determined by the customer).

  2. On the System Main Disconnect Panel, press the PDU POWER ON button. The rest of the system should automatically power on.

  3. On the PDU, press the EMO RESET button.

  4. Repeat steps 1-4 for the other EMERGENCY OFF buttons.
