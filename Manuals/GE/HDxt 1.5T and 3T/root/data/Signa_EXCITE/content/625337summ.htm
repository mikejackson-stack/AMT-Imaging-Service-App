3.0T HD T/R Knee Array Coil Setup for Coil MCQA Test

2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Place the 3T HD Knee Array, the phantom positioner, and the phantom on the patient cradle as shown in Illustration 1. Take note of the serial number of the coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the phantom positioner is aligned with the posterior coil section as indicated by the marks on the positioner (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Being careful to align the connector pins, place the anterior coil section onto the posterior coil section and latch into place (see Illustration 3). The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the coil is aligned and centered with respect to the baseplate as indicated by the arrows on the baseplate offset scale (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Establish an axial landmark using the reference mark on top of the coil (see Illustration 5). Advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

2.2   Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the lower section of the coil on the patient table as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align the alignment mark on the coil to zero position on the scale as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom into the positioner as shown in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place this phantom-positioner setup on the lower part of the coil as shown in Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the phantom positioner is aligned with the posterior coil section as indicated by the marks on the positioner and the coil (see Illustration 10).

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully align the connector pins on the lower part of the coil with the upper part of the coil and latch both the coil sections into place as in Illustration 11. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA and landmark the coil at the cross mark available near the handle of the coil (see Illustration 12). Advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool procedure to test the coil.
