1  Procedure

1.1   OVERVIEW AND FLOWCHART

  1. This procedure describes how to calibrate the AMT RF Amplifier in the 3.0T Signa system, (Also referred to as MNS - Multi-Nuclear-Spectroscopy or Broad Band Amplifier).

  1. Calibration is done to make sure that the AMT RF amplifier is producing the specified amount of RF power for broadband operation. The RF output power should be checked if any of the hardware in the RF chain is replaced. If the output levels are not within specification, then adjustments should be made to the AMT RF amplifier output to achieve the proper level of power. The AMT- (MNS) amplifier output should be adjusted to a maximum output power of 2.5kW.

    (Step Details)

  1. This procedure is for 3T Head and Body systems only.

    (Step Details)

    1. Any of the AMT- RF amplifier FRU 1’s has been changed; this change can possibly affect the power gain of the amplifier.

  1. DISABLE T/R DRIVE FAULT CONDITION

    1. The T/R drive system will generate a fault and inhibit scan if the Head T/R Networks are not attached to the RF Amp. This calibration procedure requires the disconnection of the cables that cause this fault condition, which will allow the system to scan.

    1. Verify that the system is idle, and all coils have been removed from magnet bore

    1. Remove the front cover of the 3T Accessories Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the SSM, disable T/R error reporting by setting the TR switch to the DIS (disable faults) position.

  1. DISABLING THE RF POWER MONITOR

    NOTE:HVON should remain set to EN (Enabled), and the Power Monitor switches should remain in NORMAL position. The SSM Power Monitors are NOT used in this system.

    1. This section disables the 3.0T System RF Power Monitor by removal of the RF sense cables. This is necessary to perform the RF Maximum power calibrations for both Head and Body Modes.

    1. Open the rear door of the 3.0T Accessories cabinet.

    1. If not marked, make sure to mark the 4 sense cables.

    1. Remove the 4 RF sense cables from the Power Meters. Refer to illustration 3-1.

  1. DUMMY LOAD AND CABLES CALIBRATION

    1. Overview

      1. The Signa system Exciter, Receiver, reference cables, DC Block and adaptor connectors are used as a Test Setup to measure the attenuation of the following:

      1. Dummy load, connectors and test cables that will be used in the measurement.

      1. Process: The Test Setup is connected between J1 and J2 of the System cabinet (See Illustration 3). Receiver gains R1 and R2 and transmit gain TG are set for near full scale reading on the power spectrum during pre-scan calibration. A reference scan is performed and analyzed using the ‘atten_test’ tool on a minimum Test Setup to establish a base line. After the baseline is created the Test Setup hardware is reconfigured to include all of the cables, connectors and dummy load that will be used to make the measurement (SeeIllustration 3) and another scan is performed. The data from these two scans is then analyzed using the ‘atten_test’ tool.

      1. Output: The ‘atten_test ’ program will provide the [Magnitude Squared Attenuation Factor] for each test setup. The result will be use to calculate the Attenuation in (dB) for each test setup.

  1. MEASURING THE OUTPUT POWER OF THE AMT AMPLIFIER

    1. In this section you will re-connect the calibrated cables and Dummy load as shown in Illustration 5

    1. At the Front of the TPS Chassis set the safety switch to DISABLE the UCERD RF Output.

    1. Disconnect the RF output cable from the “RF OUTPUT” connector from the back of the AMT RF Amplifier.

    1. Re-connect the 30db Dummy Load to the "RF OUTPUT" connector, using the calibrated RF connectors and cables. Refer to Illustration 5.

      (Step Details)

    1. Confirm that the channel-input impedance is not set for 1 M-ohm.

    1. Confirm that the scope bandwidth limit switch is not enabled.

NOTE:Once you Download, the “Research” and “Spectro” LED’s on the front of the DRSM should activate.

  1. Preparing to Scan

    (Step Details)

  1. Calculation of RF power

    1. In this section you will actually be measuring the AMT Amplifier output. You will be using the Power Calculator or formula included, to determine the voltage level in amplitude necessary to achieve 2.5kW of power at the rear of the AMT RF amplifier.

    1. Peak voltage should be used in the calculation in order to get an accurate result. Use the formula Step  or the Power Calculator to get this Calculated Voltage Level. Scope correction factors and actual total losses associated with the test setup need to be factored in, to get an accurate measurement.

      (Step Details)

    1. From the formula or the Power Calculator, write down the Calculated Voltage Level in Illustration 6.

      (Step Details)

    1. Select : <Spectro Prescan>

    1. Entry Point: single1

    1. nucleus: 31

    1. R1: 13

    1. R2: 30

    1. AX : Default

    1. Select <Scan TR.>

    1. Set the TG to zero to start. Look at the oscilloscope. Verify its operation and its proper connection to the AMT amplifier output.

      (Step Details)

    1. Gradually increase the TG towards maximum value (200), while watching the oscilloscope. Refer to Step .

      (Step Details)

    1. At a TG of 200, the Calculated Voltage Level should equal the ACTUAL Voltage Measurement on the scope. If it does not, then adjust the DRSM Broad Band Gain Potentiometer up or DOWN until the Actual Voltage Measured is equal to the Calculated Voltage level.

      (Step Details)

    1. Decrease the TG to Zero (0)

    1. Select [Done].

    1. When the Actual Voltage Measured is set to the Calculated Voltage Level, this completes the calibration process.

    1. Troubleshooting Notes:

      1. If you cannot adjust the BB Gain pot enough to match the Calculated Voltage Level, you may have excessive losses, a bad DRSM or a bad Exciter.

      1. Check Cabling of DRSM and AMT amp.

      1. Check to make sure all cable connectors are tight.

      1. If you suspect excessive losses restart this procedure at section 4 - Dummy Load & Cables Calibration.

      1. Check Exciter Output. (Refer to Appendix- A)

  1. This section MUST be done to return the Power Monitor to the proper configuration. This MUST be performed before patient scanning resumes.

  1. Open the rear door of the 3.0T Accessories cabinet.

  1. Re-attach the 4 RF sense cables at the rear of the Power Meters. Refer to Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

1.4   SYSTEM RESTORATION

  1. Remove all test equipment.

  1. Verify the AMT heliax cable is connected to the "RF OUTPUT" connector and tightened securely.

  1. Re-install any cabinet covers if removed.

  1. Successfully complete one body scan.

  1. Successfully complete one Head scan.

1.5   SPECTRO RF AMP TROUBLESHOOTING

  1. Problem 1: Cannot attain 2.5kW output during calibration process.

    1. Solution 1: If you have not already performed Oscilloscope Roll-off Calculation, do so Now. Using this roll-off value re-calculate the Calculated Voltage Level.

    1. Solution 2: Using Illustration 10 in , Check the exciter (UCERD) output.

    1. Solution 3: Check cable losses between UCERD and back of RF amp. Length is a factor. Temporarily replace the cable with a shorter cable and re-measure at the output. (Refer to Illustration 10).

    1. Solution 4: Replace the cable between the DRSM and the Spectro RF amp.

    1. Solution 5: Possible Bad DRSM. Consult OLC for further troubleshooting support.

      (Step Details)

    1. Problem 2: AMT Amplifier goes into standby.

      1. if a fault occurs in the AMT (MNS) Amplifier, there can be up to a 5 to 6 minute delay for it to “self-clear”.

      1. Before proceeding with any troubleshooting, wait for at least 6 minutes for this action to occur.
