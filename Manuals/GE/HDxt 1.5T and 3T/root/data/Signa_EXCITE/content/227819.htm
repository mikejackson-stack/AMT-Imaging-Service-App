Re-evacuation of Cryostat

(Click Here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

Re-evacuation of the Cryostat is required when the magnet temperature has exceeded 100K. During the warm-up, gas is released into the vacuum chamber. The cryo-pumped gas, redeposited on the magnet's internal surfaces, can result in increased heat leakage if not removed. Internal temperatures must be >273K to ensure adequate pumping of water vapor.

4  Procedure

  1. Determine the temperature of the Magnet Cartridge using a Lakeshore 208 Thermometer (46-301478P1; Kit: 46-301477G2) or equivalent.

  1. If re-evacuation of the Cryostat is required, contact the GE Regional Service Engineer (MAC Team).

    NOTE:A Vacuum Pump Cart was developed to re-evacuate the Cryostat. Connection, monitoring, and pumping procedures are included with the cart. A 3 in. Valve Port Operator (46-252210P1) is required to connect the vacuum pumping equipment to the magnet's Pump-Out Port.
