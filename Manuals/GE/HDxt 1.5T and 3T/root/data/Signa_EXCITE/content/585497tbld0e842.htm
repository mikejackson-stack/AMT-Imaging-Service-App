Table 4:  PDU Fault Symptoms and Possible Causes

SymptomPossible Cause(s)
Bus OV, Bus UV and PSUV faults in power supply
  • PDU tapped incorrectly.
  • Inadequate power feed/UPS.
Power Supply IGBT failure, PSUV faultsUnbalanced voltages or missing phases indicate a feeder, PDU transformer or internal wiring problem.
E-off not working, or E-stop contactor will not pick up
  • A connector may be loose on the PDU control board.
  • Board may be bad.
