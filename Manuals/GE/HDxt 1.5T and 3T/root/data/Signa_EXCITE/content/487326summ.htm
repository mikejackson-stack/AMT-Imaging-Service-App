Bridge Removal and Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
THE PULLEY ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, REMOVE THE ASSEMBLY DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT INto THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, REMOVE THE ASSEMBLY DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT INto THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing Front Bridge

  1. Position the LPCA to the far rear of the enclosure, and undock the patient table.

  1. Detach the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. (See LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room.)

  1. Release drive belt tension by flipping lever under rear pedestal. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

  1. Count and make a note of the number of notches above the Belt Clamp. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew the two (2) Phillips screws that hold the belt clamp plate in place on each of the two belt clamps. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. Pull the drive belt free from the rear pedestal. Carefully unthread the drive belt from the rear pulley. (See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the rear of the split bridge, use a 17 mm wrench to remove the four (4) M10 nuts and washers that attach the rear pedestal to the split bridge.

  1. Using a 5 mm Allen wrench, remove the two (2) M6 screws holding the Front Bridge Cover in place (shown in Illustration 4), and remove the cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 17 mm wrench, remove the two (2) M10 Bridge Bracket bolts. (When these bolts are removed the height adjuster and spacer will no longer be secured.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the bridge out from magnet, making sure the drive belt comes along with the bridge.

1.2   Replacing Bridge Part within Bridge Assembly

  1. Lay bridge on its top and use a 5 mm Allen wrench to remove the four (4) M6 Allen bolts that secure the Bridge Mounting Bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the belt from bridge. (See Illustration 7.

  1. Flip the bridge so the top is facing up. Using a regular screwdriver, unscrew the two bolts. (See Illustration 8 for location of bolts.)

  1. Remove pulley as shown in Illustration 9.

1.3   Bridge Replacement / Installation

  1. If necessary, reinstall (or install new) Pulley ASM and Drive Belt onto new bridge by reversing the steps in Replacing Bridge Part within Bridge Assembly.

  1. To reinstall the bridge, perform the steps in Removing Front Bridge in reverse order.
