Host LCD Monitor Setup And Cal

The operator workspace monitor must be properly adjusted for the filmed image to accurately represent the console monitor displayed image. This procedure will describe how to adjust the monitor to match the camera. Once the monitor is re-calibrated, it is essential to re-calibrate the camera before the system is used for filming. This procedure is only for the LCD color monitor and not for the CRT monitor.

Although the steps outlined below are possible for a qualified GE Service Engineer for both Camera and Display adjustments, it is recommended that the following procedure are performed with the Camera Vendor field engineer for camera adjustments. With the Camera adjustments, it is recommended that you have one of the Customer’s filming specialists available for the fine tuning and quality review of the film/monitor conformance.

There are several active monitors being used at this time on Signa Scanners. If while moving through adjustments you do not see the monitor at your site, use the Generic instructions to proceed.

After monitor setup, perform Camera Calibrating.

If you have any problems during calibrations, refer to the troubleshooting Guide.
