Gradient Amplifier Control Board Replacement

2  Overview

This procedure explains the steps needed to replace the control board in one of the three Gradient Amplifiers.

3.3  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
POSSIBLE 800 VOLTS ON CAPACITORS.
TO PREVENT ELECTRIC SHOCK, REMOVE POWER TO THE GRADIENT AMPLIFIERS BEFORE PERFORMING THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PLACE THE 208V GRADIENT POWER BREAKER AND THE 420 V GRADIENT POWER BREAKER IN THE OFF POSITION AND LOCKOUT/TAGOUT THE BREAKER PANEL. WAIT AT LEAST 5 MINUTES AFTER THE UNIT HAS BEEN TURNED OFF BEFORE SERVICING.

4  Procedure

  1. Remove Power to the Gradient Amplifiers by placing the 208 V Gradient Power Breaker and the 420 V Gradient Power Breakers on the PDU in the OFF position.

  1. Lockout/Tagout the Breaker Panel on the PDU.

  1. Wait at least 5 minutes after the Gradient Amplifier has been turned off in order for the power on the capacitors to dissipate.

  1. Verify that power has been removed from the cabinet. Take a Digital Multi-meter and set it to its highest AC voltage range and on the terminal block in the rear of the cabinet of the Gradient Amplifier-PS, measure between the ground connection and the incoming power connection at the black wire (see Illustration 1). Repeat this measurement between the ground connection and the red wire, and between the ground connection and the white wire. A measurement of 0 VAC indicates that the 420V incoming power has been successfully turned off.

    Illustration 1:  Verifying Power Removal

  1. Note the location of the Gradient Amplifier(s) Module. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Gradient Cabinet

  1. Put on the ESD Grounding Strap.

  1. Loosen the captive screws on the Control Board faceplate. Remove the board by simultaneously pressing up on the upper ejector and down on the lower ejector on the faceplate. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Releasing a Control Board

  1. Remove the Control Board.

  1. Remove the replacement Control Board from its anti-static bag.

  1. Install the new Control Board making sure the board is fully seated.

  1. Tighten captive screws.

5  Finalization

  1. Remove the Lock Out Tag Out from the Gradient PDU Breaker Panel.

  1. Place the Gradient Breakers in the ON position, restoring power to the Gradient modules.

  1. Verify that power has been restored to the cabinet. The LEDs on the Gradient Amplfiers & the Gradient Amplfier-PS will be illuminated.

  1. Run Digital DC Offsets:

    1. Select [Diagnostics] from the Service Desktop and select [Start].

    1. Select [IPG], [Manual], [Digital DC Offset], then [Close].

  1. Perform the DQA Tool.

  1. To ensure the Control Board replaced into the system is the correct board for that system, run Gradient Diagnostics Fidelity Tests. See the Gradient Diagnostics section of Peripheral Diagnostics for help.

    In the event the wrong control board is replaced in the system, 1 or all of the following errors will be logged:
