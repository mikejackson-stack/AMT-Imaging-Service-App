1  Procedure

1.1   Phantom Positioning

Perform Step  if running head coil test. Perform Step  if you are running the body coil test.

  1. Head Coil Positioning Using Head Coil

    1. Place the head coil on the patient table.

    1. Install the Head Sphere Phantom in the Head Loader.

    1. Place the phantom in the head coil. See Illustration 1.

      (Step Details)

    1. Landmark on the center of the Head Loader.

      (Step Details)

  1. Body Coil Phantom Positioning

    1. Place the short body loader and sphere on the patient table. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. Landmark on the center of the short-body loader.

1.2   Test Execution

  1. Noise-Only Test Setup

    1. Click the (scan) icon to go to the scan page.

    1. If necessary, click , then click to cancel any previous exam.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click on .

    1. Enter geservice under Patient ID.

    1. Enter 111 under Weight

  1. Noise-Only Test Execution

    1. Click the (Service Tools) icon to open the Service Desktop Manager.

    1. Click in the lower part of the Service Desktop Manager if the Common Service Desktop is not already visible.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click the (Image Quality) tab at the top of the Common Service Desktop.

    1. Select [Noise Only Test] from the menu on the left side of the screen as shown in Illustration 3.

      (Step Details)

    1. Under [Noise Only Test] on the right side of the screen, select [Click here to start this tool]. The Noise Only Test window opens as shown in Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

  1. Set up the screen as described below:

    1. Choose a coil from the list.

    1. Select R to display Raw Data or | to display Image Data (Test always uses Raw data internally)

    1. <Enter> to continue.

    1. After completion, the test displays the calculated RMS noise value, along with the upper spec limit and typical value for the selected coil.

      (Step Details)

  1. See Viewing Test Results or if problems arise, see Troubleshooting.
