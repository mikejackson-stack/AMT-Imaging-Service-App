ICW Table Adjustments

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click the Calibration tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. If it is required to be completed, the Table Adjustments checklist will be one of the first items listed on the Installation and Calibration Wizard (ICW) screen shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Longitudinal Drive Calibration. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Longitudinal Drive Calibration done?

  1. Perform Longitudinal Drive Clutch Tension Adjustment.

  1. Perform Longitudinal Drive Motor Alignment to Field. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Longitudinal Drive Motor Alignment to Field done?

  1. Perform Leveling Patient Transport.

  1. Perform Table Top Height Adjustment.

  1. Perform Cradle Release from Patient Transport.

  1. Perform Cradle Release from Carriage Adjustment.

  1. Click [Submit]. This will complete the Table Adjustment section of the ICW.

2  Finalization

  1. Return to the Installation and Calibration Wizard for instructions on advancing to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.
