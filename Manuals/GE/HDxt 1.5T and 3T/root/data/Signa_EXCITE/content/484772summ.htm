Equipment damage possibility. Completely remove the Quad Head Coil from the cradle before performing any body scans. Failure to do so may damage head coil T/R network.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   BODY FORWARD/REVERSE QUADRATURE SCANS

  1. Prepare the system for scan using the following:

    1. Click on [New Pt] and enter the following:

      (Step Details)

    1. Set Patient Protocols to Service.

    1. Under Patient Protocols select [Service] then [Other].

    1. Find "Protocol" F and R quadrature and click on the protocol, then click on the "Series" to be used.

    1. Click on [Accept] to download the series.

  1. At the scanner, remove any surface or head coil from the bore.

  1. Place the Body TLT Sphere Phantom in the SPT Body Loader and place the Body Loader/TLT Phantom in the center of the cradle.

  1. Landmark on the center of the sphere; press [LANDMARK], at the keypad on the front of the magnet enclosure, then press [ADV TO SCAN].

  1. Select [Save Series], then [Download]. Complete the scan by selecting the [Prescan] option, and then the [Scan] option.

  1. Reverse the blue RF cables connecting to J3 and J4 to the hybrid splitters from the body coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Without changing the prescan settings, rescan the phantom using the same scan protocol.

  1. Restore the system to the original configuration (i.e., return J3 and J4 to their original connection).

  1. For analysis, see Step .

1.2   FORWARD/REVERSE QUADRATURE TEST IMAGE ANALYSIS

This analysis procedure applies to either body or head scans.

  1. Display the first image.

  1. Use a square Region of Interest (ROI) of 4000 mm2 ± 50 mm2, located at the center of the image. Measure the mean pixel value (MPV1). In the viewer, this is shown as m=xxx. Jot down this value. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Display the second image.

  1. Use the same square Region of Interest (ROI) of 4000 mm2 ± 50 mm2, located at the center of the image (see note, below). Measure the mean pixel value (MPV2). In the viewer, this is shown as m=xxx. Jot down this value. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the cable configuration which produced the highest mean value from the ROI for normal system scanning.

2  Finalization

  1. Restore the system to patient scanning conditions.

  1. Do a test scan to ensure the system is running properly.
