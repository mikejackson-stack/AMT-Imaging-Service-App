Magnet Enclosure Display Test Help

1  Diagnostic Link

SRI Functional Tests

2  Purpose

This diagnostic tests the LED's on the magnet enclosure display.

3  Components Tested

4  Requirements

5  Block Diagram

6  Test Sequence

The SCP sends the Lamp Test opcode to the SRI. At this point, the SRI will stop responding to any commands, and cyclically illuminate the LED's on the display. Once the operator presses the [Done] button, the SCP will reset the SRI to end the test.
