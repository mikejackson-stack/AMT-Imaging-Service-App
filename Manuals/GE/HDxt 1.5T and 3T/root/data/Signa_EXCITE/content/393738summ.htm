1.5T Neurovascular Array Mirror Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert a #1 Philips screwdriver into the pin removal fixture supplied with the replacement mirror. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the pin removal fixture over the mirror pivot pin and begin to apply pressure. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply pressure to the pin removal fixture until the fixture is fully engaged. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove and replace the mirror assembly. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the mirror pivot pin back into place. See Illustration 5.
