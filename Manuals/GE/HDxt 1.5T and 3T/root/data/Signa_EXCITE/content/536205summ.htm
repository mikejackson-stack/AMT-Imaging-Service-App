Gradient Switch Functional Check

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Disable any software running in the background on the laptop, such as Personal Digital Assistant (PDA) syncing or “Hotlink” programs, that take exclusive control of the COM1 port and will prevent HyperTerminal from using this port. Failure to do this will result in COM1 port availability errors when attempting to configure the port from HyperTerminal.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Lock Out / Tag Out the Gradient Switch Cabinet. (The gradient switch cabinet is controlled by the breaker labelled [TAC/BRM GWC]).

  1. After ensuring the power is OFF, remove the protective plastic panel on the rear of the Gradient Switch module.

  1. Connect one end of the Serial Port Cable to the J6 Debug Terminal on the rear of the Gradient Switch and the other end to the 9 pin Sub-D COM1 serial port on the rear of the laptop.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Lock Out / Tag Out.

  1. Power on the Gradient Switch cabinet. When working properly, three (3) WB LEDs are lit and the Power Supply LED is lit.

  1. Double click the GradSwitch icon on the laptop to open the HyperTerminal window to begin a session.

  1. Type: File/Properties/Settings/ASCII Setup/and press <Enter>.

  1. Choose [Send Line Ends With Line Feeds] and [Echo Typed Characters Locally], and confirm by clicking [OK].

  1. Type in all capital letters GSC_SW 1 1 1and press <Enter>.

    1. WB is still on

    1. The Terminal response display will be: GSC_STAT 1 1 1.

  1. Type : GSC_SW 2 2 2and press <Enter>.

    1. The Switch will change to ZM status (the three green LEDs ZM will be ON).

    1. The Terminal response display will be: GSC STAT 2 2 2

    1. If an error exists, it will be displayed on the Terminal display screen.

  1. To end the HyperTerminal session: in the Call menu, click Disconnect.

3  Finalization

  1. Lock Out / Tag Out the Gradient Switch Cabinet. (The gradient switch cabinet is controlled by the breaker labelled [TAC/BRM GWC]).

  1. Disconnect the serial cable from the J6 Debug Terminal.

  1. Replace the protective plastic panel.

  1. Remove Lock Out / Tag Out.

  1. Perform a goodbye scan.
