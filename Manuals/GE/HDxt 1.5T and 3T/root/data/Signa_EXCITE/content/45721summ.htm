1.3   Turning the Auto Adjust Level Off (To prevent the display from incorrectly sizing the text or graphics after recovering from “sleep mode”)

  1. Power off the monitor

  1. Hold the <Select / 1--2> button as you power on the monitor

  1. After the Brightness/Contrast menu appears, release the <Select / 1--2> button

  1. Press the <Exit> button

  1. Press the <>> button twice to move to tab 3 ( Auto Adjust Level)

  1. Press the <Select / 1--2> button

  1. Press the <+> or <–> buttons to select “OFF”

  1. Press the <Exit> button twice to quit the monitor adjustment window.

  1. Power the monitor off and then on to reset to the OSM.
