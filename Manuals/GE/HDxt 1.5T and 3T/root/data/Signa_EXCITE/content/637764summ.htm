3  Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, select the scan icon in the desktop control panel.

  1. If necessary, exit out of any previous exams by selecting [End Exam].

  1. Click on [New Pt] and enter the following:

    1. Id: geservice

    1. Name: snr check

    1. Weight (lb.): 111

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the head coil, if present, from the cradle.

  1. Load the phantom on the table based on the loader you are using:

    1. Setup using SPT Loader: Position the SPT body loader and body SNR sphere on the table and landmark per Illustration 1. Check the temperature indicator on the SNR sphere. Make sure that the temperature is 22° C ± 2° C before starting the procedure.

      (Step Details)

    1. Setup using Long Body Loader: Position the body SNR sphere in the center of the long body loader and landmark per Illustration 2. Check the temperature indicator on SNR sphere. Make sure that the temperature is 22° C ± 2° C before starting the procedure.

      (Step Details)

  1. At the operator workspace, set Patient Protocols to Service.

  1. Select the protocol by clicking Other and select the SNR Check protocol and series 1 from the menu, then click [Accept] to load the SNR Check (Body, Axial) protocol. For TwinSpeed, set the GradMode to the desired selection.

  1. Press MOVE TO SCAN. Allow phantom solution to settle for 20 minutes before starting scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Autoview], just below the Autoview image display screen; the images will be displayed automatically.

  1. Click on [Save Series].

  1. Click on [Auto Prescan]. After prescan, check the R1 value. R1 must equal 11 to get valid results. Record R1, R2, TG, and system frequency values in the Data Sheet. If R1 does not equal 11, some of the items to check are the following:

    1. Check for proper cable length to preamp.

    1. Make sure that DQA Tool has been performed.

    1. Ensure that slice thickness is within specification.

    1. Download TPS again, then on the Service Desktop click on [Reset TPS]. Repeat Step 1 through Step 12.

  1. Click on [Scan]. After scan #1 is complete, click on [Scan] again (two back-to-back scans).

  1. After the series #1 back-to-back scans are complete, click on [New Series].

  1. Setup a scan by clicking on Other and select the SNR Check protocol and series 2 from the menu, then click [Accept] to load the SNR Check (Body, Sagittal) protocol. For TwinSpeed, select the same GradMode.

  1. Click [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click [Auto Prescan]. After prescan, check the R1 value. If R1 does not equal 11, refer to Step 11 for items to check. Record R1, R2, TG, and system frequency values in the Data Sheet.

  1. Click [Scan]. After the first scan of series #2 is complete, click [Scan] again (two back-to-back scans).

  1. After the series #2 back-to-back scans are complete, click [New Series].

  1. Setup a scan by clicking Other and select the SNR Check protocol and series 3 from the menu, then click [Accept] to load the SNR Check (Body, Coronal) protocol. For TwinSpeed, select the same GradMode.

  1. Enter the anterior offset for Scanning Range start/end location. Find the offset as follows:

    1. Click the (Image Browser) icon. When the browser is displayed, click [Viewer].

    1. Click [Measure], then select the rectangular cursor tool. Rotate the cursor box 90 degrees clockwise by dragging the rotation handle until the handle marker is placed on the display's right side. See Illustration 3.

      (Step Details)

    1. Adjust the rectangular cursor size, dragging the size/shape handle, so that all four corners touch the edge of the image.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click [Measure], then select the (report cursor) tool. Click the rectangle created in step c, then place the report cursor on top of the rotation handle of the rectangular cursor box. Read the A/P location. See Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click the (scan) icon. In the SCANNING RANGE area, enter that value for the A/P Start and End values (anterior offset) of the coronal scan.

  1. Click [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click [Auto Prescan]. After prescan, check the R1 value. If R1 does not equal 11, refer to Step 11 for items to check. Record R1, R2, TG, and system frequency values in the Data Sheet.

  1. Click [Scan]. After the first scan of series #3 is complete, click [Scan] again (two back-to-back scans).

  1. For analysis, refer to SNR Image Analysis.

  1. Return to the Signal to Noise Checks main menu.
