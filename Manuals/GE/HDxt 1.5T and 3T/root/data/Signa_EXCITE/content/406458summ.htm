3.0T HD Neurovascular Array Setup for Coil MCQA Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: HDNV ArrayServ, HDNV HeadServ, and HDNV SpectroServ.

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Setup

  1. Insert phantom on posterior section of coil. Position anterior component over phantom and secure with latches on either side.

  1. Locate and release button on chest piece and slide chest piece up into the coil. Move chest piece down onto the phantom using handle. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure phantom in the coil with Velcro straps. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark coil using positioning mark. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.

    (Step Details)

2.2   Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the base of the NV array coil on the patient table and latch it to the table as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom positioner in the coil as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantoms in the positioner as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully align the connector pins on the base of the Coil with the top part of the coil and latch both the coil sections into place as shown as in Illustration 7. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly).

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the button, and pull the chest section completely out.

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil at the markings as shown in Illustration 8. Advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the MCQA procedure to test the coil.

    (Step Details)
