2  Procedure

2.1   Powering off Host and GOCAA

2.1.1  Bring the Signa software down.

  1. Click [Toolbelt] icon.

  1. On the Service Desktop Manager, click [System Shutdown].

  1. Confirm shutdown by clicking [OK].

  1. Wait for the system to indicate on the monitor that it is safe to power off the computer before proceeding. This usually takes about 90 seconds before this message is seen.

2.1.2  Remove power to the Operators Workspace

  1. Shut off the PC (see Illustration 1) and host circuit breakers on the front of the PDU (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the circuit breakers to the left to disconnect power (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

2.2   GOC Power Distribution Supply Replacement

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by removing the four (4) screws in the rear (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cord from the rear of the GOC.

  1. Unplug the remaining cables from the power supply.

  1. Remove the two (2) screws and ground cable from the rear of the GOC Power Distribution Supply (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the attachment screws from the GOC Power Distribution Supply (see Illustration 5).

  1. Remove the four (4) screws from the door hinge.

  1. Carefully slide the GOC Power Distribution Supply out of the GOC.

  1. Insert the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply. Reverse the preceding steps to properly install the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply.

3  Finalization

  1. Turn the host and PC circuit breakers on the PDU back to the ON position (move the breakers to the right (see Illustration 3).

  1. Start the host computer by pressing the ON button (see Illustration 1).

  1. Log in.

    (Step Details)

  1. When the system is fully booted perform a scan to ensure proper operation of the system.
