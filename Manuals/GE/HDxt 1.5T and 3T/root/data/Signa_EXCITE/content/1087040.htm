Illustration 4 shows the 12-Channel HDx/HDxt Single Connector Body Array Coil. The anterior and posterior of the 12-Channel Body Array Coil is the same for the HDx, HDxt, MR450 and MR450w systems. The only difference is the system end connector. The HDx system uses a Hypertronics Connector and the MR450/MR450w systems use a P-Connector.
