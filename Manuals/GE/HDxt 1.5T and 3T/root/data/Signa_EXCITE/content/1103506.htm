2.3  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable is disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in On or Standby mode.
Disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the Workstation.

CAUTION
Memory module damage could result during removal or installation.
If the power cord is not unplugged while installing the memory module(s), damage could result, and the system will not recognize the memory module changes.
Power off the Workstation and unplug the power cord from the AC power outlet. Wait until the LED on the back of the Power Supply turns off before removing the memory module.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

3  Procedure

3.1  Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Close any PC applications, and power down the GOC. (Refer to System Startup and Shutdown.)

  1. Perform LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace before beginning the replacement.

NOTICE
When removing the side panel, avoid straining the ground lead.
 
 

  1. Remove the two screws on the left side panel of the GOC.

    Illustration 1:  Screws on Left Side Panel

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by lifting it up.

  1. Disconnect the short ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.

    Illustration 2:  Ground Lead

  1. Remove all the cables connected to the computer, ensure they are properly labeled, and note their locations for reattachment. (Illustration below shows the cable configuration.)

    Illustration 3:  HP Z400 Cable Configuration

  1. Loosen the strap and remove the hook as shown below.

    Illustration 4:  Removing Strap

  1. Refer to the illustration below, and remove the Z400 Computer as follows:

    1. Remove the two screws securing the back bracket.

    1. Loosen the two screws on back slide plate, and slide the plate backward.

    1. Loosen the two screws on the side slide plate, and slide the plate in the direction of the arrow as shown.

      Illustration 5:  Removing HP Z400 Computer

    NOTICE
    The computer weighs 50 pounds, which requires two people to move the PC cabinet.
     
     

    1. Carefully remove the computer using the back and bottom brackets from the GOC.

  1. Remove the two screws (shown below) that secure the left side panel to the rear of the PC.

    Illustration 6:  Screws on Side Panel of PC

  1. Remove the left side panel of the PC by pulling the latch (shown below) out, and sliding the cover back. Set the cover aside in a safe location.

    Illustration 7:  Latch on Side Panel

  1. Lay the Workstation on its side with the system board facing up.

3.2  Memory Module Replacement

To replace the defective Dual In-line Memory Module (DIMM):

  1. Gently push out and down on the socket levers on the slot that contains the faulty memory module.

    Illustration 8:  Socket Lever Locations

  1. Lift the memory module straight up as shown, and remove it from the slot.

    Illustration 9:  Removing Memory Module

NOTICE
DIMMs and DIMM sockets are keyed for proper installation. Be sure these guides line up before pushing down in the next step.
 
 

  1. Place the new Memory Module into the open slot, and push it down until the socket levers latch it into place.

  1. Ensure that the Memory Module is fully inserted and properly seated within the slot.

4  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Reboot and ensure that no memory BIOS errors are observed during the boot process.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
