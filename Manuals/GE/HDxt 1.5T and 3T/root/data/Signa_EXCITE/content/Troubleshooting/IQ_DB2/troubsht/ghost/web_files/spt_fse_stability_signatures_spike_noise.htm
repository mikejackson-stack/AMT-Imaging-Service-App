Spike Noise
The example below shows evidence of spike noise.  See how the spikes occur completely randomly through the echo train; which would suggest something happening at receive time.  Additionally note that in this case these spikes are affecting echo shift , constant phase, and magnitude.  This may not always be the case with spike noise.  The example below is a Body FSE stability plot.  This problem was severe enough to show some similar evidence in the Body FGRE plots though you will typically see the evidence of spike noise in the FSE plots.  This problem was due to loose hardware on the rear pedestal.  This problem did not show any evidence of spike noise in the Head FSE stability plots. I believe that this was probably due to the location of the source of the transient noise.
