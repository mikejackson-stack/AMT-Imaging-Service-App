Fine Line Artifact: Antenna Positioning

Image Info:



Symptom:
Fine Line Artifact.  

Flowchart:
Refer to the Geometric Distortion Troubleshooting Flowchart.

Root Cause/Solution:
The source of this fine line artifact is related to the antenna positioning. If the patient is very skinny, the abdomen needs to be padded to avoid the concave curving of the torso PA antenna. The antenna would normally have a convex curve (same as the curve of the bore), but if it also curves following the patient's anatomy (thoracic to abdomen curve) they will see the artifact.
