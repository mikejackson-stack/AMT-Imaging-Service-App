FAT SAT Shading

Image Info:
The system is a 1.5T 5.X.  

Symptom:
The image seen here shows some areas with shading.  

Flowchart:
See the Shading Troubleshooting Flowchart.

Root Cause:
This was a sequence done with FAT SAT.  The center frequency was off by about 100Hz, this caused the SAT pulse to saturate some of the water frequency.
