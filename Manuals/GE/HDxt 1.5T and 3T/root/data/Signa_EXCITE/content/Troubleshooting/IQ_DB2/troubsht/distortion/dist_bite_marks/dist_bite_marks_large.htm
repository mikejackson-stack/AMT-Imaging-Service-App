Bite Marks (Common Symptoms: FAT SAT or Shim)

 

Effectivity
Any currently produced Active Shield magnets. This includes the 3.0T 3T94 and Short Bore magnets, 1.5T S4, S5, CX150 and CX150K4 (LCC1.5) magnets and their 1.0T counterparts the SX, SXC_1, SXC_2, CX100, CX100K4 (LCC1.0) magnets.  Also, 0.5T VMX and VMX-II are Actively Shielded.

Symptom
Due to the interaction of the bucking coil to the main coils magnet field, artifacts sometimes refereed to as bitemarks can be observed on active shield magnets. These artifacts are only seen at large Field of Views (i.e. 38 - 48 cm) and are most pronounced in Fat-Sat scans whose effectiveness can be compromised on the outer edges of these scans. These artifacts are most often seen in the Coronal and Sagittal scan planes.

The images in Example 1 and 1a are both Coronal images of the LVshim phantom at a 48cm FOV with identical scan parameters (including Fat-Sat) .Example 1a is the Active Shield magnet and its image displays the bitemarks on the edges of the phantom. (Both images also have wrap-around, left and right.)

Common characteristics for this type of artifact:

· Can occur on Active Shield Magnets (S4, S5, SX, SXC_1, SXC_2).
· Only occurs on the edges of very large FOV scans.
· Typically, the bitemarks occur at regular evenly spaced intervals.
· The bitemarks usually have the same depth of penetration (shim dependent, i.e. the better the shim the more symmetrical the depth).
· More visible with Fat - Water suppression techniques which is very B0 homogeneity dependent.
· Bitemarks almost always occur outside imaging area of interest (i.e. in the subcutaneous fat rather than in the internal organs).

What Can Service Do to Correct This:

Refer to the Geometric Distortion Troubleshooting Flowchart for ideas.  Nothing will really make this go away. Make sure that the magnet is shimmed to spec. and any in-bore passive shimming is based on the 45cm DSV mechanical plot. All S4 magnets with a serial number less than G185 where originally shimmed on the cylindrical C6 plot. There can be a reduction in the penetration of the bitemarks, by re-doing the passive shims, if they are still based on the C6 plots. There is no other solution, other than reduction of the field of view.
