Y-Axis SGA Failure

Image Info:
This is from a 1.5T system.  

Symptom:
Site was reporting shading on body images as shown in the example image. Problem not visible on images from anything other than Body Coil.  FE ran SPT, RFT and Forward/Reverse Quadrature Check to confirm that the RF hardware was in good working order and all test and functional check results looked normal. FE confirmed that SSM was providing correct amount of high voltage to DD boards in RF Body Coil. Shading was always on right side (as shown in image) of the RF Body Coil. Test results indicated that RF hardware was in good working order and was not the problem. FE swapped X and Y gradients and was able to eliminate the problem.  

Flowchart:
See the Shading Troubleshooting Flowchart.

Root Cause:
Swapping phase and frequency selection may also have caused a beneficial change in image quality. Through this troubleshooting the FE was able to isolate the problem down to the Y SGA.  

Solution:
Replacing the Y SGA solved this problem
