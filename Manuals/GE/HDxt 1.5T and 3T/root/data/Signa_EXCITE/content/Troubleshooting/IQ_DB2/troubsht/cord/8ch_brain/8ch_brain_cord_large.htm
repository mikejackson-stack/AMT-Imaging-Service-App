Corduroy in 8-Channel Brain Coil

Image Info:
The above two images show the image data from DWIWP and the Raw Data.  

Symptom:
Notice the strong corduroy in the DWI image data on the left and the obvious spikes in the raw data on the right, however the EPI White Pixel test did not fail.  

Flowchart:
It is recommended that you follow the Corduroy/EPI White Pixel Troubleshooting Flowchart before considering any replacement parts!

Root Cause:
The root cause of this problem turned out to be due to many causes:

  1. Light bulb issues:  Customer was using long steam light bulbs, so changed them to incandescent light bulbs
  2. Arcing of DC Lighting Controllers: Caught by attaching a scope to the service BNC connection, J30,  on the back of the UTNS board.  See images below (scope setting was on envelope mode, 0.2V/division)
  3. One Dynamic Disable line was nicked and covered-up by the mechanical installers.  Re-terminated the line.
  4. 8 of the 32 Dynamic Disable boards were bad.  Discovered this by removing all DD boards in rear of magnet and installing 2 at a time.

Solution:
A special software patch was added to SPT svat in order to be able to run EPIWP on the brain coil.  (This fix will be made available to other sites at a later date).
