RF Leak

Symptom:
Lines through top of image and ghosting of the phantom

Flowchart:
See the Zipper Troubleshooting Flowchart

Root Cause:
In this case, the problem was caused by an RF screen room leak at the base of the screen room door.  The customer had earlier waxed the floor in the operator room and had also waxed the base of the screen room door. 

Solution:
The FE cleaned the wax off the base of the door and cleared-up this artifact
