Image Info:
LISA is a normal shading artifact that will become more pronounced at higher field strengths.  

Symptom:
Shading in images.

Root Cause/Flowchart:
To prove if the shading that you are seeing is LISA or not, scan with a Silicon phantom and the torso coil.  If the problem disappears, then it was LISA and there is no defective hardware in the system causing this shading.  If the shading does not go away with the torso coil and silicon phantoms, then follow the Shading Flowchart to troubleshoot a possible problem with the system.
