Quick Disconnect Connection

Image Info:
The signal void seen here with the CTL coil 

Symptom:
Signal void looking like a possible dead channel

Flowcharts:
See the Coil Troubleshooting Flowcharts and the Shading Troubleshooting Flowcharts.

Root Cause:
In this case the problem was corrected by simply reseating the connector at the quick disconnect.  There were no multicoil errors in this case but there must have been a poor connection to the pin that is connected to the channel with very low signal.
