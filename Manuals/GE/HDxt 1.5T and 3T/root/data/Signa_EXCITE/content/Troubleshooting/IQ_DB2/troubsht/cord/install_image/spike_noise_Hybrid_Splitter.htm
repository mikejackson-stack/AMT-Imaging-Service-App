Check Test Points – Diode Test:

  • J1 of Hybrid Splitter: Check resistance from Center Pin to Case.  Resistance value should be between 0.35 W  and 0.6 W.  Reversing leads should result in an open indication
  • J6 of Hybrid Splitter: Negative lead on center pin and positive lead on shield.  Resistance value should be between 0.35 W and 0.6 W.  Reversing the leads should result in an open indication.  If diodes show a resistance or short (small resistance) in both directions, the diode is bad.
