EPI Bandpass Asymmetry

Image Info:
The system is a 1.5T TwinSpeed with Excite.  This is a DWEPI scan done in ZOOM mode. 

Symptom: 
Shaded band and increased N2 ghost. 

Flowchart:
See the Ghosting Troubleshooting Flowchart. 

Root Cause:
The shaded band and Increased N2 ghost are the result of no short term eddy currents.  This was an oversight by the FE when he ran Grafidy 3 at install.  He did not enable the short term defaults.  

Solution:
In order to resolve this, FE went back into Grafidy 3 and enabled the short term defaults for both WHOLE and ZOOM and then ran EPT again on both WHOLE and ZOOM.
