3.0T 8 Channel Hi Res Brain Array Setup for Coil MCQA Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

These coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool for 15.x software and earlier: HDx 8HRBrain and HDx 8HRBrainServ.

2  Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Attach the base plate with 3.0T HD 8 Channel High Res Brain Array Coil to the cradle in the same position on the cradle as the standard head coil. Note the serial number of the coil.

  1. Slide the coil back to permit access to the head holder as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom ring on the head holder as shown in Illustration 2. (The edge of the ring that goes toward the magnet has edges that overhang the head holder to secure the ring in position.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Head TLT Sphere on the phantom ring as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the coil all the way forward (away from the magnet) on the baseplate, and set the locks as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil, and Advance to Scan.

  1. Click here to go to the MCQA test.
