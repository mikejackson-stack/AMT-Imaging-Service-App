3.0T HD Cardiac Array Coil Setup for Coil MCQA Test

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Setup

  1. Position the posterior coil component on the system table. Ensure that the Velcro straps hang free on either side of the table. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position bottom phantom positioner on top of the posterior coil component. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert phantom into positioner as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place Anterior Phantom Positioner as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place anterior portion of coil over phantom positioner as shown Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure with Velcro straps.

  1. Connect coil to system.

  1. Move the table towards the bore and landmark in center of anterior coil portion on the lines provided. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.

2.2   Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section in such a way that the phantom is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 8 and Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the anterior section on the phantom set such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantom as shown in Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the cross mark as shown in Illustration 11 and press advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
