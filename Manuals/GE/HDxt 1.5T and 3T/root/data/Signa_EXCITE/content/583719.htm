1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsProcedure

2  Overview

Follow this process to replace field replaceable units for the 1.5T HD 8-Channel High Resolution Brain Array by Invivo (M3335LL).

3  Tools and Test Equipment

1/4 in. flat-blade screwdriver (for cable replacement)

4  Replacement Parts

Refer to the FRU document.

5  Procedure

5.1  Cable Continuity Check

Troubleshooting should be performed by a GE authorized Service Engineer only.

5.2  Cable Replacement Procedure

NOTICE
This procedure is to be performed by a GE authorized Service Engineer only.
 
 

The four coil connector mounting screws are located at the superior end of the coil. Remove these 4 mounting screws as detailed in Illustration 1. Remove the coil connector by pulling away from the coil in the superior direction.

Illustration 1:  Remove Coil Connector Mounting Screws

NOTE:The steps for installation of the new cable are simply the reverse order of the steps described above for removal.

5.3  Mechanical Hardware Check

Lift the coil latches on both sides of the coil, and slide the coil to its superior and inferior extents. (The coil must slide freely on the base with no binding or grinding.) Make sure that the coil does not move with respect to the baseplate when the latches are locked down.
