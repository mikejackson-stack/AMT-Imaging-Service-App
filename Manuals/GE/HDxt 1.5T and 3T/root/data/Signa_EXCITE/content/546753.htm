Rear Pedestal Air Hose

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

These procedures describe how to remove and replace the 2” hose that is below the rear pedestal and attaches to the rear end bell and the blower box.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

ItemQtyEffectivityPart#Manufacturer
Flathead screwdriver1---

4  Procedure

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room).

  1. Unclamp the hose from the rear end bell.

  1. Unthread the hose from below the rear pedestal.

  1. Unclamp the opposite end of the hose from the Blower Box.

  1. To install a new air hose, reverse Step 1 through Step 4.
