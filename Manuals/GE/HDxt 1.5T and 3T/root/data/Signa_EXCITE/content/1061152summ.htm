Replacing Operator Display and Control Panels

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Possible Personal Injury!
This procedure requires removal of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.

1  Procedure

1.1   Replacing In-Room Display

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove Laser Light Cover. It is held in place by two (2) screws and a popper.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove top panel on back of the IRD by popping tabs with a small flathead screwdriver.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Strong magnetic fields present
eddy currents can cause resistance when pulling IRD away from magnet.
use caution when removing ird to prevent it from striking the magnet enclosure.

  1. Disconnect three (3) cables attached to the IRD (ground cable, fiber optic cable, and power cable).

  1. Remove four (4) allen head screws connecting the bracket plate to the back of the IRD. Lift monitor up and off the bracket carefully as Eddy currents may pull against it.

  1. Mount the new IRD and reconnect appropriate cables and replace covers.

1.2   Replacing Trackball Control Panels and E-Stop Button

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove the Front Panels (see Front Cover Removal and Installation).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the two (2) cables that connect control panels to magnet enclosure (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Possible Personal Injury!
This procedure requires removal of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.

  1. Remove the Trackball Control Panel by removing five (5) screws and detaching Emergency Stop Button cable (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
On the back of the control panel, the E-Stop cable connection and the Trackball cable connection are of the same size and gender, and thus can be accidentally interchanged. Confirm that the cables are connected properly, otherwise the E-Stop circuit will not be resettable. See Illustration 6 and Illustration 7.
 
 

  1. To install the new Trackball Control Panel, follow Step 2 through Step 4 in reverse order.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO to PGR/PDU and power on the PDU again.

  1. Whenever the In-Room Display or Trackball Control Panels are disconnected and then reconnected, a TPS reset must be completed. (This should not be necessary if the host computer is rebooted.)

  1. Check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary.

    (Step Details)
