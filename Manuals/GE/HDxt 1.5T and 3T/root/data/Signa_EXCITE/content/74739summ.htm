SRI Fiber Optic Loopback Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Locating the SRI and SRI2 Module

This unit is located in different locations depending upon which magnet enclosure the system has. Instructions are provided for both the WideOpen and the Horizon magnet enclosures.

  1. WideOpen Magnet Enclosure

    1. The SRI module can be located under either the right or left side of the magnet enclosure. Identify which side the SRI module is on by locating the patient leads; the unit will be on that side.

    1. Remove the lower enclosure trim covers to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

  1. Horizon Magnet Enclosure

    1. Remove right front side cover of magnet enclosure to access SRI module (see Illustration 3).

      (Step Details)

    1. Loosen SRI module cover screws and slide cover to the left. Do not remove the covers on SRI-2 or SRI-3.

1.2   Checking Fiber Optic Repeater and Cable to SRI

If the site does not have a fiber optic repeater board on the penetration panel, then skip this section and proceed to SRI Local Loopback Test Procedure.

  1. Locate the fiber optic repeater board on the equipment room side of the penetration panel. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place jumper JP1 on the fiber optic repeater board in the 'B' position to place it into loopback test mode.

  1. Place the SRI, SRI2 or SRI-3 into test mode.

    1. OLD SRI:

      1. Locate SRI board dip switch S74.

      1. Set SRI board dip switches DS2, DS3, and DS4 to On (Closed) position. Leave DS1 in its default (Off or Open) position. See Illustration 6.

      1. Press on-board RESET button, S5 for SRI and S1 for SRI2, to start test. The Loopback Test begins after the regular set of power-up diagnostics has completed running.

        (Step Details)

      1. If the SRI passes this test, then it is working properly and the fiber optic communication path between it and the fiber optic repeater board is in good working order. Please proceed to Finalization, Step 1- Finalization.

      1. If the SRI fails this test, then proceed to SRI Local Loopback Test Procedure.

    1. SRI-2 or SRI-3:

      1. Loosen the wing nuts on the access plate. See Illustration 7.

      1. Rotate the access plate clockwise to expose the DIP switches.

      1. Set the S1 DIP switches DS2, DS3, and DS4 to On (Closed) position. Leave DS1 in its default (Off or Open) position. See Illustration 7.

      1. Press RESET switch S1 to start the test.

      1. Press on-board RESET button, S1, to start test. The Loopback Test begins after the regular set of power-up diagnostics has completed running.

        (Step Details)

      1. If the SRI-2/SRI-3 passes this test, then it is working properly and the fiber optic communication path between it and the fiber optic repeater board is in good working order. Please proceed to Finalization, Step 1- Finalization.

      1. If the SRI-2/SRI-3 fails this test, then proceed to SRI Local Loopback Test Procedure.

1.3   SRI Local Loopback Test Procedure

NOTE:Equipment damage possibility. Pulling on fiber optic cable will stress the end connectors. To properly disconnect fiber optic cables, apply finger pressure on connector from inside of enclosure.

  1. Disconnect fiber optic cables from J11 and J12 (leave J13, RESET, connected). See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Loopback Test Cable from its storage location at bottom of SRI module, and connect the cable from J11 to J12, as shown in Illustration 5. This cable is not included with SRI2 or SRI-3 and must be ordered separately (46-287282G1) but it interconnects between the same connections.

  1. Locate SRI board dip switch S74. Locate SRI2 board DIP switch S2.

  1. Set the S74 or S1 DIP switches DS2, DS3, and DS4 to On (Closed) position. Leave DS1 in its default (Off or Open) position. See Illustration 6 for the SRI and Illustration 7 for SRI2/SRI3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press on-board RESET button, S5 for SRI and S1 for SRI-2/SRI-3, to start test. The Loopback Test begins after the regular set of power-up diagnostics has completed running.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. To stop test, set DS2, DS3, and DS4 to off position, and then press the S5 (SRI) or S1 (SRI2) RESET button. DS30 (yellow LED on SRI) or DS2 (green LED on SRI2) should turn on.

NOTE:Symptom if DIP switches not set to default - If DIP switches are left in a position other than default (all switches OPEN or OFF), SRI or SRI2 will not be able to communicate with system, i.e., normal SRI functioning will not be possible.

  1. Disconnect Loopback Test Cable and fasten to Velcro at bottom of SRI module. If SRI2/SRI3, store cable in convenient location for future use.

  1. Connect fiber optic cables to J11 and J12.

  1. Slide SRI module cover to the closed position, and tighten cover screws. If SRI2 or SRI3, position cover over switch access and secure with wing nuts.

  1. Replace right front side cover of magnet enclosure for SRI.

  1. Put PAC and SRI/SRI2 assembly back under magnet for a wide-open magnet.

  1. Place jumper JP1 on the Fiber Optic Repeater board in the “A” position to put the SRI back in Normal Mode.
