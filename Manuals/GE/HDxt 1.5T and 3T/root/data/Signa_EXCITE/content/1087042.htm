1.5T 12-Channel Body Array Coil Setup for MCQA Test

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 12-Channel Single or Dual Connector Body Array Coil by GE/USAI.

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  MCQA Setup for Legacy Phantom Kit

CAUTION
PERSONAL INJURY - LIFTING HAZARD
THE PHANTOM WEIGHT FOR P/N 2377425-3 IS GREATER THAN 35 POUNDS.
The Phantom requires two people working together to safely lift it into place.

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle and connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Coil Connection

    NOTE:Photograph is of the HDx single connector coil. The dual connector will be positioned in the same fashion, but the cable from the anterior will go to receptacle A and the cable from the posterior will go to receptacle B.

  1. Place a phantom in the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Phantom Placement and Positioning

  1. Landmark on the coil crosshairs as shown in Illustration 3. Do not landmark on the cross.

    Illustration 3:  Landmarking Coil

  1. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool procedure.

4.2  TL Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section as shown in Illustration 4 such that the phantoms are well centered on both S/I direction as shown in Illustration 5 and R/L direction as shown with arrows in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 4:  TL Unified Phantoms Over Posterior Section

    Illustration 5:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Posterior S/I Direction

    Illustration 6:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Posterior R/L Direction

  1. Place the anterior section on the phantoms such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in the Illustration 7 and Illustration 8.

    Illustration 7:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Anterior S/I Direction

    Illustration 8:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Anterior R/L Direction

  1. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantoms.

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil at the center cross mark as shown in the following illustration and advance to scan.

    Illustration 9:  Landmarking Coil

  1. After coil and phantom are properly positioned, perform Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
