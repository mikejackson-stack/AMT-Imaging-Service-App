Cradle Release From Carriage Adjustment

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1

2  Overview

The head coil carriage mechanism allows the cradle to be released from the head coil carriage by twisting the handle of the patient cradle. The threaded actuator on the carriage must be adjusted so that the carriage latch hook engages cradle sufficiently, yet allows hook to be raised to release cradle.

3  Procedure

  1. Remove head coil carriage cover.

  1. Dock patient transport.

  1. Latch carriage to cradle.

  1. Check that the carriage meets the following criteria:

    1. Carriage latch hook must be low enough to positively engage cradle.

    1. Carriage latch hook must be high enough to be disengaged from cradle when cradle handle is twisted.

  1. If needed, adjust threaded actuator rod at end of carriage (see Illustration 1)
