  1. For SRFD2 cabinets, refer to Illustration 2.

1.2   Procedure

This section includes both Low- and High-Frequency Bandwidth tests.

NOTE:Make sure the RF Amplifier is set up per RF Amplifier Setup, “RF Amplifier Setup.”

  1. Connect the cable from Universal 1.5T or 1.0T SST RF coil to the proper adapter.

NOTE:For 1.5T, use Extremity Coil/Linear Head Coil Adapter or 1.5T Service Tool Interface; for 1.0T, use the Service Tool Interface Adapter.

  1. Insert the proper sample vial into the coil.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
SAMPLE MOTION CAN AFFECT HSS OUTPUT. TO ENSURE THE VIAL FITS SNUGLY INTO THE COIL HOUSING, A PIECE OF TAPE CAN BE WRAPPED COMPLETELY AROUND THE VIAL BEFORE PLACING INTO THE HOLDER.
 
 

  1. Disable the TNS.

  1. Position the base plate at the head end of cradle. Place and lock the coil into the base plate in the corner; then connect the adapter to the head carriage (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark on the center of the base plate. The Coil / Sample should be off the iso-center in all 3 planes.

  1. At the MR Service Desktop, start HSS Tool as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. If this system has an SRFD2 Cabinet or RFS Cabinet, as show in Illustration 2, then Click on the [Select Port] Pull down and select [Head Port] as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. If this system has an SRF cabinet, as shown in Illustration 1, leave the [Select Port] selection defaulted to [Test Port] as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Scan] From the HSS GUI

  1. The prescan and scan will be done automatically. No user scan setup is required. The scan will take a few minutes to complete.

  1. After the scan is finished the HSS Viewer will automatically start. Please see the Viewing Results for instructions concerning how to display the results.

2  Finalization

  1. Restore the connections at the back of the RF cabinet (refer to RF Amplifier Setup initial setup.

  1. Remove all SST Coil hardware (coil, test cables, etc.) from the bore.

  1. Go to the Utilities menu – select Raw File manager – and click as shown in Illustration 7 to start the tool. A screen similar to the one as shown in the Illustration 8 will pop up.

    (Step Details)

  1. Under the DISK heading, highlight the HSS files, and click on [Delete].

  1. Perform at least one head scan to verify proper system operation. For TwinSpeed, repeat for each gradient mode.
