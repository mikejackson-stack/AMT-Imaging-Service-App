2  Procedure

2.1   Disable Vacuum Pump - TwinSpeed ONLY

  1. Disable the TwinSpeed vacuum pump if SPT FSE or FGRE stability scans are going to be run. Failing to disable the vacuum pump may result in SPT stability failures.

  1. Remove the front cover from the TwinSpeed Accessory Cabinet (TAC) , and place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near the power cord connection) in the <OFF> position. The power cord may also be removed from the pump. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

2.2   Phantom Positioning for SPT Full Test Mode

If all tests are to be run, these elements are required:

  • (For Sites with a Nesting Plate) Nesting Plate

  • Body Sphere with short loader

  • Split Head Coil DQA phantom 2321556 for use only with the new split head coil

  • Head Coil

  • Head Sphere and Loader

  • Head Loader Positioner

2.3   Head Coil

  1. For upgrade systems with the old Quad Head Coils [Non-Split Top Head Coil], insert the DQA Phantom into the Head Coil. Turn on the alignment light, and move the cradle until the alignment light is centered using the marks on phantom as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the DQA phantom and replace it with the TLT loader shell and sphere as shown in Illustration 3. Align laser cross-hairs with the loader shell cross-marks.

    (Step Details)

  1. For forward production systems with a split-top head coil as shown in Illustration 4, place the TLT sphere, loader shell and positioner into the split top head coil. Align the laser cross-hairs with the Loader shell cross-marks.

    (Step Details)

  1. Establish the landmark, but do not advance to scan at this point.

  1. (For Sites with a Nesting Plate Only) Place the nesting plate on the patient table so that it butts flush against the positioner of the head coil.

    (Step Details)

2.5   Invoking SPT Full Test Mode

NOTICE
Test functionality and system functionality may be adversely affected if the previous exam is not terminated. Click on [End Exam] to terminate any previous exam. It is not necessary to click on [New Pt] as SPT automatically enters the scan protocol when it is invoked via CSD.
 
 

  1. To start the SPT Full Test tool from the Service Browser, follow the steps in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the link: Click here to start this tool is clicked, the SPT Head Coil Selection screen displays. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the head coil currently in use, then click on [Start]. (See Illustration 10.) An SPT Test menu displays in a window on the desktop.

    NOTE:With release 15.x, Shim is not an option and will not appear in the SPT GUI.

    1. In the Select All tests field, click [No].

    1. To select a reason for the test, click the button to the right of Test Purpose. Select a reason from the list. If desired, enter text in the Comments field.

    1. For systems with a CRM Body Coil, select Shimming [Off] in the Body section.

    1. Click the [On] button to select the desired test to run.

    1. For TwinSpeed, select the GradMode(s) to be used while running the tests.

    1. To select multiple passes of the test(s), select the [Multi Passes] button, then enter the desired number of passes in the box that appears to the right.

    1. Click [Start SPT] to begin.

      (Step Details)

    1. If desired, click the [WhatFailed] button. An example of WhatFailed output can be found in Troubleshooting.

    (Step Details)

  1. To exit SPT, click the [Quit SPT] button.

  1. TwinSpeed ONLY: When finished with SPT, place the power switch on the vacuum pump back into the ON position. If power cord was disconnected, reconnect it at this time. Replace the cover onto the front of the TAC.

  1. To view SPT results, select the Utilities tab on the Service Browser, then select [Report Manager].

  1. Enter the Report Manager password (the same password used for the Service Methods CD-ROM) and press [Enter].

  1. Return to the Main SPT Menu.

3  Finalization

  1. Perform a TPS Reset after completing SPT to put the system back into a known state.

  1. If for any reason a SPT scan is aborted, Shutdown and Reboot the system.
