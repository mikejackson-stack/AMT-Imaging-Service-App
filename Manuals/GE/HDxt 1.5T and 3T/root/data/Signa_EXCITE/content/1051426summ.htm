3.1   Preparations for Field Monitoring

When a measurement of overall magnetic field strength is required, only a Single Probe accurately placed at the isocenter of the magnet is necessary. If performing Field Mapping soon after field monitoring (and Universal Field Mapping Fixture 5266042 is available), set up for field mapping in conformance with Preparations for Field Mapping, Preparation for Field Mapping, before ramping and use the single probe mounting board of the mapping fixture during ramping.

3.3   Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization

3.3.1  Teslameter Set-Up

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Teslameter can cause electrical shock.
Ensure the AC line is disconnected to the Teslameter when the Teslameter is needed.

  1. Ensure the input power to the Teslameter is disconnected. Use a DVM (or equivalent measuring device) to check that no voltage is present.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Only use nonferromagnetic tools and equipment when working in the magnet's 3.0T magnetic field.

  1. Connect the white wire from the Preamp Box to the left input jack of the Teslameter Probe and the black wire from the Preamp Box to the right, facing the connector end of the Probe.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Preamp Box to the two-probe connection input plug on the Teslameter.

  1. Use R = 0 and Z = 0 measurements to check that the Teslameter Probe is at the physical center of the magnet bore.

  1. Turn on the AC power to the Teslameter as shown below.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
If the Manual/Auto Switch is not in its Manual position when magnetic field search begins during ramping, its sweep is in the ±1 Tesla range, and the system could lock on to the mechanical oscillation harmonic of the Shield Cooler Coldhead resulting in an erroneous reading.
 
 

  1. Place the Manual/Auto Switch in the Manual position.

  1. Set the Coarse and Fine Field Search controls fully counterclockwise (CCW) .

  1. Set the Freq./Field Switch to the Field position.

    (Step Details)

3.3.2  Teslameter Adjustment for Ramp Up

  1. Once ramping is started, monitor the Current Meter of the Power Supply until an indication of ≈120 amps is approached, then start monitoring the Teslameter.

  1. As the developing magnetic field approaches the lower limit of the Teslameter probe (No. 6 Probe: ≈1.0 Tesla), the Strobe Lock LED starts blinking. When this LED goes out, reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to the Lock position. (See Illustration 14.)

    (Step Details)

  1. As magnetic field continues to increase, the HI LED, located above the Coarse Field Search control, illuminates. Turn the Coarse Field Search control clockwise (CW) until the LED goes out.

  1. As the magnetic field increases, the resonant frequency of the probe sample increases above the range setting of the Teslameter. Periodically increase the setting of the Coarse Field Search control to keep the Teslameter locked on the probe sample.

    (Step Details)

3.3.3  Teslameter Adjustment for Rampdown

  1. Increase the Coarse Field Search control setting until a magnetic field reading of ≈3.0 Tesla is obtained. This should occur near, but slightly below, the actual magnetic field.

  1. Slowly start increasing the Fine Field Search control. When the Strobe Lock LED stops blinking and remains out, reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to the Locked position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the Teslameter is locked onto the magnetic field, either the LO or HI LED illuminates. Turn the Coarse Field Search control CCW if the LO LED is lit or CW if the HI LED is lit. (Slowly the illuminated LED goes out and stays out.)

  1. As the magnetic field decreases, the resonant frequency of the probe sample decreases below the range setting of the Teslameter. Periodically decrease the Coarse Field Search control to keep the Teslameter locked on the probe sample.

    (Step Details)

3.3.4  Teslameter Resynchronization

If the Teslameter should go out of sync while ramping the magnet up (or down), it can easily be resynchronized as follows:

  1. Manual Resynchronization

    1. Reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to Manual.

      (Step Details)

    1. Read the Current Meter of the Main Power Supply, multiply the present current of the meter reading by 20 gauss (since there is ≈20 gauss/amp), and reset the Current Meter to this calculated value.

    1. Slowly start increasing (if ramping up) the Coarse and/or Fine Field Search control while monitoring the Strobe Lock LED.

    1. Once the Strobe Lock LED goes off, quickly place the Manual/Auto Switch to the Locked position. (The meter is now synchronized.)

      (Step Details)

  1. Manual Resynchronization Using an Oscilloscope

    NOTE:Set up an oscilloscope near the Teslameter to display and trigger on the Field Modulation signal from a jack on the front panel of the Teslameter. Adjust the time base to display one or two ramp waveforms. On the second channel, display the NMR Signal from the front panel of the Teslameter.

    1. Leave the Manual/Auto Switch of the Teslameter in the Locked position.

    1. Slowly turn the Coarse Field Search control in the direction the magnetic field is going. For example, if the magnetic field is increasing, turn the control towards the higher numbers; if the magnetic field is decreasing, turn the control towards the lower numbers).

      (Step Details)

    1. When locked on, the Strobe Lock LED does not illuminate. Slightly readjust the Coarse Field Search control CCW if the LO LED illuminates, or CW if the HI LED illuminates until the illuminated LED ceases to illuminate.

    1. Maintain tracking through end of ramp sequence.

3.4   After Field Mapping

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Only use non magnetic tools and equipment when working in the 3.0T field of the magnet.

  1. Turn off the power to the field camera at the Field Camera Power Supply.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized field camera can cause electrical shock.
Ensure the AC line is disconnected to the field camera when the field camera is needed.

  1. Ensure the input power to the field camera is disconnected. Use a DVM (or equivalent measuring device) to check that no voltage is present.

  1. Disconnect the cables shown in Illustration 12.

  1. Dismount the shaft of the mapping fixture from the support brackets. (See Illustration 5.) Remove the shaft from the bore of the magnet.

  1. Dismount the Probe Array (Illustration 6) or the No. 6 Probe and Single Probe Mounting Board (Illustration 7 and Illustration 8) from the shaft of the mapping fixture.

  1. Dismount the patient end support bracket of the mapping fixture. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Dismount the service end support bracket of the mapping fixture. (See Illustration 3 and Illustration 2.)

  1. Reinstall the two trim cover cap screws previously removed from the service end bell.
