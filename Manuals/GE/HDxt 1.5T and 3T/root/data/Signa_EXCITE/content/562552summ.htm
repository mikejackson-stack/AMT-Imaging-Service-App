TwinSpeed TRM Coil Emulation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

SAVE INFO must be performed prior to any changes. This will be used to restore the system back to a TwinSpeed.

-

-

Follow the standard Install instructions to run a SAVE INFO. Confirm that the SAVE INFO data is on the media. Failure of the SAVE INFO process will result in a complete system recalibration.

-

-

Ensure that all the gradient cables on the back panel of the GSW (within the TAC) are labeled, so that the correct cables are moved and returned afterwards.

-

-

Do not proceed unless all three conditions above have been completed successfully.

2  Procedure

2.1   Software Changes Required for Emulation of the Zoom Coil

This will enable the scanner to be used with the Whole-Body gradient coil only.

  1. From the Service Desktop, select [C Shell].

  1. At the scd prompt, type: <cd /w/config> and press <Enter>.

  1. Edit the stage file:

    1. For 8.x, 9.x systems ONLY. To use gedit (the easy-to-use text editor), type: <gedit ipg_stage> and press <Enter>. Or, use the command-based editor vi by typing <vi ipg_stage> and press <Enter>.

    1. For 10.x or higher systems ONLY. To use xedit (the easy-to-use text editor), type <xedit mgd_stage> and press <Enter>. Or, use the command-based editor vi by typing <vi mgd_stage> and press <Enter>.

  1. The stage files contain the following lines relevant to TRM ZM emulation:

    1. 8.x, 9.x systems ONLY. F = emulate ZOOM gradient of TRM. The system will be restricted to direct connection to WHOLE-BODY gradient without the gradient switch.

      (Step Details)

    1. 10.x or higher systems ONLY. z = emulate ZOOM gradient of TRM. Note that the “z” is lowercase. The system will be restricted to direct connection to WHOLE-BODY gradient without the gradient switch.

      (Step Details)

  1. Add the appropriate letter to the first line of the file so that it looks like this:

    (Step Details)

  1. Save this change and exit the file.

  1. Shut down the system completely.

2.2   Hardware Changes Required for Emulation of the Zoom Coil

  1. Ensure the system is shut down, including the PC.

  1. Turn the PDU to its OFF (0) state by rotating the handle on its front panel from 1 to 0.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all safety covers from the GSW back panel.

  1. Disconnect the 115 VAC power cable on the GSW back panel, J8.

  1. Disconnect the GP RS232 port connector, J5.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable XWB- (X Whole-Body minus) on the left hand side and attach it to the X- (X minus) cable directly (by removing first the bolts) on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts on this X- side to ensure good connection. The GSW will now have two cables attached directly to the X- terminal.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable YWB- (Y Whole-Body minus) on the left hand side and attach it to the Y- (Y minus) cable directly (by removing first the bolts) on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts on this Y- side to ensure good connection. The GSW will now have two cables attached directly to the Y- terminal.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable ZWB- (Z Whole-Body minus) on the left hand side and attach it to the Z- (Z minus) cable directly (by removing first the bolts) on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts on this Z- side to ensure good connection. The GSW will now have two cables attached directly to the Z- terminal.

  1. Reattach any safety covers and close the TAC rack.

  1. Power-up the scanner as normal, including booting of the host and PC.

  1. Log in as usual via the Signa icon.

  1. Run a simple Head coil and DQA phantom test to ensure the single gradient coil of the TRM is operational, and that it has the correct polarity, orientation, and size. Refer to document DQA Tool for details.

2.3   Software Changes Required to Return the Scanner to a Full TwinSpeed

Follow these instructions to return the scanner to a full TwinSpeed.

  1. From the Service Desktop, select <C Shell>.

  1. At the sdc prompt, type: <cd /w/config> and press <Enter>.

  1. Remove the emulation flag from the stage file:

    1. 8.x, 9.x systems ONLY. To use xedit, the easy-to-use text editor, type: <xedit ipg_stage> and press <Enter> or to use the command-based editor, type <vi ipg_stage> and press <Enter>

    1. 10.x or higher systems ONLY. To use gedit, the easy-to-use text editor, type: <gedit mgd_stage> and press <Enter> or to use the command-based editor, type <vi mgd_stage> and press <Enter>

  1. Remove the letter added earlier from the first line of the file so that it looks again like:

    (Step Details)

  1. Save this change and exit the file.

  1. Restore INFO must be performed. This will be used to restore the system back to the full TwinSpeed configuration. Follow the standard Install instructions to run a Restore INFO.

  1. Shut down the system completely.

2.4   Hardware Changes Required to Return the Scanner to a Full Twinspeed

  1. Ensure the system is shut down, including the PC.

  1. Turn the PDU to its OFF (0) state by rotating the handle on its front panel from 1 to 0.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove any safety covers from the GSW back panel.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable ZWB- (Z Whole-Body minus) attached currently to the Z- (Z minus) on the left hand side and attach it back to its nominal position for ZWB- on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts to both ZWB- and Z-. This will leave the Z- cable from the HFD attached to the Z- terminal.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable YWB- (Y Whole-Body minus) attached currently to the Y- (Y minus) on the left hand side and attach it back to its nominal position for YWB- on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts to both YWB- and Y-. This will leave the Y- cable from the HFD attached to the Y- terminal.

  1. Unscrew the gradient cable XWB- (X Whole-Body minus) attached currently to the X- (X minus) on the left hand side and attach it back to its nominal position for XWB- on the right hand side. Tighten the bolts to both XWB- and X-. This will leave the X- cable from the HFD attached to the X- terminal.

  1. Connect the 115 VAC power cable on the GSW back panel, J8.

  1. Connect the GP RS232 port connector, J5.

  1. Reattach any safety covers and close the TAC rack.

  1. Power-up the scanner as normal, including booting the host and PC.

  1. Log in as usual via the Signa icon.

  1. Run a simple Head coil and DQA phantom test to ensure proper operation and correct polarity and orientation for both gradient coils of the TRM and the GSW. Refer to document DQA Tool for details.

2.5   Emulation of the Whole-Body Coil

This will enable the scanner to be used with the Zoom gradient coil only. The same procedure for emulation and recovery can be used as described in Step  and Step  with the following changes:

  1. The stage files contain the following lines relevant to TRM WHOLE-BODY emulation:

    1. 8.x, 9.x systems ONLY. E = emulate WHOLE-BODY gradient of TRM. The system will be restricted to direct connection to ZOOM gradient without the gradient switch.

      (Step Details)

    1. 10.x or higher systems ONLY: Note that the “w” is lowercase). w = emulate WHOLE-BODY gradient of TRM. The system will be restricted to direct connection to ZOOM gradient without the gradient switch.

      (Step Details)

  1. For the hardware changes, use XZM-, YZM-, and ZZM- cables (instead of XWB-, YWB-, and ZWB-).
