ICW - Magnet Passive Shim

1  Procedure Overview

Magnet Passive Shimming is for 3.0T magnets only. After the 3.0T magnet is ramped, Passive Shimming must be completed. This procedure is found in Chapter 8 of Direction 2325852, LCC300 Active Shield Magnet and Cryogens Subsystem magnet manual. For details on tools, conditions, and safety requirements see the beginning of the chapter in that manual.

2  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  2. Launch the Service browser from the Common Service Desktop.

  3. Click the Calibration tab, and select [Install and Calibration Wizard] => Click here to start this tool.

  4. Magnet Passive Shim is listed as one of the first calibrations. Complete Passive Shimming for the magnet according to Chapter 8 in Direction 2325852, LCC300 Active Shield Magnet and Cryogens Subsystem magnet manual.

  5. Check the box next to indicate the task is completed.

    NOTE:For 3.0T systems with the Brainwave option that includes the Integrated Patient Comfort Module (IPCM), consider installing the IPCM thermal bore liner and modified bridge after completing Passive Shimming if the IPCM hardware is available.

  6. Click [Submit].

3  Finalization

Return to the Installation & Calibration Wizard for information on advancing to the next calibration and links to other calibration procedures that must be completed prior to turning the system over to the customer.
