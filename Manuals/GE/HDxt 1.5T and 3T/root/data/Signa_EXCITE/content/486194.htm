2  Overview

This document describes Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) of the Phoenix Power Distribution Unit (PDU) for ACGD and GRFD - OpenSpeed and power restoration.

4  Procedure

4.1  LOTO of Phoenix PDU for ACGD and GRFD - OpenSpeed

  1. Power down the computer subsystems.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the ACGD Cabinet shown below.

    Illustration 1:  PDU Breaker Location on ACGD Cabinet

  1. Power off the following two circuit breakers:

    • Operator Workspace PC

    • Operator Workspace Host

  1. Rotate the larger main circuit breaker labeled INPUT (shown below) counterclockwise to the green, Off (O) position.

    Illustration 2:  PDU Main Input Breaker

  1. Press on the left-pointing arrow on the end of the circuit breaker handle to expose the location where the lock and tag can be placed.

  1. LOTO the PDU INPUT circuit breaker as shown.

    Illustration 3:  LOTO of PDU Main Input Circuit Breaker

4.2  Restoring Power to Phoenix PDU

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the ACGD Cabinet.

  1. Remove LOTO from the Phoenix PDU INPUT circuit breaker.

  1. Rotate the larger main circuit breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the red, 1 On position. (See Illustration 2

  1. Flip the circuit breaker for the Operator Workspace PC to On.

  1. Flip the circuit breaker for the Operator Workspace Host to On.

  1. Power on any other appropriate circuit breakers that were turned off.

  1. Boot the system and log into the scanning level.

5  Finalization

  1. Ensure the system is completely powered up and ready to scan.

  1. Do a test scan using a phantom to ensure proper scanning.
