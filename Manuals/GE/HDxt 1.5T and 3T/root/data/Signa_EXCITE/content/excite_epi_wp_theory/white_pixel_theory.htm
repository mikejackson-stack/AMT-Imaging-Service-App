1.5T Excite White Pixel - Theory

One of the most difficult issues we are called upon to troubleshoot on modern MR equipment known as "White Pixels", synonymously "Spike Noise", Transients" and "Broadband Noise."  Chasing down these problems can be really tough sometimes, even for highly experienced service engineers.  Often there will be multiple sources of the noise which can make logical troubleshooting techniques imperative to success.  In this series of articles I will present some of the theory of what causes them, and some techniques that have proven successful in tracking down this scourge
