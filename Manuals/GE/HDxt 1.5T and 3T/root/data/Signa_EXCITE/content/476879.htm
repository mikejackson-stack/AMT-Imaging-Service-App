1  Diagnostic Link

2  Purpose

The ACGD Digital Tuning Test provides a means to diagnosis hardware problems in the digital tuning circuits on the GP board.

3  Components Tested

None

4  Requirements

In TwinRoom systems, the user can run this test with the GP connected to the Whole body coils, the Zoom coils, or on both coils sequentially.

5  Block Diagram

None

6  Test Sequence

The Digital Tuning Test consists of loading several combinations of time constants and gains, playing out an input step function, and measuring the response of the gradient subsystem.

7  Expected Results

The test consists of loading several combinations of time constants and gains, playing out an input step function, and measuring the response of the gradient subsystem. Each Digital Tuning Diagnostic test case analyzes how the collected data compares to the ideal exponential response of the circuit. A +/- 2% window shall be applied to the ideal exponential response. If the collected data falls outside of this window, the Digital Tuning Diagnostic fails.
