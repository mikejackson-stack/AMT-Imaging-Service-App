Patient Table Hydraulic Cylinder Replacement

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

This procedure is applicable to the following table products:

  • Signa Lightweight Patient Transport Table

  • Signa Oncology Patient Transport Table Option

  • Signa OR-Compatible Patient Transport Table Option

The cradle latch cable replacement procedure requires:

  • One person for a Signa Lightweight Patient Transport Table

  • Two people for a Signa Oncology or a Signa OR-Compatible Patient Transport Table Option

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
LIFTING MR PARTS, SUCH AS FRUS, TOOLKITS AND PARTS OF THE SYSTEM WITHOUT ASSISTANCE (MECHANICAL OR ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL), CAN RESULT IN PERSONAL INJURY.
THE USE OF EXTRA PERSONNEL IS RECOMMENDED TO LIFT AN OBJECT WEIGHING MORE THAN 35 POUNDS AND LESS THAN 50 POUNDS. THE USE OF MECHANICAL LIFT ASSISTANCE DEVICES IS REQUIRED FOR OBJECTS WEIGHING MORE THAN 90 POUNDS.

4  Procedure

4.1  Remove Hydraulic Cylinder

  1. Undock patient transport, and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove both outer covers by removing mounting screws (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  REMOVAL OF PATIENT TABLE COVERS

  1. Remove both inner covers by removing mounting screws.

  1. Remove cradle assembly (see Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  PATIENT TRANSPORT CRADLE REMOVAL

  1. Raise table to Up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Unhook lock bar spring, and lower bar to horizontal position, as shown in Illustration 3, then lower table until lock bar fully supports table top.

    Illustration 3:  PATIENT TABLE LOCK BAR

  1. Remove plug from patient transport (see Illustration 4).

  1. Remove retaining ring from end of adjustment screw.

  1. Remove retaining ring, washer, and Up Limit cable attachment pin. Then remove tubing and washer. All are in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  PATIENT TABLE HYDRAULIC CYLINDER REPLACEMENT

  1. Remove Up Limit cable attachment pin that extends through hydraulic cylinder.

    NOTE:The hydraulic cylinder cannot be pressed unless the Down valve is opened. See Illustration 7.

  1. Manually press hydraulic cylinder down until it is fully retracted.

  1. Disconnect two hydraulic lines from base of hydraulic cylinder, and fasten upward and aside to avoid excess spillage. Immediately fold and clamp the translucent (yellow) hose to prevent oil from draining from the reservoir (see Illustration 5).

  1. Remove retaining ring from either end of cylinder mounting pin at base of cylinder assembly (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  REMOVAL OF HYDRAULIC CYLINDER

  1. Remove cylinder mounting pin from base of hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Carefully move linkage to free actuation pin on Down valve (see Illustration 6).

  1. Remove hydraulic cylinder from base of table assembly.

4.2  Install Replacement Hydraulic Cylinder

  1. Prepare replacement cylinder for installation by pressing cylinder to its fully retracted position.

    NOTE:It may be necessary to move linkage to fit actuation pin of Down valve into groove of linkage, as shown in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  DOWN VALVE ACTUATION PIN

  1. Carefully set cylinder in base of table, taking care not to bend actuation pin on Down valve.

  1. Insert cylinder mounting pin securing base of hydraulic cylinder to frame of table (see Illustration 5).

  1. Insert retaining ring.

  1. Connect two hydraulic lines to base of hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Insert adjustment screw into top of hydraulic cylinder (see Illustration 4).

  1. Insert lock washer on adjustment screw.

  1. Position hydraulic cylinder so that top of adjustment screw is aligned with hole in table (see Illustration 4).

  1. Pump up cylinder until table goes up slightly.

  1. Insert retaining ring at top of adjustment screw to secure cylinder to table (see Illustration 4).

  1. Insert Up Limit cable attachment pin through hole in hydraulic cylinder (see Illustration 4). Do not attach the cable at this time.

  1. Pump table up to the top of its travel.

  1. Attach washer and tubing to Up Limit cable attachment pin.

  1. Attach cable to end of Up Limit pin, and secure with washer and retaining ring. Adjust length if necessary to install, and to remove any slack.

  1. Ensure that lock bar is at its vertical (Unlocked) position as shown in Illustration 3, with the lock bar spring secured.

  1. Partially open bleed screw on hydraulic cylinder until no air bubbles are visible (see Illustration 7).

    Illustration 7:  HYDRAULIC CYLINDER DOWN VALVE

  1. Open Down valve and lower table to its lowest position.

  1. Hold Down valve open and exercise Up Pump foot pedal. This bleeds the hydraulic system by shunting air to the hydraulic reservoir.

  1. Bleed the cylinder again by partially opening bleed screw, when table is fully up, until no air bubbles are visible.

4.3  Final Calibration and Checks

  1. Refer to procedure forTable Top Height Adjustment; perform those procedures, then perform the following steps.

  1. Fill table reservoir so that fluid is ¼” to ½” above full line when table is at its lowest position (see Illustration 8).

    Illustration 8:  PATIENT TABLE RESERVOIR FILL LEVEL

  1. Check Down valve linkage adjustment as shown in Illustration 9. Obtain desired tolerance between Down valve linkage and bracket by adjusting two set screws on bracket.

    Illustration 9:  DOWN VALVE LINKAGE ADJUSTMENT

  1. Replace plug in top of table (see Illustration 4).

  1. Replace inner and outer covers. Note position of Signa logo (if applicable) on outer covers and the semicircle cut out on the inner covers, and then move that end towards the front of table (see Illustration 1).

  1. Replace cradle assembly (see Illustration 2).
