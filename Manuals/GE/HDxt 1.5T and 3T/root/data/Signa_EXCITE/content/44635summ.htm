LCD AUTO ADJUSTMENT AND SCREEN MANAGER OPERATIONS

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. With the monitor ON, press the Auto Adjust Button.

    (Step Details)

  1. While the message is on the screen (within 5 seconds), press the Auto Adjust Button again to automatically center the screen.

  1. Press the Contrast up button. Set to 80%.

  1. Press the Brightness up button. Set to 80%.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the Enter Button. All adjustments are now saved. When Auto Adjust button is pushed, the LCD will return to these settings. These settings will remain in effect even if power is removed, or system software is re-booted. Unless manually changed by the operator in the Screen Manager.

  1. For further information, of the Screen Manager and button identification, refer to Appendix A. You may also review the EIZO User's Manual that came with the LCD Display.

  1. The LCD Display Monitor is now ready for calibration.
