1.5T Head Neck Spine (HNS) Coil MCQA Setup

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsProcedure
145 mins

2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test.

NOTE:The coil consists of five units: Posterior Head, Face, Chest, Horseshoe, and TL. This is done to ensure modularity in design and diagnostics. The MCQA test for the coil is conducted to three configurations: TL unit only, Posterior Head Chest Horseshoe TL, and Posterior Head Face.

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4.3  HNS Unified Phantom MCQA Setup

4.3.1  Mode 1: Posterior Head Face Section

  1. Place Posterior Head Face section on the table as shown in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Positioning the Posterior Head Face Section

  2. Place HNS positioner and then place large cylindrical unified phantom on the positioner as shown in Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Positioner and Large Cylindrical Unified Phantom

  3. Carefully align the face component part of the coil with the connector pins over this posterior head face section and latch both the coil sections into place as shown in Illustration 8. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)

    Illustration 8:  Aligning Coil with Connector Pins

  4. Connect the coil to the system LPCA.

  5. Landmark the coil on the markings present on the face component indicated through arrows as shown in Illustration 9 and press [advance to scan].

    Illustration 9:  Landmarking the Coil

  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.

4.3.2  Mode 2: Posterior Head Chest, Horseshoe and TL Section

  1. Place the Posterior Head Face section, horseshoe adapter, and TL section of the coil on table as shown in Illustration 10.

    Illustration 10:  Positioning the Coil Components

  2. Place the HNS phantom Positioner, large cylindrical unified phantom, and TL unified phantom as shown in Illustration 11. The TL unified phantom shall be positioned over the junction of the TL section and head components.

    Illustration 11:  Complete Setup

  3. Ensure that TL unified phantom is in contact with the cylindrical unified phantom as shown in Illustration 12.

    Illustration 12:  TL Unified Phantom in Contact with Cylindrical Unified Phantom

  4. Connect the chest part to the posterior head section as shown in Illustration 13.

    Illustration 13:  Connecting Chest Part to Positioner Head

  5. Landmark on the markings over the chest section of the coil as indicated in Illustration 14.

    Illustration 14:  Landmarking Coil

  6. Press [advance to scan].

  7. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.

4.3.3  Mode 3: TL Section

  1. Place the TL section of the HNS coil on the table as shown in Illustration 15.

    Illustration 15:  Positioning TL Section

  2. Place TL unified phantoms on the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 16.

    Illustration 16:  Positioning Phantom on Coil

  3. Align the TL unified phantoms properly in S/I direction and R/L direction of the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 17.

    Illustration 17:  Aligning the Unified Phantoms

  4. Connect the coil to Port A of system LPCA.

  5. Landmark on #5 present on the side of the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 18 and press [advance to scan].

    Illustration 18:  Landmarking Coil

  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.
