3.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
GRADIENT AMPLIFIERS MAY HAVE TOP COVERS REMOVED; EXPOSING THE OUTPUT WELLS, WHICH ARE AT A HIGH VOLTAGE.
TO PREVENT ELECTRIC SHOCK, DO NOT TOUCH OR PLACE ANYTHING ON EXPOSED OUTPUT WELLS.

DANGER
FATAL SHOCK HAZARD!!
LETHAL VOLTAGES EXIST AT GRADIENT CONNECTIONS. FOLLOW THE STEPS BELOW EXACTLY.
FAILURE TO DO SO COULD RESULT IN SEVERE INJURY OR DEATH.

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES.
PERFORM LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT/TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS 29 CFR 1910.147. DO THIS BY SECURING THE PDU CIRCUIT BREAKER FOR THE SCALABLE GRADIENT CABINET.

4  Procedure

4.1  ACGD or HFD CABINET FANS AND FILTERS

  1. The four ACGD or HFD air filters are located inside the cabinet front cover. The filters (2284074) should be cleaned or replaced on a periodic basis to ensure proper cabinet cooling. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Gradient Cabinet Filter Locations

  1. Verify the fan is operating properly. Using a piece of paper, hold over fan on the top of the cabinet (see Illustration 1), airflow should cause paper to stand up. The fan should create enough suction to hold the rear cabinet door closed. If fan is not operating properly remove and replace with Fan Assembly 2299215 for ACGD gradients or 5116803 for HFD gradients.

4.2  CHECK EDDY CURRENT COMPENSATION

Eddy currents compensation is checked when doing System Performance Test (SPT).

4.3  GRADIENT CABLE CONNECTIONS AND SUPPORT

NOTE:It is recommended that checking Gradient cables connections internal to the Gradient PDU Cabinet be performed one time, approximately 1 year after system installation. This applies only to checking the PDU cables. The gradient cabling at the pen panel and the rear of the magnet should be checked on a yearly basis.

  1. Check the gradient connections internal to the Gradient/PDU cabinet.

    DANGER
    FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
    TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE MDP BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES.
    PERFORM LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT/TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS 29 CFR 1910.147. DO THIS BY SECURING THE MDP CIRCUIT BREAKER FOR THE MDP. VERIFY THAT INPUT VOLTAGE TO THE PDU IS ZERO VOLTS AC.

    1. Remove the 2 rear covers of the PDU by removing the 8 Phillips screws.

    1. Verify that power is off by checking AC voltage at the PDU input. See Illustration 2.

      Illustration 2:  Check AC Power Using DVM Between 3 Lugs, And Each Lug To Ground

    1. Check all power connections inside the PDU. Verify connections are tight at all terminal strips, also check to verify that all crimps are tight by pulling on each cable. This includes input power, gradient connections, outgoing power connections (terminal Strip 1), Grounds and Neutrals. See Illustration 3

      Illustration 3:  Power Output At PDU

    1. Check Output Connections on the three gradient amps. See Illustration 4.

      Illustration 4:  Output Connections On The Three Gradient Amps

  1. Check the gradient connections at the Pen Panel. On the Control Room side of the Penetration Panel, remove the cover of the Gradient Filter Box and inspect for arcing. If there is any arcing, replace the Gradient Filter (46-320232G1). At this time perform a check for loose connections on the penetration panel. Tighten all connections and replace barriers and cover. See Illustration 5

    Illustration 5:  Penetration Panel Gradient Filter Box Connections

  1. Check bolts securing pen panel to screen room. Verify all bolts are tight. See Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Check Pen Panel Mounting Bolts

5  Finalization

  1. Remove Lockout/Tagout.

  1. If any of the gradient cables were moved, perform the Geometry Verification procedure to verify that gradient cables are returned to original position.
