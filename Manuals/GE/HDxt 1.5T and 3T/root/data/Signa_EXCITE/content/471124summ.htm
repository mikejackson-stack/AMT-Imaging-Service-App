Gradient Amplifier Power Supply Replacement

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
THE Gradient/PDU CABINET CONTAINS HIGH VOLTAGE.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE performing THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE.

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!

1  Procedure

1.1   Amplifier Power Supply Replacement Procedure

  1. Remove Power to the Gradient Amplifiers by placing the 208 V Gradient Power Breaker and the 420 V Gradient Power Breakers on the PDU in the OFF position. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lockout/Tagout the Breaker Panel on the PDU.

  1. Wait at least 5 minutes after the Gradient Amplifier power supply has been turned off for the power on the capacitors to dissipate.

  1. Verify that power has been removed from the cabinet. Take a Digital Multi-meter and set it to its highest AC voltage range and on the terminal block in the rear of the cabinet of the Gradient Amplifier Power Supply (Amplifier-PS), measure between the ground connection and the incoming power connection at the black wire. (See Illustration 2.) Repeat this measurement between the ground connection and the red wire, and between the ground connection and the white wire. A measurement of 0 VAC indicates that the 420 V incoming power was successfully turned off.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
CABINET TIP HAZARD!
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
CONFIRM THAT THE ALUMINUM ANTI-TIP LEGS STORED IN THE REAR OF THE CABINET ARE INSTALLED ON EITHER SIDE OF THE BASE OF THE CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE A Amplifier-POWER SUPPLY.

  1. Using the screws included with the anti-tip legs, secure the aluminum anti-tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the Gradient Cabinet. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four pin connectors (J1, J2, J3, J4, J5 and J6) from the rear of the Amplifier Power Supply Module. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Remove the data cable (J7) from the rear of the Amplifier Power Supply Module. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Disconnect the TS1 terminal. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Possible Personal injury.
Heavy object - The Hoist Kit weighs 73 lbs (33 kg).
Assistance to lift this product [mechanical (Dollies) or Additional personnel] is required.

  1. Locate the three (3) large steel rail sections in the Universal Hoist Kit, and place them on a flat surface for assembly. (See Illustration 5.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten them together with 4.75” X 1/2” caps crews and hex nuts.

  1. Use the crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws.

  1. Locate the brass-colored Inner Rail and insert it into the steel rails as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the brass-colored Inner Rail into the open end of the rear rail section.

  1. Locate one of two (2) locking pins in the lift kit, and insert one locking pin into the hole near the open end of the rear rail section. (This locking pin secures the brass-colored Inner Rail to the outer steel rail. See Illustration 7.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Steel Rail assembly on top of the Fan Enclosure at the top of the Gradient Cabinet, and center the Rail Assembly on the top of the enclosure. (See Illustration 8.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the brass-colored J-shaped bracket in the Lift Kit. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the rear door of the Gradient Cabinet, and observe how the J-shaped bracket fits into the rear doorframe. (See Illustration 10.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Rail Assembly toward the front of the Gradient Cabinet while maintaining its centered position.

  1. Attach the J-shaped bracket to the brass-colored Inner Rail so that the J-shaped bracket is flush against the rear panel of the Fan Enclosure; use the flat Phillips-head screws to secure the J-shaped bracket to the brass-colored Inner Rail. (See Illustration 11.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the J-shaped bracket is secured to the cabinet and the steel rail, attach the Saddle Bracket to the top of the Fan Enclosure. (The screw holes are located at the front end of the Fan Enclosure. The Saddle Bracket prevents the Steel Rail from sliding from center position. See Illustration 12.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. (See Illustration 13.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail, and insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. (This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 14.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) Phillips-head screws and washers from the Amplifier-PS front panel that secures the Amplifier-PS to the Gradient Cabinet. (See Illustration 15.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull out the Amplifier-PS Module until its slide rails latch.

  1. Remove the two Amplifier-PS brackets from the Universal Hoist Kit. (See Illustration 16.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the appropriate bracket (labeled left and right) into the hole on the lip of sheet metal on each side of the Amplifier-PS. (See Illustration 17.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the stabilizer bars in the Hoist Kit. (See Illustration 18.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using four screws from the Hoist Kit, attach the stabilizer brackets to the bottom of both hoist brackets. (See Illustration 19.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the spreader bar of the hoist assembly to the Amplifier Hoist Brackets. This transfers the weight of the Amplifier to the hoist. (See Illustration 20.)

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Hoist Assembly, tighten the chain to the spreader bar to remove slack and support the weight of the Amplifier module. (See Illustration 21.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the rail lever in the UP position on both sides of the Amplifier-PS. (SeeIllustration 22 for location of lever.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the entire weight of the Amplifier-PS is supported by the Universal Hoist.

  1. Slide the module out, and remove it from the rails attached to the cabinet.

  1. Turn the module to the side and lower the module. (See Illustration 23.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the spreader bar from the hoist brackets

  1. Remove the hoist brackets from the failed module.

1.2   Module Replacement

  1. Remove the rails from the failed module, and attach to the replacement module. (See Illustration 24.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the hoist brackets to the replacement module.

  1. Attach the spreader bar to the hoist brackets.

  1. Fully extend the slide rail in the cabinet of the axis that is being replaced.

  1. Raise the replacement amplifier using the hoist assembly, and move module in position to place rails into the slide mechanism on the cabinet.

  1. Install the Amplifier-PS in the cabinet by inserting the module into the mating half of the rail assemblies.

  1. Place the slide-rail lever in the DOWN Position. (See Illustration 25.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Push the Amplifier-PS assembly all the way into the cabinet.

  1. Remove the spreader bar from the hoist brackets.

  1. Remove the hoist brackets.

  1. Secure the assembly to the front panel by replacing the four (4) Phillips-head screws and washers from the Amplifier-PS front panel. (See Illustration 26.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the four pin connectors (J1, J2, J3, J4, J5 and J6) at the rear of the Amplifier-PS Module. (See Illustration 27.)

  1. Replace the data cable (J7) at the rear of the Amplifier-PS Module. (See Illustration 27.)

  1. Connect the TS1 terminal. (See Illustration 27.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Universal Hoist from the cabinet and return to the kit.

  1. Remove the power lock out tag out to the Gradient Cabinet and restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the cabinet.

  1. Set up for a scan to verify gradient driver comes up to Ready.
