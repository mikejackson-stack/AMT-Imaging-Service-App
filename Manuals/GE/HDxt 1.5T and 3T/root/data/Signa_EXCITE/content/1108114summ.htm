  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable is disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in On or Standby mode.
Disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the Workstation.

CAUTION
Memory module damage could result during removal or installation.
If the power cord is not unplugged while installing the memory module(s), damage could result, and the system will not recognize the memory module changes.
Power off the Workstation and unplug the power cord from the AC power outlet. Wait until the LED on the back of the Power Supply turns off before removing the memory module.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

1.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Close any PC applications, and power down the GOC. (Refer to System Startup and Shutdown.)

  1. Perform LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace before beginning the replacement.

When removing the side panel, avoid straining the ground lead.

1.2   Graphics Card Replacement

To remove the defective graphics card:

  1. Push down on the two green tabs (shown below) to release the locking bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Gently push outward on the green plastic tab (shown below), lift the defective card straight up, and remove it from the unit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the replacement graphics card into the open slot, and restore the locking bar.

  1. Reinstall the PC side panel (shown in Illustration 7), and secure it with the two screws that were removed earlier.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workstation.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
