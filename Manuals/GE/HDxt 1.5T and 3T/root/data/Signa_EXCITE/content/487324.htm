2  Overview

Removing the Rear Cosmetic Ring is needed when replacing the gradient coil.

3.2  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

4  Procedure

NOTE:The Rear Cosmetic Ring Cover and the Rear End Bell can be removed independently of each other.

  1. Remove Rear Trim Ring by pulling it away from the enclosure (see Illustration 1). There are no bolts holding it into place.

    Illustration 1:  Remove Rear Trim

  1. The cosmetic cover is held on by 5 (five) bolts and 5 (five) poppers. Remove 5 (five) bolts (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Cosmetic Cover Bolts

  1. Pull cover away from magnet to release the cover from the poppers. (Illustration 3 shows the location of the poppers)

    Illustration 3:  Popper Placements (with Cosmetic Cover and End Bell Removed

  1. To install the Cosmetic Cover reverse the removal procedures.
