2.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, Shutdown the Host computer and perform Lockout/Tagout of the GOC BEFORE STARTING THIS PROCEDURE.

3  Procedure

  1. Close the application software on the Host computer. Refer to Lockout/Tagout of the GOC power sources.

  1. Remove the right cover of the GOC. If applicable, disconnect the ground lead that connects the cover to the main chassis. For xw8000, xw8200, xw8400 and xw9300 GOCs, see the following illustration. For Z400 GOCs, see Illustration 2.

    NOTE:Avoid straining the short ground lead.

    Illustration 1:  Removing Cover (Legacy GOC Shown)

    Illustration 2:  Removing Cover (Z400 Shown)

  1. Cut the tie-wraps securing the power cable, if applicable.

  1. Disconnect the power cable from the Ethernet Switch or HUB.

  1. Remove all cables connected to the Ethernet Switch or HUB, and make a note of labeling for reconnection.

    Illustration 3:  Cable Removal from Ethernet Switch (Z400 GOC Shown)

  1. Remove the straps or Velcro® tapes from the Ethernet Switch or HUB.

    Illustration 4:  Strap Removal (Legacy GOC Shown)

    Illustration 5:  Velcro Removal (Z400 GOC Shown)

  1. Remove the Ethernet Switch or HUB.

  1. Install the replacement Ethernet Switch by reversing the above steps.

4  Finalization

  1. Restore power to the GOC and reboot the computer.

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure proper operation.
