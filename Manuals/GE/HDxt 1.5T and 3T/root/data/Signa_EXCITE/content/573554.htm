Gradient Amplifier-Power Supply LEDs

1  OVERVIEW

The power supply contains 3 transformers, one for each axis. The primary current is controlled by the power supply and the secondaries of the transformers feed the Gradient Amplifiers. The over current faults are separated into primary side and secondary side faults.

Last Updated01/09/2006

2  LEDS

  1. ISECX, ISECY, ISECZ: These indicate the secondary currents in the power supply for each axis. They will light up when the Gradient Amplifier for that axis pulls current from the power supply.

  2. IPX OC, IPY OC, IPZ OC: These indicate over currents in the transformer primaries. This is an uncommon fault that would indicate a shorted transformer primary.

  3. ISX OC, ISY OC, ISZ OC: These indicate over currents in the transformer secondaries. The main 2 causes are a blown IGBT in the Gradient Amplifier or a shorted diode module in the power supply. If it is a Gradient Amplifier IGBT, the gate drive indicators located above the heatsink on the Gradient Amplifier will be out.

  4. OVX, OVY, OVZ: These indicate over voltages on the output of the power supply. Loose connectors to the Gradient Amplifier can cause this problem.

  5. UVX, UVY, UVZ: These indicate under voltages on the output of the power supply. Shorted semiconductors in the Gradient Amplifier or Power Supply can cause these.

  6. ITANK OC: Indicated that the sum of the transformer primary currents is too large. This fault is uncommon. This indicates heavy loads on all three axes at once.

  7. BUS OV: This indicates an over voltage on the input bus of the power supply. The 420 VAC on the back of the power supply goes through a 3-phase rectifier to feed this bus. If you see this fault, check the tapping of the PDU and measure the 420 VAC on the terminal block in the back of the power supply.

  8. BUS UV: This indicates an undervoltage on the input bus of the power supply. Mis-tapping the PDU or line sags can cause this.

  9. PSWF (Power Supply Wire Fault): This indicates one of 2 faults. It could be an undervoltage on the 5V or 15V on the control board.

  10. FREQ: This is a control signal that indicates the frequency that the IGBTs are switching.

  11. GD (Gate Drive): This shows the switching of the Gate Drive power supply. If this light is ever-solid red or solid green, it means there is a problem on the control board or gate driver and the IGBTs will never switch so you should replace the power supply.

  12. HSUT: Heat sink under temperature. Either the equipment room is too cold or the temperature sensor is unplugged.

  13. HSOT: Heat sink over temperature. Check that the cabinet fan is running in the correct direction.

  14. FAULT: Lights whenever one of the faults described above is true, or the reset pushbutton is pressed.

  15. LEFT FF, RIGHT FF: IGBT switching indicators.

  16. VINV: Control signal that indicates the IGBTs are switching.

  17. ENABLE: Lights when the power supply is enabled by the GP.
