3  Procedure

3.1   Magnet Electric Check

The magnet's electrical circuits must be verified to be in operating condition prior to filling or ramping.

  1. Make sure Shim Lead is disengaged in conformance with Shim Lead Engagement and Disengagement.

  1. Perform electrical checks called out in Magnet Functional Checks.

3.2   Cryostat Temperature Check

This section describes the procedures and equipment used to establish the temperature inside the Cryostat Helium Vessel. This temperature must be established in order to determine the cool down and liquid helium filling requirements of the Cryostat in Cooling and Filling Procedure Determination, Cooling & Filling Procedure Determination, prior to the magnet commissioning. LCC300 magnets are equipped with two temperature sensors requiring a 10 microampere current source with a stability of +0.005 percent. Sensor (Diode) 1 is mounted on top of the patient end of the Magnet Assembly. Sensor (Diode) 2 is mounted on the bottom of the service end of the Magnet Assembly. The Magnet Assembly is inside of the Helium Vessel. (These sensor diodes are identical to those found on the Coldhead Sleeve.)

NOTICE
The magnet temperature sensors are designed to be driven by a 10 microampere source. Some ohmmeters exceed this rating. Do NOT use any sensing or troubleshooting equipment which exceeds 10 microamperes. A voltmeter can also be used to troubleshoot the sensor circuit.
 
 

  1. Verify that any sensing or troubleshooting equipment used does not exceed 10 microamperes.
