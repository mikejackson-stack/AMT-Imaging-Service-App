2  Overview

Peripheral Setup is the fifth step of the Installation Calibration Wizard. During Peripheral setup there are six tasks that must be completed and checked off:

  • Chiller Registration

  • Fill Chiller with Coolant

  • Leak Check Chiller and Coil

  • Body Coil Air Flow Check

  • Laser Light Alignment Procedure

  • Vacuum Gauge Check

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Required Conditions

All previous tasks in the Installation Calibration Wizard must be complete.

4  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click the Calibration tab.

    NOTE:a. If you are in Install or Upgrade mode, click[Install and Calibration Wizard] and then [Click here to start this tool].

  1. The fifth step in the ICW is Peripheral Setup (see Illustration 1). Verify that all previous steps in the ICW are complete. They should be highlighted and Blue.

    Illustration 1:  ICW Peripheral Setup

  1. Register any AirSys chiller by contacting:

    • E&W for US, Canada and Mexico 888-744-3195 or 513-768-3195

    • AirSys for All if Europe and South America 925-997-7263

    • All of Asia 86-10-6865-6161

    The following information must be provided when registering a Chiller:

    • Facility name

    • System ID

    • Facility Address and Contact Information

      • FE Name

      • FE Contact Information

    • Serial Number of the Chiller

  1. Fill the chiller with the proper coolant.

    NOTE:There are different chiller configurations. Prior to filling the chiller, verify that the proper coolant is being used by checking the applicable vendor manual.

    When this is complete, check the box Have you Filled Chiller and Lines with Coolant?

  1. Check all Chiller connections for leaks. Checks should be made at the chiller, at the gradient coil and at the cryo-cooler compressor. When this is complete, check the box Is Coolant Leak Checks done?

  1. Perform Body Coil Air Flow Functional Check. Airflow is directed from the rear of the magnet enclosure to the front. If there are leaks in the rear of the enclosure, the RF coil may overheat. This procedure consists of placing your hand around the rear enclosures to check for air leaks. If significant leaks are observed around the rear of the enclosure, determine the cause of the air leak and repair. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Body Coil Airflow Functional Check done?

  1. Perform Laser Light Alignment . When this is complete, check the box next to Is Laser Light Alignment done?

  1. Perform Vacuum Functional Check Procedure . When this is complete, check the box next to Is Vacuum gauge Calibration Program Check done?

  1. 11. Click [Submit]. This will complete the Table Adjustment section of the ICW.
