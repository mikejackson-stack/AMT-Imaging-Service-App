35 kW Body Maximum Power Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
THE RF AMPLIFIER IS NOT DISABLED.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, ENSURE THE RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHILE CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

WARNING
ELECTRICAL/RF BURN HAZARD!
RF BURNS Can Occur AS A RESULT OF DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES WHILE THE SYSTEM IS SCANNING.
PREVENT POSSIBLE RF BURNS WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM J3 OR J4 ON THE RF AMP BY VERIFYING THAT THE SYSTEM IS NOT MANUALLY PRESCANNING OR SCANNING. VERIFY THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE AND THE RF ENABLE SWITCH IS IN THE “OFF” POSITION.

NOTICE
Possible property damage. Prevent coil and associated switch damage by removing All phantoms and hardware (i.e., Head Coil, Surface Coil) from the Magnet Bore.
 
 

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage. Do not operate the RF amplifier with an improper load connected to its output. Doing so may permanently damage the amplifier.
 
 

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage. Do not connect/disconnect Wattmeter Sensor while Meter is ON. Always turn Meter OFF prior to plugging/unplugging Sensor.

  1. Insert the 50 kW Element into the Forward sensor port (blue, 4300A374-3). Make sure the arrow on the element points in the direction of the arrow (from Input to Output) on the Sensor. Lock Element in place using the locking tab on the sensor. (see Illustration 2)

  1. Select the <Enter> button.

  1. Check the setting by selecting the arrow button under Scale once again.

  1. Select the arrow button under Fwd Units, and make sure the scale reads W (or kW on some watt meters). If the scale reads dBm, select the arrow button under Fwd Units, again, to toggle the scale to read W (Watts or kW, kiloWatts) on the TOP, Forward Power scale. Ignore the BOTTOM reflected Power scale value. It is not used during Maximum Power Calibration.

  1. Select the <Shift> button.

  1. Select the arrow button under Setup.

3.6   Adjusting the RF Amplifier Output Power (Body)

  1. This section adjusts the gain of the solid state pre-amplifier (VVA SETUP) and if necessary, also adjusts the Attenuation of the PA stage (ATTN SETUP). The Gain Variable Voltage Adjustment (VVA) of the solid-state amplifier is set up first, maximizing its output. The Attenuation of the PA stage can also be adjusted to “fine tune” the final value as close as possible to 35 kW, (34,500 to 35,300 watts). VVA Setup and ATTN Setup interact with each other, and it may be necessary to jump back and forth between these adjustments until the RF Amplifier is properly calibrated.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Manual Prescan].

  1. Select [Scan TR].

  1. Set the Transmit Gain to 180. Make sure everything is functioning normally and there is a reading on the Wattmeter. Allow scanner to pulse at TG180 for 6 minutes. (This is due to the drop-in gain that occurs over the 6-minute period.)

  1. Slowly increase the TG to 200. (If the amplifier trips, set the TG at its highest setting before tripping.)

3.8   Adjusting ATTN SETUP (PA Attenuation)

This section only needs to be performed if 35 kW (approx. 34,500 to 35,300 watts) cannot be achieved with VVA SETUP. (Note: At this time, the RF Amplifier is still pulsing at TG of 200.)

  1. Press the [Menu] button until the LCD display on the front of the RF Amplifier reads the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Select <Enter>.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the down or up arrows on the keyboard while watching the Wattmeter to adjust the attenuation of the RF Amplifier PA stage. Slowly attenuate the RF power to as close to 35 kW as possible (approx. 34,500 to 35,300 watts). If 35 kW cannot be achieved, adjust the attenuation to a value that is below 35 kW.

  1. Press the <Menu> button to exit

  1. Press the <Menu> button again to return the LCD display to its power measurement display.

  1. If 35 kW was not achieved, proceed to Step  VVA SETUP (Solid state amplifier Gain adjust). If 35 kW (approx. 34,500 to 35,300 watts) was achieved, proceed to Step .

3.9   Saving New Calibration Settings

  1. As a quick check, look at the LCD display on the front of the RF Amplifier. It should be reading 35 kW + or - approximately 2000 watts. This will vary site to site and can be used as a confidence indicator for the actual setting. The LCD display should not be relied upon as an accurate measurement device.

  1. Stop [Manual Prescan].

  1. Press the <Menu> button multiple times until the original screen on the Amp LCD redisplays.

  1. Press the Off button. This will STORE the value in NVRAM. Wait 30 seconds and press the “Operate” (Or “standby) button. Wait another 30 seconds for the amplifier to react to the new settings.

  1. Reduce the TG to 0 (zero) once the RF Output is calibrated to specification. If the Amplifier is set up correctly, the RF Amplifier and Wattmeter displays should be very close to the same value.

  1. Test the new Maximum Power value by restarting [Manual Prescan].

  1. Select [Scan TR].

  1. Slowly increase the TG to 200. The system should not trip.

  1. Take a final look at the Wattmeter display making sure it is the desired value.

  1. Reduce TG to 0 (zero).

  1. Stop [Manual Prescan].

  1. Connect the original Body Output Cable to the RF Amplifier J4, and original Head Output Cable to RF Amp J3.

  1. Re-enable RF and connect J14 on the RF Amplifier.
