Line Voltage Checks

During the set up, calibration, and functional checks of this system, monitor the power line voltages similar to what was done during pre-installation site planning checks (Refer to the Signa HDx 1.5T and 3.0T Pre-Installation Manual, Power Requirements/Power Source Monitoring). In particular, look for variations in the day-to-day line voltage that do not meet equipment specifications. Look for any voltage disturbances that might correlate to system malfunctions or component failures. (Some recommended analyzers that are designed for unattended monitoring are the Dranetz Model 656A or 658, and the RPM Models 1651, 1656, or 1658) Ensure that the power meets site planning requirements. Refer to the Signa HDx 1.5T and 3.0T Pre-Installation Manual, Power Requirements.
