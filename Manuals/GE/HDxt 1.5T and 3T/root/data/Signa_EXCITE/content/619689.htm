2  Overview

The RF Test (RFT) is a RF loopback test that evaluates the stability, magnitude and phase linearity, droop, pulse fidelity, and bandwidth of the RF transmit chain in a Signa system.

  • The RF receive line is not verified in this procedure.

  • It is recommended to run the RFT tests in the following order:

    1. TPS Loopback Baseline Data - TPS Loopback Baseline Data

    2. Body Sense Loop - Body Sense Loop

    3. Head Sense Loop - Head Sense Loop

    4. Body Dummy Load (High Power) - Body Dummy Load (High Power)

    5. Head Dummy Load Baseline Control Variables - Head Dummy Load Baseline Control Variables

    6. Other RF Troubleshooting Other RF Troubleshooting

4  Procedure

4.1  Preparing Required Conditions

  1. Disable the UTNS by removing the UTNS Antenna from the rear of the UTNS 3 at J31.

  1. Disable the TR error condition in the Driver Module.

    1. Verify the system is in “Idle” and all coils have been removed from the bore.

    1. Remove the front cover of the Excite Systems Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the Driver Module, disable the TR error reporting by setting the TR Switch to the Disable position. (See Illustration 1.)

      Illustration 1:  Driver Module Switch

  1. Systems with TRM: Shut down the vacuum pump (disable Quiet Technology).

    1. Remove the front cover of the TwinSpeed Accessories Cabinet (TAC) , and place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near the power cord connection) in the OFF position, or remove the power cord from the pump. (See Illustration 2.)

      Illustration 2:  Vacuum Pump in TwinSpeed Accessories Cabinet

    1. Make sure the vacuum pump is powered OFF and not running.

      1. Remove the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 at the rear of the TAC, and slowly open the vacuum line connection at J11.

      1. Allow the vacuum to slowly equalize the vacuum space inside the magnet bore to atmospheric pressure.

    NOTE:Running the RFT test with the magnet bore vacuum space below atmospheric pressure will result in magnitude and phase stability plots that exhibit a sloping, sometimes sawtooth pattern.

4.2  TPS Loopback Baseline Data

Essentially, the TPC Loopback Baseline Data test is a “transmit/receive electronics loopback” for the transmit and receive chain components in the MGD Chassis only. It verifies the proper function of the Exciter and Receiver. The TPS test is performed in the “Body” mode. Results from this test are referenced automatically by subsequent RFT tests. Therefore, this test must be performed first. Control Variables for stability and linearity troubleshooting are listed in Table 3.

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! The 30 dB and DC Block Attenuators MUST be connected BEFORE connecting the sense loop to prevent damage to the TPS Receiver Board.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE POSSIBILITY! The attenuators may contain ferrous parts. Do not take them into the magnet room; doing so can cause damage to these components.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! All phantoms and coils should be removed from the bore.

5  Finalization

  1. Upon completion of RFT, the following equipment must be restored to full operation if it was put in the Service mode.

    1. UTNS 3

    1. TR Error Reporting (Driver Module)

    1. Body/Head Heliax (if removed)

    1. Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology) for TRM systems with a TAC (enabled)

    1. TPS reset (perform when system is back to normal)

    1. Body Receive Cable (reconnect)

  1. Enable the UTNS3 by reconnecting the UTNS Antenna to the rear of the UTNS 3 at J31 or refer to Stopping and Starting UTNS3.

  1. Re-enable TR Error reporting by enabling the TR Switch on the front of the Driver module. (See Illustration 12.)

    Illustration 12:  TR Switch Location

  1. If the Body or Head Heliax was removed, verify the scanner is in “Idle”, turn off the RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O Board) or disconnect J14 from the back of the RF Amplifier.

    1. Disconnect the RFT hardware and cables from the system.

    1. Connect the original Body Output Cable to the RF Amplifier J4, or connect the original Head Output Cable to RF Amplifier J3.

    1. Re-enable the RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O Board), or reattach the cable to J14 at the back of the RF Amplifier.

  1. If the system has a TRM coil and TAC, enable the vacuum pump (Quiet Technology).

    1. If not already done, remove the front cover from the TwinSpeed Accessories Cabinet (TAC) .

    1. Place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near where the power cord connects) in the ON position, OR plug the power cord back in. See Illustration 13.

      Illustration 13:  Vacuum Pump in TwinSpeed Accessories Cabinet

    1. Restore the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 at the back of the TAC.

  1. Reconnect body receive cable to Combiner output port.

  1. Perform a TPS reset.

    NOTE:If an RFT scan is not run and only RFT prescan is run, it is necessary to perform a TPS reset before attempting to perform clinical or service scans. Failure to do this will result in receiving signal loss and autoprescan error when clinical or service scanning is attempted.
