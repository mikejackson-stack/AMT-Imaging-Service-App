1.5T NV Array Cable Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Loosen the cable Strain relief retaining nut from the external cable. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lay the NVA HD on its side and remove the 14 screws holding the base cover on the unit as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cable shield lug from the system shield wire as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the signal harness from the multiplexer board. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the coax cables from the multiplexer board. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the strain relief retaining nut and remove it from the cable assembly. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cables and strain relief from the base of the NVA HD. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the strain relief seal ring from the body of the strain relief. See Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the coax cables from the body of the strain relief followed by the signal wires. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the strain relief seal and the strain relief-retaining nut from the cable. See Illustration 9.

  1. Slide the strain relief-retaining nut over the replacement cable followed by the strain relief seal, and then the strain relief body. See Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the cable assembly beginning with the signal wire connector followed by the coax cables through the NVA base. Slide the cable-retaining nut over the cables and secure as shown in Illustration 11.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the corresponding coax cables to the multiplexer board. See Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the signal harness connector to P2 of the Base Mux. See Illustration 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the cable shield lug to the system shield wire as shown in Illustration 14.

    (Step Details)

  1. The final assembly should look like the image shown in Illustration 15.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the cable strain relief retaining nut to the external cable. See Illustration 16.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the base cover and the 14 retaining screws. See Illustration 17.

    (Step Details)
