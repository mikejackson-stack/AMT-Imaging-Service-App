MCR Functional Test of Head Receive Line

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Click the (Service Tools) icon to open the Service Desktop Manager.

  1. Click in the lower part of the Service Desktop Manager if the Common Service Desktop is not already visible.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click the (Image Quality) tab at the top of the Common Service Desktop.

  1. Select [MultiCoil Receive Chain Tool] from the menu on the left side of the CSD.

  1. Under MultiCoil Receive Chain Tool on the right side of the CSD, select [Click here to start this tool]. The MultiCoil Receive Chain Test window opens as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Identify the receive cable on the Head Coil. This cable is usually marked with a blue tape on the Head Quick Disconnect Box, but it can be confirmed by the following:

    1. Insert the MCR H/W into the MultiCoil (lower) connector on the carriage cover and attach one of the cables from the head coil (with the DC blocker bullet) to the connector with the blue band.

      (Step Details)

    1. From the [Documentation] menu on the tool’s Graphical User Interface, select [Appendix] and then [Verify Head Receive Line]. See Illustration 3.

      (Step Details)

    1. Follow the instructions on the flowchart and click on the [Download] button at the bottom of the screen. This will automatically download the protocol to the scan desktop. See Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

    1. Go to the scan desktop. When the download is complete, select [Manual Prescan]. If the selected cable is the receive line, then you should get signal on receiver 1. If there is no signal, repeat the step with the other cable from the head coil.

    1. Once the correct cable has been identified as the receive cable, close the displayed flowchart document by clicking on the [Close] button. See Illustration 4.

  1. Select the appropriate test option.

  1. Click [Continue].
