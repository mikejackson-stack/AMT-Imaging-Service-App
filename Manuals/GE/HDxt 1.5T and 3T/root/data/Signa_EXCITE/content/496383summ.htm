ICW Dock Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click the Calibration tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. The second step in the ICW is Dock Adjustments as shown below. Verify that all previous steps in the ICW are complete; they should be highlighted and blue in color.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Dock Centering and Dock Hardware Adjustments. When completed, check the box next to Is Dock Centering done?”

  1. Perform Dock Hook Tension Adjustment. When completed, check the box next to Is Dock Hook Tension Adjusted?

  1. Click [Submit]. This completes the Dock Adjustment section of the ICW.

2  Finalization

  1. Return to the Installation and Calibration Wizard for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.
