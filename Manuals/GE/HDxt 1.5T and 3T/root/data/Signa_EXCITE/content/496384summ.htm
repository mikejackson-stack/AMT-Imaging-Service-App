ICW Magnet Ramping

1  Safety

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

All previous tasks in the Installation Calibration Wizard must be complete.

-

3  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. 2. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click the Calibration tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. The third step in the ICW is Magnet Ramping. See Illustration 1. Verify that all previous steps in the ICW are complete. They should be highlighted and Blue.

    (Step Details)

  1. For Magnet Pre-ramp, verify the following:

    1. Magnet has been configured for Operating condition.

    1. Cryo-cooler is running.

    1. Magnet Shield Temps are within specification.

    1. Magnet pressure is less than 4.15 psi.

    1. Tesla meter is centered in the magnet bore.

    1. All ferrous material has been removed from the magnet room.

    1. Magnet warning signs are properly posted around the magnet room.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform MRU check using the applicable Vendor Manual. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Magnet Run-Down Unit Functional Check done?

  1. Perform Magnet Ramping using the applicable magnet manual. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Magnet Ramping done?

  1. Perform Magnet Shimming Perquisites by verifying the following:

    1. Verify Magnet Stability, drift rate must be less than .1ppm per hour.

    1. Verify magnet vessel pressure is between 3.9 and 4.1 psi.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter Florence Shim Currents that are provided with the magnet ATR which is on a CD that is shipped with the magnet. When this is complete, check the box next to Is Initial Shim Current Installation done?

  1. If magnet is a 3T, perform the Passive Shim procedure that is located in the 3T magnet manual. When this is complete, check on the box that is next to Is Passive Shim (3.0T magnets only) done?

  1. Click [Submit]. This will complete the magnet Ramping section of the ICW.

4  Finalization

  1. Return to the Install Cal Wizard for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.
