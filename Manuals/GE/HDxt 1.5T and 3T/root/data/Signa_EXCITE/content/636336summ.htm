1  Procedure

1.1   External Cable Replacement

Simple removals that are clearly obvious are not described here. Unless otherwise noted, the steps for re-assembly are simply the reverse order of the steps described for disassembly.

  1. Disconnect old cable with QHTR. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect new cable with QHTR. If coil is reverse ramped. The QHTR box with swapped cables has to be used as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Mechanical Hardware Replacemen

The 4 connection pins on the top half of the coil insert into recessed spring clips on the bottom half of the coil. The spring clips are available as FRU. Please Refer FRU Document.

  1. The four spring clip connectors are located in the head coil baseplate, under the plastic caps marked “A”. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the plastic caps by removing the two screws labeled “B”. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the gold spring clip and red gasket. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new spring clip as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the red gasket and replace the plastic cap.

2  Finalization

  1. Do a test scan with the coil to verify operations.
