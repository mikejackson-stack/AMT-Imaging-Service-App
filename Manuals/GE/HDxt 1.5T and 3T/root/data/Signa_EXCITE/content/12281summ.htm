1  Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, go to the Scan Desktop.

  1. Click on [New Pt] to enable setting of a new landmark.

  1. Enter the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the DQA-III phantom in the head coil. Position it with the fill plugs up, and toward rear of magnet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark in the sagittal and axial planes (DQA-III coronal plane is not at isocenter). See Illustration 2 for positioning the phantom in the head coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. At front enclosure on scanner, press LANDMARK, then MOVE TO SCAN.

  1. Set Patient Protocols to [Service].

  1. In the Protocol field, Type o.38.1 (o=Other, 38=protocol number, 1=series number) and <Enter>.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. Click on [Save Series].

  1. Click on [Scan] (system auto pre-scans first).

  1. On the scan desktop, click on [Autoview]. When the image displays, verify that the CS appears in the upper right-hand corner (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the Operator Workspace, click on [New Series].

  1. In the Protocol field, Type o.38.2 (o=Other, 38=protocol number, 2=series number) and <Enter>.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. Click on [Save Series].

  1. In the RX MANAGER window, click on [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click on [Scan] (system auto prescans first).

  1. When the image displays, verify that the A appears in the lower-middle of the image. The image should have the GE logo in the upper right-hand corner (see Illustration 3). If the images appear backwards, go to Software Configuration to adjust the gradient polarity.

    (Step Details)

  1. In the RX MANAGER window, click on [End Exam] to exit.
