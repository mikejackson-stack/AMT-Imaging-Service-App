1  Procedure

NOTICE
Equipment damage possibility. Completely remove the head coil from the cradle before performing any body scans. Failure to do so may damage the head coil T/R network.
 
 

  1. Remove head coil if present.

  1. Click the (scan) icon to go to the scan page.

  1. If necessary, click , then click to cancel any previous exam.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom and loader on the patient table and Landmark.

  1. From the Common Service Desktop, select [Image Quality], [TR Map Test] and then click [Click here to start this tool].

  1. The TRMAP Test window opens. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the mouse cursor inside the window and then type trmap.body<Return> as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. After GNUPLOT and results are displayed, push <Enter> twice in TRMAP window to exit.

  1. Proceed to TRMAP RESULTS.
