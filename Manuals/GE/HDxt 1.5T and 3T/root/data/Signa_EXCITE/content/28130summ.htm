Standard Power Distribution Unit (PDU)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
SHOCK HAZARD!
SOME HORIZON 1.5T UPGRADES USE STANDARD PDU's. STANDARD PDU's ARE NOT LOCKOUT-COMPATIBLE AND CAN ONLY BE TAGGED.
TAKE EXTRA CAUTION TO INSURE POWER REMAINS REMOVED DURING SERVICE OPERATIONS

1  Procedure

1.1   Standard Power Distribution Unit (PDU) lockout

  1. Showndown the system computers before removing power

  1. Open the circuit breaker panel door and switch all circuit breakers to the OFF position.

  1. Close the circuit breaker panel door and affix a Lockout Tag to the Standard PDU Door.

  1. Turn off the Main Disconnect Box that feeds the Standard PDU, with the knife switch or circuit breaker. Lockout the Main Disconnect Box with a non-ferrous padlock and tag the padlock.

1.2   Standard Power Distribution Unit (PDU) - Restoring Power

  1. Turn on the Main Disconnect Box that feeds the Standard PDU, with the knife switch or circuit breaker. Remove the Lock and tag from the Main Disconnect Box.

  1. Open the circuit breaker panel door and remove the Lockout Tag on the Standard PDU Door.

  1. Switch all circuit breakers to the ON position.

  1. Power up the computer.

  1. If necessary, boot the computer. Genesis systems will automatically boot back up. (Allow 10-13 minutes). If you are working on an LX system, reboot the SGI computer using the appropriate login sequence.

2  Finalization

  1. Insure system is completely powered up and operational.

  1. Do a phantom scan to insure operation.
