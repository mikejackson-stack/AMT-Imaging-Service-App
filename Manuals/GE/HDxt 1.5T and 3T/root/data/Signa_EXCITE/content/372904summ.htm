3  Procedure

3.1   Running MCR 2 Diagnostics

  1. Start the Common Service Browser if it is not already running.

  1. Click the [Diagnostics] tab and then click the [Multi-Coil Receive II Diag ] from manual diagnostics link. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once on the MCR2 Diag page, Click the Troubleshooting [Overview] link in the center of the page for hardware hookup information. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Troubleshooting flowcharts for Connector A and Connector B can be obtained from the hyperlinks in “Troubleshooting” section as shown in Illustration 1 below.

  1. Once tests are setup, Click the [Run] button. The test should indicate in the lower Test Status window that it is executing.

  1. The test will complete in about 25 seconds. After the test runs successfully, we get the screen as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Failures are highlighted in red font while passing data is always highlighted in blue font.

  1. Once testing is complete, perform a [TPS Reset] before continuing.
