3  Procedure

  1. Remove Preamp Box cover.

  1. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable assembly. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  System Cable Connected At Box

  1. Disconnect MCX plug and the DC Plug on the cable end from the interface board. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  MCX Connectors Plugged into PCB

  1. Remove the old cable assembly.

  1. Fix a new cable assembly on the top and bottom sections of the preamp PCB and reattach the cable lock with two screws. Connect all MCX plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board (see Illustration 2).

  1. Close the Preamp Box cover.
