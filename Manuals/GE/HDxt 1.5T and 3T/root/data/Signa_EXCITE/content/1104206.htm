3.3  Safety

WARNING
All RF coil-related tests must be run on a system that is well calibrated and passes all system tests. The system should have passed Install In Spec (IIS), especially white pixel, correlated noise, and Multi-Coil-Receive (MCR) tool.

3.4  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

4  Procedure

4.1  Legacy Phantom Setup

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR Test using the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Foot/Ankle Coil by Invivo (M3340CB).

  1. Place the baseplate of the coil on the table:

    1. Lock the baseplate shown below to ensure that the coil position is in the center.

      Illustration 1:  Lock Baseplate

    1. Insert the phantom as shown below.

      Illustration 2:  Insert Phantom

  1. Place the coil on the baseplate, and connect the coil to the Hypertronics Connector, Port A in the LPCA as shown below.

    Illustration 3:  Hypertronics Connector

  1. Establish the landmark on the top of the coil as shown below, and advance to scan.

    Illustration 4:  Landmark Position

  1. Perform the MCQA Test procedure.

4.2  Unified Phantom Setup

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR Test using the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Foot/Ankle Coil by Invivo (M3340CB).

  1. Place the Foot/Ankle Coil baseplate on the table as shown below.

    Illustration 5:  Foot/Ankle Coil Baseplate on Table

  1. Place the Small Cylindrical Unified Phantom horizontally on the baseplate as shown below.

    Illustration 6:  Cylindrical Unified Phantom on Baseplate

  1. Place the Cubical Unified Phantom vertically, and fix the phantom to the baseplate using the Velcro as shown below.

    Illustration 7:  Cubical Unified Phantom on Baseplate

  1. Cover the baseplate with the coil as shown below.

    Illustration 8:  Coil Over Baseplate

  1. Connect the Foot/Ankle Coil to Port A of the system LPCA, and landmark the coil at the crossmark on the coil handle.

    1. Advance to scan.

    1. Perform the MCQA Test procedure.

    Illustration 9:  Crossmark on Coil Handle

  1. After the test is completed, carefully remove the Cubical Phantom out of the baseplate, then the Cylindrical Phantom to avoid damage to either phantom.
