NOTE:Connect the coil to the system as shown in Legacy Phantom Setup, Step 1.

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the positioner (2381683-4) of Torso into gaps present on the posterior section of the coil as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the TL Unified phantom as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the TL unified phantom such that the phantom is well centered on both S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 7 and Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the anterior section (Illustration 9) in such a way that the anterior of the coil is well set in to the positioner as shown in Illustration 10 and fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantom (Illustration 11).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil on the cross mark as shown in Illustration 12. Advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
