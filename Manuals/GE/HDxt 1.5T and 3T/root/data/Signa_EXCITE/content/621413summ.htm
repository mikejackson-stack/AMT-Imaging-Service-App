3  Procedure

3.1   Body Hybrid Splitter Removal

  1. To ensure the scanner is idle, select [End Exam].

  1. Disable the output of the RF Amplifier with the switch on the Exciter, or disconnect J14 at the rear of the RF Amplifier.

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire Systems Cabinet, boot the Applications software down to root level. The VRE System, which resides in the Systems Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the Applications software is active.
 
 

  1. To boot the Applications software to the root level, right mouse click and select Service Tools and [System Restart]. Power down the Systems Cabinet when the login GUI displays.

  1. LOTO to the Systems Cabinet according to the instructions in Lock Out / Tag Out RF Systems Cabinet and RF Amplifier.

  1. Remove DD voltage by turning Off the Driver Module and switching Off the Systems Cabinet breaker on the PDU.

  1. Remove the two lower covers from the rear bridge assembly and locate the Hybrid Splitter shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the DD Bias Cable.

  1. Disconnect the Heliax Cables.

  1. Slide the Hybrid Splitter out of its saddle.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The T/R Module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interact with the module making it difficult for one person to handle.
Always remove and/or replace the Body Hybrid 3.0T EXCITE Body T/R Module from the rear pedestal with two (2) people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it.

  1. While carrying the Hybrid Splitter from the Magnet Room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. Once outside of the Magnet Room, remove the two Pre-Amps/Combiner and Notch Filter (shown below) and set them aside for installation on the new Body Hybrid Assembly.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Body Hybrid Splitter Installation

  1. Attach the existing Pre-Amps/Combiner and Notch Filter to the new Hybrid Splitter Assembly.

  1. Attach the cables to the Pre-Amps as shown.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The T/R Module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interact with the module making it difficult for one person to handle.
Always remove and/or replace the Body Hybrid 3.0T EXCITE Body T/R Module from the rear pedestal with two (2) people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it.

  1. While moving the Body Hybrid Splitter to the rear of the magnet, maintain the maximum distance from the magnet at all times.

  1. Slide the new Hybrid Splitter into its saddle.

  1. Attach the DD Bias Cable.

  1. Attach the cables going to the body coil.

  1. Attach the Heliax Cables.

4  Finalization

  1. Replace all removed covers.

  1. Reapply power to the system, and remove all LOTO equipment.

  1. Perform a TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Complete a Body and Head scan to ensure the system is functional.
