3T RF Test (RFT) Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Disable UTNS3, Disable TR Error Reporting (Driver Module). Systems with TRM and TAC: Disable Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology) in TAC.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Preparing Required Conditions

  1. Disable the UTNS by removing the UTNS Antenna from the Rear of the UTNS 3, at J31.

  1. Disable the TR Error condition in the Driver Module.

    1. Verify the system is in idle and all coils have been removed from the bore.

    1. Remove the front cover of the EXCITE Systems Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the Driver Module, disable the T/R error reporting by setting the T/R switch to DIS (disable faults) position. See Illustration 1.

      (Step Details)

  1. Systems with TRM: Shut down the Vacuum Pump (Disable Quiet Technology).

    1. Remove the front cover of the Twinspeed Accessories Cabinet (TAC) and place power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near where the power cord connects) in the OFF position OR remove the power cord from the pump. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. The vacuum pump must be powered off and not running while this is done. Remove the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 on the rear of the TAC and slowly open the vacuum line connection at J11. Allow the vacuum to slowly equalize the vacuum space inside the magnet bore to atmospheric pressure.

    (Step Details)

2.2   TPS Loopback Baseline Data

Essentially this test is a “Transmit/Receive Electronics Loopback” for the transmit and receive chain components in the MGD chassis only. It verifies the proper function of the Exciter and Receiver. The TPS Loopback Baseline Data Test is performed in the Body mode. Results from this test are referenced automatically by subsequent RFT tests, therefore this test must be performed first. Control Variables for Stability and Linearity Troubleshooting are listed in Table 3.

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! You MUST connect the 30dB and DC Block attenuators BEFORE connecting the sense loop to prevent damage to the TPS receiver board.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE POSSIBILITY! The attenuators may contain ferrous parts. Do not take them into the magnet room; doing so can cause damage to these components.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! All phantoms and coils should be removed from the bore.

  1. Set-up the system for the TPS Loopback test. See Illustration 3.

3  Finalization

  1. Upon completion of RFT, the following equipment must be restored to full operation if it was put in Service Mode.

    1. UTNS 3

    1. TR Error Reporting (Driver Module)

    1. If removed, restore Body/Head Heliax

    1. For TRM systems with a TAC, Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology) in TAC

    1. Perform a TPS reset when system is back to normal

  1. Enable the UTNS3 by reconnecting the UTNS Antenna to the Rear of the UTNS 3, at J31.

  1. Re-enable T/R Error reporting by enabling the TR switch on the front of the Driver module. See Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the Body or Head Heliax was removed, verify the scanner is in “idle”, turn off the RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O board) or disconnect J14 from the back of the RF amplifier.

    1. Disconnect the RFT hardware and cables from the system.

    1. Connect the original Body Output Cable to the RF Amplifier J4 OR connect the original Head Output Cable to RF Amplifier J3.

    1. Re-enable RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O Board) or reattach the cable to J14 at the back of the RF amplifier.

  1. If the system is a TRM coil and TAC cabinet, Enable Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology).

    1. If not already removed, remove the front cover from the Twinspeed Accessory Cabinet (TAC) .

    1. Place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near where the power cord connects) in the ON position, OR plug the power cord back in. See Illustration 13.

      (Step Details)

    1. Restore the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 at the back of the TAC.

  1. Perform a TPS reset.

    (Step Details)
