2  Overview

This procedure is useful for troubleshooting White Pixels, as it bypasses the Receive Chain consisting of cabling, 16 Channel Switch or 8 Channel Switch and corresponding cabling back to the Pen Panel. The following process uses a single channel receive coil to bypass the normal receive chain. Test cables and an existing UTNS cable (SMB to BNC) is used to bypass all existing receive chain cabling and hardware.

4  Procedure

4.1  Receive Chain Bypass for Excite HD (12x) or HDx (14x)

  1. Run White Pixel in Body mode with normal configuration.

  1. Re-configure the system as described below, eliminating the normal Body Receive Chain. (Illustration 1)

    Illustration 1:  Re-Configure Eliminating The Normal Body Receive Chain

  1. Remove the one of the BNC connectors at the BNC Splitter (either right or left side) that goes to the UTNS, J31. This cable will temporarily be used as a test cable. (Illustration 2)

    Illustration 2:  Cable Configuration for Equipment Room

  1. Connect the removed cable to a BNC bullet, and run a test 50 ohm cable from the BNC bullet to the “Test” port on the Pen Panel, or any open BNC port.

  1. Remove the SMB end of the cable that is used in step 1 from the UTNS, J31. This cable will be temporarily used as a receive cable.

  1. Connect this end to the Body Receive port on the Receiver, Slot 14, connector J13. (Illustration 3)

    Illustration 3:  Connection for Body Receive

  1. On the Magnet Room side of the Pen Panel, connect another long BNC cable from the BNC port that was used on the Equipment Room side to a Body Preamp, output side. Using a BNC Tee, connect a single channel receive coil to the BNC Tee, then connect input of the body preamp to the BNC Tee, then connect the ¼ wavetrap to the BNC Tee.

  1. You are now able to use the Single Receive Coil to pick up signals and to hunt for the White Pixel source. This also gives the flexibility to walk around the room with the coil, and also place it in the bore, front. Rear, etc… to search for the elusive White Pixel.

    NOTE:There is a possibility that there is no continuity with the revised receive chain. Verify that the new receive chain is working by running White Pixel with the cable removed from the Receiver Body Input, J13. The noise background should be different. If the background is the same, there is an issue with the modified Receive Chain.

    Repeat White Pixel test multiple times. If results are now clean, there is a possibility that the receive chain (16 channel switch, 8W8 cables, etc) is the cause of White Pixel.

4.2  8W8 bypass for Excite II (11x)

  1. Run White Pixel in Body mode with normal configuration.

  1. Re-configure the system as described below, eliminating the normal Body Receive Chain. (Illustration 4)

    Illustration 4:  Re-Configure To Eliminate The Normal Body Receive Chain

  1. Remove the cable at the SC Interface J54 and connect test cable between J54 and any open BNC port on the Pen Panel.

  1. Remove cable going to UTNS, J31 (this is the other end of J54 at SC Interface) and connect to J13 of the receiver in slot 13. This will place the receive signal into the body receive port.

  1. On the Magnet Room side of the Pen Panel, connect another long BNC cable from the BNC port that was used on the Equipment Room side to a Body Preamp, output side. Connect a single channel receive coil to the Input of the body preamp.

    NOTE:There is a possibility that there is no continuity with the revised receive chain. Verify that the new receive chain is working by running White Pixel with the cable removed from the Receiver Body Input, J13. The noise background should be different. If the background is the same, there is an issue with the modified Receive Chain.

  1. Repeat White Pixel test multiple times. If results are now clean, there is a possibility that the receive chain (8 channel switch, cables, etc) is the cause of White Pixel.
