Table 1:  Scan Prescription - Head Calibration

  1. Click [New Pt].
  2. Id: geservice and press <Enter>.
  3. Name: apb cal Head and press <Enter>.
  4. Weight (Lb): 300 and press <Enter>.
  5. Set Patient Protocols to Service.
  6. Select [Other], and scroll through the protocol field until 3.0T apb cal is located.
  7. Select Series 2, Head.
  8. Click [Accept].
  9. Set Landmark and Advance to Scan.
  10. Select [Save Series].
  11. Right-click and select [Research Options] and [Setup Params].
    1. Set TG to 50.
    2. Select [Done].
      NOTE:In the next steps, change the CV values as indicated.

  12. Right-click and select Research Options and Display CVs from the drop-down menu.
  13. Set the CV calmode to 5 (Dual Logamp Waveform) and press <Enter>.
  14. Press [Accept] to capture the setting.
