2.4  Safety

DANGER
Electric Shock Hazard!
Lethal voltages are present inside the Global Operator Console (GOC).
before servicing any components, Ensure the power is turned off, power cord removed, and lockout/Tagout performed before opening the GOC.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

3  Procedure

3.1  Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Close any PC applications, and power down the GOC. (Refer to System Startup and Shutdown.)

  1. Perform LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace before beginning the replacement.

NOTICE
When removing the side panel, avoid straining the ground lead.
 
 

  1. Remove the two screws on the left side panel of the GOC.

    Illustration 1:  Screws on Left Side Panel

  1. Disconnect the short ground lead that connects the side panel to the GOC main chassis at the center of the lead.

    Illustration 2:  Ground Lead

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by lifting it up.

  1. Remove all the cables connected to the computer, ensure they are properly labeled, and note their locations for reattachment. (Illustration below shows the cable configuration.)

    Illustration 3:  HP Z400 Cable Configuration

  1. Loosen the strap and remove the hook as shown below.

    Illustration 4:  Removing Strap

  1. Refer to the illustration below, and remove the Z400 Computer as follows:

    1. Remove the two screws securing the back bracket.

    1. Loosen the two screws on the back slide plate, and slide the plate backward.

    1. Loosen the two screws on the side slide plate, and slide the plate in the direction of the arrow as shown.

      Illustration 5:  Removing HP Z400 Computer

    NOTICE
    The computer weighs 50 pounds, which requires two people to move the PC cabinet.
     
     

    1. Carefully remove the computer using the back and bottom brackets from the GOC.

  1. Remove the two screws (shown below) that secure the left side panel to the rear of the PC.

    Illustration 6:  Screws on PC

  1. Remove the left side panel of the PC by pulling the latch (shown below) out, and sliding the cover back. Set the cover aside in a safe location.

    Illustration 7:  Latch on Side Panel

3.2  PC Cover Removal

This portion of the procedure describes how to remove the PC covers.

NOTICE
Avoid damaging the plastic tabs when removing the front cover from the PC. Read the next two steps carefully before proceeding.
 
 

  1. Remove the front PC cover by gently unsnapping the plastic locks located on the right side, and gently pulling on the right side of the cover as shown below.

    NOTE:Ensure the cover is only pulled out far enough to disengage the locks and not any further, or damage could occur to the left side tabs.

    Illustration 8:  Front Cover Removal

  1. With the right side locks disengaged, push the cover slightly to the left while pulling out on the cover as shown.

    Illustration 9:  Cover Opened

  1. Set the cover aside in a safe location.

3.3  DVD Drive Replacement

Replace the defective DVD drive as described below.

NOTICE
Never remove a cable by pulling on the wires, use the housing connector instead.
 
 

  1. Locate and unplug the connectors for the Power and Data Cables connected in the back of the DVD drive.

    Illustration 10:  Location of Power and Data Cables

  1. Pull out on the plastic lock located on the left side of the PC, and carefully remove the DVD drive.

    Illustration 11:  DVD Drive Removal

  1. Install the new DVD Drive by pulling out on the plastic lock while sliding the new drive into place in the open slot.

  1. Reconnect the power and data cables (shown in Illustration 10) to the new DVD drive.

  1. Reinstall the front PC cover.

  1. Reattach the Ground Lead to the GOC chassis.

  1. Reinstall the PC left side panel, and secure it with the two screws removed earlier.

4  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Insert media into the new DVD drive, and verify proper operation of the drive.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure proper operation of the system.
