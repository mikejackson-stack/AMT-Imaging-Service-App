WARNING
Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
The module could fall when using The lift tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This will help keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

NOTICE
Prior to removing power from the Systems Cabinet, verify that ICNs are properly shut down. See ICN ON/OFF Procedure.

1.4   REMOVAL/REPLACEMENT OF DRIVER MODULE

  1. Attach a grounding wrist strap.

  1. Remove connections from the rear of the Driver Module.

  1. Remove the four screws that attach the Driver Module to the System Cabinet frame.

  1. Pull the driver module forward (it’s mounted on a roller tray) until the roller tray reaches its maximum extension limit.

  1. Attach the hanger (Item 20) to the hanger stud on the right side of the driver module. Repeat this process on the left side. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply slight tension to support chain with chain hoist.

  1. Press the release catch on the side of each roller tray. The roller tray should detach, and allow the driver module to swing freely on the hoist.

  1. Lower the driver module to the ground and remove the hangers.

1.5   REVERSE THE DE-INSTALL PROCESS FOR INSTALLATION

  1. Position the replacement driver module in front of the cabinet, and attach hangers to the hanger studs, which are located on both the left and right side of the Driver Module.

  1. Use the hoist to raise the Driver Module.

  1. Attach each side of the roller tray.

  1. Lower the hoist chain slightly. This will allow you to test the roller slide action, and ensure that the Driver Module is connected correctly to the roller tray and Systems Cabinet.

  1. Remove the hangers from the Driver Module.

  1. Gently push the Driver Module back into the cabinet, and reattach the four screws which connect the Driver Module to the System Cabinet frame.

  1. Re-connect all connections removed earlier the new Driver Module.

  1. Remove and disassemble the hoist, and repack it in the case.

2  Finalization

  1. Power up the Systems Cabinet.

  1. Perform a TR/DD Calibration after the replacement

  1. Run one head and one 4-, 8 - or 16-channel multi-coil scan.
