RRF Chassis RF WaveForms

This document permits the FE to quickly confirm waveform fidelity and general amplitude by comparing the scope waveform seen at the measured port to what is provided in the drawings in WaveForm Diagrams Examples.

  1. The test ports to scope for specific waveforms are shown in Illustration 1, Illustration 2, and Illustration 3.

  2. The Front Panel I/O Board Scope Trigger J7 is used whenever an external scope trigger signal is required.

  3. Each scope waveform is displayed in WAVEFORM DIAGRAMS with the proper channel input selections, time base and triggering information.
