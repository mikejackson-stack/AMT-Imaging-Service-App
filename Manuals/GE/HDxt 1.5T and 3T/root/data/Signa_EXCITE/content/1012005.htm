2  Procedure

  1. Click Here for a listing of HDx installations / upgrades. Select the type of installation or upgrade that pertains to your site. In the column associated with the installation or upgrade pertaining to your site, click on the down arrow and select “x” (this will display the applicable components that have ICD cards shipped with them and thus will need to be entered into GIB)

  1. If the system at your site has any of the items listed, locate their ICD cards and complete the Finalization procedure in Calibration Wizard.

3  Finalization

Return to Installation Calibration Wizard and complete steps in Finalization.
