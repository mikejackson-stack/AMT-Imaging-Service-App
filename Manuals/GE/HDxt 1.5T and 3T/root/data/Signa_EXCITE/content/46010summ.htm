1  Procedure

1.1   Installation of an LCD Monitor in a Fixed Site Environment

  1. Shutdown Signa and perform a safety lockout/tagout of the Operator Workspace.

  1. If necessary, remove the old LCD Monitor from the Desktop.

  1. Remove the new LCD monitor from its box and its packing and set the monitor on the workspace desktop.

  1. Rotate the monitor and connect the Video and power cables.

  1. Remove the Safety Lockout of the Operators Workspace and restore power to the system.

  1. Verify that main LCD monitor power is ON. If not, locate and press the power button on LCD. This button should remain on at all times.

  1. The monitor should be positioned no closer than 16 inches and no further away then 28 inches from your eyes. The optimal distance is 24 inches for either of the monitors.

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot up Signa.

1.2   Installation or Replacement of an LCD Monitor in a Mobile Environment

This section pertains to mobile systems only.

  1. Replacement of an LCD is performed by removing the screws under the tabletop that hold the existing LCD panel base.

  1. Determine the best way to secure the new monitor to the tabletop. 3.0T or HFO systems are not presently installed in Mobile environments.
