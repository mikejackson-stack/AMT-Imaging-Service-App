NOTE:If you see an error in detecting the ICNs (VRE Configuration failed) during VRE configuration, complete the following steps:

  • Power Down the ICN.

  • Disconnect the power cable from the back of the ICN, wait for 30 seconds, and reconnect it. The SUN ICN has 2 power sources. Both should be connected almost simultaneously (at most within 5 seconds of each other).

  • Restart VRE Configuration from Guided Install.

NOTE:If the VRE configuration continues to fail, refer to VRE Troubleshooting.
