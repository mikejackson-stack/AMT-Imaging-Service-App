Universal Power Monitor - MNS Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
Dangerous voltage levels throughout the UPM Cabinet
MAKE SURE That the RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

1  Procedure

1.1   MNS Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit TR faults. Disable the Driver Module TR fault detection in the RFS (RF in the system cabinet). See Illustration 1. Move Switch SW2 to the TR Disable position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the MNS Heliax cable from the back of the MNS Amplifier and connect the RF dummy load as indicated in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Setting up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM – Head Forward Power

NOTE:This tool must have the head coil installed in order to enable Coil ID.

  1. To establish a Landmark, Start a [New Pt].

  1. Enter the following parameters:

    1. Patient ID: geservice

    1. Weight: 111

  1. Install the head coil.

  1. Align the head coil to the alignment light.

  1. Press [Landmark] to drive the coil into the bore.

  1. Start the UPM Calibration Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select Nuclei [31P].

  1. The RF Chain will then default to MNS.

  1. Select [Forward] for the Power Monitor type.

  1. Select [Calibrate.]

  1. Select [Start ] and validate your hardware setup, then select [Yes].

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the status indicates a Pass or Fail within five minutes (tool should notify user of current step in progress). If failure occurs, see Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting.

  1. Proceed to the UPM MNS Functional Check procedure before moving to Finalization, Step 1

2  Finalization

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment.

  1. Re-attach the MNS Heliax cable.

  1. Re-enable TR Drive fault detection. See Illustration 1.

  1. Upon completion of any UPM calibration, [Reset TPS]. The system will not scan until this step is complete.
