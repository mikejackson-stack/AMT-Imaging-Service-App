2  Overview

FRU level components in the LPCA are:

  • Multi Coil Preamp Assembly.

  • Multi Coil Enclosure Assembly.

  • Head TR Assembly.

  • Coil Connector Replacement (explained in Replacement of Hypertronics Connector on LPCA.)

  • Coil ID LED.

  • Cable track.

  • RF Reflected Load Resistor.

4  Procedure

  1. For sites with RFS cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled PHPS/SSM on the PDU. Or for sites with RF cabinet and Systems cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled SSM.

  1. In order to access the LPCA Assembly and its components, unfasten the 4 screws that attach the cover to the base plate.

  1. Unfasten the 6 screws that attach the cover to the base plate.

  1. Then carefully remove the cover from the base plate assembly.

  1. The RF reflected Power load, with the resistor brackets are a part of the bottom plate. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  LPCA Carriage Cover Exploded View

  1. Loosen the Tension Arm to release the tension of Drive Belt as shown in Illustration 2 and then loosen the clamps shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    NOTE:It is a good idea to count the number of belt teeth seen from the top before removing the clamp screws.

    Illustration 2:  Loosening Of The Tension Arm

    Illustration 3:  Belts Removed From Both Ends

  1. At this point the RF Reflected Load Resistor can be removed if required. See Illustration 4. From below the plate, unscrew the 4 screws to remove the two Resistor brackets. Make sure to remove the cable connector too to release the resistor.

    Illustration 4:  RF Reflected Load Resistor

  1. Remove the connectors of the LPCA in the assembly and remove the 4 screws at the bottom of the assembly. See Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Connectors and Screw Removal

  1. The assembly is now exposed (see Illustration 6) and replacement of sub components can be made. Illustration 7 shows the front of a forward production LPCA.

    Illustration 6:  LPCA Modules

    Illustration 7:  Forward Production LPCA

5  Finalization

  1. Run MCR2 tool, refer to theMulti-coil Receive Chain (MCR) Tool procedure.

  1. Do a test scan to verify system operation.
