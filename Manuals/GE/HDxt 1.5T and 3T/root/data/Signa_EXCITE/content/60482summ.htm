Resistive Shim Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Possible personal injury!
Electrocution
Remove power from the TAC cabinet before attempting to remove the resistive shim supply. Verify that the FRI/AMP and SSM breakers on the front of the PDU are off. Lock and tag out the plexiglas cover on the front of the PDU.

DANGER
Possible personal injury!
Cabinet tip hazard
Confirm that the aluminum anti-tip legs stored in the rear of the cabinet are installed on either side of the base of the cabinet before attempting to remove an RF amplifier.

NOTE:Verify that all hardware is securely fastened; all bolts and nuts be tight prior to attaching the hoist assembly to the lift kit.

NOTE:Do not pull the resistive shim supply out farther than the stop line located on the top of the resistive shim supply.

1  Procedure

1.1   Resistive Shim Supply Removal

  1. Using the screws included with the anti-tip legs, secure the aluminum anti-tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the TAC Cabinet. Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front cover and the top bracket from the TAC cabinet.

  1. Open the rear TAC cabinet door.

  1. Remove the four (4) screws that attach the fan module to the TAC cabinet. Set the fan module in the cabinet.

  1. Disconnect the data, COM1, shim output, and power cables from the back of the resistive shim supply.

  1. Locate and open the Universal Lift Hoist Kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections and place them on a flat surface for assembly. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten them together with 4.75” X 1/2” caps crews and hex nuts.

  1. Use the crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws.

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the rail assembly. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Center the Steel Rail and elevating bracket assembly at the top of TAC Cabinet, so the hoist assembly will face the front of the cabinet. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the bolts on the Steel Rail to secure it to the top of the cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail. Insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four (4) screws from the resistive shim supply.

  1. Pull the resistive shim supply out of the cabinet until you see the STOP line. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the two side brackets to the resistive shim supply using the bolts on the side of the resistive shim supply. See Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the hoist bracket to the side brackets.

  1. Hoist the resistive shim supply out of the cabinet and place it safely on the floor. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the hoist bracket from the side brackets and remove the side brackets from the resistive shim supply.

1.2   Resistive Shim Supply Installation

  1. 1. Attach the hoist side brackets to the resistive shim supply. See Illustration 8.

  1. Attach the hoist bracket to the side brackets.

  1. Hoist the resistive shim supply up and slide it part way into the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Remove the hoist bracket and side brackets from the resistive shim supply.

  1. Slide the resistive shim supply all the way into the cabinet.

  1. Install the four (4) bolts that attach the resistive shim supply to the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Attach the data, COM1, shim output, and power cables to the back of the resistive shim supply.

  1. Remove the hoist assembly from front rail. See Illustration 6.

  1. Loosen the bolts that secure the Steel Rail to the top of the cabinet.

  1. Remove the Steel Rail from the cabinet.

  1. Replace the top bracket and front cover for the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Replace the fan module and secure with four (4) screws.

  1. Close the rear TAC cabinet door.

  1. Remove the anti-tip legs. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the lock out and tag out for the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Disassemble and return the Universal Lift Hoist Kit components to the case.
