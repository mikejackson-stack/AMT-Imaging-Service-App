LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy – five minutes.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnection(s)

  1. Two personal locks and tags per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

  1. Voltmeter to read voltage for verification that energy has dissipated in the PDU.

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the Operator Workspace, Shut down the Host PC.

  1. At the PGR cabinet, go to the front panel of the PGR PDU and press the POWER OFF button.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU rotate the large main breaker counterclockwise to the Green O (OFF) position (see Illustration 1).

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU, pull out the locking mechanism on the end of the main breaker (or, on the new breaker, press on the arrow) to expose the hole where the Lock and Tag can be placed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate and turn off the PGR PDU main breaker on the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) that feeds the PGR cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that all energy has been dissipated. Use a voltmeter to measure the line-to-line voltage between each INPUT phase voltage test point on the PDU. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for reenergizing the equipment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the MDP.

  1. Switch on the MDP's PDU breaker.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PGR PDU.

  1. Rotate the larger main breaker clockwise to the RED I (ON) position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the EMO RESET switch. (Shown above.)

  1. Power up the Host PC.
