2  Overview

To set up and run the Noise-Only Test, three general tasks must be completed:

  • Cancel out of any previous exams. Click the (scan) icon, then , then .

  • The phantom must be positioned and a landmark must be established.

  • Noise-Only Test must be invoked from the Troubleshoot menu in the MR Service Desktop.

  • For more information see: Noise Only Introduction

Last Update09/08/2004

3  Procedure

3.1  Phantom Positioning

Perform Step  if using split head coil. Perform Step 1 if you are running the body coil test. Proceed to Test Execution after positioning the phantom.

  1. Body Coil Phantom Positioning

    1. Place the short body loader and sphere on the patient table. See Illustration 1.

      Illustration 1:  SHORT BODY LOADER AND SPHERE SETUP

    1. Landmark on the center of the short-body loader.

3.2  Test Execution

  1. Noise-Only Test Setup

    1. Click the (scan) icon to go to the scan page.

    1. If necessary, click , then click to cancel any previous exam.

      NOTE:Test functionality and system functionality may be adversely affected if the previous exam is not terminated.

    1. Click on .

    1. Enter geservice under Patient ID.

    1. Enter 111 under Weight

  1. Noise-Only Test Execution

    1. Click the (Service Tools) icon to open the Service Desktop Manager.

    1. Click in the lower part of the Service Desktop Manager if the Common Service Desktop is not already visible.

      NOTE:It takes a few seconds for the Common Service Desktop to appear. If the Common Service Desktop never appears, then confirm that it isn’t already open and that it hasn’t been dragged partially off the screen or that it isn’t hidden behind another open window.

    1. Click the (Image Quality) tab at the top of the Common Service Desktop.

    1. Select [Noise Only Test] from the menu on the left side of the screen as shown in Illustration 2.

      Illustration 2:  NOISE-ONLY TEST WINDOW

    1. Under [Noise Only Test] on the right side of the screen, select [Click here to start this tool]. The Noise Only Test window opens as shown in Illustration 3.

      Illustration 3:  NOISE-ONLY TEST WINDOW (EXAMPLE)

      NOTE:Choose 'Head' for both old style head coil and split head coil.

      NOTE:Noise-Only Test can be aborted at any time by selecting [Exit] from the Noise-Only Test window menu as shown in Illustration 4.

      Illustration 4:  ABORTING THE NOISE-ONLY TEST

  1. See Viewing Test Results or if problems arise, see Troubleshooting

    To do head, you must landmark on DQA phantom in head coil.
