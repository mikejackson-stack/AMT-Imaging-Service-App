2.2  Safety

DANGER
electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present in and around the rear pedestal.
Follow the LOTO steps presented in this procedure.

3  Procedure

3.1  SSM/PHPS and Driver Module Power Down and Lockout/Tagout Procedure

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the ACGD cabinet.

  1. Power off the SSM/PHPS circuit breaker.

  1. Power off the breaker that feeds the Driver Module.

  1. Close the plastic cover plate and lock the plastic cover in place. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Lock and Tag PDU Circuit Breakers

3.2  Replace 16 Channel Switch

  1. Remove all cables connected to the 16 channel switch.

  1. Remove 4 screws holding switch to rear pedestal using a non ferrous screwdriver. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Remove Retaining Screws

  1. Install new 16 channel switch, securing with 4 screws. Reattach all cables.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO and reapply power to SSM/PHPS and Driver Module breakers.

  1. Perform TPS reset.

  1. Run Head System Gain calibration.

  1. Run 16 Channel switch diagnostics.

  1. Run a head or body scan to make sure the system is operational.
