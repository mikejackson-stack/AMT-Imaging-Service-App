4  Procedure

4.1  Remove The Cable From The Coil

  1. Remove the 9 screws holding the cover on the unit as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Remove The 9 Screws

  1. Disconnect the RF cables and the DC wires from the feed boards as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Disconnect the RF Cables

  1. Remove two screws on the clamp of strain relief as shown below: as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Remove Two Screws On The Clamp Of Strain Relief

  1. Remove the cable from the coil.

4.2  Insert The New Cable To The Coil

  1. Attach the cable with the cable clamp to the coil, see Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Cable With The Cable Clamp To The Coil

  1. Connect the DC wires and RF cables, see Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Connect the DC wires and RF cables

  1. Replace the two covers and the 9 retaining screws, See Illustration 1.
