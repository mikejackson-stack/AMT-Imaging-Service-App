2  Procedure

2.1  Removal and Installation of Rear Microphone and Microphone Housing

  1. Position the LPCA to the far rear of the enclosure.

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. (See LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room.)

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

  1. Remove the microphone housing from the LPCA as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Removing Microphone and Housing from LPCA

  1. Remove the microphone from its housing by referring to Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Microphone from Housing

    NOTE:If only replacing the housing, perform Step 3 through Step 5 in reverse order to complete the procedure.

  1. Refer to Replacement of Cable Take-up to remove the microphone wire from the Cable Track.

  1. Locate and disconnect the microphone cable on Intercom Board J7 or J9, located on the right side of the rear pedestal facing the magnet from the rear.

  1. Perform Step 2 through Step 7 in reverse order to replace the rear microphone and cable.

2.2  Removal and Installation of Front Microphone and Microphone Housing

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room

  1. Remove the front cover following to the instructions in Front Cover Removal and Installation.

  1. Remove the two mounting screws holding the microphone in place.

  1. Carefully open the fasteners and detach the microphone cable from the front cover.

  1. Remove the Teflon™ tape and thermal blanket to access the microphone.

  1. Lift the microphone from the cover.

  1. Remove the microphone from its housing referring to Illustration 2.

    NOTE:If only replacing the housing, perform Step 1 through Removal and Installation of Rear Microphone and Microphone Housing, Step 5 in reverse order to complete the procedure.

  1. Disconnect other end of the microphone cable at J7 or J9 at the Intercom Box located at the rear pedestal.

  1. Perform Removal and Installation of Rear Microphone and Microphone Housing, Step 1 through Step 8 in reverse order.
