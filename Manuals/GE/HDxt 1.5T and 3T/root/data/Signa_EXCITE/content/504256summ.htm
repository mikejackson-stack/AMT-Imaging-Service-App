ICW Finalization

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service browser or Common Service Desktop (CSD) .

  1. Click on the Calibration tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. Call the On Line Center to perform a checkout of the Magnet Monitor. Be prepared to provide the Cryogen ID. (The customer provides the Cryogen ID, usually at the same time that they provide the System ID.) When this is complete, check the box next to: Is Magnet Monitor Checked out with OLC?

  1. Perform a Magnet Pressure Control Functional Check:

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Oxygen Monitor Setup and Calibration. When this is complete, check the box next to: Is Oxygen Monitor Setup and Calibration done?

  1. Initialize Planned Maintenance. When this is complete, check the box next to: Is Planned Maintenance Initialization done?

  1. Perform a SaveINFO. When this is complete, check the box next to: Is Save Info done?

  1. Enter the Product Locator information into the Global Installed Base. This can be done one of two ways. Collect all Product Locator Cards from the equipment, and either:

    NOTE:For a full list of all the components that should have ICD cards with the installation/upgrade that being performed, see HDx/HDxt Items to be Entered in GIB.

    1. If not able to gain easy access to GE Intranet connectivity, complete the information on the cards and send via regular mail.

    1. If able to connect to the GE Intranet:

      1. Access this web link to the GIB: http://gib.gehealthcare.com/.

      1. Log on using SSO number and password.

      1. Select Global Installed Base Entries.

      1. Scroll down and enter the customer's System ID and Country.

      1. Click [Submit].

      1. Click [New Entries]:

        (Step Details)

      1. For Add (A), enter a.

      1. Click [Submit].

        (Step Details)

      1. For items that the new components replace, remove their association from the system. To do this: Locate the items in the list of items associated with the system on the GIB, and for Delete (D), type d.

  1. Click [Submit].

2  Finalization

  1. Dispatch the hours required for the installation.

  1. If there are remaining steps in the Installation & Calibration Wizard, return to the ICW procedure for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.

  1. If all the steps of the Installation & Calibration Wizard are complete and have passed, close both the Installation & Calibration Wizard and the CSD. The next time the Installation & Calibration Wizard is accessed from the CSD, Calibration tab, the wizard will be in Maintenance mode. Information for the Maintenance mode can be found in Installation and Calibration Wizard.
