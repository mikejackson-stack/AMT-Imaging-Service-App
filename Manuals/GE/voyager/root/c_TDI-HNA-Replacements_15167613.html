FRU Replacement Procedure – 1.5T TDI HNA System Cable Assembly

Follow this procedure to replace the system cable on the HNA coil.

Procedure

  1. Remove the anterior section of the HNA coil if connected.

    1. Position the posterior section of the coil to expose the bottom cover and screws.

    2. Remove the 16 screws from the bottom cover of the posterior section, and segregate the four shorter screws from the other screws.
  2. Use the side cutters to cut the zip ties holding connectors L, R and J3 in place.
  3. Remove the 2 verSI connectors labeled R and L located on either side of coil by pulling away from the connection board. Refer Direction of removal of R and L verSI connectors for direction of pull.
  4. Remove the J3 verSI connector by pulling it in a horizontal direction, illustrated below.
    Note

    Care should be taken to remove the connectors by using the PCB. Do not pull the connectors by pulling on the cables.

  5. Remove the Molex DC connector labeled J54, located beneath the cable assembly, by using pliers and pulling straight up from the board.
  6. Remove the two screws holding the system cable to the clamp.
  7. Place the new system cable on the HNA coil with the flat side of the strain relief facing up and the curved side facing down, and secure in the cable clamp with the screws just removed.

  8. Reconnect the DC cable labeled J54 on the underside of the board, ensuring the clip is facing out prior to connecting. (A click will be heard when the connector is properly seated.)

  9. Reconnect the RF connectors labeled R and L to the correct locations on the board as shown in below, and use a zip tie to secure the cables in this location.

  10. Reconnect the Versi board J3 with a zip tie.

  11. Replace the posterior cover on the HNA coil, ensuring the cables are not pinched when the cover is placed on the coil.

    1. Secure the cover with the 16 screws removed earlier.

    2. Ensure the shortest screws are located away from the system cable, as shown in Location of Screws on Bottom Cover.

Finalization

Run the MCQA Tool to verify the HNA coil operates properly.

Procedure

  1. The Latch Kit assembly contains 2 components as illustrated below. 1, is the Posterior Latch and 2, is the Latch Catch.

  2. ‘Posterior Latch’ is connected to the posterior section of the coil, whereas the ‘Latch Catch’ is connected to the anterior section of the coil.

  3. Replacements of ‘Posterior Latch’:

    1. Remove the anterior section of the HNA coil if connected.

    2. Remove the 2 screws connecting the latch to the posterior section of the coil as shown below.

    3. Replace the posterior latch part with the new part from the FRU kit.

    4. d. Secure the posterior latch components with the screws provided in the FRU kit.

  4. Replacements of ‘Latch Catch’:

    1. a. The Latch kit FRU assembly includes only 1 ‘Latch Catch’. This part can be used on either the Coil anterior section or the Adapter Block as shown in Illustration below. This procedure will explain removal of anterior latch part from the Coil Anterior. This same can be followed for the removal of the ‘Anterior Latch Part’ from the adapter block.

    2. Remove the screws connecting the ‘Latch Catch’ to the anterior.

    3. Replace the Anterior Latch part with the ‘Anterior Latch’ part from the FRU Kit.

      Note

      The Anterior Latch part must be positioned as shown in Illustration below, else the latch with fail to lock.

    4. Secure the Latch Catch by the screws provided in the FRU kit.
