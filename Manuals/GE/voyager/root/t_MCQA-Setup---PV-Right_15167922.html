MCQA Setup - PV Right

The 1.5T Coil MCQA will utilize the 1.5T phantoms, which are green in color. The 3.0T Coil MCQA will utilize the 3.0T phantoms, which are pink in color. The Small Cylindrical Unified Phantom is green in color and for both 1.5T and 3.0T coils.----

Procedure

  1. Place the Flat Filler Panel (shown below) on the table.
  2. Place the PV Coil on the cradle and connect the coil connector to P2 Port.
  3. Place the Large and Small Unified Cylindrical Phantoms under the right side of the PV Coil.
    Note Make sure the phantoms are aligned so that the phantom will be in the image FOV.
    • Cylindrical Phantoms are aligned to touch the center of assembly
    • Pink Cylindrical Phantom face is aligned to the end of assembly
  4. Perform the following steps.
    1. Place the PA Phantom Positioner fitting the alignment block on the PV Coil into the alignment notch on the PA Phantom Positioner.
    2. Flip the PV Upper unit onto the PA Phantom Positioner.
  5. Perform the following steps.
    1. Move the cradle and adjust the PV Coil position in so that the alignment light hits in the middle of the latch (shown below) of the PV Coil.
    2. Adjust the position of Unified Phantoms and PA Phantom Positioner per Step 3 and Step 4
    Note Confirm that whole coil is left/right centered to the cradle.
  6. Fasten with Velcro® straps of PV upper unit as shown.
  7. Press [Landmark] and press [Advance to Scan].
  8. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool.
    Note

    Select “3.0T_GEM_PV_Right” for Current Coil field.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
