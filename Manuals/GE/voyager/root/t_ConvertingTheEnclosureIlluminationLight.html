Converting the enclosure illumination light

Removes the defective light strip and installs the replacement light strips and their associated mounts.

About this task

This procedure is only done once on a system. Only do this task if the enclosure illumination light strip is covered in plastic tubing. Do the following steps on both left and right lower side covers.

Procedure

  1. Remove the illumination light from the light mount holder.
  2. Remove the clips securing the lights to the cover.
  3. Cut the cable ties on the back side securing the light strip wire.
  4. Remove the light strip from the cover.
  5. Clean the adhesive mount locations with the alcohol wipes supplied in the FRU kit.
  6. Mark the light strip for the location of the first tie wrap and mounting pad as shown between the first two LEDs after the pigtail.
  7. Mark the light strips for the remaining tie wrap and mounting pad locations every six LED sections.
    Note An LED section is defined as two LEDs separated by a resistor.
  8. Attach the mounting pads to the light strips using cable ties (supplied in the FRU kit) on the lines marked on the light strips until the corner is reached.
  9. Remove the adhesive backing from the mounting pads and attach them to the center mounting bracket.
    Note Locate the first mounting pad 0.25 inches from the center hole where the first anchor of the old light strip was located. The pigtails will intersect to allow for a continuous LED pattern.
  10. Remove the adhesive backing from the remaining mounting pads and attach them along the plastic braket to the end.
  11. At the corner of the panel, attach a mounting pad on each side of the corner as shown.
  12. Attach the light strip to the corner mounting locations with 14 LED sections between the mounts.
  13. Continue attaching the light strip and mounting pads to the bracket at the six LED section spacing.
  14. When the light strip has been mounted to the entire bracket, trim the LED only at the designated cut marking.
  15. Assemble the glue gun applicator using the 1:1 plunger with the Plexus MA300 50mL two-part adhesive and mixing nozzle.
    Note Nitrile gloves (supplied with FRU kit) must be worn when using the alcohol wipes and glue. Make sure that the two-part epoxy has not exceeded its shelf life of 13 months.
  16. Using the glue gun, apply glue to the sides of the adhesive mounts.
    Note The working time of the glue is three to six minutes and the bonding time is 10 to 15 minutes at room temperature.
    Important It is critical that the glue does not extend outside of the LED groove and that the glue does not interfere with the slots for cable tie installation.
