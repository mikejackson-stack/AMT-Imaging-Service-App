About this task

Overview

Use this procedure to replace the RF Body Coil.

Procedure

  1. At outside of Magnet Room, open FRU Box.
    Note

    Top Case will be used to locate the defective Body Coil.

  2. Remove Front End Bell. Refer to Front Endbell Removal.
  3. Remove Rear End Bell. Refer to Rear Endbell Removal.
    1. Remove Bridge by removing 8 screws.
    2. Disconnect 4 DC cables and 2 RF transmit cables.
  4. Disconnect the two (2) air hoses at the rear end of the body coil.
  5. Disconnect the HART ID cable at the rear end of the body coil.
  6. Disconnect Bore light cable connectors. (Front End: 2 cables, Rear End: 2 cables). Remove 2 Bore light cover at Rear End.
  7. At rear side, remove four M6 bolts and washers fixing the RF Body Coil.
  8. At front side, remove two M10 bolts and washers from the bases of RF Coil Centering Assembly.
  9. Remove the air flow sensors and tape to the magnet enclosure so it is out of the way.
  10. CAUTION
    Pinch Fingers!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow.
    Bring the Body Coil at the proper grab points.
    Slightly pull out the Body Coil.
  11. Remove the Temperature Sensor and tape to the magnet enclosure so it is out of the way.
  12. Remove RF Coil Centering Assemblies.
  13. Fully remove the coil (as shown below) and install 2 bore light covers back to the defect body coil, and then place it inside the empty bottom half of the FRU case.
  14. CAUTION
    Pinch Fingers!
    Space between VRMw and Body Coil is very narrow.
    Bring the Body Coil at the proper grab points.
    Remove the bore light cover at the end side of new body coil.
  15. Slide in the new RF body coil and stop leaving the front of Body Coil a little.
  16. Transfer the front RF Coil Centering Assembly (Front) which were used for defective RF Body Coil to the new RF Body Coil.
  17. Restore the Temperature Sensor and tape.
  18. Fix the RF Body Coil by bolts and washers by reverse order.
  19. Perform RF Body Coil Alignment. Refer to Body Coil Alignment.
  20. Perform Body Coil Tuning.
  21. Reconnect 4 DC cables and 2 RF transmit cables to RF Body coil.
  22. Restore Bore light assembly at Rear End.
  23. Restore the two (2) cooling air hoses at the rear end of the body coil.
  24. Reconnect the following cables.
    1. Hart ID cable
    2. Bore light cables
  25. Restore the air flow sensors.
  26. Restore the front end bell.
  27. Restore the rear end bell.

Finalization

  1. Note

    Required calibrations and functional checks after body coil replacement are done in the Body coil tuning.

    Remove LOTO. Refer to LOTO for ISC.

  2. Run Calibrating transmit/receive dynamic disable
  3. Run Doing the EPI White Pixel Test (PM mode)
  4. Run Calibrating LVShim
  5. Run Doing the System Performance Test (SPT).
  6. Run Doing the Echo Planar Test.
  7. Run Doing DQA calibration with the DQA III phantom.
  8. Run Saving information.
  9. Run Check Scan (Good-Bye).
  10. Return defective Body Coil in FRU Box.

    Return unused dielectric materials in the FRU box with the defective RF body coil.
