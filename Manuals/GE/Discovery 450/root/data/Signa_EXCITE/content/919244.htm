Electrical Installation and Servicing

1  Electrical Installation

All electrical installations that are preliminary to the positioning of the equipment at the site prepared for that equipment shall be performed by licensed electrical contractors. In addition, electrical feeds into the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) and the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC) shall be performed by licensed electrical contractors. Other connections between pieces of electrical equipment, calibrations, and testing shall be performed by qualified GE personnel. The products involved (and the accompanying electrical installations) are highly sophisticated and require special engineering competence. In performing all electrical work on these products GE uses its own specially-trained field engineers. All of GE's electrical work on these products will comply with the requirements of the applicable electrical codes.

2  Electrical Servicing

The purchaser of GE equipment shall only use qualified personnel (that is, GE's field engineers, personnel of third-party service companies with equivalent training, or licensed electricians) to perform electrical servicing on the equipment.

When servicing any equipment or overhead cables, the proper Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) procedures must be followed. The various LOTO procedures are listed below.

  • LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem

  • LOTO for XFRD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics)

  • LOTO for the Global Operator Console (GOC) / Operator Workspace

  • LOTO for the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC)
