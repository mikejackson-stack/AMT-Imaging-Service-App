4  Procedure

4.1  XGD Gradient Master Control (GMC) Board

4.1.1  Removing the XGD GMC

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove cables J7, J8, J9 and J10/J11.

  1. Loosen the top and bottom screws securing the XGD GMC.

  1. Using your thumbs, push in and then gently pull the extractor tab located on the top and bottom of the XGD GMC. The lever action of the ejectors disengages the XGD GMC from the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Slide the XGD GMC all the way out of the XGD Cronus Chassis.

4.1.2  Re-installing the XGD GMC

  1. Use the ejectors to insert the new XGD GMC into the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Tighten the top and bottom screws to secure the XGD GMC.

  1. Re-attach cables J7, J8, J9 and J10/J11.

4.5  XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

4.5.1  Removing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Rotate both thumb screws to the UNLOCK position.

  1. Pull out the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly from the XGD Cronus Chassis using the handle provided (top of fan).

4.5.2  Re-installing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly

  1. Insert the new XGD Cronus Fan Assembly fully into the XGD Cronus Chassis.

  1. Rotate both thumb screws to the LOCK position.

4.6  XGD Cronus Chassis

4.6.1  Removing the XGD Cronus Chassis

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PGR Cabinet by referring to the procedure, LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove the following components:

    • XGD Gradient Master Controller (GMC). See Removing the XGD GMC.

    • XGA Controller Board. See Removing the XGA Controller Board.

    • XPS Controller Board. See Removing the XPS Controller Board.

    • XGD Cronus Power Supply. See Removing the XGD Cronus Power Supply.

    • XGD Cronus Fan Assembly. See Removing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly.

  1. Loosen the two (2) thumb screws located to the left the chassis. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  XGD Cronus Chassis thumb screws

  1. Slide out the chassis from the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Remove cables J1-J6 and P1 from the rear of the chassis.

  1. Remove the six (6) screws securing the XGD Cronus Chassis to the rail bracket to remove it.

4.6.2  Re-installing the XGD Cronus Chassis

  1. Tighten the six (6) screws to secure the XGD Cronus Chassis to the rail bracket.

  1. Attach cables J1-J6 and P1 to the rear of the chassis.

  1. Slide the chassis into the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Press the slide rail, release tabs to fully insert the chassis into the PGR Cabinet.

    NOTE:Pull out the ICN directly below the chassis, if necessary, to access the release tabs.

  1. Tighten the two (2) thumb screws located to the left the chassis. See Illustration 2.

  1. Re-install the following components:

    • XGD Gradient Master Controller (GMC). See Re-installing the XGD GMC.

    • XGA Controller Board. See Re-installing the XGA Controller Board.

    • XPS Controller Board. See Re-installing the XPS Controller Board.

    • XGD Cronus Power Supply. See Re-installing the XGD Cronus Power Supply.

    • XGD Cronus Fan Assembly. See Re-installing the XGD Cronus Fan Assembly.
