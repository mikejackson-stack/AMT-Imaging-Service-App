2  Procedure

2.1   Hardware Setup

  1. Install the TR module onto the top of the LPCA cover

  1. Connect the transmit (thick) and receive (thin) cables to the connectors on the LPCA cover.

  1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

  1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the QA probe cable to the quick disconnect module.

  1. Connect the QD module to the TR module.

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

2.2   Scanner Setup

  1. Start a New Exam, click New Patient.

  1. Enter geservice<Enter> for Patient ID.

  1. Enter MNS Setup<Enter> for Patient Name.

  1. Enter 111<Enter> for Patient Weight.

  1. From the Service->Other protocols choose MNS Setup.

  1. Select the Func Test, series 1, for the nucleus under test and click [Accept].

  1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during Hardware Setup.

  1. Click [Save Series].

  1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Display CVs].

  1. Set the following CVs :

    (Step Details)

  1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Download]

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Spectro Prescan].

  1. Slide [R1] and [R2] to the maximum positions.

  1. Set [TG] (Transmit Gain) to 0 (zero).

  1. Set the top display mode to [Magnitude].

  1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a peak in the top display and a free induction decay (FID) in the bottom display as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the center frequency to center the peak in the Magnitude window, using [Delta Frequency DX ]. After each adjustment click [Apply].

  1. Record the Magnitude peak height. The peak height at TG = 0 should exceed the minimum acceptable value in Table 1.

  1. Use Zoom Mode functions to measure. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

2.3   Signal-to-Noise Test

  1. Hardware Setup

    1. Install the TR module onto the top of the LPCA cover

    1. Connect the transmit (thick) and receive (thin) cables to the connectors on the LPCA cover.

    1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

    1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place.

    1. Connect the QA probe cable to the quick disconnect module.

    1. Connect the QD module to the TR module.

    1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

  1. Scanner Setup

    1. Start a new exam.

    1. Enter geservice<Enter> for Patient ID.

    1. Enter MNS Setup<Enter> for Patient Name.

    1. Enter 111<Enter> for Patient Weight.

    1. From the [Service]->[Other protocols] choose [MNS Setup].

    1. Select the [SNR Test] series for the nucleus under test and click [Accept].

    1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during Hardware Setup.

    1. Click [Save Series].

    1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Display CVs].

    1. Set the following CVs:

      1. flip_rf1 = 10, press <Enter>

      1. pw_rf1 = 40, press <Enter>

      1. xmtaddSCAN = 200, press <Enter>

    1. Click [Accept].

    1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Download].

    1. Click [Spectro Prescan].

    1. Slide [R1] and [R1] to the maximum position.

    1. Set [TG](Transmit Gain) to 0 (zero).

    1. Set the top display mode to [Magnitude].

    1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a peak in the top display and a free induction decay (FID) in the bottom display.

    1. Adjust the center frequency to center the peak in the Magnitude window.

    1. Change the display mode in the top window to [Pure Absorp]. The shape of the peak will change with some of the signal dipping below zero. The distortion will vary depending on QA coil and scanner.

    1. Use the [Zero Order Phase] control to adjust the shape of the peak until it is fully positive and symmetric as shown in Illustration 6.

      (Step Details)

    1. Increase [TG] by units of 10 until the peak decreases and becomes slightly negative, about -180.

    1. Decrease [TG] slowly until the peak is split half-positive and half-negative. See Illustration 7.

      (Step Details)

    1. Record this null value for [TG].

    1. Subtract 60 units from the null [TG] value and set [TG] to this value. Now the peak will be positive again and at maximum amplitude.

    1. Click [Stop] and then [Done].

  1. Scanning and Analysis

    1. Click [Scan] to collect the SNR data.

    1. Switch to the [Tools] Workspace and open a Command Window.

    1. Type sage<Enter> to initiate the SAGE spectroscopy analysis tool. See Illustration 8.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click [File]->[Load]->[Rawdata] to open the load raw data dialog window. See Illustration 9.

    1. When the scan is finished click [Update List] to refresh the list of P files to choose from. See Illustration 9.

      (Step Details)

    1. The top-most P file is the latest. Select this file and click [Load]. See Illustration 9.

    1. In the main SAGE panel, select Macros->[Func Tests]->[SNR Test…]. See Illustration 10.

    1. Move the windows around if necessary.

      (Step Details)

    1. Setup the SNR Test window as listed in Table 2 for the nucleus under test.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click [Run] on the SNR Test window. The SNR will be calculated for the active data set and reported in the command window where SAGE was started.

      (Step Details)

    1. Record the n=32 value for peak SNR (AmpSNR). Compare to the minimum acceptable value in Table 1.

2.4   Noise Test

  1. Hardware Setup

    1. Install the TR module onto the top of the LPCA cover.

    1. Connect the transmit (thick) and receive (thin) cables to the connectors on the LPCA cover.

    1. Place the Grafidy base plate on the patient bed near the top of the bed.

    1. Place the QA probe in the center hole of the Grafidy plate and lock into place.

    1. Connect the QA probe cable to the quick disconnect module.

    1. Connect the QD module to the TR module.

    1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe and advance to scan.

  1. Scanner Setup

    1. Start a new exam, click [New Patient].

    1. Enter geservice<Enter> for Patient ID.

    1. Enter MNS Setup for Patient Name.

    1. Enter 111<Enter> for Patient Weight.

    1. From the [Service]->[Other protocols] choose [MNS Setup].

    1. Select the [Noise Scan] series for the nucleus under test and click [Accept].

    1. Landmark on the center of the QA probe, if not already completed during Hardware Setup.

    1. Click [Save Series].

    1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Display CVs].

    1. Set CV ia_rf1 = 0 and then click [Accept].

    1. Right-click on [Research Options] and select [Download]

    1. Click [Spectro Prescan].

    1. Slide [R1] and [R1] to the maximum position.

    1. Set [TG] to 0 (zero).

    1. Disable the RF switch at the systems cabinet. See Illustration 12.

      (Step Details)

    1. Set the top display mode to [Power].

    1. Click [Start]. After a few seconds you should see a band of noise across the power spectrum as shown in Illustration 13.

      (Step Details)

    1. The tops of the noise should be about even across the window without any peaks. Peaks in the power spectrum may indicate a source of noise in the scan room.

    1. Click [Stop] and then [Done].

  1. Scanning and Analysis

    1. Click [Scan] to collect the Noise data.

    1. Switch to the [Tools] Workspace and open a Command Window.

    1. Type sage<Enter> to initiate the SAGE spectroscopy analysis tool. See Illustration 14.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click [File]->[Load]->[Rawdata] to open the load raw data dialog window. See Illustration 15.

      (Step Details)

    1. When the scan is finished click [Update List] to refresh the list of P files to choose from. See Illustration 15.

    1. The top-most P file is the latest. Select this file and click Load.

    1. In the main SAGE panel, select [Macros]->[Func Tests]->[SNR Test…]

    1. Move the windows around if necessary.

    1. Setup the SNR Test window as listed in Table 3 for the nucleus under test.

      (Step Details)

    1. Click [Run] on the SNR Test window. The noise will be calculated for the active data set and reported in the command window where SAGE was started.

    1. Record the n=32 value for Noise. Compare to the maximum acceptable value in Table 1.

      (Step Details)

    1. In the spectrum window, select Chan->[Power].

    1. The spectrum should be flat across the window with tapering to zero at each edge as shown in Illustration 18. Broad peaks in this window suggest unwanted noise in the scan room. Use this test as a tool for tracking down noise sources if found.

      (Step Details)

    1. To zoom out and see the full spectrum, click [Zoom], then right click in the window and select [Unzoom]. Right click again and click [Quit] to exit the zoom control.

    1. Enable the RF Switch at the systems cabinet. See Illustration 19.

      (Step Details)

3  Finalization

  1. Remove any hardware used during system testing.

  1. Do a test scan of the system to ensure proper operation.
