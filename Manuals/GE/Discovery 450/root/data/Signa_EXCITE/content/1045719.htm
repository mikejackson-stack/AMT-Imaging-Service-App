Universal Power Monitor - Head and Body Setup and Calibration

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hour10 mins

2  Overview

This procedure sets up the Universal Power Monitor (UPM) for 1.5T systems in both Head Mode and Body mode using the system and the Power Measurement Kit.

Both Forward Power and Reflected Power must be completed in Head mode.

4  Procedure

4.1  Head Mode

4.1.1  Head Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the Transmit/Receive (TR) faults. Disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection in the PEN cabinet. (See Illustration 1.) Move Switches SW2 and SW3 to the TR DISABLE and DD Disable positions respectively.

    Illustration 1:  Driver Module Front Switches

  1. Move the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter in top of the Penetration Cabinet to the DISABLE (DOWN) position as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  RF ENB Switch

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Head Heliax cable (head output) from the service panel in the PGR cabinet. (See Illustration 3.)

    1. Connect either the 35 kW RF Dummy Load or 1.5T Dummy Load to the Head output of the service panel in the PGR cabinet.

    1. If the 35 kW RF Dummy Load is used, plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    NOTE:Connecting the Head output to the 1.5T Dummy Load requires the use of a 7/16 inch to HN adapter. This adapter is available in the 3T RF Power Measurement Kit.

    If the adapter is not available, you can connect the Dummy Load to connector J4 on the rear of the RF Amplifier. To access this connector, you will need to slide the RF Amplifier out of the PGR cabinet.

    NOTE:If the 35kW RF Dummy Load is used, make sure its fan is running.

    Illustration 3:  Body and Head Output Cables from RF Amplifier

4.1.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Head Forward Power

This tool must have the head coil installed in order to enable Coil ID.

4.2  Body Mode

4.2.1  Body Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the TR faults, and disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection. (See Illustration 7.) Ensure switches SW2 and SW3 are in the TR DISABLE and DD DISABLE positions respectively.

    Illustration 7:  Driver Module Switches

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Body Heliax cable (body output) from the service panel in the PGR cabinet. (See Illustration 8.)

    1. Connect either the 35 kW RF Dummy Load or 1.5T Dummy Load to the Body output of the service panel in the PGR cabinet.

    1. If the 35 kW RF Dummy Load is used, plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    NOTE:If the 35kW RF Dummy Load is used, make sure its fan is running.

    Illustration 8:  Body and Head Output Cables from RF Amplifier

5  Finalization

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment.

  1. Make sure the Body and Head Heliax cables are attached to the RF amplifier service panel.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Confirm that switch SW2 is set to TR ENABLE and switch SW3 is set to DD ENABLE for TR drive fault detection. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Upon completion of any UPM calibration, click [Reset TPS]. The system cannot scan until this step is complete.
