Each have parts to support a number of Signa configurations so there will be parts that will not be used and can be discarded at the completion of these procedures.

1.2   1.5T & 3.0T HDx LPCA Forward Production Systems

The removal of the MNS specific hardware in the LPCA and Rear Pedestal was implemented into forward production systems in June 2008. If the system was manufactured prior to this date then the hardware should already be present. This can be verified by looking at the front of the LPCA and checking for the presence of the MNS connectors located between the Port A and Port C connectors.

1.2.1  Pre-Conditions

  1. Move the LPCA to the rear of the magnet so it is parked on the rear pedestal.

  1. Perform LOTO to the system to disable RF Output and Cradle Drive.

  1. Make sure you have the two commercial catalogs, G3335CC MNS Cable Kit and G3335CD LPCA Covers, on site before starting this procedure.

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  LPCA Changes

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the belt from the LPCA.

  1. Tilt the cradle base to one side and remove the 2 flat head screws that secure the standoffs to the base of the cradle assembly. See Illustration 1. Perform this step again tilting the cradle base to the other side to access the remaining 2 screws.

  1. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cable harness assembly part number 5141260-2 by disconnecting Run 1338 from the 9-pin sub-D connector at J3. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Dismount the 9-pin sub-D connector and disconnect the plugs attached to the Red and Green LED’s at Port C. Remove this cable harness. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install new cable harness part number 5141260 by feeding the 9 pin sub-D connector down through the hole located behind the MNS Connector Bracket.

  1. Install the 9-pin sub-D to location J3 where it was previously removed.

  1. Re-connect Run 1338 to J3.

  1. Install the SMA to BNC Bulkhead, part number 2392448 to J2. See Illustration 2 for location. This view shows the backside of the bracket looking at the BNC connector of the bulkhead part.

  1. Attach the harness SMA connector to the newly installed bulkhead connector at J2. See Illustration 2.

  1. Attach the LED connector plugs to the red and green LED’s on Port C. See Illustration 3.

  1. Install the other end of the cable harness to the MNS Connector Bracket at location J2. See Illustration 3 for connector location.

    (Step Details)

  1. Re-install the 4 flat head screws to secure the standoffs to the cradle assembly base.

1.2.3  Rear Ped I/O Panel Changes

  1. Locate part 46-221865P1, N-Type Bulkhead Connector in the MNS Cable Collector Kit.

  1. Install the connector at location J3 on the Rear Ped I/O Panel. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

1.2.4  Cable Track Changes

  1. Locate Cable assembly 5141978 and Cable 5112836 in the MNS Cable Collector Kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Prepare Cable Track B for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the BNC end of cable 5141978, feed it down through the hole in the top plate and attach it to LPCA J2. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the cable in Cable Track B and route it down to 16 Channel Switch #1 – J23.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug the connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.

  1. Install the BNC end of the cable 5112836 into the MNS Connector Bracket at location J1. See Illustration 6.

  1. Install the cable in Cable Track B and route it down to the Rear Ped I/O Panel J3.

  1. Connect the cable to J3.

  1. Close all the latches on Cable Track B to secure the cables in the track.

1.2.5  LPCA Cover Change

  1. Install the new LPCA Cover, part number 5165205, that has the holes for the MNS connectors on the front face and the MNS brackets on the top.

  1. Follow your local processes for disposal of the old LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)

1.3   1.5T HD LPCA Forward Production or Upgrades to HDx Systems

1.3.1  Pre-conditions

  1. Move the LPCA to the rear of the magnet so it is parked on the rear pedestal.

  1. Perform LOTO to the system to disable RF Output and Cradle Drive.

  1. Make sure you have the two commercial catalogs, G3335CC MNS Cable Kit and G3335CD LPCA Covers, on site before starting this procedure.

    (Step Details)

1.3.2  Rear Ped I/O Panel Changes

  1. Remove the side covers on the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Locate part 46-221865P1, N-Type Bulkhead Connector in the MNS Cable Collector Kit.

  1. Install the connector at location J3 on the Rear Ped I/O Panel. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

1.3.3  LPCA Preparations

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

NOTE:The original LPCA cover will be replaced with a new cover for the MNS option.

  1. Remove the belt from the LPCA.

  1. Tilt the cradle base to one side and remove the 2 flat head screws that secure the standoffs to the base of the cradle assembly. See Illustration 8 for standoff screw locations.

  1. Repeat step 3 again but this time tilt the cradle base to the other side to access the remaining 2 screws.

    (Step Details)

1.3.4  LPCA and Cable Upgrade

Refer to Appendix A – Interconnect Diagrams, Illustration 20 for “Before” and “After” Interconnect Diagrams.

  1. Prepare Cable Track for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Illustration 9 for Latch operation and cable track identification.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect and remove the cable from the cable track, which is routed between the 16 Channel Switch J23 and Head T/R Assembly J4. See Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the following cables listed in Table 2, which are part of the MNS Cable Collector Kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the BNC end of cable Run 1153 into LPCA MNS J1. Verify that the rubber O-ring is seated on the back of the connection. See Illustration 11.

  1. Route cable Run 1153 into the cable track and down to the Rear Pedestal I/O panel, then make the connection to J3 as shown in Illustration 7.

  1. Install cable part 5112834 into the top position on the MNS Bracket part 5113332.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10 in the location shown in Illustration 11.

  1. Install the SMA-BNC Bulkhead connector, part 2392448 to the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket part 5113337. See Illustration 11.

  1. Install the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket, part 5113337 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10. See Illustration 11.

  1. Attach the SMA connector, coming from MNS LPCA J2 to the newly installed bulkhead connector at MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Illustration 11.

  1. Route Cable 5110699 (or 5110699-2 version for longer track) into the cable track.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug the combo D-Dsub connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.

  1. Attach the right angle BNC connector to J4 on the Head T/R Assembly as shown in Illustration 10.

  1. Attach the straight BNC connector to MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Illustration 11.

  1. Secure the cables with ty-wraps and close up the latches on the cable track.

1.3.5  LPCA Cover Change

  1. Install the new LPCA Cover, part number 5141723.

  1. Follow your local processes for disposal of the old LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)

    (Step Details)

1.4.4  LPCA and Cable Upgrade

Refer to Appendix A – Interconnect Diagrams, Illustration 21 for “Before” and “After” Interconnect Diagrams.

  1. See Illustration 14 for cable track identification on Dual Track Systems.

  1. Prepare Cable Track (“B” on dual track systems) for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Illustration 14.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect and remove the cable from the cable track, which is routed between the 16 Channel Switch J23 and Head T/R Assembly J4. See Illustration 15.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect and remove cable assembly 5115269-2. See Illustration 16.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the following cables listed in Table 3, which are part of the MNS Cable Collector Kit.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the BNC end of cable Run 1153 into LPCA MNS J1. Verify that the rubber O-ring is seated on the back of the connection. See Illustration 17.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route cable Run 1153 into the cable track and down to the Rear Pedestal I/O panel, then make the connection to J3 as shown in Illustration 12.

  1. Install cable part 5115269 into the top position on the MNS Bracket part 5113332.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10 in the location shown in Illustration 17.

  1. Install the SMA-BNC Bulkhead connector, part 2392448 to the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket part 5113337. See Illustration 17.

  1. Install the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket, part 5113337 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10. See Illustration 17.

  1. Attach the SMA connector, coming from MNS LPCA J2 to the newly installed bulkhead connector at MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Illustration 18.

  1. Route Cable 5110699 (or 5110699-2 version for longer track) into the cable track.

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug the combo D-Dsub connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.

  1. Attach the right angle BNC connector to J4 on the Head T/R Assembly as shown in Illustration 15.

  1. Attach the straight BNC connector to MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Illustration 16.

  1. Connect the SMB connector coming from MNS Connector J2 into the Head T/R assembly port J1. See Illustration 18.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the 9 pin sub-D connector into the MultiCoil assembly J11.

  1. Connect the red and green LED wires into the Red and Green LED’s on the microphone bracket.

  1. Secure the cables with ty-wraps and close up the latches on the cable track.

1.4.5  LPCA Cover Change

  1. Install the new LPCA Cover, part number 5141723-2.

  1. Follow your local processes for disposal of the old LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)
