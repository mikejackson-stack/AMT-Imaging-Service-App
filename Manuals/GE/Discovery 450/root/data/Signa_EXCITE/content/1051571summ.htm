Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing the Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement procedure:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting the Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement procedure:
Have all Work Assistants or Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Asphyxiation & Cold Burn Hazard!
A magnet quench during procedure could result in rapid expulsion of liquid helium through the Vertical Penetration.
Make sure magnet is ramped down to zero field before removing/replacing the Instrumentation Lead Assembly.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before starting the Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement procedure.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn or Asphyxiation Hazard!
Internal cryostat pressure build-up to >5.25 psig will open 5.25 psig relief valve.
Before removing either Ramp or Fill Port Caps or loosening any component resulting in the release of cryogen pressure:

  1. Observe the internal cryostat pressure gauge reading,

  2. Set Magnet Monitor pressure to 0.5 psig, and

  3. Vent cryostat down to 0.2 psig through Vent Valve V2.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing the Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

NOTICE
Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage.

Illustration 1:  Service Platform with Side Shield Installed

 
 

NOTE:If the site has Magnet Monitor proprietary software, connect to the Magnet Monitor and turn off the alarm for vessel pressure. Turn the vessel pressure control to a high value of 0.5 psig and a low value of 0.4 psig.

3  Procedure

3.1   Instrumentation Lead Removal

3.1.1  Removal Preparation

NOTICE
Make sure the Vertical Penetration is clear of any obstructions before inserting/extracting any tools/apparatus to prevent possible magnet damage.
 
 

  1. Ramp the magnet down to zero field in conformance with Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field.

  1. Shut down the compressor as follows:

    1. Turn On (upward I position) the Coldhead Drive Switch on the Compressor's back panel. (See the illustration below.)

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the Drive Switch on the Compressor's front panel in the O (off) position. (See the previous illustration.)

    1. Run the Coldhead for only 10 seconds with the Compressor turned off to prevent pressure build-up in the Coldhead, then turn Off the Coldhead Drive Switch and the Compressor's Main Power Switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    WARNING
    Electrical Shock Hazard!
    Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
    Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow loto procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

    1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Compressor. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Turn off all level sensor, diode and main heater sources. Disconnect the Instrumentation Box Assembly (2204623) at instrumentation connector (P1-C) shown in the illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Check the Instrumentation Connector Housing (identified in Illustration 6) at the Plenum (G-10 Sleeve) end after disconnecting P1-C. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure Lexan Top Cover 46-294765G1 (with 1/4 in. Pipe Plugs already installed) is immediately accessible for mounting on the Plenum Assembly when the Shim Lead Assembly is removed. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Open Vent Valve V2 to depressurize the Cryostat to 0.2 psig. Close V2. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen and remove the four retaining bolts holding the Vent Adaptor to the Plenum Assembly shown in Illustration 3.

  1. Remove the Shim Lead Assembly according to Shim Lead and Baffle Replacement.

  1. Immediately place the Lexan Top Cover over the opening, and secure with the eight bolts removed earlier. Tighten finger tight.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the 0.25 in. (6 mm) Instrumentation Lead Vent Line from the Male Connector on top of the Instrumentation Connector Housing. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen and remove the Male Connector from the top of the defective Instrumentation Lead Assembly.

  1. Loosen the compression fitting, and remove the four socket-head bolts holding the Feed-Thru Assembly to the Plenum Assembly. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Remove the Feed-Thru Assembly and O-Ring from the Instrumentation Lead Assembly.

NOTICE
Make sure the Lexan Plenum Cover is immediately accessible for mounting on the Vertical Penetration Flange when the Plenum Assembly is removed.
 
 

  1. Disconnect the union ball joint on the 1/2 in. plumbing from the Plenum Assembly using a pipe wrench. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the eight bolts securing the Plenum Assembly to the Vertical Penetration Flange.

  1. Remove the Plenum Assembly by carefully lifting the edge away from connector just enough to clear the flange of the Instrumentation Lead Assembly, and slide it off the end of the Instrumentation Connector Housing. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

3.1.2  Removal Using Instrumentation Connector Insertion/Removal Tool 46-281934P1

NOTICE
This procedure must be performed rapidly to prevent cryo-pumping and frost build-up.
 
 

  1. Loosen and remove the four bolts holding the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve to the Vertical Penetration Flange. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool (46-281934P1). Warm it with the heat gun to remove any moisture.

NOTICE
Make sure there is no moisture or other contamination on the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool. Any moisture remaining on the tool may result in the tool freezing to the connector.
 
 

  1. Shine a flashlight through the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve, and inspect the Instrumentation Lead Connector for frost. Remove visible frost by inserting a helium gas hose, and blowing warm helium gas at 15 psig on the frosted location.

  1. While still shining the flashlight through the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve, align the end of the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool with the threaded top on Connector P101 at the bottom of the Vertical Penetration. See Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Thread the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool onto Connector P101 clockwise (CW) until snug, then back off counterclockwise (CCW) a quarter turn. Pull upward on the tool until the connector disengages.

  1. Remove the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool and Instrumentation Lead Assembly.

  1. Immediately place the Lexan Plenum Cover (46-318561P1) over the Vertical Penetration Flange, and secure with the bolts removed earlier.

  1. Continue with Instrumentation Lead Replacement.

3.1.3  Removal Using Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool 5145459

NOTICE
This procedure must be performed rapidly to prevent cryo-pumping and frost build-up.
 
 

  1. Loosen and remove the four bolts holding the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve to the Vertical Penetration Flange. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Make sure there is no moisture or other contamination on the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool. Any moisture remaining on the tool may result in the tool freezing to the connector.
 
 

  1. Locate Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool 5145459. Warm it with the heat gun to remove any moisture.

  1. Shine a flashlight through the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve and inspect the Instrumentation Lead Connector for frost. Remove visible frost by inserting a helium gas hose, and blowing warm helium gas at 15 psig on the frosted location.

  1. While still shining the flashlight through the Instrumentation Assembly Sleeve, insert the Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool into the Vertical Penetration, down between the Instrumentation Lead Connector and the Negative (-) Main Lead Extension Connector. (See the previous illustration and the one below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. With the open section of the holder facing toward the LEMO connector, turn the Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool counterclockwise (CCW) and forward, snapping the tool onto the LEMO connector. See Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the knob down and turn the knob CW to screw the Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool onto the LEMO connector until fully engaged.

  1. Press the knob down, and pull the handle upward until the LEMO connector disengages.

  1. Remove the Latching Instrumentation LEMO Connector Removal Tool and the Instrumentation Lead Assembly.

  1. Immediately place the Lexan Plenum Cover (46-318561P1) over the Vertical Penetration Flange and secure with the bolts removed earlier.

  1. Proceed to the next section.

3.2   Instrumentation Lead Replacement

NOTICE
Make sure the Vertical Penetration is clear of any obstructions before inserting/extracting any tools/apparatus to prevent possible magnet damage.
 
 

  1. Wrap a piece of masking tape around the end of the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool opposite the threaded end. The tape will be used to mark the location of the LEMO Connector Keyway shown below.

  1. Screw the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool onto the new LEMO Connector. Place a mark on the masking tape to indicate the location of the LEMO Connector Keyway. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Lexan Plenum Cover installed earlier.

  1. Install a new Turret O-Ring (46-281101P6) on the Vertical Penetration Flange for the new Instrumentation Lead Assembly. Use vacuum grease when installing the new O-ring.

  1. Warm the insertion end of the Instrumentation Lead Assembly and the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool to remove any moisture.

  1. Insert the new Instrumentation Lead Assembly into the Penetration Flange.

NOTICE
Before connecting the Instrumentation Lead's LEMO Connector (P101) to J101, make sure there is no ice build-up on J101. Study the location of the keyways on the Connectors P101 and J101 for easy installation.
 
 

  1. Secure the new Instrumentation Lead Assembly to the Penetration Flange with the four bolts removed earlier.

  1. Shine a flashlight into the Vertical Penetration. Position the Instrumentation Lead Connector, using the previously attached Connector Insertion/Removal Tool, at the bottom of the Vertical Penetration and insert into J101 until properly seated. (See the illustration below for keyway alignment.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Unscrew the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool from the Instrumentation Lead Connector and remove.

  1. Apply vacuum grease to the new Turret O-Ring (46-281101P6), quickly replace the O-ring, and mount the Plenum Assembly with its new Instrumentation Lead Assembly onto the Vertical Penetration Flange using the bolts removed earlier.

  1. Install a new Instrumentation Lead Connector Port O-Ring (46-281101P9) and the Feed-Thru Assembly removed earlier. Tighten the compression nut.

  1. Clean the threads of the removed Male Connector, and wrap with Teflon Tape. Install the Male Connector on the new Instrumentation Lead Assembly and tighten until snug.

  1. Attach the 0.25 in. (6 mm) Instrumentation Vent Line removed previously.

  1. Reconnect the union ball joint.

  1. Reconnect the Vent Adaptor to the Plenum Assembly with the bolts removed previously.

  1. Remove the Lexan Top Cover that was installed.

  1. Prepare the Shim Lead Assembly by replacing the old O-ring with a new Shim Lead O-Ring (46-281101P5). Use vacuum grease when installing the O-ring.

NOTICE
Carefully lower the Shim Lead Assembly into the Vertical Penetration to prevent damage to the baffles.

  • The Shim Lead Assembly must be in the Disengaged position.

  • Check for ice build-up on the mating Shim Lead Assembly Connector J100 before installing the Shim Lead Assembly.

 
 

  1. Immediately install the Shim Lead Assembly into the Vertical Penetration with the scribe mark on the Shim Lead Cover Plate aligned with the Vertical Penetration Exhaust Plenum. Secure with the eight 1/4-20 screws and washers removed previously.

  1. Reconnect the 0.25 in. (6 mm) Exhaust Plumbing to side of the Shim Lead Receptacle Box.

  1. Leak test the Instrumentation Lead and Shim Lead Assembly mountings and all plumbing connections using Snoop Liquid Leak Detector (46-252065P71). Correct any leaks found.

  1. Reconnect P1-C to J1-C and perform the checks as outlined in Temperature Sensor Checks.

  1. Engage the Shim Lead Assembly in conformance with Shim Lead Engagement and Disengagement.

  1. Ramp the magnet back up to field in conformance with Ramping Magnet to Field.

  1. Disengage the Shim Lead Assembly in conformance with Shim Lead Engagement and Disengagement.

NOTICE
Perform all MRU checks in Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Checks upon completion of Instrumentation Lead Assembly replacement to make sure no Instrumentation Lead or LEMO connector damage has occurred. Correct any damage found by reconnecting the LEMO connector or replacing the Instrumentation Lead Assembly.
 
 

  1. Check the Instrumentation Lead and LEMO connector for damage by performing the MRU checks in Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Checks.
