NOTE:The ICN(s) are powered down if the green disk drive LEDs on the front of the ICN(s) are no longer illuminated. The Power LEDs will continue to blink even after power-down.(see Illustration 7).

NOTE:To power on the ICNs from the xterm command line, type: /etc/init.d/poweron_vres start and press <Enter>.
