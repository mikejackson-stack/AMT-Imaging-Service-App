2  Overview

A Teslameter Probe and Teslameter or a Probe Array and shimming software is used whenever the strength of the magnet's magnetic field must be measured, monitored or mapped. A single Teslameter Probe accurately positioned at the magnet's iso-center may be used whenever a measurement of the magnet's overall magnetic field strength is required. A Probe Array must be used to map the magnet's magnetic field strength at a large number of angles within a spherical volume during magnet shimming. Use this section to set up a Teslameter, a single Teslameter Probe (used both with and without LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758, as appropriate), a Probe Array (always mounted to LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758) and LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758, as well as to gain access to the LCC300's passive shim trays.

NOTE:Included in this section are:

  • Preparations for Field Monitoring, Preparation for Field Monitoring, to set-up for single-point field measuring/monitoring of overall magnetic field strength;

  • Preparations for Field Mapping, Preparation for Field Mapping, to assemble and install LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758 with either a Probe Array (for field mapping) or the Single Probe Mounting Board (for field strength measurement and polarity checks) and to gain access to the LCC300's shim trays for passive shimming;

  • Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization, Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization, to set-up and resynchronize the Teslameter and Teslameter Probe or Probe Array whenever they are used; and

  • After Field Mapping, After Field Mapping, to uninstall and disassemble LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758 and return the gradient and end bell to operating configuration after field mapping and passive shimming.

NOTE:If LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758 is available, install it in conformance with Preparations for Field Mapping, Preparation for Field Mapping, before ramping the magnet to field. The mapping fixture includes a single probe mounting board for use during ramping. Both the mapping fixture and LCC300 Shim Camera Kit 2386028 are required for shimming.

4  Procedure

4.1  Preparations for Field Monitoring

When a measurement of overall magnetic field strength is required, only a single probe accurately placed at the magnet's iso-center is necessary. If field mapping will be performed soon after field monitoring ― and LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758 is available ― set up for field mapping in conformance with Preparations for Field Mapping, Preparation for Field Mapping, before ramping and use the mapping fixture's single probe mounting board during ramping.

NOTE:LCC300 Field Mapping Fixture 2380758 and LCC300 Shim Camera Kit 2386028 are required for shimming.

  1. Mount a #6 Teslameter Probe horizontally on a cardboard box or non-ferromagnetic frame. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Teslameter Probe Set-Up for Field Monitoring

  1. Position the box or frame along the magnet's Z-Axis half way between the bore's patient and service ends.

  1. Verify using measurements that the Probe is centered on the magnet's X, Y and Z Axes.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Teslameter can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Teslameter is disconnected, follow lockout/tagout procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Teslameter.

  1. Make sure input power to the Teslameter is disconnected. Lockout/tagout Teslameter input power. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Connect the probe to the Teslameter and set-up the Teslameter in conformance with Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization, Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization.

  1. Remove lockout/tagout and connect input power to the Teslameter.

4.3  Teslameter Adjustment and Resynchronization

4.3.1  Teslameter Set-Up

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Teslameter can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Teslameter is disconnected, follow lockout/tagout procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Teslameter.

  1. Make sure input power to the Teslameter is disconnected. Lockout/tagout Teslameter input power. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Only use non-ferromagnetic tools & equipment when working in the magnet's 3.0T magnetic field.

  1. Connect the white wire from the Preamp Box to the Teslameter Probe's left input jack and the black wire from the Preamp Box to the right, lacing connector end of the Probe. See Illustration 17.

    Illustration 17:  Teslameter Interconnections

  1. Connect the Preamp Box to the two-probe connection input plug on the Teslameter.

  1. Verify using measurements that the Teslameter Probe is at the physical center of the magnet bore (R = 0 and Z = 0).

  1. Remove lockout/tagout and connect input power to the Teslameter.

  1. Turn on AC power to the Teslameter. See Illustration 18.

    Illustration 18:  Teslameter Front and Back Views

NOTICE
If the Manual/Auto Switch is not in its “Manual” position when magnetic field search begins during ramping, its sweep will be in the ± 1 Tesla range and the system could lock on to the mechanical oscillation harmonic of the Shield Cooler Coldhead, resulting in an erroneous reading.
 
 

  1. Place the Manual/Auto Switch in the “Manual" position.

  1. Set the Coarse and Fine Field Search controls fully counterclockwise (CCW).

  1. Set the Freq./Field Switch to the “Field” position.

    NOTE:

    • The Teslameter is now set up and prepared to start monitoring magnetic field strength.

    • The Teslameter will not lock in on the probe's signal until the magnetic field is ≈1.0 Tesla, depending on the probe being used and the magnet's magnetic field strength.

4.3.2  Teslameter Adjustment for Ramp Up

  1. Once ramping has started, monitor the Power Supply's Current Meter until an indication of ≈120 amps is approached, then start monitoring the Teslameter.

  1. As the developing magnetic field approaches the lower limit of the Teslameter probe (#6 Probe: ≈1.0 Tesla), the “Strobe Lock” LED will start blinking. When this LED goes out, reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to the “Lock" position. See Illustration 18.

    NOTE:If the signal does not lock in, reposition of the Probe Modulation Switch.

  1. As magnetic field continues to increase, the "HI" LED above the Coarse Field Search control will illuminate. Turn the Coarse Field Search control clockwise (CW) until the LED is extinguished.

  1. As magnetic field increases, the resonant frequency of the probe sample will increase above the Teslameter's range setting. Periodically increase the setting of the Coarse Field Search control to keep the Teslameter locked on the probe sample.

    NOTE:The Fine Field Search control does not function when the Manual/Auto Switch is in the “Locked” position.

4.3.3  Teslameter Adjustment for Rampdown

  1. Increase the Coarse Field Search control setting until a magnetic field reading of ≈ 3.0 Tesla is obtained. This should occur near but slightly below the actual magnetic field.

  1. Slowly start increasing the Fine Field Search control. When the “Strobe Lock” LED stops blinking and remains out, reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to the “Locked" position.

    NOTE:If the signal will not lock on, reposition of the Probe Modulation Switch.

  1. Once the Teslameter is locked onto the magnetic field, either the “LO" or “HI" LED will illuminate. Turn the Coarse Field Search control counterclockwise (CCW) if the “LO” LED is lit or clockwise (CW) if the “HI” LED is lit. Slowly the illuminated LED will go out and stay out.

  1. As the magnetic field decreases, the resonant frequency of the probe sample will decrease below Teslameter's range setting. Periodically decrease the Coarse Field Search control to keep the Teslameter locked on the probe sample.

    NOTE:The Fine Field Search control does not function when the Manual/Auto Switch is in the “Locked” position.

4.3.4  Teslameter Resynchronization

If the Teslameter should go out of sync while ramping the magnet up (or down), it can easily be resynchronized by the following procedure.

  1. Manual Resynchronization

    1. Reposition the Manual/Auto Switch to “Manual".

      NOTE:The “Strobe Lock” LED will be on and both the “LO" and “HI" LEDs will be oscillating, indicating a search mode.

    1. Read the Main Power Supply's Current Meter. Multiply the meter's present current reading by 20 gauss (since there's ≈ 20 gauss/amp). Reset the Current Meter to this calculated value.

    1. Slowly start increasing (if ramping up) the Coarse and/or Fine Field Search control while monitoring the “Strobe Lock” LED.

    1. Once the “Strobe Lock” LED illuminate extinguishes, quickly place the Manual/Auto Switch to the “Locked" position. The meter will now be synchronized.

      NOTE:If the “HI" LED is illuminated, the Coarse Field Search control will have to be turned clockwise (CW) until the LED illumination ceases. Repeat this adjustment as required until the parking field Is reached.

  1. Manual Resynchronization Using an Oscilloscope

    NOTE:An oscilloscope can be set up near the Teslameter to display and trigger on the Field Modulation signal from a jack on the Teslameter's front panel. Adjust the time base to display one or two ramp waveforms. On the second channel, display the “NMR Signal" from the Teslameter's front panel.

    1. Leave the Teslameter's Manual/Auto Switch in the “Locked" position.

    1. Slowly turn the Coarse Field Search control in the direction the magnetic field is going (i.e., if the magnetic field is increasing, turn the control towards the higher numbers; if the magnetic field is decreasing, turn the control towards the lower numbers).

      NOTE:As the meter is approaching the actual magnetic field, the baseline of the "FID" display will start to wander. Once the meter is in range of the magnetic field, the "FID" will appear on the scope trace as the meter locks on.

    1. When locked on, “Strobe Lock” LED will not be illuminated. Slightly readjust the Coarse Field Search control counterclockwise (if the “LO” LED is illuminated) or clockwise (if the “HI” LED is illuminated) until the illuminated LED ceases to be illuminated.

    1. Maintain tracking through end of ramp sequence.

4.4  After Field Mapping

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Only use non magnetic tools & equipment when working in the magnet's 3.0T field.

  1. Turn off power to the field camera at the Field Camera Power Supply.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Teslameter can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Teslameter is disconnected, follow lockout/tagout procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Teslameter.

  1. Make sure input power to the Teslameter is disconnected. Lockout/tagout Teslameter input power. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Disconnect the cables shown in Illustration 16.

  1. Dismount the mapping fixture's shaft from the support brackets. See Illustration 12. Remove the shaft from the magnet's bore.

  1. Dismount the Probe Array (Illustration 9) or the #6 Probe and Single Probe Mounting Board (Illustration 10 and Illustration 11) from the mapping fixture's shaft.

  1. Dismount the mapping fixture's patient end support bracket. See Illustration 8.

  1. Dismount the mapping fixture's service end support bracket. See Illustration 6.

  1. Reinstall the two trim cover cap screws removed from the service end bell earlier.

  1. If passive shimming has been performed:

    1. Reinstall the Support Mounting Bracket using M10 x 75 mm hex head screws. See Illustration 19.

      Illustration 19:  Reinstall Support Mounting Bracket

    1. Reinstall the Rubber Isolation Pads and G10 Fiberglass Shim Strips centered between the Support Mounting Bracket and the TRM Support Extension. See Illustration 19.

    1. Return both jack screws to the marks on their threads made in Magnet Preparation, Step 5. This should take the same number of turns as was used to raise the gradient.

    1. Remove the jack screws and jack pads.

    1. Reinstall the TRM Upper Radial Support Pads using its original M12 nuts and M12 socket head set screws. See Illustration 20. Adjust the TRM Upper Radial Support Pads as necessary until the gap around the gradient's outer diameter varies no more than 1 mm, then tighten the M12 screws.

      Illustration 20:  Reinstall TRM Upper Radial Support Pads

  1. Clean the patient end o-ring using isopropyl alcohol, then coat the o-ring with vacuum grease and reinstall the o-ring in the o-ring groove. See Illustration 21.

    Illustration 21:  Reinstall Patient End O-Ring

  1. Reinstall the front end bell in conformance with the procedure in the Service Methods documentation for the magnet/system/enclosures.

  1. Reinstall the split bridge in conformance with the procedure in the Service Methods documentation for the magnet/system/enclosures.

  1. Reinstall the patient table in conformance with the procedure in the Service Methods documentation for the magnet/system/enclosures, if previously installed.
