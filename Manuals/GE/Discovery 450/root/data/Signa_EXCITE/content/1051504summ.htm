1  Procedure

1.1   Confirm the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) Voltage Selection

The Main Disconnect Panel is shipped with the default voltage setting of 480 Vac.

  1. Confirm that the control, power transformer connections are in the correct configuration for your site.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the power connections are not configured properly, have the proper personnel correct the wiring. See MDP Operation and Maintenance Manual for additional details.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Confirm Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) Jumper Interlock Has Been Removed

The Main Disconnect Panel is shipped with the E-off jumper installed.

  1. Confirm that the E-off jumper(s) have/has been removed. Remote E-off push buttons replace the jumper(s).

    (Step Details)

  1. If the jumper is not removed, have the proper personnel correct the wiring. See MDP Operation and Maintenance Manual for additional details.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Confirm Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) Voltage Selection

  1. Follow the table below to confirm the transformer switch is in the proper position. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the switch is not in the correct position, change the switch position.

    (Step Details)

1.4   Confirm Internal Power Wiring to the Power Distribution Unit (PDU)

The PDU is shipped with the default voltage setting of 480 Vac.

  1. Remove the small cover plate which is held in place by two screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm that the three Input Voltage Selection Terminal Blocks are wired in the correct configuration. If the power connections are not configured properly, ensure that the proper personnel correct the wiring. See PDU Operation and Maintenance Manual for additional details.

    (Step Details)

1.5   Confirm Overload and Short Circuit Trip Settings for the PDU Input Circuit Breaker

Note: Overload and Short Circuit Trip Settings only apply to pre-DVapps PDU 5140621. The overload and short circuit trip settings for the Input Circuit Breaker must be set to the correct values corresponding to the input voltage.

  1. Remove the small cover plate under the main circuit breaker.

  1. Lift open the transparent plastic cover on the lower portion of the circuit breaker to access the DIP switches controlling the circuit breaker.

  1. Confirm that the switches immediately under the letter “L” are configured correctly. The illustration below shows the position of the DIP switches for each input voltage.

    (Step Details)

  1. If the DIP switches are not configured correctly, ensure that proper personnel correct the DIP switches settings per the PDU Operation and Maintenance Manual
