Rear Cosmetic Cover Removal and Installation

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hourNot Applicable

2  Overview

Removing the Rear Cosmetic Cover is needed when replacing the gradient coil, Rear Bell, Split Bridge, and Busbar.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

ItemQtyEffectivityPart#Manufacturer

3.2  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

4  Procedure

  1. Remove Rear Pedestal cover.

  1. Remove two (2) screws securing Hose Cover and remove cover. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Remove Hose Cover

NOTE:If removing the left side rear cosmetic cover, remove hose from the top and bottom section of the cosmetic cover.

  1. Remove the applicable rear cosmetic cover. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Rear Cosmetic Covers

  1. The cosmetic covers are held on by four (4) screws each (see Illustration 2).

  1. Pull cover away from magnet to release the cover from the poppers. See Illustration 3. In this case the right cosmetic cover is removed.

    Illustration 3:  Remove Rear Cosmetic Cover

  1. To install the Cosmetic Cover reverse the removal procedures.

No finalization steps.
