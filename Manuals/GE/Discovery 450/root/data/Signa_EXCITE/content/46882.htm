2  Overview

Tests in this section allow the service engineer to selectively force body mode peak power, pulse width, and duty cycle beyond allowable limits.

3.2  Safety

CAUTION
PERSONAL INJURY!
POSSIBLE RF BURNS.
PREVENT POSSIBLE RF BURNS WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM J3 OR J4 ON THE RFI or SRFD2 BY VERIFYING THAT THE SYSTEM IS NOT MANUALLY PRESCANNING OR SCANNING. VERIFY THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE.

3.3  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Perform the prerequisite calibration procedures in the sequence listed. 1a: Dummy Load & Cables Calibration (Do if NOT using the RF Power Measurement Kit)-- Calibration required whenever using the dummy load and cables. This value will be frequency specific and can change over time.

-

-

1b: Scope characterization (Do if your are NOT using the RF Power Measurement Kit) This should be done for scopes with bandwidths less than 300 MHz.

-

-

2: SRFD2 Max Power RF Out Setup and Calibration or SRF Max Power RF Out Setup and Calibration at initial installation or when the RFI, UCERD Exciter, or RF Amps are repaired or replaced.

-

-

4  Procedure

  1. Confirm that all the steps in Initial Hardware Preparation have been done.

    NOTE:PROPERTY DAMAGE! PREVENT COIL AND ASSOCIATED SWITCH DAMAGE, BY REMOVING ALL PHANTOMS AND HARDWARE (I.E., HEAD COIL, SURFACE COIL...) FROM THE MAGNET BORE.

  1. Verify that the system is not scanning and that all coils have been removed from the magnet bore. See the two DANGER messages on this page.

  1. If using the RF Power Measurement Kit then refer to the RF Power Measurement Kit laminated card set.

    1. Look in the upper, right corner of each card and find the card labeled 72 (1.5T Body Output).

      NOTE:The body RF output connection is no longer to the non-existent EFB unit, as the older reference cards from the kit show, but instead to the J4 output on the rear of the RFI or SRFD2. An RF adapter is provided in the RF Power Measurement Kit to connect between the HN J4 body RF output and the RF Power Measurement Kit 40 dB N-connector coupler.

    2. Configure the system as in the illustration on the card.

    3. Confirm that the rotary attenuator is set to the correct position indicated on the card.

  1. If using the wattmeter or scope (NOT the RF Power Measurement Kit) to measure power then refer to Alternate Equipment Setupfor the proper system body configuration.

  1. At operator workspace, select the scan desktop ICON in the desktop control panel, if you have not already done so.

  1. At the operator workspace, prepare the system for a Body Power Monitor scan using the "Service Protocols" procedure in Table 1 below. Refer to ALTERNATE EQUIPMENT SETUPS & NON-SERVICE PROTOCOLS for the non-proprietary protocol.

  1. Start at the first Test row in Table 5. Under the Scan Conditions column highlight or type-in the CVs into the CV Name box and then enter the listed corresponding number into the Current Value box. Right mouse-click on the remaining items listed in the column.

  1. Advance to the Adjustments column on the right and perform the directions listed in the column. Refer to the following steps for measuring the RF output power.

  1. If using the RF Power Measurement Kit refer to card 72 (1.5T Body Output) and note the Attenuation value listed on the calibration sticker affixed to the bottom, right of the card.

    1. Measure the amplitude of the RF waveform from the scope face in divisions.

    2. Mouse click on the ToolBelt Icon and then [Utilities], [RF Calculator], [Start].

    3. Use the RF Calculator program to determine the power output from the amplifier. Enter the actual reference and measured values in place of the sample values in the example below.

    4. Note the amplifier output power calculated by the RF Calculator program. Continue with the Table instructions.
