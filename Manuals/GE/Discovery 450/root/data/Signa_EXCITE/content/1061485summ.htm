LPCA Cable Take-up Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
The cable connectors on both tracks contain ferrous material.
Adhere to all ge magnet safety guidelines when working close to the magnet bore.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal of P1/P2 Cable Take-Up

When looking at the LPCA from the front, the left track contains the P1 and P2 coil connectors (see Table 1for all cable types) and the right track contains the A connector (see Table 2for all cable types). Each track contains cables that are different from each other, which translates into slight differences in how you remove and install a replacement cable take-up.

  1. Perform Lock-out Tag-out for rear pedestal and System Shutdown. For more information, see LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics) and System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Unlatch the LPCA from the cradle, and then drive the LPCA through the magnet bore, resting approximately one foot from the end of the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove both rear pedestal side covers.

  1. Remove the P1 and P2 Emergency Release Modules. Use sufficient force to pull the module forward so it slides off its track.

    (Step Details)

  1. To create sufficient space for removing and replacing P1/P2 cable connectors, do the following:

    1. At the rear of each cable mount bracket, remove two screws from the left side and three screws from the right side, and then set the cable connector aside.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove both bottom cable mount bracket screws, and then set the cable mount bracket aside. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four slotted brass screws from the front and back of the both baluns.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head brass screws that secure the cable take-up lead to the base of the LPCA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head screws that secure the cable take-up lead to the front of the rear pedestal bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cable connections that feed from the front end of the cable track to points throughout the rear pedestal. See Table 1 below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Feed loose end of cable connections up through the opening at the front of the rear pedestal Bridge and let them lay freely on the bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Starting at the rear of the LPCA, pull cable connectors through cable well of bridge, under the LPCA, and out of the back of rear pedestal. Allow the cable take-up to hang freely.

  1. Starting from the front of the LPCA, pull the cable take-up (the side that includes the baluns) through the opening on the LPCA in a forward direction as shown in Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Removal of A-Port Connector (Functions) Cable Take-up

When looking at the LPCA from the front, the right track contains the A connector, cables and various other functions including the limit switch microphone cable, etc., and the left track contains the P1 and P2 coil connectors. Each track contains cables that are different from each other, which translates into slight differences in how you remove and install a replacement cable take-up.

  1. Perform Lock-out Tag-out for rear pedestal and System Shutdown. For more information, see LOTO for XFRD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics) and System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Unlatch the LPCA from the cradle, and then drive the LPCA through the magnet bore, resting approximately one foot from the end of the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove both rear pedestal side covers.

  1. Remove the P1 and P2 Emergency Release Modules. Use sufficient force to pull the module forward so it slides off its track. See Illustration 2.

  1. To create sufficient space for removing and replacing the A-Port Connector (Functions) Cable Take-up, remove the two slotted brass screws from the bottom of the P2 cable mount bracket as shown in Illustration 4and set it aside.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four slotted brass screws from (right side) P2 balun and then set it aside.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the LED & Switch Harness connector.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Microphone cable from the rear of the Mic.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four slotted brass screws from top of LPCA bulkhead plate. Be careful while maneuvering wires out of the way when removing screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the bottom of the bulkhead plate, remove the J1 connector first. Tilt the plate up to allow easier access to remove the retaining screws of J1.

  1. With the plate still tilted, remove the remaining connectors (J2, J4, and J5) from the bottom of the plate.

  1. Remove the two (2) slotted head brass screws that secure the cable take-up lead to the base of the LPCA.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the back of the LPCA, pull the cable take-up through the cable frame (opening) of the LPCA Assembly and let it hang loose.

  1. Remove the two (2) standard head screws that secure the cable take-up lead to the front of the rear pedestal bridge. See Illustration 7.

  1. Disconnect all cable connections that feed from the front end of the cable track to points throughout the rear pedestal. See the table below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Feed loose end of cable connectors up through the opening at the front of the rear pedestal bridge and let it lay freely on bridge.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front of the rear pedestal bridge, pull the cable take-up through the cable well until the cable clears the underside of the LPCA.

1.3   Installation of New Cable take-up

  1. Move replacement cable take-up into position.

  1. To ensure proper installation and reconnection of cables, follow the Removal procedures above (Section 4.1 or 4.2) in reverse order.

  1. Apply two drops of Loctite to each screw then connect the four screws that secure take-up assembly to Bridge and LPCA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Lockout Tagout (For more information see LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics), restore power and start up the software (For more information see System Startup and Shutdown.

  1. Return LPCA to home position.

  1. Run the Coil Datapath Diagnostics) tool.
