ICW PAC Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Insert the Service Key.

  1. Launch the Service Browser (Common Service Desktop).

  1. Click on the Calibration Tab.

    (Step Details)

  1. PAC Checks is one of the final steps after Install In Spec (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Check the Peripheral Pulse Probe (see Peripheral Pulse Probe Check). When this is complete, check the box next to “Is Peripheral Pulse Probe Check completed?”

  1. Check the ECG Leads (see ECG Leads Installation and Functional Checks) that have been ordered by the customer. When this is complete, check the box next to “Is ECG Leads checked”

  1. Perform Respiratory Compensation Check (see Respiratory Compensation Checks). When this is complete, check the box next to “Is Respiratory Checks Done?”

  1. Click [Submit].

2  Finalization

  1. Return to the Install and Calibration Wizard for information regarding how to advance to the next calibration and for links to other calibration procedures.
