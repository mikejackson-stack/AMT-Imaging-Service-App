2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in the system, refer to Auto Coil Installation.

  1. Place the Posterior Coil on the table as shown, using the long patient pad between the table end and the posterior coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Phantom onto the posterior coil, then guide the phantom into position by lining up the locator pins on the phantom with the locator holes in coil as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Anterior Coil on top of the phantom, and use the patient straps to secure in place as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark by aligning the laser light with the coil crosshairs as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool to test the coil.

2.2   Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the TL Unified Phantoms over the posterior section so the phantom set is well centered in the S/I and R/L directions as shown in both illustrations below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the anterior section onto the phantom set so the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Fasten the Velcro® straps to firmly snug the coil to the phantom as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system's LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the crosshairs as shown below, and [Advance to Scan].

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool to test the coil.
