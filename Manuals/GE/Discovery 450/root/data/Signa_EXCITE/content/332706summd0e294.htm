RuO resistance should be 1,450 ohms - 1,325 ohms when the liquid helium pressure is 3 psig or less.

NOTE:Significant differences between readings in Step 3 or Step 4 or out of the specified range in Step 5 indicate a resistance short or open problem. A fully open sensor will read “000K," a fully shorted sensor will read “300K" on the portable temperature monitor.
