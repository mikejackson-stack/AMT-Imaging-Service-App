2267943

The Z axis XGA is reporting a power off fault

Check EMO reset and perform a TPS reset. If this error occurs again, contact your service representative.
NOTE:Notice that all axes are reporting power loss, however, visual inspection of all units shows that they have power. Common factor that would result in all 3 reporting power loss is an unstable 48 V PS2 power supply operation. The PS2 power supply is located inside the PDU. Check for the presence of the green PS2 LED (you should see two LEDs: the upper is PS1, the lower is PS2) by viewing through the vent holes located above the magnet power supply connection on the front faceplate of the PDU. A dark LED means that the PS2 unit must be replaced. See the PDU vendor doc replacement section for more details.

2267857 1

A gradient distortion fault has occurred on the X axis

Error indicates that small signal input to XGA does not match large signal output by XGA.
  • Look for other errors prior to this one and occurring at about the same time that might explain why this error occurred.
  • There are many reasons why this error would appear alone in error log and not in presence of any others. List of possible reasons in order of most to least probable include:
    • Faulty XGA or XPS fiber-optic transceiver module.
    • Faulty XGA (likely) or XPS (much less likely).
    • Poor gradient coil circuit connections.
    • Poor calibration - usually only seen during installation or troubleshooting (like when XGAs swapped).
  • Faulty XGA or XPS fiber-optic transceiver module (SFP):Refer to the information in Step  or Step  for the process to troubleshoot a defective SFP module.
  • Faulty XGA:
    • Swap fiber optic cables from failing XGA module to non-failing XGA.
    • Repeat failing sequence (scan, gradient diagnostic) and see if problem moves.
    • Problem moves: Try swapping suspect bad XGA to another axis and see if problem follows XGA.
    • Problem moves with XGA: Replace XGA.
    • Problem does not move: Calibration or circuit connections appear suspect. Proceed to next section.
    • Problem does not move: Proceed on to next troubleshooting steps as XGA appears OK.
  • Faulty XPS:
    • Swap both black power cable lines between XPS on failing axis and XPS on another axis. See Gradient Troubleshooting service methods document if details for doing this needed.
    • Repeat failing sequence (scan, gradient diagnostic) and see if problem moves.
    • Problem moves: Replace XPS.
    • Problem does not move: Proceed on to next troubleshooting steps as XPS appears OK.
  • Poor gradient coil connections:Inspect all circuit connections starting at XGA terminal lugs and check every connection in path out to bus bar connections on rear of XRMB.
  • Poor calibration – perform a saveINFO and then perform Grafidy.
