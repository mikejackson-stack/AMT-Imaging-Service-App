LOTO for the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy:

  • Internal to the MDP: Instantaneous

  • Auxiliary equipment in other cabinets: 5 minutes

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnection(s)

A personal lock and tag per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the Operator Workspace, shut down the Host PC. See “Shutting Down the Host PC” in System Startup and Shutdown.

NOTE:Observe Power On light on the MDP. If the light is on, it signifies the main breaker is closed and control power is on.

  1. Locate and depress the System Off button on the MDP which feeds the PGR Cabinet and the HEC Cabinet. See illustrations below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the Main Input Switch (labeled with “LOTO Lock” in Illustration 1and Illustration 2) to the OFF position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY
Top of the main breaker will still have high voltage after LOTO has been applied.

NOTE:Observe Power On light on the MDP. The light should now be off. Do not assume that because the light is off that power is off. You must perform the next step to measure the voltage as a confirmation.

  1. Open MDP door and check that all voltage has dissipated. At the output of the main breaker, use a voltmeter to measure the voltage between:

    (Step Details)

  1. Lock Out/Tag Out the main incoming switch on the MDP. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for reenergizing the equipment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the MDP.

  1. Switch on the Main Input Switch (labeled with “LOTO Lock” in Illustration 1 and Illustration 2) to the ON position.

  1. On the MDP, check the breaker. If it is in a middle position, reset it by cycling to the OFF position then back to the ON position.

  1. On the MDP, push the green System On button shown in Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

  1. On the PDU, press the EMO RESET button (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Power up the Host PC. See System Startup and Shutdown.
