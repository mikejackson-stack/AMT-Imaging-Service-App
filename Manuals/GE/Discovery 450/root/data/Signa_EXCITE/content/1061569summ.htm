Dock Motor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THIS PROCEDURE REQUIRES movement OF FERROUS MATERIAL IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO THE MAGNET.
Therefore, two Field engineers are required at all times.

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage
Strong Magnetic Field
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements

  • THE SERVICE PATH MUST ALLOW FOR THE DOCK TO BE MOVED ALONG THE GROUND A DISTANCE EQUAL TO THAT OF THE DISTANCE BETWEEN THE MAGNET AND THE FOOT-END OF THE PATIENT TABLE PRIOR TO BEING LIFTED VERTICALLY. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to/from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Dock Motor

  1. Remove the Dock from the Magnet Room, see Dock Removal and Reinstallation.

  1. Using a 3/4 inch wrench, remove four bolts (2 on each side) to unfasten the dock cover from the dock base.

    (Step Details)

  1. While removing the dock cover disconnect the switch connector.

  1. Carefully remove the dock cover from the dock base and then turn the dock cover upside down.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a standard head screwdriver to disconnect the dock motor power cable, terminal wires, and ground wire.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 3/16 inch ball-end hex key, remove 4 screws (2 each side) from the dock motor mount plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove dock motor.

NOTICE
DO NOT DISCARD OLD DOCK MOTOR. YOU ARE GOING TO REUSE THE DOCK GEARBOX SHAFT FROM THE OLD DOCK MOTOR.
 
 

  1. Go to Installing the Replacement Dock Motor for instructions on how to reuse the coupling and install the new dock motor.

1.2   Installing the Replacement Dock Motor

  1. Using the blade of a slotted screwdriver, remove the e-clip from the end of the dock gearbox shaft on the old dock motor. Secure the e-clip during removal, to prevent the clip from flying away when pressured. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the dock gearbox shaft and key out of the dock motor and set it aside. Remove four screws and mounting plate. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the new dock motor, use Loctite 242 Threadlocker on the hex screws as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the Mount Plate on the dock motor and fasten with four screws using a 5 mm hex key.

  1. If replacing the shaft only, apply a small amount of anti-seize (from shaft assembly FRU kit) to the shaft key. If the complete Dock Motor Assembly is being replaced, this step is not required.

  1. Assemble the drive coupling on new shaft, then slide the new gearbox shaft, with spring and key, into the motor and replace the e-clip. See Illustration 5.

  1. Use Loctite 242 Threadlocker on the four Mount Plate screws, position the dock motor onto the dock cover and then re-attach the dock motor. See Illustration 3.

  1. Reconnect the terminal wires, ground wire, and power cable to original terminal strips. See Illustration 2.

  1. Reconnect the switch connector.

  1. Place the dock cover onto the dock base and reattach dock cover bolts. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the dock. Refer to Dock Removal and Reinstallation
