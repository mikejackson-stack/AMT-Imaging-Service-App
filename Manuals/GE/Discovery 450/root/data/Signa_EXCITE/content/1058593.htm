Replacement of SRI-4 (Scan Room Interface)

2  Overview

Procedure for replacement of the SRI (Scan Room Interface) assembly on a fixed site system.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

ItemQtyEffectivityPart#Manufacturer
Nonferrous 19mm Hex Head Wrench1---

3.2  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages are Present around the Magnet Enclosure and Rear Pedestal electronics.
Remove Power to The Magnet Enclosure and lockout/tagout.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal of SRI

  1. Turn off power to the PDU by following LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. The SRI is located under the left side of the magnet enclosure.

    Illustration 1:  Location of SRI

  1. Remove the left skirt covers (see Skirt Cover Removal and Install) to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI (see Illustration 2)

    Illustration 2:  SRI Tray

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the SRI.

  1. The tray is held in place by one hex head screw on each slide. Remove the two (2) screws and slide tray out for access to the SRI board.

    Illustration 3:  Screw Location

  1. Velcro holds the SRI to tray. Lift SRI off tray and remove.

4.2  Installation of New SRI

  1. Place new SRI in the proper location and install in the reverse order as it was removed.

  1. Reconnect the cables.

  1. Slide tray under magnet and reinstall the two (2) hex head screws.

  1. Replace enclosure trim panels.

  1. Remove Lockout / Tagout hardware.

  1. Restore power.

5  Finalization

  1. Perform good bye scan. Use “test” as patient ID, as this will verify proper operation of Coil-ID.
