NOTE:

  • If Dewar pressure does not vent down to 1 psig, verify that the Dewar pressure relief valve is left in the open position. Contact the cryogen supplier immediately.

  • The Pressure Relief Valve is normally open during shipping and storage to prevent excessive build up of pressure in the Dewar. Therefore, always reopen the Pressure Relief Valve after using a Dewar.

  • If 99.999% Helium Gas is used (five nines certified gas), the purity of the gas remaining in the cylinder will degrade as a result of this process (i.e., the purity of the remaining gas will be something less than 99.999%).
