2  Overview

This procedure documents how to replace an eXtreme Gradient Amplifier (XGA) unit in the PGR cabinet. This unit is about 200 lbs. so the use of a hoist kit is required. This procedure details the correct way to use a hoist kit to remove the XGA unit and replace with the XGA field replaceable unit (FRU) safely.

4  Procedure

  1. Perform the LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Before setting up the hoist kit, remove the XPS J9 connector to properly remove the main lifting beam.

    Illustration 1:  Lifting Beam and Connector in PGR Cabinet

  1. Perform the Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories procedure to set up and secure the hoist.

  1. Turn off pump.

NOTE:Prior to the next step, prepare to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is disconnected.

  1. Turn off the Pump breaker , and then disconnect the cables and hoses from the front of the XGA unit. This includes the XPS to XGA power cables, the coolant lines, and the fiber optic cables.

    NOTE:Do not bend the fiber optic loopback cables. These are very delicate cables and can break easily.

    Illustration 2:  Front of XGA Unit - Cables Connected

  1. Remove the nut and washer on the Plus connector at the top of the PGR Cabinet.

    Illustration 3:  PGR Cabinet - Plus Connector

  1. Unscrew the two screws from the bottom module retaining bracket of the XGA unit. The two screws are captive screws and will not fully detach from the bracket. Remove the module retaining bracket, when XGA is extended out and access to the three screws is available, and set this aside as you will need this module retaining bracket for the FRU.

    Illustration 4:  Module Retaining Bracket

    NOTE:If replacing the X-axis XGA unit, you may need to remove the PGR Cabinet door and lean it to the side of the cabinet.

  1. Carefully slide out the XGA unit. Pull on the plus connector cable and front handle until the top of the XGA is completely exposed, but do not fully slide the unit out.

  1. Use two wrenches to disconnect the J17 minus cable. One wrench holds the bottom nut and the other wrench removes the top nut (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  Disconnecting the Minus Cable

  1. Place the lifting bracket from the FRU crate on top of the XGA. The attachment screws are stored on the bracket. Match the four openings and screw in the lifting bracket.

    Illustration 6:  Lifting Bracket

    NOTE:Make sure to use a washer with each attachment screw.

  1. Hook the winch from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.

    Illustration 7:  XGA Unit - Connected to Hoist Kit

    NOTE:Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.

  1. With the XGA unit being held by the slide rails and the hoist kit, it is now secure enough to extend the unit until the slide rails lock (see Illustration 8). Press the release tabs in on each side as you pull the unit out further.

    Illustration 8:  XGA Unit - Extended Out of Cabinet

  1. Push the release mechanism on the back end of the outer slide rail to release the outer rail (see Illustration 9). Once the unit is removed from the outer rails, slide the outer rails into the cabinet. The cabinet rails are a reverse image of each other.

    NOTE:You may want to use a screwdriver to release the outer slide rail.

    Illustration 9:  Slide Rail Latch Release

  1. The FRU is packaged in a wooden crate. Remove the top of the wooden crate and turn it on its wheels. The XGA unit being removed can be placed into the top. Roll the top underneath the XGA unit. Lock the wheels so that the container does not move.

    Illustration 10:  FRU Package

  1. Ratchet the winch to lower the XGA unit into the package.

  1. Once lowered, unscrew side rails from both sides of the XGA unit.

  1. Remove the hook from the lifting bracket.

  1. Unscrew the lifting bracket from the top of the unit. Roll the removed XGA unit to the side.

  1. Remove the center section of the FRU crate and set aside. This is used to package the XGA unit being removed.

  1. Bring the FRU to the front of the cabinet. Connect the slide rails to the FRU.

    Illustration 11:  XGA FRU

  1. Screw the lifting bracket to the top of the new unit.

  1. Hook the new XGA unit to the hoist kit.

    NOTE:Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.

  1. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chains and begin lifting the unit. Slide out the rail a short amount to gauge how high to lift the new XGA unit. The slide rails of the XGA unit and cabinet must align for correct insertion.

  1. When the slide rails are aligned, pull the slide rails out a short distance to get each side started. Once both of the slide rails are attached, pull the slide rails on to the FRU until you hear a click. This click is a signal that the unit is docked in the rails.

  1. Ensure the XGA unit is secure on the rails and slightly loosen the chains with the winch to allow some liberty of movement. Press in both release tabs and begin to slide the XGA unit in until only the top of the unit is exposed.

  1. Unhook the hoist kit from the lifting bracket. Remove the lifting bracket from the top of the new XGA unit.

    NOTE:Place the lifting bracket into the XGA FRU crate with the four screws attached to the lifting bracket.

  1. Attach the J17 minus cable using two wrenches (see Illustration 5).

  1. Tighten the module retaining bracket to the PGR cabinet.

  1. Reconnect the cables to the new XGA unit.

    The dual fiber optic loopback cable may be difficult to reinsert into the new XGA unit. Follow these steps if you are having difficulty connecting this cable:

    1. Remove sleeve from the XGA unit.

    2. Insert the dual fiber optic loopback cable into the sleeve by pushing the black section on the cable. You should feel the cable click into the sleeve.

      Illustration 12:  Black Section on Dual Fiber Optic Loopback Cable

    3. Insert the sleeve into the new XGA unit.

    NOTE:If the cable is inserted backwards, it will not go in all the way.

  1. Disassemble the hoist kit. Return the main lifting beam to the PGR cabinet. Once the beam is secure, reconnect the XPS J9 connector. Return the support tube to the HEC.

5  Finalization

  1. Restore the power using the Lockout/Tagout for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Turn on the PPMP CB at the HEC. Start the pump by pressing ▲ F3 simultaneously.

  1. Remove coolant from the XGA unit using the manual coolant removal tool (see Coolant Draining).

  1. Attach the center of the FRU package to the removed FRU unit base. Then attach the top of the FRU crate to prepare the FRU for return.
