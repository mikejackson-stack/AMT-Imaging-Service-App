XRMB Coil Replacement

1  Safety

DANGER
STRONG MAGNETIC FIELD!
FERROUS MATERIALS CAN BECOME DANGEROUS PROJECTILES IN THE PRESENCE OF THE MAGNETIC FIELD PRODUCED BY THE MAGNET.
DO NOT BRING ANY FERROMAGNETIC TOOLS OR EQUIPMENT INTO THE MAGNET ROOM.

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage
Strong Magnetic Field
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements

  • Movement of ferrous material in the magnet room must follow the GE service procedure for that device. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to and from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss.

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

WARNING
The jack on the Tube Jacking Assembly is ferrous.
Personal injury or equipment damage may result if taken too close to the magnet.
Keep as far away from magnet bore as possible when removing from magnet room. Always keep the tube jacking assembly mounted and screwed to the Aluminum coil cradle when used in the magnet room.

CAUTION
ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD!
CONTACT WITH CONNECTORS LEADING TO AN ENERGIZED POWER SUPPLY CAN CAUSE ELECTRICAL SHOCK.
FOLLOW LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURES WHILE PERFORMING SERVICES TO ELECTRICAL CONNECTION COMPONENTS.

CAUTION
ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD!
COOLANT CIRCULATING THROUGH THE GRADIENT COIL IS IN CONTACT WITH LIVE ELECTRICAL CONDUCTORS. USING WRONG TYPE OF COOLANT MAY RESULT IN ELECTRICAL SHOCK.
ALWAYS USE COOLANT SPECIFIED BY THE GRADIENT COIL SERVICE PROCEDURE.

CAUTION
Dangerous Work Area!
Use of heavy equipment for coil removal and replacement presents hazards to all personnel in the area.

  • Set up restricted barriers and signs limiting access to the work area.

  • Notify all effected personnel in the area prior to starting the service activity.

  • Review safety procedures and restricted areas for the job.

  • Personal Protective Equipment should be worn at all times.

3  Procedure

3.1   XRMB Coil Water Removal

  1. Verify that the coolant circulation pump is turned off and the LOTO procedures are implemented for the coolant line services. See LOTO for Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC).

  1. Turn off both PVC valves in XRMB coolant Supply (blue hose) and Return line (black hose).

  1. Obtain a bucket.

CAUTION
Cutting Hazard!
Use of a knife to cut a hose presents a hazard to all personnel in the area.
Use a nonferrous knife, make sure no personnel are in front of you, make sure fingers are clear of cutting area, and always cut away from your body.

NOTE:Do not cut the barb fittings out of the hoses unless the XRMB coil needs to be drained and returned. This process ensures there will be enough length of hose to reconnect the fitting to the valve.

The connection between hose and fitting is pressure only. To properly remove hose from fitting, carefully cut a slit on the hose but do not damage the water fitting; if coil needs to be drained, carefully make a slit on the manifold hose and slide it off the barb to disconnect the Supply Manifold from the fitting. Repeat for Return line. Must retain fitting at the site.

  1. To remove the XRMB manifold hoses from the PVC valves, use wrenches to unthread the barb fitting from the PVC valve.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
There is coolant water inside the Manifold. Keep the Manifold in upright position to prevent water spill.
 
 

  1. Slowly disconnect the Supply and then the Return Manifold from the valve. Drain fluid into bucket.

  1. Join the Supply and Return Manifold together using a zip tie; kink, if necessary. (This is to form a close loop of the coolant line so water will not spill out during the XRMB coil removal.)

3.2   Busbar Assembly Removal

  1. Refer to XRMB Busbar Removal for procedure.

NOTICE
The copper leads of the Busbar Assembly must be protected to avoid distortion. Place the Busbar at a location that is safe for the copper leads.

Do NOT lift the Busbar Assembly by holding the leads.

 
 

  1. Pull the Busbar Assembly off the Magnet and carefully place it in a safe location.

3.3   XRMB Gradient Coil Removal

  1. Obtain the two Tube Guide Roller Assemblies (5308402) and XRMB Mounting Plates (5161983, 5161985) from the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit's shipping crate. Make sure the Tube Guide Roller Assemblies are tightly attached to the XRMB Mounting Plates. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Patient End Mounting Plate 5161985 (with a Tube Guide Roller Assembly) on the gradient coil's patient end and the Service End Mounting Plate 5161983 (also with a Tube Guide Roller Assembly) on the gradient coil's service end. Use the M10 x 25 SS Hex Cap screws (46-318508P20) included in the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain Tube Support Plate Assembly 2284928-2 from the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit's shipping crate.

  1. Adjust the Tube Support Plate Assembly's Horizontal Adjustment Screws until the Tube Support Bearing is centered left-to-right. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the Tube Support Plate to the magnet service-end using the 100 mm M10 x 25 SS studs (5303994), SS M10 nuts and washers included in the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit. Make sure the four extension spacers face towards the Magnet Interface Ring (Illustration 5) and nuts are securely tightened.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the eight (8) brass bolts and G10 washers that are securing the XRMB Wedges to the front of the magnet shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Wedges at 0°, 45°, 90°, 270° and 325° locations from the front of the magnet. Note the location of individual wedges as they are different.

  1. At the rear end of the magnet, remove four (4) wedges from 45, 90, 270 and 325 degree. (The three (3) lower wedges (135, 180 and 225 degree) shall remain on the rear end of the magnet. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Maneuver the Gradient Insertion Cart to the patient-end of the magnet. Position the cart in front of the magnet with a gap of at least 2 inches (50.8 mm) between the cart and the Magnet Interface Ring. The two-inch gap (shown in Illustration 8) is required for lower wedges removal.

    (Step Details)

  1. Center the cart left to right with respect to the magnet bore.

  1. Release the hand lever on the cart handle to set the brakes. (This will keep the cart from moving after the cart is aligned with the bore.)

WARNING
The jack on the Tube Jacking Assembly is ferrous.
Personal injury or equipment damage may result if taken too close to the magnet.
Keep as far away from magnet bore as possible when removing from magnet room. Always keep the tube jacking assembly mounted and screwed to the Aluminum coil cradle when used in the magnet room.

  1. Place Tube Jack Assembly (5191132) in the cart, and make sure the back of the Jack Assembly fits properly into the cart. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain the Male Insertion Tube (2284929) and XRMB Tube Standoff (5191626).

    1. Attach the Tube Standoff to the Male Insertion Tube using the four-inch, 0.375-16UNC Hex Socket Screw (5303993) included in the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit.

    1. Once the Standoff is secured to the Tube, attach the Tube Pilot Shaft to the Standoff as shown in Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the PVC shield from the brass thread of the Male Insertion Tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to Illustration 12. Slide the Male Insertion Tube, with the Standoff attached, through the Tube Guide Roller Assemblies at both sides of the XRMB.

    1. Push the Pilot Shaft through the Support Plate mounting bearing.

    1. Insert a nonmagnetic safety pin through the hole in the Pilot Shaft.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Do not force the threads. Make vertical or horizontal adjustments to the cart or Tube Support Bearing, as necessary, to make the threading easier.
 
 

  1. Obtain the Female Insertion Tube from the shipping crate (may require two persons). While one person is holding the Male Insertion Tube to keep it from rolling, the other person should thread the two Tubes together as shown in Illustration 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Recheck the centering of the cart left to right with respect to the magnet bore, and adjust the cart's longitudinal alignment if required.

NOTICE
Keep the XRMB Coil approximately level with the magnet bore during the lifting.
 
 

  1. Raise the Tube Jack and the Support Plate mounting bearing simultaneously to lift the XRMB Coil up.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the lower 3 wedges from the front of the Magnet only. (Should be able to freely rotate the coil.)

  1. Slowly push the XRMB coil out of the magnet bore while watching the clearances to the left, right, top and bottom. Adjust the Tube Jack and Support Plate mounting bearing as required to keep the gradient coil from touching the magnet bore surface.

  1. Rotate the XRMB coil until the Return coolant line at service-end is oriented downward.

  1. Gradually lower the Tube Jack and the Support Plate bearing until the weight of the XRMB coil settles on the cart.

CAUTION
Possible Equipment Damage or Personal Injury
Failure to properly secure coil to cart can result in equipment damage or injury.
XRMB Coil must be properly secured to coil cart.

  1. Properly secure XRMB coil to the cart.

  1. Using three people, move XRMB coil and cart out of the Magnet Room.

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain the Water Removal Pump Kit shown in Illustration 15.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the supply hose from the coolant removal pump kit to the Supply Manifold of the XRMB Coil with the one-inch barb adapter.

  1. Place the Return Manifold hose into a container (5 gallons or greater volume capacity). Allow the water to drain by itself for two to three minutes, using the coolant removal barb adapter to drain hose as required.

    1. Start pumping with the hand pump.

    1. Observe the water level inside the container, and swap to a different container when the previous one is full.

  1. Repeat Step 27 until the water no longer drains from the XRMB Coil.

  1. Close the coolant line loop by joining the Supply and Return Manifold together.

3.4   Preparation Before Installing XRMB

  1. Wipe the magnet bore using a clean towel and isopropyl alcohol as shown in Illustration 16.

    (Step Details)

  1. Examine the electrical connection terminals at the XRMB Coil service-end. If necessary, clean the terminal surface with isopropyl alcohol.

  1. Remove the shipping brackets (Illustration 17 ) securing the XRMB Coil to the cart.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Gradient Coil cradle fasteners, two per side, from the cradle. (See Illustration 18.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a vacuum cleaner to clean the XRMB Coil inside and out. Remove any possible metallic dust/shavings from the coil.

  1. Obtain the two Tube Guide Roller Assemblies (5308402) and XRMB Mounting Plates (5161983, 5161985) from the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit's shipping crate. Make sure the Tube Guide Roller Assemblies are attached to the XRMB Mounting Plates. (See Illustration 19.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain the Male Insertion Tube (2284929) and XRMB Tube Standoff (5191626).

    1. Attach the Tube Standoff to the Male Insertion Tube using the four-inch, 0.375-16UNC Hex Socket Screw (5303993) included in the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit.

    1. Once the Standoff is secured to the Tube, attach the Tube Pilot Shaft to the Standoff as shown in Illustration 20.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the PVC shield from the brass thread of the Male Insertion Tube.

    (Step Details)

3.5   Installing XRMB in Magnet

  1. Install the Patient End Mounting Plate 5161985 (with a Tube Guide Roller Assembly) on the gradient coil's patient end and the Service End Mounting Plate 5161983 (also with a Tube Guide Roller Assembly) on the gradient coil's service end. Use the M10 x 25 SS Hex Cap screws (46-318508P20) included in the Gradient Coil Insertion and Lift Kit. (See Illustration 22.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the 135° and 225° XRMB Wedges (5154485) to the service-end magnet flange using one M10 x 30 brass bolt (5110638-64) and two G10 flat washers (5146169).

    NOTE:These two wedges will serve as the positioning blocks for XRMB longitudinal (Z axis) alignment.

    1. To allow future adjustment, tighten the bolt only enough to secure the wedges.

    1. Do not apply Loctite® at this time.

    (Step Details)

  1. Maneuver the cart and XRMB Coil to the patient-end of the magnet. Position the cart in front of the magnet with a gap of at least 2 inches (50.8 mm) between the cart and the Magnet Interface Ring. (The two-inch gap is required for lower wedges installation.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Center the cart left to right with respect to the magnet bore.

  1. Release the hand lever on the cart handle to set the brakes. (This will keep the cart from moving after the cart is aligned with the bore.)

  1. Raise or lower the cradle of the Cart as required to align the two support tub sections (Tube Support Plate at the magnet and Gradient Mounting Plates at the XRMB) vertically. (See Illustration 25.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
The jack on the Tube Jacking Assembly is ferrous.
Personal injury or equipment damage may result if taken too close to the magnet.
Keep as far away from magnet bore as possible when removing from magnet room. Always keep the tube jacking assembly mounted and screwed to the Aluminum coil cradle when used in the magnet room.

  1. Place Tube Jack Assembly (5191132) in the cart. Make sure the back of the Jack Assembly fits properly into the cart. (See Illustration 26.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the Male Insertion Tube, with the Standoff attached, through the Tube Guide Roller Assemblies at both sides of the XRMB coil. Make sure the end with the Standoff and Pilot Shaft faces towards the magnet.

NOTICE
Do not force the threads. Make vertical or horizontal adjustments to the cart or Tube Support Bearing, as necessary, to make the threading easier.
 
 

  1. Obtain the Female Insertion Tube from the shipping crate (may require two persons). While one person is holding the Male Insertion Tube to keep it from rolling, the other person should thread the two Tubes together as shown in Illustration 27.

    (Step Details)

  1. Push the complete Insertion Tube Assembly into the magnet bore. Get the Tube Pilot Shaft as close to, but not touching, the Tube Support Plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align the Support Plate mounting bearing with the Tube Pilot Shaft by adjusting the vertical height of the Support Plate bearing using the stainless steel lever, and/or raising or lowering the cart.

  1. Recheck the centering of the cart left to right with respect to the magnet bore, and adjust the cart's longitudinal alignment if required.

  1. Push the Pilot Shaft through the Support Plate mounting bearing. Insert a nonmagnetic safety pin through the hole in the Pilot Shaft.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise the Tube Jack and Support Plate mounting bearing to lift the XRMB Coil up, keeping the coil approximately level with the magnet bore while lifting. (Should be able to freely rotate the coil once it is completely lifted off the cart.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the XRMB coil, if necessary, until the electrical connection terminals at the XRMB's service-end are oriented upward.

  1. Slowly push the XRMB into the magnet bore while watching the clearances to the left, right, top and bottom. Adjust the Tube Jack and Support Plate mounting bearing as required to keep the gradient coil from touching the magnet bore surface.

  1. Refer to Illustration 31. Slowly push the XRMB until stopped by the two positioning wedges (135° and 225°) already attached to the magnet service-end flange.

    1. Push the XRMB against the two wedges so that the coil’s end surface is in good contact with the vertical side of the two wedges.

    1. Raise the Support Plate bearing to gain more clearance between the coil and the lower wedges if necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the patient end of the magnet, attach the XRMB Radial Alignment Tool (5266408) to the magnet interface ring at 0° (top) using M10 studs and nuts. (See Illustration 34.)

  1. Slowly rotate the XRMB Coil to align its 0° bolt hole with the slot opening in the Radial Alignment Tool. Once aligned, put the thumbscrew through the Alignment Tool slot and thread it into the 0° hole in XRMB Coil. (See Illustration 32.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply red Loctite® (#271) to the 135° and 225° holes in the patient-end magnet flange.

    1. Attach the 135° and 225° wedge to the magnet flange using one M10 x 30 brass bolt (5110638-64) and two G10 flat washers (5146169).

    1. Tighten the brass screws firmly to secure the wedges.

    (Step Details)

  1. Repeat Step 20 for the 180° wedge.

  1. Repeat Step 20 at the service end to secure the 135°, 225° and 180° wedges at this end.

NOTICE
Keep the Radial Alignment Tool attached and secured during this step.
 
 

  1. Slowly lower Support Plate bearing and Tube Jack. (The full weight of the XRMB is now loaded onto the six lower wedges.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the same method as described in Step 20 and install the 90° and 270° wedge to both ends of the XRMB Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the same method in Step 20 to install the 45° and 315° wedges to both ends of the magnet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Radial Alignment Tool. Replace the original screws in the Magnet Interface Ring.

  1. Use the same method as in Step 20 to install the 0° wedge to the patient end of the XRMB Coil.

4  Finalization

  1. Restoring Insertion Tool Kit

    1. Move the cart out of the magnet room.

    1. While one person is holding the Male Insertion Tube at the service end, the other person will unthread the Female Insertion Tube from the assembly. It will take two people to return it to the shipping crate.

    1. Remove the safety pin from the Pilot shaft of the Male Insertion Tube, and the Male Insertion Tube from the magnet.

    1. Disassemble the Standoff from the Male Insertion Tube, restore the Pilot Shaft to the Tube, and return all components to the shipping crate.

    1. Remove the two mounting Plates with the Tube Guide Roller Assembly from the XRMB and place them back to the shipping crate.

    1. Remove the Tube Support Plate and studs from the magnet, and return them to the shipping crate.

CAUTION
XRMB Coil Damage!
The XRMB Coil is temperature sensitive. Exposure to temperatures below 0° C could result in coil damage.
Do NOT expose the coil to temperatures below 0° C.

  1. Ensure the temperature data logger is shipped back with the coil. The temperature data logger has to be attached to the coil. Arrange for temperature controlled transportation prior to removal of the XRMB Coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. For XRMB electrical connection, refer to XRMB Solid Busbar Replacement.

  1. If the barb fitting was cut out of XRMB coolant hose connection:

    1. Verify that the hose has a straight or perpendicular cut.

    1. Push the hose onto the fitting.

  1. If barb fitting remained in the manifold hose:

    1. Apply Teflon tape (two layers) to the one-inch male threads.

    1. When looking at the free end of the valve, rotate it six full, clockwise revolutions to start threading the barb fitting into the valve, and tighten firmly with wrenches to prevent leaks.

    1. Open the PVC valves at the Coolant Hose and XGD Heat Exchanger.

    1. Turn On the XGD Heat Exchanger, and start the Gradient Coil coolant circulation.

      1. Immediately exam the hose connection at the XRMB service end. If any water leakage is observed, stop the coolant circulation, and retighten fitting to the valve.

      1. If required, add more coolant fluid to the tank at XGD Heat Exchanger.

  1. Restore all Passive Shim Trays to the XRMB Coil in the correct order.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the RF Body Coil described in RF Body Coil Replacement.

  1. Apply air seal along the ID on the XRMB endplate (Service end only).

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the end bells.

  1. Reinstall the bridge.

  1. Perform all applicable calibrations.
