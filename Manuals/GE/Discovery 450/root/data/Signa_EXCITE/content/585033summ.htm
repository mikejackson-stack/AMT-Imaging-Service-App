1.5T HD 8-Channel Neurovascular Array Setup for MCQA Test (Invivo)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For Discovery MR450 and Optima MR450w, the following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: 8NVHEAD_A and 8NVARRAY_A. For HDx, HDxt, and HDe, the following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: 8NVHEAD_A and 8NVARRAY_A.

2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. End the previous exam if not already done.

  1. Make a note of the serial number of the coil.

  1. Remove the top of the coil, and secure the base of the Invivo HD 1.5T 8-Channel Neurovascular Array to the cradle in the same position (shown in Illustration 1) as the standard head coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom positioner on the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom on the positioner as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the top to the coil as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool if available, otherwise perform the Surface Coils SNR Test.

2.2   Unified Phantom Procedure

  1. Place the base of the NV array coil on the patient table and latch it to the table as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantom positioner in the coil as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the phantoms in the positioner as shown in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully align the connector pins on the base of the coil with the top part of the coil and then latch both the coil sections into place as in Illustration 9. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil at the cross mark as shown in Illustration 10 and advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool with the Unified Phantom Set.
