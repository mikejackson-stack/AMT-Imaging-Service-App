Table Interface Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Possible personal injury!
Electric shock hazard.
follow all lockout/tagout procedures for magnet room when replacing component.

1  Procedure

  1. Follow all Lockout/Tagout procedures as outlined in LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove two (2) rear pedestal covers (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect cables attached to Table Interface Module (TIM). See Illustration 2and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the five (5) screws holding the TIM to the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Replace the TIM and follow steps Step 1 through Step 4 in reverse order.
