GOC Power Distribution Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Hazardous voltages exist in the goc when energized.
before beginning any work, shut down the software if still running. lockout/tagout power to the GOC before Continuing. Since the GOC supports an optional ups, confirm your respective site configuration.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Host Computer and GOCAA are powered off as described below.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Powering Off Host and GOCAA

3.1.1  Shut Down Software

  1. Click [Tools] icon.

  1. On the Service Desktop Manager, click [System Shutdown].

  1. Confirm shutdown by clicking [OK].

  1. Wait for the system to indicate on the monitor that it is safe to power off the computer before proceeding. It usually takes about 90 seconds for this message to display.

    (Step Details)

3.1.2  Remove Power to Operator Workspace

  1. Verify that the power indicator on the Operator Workspace is off.

  1. Host circuit breakers are located on the front of the PDU as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

3.2   GOC Power Distribution Supply Replacement

  1. Shut down system applications if still running. LOTO power to the GOC before continuing.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws that secure the left and right side panels of the GOC as shown below.

    Illustration 3:  GOC Left Panel

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Ground Leads from both side panels as shown below.

    Illustration 5:  Left Side Panel Ground Lead

    Illustration 6:  Right Side Panel Ground Lead

  1. Remove both GOC side panels and set aside.

  1. Remove Strain Relief Plate (shown below) from the power cable.

    Illustration 7:  Strain Relief Plate

  1. Disconnect the power cable and connector (both shown below) from the GOC Power Distribution Supply, and remove the retaining screw from the plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two screws (shown below) from the side of the GOC Power Distribution Supply, and disconnect the AC power cables from the PDU.

    Illustration 9:  Disconnecting AC Cables

  1. Remove the two screws at the top of the plate (shown below), and lift the plate up to remove.

    Illustration 10:  Removing Screws and Plate

  1. Carefully slide the GOC Power Distribution Supply out of the GOC.

  1. Insert the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply. Reverse the preceding steps to properly install the replacement GOC Power Distribution Supply.

4  Finalization

  1. Turn the Host and PC circuit breakers on the PDU back to the On position by moving the breakers to the right as shown in Illustration 1.

  1. Start the Host computer by pressing the On button shown in Step .

  1. Log in and verify proper operation of the system by entering your user name and password.

  1. When the system is fully booted, perform a scan to ensure proper operation of the system.
