3  Procedure

3.1  Remove Skirt Covers

  1. The skirt covers are held into place using poppers and screws. There is one screw that secures each skirt cover to the magnet. Remove the screw from the skirt cover being removed.

    Illustration 1:  Screw Location - Front Cover

    Illustration 2:  Screw Location - Rear Cover

  1. To remove a skirt cover simply slide it towards the front (or, in the case of the back covers, to the rear) of the magnet (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  Removing Skirt Covers

    NOTE:The rear pedestal cover has to be removed before removing the rear skirt cover.

3.2  Install Skirt Covers

  1. Align fastener on skirt cover to the notch on the arc cover and slide cover towards the center of the magnet (Illustration 4).

  1. Align tabs so to interlock with tabs on adjoining skirt cover (see Illustration 5).

  1. Snap popper on the skirt cover into place and replace the screw, located toward the center of the magnet (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  Aligning the Skirt's Hook with the Arc Cover's Slot

    Illustration 5:  Aligning Skirt Tabs
