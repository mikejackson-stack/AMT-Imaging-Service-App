Patient Table Caster and Pad Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Back injury
heavy lifting or strain on back when twisting off caster from patient table.
ensure there are two people available to perform this procedure.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing Caster

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it outside of the magnet room.

  1. Remove screws from bottom of the Bellows where it attaches to the top of front or rear Base Cover (depending on which caster you are replacing).

  1. Remove the Patient Table Lower Base Covers. Refer to the Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal procedure.

  1. Remove the front or rear Base Cover.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Per factory settings, the default height for each caster from the floor to the indent (as described in Leveling Patient Table in the caster assembly base is 207 mm. However, measure and record the reading for the caster you intend to replace because that is the height you want to achieve after replacing the caster. Remember that the table was adjusted for levelness per the Leveling Patient Table document.
 
 

  1. Disengage rear caster brake lock pedal.

  1. At the desired caster you want to replace, use a 3/8 wrench to remove the caster linkage pin.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the collar bolt using a 7/16 wrench. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the collar down the shaft so it rests on top of the Wheel Lock Lever.

  1. Looking from a top-down perspective, move the Wheel Lock Lever to the left position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a block of wood to prop up the bottom of the Patient Table, turn the caster counterclockwise until it releases from the caster assembly.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Replacing Caster

  1. Attach the collar to the new caster. Refer to your notes regarding the correct orientation of the collar on the caster shaft.

    (Step Details)

  1. Thread the new caster into the caster base by turning it counterclockwise.

  1. Return the table to all four casters on the floor and measure the height of the new caster. Achieve a measurement of 8.2 inches (207 mm). Refer to the Leveling Patient Table procedure. Use a nonferrous level to check the levelness of the Patient Table; make appropriate adjustments.

  1. Slide the collar up the caster shaft to its home position and re-bolt the collar to the caster base. Suggestion: Wiggle the caster until the collar bolt holes line up.

  1. Apply Loctite and replace the Caster Linkage Pin on the new caster.

NOTE:The pin should be captured inside the Caster linkage before threading into the Caster lever.

  1. Move the Wheel Lever Lock to the Free Swivel or middle position.

  1. Replace the caster base cover and Patient Table Lower Base Cover.

  1. Dock the Patient Table and check the levelness.

1.3   Pedal Top Rubber Replacement

  1. Remove the lower side covers from the Table.

  1. Remove the Rear Base Cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the old rubber piece and clean the contact area with isopropyl alcohol.

  1. The replacement rubber is a self-adhesive backed piece. Position and install the rubber as shown below.

    (Step Details)
