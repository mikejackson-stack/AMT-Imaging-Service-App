2  Overview

The DQA II tool will determine proper gradient polarity (positive to positive and negative to negative) and proper gradient wiring (X amp drives X coil, Y amp drives Y coil, Z amp drives Z coil) by scanning a series of Axial and Coronal images of the DQA III Phantom.

The tool will also verify that the proper phantom is being used. After proper Gradient orientation is confirmed, the tool will adjust for Z-ISO-Center and gradient calibration. If Z-ISO adjustments or gradient calibration adjustments are needed, the DQA-II tool will perform the appropriate iterations to complete. Therefore, the DQA II tool's completion time varies.

4  Procedure

4.1  Running the DQA II Tool

NOTICE
Verify that the Laser Light Alignment has been completed. Pay close attention to position the alignment light on the phantom as this procedure requires an accurate landmark.
 
 

NOTICE
If you encounter problems performing this calibration, see the Troubleshooting section.
 
 

  1. Insert the DQA-III phantom into the head coil, verify its orientation and levelness.

    Table 1:  Phantom Type

    PhantomPart Number
    DQA-III Phantom2321556
    NOTE:The correct phantom type, position, and landmark are critical to successful operation of the DQA II tool.

    It is very important that you securely clamp the head coil to the table, otherwise there is a chance the coil can move out of position during table advance to isocenter.

    You should also ensure that landmarking is positioned precisely on the black circumferential line around the phantom's waist.

    NOTE:Touch-and-go landmarking strip should NEVER be used by Field Engineers when manually landmarking as part of a calibration tool procedure.

  1. Landmark on the center line of the DQA III phantom. Laser must be in middle of black circumferential marker line. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Start the DQA II tool.

    • Make sure the Service key is installed. On the Common Service Desktop, select [Calibration Wizard] from the Calibration menu and select [Click here to start this tool].

    • After the Calibration Wizard starts, select [DQA] from the menu. Review and approve the pre & post-dependencies, and then select [Click here to start this tool] to start the procedure.

    • For non-proprietary mode, on the Common Service Desktop, select [Calibration Wizard] from the Calibration menu and select [Click here to start this tool].

  1. In the new window that appears, click the [Start ] button. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. As the DQA II tool progresses, the status appears in the Progress area. If problems are encountered, they will appear in the Actions Required section.

  1. After the tool successfully completes its tasks, click the [Exit] button.

    Illustration 4:  Successful Test

4.3  Reviewing DQA-II Images for Verification of Proper Head Scan

NOTE:Before performing the steps in this section, check the following:

  • If the latest exam has images labeled Axial (Coronal), then check to see if at least one of those Axial (Coronal) images looks "like" (regarding the shapes & positions of phantom features) the Axial (Coronal) example image shown in this section.

  • If your acquired AXIAL (CORONAL) image looks "like" the AXIAL (CORONAL) example in the doc (even if degraded via low SNR, ghosting, distortion, etc.) then suboptimal image quality may have confused the DQA II tool and the IQ related issues must be resolved before running DQA II calibration.

  • If your AXIAL (CORONAL) image DOES NOT look "like" the AXIAL (CORONAL) image example in the document, then you probably DO have a gradient wiring/setup problem. Refer to Discovery MR750/MR450 Installation manual (Part # 5500102), Section 4-5, Gradient Cable Installation.
