NOTE:

  1. Before November 2006:

    • The Infiniband Switch was included to integrate systems with four ICNs. Since that time, this switch is no longer required.

    • If the Infiniband Switch fails, the system can be rewired to operate without it. (See the Infiniband Switch replacement procedure referred to in Section 2 for details.)

  2. Before July 2008:

    • Forward production and HDx upgrades were shipped with 5127452 ICNs. Since that time, forward production and upgrades have been shipped with 5127452-3 ICNs, which have twice the capabilities of the 5127452 ICNs.

    • A site containing a 5127452 ICN must order a 5127452 replacement, and a site containing a 5127452-3 must order a 5127452-3 replacement.

  3. ICNs on upgraded systems (12.x) are shown in Illustration 6 and Illustration 7.

    • For 5127452 ICNs only, the ICN at the bottom is placed on rails on both sides. The second ICN is placed on top of the first ICN.

    • The 5127452-3 ICNs are each mounted on their own rail.

  4. Illustration 6 shows the VRE setup in 1.5T system cabinet and Illustration 7 shows the VRE setup in the 3.0T system cabinet.

NOTICE
When running VRE diagnostics, do NOT click any other links until the diagnostics are complete, or they may fail.
