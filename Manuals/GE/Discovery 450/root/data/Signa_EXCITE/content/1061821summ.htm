1  Procedure

1.1   Removing and Replacing the LPCA Cover

  1. At the foot of the Patient Table, twist the Cradle Release Handle to free the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Using a standard head, non-ferrous screwdriver, remove 4 screws on the top of the LPCA cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the new cover on the LPCA Assembly starting at the rear, and then the front.

  1. Reattach the 4 cover screws.

1.2   A-Port Bezel

The Sub-D Plate (Part # 5184915) shall be referred to as Bulkhead Plate in this procedure.

1.2.1  Removing the A-Port Bezel

  1. At the foot of the Patient Table, twist the Cradle Release Handle to free the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Removing and Replacing the LPCA Cover.

NOTICE
PAY CLOSE ATTENTION TO THE ROUTING OF THE BEZEL CABLES BEFORE DISCONNECTING. YOU NEED TO RECREATE THE ROUTING PATTERNS WHEN RECONNECTING THE CABLES.
 
 

  1. Behind the A-Port Bezel, disconnect all cable connections (J1, J2, J4, and J5) from the Bulkhead Plate. A 3/16 Nut Driver is required to remove J1 and J2.

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove the microphone cable, pull out the silver block attached to the end of the microphone cable from behind the mic bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the LEDs from the Bezel. Do not disconnect the cable from the LEDs.

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove the A-Port Bezel from the Bulkhead Plate, remove two brass pan head screws (left and right) from the plate bracket at the rear of the A-Port Bezel.

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  Replacing the A-Port Bezel

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room. Perform insertion of leds into bezel sockets outside of magnet room if possible.

  1. To replace the LED into the socket of the new A-Port Bezel, use a small slotted screwdriver to push the LED into place making sure the LED is flush with the face of the bezel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the new bezel to the Bulkhead Plate by replacing the brass pan head screws (left and right). See Illustration 5.

  1. To properly reconnect all cables, do the following:

    1. Spread the two sections of wire on J2 and feed J1 though the separation as shown.

      (Step Details)

    1. Leave J1 lying straight out, then rotate J2 counter-clockwise as needed until it meets up properly with J2 through the bulkhead plate. Push the two connectors together and then secure them using 3/16 inch nuts and a 3/16 inch nut driver.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect J5 to the N Bulkhead Adapter on the bulkhead plate, as shown.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect J1 to J1, through the bulkhead plate, as shown. Push the two connectors together and then secure them using 3/16 inch nuts and a 3/16 inch nut driver.

      (Step Details)

    1. Feed J4 between J1 and J5 as shown. Then wrap J4 around the front of the bulkhead plate ensuring that it routes below the bezel. Connect J4 to the BNC Bulkhead Adapter on the top of the bulkhead plate, as shown.

      (Step Details)

  1. Verify that the cables are routed and connected as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Replace the Mic cable into the Mic bracket. See Illustration 3.

  1. Replace the LPCA cover and reattach the LPCA to the Patient Cradle.

1.3   Removing and Replacing the LED & Switch Harness

  1. At the foot of the Patient Table, twist the Cradle Release Handle to free the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Removing and Replacing the LPCA Cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. In the middle of the LPCA near the drive belt well, disconnect the LED & Switch Harness from the Cable Track A connection.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the rear of the LPCA, remove LEDs from the top of the A-Port Bezel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all screws holding all three switches.

  1. Remove the harness.

  1. Replace the switches, label side visible at front and rear of the LPCA, then to the Cable Track A connection in the middle, and then reinstall the LEDs.

  1. Replace the LPCA cover and reattach the LPCA to the Patient Table.

1.4   Removing and Replacing the Cradle Latch

  1. At the foot of the Patient Table, twist the Cradle Release Handle to free the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Removing and Replacing the LPCA Cover.

  1. Remove the four brass slotted head screws at the base of the cradle latch.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the bottom side of the latch, use a standard head screwdriver to remove the Compression Spring screw.

  1. To reassemble the new cradle latch, reverse Steps 4-5 above.

  1. Replace the LPCA cover and reattach the LPCA to the Patient Table.

1.5   Removing and Replacing the Sub-D Plate (Bulkhead Plate)

The Sub-D Plate (Part # 5184915) shall be referred to as Bulkhead Plate in this procedure. The Bulkhead Plate contains two components— A-Port Bezel and microphone. In order to replace the Bulkhead Plate, both components and their respective cable connections must be completely removed.

  1. At the foot of the Patient Table, twist the Cradle Release Handle to free the cradle from the LPCA.

  1. Drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover. See Removing and Replacing the LPCA Cover.

NOTICE
VERY IMPORTANT!! AFTER DISCONNECTING THE BEZEL CABLE CONNECTIONS, TRY TO LEAVE THE ROUTING OF THE CABLES IN PLACE AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE. THIS WILL HELP SPEED UP THE REPLACEMENT PROCESS. EQUALLY IMPORTANT IS NOT TO DISLODGE, MOVE, OR DISTURB OTHER CABLE CONNECTIONS ASSOCIATED WITH THE BULKHEAD PLATE, LIKE THE J2 CONNECTOR AND MIC CABLE.
 
 

  1. To remove the A-Port Bezel and all of its cable connections on the top of the Bulkhead Plate, refer to Removing the A-Port Bezel, Steps 4-7.

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove the microphone cable, pull out the silver cable block from behind the mic bracket. See Illustration 3.

  1. After removing and setting aside the A-Port Bezel and its cables (See Notice associated with Step 4 above), remove the Bulkhead Plate screws. See Illustration 5.

  1. On the bottom of the Bulkhead Plate, remove cable connections, and then use a 16 MM combination wrench and a 3/4 inch combination wrench to remove both bulkhead adapters. See Illustration 16.

  1. On the replacement Bulkhead Plate, reattach cable connections and bulkhead adapters on the bottom of the plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. To reattach the Bulkhead Plate to the LPCA assembly, do the following:

    1. Leave the Mic cable (silver cable block) lying straight making sure that it is on top of J1, J4, & J5 and not connected.

      (Step Details)

    1. Position J2 to the right side of the cable track and curl it until the connector is turned 180 degrees. Then locate J2 into its slot on the bulkhead plate as shown and move the plate down towards its mounting position while holding J2 in place.

    1. Locate the J1 connector into its slot. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 10.

    1. Reattach the four Bulkhead Plate screws. See Illustration 5.

  1. To reattach the A-Port Bezel and its cables, see Replacing the A-Port Bezel, Steps 3-5.

  1. Replace the Mic cable into the Mic bracket. See Illustration 3.

  1. Replace the LPCA cover and reattach the LPCA to the Patient Table.

2  Finalization

  1. Run Coil Datapath Diagnostics tool.

  1. Do a test scan to verify system operation.
