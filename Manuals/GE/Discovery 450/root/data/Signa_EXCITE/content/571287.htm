1.5T 8 CH Body Coil Cable Replacement

2  Overview

This procedure will replace the cable assembly on the 1.5T HD 8CH Body Array Coil (P/N: 2415366) by GE/USAI, Catalog Number M3335MC.

4  Procedure

  1. Unplug the Anterior Cable Interface Box (Labeled with UP) from the Anterior Body Array Coil as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Removal of Anterior Cable Interface

  1. Remove all the 8 screws form the lower Posterior Coil cover as shown in Illustration 2. Remove the cover after removing the screws.

    Illustration 2:  Removal of 8 Screws from Posterior Coil

  1. Remove all the four screws as shown in Fig 3 below; Unplug the RF cables labeled with CH5-CH8 and DC PIN Header Connector carefully as shown in the Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Posterior Coil RF Cable and DC Wire Removal

  1. Remove the cable from the Posterior Coil completely.

  1. Take the new 8 Ch Body array cable assembly and connect the posterior cable assembly into the Posterior Coil side as shown in Illustration 3.

  1. Connect the RF Cables (J5-J8) and DC PIN Header connector into the Body Array Coil Feed board PCB as shown in Fig 3 as per Table 1

    Table 1:  Posterior Cable Connection

    S.NCable DesignatorPCB Designator
    1CH5/BROWNJ8
    2CH6/BLACKJ11
    3CH7/GREYJ5
    4CH8/CLEARJ13
    5DC PIN HeaderJ9

  1. Apply all the four screws and tighten them as shown in Illustration 3.

  1. Place the Lower Posterior Coil Cover on the coil and tighten all the screws (8) as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Plug the Anterior Cable Interface Box (Labeled with UP) of the new cable assembly into the Anterior Coil as shown in Illustration 1.
