2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T NV Array by Medrad (M3087JJ).

4  Procedure

  1. Position the coil on the table.

  1. Place the positioner and phantom in the coil. Do not use any pads when running the SNR test.

  1. Press the chest section down till resting on phantom. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Coil Loaded with Phantoms for SNR Test

  1. Slide the head section of the coil all the way in the inferior direction, till it locks in place. See Illustration 1.

  1. Landmark on the coil crosshairs on the top part of the head section. See Illustration 1.

  1. Perform the MCQA Test if available, otherwise perform the Surface Coils SNR Test.
