2267873

Y axis XGA-SB is reporting a TEC out of temperature range fault.

TEC temperature is 124C

A thermoelectric device (TEC) is present on the internal system board of the XGA to control the temperature stability of critical compensation circuits. The TEC can become dislodged or malfunction thus causing this error. The TEC can only compensate (by adding heat) within a certain temperature range of the XGA cold-plate.

Confirm that the PE cooling loop of the HEC is stable and operating in the specified range (30-35°C).

NOTE:If the TEC is within range, the TEC is operating properly. If the TEC is out of range, the TEC is faulty. The TEC is not serviceable. Replace the XGA unit.

NOTE:The XGA will operate without temperature stable TEC. However, a consequence is that FMRI scans may exhibit ghosting artifact.
