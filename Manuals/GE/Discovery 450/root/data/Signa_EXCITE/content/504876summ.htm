1  Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, select the [SCAN] icon

  1. Click on [New Pt] to enable setting of a new landmark.

  1. the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the DQA-III phantom and loader in the head coil. Position it with the fill plugs up, and toward rear of magnet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark in the sagittal and axial planes (DQA-III coronal plane is not at isocenter). See Illustration 2for positioning the phantom in the quad head coil.

    (Step Details)

  1. At front enclosure on scanner, press <LANDMARK>, then <MOVE TO SCAN>.

  1. Set Patient Protocols to Service.

  1. In the Protocol field, Type o.38.1 (o=Other, 38=protocol number, 1=series number) and <Enter>. OR Click on Other and select protocol 38 and series 1 from the menu. Click on [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. On the scan desktop, click on [Autoview]. When the image displays, verify that the CS appears in the upper right-hand corner (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the Operator Workspace, click on [New Series].

  1. In the Protocol field, Type <o.38.2> (o=Other, 38=protocol number, 2=series number) and <Enter>. OR Select the Service protocol corresponding to Geometry Verification under “Other”

  1. Click on [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. For TwinSpeed, select GradMode=WHOLE.

  1. Click on [Save Series]

  1. In the RX MANAGER window, click on [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. When the axial image displays, verify that the A appears in the lower-middle of the image. The image should have the GE logo in the upper right-hand corner (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. For TwinSpeed, repeat scans for GradMode=ZOOM.

  1. In the RX MANAGER window, click on [End Exam] to exit.

2  Finalization

  1. Put away any phantoms and any other tools used for the test.

  1. Return to Geometry Verification Start Page or Go back to Software Configuration Checks
