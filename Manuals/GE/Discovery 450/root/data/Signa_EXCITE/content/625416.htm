2  Overview

This document contains the replacement procedure for the 3T HD T/R Knee Array by Invivo, catalog M3335LB. The field replaceable units and additional accessories are listed in Section 7.

For latch replacement, locking bail insert removal tool (included in FRU kit).

6  Mechanical Parts Replacement

6.1  Mechanical Hardware Check

Exercise the coil latches by moving the latch handle back and forth. The latches must move smoothly and lock positively into place. The latches must not loosen or open during the course of an examination.

Move the coil from side to side on the baseplate. The coil must move smoothly and without resistance. Swivel the coil on the baseplate. The coil must move smoothly within a 10-15 degree rotation. Lock the coil into position on the baseplate. The coil must remain in place while locked down, and resume smooth movement when unlocked.
