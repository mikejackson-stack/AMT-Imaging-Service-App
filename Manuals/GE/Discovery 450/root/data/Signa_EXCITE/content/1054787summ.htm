ICN On/Off Procedure

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
DANGEROUS VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT THROUGH OUT THE SYSTEM CABINET WHEN POWER IS APPLIED.
REFER TO osha lockout tagout requirements.

NOTICE
Follow this procedure for removing power to the ICN Chassis. Failure to properly power down ICNs may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Do not turn power off on the ICNs from the power button on the front panel. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Powering Down from Root Level (Sun 4100 and Sun 4170)

If the application's software is down, you can power down the ICNs by using command line at the root level.

  1. Log into the application using the root login.

  1. In the xterm window, type: /etc/init.d/poweron_vres stop and press <Enter>. The ICN(s) should turn off after one minute.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Powering Down ICNs from VRE Power Control

  1. At the host computer, go to the Service Browser Utilities → ToolboxVRE → Power Control. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm the ICN numbers and their MAC Addresses. The MAC addresses are printed on a sticker on the front of the ICNs. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [POWER OFF ICN(x)](see Illustration 2). Wait for the ICN to power off (see Illustration 5). Upon refreshing the Power Control link, Illustration 6 shows the ICNs in its current state.

    (Step Details)

  1. The ICN(s) are powered down if the green disk drive LEDs on the front of the ICN(s) are no longer illuminated. The Power LEDs will continue to blink even after power-down. (Shown below.)

    (Step Details)

1.3   Power-Off ICNs Manually

NOTICE
In the event you do not have access to the browser or the command line, you will have to power off the ICNs manually. Please try this method before powering off the GOC. This is the preferred method because it does not corrupt the files on the hard drive.
 
 

  1. Momentarily press the power button (shown below). This brings down the ICN in a “soft” method. It takes about a minute or two for the operating system (OS) to boot down. (After power-down, the green Power LED will continue to slowly blink, but the disk drive LEDs will go dark.)

    Illustration 9:  ICN Power LED and Power Button (Sun 4100)

    Illustration 10:  ICN Power LED and Power Button (Sun 4170)

  1. If the OS freezes or is not responding, then the “soft” method did not work properly. In this case, hold the power button down for six seconds to force the power off.

1.4   Switch ON ICNs from the VRE Power Control

  1. Proceed to the Common Service Desktop and go to Utilities → Toolbox → VRE → Power Control. As shown in Illustration 6, click [POWER ON ICN] for the respective ICN. The new ICN will automatically be powered on.

    (Step Details)

  1. To power-on the ICNs from the xterm command line, type: /etc/init.d/poweron_vres start and press <Enter>.
