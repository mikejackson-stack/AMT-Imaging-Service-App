2  Overview

Prior to performing work on the RF Amplifier, Systems Cabinet, and/or Penetration Panel electronics (such as the Patient Handling Power Supply) and/or Magnet Room electronics (such as the SRI and Patient Blower), the steps in this Lock Out Tag Out procedure must be performed by a LOTO Authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all LOTO steps will ensure a safe environment when working on these parts.

4  Procedure

4.1  Type of Equipment and/or Method to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Multimeter set to read voltage for verification of RF Amplifier capacitor discharge.

4.2  Type of Equipment and/or Method to Ensure Disconnections

Personal lock and tag per FE working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO at the site.

4.3  Applying LOTO

  1. Before removing power, the host must be powered down. Refer to the ICN ON/OFF Procedure.

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire Systems Cabinet, boot the Applications software down to root level. The VRE System, which resides in the Systems Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the Applications software is active.
 
 

  1. To boot the Applications software down to root level, right mouse click and select [Service Tools] and [System Restart]. The Systems Cabinet can be powered down when the GUI login displays.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches to the OFF position for the following:

    1. (For 1.5T Systems with the RF Amplifier internal to the RFS Cabinet) RF / Systems Cabinet

      • Power down the Systems (RFS) Cabinet when the power LED on the host computer is no longer illuminated.

      • All power to the RFS Cabinet should be turned off prior to working on the RF Amplifier.

    1. (For all other 1.5T and all 3.0T Systems) RFI / Amplifier

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breaker switches, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment.

  1. Test that power has dissipated for each item:

    1. RF Systems Cabinet Components - Verify the LEDs on all boards internal to the RF Systems Cabinet are Off.

4.4  Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches to the ON position for the following:

    1. RF / Systems Cabinet

    1. RFI / Amplifier

5  Finalization

  1. Boot the host computer and use the SDS ID to log in.
