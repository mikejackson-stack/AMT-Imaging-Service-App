Front Cover Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

    (Step Details)

  1. Undock the patient table, and position the table away from the magnet.

  1. Remove the side cover.

  1. Remove the skirt cover.

  1. For an In-Room Display system:

    1. Remove the alignment laser light cover by unscrewing the two (2) screws. The cover is held in place by a popper on the lower left corner and snaps off. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the two trim rings by pulling away from the enclosure. They are held in place by four (4) poppers on each side. See Illustration 3.

      (Step Details)

  1. For a 7-Segment Display system:

    1. Remove the left trim ring.

    1. Remove the one (1) screw holding the 7-Segment Display bezel.

    1. Remove the right trim ring. See Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

  1. For both systems, remove the two (2) M6 screws that secure the front bridge cover. See Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Front Bridge Cover Screws

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front cover(s) by unscrewing the seven (7) screws on the left-hand front cover and the six (6) screws on the right-hand front cover. Unscrew the two (2) additional screws shared by both sides. There are fifteen (15) screws total. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. To free each front cover, disconnect the cables that connect the control panel to the magnet (see Replacing Operator Display and Control Panels). Also, see Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reverse the steps to install the replacement front cover.

2  Finalization

  1. For the In-Room Display system:

    1. Whenever the In-Room display or trackball control panels are disconnected and then reconnected, the host PC needs to be rebooted.

    1. After reboot, from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware, then Magnet Room, then In Room Display

    1. The diagnostic will display the current status of the IRD and trackball connectivity. If status is not installed, recheck connections, reboot, and check the In Room Display diagnostic again.

  1. For the 7-Segment Display system, check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary: From the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then SRI Functional Tests.
