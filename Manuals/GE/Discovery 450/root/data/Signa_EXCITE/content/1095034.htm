Rear Cover Removal and Installation

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hourNot Applicable

2  Overview

The rear cover is made up of two pieces. Removing both rear covers is needed when replacing the gradient coil, rear end bell, and busbar. Only the right rear cover is removed when replacing the hybrid splitter module.

3.2  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

4  Procedure

  1. Remove the rear pedestal cover. (See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 1:  Remove Screws Holding Rear Pedestal Side Covers

    Illustration 2:  Remove Rear Pedestal Side Cover

  1. Remove two (2) screws securing the rear cable cover and remove the cover. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Remove Rear Cable Cover

    NOTE:After removing the screws, lift the cover up to disengage the upper hooks.

  1. Remove the skirt cover from the appropriate rear cover.

  1. Remove the side cover from the appropriate rear cover.

  1. Remove the top side cover.

  1. Remove the appropriate rear cover. Each of the two rear covers is held on by five (5) screws. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Rear Covers

  1. To install the rear cover, reverse the removal procedures.
