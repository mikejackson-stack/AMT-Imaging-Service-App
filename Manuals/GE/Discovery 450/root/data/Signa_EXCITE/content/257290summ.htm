1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing nitrogen purge/precool:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions, and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for Nitrogen Purge/Precool in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE MR Magnet Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting nitrogen purge/precool:
Have all Work Assistant or Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of the 2301164PRE MR Magnet Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
Gas cylinder rupture/damage or opening cylinder valve fast will release 2400 psi gas from cylinder.
Make sure gas cylinders are secure from falls and away from moving objects before using. Always open cylinder valve slowly.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Nitrogen Purge/Precool.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing Gaseous Nitrogen Purge.

3  Procedure

3.1   Gaseous Nitrogen Purge

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Nitrogen Purge/Precool Procedures release Large quantities of nitrogen gas, which will displace oxygen in the air and potentially cause asphyxiation.
Make sure sufficient ventilation exists in the magnet room to dispel large quantities of nitrogen gas.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Gaseous Nitrogen Purge.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing Gaseous Nitrogen Purge.

NOTICE
Maintain internal Cryostat pressure <5 psig at all times while precooling/filling with cryogens to prevent the pressure relief valve from activating, thereby reducing transfer efficiency. (See Illustration 3.)
 
 

  1. Obtain a full liquid nitrogen Dewar, and verify that all valves are in the closed position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the Dewar's Pressure Building Valve, and verify that the pressure gauge reading does not exceed 20 psig.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Nitrogen Transfer Line to the Dewar's Gas Use valve using a Nitrogen Fill Line Adapter.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Purge/Precool Adapter to opposite end of Nitrogen Transfer Line.

NOTICE
Make sure the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension and Teflon Tip are firmly secured during installation to prevent them from loosening and falling into magnet.
 
 

  1. Screw the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension onto the Cryostat Stinger Assembly. Make sure the Teflon Tip is firmly attached to end of the extension tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Observe the Cryostat Pressure Gauge. If pressure exceeds 0.5 psig, temporarily open Helium Vent Valve V2 allowing pressure to decrease to 0.5 psig.

    (Step Details)

  1. Uncap Fill Port V1, and remove the Fill Port Plug.

  1. Purge the Nitrogen Transfer Line Assembly by partially opening the Dewar's Gas Use Valve. Purge the line for a minimum of 10 seconds, then connect the Purge/Precool Adapter to the Cryostat Stinger Assembly.

NOTE:If ceiling height prevents insertion of the Cryostat Stinger in the next step:

  • Remove the Extension from the Cryostat Stinger and partially insert the Stinger into Fill Port V1 before attaching it to the Cryostat Stinger.

  • Grip the Extension firmly when inserting to prevent it from falling into the magnet.

  1. Fully insert the Cryostat Stinger Assembly with Extension into Fill Port V1. Make sure the Extension is firmly seated, then tighten the Fill Port Compression Fitting.

  1. Open Helium Vent Valve V2.

  1. Fully open the Dewar's Gas Use valve.

NOTICE
Do NOT allow cryostat pressure to exceed 5 psig in the next step to prevent any possible damage to the Burst Disc and Cryostat Pressure Gauge. Cycling in the next two steps is necessary to adequately purge the Helium Vessel.
 
 

  1. Close V2 allowing cryostat pressure to increase to 5 psig as observed on the Cryostat Pressure Gauge, then open V2 and allow pressure to decrease to 1 psig.

  1. Repeat the above step continually for a 10-minute period.

  1. Close the Dewar's Gas Use Valve.

  1. Close Helium Vent Valve V2.

  1. Proceed to the next section.

3.2   Liquid Nitrogen Precool

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Nitrogen Purge/Precool Procedure releases Large quantities of nitrogen gas, which will displace oxygen in the air and potentially cause Asphyxiation.
Make sure sufficient ventilation exists in the magnet room to dispel large quantities of nitrogen gas.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Nitrogen Precool Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before starting Liquid Nitrogen Precool.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Liquid Nitrogen Precool.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing Liquid Nitrogen Precool.

WARNING
Explosion Hazard!
Liquid cryogens can liquefy atmospheric oxygen, producing a highly combustible oxygen liquid.
No smoking in magnet and cryogen storage rooms!

  1. Obtain a full liquid nitrogen Dewar, and verify that all valves are in the closed position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the Dewar's Pressure Building valve, and verify that the pressure gauge reading does not exceed 20 psig. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Connect the Nitrogen Transfer Line to the Liquid valve on the Dewar. Before connecting, remove the special Nitrogen Fill Line Adapter if attached to the Transfer Line.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the Purge/Precool Adapter to the opposite end of the Nitrogen Transfer Line.

NOTICE
Make sure the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension and Teflon Tip are firmly secured during installation to prevent them from loosening and falling into magnet.
 
 

  1. Screw the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension onto the Cryostat Stinger Assembly. Make sure the Teflon Tip is firmly attached to end of the extension tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Observe the cryostat pressure gauge. If pressure exceeds 1.0 psig, temporarily open Helium Vent Valve V2, allowing pressure to decrease to 1.0 psig. (See Illustration 3.)

  1. Uncap Fill Port V1.

  1. Purge the Nitrogen Transfer Line Assembly by partially opening the Dewar's Liquid valve.

NOTE:If ceiling height prevents insertion of the Cryostat Stinger in the next step:

  • Remove the Extension Tip from the Cryostat Stinger, and partially insert the Stinger into Fill Port V1 before attaching it to the Cryostat Stinger.

  • Grip the Extension firmly when inserting to prevent it from falling into the magnet.

  1. Fully insert the Cryostat Stinger Assembly with Extension Tip into Fill Port V1. Make sure the Extension Tip is firmly seated, then tighten the Fill Port Compression Fitting.

  1. Open Helium Vent Valve V2.

  1. Close the Dewar's Liquid valve, then immediately connect the Purge/Precool Adapter to the Cryostat Stinger Assembly.

NOTICE
Do not allow cryostat pressure to exceed 5 psig in the next step to prevent any possible damage to the Burst Disc and Cryostat Pressure Gauge.
 
 

  1. Open the Liquid Valve and monitor the Cryostat Pressure Gauge. Adjust the valve as required to maintain 3 to 4 psig as displayed on the Cryostat Pressure Gauge.

  1. Connect a Lakeshore 208 Thermometer, MTM-101 Handheld Magnet Temperature Meter, or equivalent to the Diode Connector on the Magnet Connector Assembly (MSI-A3, A1) in conformance with Magnet Commissioning Checks.

  1. During magnet precool:

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Do not cool the cryostat below 90K with liquid nitrogen to prevent liquid nitrogen from collecting in the helium vessel.

A sudden drop in temperature to 77 to 80K during precool indicates that liquid nitrogen is collecting in the bottom of the helium vessel. Discontinue liquid nitrogen flow immediately, and purge the collected liquid from the cryostat with gaseous nitrogen, in conformance with Gaseous Nitrogen Purge, until a temperature of 95K is indicated on both diodes.

 
 

  1. Continue until a 95 K reading is reached on either of the magnet diodes. When a nitrogen Dewar is empty:

    1. Close all Dewar valves. (See Illustration 4.)

    1. Close V2.

    1. Disconnect the Purge/Precool Adapter from the Cryostat Stinger Assembly, and immediately install a Brass Blanking Cap onto the Cryostat Stinger Assembly.

    1. Remove the Nitrogen Transfer Line Assembly.

    1. Repeat Step 1 through Step 15 for each consecutive Dewar.

    (Step Details)

  1. When a 95 K reading is reached on either of the magnet diodes:

    1. Close all Dewar valves.

    1. Close V2.

    1. Disconnect the Purge/Precool Adapter from the Cryostat Stinger Assembly, and immediately install a Brass Blanking Cap onto the Cryostat Stinger Assembly.

    1. Remove the Nitrogen Transfer Line Assembly and Dewar from the Magnet Room.

  1. Proceed to the next section.

3.3   Helium Purge

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
the Helium Purge Procedure releases Large quantites of helium gas, which will displace oxygen in the air and potentially cause Asphyxiation.
Make sure sufficient ventilation exists in the magnet room to dispel large quantities of helium gas.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Helium Purge.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing Helium Purge.

  1. Obtain two large (235 SCF), full aluminum cylinders of gaseous helium (GHe).

WARNING
Potential High Pressure Gas Release!
If a gas cylinder falls, the valve outlet could shear, releasing hazardous high pressure gas.
Secure the gas cylinder before removing the protective valve cap.

  1. Connect a standard high pressure GHe regulator and hose assembly to the valve outlet (CGA 580) on the GHe cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the gas cylinder and regulator to the flexible gas hose. (See Illustration 5.)

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
gas Cylinder rupture/damage or opening cylinder valve fast will release 2400 psi gas from cylinder.
Make sure gas cylinders are secure from falls and away from moving objects before using. Always open cylinder valve slowly.

NOTICE
Make sure the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension and Teflon Tip are firmly secured during installation to prevent them from loosening and falling into magnet.
 
 

  1. Screw the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension onto the Cryostat Stinger Assembly. Make sure the Teflon Tip is firmly attached to end of the extension tube.

    (Step Details)

  1. Observe the Cryostat Pressure Gauge. If pressure exceeds 5.0 psig, temporarily open Helium Vent Valve V2 allowing pressure to decrease to 0.5 psig. (See Illustration 3.)

  1. Uncap Fill Port V1, and remove the Fill Port Plug.

NOTE:If ceiling height prevents insertion of the Cryostat Stinger in the next step:

  • Remove the Extension Tip from the Cryostat Stinger and partially insert the Stinger into Fill Port V1 before attaching it to the Cryostat Stinger.

  • Grip the Extension firmly when inserting to prevent it from falling into the magnet.

  1. Fully insert the Cryostat Stinger Assembly with Extension Tip into Fill Port V1. Make sure the Extension Tip is firmly seated, then tighten the Fill Port Compression Fitting.

  1. Loosely tie a large, lightweight, thin-walled trash bag over the Stinger outlet so that bag inflation and float will indicate helium collection and flow.

NOTICE
Two full gas cylinders are required for this Nitrogen Purge/Precool procedure.
 
 

  1. Make sure the regulator handle is backed out (CCW) to avoid regulator damage, then open the gas cylinder slowly. The high pressure gauge should indicate 2,100 to 2,400 psig (150 to 170 kg/cm2) if the cylinder is full.

  1. Open the cylinder valve, and set a low pressure gas flow at 1 psig. Purge the apparatus for one minute.

  1. Remove the 1/2 in. NPT plug from the Relief valve tee, and screw the gas hose onto the tee via the hose's 1/2 in. NPT male fitting. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Increase the pressure of the helium gas flow to 4 psig. Listen for gas flowing in the stinger vent line to make sure the flow continues to be present during the purge.

NOTICE
Do not allow cryostat pressure to exceed 5 psig in the next step to prevent any possible damage to the Burst Disc and Cryostat Pressure Gauge.
 
 

  1. When the first cylinder's pressure decreases to 1,000 psig, open V2 for one minute to purge the penetration volume.

  1. Monitor cryostat pressure during the purge to make sure the cryostat does not depressurize below 0.3 psig.

  1. Observe the regulator high pressure gauge while purging with the second cylinder of gaseous helium.

    1. When pressure decreases to 250 psig, close cylinder valve, and fully back out the regulator's adjusting handle.

    1. Plug the stinger outlet while changing cylinders.

    (Step Details)

  1. When the purge is complete, make sure the V2 is closed. Remove the trash bag from the stinger, remove the stinger itself, and cap the fill port.

  1. Remove the gas line from the tee, and immediately place the plug back into the tee.

  1. Continue with Liquid Helium Fill.
