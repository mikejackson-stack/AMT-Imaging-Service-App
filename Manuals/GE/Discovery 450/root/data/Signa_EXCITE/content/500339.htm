2.3  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present through out the System Cabinet when Power is applied.
Refer To OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.

NOTICE
Follow the procedure mentioned below for removing power to ICN chassis. Failure to properly power down ICN’s may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICN's be turned ON / OFF from the power button on the front. The ICN's have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

3  Procedure

  1. Locate the ICN's in the system cabinet. Depending on the configuration of the system, the ICN's can be located either below the MGD Chassis or at the bottom of the cabinet. See below.

    Illustration 1:  ICN Location - 1.5T

    Illustration 2:  ICN Location - 3.0T

NOTICE
Under NO circumstances should the ICN's be turned ON / OFF from the power button on the front. The ICN's have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted if not shut down properly.
 
 

NOTE:If an ICN in the stack (an ICN not on the top of the rack) requires replacement, then all the ICN's above it must be uncabled and removed before the failed ICN can be removed. If the ICN's are not individually supported, then attempting to remove any one ICN in the stack can result in damage to any ICN's above it. Refer to Illustration 6, Illustration 7 and Illustration 8 for reference.

  1. Switch OFF the ICN's from the service browser. See ICN ON/OFF Procedure . If there is an ICN in the rack, then switch off ALL the ICN's before removing any from the rack.

  1. Perform LOTO for Systems Cabinet or RF Systems Cabinet (RFS).

  1. Remove power cables, Infiniband and all three Ethernet Switch Connections from the rear of the ICN's. See below for the 5127452 ICN, 5127452-3 ICN's and 5337894-3 ICN and 5337894-4 ICN.

    NOTE:Note the Infiniband and Ethernet connection ports for easier installation.

    Illustration 3:  Infiniband and Ethernet Switch Connections for 5127452 (Dedicated Computing Boxes)

    Illustration 4:  Infiniband and Ethernet connections on a 5127452-3 ICN (Sun 4100)

    Illustration 5:  Infiniband and Ethernet Switch Connections for 5337894-3 and 5337894-4 ICNs (Sun 4170)

  1. Remove the screws securing the ICN's at the front, as shown below.

    Illustration 6:  Retaining Screws At The Front Of The ICN 5127452 (Dedicated Computing Boxes)

    Illustration 7:  Retaining Screws at the front of ICN 5127452-3 (Sun 4100)

    Illustration 8:  Retaining Screws at the front of ICN 5337894-3 and ICN 5337894-4 (Sun 4170)

  1. Slide the ICN's out from the front of the cabinet.

  1. For the 5127452-3, 5337894-3 and 5337894-4 ICNs only: The bracket on the front of the ICN must be removed (unsnapped) and installed on the replacement ICN.

    1. To remove right side bracket, push downward and rotate to a 45 degree angle to remove, as shown below.

      Illustration 9:  Right ICN Bracket

    1. To remove left side bracket, slide bracket upward, as shown below.

      Illustration 10:  Left ICN Bracket

    1. Install the brackets on the new ICN by performing these steps in reverse order.

  1. Replace the old ICN with the new one and connect all the cables back in reverse order. Consult the appropriate system diagrams, if assistance is needed. See either 5133310 1.5T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams or 5133320 3.0T HD, HDx and HDxt Block Diagrams .

  1. Configure the new ICN, see ICN Reconfiguration.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure the system is working properly.
