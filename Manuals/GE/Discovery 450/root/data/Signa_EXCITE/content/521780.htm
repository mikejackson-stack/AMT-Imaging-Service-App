SRI Fiber Optic Transmitter Test

1  Diagnostic Link

Diagnostics >> Peripherals >> SRI Functional Tests

2  Purpose

This test permits the operator to check the functionality of the LED either visually or with a fiber optic light meter.

3  Components Tested

4  Requirements

For best results, start this test when all of the SRI fiber cables are connected in their normal operating state.

Once the test has started to run, disconnect the SRI fiber cable and verify that SRI fiber output LED is brightly lit. You can check for this light at the SRI module, the Pen Panel, and at the end of the fiber cable that connects to the STIF board.

NOTICE
Reconnect all of the SRI fiber cables before stopping this test.
 
 

5  Block Diagram

6  Test Sequence

The SCP commands the SRI to continuously illuminate the optical transmitter LED on the SRI module.

7  Expected Results

The LED will stay on until the operator clicks [Stop].
