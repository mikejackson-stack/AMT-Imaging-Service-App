XRMB Cable Busbar Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic field!
ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipments into the magnet room.

CAUTION
ELECTRICAL SHOCK HAZARD!
Contact with the connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow lockout/tagout procedures while performing services to electrical connection components.

3  Procedure

3.1   XRMB Gradient Busbar Removal

  1. Follow all safety lockout/tagout procedures.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 2 (two) M6x16mm SS screws and remove the top part (clear plastic) of the XRMB busbar safety cover (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 2 (two) M10x45mm SS hex head bolts that are securing the base part of the busbar safety cover. Pull the safety cover away from the terminals to get access to the brass nuts and the Nord-lock washers at the lug/terminal interface (see Illustration 2)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 6 (six) brass nuts and Nord-lock washers that are securing the busbar lugs (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the top of the busbar, remove the 5 (five) M6 SS screws and the clear plastic cover (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the retainer blocks to allow slack in order to remove gradient power terminals from busbar.

  1. Remove the 12 (twelve) brass nuts and Nord-lock washers that are securing the gradient power cables to the busbar terminals (see Illustration 4).

  1. Remove the 4 (four) brass nuts, G10 flat washer, and rubber isolators that are securing the busbar assembly to the magnet. Remove the tie-wraps that are securing the manifold hoses to the busbar (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. The busbar can now be removed from the magnet.

3.2   XRMB Busbar Mechanical Installation

  1. Install 2 (two) M10x150 mounting studs 5324035-6 into the 2 lower mounting points using Loctite 242. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. At each of the lower mounting studs, install 1 (one) Phenolic Spacer 5177686 and 1 (one) rubber washer 46-294167P96 with the pilot step facing outward. See Illustration 6.

  1. At each of the upper mounting studs, install 1 (one) Rubber Isolator as shown in Illustration 6.

  1. Obtain the new busbar assembly from the busbar shipping crate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the base part of the XRMB terminal safety cover on the busbar leads and let it hang loosely (see Illustration 7).

  1. Align the 6 (six) busbar lugs with the XRMB coil terminal studs, smoothly push the lugs onto the terminal studs (see Illustration 7). Meanwhile, slide the busbar assembly onto the 4 (four) busbar support studs (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the busbar is mounted, place 1 (one) rubber isolator, 1 (one) large G10 flat washer (32mm OD, part number 5268439), 1 (one) M10x24 mm washer (part number 5146169), and 1 (one) brass nut at each of the 4 (four) mounting studs. The step of the rubber isolator faces inward.

  1. Gradually tighten the 4 (four) brass nuts until the height of the G10 washer surface (front side) reaches 7.2mm. Use the step gauge (part of the busbar FRU) to verify the height of the G10 washer (see Illustration 9). Verify the busbar lugs are not hung up on the stud threads.

    (Step Details)

3.3   XRMB Electrical Connection

  1. Place 1 (one) Nord-lock washer and 1 (one) brass nut at each of the 6 (six) XRMB terminal studs. Pull the safety cover away from the terminals to get access to the lug/terminal interface (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Gradually turn the 6 (six) nuts “finger-tight” - apply just enough torque so that the Nord-lock washers under the brass nuts are not moving freely. Do not over-tighten nuts.

  1. Once the nuts are “finger tight”, use a 17mm non-magnetic wrench to continue tightening the nuts by an extra half turn. This applies about 20 ft-lbs torque to the nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the base part of the terminal safety cover using 2 (two) M10x45mm SS hex head bolts and a regular G10 flat washer (24mm OD, part number 5146169). Secure the top part of the safety cover using 2 (two) M6x16mm SS screws (see Illustration 11).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the top of the busbar, connect the 12 (twelve) gradient power cables to the busbar terminals and secure them using 1 (one) brass nut and 1 (one) Nord-lock washer at each of the 12 (twelve) terminals (see Illustration 12). Turn the nuts “finger tight.” Apply just enough torque so that the Nord-lock washers under the brass nuts are not moving freely. Do not overtighten nuts. Once the nuts are finger tight, use a non-magnetic wrench to continue tightening the nuts by an extra half-turn. This applies about 20 foot-pounds of torque to the nuts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the gradient cable guard (clear plastic cover) using 5 (five) M6 SS screws (see Illustration 11).

  1. Secure the manifold hoses to the busbar using nylon tie-wraps.

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Install enclosure for rear end bell

  1. Install rear pedestal.

  1. Install patient bridge.

  1. Replace all covers that were previously removed.

  1. Remove lockout/tagout to turn system on.

  1. Execute DQA scan in all 3 planes to verify proper geometry.
