Running mgd_term

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Click the [Utilities] tab on the Common Service Desktop.

  1. On the [Utilities] menu, select [Log Into AGP/SCP](see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:If the Service Browser or the [Utilities] button is not available, use the following method:

  1. Click on the [C-shell] button.

  1. In the C-shell window, type mgd_term and press <Enter>

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. There should now be two windows open.

  1. Proceed to Results of Resetting TPS.
