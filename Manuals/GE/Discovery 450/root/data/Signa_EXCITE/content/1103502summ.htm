before servicing any components, Ensure the power is turned off, power cord removed, and lockout/Tagout performed before opening the GOC.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Please ask the customer to archive all images to an external storage device (for example PACS, DVDs, etc.).

  1. Perform the Save Info procedure. For details, see Save and Restore Tool.

  1. Close any PC applications, and power down the GOC. (Refer to System Startup and Shutdown.)

  1. Perform LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace before beginning the replacement.

NOTICE
When removing the side panel, avoid straining the ground lead.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all the cables connected to the computer, ensure they are properly labeled, and note their locations for reattachment. (Illustration below shows the cable configuration.)

  1. Loosen the strap and remove the hook.

1.2   Hard Disk Drive Replacement

Replace the defective Hard Disk Drive as follows:

  1. Remove the power and data cables from each of the hard disk drives.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the defective hard disk by gently pulling out the plastic tab on the left side of the drive, and extracting the hard drive as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm that the Data Cable from all three devices are connected to the correct SAS device locations as shown in the illustration and table below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the replacement hard drive by inserting the new disk drive into the slot where the original one was removed. (See Illustration 9.)

  1. Reconnect the Power and Data Cables to the drives. (Refer to the table above.)

2  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Perform the applicable Loading Host System Software procedure to load software and applications onto the operating system.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
