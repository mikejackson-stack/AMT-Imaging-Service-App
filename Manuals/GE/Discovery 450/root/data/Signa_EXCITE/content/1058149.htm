2  Overview

This document contains the procedures for replacing the LED bore light source used for the fiber optic bore lights.

4  Procedure

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove rear pedestal side covers. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 1:  Remove Screws Holding Rear Pedestal Side Covers

    Illustration 2:  Remove Rear Pedestal Side Cover

  1. Loosen the two (2) hand-tighten screws that secure the fiber optic cables and remove cables (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  LED Bore Light Module

  1. Disconnect power cable on right side of module.

  1. Remove two (2) screws securing bore light module to the rear pedestal panel and remove module.

  1. Attach replacement module and reconnect power cables and fiber optic cables.

  1. Put the rear pedestal side covers back in place and secure.

  1. Remove tagout tag and restore power by turning on the applicable breaker in the PDU.
