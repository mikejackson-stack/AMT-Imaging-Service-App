About this task

This procedure describes the replacement of the gradient and RF body coil air flow sensor.

Procedure

  1. Perform LOTO on the RF amplifier and PEN cabinet. Applying LOTO - RF Subsystem (magnet room electronics) and Applying LOTO - PEN Cabinet (magnet room electronics).
  2. Remove the side covers and left skirt cover to access the SRI. See Side Cover Removal and Installation and Skirt Cover Removal and Installation.
  3. Remove the front covers. See Front Cover Removal and Installation.
  4. Disconnect the RF body coil air flow sensor housing from the scan room interface (SRI).
    Note

    The illustration below is for reference only; sensor head housings are not shown.

  5. Locate the RF coil air flow sensor head bracket at approximately the 1 o'clock position on the magnet enclosure.
  6. Loosen the two screws securing the sensor head bracket to the display plate.
  7. Remove the sensor head bracket from the display plate, and free the cable from the magnet enclosure.
  8. Carefully disassemble the sensors from the plastic blocks, discard the failed sensor assembly, and reassemble the new sensors into the plastic blocks. The old plastic blocks must be reused, because the sensor FRU does not contain plastic blocks.
    Note Be careful not to touch the exposed sensor elements.
  9. Route the new cable through the magnet enclosure, reattach the sensor head bracket under the two loosened screws, and retighten them.
    Note

    Make sure the air flow sensor is all the way behind the front end bell, but not touching the magnet ring (see RF Coil Air Flow Sensor Head Bracket (Front End Bell Removed)). To adjust, move the sensor head bracket down until the it touches the magnet ring, then move it up 1/16 inch (2 mm).

  10. Reconnect the RF body coil air flow sensor housing to the SRI.
  11. Replace the front and side covers and the skirt by reversing the procedures listed in Step 1 through Step 3.
  12. Remove LOTO. See Removing LOTO - RF Subsystem and/or PEN Cabinet.

Finalization

  1. Ensure that sensors are operating correctly. See Body Air Flow Functional Check.
  2. Ensure the laser light is aligned. See Laser Light Alignment.
