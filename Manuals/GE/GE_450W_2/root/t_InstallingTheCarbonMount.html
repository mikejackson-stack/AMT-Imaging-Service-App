Installing the carbon mount

Install the carbon mount.

Procedure

  1. Loosen the six M10 1.5 x 30 mm socket head cap screws from the carbon mount.
  2. Make sure that the two dock blocks and U-blocks are assembled and loosely attached to the carbon mount.
  3. Install the carbon mount on the patient end:
    1. Bring the carbon mount into the magnet room.
    2. Loosely secure the two right brackets on the carbon mount with the four M10 1.5 x 70 mm socket head cap screws.
      Note After all components are in position, begin the alignment process. Tighten gradually as alignments are made.
      Note Make sure to only use M10 FR4 washers (5497711). Using the wrong washers may cause white pixels in images.
    3. Using the centering gauge, center the carbon mount in front of the magnet, leaving at least 8 mm of space between the carbon mount and the magnet.
    4. Attach the dock onto the two studs on the carbon mount.
      Note In Attach the dock to the carbon mount, the carbon mount is shown as black, but it is white.
    5. Make sure that the cables are out of the way of the bracket assemblies.
    6. Adjust the feet (Attach the dock to the carbon mount) on the bottom of the carbon mount by turning them so the mount is 4 to 8 mm above the magnet bore and 7 to 8 mm from the front of the magnet, see Gaps between carbon mount and magnet.
    7. Using a 17 mm wrench, loosely attach the two legs of the carbon mount with the right bracket to the wing bracket with two M10 x 70 mm socket head cap screws and lock washers.
      Note The wing bracket is attached to the base of the magnet.
    8. Do a mechanical alignment of the brackets. Tighten the hardware as alignments are made, but leave the brackets loose enough to check the PET/MR insert alignment within the magnet bore.
    9. After the alignment has been checked, fully tighten the bracket hardware.
  4. Install the carbon mount on the service end:
    1. Put the floor mount assembly (service end) in the magnet room.
      Note The two feet on the carbon mount at the service end of the magnet must touch the floor without any gaps.
    2. Loosen the six M10 1.5 x 30 mm socket head cap screws on the carbon mount.
    3. Hand-tighten each right bracket onto the carbon mount using four M10 1.5 x 70 mm socket head cap screws.
      Note Only thread the nut and bolts loosely onto the components. When all components are in position, begin the alignment process. Tighten gradually as alignments are made.
      Note Make sure to only use M10 FR4 washers (5497711). Using the wrong washers may cause white pixels in images.
    4. Using the centering gauge, center the carbon mount in front of the magnet. Leave at least 8 mm of space between the carbon mount and the magnet.
    5. Adjust the feet (Gaps between carbon mount and magnet) on the bottom of the carbon mount by turning them so the mount is 4 to 8 mm above the magnet bore and 7 to 8 mm from the front of the magnet.
    6. Use a 17 mm wrench to loosely attach each leg of the carbon mount with the right bracket to each wing bracket with two M10 x 70 mm socket head cap screws and lock washers.
      Note The wing bracket is attached to the base of the magnet.
    7. Make sure that the feet on the carbon mount are fully extended to the floor to support the weight of the mount and insert.
