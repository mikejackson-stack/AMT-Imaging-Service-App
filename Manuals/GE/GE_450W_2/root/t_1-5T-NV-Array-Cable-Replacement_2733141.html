About this task

Follow this process to replace the cable assembly on the 1.5T Neurovascular Array by Medrad (M3087JJ).

Procedure

  1. Loosen the cable Strain relief retaining nut from the external cable. See External Cable's Retaining Nut Location.
  2. Lay the NVA HD on its side and remove the 14 screws holding the base cover on the unit as shown in Location of Screws on Base Cover.
  3. Disconnect the cable shield lug from the system shield wire as shown in Disconnecting the Cable Shield Lug.
  4. Disconnect the signal harness from the multiplexer board. See Location of Signal Harness.
  5. Disconnect the coax cables from the multiplexer board. See Disconnecting Coax Cables.
  6. Remove the strain relief retaining nut and remove it from the cable assembly. See Removing the Strain Relief Retaining Nut.
  7. Remove the cables and strain relief from the base of the NVA HD. See Removing Cables and Strain Relief from the Base.
  8. Remove the strain relief seal ring from the body of the strain relief. See Removing the Strain Relief Seal Ring.
  9. Remove the coax cables from the body of the strain relief followed by the signal wires. See Removing Strain Relief from Cable.
  10. Remove the strain relief seal and the strain relief-retaining nut from the cable. See Removing Strain Relief from Cable.
  11. Slide the strain relief-retaining nut over the replacement cable followed by the strain relief seal, and then the strain relief body. See Putting the Strain Relief Parts on the Replacement Cable.
  12. Insert the cable assembly beginning with the signal wire connector followed by the coax cables through the NVA base. Slide the cable-retaining nut over the cables and secure as shown in Attaching Cable to the NVA Base.
  13. Connect the corresponding coax cables to the multiplexer board. See Connecting the Coax Cables.
    Note

    The cables are marked for proper placement.

    Wire Color Channel
    White1
    White/Black2
    White/Red3

  14. Connect the signal harness connector to P2 of the Base Mux. See Connecting Cable Harness.
  15. Connect the cable shield lug to the system shield wire as shown in Connecting the Cable Shield Lug.
  16. The final assembly should look like the image shown in Final Assembly.
  17. Tighten the cable strain relief retaining nut to the external cable. See Securing the External Cable.
  18. Replace the base cover and the 14 retaining screws. See Replacing the Base Cover.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
