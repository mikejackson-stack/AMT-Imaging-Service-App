Connecting the gradient cables to the PGR cabinet

Connect the gradient cables to the PGR cabinet.

CAUTION
Damage to overhead cable routing mechanisms/trays
Horizontal movement of cable trays while routing cables can damage cable tray rods attached to ceiling.
Do not pull on cables at end of cable trays. Route cables in trays by lifting and moving cables to connection points in overhead cable trays.

Route the gradient cables in the overhead cable tray

It is recommended that the three gradient cables be routed through the overhead cable trough before any of the other cables are routed.
  1. Make sure all non-magnetic tools, ropes, and/or ladders that may be required for routing of cables are assembled and ready for use.
  2. Starting at the secondary pen wall end of the cable tray, route the pre-terminated end of each cable through the designated overhead cable tray towards the PGR cabinet.
    • Put the cables in the cable tray.
    • Gradient cables MUST lay in a single layer next to each other.
  3. Route the pre-terminated ends of each gradient cable to the top/front of the PGR cabinet for connection to terminals.

Replace the gradient cable bracket and clamps at the PGR

This procedure may not be required if the cable bracket and clamps already installed at the top of the cabinet are the same as described in the following steps. Check to see that the cable clamp for the Z-cable is larger than the other two clamps. If this is the case, then the cable bracket and clamps do not need to be replaced.

  1. Remove the safety shield at the top of the PGR cabinet to allow easier access to gradient cable clamps.
  2. If attached, remove the ground wire from the ground stud on the cable support bracket.
  3. Remove the screws that fasten the cable clamps to the cable support bracket.
  4. Remove the screws that secure the cable support bracket to the PGR cabinet. Discard all three clamps and the cable support bracket.
  5. Install the new equipment room cable support bracket included in the hardware kit that came with the gradient cables per the table below.
    Note The support bracket has one cable slot with a larger diameter than the other two. Be sure to install the support bracket so this larger slot is on the right side when viewed from the front of the PGR cabinet. This larger slot is sized to fit the Z-cable.
  6. Determine the proper clamps to attach to the support bracket.
  7. Loosely attach the appropriate clamps and secure the clamps with the screws provided.

Connect the gradient cable to the PGR cabinet

Important The steps for this procedure install just one of the gradient cables. Repeat the steps below for installation and connection of the remaining gradient cables and the ground wires.
Note If the gradient cable clamp bracket was removed in the earlier cabinet positioning procedures, reattach it using the removed screws.
  1. If installed, remove the cover at the top of the cabinet to access the gradient cable clamps.
  2. Put the braided area of the cable in the clamp at the top of the cabinet.
  3. Close the clamp around the cable, but do not tighten completely. Adjustment may be necessary as the terminated ends of the wires are attached.
  4. Put the two black wires on the right stud of the terminal block. Install flat washers and then install split lock washers. Fasten with nuts.
  5. Put the two red wires on the left stud of the terminal block. Install flat washers and then install split lock washers. Fasten with nuts.
  6. Attach the ground wire on the ground stud. Install flat washers and then install split lock washers. Fasten with nuts.
  7. Reinstall the safety shield at the top of the PGR cabinet.
  8. Reinstall the cover at the top of the PGR cabinet.
