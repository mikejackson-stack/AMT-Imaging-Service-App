Remove skirt covers

Procedure

  1. Remove the side cover (see Side Cover Removal and Installation).
  2. The skirt covers are held into place using poppers and screws. There are two screws and one popper securing each skirt cover to the magnet. Unscrew the screws from the skirt cover that is being removed.
  3. To remove a skirt cover simply slide it towards the front (or, in the case of the back covers, to the rear) of the magnet (see Removing skirt covers).
    Note The rear pedestal cover has to be removed before removing the rear skirt cover.
Topic ID: id_SL1956813-1057045

Install skirt covers

Procedure

  1. Align fastener on skirt cover and slide cover towards the center of the magnet.
  2. Align tabs so they interlock with the tabs on the adjoining skirt cover (see Aligning Skirt Tabs).
  3. Snap popper on the skirt cover into place and replace the two (2) screws (see Installing skirt covers).
