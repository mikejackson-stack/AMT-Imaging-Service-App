Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field produced by the magnet.
Do not bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Warning
Personal injury possibility
When doing maintenance on the table in its up position, severe personal injury can occur if the table is accidentally lowered during maintenance.
Make sure the lock safety bar is safely in place.

About this task

The 32-channel curved table has P3 and P4 port connectors in the table.

This procedure describes the replacement of the patient table cable take-up from the P-port connection to the table interface plate (TIP).

Note
It is important to use the correct replacement cable track because the coil ID is contained in the P-port connector.

This instruction contains the following procedures:

  • Removing patient table cable take-up for 32-channel curved table

  • Replacing patient table cable take-up for 32-channel curved table

Removing patient table cable take-up for 32-channel curved table

Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table and remove it from the magnet room.
  2. Depending on which cable take-up (left or right) is being replaced, remove the appropriate lower base side cover, and fasten the upper bellows in the up position. See Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal.
  3. Lower the table lock safety bar, then lower the table and verify that its weight is resting on the safety bar.
  4. Remove the bottom front cover (located on the front underside of the patient table) and the right or left table side cover, depending on which cable track is being removed.
    Note

    When standing at the foot of the patient table facing the magnet, the P3 cable take-up is on the right side and P4 cable take-up is on the left side.

  5. Disconnect the gray TBL P3-J7 or P4-J7 cable from the Touch and Go connector located in the upper left or right corner inside the table top.
  6. Manually unlock the cradle, and move it forward approximately 24 in (61 cm) to clear the guide rails.
  7. Prop up the rear of the cradle using two blocks of wood or similar non-ferrous material, each approximately 10 in (25 cm) long.
  8. While another person holds the front of the cradle to ensure it does not slide off the table, use a 9/64 Allen wrench to remove the screws in order from the cable cover, coil connector, and balun for both cable take-ups (located on the rear underside of the cradle).
    Note

    Although only one cable take-up is being replaced, this step requires removal of both cable take-up connections from the bottom rear of the cradle because the cradle must be removed from the patient table to access the cable take-up in the track wells.

  9. Remove the cradle from the patient table and set it on a flat surface.
  10. Depending on which take-up being replaced, go to the left or right side of the front lower table chassis and disconnect all take-up connectors from the rear side of the TIP.
    Note

    Standing at rear of the table, the right side is the P3 cable take-up, and the left side is the P4 cable take-up. To make accessing the P4 connectors on the TIP easier, remove the 3/8 inch nut and washer from the front and rear of upper chassis rail.

    Note

    Be careful with the J1/J2 and J4/J5 black connectors. They use small, standard head screws that are made of lightweight aluminum and can be easily stripped.

  11. Notice
    The next step applies to the P4 cable take-up only. If replacing the P3 cable take-up, skip this step.
    To remove the main cradle latch cable, which is threaded through the P4 take-up track, do the following:
    1. Use a 5/64 Allen wrench to loosen the set screw at the down link clevis, then unthread the cable.
    2. Pull cable through to clear the back side of the cable track and underside of cable take-up lead.
  12. Remove both cable track end screws from the TIP connector.
  13. Follow the cable track up to the underside of table top, and pull out the disconnected gray P3-J7 or P4-J7 cable that was previously disconnected.
  14. Remove the cable take-up lead brass screws.
  15. Remove the two slotted brass screws from the cable take-up lead in the cable well.
  16. Pull the rear of the cable take-up, lay it out in the front of the patient table to expose the cover plate and screws, and remove both screws.
  17. To fully remove the cable take-up assembly from the patient table, feed the cable take-up assembly up through the hole in the table top.

Replacing patient table cable take-up for 32-channel curved table

Procedure

  1. Place the new cable take-up assembly into the table cable track well, and feed the take-up through the table hole.
  2. Secure the cover plate with the two brass screws. (See Cover plate.)
  3. Lay out the cable take-up assembly to point to the rear of the patient table and allow access to the cable take-up lead.
  4. Secure the cable take-up lead to the cover, and replace the two brass screws.
  5. At the underside of the table top, secure the cable take-up lead to the table. (See Underside of table top.)
  6. Reroute and reconnect P3-J7 or P4-J7 Touch and Go gray cable, making sure to route the cable through the two cable supports (tie wraps) before reconnecting.
  7. Notice
    Do not make main cradle latch cable adjustments at this time.
    (For P4 cable take-up replacement only) Reroute and reconnect the main cradle latch cable to the down link clevis, and make appropriate adjustments at the end of this procedure to the cable according to Cradle Release Block Retraction Adjustment in Cradle/Table Release Block Adjustment.
  8. Reconnect all cables on the TIP.
    Note

    Be careful with the J1/J2 and J4/J5 black connectors. They use small, standard head screws that are made of lightweight aluminum and can be easily stripped.

  9. Secure the cable take-up lead to the TIP with the brass screws. (See Cable track end screws.)
  10. If replacing the P4 cable take-up assembly, replace the upper chassis rail front and rear 3/8 nut and washer. (See Removing left side upper chassis rail.)
  11. Place the cradle back onto the patient table, and prop it up with the two blocks of wood or similar non-ferrous material as shown in Cradle removal.
  12. On the rear underside of the cradle, replace in order: balun, coil connector, and cable cover for each cable take-up. Remove the blocks of wood and set the cradle back onto the table top at the home position.
  13. Replace the front bottom and table side covers.
  14. Ensure that the lock safety bar is in the vertical (up) position.
  15. Move the patient table into the magnet room and dock the table.
  16. If the P4 cable take-up was replaced, make the main cradle latch cable adjustments as described in Step 7.
  17. Undock the patient table and use the non-ferrous Phillips screwdriver to replace the lower base side cover, then place the upper bellows in the down position.

Finalization

Finalization

  1. Dock the patient table.

  2. Confirm that the cradle release functions properly.

  3. Confirm that the LPCA connects to the cradle and the cradle moves into the bore.
