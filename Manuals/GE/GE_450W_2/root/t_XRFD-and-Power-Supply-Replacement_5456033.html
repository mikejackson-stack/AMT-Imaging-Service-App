Required conditions
The PGR PDU/gradient subsystem must be locked and tagged out before starting this procedure. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
Before disconnecting any coolant lines on the front of the dual drive RF amplifier unit, shutdown the Power Electronics Pump (PPMP) at the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC).
Safety

Before working in any GE HealthCare MR suite or doing any GE HealthCare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE HealthCare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE HealthCare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE HealthCare employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Warning
Electrocution hazard
High voltage present.
Use proper LOTO procedures before servicing and/or maintaining.
Warning
Possible personal injury or equipment damage
This unit could fall when using the hoist tool.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This helps keep the chain from binding up or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

About this task

There are two styles of PGR Cabinets. The “old” style contains a Power Distribution Module (PDM) in the upper right corner of the cabinet. The “new” style does not contain a PDM.

This document contains four procedures (two for each style of cabinet): XRFD replacement and XRFD power supply replacement. Both units are in the PGR cabinet. The XRFD weighs about 315 pounds and the XRFD PS weighs 80 pounds. Each unit weighs enough that a hoist kit is required. This document details the correct way to use a hoist kit to safely remove and replace the XRFD and XRFD PS units.

XRFD replacement (with PDM)

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Before disconnecting any coolant lines on the front of the XRFD unit, shut down the PPMP at the HEC by pressing ▲ and F3 simultaneously. Then, switch the PPMP circuit breaker to OFF.
  5. Before setting up the hoist kit, detach the XPS J9 connector to properly remove the main lifting beam.
  6. See Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories to set up and secure the hoist.
  7. Remove the tie-wraps that hold the power cable. The cable goes underneath the XRFD and along the side of the cabinet to the top of the PDM.
    Note Before continuing, be prepared to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is disconnected.
  8. Disconnect the power cable at the terminal, the cables on the front of the XRFD, and the coolant lines. Carefully move the cables and lines to the side of the cabinet, so the surrounding area is free from any obstructions when the amplifier is pulled out.
  9. Unscrew the eight attachment screws.
  10. Using the two front handles, carefully slide out the XRFD unit.
  11. Attach the lifting brackets (found in the XRFD FRU crate) to both sides of the XRFD. The attachment screws are captive screws and remain with the lifting brackets.
    Warning
    Potential crush hazard
    If the replacement part accidentally falls, it can cause serious injury.
    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.
  12. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    Note Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.
  13. With the XRFD unit supported by the hoist kit, unscrew the four screws and remove the slide rails from both sides.
  14. The FRU is packaged in a wooden crate. For the XRFD, there are four parts to the FRU crate. Remove the top of the wooden crate and turn it on its base. The XRFD being removed can be placed into the top. Roll the top underneath the XRFD and lock the wheels so that the container does not move.
  15. Ratchet the winch to lower the XRFD into the package.
  16. Remove the hook from the lifting bracket.
  17. Unscrew the lifting brackets from the sides of the XRFD. Roll the removed XRFD to the side.
  18. Remove the center sections of the FRU crate.
  19. Remove the shipping screw from each side of the XRFD FRU.
  20. Bring the FRU to the front of the cabinet. Attach the slide rails to the FRU.
  21. Attach the lifting brackets to the sides of the new amplifier.
  22. Attach the new XRFD lifting brackets to the winch and spreader bars.
    Note Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.
  23. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chains and begin lifting the unit. Slide out the rail a short amount to see how high to lift the new XRFD. The XRFD slide rails and the cabinet slide rails must align for correct insertion.
  24. When the slide rails are aligned, pull the slide rails out a short distance to get each side started. After both slide rails are attached, pull the slide rails onto the FRU until you hear a click, indicating that the unit is docked in the rails. Ensure the XRFD is secure on the rails
  25. Unhook the winch and spreader bars from the lifting brackets. Remove the lifting brackets from the sides of the new XRFD.
    Note Put the lifting brackets into the XRFD FRU crate.
  26. Slide the XRFD into the cabinet completely.
  27. Attach the XRFD to the cabinet using the front eight attachment screws XRFD attachment screws.
  28. Reconnect the cables and coolant lines to the XRFD.
  29. Note Before continuing, be prepared to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is connected.
    Disassemble the hoist kit (see Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories).
  30. Return the main lifting beam to the PGR cabinet. After the beam is secure, reconnect the XPS J9 connector. Return the support tube to the HEC.
  31. Proceed to Finalization.

XRFD power supply replacement (with PDM)

Procedure

  1. Make sure that you have read and understood the electrical safety requirements before starting this procedure. See Electrical Safety.
  2. Shut down and power off the host computer. See Shutting down the host computer.
  3. Apply PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  4. Before disconnecting any coolant lines on the front of the XRFD unit, shutdown the PPMP at the HEC. Press ▲ F3 simultaneously to stop the pump. Then, switch the PPMP circuit breaker to OFF.
  5. Before setting up the hoist kit, detach the XPS J9 connector to properly remove the main lifting beam Lifting beam and connector in PGR cabinet.
  6. See Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories to set up and secure the hoist.
  7. Remove the tie-wraps that hold the power cable. The cable goes under the XRFD and along the side of the cabinet to the top Tie-Wraps on power cable.
  8. Note Before continuing, be prepared to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is disconnected.
    Disconnect the power cable at the terminal, the cables on the front of the XRFD PS, and the coolant lines. Carefully move the cables and lines to the side of the cabinet, so the surrounding area is free when the PS unit is pulled out Coolant lines placement.
  9. Unscrew the eight large screws that attach the XRFD to the cabinet, the four screws that attach to the manifold, the 22 faceplate screws, and the two BNC locking nuts.
  10. Remove the XRFD PS faceplate. After the faceplate is pulled away from the unit, do these steps:
    1. Loosen the two front thumbscrews to allow the manifold tray to swing up.
    2. Lift the manifold tray and pull it out of the way of the power supply. The manifold has a spring loaded pin that allows the tray to swivel up and down.
    3. Disconnect the two internal coolant lines.
    4. Find the long, thin retaining bracket mounted directly above the supply opening. Remove the screws and then the bracket.
    5. Note If the supply does not slide forward, extend the XRFD from the cabinet and check that the shipping screws have been removed from each side of the XRFD.
      Remove the two sub-D cables, PSU-1 and PSU-2.
  11. Slide out the XRFD PS unit.
  12. Attach the lifting brackets to both sides of the XRFD PS. The lifting brackets can be found in the XRFD PS FRU crate. The attachment screws are captive screws and remain with the lifting brackets.
  13. Hook the winch and spreader bar from the hoist kit to the lifting bracket. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chain.
    Note Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.
  14. The FRU is packaged in a wooden crate. For the XRFD PS, there are three parts to the FRU crate. Remove the top of the wooden crate and turn it on its base. The XRFD PS being removed can be placed into the top. Roll the top underneath the XRFD PS and lock the wheels so that the container does not move.
  15. Ratchet the winch to lower the XRFD PS into the package.
  16. Remove the hook from the lifting bracket.
  17. Unscrew the lifting brackets from the sides of the XRFD. Roll the removed XRFD PS to the side.
  18. Remove the center section of the FRU crate.
  19. Remove the shipping cover from the XRFD PS FRU.
  20. Attach the shipping cover to the top of the removed XRFD PS FRU.
  21. Bring the FRU to the front of the cabinet and attach the lifting brackets to the sides of the new PS.
  22. Attach the new XRFD PS lifting brackets to the winch and spreader bars.
    Note Make sure the hook is seated in the lifting bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or the engineer.
  23. Ratchet the winch to tighten the chains and begin lifting the unit.
  24. Place the XRFD PS into the XRFD.
  25. Unhook the winch and spreader bars from the lifting brackets. Remove the lifting brackets from the sides of the new XRFD PS.
    Note Put the lifting brackets into the XRFD PS FRU crate.
  26. Move the XRFD PS into the XRFD. After the XRFD PS is fully in the XRFD, do these steps:
    1. Attach the two sub-D cables.
    2. Attach the upper retaining bracket.
    3. Connect the two quick disconnect lines.
    4. Release the manifold and swing the tray down.
    5. Tighten the two front thumbscrews.
  27. Attach the XRFD faceplate.
  28. Note Before continuing, be prepared to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is connected.
    Reconnect the cables and coolant lines to the XRFD. For the power cable, use the tie-wraps along the inner edge of the cabinet and along the top so that the cable is safely out of the way.
  29. Attach the XRFD to the cabinet using the front eight attachment screws.
  30. Disassemble the hoist kit (see Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories).
  31. Return the main lifting beam to the PGR cabinet. After the beam is secure, reconnect the XPS J9 connector. Return the support tube to the HEC.
  32. Proceed to Finalization.

  9. Unscrew the eight attachment screws.

    Warning

    Do not put any part of the body below the replacement part.

  20. Remove the center sections of the FRU crate.

    Note Put the lifting brackets into the XRFD FRU crate.
  28. Slide the XRFD into the cabinet completely.
  29. Attach the XRFD to the cabinet using the front eight attachment screws (see XRFD attachment screws).
  30. Reattach the PGR base plate using the two attachment screws (see PGR base plate).
  31. Note Before continuing, be prepared to absorb any leakage from the coolant lines with a towel in hand as the line is connected.
    Reconnect the remaining cables and coolant lines to the XRFD.
  32. Disassemble the hoist kit (see Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories).
  33. Return the main lifting beam to the PGR cabinet. After the beam is secure, reconnect the XPS J9 connector. Return the support tube to the HEC.
  34. Proceed to Finalization.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem LOTO. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Start up the host computer/system. See Starting up the host computer.
  3. Turn on the PPMP circuit breaker at the HEC. Start the pump by pressing ▲ and F3 at the same time.
  4. If the full XRFD was replaced, see Calibrating RF Amplifier and UPM.
    Note XRFD calibration is not necessary if only the XRFD power supply was replaced.
  5. Do Doing the System Performance Test (SPT) (for systems DV26 or earlier).
    Note (For software version MR30.2 and later) If all finalization tasks are not completed, a warning window will show. More information can be found in System Health Alert.
  6. Remove coolant from the old XRFD unit using the manual coolant removal tool. See Coolant Draining.
  7. After attaching the center sections of the FRU package to the removed FRU unit base, attach the top of the FRU crate to prepare the FRU for return.
    Note Make sure the lifting brackets with the attachment screws are in the FRU crate before returning.
    Note The XRFD has two center sections. The XRFD PS only has one center section.
