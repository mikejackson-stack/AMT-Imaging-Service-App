Cradle Latch Assembly Replacement

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1Not applicable45 minutesNot applicable
Table 2. Safety
CAUTION
PINCHED FINGERS
FORWARD MOVEMENT OF CRADLE CAN PINCH FINGER WHILE HOLDING DOWN CRADLE LATCH BLOCK.
MOVE CRADLE FORWARD SLOWLY AND DELIBERATELY UNTIL BOTTOM EDGE OF CRADLE SUFFICIENTLY COVERS TOP OF CRADLE LATCH BLOCK WHERE YOU CAN REMOVE YOUR FINGER FROM BLOCK.

About this task

Overview

This procedure outlines the necessary steps for replacing the cradle latch housing for a 32-channel system or a 16-channel system. Although it should be noted that the 16-Channel system does not allow for the complete removal of the cradle assembly for ease of work.

Topic ID: id_SL3474034-1114808

Removing the Cradle Latch Housing

Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table.
    Note

    Removing the table from the magnet room depends entirely on servicing needs.

  2. Hold down the cradle latch block and press the cradle release button at the same time.
  3. While holding down the cradle latch block and pressing the cradle release button, slide the cradle forward enough so it covers the top area of the latch block. Let go of the cradle latch block and cradle release button.
  4. Push in the right actuator on the table front base.
    Note

    Pressing the actuator button engages the secondary cradle latch cable and causes the pin stop to move down flush with the table top surface, allowing the cradle to slide forward.

  5. For a 16-channel system, prop up the cradle (see below) or remove it from the patient table to perform desired maintenance.
  6. For a 32-channel system, do one of the following:
    OptionDescription
    Remove the cradle from the table

    1. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the rear of the cradle at least 12 inches above the table top to expose the cable take-up assembly.

    2. Remove screws and cable cover.

      Note

      These are specific, pure-brass screws. Do not mix up or replace with standard brass screws.

    3. Remove the four screws.

    4. Remove the two coil connector screws.

    Partially remove the cradle from the table

    1. Slide the cradle forward far enough to clear the cradle guide rail latches at the rear of the table.

    2. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the front of the cradle.

  7. With the cradle raised, remove the cable covers at the head of the cradle held by two screws on each side. See below.
  8. Untie the knot in the Kevlar cord. A scribe or other tool may be needed to loosen the knot. See below.
  9. Remove the screw on the bottom of the release lever that holds the Kevlar cord in place. See above.
  10. Pull out the rivet from the side of the release lever, noting that the Kevlar cord is positioned over the rivet. See below.
  11. Pull the Kevlar cord through the release lever approximately 6 inches. DO NOT pull the cord out of the release lever completely.
  12. Remove the blocks or other structure used to elevate the cradle. Put the cradle back down so that there is approximately 18 inches overhang at the foot of the table. See below.
  13. Pull the cradle hinge pin out. The cradle hinge pin can only be removed on the left side of the table. Push out the pin on the side using large needle nose pliers. See below.
  14. After the hinge pin is removed, slide the front cradle section out. See below.
  15. Remove the rubber latch spring. See below.
  16. Remove the two nylon screws. See below.
  17. Unhook the release lever from the release bar. This is accomplished by pulling the release bar, lifting slightly, and away from the lever. See below. The latch assembly will then be free to pull down and remove.
    Note

    There are two Delrin springs installed on each side of the latch assembly. These springs are required for reuse with the new cradle latch assembly.

  18. Remove the cradle latch assembly from table cradle.
  19. Remove the two plastic screws on top of the removed latch assembly. Then remove the latch blocks. Note the orientation of the angles on the latch blocks for installing the new cradle latch assembly. See below.

Installing the Cradle Latch Assembly

Procedure

  1. Install latch blocks and plastic screws into the new latch assembly. Make sure the screws go through the slots on the latch blocks. See below. When assembled properly, the latch blocks will have limited side-to-side movement but will not fall out of the assembly.
  2. After the new cradle latch assembly is assembled, position the flat side of the latch blocks on the bottom. See below.
  3. Install the new cradle latch assembly into the table as shown below.
  4. Install nylon screws. See Remove Nylon Screws.
  5. Install the new release bar with the new latch assembly. This requires lifting the end of the release lever in order to place the release bar into the guide slot as performed in Step 17.
  6. Replace the rubber latch spring as shown below.
  7. Position the front cradle plunger onto the cradle rod. See below.
  8. Slide the cradle back together. Install the hinge rod. A hammer may be required to tap the hinge rod back into position. See below.
  9. Lift the cradle and support it to gain access to the Kevlar cord. See Propping Up the Back of the Cradle .
  10. Insert the rivet back into the release lever, ensuring that the Kevlar cord is routed OVER the rivet when it is installed as shown Release Lever Rivet . Pull the Kevlar cord taut without causing the release bar to protrude into the latch housing. The illustration below shows that the lever should be pushed toward the front of the cradle when performing this step.
  11. Verify that the release mechanism is not pulled out, then tighten the screw in the release lever.
  12. Tie a new knot in the Kevlar cord running through the release lever. See Kevlar Cord Knot and Release Lever Screw.
  13. Install the covers. See Step 7.
  14. Perform Cradle Release Block Adjustment.

Finalization

Finalization

Verify that the cradle attaches to the LPCA and the latch mechanism operates properly.
