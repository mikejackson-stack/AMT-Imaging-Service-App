High Order Shim Supply Power Down and Lockout/Tagout Procedure

Procedure

  1. Prepare for the shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.
  2. To turn power off, press the power switch at the back of the HO Shim Supply
  3. Remove the power cord from the plug it is plugged into
  4. Using DMM, measure voltage at plug to ensure no power present
  5. Lock out the power plug.
Topic ID: id_SL2050911-1062666

HO Shim Supply Replacement

Procedure

  1. Note

    The HO Shim Supply (NAV version) is light enough to replace without the use of the Hoist Kit HO Shim Supply Replacement (Front View).

    Loosen the 16 thumbs screws securing the penetration panel to the magnet room wall Removing the Penetration Panel. This will allow access to the rear of the Shim Supply.
  2. Remove all cables that attach to the back of the supply HO Shim Supply Replacement (Front View).
  3. Remove 4 screws that attach the shim supply to the cabinet
  4. Carefully slide out and remove the shim supply.
    Note

    Fiber Optic cables are present in front of Shim Supply. You can remove Shim Supply without removing cables, but should be careful about not breaking the Fiber Optic cables.

  5. Install the new supply and secure with 4 screws
  6. Attach all cables/connectors removed in Step 2.
  7. Attach Pen Panel removed in Step 1
  8. Proceed to Step 1.
Topic ID: id_15215743

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from power cable to shim supply.
  2. Plug in shim supply power cable and turn on power switch at back of supply
  3. Reset TPS
  4. If NAV supply is used, proceed to HO Shim Supply Functional Checks and run shim coil continuity check.
  5. Run Goodbye Scan to make sure system is ok. Check this document for details.
