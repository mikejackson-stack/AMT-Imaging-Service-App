About this task

This document provides the procedures for replacing operator control/display hardware in the front magnet enclosure. This document covers both the in-room display (IRD) version and the version without the IRD.

Topic ID: id_SL1996250-1061152

Replacing In-Room Display

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Note

    The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

    Remove the laser light cover. It is held in place by two screws and a popper.
  3. Remove top panel on back of the IRD by popping tabs with a small flat head screwdriver.
  4. Warning
    Strong magnetic field!
    eddy currents can cause resistance when pulling the IRD away from the magnet.
    use caution when removing the ird to prevent it from striking the magnet enclosure.
    Disconnect the three cables attached to the IRD (ground cable, fiber optic cable, and power cable).
  5. Remove four Allen head screws connecting the bracket plate to the back of the IRD. Lift the monitor up and off the bracket carefully, because eddy currents may pull against it.
  6. Mount the new IRD, reconnect the appropriate cables, and replace the covers.
Topic ID: id_SL1996259-1061152

Replacing Trackball Control Panels and E-Stop Button

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Note

    The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

    Remove the front panels. See Front Cover Removal and Installation.
  3. Disconnect the two cables that connect the control panels to the magnet enclosure.
    Note

    If cable connectors are not labeled with a J number, mark the applicable J number on each cable connector before removal.

  4. Warning
    Possible Personal Injury!
    This procedure requires removal of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
    Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.
    Remove the trackball control panel by removing five screws and detaching the emergency stop (e-stop) button cable.
    Note

    If you are only replacing the e-stop button board, disconnect the e-stop cable and remove two screws. Install the new button.

  5. Notice
    On the back of the control panel, the e-stop cable connection and the trackball cable connection are of the same size and gender, so they can be accidentally interchanged. Confirm that the cables are connected properly; otherwise, the e-stop circuit will not be resettable. See Removing Trackball Control Panel and Trackball Connector.
    To install the new trackball control panel, follow Step 2 through Step 4 in reverse order.
Topic ID: id_SL2936723-1061152

Replacing 7-Segment Display (7SD)

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
    Note

    The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  2. Remove the left trim ring. It is held in place by three poppers.
  3. Remove the 7SD bezel. It is held in place by one screw and four poppers.
  4. After the bezel is removed from the electrical assembly, disconnect the safety tether.
  5. Remove the four screws to remove the 7SD from the mounting bracket.
  6. Warning
    Strong magnetic field!
    eddy currents can cause resistance when pulling the 7SD away from the magnet.
    use caution when removing the 7SD to prevent it from striking the magnet enclosure.
    Disconnect the two cables attached to the 7SD. The mounting bracket can be reused.
  7. Mount the new 7SD and reconnect the appropriate cables and safety tether, and then replace the front bezel and trim rings.
Topic ID: id_SL2936733-1061152

Replacing Push Button (Non-Trackball) Control Panel and E-Stop Button

Procedure

  1. Apply LOTO to the PGR PDU/gradient subsystem. See Applying LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Remove the front covers. See Front Cover Removal and Installation.
  3. Disconnect the cable that connects each control panel to the magnet enclosure.
    Note

    If cable connectors are not labeled with a J number, mark the applicable J number on each cable connector before removal.

  4. Remove the 7SD Control Panel by removing five screws, and detaching the e-stop button cable.
    Note

    If you are only replacing the e-stop panel, disconnect the e-stop cable, remove two screws, and install the new button.

  5. To install the new 7SD control panel, follow Step 2 through Step 4 in reverse order.
Topic ID: id_14985891

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove LOTO from the PGR/PDU and power on the PDU. See Removing LOTO - PGR Power Distribution Unit (PDU)/Gradient Subsystem.
  2. Whenever the IRD or trackball control panels are disconnected and then reconnected, a TPS reset must be completed. (This is not necessary if the host computer is rebooted.)
  3. Check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary.

    • For the IRD: From the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Magnet Room > In Room Display.

    • For the control panel: From the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics > Hardware Location > Magnet Room > SRI Functional Tests.
