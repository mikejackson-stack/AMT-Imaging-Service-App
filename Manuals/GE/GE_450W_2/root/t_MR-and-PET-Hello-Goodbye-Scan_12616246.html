About this task

Overview

This procedure runs a check scan on PET-MR system using a Body TLT Phantom to ensure PET and MR data acquisition.

Procedure

  1. Retrieve and place a Body TLT Sphere and Loader on the patient table, and landmark the center of the sphere.
  2. Click the New Patient icon.
    1. Type geservice for the Patient ID.
    2. Type a recognizable name, such as Hello Goodbye into the Patient Name field.
    3. Type values into the Weight (111 lb) and Height (5' 0") fields.
  3. Click the Service radio button (1) on the Protocol Selector screen.
  4. Select the PET-MR Hello Goodbye Scan protocol (2) from the list, then click the Accept button (3).
  5. Click the Start Exam button.
  6. Make sure 3-plane Localizer FG... is selected (1), then click the Save Rx (2) and Scan buttons (3).
  7. When the localizer scan is done, check the images on the image viewer by sliding the bar on the right side of the screen to scroll through the image sequence.
  8. Select PET Task (1), then click the Setup button (2).
  9. When the PET Graphic Rx Toolbar displays, click the Boundary icon (3).
    Note

    The PET Graphic RX Toolbar screen changes to illustrate the scan boundaries for PET.

  10. Click the Save Rx, then the Scan buttons shown in MR Localizer Component.
  11. Select the PET Scan Status tab to monitor the scan progression. Scan is completed when the message, Scan completed normally, displays in the message area at the bottom of the screen.
  12. Select the Image Management icon on the screen header.
  13. Review the PET images resulting from the completed Hello Goodbye Scan.
  14. End the exam when the scan is satisfactorily completed.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
