Forward Reverse Quadrature Check

About this task

This section determines the quadrature balance of the quad coil and the phase splitter network. In this test, the body TLT sphere phantom is scanned with the quadrature coil using two different methods. First, an image is taken with the quadrature coil as presently connected. Next, the cables to the quadrature switches are reversed, and the phantom is scanned a second time using the same protocol. The resulting images are analyzed to verify proper quadrature drive function and cable configuration. This procedure consists of the following:

  • Body forward/reverse quadrature scans

  • Forward/reverse quadrature test image analysis

Body Forward/Reverse Quadrature Scans

Procedure

  1. To prepare the system for scan, set the following parameters:
    1. Click New Pt and then specify these settings:

      1. ID: geserviceEnter

      2. Name: body f/r quadEnter

      3. Weight (lb): 111Enter

    2. Set patient protocols to Service.
    3. In the Patient Protocols section, click Service and then click Other.
    4. Locate 49_24-f_r_quadrature, click the protocol name, and then click the arrow in between to add it to the Multi Protocol List or remove it.
    5. Click Accept to download the series.
  2. At the scanner, remove any surface or head coil from the bore.
  3. Place the body TLT sphere phantom in the SPT body loader and place the body loader/TLT phantom in the center of the cradle.
  4. Landmark on the center of the sphere. On the front of the magnet enclosure, press LANDMARK on the keypad, and then press ADV TO SCAN.
  5. Start the exam, then select Save Rx.

    • Select Manual Prescan from the Scan option drop-down list.

    • Set TG=100, confirm R1=13 and R2=28, and then select Done to exit.

    • Select Scan.

  6. Disable RF output by placing the RF Enable switch on the DTX1 exciter, located in the top of the PEN cabinet, into the Disable (down) position.
  7. Locate the hybrid splitter.
  8. Enable RF output by placing the RF Enable switch on the DTX1 exciter into the Enable (up) position.
  9. Without changing the prescan settings, scan the phantom again using the same scan protocol and save the images to a new series.
  10. At the top of the screen, select End and select End Exam.

Forward/Reverse Quadrature Test Image Analysis

About this task

This analysis procedure applies only to body scans.

Procedure

  1. Display each image so you can compare mean values.
  2. Use an ROI of 4000 mm2 ± 50 mm2, located at the center of the image. Measure the mean pixel value (MPV1). In the viewer, this is shown as m=xxx. Write down this value.

    Compare the mean values of the images. The image with higher mean value is forward quadrature. Ensure that the cables are connected so that the system is in forward quadrature mode.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Disable RF output by placing the RF Enable switch on the DTX1 exciter into the Disable (down) position.
  2. Enable RF output by placing the RF Enable switch on the DTX1 exciter into the Enable (up) position.
  3. Perform a check scan (good-bye scan) to verify that the system is operating properly. See Check Scan.
