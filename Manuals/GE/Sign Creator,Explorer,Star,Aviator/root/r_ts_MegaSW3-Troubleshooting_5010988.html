MegaSW3 Troubleshooting

Normally there are three kinds of failure mode related with Megaswitch3:

  1. TPS reset fail with energy too low, or DVMR link not ready to send. Issue1: TPS Reset Failed

  2. Low SNR issue Issue2: Coil SNR is Low

  3. Coil ID not identified or there is a new coil connected. Issue3: System can’t identify Coil ID, report a new coil is connected
