About this task

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test using the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Wrist Array by Invivo.

Topic ID: id_SL2976019-1066132

Legacy Phantom Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the base plate on the table as shown in Figure 1.
  2. Attach the base plate riser to the base plate as shown in Figure 2 and Figure 3.
  3. Attach the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Wrist Array to the cradle . Note the serial number of the coil. Figure Figure 4.
  4. Slide the locked coil to center position and mechanically locate and align. Figure 5.
  5. Place phantom in the coil. Figure 5.
  6. Lock the coil using the lock on inferior top lock. Figure 6.
  7. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil. Advance to scan. Figure 6.
  8. Click here to go to the MCQA Test.
Topic ID: id_SL2976020-1066132

Unified Phantom Setup

Procedure

  1. Place the baseplate of Wrist coil on the patient table as shown in Figure 7.
  2. Attach the baseplate riser to the baseplate as shown in the Figure 8. Baseplate and the Baseplate riser in the locked position are shown in Figure 9.
  3. Attach 1.5T HD 8Ch Wrist Array to the baseplate assembly using the knobs is as shown in Figure 10. Slide the locked coil to centre and align properly.
  4. Place the cubical unified phantom in the coil by lifting the upper portion of the coil as shown in the Figure 11. Close the coil and lock it (lock in Figure 12.
  5. Connect the coil to respective port of the LPCA and landmark the coil at the marks shown using arrows as shown in Figure 12 and advance to scan.
  6. Click here to go to the MCQA Test
