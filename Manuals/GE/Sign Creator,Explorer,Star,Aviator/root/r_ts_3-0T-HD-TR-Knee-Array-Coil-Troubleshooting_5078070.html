3.0T HD T/R Knee Array Coil Troubleshooting

Topic ID: id_SL1915225-1058351

Personnel Requirements

Required Persons Procedure
130 minutes
Topic ID: id_SL1915226-1058351

Overview

The following information can be used to troubleshoot common problems with the 3.0T HD T/R Knee Array Coil by Invivo, catalog M3335LB (for curved table) or catalog M7000GS (for flat table).

Topic ID: id_SL1915227-1058351

Tools and Test Equipment

Digital volt meter (DVM)

Topic ID: id_SL1915228-1058351

Required Conditions

The HD TR Knee PA coil configuration name must be installed to run this tool.

Topic ID: id_SL1915231-1058351

Procedure

Topic ID: id_SL1915232-1058351

Receiving No Signal

Problem:

Unable to prescan, or scanning and not receiving a signal.

Possible Solution:

  • Ensure the appropriate system coil is selected. Refer to the Operator Manual for additional information.

  • Ensure the green light above port A is illuminated. This indicates the coil is properly plugged into the system.

  • Ensure the landmark is correct and the cradle did not unlatch.

  • Ensure the scan locations and any FOV offsets are correct.

  • Perform a cable continuity check on the external cable Cable Continuity Check. The continuity check must be performed by a GE authorized Service Engineer.

  • Ensure the coil is positioned with the cable exiting toward the bore, and the system and coil polarity match.

Topic ID: id_SL1915239-1058351

Image Quality

Problem:

Image quality is not what is expected, given the parameters selected.

Possible Solution:

  • Review the selected protocol. If performing the periodic quality assurance, be sure the protocol is identical to the protocol provided in the system recommended protocols. If performing diagnostic imaging, NEX or FOV may need to be increased.

  • Perform a cable continuity check on the external cable (see Cable Continuity Check). The continuity check must be performed by a GE authorized Service Engineer.

  • Ensure there are no loops in the cables.

  • Ensure there are no metal or ferromagnetic objects close to the coil, patient, or magnet (such as safety pin or hair pin).

  • Ensure the coil is properly positioned.

  • Ensure the center frequency is within the frequency adjustment range for the system.

  • Ensure the R1, R2, and TG values from the prescan are within normally expected ranges.

Cleaning the electrical contacts that connect the upper and lower halves with isopropyl alcohol and a cotton swab sometimes improves the coil’s SNR. If not done already, perform a MCQA test. If the values obtained do not fall within normal operating parameters, investigate further by performing a phantom scan with the body coil. For this test, be sure to remove the imaging coil from the magnet bore before scanning with the body coil. If the same problems occur, the issue is probably with the MR system. If the body coil scan is satisfactory, acquire a scan using another coil of the same type plugged into the same system port (port A). If the image quality is visibly improved, there may be a problem with the Invivo coil. Contact GE for further assistance. If the image quality still suffers, there may be a system problem related to imaging with this type of coil.

Topic ID: id_SL1915247-1058351

Artifacts

Problem:

There is a black line or signal void on the image.

Possible Solution

Verify there is no metal present in the area being scanned or on the patient.

Problem:

Some or all of the images appear shaded or exhibit uneven signal or banding.

Possible Solution

Confirm that no metallic objects are located nearby, outside the FOV. This is especially important on images utilizing fat saturation.

If fat saturation is being used, verify that the CFA fine adjustment is optimized.

Topic ID: id_SL1915248-1058351

External Cable Wear

Problem:

The system does not recognize the coil, or scan with the coil attached.

Possible Solution:

Perform the cable continuity check (see Cable Continuity Check).

Mechanical Parts Wear

Inspect the coil for visible signs of wear. Verify that the coil housings seat with each other and that the latch mechanism works properly.
