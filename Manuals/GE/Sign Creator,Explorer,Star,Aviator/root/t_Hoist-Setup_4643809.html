Warning
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE!
THE MODULE COULD FALL WHEN USING IS LIFT TOOL.
Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly. This will help keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.
Notice
There are two types of hoist bracket.
Hoist Bracket for SRFD: Use 5160839 bracket including the hoist kit.
Hoist Bracket for XFA: Use 5160838 in SC.

Procedure

  1. Remove R and L Top Finish Plates.
  2. Remove L top cover by loosening 10 screws.
  3. Remove Support Bracket assembly on the top of the cabinet top plate of System Cabinet by removing 4 screws (M6x25).
  4. Set up Hoist tool. De-assembly Support Brackets by removing 3 screws.
  5. Fix the bigger Bracket on the top of System Cabinet by fixing 4 screws.
  6. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections and place them on a flat surface for assembly.
  7. Fix Rail and Brackets by fixing 3 screws
    Note:

    Rail is depending on the type of Fixed Site Lift Tool Kit.

    Note:

    After fix 3 screws, please remove the rail. At this location, the rail is not mandatory.

  8. Put large steel rail on the top of the System Cabinet and insert it bracket.
  9. Fixing another bracket and rail by 2 screws.
    Note:

    Make sure that two yellow screws are tighten.

  10. Locate the hoist assembly in the lift kit.
  11. Insert the hoist assembly into the front end of hoist rail, and then insert a locking pin into the hole of front rail. This locking pin prevents the hoist assembly from sliding out of the rail.

Finalization

  1. After replacement is completed, restore it by reverse order.
