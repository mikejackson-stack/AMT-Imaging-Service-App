Procedure

  1. Move the cradle In by 400mm.
  2. Remove the panel screws and bent down the Panel.
  3. Actuate the sensor Pin on the Tabletop manually.
  4. Ensure that the Push rod engages the Limit switch Dog. If its not engaging properly adjust the Push rod length by taking in/out so that it engages properly. Tighten the Push Rod Lock nut after the adjustment.
  5. Ensure that the sensor cables are connected properly with the Limit switch.
  6. Check for the home sensor function by pressing the Down pedal near dockside. If still the Home positions inter-Lock not functioning, then need to change the Control Box or the Home sensor assembly kit. Follow Cradle Home sensor replacement procedure, section 6B of this document, to replace the Cradle Home sensor.
  7. Fix back the Bottom panels by properly inserting the Side and Rear bumpers to Panel and FRP.
  8. Tighten the Screws of the Tabletop panels.

No finalization steps.
