1.5T & 3.0T HDx LPCA Forward Production Systems

About this task

The removal of the MNS specific hardware in the LPCA and Rear Pedestal was implemented into forward production systems in June 2008. If the system was manufactured prior to this date then the hardware should already be present. This can be verified by looking at the front of the LPCA and checking for the presence of the MNS connectors located between the Port A and Port C connectors.

Topic ID: id_SL2770925-1090499

Pre-Conditions

About this task

Procedure

  1. Move the LPCA to the rear of the magnet so it is parked on the rear pedestal.
  2. Perform LOTO to the system to disable RF Output and Cradle Drive.
  3. Make sure you have the two commercial catalogs, G3335CC MNS Cable Kit and G3335CD LPCA Covers, on site before starting this procedure.
    Note:

    The contents of these catalogs have parts to support a number of Signa configurations so there will be parts that will not be used and can be discarded at the completion of these procedures.

Topic ID: id_SL2770929-1090499

LPCA Changes

Procedure

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.
    Note:

    The original LPCA cover will be replaced with a new cover for the MNS option.

  2. Remove the belt from the LPCA.
  3. Tilt the cradle base to one side and remove the 2 flat head screws that secure the standoffs to the base of the cradle assembly. See Figure 1. Perform this step again tilting the cradle base to the other side to access the remaining 2 screws.
  4. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10.
  5. Remove cable harness assembly part number 5141260-2 by disconnecting Run 1338 from the 9-pin sub-D connector at J3. See Figure 2.
  6. Dismount the 9-pin sub-D connector and disconnect the plugs attached to the Red and Green LED’s at Port C. Remove this cable harness. See Figure 3.
  7. Install new cable harness part number 5141260 by feeding the 9 pin sub-D connector down through the hole located behind the MNS Connector Bracket.
  8. Install the 9-pin sub-D to location J3 where it was previously removed.
  9. Re-connect Run 1338 to J3.
  10. Install the SMA to BNC Bulkhead, part number 2392448 to J2. See Figure 2 for location. This view shows the backside of the bracket looking at the BNC connector of the bulkhead part.
  11. Attach the harness SMA connector to the newly installed bulkhead connector at J2. See Figure 2.
  12. Attach the LED connector plugs to the red and green LED’s on Port C. See Figure 3.
  13. Install the other end of the cable harness to the MNS Connector Bracket at location J2. See Figure 3 for connector location.
    Note:

    The face of the connector has a flat side, which must be oriented at the bottom of the opening so the connector will match the opening in new LPCA cover.

  14. Re-install the 4 flat head screws to secure the standoffs to the cradle assembly base.

Topic ID: id_SL2770947-1090499

Cable Track Changes

Procedure

  1. Locate Cable assembly 5141978 and Cable 5112836 in the MNS Cable Collector Kit.
    Note:

    Both of these cables will terminate at different locations within the LPCA and will be installed within Cable Track B and routed to their final destinations within the Rear Pedestal. See Figure 5 for cable track identification.

  2. Prepare Cable Track B for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Figure 5.
  3. Using the BNC end of cable 5141978, feed it down through the hole in the top plate and attach it to LPCA J2. See Figure 6.
  4. Install the cable in Cable Track B and route it down to 16 Channel Switch #1 – J23.
    Note:

    The cable must plug into 16 Channel Switch #1 on a 32 Channel System.

  5. Plug the connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.
  6. Install the BNC end of the cable 5112836 into the MNS Connector Bracket at location J1. See Figure 6.
  7. Install the cable in Cable Track B and route it down to the Rear Ped I/O Panel J3.
  8. Connect the cable to J3.
  9. Close all the latches on Cable Track B to secure the cables in the track.
Topic ID: id_SL2770957-1090499

LPCA Cover Change

Procedure

  1. Install the new LPCA Cover, part number 5165205, that has the holes for the MNS connectors on the front face and the MNS brackets on the top.
  2. Follow your local processes for disposal of the old LPCA cover.
    Note:

    This completes the hardware upgrade to the LPCA. Follow the MNS upgrade instructions that were delivered with the other hardware. Once complete perform the Multi Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) Functional Checks found in the Installation/Spectroscopy folder on Service Methods DVD for the system.

LPCA and Cable Upgrade

About this task

Refer to Appendix A – Interconnect Diagrams, Figure 20 for “Before” and “After” Interconnect Diagrams.

Procedure

  1. Prepare Cable Track for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Figure 9 for Latch operation and cable track identification.
    Note:

    On dual track systems use track “B” as identified in Figure 9.

  2. Disconnect and remove the cable from the cable track, which is routed between the 16 Channel Switch J23 and Head T/R Assembly J4. See Figure 10.
    Note:

    Depending on cable length the part number for this cable is either 5110699-3 or 5110699-4.

  3. Locate the following cables listed in Table 4, which are part of the MNS Cable Collector Kit.
    Table 4. Cable Descriptions
    Part # Run # Routing Cable Track Type
    51128361153LPCA MNS J1 to Rear Ped I/O Panel J3Short Cable Track
    5112836-21153LPCA MNS J1 to Rear Ped I/O Panel J3Long Cable Track
    5110699N/AMG2 LPCA J2 & Head T/R Assembly J4 to 16 Ch Switch J23Short Cable Track
    5110699-2N/AMG2 LPCA J2 & Head T/R Assembly J4 to 16 Ch Switch J23Long Cable Track
    Note:

    Both of these cables will terminate at different locations within the LPCA and will be installed within Cable Track B (on dual track systems) and are routed to their final destinations within the Rear Pedestal.

    Note:

    Use the appropriate length of cable as determined in Table 4 to match your system.

  4. Install the BNC end of cable Run 1153 into LPCA MNS J1. Verify that the rubber O-ring is seated on the back of the connection. See Figure 11.
  5. Route cable Run 1153 into the cable track and down to the Rear Pedestal I/O panel, then make the connection to J3 as shown in Figure 7.
  6. Install cable part 5112834 into the top position on the MNS Bracket part 5113332.
    Note:

    The face of the connector has a flat side, which must be oriented at the bottom of the opening so the connector will match the opening in new LPCA cover.

  7. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10 in the location shown in Figure 11.
  8. Install the SMA-BNC Bulkhead connector, part 2392448 to the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket part 5113337. See Figure 11.
  9. Install the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket, part 5113337 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10. See Figure 11.
  10. Attach the SMA connector, coming from MNS LPCA J2 to the newly installed bulkhead connector at MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Figure 11.
  11. Route Cable 5110699 (or 5110699-2 version for longer track) into the cable track.
    Note:

    The BNC connectors will terminate within the LPCA.

  12. Plug the combo D-Dsub connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.
  13. Attach the right angle BNC connector to J4 on the Head T/R Assembly as shown in Figure 10.
  14. Attach the straight BNC connector to MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Figure 11.
  15. Secure the cables with ty-wraps and close up the latches on the cable track.

LPCA and Cable Upgrade

About this task

Refer to Appendix A – Interconnect Diagrams, Figure 21 for “Before” and “After” Interconnect Diagrams.

Procedure

  1. See Figure 14 for cable track identification on Dual Track Systems.
  2. Prepare Cable Track (“B” on dual track systems) for the cable installation by using a flat blade screwdriver inserted into the slot on the side and rotating the screwdriver to open the latch. See Figure 14.
  3. Disconnect and remove the cable from the cable track, which is routed between the 16 Channel Switch J23 and Head T/R Assembly J4. See Figure 15.
    Note:

    Depending on cable length the part number for this cable is either 5110699-3 or 5110699-4.

  4. Disconnect and remove cable assembly 5115269-2. See Figure 16.
    Note:

    Without MNS this cable connects to three places:

    1. SMB port J1 on the side of the Head T/R Assembly.

    2. 9-Pin Sub-D port J11 on the front of the MultiCoil Assembly.

    3. Coil ID LED’s on the Microphone bracket.

  5. Locate the following cables listed in Table 5, which are part of the MNS Cable Collector Kit.
    Table 5. Cable Descriptions
    Part # Run # Routing Cable Track Type
    5115269N/ALPCA MNS J2 to MG2 LPCA J2 and Head T/R Assembly SMB Port J1 andMultiCoil Assembly Port J11

    Coil ID LED’s on Microphone Bracket

    		N/A
    51128361153LPCA MNS J1 to Rear Ped I/O Panel J3Short Cable Track
    5112836-21153LPCA MNS J1 to Rear Ped I/O Panel J3Long Cable Track
    5110699N/AMG2 LPCA J2 & Head T/R Assembly J4 to 16 Ch Switch J23Short Cable Track
    5110699-2N/AMG2 LPCA J2 & Head T/R Assembly J4 to 16 Ch Switch J23Long Cable Track
    Note:

    Use the appropriate length of cable as determined in Table 5 to match your systems cable track.

  6. Install the BNC end of cable Run 1153 into LPCA MNS J1. Verify that the rubber O-ring is seated on the back of the connection. See Figure 17.
  7. Route cable Run 1153 into the cable track and down to the Rear Pedestal I/O panel, then make the connection to J3 as shown in Figure 12.
  8. Install cable part 5115269 into the top position on the MNS Bracket part 5113332.
    Note:

    The face of the connector has a flat side, which must be oriented at the bottom of the opening so the connector will match the opening in new LPCA cover.

  9. Install the MNS Cable Connector Bracket, part 5113332 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10 in the location shown in Figure 17.
  10. Install the SMA-BNC Bulkhead connector, part 2392448 to the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket part 5113337. See Figure 17.
  11. Install the SMA-BNC Connector Bracket, part 5113337 to the Connector Support Plate using qty 2, part number 2358526, Slotted Pan Head Screw Brass M4X10. See Figure 17.
  12. Attach the SMA connector, coming from MNS LPCA J2 to the newly installed bulkhead connector at MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Figure 18.
  13. Route Cable 5110699 (or 5110699-2 version for longer track) into the cable track.
    Note:

    The BNC connectors will terminate within the LPCA.

  14. Plug the combo D-Dsub connector into port J23 on the 16 Channel Switch.
  15. Attach the right angle BNC connector to J4 on the Head T/R Assembly as shown in Figure 15.
  16. Attach the straight BNC connector to MG2 LPCA J2 as shown in Figure 16.
  17. Connect the SMB connector coming from MNS Connector J2 into the Head T/R assembly port J1. See Figure 18.
  18. Connect the 9 pin sub-D connector into the MultiCoil assembly J11.
  19. Connect the red and green LED wires into the Red and Green LED’s on the microphone bracket.
  20. Secure the cables with ty-wraps and close up the latches on the cable track.
