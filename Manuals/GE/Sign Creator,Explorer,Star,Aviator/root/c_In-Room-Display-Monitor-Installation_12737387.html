In Room Display Monitor Installation

This is the installation procedure for installing the cables, brackets, and In Room Display (IRD) Monitor for the system.

  1. Attach the Cable Tie Mount Base (P/N 46-208746P9) to the plate with the M4 X 10 screw (P/N 2109873-14).

  2. Ensure the air sensor bracket is attached to the plate.

  3. Attach Run M3366 to the arm with one M6 X 25 hex socket-head cap screw (P/N 2109867-15), but do not tighten at this time.

  4. Route the ground wire and fiber-optic cable adjacent to the main cable, one on each side. Do not tie wrap either one to the tie mount; both are to remain free sliding.

  5. Slide the tie-wrap through the mount to hold the cable in place, but do not tighten.

  6. Route the fiber-optic cable to the top of the magnet, and connect to J28 on the fiber-optic I/O board.

  7. Connect the Monitor Cable Assembly ground wire to the front ground stud using one brass M10 x 16 screw nut and one M10 stainless steel washer (P/N 2109878-4).

  8. Locate the IRD Pivot Assembly (P/N 5394815).

  9. Attach the IRD Pivot Assembly to the IRD mounting plate using two M6 X 40 hex socket-head cap screws (P/N 1000-M6C040-31) and M6 hex nuts (P/N 3002-M6C-31).

  10. Route the cable labeled MAG-Track Ball 1 to the left side at the front of the magnet.

  11. Route the cable labeled MAG-Track Ball 2 to the right side at the front of the magnet

  12. Route the cable labeled MAG-J2 to the top of the magnet, and connect to J2 on the magnet I/O board.

  13. Remove the cable clamp from Run M3366 cable assembly, and insert a small screwdriver into the side of the clamp labeled Open until the clamp releases.

  14. Attach the cable clamp to the Pivot Assembly with the M6 X 12 screw (P/N 2109873-24).

  15. Locate the IRD monitor. Loosen, but do not remove, the top two screws on the back of the monitor.

  16. Remove the two bottom screws on the back of the monitor, and position the monitor on IRD monitor mounting plate.

  17. Apply Loctite® 242 to the mounting screws.

  18. Fasten the two bottom screws and tighten the two top screws to secure the monitor to the mounting plate with a hex wrench No. 3.

  19. Secure the cable assembly into the cable clamp on top of the bracket, and close the clamp to secure the cable.

  20. Connect the three Run M3366 cables (Ground, Fiber-optic and Power) to the back of the monitor.
