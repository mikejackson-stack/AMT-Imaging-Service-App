In this procedure, Body Coil position will be centered to the inner Gradient Coil.

Measurement will be performed at 4 locations for both Front and Rear.

Since there are former supports at 0 degree, 90 degree, and 270 degree positions and it prevents the correct measurement, the gauge will be placed next to the screws near former support (Position A, C, and D in illustration below). At 180 degree (Position B), there is no former support and measurement will be done at 180 degree position.

1 Body Coil AP Alignment

Procedure

  1. If not done, disconnect the two (2) cooling air ducts at the rear end of the body coil.
  2. Find Step Gauge which is placed at rear mounting bracket and remove it.
  3. Confirm that screws and nuts shown in the following illustration are tightened for Front side and Rear side.
  4. At front end, check the gaps between Body Coil and Inner Gradient Coil at 2 vertical measurement locations (A and B) using Step Gauge.

    Spec: Differential of Gap (A) and Gap (B) ≤ 0.5mm (Identical or no more than 1 Adjacent Step).

    Options of 1 Adjacent Step: a) 9.5 & 10; b) 10 & 10.5; c) 10.5 & 11; d) 11 & 11.5, e) 11.5 & 12

    Table 6. Gap Option Matrix
    If gap at A = Then gap at B must =
    9.5 9.5 or 10
    10 9.5 or 10 or 10.5
    10.5 10 or 10.5 or 11
    11 10.5 or 11 or 11.5
    11.5 11 or 11.5 or 12
    12 11.5 or 12
    note:

    There are several notes when measuring the gap.

    1. Since there is a former support at 0 degree position, place the gauge next to the screw shown in illustration below (Position A).

    2. Push fully to the inner bore side so that the gauge stops at inner gradient coil.

      Check that the gauge is not stopped at the level difference between bore light panel and hard body coil.

    3. Read the gauge at the edge of body coil.

    4. Insert side A first (Figure 5). If the gauge stops at 10.5 position, insert side B. If the gauge stops before 11 position line, the gap is 10.5.

  5. If the gaps does not satisfy the specification in step 4, adjust AP position using RF Body Coil support to meet the specification.
    1. Loosen two M10 Hex Nuts.
    2. Loosen two M10 Hex Socket screws.
    3. Adjust AP position by two M10 Hex Socket screw using Allen wrench.
      note:

      1 CW turn → 1.5mm up

    4. Fix two M10 Hex Socket screws.
    5. Fix two M10 Hex Nuts for lock of M10 Hex Socket screws.
  6. Repeat Step 4 and Step 5 at Rear end.

2 Body Coil RL Alignment

Procedure

  1. Confirm that screws and nuts shown in the following illustration are tightened for Front side and Rear side.
  2. At front end, check the gaps between Body Coil and Inner Gradient Coil at 2 horizontal measurement locations using Step Gauge. Slide the Step Gauge between the hard body coil outside diameter and the hard gradient coil inside diameter until it will not slide any further. The value on the last step to slide between the body coil and the gradient coil indicates the gap. The step gauge is marked in ‘mm’.

    Spec: Differential of Gap (C) and (D) ≤ 0.5mm (Identical or no more than 1 Adjacent Step).

    Options of 1 Adjacent Step: a) 9 & 9.5; b) 9.5 & 10; c) 10 & 10.5; d) 10.5 & 11, e) 11 & 11.5

    Table 7. Gap Option Matrix
    If gap at C = Then gap at D must =
    9.5 9.5 or 10
    10 9.5 or 10 or 10.5
    10.5 10 or 10.5 or 11
    11 10.5 or 11 or 11.5
    11.5 11 or 11.5 or 12
    12 11.5 or 12
    note:

    There are several notes when measuring the gap.

    1. Since there is a former support at 90 degree and 270 degree position, place the gauge next to the screw shown in illustration below (Position C and D).

    2. Push fully to the inner bore side so that the gauge stops at inner gradient coil.

      Check that the gauge is not stopped at the level difference between bore light panel and hard body coil.

    3. Read the gauge at the edge of body coil.

    4. Insert side A first (Figure 9). If the gauge stops at 10.5 position, insert side B. If the gauge stops before 11 position line, the gap is 10.5.

  3. If the gaps does not satisfy the specification in step 1, adjust RL position using RF Body Coil support to meet the specification.
    1. Loosen screws.
    2. Adjust RL position using allen wrench.
      note:

      • When centering at Front side, access from right hand side, and access from left hand side at Rear. See illustration below.

      • 1 CW turn → 1.5mm push

    3. Fix screws.
  4. Repeat Step1 and Step2 at Rear end.

3 Final Check

Procedure

  1. After re-tightening any loosened nuts, check RL position and AP position of Front End and Rear End again per Body Coil AP Alignment and Body Coil RL Alignment and adjust the position if necessary until the specifications are satisfied.
  2. Restore Step Gauge at rear mounting bracket.
  3. If you are doing RF Body Coil Replacement Procedure, return to the original procedure. Changing RF Body coil isolation/alignment will affect coil tuning
