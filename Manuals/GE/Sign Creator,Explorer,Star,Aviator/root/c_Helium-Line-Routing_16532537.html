Helium Line Routing

  • notice
  • It is recommended to perform Helium Line Routing before connection of Helium Line to the coldhead. Please refer to the Magnet Manual for Helium Line connection.

For R series magnet:

  1. Loosen screws of each bracket.

  2. Route Helium Line as shown in Figure 1.

  3. Tighten screws of each bracket.

  • notice
  • Please ensure the bumper of the helium pipe is on the side of cold head.

For RD series magnet:

  1. Unfasten the Velcro belt on magnet.

  2. Route Helium Line.

  3. Fasten the Velcro belt.
