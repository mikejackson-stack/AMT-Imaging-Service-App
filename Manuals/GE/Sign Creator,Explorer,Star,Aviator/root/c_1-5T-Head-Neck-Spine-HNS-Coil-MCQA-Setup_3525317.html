1.5T Head Neck Spine (HNS) Coil MCQA Setup

Topic ID: id_SL2345531-718255

Personnel Requirements

Required Persons Procedure
145 mins
Topic ID: id_SL2345532-718255

Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test.

Note:

The coil consists of five units: Posterior Head, Face, Chest, Horseshoe, and TL. This is done to ensure modularity in design and diagnostics. The MCQA test for the coil is conducted to three configurations: TL unit only, Posterior Head Chest Horseshoe TL, and Posterior Head Face.

Note:

Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

HNS Unified Phantom MCQA Setup

Topic ID: id_SL2964881-718255

Mode 1: Posterior Head Face Section

  1. Place Posterior Head Face section on the table as shown in Figure 6.

  2. Place HNS positioner and then place large cylindrical unified phantom on the positioner as shown in Figure 7.

  3. Carefully align the face component part of the coil with the connector pins over this posterior head face section and latch both the coil sections into place as shown in Figure 8. (The scanner will not operate if the coil sections are not latched correctly.)

  4. Connect the coil to the system LPCA.

  5. Landmark the coil on the markings present on the face component indicated through arrows as shown in Figure 9 and press advance to scan.

  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.

    Note: In the MCQA tool, select 1.5T_HNS_FP_by_GE_PostHeadChestHorseshoeTL.Unified if it is a Silent HNS coil , otherwise select 1.5T_L_HNS_FP_by_GE_PostHeadChestHorseshoeTL.Unified.
    Note: If the wrong coil database selection is made, MCQA will not be able to proceed.
Topic ID: id_SL2964888-718255

Mode 2: Posterior Head Chest, Horseshoe and TL Section

  1. Place the Posterior Head Face section, horseshoe adapter, and TL section of the coil on table as shown in Figure 10.

  2. Place the HNS phantom Positioner, large cylindrical unified phantom, and TL unified phantom as shown in Figure 11. The TL unified phantom shall be positioned over the junction of the TL section and head components.

  3. Ensure that TL unified phantom is in contact with the cylindrical unified phantom as shown in Figure 12.

  4. Connect the chest part to the posterior head section as shown in Figure 13.

  5. Landmark on the markings over the chest section of the coil as indicated in Figure 14.

  6. Press advance to scan.

  7. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.

Topic ID: id_SL2964897-718255

Mode 3: TL Section

  1. Place the TL section of the HNS coil on the table as shown in Figure 15.

  2. Place TL unified phantoms on the TL section of the coil as shown in Figure 16.

  3. Align the TL unified phantoms properly in S/I direction and R/L direction of the TL section of the coil as shown in Figure 17.

  4. Connect the coil to Port A of system LPCA.

  5. Landmark on #5 present on the side of the TL section of the coil as shown in Figure 18 and press advance to scan.

  6. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool test.
