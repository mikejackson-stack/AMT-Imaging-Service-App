Safety

Before working in any GE Healthcare MR suite or performing any GE Healthcare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE Healthcare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE Healthcare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

Procedure

  1. Release the bands from the shipping base, flip the side plate down, and roll out the FRU box.
  2. Open the ICC front doors far enough to remove the cooling unit.
    note: The front doors can also be removed if there is not enough space to open it all the way. Disconnect the ground wire and lift the door up off the hinges and away from the cabinet.
  3. Make sure the hoist is installed. Refer to Installing the hoist.
    The hoist beam is stored on top of the ISC cabinet. It is secured in place with two captive fasteners.
  4. Return the ICC control unit back to its original position and secure it in place with the retaining screws previously removed.

    1. Prepare a 20L draining tank and a draining hose with a quick disconnect (supplied with the ICC).
    2. Insert the quick disconnect to the pump drain valve.
      Once connected, the valve inside will open automatically and the tank will start to drain.
    3. Repeat for the dirt trap drain valve and the heat exchanger drain valve.
  6. Hook the winch from the hoist kit to the hoist bracket.
    note: Make sure the hook is seated in the hoist bracket. If not, the weight can shift causing harm to the equipment or engineer.
  7. Ratchet the winch to slightly tighten the chain just enough to take the weight off of the slide rails.
  8. Press the release tabs in on each side and push the outer slide rail back into the ICC.
    note: Make sure the outer slide rails are completely inside the cabinet before lowering the cooling unit.
  9. Line up the slide rails and insert one into the other until they click into place.
    note: Slightly push the outer slide rails back (towards the ICC) to make sure they are securely attached.
  10. Carefully take tension off the winch to loosen and unhook the chain.
  11. Disassemble the hoist kit and return it to the storage location. Refer to Removing the hoist.
  12. Locate the facility plumbing unit (FPU) at the bottom right corner of the ICC.
  13. Close both the facility supply and return valves using the blue handles.
  14. Close the small black valve from the top of the FPU, for the cryocooler compressor water supply lines.
  15. Disconnect the FPU sensor cable connector coming from the XJ33 on the ICC control unit.
    Make sure the FPU sensor cable is out of the way before removing the FPU.
  16. Remove the MS5 A5 water flow sensor cable from the cryocooler compressor and move it away from the FPU removal.
  17. Drain the ICC primary water circuit into a 20L draining tank.
    note: The water/glycol mixture must be filled into an approved container and be disposed of properly or reused.
  18. Connect one end of the draining hose to the drain valve.
  19. Insert the other end of the draining hose into the 20L draining tank and open the drain valve.
  20. Open the city water supply valve.
    note: Leave the city water supply valve open.
  21. Open the cryocooler compressor water return valve slightly to allow the air into the circuit. Use a towel at the city water supply valve to collect any water leakage.
  22. When the coolant has finished draining from the drain valve, disconnect the draining hose and connect it to the city water return valve.
  23. Open the city water return valve.
  24. Drain the coolant from the cryocooler compressor lines. Refer to Draining coolant from cryocooler compressor water lines.
  25. Remove the insulations, supply and return connections, and hoses from the FPU, listed below:
  26. Positioning
  27. Position the crate with a minimum of 4 ft of clearance behind the crate and 14 ft of clearance in front of the crate.
    • warning
    • Potential Crush Hazard
    • The crate and ICC are heavy. During crate disassembly, the front panel can fall. During ICC positioning, the ICC can tip. Both situations can cause injury upon impact.
    • Make sure that two people are present to disassemble the crate and move the ICC.
    note: Weight of ICC is 1,235 lbs (560 kg).
  28. Remove the eight screws from the bottom of the front panel.
    note: The screws that you need to remove are marked in red on the crate.
  29. On the left side panel, remove the six upper screws along the front edge.
  30. On the right side panel, remove the six upper screws along the front edge.
  31. With an assistant holding the front panel in place, remove the eight screws from the top of the front panel.
  32. Remove the front panel and lay it on the floor, front side down, in front of the crate.
  33. Remove the screw from the folding subpanel that is located at the bottom of the front panel.
  34. Lift the subpanel away from the front panel, extending it from the end of the front panel.
  35. Turn the front panel over, making it into a ramp for the cabinet. The subpanel should still extend out from the end of the ramp.
  36. Engage the two hooks on the opposite end of the ramp with the two brackets on the crate base.
  37. Make sure the ramp is secure and flush with the base.
  38. Remove the six screws from the lower front edge of the left panel to release the left wooden brace that holds the cabinet. Remove and discard the brace.
  39. Remove the six screws from the lower front edge of the right panel to release the right wooden brace that holds the cabinet. Remove and discard the brace.
  40. Remove the eight screws from the bottom of the left panel.
  41. Remove the eight screws from the bottom of the right panel.
  42. Remove the eight screws from the bottom of the rear panel.
  43. Pull the two webbed handles on the rear of the crate until the crate clears the cabinet and crate base.
  44. With a minimum of two people, roll the cabinet straight down the ramp.
  45. Break down and discard the crate.
  46. Remove the wrapper, shipping tape, and protective foam from the cabinet.
  47. Cleaning/Replacing the Air FilterCleaning or replacing the ICC air filters
  48. Visually check the air filter. If cleaning or replacement is required, perform the following procedure.
  49. Apply ISC LOTO. See Applying LOTO - ISC.
  50. Loosen the air filter clamp and remove the air filter.
  51. If needed, clean the filter outside of the magnet room.
  52. Install the new or clean air filter following the reverse order of removal.
  53. Turn on the ISC power. See Removing LOTO - ISC.
  54. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.
  55. Do a check scan. See Doing a check scan.

Finalization

  1. Restore the power. See Removing LOTO - ISC.
  2. Perform a check scan. See Doing a check scan.

  2. Restore the control box and plug the bolt.
  3. Open the side panel.
  4. Close the six valves.
