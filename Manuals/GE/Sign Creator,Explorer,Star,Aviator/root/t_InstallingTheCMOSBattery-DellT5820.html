Installing the CMOS battery - Dell T5820

Install the CMOS battery in the host computer.

Procedure

  1. Put the new battery in the socket on the Dell computer system board. Push the battery down until the release latch moves back into place and secures the battery.
    Note: The battery's positive (+) side must point up.
  2. Put the cover on the host computer and latch the cover.
  3. Set the computer upright.
  4. Connect the short ground lead that connects the left side panel to the Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) main chassis at the center of the lead.
  5. Secure the left side panel to the GOC.
  6. Get a GE equipment maintenance sticker (5661793), and write today's date on the sticker. Put the sticker on the GOC where it is easily visible.
