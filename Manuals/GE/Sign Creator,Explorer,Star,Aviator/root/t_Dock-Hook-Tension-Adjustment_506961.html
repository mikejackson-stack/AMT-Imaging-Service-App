Dock Hook Tension Adjustment

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements
Required persons Preliminary requirements Procedure Finalization
1 Not Applicable 15 minutes Not Applicable

Procedure

  1. Attempt to dock the patient transport, paying attention to the force required to engage the docking mechanism.
  2. If the force to dock seems excessive, STOP and loosen hook (see next step). Verify that neither of the problem symptoms indicated in Table 2 is present.
    Table 2. DOCK HOOK MIS-ADJUSTMENT SYMPTOMS
    SYMPTOM CAUSE ACTION
    Excessive force is required to depress dock pedal and fully engage dock latch.

    See Figure 1.

    		 Hook tension too tight Loosen hook one turn and retest
    Excessive back-and-forth vibration of patient transport when using “TABLE UP” foot pedal at dock. Especially noticeable when table top is already in fully raised position. See Figure 1 Hook tension too loose Tighten hook one turn and retest
    note:

    Hook tension adjustment is very sensitive. Never adjust hook more than one turn at a time without retesting.

  3. If dock hook tension requires adjusting, loosen set screw and lock nut near base of dock hook, turn hook one turn, tighten set screw and lock nut, and recheck hook adjustment. See Figure 2.
    note:

    Do not adjust the dock hook tension more than one turn at a time. Adjusting more than one turn at a time may cause damage to internal Patient Transport mechanisms.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
