Respiratory Compensation Checks

Table 3. Safety
Notice
Never vent the bellows. Always connect the bellows to the PAC Assembly before stretching it. Failure to connect bellows to PAC Assembly first causes inaccurate waveforms and, therefore, inaccurate results.

Procedure

  1. At Operator Workspace, select Service Desktop and click on 'Gating' tab.
  2. Select 'Respiratory' check box.
  3. The respiratory waveforms window appears on the LCD Panel Display.
  4. Take the respiratory bellows in both hands and gently pull on either end to duplicate the motion of typical regular breathing.
  5. Verify that the LED of Magnet Center Display move. If the bellows and respiratory compensation software is working properly, the LED's should follow the actions of the bellows.
  6. Verify that the waveform appears on the window of LCD Panel Display. If the bellows and respiratory compensation software is working properly, the waveform should follow the actions of the bellows.
  7. If the LED or waveform does not work properly, proceed troubleshoot the bellows (for tears or holes), connections and then the PAC assembly is self for problems.

Finalization

  1. Restore the system to patient scanning condition.
