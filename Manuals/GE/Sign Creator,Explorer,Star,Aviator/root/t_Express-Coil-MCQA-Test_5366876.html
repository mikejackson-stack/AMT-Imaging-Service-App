AA Coil/PA Coil Check

Procedure

  1. Connect the AA cable as shown in Figure 7.
  2. Position the Phantoms on the cradle between marker 3 and marker 4. Make sure that the space between the phantoms is aligned with the line between marker 3 and marker 4. Place the AA coil on top of the phantoms. Center it as shown in Figure 8.
  3. Wrap the strap as shown in Figure 10.
  4. Landmark on the AA coil marking as shown in Figure 11.
  5. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool procedure.
  6. Take note of which Test# and which Sig_Img failed(if any). To determine which coil/s has issue, refer to the matrix below.
