Procedure

  1. Remove Front Lower Cover. Refer to SC Cover Removal.
  2. Remove the water manifold access cover of SC.
  3. Close the IN/OUT valves of water manifold.
  4. Remove top cover of LCS by removing 2 screws.
  5. Remove R side cover of LCS by removing 2 screws.
  6. Disconnect the power and signal cables from LCS.
  7. Set the water hand pump to LCS tank and draining tank.
  8. Drain the all coolant from LCS tank.
  9. Before removing the hoses from LCS, fold and bind each hose to prevent from draining. Spread unused news paper for cloth on the floor.
  10. Disconnect hoses from LCS rear panel.
  11. Notice
    Before disconnecting hoses of chiller side, make still coolant with the chiller side. If not, the coolant will spill out to floor.
    Note:

    Be careful the coolant does not spill to floor. If needed, use funnel and 5L tank.

    Connect 500 mm hose to inlet and outlet of LCS with clamps.
  12. Restore the tank cap and covers of LCS.
  13. Set the new LCS to the site.
  14. Refill the drained coolant to new LCS tank.
  15. Restore LCS and SC with reverse order of procedure.
Topic ID: task_h4l_mqm_lgb

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Verify that four casters are attached on the floor.
  2. Restore the Power. Refer to Lockout / Tagout for System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker.
  3. Check the coolant level by checking that the float is in the range. Refer to Figure 7.
  4. Verify that there is no abnormal sounds or vibration while power is on.
  5. Verify that LED on Reverse Phase Repay is NOT lit while MCS/LCS is running.
  6. Run one head or body scan.
  7. Return the defective LCS and tank which was attached with LCS FRU kit.
