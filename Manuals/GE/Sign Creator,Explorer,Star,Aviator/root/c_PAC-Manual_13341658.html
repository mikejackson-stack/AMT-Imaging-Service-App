3 Purpose

  • PAC to PSE LED: This test allows you to visually inspect the PAC serial transmitter and the fiber optic cables that connect to it.

  • PSE to PAC LED: This test allows you to visually inspect the PSE serial transmitter and the fiber optic cables that connect to it.

4 Components Tested

  • PAC to PSE LED: This test causes the SPU (at the TRF board) to command the PAC module to turn on the fiber optic driver LED that is normally used to send serial messages from the PAC module to the PSE board in the MGD chassis.

  • PSE to PAC LED: This test causes the MGD chassis to turn on the PAC serial port transmitter on the PSE board.

5 Requirements

After running this diagnostic, a TPS reset is required prior to scanning.

7 Test Sequence

7.1 PAC to PSE LED

  1. Click Turn on the PAC to PSE Fiber Optic LED.

  2. Disconnect the output fiber cable on the PAC to inspect the LED at the PAC Tx port. The LED at the fiber optic cable should be blinking.

  3. Reconnect the PAC fiber cable to the PAC end.

  4. Click Stop.

7.2 PSE to PAC LED

  1. Disconnect the PAC output fiber cable on the PSE.

  2. Click Turn on the PSE to PAC Fiber Optic LED.

  3. Inspect the LED at the receive line of the fiber optic cable PSE J10 port. The LED at the fiber optic cable should be blinking.

  4. Reconnect the fiber cable to the PSE.

  5. Click Stop.

8 Expected Results

The Test Output field will indicate when the LED should be lit and other current status information.
