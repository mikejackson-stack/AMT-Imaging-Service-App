Topic ID: id_SL1526571-1210351

Hose Setting of System Cabinet

Procedure

  1. Remove Right and Left Front Covers from system cabinet.
  2. Remove Water Manifold Access Cover by removing 2 screws.
  3. Connect Hoses to SC Water Manifold
  4. Push in manifold with water plumbing
  5. Fix the main fold front screws, washer（2000–M6–02）nut（3100–M6C-29）.
  6. Restore all the water pipe.

Power Cable Connection of 8kW LCS

Procedure

Connect Power Cable.
  1. Open LCS Top cover.
  2. Route the cable through rear panel.
  3. Connect 3 phase, and Ground cable (Run E0501)
  4. After cable connection, tighten plastic clamp at the rear panel.
  5. For Type A, connect Run E3504 signal cable. For Type B (B’), connect Run E3505 signal cable.
Topic ID: id_SL3088165-1210351

Power Cable Connection of 4kW LCS

Procedure

Connect Power Cable.
  1. Open LCS Top cover.
  2. Route the cable through rear panel from 8KW LCS as shown in Figure 18.
  3. Connect power cable (Run E0502).
  4. After cable connection, tighten plastic clamp at the rear panel.
  5. Connect Run E3505 signal cable.

Power Cable Connection of MCS

Topic ID: id_SL3156985-1210351

Power Cable Connection for BRM chiller

Procedure

  1. Route the cable (E0507) between BRM chiller and facility power/MDP or stepdown transformer (M3335TZ, option).
  2. Connect the cable (E0507) with facility power or MDP.
  3. If stepdown transformer exist (M3335TZ , option), connect the cable (E0507) with transformer as below
    1. The Transformer Input Connection.
      As showing in below picture 3–phase power input is needed. And the phase sequence is restricted. If there is no power output please check and modify sequence of U1, V1 and W1.
    2. The Transformer Output Connection
      BRM chiller is single phase power input. There are three cables from BRM chiller. Ground cable connect to the ground point, the other two cables connect to any 2 phases of U2,V2 and W2 as showing in below illustration.
    3. The Relay Setting and Adjustment
      Note: Based on measurement data, the recommended transformer input power range is Vin-10% ~ Vin+6% to meet the step down transformer output voltage within 208VAC-10% ~ +10%. (Vin: input Voltage; it is different in different country)
  4. Connect the cable (E0507) with BRM chiller.
