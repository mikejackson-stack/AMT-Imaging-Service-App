About this task

This calibration procedure sets up the Universal Power Monitor (UPM) for MNS, using the system and the power measurement kit. Both forward and reflected power must be calibrated.

Topic ID: id_SL15196133-508239

Disable Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR) fault detection in AMT amplifier

About this task

This procedure is for the AMT amplifier only. For the CPC amplifier, proceed to UPM MNS forward power calibration.

Procedure

  1. Apply Lock Out, Tag Out (LOTO) on the RF amplifier and Penetration (PEN) cabinet to remove power to the MNS amplifier. See the MR Service Safety Manual, Part Number (PN) 5452735.
  2. Remove the top panel from the MNS amplifier cabinet.
  3. Remove the top cover of the controller module in the MNS amplifier cabinet.
  4. Locate the SMA cable connections to J7 and J8 on the controller module Printed Circuit Board (PCB). Remove the connections, and move the cables away from the PCB. Restrain the cables with tape, if necessary.
  5. Replace the top panel of the MNS amplifier cabinet.
  6. Remove LOTO (see the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735) and reapply power to the MNS amplifier.
Topic ID: id_SL176896-508239

UPM MNS forward power calibration

Procedure

  1. Inhibit T/R faults by moving switch 2 (SW2) on the driver module in the RF systems cabinet to the TR Disable position.
  2. At the rear of the MNS amplifier, remove the MNS RF output cable and connect the dummy load to the back of the amplifier.
Topic ID: id_SL127106-508239

If UPM Failure Occurs

Procedure

  1. Verify proper calibration equipment setup.
  2. Check the cabling between the UPM and amplifier.
  3. Check the mgd_stage file by typing: cd /w/config. Remove the p if necessary (p = UPM emulated).
  4. Perform the maximum RF power calibration procedure, and repeat the UPM calibration.
  5. Check the UPM for power.
  6. Check the error log for details of UPM failure.
  7. Perform the body UPM calibration.

    • If calibration fails, replace the detector boards. Perform UPM calibration to put the new boards into service.

    • If calibration passes, continue to troubleshoot the problem with the body mode. Most likely, the problem is with the PA section of the RF amplifier and the cabling back to the UPM.

Topic ID: id_15011731

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Perform 1.5T MNS Functional Tests.
  2. Perform LOTO on the MNS cabinet. See the latest revision of the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735, available from the Online Documentation Library for information about LOTO.
  3. Connect cable J7 and cable J8 to the PCB.
  4. Install the top cover.
  5. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment from the MNS amplifier.
  6. Attach the MNS heliax cables.
  7. Enable the T/R driver module by moving switch 2 (SW2) to TR Enable. (See Figure 4.)
  8. Upon completion of both forward and reflected calibration, perform a TPS reset.
  9. Perform a TPS reset.
  10. Remove LOTO on the MNS cabinet. See the latest revision of the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735, available from the online documentation library for information about LOTO.
