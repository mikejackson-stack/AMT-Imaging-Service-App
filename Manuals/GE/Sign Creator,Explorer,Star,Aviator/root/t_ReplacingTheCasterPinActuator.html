Replacing the caster pin actuator

Replaces the brake system caster pin in a dockable patient table.

The pin actuator is a threaded pin that bolts into the caster arm.

Procedure

  1. Loosen and remove the caster pin actuator.
  2. Replace the caster pin actuator. Apply Loctite #243 when reinstalling.
