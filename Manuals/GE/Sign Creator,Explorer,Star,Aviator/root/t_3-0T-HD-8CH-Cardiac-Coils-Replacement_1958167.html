Replace The Coil Cable

Procedure

  1. Remove anterior coil cover.
  2. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly.
  3. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the anterior coil cable end from the anterior interface board. See Figure 1.
  4. Remove the old anterior cable assembly. See Figure 2.
  5. Fix the anterior cable section of a new coil cable assembly on the anterior section of the coil and reattach the cable clamp with the two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board. See Figure 3.
  6. Close the anterior coil cover.
  7. Remove posterior coil cover.
  8. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly. See Figure 4.
  9. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the posterior cable end from the posterior interface board. See Figure 5.
  10. Remove the old posterior cable assembly. See Figure 6.
  11. Fix the posterior section of a new coil cable assembly on the posterior section of the coil and reattach the cable clamp with two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board. See Figure 6 and Figure 7.
  12. Close the posterior coil cover.

Replace The Anterior Coil Assembly

Procedure

  1. Remove anterior coil cover.
  2. Remove the two screws that hold down the cable clamp assembly. See Figure 3.
  3. Disconnect SMB plug and the DC Plug on the anterior coil cable end from the anterior interface board. See Figure 1.
  4. Remove the old anterior coil assembly.
  5. Fix the anterior coil cable on a new anterior coil assembly and reattach the cable clamp with the two screws. Connect all four SMB plugs and the DC Plug to the corresponding receptacles on the interface board. See Figure 3.
