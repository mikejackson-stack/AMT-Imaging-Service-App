7.1 Cable Connection and Disconnection

7.1.1 Disconnect the VRF Single Fiber Optic Clock Cable or Single Fiber Optic Service Loopback Cable

  1. Rotate the connector counter-clockwise until you feel a click.

  2. Pull back the metallic part till the key disengages and slowly pull out the cable.

7.1.2 Connect the VRF Single Fiber Optic Clock Cable or Single Fiber Optic Service Loopback Cable

  1. Insert the protruding white portion of the clock loopback cable into the CLK IN connector socket on the ICN.

  2. Turn the entire cable (not just the metallic connector) until the key wedges into the gap on the socket and push the cable inside.

  3. Push the metallic part and rotate it clockwise until you fell a click. Gently tug the cable to make sure it is securely fastened.

  4. Connect the other end of the clock loopback cable to CLK OUT to the ICN following the same process (see Steps 1 - 3).

7.1.3 Disconnect the VRF Dual Fiber Optic Data Cable or Dual Fiber Optic Service Loopback Connector

  1. Press the tab on the bottom side of the cable connector.

  2. Pull the connector out of socket on the ICN.

7.1.4 Connect the VRF Dual Fiber Optic Data Cable or Dual Fiber Optic Service Loopback Connector

  1. Align the cable connector with the socket on the ICN.

  2. Press the tab on the underside and push the connect into the socket.

7.2 Manual Loopback Diagnostic Test Sequence

  1. Select Start Loopback Mode.

  2. Click Run.

  3. Connect the required cables for the selected test #id_SL1951716-1244037/SL1951717-1244037.

    note:

    If you want to use a non-default value to test, under Options fill in the Iterations field.

  4. Select a loopback test which is allowed for the applied cable connections.

  5. Click Run.

  6. To try a different loopback test, repeat steps 3 - 6.

  7. Recover the cable connections to product configuration.

  8. Select Stop Loopback Mode.

  9. Click Run.
