Hazard Statements - collection file

Table 1. Safety

Before working in any GE Healthcare MR suite or performing any GE Healthcare service procedure, you must:

  • Have read and understood all hazard conditions and safety requirements in the latest revision of the GE Healthcare MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  • Have successfully completed all relevant GE Healthcare Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) courses (or for non-GE employees, equivalent workplace training courses).
  • Comply with all site-specific training and workplace safety requirements.

If you have any safety concerns at any time, do not begin work or immediately stop work and move to a safe location. Immediately contact your supervisor or site safety officer for instructions on how to proceed.

  • Vents are at risk for ruptures after a quench.

  • Use care to ensure proper latch alignment. Adjust the front trim ring to align the connection points as needed.

  • Projectile hazard
  • Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles. Steel shims in a magnetic field are subject to a force proportional to the steel's mass and strength; in a 1.5T field, this can equate to a force greater than 50 lbs (22.6 kg).
  • You will feel this force pulling the shim tray into the magnet when you bring a loaded tray within 3 ft (1m) of the magnet bore. You will feel an attracting force when inserting a loaded shim tray into the track inside the magnet bore; you will feel a repelling force when removing a loaded shim tray from the track. A repelling force can propel the tray from the magnet when the tray is released from its fixed position.
  • To reduce your risk of projectile hazards, follow these safety requirements during passive shim installation and removal:
    • Assemble all equipment and check its functionality in conformance with passive shim tray assembly.
    • Load the shim trays outside of the magnet room and never loosen the individual shims inside the magnet room.
    • Secure the shims in the shim trays and insert the shim trays into the magnet in strict conformance with the passive shim tray assembly and insertion procedures.
    • Wear safety goggles/glasses during shim tray insertion or removal to prevent eye injury from sudden tray movement.
    • Hold the shim trays firmly in place when releasing the inserted trays; make sure all loaded shim trays are restrained (by hand, shim tray puller, or fixed into a track), especially the trays marked on the end flange as mounted in an outward repelling force.
    • Insert and remove the shim trays with a gradual motion.
    • Two personnel are required to insert or remove shim trays.
    • Do not load a shim tray while anyone is directly in line with the tray on either end of the magnet.
    • Never put yourself between a shim tray and the magnet.
    • Have a clear path for passive shim removal and insertion.
