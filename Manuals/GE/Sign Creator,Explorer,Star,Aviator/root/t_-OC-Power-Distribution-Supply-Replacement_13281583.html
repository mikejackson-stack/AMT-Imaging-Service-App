Procedure

  1. Remove Left side cover and Right Side Cover.
  2. Shut off the CB of Power Distribution Supply
  3. Disconnect the all power plugs from the receptacle of power supply. See Figure 1
  4. Loosen 2 screws and remove the cable cover.
  5. Loosen the other 2 screws and take away the cable fixed bracket. See Figure 2
  6. Take out the Power Distribution Supply.
  7. Restore the new OC Power Distribution Supply
  8. Turn the CB of the new Power Distribution Supply to the ON position.
  9. Restore OC by reverse order of removal.

Finalization

  1. Turn the OC circuit breakers on the PDU of System Cabinet back to the ON position.
  2. Verify that the system boot up.
  3. Log in.

    • User name: sdc

    • Password: adw2.0

  4. Verify that the system is fully booted.
  5. Perform Signal to Noise - Head Scan
