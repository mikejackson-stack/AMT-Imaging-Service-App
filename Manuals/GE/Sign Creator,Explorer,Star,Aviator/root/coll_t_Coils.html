Phantom Notes

note: Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the system.
note:

1.5T phantoms are light green.

3.0T phantoms are pink.

The 3.0T coil MCQA uses the 3.0T phantoms, which are pink in color.

Procedure

  1. Common steps
  2. Put the flat filler panel on the table.
  3. Run the MCQA check.
  4. Setting Up Posterior Arrays
  5. Press the Alignment Light button.

  6. note: There are lines on both sides of the cradle.
  7. Do the following steps:
    • notice
    • Phantom spacer must be placed between two phantoms, otherwise the test will fail.
    1. Place the two TL unified phantoms and the spacer on the table.
    2. Place PA phantom positioner over the two TL unified phantoms.
  8. Move the phantom with positioner so that alignment line and alignment dent of the positioner are aligned with the beams.
  9. Press Landmark and then press Advance to Scan.
  10. Setting Up 3.0T GEMPVA
  11. Place the phantom positioner on the table.
  12. Place the PV coil on the cradle and connect the coil connector to port P2.

  13. note: Make sure the phantoms are aligned so that the phantom will be in the image field of vision.
    • Cylindrical phantoms are aligned to touch the center of assembly.
    • Pink cylindrical phantom face is aligned to the end of assembly.
  14. Place the PA phantom positioner on top of the TL unified phantoms, fitting the alignment block on the PV coil into the alignment notch on the PA phantom positioner.
  15. Flip the PV upper unit onto the PA phantom positioner.
  16. Move the cradle and adjust the PV coil position so that the alignment light shines in the middle of the latch of the PV coil.
    note: Make sure the whole coil is left/right centered to the cradle.
  17. Fasten with Velcro straps of PV upper unit.
  18. Press Landmark and then press Advance to Scan.
