Dock Anchor Hole

  1. Place the Alignment Plate on the Bridge. SeeFigure 1.

  2. Wait for a while until the weight stop it's movement.

  3. Mark on the floor at the point of the weight. See Figure 2.
  4. Set the String with weight as shown in Figure 3.

  5. Wait for a while until the weight stop it's movement.

  6. Mark on the floor at the point of the weight. See Figure 4.
  7. Place the Magnet Protection Plate to align the corners and marked points as shown in Figure 5.

  8. Mark the edge of the anchor hole on the floor with pen. See Figure 5.

  9. Mark the Center of Anchor Hole with pen.
