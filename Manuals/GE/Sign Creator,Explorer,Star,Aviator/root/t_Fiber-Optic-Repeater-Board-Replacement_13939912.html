Fiber Optic Repeater Board Removal

Procedure

  1. Follow the procedures to lock out and tag out the penetration panel. See the latest revision of the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735, available from the online documentation library.
  2. The repeater board is located on the pen panel. Using a ¼” wrench, remove the four screws that secure the Protective Cover.
  3. Disconnect the power harness.
  4. Disconnect the fiber optic lines from connectors J1-J5 on one side and J7-J11 on the other.
  5. Carefully remove the fiber optic repeater board from the chassis.

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Remove the LOTO tags from the system and restore all power.
  2. Perform Fiber Optic Repeater Loopback Test.
