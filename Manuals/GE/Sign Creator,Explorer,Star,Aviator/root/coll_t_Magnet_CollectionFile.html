  95. Replacing the cable busbars
  96. Inspect the rubber bushings as follows:
    1. Make sure they are installed correctly (smaller side faces out) on each mounting stud.
    2. Make sure the coil-side mating surface is clean and has no epoxy on it.
    3. Replace damaged bushings.
    note:
    • Before installing the replacement busbar, inspect the surface that will connect the gradient coil and cables for oxidation. If the surface is oxidized, clean the surface with a light abrasive pad.
    • DO NOT lift the busbar assembly by holding the leads.
    • Clean the magnet mounting stud threads with a non-magnetic brush to remove any remnants of thread-lock material.
  97. Hand thread a clean M10 nut onto the stud and check that the nut rotates freely. The thread should be free of high friction or locking spots.
    Wipe the studs and the gradient coil terminals clean with alcohol to assure particle and dust removal.
  98. Insert the rubber bushings onto each mounting stud.
  99. Apply Loctite #243 (approximately 1 or 2 drops) to the exposed threads of each mounting stud.
  100. Starting at the top and moving clockwise between mounting studs, install and hand-tighten a brass nut with G10 washer evenly on each stud. Tighten each nut 1/2 turn at a time until 2-3 threads show above each nut.
  101. Align the V groove of the lugs with the gradient coil terminal so that the lug is flush with the terminal.
  102. Prepare SmartBolts as needed by placing a new Nord-Lock washer over each M12 x 30 SmartBolt.
  103. Clear the area between the busbar lugs and copper end rings of all debris.
    104. note: Use a nonmagnetic torque wrench is the magnet is ramped.
  104. Using the adjustable nonmagnetic torque wrench (5534134), set the torque to 25 ft-lbs or 33.9 Nm. Tighten the nuts until the wrench clicks.
    note: Make sure that the arrow on the torque wrench is visible. If the arrow is not visible, the torque wrench will not “click” when the proper torque is reached.

  105. note:
    • Always make sure that the stud extends beyond the end of the nut.
    • Correct torque to the nuts holding the busbar lugs is critical to the long-term stability of the gradient subsystem.
    note:
    • Do not lift the busbar assembly by holding the leads.
    • Make sure the bushings remain on each mounting stud.
  106. Install the busbar terminal lugs to the HRMw gradient coil terminal using the SmartBolts with the new Nord-Lock washer and Loctite.
  107. Tighten the M12 x 30 SmartBolt with your fingers. Discard the old washers.
    note:
    • When installing the SmartBolt, be careful not to let the hardware fall into the magnet.
    • Always use the new Nord-Lock washers that come with the FRU package. DO NOT reuse the old Nord-Lock washers.
    • Nord-Lock washers consist of two pieces, which are generally glued together. Always use a complete set with two pieces joined together in correct orientation. DO NOT use a separated single piece.
  108. Using the nonmagnetic 80 Nm fixed torque wrench (5790177), torque the SmartBolt in the direction of the arrow until the wrench clicks.
  109. Make sure the window in the top of the SmartBolt is black. If it is not black, re-torque using the nonmagnetic 80 Nm fixed torque wrench.
    note: When the SmartBolt is red, it is loose. Image shows right side SmartBolt black, left side red.

  111. Perform a second torque operation at each nut to confirm the proper torque of the nut.
    • If the nut moved more than one flat from the original position, you must re-torque the nut a third time.
    • If re-torqueing was required, re-mark the line on the nut and stud.

    note:

    During LVShim procedures, the system performs an SNR check to verify system integrity. If the SNR check fails, troubleshoot the SNR issue (in particular, check the I and Q drive cable connections) before continuing with LVShim.

    After the scan is completed, the tool calculates the harmonics and compares them with the specifications.

    • Green cell = Passed specification
    • Red cell = Failed specification

    The tool calculates the delta currents and the new currents, and displays currents in a pop-up dialogue.

  158. Accept or decline the new currents:
    • If all harmonics data cells are green, decline the new currents by clicking Cancel.

    note: If the current is not accepted, the delta currents in the LVShim user interface change to purple and the new Loaded Current column is not displayed.
