Setting up grafidy hardware

Procedure

  1. Unfold the six-channel Grafidy fixture and lock the arms into place (90° from center plate).
    note: Make sure the six-channel Grafidy fixture is firmly pushed into the slit of foam support without any gap.
  2. Connect the P-port connector to port .
  3. Center the laser cross hairs on top of the fixture.
