3.0T 32-Channel Body Array Coil Introduction

This document describes the 3.0T 32-channel body array coil made by General Electric Healthcare, which is a receive-only coil. The catalog number is M7000BG.

Product Identification and Shipping List

The 3.0T 32-channel body array coil consists of the coil (anterior and posterior) and coil positioners. To identify the 3.0T body array parts, refer to the illustrations below.

Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements: All coils should be stored in the scan room or in a room that has conditions that fall within the GE recommended MR suite conditions.

Dimensions: 25.6 inches (650 mm) L x 25.6 inches (650 mm) W x 23.6 inches (600 mm) H

Weight: Anterior 9 lb (4.1 kg); posterior with nest pad 12 lb (5.5 kg)
