Rear End Bell Removal

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements

Table 3. Safety
DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.
Table 4. Required conditions
ConditionReferenceEffectivity

System Power must be turned OFF. Refer to System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker LOTO Procedure.

--

Procedure

  1. Remove following assembly and covers.

    • Bridge assembly (Refer to Bridge Assembly.)

    • Rear pedestal (Refer to Rear Pedestal Removal.)

    • Rear trim ring (Refer to Rear Trim Ring.)

  2. Remove the patient light end covers from the upper side of magnet bore by removing 2 bolts.
  3. Remove the patient lights from the magnet bore upper side. The patient light is secured by Velcro tape.
  4. Loosen the clamp screw, and remove hoses. (Refer to illustration 3)
  5. Using an M5 Allen wrench, remove the two M6 bolts of fasteners which connect the RF coil and the rear end bell. (Refer to illustration 3)
  6. Remove the cable cover from the rear end bell by removing 2 screws.
  7. Unlatch the clamp of OPT cables. (Refer to illustration 5.)
  8. Disconnect the OPT fiber cables from LED Power Box assembly. (Refer to illustration 5.)
  9. Remove the 6 bolts (M10 x6) securing rear end bell to the magnet.
    Note:

    Do not remove the rear end bell. The rear end bell is connected by speaker cable.

  10. Withdraw rear end bell until can access to speaker cable connector.
  11. Disconnect the speaker cable from the magnet upper side.
  12. Remove the rear end bell from the magnet bore.
  13. Installation is reverse order of removal.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
