Cable Replacement Procedure

Procedure

  1. The 4 coil connector mounting screws are located at the superior end of the coil. Remove the 4 coil connector mounting screws as detailed in illustration1.
  2. Remove the coil connector by pulling away from the coil in the superior direction.
  3. The steps for installation of the new cable are simply the reverse order of the steps described for removal.

Mechanical Hardware Check

About this task

Lift the coil latches on both sides of the coil, and slide the coil to its superior and inferior extents. The coil must slide freely on the base with no binding or grinding. Make sure that the coil does not move with respect to the baseplate when the latches are locked down.

    Replacement of Inferior or Superior Mirror

    About this task

    There are two mirrors included with the 1.5T HD 8 Channel High Res Brain Array: To install either mirror, align the grooves in the base of the mirror with the grooves in the coil mirror mount, and slide the mirror into position. Press and hold the release button on the mirror before attempting to remove the mirror.

      Replacing the Mount Plate Assembly

      Procedure

      1. Lift the coil latches on both sides of the coil, and slide the coil to its superior extent.

        Press on the coil release button on the base plate.

        Slide the coil complete off the baseplate

      2. Remove the screws (eight) .
      3. Replace the mount plate assembly and fastened the screws removed in the previous step.
      4. Slide the coil back on to the baseplate
      Topic ID: id_SL2218493-1066269

      Replacing the Spring Foam

      Procedure

      1. Remove the screw from the release button assembly .
      2. Remove the slider from the release button mounting block .
      3. Remove and replace the spring foam.
        Note:

        There are 2 spring foams in each Spring foam FRU set. Place the slider with the new spring foams back in to the mounting block Attach the mounting block back to the baseplate using the screw removed in step 1
