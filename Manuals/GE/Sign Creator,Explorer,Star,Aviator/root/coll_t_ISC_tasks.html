ISC tasks

Common steps for hardware replacements within the ISC

Prerequisites

Procedure

  1. ICN Replacement
  2. Loosen the fixing screws and lift the ISC PW cover a small distance to remove it.
    note: If the furnished closet door prevents removal, you can separate the PW cover into two pieces by removing 6 screws at the center.
  3. Remove the rear access cover.

  5. Beijing steps
  6. Scans
  7. Click the New Worklist icon.
  8. Enter for weight: 111.
  9. Click Show All Protocols....
  10. When the Protocol Selector menu opens, click Protocol Details.
  11. Select the Protocol Library: GE.
  12. Select Adult.
  13. Click the Template tab.
  14. Select 3 - Plane 2D Localizer and click the arrow to move the selection to the box on the right.
  15. Click Accept.
  16. Click Save.
  17. Click Start Exam.
  18. Enter the following image parameters.
    Option Description
    Frequency field of view (FOV) (Freq. FOV) 30.0
    Phase FOV 1.00
    Slice Thickness 5.0
  19. Click Save RX.
  20. Click Scan.
