Procedure

  1. Remove L Upper Front Cover and R Upper Front Cover. SC Cover Removal.
  2. Disconnect the following connectors from front panel of CAM lite 3 and HUB.

    • Ethernet switch (HUB): port 6, port 7, port 8

    • IXG: J8, J11, J13

    • PSE:J7, J10, J11, J12, J13

  3. Loosen the screws of FAN Assy.
    4. note:

    The fan is plug and play with no cables. You can pull it out or push it in with ease.

  4. Draw out the malfunction fan from the CAM lite 3.
  5. Restore System Cabinet by reverse order.

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker LOTO Procedure.
  2. Run MGD Diagnostics to ensure proper functionality.
  3. Run one head or body scan.
