Replacing the reversing link

Replaces the shaft linkage reversing link in a dockable patient table.

Procedure

  1. Remove the three shoulder screws. Shoulder screws are accessible from the bottom of the table.
    note: There are plastic washers on both sides of the mounting holes. Take care to prevent damaging the washers during the replacement procedure. Also, both outer shoulder screws have brass washers installed.
  2. Install the new reversing link and shoulder screws as required. Tighten the shoulder screws. The shoulder screws will allow some movement of the reversing link when installed.
