Procedure

  1. Remove L Front Cover. Refer to SC Cover Removal.
  2. Remove Rear Cover (Magnet Room Side). Refer to SC Cover Removal .
  3. Remove Rear access cover.
  4. Disconnect all of cables from the rear of the ICN.
  5. On the front of the ICN, remove the three screws and remove ICN Noise Duct.
  6. Loosen two screws and slide the ICN out.
  7. Pull up and remove ICN bracket.
  8. Install ICN bracket to the new ICN.
  9. Insert new ICN into the Cabinet and fix by two screws.
  10. Restore ICN Noise Duct.
  11. Connect all the cables back in a reverse operation and restore covers.

Finalization

  1. Restore the Power. Refer to System Cabinet PDU Main Breaker LOTO Procedure.
  2. Perform following procedure of VRE configuration. This procedure will take between 15-20 minutes to complete.
    1. With the Signa up and running, go to the service desktop and push the [Guided Install] button on the toolbar. Then, Select "FE mode" and click [start]. A Cshell will open and ask for root password ("operator" < enter> is the default) The Install GUI will load and start.
    2. Select "Configure VRE" and click on [Configure VRE Blades].
  3. note:

    ICN error log remains even though ICN is replaced.

    ICN Monitor Display shows ICN sensor logs stored in '/usr/g/service/log/sensor_logs' directory.

    ('ICN Monitor Display' is shown from 'MR Service Desktop/Diagnostics/VRE Subsystems/ICN Monitor Display'.)

    This log is not automatically removed after ICN is replaced.

    As a result, you will see error log of previouse ICN even though it is replaced to the new ICN.

    By performing the following procedure, the old log will be removed.

    Remove log stored in '/usr/g/service/log/sensor_logs' directory as follows.
    1. Open C Shell Window.
    2. Enter 'cd /usr/g/service/log'.
