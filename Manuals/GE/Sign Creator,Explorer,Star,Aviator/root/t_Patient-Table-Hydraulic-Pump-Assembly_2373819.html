1 Remove Hydraulic Pump Assembly

Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport, and move it out of exam room.
  2. Remove both outer covers by removing mounting screws (see Figure 1).
  3. Remove inner covers by removing mounting screws.
  4. Raise table to up position.
    • warning
    • PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
    • SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
    • WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.
  5. Unhook lock bar spring and move lock bar to horizontal position, as shown in Figure 2, then lower table until lock bar is supporting table in its full up position.
  6. Disconnect the two hydraulic lines to hydraulic pump assembly, and fasten upward and aside to avoid excess spillage. Immediately fold and clamp the translucent (yellow) hose from reservoir to prevent oil from draining from the reservoir (see Figure 3).
  7. Loosen two mounting screws attaching upper drip pan to lower drip pan (see Figure 4).
  8. Remove upper drip pan (see Figure 4).
    • notice
    • Equipment damage possibility. Do not remove spring pins from either end of pump pull rods; this will cause a side loading on the pump, requiring its replacement. The pump pull rods are disconnected from the pump linkage by turning the pump pull rods in a counterclockwise direction after tension has been removed from the pump springs. This is explained in the following procedure. The pump pull rod spring pins are exposed only when the Table Up pedal is pressed (see Figure 5).
  9. Compress main pump spring, as shown in Figure 7, to remove tension from pull rods. Block the pump Up pedal fully up to take its weight off of the pull rods.
  10. Push the yoke back and unscrew the pull rods form the pump linkage (see Figure 6).
  11. Remove c-clamps used to compress main spring, as shown in Figure 7.
  12. Remove machine screw securing bumper to the pump piston (see Figure 8).
  13. Remove three mounting cap screws that secure pump assembly to front table support.
  14. Remove hydraulic pump assembly and gasket.
  15. If absorbent pad attached to upper drip pan appears to be saturated with fluid, remove it and squeeze out fluid.

2 Install Replacement Hydraulic Pump Assembly

Procedure

  1. Prepare replacement pump for installation by applying Locktite 242 to three mounting screws and to machine screw that secures bumper to pump piston.
  2. Set replacement pump assembly on front support of table, making sure that gasket is installed properly, and secure with three mounting screws (see Figure 8).
  3. Secure the bumper to the pump piston with machine screw.
  4. Reinstall c-clamps on front support, as shown in Figure 9, to compress main pump spring. Screw pump pull rods into pump linkage, alternately tightening each side to avoid side loading the pump. Stop when the rear spring pin on each pull rod is 1/8-inch (3.2 mm) from pump body (flush with back surface of front support) (see Figure 10).
  5. Connect two hydraulic lines to hydraulic pump assembly (see Figure 11).
  6. Attach upper and lower drip pans (see Figure 12).
  7. Block open the Down valve, and pump the Up pedal at least 20 full strokes to purge air from the lines.
  8. Close Down valve and raise table.
  9. Retract lock bar and secure with spring, then lower table.
  10. Fill table reservoir so that fluid is 1/4 to 1/2 inch above full line when table is at its lowest position.
    note:

    If any air gets into the cylinder, open the hydraulic cylinder bleed screw, when the table is raised, until air is removed from the cylinder. Very little fluid loss should occur during a normal bleed procedure.

  11. Verify that pump pull rods are adjusted equally by comparing position of rear spring pins on pump pull rods (see Figure 13).
  12. Replace inner and outer covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Figure 14).

3 Finalization

Procedure

  1. Dock the Table and move the Table to the up limit position. Verify that the Table and Bridge are horizontally aligned and there is no height difference of Table and Bridge at right and left position.
  2. Verify that there is no part caught by wire or any movable module.
  3. Place the 159Kg Load evenly on the Cradle. If using the phantom, refer to the following illustration.
  4. Verify that Table moves up and down fully and smoothly using the pedal of Table.
  5. Verify that Table moves up and down fully and smoothly using the pedal of Dock.
  6. Verify that Cradle moves in and out fully and smoothly.
  7. Verify that the amount of oil is properly filled.
  8. Verify that oil is not leaked from the cylinder. Refer to following illustration for check points.
  9. Verify that all of the parts and the screws are installed.
  10. Verify that there is no wound or dirt on the Table, Cradle, Pad, and Patient Strap.
