Procedure

  1. Remove cradle. Refer to Posterior Array Coil Replacement with removing Fixed Table or Posterior Array Coil Replacement without removing Fixed Table
  2. Invert the cradle and place on a flat surface.
  3. Loosen the Delrin screw.
  4. Remove the wheel housing nearest to the Delrin lock.
  5. Insert Finger to reach the Push rod and rotate the push rod using finger, so that the Delrin lock length increases to 10mm.
  6. Push the Delrin screw block to tension the tygon tube inside and under tension lock the Delrin screw.
  7. Restore cradle back on table.
  8. Check if it is locking, by depressing the Push rod at front of cradle by 4 mm and slide cradle out and check if lock is releasing smoothly.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
