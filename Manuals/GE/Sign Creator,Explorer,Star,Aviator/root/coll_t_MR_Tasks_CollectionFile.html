Procedure

  1. Replacing the longitudinal drive motor
  2. Disconnect the cables from the motor.
  3. Loosen the two upper set screws on the coupling between motor and gear box. Rotate the motor axis and coupling by hand in order to get an access to both hex screws on the coupling.
    • caution
    • Ferrous material hazard!
    • Longitudinal drive motor contains ferrous material.
    • Holding this assembly too close to the magnet bore will forcibly attract it to the magnet. To prevent possible bodily injury or material damage, remove the longitudinal drive motor and mounting bracket towards the rear wall below the original interim drive assembly level and move it out of the magnet room.
  4. Remove side support plate by release screws.
  5. While one person holds onto the motor firmly with both hands, the other person should remove flathead screws and nuts that secure the gear box to the bridge.
  6. With the last screw removed, take off the motor and its frame from the bridge. Then carefully remove the motor from Magnet Room by placing the motor on the floor and against the wall farthest from the magnet. The service path to take the motor out of the scan room is shown in Figure 5.
  7. With the motor outside of the Magnet Room, remove the frame and coupling. Replace the failed motor with the new one.
  8. Carefully bring the motor and bracket assembly into the Magnet Room along the same path used to remove the assembly.
  9. To reinstall the motor and bracket assembly in the Rear Pedestal, ensure that a second person holds onto the motor firmly with both hands, while the first person reconnects the motor and bracket assembly to the gear assembly. Tighten the screws and nuts to secure the motor frame.
  10. Tighten the two set screws on the coupling to finish the motor installation. Make sure one of the hex set screws must be in front of axis flat to prevent free rotation under the load.
  11. Reattach the cables to the motor.

  185. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the disabled (down) position.
  186. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the enabled (up) position.

  188. Amplifier
  189. Set the RF amp breaker to the Off position.
  190. Restore the system power. See Removing LOTO - ISC.
  191. Wait for the pressure meter (black meter) of CCU to move for about 40 seconds.
  192. Turn the RF amplifier breaker on.
  193. wattmeter
  194. Locate the element offset factors card in the kit and find the offset for "3T DVMR Head Output” and "4500 W". The meter is now ready to calibrate the RF amplifier output power for body channel 1. The meter is now configured to calibrate the RF amplifier output power for the head channel. Locate the Element Offset Factors card in the kit and find the offset for “3T Dual Drive Body Output.”
  195. Press ◄ to return to measurement mode.
  196. Press the OK button on the wattmeter.
  197. Make sure that the scanner is idle and that the RF ENB switch on the DTX1 exciter is set to the disabled (down) position.
  198. Plug the wattmeter power supply into a service outlet (batteries are often not charged). Connect one end of the wattmeter cable to the RS232 sensor connector. Connect the other end to the coupler.
  199. Plug the fan for the dummy load into a service outlet. Never use this dummy load without its fan running.
    • notice
    • Possible equipment damage
    • The dummy load must be cooled or it can overheat and become damaged.
    • Do not apply RF power to the dummy load when its fan is not on.
  200. Set the RF ENB switch to the enabled (up) position.
  201. EPI White Pixel test
  202. Remove any phantoms from the cradle.
  203. Move the cradle from the home position, landmark, and then advance to scan.
  204. Measuring fixed table height
  205. Make sure the height difference between the table top and the bridge top on both the left and right sides is less than 1 mm.
  206. Remove the screw securing the actuator lower pin holder.
  207. Remove the actuator lower pin holder.
  208. Remove the lower pin.
  209. Turn on or restart the computer. Press F2 to enter the BIOS Setup menu.
    note: Optionally, press F12, then select the BIOS Setup menu.
  210. Clutch
  211. Manually turn the clutch so that the upper and lower clutch screws are positioned as shown below.
  212. Press the cradle in button on the control panel and position the cradle approximately 500 mm (20 inches) into the bore.
  213. Pull up the cradle release handle and release it. The cradle will move back to the home position.
    note: While in egress, the handle must be continuously activated for the cradle to move. If the cradle is positioned between 0 mm and 750 mm, releasing the egress handle will cause the cradle to decelerate and move slowly to the home position. If the cradle is positioned beyond 750 mm, releasing the egress handle will cause the cradle to decelerate and stop.
  214. Make sure the cradle is locked in place at the home position.
  215. Press the cradle in button on the control panel and position the cradle approximately 2500 mm (100 inches) into the bore.
  216. Pull up the cradle release handle and hold it. The table will start to move automatically. Continue to hold the handle and walk with the table until it reaches the home position.
  217. Make sure the cradle is locked in place at the home position.
