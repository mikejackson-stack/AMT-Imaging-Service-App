About this task

Overview

The blower box is a large ferrous object that must be secured to prevent it from being pulled in into the magnet. This PM check ensures the blower box remains secure to prevent any hazardous conditions.

IMPORTANT: One FE is needed to check the anchoring of the blower box. If the blower box needs to be re-anchored, two FEs are required.

Topic ID: id_SL13478904-1267637

Inspection (one FE required)

Topic ID: id_SL13482794-1267637

Push Test

About this task

One FE will verify that the blower box is secure by pushing on the blower box. If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.

Procedure

  1. Warning
    DEATH OR SERIOUS INJURY!
    This procedure requires handling highly ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet. Any field strength greater than 200 gauss will forcibly attract the blower box.
    Ensure your body is in a safe position and not between the blower box and the magnet. If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.
    Position yourself so that your body is in a safe position and not between the blower box and the magnet.
  2. Bend at the knees or kneel until you are about chest-level to the blower box.
  3. Put your hands on the blower box in a position to push it away from the magnet. Lean into the blower box and put your weight into it, but without straining yourself. Apply a force equivalent to pushing a fully loaded phantom cart over a carpeted floor. Your body must not come between the blower box and the magnet during this test.
  4. If the blower box became loose or was dislodged during the test, the test failed. Continue to Re-Mounting after Failed Inspection (Requires Two FEs).
  5. If this test passed, go to Rock and Rotate Test.
Topic ID: id_SL13482765-1267637

Rock and Rotate Test

About this task

One FE will verify that the blower box is secure by attempting to rock and rotate the blower box. If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.

Procedure

  1. Warning
    DEATH OR SERIOUS INJURY!
    This procedure requires handling highly ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet. Any field strength greater than 200 gauss will forcibly attract the blower box.
    Ensure your body is in a safe position and not between the blower box and the magnet. If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.
    Position yourself so that your body is not between the blower box and the magnet.
  2. Bend at the knees or kneel until your chest is one or two feet above the blower box.
  3. Place your hands at opposite corners of the blower box. Attempt to rock and rotate the blower box, without straining yourself. Be sure that your body does not come between the blower box and the magnet at any time during the test.
  4. If the blower box became loose or was dislodged during the rocking and rotating, the test failed. Proceed to Re-Mounting after Failed Inspection (Requires Two FEs).
  5. If the blower box remained secured, the test passed. Proceed to Finalization.
Topic ID: id_SL13482770-1267637

Re-Mounting after Failed Inspection (Requires Two FEs)

About this task

If the inspection failed, the blower box must be re-mounted.

Procedure

  1. DANGER
    Electrocution Hazard!
    High Voltage Present
    Power Down and perform LOTO. See the “MR Service Safety Manual” PN 5452735.
    Power down and perform LOTO. See the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.
  2. After powering down, wait five minutes to allow the capacitors to discharge.
  3. DANGER
    Death or Serious Injury!
    This procedure requires handling highly ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet. Any field strength greater than 200 gauss will forcibly attract the blower box. The blower box must remain outside the 200 gauss zone at all times.
    • Two safety-trained FEs must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields. Two safety-trained FEs are required to perform this procedure.
    • Ensure your body is in a safe position and not between the blower box and the magnet while performing this test.
    • If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.
    CAUTION
    Personal Injury!
    The blower box weighs up to 50 lb.
    Two people are required to lift the blower box due to the weight of the box and two people are required when working near a ramped magnet.
    Create a clear path and clear work area to ensure that the blower box remains outside of the 200 gauss zone. If needed, mark the 200 gauss line on the floor.
  4. Order the appropriate part for the repair:
    1. If the blower box was not secured and no Velcro is present, order the appropriate FMI kit and install it per the instructions:

      • FMI 60798 – MR Premium (HDx based)

      • FMI 67902 - HDe

      • FMI 67812 – SV

    2. If Velcro is present but is no longer secured, order part number 5611176. This kit contains two different Velcro pieces to secure the blower box.
  5. If necessary, remove the power cable, ground cable and air hoses from the blower box.
  6. Mark the floor or plate so that you can easily return the blower box to its original position. Be aware that the plate is larger than the base of the blower box.
  7. Warning
    DEATH OR SERIOUS INJURY!
    This procedure requires handling highly ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet. Any field strength greater than 200 gauss will forcibly attract the blower box.
    Ensure your body is in a safe position and not between the blower box and the magnet. If at any point the qualitative test cannot be performed safely, do not proceed. Escalate the issue as a safety hazard.
    With both FEs working together, tilt the blower box to access the base of the blower box. Be careful to stay outside of the 200 gauss zone.
  8. With the blower box in a safe position outside the 200 gauss line, remove the failed Velcro from the blower box and the floor or mounting plate.
  9. At this point, do not remove the adhesive backing from the replacement Velcro.
  10. Clean and prepare the surface for the Velcro adhesive. If necessary, use isopropyl alcohol and cotton swabs or a rag. If necessary, involve site maintenance to ensure the surface is properly prepared for adhesion. Make sure the surface is dry before applying the adhesive.
  11. With the Velcro facing away from the base of the blower box, bolt the adapter plate to the blower box. The plate is universally designed with slots to fit each blower box style.
    ItemPart NumberDescriptionQuantity
    15354663Velcro 4 inch wide 1000 Loop Black 30 in.
    25354656Velcro 4 inch wide 1000 Loop White15 in.
    5611176Orderable FRU which contains both items 1 and 2
  12. The mounting adapter plate is designed with slots (1) to prevent the bolt heads (2) from turning. Install the split washer (lock washer) (4) against the nut (3) and the flat washer (5) against the blower box surface.
  13. Remove the adhesive backing from the Velcro.
  14. With both FEs working together, carefully lower the blower box into its permanent position, as marked on the floor or surface. Apply downward pressure or sit on the box for 30-60 seconds to seal the adhesive securely to the surface.

What to do next

Finalization

  1. Reattach the power cable, ground cable, and air hose to the blower box.

  2. Remove LOTO and restore power to the system. See the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.

  3. Confirm that the blower box is working properly. Listen for airflow to the filter.

  4. Perform a basic scan to ensure a properly working system.
