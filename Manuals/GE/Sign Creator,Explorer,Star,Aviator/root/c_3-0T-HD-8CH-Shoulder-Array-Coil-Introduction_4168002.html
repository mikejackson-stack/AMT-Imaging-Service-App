Environmental Requirements

Storage

This equipment must be stored under the following conditions:

  • Ambient temperature range: Packaged, -15°C to 55°C; Unpackaged, 21°C to 32°C

  • Relative humidity range: Packaged, 20% to 95%; Unpackaged, 45% to 75%

  • Atmospheric pressure range of 645 hPa to 795 hPa

To store the coil, a storage space of 40 cm (W) x 81 cm (L) x 30.5 cm (H) (16 in x 32 in x 12 in) is required.

Dimensions

  • Coil: 25.4 cm x 22.9 cm x 25.4 cm (10 in x 9 in x 10 in)

  • Phantom Holder Assembly: 25.4 cm x 25.4 cmx 18.4 cm (10in x 10 in x 7.25 in)

Weight

  • Coil: 1.5 Kg (3.4 lbs.)

  • Phantom Holder Assembly: 1.5 Kg (3.3 lbs.)
