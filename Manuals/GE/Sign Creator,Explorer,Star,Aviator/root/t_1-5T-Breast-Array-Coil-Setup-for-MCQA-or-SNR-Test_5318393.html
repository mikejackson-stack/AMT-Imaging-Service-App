About this task

Follow this procedure to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HD Vibrant Breast Array Coil, GE/USAI catalog number M3087JG or M7000FZ.

Note:

Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

Topic ID: id_SL2928950-404922

Legacy Phantom Procedure

Procedure

  1. Position the coil on the table as shown below.
  2. Position the phantom in the coil base as shown below.
  3. Landmark on the coil crosshairs.
  4. Perform MCQA Test, if available. Otherwise, perform Surface Coils SNR Test.

Unified Phantom Procedure

Procedure

  1. Place the coil on the patient table, and insert the phantom positioner into the coil as shown below.

    The position of the spherical phantoms from the side of the coil is shown below.

  2. Place the phantoms on the phantom positioner as shown below, connect the coil to Port A of the LPCA, landmark on the center of the bridge between both chambers, and Advance to Scan.
  3. Perform MCQA Test, if available. Otherwise, perform Surface Coils SNR Test.
