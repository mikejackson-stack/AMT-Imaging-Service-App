Pneumatic Patient Alert System Inspection

Prerequisites

Table 1. Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable5 minutesNot Applicable

About this task

Overview

The Pneumatic Patient Alert System is a stand-alone system that allows the patient to alert the operator even when the console volume is turned down. Patient-to-Operator contact is accomplished when the patient compresses a hand-held squeeze bulb that activates an audible and visual alarm near the operator. Either a pulse or steady audible tone can be selected at the control box. The operator must reset the control box to stop the alarms. Follow the procedure below to verify proper functioning of the Pneumatic Patient Alert System.

Procedure

  1. Check that the green LED on the control box is lit.
  2. Set the control box steady/pulse switch to Pulse.
  3. Compress the squeeze bulb slowly until fully collapsed.
  4. Make sure the red LED on the control box is lit, and the audible alarm is emitting a pulsating tone.
  5. Set the control box steady/pulse switch to Steady.
  6. Make sure the red LED on the control box remains lit, and the audible alarm is emitting a steady tone.
  7. Press the Reset button on the control box.
  8. Make sure the red LED on the control box and the audible alarm are off, but the green LED remains lit.

Finalization

No finalization steps.
