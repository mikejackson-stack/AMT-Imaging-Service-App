3.0T HD 8CH Shoulder Array Coil FRUs and Replacement

Procedure Overview

This document contains the cable replacement procedure for the 3.0T HD 8CH Shoulder Array Coil by NeoCoil, catalog M3340CC and the field replaceable units (FRUs) (see Field Replaceable Units).

Procedure

Topic ID: id_SL2077495-1064560

Cable Removal

Note:

You will need the torque screw driver (8in-lbs) from the tool kit and the anti-static station.

  1. Remove screws from preamp housing.

  2. Carefully open housing halves and set aside cover.

  3. Remove four cable clamp screws from the housing.
  4. Remove screws from four corner locations on the large PCB.
  5. Remove all eight cable connectors. Pull cable connector straight from socket to ensure no damage is done to the socket center pin. Check center pin for damage.
  6. Remove DC Connector. Use a flat screw driver to gently pry each side until loose.

  7. Discard cable.

  3. Replace cable clamp screws and torque to 8in-lbs.

  4. Carefully replace cover ensuring no cables or wires are pinched by the housing.

  5. Replace housing screws and torque to 8in-lbs.
