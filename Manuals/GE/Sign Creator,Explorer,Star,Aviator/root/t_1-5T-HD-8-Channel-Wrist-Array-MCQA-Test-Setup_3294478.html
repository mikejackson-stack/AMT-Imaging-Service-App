About this task

Follow this process to prepare for the MCQA test using the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Wrist Array by Invivo.

Procedure

  1. Place the baseplate of Wrist coil on the patient table as shown in Figure 1.
  2. Attach the baseplate riser to the baseplate as shown in the Figure 2. Baseplate and the Baseplate riser in the locked position are shown in Figure 3.
  3. Attach 1.5T HD 8Ch Wrist Array to the baseplate assembly using the knobs is as shown in Figure 4. Slide the locked coil to centre and align properly.
  4. Place the cubical unified phantom in the coil by lifting the upper portion of the coil as shown in the Figure 5. Close the coil and lock it (lock in Figure 6.
  5. Connect the coil to respective port of the LPCA and landmark the coil at the marks shown using arrows as shown in Figure 6 and advance to scan.
  6. Click here to go to the MCQA Test

No finalization steps.
