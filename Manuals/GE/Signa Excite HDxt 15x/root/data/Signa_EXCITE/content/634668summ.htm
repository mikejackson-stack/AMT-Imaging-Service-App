Caster Brake Check

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Apply the brake on the three lock casters, one at a time. See Illustration 1. Refer to Patient Transport Theory for more information.

    (Step Details)

  1. While holding the handle of the table, push and pull the table and observe any play in the casters. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. If there is movement in any of the braked casters, it must be replaced.

  1. Refer to the Caster Replacement procedure, Patient Table Casters

  1. If needed, replace casters Total Lock Caster, 2330261 and Directional Lock Caster, 2330261-2.
