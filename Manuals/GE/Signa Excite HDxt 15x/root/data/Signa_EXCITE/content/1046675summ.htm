System Gain Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

No image artifacts, verified by reviewing the images after scan is complete. (See Installation & Calibration Wizard)

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Preliminary Setup

  1. Position the head sphere in the head loader and fasten the strap. Position the head loader on top of the head loader positioner in the head coil (see Illustration 1) so the loader's black center line lies directly below the head coil's Superior/Inferior center marker. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Properly position the Head Sphere and Loader as previously noted.

  1. Establish landmark on the center line of the Head Loader, but do not Advance to Scan.

  1. Run the cradle in so the display reads 1135 mm. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn on the alignment light and then position the body loader on the cradle so that the alignment light is properly centered on the loader crosshair. Place wedges on both sides of the body loader to prevent movement.

  1. Press [Advance to Scan].

  1. Start the System Gain Calibration Tool.

    (Step Details)

2.2   Head and Body Calibration

  1. Type 3 and press <Enter> to select Both Head and Body Mode, and the calibration begins. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. The calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, “Do you want to save the recon scale factor”. Type Y and press <Enter> to save the recon scale factor. (See Illustration 5.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is complete.

2.3   Body Calibration

NOTE:The system is dependent on landmarking the head coil, even for body mode only scans.

  1. Type 2 and press <Enter> to select Body Mode, and the calibration begins. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. When the test is complete, the calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, “Do you want to save the BODY recon scale factor”. Type Y and press <Enter> to save the calibration file. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is complete.

2.4   Head Calibration

  1. Type 1 and press <Enter> to select Head Mode only calibration, and the calibration begins. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. When the test is complete, the calibration results display on the screen. If adjustments are required, the system asks, “Do you want to save the HEAD recon scale factor”. Type Y and press <Enter> to save the calibration file. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Type 4 and press <Enter> to exit the tool. The calibration is complete.

3  Finalization

  1. Remove all phantoms from the patient table used during the calibration.

  1. Remove all service tools from the patient table used during the calibration.
