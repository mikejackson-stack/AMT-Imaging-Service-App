Patient Table Caster Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport and move from exam room.

  1. Prepare block of wood to support table when casters are replaced.

  1. Remove rubber cap from the caster to be replaced (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove caster screw (center) from the caster to be replaced.

  1. Obtain another caster screw from any one of the other casters by repeating steps Step  and Step .

  1. Return to the caster to be replaced. Thread the two caster screws in the vacant Caster Release shafts of the caster bushing (see Illustration 2). Force the tapered caster shaft out of the caster bushing by tightening two caster screws, equally, a little at a time, until caster taper fit breaks loose and caster falls off.

    (Step Details)

  1. Be sure that bushing is not bell-mouthed at bottom. If bushing is damaged, remove by turning out of the bottom of the casting using a ¾-inch (19 mm) socket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using synthetic lubricating grease, lubricate tapered stem of caster and threads of caster clamp screw.

  1. Install new caster into bushing. Straight line tracking (steering) caster should be installed so that it is oriented in position shown when locked in detent position (see Illustration 1).

  1. Remove screws from Caster Release shafts and return each screw to the Caster Clamp shaft it originated from.

  1. Tighten screws into Caster Clamp shafts.

  1. Remove support wood blocks.

  1. Perform procedure for checking caster height in Leveling Patient Transport Procedure.

    (Step Details)
