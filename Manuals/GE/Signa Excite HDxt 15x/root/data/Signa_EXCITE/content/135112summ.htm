Fan Replacement For Later SRFD2 Amplifiers Only

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION Hazard!
POWER NOT REMOVED FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO SERVICE THE UNIT
REMOVE POWER FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO SERVICE THE UNIT. VERIFY THAT THE RFI/AMP AND SSM BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU ARE OFF. LOCK AND TAG OUT THE PLEXI-GLASS COVER ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Lockout/Tagout is performed for the SRFD2 Amplifier Cabinet.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. If either of the lower two fan units needs replacement then disconnect all the RF coaxial cables shown in Illustration 1. Confirm that the labeling on each cable matches the port to which it is connected before removing the cable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the red and black power spade leads to the failed fan. Use needle nose pliers to carefully grasp the lower body of the spade lead connector (NOT THE WIRE!) and then pull each spade lead connector loose from the fan. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Each fan is held in place with 3 nuts on 3 threaded studs mounted on the RF amplifier chassis. The location of each of the fan nuts is shown in Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. Use the 7/32” socket, extension, and drive handle to remove each of the 3 nuts from the failed fan. See Illustration 4

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the fan away from the RF amplifier. SeeIllustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain the replacement fan and notice that the fan power lead spade connectors are polarized. See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. Before affixing replacement fan to RF chassis, first connect fan power leads to fan so that black (negative) lead connects to rear spade lead on fan while red (positive) lead connects to front space lead on fan

  1. Place replacement fan over threaded studs on RF chassis and use fingers to thread nuts one or two turns onto stud. Threading the nuts in this way allows less opportunity for dropping them into the cabinet.

  1. Once all nuts are threaded onto studs then use 7/32” socket, extension, and drive handle assembly to tighten nuts and secure fan to RF chassis

4  Finalization

  1. Reconnect any coaxial cables removed earlier to rear of RF amplifier.

  1. Remove Lockout/Tagout hardware from PDU and follow proper Lockout/Tagout procedures to put PDU breaker in on position and reapply power to system

  1. Place power breaker on rear of RF amplifier into up (ON) position. Note that fans all rotate in same clockwise direction. If replacement fan does not rotate in correct direction then power leads are swapped. This must be corrected or early amplifier failure may result.

  1. Perform one head and body scan to confirm that the system is fully operational

  1. Remove the anti-tip legs from the RF Cabinet.
