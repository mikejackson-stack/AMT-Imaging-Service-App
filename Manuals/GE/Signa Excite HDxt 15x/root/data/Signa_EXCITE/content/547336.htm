Cradle Release From Patient Transport Adjustment

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1

2  Overview

The actuator on the longitudinal drive carriage must be set so that it presses a button on the cradle to mechanically release the cradle from the patient transport, allowing it to move forward into the magnet bore.

3  Procedure

  1. To properly adjust the cradle release, slide the plunger back as far as it can go from the front of the carriage (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  CRADLE RELEASE FROM TRANSPORT ADJUSTMENT

  1. Loosen the set screw and rotate the threaded actuator in or out as needed, then tighten the set screw.
