Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   To Fasten the Upper Bellows

  1. Depending on servicing needs, the Patient Table may be docked or undocked.

  1. Remove four screws (two at front, two at rear) that connects the bottom end of the Upper Bellows to the top of the Lower Base Side Cover. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. To fasten the Upper Bellows in an up position, lift the bellows from the bottom of both sides and use the Velcro straps located on the inside of the bellows to secure the cover in place.

1.2   To Remove the Lower Base Side Cover

  1. Remove four screws (two at front, two at rear) that connects the bottom end of the Upper Bellows to the top of the Lower Base Side Cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. On the left or right side of the table, remove two screws from the Lower Base Side Cover. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift Lower Base Side Cover off of front and rear guide studs on the Front and Rear Base Covers.

    (Step Details)
