  • Id: geservice Enter

  • Name: LVShim

  • Weight (lb.): 300< Enter>

  • Set Patient Protocols to Service

  • At the front enclosure, press <LANDMARK>, then <MOVE TO SCAN>.

  • Under Patient Protocols select [Service], then [Other].

  • Find "Protocol" LVShim and click on the protocol, then click on the "Series" to be used.

    NOTE:It is strongly recommend that the localizer scan be done first to verify that the phantom is correctly centered. Once the phantom is centered, the LVShim (scan) should be performed

  • Click on [Accept] to download the series.

  • In Additional Parameters, click [User CVs Screen].

  • In the User Control Variables window, enter the following:

    • No. of Scan Planes. 6

    • Bandwidth: 200 - 1000 Hz. (Begin with higher band width and reduce each interation . Final LVShim specs require 200 Hz band width.

    • Bandwidth: 400 - 1000 Hz. (Begin with higher band width and reduce each iteration . Final LVShim specs require 400 Hz band width.
