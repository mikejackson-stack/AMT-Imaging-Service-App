Universal Power Monitor - MNS Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
fatal electric shock hazard!
dangerous voltage levels throughout the upm cabinet.
Ensure that the rf amplifier IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Disable VSWR Fault Detection in the MNS Amplifier

  1. Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) power to the MNS Amplifier.

  1. Remove the top panel from the MNS Amplifier cabinet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top cover to the Controller Module in the MNS Amplifier cabinet (see Illustration 2 ).

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate SMA cable connections to J7 and J8 on the Controller Module PCB. (see Illustration 3). Remove the connections and move the cables away from the PCB. Restrain the cables with tape, if necessary.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the top panel of the MNS Amplifier cabinet.

  1. Remove LOTO and re-apply power to the MNS Amplifier.

1.2   MNS Amplifier Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit TR Faults by moving SW2 on the driver module in PEN Cabinet to the TR Disable position (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the RF Enable S2 switch on the MNS Exciter into the disable (down) position (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Plug the power supply on the dummy load into a 110 VAC service outlet in the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Use digital multimeter to measure resistance between dummy load RF connector center pin and shield. Confirm a reading of 50 ohm +/- 5 ohm. Replace dummy load if resistance is out of range.

  1. Attach dummy load as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the RF Enable S2 switch into the enable (up) position.

1.3   Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - MNS Forward Power

  1. To establish a Landmark, at the workstation, click the [Create a new worklist] button to start a new patient.

  1. Enter the following parameters:

    1. Patient ID: geservice.

    1. Weight: 111.

  1. Press the Alignment Lights button to turn on.

  1. Attach the MNS 31P T/R switch cabling to the LPCA A Connector (see Illustration 7).

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the cradle to the Home position and then drive it into the bore.

  1. Press Landmark button when the alignment light is approximately where the center of the head coil would be.

  1. Start the UPM Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the Nuclei [3IP].

  1. Select Power Monitor type. [Forward].

  1. Ensure the [Calibrate] button is selected in Universal Power Monitor Tool window.

  1. Select [Start] button, ensure the hardware setup is ok, and select [Yes].

  1. Status should notify user of current step in progress and indicate Pass/Fail when complete. If failure occurs, see UPM Failure Occurs.

  1. Proceed to MNS Reflected Power Calibration.

1.4   MNS Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Move RF enable switch into disabled (Down) position.

  1. Disconnect cable from MNS output J4 and leave J4 open.

  1. Move RF enable switch into enabled (Up) position.

  1. Return to the UPM tool user interface.

  1. Make sure Nuclei [3IP] is selected.

  1. Select Power Monitor type [Reflected].

  1. Select the [Start] button.

  1. In a few minutes, tool displays Pass/Fail. If failure occurs, see UPM Failure Occurs.

1.5   UPM Failure Occurs

  1. Ensure proper calibration equipment setup.

  1. Check cabling between UPM and amplifier.

  1. Check mgd_stage File: cd /w/config. Remove the “p” if necessary. (p=UPM emulated).

  1. Connect J7 and J8 cables (previously disconnected) to the Controller Module PCB.

  1. Perform Maximum RF power calibration procedure. Repeat UPM calibration.

  1. Check UPM for power.

  1. Check error log for details of UPM failure.

  1. Perform Body UPM calibration. If it fails, check error log.

  1. If detector boards are replaced, perform the UPM calibration to put the new boards into service.

1.6   Re-enable VSWR Fault Detection in the MNS Amplifier

  1. Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) power to the MNS Amplifier.

  1. Remove the top panel of the MNS Amplifier cabinet (see Illustration 1).

  1. Re-connect the SMA cable connections to J7 and J8 on the Controller Module PCB, previously disconnected above (see Illustration 3).

  1. Replace the top cover of the Controller Module in the MNS Amplifier cabinet (see Illustration 2).

  1. Replace the top panel of the MNS Amplifier cabinet.

  1. Remove LOTO and re-apply power to the MNS Amplifier.

2  Finalization

NOTE:If able to complete UPM MNS calibration, immediately proceed to UPM - MNS Functional Checks; otherwise, proceed to Finalization steps.

  1. Select [End Exam].

  1. Place the RF Enable S2 switch in the disable (down) position (see Illustration 5).

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment from the MNS Amplifier.

  1. Reattach the MNS J4 Heliax cable.

  1. Re-enable the T/R drive (see Illustration 4).

  1. Place the RF Enable S2 switch in the enable (up) position (see Illustration 5).
