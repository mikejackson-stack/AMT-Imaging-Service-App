2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HD Body Array Coil by GE/USAI (M3335MC).

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Legacy Phantom Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Connecting the Coil

  1. Place a phantom between Posterior and Anterior parts of the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Positioning Phantom

  1. Secure the coil over the phantom by strips as it shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Securing Coil Over Phantom

  1. Landmark the coil at the indicated line (arrows) as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Landmarking the Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test if it is available, otherwise perform the SNR Test.

4.2  Unified Phantom Procedure

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table. See Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Posterior Section of Coil on Table

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section as in Illustration 5 such that the TL unified phantoms are well centered on both S/I directions as shown in Illustration 6 and R/L direction as shown in Illustration 7.

    Illustration 6:  Phantom Posterior Aligned on S/I Direction

    Illustration 7:  Phantom Posterior Aligned on R/L Direction

  1. Place the anterior section on the TL unified phantoms such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in Illustration 8 and Illustration 9.

    Illustration 8:  Phantom Anterior Aligned on S/I Direction

    Illustration 9:  Phantom Anterior Aligned on R/L Direction

  1. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantoms.

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the center cross mark as shown in Illustration 10 and advance to scan.

    Illustration 10:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test.
