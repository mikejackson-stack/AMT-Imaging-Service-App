Longitudinal Drive Motor Alignment to Field

(Click Here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

This procedure must be performed on either a replacement motor or upon initial system installation to ensure the motor is properly aligned to the magnetic field.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

WARNING
Personal injury and equipment damage!
Strong Magnetic Field present.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. the path should be as far from the magnet as practical and avoid high flux-density fields.

Safety Requirements:

  • Movement of ferrous material in the magnet room must follow the GE service procedure for that device. When exiting, move away from the magnet in the most direct manner possible. Except when moving ferrous material to and from its native location on or near the magnet, the static magnetic field in any portion of the service path shall not exceed 200 Gauss.

  • Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.

When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. It is required to walk the path prior to beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.

CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!
THE MOTOR IS MADE OF FERROUS MATERIAL. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL CAUSE IT TO BE FORCIBLY ATTRACTED TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO MOTOR, MAGNET ENCLOSURE, OR MAGNET, DO NOT LIFT THIS MOTOR ABOVE THE LEVEL OF THE MOUNTING ON THE REAR PEDESTAL FRAME. TWO (2) PEOPLE ARE REQUIRED TO ALIGN THE MOTOR.

4  Procedure

  1. Refer to OSHA Lock-Out/Tag-Out Requirements, and remove power to the Longitudinal (Long) Drive Motor by switching off these circuit breakers:

    • RF/PEN Cabinet: Lock-Out and Tag-Out (LOTO) circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of the cabinet on the magnet enclosure power supply module.

    • RF/PEN II Cabinet: LOTO circuit breaker MR1 A20 CB4 at rear of the cabinet.

  1. Verify that voltage between 38.5V Dock and Dock/Light RTN is 0 VDC.

  1. Remove the rear pedestal left side cover.

  1. Remove the four mounting bracket screws and loosen the lower set screw (shown below) for initial system installation.

  1. Refer to the illustration below for the remaining steps when aligning the long drive motor.

CAUTION
PERSONAL INJURY OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE!
THE MOTOR IS MADE OF FERROUS MATERIAL. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL CAUSE IT TO BE FORCIBLY ATTRACTED TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR DAMAGE TO MOTOR, MAGNET ENCLOSURE, OR MAGNET, DO NOT LIFT THIS MOTOR ABOVE THE LEVEL OF THE MOUNTING ON THE REAR PEDESTAL FRAME. TWO (2) PEOPLE ARE REQUIRED TO ALIGN THE MOTOR.

  1. Holding the motor firmly with both hands, and staying as far away from magnet as possible, loosen the two mounting bracket screws, and insert the motor assembly into the shaft coupling on the rear pedestal.

  1. Carefully loosen the grip on the motor just enough to allow it to align itself to magnetic field.

  1. While one person is holding the motor and aligning it to the mounting bracket screw holes, the second person installs the four screws to secure motor assembly to the mounting bracket.

  1. Tighten the lower set screw on the motor shaft coupling.

  1. Connect the wires onto the motor terminals: Black leads to black screw cap and multi-colored leads to red screw cap.

  1. Install the rear pedestal left side cover.

  1. Remove LOTO devices and restore power to the system.

  1. Verify that pressing the [In Fast] button on Control Switch Board (MG2A4, A5) moves the cradle into the magnet bore, and pressing [Out Fast] button moves the cradle out of magnet bore.

    NOTE:If cradle movement is opposite of what it should be, reverse wires on the terminals of the longitudinal motor.
