Gradient Load Continuity Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
THE Gradient AMPLIFIERS ACT AS CONSTANT LOAD SOURCES AND WILL SEND MAXIMUM CURRENT TO ANY LOAD (INCLUDING YOU!).
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, ENSURE THAT THE POWER IS OFF BEFORE CONTINUING WITH THIS PROCEDURE.

  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

1  Procedure

1.1   Preliminary Setup

  1. Shutdown the operating software and then power at the Host Computer.

  1. Shut down power to the system at the Main Disconnect Unit (MDU) and then securing the MDU circuit breaker for the entire system (except the Compressor) with the lock out tag out devices.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that all power has dissipated by measuring incoming power to the ACGD / HFD Power Supply by waiting for more than 5 minutes after doing the LOTO process and measuring the VOLTAGES between the terminals in all the 3 subsystems as shown in Illustration 1. The voltages there should read 0 Volts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Also confirm that the LEDs in the front are not lit up as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Gradient Driver Subsystem Load Continuity Test Procedure

  1. Switch the Multi meter to the ohms position as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Short the two leads to ensure that the multimeter (in the ohms mode) reads zero ohms. If it reads some minor resistance (say > 0.5 ohms), take note of it in the future measurements.

  1. Measure the resistance between the two terminals as shown in Illustration 4. Do the measurements for all the 3 driver amplifier. It should read less than 1ohm. (Remember to take into consideration the zero adjustment described in the previous step).

    (Step Details)

  1. Measure the resistance between the stud and the ground as shown in Illustration 5. It should be greater than 10K ohms (<10,000 ohms)

    (Step Details)

  1. Continue the resistance measurement procedure for all the 6 studs in the 3 driver subsystems. It must be greater than 10,000 ohms (< 10,000 ohms).

  1. Measure the resistance between the stud of one subsystem and each of the others as shown in Illustration 6. Again, it must be greater than 10K ohms (<10,000 ohms).

    (Step Details)

1.3   System Restoration

  1. If any of the measurements were not in spec, then do the tests after removing the gradient cables.

  1. If the problem still persists, replace that component or module.

  1. To restore the system to specifications, follow the steps found in the Finalization section.

2  Finalization

  1. Ensure that all connections are tight.

  1. Measure for open circuit load and short to ground. The Field Engineer will disconnect load cables at measurement points as he continues to isolate opens and shorts.

  1. Enable power at the PDU by removing the lock out tag out devices.

  1. Apply power to the Gradient Cabinet.

  1. Replace all the covers on the cabinets.

  1. Perform a body or head scan to ensure system functionality.
