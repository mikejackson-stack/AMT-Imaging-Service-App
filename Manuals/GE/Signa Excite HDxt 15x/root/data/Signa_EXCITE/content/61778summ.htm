Patient Table Casters

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Unlock patient transport, and move from exam room.

  1. Prepare block of wood to support table when casters are replaced.

  1. Remove rubber cap from the caster to be replaced (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove caster clamp screw (center screw) from the caster to be replaced.

  1. Repeat steps Step 3 and Step 4 on any one of the other casters, because two caster clamp screws are required to force the tapered caster shaft out of the caster bushing on the defective caster.

  1. Return to the caster to be replaced. Thread the two caster clamp screws in the vacant caster release screw holes of the caster bushing. Force the tapered caster shaft out of the caster bushing by tightening two caster clamp screws, equally, a little at a time, until caster taper fit breaks loose and caster falls off.

  1. Be sure that bushing is not bell-mouthed at bottom. If bushing is damaged, remove by turning out of the bottom of the casting using a 3/4-inch (19 mm) socket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using synthetic lubricating grease, lubricate tapered stem of caster and threads of caster clamp screw.

  1. Install new caster into bushing. Straight line tracking (steering) caster should be installed so that it is oriented in position shown when locked in detent position (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Install caster clamp screw and tighten.

  1. Remove support wood blocks.

  1. Perform procedure for Checking Caster Height Adjustment in Leveling Patient Transport Procedure.

    (Step Details)
