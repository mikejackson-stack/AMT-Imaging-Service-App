System Restoration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Close the SSM lids, replace all the lid screws, slide the SSM chassis back into the rack, and replace the four front screws.

  1. Remove Laptop computer connection to SSM (J46), if applicable.

  1. Replace cabinet covers.

  1. Verify J5 is connected.

  1. Perform a TPS reset.

  1. Perform a Head Scan, Body Scan, and Multi-coil scan to verify the system is scanning normally.
