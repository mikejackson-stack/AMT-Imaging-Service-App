Simplified Gradient Shim Procedure for Mobile MR's

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
Completely remove the Quad Head Coil from the cradle before performing any body scans. Failure to do this may damage the Head Coil T/R network.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the Quad Head Coil from the cradle and remove any other phantoms from the bore.

  1. Set up and align the LVshim Phantom as indicated in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter the scan as shown in Table 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark at the center of the phantom (see Illustration 1) and advance to scan.

  1. [Save RX]

  1. [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan]

  1. [Auto prescan] (APS).

  1. [Manual prescan] (MPS).

    1. Record gradient settings x, y, and z on the data sheet in Table 2.

    1. Save gradient setting by selecting; File -> Save

    1. Press DONE

  1. Repeat steps 4 and 8 until gradient setting x, y, and z do not change or toggle between +/- 1 count. For TwinSpeed, repeat steps 1 to 6 for each GradMode

  1. Gradient Shim Data Sheet

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a test scan to insure the system is running properly and then remove any tools used during the procedure.
