1.2   Adjusting for proper Window and Level Setting

  1. With the cursor inside the displayed image in Illustration 3, hold down the middle mouse button and move the mouse in the horizontal plane. View the window control value at the base of the image and set window control value to 100.

    (Step Details)

  1. With the cursor inside the displayed image, hold down the middle mouse button and move the mouse in the vertical plane. View the level control value at the base of the image and set level control value to 1024.

1.3   Fine Tuning the Contrast and Brightness using the SMPTE Pattern

In this section you will adjust the Contrast and brightness controls of the LCD Monitor to optimize the SMPTE Test Pattern for the Window and Level controls. Minimal adjustment should be necessary at this time. However, it is important that you "fine tune" the Contrast and Brightness level using an industry Standard Test pattern. This is an iterative process by adjusting the contrast, then brightness, and then back to contrast. Refer to Illustration 4 on the next page as you continue with this section.

  1. Look at the Pattern. If the 5% and 95% patches are not visible.(item 6, Illustration 3), adjust the brightness control of the display monitor until you can just barely see them. You may also need to re-adjust the contrast, if tearing or smearing of the pattern occurs. (items 1 and 2, and 5 through 8, Illustration 3).Continue adjusting both brightness and contrast until the image is as crisp and clear as you can make it, and the 5% and 95% patches are just barely visible.

    (Step Details)

  1. When satisfied with the display, push the Enter Button on the monitor to save the adjusted Contrast/Brightness settings.

1.4   Locking the Screen Manager Settings

The Eizo L660 is equipped with an adjustment-locking feature. However, it does not prevent the Operator from adjusting Contrast and Brightness settings. To lock access to the Screen Manager:

  1. Turn off the LCD Display with the front power switch.

  1. Press and HOLD the Auto Adjustment button, at the same time push the Power button to turn the LCD Display ON. This will disable access to the Screen Manager.

1.5   Unlocking the Screen Manager Settings

To unlock the Screen Manager:

  1. Press and HOLD the Auto Adjustment button, at the same time push the Power button to turn the LCD Display OFF. This will enable access to the Screen Manager.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the SMPTE Pattern by pressing the <ESC> key.
