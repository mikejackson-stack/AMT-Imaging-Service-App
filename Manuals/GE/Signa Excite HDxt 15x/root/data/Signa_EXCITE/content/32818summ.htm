1  Safety

WARNING
POISON HAZARD!
1.5T and 1.0T PHANTOMs CONTAINS NICKEL, A SUSPECT CARCINOGEN.
DO NOT INGEST. DISPOSE OF AS A HAZARDOUS WASTE ACCORDING TO STATE AND FEDERAL REGULATIONS.

WARNING
Poison Hazard!
3.0T phantoms contain dimethyl Silicone Fluid. Do Not ingest.
If Lead or spill occurs, wipe up or absorb in inert material and place in a container for disposal. Incineration or landfill is the recommended disposal method.

3  Procedure

  1. Landmark at the landmark lines of the phantom.

  1. At the keypad on the front of the magnet enclosure, press LANDMARK and MOVE TO SCAN.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the operator work space, prepare the system for a “First Image” scan using the scan protocol shown in the Service Protocols procedure located on the service methods CD-ROM, or for the alternate proprietary procedure, see Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. DISPLAY IMAGE
