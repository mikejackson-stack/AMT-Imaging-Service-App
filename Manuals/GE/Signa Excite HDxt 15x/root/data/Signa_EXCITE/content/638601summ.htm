3  Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, select the scan icon in the desktop control panel.

  1. If necessary, exit out of any previous exams by selecting [End Exam].

  1. Click on [New Pt ]and enter the following:

    1. Id: geservice

    1. Name: snr check

    1. Weight (lb.): 111

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Quad Head Coil (if present) from the cradle.

  1. Place the surface coil to be tested on the table.

  1. Connect the coil connector to its mating connector in the Carriage Assembly.

  1. For Coils using the Universal Phantom:

    1. Position the Universal Phantom Holder on the coil. Refer to Illustration 1 for specific details for each coil type.

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the 100-mm phantom in the phantom receptacle, LANDMARK, and MOVE TO SCAN. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

  1. GP Flex Coil Setup: Set up Flex Coil per Illustration 3. LANDMARK, and MOVE TO SCAN.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Accept] to load the SNR Check (Surface Coils) protocol.

  1. Click on [Autoview], just below the Autoview image display screen, to automatically display your images.

  1. Click on [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click on [Auto Prescan] to properly calibrate the RF power level for the 90-degree and 180-degree pulses.

  1. Click on [Scan]. Record the Exam number and Series number for SNR Calculations. After the first scan is complete, click on [Scan] again (two back-to-back scans).

  1. For analysis, see Section Image Analysis.

  1. Return to the Signal to Noise Check main menu.

    (Step Details)
