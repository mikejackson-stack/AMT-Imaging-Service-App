2  Overview

Prior to performing work on the Penetration Panel electronics (such as Patient Handling Power Supply) and/or Magnet Room electronics (such as the SRI and Patient Blower), the steps in this Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) procedure must be performed by a LOTO authorized GE Field Engineer (FE). Completing all steps in this procedure will ensure a safe environment when working on these subsystems.

NOTE:This procedure does not cover the Gradient Filter. For those LOTO requirements, refer to Gradient Subsystem LOTO.

4  Procedure

4.1  Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

  1. Multimeter set to read voltage for verification of PHPS capacitor discharge.

  1. Visual inspection for all other equipment in Magnet Room and Penetration Panel.

4.2  Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnections

  1. Personal lock and tag for each FE at the site, working on the de-energized equipment.

4.3  Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. When replacing the SRI only: Press an <E-Stop> button either on the SCIM or on the front of the scanner.

  1. All items (including SRI): At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches to the OFF position for:

    • PHPS/SSM

    • TAC/BRM GWC

    • Patient Blower

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breaker switches: Apply a personal LOTO device for each FE who will be working on the equipment.

  1. Test that power is dissipated for each item by verifying all LEDs and displays are Off:

    • Gradient Water Chiller

    • Rear Pedestal, LPCA, Dock, SRI and Magnet Room Electronics (All items in the rear pedestal, LPCA, Dock and SRI fall into the category of low voltage low energy.)

4.4  Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare to re-energize the equipment by notifying affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches to the On position for:

    • PHPS/SSM

    • TAC/BRM GWC

    • Patient Blower

No finalization steps.
