2  Procedure

  1. At the operator workspace, prepare the system for a Gradcal-Z scan using the Service Protocols procedure located on the Service Methods CD-ROM, or for the alternate proprietary procedure, see below.

  1. Click on [Scan] (the system auto prescans first).

  1. Perform a Calibration Image Analysis. For TwinSpeed, highlight GradMode=WHOLE and click on OK.

  1. For TwinSpeed, repeat this section for the GradMode=ZOOM.
