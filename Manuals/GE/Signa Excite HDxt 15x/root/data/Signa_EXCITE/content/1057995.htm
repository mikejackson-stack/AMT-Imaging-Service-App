Patient Table Movement Verification

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable20 minutesNot Applicable

2  Overview

The patient table may be operated in either the docked or undocked position. Undocked, it may be raised or lowered via the foot pedals located at the rear of the table. In addition, floor locks are available for locking the wheels during patient transfer. While the patient table is docked to the magnet enclosure, the table may be driven up or down by either the dock pedals or the foot pedals at the rear of the table.

3  Procedure

  1. Undock patient table by pressing second pedal from left at rear of table.

  1. Press the Table Down pedal (far right) to initiate downward movement of the table top. Downward movement of table top should stop when pedal is released. Verify that table travels from highest to lowest position in no more than 15 seconds with table unloaded.

  1. Pump the Table Up pedal (second from right) to initiate upward movement of table. Verify that the table reaches its highest position in 15 pedal strokes or fewer. For accurate check of this function to specification given, pedal strokes must be complete (i.e. push pedal all the way down and let pedal come all the way up before starting next downward movement).

  1. Press the Caster Lock pedal on one of the rear casters to lock both rear casters. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Engaging the Caster Lock Pedal

    1. Verify caster locks inhibit all Patient Table movement when pedal is in locked position.

    1. Verify there is no movement of cradle while Patient Table is undocked.

  1. Dock Patient Table by aligning table with dock and then pressing the far left dock pedal (see Illustration 2). Successful docking should not require excessive force. If table is fully up when docked, verify that carriage advances forward until it connects with cradle latch immediately after docking.

    Illustration 2:  Dock Pedal on Patient Table

  1. Verify that the emergency release lever (located on the right side of the dock) releases the Patient Table from the dock when it is pulled (make sure LPCA has been disengaged from cradle). See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Dock Emergency Release Handle

  1. Redock the patient table.

  1. Verify that when you turn the cradle release handle at the rear of the cradle, it disengages cradle from LPCA. Refer to procedure for Cradle Release Adjustments (Cradle Release Block Adjustment, Cradle Release From Carriage Adjustment, or Cradle Release from Patient Table).

  1. With the cradle fully in the bore, verify that the Table Down pedals on both the dock and table itself do not allow the table to go down. If the table does move down, perform Cradle Home Table Down Interlock Adjustment.
