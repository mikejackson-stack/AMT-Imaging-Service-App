Replacing Magnet Enclosure Control Panels

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. The three control panels can be removed independently.

    (Step Details)

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room).

  1. These panels are secured with poppers, but have hidden cables attached to each of them. Pull Control Panels away from the cover, being careful not to inadvertently pull the cables out (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the panels are free from the enclosure, detach all the cables from each Control Panel.

  1. To replace the control panels reverse these steps.
