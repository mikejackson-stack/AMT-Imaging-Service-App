LCD Monitor Screen Adjustments

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   LCD Monitor Input Priority Setting (For VP231 wb and VP2330 wb)

  1. Setup the LCD monitor Input Priority according to Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Calibrate ViewSonic Monitor (for VP231 wb and VP 2330 wb)

  1. Refer to Illustration 2 for Control locations and functions.

    (Step Details)

  1. Look in the upper-right corner of the front of your monitor to determine the model number. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. To display the Main Menu, press button [1] (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Modify the RGB setting by navigating to Color Adjustments -> User Color.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For VP2330wb) On the VP2330wb monitor, numeric values are used for adjustments. Calibrate your monitor to match the settings in Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For VP231wb) On the VP231wb monitor, a green slider is used for adjustments instead of numerical values. To calibrate the monitor count the major tick marks (there are a total of 14) to determine the proper slider location (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

1.3   Calibrate NEC Monitor (For LCD2490WUXi and LCD2490WUXi2)

  1. See Illustration 6 and Illustration 7 for control locations and functions.

    (Step Details)

  1. To display the main menu, press button <8>, see Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to Table 3 for Monitor Settings.

    (Step Details)

  1. Camera calibration may be required, if GE services the camera, refer to Camera Calibration procedure. If the camera is serviced by another provider, please have the customer contact the vendor to perform camera calibration.

2  Finalization

  1. To save the adjustments, press the [1] button.

  1. To exit the menu, press the [1] button again.
