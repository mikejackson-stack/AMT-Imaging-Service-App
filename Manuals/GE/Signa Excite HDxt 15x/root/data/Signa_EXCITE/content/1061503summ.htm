Removing Cradle Assembly

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock the Patient Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the same time, depress the cradle latch block and push in the Cradle Release button at front of cradle.

    (Step Details)

  1. While holding down the block and pressing the Cradle Release button, slide cradle forward enough so that the cradle covers the top area of the cradle latch block. Release the block and Cradle Release button.

CAUTION
PINCHED FINGERS
FORWARD MOVEMENT OF CRADLE CAN PINCH FINGER WHILE HOLDING DOWN CRADLE LATCH BLOCK
MOVE CRADLE FORWARD SLOWLY AND DELIBERATELY UNTIL BOTTOM EDGE OF CRADLE SUFFICIENTLY COVERS TOP OF CRADLE LATCH BLOCK WHERE YOU CAN REMOVE YOUR FINGER FROM BLOCK.

  1. Push in right actuator on the table front base.

    (Step Details)

  1. Continue to Step 6 or 7.

  1. If you are using a 16-channel system, prop up the cradle (see Illustration 3) or remove it from the Patient Table to perform desired maintenance.

  1. If you are using a 32-channel system, do one of the following:

    1. If you need to fully remove the cradle from the table, perform these steps:

      1. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the rear of the cradle at least 12 inches above the table top to expose the cable take-up assembly.

      1. Remove screws and cable cover.

        (Step Details)

      1. Remove the four balun screws.

      1. Remove the two coil connector screws.

    1. If you do not need to fully remove the cradle:

      1. Slide the cradle forward far enough to clear the cradle guide rail latches at the rear of the table.

      1. Use wood blocks (or similar) to prop up the front of the cradle.

        (Step Details)
