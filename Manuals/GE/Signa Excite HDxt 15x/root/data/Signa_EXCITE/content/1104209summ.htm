2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Installation.

  1. Place the baseplate on the table as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the baseplate riser to the baseplate as shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Wrist Array to the cradle . Note the serial number of the coil. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the locked coil to center position and mechanically locate and align. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place phantom in the coil. See Illustration 5.

  1. Lock the coil using the lock on inferior top lock. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil. See Illustration 6. Advance to scan.

  1. Click here to go to the MCQA Test procedure.

2.2   Unified Phantom Procedure

  1. Place the baseplate of the wrist coil on the patient table as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the base plate riser to the base plate as shown in Illustration 8. Base plate and the Base plate riser in the locked position are shown in Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach 3.0T HD 8-Channel Wrist Array to the baseplate assembly using the knobs is as shown in Illustration 10. Slide the locked coil to center and align properly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the cubical unified phantom in the coil by lifting the upper portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 11. Close the coil and lock it as shown in Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to port A of the LPCA and landmark the coil at the marks shown in Illustration 13 and advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click here to go to the MCQA Test procedure.
