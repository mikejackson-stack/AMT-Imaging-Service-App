Intercom Adjustment for GOC Audio Box

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Overview

  1. There are two speaker volume settings to adjust:

    (Step Details)

  1. The Audio Assembly is located in the Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) .

  1. Volume controls are shown below. Perform the following adjustments:

    (Step Details)

1.2   Accessing Intercom Board

  1. Remove the two securing screws on the rear of the right side GOC cover.

  1. Slide the cover up and remove it.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Maximum Keyboard Speaker Volume R28 (Patient’s Voice)

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control to the maximum setting by rotating the volume wheel Up with your finger.

    (Step Details)

  1. While one person speaks into the patient microphone in the magnet bore, the second person should adjust R28 on the Intercom Board for the desired maximum volume at the keyboard speaker. This is an iterative process. Repeat the voice test until the maximum desired level is achieved.

1.4   Minimum Keyboard Speaker Volume (R13) - Patient’s Voice

This section explains how to adjust the minimum volume of the Patient’s voice in the magnet bore so the Operator can hear the patient during a scan.

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control to the minimum setting by rotating the volume wheel Down with your finger.

    (Step Details)

  1. While one person listens at the keyboard speaker (Patient’s Voice), the other person should speak into the patient microphone in the magnet bore.

    (Step Details)

1.5   Maximum Patient Speaker Volume (R67) - Operator’s Voice

  1. At the Operator Workspace keyboard, set the Patient Volume Control to the maximum setting by rotating the Operators volume wheel Up with your finger.

    (Step Details)

  1. While one person listens at the patient speaker, the other person should speak into the keyboard microphone and adjust R67 on the Intercom Board for the desired maximum volume at the keyboard speaker. This is an iterative process. Repeat the voice test until the maximum desired level is achieved.

    1. If R67 is turned Up too much, a howling sound (feedback) may be heard from the Patient Speaker. Adjust R67 to remove this problem.

    1. Set the Operator Volume to 4, and make sure a howling sound (feedback) is not heard. (A howling sound is often heard when the Operator Volume is set to 4.

2  Finalization

  1. Restore the GOC.

  1. Perform a Head and Body scan to ensure the functionality of the system.
