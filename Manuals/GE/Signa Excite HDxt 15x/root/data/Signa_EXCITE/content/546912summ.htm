Patient Table Hydraulic Pump Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove Hydraulic Pump Assembly

  1. Undock patient transport and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove both outer covers by removing mounting screws (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove inner covers by removing mounting screws.

  1. Raise table to up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Unhook lock bar spring and move lock bar to horizontal position, as shown in Illustration 2, then lower table until lock bar is supporting table in its full up position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the two hydraulic lines to hydraulic pump assembly and fasten upward and aside to avoid excess spillage. Immediately fold and clamp the translucent (yellow) hose from reservoir to prevent oil from draining from the reservoir (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen two mounting screws attaching upper drip pan to lower drip pan (see Illustration 4).

  1. Remove upper drip pan (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Equipment damage possibility. Do not remove spring pins from either end of pump pull rods; this will cause a side loading on the pump, requiring its replacement. The pump pull rods are disconnected from the pump linkage by turning the pump pull rods in a counterclockwise direction after tension has been removed from the pump springs. This is explained in the following procedure. The pump pull rod spring pins are exposed only when the Table Up pedal is pressed (see Illustration 5).
 
 

  1. To remove tension from pull rods, compress main pump spring, as shown in Illustration 7. Block the pump Up pedal fully up to take its weight off of the pull rods.

  1. Push the yoke back and unscrew the pull rods form the pump linkage (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove C-clamps used to compress main spring, as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove machine screw securing bumper to the pump piston (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove three mounting cap screws that secure pump assembly to front table support.

  1. Remove hydraulic pump assembly and gasket.

  1. If absorbent pad attached to upper drip pan appears to be saturated with fluid, remove it and squeeze out fluid.

1.2   Install Replacement Hydraulic Pump Assembly

  1. Prepare replacement pump for installation by applying Locktite 242 to three (3) mounting screws and to machine screw that secures bumper to pump piston.

  1. Set replacement pump assembly on front support of table, making sure that gasket is installed properly, and secure with three mounting screws (see Illustration 8).

  1. Secure the bumper to the pump piston with machine screw.

  1. To compress main pump spring, reinstall C-clamps on front support, as shown in Illustration 9. Screw pump pull rods into pump linkage, alternately tightening each side to avoid side loading the pump. Stop when the rear spring pin on each pull rod is 1/8-inch (3.2 mm) from pump body (flush with back surface of front support) (see Illustration 10).

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect two hydraulic lines to hydraulic pump assembly (see Illustration 11).

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach upper and lower drip pans (see Illustration 12).

    (Step Details)

  1. Block open the Down valve, and pump the Up pedal at least 20 full strokes to purge air from the lines.

  1. Close Down valve and raise table.

  1. Retract lock bar and secure with spring, then lower table.

  1. Fill table reservoir so that fluid is ¼” to ½” above full line when table is at its lowest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that pump pull rods are adjusted equally by comparing position of rear spring pins on pump pull rods (see Illustration 13).

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace inner and outer covers. Note position of Signa logo (if applicable) on outer covers and the semicircle cut out on the inner covers, and then move that end towards the front of table (see Illustration 14).

    (Step Details)
