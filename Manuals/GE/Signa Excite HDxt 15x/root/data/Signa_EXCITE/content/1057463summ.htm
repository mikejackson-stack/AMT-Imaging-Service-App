Cradle Home Table Down Interlock Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Fasten the Upper Bellows in the up position and remove both Lower Base Side covers from the Patient Table. See the Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal document for details.

  1. With cradle in NOT HOME position (removed), check the interlock mechanisms for tolerances. To make adjustments, do one of the following:

    1. 1/16 to 1/32 inch (1.59 to 0.79 mm) gap - carefully bend interlock rod.

      (Step Details)

    1. 1/8 inch (3.18 mm) gap - loosen lock nut and tighten or loosen the cable adjustment screw to achieve the proper gap. Tighten lock nut.
