Main Cradle Latch Cable Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
To reduce the risk of exposing loose ferrous material to the magnetic field, it is mandatory that the patient transport is removed from magnet room during this procedure.

WARNING
RISK OF PERSONAL INJURY!
Lifting Mr parts, such as frus, toolkits and parts of the system without assistance (mechanical or additional personnel), can result in personal injury.
the use of extra personnel is recommended to lift an object weighing more than 35 pounds AND less than 50 pounds. the use of mechanical lift assistance is required for objects weighing more than 90 pounds.

1  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove upper and lower left side covers (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cradle (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Using an M2 Allen wrench, loosen set screw at down link clevis. See Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
POTENTIAL BODY INJURY AND DAMAGE TO A CRITICAL COMPONENT OF THE PATIENT TABLE.
FALLING OF UNSECURED OBJECT
WHEN REMOVING PLATE THAT SECURES THE CRADLE LATCH HOUSING, MAKE SURE TO HOLD THE PLATE IN PLACE AS THE SCREWS ARE REMOVED. FAILURE TO DO SO WILL RESULT IN THE HOUSING FALLING ONCE NO LONGER SECURED TO THE PATIENT TABLE.

  1. Remove four bolts securing cradle latch housing and cradle latch cover (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Unfasten cable end from Down Link Clevis (see Illustration 3).

  1. Pull cable out of Cradle Latch Block on front of patient transport (see Illustration 4).

  1. Insert new cable through head of Cradle Latch Block so once fully threaded, the swaged end fits into hole in latch pin. See Illustration 5 for details on installing cable.

    (Step Details)

  1. Route cable through cable casing and insert through cable Down Link Clevis (see Illustration 3).

  1. Loop cable twice through hole in Down Link Clevis (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Using an M2 Allen wrench, tighten set screw (see Illustration 3).

  1. Return patient transport to exam room.

  1. Dock patient transport.

  1. Adjust cable so that when docked, main latch will be flush with casting. Refer to procedure for Cradle Release Block Adjustment. Cut off excess length after adjustment is complete (see Illustration 6 for appropriate excess length). Secure adjustment by tightening set screw after adjustment is complete (see Illustration 3).

  1. Replace cradle and upper and lower side covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Illustration 1).
