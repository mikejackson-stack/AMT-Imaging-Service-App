Head and Body Cables Possibly SwappedSystem is detecting current on the Head and Body T/R cables that is consistent with them being swapped.Head and Body TR Cable Swap: Swap head TR and body TR cables at Driver Module or input to the RF Amp.
Head and Connector A T/R Cables Possibly SwappedSystem is detecting current on the Head and Connector A cables that is consistent with them being swapped. This error would only occur in an upgrade system, which still has the old LPCA with a Legacy connectorHead and Connector A Cable Swap: Swap head and Conn A TR cables at the TR switch on the pen wall, or Cable I/F at Rear Ped.
MC Cables SwappedSystem is detecting that the MC Cables are potentially swapped. MC Cable Swap: Swap the MC Cables at the Driver Module, Pen-panel, or 16-channel switch.
MC Cables Disconnected The Driver Module has detected that Multicoil Driver Cable <1,2> is disconnected.MC Cable Disconnected: Check for Disconnected cables at the Driver Module, Pen-panel, or 16-channel switch.
