TEAL - Power Distribution Unit (PDU)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
POSSIBLE SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH
Electrical Shock Hazard While The Cabinet is Energized.
REMOVE POWER FROM THE CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE ANY COMPONENTS FROM THE SYSTEM

1  Procedure

1.1   TEAL - Power Distribution Unit (PDU) - Lockout

  1. Bring Down System Software.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the RF/PDU cabinet to expose the front of the PDU.

  1. Refer to Illustration 1 and power off the following circuit breakers:

    1. Gradient Power Supply

    1. SSM / RFI / RF Amp

    1. System Cabinet)

    1. UPS

    1. PC

    1. Host

    1. Gradient Control Power

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green, O (OFF) position. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

  1. Lock Out / Tag Out the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker.

    1. a. Press on the left-pointing arrow on the end of the handle to expose the location where the lock and tag can be placed.

    1. Lock and tag out the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker as shown in Illustration 3

      (Step Details)

1.2   TEAL - Power Distribution Unit (PDU) - Restoring Power

  1. Remove the Lock and the Tag from the PDU “INPUT” circuit breaker.

  1. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to the Red, 1 (ON) position

  1. Power on subsystems powered off earlier.

    1. Gradient Power Supply

    1. SSM / RFI / RF Amp

    1. System Cabinet

    1. UPS

    1. PC

    1. Host

    1. Gradient

  1. Reset the Emergency Off switch

  1. Bring the Signa software back up.

2  Finalization

  1. Insure all subsystems have powered up successfully

  1. Do a test phantom scan to insure proper scanning
