4  Procedure

4.1  Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1 .

    Illustration 1:  Connecting The Coil

  1. Place the phantoms between Posterior and Anterior parts of the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Positioning Phantom

  1. Landmark on the coil crosshairs as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Landmarking Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test if available, otherwise perform the Surface Coils SNR Test.

4.2  Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and latch it to the table.

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms on the center of the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  TL Unified Phantoms on Center

  1. Align the phantoms to the center in S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 5 and Illustration 6.

    Illustration 5:  Alignment in S/I

    Illustration 6:  Alignment in R/L

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom on the CT section and fix the C-Spine Bridge to the hooks provided as shown Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Secure the Large Cylindrical Unified Phantom

  1. Adjust the large cylindrical unified phantom such that it is in complete contact with the TL unified phantom (see Illustration 8).

    Illustration 8:  Adjust the Large Cylindrical Phantom

  1. Adjust TL unified phantoms such that both the phantoms are in complete contact with each other as shown in Illustration 9.

    Illustration 9:  Adjust the TL Unified Phantoms

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the cross mark line on the C-Spine Bridge (see Illustration 10) and advance to scan.

    Illustration 10:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test.
