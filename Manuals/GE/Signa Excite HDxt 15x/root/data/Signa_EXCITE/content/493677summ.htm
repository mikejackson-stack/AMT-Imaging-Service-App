Loading Operator Documentation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Launch Guided Install. Login id is root and password is operator (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. In the content window on the left side, select Service SW/Insite (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Click the [Install Online Help] button (see Illustration 2).

  1. When prompted to “insert the media containing online documentation” insert the DVD Media for Operator Manual into the host computer's DVD-ROM drive and then click [OK] (seeIllustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the installation is complete, a message will appear indicating that the installation was successful. Click [OK] (see Illustration 4) and remove the DVD from the DVD-ROM drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify if the Online Help opens up. To verify this, click the book icon on the lower right corner of the LCD Monitor and view the documents.
