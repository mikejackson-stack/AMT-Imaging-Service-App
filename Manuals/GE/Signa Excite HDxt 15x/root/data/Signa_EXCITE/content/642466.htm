2  Overview

It is possible to have a coolant leak into the vacuum space on TRM (twin resonant module) systems. If this should occur, this document describes the steps to check/follow to return the equipment back to a usable condition. Note, this document links to multiple procedures to provide the latest procedure for each step. Depending on the extent of the system leak, not all procedures may be necessary, but all are included for completeness. Each linked procedure will contain the required tools and test equipment, consumables, and a list of replacement parts. Procedures Referenced:

  • Lockout / Tagout for the Gradient Amplifier / PDU.

  • Twin RF Tube Removal, see Twin RF Body Coil Replacement.

  • RF Shield Removal Procedure (includes removal and installation).

  • TRM Removal Procedure

  • Shimming (Shim Tray removal and installation procedure, see LCC300 Magnet Service Manual: - Chapter 3, section 8. This only applies to 3T systems.

  • Installing the TRM Procedure.

  • Twin RF Body Coil Installation Procedure, See Twin RF Body Coil Replacement.

4  Procedure

  1. Follow the Lockout / Tagout procedure for shutting down the system. Refer to the Lockout / Tagout for the Gradient Amplifier / PDU procedure.

NOTICE
Note the highest fluid level. This may aid in defining what components could have damage. Example, If the level did not reach the body coil, the body coil and shield may be OK.
 
 

  1. Remove the RF Body Coil. Refer to Twin RF Body Coil Replacement.

  1. Inspect the RF Body Coil for arcing or other corrosive damage. The liquid on the RF body coil can cause high voltage on the DD lines, or End Ring caps which could cause arcing. If arcing or corrosive damage is found, replace the RF Body coil. Otherwise wipe off the RF Body Coil using isopropyl alcohol and a clean, lint-free rag. Allow to dry thoroughly.

  1. Remove the RF shield. Refer to RF Shield Replacement Procedure. The RF shield probably will require replacement since the liquid will penetrate between the layers of the RF shield leaving contamination that you cannot clean.

  1. Clean the inside of the Gradient Coil using a clean, lint-free rag and, if available, a small amount of isopropyl alcohol. No loose solder material, debris, or other contaminants should be left inside the Gradient Coil. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Cleaning Bore

  1. Remove the TRM. Refer to the TRM Removal Procedure.

  1. Inspect the TRM for damage. If damage is found, replace the TRM. Otherwise wipe off the TRM using isopropyl alcohol and a clean, lint-free rag.

  1. Perform a resistance check/meggar check between each gradient axis to make sure there isn’t a short internally. If a short is found, replace the TRM.

  1. For 3T system, remove the shim trays that may have been affected by the coolant leak. Remove one shim tray at a time. Refer to the LCC300 Magnet Service Manual , Chapter 3, section 8. For 1.5T systems, remove the tape and dry the shim stack that may have been affected by the coolant leak.

  1. For 1.5T system, replace the tape on the dried shim stack.

  1. For 3T system, thoroughly clean and dry off the shim tray using isopropyl alcohol and a clean, lint-free rag. Any shims showing signs of corrosion should be replaced.

  1. For 3T system, thoroughly clean and dry the shim tray rails.

  1. For 3T system, re-insert the shim tray. Refer to the LCC300 Magnet Service Manual , Chapter 3, section 8.

  1. Inspect the inner magnet diameter for any additional clean-up. If rusty, the magnet will probably need to be ramped down to clean the rust off.

  1. Clean the enclosures as necessary. Use isopropyl alcohol or a mild soap and water solution to clean the coolant residue.

  1. Install the TRM. Refer to Installing the TRM Procedure.

  1. If needed, install the RF shield. Refer to RF Shield Replacement Procedure.

  1. If the RF Body coil is completely dry, Re-Install the RF Body coil. Refer to Twin RF Body Coil Replacement Procedure.

5  Finalization

  1. Run TT/R Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Run SPT Quick Head Check.

  1. Run Echo Planar Test (EPT), B0 image and EPIWP must pass

  1. Dispose of excess gradient coolant drained earlier from the gradient coil as per local ordinances and regulations.

  1. Install the cover on the front of the cabinet containing the PDU.
