Isolation Box Troubleshooting and Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Fatal Electrical Hazard!
Dangerous voltages are present in the rear pedestal during normal operation.
Perform lock out tag out as described in the first step of this document.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and SSM/Driver Module are locked out and tagged out at the PDU per the LOTO procedure.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Troubleshooting Isolation Box Assemblies

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire Systems Cabinet, boot the Applications software down to the root level. The VRE System, which resides in the Systems Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the Applications software is active.
 
 

  1. To boot the Applications software down to the root level, right mouse click and select [Service Tools] and [System Restart]. The Systems Cabinet can be powered down when the GUI login displays.

  1. Perform LOTO on the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and SSM/Driver Module:

    1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

    1. Flip the breaker switches for the System Cabinet, RF Amplifier and SSM to the OFF position.

    1. Close the cover over the breaker switches, and install LOTO as instructed in Lock Out / Tag Out RF Systems Cabinet and RF Amplifier.

    1. Verify the power is off by checking the fans and the LEDs on the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and the SSM/Driver Module.

  1. Remove the side covers on the rear pedestal.

  1. Disconnect the I or Q cable from the suspected Isolation Box Assembly. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a non-ferrous screwdriver, remove the suspected Isolation Box Assembly. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. If there is a 90-degree connector attached to one of the outputs, be sure to remove it from the failed Isolation Box Assembly prior to continuing with the troubleshooting. (This 90-degree connector will need to be saved and used on the new Isolation Box Assembly.)

  1. Set the DMM to measure resistance:

    1. Measure the Isolation Box Assembly connector center pins from one end to the other and to the shell of the connectors. (See Illustration 5.)

    1. Check that values are as expected. If not, replace the Isolation Box Assembly.

      (Step Details)

3.2   Replacing Isolation Box Assembly

  1. Reinstall the 90-degree connector (removed previously from the failed Isolation Box Assembly) onto the new assembly.

  1. Remove the existing Isolation Box mounting brackets from the failed Isolation Box Assembly, and reinstall on the new assembly.

  1. Screw the new Isolation Box Assembly to the Body Hybrid.

  1. Connect the Hybrid Splitter to the Isolation Box. Connect the I or Q cable to the other side of the Isolation Box.

  1. Install the side covers on the rear pedestal.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO:

    1. Notify affected personnel that equipment will be re-energized, and LOTO devices are being removed.

    1. Remove LOTO equipment from PDU as described in Lock Out / Tag Out RF Systems Cabinet and RF Amplifier, and flip the breaker switches for the Systems Cabinet, RF Amplifier and SSM to the ON position.

    1. Reset the Emergency Off switch.

    1. Reset the TPS. (It should reset in about 1 minute and 20 seconds).

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure system operation.
