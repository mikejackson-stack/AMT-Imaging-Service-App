2.1  Configuring the Frequency Rate for an LCD Monitor (Frame Rate and Swap Rate)

To permanently set the frequency rate to 60Hz and Resolution to 1280 X1024:

  1. Open a C-shell

  1. Switch to root owner (Type: su<Enter> password: operator<Enter>).

  1. Type: chkconfig fixed72hz off

  1. Type: toolchest&

  1. Click on [Find ]-> [Control Panels]

  1. Click on [Display] to open this icon.

  1. Select 1280x1024_60 selection

  1. Click on [load]

  1. Click on [OK] in the following pop-up.

    Illustration 1:  DISPLAY POP-UP 1

  1. Click on [OK] in the following pop-up.

    Illustration 2:  DISPLAY POP-UP 2

  1. Click on [OK] in the following pop-up.

    Illustration 3:  DISPLAY POP-UP 3

  1. Reboot the system.

This section should NOT be skipped. It is extremely important to image quality, that you verify that the Frequency Rate, (Frame Rate and Swap Rate) is set to 60 HZ for ALL LCD Monitors.

  1. Switch to root owner. (Type: su<Enter> password: operator<Enter>)

  1. Click on [Find] -> [Control Panels]. (Make selections as shown in Illustration 5 and Illustration 6).

2.3  Configuring the Frequency Rate for a CRT Monitor (Frame Rate and Swap Rate)

This section is for CRT’s ONLY. The Frequency Rate, (Frame Rate and Swap Rate) is set to 72Hz HZ for ALL CRT Monitors. THE FREQUENCY RATE (FRAME RATE AND SWAP RATE) MUST BE SET TO 72HZ FOR THE CRT MONITORS. THEY ARE FIXED SYNC MONITORS AND WILL NOT TOLERATE ANY OTHER SETTING. FAILURE TO SET THEM TO 72HZ WILL RESULT IN PERMENENT VERTICAL ROLL WITHOUT ANY WAY TO RECOVER, EXCEPT THE RELOAD OF SOFTWARE.

  1. Open a [C-shell]

  1. Switch to root owner. (Type: su<Enter> password: operator<Enter>

  1. Type: toolchest& (A menu like that shown in Illustration 7 should appear in the upper left corner of the display within 20 seconds).

  1. Click on Find -> Control Panels . (Make selections as shown in Illustration 8 and Illustration 9).
