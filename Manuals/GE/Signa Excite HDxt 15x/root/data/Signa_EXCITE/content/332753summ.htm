Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Inadvertently pressing the MRU 'RUNDOWN' button on any MRU connected to the magnet will quench the magnet, resulting in the rapid expulsion of helium through the Vertical Penetration.
Make sure no 'Rundown' button is pressed while performing MRU functional checks.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with MRU wiring while power is supplied can cause electrical shock.
Disconnect input power to MRU being checked and follow lockout/tagout procedures while performing yearly MRU check. See OSHA Lockout-Tagout.

1  Procedure

1.1   Weekly MRU Checks

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench if the “RUNDOWN” button on any MRU installed is activated < 3 minutes after either MRU has received AC power.
Do not activate the “RUNDOWN” button during the Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Checks procedure.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench if 'P2' is connected to 'J2' < 3 minutes after any MRU being installed has received AC power
Do not connect 'P2' to 'J2' within 3 minutes of powering on either MRU.

  1. Verify that the green “Charger Power" LED is lit. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Depress the “Test Battery" Button. The green “Battery Test" LED should light.

  1. Place the “Test Heater" Switch in its “A" position. The green “Heater Test" LED should light.

  1. Place the “Test Heater" Switch in its “B" position. The green “Heater Test" LED should light.

  1. If the “Heater Test" LED does not light, depress the “Test Heater LED" Button to verify the LED is functioning.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Yearly MRU Check

  1. Perform weekly checks in conformance with Weekly MRU Checks, Weekly MRU Checks.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with MRU wiring while power is supplied can cause electrical shock.
Disconnect input power to MRU being checked and Follow lockout/tagout procedures while performing yearly MRU check.

  1. Check each MRU installed:

    1. Disconnect and lockout/tagout input power to the MRU being checked. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

    1. Disconnect the 4-pin Lemo Connector (P2) end of the cable to the magnet from connector J3 behind the MRU cover.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove lockout/tagout and connect input power to the MRU being checked.

    1. Perform yearly MRU checks in conformance with the vendor manual

    1. Disconnect and lockout/tagout input power to the MRU being checked. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

    1. Reconnect the 4-pin Lemo Connector (P2) end of the cable to the magnet to connector J3 behind the MRU cover.

    1. Remove lockout/tagout and connect input power to the MRU being checked.
