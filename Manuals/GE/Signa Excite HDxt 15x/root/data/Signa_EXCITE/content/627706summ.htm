SRFD2 Amplifier Module Replacement - 1.5T Systems

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY!
THE SRFD2 MODULE WEIGHS 200 LBS (~91 KG).
THE UNIVERSAL HOIST KIT IS REQUIRED TO REMOVE/REPLACE THE MODULE FROM THE CABINET.

WARNING
Possible Personal injury!
Heavy object - The Hoist Kit weighs 73 lbs (33 kg).
Assistance to lift this product [mechanical (Dollies) or Additional personnel] is required.

1  Procedure

1.1   SRFD2 Amplifier Module Removal

DANGER
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
AVOID SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH BY ELECTROCUTION.
REMOVE POWER FROM THE SRFD2 Module BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE it. VERIFY THAT THE RFI/AMP AND SSM BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU ARE OFF for systems with a Separate SRFD2 Cabinet. If working on a RF System cabinet, Verify that the RF/Systems Cabinet and the RFI AMP breakers on the front of the PDU are in the OFF position. Lock out AND TAG OUT THE PLEXIGLASS COVER ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU.

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire Systems Cabinet, boot the Applications software down to root level. The VRE System, which resides in the Systems Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the Applications software is active.
 
 

  1. Perform LOTO for the appropriate cabinet, described in:

    1. SRFD2 Cabinet Power Lock Out / Tag Out

    1. Lock Out / Tag Out RF Systems Cabinet and RF Amplifier

DANGER
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
CABINET TIP HAZARD IF THE RF AMPLIFIER IS EXTENDED WITHOUT installed ANTI-TIP LEGS.
CONFIRM THAT THE ALUMINUM ANTI-TIP LEGS STORED IN THE REAR OF THE CABINET ARE INSTALLED ON EITHER SIDE OF THE BASE OF THE CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE AN Amplifier.

  1. Using the screws included with the anti-tip legs, secure the aluminum anti-tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the SRFD2 or RFS Cabinet. Screw down the leveling pads at the front of the anti-tip legs so that they touch the floor. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the wires from L1, L2 and L3 of the Load AC connectors and the grounding wire at the rear of the AC Panel. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J14 and J18 connections at the rear of the SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the eight screws securing the Amplifier to the cabinet. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
Possible Personal injury!
Heavy object - The Hoist Kit weighs 73 lbs (33 kg).
Assistance to lift this product [mechanical (Dollies) or Additional personnel] is required.

  1. Locate the three large steel rail sections (shown in Illustration 6), and place them on a flat surface for assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, front) and fasten them together with 4.75 in. x 1/2 in. caps crews and hex nuts.

  1. Use the crescent wrenches to tighten the cap screws.

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the Top Rail Assembly. (See Illustration 7.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the front and rear covers on the SRFD2 Cabinet.

  1. Remove the front cover and, if present, the top spacer panel (shown in Illustration 8) on the RFS Cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the Steel Rail and Elevating Bracket Assembly at the top of the SRFD2 or RFS Cabinet, and center the assembly on the top of the RF Amplifier. (See Illustration 9.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. (See Illustration 10.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
The hoist assembly could fall!
The locking pin keeps the hoist in the rail. Failure to use the locking pin as designed may cause the hoist and anything being lifted by it to fall to the floor endangering person and property.
Do not lift anything with the hoist without the hoist locking pin in place.

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail of the Steel Rail Assembly, and insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. (See Illustration 11. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. (See Illustration 12.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top from the FRU case by releasing the butterfly clamp on each side, and place the top in front of the SRFD2 or RFS Cabinet for placement of the failed module, which becomes the bottom of the case for returning the failed module. (See Illustration 13.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the Lift Brackets (P/N 46-317508P1) to the side of the module.

    1. Attach the Hoist Assembly to the lift brackets. (See Illustration 14.)

    1. Reuse the screws removed from the front of the cabinet to secure the panels.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the four screws from each sliding rail as follows:

    1. Pull the module out of the cabinet to the point that three screw heads on the side of the module are in view and the hole in the rail provides access to the screw. (See Illustration 15.) Remove the screws from all rails.

      (Step Details)

    1. Pull module out so the rails are fully extended, and remove the three remaining screws from each rail. (See Illustration 16.)

      (Step Details)

  1. Push the sliding rails all the way into the cabinet, turn the module so the fans are properly placed in the FRU case, and lower the module.

  1. Remove the Hoist Assembly from the module.

1.2   SRFD2 Amplifier Installation

  1. Place the new module on the FRU case base in front of the cabinet by sliding the base between the anti-tip legs; remove the side walls by releasing the butterfly clamp. (See Illustration 17.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the shipping strap.

  1. Attach the Hoist Assembly to the lift brackets.

  1. Attach the lift brackets to the SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 18.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the hoist, raise the module to align with the sliding rails.

  1. Once the module is at the correct height, slide the rails out completely and replace the screws in the accessible holes. (See Illustration 19.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Host Assembly from the module, and return it to the Hoist Kit.

  1. Push the module in to gain access to the fourth screw hole on each sliding rail, and secure each rail by replacing each screw. (See Illustration 20.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the module completely into the cabinet, and replace the eight screws in the front of the amplifier to secure it to the cabinet. (See Illustration 21.)

    (Step Details)

WARNING
SHOCK HAZARD!
POSSIBLE ELECTRIC SHOCK FROM AN EXPOSED POWER CABLE.
To prevent electrical shock, DO NOT FEED THE SRFD2 PIGTAIL/POWER CABLE BEHIND THE SLIDING RAILS. THIS MAY CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE CABLE, POTENTIALLY EXPOSING it. SEE the next step FOR CORRECT CABLE DRESSING.

  1. Dress the pigtail cable to provide a service loop (40.15 in./1,020 mm) for the SRFD2 Module. Attach the cable to the upper horizontal side strut in cabinet using tie-wraps. (See Illustration 22.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J14 and J18 at the rear of the SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 23.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the wires from L1, L2 and L3 of the Load AC connectors and the grounding wire at the rear of the AC Panel. (See Illustration 24.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the plastic cover on the AC Panel by securing the four screws. (See Illustration 25.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the four screws to secure the AC Panel to the rear of the cabinet, and reconnect J1 if necessary. (See Illustration 26.)

    (Step Details)

  1. At the rear of the SRFD2 Module, place the CB1 Breaker in the ON position, lever up. (SeeIllustration 27.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Hoist Assembly from the cabinet.

  1. Close the SRFD2 or RFS rear cabinet door.

  1. Reattach the top spacer panel on the RFS Cabinet.

  1. Remove LOTO from the Circuit Breaker cover on the front of the PDU cabinet.

    1. For the SRFD2 Cabinet, place the RFI/Amplifier and SSM Breakers in the ON position.

    1. For the RFS Cabinet, place the RFS and RFI/Amplifier Breakers in the ON position.

  1. Make sure the LEDs illuminate on the front of the SRFD2 Module and SSM/Driver Module, and the fans at the rear of the SRFD2 Module are rotating.

  1. If the SRFD2 Module was replaced, perform the calibration procedure described in 1.5T SRFD2 Max Power RF Out Setup and Calibration.

1.3   Returning Failed Module

Prepare to return the failed module as follows:

  1. Secure the module with the shipping strap, wrap and tuck the pigtail power cord inside the shipping strap on the side of the module. (See Illustration 28.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the side walls on the base and secure with the butterfly locking mechanism. The black stripe on the outside of the box should align with the one on the base of the module. (See Illustration 29.

    (Step Details)

  1. Return the 16 in. Steel Rail to the case. (See Illustration 30.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the top on the case, keeping the black stripes aligned and secure. (See Illustration 29.)

  1. Return the module for root cause analysis by the vendor.
