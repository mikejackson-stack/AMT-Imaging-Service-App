NOTE:The magnet monitor is not accurate; there can be an error margin of 15%. Use the RUO Meter (2171219) for accurate readings. Actual values should be between 4.1 and 4.5 K. The delta “T” between coldhead and recondenser RUO should be less than 0.20 K.

If the shield, coldhead or recondenser temperatures are out of specification, a thermal issue may result. Refer to the "High Pressure and/or Helium Loss Troubleshooting" section in Direction 2192624 to resolve any potential issues.
