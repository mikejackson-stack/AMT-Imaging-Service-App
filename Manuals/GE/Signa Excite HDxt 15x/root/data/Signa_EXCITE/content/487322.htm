2  Preliminary Requirements

2.1  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

3  Procedure

3.1  Removing Skirt Covers

  1. Skirt covers are held in place primarily using poppers. To remove a skirt cover, simply slide it toward the front (for a back cover, slide it to the rear) of the magnet as shown below.

    Illustration 1:  Removing Skirt Covers

3.2  Installing Skirt Covers

  1. Align fastener on the skirt cover with the notch on the arc cover (shown below), and slide cover toward the center of the magnet.

    Illustration 2:  Aligning Skirt Cover Fastener (Hook) with Slot on Arc Cover

  1. Align tabs on arc cover so they interlock with tabs on the adjoining skirt cover as shown.

    Illustration 3:  Aligning Skirt Tabs

  1. Snap poppers (shown below) into place on the front end of the arc cover.

    Illustration 4:  Popper Locations
