1  Procedure

1.1   Adjusting Patient Table for Floor Levelness

  1. Dock the Patient Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lock the rear caster brakes.

  1. Raise the patient table to a fully raised position, and check levelness from front to back along the top right or left edge of the bridge, and from left to right across the front of the bridge using a nonferrous level.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Checking Caster Height Adjustment

During installation, this procedure describes how to check the caster height (factory default) settings. If the patient table requires adjustment, proceed to Leveling Patient Table.

  1. To help keep track of measurement data, temporarily label each caster as 1, 2, 3 and 4. Based on the Caster Wheel Labels in Illustration 1, label the steering caster as 1 (front left while facing the magnet), and proceed labeling in a clockwise direction.

  1. Remove the lower side covers and front and rear caster assembly covers. For more information on removing the side covers, see Patient Table Cover Removal.

  1. At each of the four caster wheel assemblies, measure the height from the floor to the bottom edge of the aluminum support casting a shown below. Record the values for each caster in the table that follows.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Leveling Patient Table

While all four casters look identical from an exterior perspective, there is one notable difference regarding design when observing each caster assembly inside the caster covers. The Patient Table front left caster is known as the Steer Lock Wheel, whose caster assembly design has a triangle-shaped bracket that differs from the other three casters.

  1. Undock the Patient Table and position it 1 to 2 inches in front of the dock to perform work as close to the docked position as possible.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage the rear caster brake lock pedal.

  1. At the desired caster that requires adjustment, remove the caster linkage pin using a 10 mm wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the collar bolt using an 11 mm wrench. (The illustration below depicts a floor-level view looking at the right underside of the caster assembly; the same view for all four casters.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the collar down the shaft so it rests on top of the caster shaft housing.

  1. Looking from a top-down perspective, move the Wheel Lock Lever to the Full Lock position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the caster clockwise (raises the table) or counterclockwise (lowers the table) to make adjustments.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Per design specification, do NOT exceed a raising or lowering adjustment greater than 1/4 in. (6 mm). Do not exceed more than 6 revolutions in either direction.
 
 

  1. Remeasure the caster height and record the value in Table 1.

  1. Perform Step 3 through Step 6 in reverse order to reassemble the caster bolt and linkage pin.

  1. Once all casters are adjusted, remeasure the table levelness, and adjust the casters as necessary by repeating this procedure.

  1. Once table is level, reattach the caster assembly covers, lower the side covers, and redock the Patient Table.
