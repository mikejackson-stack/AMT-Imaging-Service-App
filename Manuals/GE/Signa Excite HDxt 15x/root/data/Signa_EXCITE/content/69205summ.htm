  1. Laser Light Alignment for TwinSpeed Enclosures:

    1. Remove the Lens cover for the laser lights.

    1. Turn the laser lights on.

    1. Verify that the Coronal lights align with the light on the opposite side. If adjustment is needed, loosen the flat head screw, and move the “joystick” until the laser light is properly aligned. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify that the Axial and Sagittal laser lights are properly aligned using the DQA Phantom in the head coil as the reference. If adjustments are needed, remove the upper display by carefully prying the cover off. See Illustration 3. The joysticks for both the Sagital and Axial can be accessed.
