1  Procedure

1.1   MGD Circuit Board Removal/Replacement

1.1.1  Power Down CAM Chassis

Prior to powering down the entire CAM Chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE power control. The Volume Recon Engine (VRE) System, which resides in the CAM Chassis, can become corrupted if it loses power when the applications software is active.

  1. At the host computer, open the Service browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.

  1. Next, select [Power Off VRE].

  1. Perform LOTO for Recon.

1.1.2  Board Positions and Identification

Refer to Illustration 3, Illustration 4 and their accompanying tables for circuit board parts identification.

  1. Refer to the illustration and table below for the MGD Chassis front board slot assignments.

    (Step Details)

  1. Refer to the illustration and table below for the MGD Chassis Rear Board Slot Assignments.

    (Step Details)

1.1.3  Circuit Board Removal Procedure

  1. Attach an Electro Static Discharge (ESD) grounding wrist strap.

  1. Remove the cables (PCI Card, 10/100 BaseT, Com1, etc.) from the face of the board.

  1. Loosen the faceplate holding screws. (These screws are captive to the faceplate, and do not need to be completely removed.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using your thumb, push in the grey release tabs (shown in Illustration 6), and gently pull the extractor tabs located on the top and bottom of the circuit board. (See Illustration 7 for the proper direction.) The lever action of the ejector disengages the circuit board connections from the chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Being careful not to bend or bind the card in any way, slide the circuit board all the way out of the card cage.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the card in an appropriate shipping package, or discard if applicable.

1.1.4  Circuit Board Replacement

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement board, and gently slide the circuit board along the slot guides into the chassis.

DANGER
Possible equipment damage!
Exercise extreme caution when removing and replacing circuit boards.
All boards that have female connectors (shown in Illustration 8) must be carefully disengaged, and re-engaged to the male pins located on the backplane chassis. If any of the pins are damaged, the entire chassis needs to be replaced.

  1. Gently engage the circuit board connectors with the backplane.

    1. If the circuit board is not properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.

    1. Once the board ejectors contact the CAM chassis faceplate, gently push the ejectors into the center of the board. This causes the ejectors to insert the board into the chassis. (Do not attempt to use anything other than the ejectors to insert or remove the board from the chassis.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten, but do not overtighten, the faceplate holding screws to secure the circuit board to the card rack.

  1. Where applicable, reattach the cables to the face of the board.

1.1.5  Restoring System for Clinical Applications

  1. Restore power to the CAM Chassis.

  1. Refer to Step  for checkout and proper operation.

1.3   Power Supply Module Removal/Replacement

1.3.1  Power Down CAM Chassis

Prior to powering down the entire CAM Chassis, power down the ICN's from the VRE Power Control. The VRE System, which resides in the PGR Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the applications software is active.

NOTE:Beginning August 2009, CAM Chassis went to a one Power Supply configuration in Slots 14/15. Functionality is maintained, and the empty slot is covered with a front panel airflow blocker. If a new CAM Chassis FRU is ordered, one power supply (Slots 14/15) and an additional front panel and airflow blocker (Slots 12/13) will be installed in the new CAM unit.

  1. At the host computer, open the service browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.

  1. Next, select [Power Off VRE].

  1. Perform LOTO for Recon.

1.3.2  Power Supply Module Removal

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.

  1. Loosen the two faceplate holding screws, one each located at the top and bottom of the faceplate as shown in Illustration 10. (These screws are captive to the faceplate, and do not need to be completely removed.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Using your thumb, push in and gently pull the extractor tab located on the circuit board. (See Illustration 7 for the proper direction.) The lever action of the ejector disengages the supply module connections from the chassis.

  1. Slide the power supply module all the way out of the card cage.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the module in an appropriate shipping package.

1.3.3  Power Supply Module Replacement

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement module, and gently slide the module into the chassis.

  1. Use the ejectors to insert the module into the chassis.

  1. Tighten, but do not overtighten, the faceplate holding screws to secure the power supply module to the card rack.

1.3.4  Restoring System for Clinical Applications

  1. Restore power to the CAM Chassis.

  1. Refer to Step  for checkout and proper operation.

1.4   Fan Blower Removal/Replacement

Prior to powering down the entire CAM Chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE Power Control. The VRE System, which resides in the PGR Cabinet, can become corrupted if it loses power when the applications software is active.

  1. At the host computer, open the service browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.

  1. Next, select [Power Off VRE].

  1. Perform LOTO for Recon.

  1. On the front of the CAM Chassis are two fan blower assemblies (each is held in place with two thumb screws). Loosen the two thumb screws that secure the fan assembly that needs to be replaced. (See Illustration 11.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull out the defective fan(s).

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the new fan (P/N 2294300-7) into same slot from which the defective fan was removed, and push it back into the slot until snug.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the two thumb screws to secure the fan assembly. (See Illustration 11.)

  1. Restore power to the CAM Chassis.

  1. Refer to Step  for checkout and proper operation.

1.5   CAM Chassis Removal/Replacement

1.5.1  Power Down CAM Chassis

Prior to powering down the entire CAM Chassis, power down the ICNs from the VRE Power Control.

  1. At the host computer, open the service browser and select Utilities > Tool Box > VRE > Power Control.

  1. Next, select [Power Off VRE].

  1. Perform LOTO for Recon .

1.5.2  CAM Chassis Removal

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.

  1. Remove all the circuit boards and store them in a static-safe location. (Refer to Circuit Board Removal Procedure and Circuit Board Removal for removal instructions.)

  1. Remove the Power Supply Modules. (Refer to Power Supply Module Removal for removal instructions.)

  1. Remove the six screws that hold the CAM Chassis to the cabinet frame. (See the illustration, below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables.

  1. Slide the CAM Chassis out of the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Remove the top tray on CAM Chassis by loosening the four screws shown in Illustration 13.

    Illustration 13:  Top Tray Screws and Term Server

  1. Disengage the slide rails, and safely place the CAM Chassis on a flat surface.

  1. Remove the slide rails and the four screws to remove the bottom tray.

  1. Install the slide rails and bottom tray on the new chassis.

    (Step Details)

1.5.3  CAM Chassis Installation

  1. Attach an ESD grounding wrist strap.

  1. Slide the CAM Chassis into the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Install the top tray with the Term Server, and reconnect the cables.

  1. Replace the six screws that hold the CAM Chassis to the cabinet frame.

  1. Install all boards. (Refer to Circuit Board Replacement and Circuit Board Replacement for installation instructions.)

  1. Replace the Power Supply Modules. (Refer to Power Supply Module Replacement for installation instructions.)

  1. Reattach all cables.

1.5.4  Restoring System for Clinical Applications

  1. Restore power to the CAM Chassis.

  1. Refer to Step  for checkout and proper operation.

2  Finalization

  1. If an NB RF Detector Board, a NB Amp Interface Board, or a UPM processor board was replaced, run the Universsal Power Monitor - Head Setup and Calibration and UPM - Body Setup and Calibration. If a BB RF Detector Board or a BB Amp Interface Board was replaced, run the UPM - MNS Setup and Calibration.

  1. Run Quick Diagnostics (15 minutes) to verify operations.

  1. Run Goodbye scan. (Refer to Check Scan for details.)
