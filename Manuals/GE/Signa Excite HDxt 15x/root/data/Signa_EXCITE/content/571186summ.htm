1.5T 8-CH CTL Cable Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Lay the CTL coil on its side and remove the 19 screws holding the base cover on the unit as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the RF cables and the DC wires from the Multiplexer Board, as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove two screws on the clamp of Strain Relief as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cable from the base of the coil.

  1. Insert the new cable to the coil.

  1. Connect the DC wires to the multiplexer board.

  1. Connect the corresponding RF cables to the multiplexer board (note: the cables are marked for proper placement).

  1. Place the clamp and tighten the two screws.

  1. Replace the bottom cover and the 19 retaining screws.
