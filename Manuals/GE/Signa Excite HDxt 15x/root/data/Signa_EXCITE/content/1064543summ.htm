LOTO for Reconstruction Equipment

1  Safety

DANGER
electrocution hazard!
High voltage present.
user proper lock out/tag out procedures before service and/or maintenance.

NOTICE
Follow the ICN On/Off Procedure to remove power to the ICN Chassis. Failure to properly power down ICNs may result in premature ICN failure.
 
 

NOTICE
Do not turn power off on the ICNs from the power button on the front panel. The ICNs have software running on disk drives that may become corrupted is not shut down properly.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Affected personnel working in that area must be notified that this LOTO is being performed and/or removed.

3  Procedure

3.1   Pre-DV Apps PGR

The procedures in this section apply ONLY to Pre-DV Apps PGRs. For DV Apps PGRs, refer to DV Apps PGR. Pre-DV Apps PGR is equipped with Power Distribution Module (PDM) in upper right of cabinet.

3.1.1  Applying LOTO

  1. Use DMM to check either Service outlet on PGR PDM for presence of 115 VAC. Circuit Breaker (CB) 6 must be in ON (up) position for power to be present at Service outlet. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 1:  PGR Cabinet Power Distribution Module

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform orderly shutdown of ICNs from VRE Power Control. See Section 4.2 in the ICN On/Off Procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the PDM -RECON circuit breaker to the OFF (down) position. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 2:  PDM-RECON Circuit Breaker on PDU

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breakers, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment. (Shown below.)

    Illustration 3:  PDU LOTO Device

  1. Confirm that the power is OFF. Verify Zero Energy by a) Checking the service outlet on the PGR PDM for 115 VAC power with the DMM or b) Manually trying to reenergize the system. If the power is still on, confirm that the PDM_RECON CB on the PDU is in the OFF position.

3.1.2  Removing the LOTO

  1. Prepare for reenergizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that the LOTO is being removed.

  1. Remove the personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the PDM-RECON circuit breaker switch to the ON position.

3.2   DV Apps PGR

The procedures in this section apply ONLY to DV Apps PGRs. For Pre-DV Apps PGRs, refer to Pre-DV Apps PGR. DV Apps PGR does not contain PDM. Terminal strip installed in place of PDM.

3.2.1  Applying LOTO

  1. Use DMM to check unused outlet on terminal strip for presence of 115 VAC.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform orderly shutdown of ICNs from VRE Power Control. See Section 4.2 in the ICN On/Off Procedure.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the RECON circuit breaker to the OFF (down) position.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breakers, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment.

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm that the power is OFF. Check the service power strip for 115 VAC power with the DMM. If the power is still ON, confirm that the RECON CB on the PDU is in the OFF position.

3.2.2  Removing the LOTO

  1. Prepare for reenergizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that the LOTO is being removed.

  1. Remove the personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, switch the RECON circuit breaker switch to the ON position.

4  Finalization

  1. In the Common Service Desktop, navigate to Utilities → Toolbox → VRE → Power Control.

  1. Click [POWER ON ICN] for the respective ICN. The ICN will automatically be powered-on.
