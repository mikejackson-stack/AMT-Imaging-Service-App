Replacement of SRI (Scan Room Interface)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal of SRI

  1. The SRI can be located under either the right or left side of the magnet enclosure. Identify which side the SRI is on by locating the patient leads, the SRI will be on that side.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the lower enclosure trim covers to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the SRI. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four (4) screws holding SRI to tray. See Illustration 4.

  1. Lift SRI off tray and remove.

    (Step Details)

1.2   SRI Replacement for Salt Box Magnet Enclosure

  1. Re-position the table away from the magnet. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. The SRI is located on the Right side (when facing magnet from front) on the upper slide rail, see Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect all cables to the SRI.

1.3   Installation of New SRI

  1. Place new SRI on tray.

  1. Install four (4) screws.

  1. Connect the cables.

  1. Slide tray under magnet and secure latches.

  1. Replace enclosure trim panels.

  1. Remove tag-out tags and locksw.

  1. Restore power.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform good by scan. Do not use “geservice” as patient ID, as this will verify proper operation of Coil-ID.
