SCSI Card Replacement for HP8400

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages Exist Inside The Global operator console (Goc) when Power Is Applied.
Before Servicing Any Components Inside The Goc, Perform lock out/tag out.

NOTICE
To prevent damage to equipment components, observe the following Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electro-static charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment you are working on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap, and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Shut down system applications if still running, and LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace power to the HP8400 before continuing.

  1. Attach a grounding wrist strap.

  1. On the GOC, remove the four screws in the rear securing the left side panel and the three screws securing the right side panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the left and right side panels on the GOC by sliding them back.

  1. Loosen, but do not remove, the four screws that secure the back panel, and remove the back panel.

  1. Unlatch the PC side panel using the latch shown below. (It may be necessary to remove the screw holding the side panel from the back of the PC.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the two black tabs on the top and bottom of the spring-loaded card retainer.

  1. While depressing both tabs, remove the retainer as shown.

    (Step Details)

  1. Depress the two green tabs on the inside of the PC to release the locking mechanism holding the boards in place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the SCSI Card (P/N 5342621) firmly into the fifth slot from the top.

  1. Restore the locking mechanism holding the boards in place by pulling back the two green tabs on the inside of the PC.

  1. Restore the spring-loaded card retainer.

  1. Connect the SCSI Tower Cable (P/N 5305021) to the left port of the SCSI card, and route the other end of the cable to the back of the SCSI tower.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Perform the applicable Loading Host System Software procedure to load software and applications onto the operating system.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
