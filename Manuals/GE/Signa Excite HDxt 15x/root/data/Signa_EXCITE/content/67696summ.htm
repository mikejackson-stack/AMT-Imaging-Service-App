Lockout and Tagout

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
AVOID SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH BY ELECTROCUTION.
REMOVE POWER FROM THE MR SYSTEM OR RF CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO SERVICE THE TWIN RF BODY COIL. PERFORM THE LOCKOUT AND TAGOUT PROCESS TO THE APPROPRIATE CIRCUIT BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU TO SAFELY REMOVE POWER FROM THE RF BODY COIL CIRCUITS.

1  Procedure

  1. Refer to MR SYSTEM LOCKOUT PROCEDURES.

  1. Locate the subsection referring to the PDU used in the MR system.

  1. Perform the Lockout and Tagout procedures described in that section.
