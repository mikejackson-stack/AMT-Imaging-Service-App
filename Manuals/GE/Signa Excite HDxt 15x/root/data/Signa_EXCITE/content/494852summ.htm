System Restoration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Verify the system is not scanning and the scan desktop icon is displaying the “Idle” message.

  1. Refer to Illustration 1. At the front of the Driver Module, return all switches to “Enable” position:

    (Step Details)

  1. (For SSM Only) Refer to Illustration 2. At the front of the SSM place the:

    (Step Details)

  1. (For SSM Only) Place DIP switches 4, 3, 2, and 1 of the Test Switches in the DOWN position or disconnect laptop computer from J46 on the rear of the SSM.

  1. Disable RF at RF I/O

  1. Ensure that the dummy load (all test equipment) is disconnected from the rear of the cabinet.

  1. Ensure that the head and body Heliax cables are reconnected to the rear of the cabinet.

  1. Put RF back to “ON” at RF I/O

2  Finalization

  1. Complete one head scan satisfactorily.

  1. Complete one body scan satisfactorily.

  1. Close the rear door and replace the front cover on the cabinet.

  1. (For SSM Only) Re-install the SSM switch cover if previously removed.

  1. Prepare and file power monitor functional check data sheet according to current company policy. See DECLARATION FORM PREPARATION.
