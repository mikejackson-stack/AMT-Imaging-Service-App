Twin RF Body Coil Replacement

The Twin RF body coil cartridge is a field-replaceable unit (FRU) that mounts inside the Twin Gradient Coil (also a FRU). If the RF coil fails, it must be removed from within the Twin Gradient Coil and replaced. See Illustration 1.

Illustration 1:  Rear View Of 1.5T Twin RF Coil Removed From Inside Gradient Coil

Since the replacement procedures for the RF and gradient body coils are closely related, references are made below to the epoxy-filled gradient coil replacement procedure where the instructions for removal of alike parts (bridge removal, for example) can be found.

NOTICE
Replacing A Failed RF Body Coil Requires 3 People To Complete These Procedures.
 
 

Steps to complete a replacement are:

  1. Lockout and Tagout.

  2. Disassembly and RF Coil Removal.

  3. Installing the Replacement Twin RF Coil.

  4. Restoration After Coil Installation.
