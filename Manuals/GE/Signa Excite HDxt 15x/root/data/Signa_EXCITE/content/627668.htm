2  Overview

Prior to performing maintenance work on the SRFD2 Cabinet or items in the SRFD2 Cabinet, the steps in this Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) procedure must be performed by a LOTO Authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all steps in this procedure will ensure a safe environment and avoid equipment damage when working on these parts.

3.2  Safety

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
AVOID SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH BY ELECTROCUTION.
REMOVE POWER FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REMOVE THE SRFD2 MODULE. VERIFY THAT THE RFI/AMP AND SSM BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU ARE OFF. Apply LOTO to THE PLEXI-GLASS COVER ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU.

4  Procedure

4.1  Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy: 5 minutes

4.2  Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconection(s)

Three (3) personal locks and tags per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

4.3  Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the Gradient Cabinet.

  1. Open the Circuit Breaker cover on the front of the PDU, and switch OFF the RFI/AMP and SSM breakers. (See Illustration 1.)

    Illustration 1:   Breaker Identification

  1. Close the plexi-glass door and apply LOTO. (See Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:   LOTO of Breakers

  1. Remove the front cover from the SRFD2 Cabinet.

  1. Verify all LEDs are OFF (not illuminated) on the front of the SSM and on the front of the SRFD2 Module. (See Illustration 3.)

    Illustration 3:   SRFD2 LED Location

  1. At the rear of the SRFD2 Module, place the CB1 Breaker in the OFF position (lever down). See Illustration 4 for the CB1 Breaker location.

    Illustration 4:   CB1 Breaker Location

  1. Disconnect J1 from the AC Panel from the rear of the cabinet, and remove the 4 (four) screws securing the panel to the cabinet. (See Illustration 5.)

    Illustration 5:  Disconnection Locations on AC Panel

  1. Remove the plastic cover from the AC Panel. (See Illustration 6.)

    Illustration 6:  Plastic Cover on AC Panel

  1. Using an AC voltmeter (DVM), measure the input power leads on the rear of the AC Panel TS1 from the rear (right side) of the SRFD2 Cabinet.

    Measure between L1 and L2 and verify the voltmeter measures 0 VAC. (See Illustration 7.)

    Illustration 7:  L1 & L2 Locations
