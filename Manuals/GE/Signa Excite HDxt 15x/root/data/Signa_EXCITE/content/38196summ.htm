Spike Noise Check

Possible Equipment Damage! Remove the Head Coil from the Patient Table before performing this procedure.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Hardware Preparation for Spike Noise Data Collection

  1. Remove the front panel from the Systems Cabinet.

  1. Disable the RF Output from the RRF Master Exciter by setting the RF Out toggle switch (located on the RRF front panel I/O board) to the Disable position.

  1. Set the oscilloscope to 1M ohm impedance.

  1. Connect the oscilloscope power plug to the service outlet of the system cabinet to prevent ground loops that may cause higher floor noise. .

    1. Channel 1 to J30 on the RRF UTNS Slot 12, using test cable 46-301549P5. (See Illustration 1.)

      (Step Details)

    1. Channel 2 to Receiver, IN VIEW. See Illustration 2.

      (Step Details)

    1. External Trigger to J7 Scope Trigger on the RRF Front Panel I/O board.

  1. Set up the oscilloscope as follows:

    1. Channel 1 - 5 mV/div, 1 mW input

    1. Channel 2 - 5 V/div, 1 mW input

    1. Time Base - 5 msec/div

    1. Trigger - auto, external

1.2   Spike Noise Scan Preparation

  1. At the operator workspace, select the Scan icon on the desktop control panel.

  1. If necessary, exit out of any previous exams by selecting [End Exam].

  1. Click on [New Pt] and enter the following:

    1. ID: geservice

    1. Name: spike noise

    1. Weight (lb.): 111

  1. Remove all phantoms from the patient table. This is an empty bore test.

  1. Place the head coil without a phantom on the cradle. Landmark on the center of the head coil. At the keypad on the front magnet enclosure, press <LANDMARK>, and <MOVE TO SCAN>.

  1. The following three steps are proprietary and only available for GE use, and to sites with a valid Advanced Service Package Limited License. The non-proprietary procedure is listed after these steps.

    1. Set Patient Protocols to Service.

    1. Select the scan protocol by clicking on Other, select the Spike Noise protocol and series 1 from the menu then click [Accept] to load the Spike Noise (Head Axial, A-P) protocol.

    1. Click on [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. Click on [Save Series] and then [Prepare to scan], then press [Move to Scan].

    (Step Details)

1.3   Sample Noise Data Collection and Analysis

  1. Click on [Manual Prescan] and set R1=11, R2=15, TG=0.

  1. Press the [PAUSE SCAN] button on the console keyboard. Press the <FAN ON> button on the magnet enclosure to turn off the patient comfort fan (the fan turns on automatically with scan software).

    (Step Details)

  1. Put the scope in continuous acquisition mode. The method for doing this will vary with scope type. Clear the oscilloscope display and ensure the trigger is in the auto position.

  1. Observe the scope display for about two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) is typically 45-50 mV p–p. Verify that there are no spikes >0.5 Nenv (where Nenv is the noise envelope peak to peak value) above the noise envelope. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the <FAN ON> button on the magnet enclosure to turn on the patient comfort fan. Clear the oscilloscope display and ensure that the trigger is in the Auto position. Observe the oscilloscope display for about two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) should remain as described in Step 4 above.

  1. Clear the oscilloscope display and change the trigger from Auto to Normal.

  1. Press the <FAN ON> button on the magnet enclosure to turn off the patient comfort fan.

  1. Press the <START SCAN> button on the console keyboard to resume prescan. Click on [Scan T/R].

  1. Observe the scope display after two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) should be approximately 50mV (1.5T) or 130mV (3.0T). Verify that there are no spikes >0.5 Nenv (where Nenv is the noise envelope peak-to-peak value) above the noise envelope in the region of interest (except for the beginning and end transition spike). See Illustration 1-4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Done].

  1. Click on [New Series]. Set up scan prescription for series #2 scan per notes and Table 1, then repeat steps Sample Noise Data Collection and Analysis .

  1. Repeat the previous step until all head prescans (series #1 through #4) have been completed

1.4   Image Data Collection/Analysis

  1. Disconnect the oscilloscope from J30 on the RRF UTNS and restore all cables to their normal configuration.

  1. Reset the RF Out switch on the RRF Front Panel I/O board to the Enable (left) position.

  1. Install the front cover panel on the Systems Cabinet.

  1. Setup using SPT Loader: Position the SPT body loader and TLT sphere on the table and landmark per Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Setup using Long Body Loader. Position the body TLT sphere in the center of the long body loader and landmark per Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [New Series] and Last Series from the procedure in Spike Noise Scan Preparation .

  1. At the front enclosure, press< LANDMARK>, then <MOVE TO SCAN>.

  1. Click on [Save Series], then [Prepare to Scan].

  1. Click on [Scan] (system auto prescans first).

  1. Display and examine each of the twenty images for tweed-like or stripe-like artifacts. See Illustration 7. If artifacts are present, use the procedures described in the EPI White Pixel Troubleshooting Guide to troubleshoot the system.

    (Step Details)
