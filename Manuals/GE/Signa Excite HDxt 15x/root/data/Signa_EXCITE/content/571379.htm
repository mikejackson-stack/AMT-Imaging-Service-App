Replacement of Hypertronics Connector on LPCA

(Click Here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

New forward production LPCA's contain a B and C connector on outside connector positions. The A connector is in the Center for this LPCA, and the cables for the A connector run to the Multi-coil bias assembly. The older LPCA style, used with upgrade systems, contains an A and B connector on the outside connector positions, while the Legacy connector is in the center. This document details the procedure to replace any of these Hypertronics (A,B, or C) connectors in the LPCA.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal of Cable Connector

  1. Run LPCA to the rear of the magnet and remove the cover to the LPCA.

  1. For sites with RFS cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled PHPS/SSM on the PDU. Or for sites with RF cabinet and Systems cabinet, Lock out and tag out circuit breaker labeled SSM.

  1. Remove the cabling for the connector to be replaced. See Illustration 1 for the A and B connectors on an upgrade LPCA. These will be the B and C connectors on a forward production LPCA. . Connector A cables on a forward production system run to the Multi-coil Preamp Assembly. See Illustration 2 for this connector’s cables.

    Illustration 2:  Cable Removal For Connector A Forward Production

  1. Remove the LED outputs from the connector. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  LED Removal

  1. Loosen the two Phillips screws that secure the connector to the LPCA. See Illustration 4. Remove the connector.

    Illustration 4:  Connector Removal

4.2  Installation of New Cable take-up

  1. Place replacement connector in position and tighten screws.

  1. Connect all cables from connector to LPCA, including LED cables.

  1. Re-install cover on LPCA.

  1. Remove Lock-out Tag-out and restore power.
