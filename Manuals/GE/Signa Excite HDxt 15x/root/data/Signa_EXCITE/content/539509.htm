2  Overview

Procedures for replacing the halogen light bulb used for the fiber optic bore lights.

3  Procedure

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU (see LOTO for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room).

  1. Lift and remove rear pedestal side covers. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Access to Rear Pedestal

  1. Remove left rear skirt cover (see Skirt Cover Removal for details).

  1. Turn latch holding light source assembly ¼ turn to the clockwise and pull assembly out of left rear magnet leg (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Fiber Optic Bore Light Source

  1. Disconnect halogen light's plug.

  1. Pull halogen light and heat sink assembly off plate. Halogen light and heat sink will need to be rocked back and forth to accomplish this (see Illustration 2.

  1. Disconnect heat sink from halogen light bulb.

  1. Slide heat sink onto new halogen light bulb.

  1. Press light/heat sink assembly in place on plate.

  1. Feed light assembly into hole in magnet foot and turn latch ¼ turn counter clockwise to close.

  1. Put left rear skirt cover and the rear pedestal side covers back in place.

  1. Remove tag-out tag and restore power by turning on the applicable breaker in the PDU.

No finalization steps.
