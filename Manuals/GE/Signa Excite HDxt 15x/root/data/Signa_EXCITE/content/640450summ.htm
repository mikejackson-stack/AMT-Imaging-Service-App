  1. If there is a drift in time, time adjustment will be needed. The procedure is as follows:

    1. Select [Service Desktop Manager]

    1. Click [Guided Install] > [Start] > Password = “operator”

    1. The Guided Install window will pop up.

    1. Click [SET TIME/DATE].

    1. Adjust the Time, Month, Date and Year.

    1. Click [SET TIME/DATE ] bottom.

    1. The time will be reset now. Close the Guided Install Window.
