2  Procedure

NOTICE
Verify That The Laser Alignment Has Been Complete. Pay Close Attention To Position The Alignment Light On The Phantom, As This Procedure Requires An Accurate Landmark.
 
 

NOTICE
If Problems Are Encountered Performing This Calibration, See The DQA Troubleshooting Document.
 
 

NOTE:For 3T systems with G3 Magnet, the DQA phantom with pink solution is no longer used. Use the DQA phantom with green solution part number 2322997-2.

  1. Insert the appropriate DQA-III Phantom into head coil, and verify its orientation and that it is level. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
The correct phantom type, position and landmark are all critical to successful operation of the DQA-II tool.
 
 

  1. Landmark on the centerline of the DQA-III Phantom. Laser must be in middle of black circumferential maker line.

    (Step Details)

  1. Start the DQA Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. A new window will appear (see Illustration 3). Select [Start ]to begin the DQA calibration.

    (Step Details)

  1. As the DQA tool progresses, the status appears in the Progress area. If problems are encountered, they will appear in the Action Required section (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. The tool will indicate when it has completed successfully (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

3  Finalization

  1. Click [Exit] to exit the tool.
