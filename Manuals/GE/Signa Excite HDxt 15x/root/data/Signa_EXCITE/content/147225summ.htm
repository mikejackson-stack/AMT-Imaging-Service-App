GOC SCSI Tower and DVD/MOD Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Electric Shock Hazard!
Lethal voltages are present inside the Global Operator Console (GOC) Tower.
Ensure power is turned off and that the power cord is removed before opening the drive tower.

1  Procedure

1.1   SCSI Tower Assembly

1.1.1  Replacing the SCSI Tower Assembly

  1. Shut down the PC software.

  1. Power down the Global Operator Console (GOC).

  1. Turn off power to the SCSI Tower (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the SCSI cable, power cable, and SCSI terminator from the rear of the original SCSI Tower Assembly (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Discard the external terminator.

  1. Remove three screws from the original SCSI Tower cover (as shown in Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the cover to the rear to remove it. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the faceplate by releasing the four tabs while pulling the faceplate away from the unit. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove four screws from the drive (two on each side) and slide it out the from of the tower as shown in Illustration 7 and Illustration 8. The DVD drive is on the top and the MOD drive is on the bottom (or secondary) drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For DVD Drive) Verify jumper positions on DVD as shown in Illustration 10 and Illustration 11, based on the version of the SCSI bridge. In addition, JP2 and JP4 on the bridge should be shorted with a jumper.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For  Sony MOD Drive) Verify the following settings and jumpers:

    (Step Details)

1.1.2  Reassembly

  1. Partially slide the drive into the new tower and connect the power and data cable. Connect power and ribbon cables to drives. Note that a new power/data cable (contained in p/n 5119212) for the DVD drive is provided. This must be utilized and the data portion installed along with the power cord. MOD drive should be the last connector on the ribbon cable.

  1. Reinstall the four drive-mounting screws.

  1. Install the SCSI Tower cover. See Illustration 4.

  1. Reconnect the power cable and SCSI cable.

  1. Power on the GOC.

  1. Apply power to the SCSI Tower Assembly on the back of the unit, as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

1.1.3  Configuring Software

  1. Open a command window.

  1. Run the command scsistat.

    (Step Details)

  1. Launch MR Service Desktop.

  1. Click [Configure].

  1. Click [FE mode].

  1. Click [Click here to start the tool].

    (Step Details)

  1. Login window will open.

  1. Login: Root.

    (Step Details)

  1. Password: operator.

  1. From the Utilities heading in the left navigation tree, select [Display MOD/CD-ROM].

  1. Click [Setup Devices].

    (Step Details)

  1. When this procedure is complete, exit the guided install module and go to Finalization, Step 1 (plus remaining steps of the Finalization section).

1.2   Replacing a DVD/MOD Drive in an Original Tower

1.2.1  Removing DVD/MOD Drives

  1. Shut down the PC software.

  1. Power down the Global Operator Console (GOC).

  1. Turn off power to the SCSI Tower (see Illustration 19).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the SCSI cable, power cable, and external terminator from the rear of the SCSI Tower Assembly (see Illustration 20).

    (Step Details)

  1. From the SCSI Tower cover, remove three screws (as shown in Illustration 21).

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect power and data connections from the rear of the device.

NOTE:Steps to remove the MOD drive are similar to those for removing the DVD Drive.

  1. Remove four screws from the drive (two on each side) and slide it out the front of the tower as shown in Illustration 22. The DVD drive is on the top and the MOD drive is the on the bottom (or secondary) drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For replacing the MOD drive) Remove one jumper from the functional switch (see Illustration 23) and save it for the replacement MOD.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For replacing the DVD drive) Verify jumper positions on DVD as shown in Illustration 24.

    (Step Details)

  1. (For Sony MOD Drive) Verify the following settings and jumpers:

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  Reassembly

  1. Partially slide the drive into the tower and connect the power and data cable.

  1. Reinstall the four drive-mounting screws.

  1. Install the SCSI Tower Cover and replace the three screws. See Illustration 28.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reinstall the SCSI cable, power cable, and SCSI terminator to the rear of the SCSI Tower Assembly. See Illustration 29.

    (Step Details)

  1. Power ON the GOC.

  1. Confirm the green power light on the right front of the SCSI Tower is illuminated. If not, apply power to the SCSI Tower Assembly from the power button on the front of the unit as shown in Illustration 30.

    Illustration 30:  SCSI Tower Assembly Power Switch

    1. Confirm the SCSI Tower Assembly is receiving power.

    1. Confirm the SCSI data cable and SCSI terminator are connected as shown in Illustration 29 (locations not important).

    1. Confirm that the other end of the SCSI data cable is connected to the PC.

    1. If power was applied to the SCSI Tower too late in the boot process, the software may not have detected it. If this is the case, reboot.

1.3   Replacing a DVD/MOD Drive in a New Tower

1.3.1  Removing DVD/MOD Drives

  1. Shut down the PC software.

  1. Power down the Global Operator Console (GOC).

  1. Turn off power at the rear of the SCSI Tower (see Illustration 31).

  1. Remove the SCSI cable and power cable from the rear of the SCSI Tower Assembly (see Illustration 31).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 4 screws from the bottom of the SCSi tower. See Illustration 32.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the tower upright and slide the enclosure to the rear to remove. See Illustration 33.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the faceplate using a standard screwdriver to release the 4 tabs while pulling faceplate away from the unit. See Illustration 34.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the lower drive cover from the faceplate of the new SCSI tower if you will be installing a DVD-RW drive. See Illustration 35.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the old MOD and DVD-RW drives.

  1. (For DVD Drive) Verify jumper positions on DVD as shown in Illustration 36 and Illustration 37, based on the version of the SCSI bridge. In addition, JP2 and JP4 on the bridge should be shorted with a jumper. .

    (Step Details)

  1. (For  Sony MOD Drive) Verify the following settings and jumpers:

    (Step Details)

1.3.2  Reassembly

  1. Partially slide the drive into the new tower and connect the power and data cable. Connect power and ribbon cables to drives. Note that a new power/data cable (contained in p/n 5119212) for the DVD drive is provided. This must be utilized and the data portion installed along with the power cord. MOD drive should be the last connector on the ribbon cable. See Illustration 41.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure the drives into the drive cage with screws as shown in Illustration 42.

    (Step Details)

  1. Snap the faceplate back into position.

  1. Slide enclosure over tower and secure with 4 screws. See Illustration 43.

    (Step Details)

  1. Apply power to the SCSI Tower Assembly on the back of the unit.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

NOTE:Perform the following procedure for all drives in the tower. For DVD-R/W, write to the DVD drive to make sure it is functional.

  1. In the left navigation pane, go to Utilities and select SaveRestore.

  1. Click [Save Information & SaveGI]. (This operation is often referred to as performing a “SaveInfo”.)

  1. Insert the media to be used into the drive.

  1. Perform a Verification of SaveInfo Data to ensure information was saved correctly.
