Home Limit Switch Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Perform Lock out and tag out of the PDU.

  1. Remove rear pedestal right side cover (as viewed from rear of magnet).

  1. Remove screws from cover of Magnet I/F #1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect sensor plug from J2 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Disconnect harness from J3 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Replace failed board.

  1. Connect harness to J3 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Connect sensor plug from J2 of Home Limit Switch Board.

  1. Install screws onto cover of Magnet I/F #1.

  1. Install rear pedestal right side cover.

  1. Remove Lock out tag-out.

  1. Restore system power.

  1. Verify that cradle returns to Home position and that longitudinal drive motor has stopped trying to drive the cradle.

2  Finalization
