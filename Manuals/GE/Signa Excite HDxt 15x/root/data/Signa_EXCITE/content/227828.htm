3  Procedure

  1. Insert a flat head screwdriver into the .5 inch (12.7 mm) diameter contact opening on the end of the Main Lead Extension. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Contact Band Removal

  1. Engage the Contact Band's louvers with the screwdriver tip and gradually pry the old Contact Band out of the contact opening without scoring the contact opening's wall.

NOTICE
The contact area on which the Contact Band seats is silver plated and must be cleaned with a nonabrasive cloth and cleaning solution before a new Contact Band is inserted.
 
 

  1. Clean the cavity in which the Contact Band seats with a nonabrasive cloth and a degreasing solution such as trichlorethylene.

  1. Obtain and inspect a new Contact Band (46-281256P1).

  1. Roll the new Contact Band into a uniform cylinder of less than .5 inch (12.7 mm) diameter and fully insert it into the contact opening. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Contact Band Insertion

  1. Make sure the Contact Band has expanded against walls of the contact opening and is fully seated below rim of the opening.
