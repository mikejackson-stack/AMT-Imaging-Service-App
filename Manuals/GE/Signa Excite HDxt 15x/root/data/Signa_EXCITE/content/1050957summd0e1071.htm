NOTE:Check that the Teslameter reading does not decrease as the Voltage Adjust control is turned to zero. Only the last two digits on the Teslameter should change.

  • If the field decreases as the Voltage control is reduced, the Main Coil Switch is not persistent.

  • Slowly adjust the Voltage control to return to parking field. (The field drops approximately 1 kHz when the power supply is being dialed down to zero amps.)
