3  Procedure

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, refer to Auto Coil Installation.

  1. Attach the base plate with 1.5T HD 8-Channel High Res Brain Array to the cradle in the same position on the cradle as the standard head coil. Record the serial number of the coil.

  1. Slide the coil back to permit access to the head holder as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place phantom ring on head holder as shown in Illustration 2. (The edge of the ring that goes toward the magnet has edges that overhang the head holder to secure the ring in place.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Place head TLT Sphere on the phantom ring as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the coil all the way forward (away from the magnet) on the baseplate, and set the locks as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil, and press [Advance to Scan].

  1. Click here to access the MCQA Test.
