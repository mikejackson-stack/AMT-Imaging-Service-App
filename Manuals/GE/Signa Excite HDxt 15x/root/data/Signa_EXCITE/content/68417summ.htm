Magnet Enclosure Button Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. From The Common Service Desktop select:

    1. [Diags] Button

    1. Select: [Manual Tests]

    1. Select: [SRI Functional Tests]

    1. Select: [Magnet Enclosure Button Test]

NOTICE
Do not press Emergency Stop button - Pressing the top, outer buttons (see Illustration 1) does not provide a display reading. Pressing these buttons causes the display to become blank as Emergency Stop shuts down power to the magnet enclosure.
 
 

  1. Press each button and verify the code displayed on the Scan Time Remaining display corresponds to the button numbers shown in Illustration 1. Verify the Scan Time Remaining display increments as buttons are pressed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Stop Diags] to end the test. Select [Quit] to exit diagnostics.
