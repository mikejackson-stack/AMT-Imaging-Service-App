1.5T HD 8-Channel Foot Ankle Coil Replacements

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
This procedure must be performed by an authorized GE Service Engineer only.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Cable Replacement

  1. Perform the Cable Continuity Check found in 1.5T HD 8-Channel Foot Ankle Coil Troubleshooting Tips.

  1. Proceed as follows for the Cable Replacement procedure:

    1. Remove the two Coil Connector mounting screws as detailed below.

      (Step Details)

    1. Disconnect the cable by grasping the coil connector housing, and pulling the cable away from the coil connector in a downward direction.

      (Step Details)

1.2   Mechanical Parts Replacement

Refer to the Using the Foot Ankle Coil section in 1.5T HD 8-Channel Foot Ankle Coil Operator Manual for both steps below.

  1. Exercise the latches on the baseplate by moving the blue latch handle back and forth. (The foot support and coil is capable of a 15 degree plantar flexion.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the coil from side to side on the baseplate, making sure the coil moves smoothly.

    1. Swivel the coil on the baseplate. The coil must move smoothly within a 10 to 15 degree rotation.

    1. Lock the coil into position on the baseplate. The coil must remain in place while locked down, and resume smooth movement when unlocked.
