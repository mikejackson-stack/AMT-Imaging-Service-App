HD Service Platform 5155291 Set-Up

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential Pinch Hazard!
Fingers placed in the path of moving components while HD Service Platform 5155291 is being assembled/adjusted can be pinched.
Do not place fingers in the path of any HD Service Platform components being assembled/adjusted.

CAUTION
Potential Scratches/Cuts!
HDx Service Platform 5155291 may have edges sharp enough to scratch or cut skin.
Wear leather gloves while assembling, adjusting or disassembling HD Service Platform to protect against scratches/cuts and to keep your hands clean.

  1. Position the ladder components in an open area away from the magnet about 6 ft. (1,800 mm) square.

    1. Disengage each Telescoping Leg Locking Pin.

    1. Simultaneously slide both sides of each ladder section until the pinning hole for the platform height desired aligns with the Locking Pin.

      (Step Details)

    1. Securely engage all four Locking Pins in their pinning holes.

    1. If adjusting the platform to its 60 in. (1,524 mm) height, fully extend and securely pin all four Ladder Outriggers as shown below.

  1. Verify that all connections/adjustments are secure, then carefully flip the platform and legs upright.

CAUTION
Potential Back Strain!
Poor posture when lifting can cause back strain/damage.
Keep the upper body directly over your legs and use the service ladder's lifting handles when lifting an end of the service ladder.

NOTICE
Do NOT push the Service Platform across the floor to avoid marking or damaging the floor.
 
 

  1. Use the Service Platform's lifting handles to lift one ladder end high enough to engage the other ladder end's wheels. Move the platform into the Magnet Room before lowering the end lifted back to the floor.

  1. Insert the Short Side Safety Rail into two Pipe Sleeves along a short edge of the platform (shown above), typically at the service end of the magnet.

  1. Insert the Magnet-Side Toe Board (shown in Illustration 4) along the magnet side of the platform.

  1. Repeat Step 7 whenever relocating the assembled HD Service Platform inside the Magnet Room.

2  Finalization

  1. Disassemble HD Service Platform 5155291 in reverse order to assembling it.

  1. Always return HD Service Platform to its designated storage location after usage.
