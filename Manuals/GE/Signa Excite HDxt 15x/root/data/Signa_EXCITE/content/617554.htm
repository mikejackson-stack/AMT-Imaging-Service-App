2  Overview

Follow this process to replace field replaceable units on the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Wrist Array by Invivo.

4  Procedure

  1. Turn the coil upside down to access the cable assembly. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  HD Wrist Array FRU

  1. Remove the 4 screws securing the cable assembly. Make sure to identify which screws are located in each hole as the screw sizes differ. See Illustration 1.

  1. Use indentation on each side of the part to remove the FRU after removing screws. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    Illustration 2:  Remove The FRU

    Illustration 3:   HD Wrist Array FRU Interior View

  1. Replace the cable assembly with the new one. Make sure to seat the connector firmly once located in place and then reinstall 4 screws. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Wrist Array FRU Replacement

5  Finalization

Perform an MCQA test as explained in the1.5T 8 Channel Wrist Array MCQA Test Setup document.
