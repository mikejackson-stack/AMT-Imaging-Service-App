Replacement of PAC

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages are Present around the Magnet Enclosure and Rear Pedestal electronics.
Remove Power to The Magnet Enclosure and Lockout/Tagout.

1  Procedure

1.1   Secure The System

  1. Turn off the Circuit Breaker labeled PHPS/SSM on the Gradient/PDU cabinet. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the Lock-out, Tag-out procedure.

1.2   PAC Replacement For LCC Magnets With Skin Tight Covers

  1. The PAC can be located under either the right or left side of the magnet enclosure. Identify which side the PAC is on by locating the patient leads, the PAC will be on that side.

  1. Remove the lower enclosure trim covers to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn latches in holding tray in place. Slide tray out far enough to gain access to the PAC cables. See Illustration 4.

  1. Disconnect the six (6) cable attached to the PAC. See Illustration 4.

  1. Remove four (4) screws holding PAC to tray. See Illustration 4.

  1. Lift PAC off tray and remove.

    (Step Details)

1.3   PAC Location For Saltbox Magnets

  1. Remove the PAC and all applicable cabling from the PAC that is located on the front enclosure. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to Installation of New PAC .

1.4   Installation of New PAC

  1. Place the PAC in the proper location and install in the reverse order as it was removed.

  1. Reinstall all the cabling.

  1. 6. Remove Lockout / Tagout hardware.

2  Finalization

  1. Setup a test scan and test system operation.
