Test GE Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU)

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
IF ERU FAILS TEST, ARRANGE FOR IMMEDIATE REMEDIAL SERVICE. FAILURE TO HAVE AN OPERATING MRU WILL PREVENT BRINGING DOWN THE MAGNETIC FIELD IN THE EVENT OF AN EMERGENCY.
 
 

1  Procedure

NOTICE
AVOID A QUENCH OF MAGNET! DO NOT ATTEMPT ANY TESTS OR MAINTENANCE ON MRU MODULE UNLESS YOU ARE THOROUGHLY FAMILIAR WITH IT. NO PM STEP WILL ASK YOU TO PRESS [EMERGENCY RUNDOWN] BUTTON IN CENTER OF MRU MODULE.
 
 

  1. Verify green LED on front panel is lit (indicating battery charger has power). See the illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the TEST BATTERY switch. The green LED labeled BATTERY will illuminate if the battery is OK. If LED illuminates, proceed to Step 7. Otherwise, proceed to Step 3.

  1. Open front cover and check for 20.7V between TP-1 and TP-2 on circuit board. If voltage is 20.7V or greater the comparator circuit or the LED driver circuit require adjustment or repair. If the voltage is below 20.7 V, proceed to Step 4, Step 5, and Step 6.

  1. Check for an open battery fuse F2. If blown, replace with a 2.5A fuse.

  1. Visually inspect the battery for signs of leakage.

  1. Remove fuse F2 and check the battery voltage. If battery replacement is needed, order three 6V, Gel Cell Batteries (46-294231P5).

  1. Press the TEST HEATER switch to Position A, and verify green LED labeled HEATER illuminates. If LED illuminates, propeed to Step 10. Otherwise, proceed to Step 8.

  1. Press TEST HEATER LED switch to verify LED is working. If LED is OK, but does not illuminate when the TEST HEATER switch is pressed, a serious circuit failure has been detected.

NOTICE
AVOID A QUENCH OF MAGNET! DO NOT USE AN OHMMETER ON QUENCH HEATER CIRCUITS OR IN SWITCH HEATER CIRCUITS ON AN ENERGIZED MAGNET.
 
 

  1. Contact your MAC Team Leader, MR RSE, or the NSC 24 hr. Magnet Support Line at 1-800-321-7937 for correct troubleshooting procedure.

  1. Press TEST HEATER switch to position B and verify green LED labeled HEATER lights. If the LED illuminates, proceed to Step 13. If LED does not illuminate, proceed to Step 11.

  1. Press the TEST HEATER LED switch to verify LED is working. If LED is OK, but does not illuminate when the TEST HEATER switch is pressed, a serious circuit failure has been detected.

  1. Contact your MAC Team Leader, MR RSE, or the NSC 24hr. Magnet Support Line at 1-800-321-7937 for correct troubleshooting procedure.

  1. Replace front cover.
