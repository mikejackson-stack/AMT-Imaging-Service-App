Section 2 RF Cabinet Power Lockout and Tagout

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION Hazard!
POWER NOT REMOVED FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO SERVICE THE UNIT
REMOVE POWER FROM THE SRFD2 CABINET BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO SERVICE THE UNIT. VERIFY THAT THE RFI/AMP AND SSM BREAKERS ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU ARE OFF. LOCK AND TAG OUT THE PLEXI-GLASS COVER ON THE FRONT OF THE PDU.

  1. Remove the cover from the front of the ACGD cabinet.

  2. Open Circuit Breaker Cover on the front of the PDU and switch OFF the RFI/AMP and SSM breakers. See Illustration 1

  3. Close the plexi-glass door and lock and tag it out. SeeIllustration 2. (Refer to OSHA LOCKOUT/TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS)

  4. Remove the front cover from the SRFD2 Cabinet.

  5. Verify all LEDs are OFF (not illuminated) on front of SSM. If the DCHV LED is ON, on the front of the SRFD2 Module, there is still charge stored in the High Voltage power supply capacitors. Wait till all LEDs are OFF. See Illustration 3.

    NOTE:The front panel grate may appear different on newer SRFD2 modules. Illustration 4
