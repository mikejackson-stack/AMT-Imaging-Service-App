1.5T 8 Channel High Res Brain Array Replacements

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsProcedure
130 min

2  Overview

Follow this process to replace field replaceable units for the 1.5T HD 8 Channel High Res Brain Array by Invivo (Catalog Number M3335LZ).

3  Tools and Test Equipment

For cable replacement, a ¼ inch flat blade screwdriver.

4  Replacement Parts

Refer to the FRU document.

5  Procedure

5.1  Cable Continuity Check

Troubleshooting should be Performed by Authorized Service Engineer Only.

5.2  Cable Replacement Procedure

To be performed by Authorized Service Engineer Only.

The 4 coil connector mounting screws are located at the superior end of the coil. Remove the 4 coil connector mounting screws as detailed in Figure 1. Remove the coil connector by pulling away from the coil in the superior direction.

Illustration 1:  Remove 4 Screws

The steps for installation of the new cable are simply the reverse order of the steps described for removal.

5.3  Mechanical Hardware Check

Lift the coil latches on both sides of the coil, and slide the coil to its superior and inferior extents. The coil must slide freely on the base with no binding or grinding. Make sure that the coil does not move with respect to the baseplate when the latches are locked down.
