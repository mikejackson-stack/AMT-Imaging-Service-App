WARNING
Flying Projectile Danger!
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD !! TORQUE WRENCH AND SOCKET DRIVE CONTAIN FERROUS MATERIAL AND CAN BECOME FORCIBLY ATTRACTED TO THE MAGNET.
DO NOT BRING THESE TOOLS INTO THE MAGNET ROOM.

1  Procedure

  1. Un-dock the table and move the table to a level area in the scan room.

  1. With the table un-docked and away from the dock, lift and press down on the four corners of the table, see Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. If any of the casters can be lifted off of the floor, perform Table Leveling Procedure.

2  Finalization

  1. Re-dock the table and test for proper operation before customer turn over.
