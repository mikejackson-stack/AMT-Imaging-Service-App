      • Adjusting the patient handling system with the laser leveling tool

        The proper alignment of the entire patient handling system is important for good image quality output from the GE MR scanners.

        • Tools overview

          The patient handling alignment procedure is to be completed using the patient handling and magnet leveling tool kit (5897979) and the MR Patient Handling Alignment Macro Tool (DOC2372458).

        • Aligning the bridge and rear pedestal (z-dir and x-dir)

          Align the front endbell-bridge longitudinal (z-dir) offset and the bridge in the lateral (x-dir) direction (left to right of the magnet bore).

        • Adjusting the dock hook tension

          Adjust the dock hook tension when the force required to engage the dock pedal is minimal or too much (indicating the hook is either too loose or too tight).

        • Aligning the patient table angle (rotation about y-axis) and offset (x-dir)

          Measure the distance between the table top and bridge top, measure the angle and offset of the table with the horizontal rulers, and adjust the table accordingly.

        • Measuring the bridge and rear pedestal heights

          Measure the height of the rear pedestal, bridge, and bridge in the body coil, and record the measurements in the macro tool.

        • Aligning the bridge and rear pedestal (y-dir)

          If necessary, adjust the height of the rear pedestal or bridge, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

        • Measuring the patient table height

          Measure the height of the dock bosses and patient table, make adjustments to the dock boss height if necessary, and note the measurements for entry in the macro tool.

          If necessary, adjust the height of the patient table and table casters, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

Adjusting the patient table height (y-dir)

If necessary, adjust the height of the patient table and table casters, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

Topic ID: task_nwp_1yv_3lb

Table 1 output

Procedure

  1. Put the vertical rulers at the magnet-side corners of the table approximately 6 cm in from the end. Make sure the rulers do not rest on the chamfered surfaces.
  2. Remove the table top height adjustment plastic screw cover.
  3. Use a flat head screwdriver to raise or lower the table to match the Magnet Side Table 1 output values shown in the macro tool.
  4. Put the vertical rulers at the handle-side corners of the table approximately 6 cm in from the end. Make sure the rulers do not rest on the chamfered surfaces.
  5. Adjust the rear casters of the table to match the Handle Side Table 1 output values shown in the macro tool. If the measurement is within specification (± 4 mm), no adjustments to the table height are required.
    Note: It is helpful to have an idea of how much to adjust the rear casters before executing the procedure. Each caster turn is approximately 1.5 mm of height adjustment.
  6. To adjust the casters:
    1. Undock the table.
    2. Engage the table brakes in the full lock position.
    3. Remove the collar bolt from the collar of the caster.
    4. Remove the caster pin.
    5. Warning
      Ferrous material hazard
      The scissor jack is ferrous and will be attracted to the magnet.
      Do not use the scissor jack in the scan room. Move the table out of the scan room prior to jacking. Failure to comply may result in serious injury or death.
      Raise the table with the scissor jack so that the caster being adjusted is off the floor sufficient for adjustment.
      Note: Position the scissor jack under a solid surface at the bore or handle end of the table such as the examples shown.
    6. Rotate the entire caster assembly (make sure the threaded portion of the caster is rotating) clockwise or counterclockwise to raise or lower the caster and align the bosses to within specifications. Each caster turn is approximately 1.5 mm of height adjustment.
      • Rotating the caster clockwise, as viewed from the top, raises the table bosses.
      • Rotating the caster counterclockwise, as viewed from the top, lowers the table bosses.
    7. Reinstall the collar lock nut and caster pin.
    8. Make sure the nut portion of the caster pin is above the hook of the brake linkage plate.
    9. Make sure the caster pin engagement with the brake linkage plate hook has enough contact area.
    10. If the nut portion of the caster pin is in the hook of the brake linkage plate or does not have enough engagement with the linkage plate, minimally adjust the caster.
Topic ID: task_jf4_zqw_3lb

Table 1 check

Procedure

  1. Put the vertical rulers at each of the four corners of the table approximately 6 cm in from the ends. Make sure the rulers do not rest on the chamfered surfaces.
  2. Record the final table values in the macro tool in centimeters. The tolerance is ± 2 mm of the output values.
Topic ID: task_i2b_vrw_3lb

Casters 1 check

Procedure

  1. At each of the four caster wheel assemblies, measure the height from the floor to the bottom edge of the aluminum support casting.
  2. Record the values for each caster in the macro tool in millimeters. The tolerance is 207 ± 10 mm.
    Note: If the table casters are not within specifications (207 ± 10 mm), lower or raise the casters until they are at the minimum or maximum depending on the situation.
Topic ID: task_brf_p5w_3lb

Multi-table configurations

Procedure

If the system has a multi-table configuration, repeat Table 1 output, Table 1 check, and Casters 1 check for table 2 in the tool.
