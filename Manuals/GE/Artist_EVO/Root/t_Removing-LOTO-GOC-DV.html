Removing LOTO - Global Operator Cabinet (GOC) and operator workspace

Remove LOTO from the GOC and operator workspace before restoring power to these items.

Procedure

  1. Alert necessary personnel that power is being restored.
  2. Replace the cover on the host PC cabinet.
  3. Remove the personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the Power, Gradient, RF Cabinet (PGR) Power Distribution Unit (PDU).
  4. At the front panel of the PGR PDU, switch the Host circuit breaker to the ON position.
  5. Restart the host computer.
