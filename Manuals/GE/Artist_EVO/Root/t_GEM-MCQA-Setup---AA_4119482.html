Procedure

  1. Place the Flat Filler Panel (shown below) on the GEM table.
  2. Place two TL Unified Phantoms with the PA Phantom Positioner on the GEM table.
    1. Ensure the Alignment notch on the PA Phantom Positioner faces toward the bore, and the side opposite the alignment notch on the PA Phantom Positioner embraces the TL Unified Phantom.
    2. Ensure the edge of the lower part of PA Phantom Positioner aligns with the edge of the flat filler panel as shown.
  3. Place the AA Coil on top of the PA Phantom Positioner, and secure to each other with Velcro® straps as shown. Connect the coil to the P1 port.
  4. Set center position of the AA Coil connected on Port 1 using the Touch and Go strips on the side of the table.
  5. Landmark on the AA Coil crosshairs as shown, and press Advance to Scan
  6. Run the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance Tool. Ensure that the correct coil is selected in the Current Coil field: 1.5T_GEM_AA (shown below) or 3.0T_GEM_AA for a 3.0T system.

No finalization steps.
