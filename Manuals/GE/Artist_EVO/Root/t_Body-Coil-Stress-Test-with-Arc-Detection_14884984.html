Setup for Stress Test

About this task

A body sphere is required for the arc detection test.

Procedure

  1. Place the body sphere on the cradle. Landmark the phantom near or in the middle of the cradle to minimize any potential coupling of body RF energy into A, P1, and P2 receive lines, and then press Advance to Scan.
  2. Place the spectrum analyzer from the arc detection kit near the operator console (if possible – the maximum length of the cable from the analyzer to the PEN panel is 100 feet).
  3. Plug the spectrum analyzer power adaptor into the proper outlet and into the right side of the spectrum analyzer.
  4. Turn on the spectrum analyzer by pressing the yellow button on the lower left side of the analyzer.
  5. Press the Preset button on the analyzer to download the default settings, then press the SWEEP button.
  6. To set the trigger: Press SWEEP, F5, press either the up ^ or down v button to select video, type 8 and 0, and press ENTER.
    Note:

    The spectrum analyzer automatically loads the default settings on power-up. No programming should be necessary. If programming is required, follow the instructions in Step 7. If no programming is required, proceed to Step 8.

  7. Continue with the spectrum analyzer setup as follows:
    1. Set the frequency to 200 MHz: Press FREQ, type 200, press MHz dBm and ENTER.
    2. Set the frequency span: Press SPAN, type 0, and press ENTER.
    3. Set the range: Press AMPT, F2, either the up ^ or down v button to obtain 5 dB/DIV, and press ENTER.
    4. Set the ref level: Press AMPT. (Press F1 if need so that REF LEVEL is highlighted in red.), – (minus), type 2 and 5 and then press ENTER.
    5. Set the resolution: Press BW, (Press F1 if need so that MANUAL RES BW is highlighted in red.), type 1, press MHz, and ENTER.
    6. Set the video: Press BW, F3, type 1, press MHz, and then ENTER.
    7. Set the sweep time: Press SWEEP, type 2 and 0, and then press ENTER.
    8. Set the detector: Press TRACE, DETECTOR, press either the up ^ or down v button for Auto Peak, and press ENTER.
    9. Set the trigger: Press SWEEP, F5, press either the up ^ or down v button to select Video, type 8 and 0, and press ENTER.
  8. Make the following connections for the spectrum analyzer:
    1. Connect the port labeled RF Input to a short 1 ft (0.3 m) cable.
    2. Connect the other end of the short cable to the BNC-T connector that is part of the 63.86 MHz BNC quarter-wave stub filter cable or long filter.
    3. Connect one of the 50 ft coaxial cables to the other side of the BNC-T connector on the stub filter.
    4. Attach the other end of the 50 ft BNC cable to an open BNC connector (service J112 or other open connector) on the PEN panel.
      Note:

      The kit contains three 50 ft BNC cables. One is used in the scan room, and one is used in the equipment room. The third 50 ft cable can be connected in series either in the scan or equipment room if necessary; however, it is recommended that the minimum cable length be used.

  9. Adjust the two individual dipoles of the antenna out to approximately 13 5/8 inches (34.6 cm) in length. There are fixed holes for the two dipoles. Insert the screws and wing nuts.
  10. Temporarily place the antenna in the operator room so it is outside of the magnet room.
  11. Connect the BNC cable between the antenna and the BNC port on the PEN panel, which is connected to the spectrum analyzer.

Verification Using Pizeo Gun

Procedure

  1. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
    The Pizeo gun is ferrous and will be pulled toward the magnet.
    Avoid positioning the gun near the bore of the magnet.
    Make sure the antenna is outside of the magnet room, away from the magnet.
  2. Hold the Pizeo gun against the antenna, and press the black button on the gun. A spark should be visible at the tip of the gun. Press the black button on the gun several times.
  3. Observe the spectrum analyzer. Spikes should display as shown.
    1. Note:

      When arcs are observed using the spectrum analyzer, make sure that the hardware setup does not change so that arcs will be observed during the scan.

      Reset the spectrum analyzer by pressing either the F5 or Trigger button, Free Run, and ENTER.
    2. Press F5 or Trigger, Video, and press ENTER twice. This clears the spectrum analyzer in preparation for the arc detection scan.

      A star shows on the right side of the display as shown below.

Arc Detection Scan

About this task

Proceed according to the instructions that follow for the arc detection scan.

Procedure

  1. Place the antenna on the table as shown. (The antenna should be placed just behind the IV pole inserts.)
  2. Set up a scan as follows:
    1. Select New Patient and type geservice for the Patient ID.
    2. Type 300 for Weight (Lb), and press Enter.
    3. Select Show All Protocols and Service. Select APBCAL, the center arrow, and click Accept.
    4. Select Start Scan.
    5. Select Research Options from the Scan button drop-down menu, and change the following:

      • calmode = 2

      • p2 = 3700

      • t2 = 50000

    6. From the Research pull-down menu, select Download.
    7. Before prescan, make sure that the “star” is present on the spectrum analyzer as shown in Figure 8.
  3. On the Scan drop-down list, select Manual Prescan, then Scan TR.
  4. Increase TG to 200.
  5. Allow manual prescan to run for 15 minutes. At the end of the 15 minute prescan at TG of 200, check the spectrum analyzer for any spike events.

    • Observe the amplitude of the spike and the star shown in Figure 8. If the star is present and there are no spikes, the coil is fine.

    • If any spike greater than -35 dbm occurs, or the star is missing (as shown below), the test failed. The spike must be fixed before returning the system to the customer.

  6. Press Done to exit manual prescan.
  7. Move the antenna out of the scan room.
  8. Repeat Verification Using Pizeo Gun. This verifies that the arc detection tool is working properly.

Troubleshooting Arc Issues

About this task

Use this process to confirm spikes not coming from RF transmit hardware.

Procedure

  1. Confirm BNC quarter-wave shorted stub filter cable of correct field strength is installed as shown in Figure 3 and Figure 4.
  2. Place the RF ENABLE switch on the DTX1 exciter (top of the PEN cabinet) in the DISABLE (down) position.
  3. Notice
    Perform LOTO on the RF amplifier and PEN cabinet. See the MR Service Safety Manual, PN 5452735.
    Starting with normal configuration, remove the “I” RF drive cable (Q remains connected) between the hybrid and RF body coil. Set the RF ENABLE switch on DTX1 to ENABLE (Up) position. Run a scan at TG=170.

    • If there is no arcing after 15 minutes, end the test and inspect the “I” RF drive cable and both of the two RF connections to which it attaches. Repair or replace as needed. Proceed to Finalization.

    • If arcing occurs, set the RF ENABLE switch on DTX1 to DISABLE (down) position and proceed to the next step.

  4. Remove the “Q” RF drive cable that runs from the hybrid to the RF body coil and replace it with the “I” RF drive cable. Set the RF ENABLE switch on DTX1 to ENABLE (up) position. Run a scan at TG=170.

    • If no arcing occurs, end this test and inspect the “Q” RF drive cable or the two RF connections to which it attaches. Repair or replace as needed. Proceed to Finalization.

    • If arcing occurs, set the RF ENABLE switch on DTX1 to DISABLE (down) position and proceed to the next step.

  5. Remove the remaining RF drive cable from the hybrid and RF body coil so that neither the “I” nor “Q” RF drive cable is connected at either end. Set the RF ENABLE switch on DTX1 to ENABLE (up) position. Run a scan at TG=140.

    • If there is no arcing, the RF body coil may have failed. Repair or replace as needed. Proceed to Finalization.

    • If arcing occurs, it may be coming from somewhere else in the body transmit chain. Inspect the body transmission line connections at each connection point starting at the body hybrid splitter input. Next, inspect both sides of the PEN panel, top connection at PGR cabinet, internal connection at top inside of PGR cabinet, and connection at the RF amplifier body port.

Finalization

Finalization

  1. If arcs are observed, inspect the body coil and connections for source of the arcs. Do not return the coil to service until the problem is resolved and no arcs are seen.

  2. Proceed to RF body coil replacement and complete the remaining steps.
