Restoring the RF cables

Procedure

  1. Restore the body RF cables.
    1. On the front of the exciter, set the RF ENB switch to the disabled (down) position.
    2. Disconnect the stub cable and dummy load.
    3. Reconnect the system body channel 1 transmit cable to J4 RF OUT.
  2. Restore the head RF cables.
    1. Disconnect the dummy load.
    2. Reconnect the system head channel transmit cable to J3 RF OUT.
  3. Set the RF to the Enabled position.
  4. Re-enable the TR and DD fault switches on the front of the driver module.
  5. Go to Doing a check scan.
  • Doing a check scan
    A check scan is done at the conclusion of many procedures. This functional check assures the unit is in good clinical working order.
