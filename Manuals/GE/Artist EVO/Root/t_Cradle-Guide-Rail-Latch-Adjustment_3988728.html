Adjusting the cradle guide rail latch

This procedure describes how to adjust the cradle guide rail latch so that the cradle does not move when the table is undocked.

Prerequisites

Personnel requirements
Required personsPreliminary requirementsProcedureFinalization
1 -30 minutes -
Tools and test equipment
ItemQuantityPart numberManufacturer
Nonmagnetic Titanium Service Tool Kit, Large Set15112581-

About this task

The cradle is locked in the home position with a latch on the patient table bridge that engages a notch on the cradle guide rail at the rear of the table. The cradle should engage this notch just enough (without extra force) so when the cradle is pushed to the home position, it tightly engages the notch to prevent the table top from moving when the patient table is undocked.

Procedure

  1. Undock the patient table.
  2. Press the cradle release shown below, and make sure that the cradle rail lock prevents the cradle from moving. (Up to 2 mm of movement is acceptable.)
    1. Note:
      The cradle rail lock is located at the left rear of the cradle.
      If the cradle releases, pull the cradle to the front of the table and expose the cradle guide rails.
    2. Loosen (do not remove) the five screws on each guide rail, and slide each cradle guide rail to the front of the table.
    3. After adjustment, make sure that the cradle rail lock prevents the cradle from moving when the cradle release is pressed.
  3. Push the cradle to the rear of the table, and make sure that the cradle does not go past the end of the table.
    1. If the cradle does go past the end of the table, loosen (do not remove) the five screws on each guide rail, and lower the guide rail to approximately 1 mm above the cradle ledge. (This should prevent the cradle from traveling past the end of the table.)

Finalization

Make sure that the table operates properly:
  1. Dock the patient table.
  2. Make sure that the Low Profile Carriage Assembly (LPCA) automatically drives out to engage the cradle.
  3. Undock the table to make sure that the cradle release operates properly.
