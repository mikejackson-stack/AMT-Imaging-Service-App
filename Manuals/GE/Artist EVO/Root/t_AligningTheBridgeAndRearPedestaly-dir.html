      • Adjusting the patient handling system with the laser leveling tool

        The proper alignment of the entire patient handling system is important for good image quality output from the GE MR scanners.

        • Tools overview

          The patient handling alignment procedure is to be completed using the patient handling and magnet leveling tool kit (5897979) and the MR Patient Handling Alignment Macro Tool (DOC2372458).

        • Aligning the bridge and rear pedestal (z-dir and x-dir)

          Align the front endbell-bridge longitudinal (z-dir) offset and the bridge in the lateral (x-dir) direction (left to right of the magnet bore).

        • Adjusting the dock hook tension

          Adjust the dock hook tension when the force required to engage the dock pedal is minimal or too much (indicating the hook is either too loose or too tight).

        • Aligning the patient table angle (rotation about y-axis) and offset (x-dir)

          Measure the distance between the table top and bridge top, measure the angle and offset of the table with the horizontal rulers, and adjust the table accordingly.

        • Measuring the bridge and rear pedestal heights

          Measure the height of the rear pedestal, bridge, and bridge in the body coil, and record the measurements in the macro tool.

        • Aligning the bridge and rear pedestal (y-dir)

          If necessary, adjust the height of the rear pedestal or bridge, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

        • Measuring the patient table height

          Measure the height of the dock bosses and patient table, make adjustments to the dock boss height if necessary, and note the measurements for entry in the macro tool.

        • Adjusting the patient table height (y-dir)

          If necessary, adjust the height of the patient table and table casters, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

Aligning the bridge and rear pedestal (y-dir)

If necessary, adjust the height of the rear pedestal or bridge, and record new height measurements in the macro tool.

Topic ID: task_vqr_fbm_hlb

Rear pedestal output

Procedure

  1. Review the output of the spreadsheet to identify if adjustment is required.
    Note: If the first height measurements, entered in macro tool section 4, are within specification, the macro tool may still produce an output value requiring minor adjustment (for example, 0.5 mm). In this case, enter the height measurements into macro tool section 5 and look for feedback. If the cells turn green, then the values are within specification and you can continue to the next step.
  2. If height adjustment of the bridge or rear pedestal is required, loosen the lock nuts on the front and rear leveling feet.
    Note: Use an adjustable wrench for adjusting the leveling feet. The leveling feet on the rear pedestal have two flats on the bottom of the stud just above the leveling feet.
  3. Put the vertical rulers on the rear pedestal above the rear leveling feet.
  4. Adjust the rear leveling feet until the vertical rulers above the rear leveling feet match the output values. If the measurement is within specification (± 2 mm), no adjustments to the rear pedestal height are required.
  5. Record the final values in centimeters in the printout to be entered in the macro tool after bridge adjustments are complete.
  6. Put the vertical rulers at the bridge side of the bridge-rear pedestal joint.
  7. Adjust the front leveling feet until the vertical rulers at the bridge-rear pedestal joint match the output values. If the measurement is within specification (± 2 mm), no adjustments to the rear pedestal height are required.
  8. Record the final values in centimeters in the macro printout to be entered in the macro tool after bridge adjustments are complete.
  9. Tighten the lock nuts on the front and rear leveling feet.
Topic ID: task_kq1_b3n_hlb

Bridge output

Procedure

  1. Put vertical rulers at the front of the bridge approximately 6 cm into the bore, measured from the end of the bridge and on the bridge side of the bridge-rear pedestal joint. Make sure the rulers do not rest on the chamfered surfaces.
  2. If adjustments are required, loosen the two M10 bolts and the M10 nylon hex nuts on each side.
    Note: The threads on the nylon hex nuts can easily strip. If raising the front bridge assembly is required, lift the front of the bridge while turning the nylon hex nuts.
  3. To adjust, raise or lower the front bridge assembly by turning the nylon hex nuts to match the output of the macro tool. If the measurement is within specification (± 2 mm), no adjustments to the bridge height are required.
  4. Record the final values in centimeters in the macro printout to be entered in the macro tool after bridge adjustments are complete.
  5. Tighten the two M10 bolts and the M10 nylon hex nuts on each side.
Topic ID: task_fnj_dsn_hlb

Bridge height in body coil output

Procedure

  1. Use the brass height tool to measure the height of the bridge in the body coil in the front and rear. The bridge height in body coil output from the macro tool is for reference only.
    Note: Make sure the bridge height in the body coil is within specifications (502 to 505 mm for narrow bore; 582 to 585 mm for wide bore).
  2. If the bridge height in body coil is outside of specifications in the front, minimally adjust the front bridge assembly bracket and capture the new measurements.
  3. If the bridge height in body coil is outside of specifications in the rear, minimally adjust the front and rear leveling feet of the rear pedestal and capture the new measurements.
  4. Record the final values of the bridge height in body coil in millimeters in the macro printout.
Topic ID: task_zwl_btn_hlb

Bridge check

Procedure

Capture the final values in centimeters of the horizontal laser line height on the vertical rulers to be entered in the macro tool. The tolerance is ± 2 mm of the output values.
Topic ID: task_lwk_qtn_hlb

Enter final bridge and rear pedestal data in the macro tool

Procedure

Record the following measurements in the macro tool:
  • Rear pedestal
  • Bridge
  • Body coil size
  • Bridge height in body coil
