About this task

Follow this process to replace field replaceable units on the 1.5T HD 8 Channel Wrist Array by Invivo.

Topic ID: id_SL3902757-617554

Cable Replacement

Procedure

  1. Turn the coil upside down to access the cable assembly.
  2. Remove the 4 screws securing the cable assembly. Make sure to identify which screws are located in each hole as the screw sizes differ.
  3. Use indentation on each side of the part to remove the FRU after removing screws.
  4. Replace the cable assembly with the new one. Make sure to seat the connector firmly once located in place and then reinstall 4 screws.
Topic ID: id_SL3902758-617554

Post Replacement

Procedure

  1. CAUTION
    Electrostatic Damage (ESD)
    Removing the coil cable and top cover exposes ESD sensitive components.
    Use all applicable ESD prevention procedures (including a ESD Grounding Wrist Strap) when handling the coil.
    Remove the six cable assembly screws and the cable assembly, then remove the four cover screws and the top cover.
  2. Turn the Top Cover over and remove the six top post block screws, then remove the top post block assembly. Replace the parts as shown below as needed.
  3. Remove the four side post block assembly screws (see Figure 5). Replace the parts as shown below as needed.
  4. Reassemble the coil using the steps above.
Topic ID: id_14922709

Finalization

Finalization

For Cable Replacement: Perform an MCQA test as explained in the 1.5T 8 Channel Wrist Array MCQA Test Setup document.

For Post Replacement: Attach coil to baseplate to ensure proper part installation.
