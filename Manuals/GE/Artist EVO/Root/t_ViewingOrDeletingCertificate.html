Viewing or deleting a certificate

View or delete a certificate.

Procedure

  1. To view a certificate in more detail:
    1. Click the view (binoculars) icon.
  2. To delete a certificate:
    1. Click the trash (trash can) icon.
    2. If you attempt to delete the certificate while it is associated with some applications (EAT, EA3, or DICOM) the following error message displays.
    3. If this happens, click Configure Certificate.
    4. Clear the application from the certificate (eat, ea3, or dicom).
    5. Try the delete operation again.
