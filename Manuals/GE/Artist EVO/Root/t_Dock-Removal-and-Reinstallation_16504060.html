Removing and reinstalling the dock

This document contains procedures to remove and reinstall the patient table dock, which is necessary for replacement of hardware internal to the dock such as switches, motor, and springs.

Safety
DANGER
Fatal electric shock hazard
Hazardous voltage is present throughout the cabinet when it is energized.
To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect power and do lockout/tagout on the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) before beginning any work. See the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
Warning
Ferrous material hazard
This item will be attracted to the magnet.
Use two people when removing this item. Failure to comply may result in serious injury or death.
Warning
Personal injury and equipment damage!
Strong Magnetic Field.
When servicing any magnetic equipment, it is critically important that the service engineer consciously plan the path to be taken when moving highly ferrous devices in the magnet environment. The path should be as far from the magnet as practical.
Safety Requirements
The service path must allow for the dock to be moved along the ground a distance equal to that of the distance between the magnet and the foot-end of the patient table prior to being lifted vertically.
Two (2) MR safety trained personnel must be present at all times when servicing highly ferrous devices in the areas of magnetic fields.
When planning a service path, it is critical that the path be clear and sufficiently wide. Ensure that there are no trip hazards, obstacles, clutter, slippery surfaces or other items even partially restricting the path. If there are portable obstacles in a path, remove them from the area and replace them after the service action is completed. You must walk the path before beginning service to ensure that there is sufficient space through which to pass for yourself and the object being serviced.
Warning
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
Strong Magnetic Field.
Never disassemble the dock in the magnetic field. Remove the dock assembly from the magnet room as described in this procedure before disassembling the dock.

About this task

This document contains procedures to remove and reinstall the patient table dock, which is necessary for replacement of hardware internal to the dock such as switches, motor, and springs.

During this procedure, the following tasks are done:

  • Prepare the dock assembly for removal.
  • Cut the anchor stud, if necessary.
  • Remove the patient table dock assembly from the magnet room.
  • Reinstall the patient table dock assembly.
  • Make sure that the patient table dock assembly is installed correctly and the system is working properly and ready to be turned over to the customer.

The dock assembly is the most ferrous object in the magnet room. Utmost care must be taken when following these procedures. Never allow the end of the dock assembly (closest to the magnet) to come off the floor while in the magnet room. During the procedure, you will be asked to lift the front end of the dock assembly; the front end of the dock should never be lifted more than 7.6 cm (3 inches) while in the magnet room.

Preparing the dock for removal

Procedure

  1. Do LOTO on the RF amplifier and PEN cabinet. Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).
  2. Undock the patient table, and move it away from the magnet and out of the path between the dock and the magnet room exit door.
  3. Remove the following covers for access to the dock cable connections:
    1. Right and left side covers (see Side and Top Cover Removal and Installation).
    2. Rear pedestal covers.
    3. Bridge and front covers (see Front Cover Removal and Installation).
  4. Disconnect all cable connections from the magnet and the rear pedestal to the rear of the dock.

    Depending on the site’s system, there are different configurations possible:

    • Systems that have the cable interface for receive cables on magnet front right foot

    • Systems that have the dock receive cables going all the way to the RF hub

    • Systems that have SRI cables going to magnet left front foot

    Remove the associated cables that pertain to the site’s system configuration.

  5. To assist in repositioning the dock during reinstallation, mark the placement of the dock on the floor. Depending on the site’s need, put tape on the floor at the leading edge of the dock, and on the right and left sides. Both are shown in the illustration.
  6. Visually examine the height of the anchor stud that bolts the dock assembly to the floor. The height of the stud should not exceed 7 cm (2.75 inches) as measured from the floor or 5 cm (2 inches) as measured from the top of the clamp bracket.

    If you do not have something with which to measure the anchor stud, look straight into the opening on the right side of the dock assembly. If you cannot see the top of the stud, the stud is too tall and might need to be cut.

  7. If the height of the stud does NOT exceed 7 cm (2.75 inches) as measured from the floor, go to Removing the dock from the magnet room.

    If the height exceeds 7 cm (2.75 inches) as measured from the floor:

    • Loosen the stud anchor nut with a nonferrous wrench and unscrew the nut a few turns. If the nut and washers are in the way, remove them.
    • Try to remove the anchor stud with your fingers. Do not use any tools, because this could damage the anchoring system.
    • If you cannot remove the anchor stud, try to lower the anchor stud by turning the stud to make it shorter. Use only your fingers to turn the screw, because a tool might damage the anchoring system.

    • If you are able to remove or lower the stud, go to Removing the dock from the magnet room.

    • If you cannot remove the stud or lower the stud, order the Magnet Dock Bolt Cutting Kit (5435943) and go to Cutting the anchor stud.

Cutting the anchor stud

Procedure

  1. Notice
    Make sure to wear gloves during the cutting procedure.
    At the rear of the dock, use a 3/4 inch nonferrous wrench to LOOSEN (not remove) four nuts (two on each side) from the dock bracket bolts.
  2. Loosen the nut on the stud anchor, if not already loosened.
  3. Push the dock assembly as far to the left as possible, putting the stud anchor close to the opening. The stud needs to be as accessible as possible to ease the cutting procedure.
  4. Retighten the four nuts on the bolts attaching the dock assembly to the magnet, and also retighten the nut on the stud anchor. This prevents any movement of the dock assembly during the cutting procedure.
  5. The stud removal kit has a selection of different nuts. Select a spare nut of the same dimension and threading as the anchor stud. Attach it to the stud on top of the original nut. The top of the second nut is used as the cutting mark.

    If you do not have a spare nut that fits, remove the anchor stud nut, remove the washers, and thread the nut completely to the base of the stud. Then cut a small hole in the tack cloth and fit it over the stud. Put all the washers from the kit on the stud, over the tack cloth, as shown in Figure 5, to achieve the height of the additional nut.

  6. Cut a small hole in the center of a tack cloth square and fit it over the top of the stud. The tack cloth will catch and retain the metal filings as the stud is cut.
  7. Insert a second tack cloth square into the entrance, covering as much of the area as you can. These pieces of tack cloth will help trap any metal filings.
  8. Warning
    Possible personal injury
    This procedure requires movement of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
    Two field engineers are required at all times.
    CAUTION
    Ferrous material hazard. Risk of equipment damage or personal injury.
    The hacksaw blade is ferrous.
    Do all adjustments and blade replacements of the hacksaw outside the magnet room. Make sure that the blade is secure in the hacksaw handle BEFORE entering the magnet room. Make sure that the wrist strap is secured on the wrist before entering the magnet room.
    Notice
    Possible equipment damage
    Cutting the stud creates metal filings.
    As you cut the stud, avoid removing the blade to prevent metal filings escaping the hole. If you need to remove the blade, carefully use a clean piece of tack cloth to wipe the blade as you remove it from the hole.
    Note:

    If the blade on the hacksaw becomes dull during the cutting process, leave the magnet room, disassemble the hacksaw, and flip the blade so a new set of teeth are now cutting the stud.

    Make sure that the end of the blade extends about 3.8 cm (1.5 inches) beyond the top edge of the hacksaw. This is the portion of the blade that you will use to cut the anchor stud.
  9. Cut the stud just above the top nut.
  10. Carefully remove the pieces of tack cloth. Roll them up to contain any loose metal filings.
  11. Use a clean piece of tack cloth to wipe the area, removing any metal filings that might be present.
  12. Remove the cut end of the stud and the soiled tack cloth from the magnet room.
  13. Remove the second nut.

    If you used the washers from the kit, remove the washers and discard them. Place the original washer on the anchor stud and reattach the nut.

Removing the dock from the magnet room

Procedure

  1. Note: Remove only the nuts from the rear of the dock that connect it to the dock brackets. You do not have to remove the dock brackets from the magnet.
    At the rear of the dock, use a 3/4 inch nonferrous wrench to remove four nuts (two on each side) from the dock bracket bolts. Make note of the number of washers between the dock mounting bracket and the dock at each stud location.
  2. Remove the nut, washers, and clamp bracket from the anchor stud.

    If the site has followed the Pre-Installation Manual (PIM) and the anchor is a two-part assembly, remove the anchor stud (bolt) from the female sleeve anchor, using hand-force to turn the stud. This lets the dock remain flush with the floor during removal. This is the preferred method. If you can remove the anchor stud, go to Step 8.

    Otherwise, the dock must be lifted slightly to clear the anchor stud, as described below.

  3. Warning
    Risk of injury
    The dock is heavy, weighing more than 50 pounds, and it contains ferrous material, making it strongly attracted to the magnet.
    Two people are necessary to move the dock out of the magnet room to do maintenance. From this point in the procedure, downward pressure must be continuously applied to the dock to prevent it from being pulled into the magnet bore.
    Warning
    Possible personal injury
    To oppose any pull from the magnet, field engineers must apply continuous downward pressure to the top of the dock, even when lifting the dock in this step.
    Do not put any part of the human body in the path between the dock and the magnet.
    Applying pressure to the top of the dock, completely remove all dock attachment bolts, then remove washers.

    Make a note of the number of washers between the dock and the bracket, because the washers will need to be reinstalled in the same quantities and locations.

    See Figure 4 for location of washers and Figure 9 for position of field service personnel when removing hardware.

    Note:

    Remove only the nuts from the rear of the dock that connect it to the dock brackets. Do NOT remove the dock brackets from the magnet.

  4. Use two people to pull the dock away from the magnet and hold down the dock assembly. Move the dock until the stud in the front is hitting the inner frame (labeled as 1 in Figure 10).
  5. Warning
    Possible personal injury
    The dock contains highly ferrous material.
    Never lift the front of the dock (the portion of the dock farthest from the magnet) more than 7.6 cm (3 inches) from the floor.
    The rear of the dock (the portion of the dock closest to the magnet) should always remain in contact with the floor. Never lift the rear of the dock off the floor in the magnet room.
    Use two people to lift the front of the dock JUST ENOUGH to clear the top of the stud in the floor (labeled as 2 in Figure 10). Avoid scraping the dock assembly or the base of the magnet.

    Do not lift the front of the dock more than 7.6 cm (3 inches) from the floor. The rear of the dock must remain in contact with the floor.

  6. Use two people to move the dock to the left, just beyond the anchor stud (labeled as 3 in Figure 10).
  7. Use two people to lower the dock back on the floor immediately to the side of the anchor stud (labeled as 4 in Figure 10).
  8. With two field engineers, one holding each side of the dock, slowly and deliberately slide the dock away from the magnet, keeping downward pressure on the top of the dock assembly. Use care as the loose cables will be trailing behind the dock. Move the dock to a distance equivalent to where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.
  9. When the distance between the dock and the magnet is at least equivalent to the distance between the magnet and where the foot of the patient table would normally be located, both field engineers may slowly lift the dock from the floor and exit the room.
  10. CAUTION
    Possible personal injury
    The exposed stud poses a trip hazard.
    Put a cone or similar item over the anchor stud (if not removable) to indicate a potential trip hazard.
    Make sure to wear gloves during this cleaning procedure.

    If the anchor stud was cut, return to the magnet room with a piece of tack cloth and clean the floor area where the dock was to remove any metal filings that may be present.

    Also, clean the bottom of the dock assembly and the area of the anchor stud to remove any remaining metal filings before returning it to the magnet room.

Reinstalling the dock

Procedure

  1. Warning
    Possible personal injury
    This procedure requires movement of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
    Two field engineers are required at all times.
    Note:

    Any time you remove the dock from the magnet, you must check levelness and alignment of the dock and patient table when you reattach the dock to the magnet.

    With two field engineers, one holding each side of the dock, enter the magnet room and put the dock on the floor where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.
  2. With two field engineers, one applying vertical downward pressure to each side of the dock, slide the dock toward the magnet front.
  3. Put the dock in position to clear the floor stud. There are two ways to do this:
    1. If the site has followed the Pre-Installation Manual (PIM), then when reinstalling the dock it should be possible to remove the top portion of the stud. If this is the case, then continue to maintain pressure on the top of the dock and carefully slide the dock against the base of the magnet. Reinstall the top portion of the anchor stud.
    2. If the site has an anchor stud that protrudes vertically from the floor and cannot be removed, a non-conformance MUST be raised with the customer and the anchor must be fixed to comply with the Pre-Installation Manual (PIM) before any future servicing of the dock. To complete the task this time, follow these instructions per the reverse directions shown in Figure 12.
      1. While maintaining pressure on the top of the dock, position the dock against the base of the magnet to the side of the floor stud at the front of the dock (labeled as 3).
      2. Lift the dock just enough to clear the top of the stud in the floor (labeled as 2).
      3. While rotating the dock, align the hole at the base of the dock with the stud and put the dock down into position and re-anchor the dock (labeled as 1).
  4. Reattach the nuts and washers to the dock bracket.
    Note:

    Take care with the washers that are placed between the dock and the bracket. Make sure that the same number of washers is installed behind the dock on each bolt.

  5. While putting continuous downward force on the top of the dock assembly, reconnect all cables from rear of dock to the magnet I/O panels and the rear pedestal.
  6. Replace the front, bridge, and side covers in reverse order of removal.
  7. Dock the patient table.
  8. Remove LOTO from the RF amplifier and PEN cabinet (magnet room). Refer to the MR Service Safety Manual (5452735).

Finalization

Procedure

  1. Do all finalization steps required in other referenced procedures.
  2. Make sure that the dock motor on switch operates properly when the table UP pedal is pressed.
  3. Do dock adjustments. See Centering the dock and adjusting the dock hook tension. Make sure that the dock and table are properly aligned and centered.
  4. Make sure that cradle travel into the bore is smooth.
  5. Do Laser light alignment.
  6. Do Doing DQA calibration.
  7. Do Doing a check scan.
