AMT Imaging Solutions — Field Service Quick Reference

Applies to: GE Signa Excite (9x–15x), HDxt, Discovery MR450/750, Optima 450w/750w. Covers Error E203 (Shim power supply fault), shim system diagnostics, and image uniformity troubleshooting.

Error E203 — Shim Power Supply Fault

CheckAction
Shim Power Supply (SPS) StatusCheck the shim power supply located in the electronics cabinet. Verify status LEDs — fault LED on = SPS self-test failed. Measure output voltages on the SPS at the designated test points (typically ±15V, ±5V, and the shim channel output rails). Compare to service manual specifications.
Shim Power Supply FusesInspect SPS fuses — blown fuses are a common cause of E203 after power events. Replace only with OEM-specified fuse rating. Do not increase fuse rating as this masks underlying overcurrent conditions.
Shim Coil ContinuityRun shim coil test: MR Service Desktop → Diagnostics → System Function → Magnet → Shim Test. This tests X, Y, Z, and higher-order shim coil channels. An open or shorted channel isolates the fault to a specific shim channel or its driver output.
Shim Cable ConnectionsInspect shim coil cable connections at the magnet feedthrough and at the SPS. These are low-voltage DC connections but poor contact causes channel faults. Reseat all shim cable connectors.
Shim Channel CardsOn systems with individual shim channel driver cards, check each card for fault indicators. Swap or replace a suspected failed card. Run shim test after any card replacement.
Thermal StateE203 on a warm system after extended scanning may indicate thermal protection triggering on the SPS or shim driver cards. Allow system to cool. Check SPS ventilation — clean fans and filters. Run shim test after cooldown.

Shim-Related Image Quality Issues

Shim problems don't always generate hard faults. Degraded shim performance causes subtle to severe image quality degradation — particularly in fat suppression, EPI, and spectroscopy sequences.
SymptomLikely CauseDiagnosis / Fix
Poor fat suppression (inhomogeneous, patchy)B0 field non-uniformity — shim degraded or B0 disturbed by environment changeRun auto-shim with calibration phantom: Service Desktop → Magnet → Auto Shim. Verify center frequency and shim current settings. Check if ferromagnetic items have been moved near the scanner.
EPI images have geometric distortion / ghostingB0 inhomogeneity severely affects EPI — shim dependentRun field map and assess B0 uniformity. Re-shim the system. EPI is extremely sensitive to even small B0 deviations — verify shim quality with dedicated EPI test sequence.
Image uniformity poor — bright center / dark edges or vice versaB1 inhomogeneity (RF) — not shim; or shim if low-orderDifferentiate: run phantom scan. If non-uniformity follows phase encode direction = shim. If non-uniformity is circular or radial = RF (body coil issue). Address accordingly.
Chemical shift artifacts worse than usualCenter frequency drift combined with shim degradationPerform center frequency calibration. Re-run auto-shim. Verify shim holds stable after calibration (check frequency over 30 minutes with phantom).
Spectroscopy (MRS) linewidth too wideB0 homogeneity insufficient — shim inadequate or failed channelRun shim coil test to identify failed channels. Re-shim with spectroscopy-optimized protocol (full-volume manual or automated shim). Linewidth spec for 1H MRS: typically <10 Hz for clinical, <5 Hz for research.
Shim currents much higher than baselineEnvironmental change (new ferromagnetic object near magnet); magnet driftSurvey scan room and adjacent areas for new ferromagnetic installations (new equipment, plumbing, structural work). Compare shim current values to installation baseline. Contact GE if drift is unexplained.

Auto-Shim Procedure (Field)

StepNotes
1. Place calibration phantom at isocenterUse the GE spherical calibration phantom — center with alignment lasers. Allow phantom to equilibrate to room temperature (30 min minimum).
2. Run center frequency calibrationMR Service Desktop → Diagnostics → Magnet → Frequency. System must lock center frequency before shimming.
3. Run Auto ShimMR Service Desktop → Diagnostics → Magnet → Auto Shim. System performs field mapping and calculates optimal shim currents. Process takes 5–15 minutes.
4. Verify shim resultReview field homogeneity report. Residual field deviation should be within spec (<1–2 ppm typical for clinical). If not, run a second auto-shim pass.
5. Scan verificationRun a clinical test scan (fat-suppressed sequence is a good indicator). Verify fat suppression is uniform across the FOV.
6. Document shim currentsRecord X, Y, Z, and higher-order shim current values. Compare to installation baseline and previous PM values. Large changes warrant investigation.

Tip: Shim current drift over years is normal as the magnet ages and building materials settle. Trending the auto-shim current values at each annual PM lets you catch gradual drift before it degrades image quality clinically.
Tip: Before assuming a shim hardware fault, always check the environment. A new MRI-incompatible item moved into or near the scan room (even in an adjacent hallway) can dramatically shift the B0 field and make auto-shim fail or produce poor results.
