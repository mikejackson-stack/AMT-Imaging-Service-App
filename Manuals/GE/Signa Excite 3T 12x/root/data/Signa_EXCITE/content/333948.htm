3.3  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD !!
Fatal electrical voltages are present when the patient handling power supply is energized.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PHPS BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

4  Procedure

4.1  Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) Replacement Procedure (Part Number 5109368)

  1. Disconnect the AC power plug. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  PHPS Equipment Room View

  1. Disconnect the cable connections on both the equipment side and the magnet side of the penetration panel. See Illustration 1 and Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  PHPS Magnet Room View

  1. Loosen the screws that connect the PHPS to the equipment side of the penetration panel.

  1. When all of the screws have been loosened, carefully pull the PHPS through the penetration panel cut-out on the equipment side.

  1. Re-insert the new PHPS into the penetration panel cut-out and secure the PHPS to the penetration panel.

  1. Reconnect the cable connections on both the equipment side and the magnet side of the penetration panel.

  1. Reconnect the AC power plug.

4.2  Magnet Room Power Supply Module Replacement Procedure (Part Number 5110906)

  1. Disconnect the AC power plug.

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the front of the PHPS assembly.

  1. Loosen the screws that connect the front face plate to the PHPS assembly. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  PHPS Front Face Plate View

  1. After the face plate is loose, disconnect the two harness’ attached to the front cover and set the cover aside. The magnet power supply is located on the right.

  1. Remove J3 located at the bottom of the power supply. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  PHPS Front Power Supply View

  1. Loosen the bottom screw that holds the power supply in place.

  1. Unplug the 3 harness connections and the power plug from the right side of the power supply.

    NOTE:There are cut-outs in the shell side that allow the connectors to be brought out of the shell for disconnecting the harness’. See Illustration 4.

  1. Partially remove the power supply until the grounding screw hole located at the top of the power supply is visible. Loosen the grounding screw and remove the ground wire. See Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  PHPS Top Power Supply View

  1. Remove the power supply and disconnect the remaining two connectors, J1 and J2 attached at the top of the power supply.

  1. Remove the plastic cover making note of the screw locations as the plastic cover will be re-used with the new power supply.

  1. To install the new power supply, reverse the removal steps. Attach the plastic cover to the replacement power supply.

  1. Reconnect the two power connectors J1 and J2 located at the top of the power supply. Note: The connectors are keyed and a diagram of the power supply connections can be found on the side of the power supply for reference. See Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  PHPS Side Power Supply View

  1. Partially slide the power supply into the slot until the grounding cable can be re-attached.

    NOTE:Make sure all harness’ are located to the right of the power supply before sliding in the power supply.

  1. Attach the ground wire.

  1. Slide the power supply fully into the slot. Reconnect the three harness connections and the power plug and push the harness’ into the shell on the right side. Note: There are cut-outs in the shell side that allow the cables to be placed inside the shell.

  1. Tighten the bottom screw.

  1. Reconnect the J3 cable to the bottom of the power supply.

  1. Reconnect the two face plate harness.

  1. Re-attach the face plate cover to the PHPS assembly.

    NOTE:Try to attach the four center screws before attaching the outside screws. Caution, keep the test point wires from getting pinched when the face plate is being re-attached.

  1. Reconnect the cables attached to the font of the PHPS assembly.

4.3  Table Power Supply Module Replacement Procedure (Part Number 5110909)

  1. Disconnect the AC power plug.

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the front of the PHPS assembly.

  1. Loosen the screws that connect the front face plate to the PHPS assembly. See Illustration 3.

  1. After the face plate is loose, disconnect the two harness’ attached to the front cover and set the cover aside. The table power supply is located on the left.

  1. Remove J5 located at the bottom of the power supply. See Illustration 4.

  1. Loosen the bottom screw that holds the power supply in place.

  1. Unplug the 2 harness connections and the power plug from the left side of the power supply.

    NOTE:There are cut-outs in the shell side that allow the connectors to be brought out of the shell for disconnecting the harness’. See Illustration 4.

  1. Partially remove the power supply until the grounding screw hole located at the top of the power supply is visible. Loosen the grounding screw and remove the ground wire. See Illustration 5.

  1. Remove the power supply and disconnect the remaining connector, J4 attached at the top of the power supply.

  1. Remove the plastic cover making note of the screw locations as the plastic cover will be re-used with the new power supply.

  1. To install the new power supply, reverse the removal steps. Attach the plastic cover to the replacement power supply.

  1. Re-connect the power connector J4 located at the top of the power supply. Note: The connectors are keyed and a diagram of the power supply connections can be found on the side of the power supply for reference.

  1. Partially slide the power supply into the slot until the grounding cable can be re-attached. Note: Make sure all harness’ are located to the left of the power supply before sliding in the power supply.

  1. Attach the ground wire.

  1. Slide the power supply fully into the slot. Reconnect the two harness connections and the power plug and push the harness’ into the shell on the left side. Note: There are cut-outs in the shell side that allow the cables to be placed inside the shell.

  1. Tighten the bottom screw.

  1. Reconnect the J5 cable to the bottom of the power supply.

  1. Reconnect the two face plate harness connections.

  1. Re-attach the face plate cover to the PHPS assembly.

    NOTE:Try to attach the four center screws before attaching the outside screws. Caution, keep the test point wires from getting pinched when the face plate is being re-attached.

  1. Reconnect the cables attached to the font of the PHPS assembly.

5  Finalization

  1. Test the patient handling system for proper operation.
