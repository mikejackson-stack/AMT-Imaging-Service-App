Start and Shut Down Linux Computer

  Safety

NOTICE
THERE CAN BE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE IF SHUT DOWN PROCEDURE IS NOT FOLLOWED. COLD BOOTING CAN CAUSE IRREPARABLE DATA LOSSES. ENSURE THAT YOU HAVE SYSTEMATICALLY SHUT DOWN THE MACHINE.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   System Shutdown Procedure

NOTE:Follow these steps in the exact order to shutdown the machine

  1. Right-click the mouse on your service desktop. See Illustration 1

    (Step Details)

  1. Right click with the mouse to Open the Root Menu. Select Service Tools > System shutdown. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

  1. You will now see a pop-up asking you to confirm shutdown. Click on “[Yes]” if you want to shutdown. See Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. The system will close all processing and automatically turns off the power to the computer.

1.2   Bringing the system up from Power-down condition

  1. To bring the system up from a power down condition, press the Power button on the CPU. See Illustration 4

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot the software as shown in System Restart.

1.3   System Restart

  1. Go to the Service Desktop, see Illustration 1.

  1. Click on the [System Restart] button from the service desktop manager. See Illustration 5

    (Step Details)

  1. You will now see a pop-up which prompts you to confirm the restart. Click on [YES] to confirm. See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. The system will now restart, which will take approximately 10 minutes to complete.

  1. As the software restarts, it will stop on a login screen. To bring the scanner up to Signa scanning level, the login default is:

    (Step Details)

  1. As it comes back to Signa, a window prompts you to approach the service representative if the system is not activated. See Illustration 8. If you do not see the system restarting in few minutes, please contact your service representative.

    (Step Details)
