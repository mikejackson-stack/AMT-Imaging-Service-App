2  Overview

This procedure is intended to be performed after a Mobile MR System move, at the beginning of the day, or any other time shim is suspected of affecting image quality.

NOTE:Foreign material (such as staples, metal filings, dirt, etc.) on the LVshim phantom or nesting plate will alter shim harmonics, resulting in shim divergence. Therefore, before using the phantom and nesting plate, be sure that all foreign material has been cleaned off.
