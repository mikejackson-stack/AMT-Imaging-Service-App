1  Procedure

  1. At the Operator WorkSpace go to the Service Desktop and open the Service Browser, if it's not already running.

  1. To run the LVShim tool from the Service Browser, follow the instructions on Illustration 1 below:

    (Step Details)

  1. For TwinSpeed, highlight WHOLE and click [OK] Proceed to Table 1 to for help using the LVShim Analysis tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. If a new bandwidth is suggested by the software, perform the following:

    1. Select the scan icon in the desktop control panel to redisplay the scan window.

    1. Under [Research Operations], select [Display CV’s].

    1. In the Display CV’s window, enter the new bandwidth value for CV opuser2. Click [Accept].

    1. Under [Research Operations] select Download.

      (Step Details)

  1. If performing gradient shimming, go to Step 7 (the new currents are automatically entered into the gradient amplifiers by the software).

    (Step Details)

  1. For all other types of shimming, enter the new currents at the appropriate shim power supply, then go to step 5. (Refer to Shim Power Supply Details, for more information about entering new shim currents.)

  1. Click [Scan].

  1. When the scan is completed, repeat the LVshim Analysis procedure.
