2  Overview

The body and Head RFT loopback Baseline Data Tests have been designed to quickly evaluate stability , magnitude and phase linearity of the RF transmit chain in a Signa system. The tests should be run in the following order:

  • TPS Loopback Baseline Data Test

  • Body Dummy Load Baseline Data Test

  • Head Dummy Load Data Test

  • The Receive Path is not verified in this procedure.

Also See:

Additional troubleshooting - Hybrid Splitter and Body Coil

4  Procedure

4.1  Disabling the UTNS3

NOTE:Before running any RFT test, disable the (U)TNS(3)

  1. Open a C-Shell

  1. Type: gedit/w/config/mgd_stage

  1. (If not present) ADD the following letter to the top line: L (Upper case “L”)

  1. Select: [File, Save]

  1. Select: [File, Exit] and close the C-Shell

  1. Type: exit twice to exit the C- Shell

  1. Reset TPS

4.2  Disabling the TR Error Condition in the Driver Module

  1. Verify that the system is idle and all coils have been removed from magnet bore.

  1. Remove the front cover from the EXCITE Systems Cabinet

  1. On the front of the Driver Module, disable T/R error reporting by setting the TR switch to the DIS (disable faults) position. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Driver Module Switch

4.3  Shutting Down Vacuum Pump (Disable Quiet Technology for sustems with TRM)

This section need only be done if the system has a TRM coil or TAC cabinet. (Eclipse and Eclipse with Excite technology do not have a TAC or TRM coil. Therefore this section should only be skipped for those systems).

  1. Remove the front cover from Twinspeed Accessory Cabinet (TAC) and place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near where the power cord connects) in the PFF position, OR remove the power cord from the pump.

    Illustration 2:  Vacuum Pump Inside Twinspeed Accessory Cabinet

  1. Remove the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 on the rear if the TAC and slowly open the vacuum line connection at J11. allow the vacuum to slowly equalize the vacuum space inside magnet bore to atmospheric pressure. The vacuum pump must be powered off and not run while this is done.

    NOTE:The vacuum space inside the magnet bore must be at atmospheric pressure when this test is run. Running the test with the bore under vacuum will result in magnitude and phase stability plots that exhibit a sloping, sometimes saw - tooth pattern.

4.4  TPS Loopback Baseline Data Test

Essentially this test is a “Transmit/Receive Electronics Loopback” for the transmit and Receive chain components in the MGD chassis only. It verifies the proper function of the Exciter and Receiver. The TPS Loopback Baseline Data Test is performed in the Body mode. Results from this test are referenced automatically by subsequent RFT tests; therefore this test MUST be performed first.

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! You MUST connect switchable attenuator BEFORE connecting sense loop to prevent damage to TPS receiver board. Also, leave switch S1 (Preamp bias) on switchable attenuator in “out” (Default) position or the internal protective DC blocker is bypassed. (With S1 out, DSI “Bias On” led should be off after switchable attenuator is connected.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE POSSIBILITY! The switchable attenuator contains ferrous switch relays. Do not take the switchable attenuator into the magnet room; doing so can cause damage to the switch relays.
 
 

NOTICE
EQUIPMENT DAMAGE POSSIBILITY! All phantoms and coils should be removed from the bore.
 
 

NOTE:On some switchable attenuators: DS1 should always be OFF. If necessary, push in S1, then remove J5 power to restore the DS1 to OFF (DS1 latches).

NOTE:As used in this procedure, the switchable attenuator attenuates by 30 dB. It also contains a DC block.

  1. Disconnect the BNC cables on J1 and J2 at the rear bulkhead of the System cabinet,

  1. To Proceed:

    1. If you are JUST performing the TPS Loopback Baseline Test and are not going to any other RF Procedures Refer to Illustration 3.

    1. If your intent is to do this entire RFT Procedure, Install the switchable attenuator, 46-288458G1, at the reat of the systems Cabinet as shown in illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  TPS Loopback Setup

5  Finalization

  1. Upon completion of RFT the following equipment must be restored to full operation, if it was put in service mode;

    • UTNS3

    • TR Error Reporting (Driver Module)

    • Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology) in TAC. (Eclipse systems can skip this section)

    • Perform a TPS reset when system is back into normal

  1. Enabling the UTNS3:

    1. Open a C-Shell

    1. Type: gedit/w/config/mgd_stage

    1. Remove the following letter from the top line: L (Upper case “L”).

    1. Select: [File], [Save]

    1. Select: [File], [Exit] and close the shell

    1. Type: exit twice, to exit the C-Shell

    1. Reset TPS

  1. Re-enabling TR Error Reporting (Driver Module)

    NOTE:Possible equipment damage. Do not turn connect/disconnect Wattmeter Sensor while Meter is ON. Always turn meter OFF prior to plugging/unplugging Sensor.

  1. If you remove the Body of Head Heliax, verify scanner is in “idle”, turn off RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O Board) or disconnect J14 from the back of the RF amplifier.

    1. Disconnect the RFT Hardware and cables from the system.

    1. Connect the Original Body Output Cable to RF Amplifier, J4, OR connect the Original Head Output Cable to RF Amplifier, J3.

    1. Re-enable RF Enable at the Exciter (FP-I/O Board) or reattach the RD Drive Cable to J14 at the rear of the RF ampllifier.

  1. Re-enable TR error reporting by enabling the TR switch on the front of the Driver Module. See Illustration 8.

    Illustration 8:  Driver Module Switch

  1. Replace all covers.

  1. Perform a Reset of the TPS.

    NOTE:If an RFT scan is not run and only RFT prescan is run, it is necessary to perform a TPS Reset before attempting to perform clinical or service scans. failure to do this will result in receiving signal loss and autoprescan error when clinical or service scanning is attempted.

  1. Enabling the Vacuum Pump (Enable Quiet Technology for Systems with TRM). This section need only be done if the system has a TRM coil or TAC cabinet. (Eclipse and eclipse with Excite Technology do not have a TAC or TRM coil. Therefore this section should be skipped for those systems).

  1. If you disabled the Vacuum Pump (Quiet Technology) it must be restored now.

  1. If not already removed, remove the front cover from the Twinspeed Accessory Cabinet (TAC) and place the power switch on the right side of the vacuum pump (near where the power cord connects) in the ON position, OR plug the power cord back in.

    Illustration 9:  Vacuum Pump Inside Twinspeed Accessory Cabinet

  1. Restore the chain from around the vacuum line at J11 on the rear of the TAC.
