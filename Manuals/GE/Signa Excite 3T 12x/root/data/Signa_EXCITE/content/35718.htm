4  Procedure

  1. Perform a 12-plane, 500 Hz, LVshim scan. For TwinSpeed, use GradMode=WHOLE.

    Click here if you need to see the basic LVShim scan protocol . Hint: right click and select “Open in new window” if you would like the scan protocol not to replace the page you are currently viewing.

  1. After the scan is complete, go to the Service Desktop and open the Service Browser if it's not already running.

  1. Start the LVShim tools as shown in .

    Illustration 1:  STARTING THE LVSHIM TOOL FROM THE SERVICE BROWSER

  1. TwinSpeed, highlight WHOLE and click on OK.

  1. Continue as follows:

  1. Install passive shims per the LVshim analysis.

  1. Re-enter S/C shim currents from last iteration.

  1. Perform another LVshim scan, analyze the results, and perform another passive shim iteration if analysis does not meet specifications.
