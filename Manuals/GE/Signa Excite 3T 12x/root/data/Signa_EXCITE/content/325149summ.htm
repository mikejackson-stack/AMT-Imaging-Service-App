UTNS RF Splitter Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Make sure scanner is IDLE by checking the scan desktop and seeing IDLE in scan square (not prescan or scanning).

  1. Remove 3 BNC cables connected to UTNS RF Splitter in back of Systems or RFS cabinet. Note location (1, 2, 3 of cable connections). See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove tiewraps connecting UTNS RF Splitter to cabinet.

  1. Attach 3 BNC cables to new UTNS RF Splitter and tiewrap to cabinet.

2  Finalization

  1. Reset TPS

  1. Run a head or body scan to make sure the system is operational.
