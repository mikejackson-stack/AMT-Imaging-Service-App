Configuring System to Run On One Reflex 400/800 PCB

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

NOTICE
Be sure to remove power from the MGD Chassis before removing any modules to avoid damage to the circuits.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Check that the AP boards are recognized by the system by checking for their presents in the HART logs. Check the system via the service browser. (Go to Service browser – Error log – HART Tools – HART logs and see that both the pcbs are recognized by the system). See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. We can also verify the system can see the AP boards from MGD Diagnostics. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Note that both PCBs in the MGD slot 2 and 3 are present. (The “Slave” PCB, in spite of being defective, may be recognized by the system)

  1. Stop all scans in the system.

  1. Switch off the Power at the rear of the cabinet shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the PCB in slot 2 – This is the defective one

  1. Remove PCB from Slot 3 as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the piggyback PCB from the main PCB as shown in Illustration 5 by removing the five Philips head screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Change the address switch setting from “3” to “0” as shown below in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Put back the Piggyback PCB back in to the main PCB.

  1. Slide the PCB (pulled out from Slot 3 and reconfigured address switch settings) into Slot 2 of the MGD Chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Return power to the MGD Chassis.

  1. Perform a [TPS Reset] from the Service Desktop.

  1. Go to Service browser – Error log – HART Tools – HART logs and to verify that the PCB is present in the slot 2.

  1. In the Service Browser on the Service Desktop, Go to the Diagnostics – MGD chassis – AP Analysis Check and Press the Run icon to confirm that one PCB is present.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Perform a TPS reset, if required.

  1. Do a checkout scan to verify the system is performing optimally.
