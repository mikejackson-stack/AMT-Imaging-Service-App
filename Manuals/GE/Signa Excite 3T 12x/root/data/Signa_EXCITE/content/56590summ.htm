Gradient Amplfier Power Supply Control Board Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
POSSIBLE 800 VOLTS ON CAPACITORS.
WAIT AT LEAST 5 MINUTES AFTER UNIT HAS BEEN TURNED OFF BEFORE SERVICING. TO PREVENT ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

1  Procedure

  1. Perform the Lockout Tag Out Procedure For The Gradient Cabinet.

  1. Note the location of the Gradient Amplfier Module (Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Put on the ESD Grounding Strap.

  1. Loosen the captive screws on the Gradient Amplfier Control Board faceplate. Remove the board by simultaneously pressing up on the upper ejector and down on the lower ejector on the faceplate. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Control Board.

  1. Remove the replacement Gradient Amplfier Control Board (P/N 2250375) from its anti-static bag.

  1. Install new Gradient Amplfier Control Board making sure the board is fully seated.

  1. Tighten captive screws.

2  Finalization

  1. Remove the Lock Out Tag Out from the Gradient PDU Breakers.

  1. Place the Gradient Breakers in the ON position, restoring power to the Gradient modules.

  1. Verify that power has been restored to the cabinet. The LEDs on the Gradient Amplfiers & the Gradient Amplfier-PS will be illuminated.

  1. Run Digital DC Offsets:

    1. Select [Diagnostics] from the Service Desktop and select [Start].

    1. Select [IPG], [Manual], [Digital DC Offset], then [Close].

  1. Perform the Gradient (GRADCAL).

  1. HFD Gradients and ACGD Gradients use different Control Boards. To ensure the Control Board replaced into the system is the correct board for that system, run Gradient Diagnostics Fidelity Tests. See the Gradient Diagnostics section of Peripheral Diagnostics for help.

    (Step Details)

  1. If no errors are reported by the diagnostics, run a test scan and again check for errors.
