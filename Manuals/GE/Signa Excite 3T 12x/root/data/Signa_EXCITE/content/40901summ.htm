Setting System Time and Date

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Set the time and date.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the Time Zone your system is located in, then push the [Set Time Zone] button.

  1. Select the date and local time, then push the [Set Time/Date] button.

2  Finalization

  1. Insure the Date and Time is correct for the system.
