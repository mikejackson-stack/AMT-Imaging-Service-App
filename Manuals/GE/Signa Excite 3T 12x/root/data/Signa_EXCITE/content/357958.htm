Table Up/Down Check From Table Pedal

  1. Press the Table Down pedal indicated by the number 4 in Illustration 1. Check that table goes down in 20 seconds or less.

    If it takes more than 20 seconds for the table, check hydraulic fluid and hoses. Possible problems are dirty filter or obstruction in hydraulic line.

  2. Press the Table Up pedal indicated by the number 3 in Illustration 1. Verify that the table goes up in less than 14 pedal strokes. If the table takes more than 14 strokes to raise, the necessary adjustments can be found in Dock Pedal Stroke Adjustment.
