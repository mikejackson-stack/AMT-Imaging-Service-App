Body Hybrid Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Shock Hazard!
high bias voltages are present at the Hybrid/combiner during normal signa operation.
Remove power, lock-out and tag-out (LOTO) the RF cabinet before accessing the hybrid/combiner.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interact with the module making difficult for one person to handle.
Always remove and/or replace the Body Hybrid Module from the rear pedestal with two (2) people keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it.

1  Procedure

1.1   Body Hybrid Splitter Removal

  1. Ensure the scanner is idle. Select [End Exam] if the scanner is at any portion of a scan prescription.

  1. Disable the output of the RF Amplifier using the switch on the Exciter, or disconnecting J14 at the rear of the RF Amplifier.

  1. Remove DD voltage by turning off the Driver Module and switching Off the Systems Cabinet breaker on the PDU. Follow OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.

  1. Remove the two lower covers from the rear bridge assembly and locate the Hybrid Splitter shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the DD BNC.

  1. Disconnect the Heliax Cables.

  1. Slide the Hybrid Splitter out of its saddle.

  1. While carrying the unit from the Magnet Room stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. Once outside of the Magnet Room, remove the two Preamps/Combiner and Notch Filter shown below. (They will be reinstalled on the new Body Hybrid assembly.)

    (Step Details)

1.2   Body Hybrid Splitter Installation

  1. Attach the existing Preamps/Combiner and Notch Filter to the new Hybrid Splitter assembly.

  1. Attach the cables to the Preamps as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. While moving the Body Hybrid Splitter to the rear of the magnet, maintain maximum distance from magnet at all times.

  1. Slide the New Hybrid Splitter into it saddle.

  1. Attach the DD Bias Cable.

  1. Attach the cables going to the body coil.

  1. Attach the Heliax Cables.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace all removed covers.

  1. Reapply power to the system, remove all LOTO equipment.

  1. Perform a TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Complete a Body and Head scan to make sure the system is functional.
