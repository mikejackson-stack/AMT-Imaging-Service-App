Longitudinal Drive Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Dangerous Magnetic Field.
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY AND/OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE! TOOLS MADE OF FERROUS OR MAGNETIC MATERIAL MAY BECOME DANGEROUS PROJECTILES AND CAUSE EQUIPMENT DAMAGE OR BODILY INJURY WHEN USED NEAR THE MAGNET.
USE ONLY A NONFERROUS SCALE FOR THIS PROCEDURE.

1  Procedure

1.1   LONGITUDINAL DRIVE CALIBRATION

NOTE:Longitudinal Drive Calibration must be performed before the Electrical Isocenter Calibration procedure.

  1. During Load From Cold, Begin of cradle travel should have been set to 50 and End of travel set to 2550. This procedure assumes this starting point. If values are different, change them back to 50 and 2550, and download the TPS/ISE before continuing, procedure for Configuration File.

  1. Move the cradle to the home position.

  1. Move the cradle approximately 10 cm into the bore.

  1. Turn on the alignment lights and press landmark to set the position display on the gantry to zero.

  1. Place a piece of tape on the cradle near the point farthest from the magnet and another piece aligned with it on the edge of the table. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Fast forward the cradle into the bore until the first piece of tape is located near the end of the table.

  1. Place a third piece of tape on the edge of the table aligned with the first piece that is on the cradle. There are now two pieces of tape on the table. The two pieces of tape are spaced apart the same distance that the cradle has moved. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the cradle position displayed on the gantry. This is the displayed distance.

  1. Measure the distance on the table between the second and third pieces of tape. This is the measured distance. Move the table away from the magnet if necessary to use a steel rule but DO NOT move the cradle. The display on the gantry should not change, (1" = 25.4 mm).

  1. The new End of cradle travel variable = [2500 (displayed distance) / (measured distance)] +50.

  1. Enter this new value in the configuration file.

  1. Download the TPS/ISE.

  1. After the download, the cradle travel distance displayed on the gantry will equal the measured distance. Illustration 2 shows table, cradle 10 cm from home position, bore of magnet, and the first two pieces of tape. Illustration 1 shows same as Illustration 2, but with end of cradle near the bore of the magnet and the addition of the third piece of tape.

1.2   Setting 2500 mm Beginning and End of Travel Parameters

  1. Locate service desktop manager and click on [C Shell].

  1. Edit the config file by typing cfe<Enter>

    (Step Details)

  1. Search file for 2500 mm travel parameters by scrolling down with the <Enter> button.

  1. Change the parameter beg Travel to 50 mm.

  1. Change the parameter end Travel to 2500 mm.

  1. Save the changes just made and quit the configuration file editor by typing <Ctrl>Q.

  1. Close the configuration file editor by typing <Ctrl>Q.

  1. [SYSTEM SHUTDOWN] to bring down the system.

1.3   Determine End Of Travel Calibration Constant

  1. [Reset TPS]

  1. After system has initialized, move the cradle into magnet until longitudinal display reads between 50 to 100 (mm); use IN FAST and IN SLOW buttons on either control switch board.

  1. Apply a piece of tape, aligned with the carriage pad edge, to the patient transport. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the scale along the patient transport and align a convenient reference point at the end of the scale to the tape edge (e.g., 10, or 800 mm). Record the scale point as A1 in Table 1. Record the shroud display reading as D1.

  1. Move the table to a point near the opposite end of the scale, at least 700 mm from the first point, using the IN FAST and IN SLOW buttons. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the scale reading as A2 and the position on the shroud display as D2 in Table 1.

  1. Calculate the actual distance travelled as DA = |(A2-A1)|, and record value in Table 1.

  1. Calculate the shroud distance as DD = |(D2-D1)|, and record value in Table 1. If DD differs from DA by 1 mm or less, no further calibration is needed. If DD differs from DA by more than 1 mm, continue with this procedure.

  1. Calculate the parameter End of 2500 mm Travel in Table 1 (round off calculated value to nearest mm).

1.4   Update Configuration File

  1. Edit the configuration file and using the instructions in LONGITUDINAL DRIVE CALIBRATION change the parameter end Travel to the calculated End of Travel value from Table 1 2500 mm.

    (Step Details)

  1. Note: Calculated End of 2500mm Travel is typically around 2570mm and cannot exceed 2600mm.

1.5   Check Calibration

  1. Repeat Setting 2500 mm Beginning and End of Travel Parameters , Setting 2500mm Beginning and End of Travel Parameters.

  1. DD should now equal DA within 1 mm; if it does not, repeat Setting 2500 mm Beginning and End of Travel Parameters and Determine End Of Travel Calibration Constant and recheck calibration.
