Patient Table Hydraulic Cylinder

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY IS LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

1  Procedure

1.1   Remove Hydraulic Cylinder

  1. Undock patient transport, and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove both outer covers by removing mounting screws (see Illustration 11).

  1. Remove both inner covers by removing mounting screws.

  1. Remove cradle assembly (see Illustration 12.

  1. Raise table to up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY IS LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Unhook lock bar spring, and lower bar to horizontal position, as shown in Illustration 1, then lower table until lock bar fully supports table top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove plug from patient transport (see Illustration 2).

  1. Remove retaining ring from end of adjustment screw.

  1. Remove retaining ring, washer, and Up Limit cable attachment pin. Then remove tubing and washer. All are in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Up Limit cable attachment pin that extends through hydraulic cylinder.

    (Step Details)

  1. Manually press hydraulic cylinder down until it is fully retracted.

  1. Disconnect two hydraulic lines from base of hydraulic cylinder, and fasten upward and aside to avoid excess spillage. Immediately fold and clamp the translucent (yellow) hose to prevent oil from draining from the reservoir (see Illustration 3).

  1. Remove retaining ring from either end of cylinder mounting pin at base of cylinder assembly (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cylinder mounting pin from base of hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Carefully move linkage to free actuation pin on Down valve (see Illustration 4).

  1. Remove hydraulic cylinder from base of table assembly.

1.2   Install Replacement Hydraulic Cylinder

  1. Prepare replacement cylinder for installation by pressing cylinder to its fully retracted position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully set cylinder in base of table, taking care not to bend actuation pin on Down valve.

  1. Insert cylinder mounting pin securing base of hydraulic cylinder to frame of table (see Illustration 3).

  1. Insert retaining ring.

  1. Connect two hydraulic lines to base of hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Insert adjustment screw into top of hydraulic cylinder (see Illustration 5).

  1. Insert lock washer on adjustment screw.

  1. Position hydraulic cylinder so that top of adjustment screw is aligned with hole in table (see Illustration 5).

  1. Pump up cylinder until table goes up slightly.

  1. Insert retaining ring at top of adjustment screw to secure cylinder to table (see Illustration 5).

  1. Insert Up Limit cable attachment pin through hole in hydraulic cylinder (see Illustration 5). Do not attach the cable at this time.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pump table up to the top of its travel.

  1. Attach washer and tubing to Up Limit cable attachment pin.

  1. Attach cable to end of Up Limit pin, and secure with washer and retaining ring. Adjust length if necessary to install, and to remove any slack.

  1. Ensure that lock bar is at its vertical (Unlocked) position as shown in Illustration 6, with the lock bar spring secured.

    (Step Details)

  1. Partially open bleed screw on hydraulic cylinder until no air is visible (see Illustration 7).

    (Step Details)

  1. Open Down valve and lower table to its lowest position.

  1. Hold Down valve open and exercise Up Pump foot pedal. This bleeds the hydraulic system by shunting air to the hydraulic reservoir.

  1. Bleed the cylinder again by partially opening bleed screw, when table is fully up, until no air is visible.

1.3   Final Calibration and Checks

  1. Refer to procedure for Table Top Height Adjustment; perform those procedures, then perform the following steps.

  1. Fill table reservoir so that fluid is 1/4 to 1/2 inch above full line when table is at its lowest position (see Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Check Down valve linkage adjustment as shown in Illustration 9. Obtain desired tolerance between Down valve linkage and bracket by adjusting two set screws on bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace plug in top of table (see Illustration 10).

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace inner and outer covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Illustration 11).

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace cradle assembly (see Illustration 12).

    (Step Details)
