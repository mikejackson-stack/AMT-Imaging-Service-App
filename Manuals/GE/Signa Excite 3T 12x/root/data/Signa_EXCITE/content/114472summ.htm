MNS Amplifier Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
Fatal voltages throughout the cabinet when entergized.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES. PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

CAUTION
Heavy Component.
The CPC Amplifier is heavy and will need a significant amount of balancing to remove it from the RF Accessories Cabinet.
Two individuals should perform this procedure.

1  Procedure

1.1   Lockout Tagout

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the OFF position:

    1. RF / Systems Cabinet

    1. RFI / Amp

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breaker switches, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE who will be working on the equipment.

  1. Test that power has dissipated for each item by:

    NOTICE
    There are extremely high voltages internal to the 3.0T RF Amplifier. There should be very few circumstances for which cover removal of the RF Amplifier is required. If you are not sure if you should remove the covers of the amplifier prior to troubleshooting, please contact support.
     
     

    1. Wait at least ten minutes after applying LOTO before removing any covers of the 3.0T RF Amplifier.

    1. If RF Deck (top) covers must be removed, be sure to discharge all energy from capacitors by touching one end of a thick, long neck screwdriver to the amplifier chassis lip and the other end to each of the capacitors and metal straps inside the RF Deck. See Illustration 1.

      (Step Details)

    1. Verify that all energy has been discharged using a Multimeter and reading the voltage between the RF Amplifier Chassis and each capacitor and metal strap internal to the 3.0T RF Amplifier. Only when this voltage reads close to 0V is it safe to work inside the amplifier. See Illustration 1.

1.2   MNS CPC RF Amplifier Replacement

  1. Remove the Front Cover from the 3.0T RF accessories Cabinet.

  1. Remove the Screws holding the CPC MNS Amplifier to the Front Rails.

  1. Remove the Cables from the back of the CPC MNS Amplifier.

  1. Assemble the Universal Hoist Rail and attach it to the 3.0T Accessories Cabinet. See Illustration 2

    (Step Details)

  1. Assemble the Kick Stand. See Illustration 3

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Kick Stand in Front of the amplifier. See Illustration 4

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the Amplifier out of the front of the 3.0T Accessories Cabinet to the mark that indicates where to stop. See Illustration 5

    (Step Details)

  1. Find and attach the brackets that will best fit to the side of the CPC Amplifier.

  1. Attach the Hoist Spreader bar. See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the Amplifier and ease it out on to the Kick Stand. See Illustration 7

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the amp slightly while holding it from swinging.

  1. Remove the Kick Stand and Drop the amplifier to the Floor. See Illustration 8

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide it out of the way.

  1. Using the hoist and additional FE Help, lift the Replacement amplifier from its shipping box and place it on the floor in front of the 3.0T Accessories Cabinet.

  1. Attach the Brackets.

  1. Attach the Spreader Bar. See Illustration 9

    (Step Details)

  1. While one FE stabilizes the Amplifier use the hoist to lift it into place. See Illustration 10

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Amplifier as far as possible in the cabinet.

  1. 20. Place the Kick Stand under the amplifier. See Illustration 11

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the brackets and hoist from the amplifier.

  1. Slide it into the 3.0T RF Accessories Cabinet.

  1. Reinstall the Screws on the Front of the 3.0T RF Accessories cabinet that hold the Amp in place.

  1. Reinstall all the Cables.

2  Finalization

  1. Perform: 2KW 1.5T, 4KW, and 8KW 3.0T MNS Amplifier Calibration.

  1. Perform: MNS RF UPM Calibration.
