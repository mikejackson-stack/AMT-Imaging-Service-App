Replace the Vacuum in Cabinet

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not ApplicableNot ApplicableNot Applicable

2  Procedure

  1. Attach the vacuum pump lift bracket to the pump handle. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Vacuum Pump Lift Bracket

  1. Hoist the vacuum pump up and into the cabinet

  1. Remove the vacuum pump lift bracket from the vacuum pump

  1. Replace the four (4) bolts that connect the vacuum pump to the shelf

  1. Reconnect the vacuum hose to the pump

  1. Replace the solenoid valve and secure bracket in place

  1. Slide the vacuum pump shelf into the cabinet

  1. Replace the two screws that hold the vacuum pump shelf in place. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Vacuum Pump Shelf

  1. Remove the clamp support

  1. Replace the Spreader Bar on the Hoist

  1. Replace the two cotter pins that hold the clamp to the hoist chain. See Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Hoist Chain Without Spreader Bar

  1. Connect the vacuum pump to the electrical supply and remove the lock out tag out of the breakers on the PDU

  1. Check that the electrical fan is running by feeling for air flow at the fan cowl

  1. Remove Hoist Kit from cabinet and return parts to case

3  Finalization

No finalization steps.
