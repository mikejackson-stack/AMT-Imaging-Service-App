Barcode Reader Installation

(Click Here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable30 minsNot Applicable

2  Procedure

  1. Shut down Signa and turn power off the system computer.

  1. The barcode reader installs in series with the system keyboard. Flip upside down the keyboard and the SCIM module.

  1. Remove the cable that connects to the keyboard. (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Remove The Cable Connecting The Keyboard

  1. Install the removed cable into the “Octupus cable. (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Barcode Reader Cables Installed

  1. Install the barcode reader cable into the keyboard. (see Illustration 2).

3  Finalization

  1. Reboot Signa to the scanning level.

  1. Do a quick phantom scan to ensure the scanner is still working properly.
