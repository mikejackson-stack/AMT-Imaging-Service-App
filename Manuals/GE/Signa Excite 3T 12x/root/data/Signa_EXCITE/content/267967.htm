This procedure describes how to execute a Body Hybrid replacement.

3.2  Safety

DANGER
shock hazard!
high bias voltages are present at the hybrid/combiner during normal signa operation.
remove power, lock-out and tag-out (LOTO) the rf cabinet before accessing the hybrid/combiner.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interact with the T/R module making it difficult for one person to handle.
Always remove and/or replace the Body Hybrid 3.0T EXCITE Body T/R Module from the rear pedestal with two (2) people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible while moving it.

4  Procedure

4.1  Body Hybrid Splitter Removal

  1. Select [End Exam] from the Operator Workspace to end any set scans.

  1. Remove power and LOTO the RF Cabinet.

  1. Remove the covers (shown below) from the rear pedestal.

    Illustration 1:  Magnet Enclosure Rear View

  1. Move the Low Profile Carriage Assembly (LPCA) to the rear of the magnet.

  1. Remove the LPCA cover.

  1. Remove the two screws clamping the belt to the LPCA as shown.

    Illustration 2:  Location of LPCA Belt Connection

  1. Manually move the LPCA to the front of the magnet.

  1. Turn the Driver Module Off at the rear of the PDU Cabinet.

    NOTE:LOTO of PDU is not necessary. Removing power is only to protect the module; there is no direct personal danger.

  1. Using the 17 mm wrench, remove the six bolts (three on each side) connecting the rear pedestal split bridge assembly from the rest of the split bridge assembly and magnet enclosure. (See illustration below.)

    Illustration 3:  Removing Bolts Connecting Rear Pedestal

  1. Remove the four screws from the Take-up Cable connection (as shown) to clear split bridge assembly for removal.

    Illustration 4:  Take-up Cable to Bridge Connection

  1. Remove the four screws from the rear of the Split Bridge assembly (as shown) to detach the back cover.

    Illustration 5:  Split Bridge Assembly Back Cover

  1. While supporting the flag sensor plate underneath the bridge, remove the two screws from the back of split bridge assembly that connects the bridge to the rear pedestal.

    NOTE:Do not allow sensor plate to hang by its cables. Rest on part of the rear pedestal, or use another means of support.

    Illustration 6:  Rear Bridge Assembly Screws

  1. Pull the LPCA belt out of from the rear of Bridge Assembly.

  1. Slide the rear pedestal assembly back far enough to clear the split bridge assembly for removal.

  1. Remove rear split bridge assembly by sliding forward and up toward the magnet as shown.

    Illustration 7:  Rear Split Bridge Assembly

  1. Using the 10 mm wrench, remove the two bolts from Transient Noise Suppressor (TNS) as shown.

    Illustration 8:  TNS Location

  1. Disconnect the J1 Cable from the TNS to detach it from the rear pedestal.

  1. Disconnect the J3 Coil No. 1, J4 Coil No. 2, and J6 DD Voltage Cables at the front (magnet side) of the Hybrid Splitter.

    Illustration 9:  Hybrid Splitter Front Cables

  1. Disconnect the J1-RF, J7-REF No. 1 and J8-REF No. 2 Cables at the rear of the Hybrid Splitter.

    Illustration 10:  Hybrid Splitter Rear Cables

  1. Remove the screws from the top support to detach it.

    Illustration 11:  Top Support Location

  1. Clear the cables from underneath the Hybrid Splitter.

  1. Remove the two screws from the back left leg of the rear pedestal (facing rear of magnet) to disconnect the bottom of the Hybrid Splitter saddle.

    Illustration 12:  Rear Pedestal Saddle Screws

  1. Slide the Hybrid Splitter down over the saddle and away from the magnet to gain further access.

  1. Remove the two screws from the top of the Hybrid Splitter saddle.

    Illustration 13:  Hybrid Splitter Saddle Top Screws

  1. Remove the Hybrid Splitter saddle. Two people are necessary: One to hold the Hybrid Splitter and one to remove the saddle as shown.

    Illustration 14:  Hybrid Splitter Saddle

  1. Slide the Hybrid Splitter saddle down and away from the magnet; turn the magnet side of the Hybrid Splitter up and place in vertical position as shown.

    Illustration 15:  Hybrid Splitter

  1. With two people, pull the Hybrid Splitter up through the rear pedestal, and remove it from the Magnet Room staying as far away from the magnet as possible.

    Illustration 16:  Hybrid Splitter Removal

4.2  Body Hybrid Splitter Instalaltion

  1. With two people, bring the Hybrid Splitter into the Magnet Room staying as far away from the magnet as possible. Turn the Hybrid Splitter magnet side up and lower it down through the rear pedestal. (See Illustration 16.)

  1. Turn the Hybrid Splitter down toward the magnet and replace the saddle underneath. (See Illustration 14.)

  1. Replace the screws at the rear pedestal leg into the bottom and top of the saddle. (See Illustration 12 and Illustration 13.)

  1. Set the Hybrid Splitter into the saddle.

  1. Replace the top support and screws as shown in Illustration 11.

  1. Connect the J1-RF in, J7-REF No. 1 and J8-REF No. 2 Cables at the rear of the Hybrid Splitter. (See Illustration 10.)

  1. Connect the J3 Coil No. 1, J4 Coil No. 2, and J6 DD Voltage Cables at the front (magnet side) of the Hybrid Splitter as shown in Illustration 9.

  1. Connect the J1 Cable to the TNS.

  1. Attach the TNS using the 10 mm wrench to replace the two bolts. (See Illustration 8.)

  1. Replace the rear Split Bridge assembly by inserting down and away from magnet onto the rear pedestal. (See Illustration 7.)

  1. Slide the Rear Pedestal assembly back to magnet.

  1. Replace the two screws in the back of the split bridge assembly that connects the bridge to the rear pedestal. Support the flag sensor plate underneath the bridge while replacing the screws, making sure to align the flag correctly. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Replace the four screws to reattach the rear cover of the split bridge assembly as shown in Illustration 5.

  1. Replace the two screws from Take-up Cable connection. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Using the 17 mm wrench, replace the six bolts (three on each side) connecting the rear pedestal split bridge assembly to the rest of the split bridge assembly and magnet enclosure. (See Illustration 3.)

  1. Return power to the Driver Module at the rear of PDU Cabinet

  1. Move the LPCA to rear of the magnet.

  1. Replace the belt through the bridge and clamp to the LPCA by replacing the two screws. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. Replace the LPCA cover.

  1. Replace the rear pedestal covers shown in Illustration 1.

5  Finalization

  1. Restore power to the RF Cabinet.

  1. Perform a TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Complete a Body scan.

  1. Confirm the system is functional.
