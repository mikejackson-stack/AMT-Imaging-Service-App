3.2  Safety

WARNING
POISON HAZARD!
1.5T and 1.0T PHANTOMs CONTAINS NICKEL, A SUSPECT CARCINOGEN.
DO NOT INGEST. DISPOSE OF AS A HAZARDOUS WASTE ACCORDING TO STATE AND FEDERAL REGULATIONS.

WARNING
Poison Hazard!
3.0T phantoms contain dimethyl Silicone Fluid. Do Not ingest.
If Lead or spill occurs, wipe up or absorb in inert material and place in a container for disposal. Incineration or landfill is the recommended disposal method.

4  Procedure

  1. Place the DQA phantom in the head coil. Positioning the fill plugs up and toward the rear of the magnet. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  DQA PHANTOM POSITIONING

  1. Landmark at the landmark lines of the phantom.

  1. At the keypad on the front of the magnet enclosure, press LANDMARK and MOVE TO SCAN.

    NOTE:Exact location of isocenter will be found in a later procedure. The DQA should now be approximately centered longitudinally in body coil assembly. A good first estimate is about 32 inches behind front edge of body coil assembly.

  1. At the operator work space, prepare the system for a “First Image” scan using the scan protocol shown in the Service Protocols procedure located on the service methods CD-ROM, or for the alternate proprietary procedure, see Table 1.

    Table 1:  FIRST IMAGE PROTOCOL

    Note: This is the alternate proprietary procedure available for GE use, and to sites with a valid Advanced Service Package Limited License.

    (If you have not already done so:)

    • [Click on New Pt], and <Enter>
    • Patient Id: geservice<Enter>
    • Patient Name: first image<Enter>
    • Weight (Lb): 111<Enter>
    • Set Patient Protocols to [Service].

    2. In the Protocol field, type o.20.1 (o=Other, 1=series number), or select the corresponding “first image” protocol from the Service list.

    3. For TwinSpeed, select GradMode=WHOLE

    [Save Series], then [Download].

  1. Select [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. For TwinSpeed, repeat Step 4 and Step 5 with GradMode=ZOOM.

  1. DISPLAY IMAGE

    Verify that the images on the monitor correspond with Illustration 2. Do not be concerned with image quality at this time; it will be addressed during the Performance Checks.

    Illustration 2:  DQA PHANTOM GEOMETRY FOR "FIRST IMAGE"
