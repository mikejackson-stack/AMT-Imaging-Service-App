3  Procedure

3.1  Removal of Pump From Cabinet

  1. Switch off the vacuum pump; allowing the pump to cool to a safe temperature

DANGER
possible personal injury!
avoid serious injury or death by electrocution.
remove power from the tac cabinet before attempting to remove the vacuum pump. verify that the tac cabinet breaker on the front of the pdu are off. lock and tag out the plexi glass cover on the front of the pdu

  1. Lock out and tag out the TAC cabinet. (Refer to 8.X Service Methods CD ROM, Safety (SYSMSA2.DOC), Section 6, OSHA LOCKOUT/TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS)

DANGER
cabinet tip hazard!
possible personal injury!
confirm that the aluminum anti-tip legs stored in the rear of the cabinet before attempting to remove the vacuum pump.

  1. Using the screws included with the anti tip legs, secure the aluminum anti tip legs to the threaded holes on each side of the base of the TAC Cabinet. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Installing Anti-Tip Legs on Sides of Cabinet

  1. Remove the front cover and the top bracket from the TAC Cabinet

  1. Open the rear TAC cabinet door

  1. Remove the four (4) screws that attach the fan module to the TAC cabinet. Set the fan module in the cabinet

  1. Unplug the Vacuum Pump power cord from the side of the vacuum pump

  1. Remove the vacuum connector at the vacuum pump. See Illustration 2

    Illustration 2:  Vacuum Connector Hose Location

  1. Locate and open the Universal Lift Hoist Kit

    NOTE:The Universal Lift Hoist is assembled from parts stored in the Universal Lift Hoist Kit. Lift attachments specially designed for the vacuum pump are located in the cardboard box at the bottom of the TAC Cabinet.

  1. Locate the three (3) large Steel Rail sections and place them on a flat surface for assembly. See Illustration 3

    Illustration 3:  Steel Rail Sections, Hex Bolts, and Hex Nuts

  1. Arrange the rail sections in the proper order (rear, center, then front) and fasten thrm together with 4.75” X 1/2” caps crews and hex nuts

  1. Use the crescent wrench to tighten the cap screws

  1. Assemble and fasten the elevating brackets to the rail assembly. See Illustration 4

    Illustration 4:  Hoist Top Rails with Elevating Brackets

  1. Center the Steel Rail and elevating bracket assembly at the top of TAC Cabinet, so the hoist assembly will face the front of the cabinet. See Illustration 5

    Illustration 5:  Hoist Installed on TAC Cabinet

  1. Tighten the bolts on the Steel Rail to secure it to the top of the cabinet

NOTICE
Possible personal and/or property damage! Verify that all hardware is securely fasten; all bolts and nuts must be tight prior to attaching the hoist assembly to the lift kit
 
 

  1. Locate the Hoist Assembly in the Lift Kit. See Illustration 6

    Illustration 6:  Hoist Assembly

  1. Insert the Hoist Assembly into the open end of the front rail. Insert a locking pin into the holes near the front edge of the front rail. This locking pin prevents the Hoist Assembly from sliding out of the rails. See Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Placement of Hoist Assembly Into Front End of Steel Rail Assembly

  1. Remove the two cotter pins that hold the clamp to the hoist chain. See Illustration 8

    Illustration 8:  Hoist Chain without Spreader Bar

  1. Remove the clamp support

  1. Remove the Spreader Bar. See Illustration 7

  1. Replace the clamp support and cotter pins

  1. Remove the two screws that hold the vacuum pump shelf in place. See Illustration 9

    Illustration 9:  Vacuum Pump Shelf

  1. Slide the vacuum pump out of the cabinet until you can access the pump handle

  1. Attach the vacuum pump lift bracket to the pump handle. The vacuum pump lift bracket is located in the cardboard box at the bottom of the TAC cabinet. See Illustration 10

  1. Attach the vacuum pump lift bracket to the hoist

    Illustration 10:  Vacuum Pump Lift Bracket

  1. Remove the four (4) bolts that connect the vacuum pump to the shelf

  1. Hoist the vacuum pump up and out of the cabinet, then place it safely on a cart

  1. Remove the vacuum pump lift bracket from the vacuum pump

3.2  Replacing the Tip Seal

  1. Loosen the four (4) cap screws that secure the fan cowl to the pump body. See Illustration 11

    Illustration 11:  Vacuum Pump Cap Screws

  1. Pull the rear of the vacuum pump. See Illustration 12

    Illustration 12:  Removing the Rear of the Vacuum Pump

  1. Remove the first tip seal. Use a small standard screw driver to start. See Illustration 13

    Illustration 13:  Tip Seal Removal

  1. Remove the entire seal. Make sure to dispose of the seals in accordance with all local and national safety and environmental requirements. See Illustration 14

    Illustration 14:  Tip Seal Removal

  1. Make sure that there is no debris visible on the inside of the scrolls, especially in the tip seal slot. Use a soft, dry, lint-free cloth to remove any debris

  1. Replace the new seal, starting from the center and moving in a counter clockwise direction. make sure that the grooved surface of the seal faces down in the slot. The seal is longer than required, so it must be cut to the correct length. See Illustration 15

    Illustration 15:  Tip Seal Installation

  1. Remove the second tip seal. See Illustration 16

    Illustration 16:  Second Tip Seal Removal

  1. Replace the seal starting from the center and working in a clockwise direction. See Illustration 17

    Illustration 17:  Second Tip Seal Installation

  1. Replace the large O ring on the perimeter of the motor assembly. See Illustration 18

    Illustration 18:  O Ring Replacement

  1. Replace the rear of the vacuum pump. Make sure that the seals do not drop out during assembly. See Illustration 12

  1. Install the four (4) cap screws. See Illustration 11
