ConditionReferenceEffectivity

No scan in progress. Hit [End Exam] to end any exam in progress.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Go to the Service Desktop and select [Calibrations] from the Service Browser. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [DC Offset Calibration], then [Run for Normal Mode] or [Run for TRM Zoom Mode]. You will need to run both modes if your system is a TwinSpeed.

    (Step Details)

  1. The calibration values for ACGD are stored in /usr/g/caldir/gram_tune.dat.WHOLE for Whole Body coil (or gram_tune.dat.ZOOM for Zoom coil) file.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform [TPS Reset] or end diagnostics.

  1. Go to grafidy 3
