HP8200 DVD Optical Drive Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable has been disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in On or Standby mode.
The power cord must be disconnected from the power source before opening the workstation.

CAUTION
Ensure that the optical drive is secure when moving the workstation.
Failure to do so can damage the drive.

NOTICE
Turn off the workstation before disconnecting any cables.
 
 

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HSDA, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD Protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Remove/disengage any security devices that prohibit opening the workstation.

  1. Disconnect all peripheral device cables from the workstation.

1.2   Optical Drive Removal

  1. Remove access panel cover per HP8200 Lower Level FRU Replacement.

  1. Lift up on the three tabs located on the front bezel (Item 1 in Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the front bezel away from the chassis, and remove the bezel (Item 2 in Illustration 1).

  1. Disconnect the power, drive and audio cables from the drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the green drivelock release lever and gently slide the drive out of the chassis (Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

1.3   Optical DVD Installation

  1. Lift the green drivelock release lever while sliding the optical drive into the slot.

  1. When the optical drive is partially inserted, release the drivelock release lever and slide the drive completely into the slot until the drive is secured.

  1. Connect the power, drive, and audio (if required) cables to the drive and workstation.

    (Step Details)
