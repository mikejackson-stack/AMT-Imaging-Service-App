Cradle Release from Carriage Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove head coil carriage cover.

  1. Dock patient transport.

  1. Latch carriage to cradle.

  1. Check that the carriage meets the following criteria:

    1. Carriage latch hook must be low enough to positively engage cradle.

    1. Carriage latch hook must be high enough to be disengaged from cradle when cradle handle is squeezed or twisted.

  1. If needed, adjust Cradle Release Mechanism Tension (see Illustration 1). Refer to Cradle Release Tension Adjustment and Front Guide Roller Height Adjustment procedure.
