Verification of AirSys Chiller Registration

1  Overview

All AirSys Chillers must be registered by contacting AirSys at the following number:

  • 877-485-0402 For sites within U.S.

  • 925 997 7263 For sites outside of the U.S.

Last Updated04/22/2005

2  Registration Information

The following information must be provided.

  • Facility name, Address and contact information

  • FE name and contact information

  • Serial number of the chiller

Illustration 1 is a picture of the AirSys Chiller. This is the only device that needs to be registered, the AirSys Heat Exchanger does not need to be registered as it is serviced by GE.
