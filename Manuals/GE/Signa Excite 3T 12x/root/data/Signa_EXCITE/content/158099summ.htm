2  Procedure

2.1   Procedure for Network Printer

  1. Log out of Signa by right clicking on the mouse, Select [System tools], then [System Restart]. The system will then reboot.

  1. When the login screen appears, login as root password is operator. Place cursor in the background, and “right click” on the mouse.

  1. Configure Printer option. The following GUI will appear, see Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [New] to add printer to the system. See Illustration 1. A new window will appear. Click Next to continue.

  1. Select Queue type as Networked Unix (LPD). See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select the printer type. See Illustration 3. Then Select [Forward].

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Finish] to complete printer installation.

2.2   Procedure for Local Printer

  1. Connect local printer to the parallel port in the rear of the LINUX PC.

  1. Log out of Signa by right clicking on the mouse, Select [System tools], then [System Restart]. The system will then reboot.

  1. When the login screen appears, login as root password is operator.

  1. Place cursor in the background, and “right click” on the mouse.

  1. Select Configure Printer option. The GUI shown in Illustration 4 will start.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [New]. A GUI will appear, see Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Forward]. Then enter printer name if needed and short description. Click on [Forward] again.

  1. The “Queue Type” will now appear. If the printer is recognized by the system, it will appear. See Illustration 5.

  1. Verify that “Locally-connected” option on top is selected. Click on [Forward].

  1. Verify that the proper printer model is selected. Click on [Forward].

  1. If the system does not recognize the printer that is connected, select Printer Manufacturer and Model from the top screen. See Illustration 6. Then select the exact printer.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click on [Finish]. The printer is now selected. If needed print a test page.
