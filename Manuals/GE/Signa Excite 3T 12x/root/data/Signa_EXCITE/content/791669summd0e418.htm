NOTE:To aid in re-positioning the phantom during subsequent measurements, once the phantom is at it’s scan location, place a small piece of tape on the cradle, and another on the bridge, and make a small reference mark on each. Also make reference marks between the phantom and the cradle. These references will help once the cradle drive hardware has been disconnected.

NOTE:The S21 measurement between the I and Q coil ports, represents the transfer coefficient between those two ports and it is typically reported in dB. Since the transfer coefficient in this case is always < 1, the measurement of S21 in dB, is always negative. This measurement represents the Isolation between Port 1 and Port 2, from this point on, S21 and Isolation may be used interchangeably. Note that Isolation is a positive value in dB.
