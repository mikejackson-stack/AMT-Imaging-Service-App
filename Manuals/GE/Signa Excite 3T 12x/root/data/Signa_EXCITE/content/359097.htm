Signa EXCITE HD 3.0T Service Methods
Module Version  1.0,  Version Date  5/15/07

Inspect Cryogen Vent

1. Inspect helium vent pipes and vent pipe supports inside exam room.

2. Check between magnet and outside exhaust diffuser for signs of wear/tear at vent cloth joints.

3. Check vent runs above any false ceilings in exam room.

4. If any problems are found, arrange for repair.

WARNING
Explosive hazard!
VENT SYSTEMS weakened by previous quenches, HAVE BLOWN APART DURING 2ND OR 3RD QUENCH.
USE EXTRA CAUTION WHILE INSPECTING SITES THAT HAVE EXPERIENCED A MAGNET QUENCH.

Check vent system for loose or disconnected joints. Tighten any loose clamps. Arrange for repair of any disconnected joints.
