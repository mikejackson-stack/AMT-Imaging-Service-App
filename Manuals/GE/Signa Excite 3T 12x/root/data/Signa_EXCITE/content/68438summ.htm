Cradle Motion Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Possible personal injury and/or equipment damage.
Obstructions or personnel in cradle path may cause personal injury or equipment damage.
Verify that magnet bore is clear of personnel and equipment before starting Cradle Motion Test.

1  Procedure

  1. Start the Service Browser and go to the MR Service Desktop on the host computer.

  1. Go to the [Diagnostics tab], [Peripherals], [SRI Functional Tests], and select the [Motion Test]. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select [Motion Test] and then click [Run].

  1. A pop-up window will appear warning that the table is about to move. Press [Yes] to continue.

  1. Verify that the cradle moves smoothly, and that magnet enclosure cradle position display increments, or decrements, corresponding to cradle travel into or out of magnet bore.

  1. After the cradle stops, verify the test passed in the status window of the diagnostic tool.

  1. Go to [Diagnostics], End Diagnostics and select the [End Diagnostics] Button to exit the diagnostics mode.
