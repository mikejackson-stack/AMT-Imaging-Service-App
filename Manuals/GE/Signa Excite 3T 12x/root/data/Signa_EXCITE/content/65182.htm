2  Procedure

  1. Properly power down the computer.

  1. Remove the four screws in the rear that secure the left side panel. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the left side panel by sliding it back.

  1. Remove the left panel of the PC by lifting up on the latch.

    Illustration 2:  Removing the Left Panel

  1. Remove the circuit board securing bracket by pinching bracket.

    Illustration 3:  Removing the Circuit Board

  1. Remove the Bit 3 Board.

    Illustration 4:  Removing the Bit 3 Board

  1. Install the replacement bit 3 board

  1. Install the replacement hard drive by reversing the preceding steps.

3  Finalization

No finalization steps.
