4  Procedure

4.1  3.0T Body UPM Functional Check

4.1.1  3.0T Body UPM Functional Check Setup

  1. Inhibit TR faults. Disable the Driver Module TR fault detection in the RFS (RF in the System Cabinet), see Illustration 1. Move switch 2 to the TR Disable position.

    Illustration 1:  Driver Module Switches

  1. Remove the Body Heliax Cable from J4, and connect the 35 kW RF Dummy Load from the Power Measurement Kit into J4. Plug the power supply on the Dummy Load into the 110 VAC service outlet in the System Cabinet. (See Illustration 2.)

    NOTE:If the RF Max Power Out procedure was just completed, configuration is the same. Leave the Body RF Heliax Cable disconnected, and the head output can keep the coupler (1.5T) and wattmeter (3.0T) in line. It will not change the outcome of the calibration.

    Illustration 2:  Plug Dummy Load into J4, Body Out

4.1.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM – Body Forward Power

  1. Press the [Align on] button.

  1. Move the cradle to place the laser alignment light on the center of the Body Loader and Phantom.

  1. Press the [Landmark] button.

  1. Press the [Advance to Scan] button.

  1. From the Common Service Desktop:

    1. Select the Calibration tab.

    1. Select UPM Tool from the Calibration menu, and [Click here to start this tool]. Illustration 3 shows an example of the UPM tool.

      Illustration 3:  UPM Tool

  1. Select the Nuclei: 1H.

  1. Select the RF Chain to check: Body.

  1. Click the [Func Chk] button.

    NOTE:Power Type selection (not used) will be grayed out; this is normal.

  1. Click the [Start] button. When the message shown in Illustration 4 displays, click [Yes] if the Body Coil is ready to go.

    Illustration 4:  Body Coil Setup Confirmation

  1. The UPM tool takes full control and runs the 3.0T ten-second average power test for Body. The result of the test is compared against a Specification File on the system, and the UPM status should indicate Pass/Fail.

  1. The user will be prompted when the test completes. Look in the error log to confirm that the test completed successfully, and a trip error occurred. The following illustrations are examples of the pop-up and error log that should display.

    Illustration 5:  UPM 10-Second Average Power Trip

    Illustration 6:  UPM 10-Second Average Power Trip Error Log

  1. To proceed:

    1. If the test fails, refer to Troubleshooting UPM.

    1. If the test passes, proceed to 3.0T Head UPM Functional Check.

4.2  3.0T Head UPM Functional Check

4.2.1  3.0T Head UPM Functional Check Setup

  1. To inhibit TR faults, disable the Driver Module TR fault detection in the RFS (RF in the System Cabinet) shown in Illustration 7. Move Switch 2 to the TR Disable position.

    Illustration 7:  Driver Module Front Switches

  1. Remove the Head Heliax Cable from J3, and connect directly to the 35 kW RF Dummy Load. Plug the power supply into the Dummy Load and the power supply into 110 VAC service outlet in the System Cabinet. (See Illustration 8.)

    NOTE:If the RF Max Power Out procedure was just completed, configuration is the same. Leave the Body RF Heliax Cable disconnected, and the head output can keep the coupler (1.5T) and wattmeter (3.0T) in line. It will not change the outcome of the calibration.

    Illustration 8:  Setup for Head Output Measurement

4.2.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM – Head Forward Power

NOTE:It is necessary to have a Head Coil in place. It is not necessary to have any scan parameter entered other than what is described below. Simply landmark on the cradle where the Head Coil is located.

  1. Press the [Advance to Scan] button.

  1. Select the Nuclei: 1H.

  1. Select the RF Chain to check: Head.

  1. Select the [Func Chk] button.

  1. Click the [Start] button. When the message shown in Illustration 9 displays, click [Yes] if the Head Coil if ready to go.

    Illustration 9:  Head Coil Setup Confirmation

  1. The UPM tool takes full control and runs the functional test, and the UPM status should indicate Pass/Fail.

  1. The user will be prompted as the test completes. Look in the error log to confirm that the test completed successfully, and a trip error occurred. Illustration 5 and Illustration 6 are examples of the pop-up and error log that should display.

  1. To proceed:

    1. If the test passes, select [Quit] from the UPM Main screen shown in Illustration 3.

    1. If the test fails, refer to Troubleshooting UPM.

4.3  Supplemental UPM Checks

For troubleshooting purposes, the UPM Functional Check tool can be run using the system load setup shown in Illustration 10. Note that a “PASS” result does not provide the required verification of UPM functionality, nor does a “FAIL” result indicate that the UPM or any other part of the transmit chain is faulty or out of specification using this setup.

NOTE:The UPM Functional Checks must pass with the prescribed dummy setups in 3.0T Body UPM Functional Check and 3.0T Head UPM Functional Check as part of the system verification. However, running the Functional Check tool with the system load can be useful when trying to isolate an issue to a particular part of the chain. For this purpose, the supplemental check procedure is defined.

  1. Place the Head Coil on the cradle.

  1. Insert the Head Sphere and Loader into the head coil as shown in Illustration 10.

    Illustration 10:  SPT Nesting Plate Setup

  1. Place the SPT Nesting Plate on the cradle, as tight against the head coil as possible.

  1. Place the Body Sphere and Loader on the nesting plate as shown in Illustration 10.

  1. Press the [Align on] button.

  1. Move the cradle to place the laser alignment light on the center of the Body Loader and Phantom if doing Body Functional Checks, or on the Head Coil Phantom if doing Head Functional Checks.

  1. Proceed to Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM – Body Forward Power, Step 5 for Body Functional Check; proceed to Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM – Head Forward Power, Step 5 for Head Functional Check.

4.4  Troubleshooting UPM

  1. Solution 1:

    1. Check all cabling to the UPM components to make sure they are properly installed.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. Solution 2:

    1. Cycle power to the UPM Chassis.

    1. Reset TPS.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. Solution 3: Reboot the system.

  1. Solution 4:

    1. Remove power from the UPM.

    1. Reset the Processor Module.

    1. Restore power to the UPM.

    1. Reset TPS.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. Solution 5:

    1. Remove power from the UPM.

    1. Reset the Detector Boards.

    1. Restore power to the UPM.

    1. Reset TPS.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. Solution 6:

    1. Check the Processor Module. (Problem could be that it is bad.)

    1. Remove power from the UPM.

    1. Replace the Processor Module.

    1. Restore power to the UPM.

    1. Reset TPS.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. Solution 7:

    1. Check the Detector Boards. (Problem could be that one is bad.)

    1. Remove power from the UPM

    1. Replace both Detector Boards.

    1. Restore power to the UPM.

    1. Reset TPS.

    1. Repeat the UPM Functional Test for the receive chain that failed (Head or Body).

  1. For additional troubleshooting information, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting.

4.5  UPM Theory

The Universal Power Monitor calibration parameters found in the UPM calibration file (/export/home/signa/tools/upm.config) are used in the Calibration/Functional Check program. The Program code sets the Digital Attenuators in the UPM based on the results of the tests run and a comparison to these UPM Calibration parameters.

  • The UPM calibration file is accessible within the UPM Calibration tool from the “File” pull-down menu, “Edit Config” option. These values can be edited and saved during troubleshooting to perhaps understand where the power monitor is tripping. Before changing any parameter, make sure to select and run the Bkup UPM Cal option first to allow a return to the default values at any time.

  • A screen shot of the UPM Calibration file (shown in Illustration 11) contains the default configuration values for all of the UPM parameters. This illustration is for reference only, as the content and values will be different based on the system type and calibration results. (Use the [Restore Defaults] on the Config File Editor GUI to get back to known default values.) The final UPM Digital Attenuator values are written to the UPM Calibration Configuration files (Upm1Cal.cfg, Upm1Cal.cfg, etc.) located in directory: w/config.

  1. Note that the format of the Upm1Cal.cfg and Upm2Cal.cfg examples is as follows:

    1. 85←Head FORWARD Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 9f←Head REFLECTED Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 0← Head Quad Hybrid Reflected Power, attenuator setting in HEX (currently not used)

    1. 8d← Body FORWARD Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 8e←Body REFLECTED Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 9d←Body Quad Hybrid Reflected Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 93←MNS FORWARD Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 83←MNS REFLECTED Power, attenuator setting in HEX

    1. 0 ← MNS Quad Hybrid Reflected Power, attenuator setting in HEX (currently not used)

    1. 0 ←CW FORWARD Power, attenuator setting in HEX (currently not used)

    1.  0←CW REFLECTED Power, attenuator setting in HEX (currently not used)

    1. 0←CW Quad Hybrid Reflected Power, attenuator setting in HEX (currently not used)

  1. Any changes to these values are temporary, and can be quickly restored by selecting the [Restore Defaults] button shown in Illustration 11.

    Illustration 11:  UPM Config Editor

5  Finalization

  1. Remove the cables and measurement equipment.

  1. Reattach Head and Body Heliax Cable.

  1. Re-enable TR Driver fault detection.

  1. Complete a TPS reset and do a test scan.

  1. Any failure will require a recalibration of the UPM system. Refer to:

    • Universal Power Monitor - Body Setup and Calibration and

    • Universal Power Monitor - Head Setup and Calibration.
