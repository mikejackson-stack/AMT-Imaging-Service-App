1.1   RRF CHASSIS CIRCUIT BOARDS

The following removal and insertion procedures are applicable for all of the types of circuit boards located within the cardrack.

  1. POWER-DOWN:

    (Step Details)

  1. RRF Chassis Circuit Board Removal/Replacement Procedure

    1. Attach a grounding wrist strap.

    1. Where applicable, remove the fiber-optic cables (J9, J10, Gigalink, etc.) from the face of the board.

      (Step Details)

    1. Loosen the two faceplate holding screws (one at the top and one at the bottom of the faceplate). These screws are captive to the faceplate and do not need to be completely removed.

      (Step Details)

    1. Using your thumbs, push in the grey release tabs (Illustration 4), and gently push out the extractor tabs located on the top and bottom of the circuit board (refer to Illustration 5 for proper direction). The lever action of the ejectors will disengage the circuit board connections from the chassis.

    1. Slide the circuit board all the way out of the card cage, being careful not to bend or bind the card in any way.

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the card in an appropriate shipping package, or discard it, if applicable.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the packing material from the replacement board, and gently slide the circuit board into the chassis.

      (Step Details)

    1. Gently engage the circuit board to the front of the RRF chassis. If it doesn’t feel like the circuit board is properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.

      (Step Details)

    1. Using your thumbs, push the ejectors towards the center of the board and allow the lever action of the ejectors to insert the board into the chassis.

    1. Tighten the faceplate holding screws to secure the circuit board to the cardrack.

      (Step Details)

    1. Where applicable, re-attach the fiber-optic cables to the face of the board.

    1. Replace the cable harnesses. Each fits only one way.

      (Step Details)

  1. Test Procedure:

1.2   RRF POWER SUPPLY UNITS

There are three RRF Power Supply units contained within the RRF Chassis which transform and transmit power to the cabinet’s components. All three units are clustered together on the front of the RRF Chassis, and the following removal and insertion procedure is applicable to all three units.

  1. POWER-DOWN

    (Step Details)

  1. RRF Power Supply Unit Removal/Replacement Procedure

    1. Attach a grounding wrist strap

    1. Remove the four holding screws (located at the corners of the power unit).

    1. While firmly grasping the handle, gently pull the power supply unit out of the chassis.

    1. Place the PSU in an appropriate shipping package.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the packing material from the replacement power supply unit, and gently slide the PSU into the chassis.

    1. Gently engage the power supply unit’s connectors with the backplane. If it doesn’t feel like the PSU is properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.

      (Step Details)

    1. Using both hands, apply pressure evenly over the whole surface of the PSU and firmly push the PSU into the connectors, seating the PSU with the backplane.

    1. Tighten the faceplate holding screws to secure the PSU to the cardrack.

      (Step Details)

  1. POWER-UP

    (Step Details)

  1. Test Procedure

1.3   RRF COOLING FANS

There are two cooling fans located on the RRF Chassis (one on the front, one on the back), and the removal process is the same for both.

  1. POWER-DOWN

    (Step Details)

  1. RRF Cooling Fan Removal/Replacement Procedure

    1. Attach a grounding wrist strap.

    1. Remove the four holding screws (located at the outside corners of the power unit). You do not need to remove the inner screws that secure the fan guard to the fan housing.

    1. Gently remove the fan from the chassis.

    1. Unclip the fan from the chassis (See Illustration 8).

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the fan in an appropriate shipping package or discard where appropriate.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the packing material from the replacement fan, and clip the fan power supply to the fan.

    1. Slide the fan into the assembly and install the four holding screws.

    1. Tighten the holding screws to secure the cooling fan to the assembly.

      (Step Details)

1.4   REMOVAL/INSTALLATION OF RRF CHASSIS

  1. Power-Down

    (Step Details)

  1. RRF Chassis Removal/Replacement Procedure

    (Step Details)

  1. Assembly and Attachment of the Lift Tool Kit Procedure

    NOTE:Steps 1-6 below are for fixed sites only. Mobile sites begin with step 7.

    1. Assemble the three-piece rail (Items 9, 10, and 11) with 4.75” x ½” cap screws (Item 15) and hex nuts (item 37) See Illustration 7-5. Tighten with a crescent wrench.

    1. Assemble the rear bracket (Item 13) to the plate (Item 12) with two cap screws (Item 35).

    1. Insert the plate (Item 12) into the rear rail section (Item 11) until flush, and tighten the cap screws (Item 35).

    1. Place the assembly on top of the cabinet with the attached bracket to the rear of the cabinet. See Illustration 9.

      (Step Details)

    CAUTION
    Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
    Throughout this entire procedure, please make sure to watch the lock pin once it is inserted in the front rail section.
    The motion of the chain may cause the pin to shift position slightly.

    1. Attach the other bracket (Item 13) to the plate (Item 12) with two cap screws (Item 35).

      (Step Details)

    1. Insert the plate (Item 12) into the front rail section (Item 9). Push the bracket against the cabinet front, and tighten the cap screws. See Illustration 11.

      (Step Details)

    1. Insert the Hoist Assembly pivot bearings with the handle at left, into the front rail section for a fixed site (see Illustration 12) or the ceiling channel for a mobile site (Illustration 13).

      (Step Details)

    CAUTION
    Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
    Throughout this entire procedure, please make sure to watch the lock pin once it is inserted in the front rail section.
    The motion of the chain may cause the pin to shift position slightly.

    1. Insert the lock pin (Item 14) through the holes in the front rail section. (See Illustration 12).

    1. Remove the four screws located on the front of the cabinet frame that hold the RRF Chassis to the cabinet.

      (Step Details)

    1. Slide the RRF Chassis toward you until it’s slightly less than halfway out of the Systems Cabinet. See Illustration 15.

      (Step Details)

    1. Remove the two Phillips screws from the left side of the Chassis in preparation for mounting the L-shaped bracket to the Chassis and the spreader bar. See Illustration 15 for the location of the bracket and screws.

    1. Guide the shoulder bolt on the spreader bar into the top of the keyhole on the L-bracket.

    1. Remove the two Phillips screws from the right side of the Chassis. Attach the straight bracket to the side-by-side holes on the right side of the Chassis. Once the bracket is attached, guide the right shoulder bolt on the spreader bar into the top of the keyhole.

    CAUTION
    Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
    Before putting weight on the hoist chain, be sure that the links are aligned properly (see Illustration 7-10).
    This will help keep the chain from binding up, or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

    1. With one hand, pull the back of the Chassis toward you as you gently pull the chain hoist outward with the other hand. When the module clears the cabinet, it will swing freely on the hoist.

    1. Move the end of the chain with the ring on it to one side, if necessary, to keep it out of your way. Rotate the Chassis 90 degrees, being careful to avoid contact with any protruding power or toggle switches in the Systems Cabinet. See Illustration 16.

      (Step Details)

    1. Use the hoist to slowly lower the RRF Chassis to the ground. Remove the two side brackets and the spreader bar.

      (Step Details)

    1. Attach a grounding wrist strap.

    1. Install the four screws that hold the RRF Chassis to the cabinet frame.

      (Step Details)

    1. Install all boards into the new RRF Chassis. See RRF CHASSIS CIRCUIT BOARDS, Step 2.d for installation instructions.

    1. Replace the Power Supply Units. See RRF POWER SUPPLY UNITS, Step 2 for installation instructions.

    1. Remove and disassemble the hoist, and repack it in the case.

2  Finalization

  1. Refer to the Power-Up procedure for the steps required to restore power to the Systems Cabinet.

  1. Perform 35KW Body Maximum Power Setup and Calibration if MUX boards in slots 13/14 are replaced or if Exciter boards in slots 6/7 are replaced.

  1. If MNS Option is present and the MUX boards in slots 13/14 are replaced or if Exciter boards in slots 8/9 are replaced, perform MNS RF output Calibration.

  1. If MUX UTNS or Receiver Boards are replaced, run Bandpass Asymmetry Correction Tool (BAT).
