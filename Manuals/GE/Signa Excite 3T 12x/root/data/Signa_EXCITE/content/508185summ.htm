2kW 1.5T, 4kW, 4kW Used as 2kW Amplifier, and 8kW 3.0T MNS Amplifier Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
FATAL ELECTRICAL VOLTAGES THROUGHOUT THE MNS CABINET.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THE MNS RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY!
POSSIBLE RF BURN WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM THE RF AMPLIFIER.
VERIFY THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE AND THE UCERD SWITCH IS IN THE "DISABLE" POSITION.

NOTICE
Property damage to the coils could result if not removed from the magnet bore during body scans. Verify that the scan desktop icon displays the “Idle” message and the UCERD Switch is in the "Disable" position.
 
 

NOTICE
If the RF amplifier is operated with an improper load connected to its output, permanent damage to the amplifier could result.
 
 

1  Procedure

  1. Disabling T/R Drive error condition:

    1. Verify the system is idle and all coils have been removed from the magnet bore.

    1. Remove the front cover of the RF Accessories Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the driver module, set the T/R Switch (shown inIllustration 1) to Disable. This will disable T/R fault reporting.

      (Step Details)

    1. Disconnect the T/R Bias Cable from the RF Amplifier (J11 on AMT Amplifier or J14 on CPC Amplifier).

  1. Set up the measurement system according to one of the three diagrams shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Power Attenuator Method:

    1. Using an RG214 Cable, connect the RF output of the amplifier to the input of a high-power attenuator.

    1. Connect additional attenuation to the output of the high-power attenuator until a total of 65 to 70 dB of attenuation is attained.

    1. Using a BNC Cable, connect the output of the attenuators to the input of the oscilloscope.

    1. Set the oscilloscope to full bandwidth (>150 MHz) and direct current of 50-ohm input impedance.

  1. Coupler Method:

    1. Connect the RF output of the amplifier to the input of power coupler.

    1. Connect the output of the power coupler to a suitable 50-ohm Dummy Load.

    1. Connect additional attenuation to the coupled output of the power coupler until at total of 65 to 70 dB of attenuation is attained.

    1. Connect the output of the attenuators to the input of the oscilloscope.

    1. Set the oscilloscope to full bandwidth (>150 MHz) and direct current of 50 ohm input impedance.

  1. For the Power Meter method, set up according to the directions in MNS Max RF Power Meter (Watt Meter) Setup.

  1. Set up the scan parameters according to Table 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Calibrate the Amplifier.

    1. For CPC 2kW 1.5T, 4kW 3.0T, and 4kW RF amplifier cabinet with attenuator calibration:

      1. The manual prescan should already be running. Measure the peak envelope power using the oscilloscope (power attenuator, coupler or power meter method).

      1. For ease of calculation, an Excel tool is available. Enter the measurement data into the respective calculator.

      1. If the gain adjustment is positive, turn the amplifier gain potentiometer adjustment clockwise. (The amplifier gain potentiometer is located at the back of the CPC Amplifier.)

        (Step Details)

      1. Increase the TG to 200. When 2kW or 4kW is measured at TG = 200 (depending on amp calibrated), the amplifier is successfully calibrated. The 4kW amplifier with pre-installed attenuator will be calibrated to 2kW.

        (Step Details)

    1. For AMT 8kW 3.0T calibration:

      1. Remove the front cover of the AMT Amplifier (takes about 8 minutes as there are 50+ screws to remove). Locate two holes for gross (6.0 dB) and fine (0.5 dB) adjustments as pictured in Illustration 4 and Illustration 5.

        (Step Details)

      1. The manual prescan should already be running. Measure the peak envelope power using the oscilloscope (power attenuator, coupler or power meter method).

      1. Enter the measurement data into the respective calculator.

      1. [STOP] or [PAUSE] the manual prescan before adjusting the gain controls on the AMT Amplifier.

      1. If the calculator specifies an adjustment for the 6.0 dB control, insert a long Phillips screwdriver (size #0 or #00) into the 6.0 dB adjuster knob, and turn the control clockwise the specified number of clicks.

      1. Restart the manual prescan and measure the power. Use the calculator provided to recalculate the remaining gain adjustment.

      1. [STOP] or [PAUSE] the manual prescan before adjusting the gain controls on the AMT Amplifier.

      1. Insert a Phillips screwdriver into the 0.5 dB control, and turn the specified number of turns. If the gain of the system jumps by about 8.0 dB when adjusting this control, it was rotated past its zero point as per Illustration 5. Adjust as follows:

        (Step Details)

      1. Repeat Step , Step  and Step  as necessary to achieve the correct gain.

      1. Once the calculated gain adjustment is almost zero (0), set the TG to 200 and verify full power output without peak power faults. It may be necessary to add one additional clockwise click of the 0.5 dB control if the system is sensitive to faults at the 8kW peak power level.

      1. When 8kW is measured on the oscilloscope at TG = 200, the amplifier is correctly calibrated.

        (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Verify that scanner is “Idle” and turn off the RF Enable.

  1. If Power Meter method was used, disconnect the RF Watt Sensor from the RF Output connection.

  1. Connect the original Output Cable to the MNS RF Amplifier and tighten the connector.

  1. Perform remaining cleanup procedures.

  1. Turn on the RF Enable.

  1. Reset the TPS to put the amp back into a known state.

  1. Perform a goodbye scan.

  1. Perform Universal Power Monitor - MNS Setup and Calibration.
