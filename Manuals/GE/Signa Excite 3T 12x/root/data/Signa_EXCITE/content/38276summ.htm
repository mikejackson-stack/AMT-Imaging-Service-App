Slice Offset Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   2D SCANS

  1. Place the phantom in the head coil on top of the head holder. Position the filler plugs (resolution end of phantom) toward the foot-end of table. See Illustration 1. Landmark the phantom on the center plate (see Illustration 2 for landmarking phantom). Press MOVE TO SCAN.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the operator workspace, select the [Scan] icon in the desktop control panel, if you have not already done so.

  1. Click on [Autoview], just below the Autoview image display screen; your images will be automatically displayed.

  1. At the operator workspace, prepare the system for a Slice Offset Series 1 scan using the "Service Protocols".

  1. Press MOVE TO SCAN button on the operator workspace, then click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. After scan #1 is complete, click on [New Series]. Prepare scan #2 (Slice Offset Series 2 scan, 2D -50mm Table Delta).

  1. Press the MOVE TO SCAN button on the operator workspace, then click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. For analysis, refer to SLICE OFFSET IMAGE ANALYSIS.

1.2   3D SCANS

  1. After Scan #2 is complete, click on [New Series]. Prepare Scan #3 (Slice Offset Series 3 scan, 3D GRE Localizer).

  1. Press the MOVE TO SCAN button on the operator workspace, then click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. After Scan #3 is complete, click on [New Series]. Prepare Scan #4 (Slice Offset Series 4 scan, 3D GRE 50mm Table Delta).

  1. Click on the [Graphic Rx] window.

  1. A slab box cursor appears on the image. Position the cursor over the center bar of the image (see Illustration 3), then click on [Accept], [Save Series]. Then [Prepare to Scan].

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the MOVE TO SCAN button at the operator workspace, then click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. After Scan #4 is complete, click on [New Series]. Prepare Scan #5 (Slice Offset Series 5 scan, 3D GRE -50mm Table Delta).

  1. Press MOVE TO SCAN button at the operator work space, then click on [Scan] (system Auto Prescans first).

  1. For analysis, refer to SLICE OFFSET IMAGE ANALYSIS.

    (Step Details)

1.3   SLICE OFFSET IMAGE ANALYSIS

  1. To run the Image Quality Analysis – NEMA tool from the Service Desktop, follow the instructions on Illustration 4, below.

    (Step Details)

  1. For TwinSpeed, highlight the same GradMode that was used with sections 2D SCANS and 3D SCANS above, then click on [OK].

  1. A NEMA Image Quality window appears on the desktop. Type 2<Enter> for Slice Offset Checks. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter the first image exam, series, and image numbers.

    (Step Details)

  1. Enter the second image series and image numbers. The second image Exam Number should be the same as the first image.

    (Step Details)

  1. Analysis then begins. The final values are displayed on the screen, as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the Measured Offset in Data Sheet 1, Head Slice Offset Data. For TwinSpeed, identify the GradMode used.

  1. Repeat steps Step 3 through 7 above for analysis of the remaining images.

  1. Type 6 and press <Enter> to exit the NEMA Image Quality Analysis Menu.

2  Finalization

  1. Return the system to a patient scanning condition.

  1. Do a check scan to insure proper operation.
