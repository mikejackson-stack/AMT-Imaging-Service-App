2  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport, and move it out of exam room.

  1. Remove upper and lower side covers (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  REMOVAL OF PATIENT TABLE COVERS

  1. Remove cradle (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  PATIENT TRANSPORT CRADLE REMOVAL

  1. Loosen set screw at down link clevis. See Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  MAIN CRADLE LATCH DOWN LINK LEVER

  1. Remove four bolts securing cradle latch housing and cradle latch cover (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  INTERLOCK CABLE AND MAIN CRADLE LATCH CABLE ROUTING

  1. Unfasten cable end from down link clevis (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  MAIN CRADLE LATCH DOWN LINK LEVER

  1. Pull cable out of latch pin on front of patient transport (see Illustration 6).

    Illustration 6:  INTERLOCK CABLE AND MAIN CRADLE LATCH CABLE ROUTING

  1. Insert new cable through head of latch pin so that swaged end fits into hole in latch pin. See Illustration 7 for details on installing cable.

    Illustration 7:  INSTALLATION OF MAIN CRADLE LATCH CABLE

  1. Route cable through cable casing and insert through cable clevis (see Illustration 8).

  1. Loop cable twice through hole in down link clevis (see Illustration 8).

  1. Tighten set screw (see Illustration 8).

    Illustration 8:  MAIN CRADLE LATCH DOWN LINK LEVER

  1. Return patient transport to exam room.

  1. Dock patient transport.

  1. Adjust cable so that when docked, main latch will be flush with casting. Refer to procedure for Cradle Release Block Retraction Adjustment. Cut off excess length after adjustment is complete. Secure adjustment by tightening lock nut after adjustment is complete (see Illustration 9).

    Illustration 9:  MAIN CRADLE LATCH DOWN LINK LEVER

  1. Replace cradle and upper and lower side covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Illustration 10).

    Illustration 10:  REMOVAL OF PATIENT TABLE COVERS
