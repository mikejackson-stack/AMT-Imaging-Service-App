2  Procedure

2.1  Fiber Optic Repeater Board Removal

  1. Lock out and tag out circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet on Magnet Enclosure Power Supply (MEPS) Module. (Refer to OSHA Lockout Tagout Requirements.)

  1. Verify that voltage is between MR1 A14 TP4 (+) and TP13 (–) is 0 Vdc.

  1. Follow steps 1 through 4 in Illustration 1.

3  Finalization

  1. Remove tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet on Magnet Enclosure Power Supply (MEPS) Module.

  1. Restore power to the Fiber Optic Repeater by switching on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6.

  1. Perform Fiber Optic Repeater Loopback Test.
