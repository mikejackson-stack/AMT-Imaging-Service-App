HP8200 Hard Drive Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable is disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in On or Standby mode.
The power cord must be disconnected from the power source before opening the workstation.

NOTICE
Turn off the workstation before disconnecting any cables.
 
 

NOTICE
To prevent damage to components within the HSDA, observe the following ESD precautions:

  • Work on a static-free mat.

  • Wear a static strap to ensure that any accumulated electrostatic charge is discharged from your body to the ground.

  • Create a common ground for the equipment being worked on by connecting the static-free mat, static strap and peripheral units to that piece of equipment.

For further information regarding ESD Protection, refer to the Safety chapter in this publication.

 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation:

  1. Remove/disengage any security devices that prohibit opening the workstation.

  1. Disconnect all peripheral device cables from the workstation.

1.2   Hard Drive Removal

  1. Remove access panel cover according to HP8200 Lower Level FRU Replacement.

  1. Disconnect the cables from the back of the suspected faulty hard drive.

  1. Push in on the green drivelock release tabs and slide the hard drive out of the chassis. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

1.3   Hard Drive Installation

1.3.1  SCSI Drive

There are three drives in this system. SCSI addresses that need to be set for these drives are provided in the table below.

Table 1:  SCSI Addresses for Drives

Drive SlotSCSI ID
Top0
Middle1
Bottom2

  1. To configure the SCSI address for the drive, first look at the drive and determine the manufacturer. If the drive is made by Seagate, proceed to Step 1.a. If the drive was made by Fujitsu, proceed to Step 1.b.

    NOTE:If the drive is from a vendor other than Fujitsu or Seagate, or it is not shown in this procedure, reference the jumper setup label on top of the drive for guidance.

    1. Refer to Illustration 2 to configure a Seagate drive.

      (Step Details)

    1. Refer to Illustration 3 and Table 2 for Fujitsu Disk Drive Jumper Settings.

      (Step Details)

  1. After giving the hard drive a unique SCSI ID, select a slot and install the hard drive into the appropriate slot.

    (Step Details)

  1. Simultaneously disengage the green tabs of the rail assembly, and slide the rails out of the empty slot.

  1. Attach the rails to the hard drive by inserting the hard drive rail assembly pins into one side of the hard drive screw holes first.

NOTICE
If installing the hard drive into slot 5, proceed to Step 7.
 
 

  1. Gently flex open the opposite side of the hard drive rail assembly, and insert the remaining pins into the holes in the hard drive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Push the drive into the selected slot until it snaps into place, and attach the power and SCSI cable to the drive. (See Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

  1. If installing a hard drive into slot 5, do the following:

    1. Lay the workstation on its side, and remove the three drive screws that are located near Slot 5.

    1. Insert the drive into Slot 5, and align the holes in the bottom of the hard drive with the screw holes at the base of the chassis.

    1. Insert the screws through the base, and tighten the hard drive to the chassis.

  1. Connect the data cable to the SCSI1 connector on the system board (shown in Illustration 7).

    (Step Details)

1.3.2  SATA Drives

  1. Select a drive slot in which to install the drive. Squeeze the green tabs and slide the rails out of the empty slot (Illustration 1).

  1. Attach the rails to the hard drive by aligning the notches with the holes and squeezing them in place. (See Illustration 5.)

  1. Push the drive into the selected slot until it snaps into place.

  1. Attach the power cable and data cable to the drive.

  1. Connect the data cable to the serial ATA port (Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. Back up data and perform a Disk Wipe.

  1. Perform a Load from Cold software installation. See Host Operating System Software Load 12.x.

    (Step Details)
