Bridge Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE FRONT SUPPORT ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE DRIVE ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

1  Procedure

  1. Lay belt in bore with teeth facing up. Have excess length at front of magnet.

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE FRONT SUPPORT ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

  1. Install two front bridge support allen bolts.

  1. Install front bridge support assembly with two bolts.

  1. Connect cables to J1 and J2 on dock control module.

  1. Carefully lift and slide bridge into the magnet bore.

  1. Feed the drive belt into and through front pulley assembly.

  1. Insert the end of the belt, and tongue of the pulley assembly into the bridge.

  1. Install two screws into the tongue of the front bridge mount and position clamp plate under the bridge. Tighten screws into the clamp plate (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE DRIVE ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

  1. At the back of the magnet, at the rear pedestal, tilt drive assembly so tongue points upward and insert drive assembly tongue into bridge, taking care not to damage motor by bumping it against rear pedestal (see Illustration 6 in Section 1).

  1. Install two bolts holding longitudinal drive assembly to the rear pedestal.

  1. Connect Longitudinal Encoder cable to J3 on Magnet I/F #1.

  1. Connect run 318 to short cable on Longitudinal Drive motor.

  1. Insert two screws through bridge and tongue at back of bridge, and support sensor plate beneath bridge as you tighten the screws (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Set carriage into position on the bridge.

  1. Carefully connect the PlastiTrak to carriage by sliding pins into retaining holes in bracket.

  1. Connect eleven cables to connectors on rear pedestal.

  1. Insert both ends of the drive belt up into the carriage base. Illustration 3 shows routing of belt.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install four screws holding belt clamp plate. See Illustration 3 in Section 1 and Illustration 3 above.

  1. Push down the tension arm to apply tension to carriage drive belt (see Illustration 2 in Section 1).

  1. With carriage cover in approximate position, connect eleven cable connections inside of carriage cover.

  1. Replace carriage cover; install four screws in carriage cover.

  1. Push in and latch front cover of the magnet and install covers on rear pedestal.

  1. Remove tag-out tag from circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Switch on circuit breaker MR1 A14 CB6 at rear of RF/Pen Cabinet (MR1) on MEPS module.

    (Step Details)
