Longitudinal Encoder Supply Voltage Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove right front magnet enclosure cover to gain access to the SRI module (MG2 A33).

  1. Loosen SRI module cover screws and slide cover to the left. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. With system powered up, connect meter set for dc volts to TP5 and TP6 (see Illustration 1).

  1. Adjust R193 (located above J2 and left of J1) until a 5.0 + 0.1 Vdc reading is indicated on the meter.

  1. Slide SRI module cover closed and tighten cover screws.

  1. Install magnet enclosure cover.
