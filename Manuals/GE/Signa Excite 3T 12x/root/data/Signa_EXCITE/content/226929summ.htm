GOC Audio Box Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, TURN THE SYSTEM POWER OFF AND PERFORM LOTO PROCEDURE BEFORE STARTING THIS PROCEDURE.

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Turn the system power OFF and perform LOTO procedure. Emergency line is connected to the Board. Without turning the system Power OFF, Emergency function works when disconnecting the SCIM-Audio CBL and it may damage the system.
 
 

  1. Properly turn the system power OFF.

  1. Remove the three screws in the rear that secure the right side panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the right side panel by sliding in backwards.

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the Audio Box.

  1. Remove all cables that are connected to the Audio Box. Verify proper labeling.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two screws at the bottom and loosen two screws at the top. Then, remove Audio Assy.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install replacement Audio Box by reversing the proceeding steps.

2  Finalization

  1. Turn the system power on. Verify system boot up without any problem.

  1. Press Emergency OFF button on SCIM to check the E-OFF line is working correctly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify the power to the Gradient subsystem, RF subsystem, and System cabinet is shut off by checking that the cabinet cooling fans have stopped. Also, verify that the Display LEDs on the Magnet Enclosure are not illuminated.

  1. At scan room. Press [Landmark] and press Move to scan. At Operator Room. Press [Stop Move] button. Verify Table stop the movement.

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform Audio Functional Check.
