Table Up Limit Cable

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock patient transport, and move from exam room.

  1. Remove upper and lower side covers (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise table to maximum height.

  1. Remove cotter pin and retaining pin securing bottom end of cable to bell crank (see Illustration 2).

  1. Remove clamp securing cable to bracket (see Illustration 2).

  1. Remove retaining ring and washer securing end of cable to Up Limit pin (see Illustration 2).

  1. Install new cable and attach to Up Limit pin per Step 6 above.

  1. Fasten other end of cable to bell crank, and secure retaining pin with cotter pin (see Illustration 2).

  1. Attach clamp securing cable to bracket (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust table Up Limit cable to remove any slack when hydraulic cylinder is fully extended.

  1. Check for proper operation of Up Limit switch when table is docked.

  1. Replace upper and lower side covers. Note position of Signa logo on outer covers and position toward front of table (see Illustration 3).
