3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Safety

CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE FRONT PULLY ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

4  Procedure

4.1  BRIDGE REMOVAL

  1. With the patient transport docked, move the carriage to the rear of the magnet by pressing the “In Fast” button on the enclosure.

  1. Undock and move patient transport out of the way of the front cover of the magnet.

  1. Remove the two rear pedestal magnet corner covers. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Magnet Corner Covers

  1. Remove the rear pedestal covers. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:   Rear Covers

    Lock out and tag out circuit breaker for the System Support Module (SSM)/Patient Handling Power Supply (PHPS) and the Driver Module. Verify that voltage between MR1 A14 TP1 (+) and TP14 (–) is 0 Vdc.

  1. Remove four screws from the top of the carriage cover and the 6 screws from the back. See Illustration 4.

  1. Lift the carriage cover and remove it from the carriage.

  1. Lift the tension arm to release tension from carriage drive belt. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Right Side View Of Rear Pedestal (As Viewed From Rear)

  1. Unclamp both ends of the belt by removing the four screws holding down the two belt clamp plates. See Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Belt Clamp Plates

  1. Remove the rear section of the belt from being routed through the drive motor and rear pedestal. DO NOT pull the belt completely out from beneath the bridge.

  1. Remove the 8 bolts holding the cable track cup. See Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Cable Track Plate

  1. Remove the four M10 hex nuts holding the front bridge to the rear bridge, and slide the rear pedestal assembly back. The nuts are located on the rear pedestal side of the mating flanges. See Illustration 6.

    NOTE:The carriage cover cables do not need to be disconnected from the rear pedestal.

    Illustration 6:  Front And Rear Bridge Connection

4.2  Removal and Replacement of the Front Bridge

  1. Remove the two screws holding the front bridge cover. See Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Front And Rear Bridge Connection

  1. Remove the two bolts that attach the bridge bracket to the magnet end ring.

  1. Carefully lift and slide the existing front bridge out of the magnet bore.

  1. Carefully lay the replacement front bridge in the magnet bore, lining up the support bracket with the screw holes.

CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
THE FRONT PULLY ASSEMBLY CONTAINS PARTS MADE WITH FERROUS COMPONENTS. HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET.
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS, PUT THE ASSEMBLY IN PLACE DIRECTLY, NOT MOVING IT IN THE MAGNETIC FIELD MORE THAN IS NECESSARY.

  1. Install the two front bridge bracket bolts and support hardware. Do not fully tighten the bridge supports at this time. Wait until the front and rear sections have been secured together before fully tightening the front bridge support.

4.3  Removal and Replacement of the rear bridge

  1. Remove the two screws that secure the rear bridge to the rear pedestal. See Illustration 8.

    Illustration 8:  Rear Bridge Removal

  1. Slowly lift the rear section of the bridge from the rear pedestal.

  1. Install the replacement rear bridge by placing it on top of the rear pedestal.

  1. Secure the rear bridge to the rear pedestal by reinstalling the two screws from Step 1.
