Applies to: GE Signa Excite (9x–15x), HDxt, Discovery MR450/750, Optima 450w/750w and related platforms. Error E410 and related gradient fault diagnostics.

Error E410 — Gradient Amplifier Fault

CheckAction
Gradient Amp CoolingVerify gradient amplifier cooling fans are running. Check cooling water flow (liquid-cooled systems). Overtemp is a leading cause of E410. Check the temperature status in MR Service Desktop → Gradient Real Time Data.
DC Bus VoltageVerify DC bus voltage to gradient amps. Check for blown fuses on the gradient power supply. Measure DC rails per service manual test points.
Gradient Amp EnableVerify gradient amp enable signal is present. Run Gradient Amplifier Diagnostics: MR Service Desktop → Diagnostics → System Function → Gradient → Gradient Amplifier Test.
Gradient Cable ConnectionsInspect all gradient cables from amp to gradient coil assembly. Reseat all connectors. Look for arcing or insulation damage.
Gradient Coil ContinuityRun gradient coil resistance check: Diagnostics → Gradient → Gradient Coil Test. X, Y, Z coils tested independently. Note any open or shorted coil.
Thermal InterlockCheck thermal interlock sensors on gradient amplifiers. A tripped thermal switch requires cooldown before reset. Inspect for obstructed airflow or failed fan.

Common Gradient Fault Scenarios

SymptomLikely CauseFix
E410 on specific axis only (X, Y, or Z)Single gradient amp failure or coil fault on that axisRun per-axis gradient diagnostic. Isolate to amp or coil with resistance measurement. Replace failed amp or schedule coil service.
E410 on startup, clears on retryDC bus inrush / slow rail stabilizationCheck DC bus caps and pre-charge circuit. Test gradient power supply under load.
E410 intermittent under heavy scan loadThermal shutdown — cooling inadequacyClean heat exchangers (liquid-cooled) or fans (air-cooled). Verify coolant flow rate and temperature. Check room ambient temperature.
Gradient-related image artifact (banding, geometric distortion)Gradient amp performance degraded — not fully failedRun gradient performance tests. Check eddy current compensation. May require amp recalibration or replacement.
E410 with loud click/bang before faultGradient arc or insulation failure in coil assemblyInspect gradient coil connectors for arcing. Measure coil resistance and insulation resistance. Coil assembly replacement likely needed.
E410 after power outage or surgeBlown fuse or damaged power supply componentInspect gradient power supply fuses. Check DC bus voltage. Test power supply output.

Tip: When troubleshooting E410, always start with the gradient diagnostics in MR Service Desktop before making any physical measurements. The diagnostic output narrows the fault to a specific amp channel or coil axis quickly.
Tip: On liquid-cooled gradient systems, confirm coolant flow and temperature before replacing a gradient amplifier — an overheated amp that looks failed may simply need the cooling system serviced.
Critical: Gradient amplifier DC bus voltages are HIGH VOLTAGE (350–400 VDC). Always allow full discharge after power-off before touching internal amp components. Confirm with a meter before touching.
