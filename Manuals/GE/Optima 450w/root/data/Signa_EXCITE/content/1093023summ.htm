1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while ramping down the magnet:

  • MAKE SURE TO REVIEW AND FULLY UNDERSTAND ALL SUPERCONDUCTING MAGNET PORTIONS, AND

  • MAKE SURE TO FULLY COMPLY WITH ALL REQUIRED ITEMS FOR MAGNET RAMPDOWN TO ZERO FIELD IN THE MAGNET & CRYOGEN SERVICE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS SUBSECTION OF 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting to ramp down the magnet:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
This Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure THE magnet room vent exhaust fan is on (or the hatch is opened if a mobile van) before starting to ramp down the magnet.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.

  • Remove all loose ferromagnetic material from the magnet room.

  • Make sure all equipment and tools used in the magnet room are nonferromagnetic.

  • Locate power supplies as far away from the magnet as the cables and room geometry allows.

  • Do NOT allow anyone carrying ferromagnetic materials (including cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators or steel plates) inside the 5 gauss zone.

WARNING
Explosion Hazard!
Liquid cryogens can liquify atmospheric oxygen, producing a highly combustible oxygen liquid.
No smoking in magnet and cryogen storage rooms.

WARNING
Potential Quench Hazard!
The magnet may quench, rapidly releasing cryogenic gases if THE liquid helium level drops too low during magnet rampdown.
Make sure the magnet IS ≥90% full of liquid helium before starting to ramp down the magnet.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during magnet rampdown.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when ramping down the magnet.

WARNING
Electrical shock hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.
Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other. Make sure input power to the Main Power Supply is disconnected when connecting the Main Lead Extensions.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

NOTICE
Locate the Main Power Supply outside of the Magnet Room.
 
 

NOTICE
The Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage. (See Illustration 2.)

3  Procedure

3.1   Preparation for Rampdown

  1. Top fill the magnet to ≥90% helium level in conformance with Liquid Helium Fill before continuing with this Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field procedure.

  1. Set up MetroLab Teslameter 46-255839P2 and No. 5 Teslameter Probe 46-255839P104 for single-point magnetic field strength monitoring in conformance with Preparations for Field Measurement.

  1. Perform power supply checks in conformance with Main Power Supply Tests. Verify the Main Coil Heater currents are set at 610 mA ± 10 mA.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loto procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Replace the Main Lead Contact Bands in conformance to Main Lead Extension Contact Band Replacement.

  1. Connect the HFO/DVw Switch Heater Cable between the Instrumentation Box (P304) at the magnet and the Main Power Supply (J3).

    (Step Details)

3.2   Resistance Checks

WARNING
Potential Quench Hazard!
The magnet may quench, rapidly releasing cryogenic gases, if the liquid helium level drops too low during magnet rampdown.
Make sure the magnet is ≥90% full of liquid helium before starting this Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loto procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

  1. Make sure the input power cable for the Magnet Power Supply is disconnected and LOTO applied. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Connect the Main Power Supply and Main Lead Extensions to the magnet by making all cable connections in conformance with Connections for Ramping.

  1. Set the Heater 1 Main and Heater 2 Shim Axial switches to the O (Off) position. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Set the Current Adjust and Voltage Adjust controls to zero (fully CCW).

  1. Remove LOTO and connect the input power to the Main Power Supply.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Turning on the Main Heater Switch while performing these resistance checks will cause a magnet quench.
Make sure the Main Heater Switch stays OFF during resistance checks.

  1. Set the Main Power and Power On switches to ON.

  1. Connect a DVM or equivalent measuring device to the end of the Voltage Sense Leads.

  1. Set the Current Adjust Controls on the Magnet Power Supply to maximum (fully CW).

  1. Observe the Digital Current Meter and slowly turn the Voltage Control (CW) to set a 500 A current through the Main Leads, Main Lead Extensions and the persistent Main Switch.

  1. Record the voltage reading on the DVM in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Voltage readings >150 millivolts at 750 amps indicate unacceptable internal contact resistance at the Main Lead Extensions. Higher resistances will add more heat to the magnet, increasing boil-off and possibly causing a quench during ramping.
Make sure the voltage readings are ≤150 mV at 750 amps.

  1. If the DVM reads >150 mV, perform one or more of the following steps to reduce the contact resistance.

    1. Wait approximately one minute with current running, readings may drop as the Main Lead Extensions cool.

    1. Make sure the Hold-Down Tool is properly mounted and tightened.

    1. If the reading still exceeds 150 mV:

      1. Turn the Current Adjust and Voltage Adjust controls to zero (fully CCW).

      1. Tighten the bolts securing the Ramp Cables to the power supply and Main Leads Extensions.

      1. Lift and reseat the Main Leads.

      1. Remove LOTO and reconnect input power to the power supply.

    1. Repeatedly failing the contact resistance check may indicate one of the following:

    1. If changes are made in Step 11, repeat Step 1 through Step 11.

  1. Set the Voltmeter Select Switch to the MAIN POWER SUPPLY position, and increase the current to 750 amps. If the voltage reading at 750 amps is >2.2 volts:

    1. Reduce the voltage/current to zero.

    1. Turn off the Main Power Supply input power, disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply, and verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

    1. Remove, clean and tighten the ramp cable contacts at both the power supply and the Main Lead Extensions. Make sure the proper hardware is used to connect the ramp cables.

    1. Repeat the resistance check in Step 11.

  1. When the resistance check voltage reading in Step 12 is <2.2 volts, turn the Voltage and Current Controls to zero and turn off the power supply.

3.3   Magnet Rampdown

WARNING
Potential Quench Hazard!
Incorrect connection polarity and power supply current settings can cause the magnet's Main Coils to quench and can burn up the power supply when turning on the Main Switch.
Make sure the connection polarity and power supply current settings are the same as last recorded in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

WARNING
Potential Quench Hazard!
If the Axial Shim Heater Switch is not ON during the entire magnet rampdown process, a magnet quench can occur and irreparable damage Shim Coil damage can be done. The power supply will not pass current to the Main Lead Extensions with the Axial Shim Heater off.
Make sure the Axial Shim Heater Switch remains ON during this entire magnet rampdown procedure.

WARNING
Potential Quench Hazard!
The magnet may quench if the Main Power Supply experiences large output fluctuations and/or excessive ripple.
Make sure the Main Power Supply is routinely calibrated at an approved facility.

NOTICE
If a Quench occurs during change of magnetic field, immediately turn the Voltage Adjust and Current Adjust controls to zero.
 
 

NOTE:Ice will build up around the Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool flow holes during the rampdown procedure. Remove ice as needed to maintain helium gas flow through the flow holes.

WARNING
Potential Asphyxiation and Cold Burn Hazard!
Improper engagement/disengagement of the Main Lead Extensions with the Main Lead Pins can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
Insert Main Lead Extensions one at a time and, if the magnet is at field, allow them to cool sufficiently (a fog or water vapor forms around the Lead Extensions) before they contact the Main Lead Pins.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.
Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
while inserting or extracting the Main Lead Extensions:
Do not allow exposed body areas to come in contact with the cryostat or plumbing.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Contact with cold objects is possible during magnet rampdown.
Wear nonabsorbent leather gloves with no holes or tears during insertion or extraction of the Main Lead Extensions.

NOTICE
Replace the Ramp Port Caps immediately after removing Main Lead Extensions to prevent ice build-up inside the Vertical Penetration.
 
 

  1. After ramping down the magnet and disconnecting the main power supply, plug and remove one Main Extension at a time in the following sequence:

    1. Open Valve V2, and bleed vessel pressure down below 1.0 psig. (Do not allow pressure to go below 0.5 psig as read on the magnet pressure gauge or Magnet Monitor.) Close V2.

    1. Remove all ice around the ramp lead port compression fitting on the first Main Lead Extension to be removed. (See Illustration 5.)

    1. Unscrew the ramp lead port compression fitting, and remove the first Main Lead Extension from the magnet. Immediately replace the cap onto the Ramp Lead Port.

    1. Remove all ice around the ramp lead port compression fitting on the other Main Lead Extension.

    1. Unscrew the ramp lead port compression fitting, and remove the second Main Lead Extension from the magnet. Immediately replace the cap onto the Ramp Lead Port.

    1. Install the flow hole screws in the flow hole of both Main Lead Extensions after both leads are removed.

    1. Check the Main Leads and Fill Port Cap for leaks.
