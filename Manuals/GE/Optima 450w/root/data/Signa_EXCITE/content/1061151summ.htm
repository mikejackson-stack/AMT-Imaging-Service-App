Patient Table Reciprocating Pump Assembly Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Reciprocating Pump Assembly

  1. Undock Patient Table and move it out of magnet room.

  1. Remove Upper and Lower Base Covers. Refer to the Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Cover Removal procedure.

  1. Raise Patient Table to full up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK Safety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Release hydraulic pressure by gently pressing Down foot pedal until table is supported by safety latch. See Patient Table Hydraulics Bleeding document for details.

  1. Clamp the Hydraulic Oil line.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a 3/4 inch open-end wrench to disconnect Hydraulic Oil hose connection from pump. Fasten end of hose in upright position to prevent oil from leaking.

  1. Use a 3/4 inch open-end wrench to disconnect both black High Pressure hoses from top and side of pump. See Illustration 2.

  1. Remove clip and pin from front of pump.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove clip and shaft from rear of pump.

  1. Slide pump assembly away from table chassis to remove.

1.2   Installing the Reciprocating Pump Assembly

  1. Slide new pump onto front pin and rear shaft, replacing clips on both ends.

  1. Use a 3/4 inch open-end wrench to reconnect both black High Pressure hoses to top and side of pump.

  1. Use a 3/4 inch open-end wrench to reconnect Hydraulic Oil line to pump.

  1. Remove clamp from Hydraulic Oil line.

  1. Raise the Table Lock Safety Bar to its upright position.

  1. Lower the Patient Table.

  1. Use the Up foot pedal to raise the table to the full up position. Lower table and repeat to allow hydraulic oil to circulate within the pump.

  1. If necessary, add hydraulic oil to reservoir (See Illustration 4. Level should be 6.5 to 12.5 mm above “FULL LINE” when table is at lowest position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pump up the table, only this time count the number of pumps it takes to raise the table. The baseline number is 15 +/- 1 pump.

  1. Replace the Upper and Lower Base Covers.

  1. Move the table back into the magnet room and redock the table.

  1. Perform the Patient Table Hydraulics Bleeding procedure.
