Field Adjustment after Shimming

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing the Field Adjustment after Shimming:

  • MAKE SURE TO REVIEW AND FULLY UNDERSTAND ALL SUPERCONDUCTING MAGNET PORTIONS, AND

  • MAKE SURE TO FULLY COMPLY WITH ALL REQUIRED ITEMS FOR THE FIELD ADJUSTMENT AFTER SHIMMING IN THE MAGNET & CRYOGEN SERVICE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS SUBSECTION OF 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
The Field Adjustment after Shimming Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before starting the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench, rapidly releasing cryogenic gases if its liquid helium level drops too low during the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.
Make sure the magnet is ≥90% full of liquid helium before starting the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Connecting the Main Lead Extensions while the Main Power Supply is on will create a rapid electrical discharge of 100 volts or more.
Make sure input power to the Main Power Supply is disconnected when connecting the Main Lead Extensions.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time, or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field, will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.
Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures while making electrical connections to a main power supply or a shim power supply.

NOTICE
For the MR450w, the DVw Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage. (See Illustration 1.)

3  Procedure

3.1   Resistance Checks

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loTO procedures while making electrical connections to the Main power supply.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply, and verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. If resistance checks were not previously performed on the ramping circuit, connect the Main Power Supply and Main Lead Extensions to the magnet in conformance with Connections for Ramping.

  1. Make sure all power supply Heater Switches are set to “O” (off). (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the Current Adjust and Voltage Adjust controls on the Main Power Supply to zero (fully CCW).

  1. Remove LOTO and connect input power to the Main Power Supply.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Turning on the Main Heater Switch while performing these resistance checks will cause a magnet quench.
Make sure the Main Heater Switch stays OFF during resistance checks.

  1. Turn on the Main Power Supply's Input Power Switches.

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure the Main Heater Switch is off, then turn on the Axial Shim Heaters and observe the current rise in the Main Power Supply Ammeter (610 mA ±10 mA) to verify circuit continuity. If the heater current is not correct, adjust it with the Current Adjust control on the rear of the power supply.

  1. Connect a DVM or equivalent measuring device to the end of the Voltage Sense Leads.

  1. Set the power supply voltmeter toggle switch to the MAIN COIL position.

  1. Set the Current Adjust Coarse Control on the power supply to maximum (fully CW).

  1. Observe the Main Power Supply's Ammeter, and slowly turn the Voltage Adjust Control clockwise to set a 750 amp current through the Main Leads, Main Lead Extensions and persistent Main Switch.

  1. Record the voltage reading on the DVM in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Voltage readings >150 millivolts at 750 amps indicate unacceptable internal contact resistance at the Main Lead Extensions. Higher resistances will add more heat to the magnet, increasing boil-off and possibly causing a quench during ramping.
Make sure the voltage readings are ≤150 mV at 750 amps.

  1. If the DVM voltage is >150 millivolts, perform one or more of the following to reduce contact resistance:

    1. Wait approximately one minute with current running, readings may drop as the Main Lead Extensions cool.

    1. Tighten the nuts (one flat at a time) on top of the Hold-Down Tool while measuring DVM readings.

    1. If the reading still exceeds 150 mV:

      1. Turn the Voltage and Current Adjust controls to zero (fully CCW).

      WARNING
      Electrical Shock Hazard
      Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
      Follow lockout/tagout procedures while making electrical connections to the Main power supply.

      1. Turn off the Magnet Power Supply input power, disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply, and verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

      1. Check and tighten the bolts securing the Ramp Cables to the Power Supply and Main Leads Extensions.

      1. Lift and reseat the Main Leads.

      1. Remove LOTO and reconnect input power to the power supply.

    1. Repeatedly failing the contact resistance check may indicate one of the following:

      (Step Details)

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the power supply's Voltmeter Select Switch to the MAIN POWER SUPPLY position so the voltmeter will display the power supply output to the output lugs. If the voltage exceeds 2.2 V at 750 Amps during the test, repeat Step 13.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the Current Adjust Controls and the Voltage Adjust Control to zero (fully CCW), and continue with Main Field Adjustment.

3.2   Main Field Adjustment

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Incorrect connection polarity and power supply current settings can cause the magnet's Main Coils to quench and can burn up the power supply when turning on the Main Switch.
Make sure the connection polarity and power supply current settings are the same as last recorded in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
If the Axial Shim Heater Switch is not “ON” during the entire Main Field Adjustment process, a magnet quench can occur and irreparable Shim Coil damage can result. The power supply will not pass current to the Main Lead Extensions with the Axial Shim Heater off.
Make sure the Axial Shim Heater Switch remains “ON” during this entire procedure.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench if the Main Power Supply experiences large output fluctuations and/or excessive ripple.
Make sure the Main Power Supply is routinely calibrated at an approved facility.

NOTICE
If a Quench occurs during change of magnetic field, immediately turn the Voltage Adjust and Current Adjust controls to zero.
 
 

  1. Retrieve the last recorded Main Coil Connection Polarity from Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure the Axial Shim Heater is on and the Main Leads are connected with the polarity retrieved in Step 1.

  1. Set the power supply's Voltmeter Select Switch to the MAIN COIL position. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. Set the power supply's Current Adjust Controls to maximum (fully CW).

  1. Adjust the power supply's Voltage Adjust Control to the previous Parking Current.

  1. Turn on the Main Switch Heater.

  1. Make sure the Axial Shim Switch Heater power supply is on and remains on throughout the Main Field Adjustment procedure.

  1. Allow approximately three minutes for the Main Switch to go resistive.

  1. When the Main Switch is resistive, slowly adjust the Voltage Adjust Control until the measured main magnetic field Base Frequency matches the desired Center Frequency.

    (Step Details)

  1. Allow six minutes for the magnetic field to stabilize, then turn off the Main Switch Heater.

  1. Wait a minimum of 15 minutes for the switch to fully cool and go persistent, then record the current value at which the switch went persistent in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

  1. When the switch goes persistent, slowly turn the power supply's Voltage Adjust Control to zero (fully CCW) over a two-minute period.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the Axial Shim Heater.

  1. Gradually turn the Current Adjust Controls to zero (fully CCW) over a one-minute period.

  1. Turn the Main Power Supply off.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow lockout/tagout procedures while making electrical connections to the Main power supply.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply, and verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Improper engagement/disengagement of the Main Lead Extensions with the Main Lead Pins can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
Insert Main Lead Extensions one at a time and allow them to cool sufficiently (a fog or water vapor forms around the Lead Extensions) before they contact the Main Lead Pins.

WARNING
Electrical shock hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.
Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

WARNING
Electrical shock hazard!
while inserting or extracting the Main Lead Extensions:
Do not allow exposed body areas to come in contact with the cryostat or plumbing.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Contact with cold objects is possible during the Field Adjustment after Shimming procedure.
Wear nonabsorbent leather gloves with no holes or tears during insertion or extraction of the Main Lead Extensions.

NOTICE
Follow the next step precisely to avoid an excessive heat load being applied to the magnet cartridge and a possible quench following a magnet ramp.
 
 

  1. After parking the magnet, disconnecting and applying LOTO to input power of the Main Power Supply and verifying that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device, plug and remove one Main Extension at a time in the following sequence:

    1. Open Valve V2, and bleed vessel pressure below 1.0 psig. (Do not allow pressure to go below 0.5 psig as read on the magnet pressure gauge or Magnet Monitor.) Close V2.

    1. Remove all ice around the ramp lead port compression fitting on the Main Lead Extension that is being removed first. See Illustration 4.

      (Step Details)

    WARNING
    Potential Magnet Quench!
    Plugging the flow hole of an engaged Main Lead Extension will cause the magnet to quench.
    Do not insert flow hole screws into Main Lead Extensions until after extensions are removed.

    1. Unscrew the ramp lead port compression fitting, and remove the first Main Lead Extension from the magnet. Immediately replace the cap onto the Ramp Lead Port.

    1. Remove all ice around the ramp lead port compression fitting on the other Main Lead Extension.

    1. Unscrew the ramp lead port compression fitting, and remove the second Main Lead Extension from the magnet. Immediately replace the cap onto the Ramp Lead Port.

    1. Install the flow hole screws in the flow hole of both Main Lead Extensions after both leads are removed.

    1. Check the Main Leads and Fill Port Cap for leaks.

4  Finalization

  1. Disconnect all cables between the magnet and the ramp supply.

NOTICE
Make sure the Ramp Lead Port Caps are closed, sealed and do not leak in order to prevent gaseous helium loss and/or frost and ice formation in the Vertical Penetration.
 
 

  1. Turn off input power to the Ramp Power Supply, disconnect and LOTO all power supply cables, and verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Check the magnet's helium level, and add liquid helium in conformance with Liquid Helium Fill if the level is <60%.
