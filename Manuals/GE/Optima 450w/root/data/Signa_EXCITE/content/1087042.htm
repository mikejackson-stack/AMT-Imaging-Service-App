Lifting Hazard!
The Phantom weight for P/N 2377425-3 is greater than 35 lbs.
The Phantom requires two people working together to safely lift it into place.

3.3  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

For Discovery MR450 and Optima MR450w, the following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: GE_HDx BodyUpper and GE_HDx BodyLower. For HDx, HDxt, and HDe, the following coil configuration names for a single connector must be installed to run this tool: GE_HDx BodyUpper and GE_HDx BodyLower. For HDx, HDxt, and HDe, the following coil configuration names for a dual connector must be installed to run this tool: GE_HDx BodyUpper2 and GE_HDx BodyLower2.

4  Procedure

4.1  TL Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section as shown in Illustration 1 such that the phantoms are well centered on both S/I direction as shown in Illustration 2 and R/L direction as shown with arrows in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 1:  TL Unified Phantoms Over Posterior Section

    Illustration 2:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Posterior S/I Direction

    Illustration 3:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Posterior R/L Direction

  1. Place the anterior section on the phantoms such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in the Illustration 4 and Illustration 5.

    Illustration 4:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Anterior S/I Direction

    Illustration 5:  TL Unified Phantoms Centered on Anterior R/L Direction

  1. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantoms.

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil at the center cross mark as shown in Illustration 6 and advance to scan.

    Illustration 6:  Landmarking Coil

  1. After coil and phantom are properly positioned, perform Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Tool.
