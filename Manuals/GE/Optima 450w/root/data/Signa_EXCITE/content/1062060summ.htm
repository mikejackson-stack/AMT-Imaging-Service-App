RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove side cover and left skirt cover. See Side Cover Removal and Install and Skirt Cover Removal and Install.

  1. Disconnect the RF Body Coil Air Flow Sensor housing connector from the SRI. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. The RF Coil Air Flow sensor head housings are located at approximately the 1 o'clock position on the magnet enclosure as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two (2) screws from each clamp which holds the sensor head in place (see Illustration 4). You may need a screwdriver to separate the clamp from the bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the sensor head from the housing and free the cable from the magnetic enclosure.

  1. Route the new cable through the magnet enclosure and insert the sensor head under the clamp so that the sensor is positioned in the center of the housing cutout. Retighten the two (2) screws.

  1. Reconnect the Sensor housing to the SRI.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace the Skirt Covers and Side Covers following the procedures listed in Step 1 through Step 2 in reverse order.

  1. Ensure that sensors are operating appropriately. See Body Air Flow Functional Check document for details.

  1. Ensure the laser light is aligned. See Laser Light Alignment document for details.
