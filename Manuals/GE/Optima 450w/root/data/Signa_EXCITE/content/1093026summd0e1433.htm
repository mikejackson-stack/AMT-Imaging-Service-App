NOTE:On the first stage, aluminum tape must be used. On the second stage, either aluminum (46-260802P1) or Permacel (46-252282P1) tape can be used.

Aluminum tape 46-260802P1 and Permacel tape 46-252282P1 are 0.5 in. (12.6 mm) wide and must be cut lengthwise into 0.25 in. (6.4 mm) wide strips before usage.
