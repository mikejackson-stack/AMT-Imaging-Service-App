NOTE:If the Scope “Cal Factor” or “Roll off” is not known you can assume its value is “1”. However, for an accurate measurement it is recommended that the Oscilloscope Roll-Off Calibration Procedure, (Characterization for Scopes With Less Than 300 MHz Bandwidth) be performed. Signal “Roll off” is due to the inability of the oscilloscope to measure high-speed pulses. At 51.7MHz (Phosphor Center Frequency), a 100 MHz scope may experience some signal “Roll off”. All scopes have different “roll off” values.
