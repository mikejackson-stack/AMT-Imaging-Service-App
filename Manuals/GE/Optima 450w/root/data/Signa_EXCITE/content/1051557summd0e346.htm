Table 1:  MTM-101 Indicator LEDs

For this connector:This LED on the MTM-101 meter:Indicates the meter is measuring:
ColdheadRuOSecond-Stage Coldhead Temperature (RuO A)
RecondenserRuORecondenser Temperature (RuO B)
RecondenserSI-410D1First-Stage Coldhead Temperature
RecondenserSI-410D2Second-Stage Coldhead Temperature

For example, to measure the first stage coldhead temperature, connect the meter to the recondenser measurement point, and press the green button twice. To measure the second stage coldhead temperature, press the green button a third time.
