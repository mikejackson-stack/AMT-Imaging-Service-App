Module Version  5.0,  Version Date  5/18/09

System Power On

  1. Confirm the main disconnect panel (MDP) locks and tags are in place (see LOTO for the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP)).

    DANGER
    ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
    HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
    USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

    Illustration 1:  MDP

  2. Confirm the MDP voltage selection is correct. The MDP is shipped with the default voltage setting of 480 VAC.

    1. Confirm that the power transformer connections are in the correct configuration for your site.

      Table 1:  Power Transformer Connections

      Incoming Voltage:Transformer Terminal:
      480 VACH4
      380 VACH3
      400 VAC
      420 VAC

    2. If the power connections are not configured properly, have the proper personnel correct the wiring connection. See MDP Operation and Maintenance Manual for additional details.

      Illustration 2:  MDP Control Power Transformer H4 Input Wiring

  3. Confirm the Heat Exchange Cabinet (HEC) voltage selection is correct.

    Illustration 3:  HEC Cabinet

    1. Confirm the switch is in the proper position.

      Table 2:  HEC Voltage Switch Positions

      Incoming Voltage:HEC Voltage Switch Position:
      480 VACUp
      420 VAC
      400 VAC
      380 VACDown
      NOTE:The HEC can operate at 480 VAC or 380 VAC.

    2. If the switch is not in the correct position, change the switch position to the correct setting.

      Illustration 4:  HEC Voltage Selection Switch

  4. Confirm incoming power wiring to the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) , which is located in the PGR cabinet.

    Illustration 5:  PGR Cabinet

    1. Remove the small cover plate which is held in place by two screws.

      Illustration 6:  Input Voltage Select - Small Cover

    2. Confirm that the three input voltage selection terminal blocks are wired in the correct configuration for your site. If the power connections are not configured properly, ensure that the proper personnel corrects the wiring connection. See the Teal PDU Installation and Maintenance manual for additional details.

      Illustration 7:  Input Voltage Selection - Set at 480 VAC

  5. Confirm the overload and short circuit trip settings for the PDU input circuit breaker.

    1. Remove the small cover plate under the main circuit breaker which is held in place by a single screw on each side. Lift open the transparent plastic cover on the lower portion of the circuit breaker to give access to the DIP switches controlling the circuit breaker operation.

    2. Illustration 8 shows the position of the DIP switches for each input voltage selection. Immediately confirm the switches under the letter “L” are configured correctly.

      Illustration 8:  Circuit Breaker DIP Switch Settings

      NOTE:If the DIP switches are not configured correctly, ensure that the proper personnel corrects the DIP switch settings after following the instruction in the Teal PDU Installation and Maintenance manual.

  6. Notify field service and other installation personnel that are working at the installation site that the MDP locks and tags will be removed so that the MDP power can be turned on.

    NOTE:Before powering on HEC, check all plumbing fittings and valves. Verify no leaks after applying power to pumps via HEC. Check back of XRM gradient coil hose fittings for leaks.

    NOTE:Proper installation of XRM cooling hoses: This hose can leak if the hose is pushed into connector before blue lock ring is installed. The preferred method is to first install the blue lock ring. Next, shove the hose into XRM until a “click” is heard. You then know the hose connection is secure.

    NOTE:The blue coolant fluid can easily stain white floors.

  7. Restore power to the MDP, HEC, and PDU (in this order).

  8. Switch on the PDU's main input circuit breaker.

  9. Press EMO RESET button on the PDU front control panel.

    NOTE:In the following step, turn on the GRADIENT 420V POWER CB breaker last.

  10. Except for the Gradient Cabinet power modules, switch all cabinet circuit breakers to ON (GRADIENT 420V POWER CB last). The Gradient Cabinet power module circuit breakers should remain OFF until the room humidity stabilizes under 60%.

  11. Confirm the magnet monitor power is connected to the customer source.

Leak Sensor Functionality Check

WARNING
Electrocution
touching the leak sensor strip when powered on
do not touch the leak sensor strip

This check ensures that the Leak Sensors are functioning appropriately.

  1. Power ON the Gradient 48V switch in the PDU. Ensure that all other circuit breakers are off.

  2. Ensure that the AUX board power is ON.

  3. Put a small drop of water on the leak sensor strip. Make sure you do not physically touch the leak sensor strip while doing this.

    Illustration 9:  Leak Sensor Strip

  4. The PDU Main Circuit Breaker should trip (power down). This ensures that the Leak Sensor is functioning appropriately. If it is not functioning properly, refer to Leak Detection Sensor Theory & Troubleshooting.

  5. Before you power on the PDU, ensure that the Leak Sensor strip is wiped clean and dry.
