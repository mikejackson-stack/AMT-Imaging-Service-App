LPCA Quick Release Module Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrical Hazard!
Strong voltages are present in or around the LPCA when energized.
Remove power from the LPCA at the pdu. then lockout and tagout the pdu.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removal and Replacement of Quick Disconnect Connector

  1. Run LPCA to the rear of the magnet and remove the LPCA cover.

  1. Use sufficient human force to pull the Connector forward, thereby releasing it from its track.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the new Connector onto its track and press firmly until connection is made.

  1. Replace LPCA cover and reconnect to Patient Table.

1.2   Run Coil Datapath Diagnostics

Run the Coil Datapath Diagnostics tool to confirm coil signal.
