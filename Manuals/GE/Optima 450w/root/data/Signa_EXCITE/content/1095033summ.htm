Bore Light Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Front Bore Light Replacement

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Move the patient table away from the magnet.

  1. Remove the bridge assembly. See Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement.

  1. Remove the front end bell and place on the ground. See Front End Bell Removal and Installation.

  1. Remove the two (2) attached screws. See Illustration 1.

  1. Disconnect the bore light assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the bore light assembly, using the two (2) screws.

  1. Connect the cable to the bore light assembly.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the front end bell.

  1. Install the bridge.

  1. Remove the lockout/tagout tag and restore power by turning on the applicable breaker in the PDU.

1.2   Rear Bore Light Replacement

  1. Remove the three (3) attachment screws.

  1. Gently slide the bore light assembly down the inside wall of the rear end bell (this disconnects the bore light assembly from the cable).

    Illustration 2:  Rear Bore Light Assembly

  1. Slide in the new bore light assembly, making sure the cable connections mate to the cable.

  1. Install the bore light assembly.

    (Step Details)
