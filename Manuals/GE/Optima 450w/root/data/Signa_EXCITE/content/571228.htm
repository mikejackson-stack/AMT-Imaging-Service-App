2  Overview

Follow this process to prepare for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HD Body Array Coil by GE/USAI (M3335MC).

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Procedure

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Posterior Section of Coil on Table

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section as in Illustration 1 such that the TL unified phantoms are well centered on both S/I directions as shown in Illustration 2 and R/L direction as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 2:  Phantom Posterior Aligned on S/I Direction

    Illustration 3:  Phantom Posterior Aligned on R/L Direction

  1. Place the anterior section on the TL unified phantoms such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in Illustration 4 and Illustration 5.

    Illustration 4:  Phantom Anterior Aligned on S/I Direction

    Illustration 5:  Phantom Anterior Aligned on R/L Direction

  1. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantoms.

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the center cross mark as shown in Illustration 6 and advance to scan.

    Illustration 6:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test.
