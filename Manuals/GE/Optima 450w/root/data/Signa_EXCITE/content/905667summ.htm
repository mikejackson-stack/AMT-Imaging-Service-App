LOTO Global Operator Console / Operator Workspace

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Visually inspect the LEDs (light emitting diodes) on the GOC assembly and the host LCD (liquid crystal display) display.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) used to ensure Disconnection(s)

One personal lock and tag per FE at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of the equipment. Notify the affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the Operator Workspace, shut down the Host PC. See Startup and Shutdown Procedures: Shutting Down the Host PC.

  1. At the front panel of the PGR (Power, Gradient, RF Cabinet) PDU, switch the Host circuit breaker to the OFF position.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU covering the circuit breaker switches, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE working on the equipment. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove right side cover from Host PC cabinet.

  1. Using a DVM, verify 0VAC at the power strip supplying the cabinet.

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being removed.

  1. Replace the cover on the Host PC cabinet.

  1. Remove the personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PGR PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU, switch the Host circuit breaker to the ON position.

  1. Power up the host PC. See Startup and Shutdown Procedures: Restarting the Host PC.
