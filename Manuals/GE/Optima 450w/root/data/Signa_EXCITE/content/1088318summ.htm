1.5 T MR450 RF Amplifier Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
electric shock hazard!
HIGH voltages are present in the PGR PGU/GRADIENT cabinet.
Lock out and tag out the PGR PGU/Gradient SUBSYSTEM before proceeding.

DANGER
Possible personal injury or equipment damage!
The RF AMPLIFIER could fall while using the hoist.
BEFORE PUTTING weight on the hoist, be sure that the hoist chain links are aligned properly. this will help keep the chain from binding up or becoming kinked, which is nearly impossible to correct when the chain is under tension.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

The PGR PGU Gradient subsystem must be locked out and tagged out before starting this procedure.

-

-

3  Procedure

  1. Lock out and tag out the PGR PGU/Gradient subsystem. See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Disconnect cables from J2, J3, J11, J21, and J22 on the RF amplifier service bracket. See Illustration 1. Move these cables up and to the right of the service bracket.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove eight screws and washers securing the RF amplifier to the cabinet mounting rails. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the RF amplifier and service bracket out of the PGR cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove tie wraps securing MGD/CAM cables to the service bracket.

  1. Cut the cable ties securing the power cable to the cabinet, and remove the power cable from the cabinet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Mount the Hoist Service Kit to the top of the RF side of the cabinet. See Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.

  1. Connect the lifting hooks from the MR Equipment Room Hoist Kit to the service bracket as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the tie wraps from the front of the service bracket. Move the cables going to the front of the MGD to behind the lifting hooks and spreader bar. This allows for the amplifier to be removed and placed on its skid without having to disconnect the cable from the MGD when removing the service bracket from the amplifier in Step 19. (Cables may remain on top of the amplifier at this time.)

  1. Using the hoist, apply tension to the lifting chain until the weight of the amplifier is supported by the hoist.

NOTICE
Tie up the cables going to MGD/CAM to prevent damage to those cables.
 
 

NOTE:It is recommended that cable ties be cut before disconnecting cables from the RF amplifier. This allows the amplifier to be fully removed from the cabinet, making it easier to disconnect cables from the amplifier.

  1. Cut cable ties securing cables to the rear of the service bracket.

  1. From the power distribution unit in the RF side of the cabinet, disconnect the power cable from the mounting studs. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove eight screws securing the service bracket to the slide rails. See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the top of the crate containing the replacement amplifier. The top of the crate for the replacement amplifier becomes the bottom of the crate for returning the failed amplifier.

  1. Place the removed crate top under the failed amplifier with the pads facing the floor. See Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using the hoist, lower the RF amplifier onto the crate top.

  1. Disconnect cables from J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J13, J14, and J18 from the rear of the RF amplifier. See Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the lifting hooks from the service bracket.

  1. Remove the service bracket from the RF amplifier by removing eight screws. See Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the defective RF amplifier you removed away from the cabinet.

  1. Attach the service bracket to the new RF amplifier using eight screws. See Illustration 8.

  1. Connect the lifting hooks to the service bracket. See Illustration 3.

  1. Using the hoist, lift the replacement RF amplifier up to the level where the holes for the rail mounting screws align with the holes on the slide rails.

  1. Route the power cable from the RF amplifier to the cabinet power distribution unit and connect the cable to the mounting studs. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect cables J3, J4, J5, J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J13, J14, and J18 to the rear of the RF amplifier. See Step .

  1. Secure the RF amplifier to the slide rails with eight screws. See Illustration 5.

  1. Remove the lifting hooks from the service bracket.

  1. Remove and stow the Hoist Service Kit. See Hoist Service Kit and Lifting Accessories.

  1. Slide the RF amplifier into the cabinet, and secure it to the cabinet mounting rails with eight screws and washers. See Illustration 2.

  1. Connect cables J2, J3, J13, J22, and J23 to the front of the service bracket. See Illustration 1.

  1. Secure the cables that were tied with cable ties.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PGR PDU/Gradient subsystem. See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Calibrate the RF amplifier. See Body and Head Maximum Power Setup and Calibration.
