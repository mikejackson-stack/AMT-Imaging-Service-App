Save and Restore Tool

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Performing Save Information

  1. Launch the Guided Install.

  1. In the left navigation pane, expand Utilities and select SaveRestore.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Save Information & Save GI].

  1. Insert the media you want to use.

    (Step Details)

  1. In the Dialog window, select the media type, and then click [OK].

    Illustration 2:  Dialog Window

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the SaveInfo is complete, remove the media and write the date and time on it.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Performing Check Information

  1. From the Save/Restore screen, click [Check Information]. See Illustration 1.

  1. Insert the SaveInfo media into the drive.

  1. In the Dialog window, select the media type, and then click [OK].

    (Step Details)

  1. Once verification is complete, when the system asks if you want to view the log, Click [Yes].

    (Step Details)
