4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and latch it to the table.

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms on the center of the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  TL Unified Phantoms on Center

  1. Align the phantoms to the center in S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    Illustration 2:  Alignment in S/I

    Illustration 3:  Alignment in R/L

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom on the CT section and fix the C-Spine Bridge to the hooks provided as shown Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Secure the Large Cylindrical Unified Phantom

  1. Adjust the large cylindrical unified phantom such that it is in complete contact with the TL unified phantom (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  Adjust the Large Cylindrical Phantom

  1. Adjust TL unified phantoms such that both the phantoms are in complete contact with each other as shown in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Adjust the TL Unified Phantoms

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the cross mark line on the C-Spine Bridge (see Illustration 7) and advance to scan.

    Illustration 7:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Perform the MCQA Test.
