2.1   Using Portable RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219

NOTICE
RuO Temperature Monitor readings will increase slightly if the meter is cold. For best results the meter should be at or near room temperature.
 
 

  1. Attach either cable of Portable RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219 to the CAL receptacle located on the upper left side of the monitor as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the [Push To Read] button. The reading for the first cable should be 3.95K to 4.05K.

  1. Attach Portable RuO Temperature Monitor's other cable to the CAL receptacle.

  1. Press the [Push To Read] button. The reading for the second cable should be 3.95K to 4.05K.

WARNING
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
The RuO Temperature Monitor contains ferrous materials. Keep the meter away from the magnet bore.

  1. Place the RuO Temperature Monitor on the magnet's left hand (service) side, close enough to the Coldhead for the monitor's cables to reach the Coldhead's connection ports.

  1. Run the two cables up to the Coldhead box and attach the cables as shown in Illustration 1, connecting to the Coldhead and Recondenser Connection Ports shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Read the Recondenser temperature:

    1. Rotate the switch on the RuO Temperature Monitor to the Recondenser position.

    1. Depress the [Push To Read] button.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds, then record the reading.

  1. To read Recondenser temperature:

    NOTE:Since this reading will vary slightly, record the average of the high and low readings.

    1. Rotate the switch to the Coldhead position, then depress the [Push To Read] button.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds and record the reading.

    1. Wait a few minutes, then depress the [Push To Read] button again.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds.

    1. Average the first and second Coldhead readings.

    (Step Details)

2.3   Using Handheld Magnet Temperature Meter MTM-101 (5119507)

When troubleshooting thermal issues on magnets, use of the MTM-101 meter is mandatory. Measurements made with the MagMon 3 are not accurate enough. There are two connection points to take measurements (see Illustration 6):

  • Coldhead – This point allows the measurement of RuO A (also known as the second-stage coldhead temperature).

  • Recondenser – This point allows the measurement of:

    • RuO B (also known as the recondenser temperature)

    • Shield temperature diode (also known as the first stage coldhead temperature)

    • Second-stage coldhead temperature
