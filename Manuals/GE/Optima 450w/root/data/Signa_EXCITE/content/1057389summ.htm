Ground Leakage Current Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
LIKELY FATAL PERSONAL INJURY!
LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT WITHIN THE MDP EVEN WHEN ALL MDP BREAKERS ARE OFF.
CHECK THAT POWER AT THE MAIN DISCONNECT IS OFF, LOCKED, AND TAGGED BEFORE PROCEEDING.

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Ensure the Ground Resistance Checks are completed and passed

-

-

All cable installation is complete.

-

-

The facility disconnect is off.

-

-

The power distribution unit (PDU) secondary power circuit breakers are off.

-

-

The Main Disconnect of the PDU is on.

-

-

All subsystem cabinet circuit breakers and subsystem power switches are on.

-

-

No hard-wired signal cables are connected to any subsystem cabinets. Fiber optic cables may be connected and not impact this check.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Ground Leakage Current Test Using Dale 600, 600E, 601, or 601E Safety Analyzer

  1. Check that the Main Disconnect and the PDU indicators lights are off and that the power switches are inactive

  1. With a DVM, check that all energy has dissipated.

  1. Connect one clamp-type lead to the EXTERNAL jack on the top of the Dale safety analyzer. Connect the other clamp-type lead to the CHASSIS jack on the top of the Dale safety analyzer (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. On the Dale safety analyzer, set the main selector switch to Leakage mA - External.

  1. Connect the CHASSIS clamp-type lead to the PDU ground bus bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the PDU bus bar, disconnect the ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet under test. (Ground leads on the PDU bus can best be reached by opening the front cover of the PDU. The ground leads can be removed using appropriate deep sockets.)

  1. Connect one of the ground cables disconnected in the previous step to the EXTERNAL clamp-type lead.

  1. Turn on the facility disconnect.

  1. Turn on the main PDU circuit breaker (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. When the mode sequencing is complete (when all relays stop clicking on standard PDU), turn on the circuit breakers labeled GRADIENT 420V POWER 420V/90A and PDM-RF 208V/70A for the gradient cabinet.

  1. Measure the leakage current.

    1. If the subsystem cabinet under test had only one ground connection at the PDU ground bus bar, verify that less than 500 mA is measured. If more than 500 mA is measured, a second ground cable must be added between the subsystem cabinet under test and the PDU ground bus bar. Furthermore, if more than 5000 mA is measured, a serious leakage current problem exists which must be corrected. Use the appropriate procedures to troubleshoot the system.

    1. If the subsystem cabinet under test had two ground connections at the PDU ground bus bar, verify that less than 5000 mA is measured. If more than 5000 mA is measured, a serious leakage current problem exists which must be corrected. Use the appropriate procedures to troubleshoot the system.

  1. Turn the circuit breakers labeled GRADIENT 420V POWER 420V/90A and PDM-RF 208V/70A off.

  1. Turn off the main PDU circuit breaker. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the facility disconnect.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the PDU ground bus bar, reconnect the ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet under test.

  1. Restore the system:

    1. Remove the leads from the Dale safety analyzer.

    1. Turn on all PDU secondary power circuit breakers (SPARE 208V/25A, BRAINWAVE 120V/15A, HOST 120V/20A, SUPERCON SHIM PS 208V/32A, MAGNET PS 208V/32A, GRADIENT 48V CONTROL 208V/10A, and PDM-RECON 208V/20A).

    1. Close the PGR cabinet.

3.2   Ground Leakage Current Test Using Digital Voltmeter (DVM) and 1k Ohm Resistor Procedure

  1. Check that the Main Disconnect and the PDU indicators lights are off and that the power switches are inactive.

  1. With a digital voltmeter (DVM) , check that all energy has dissipated.

  1. Connect the 1k Ohm resistor to the PDU ground bus bar (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the PDU bus bar, disconnect the ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet under test.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a jumper, connect one of the ground cables disconnected in Step 4 to the unconnected end of the 1k Ohm resistor (see Illustration 4).

  1. Connect the DVM across the 1k Ohm resistor (to measure AC volts).

  1. Turn on the facility disconnect.

  1. Turn on the main PDU circuit breaker (see Illustration 2).

  1. When the mode sequencing finishes (when all relays stop clicking on the standard PDU), turn on the breakers marked GRADIENT 420V POWER 420V/90A and PDM-RF 208V/70A.

  1. Measure the voltage across the 1k Ohm resistor.

    1. If the subsystem cabinet under test only had one ground connection at the PDU ground bus bar, verify that less than 500 mV (500 mA) is measured. If more than 500 mV (500 mA) is measured, adding a second ground cable between the subsystem cabinet under test and the PDU ground bus bar is a must. Furthermore, if more than 5000 mV (5000 mA) is measured, a serious leakage current problem exists which must be corrected. Use the appropriate procedures to troubleshoot the system.

    1. If the subsystem cabinet under test had two ground connections at the PDU ground bus bar, verify that less than 5000 mV (5000 mA) is measured. If more than 5000 mV (5000 mA) is measured, a serious leakage current problem exists which must be corrected. Use the appropriate procedures to troubleshoot the system.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the circuit breakers labeled GRADIENT 420V POWER 420V/90A and PDM-RF 208V/70A off.

  1. Turn off the main PDU circuit breaker (see Illustration 3).

  1. Turn off the facility disconnect.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the PDU ground bus bar, reconnect the ground connection(s) of the subsystem cabinet under test.

  1. Restore the system:

    1. Remove the DVM leads.

    1. Remove the 1k Ohm resistor and jumper.

    1. Turn on all PDU secondary power circuit breakers (SPARE 208V/25A, BRAINWAVE 120V/15A, HOST 120V/20A, SUPERCON SHIM PS 208V/32A, MAGNET PS 208V/32A, GRADIENT 48V CONTROL 208V/10A, and PDM-RECON 208V/20A).

    1. Close the PDU cabinet.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform a Check Scan to ensure the system is ready for patient scanning.

  1. Return to the Grounding and Leakage Current Checks or to the PAC Assembly Leakage Current Test.
