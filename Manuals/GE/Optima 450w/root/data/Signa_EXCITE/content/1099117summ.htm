1.5T Phased Array Shoulder Coil Cable Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the coil covers by taking out the screws located in the covers (six on right side, three on left).

  1. Remove the cover from the shoulder coil. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two screws that hold the cable clamp in position (cable is captured beneath). See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the three PCX connectors for RF & DC connectors located on the Feed boards for CH 1, CH 2 & CH 3.

  1. Remove the cable assembly from the coil.

  1. To install the new cable assembly, first connect BRN-WHT DC connector & CH 2 RF PCX connectors first, before Cable Clamp is attached & screws tightened. To install the clamp use the two screws removed in Step 3.

  1. Connect the PCX & DC connectors to appropriate feed boards, namely, Clear RF & RED-BLK DC connectors to Ch 1 & GRY RF & BLU-YEL DC to Ch 3 (Ch 2 RF & DC connection already completed at previous step).

  1. Install the covers and replace the screws removed in Step 1. Make sure to capture the strain relief inside of the covers.

    (Step Details)
