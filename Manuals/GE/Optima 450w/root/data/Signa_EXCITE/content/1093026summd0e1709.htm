NOTE:Gap adjustments can be made before fully tightening the mounting bolts by using two flat-head screwdrivers wedged between the transition and sleeve flanges.

Gap adjustments can also be made by temporarily removing the desired mounting bolt, inserting and fully threading a M10 x 50 mm (2 in.) long hex-head bolt through the transition flange and using that bolt like a jack screw against a flat washer on the sleeve flange to wedge open the gap slightly. (See Illustration 26.)
