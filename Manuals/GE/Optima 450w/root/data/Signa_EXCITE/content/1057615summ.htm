1  Procedure

1.1   Adjusting Patient Table for Floor Levelness

  1. Dock the Patient Table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lock the rear caster brakes.

  1. Raise the patient table to a fully-raised position, and then check levelness from front-to-back along the top right or left edge of the bridge and from left-to-right across the front of the bridge using a non-ferrous level.

    (Step Details)

1.2   Checking Caster Height Adjustment

During installation, this procedure describes how to “check” the caster height (factory default) settings. If the patient table requires adjustment, go to the next procedure “Leveling the Patient Table.”

  1. To help keep track of measurement data, temporarily label each caster as 1, 2, 3, and 4. Based on the Caster Wheel Labels in Illustration 1, we suggest you label the steering caster (front left facing the magnet) as 1, and proceed labeling in a clockwise direction.

  1. Remove the lower side covers and front and rear caster assembly covers. For more information on removing the side covers, see Patient Table Cover Removal.

  1. At each of the four caster wheel assemblies, measure the height from the floor to the bottom edge of the aluminum support casting (see Illustration 2). Record the values for each caster in the table below.

    (Step Details)

1.3   Leveling the Patient Table

While all four casters look identical from an exterior perspective, there is one notable difference regarding design when observing each caster assembly inside the caster covers. The Patient Table front left caster is known as the steer lock wheel, whose caster assembly design differs from the other three casters. It has a triangle shaped bracket

  1. Undock Patient Table and position it 1-2 inches in front of the dock to perform work as close to the “docked” position as possible.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disengage rear caster brake lock pedal.

  1. At the desired caster that requires adjustment, remove the caster linkage pin using a 10 mm wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the collar bolt using an 11 mm wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the collar down the shaft so it rests on top of the caster shaft housing.

  1. Looking from a top-down perspective, move the Wheel Lock Lever to the Full Lock position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn the caster clockwise (raises the table) or counterclockwise (lowers the table) to make adjustments.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
PER DESIGN SPECIFICATION, DO NOT EXCEED A RAISING OR LOWERING ADJUSTMENT GREATER THAN 1/4 INCH (6 mm). IN OTHER WORDS, DO NOT EXCEED MORE THAN 6 REVOLUTIONS IN EITHER DIRECTION.
 
 

  1. Remeasure the caster height and record the value in Table 1.

  1. Perform Steps 3 to 6 in reverse order to reassemble the caster bolt and linkage pin.

  1. Once all casters have been adjusted, re-measure the table levelness and adjust the casters again as necessary by repeating this procedure.

  1. Once table is level, reattach the caster assembly covers and lower side covers, and then redock the Patient Table.
