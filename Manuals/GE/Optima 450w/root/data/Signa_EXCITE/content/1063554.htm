Planned Maintenance Schedules

Planned Maintenance (PM) was developed to use system access time as productively as possible. System access time is the time the customer makes the system available to GE for service. PM contains most of the tasks required, written as PM procedures. Many proven, time-saving steps were incorporated to reduce the amount of time needed to complete the recommended tasks. PM does not include time spent performing tasks that can be done while the customer is scanning patients. System access time is extremely valuable. In addition to accomplishing PM tasks, FMIs and deferred maintenance can also be performed.

The recommended PM tasks require approximately 18 hours per year with a four list, annual schedule. This time estimate includes allowances for certain optional equipment, which some sites do not have or which GE may not have service responsibility for. It does not allow any additional time for customer-deferred maintenance, or the corrective maintenance of any issues discovered during these PM checks. Additional time should be scheduled (either separately or in conjunction with a PM visit) for corrective maintenance.

The recommended PM items on each list can be completed on any time schedule to best accommodate a customer’s individual requirements. Customers wishing the least number of interruptions of their scanning schedule may want GE to perform all checks during a single extended site visit, four times per year. Other customers, desiring shorter and more frequent site visits by their Field Engineers, may find it advantageous to have a portion of the checks accomplished at each visit. All of the checks on each checklist should be completed within the prescribed timeframe to ensure optimal system performance and reliability.
