Skirt Cover Removal and Installation

1  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

3  Procedure

3.1   Remove Skirt Covers

  1. Remove the side cover (see Side Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. The skirt covers are held into place using poppers and screws. There are two screws and one popper securing each skirt cover to the magnet. Unscrew the screws from the skirt cover that is being removed.

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove a skirt cover simply slide it towards the front (or, in the case of the back covers, to the rear) of the magnet (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

3.2   Install Skirt Covers

  1. Align fastener on skirt cover and slide cover towards the center of the magnet.

  1. Align tabs so they interlock with the tabs on the adjoining skirt cover (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Snap popper on the skirt cover into place and replace the two (2) screws (see Illustration 6).

    (Step Details)
