5  Environmental Requirements

Storage Requirements

Store the coil in an air-conditioned scan room or equipment room. To store the coil, a storage space of (55cm) x (45cm) x (35cm) is required.

The equipment should be transported and stored under the following conditions:

  • Ambient temperature range of +5° C to +65° C.

  • Relative humidity range of 10% to 100%, non-condensing.

  • Atmospheric pressure range of 500 hPa to 1060 hPa.
