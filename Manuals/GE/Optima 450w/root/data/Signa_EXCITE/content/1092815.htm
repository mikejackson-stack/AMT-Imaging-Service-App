3.3  Safety

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Inadvertently pressing the MRU RUNDOWN button on any MRU connected to the magnet will quench the magnet, resulting in the rapid expulsion of helium.
DO NOT PRESS THE RUNDOWN BUTTON DURING MRU FUNCTIONAL CHECKS.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with MRU wiring while power is supplied can cause electrical shock.
Disconnect input power to the MRU being checked, and follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures while performing yearly MRU check.

4  Procedure

4.1  Weekly MRU Checks

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
RELAY MAY STILL BE ACTIVE.
do not connect the MRU CABLE TO THE MAGNET UNTIL 3 MINUTES AFTER activating AC POWER TO THE MRU.

  1. Verify that the green Charger Power LED is illuminated. (See Illustration 1.)

    Illustration 1:  Magnet Rundown Unit

  1. Depress the [Test Battery] button, and the green Test Battery LED should illuminate.

  1. Place the Test Heater Switch in the A position, and the green Heater Test LED should illuminate.

  1. Place the Heater Tester Switch in the B position, and the green Heater Test LED should light.

  1. If the Heater Test LED does not illuminate, depress the [Test Heater LED] button to verify that the LED is functioning.

    NOTE:See Magnet Electrical Checks for the Main Switch Heaters continuity check.

4.2  Yearly MRU Check

Perform yearly MRU checks in conformance with the vendor manual.

  1. Perform weekly checks in conformance with Weekly MRU Checks, Weekly MRU Checks.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with MRU wiring while power is supplied can cause electrical shock.
Disconnect input power to MRU being checked and Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures while performing yearly MRU check.

  1. Check each installed MRU:

    1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the MRU being checked. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

    1. Open the MRU front cover, and disconnect the 4-pin Lemo Connector (P2) from the Connector J3 behind the MRU cover.

      NOTE:See Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Installation for a dual MRU block diagram and Magnet System Interconnects for wiring diagrams.

    1. Remove LOTO, and connect input power to the MRU being checked.

    1. Perform yearly MRU checks in conformance with the vendor manual.

    1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the MRU being checked. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

    1. Reconnect the 4-pin Lemo Connector (P2) to Connector J3 behind the MRU cover, and close the MRU front cover.

    1. Remove LOTO, and connect input power to the MRU being checked.
