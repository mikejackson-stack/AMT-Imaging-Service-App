Upper and Side Wide-Open Enclosure Removal

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

NOTICE
Only use non-ferromagnetic tools when working in the magnetic field of a ramped magnet.
 
 

  1. Remove Item 1 of Illustration 1 first. There are typically two, each held in place by Velcro.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Item 2 of Illustration 1. It is held in place by two screws in the Top Side Bracket, one on each side of the magnet enclosure. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove Item 3 of Illustration 1 from lower portion of the magnet. It is supported on hangers inserted into the Bottom Side Bracket, which is shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Loosen two screws on the Bottom Side Bracket, shown in Illustration 2, before attempting to remove Illustration 1's Item 4. This will relieve pressure on the lower end of Item 4.

NOTICE
Cables may be attached to the inside of Illustration 1's Item 4. Care must be taken not to damage the cables if removal of Item 4 is necessary.
 
 

  1. Remove Illustration 1's Item 4. It is held in place by four bolts along the Top Side Bracket, shown in Illustration 2. Rotate the enclosure piece away, pulling up to remove.

  1. Remove the frame section (Illustration 1-3's Item 5) just below the Coldhead guard. Two bolts fasten the frame section at either end.

    (Step Details)

2  Finalization

  1. When service or replacement of magnet components is complete, reassemble the side & upper enclosure components in reverse order of removal.
