LCD Monitor Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Remove the new LCD monitor from its box and its packing and set the LCD color monitor on the operator workspace table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the video and power cables. See Illustration 1, Illustration 2, and Illustration 3. Route these cables neatly to the back of the monitor base and the operator's workspace.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the safety lockout of the operator's workspace and restore power to the system.

  1. Turn on the monitor by pressing the power switch on the back of the monitor (see Illustration 1 or Illustration 2). Check that the main LCD monitor power is on. If not, press the Power On/Off button on the front panel (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the monitor no closer than 16 inches and no further then 28 inches from the eyes. The optimal distance is 24 inches for either of the monitors.

    (Step Details)

  1. Boot up the GOC and enter sdc for the login.

  1. Enter password adm2.0 and press <Enter>.

  1. Open Guided Install and click System Configure under Basic Configuration

  1. Refer to Illustration 5 to make sure of the following:

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Configure].

  1. Adjust the screen of the monitor to suit the preference of the customer. For details and recommended settings, see LCD Monitor Screen Adjustments.
