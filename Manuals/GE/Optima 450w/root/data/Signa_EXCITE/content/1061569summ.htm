Dock Motor Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD!
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE servicing and/or maintaining.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THIS PROCEDURE REQUIRES REMOVAL OF FERROUS MATERIAL IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO THE MAGNET.
Therefore, two Field engineers are required at all times.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Dock from Magnet Room

  1. Perform Lock Out Tag Out.

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it away from the magnet.

  1. On the magnet, remove the right and left side skirt cover. For more information, see the Skirt Cover Removal and Install procedure.

  1. Disconnect all cable connections from the magnet to the rear of the dock.

  1. At rear of the dock, use a 3/4 inch non-ferrous wrench to remove four (4) nuts (2 on each side) from dock bracket bolts. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
back injury
dock is heavy, weighing more than 50 lbs, and it contains ferrous material, making it strongly attracted to the magnet.
two people are necessary to move dock out of magnet room to perform maintenance. FROM THIS POINT in the PROCEDURE, DOWNWARD PRESSURE MUST BE CONTINUOUSLY APPLIED TO THE DOCK SO AS TO PREVENT IT FROM BEING PULLED INTO THE MAGNET BORE.

  1. Using two people, remove the dock from the magnet room. Refer to instructions in LCC Dock Replacement for proper maneuvering of the dock when removing from the magnet.

    1. With two field engineers, one holding each side of the dock, slowly and deliberately slide the dock away from the magnet, keeping downward pressure on the dock. Move it to a distance equivalent of where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.

    1. When a safe distance away from the magnet is achieved, both field engineers may slowly lift the dock from the floor and exit the room.

  1. Proceed to Removing the Dock Motor.

1.2   Removing the Dock Motor

  1. Using a 3/4 inch wrench, remove four bolts (2 on each side) to unfasten the dock cover from the dock base. See Illustration 1.

  1. While removing the dock cover disconnect the switch connector.

  1. Carefully remove the dock cover from the dock base and then turn the dock cover upside down.

    (Step Details)

  1. Use a standard head screwdriver to disconnect the dock motor power cable, terminal wires, and ground wire.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 3/16 inch ball-end hex key, remove 4 screws (2 each side) from the dock motor mount plate.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove dock motor.

NOTICE
DO NOT DISCARD OLD DOCK MOTOR. YOU ARE GOING TO REUSE THE DOCK GEARBOX SHAFT FROM THE OLD DOCK MOTOR.
 
 

  1. Go to Installing the Replacement Dock Motor for instructions on how to reuse the coupling and install the new dock motor.

1.3   Installing the Replacement Dock Motor

  1. Using the blade of a slotted screwdriver, remove the e-clip from the end of the dock gearbox shaft on the old dock motor. Secure the e-clip during removal, to prevent the clip from flying away when pressured.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the dock gearbox shaft and key out of the dock motor and set it aside. Remove four screws and mounting plate. See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. On the new dock motor, use Loctite 242 Threadlocker on the hex screws as shown in Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the Mount Plate on the dock motor and fasten with four screws using a 5 mm hex key.

  1. If replacing the shaft only, apply a small amount of anti-seize (from shaft assembly FRU kit) to the shaft key. If the complete Dock Motor Assembly is being replaced, this step is not required.

  1. Assemble the drive coupling on new shaft, then slide the new gearbox shaft, with spring and key, into the motor and replace the e-clip. See Illustration 5.

  1. Use Loctite 242 Threadlocker on the four Mount Plate screws, position the dock motor onto the dock cover and then re-attach the dock motor. See Illustration 3.

  1. Reconnect the terminal wires, ground wire, and power cable to original terminal strips. See Illustration 2.

  1. Place the dock cover onto the dock base and reattach dock cover bolts. See Illustration 1.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THIS PROCEDURE REQUIRES REMOVAL OF FERROUS MATERIAL IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO THE MAGNET.
Therefore, two Field engineers are required at all times.

  1. Move dock back into magnet room.

    1. With two field engineers, one holding each side of the dock, enter the magnet room and place the dock on the floor where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.

    1. With two field engineers, one applying vertical downward pressure to each side of the dock, slide the dock toward the magnet front.

  1. Reattach nuts to dock bracket. See Illustration 1.

  1. Reconnect all cables from rear of dock to magnet I/O panels.

  1. Replace magnet right and left side skirt covers.

  1. Redock the Patient Table.
