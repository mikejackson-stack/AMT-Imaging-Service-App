1. If using the newer Oxygen Monitor Calibration Kit 2173689, proceed as follows:

    1. Connect one end of the hose from the calibration kit to the 17% regulator hose barb. (Leave the other end of the hose unattached at this time, so that the hose can be purged when the gas is turned on.)

      NOTE:Adjust the valves slowly and only use low pressure. Adjusting the valves too fast or adjusting to excessive pressures may damage the sensor cell.

    2. With the 17% regulator pressure adjust valve closed (turned completely counterclockwise) and the regulator toggle valve closed, open the 17% gas cylinder valve. The inlet pressure gauge will show the cylinder pressure.

    3. Open the 17% regulator toggle valve, and slowly adjust the regulator pressure adjust valve for an outlet pressure of one or two PSIG as indicated on the outlet pressure gauge.

    4. Connect the other end of the hose to the hose barb on the Remote Sensor Module.

  2. If using the older Oxygen Monitor Calibration Kit 46-271523G1, have a second FE available to expose the sensor to 17% oxygen. If the 17% oxygen cylinder is not available:

    1. Have the second FE inhale and hold breath for 10 seconds.

    2. Have that person exhale on the sensor, then cover the sensor with a hand.
