3  Procedure

  1. Remove the coil covers by taking out the screws located in the covers (six on right side, three on left).

  1. Remove the cover from the shoulder coil. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Shoulder Coil Cover Removed

  1. Remove the two screws that hold the cable clamp in position (cable is captured beneath). See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Cable Clamp Screws

  1. Remove the three SMB connectors located on the tuning boards (TP1, TP2, TP3).

  1. Remove the cable assembly from the coil.

  1. To install the new cable assembly, start by capturing the cable beneath the cable clamp and install the two screws removed in Step 3.

  1. Connect the SMB connectors to the correct PCB (Black RF-TP1, Silver RF-TP2, Brown RF-TP3).

  1. Install the covers and replace the screws removed in Step 1. Make sure to capture the strain relief inside of the covers.

    Illustration 3:  Coil Cover Re-Installed
