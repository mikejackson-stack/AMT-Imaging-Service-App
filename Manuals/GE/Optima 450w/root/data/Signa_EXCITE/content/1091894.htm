2  Overview

After the magnet is converted to its operating condition, it is important to identify and eliminate any leak at installation.

NOTE:Cryostat pressure can increase up to 5 psig during operation, thereby increasing any leakages. A leak will slowly deplete the helium supply, possibly causing cryopumping or icing, and may interfere with Recondenser operation.

4  Procedure

  1. Make sure all hospital personnel and contractors in the area know that the magnet is being commissioned.

  1. Secure the area from unauthorized personnel. Post signs outside the area alerting of magnet commissioning.

  1. Post Authorized Personnel Only (2289812) signs at the MR suite entrance.

  1. Secure magnet room doors in an open position. Make sure there is ventilation through the magnet room flowing towards unoccupied areas.

  1. Allow the cryostat to build pressure prior to operating the cryocooler coldhead.

  1. When the cryostat pressure reaches 5 psig, check the following areas for leaks using a Snoop brand or leak detector liquid:

    • All Plumbing Joints

    • Vertical Turret and Instrumentation Lead Cover

    • Ramp Lead and Fill Port Caps

    • 360° Around Burst Disc to Plenum Contact

    • Pressure Gauge and Relief Valves

    NOTE:Commercial leak detector fluids or instruments available through HVAC distributors can be used to determine small leaks in the helium plumbing and venting.

  1. Repair any leaks found prior to commissioning the magnet.
