Magnet Microphone Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Replacing Rear Microphone

Prior to installing a replacement microphone, plug the replacement into the SRI (J31) to verify that the replacement microphone will address the issue.

  1. Position the LPCA to the far rear of the enclosure.

  1. Follow all Lock Out/Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. (See LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics.)

  1. To remove the LPCA Cover, remove the four (4) screws shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull the microphone and housing out of the LPCA. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the microphone from its housing by gently pulling microphone to the rear. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. To remove and install rear microphone, refer to LPCA Cable Take-up Replacement to replace the Cable Track.

    (Step Details)

  1. The rear microphone attaches to the SRI (located on the side of the magnet) at J31. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the Microphone into the housing.

  1. Install the Microphone and housing into the LPCA.

NOTE:Take care that the receive cables are tucked in well, so as to not nick the exposed insulation on the cable track wires.

  1. Install the LPCA cover and 4 (four) screws.

1.2   Removal and Installation of Front Microphone

  1. Follow all Lock Out/Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. (See LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).)

  1. Remove the Split Bridge. (See Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement.)

  1. Disconnect the other end of microphone cable at J32 located on the SRI. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Remove the front end bell, and handle the microphone cabling carefully while pulling it out. (See Front End Bell Removal and Installation.)

  1. Detach the microphone cable from the front cover.

  1. Carefully open the fasteners, and detach the microphone cable from the front cover.

  1. Align the new microphone assembly in the rectangular cut-out located under the foam of the front end bell. (See Illustration 5.)

    1. Place a 6 mm diameter pin through the end bell hole for the microphone to a 4mm distance above nominal finished surface.

    1. Locate the microphone assembly over the pin, and glue in place with Plexus MA300 or MA310 adhesive.

    (Step Details)

  1. Glue all around the microphone assembly bottom plate only. Remove the pin when the bonding agent has cured.

  1. Perform Step 1 through Step 4 in reverse order to complete the procedure.
