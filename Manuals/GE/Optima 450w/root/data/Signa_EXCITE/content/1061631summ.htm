Patient Table Cable Take-up Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Patient Table Cable Take-up

  1. Undock Patient Table and move it out of magnet room.

  1. Depending on which Cable Take-up (left or right) you are replacing, remove the appropriate Lower Base Side Cover, and then fasten the Upper Bellows in the up position. For more information, see the Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal procedure.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar, and then lower the table to ensure weight is resting on Safety Bar.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove bottom front cover (located on the front underside of the Patient Table, see Illustration 3) and the right or left table side cover (see Illustration 4) depending on which cable track is being removed.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the gray TBL P3-J7 or P4-J7 cable from the Touch and Go connector located in the upper left or right corner inside the Table Top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Manually unlock the cradle and move it forward approximately 24 inches to clear the guide rails.

    (Step Details)

  1. Prop up the rear of the cradle using two blocks of wood each approximately 10 inches in length.

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a 9/64 Allen wrench, one person removes the screws from (in order) the cable cover, coil connector, and balun for both cable take-ups (located on the rear underside of the cradle) while the second person holds onto the front of the cradle to make sure it doesn’t slide off the table top.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the cradle from the Patient Table and set it on a flat surface.

  1. Depending on which take-up you are replacing, go to the left or right side of the front lower table chassis and disconnect all take-up connectors from the rear side of the Table Interface Panel (see Illustration 8 and Illustration 13).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Step 11 coincides with the P4 Cable Take-up only. If replacing the P3 Cable Take-up, skip Step 11.

  1. To remove the main cradle latch cable, which is threaded through the P4 take-up track, do the following:

    1. Using an 5/64 Allen wrench, loosen set screw at Down Link Clevis and then unthread cable.

      (Step Details)

    1. Pull cable through to clear the back side of the cable track and underside of cable take-up lead.

      (Step Details)

  1. Remove both cable track end screws from Table Interface Plate Connector.

    (Step Details)

  1. Follow cable track up to underside of Table Top and pull out disconnected gray P3-J7 or P4-J7 cable (previously disconnected in Step 6).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove cable take-up lead brass screws. See Illustration 14.

  1. Remove two slotted brass screws from cable take-up lead in cable well.

    (Step Details)

  1. Pull rear of cable take-up and position it to lay out at front of Patient Table to expose cover plate and screws. Remove both screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. To fully remove the cable take-up assembly from the Patient Table, feed the cable take-up assembly up through the hole in the Table Top.

1.2   Replacing the Patient Table Cable Take-up

  1. Place the new cable-take up assembly into the Table cable track well and feed the take-up through the Table hole.

  1. Secure cover plate with two brass screws. See Illustration 16.

  1. Lay out cable take-up assembly to point to rear of Patient Table to allow access to the cable-take up lead.

  1. Secure cable take-up lead to cover place with two brass screws.

  1. At underside of Table Top, secure cable take-up lead to table. See Illustration 14.

  1. Reroute and reconnect P3-J7 or P4-J7 Touch and Go gray cable.

    (Step Details)

  1. For P4 cable take-up replacement only, reroute and reconnect main cradle latch cable to Down Link Clevis and at end of this procedure, make appropriate adjustments to cable per instructions in Section 3.2 Cradle Release Block Retraction Adjustment, Cradle Release Block Adjustment .

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect all cables on the Table Interface Panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. Secure cable take-up lead brass screws onto Table Interface Panel. See Illustration 13.

  1. If replacing the P4 cable take-up assembly, then replace the upper chassis rail front and rear 3/8 nut and washer. See Illustration 9.

  1. Place cradle back onto Patient Table and prop up with two blocks of wood (see Illustration 7).

  1. On the rear underside of the cradle, replace (in order) the balun, coil connector, and cable cover for each cable take-up. Remove the blocks of wood and set cradle back onto Table Top at the home position.

  1. Replace Front Bottom Cover and Table Side Cover.

  1. Ensure that Lock Safety Bar is in the vertical (up) position.

  1. Move Patient Table into magnet room and dock.

  1. Make Main Cradle Latch cable adjustments as identified in Step 7 if P4 cable take-up was replaced

  1. Undock Patient Table and use non-ferrous Phillips screwdriver to replace lower base side cover, and then place Upper bellows in down position.

  1. Redock Patient Table.
