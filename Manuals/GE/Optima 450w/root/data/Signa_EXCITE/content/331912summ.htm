3  Procedure

3.1   Cryocooler Compressor Installation

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow loto procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

  1. Unpack and install the Compressor in conformance with the Compressor vendor's service manual.

    1. Note any visible damage and notify the Shipper.

    1. LOTO input power to the Compressor immediately after unpacking the Compressor.

    1. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Position the Compressor in the Equipment Room as required in the site plan. Refer to the site planning manual.

NOTICE
Observe the following:

  • Do NOT connect the flexlines until the system gas pressure check is performed to prevent line contamination.

  • If using the Air-Cooled Compressor M29762SS and the GE Main Disconnect Panel (MDP), the Step-Down Transformer M3335TZ MUST be used since the input voltage for the air-cooled compressor is 200 VAC for three-phase power. Install the step-down transformer in conformance with the vendor manual.

 
 

  1. Remove LOTO, and connect the input power cable in conformance with the vendor manual and the local electrical code.

3.2   System Gas Pressure Check

  1. Check and adjust the Cryocooler system static gas pressure in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments.

3.3   Cooling Water Supply Connection

WARNING
Poison Hazard!
Antifreeze is poisonous to aquatic, animal and human life. Because of its sweetness, animals are drawn to it. Consequently, it is classified as hazardous both for shipment and as a waste and not shipped by untrained people.
Cryocooler Compressors, which contain antifreeze, must be drained before being returned to the supplier. Make arrangements with the hospital maintenance department for disposal.

  1. Verify that the cooling water supply meets all requirements specified in the vendor's service manual.

  1. Connect the water supply hose to the cooling water inlet connection, and secure with a worm-drive screw-type hose clamp or similarly secure connection.

  1. Similarly connect and secure the water return hose to the cooling water outlet connection of the Compressor.

  1. Turn on the cooling water supply, and allow air to purge from the system.

  1. Check the system for water leaks.

3.4   F-50 Compressor Water-Flow Adapter

The recently released F-50 compressors allow greater water flow than the CSW-71D. This may sometimes trigger a high water-flow alarm. The water chillers that are hooked up to cool F-50 compressors often cannot be adjusted to compensate. A water-flow adapter (GE P/N 5335002) addresses the issue. The adapter will need to be installed only on the inlet coolant port of the F-50 compressor as described below.

  • The water-flow adapter has a Japanese standard male PT connection on one end that comes pre-wrapped with Teflon® tape, which is threaded into the compressor.

    NOTE:Although similar, PT thread is not the same as NPT thread. Be sure to use only Sumitomo-supplied parts and fittings.

  • The other end of the water-flow adapter has a Japanese standard female PT connection thread that will attach directly to the hose barb fitting supplied by Sumitomo.

  1. Locate the coolant (Water Inlet) port on the front face of the compressor.

  1. Insert the water-flow adapter, thread the male end into the coolant inlet port of the compressor, and tighten as appropriate to prevent leaks.

    (Step Details)

  1. Thread the hose barb fitting (shown below) into the water-flow adapter, and tighten appropriately to prevent leaks.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the water-flow adapter installation is completed, all coolant hoses must be connected appropriately and checked for leaks.

3.5.2  Flexible Gas Line Connections

WARNING
Potential Hazard!
Flexlines with Broken Aeroquip fittings may disconnect and move erratically.
Do not put any bending force on Aeroquip fittings while connecting/disconnecting helium flexlines. A bending force can result in bending or breakage of the Aeroquip stems.

NOTICE
Do NOT put any bending force on Aeroquip fittings while connecting/disconnecting helium flexlines. A bending force will create difficulty in the rapid engagement/disengagement required to prevent helium loss and system contamination.

  • Support the gas lines when connecting or disconnecting to the Coldhead at the top of the magnet or to the Compressor.

3.6   Cryocooler Leak Test

  1. If helium pressure continues to drop after increasing the helium charge, remove the flexlines from the Compressor using the two-wrench technique shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect a regulator, charging line and manifold, and purge in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments.

  1. Connect one of the flexlines to the appropriate fitting on the manifold, and pressurize to approximately 100 psig.

  1. Reduce the supply pressure using the regulator control, and observe whether the reduced pressure is maintained on the manifold pressure gauge.

    (Step Details)

  1. Maintain 100 psig with the regulator, and check the line with a liquid soap solution or a commercial leak-testing solution, such as Leak-Tec.

  1. If the line has a leak, notify Installed Base Support at 843-664-1715 to obtain a replacement. Leave a message if no one answers.

  1. Repeat Step 3 through Step 6 for the other gas line.

3.9   Monitoring Cryocooler Temperatures

RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219 (included in RuO Temperature Monitor Kit 2183710) is used to monitor the second-stage RuO temperature and the mating Recondenser surface temperature on a Cryocooler Coldhead during installation and troubleshooting of the Coldhead. During operation, second-stage Coldhead temperature is monitored indirectly by monitoring the silicone diode temperature of the Recondenser at the System Monitor Cabinet. First-stage Coldhead temperature is monitored indirectly by monitoring the silicone diode Coldhead Sleeve temperature at the System Monitor Cabinet.

NOTE:Magnet Monitor (2209000) or Magnet Monitor 3 (2394952) can be used to monitor cool down and temperature trends, but does not give accurate temperature readings at 4.2K.

3.10   Setting Coldhead Tension

When the Cryocooler Coldhead cools down during magnet installation, or when it is turned on after being shut off for weeks, it will contract within the Coldhead Sleeve, and the mounting bolts will need checking for tightness to make sure sufficient contact is made at the first and second-stage Heat Stations.

  1. Monitor the Cryocooler Coldhead first and second-stage heat station temperatures in conformance with Monitoring Cryocooler Temperatures until they stabilize.

  1. Check the Coldhead mounting bolts during cool down. Make sure they are hand tight.

  1. When Coldhead temperatures have stabilized, verify that the Belleville washer gap settings are within the specified range of 0.003 to 0.005 in. (0.08 to 0.13 mm).

(0.13 mm), tighten the Coldhead mounting bolts in the staggered bolt pattern (shown above) until even gaps within specification are set for all bolts.
