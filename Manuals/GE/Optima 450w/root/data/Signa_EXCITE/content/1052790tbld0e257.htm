Table 1:  Diagnostic Results

ResultsDescriptionGE Field Action
IDEC and EDEC passed.The VRF board is OK.No further action required.
IDEC passed, but EDEC failed.The VRF link module may have problems.
  1. Check the dual fiber-optic service loopback connector. When conducting the External Clock tests, ensure that the connector is fully connected to the socket on the ICN, then retry this diagnostic.
  2. If retry fails, replace the ICN following ICN Replacement procedure.
IDEC failed, but IDIC passed.The clock input to VRF may have problems.Check the clock cable and Excite clock module.
IDEC and IDIC failed.VRF clock module may have problems.
  1. Check the single fiber-optic clock cable. When conducting the Internal Data Loopback with External Clock (BLD) test, ensure the cable is fully connected to the CLK IN socket on the ICN.
  2. When conducting the Internal Data Loopback with Internal Clock test, make sure the single fiber-optic service loopback cable is fully connect to the CLK IN socket at one end and the CLK OUT socket at the other end.
  3. If everything is connected properly, but the tests are still failing, replace the ICN following ICN Replacement procedure.
