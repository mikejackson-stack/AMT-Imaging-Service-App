Touch and Go Strip and Side Bumper Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it out of the magnet room.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front of the table, remove the Front Bottom Cover. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. At the foot of the table, remove the Rear Bottom Cover (located above the pedals).

  1. Remove the screws from the top of the bellows on the side being worked on (five screws on each side holds the Upper Bellows to the bottom side of the table). Do not remove the bellows from the table; leave it in the down position.

  1. To remove the table handle, at the foot of the table rotate the handle down by pushing the silver button on the left side of the table to release the table. Use a 5/32 inch Allen wrench to remove hex socket screws, one on each side, from the table handle. See Illustration 3. Push the silver button again to rotate the handle back up to allow the handle to slip off.

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Side Cover Removal Strategy. determine which T and G Strip or Side Bumper to replace, and then pay attention to the following:

  • If replacing the front T and G Strip or Side Bumper, then remove the front Table Side Cover.

  • If replacing the middle T and G Strip or Side Bumper, then remove both Table Side Covers.

  • If replacing the rear T and G Strip or Side Bumper, then remove the rear Table Side Cover.

  1. On the bottom right or left side of the table, remove appropriate Side Cover screws and then slide the Side Cover down. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the connection(s) from the touch and go strip/side bumper assembly that is being replaced.

  1. Use a 3/8 inch open end wrench to remove nuts and washers from the appropriate front, middle, or rear T and G Strip/Side Bumper studs.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the T and G Strip/Side Bumper assembly away from the table chassis. See Illustration 5.

  1. Disassemble the T and G Strip from the Side Bumper and replace desired part.

  1. Reverse Steps 7-10 to reassemble T and G Strip(s), Side Bumper(s), and Table Side Cover(s).

  1. Dock the table and ensure that the touch and go strips are working correctly.

  1. Reinstall the table side covers.

  1. Replace Table Handle screws, one on each side using a 5/32 inch Allen wrench.

  1. Reattach the top side Bellows screws to bottom side of Table.
