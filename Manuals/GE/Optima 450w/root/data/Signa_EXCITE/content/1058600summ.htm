Longitudinal Drive Motor/Encoder Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
MOUNTING BRACKET, ENCODER, AND DRIVE MOTOR CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, USE TWO PEOPLE TO PROMPTLY REMOVE MOUNTING BRACKET ASSEMBLY FROM MAGNET ROOM.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing Drive Motor/Encoder Bracket Assembly

  1. Remove the right Rear Pedestal cover by removing two (2) M6 hex screws on one side using an Allen wrench. Unsnap the side and rear pivot fasteners and lift up out of bottom support brackets.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the left rear pedestal cover by removing the two (2) M6 hex screws on one side using an Allen wrench. Unsnap the single side pivot fastener and lift out of the bottom support brackets.

  1. Unlatch the LPCA from the cradle and then drive the LPCA through the magnet to the rear pedestal.

  1. Remove the cover from the LPCA by removing the four (4) brass M6 screws using a medium slotted screwdriver.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the two (2) drive belt tensioner screws located at the back of the drive motor Illustration 3. Loosen the tensioner bolt until the belt becomes loose.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the two (2) hex nuts of the rear position belt clamp (see Illustration 4). Remove the belt and pull it out of the LPCA.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the bridge rear hood (see Illustration 5) by removing the four (4) brass M4 screws using a small slotted screwdriver.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the Drive Motor commutator cable from the Table Interface Module (TIF) at J3, and disconnect J1 and J2 at the motor assembly by hand or by using a small slotted screwdriver .

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the drive motor encoder cable from the TIF at J2.

  1. Pull both cables through the grommet opening in the RF Attenuator bracket.

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
MOUNTING BRACKET, ENCODER, AND DRIVE MOTOR CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, use two people to PROMPTLY REMOVE MOUNTING BRACKET ASSEMBLY FROM MAGNET ROOM.

  1. Loosen (do not remove) the four (4) M10 head bolts, two (2) on each side from the motor mounting bracket assembly using a 17mm socket wrench.

    (Step Details)

  1. Carefully remove the two (2) M10 bolts and lock-washers on one side while holding the bracket assembly in place, then remove the bolts and lock-washers on the other side. The assembly drops straight down from the bottom of the bridge. Handle with care per the above warning.

  1. Carefully maneuver the drive belt from the rear of the LPCA through the bracket assembly.

1.2   Installing Drive Motor Assembly

WARNING
FERROUS MATERIAL HAZARD!!
MOUNTING BRACKET, ENCODER, AND DRIVE MOTOR CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIAL.
HOLDING THIS ASSEMBLY TOO CLOSE TO THE MAGNET BORE WILL FORCIBLY ATTRACT IT TO THE MAGNET. TO PREVENT POSSIBLE BODILY INJURY OR MATERIAL DAMAGE, use two people to PROMPTLY REMOVE MOUNTING BRACKET ASSEMBLY FROM MAGNET ROOM.

  1. Rethread the belt through the motor. Hold the bracket assembly in place while replacing the two (2) M10 bolts and lock washers on each side using a 17mm socket wrench.

  1. Feed the drive belt up through the bridge and into the LPCA under the belt clamp. Position the belt so that the same number of belt teeth are exposed as was noted during disassembly. Tighten the two M4 hex nuts.

  1. Tighten the Tensioner Bolt clockwise until the Drive Belt Tensioner socket screws bottom out in the slots. Tighten the tensioner socket screws. See Illustration 3.

  1. Replace the bridge rear hood by angling the hood onto the bridge and securing it with four brass M4 screws.

  1. (For 3.0T systems only) Feed the Drive Motor Commutator and Encoder cable through the RF Attenuator bracket grommet opening. See Illustration 6

  1. Reattach the Drive Motor commutator cable to J3 and the Encoder cable to J2 on the Table Interface Module (TIF) . See Illustration 6

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the LPCA cover with the four (4) brass M6 pan head screws using a medium slotted screwdriver.

  1. To replace the Rear Pedestal side covers, do the following:

    1. Replace the right cover by hooking the bottom two cover supports and pivot up. Snap in the center pivot fastener.

    1. Using an Allen wrench, replace both M6 hex screws.

    1. Repeat Steps 1-2 for the left cover.

    1. Align the two cover flanges at the back of the unit. Snap in the center pivot fastener. Snap in the two pivot fasteners that hold the two covers together in the back of the unit.

    1. Use an Allen wrench to replace both M6 hex screws.

  1. Drive the LPCA to the front of the magnet and re-latch LPCA to cradle.
