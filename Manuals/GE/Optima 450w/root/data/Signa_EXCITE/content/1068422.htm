2.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, Shutdown the Host computer and perform Lockout/Tagout of the GOC BEFORE STARTING THIS PROCEDURE.

3  Procedure

  1. Shutdown application software on the Host computer. Lockout/Tagout of the GOC power sources.

  1. Remove the three screws in the rear that secure the right side panel.

    Illustration 1:  Screws to Remove

  1. Remove the right side panel by sliding in backwards.

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the Ethernet Switch.

  1. Remove all cables that are connected to the Ethernet Switch. Verify proper labeling.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Cables

  1. Loosen the strap that secures the Ethernet Switch and carefully remove it.

  1. Install the replacement Ethernet Switch by reversing the steps.

4  Finalization

  1. Restore power to the GOC and reboot the computer.

  1. Do a test scan to endure proper operation.
