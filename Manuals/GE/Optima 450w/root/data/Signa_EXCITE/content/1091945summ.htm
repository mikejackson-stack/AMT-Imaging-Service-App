1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing the ramping procedure:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions, and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for Ramping in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting the ramping procedure:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the 'Buddy System Requirements & Certification' subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.

  • Remove all loose ferromagnetic material from the magnet room. Make sure all equipment and tools used in the magnet room are non-ferromagnetic.

  • Locate power supplies as far away from the magnet as the cables and room geometry allows.

  • Do not allow anyone carrying ferromagnetic materials (including cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators or steel plates) inside the 5 gauss zone.

WARNING
High Magnetic Field!
Cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators (or other biostimulators), steel plates and ferromagnetic material, equipment and tools will be adversely effected by the strong magnetic field.

  • Notify Site Administration before ramping the magnet that all magnetic safety precautions must be taken.

  • Post warning signs outside the 5 gauss zone to alert personnel with cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators or other biostimulators not to proceed into the designated area. Post these signs on the magnet room level as well as areas below the magnet to which the 5 gauss zone extends. See 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

  • Post “Ramped Magnet” and “Authorized Personnel Only” Warning signs at the magnet room entrance and at the MR Suite entrance, respectively, prior to ramping the magnet. Warnings are to alert personnel that no ferromagnetic material or individuals with cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators or steel plates are allowed in the magnet room when the magnet is ramped.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
The Ramping Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on (or the hatch is opened if a mobile van) before starting the ramping procedure.

WARNING
Explosion Hazard!
Liquid cryogens can liquify atmospheric oxygen, producing a highly combustible oxygen liquid.
No smoking in magnet and cryogen storage rooms!

WARNING
High Magnetic Field!
Be prepared for emergencies.
Make sure the Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) is installed and operational to enable the magnetic field to be quickly discharged in case of an emergency. See Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Installation.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench, rapidly releasing cryogenic gases, if its liquid helium level drops too low during the Ramping Procedure.
Make sure the magnet ≥90% full of liquid helium before starting the ramping procedure.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during the Ramping Procedure.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing the ramping procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

NOTICE
For the MR450w, the DVw Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage. (See Illustration 1.)

3  Procedure

3.1   Ramping Preparations

  1. Make sure Magnet Monitor's Level 1 and Level 2 helium level readings are approximately equal. If problems are found, troubleshoot before ramping.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the MetroLab Teslameter (46-255839P2) and a No. 5 Teslameter Probe (46-255839P104), for general magnetic field strength monitoring in conformance with Preparations for Field Measurement.

  1. Check for ice on the Recondensor. De-ice in conformance with the Vertical Penetration Deicing subsection of Ice Removal Procedures before ramping the magnet.

  1. Make sure the magnet is >90% full of helium and Vent Valve V2 (Illustration 2) is closed before ramping the magnet. If necessary, fill the magnet in conformance with Liquid Helium Fill.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench if the Main Power Supply experiences large output fluctuations and/or excessive ripple.
Make sure the Main Power Supply is routinely calibrated at an approved facility.

  1. Make sure that the Main Power Supply is installed, checked, and adjusted in conformance with the vendor manual supplied with the unit and Main Power Supply Tests for 1000 Amp power supplies.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loto procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Improper engagement/disengagement of the Main Lead Extensions with the Main Lead Pins can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
Insert Main Lead Extensions one at a time and allow them to cool sufficiently (a fog or water vapor forms around the Lead Extensions) before they contact the Main Lead Pins.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.

  • Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

  • Do not allow exposed body areas to come in contact with the cryostat or plumbing while inserting or extracting the Main Lead Extensions.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Contact with cold objects is possible during the Ramping Procedure.
Wear nonabsorbent leather gloves with no holes or tears during insertion or extraction of the Main Lead Extensions.

  1. Connect the Main Power Supply to the magnet by making all cable connections in conformance with Connections for Ramping.

  1. Record the Main Coil connection polarity in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

  1. Set all heater switches on the power supply ( Illustration 3 for 1000 Amp Main Power Supply) to the OFF position. Set the Current Adjust and Voltage controls to zero (fully CCW).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove LOTO and connect input power to the Main Power Supply (and to the Shim Power Supply at mobile sites).

3.2   Resistance Checks

  1. If the magnet is not at field, check Switch Heater and Shim Coil resistances in conformance with Magnet Electrical Checks.

  1. Make sure the Current Adjust and Voltage Adjust controls on the Main Power Supply are OFF (fully CCW).

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the Main Power Supply's Main Power and Power On switches to ON.

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure the Main Heater Switch is OFF, then set the Heater 2 Shim Axial Switch to I (On), and observe the current rise in the Main Power Supply Ammeter (600 to 620 mA) to verify circuit continuity.

  1. Connect a Digital Voltmeter (DVM) to the end of the Voltage Sense Leads. (See Illustration 5.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the Current Adjust Coarse Control on the power supply to maximum (fully CW). See Illustration 3 for the 1000 Amp power supply.

  1. Observe the Main Power Supply Ammeter and slowly turn the Voltage Adjust Control clockwise to set a 750 Amp current through the Main Leads, Main Lead Extensions and persistent Main Switch.

  1. Record the voltage reading on the DVM in the Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Voltage readings >150 millivolts at 750 Amps indicate unacceptable internal contact resistance at the Main Lead Extensions. Higher resistances will add more heat to the magnet, increasing boil-off and possibly causing a quench during ramping.
Make sure the voltage readings are ≤150 mV at 750 Amps.

  1. If the DVM voltage is >150 millivolts, perform one or more of the following to reduce contact resistance:

    1. Wait approximately 1 minute with current running; readings may drop as the Main Lead Extensions cool.

    1. Tighten the nuts (one flat at a time) on top of the Hold-Down Tool while measuring DVM readings.

    1. If the reading still exceeds 150 mV:

      1. Turn the Voltage and Current Adjust Controls to zero (fully CCW).

      WARNING
      Electrical Shock Hazard!
      Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
      Follow lOTO procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

      1. Turn off the Main Power Supply input power. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Power Supply. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

      1. Check and tighten the bolts securing the Ramp Cables to the Power Supply and Main Leads Extensions.

      1. Remove LOTO and reconnect input power to the power supply.

    1. Repeatedly failing the contact resistance check may indicate one of the following:

      (Step Details)

    1. If changes are made in Step 9.d, repeat Step 2 through Step 9.c.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the power supply's Voltmeter Select Switch to the MAIN POWER SUPPLY position. This will display the output at the output lugs of the power supply monitored. A voltage <2.2 V at 750 A indicates acceptable system resistance. If the voltage exceeds 2.2 V during the test, follow the procedures in Step 11 for decreasing contact resistance.

  1. Gradually increase the Voltage Adjust Control to pass 750 Amps through the Main Power Leads, Lead Extensions and persistent Main Switch while observing the power supply's Voltmeter. If the voltage exceeds 2.2 V, then retighten the bolts securing the Ramp Cables to the Power Supply and Main Lead Extensions.

  1. Turn the Current Adjust and Voltage controls OFF (fully CCW).

3.3   Ramping

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
The magnet may quench and the coils become damaged if the Axial Switch Heaters are not ON during ramping.
Make sure the Axial Shim Switch Heaters are ON during ramping to prevent coil damage and a magnet quench. (GE Power Supplies have protective circuitry to prevent ramping voltage with the Axial Heater Switch in the OFF position.)

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
A quench is a rapid discharge of the magnetic field which will result in the rapid generation and expulsion of helium gas, rupturing the Burst Disc in the vent system.
If a quench occurs during ramping, immediately:

  1. Turn the Main Power Supply Voltage and Current Controls to zero (fully CCW),

  2. Turn off the Power Supply and leave the magnet room,

  3. Turn off the Cryocooler Compressor outside the magnet room. Do not re-enter the magnet room until the quench is over.

NOTE:Ice will form around the flow holes in the Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool during ramping. Remove ice as needed to maintain helium gas flow.

  1. Make sure the power supply controls are set as follows:

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the Heater 1 Main Switch to I (On), and wait 3 minutes. Heater current should be approximately 610 mA.

  1. Use a Range 5 Teslameter Probe (46-255839P104) to monitor magnetic field strength. The probe will lock on when magnet current rises to approximately 325 Amps. This usually occurs between 0.6 Tesla and 0.7 Tesla (25.545900 MHz to 29.803550 MHz).

  1. Ramp the magnet in conformance with Table 1 as follows:

    1. Slowly turn the power supply's Voltage Control to obtain the Main Coil (Ramp) Voltage value shown in the table, starting with 3.00 volts.

    1. Allow the voltage to decay over the current interval shown in the table.

    1. Monitor the power supply's Current Meter. When the limit of the current range for the voltage setting is reached, slowly turn the Voltage Control to obtain the Main Coil Voltage for the next current range.

    (Step Details)

  1. If inductance (L) is outside the range of 13.5 to 15.5 henries, discontinue ramping, measure Main Coil resistance in conformance with Magnet Electrical Checks and contact your regional MAC Team representative.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
1.5T Magnets can be quenched if large current or voltage changes occur near parking.
Adjust the Voltage Control in a slow, continuous motion in the following steps.

  1. When magnetic field strength reaches 1.499 Tesla, slowly adjust the Voltage Adjust Control to a 0.1 V Main Coil Voltage.

NOTICE
If there are multiple 1.5T systems that will share the same Equipment Room, the frequencies of the systems must be offset from each other to avoid cross talk. Specification for 1.5T Center Frequency 63.864 MHz ±64 KHz or 63.796 to 63.924 MHz. Recommendation is to park one system at the high end of the spec and the other at the low end of the spec.
 
 

  1. Check the Teslameter reading, and slowly adjust the Voltage Adjust Controls as required to bring the magnetic field to 1.500000 Tesla. The total current will be approximately 742 Amps. Allow the magnetic field to stabilize. The last two digits on the Teslameter should be the only digits changing.

  1. Maintain magnetic field at the Step 8 setting for 5 minutes before proceeding to Step 10.

  1. Turn off the Main Switch Heater. Wait a minimum of 15 minutes for the switch to fully cool and go persistent.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Incorrect Main Coil Connection polarity and ramping current settings at a later date can result in a magnet quench when the Main Power Supply Switch is turned on.
When changing magnetic field in the future, the Main Power Supply must be set to the same current and polarity in the Main Coils. Upon completion of each ramp, record Main Coil connection polarities and final ramping currents.

  1. Record current, frequency and lead extension voltage values at which the switch went persistent in the Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

  1. When the switch goes persistent, slowly turn the power supply's Voltage Adjust Control to zero (fully CCW) over a two-minute period.

  1. Gradually turn the Current Adjust Control to zero (over a one-minute period).

  1. Set the Heater 2 Shim Axial Switch to O (Off).

  1. Set the Main Power and Power On switches on the Main Power Supply to OFF.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.

  • Do not touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time. Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

  • Do not allow exposed body areas to come in contact with the cryostat or plumbing while inserting or extracting the Main Lead Extensions.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Contact with cold objects is possible during the Ramping Procedure.
Wear nonabsorbent leather gloves with no holes or tears during insertion or extraction of the Main Lead Extensions.
