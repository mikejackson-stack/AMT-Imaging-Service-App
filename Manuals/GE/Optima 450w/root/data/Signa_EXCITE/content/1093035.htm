2.2  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

3  Procedure

3.1  Removing Left Side Covers

  1. Remove any cables that are connected to the PAC, except for the “PG” cable (this cable is permenantly attached to the PAC). See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  PG Cable Location

  1. Loosen the large screw on lower portion of the lower side cover by using a non-ferrous Allen wrench and turning the screw counter-clockwise (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Side Cover Screw

  1. Grab sides of lower cover and lift upwards until the cover is released from the upper cover. See Illustration 3. Set lower cover aside. Route the PG cable through the cover opening.

    Illustration 3:  Removing Left Side Cover

  1. The upper cover is secured to the arc covers primarily by two round feet on the lower portion of the side cover. To remove the cover, grab the two handles on the upper cover firmly and push the cover up and then pull out away from the enclosure. This should release the cover’s feet and allow you to move it freely (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  Removing Left Top Side Cover

  1. Once the feet are released, use the arc cover as a guide rail, and allow the cover to slide down the side of the enclosure. Once the cover clears the fixtures on the upper portion of the magnet, the cover can either be safely set aside or it can be rested on the side of the magnet using the cover’s hooks that latch on to the arc covers (see Illustration 5).

    Illustration 5:  Resting Top Cover on Side of Magnet

3.2  Installing Left Side Covers

NOTE:To replace the left side covers, the top right side cover must be installed.

  1. Using the arc covers as guide rails, slide the side cover up to meet the right side cover at the top of the magnet (see Illustration 6).

    Illustration 6:  Using the Arc Covers as Guide Rails

  1. Hold the handles away from the enclosure to ensure that the alignment tabs get properly slotted under the right side’s slot (see Illustration 7).

    Illustration 7:  Slide Alignment Tabs below Right Side Cover

    NOTE:This is not easily accomplished since you cannot see all the alignment tabs while holding on to the cover’s handles. Before proceeding to install the lower side cover, go over to the front and rear of the magnet and visually verify that the alignment tabs have been properly inserted and are not protruding over the top of the right side cover (see Illustration 8).

    Illustration 8:  Ensure Tab Is Properly Placed

  1. Set the round foot on the magnet side of the cover into the upper oval slots on the arc cover (see Illustration 9). Snap into place.

    Illustration 9:  Place Round Foot in Arc Cover's Oval Hole

  1. Hold the sides of the Lower Cover and align the round feet on the magnet side of the Lower Cover with the arc cover’s oval slots (see Illustration 10). Snap into place.

    Illustration 10:  Installing Left Side Cover

  1. Route the PG cable through the cover opening. Latch the cover into place by turning the screw clockwise so the latch is secure (see Illustration 11).

    Illustration 11:  Side Cover Screw

  1. Connect any removed cables to the PAC.
