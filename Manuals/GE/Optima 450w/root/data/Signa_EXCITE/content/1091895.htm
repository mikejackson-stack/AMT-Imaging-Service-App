2  Overview

The Cryocooler comes with a Coldhead already installed on the magnet and a separate Compressor Unit, which will be located in the Equipment Room. A power cable, gas supply and return lines connect the two units. Installation and checkout of these components are performed in this procedure.

NOTE:Prior to installation, locate and read the vendor manual(s) supplied with the system. Become thoroughly familiar with the configuration and procedures of the system. Compressor installation instructions are covered in the Compressor vendor's manual. The Cryocooler Interconnect Diagram is shown in Cryocooler System Interconnects.

4  Procedure

4.1  Cryocooler Compressor Installation

  1. Unpack and install the compressor in conformance with the Compressor Vendor Service Manual.

    1. Note any visible damage and notify the shipper.

    1. LOTO input power to the Compressor immediately after unpacking it.

    1. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Position the Compressor in the equipment room as required in the site plan. Refer to the site planning manual.

NOTICE
Do NOT connect the flexlines until the system gas pressure check is performed to prevent line contamination.
 
 

  1. Remove LOTO, and connect the input power cable in conformance with the vendor manual and the local electrical code.

4.2  System Gas Pressure Check

  1. Check and adjust the Cryocooler system static gas pressure in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments.

4.3  Cooling Water Supply Connection

WARNING
Poison Hazard!
Antifreeze is poisonous to aquatic, animal and human life. Because of its sweetness, animals are drawn to it. since it is classified as hazardous both for shipment and as waste, it is not shipped by untrained people.
Cryocooler Compressors that contain antifreeze must be drained before being returned to the supplier. Make arrangements with the hospital maintenance department for disposal.

NOTE:It is recommended to install the Water Flow Meter that is included with the magnet monitor collector, using the applicable magnet monitor installation document.

  1. Verify that the cooling water supply meets all requirements specified in the vendor service manual.

  1. Connect the water supply hose to the cooling water inlet connection, and secure with a worm-drive, screw-type hose clamp or similar secure connection.

  1. Connect and secure the water return hose to the cooling water outlet connection of the compressor by the same method.

  1. Turn on the cooling water supply, and allow air to purge from the system.

  1. Check the system for water leaks.

4.5.2  Flexible Gas Line Connections

WARNING
Potential Injury Hazard!
Flexlines with Broken Aeroquip fittings may disconnect and move erratically.
Do not put any bending force on Aeroquip fittings while connecting / Disconnecting helium flexlines. A bending force:

  • CAN CAUSE AEROQUIP STEMS TO BEND OR BREAK

  • WILL CREATE DIFFICULTY IN THE RAPID ENGAGEMENT OR DISENGAGEMENT REQUIRED TO PREVENT HELIUM LOSS AND SYSTEM CONTAMINATION.

NOTICE
Support the gas lines when connecting or disconnecting to the Coldhead at the top of the magnet or to the Compressor. Order and technique are important when connecting helium flexlines to prevent inoperative Coldheads and system contamination.
 
 

  1. Connect both helium flexlines to the compressor using the appropriate Aeroquip connection wrenches. Always use two wrenches: one to hold and one to tighten. The hold wrench maintains the backside adapter connection, making gas leaks in the system less likely. (See Illustration 6 and Helium Flexline Connections and Casing Replacement.)

    Illustration 6:  Two-Wrench Connection Technique for Aeroquip Coupling

  1. Observe the Compressor pressure gauge readings after connecting the gas lines. Readings should remain constant.

    • Lower pressure gauge readings indicate either the gas lines were not fully charged when received or one line could be leaking.

    • If a leak is suspected, proceed to System Leak Test.

NOTICE
When routing gas lines make sure there is sufficient slack at both ends and that any excess length is not coiled in a less than 12 in. (300 mm) radius to prevent bending force on Aeroquip fittings and strain that may cause “chirping" noise on the line.
 
 

  1. Route the gas lines through the magnet's rear flange and then in a wide arc to the vicinity of the Coldhead. (See Illustration 7.)

    NOTE:Do not connect the gas lines to the Coldhead until the lines are secured to the Cryocooler Safety Strap.

    Illustration 7:  Flexible Gas Line Routing at the Magnet

  1. Position the gas lines in a gentle curve toward the center of the magnet, and adjust line slack and angles to keep the lines from kinking and to minimize tension and noise (chirping) as shown in Illustration 7.

  1. Verify that the Cryocooler Safety Strap 2219776 is secured onto the Motorshield Bracket by a 0.50-13 UNC x 1.25 in. stainless steel bolt 46-260950P46, flat washer 46-252635P11, and lock washer 46-281162P13. (See Illustration 8.)

    Illustration 8:  Cryocooler Safety Strap (2219776) Connected to Motorshield Bracket

  1. Using tie-wraps, secure the gas return line to the Cryocooler Safety Strap, then the gas supply line. (See Illustration 8.)

NOTICE
Make sure the helium gas lines are connected to the Coldhead in the sequence given in Flexible Gas Line Connections. Gas back pressure may prevent the Coldhead from starting if gas lines are connected in the reverse sequence.

Order and technique are important when connecting helium flexlines to prevent inoperative Coldheads and system contamination. Read and follow Helium Flexline Connections and Casing Replacement when connecting or disconnecting Aeroquip fittings.

 
 

  1. Connect the gas return line (green mark) to the Coldhead, then connect the gas supply line (yellow mark) to the Coldhead. (See Illustration 8.)

  1. Make sure the flexible gas lines are still routed across the magnet approximately as shown in Illustration 8.

  1. Observe the readings of the Compressor pressure gauges:

    • Constant pressure readings at the Two-Stage Coldhead specification: No leakage is suspected.

    • Readings above the Two-Stage Coldhead specification given in Table 2: Vent the pressure down in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments.

    • Readings below the Two-Stage Coldhead specification given in Table 2: Increase the helium gas charge in the complete system by supplying helium gas through the Compressor supply fitting in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments. If is a leak is suspected, see Flexible Gas Line Installation, Cryocooler Leak Test.

4.6  Cryocooler Leak Test

  1. If helium pressure continues to drop after increasing the helium charge, remove the flexlines from the Compressor using the two-wrench technique shown in Illustration 6.

    NOTE:Lines suspected of leaking may be tested using a regulator, charging line and manifold.

  1. Connect a regulator, charging line and manifold and purge in conformance with Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments.

  1. Connect one of the flexlines to the appropriate fitting on the manifold, and pressurize to approximately 100 psig.

  1. Reduce the supply pressure using the regulator control, and observe whether the reduced pressure is maintained on the manifold pressure gauge. If the pressure continues to drop, that line may have a gas leak.

    NOTE:If the Compressor pressure gauge readings drop and stay constant, the charge may be low. Recharge the system.

  1. Maintain 100 psig with the regulator and check the line with a liquid soap solution or a commercial leak-testing solution such as Leak-Tec®.

  1. If the line has a leak, notify Installed Base Support at 843-664-1715 to obtain a replacement. Leave a message if no one answers.

  1. Repeat Step 3 through Step 6 for the other gas line.

4.9  Monitoring Cryocooler Temperatures

The RuO Temperature Monitor is used to monitor the second stage RuO temperature and the mating Recondenser surface temperature on a Cryocooler Coldhead during installation and troubleshooting of the Coldhead. During operation, the second stage Coldhead temperature is monitored indirectly by monitoring the silicone diode temperature of the Recondenser at the system monitor cabinet. The first stage Coldhead temperature is monitored indirectly by monitoring the silicone diode Coldhead Sleeve temperature at the system monitor cabinet.

NOTE:Magnet Monitor can be used to monitor cool down and temperature trends, but does not give accurate temperature readings at 4.2K.

  1. Connect the test plug to temperature monitor's cables to confirm calibration.

  1. Connect the temperature monitor's cables to the magnet's connectors as shown in Illustration 10 and Illustration 11.

    Illustration 10:  RuO Temperature Monitor Connections

    Illustration 11:  RuO Temperature Monitor Connections at Coldhead

  1. Set the Channel Selector Switch to the desired channel (A or B), and depress the button to read the temperature on that channel.

  1. Make sure the first and second stage Coldhead temperatures are cooling down.

4.10  Cryocooler Coldhead Temperature Checks

  1. Monitor the Cryocooler Coldhead first and second stage heat station temperatures in conformance with Monitoring Cryocooler Temperatures until they stabilize.

  1. Record final temperatures below and in Magnet Commissioning Log.

    Final First Stage Temperature: 
    Final Second Stage Temperature: 
    Final Recondenser Temperature: 

  1. Make sure the final temperature readings on the RuO Temperature Monitor (2171219) are within the following limits:

    • Recondenser: 4.0 to 4.5K

    • ΔT [Recondenser(at position B) - Coldhead(at position A)] ≤0.2K

    NOTE:Second stage Coldhead RuO temperature may show a small oscillation (0.0K - 0.2K) on the temperature monitor. This is a result of the displacer motion inside the second stage and the sampling rate of the monitor. Record the average temperature for all checks:

    Second stage temperature = (Tmax + Tmin) / 2

  1. If temperature limits are exceeded, perform the Coldhead Interface Adjustment procedure in Two-Stage Cryocooler Coldhead Replacement.
