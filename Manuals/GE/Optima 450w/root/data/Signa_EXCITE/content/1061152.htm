2  Overview

Procedures for replacing operator control/display hardware in the front magnet enclosure. This document covers both the In-Room Display (IRD) version and the version without the IRD.

Possible Personal Injury!

Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.

4  Procedure

4.1  Replacing In-Room Display

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove Laser Light Cover. It is held in place by two (2) screws and a popper.

    Illustration 1:  In-Room Display

    Illustration 2:  Removing Laser Light Cover

  1. Remove top panel on back of the IRD by popping tabs with a small flathead screwdriver.

    Illustration 3:  Removing IRD

WARNING
Strong magnetic fields present
eddy currents can cause resistance when pulling IRD away from magnet.
use caution when removing ird to prevent it from striking the magnet enclosure.

  1. Disconnect three (3) cables attached to the IRD (ground cable, fiber optic cable, and power cable).

  1. Remove four (4) allen head screws connecting the bracket plate to the back of the IRD. Lift monitor up and off the bracket carefully as Eddy currents may pull against it.

  1. Mount the new IRD and reconnect appropriate cables and replace covers.

4.2  Replacing Trackball Control Panels and E-Stop Button

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove the Front Panels (see Front Cover Removal and Installation).

    Illustration 4:  Removing Front Cover

  1. Disconnect the two (2) cables that connect control panels to magnet enclosure (see Illustration 5).

    NOTE:If cable connectors are not labeled with a “J” number, mark the applicable “J” number on each cable connector prior to removal.

    Illustration 5:  Removing Control Panel Cables

WARNING
Possible Personal Injury!
This procedure requires removal of ferrous material in close proximity to the magnet.
Keep operator control panel away from front of the magnet bore.

  1. Remove the Trackball Control Panel by removing five (5) screws and detaching Emergency Stop Button cable (see Illustration 6).

    Illustration 6:  Removing Trackball Control Panel

    NOTE:If only replacing the E-Stop Button Board, disconnect the E-Stop cable and remove two (2) screws. Install new button.

    Illustration 7:  Trackball Connector

NOTICE
On the back of the control panel, the E-Stop cable connection and the Trackball cable connection are of the same size and gender, and thus can be accidentally interchanged. Confirm that the cables are connected properly, otherwise the E-Stop circuit will not be resettable. See Illustration 6 and Illustration 7.
 
 

  1. To install the new Trackball Control Panel, follow Step 2 through Step 4 in reverse order.

    Illustration 8:  EMO Reset on PDU

4.3  Replacing 7-Segment Display

  1. Follow Lockout/Tagout procedures per LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

    NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Remove the left trim ring. It is held in place by three (3) poppers.

  1. Remove the 7-Segment Display bezel. It is held in place by one (1) screw and four (4) poppers. Once the bezel is removed from the electrical assembly, disconnect the safety tether.

    Illustration 9:  7-Segment Display

    Illustration 10:  Removing the 7-Segment Display Bezel

  1. Remove the four (4) screws to remove the 7-Segment Display from the mounting bracket.

    Illustration 11:  Removing 7-Segment Display

WARNING
Strong magnetic fields present
eddy currents can cause resistance when pulling 7-Segment Display away from magnet.
use caution when removing 7-Segment Display to prevent it from striking the magnet enclosure.

  1. Disconnect the two (2) cables attached to the 7-Segment Display (Note that the mounting bracket can be re-used).

  1. Mount the new 7-Segment Display and reconnect the appropriate cables and safety tether, then replace the front bezel and trim rings.

4.4  Replacing Push Button (Non-Trackball) Control Panel and E-Stop Button

  1. Follow all Lock Out and Tag Out procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the PDU. See LOTO for PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove the Front Covers. See Front Cover Removal and Installation.

    Illustration 12:  Removing Front Covers

  1. Disconnect the cable that connects each control panel to the magnet enclosure (see Illustration 12).

    NOTE:If cable connectors are not labeled with a “J” number, mark the applicable “J” number on each cable connector prior to removal.

    Illustration 13:  Removing Control Panel Cables

  1. Remove the 7-Segment Control Panel by removing five (5) screws, and detaching the Emergency Stop Button Cable. (See Illustration 13.)

    NOTE:If only replacing the E-Stop Panel, disconnect the E-Stop cable, remove two (2) screws, and install the new button.

  1. To install the new 7-Segment Control Panel, follow Step 2 through Step 4 in reverse order.

    Illustration 14:  EMO Reset on the PDU

5  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO to PGR/PDU and power on the PDU again.

  1. Whenever the In-Room Display or Trackball Control Panels are disconnected and then reconnected, a TPS reset must be completed. (This should not be necessary if the host computer is rebooted.)

  1. Check the functionality of the item replaced. Run the associated diagnostic, if necessary.

    • For the In-Room Display - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then In Room Display.

    • For the Control Panel - from the Common Service Desktop, select Diagnostics, then Hardware Location, then Magnet Room, then SRI Functional Tests.
