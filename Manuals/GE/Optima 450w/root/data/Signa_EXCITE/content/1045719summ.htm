Universal Power Monitor - Head and Body Setup and Calibration

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
Dangerous voltages throughout the cabinet.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THE RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Head Mode

1.1.1  Head Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the Transmit/Receive (TR) faults. Disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection in the PEN cabinet. (See Illustration 1.) Move Switches SW2 and SW3 to the TR DISABLE and DD Disable positions respectively.

    (Step Details)

  1. Move the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter in top of the Penetration Cabinet to the DISABLE (DOWN) position as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Head Heliax cable (head output) from the service panel in the PGR cabinet. (See Illustration 3.)

    1. Connect either the 35 kW RF Dummy Load or 1.5T Dummy Load to the Head output of the service panel in the PGR cabinet.

    1. If the 35 kW RF Dummy Load is used, plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    (Step Details)

1.1.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Head Forward Power

NOTICE
This tool must have the head coil installed in order to enable Coil ID.
 
 

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter in top of the Penetration Cabinet by moving it to the ENABLE (UP) position.

  1. With head coil attached to the LPCA, landmark on the head coil and advance to scan.

  1. Start the Common Service Desktop (CSD) , then click the Calibration browser tab.

  1. Start the UPM Calibration Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. For Nuclei, select [1H].

  1. For RF Chain, select [Head].

  1. For monitor Power Type, select [Forward].

  1. Select [Calibrate].

  1. Select [Start] and validate the hardware setup, then select [Yes].

  1. Verify that the status indicates a PASS or FAIL within about 2.5 minutes. The tool should notify user of current step in progress. (See Illustration 6.) If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting .

    Illustration 6:  Head Calibration, PASS Status

1.2   Body Mode

1.2.1  Body Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the TR faults, and disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection. (See Illustration 7.) Ensure switches SW2 and SW3 are in the TR DISABLE and DD DISABLE positions respectively.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Body Heliax cable (body output) from the service panel in the PGR cabinet. (See Illustration 8.)

    1. Connect either the 35 kW RF Dummy Load or 1.5T Dummy Load to the Body output of the service panel in the PGR cabinet.

    1. If the 35 kW RF Dummy Load is used, plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Body Forward Power

This tool must see a Landmark only. It is not necessary to have a Body Phantom in place or any other scan parameter entered other than what is described below. Simply Landmark on the cradle where the Body Phantom would be.

NOTE:Remove the Head coil, and ensure it is not connected.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Start the CSD, then click on the Service browser tab.

  1. Start the UPM Calibration Tool.

    (Step Details)

  1. For Nuclei, select [1H].

  1. For RF Chain, select [Body].

  1. For monitor Power Type, select [Forward].

  1. Select [Calibrate].

  1. Select [Start] and verify the hardware setup works, then select [Yes].

  1. Check that the status indicates a PASS or FAIL within about three minutes. (he tTool should notify user of current step in progress. (See Illustration 11.) If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting .

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to UPM - Body & Head Functional Check and perform the Body Functional Check procedure before moving to Finalization.

    Illustration 11:  Body Calibration, PASS Staus

2  Finalization

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment.

  1. Make sure the Body and Head Heliax cables are attached to the RF amplifier service panel.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Confirm that switch SW2 is set to TR ENABLE and switch SW3 is set to DD ENABLE for TR drive fault detection. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Upon completion of any UPM calibration, click [Reset TPS]. The system cannot scan until this step is complete.
