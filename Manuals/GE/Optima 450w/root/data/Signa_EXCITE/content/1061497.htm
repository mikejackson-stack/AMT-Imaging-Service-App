3  Procedure

  1. Undock Patient Table and remove from magnet room.

  1. Raise Patient Table to highest elevation with Up foot pedal.

  1. Remove Patient Table right upper and lower side covers. See Patient Table Upper Bellows and Lower Base Side Cover Removal document for details.

  1. Lower the safety latch by disconnecting spring from scissors assembly (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Table 1

  1. Release hydraulic pressure by gently pressing Down foot pedal until table is supported by safety latch.

  1. Hold the Down valve on the lift cylinder open while pumping the Up foot pedal at the same time. This can be done by one person by propping the Down valve in the open position using a standard blade screwdriver between the Down valve and the cylinder block. See Illustration 2 for location of Down valve.

    Illustration 2:  Hydraulics Valve

  1. After all the air bubbles pass though the clear tubes (back to the reservoir), stop pumping and release the Down valve.

  1. Remove the screwdriver to close the Down valve.

  1. Purge the lift cylinder by pumping the table up to the top.

  1. Open the bleed screw at the cylinder top a few turns until oil flows steadily from the screw after the air bubbles purge.

  1. Tighten bleed screw (just snug).

  1. With the table at highest elevation, raise the safety latch and reconnect spring to scissors assembly.

  1. Lower table to its lowest position using Down foot pedal.

  1. Fill table reservoir with hydraulic oil (Part # 46-230006P2) so that fluid is ¼” to ½” above full line when table is at its lowest position (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  Hydraulics Filter

4  Finalization

  1. Replace side covers.

  1. Ensure to re-dock table.
