2  Overview

Compressor gas pressure must be within the range stated in the vendor's service manual for proper operation of the Cryocooler. Use the following procedures to check and adjust the pressure to specification, if required.

3.3  Safety

WARNING
Potential High Pressure Gas Release Hazard
Potential explosive release of high pressure (~1.62 MPa, 16.5 KgF/cm*CM*G, 235 psig) helium gas if cryocooler system components are hit with sharp edge or touched with pointed object.
Handle equipment with care. Keep sharp edged/pointed objects away from Cryocooler system.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow lockout/tagout procedures while making electrical connections to a cryocooler Compressor. See OSHA Lockout-Tagout.

NOTICE
Charge Compressor in strict conformance with the Cryocooler System Gas Pressure Adjustments procedure to prevent contamination. Contamination may result in noise at the Coldhead or decreasing cooling capacity.
 
 

NOTICE
Cryocooler filling pressure varies with Compressor and Coldhead temperature. Make sure the Cryocooler power is off and gas pressure has stabilized (static pressure) before making pressure adjustments.
 
 

4  Procedure

4.1  Gas Pressure Check

Check Cryocooler system gas pressure as required for magnet temperature/pressure troubleshooting.

NOTICE
Operating (dynamic high-side) pressure checks done while the Cryocooler system is NOT at equilibrium (i.e., the Coldhead is not at ~ 4.2K) will not be accurate.
 
 

  1. Verify that the Cryocooler system is at equilibrium (i.e., Coldhead at ~ 4.2K) by performing Temperature Sensor Checks.

  1. Check the operating (dynamic high-side) pressure of the Cryocooler system. Compare to the specifications in Table 1.

    NOTE:The supply pressure gauge (Illustration 1) displays static pressure when the compressor is off and high-side dynamic pressure when the compressor is on.

  1. If the dynamic high-side pressure is outside the acceptable range:

    1. Turn the Cryocooler system off.

4.2  Gas Pressure Adjustment

4.2.1  Increasing Gas Pressure

  1. Obtain a full cylinder of certified 99.999% helium gas.

  1. Attach and tighten the high pressure regulator (46-294009P1) to the gas cylinder.

  1. Adjust the regulator handle clockwise (CW) as required to allow gas flow.

  1. Attach and tighten the high pressure helium line (46-294002P1) to the regulator. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Purging the Helium Line

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
Potential release of high pressure (2400 psig) gas.
To prevent possible fatal explosive release of gas, open the gas cylinder main valve very slowly.

  1. Establish gas flow through the regulator and high-pressure helium gas line by slowly opening the valve on the gas cylinder counterclockwise (CCW), and adjusting the regulator handle clockwise (CW) to produce an audible gas flow.

  1. Purge the regulator and gas line for 30 seconds, then back out the regulator handle (CCW) to limit the gas flow until it is just detectable at the end of the gas line.

  1. Verify that the compressor's main power switch is off (Illustration 1) and that static pressure is stable (Illustration 3).

    NOTE:The supply pressure gauge displays static pressure when the compressor is off and high-side dynamic pressure when the compressor is on.

    Illustration 3:  Purging the Gas Charging Tool

  1. Verify that the Gas Charging Tool (5102120) valve is fully closed (CW), then slowly open (CCW) the valve one (1) turn to allow gas flow.

  1. Remove the protective cap on the #4 male Aeroquip coupling at the compressor's charge/discharge fitting. See Illustration 3.

  1. Immediately attach and tighten the Gas Charging Tool's #4 female Aeroquip coupling to the compressor's charge/discharge fitting.

  1. Adjust the valve on the Gas Charging Tool (CCW) to produce an audible gas flow.

  1. Purge the Gas Charging Tool for 5 seconds, then turn the valve (CW) until the flow is just detectable at the end of the Gas Charging Tool.

  1. Connect and tighten the 0.25 inch flare swivel adapter at the end of the high pressure helium gas line to the 0.25 inch male connector at the end of the Gas Charging Tool while a trickle of helium gas is still flowing from each fitting. See Illustration 4.

    NOTE:The illustration shows the CSW-71 compressor. The procedure for the F-50 compressor is identical.

    Illustration 4:  Connecting Purged Helium Line to Purged Gas Charging Tool

  1. Open the Gas Charging Tool's valve and adjust the regulator handle to set the static pressure in the Cryocooler at the middle of the pressure range shown in Table 2.

  1. When pressure is reached within the middle of the range shown in Table 2, close the Gas Charging Tool's valve (full CW) and then close the gas cylinder's valve (full CW).

  1. Disconnect the Gas Charging Tool from the compressor, then gradually unthread the tool from the gas line to bleed pressure from the line.

  1. Disconnect the line and regulator from the Gas Charging Tool and the gas cylinder.

  1. Remove lockout/tagout from the compressor.

  1. Restore power to the compressor and replace the cap on the compressor's charge/discharge fitting.

4.2.2  Decreasing Gas Pressure

  1. Verify that the compressor's main power switch is off (Illustration 3) and that static pressure is stable (Illustration 1).

    NOTE:The supply pressure gauge (Illustration 1) displays static pressure when the compressor is off and high-side dynamic pressure when the compressor is on.

  1. Verify that the Gas Charging Tool (5102120) valve is fully closed (CW), then slowly open (CCW) the valve one (1) turn to allow gas flow.

  1. Remove the protective cap on the #4 male Aeroquip coupling at the compressor's charge/discharge fitting. See Illustration 3.

  1. Immediately attach and tighten the Gas Charging Tool's #4 female Aeroquip coupling to the compressor's charge/discharge fitting.

  1. Open (CCW) the valve on the Gas Charging Tool as required to decrease gas pressure.

  1. When the gas pressure reaches the high end of the pressure range shown in Table 2, immediately close (CW) the valve on the Gas Charging Tool.

  1. Disconnect the Gas Charging Tool, and replace the protective cap on the Compressor's charge/discharge fitting.

5  Finalization

  1. Check both static and dynamic high-side pressures (Gas Pressure Check, Gas Pressure Check) after any adjustments.

  1. If the pressures are still out of specification, contact the Online Center or your regional MAC Team specialist.

  1. When pressures are with the specifications stated in Table 1 and Table 2, return the Cryocooler Compressor to operating condition by removing any lockout/tagout, reconnecting power, and allowing the Cryocooler system to return to equilibrium.
