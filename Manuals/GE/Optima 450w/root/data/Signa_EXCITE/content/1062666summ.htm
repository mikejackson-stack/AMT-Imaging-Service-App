High Order (HO) Shim Supply Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard.
Dangerous and or Fatal Voltages are present in the energized cabinet.
Follow LOTO steps as given in first section of this document to disable and verify safe voltage levels

1  Procedure

1.1   High Order Shim Supply Power Down and Lockout/Tagout Procedure

  1. Prepare for the shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. To turn power off, press the power switch at the back of the HO Shim Supply

  1. Remove the power cord from the plug it is plugged into

  1. Using DMM, measure voltage at plug to ensure no power present

  1. Lock out the power plug.

1.2   HO Shim Supply Replacement

NOTE:The HO Shim Supply (NAV version) is light enough to replace without the use of the Hoist Kit Illustration 2.

  1. Loosen the 16 thumbs screws securing the penetration panel to the magnet room wall (see Illustration 1). This will allow access to the rear of the Shim Supply.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all cables that attach to the back of the supply (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove 4 screws that attach the shim supply to the cabinet

  1. Carefully slide out and remove the shim supply.

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the new supply and secure with 4 screws

  1. Attach all cables/connectors removed in Step 2.

  1. Attach Pen Panel removed in Step 1

  1. Proceed to Finalization, Step 1.

2  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from power cable to shim supply.

  1. Plug in shim supply power cable and turn on power switch at back of supply

  1. Reset TPS

  1. If NAV supply is used, proceed to HO Shim Supply Functional Checks and run shim coil continuity check.

  1. Run Goodbye Scan to make sure system is ok. Check this document for details.
