4  Procedure

4.1  Magnet Electrical Check

To verify that the magnet's electrical circuits are in operating condition prior to filling or ramping, perform electrical checks called out in Magnet Electrical Checks.

4.2  Cryostat Temperature Check

This section describes the procedures and equipment used to establish the temperature inside the Cryostat Helium Vessel. The temperature must be established to determine the cooldown and liquid helium filling requirements of the Cryostat in Cryostat Cooling and Cryogen Filling Requirements, Cryostat Cooling and Cryogen Filling Requirements, prior to the magnet commissioning. Magnets are equipped with two temperature sensors requiring a 10 microampere current source with a stability of +0.005 percent. Sensor (Diode) 1 is mounted on top of the patient end of the Magnet Assembly. Sensor (Diode) 2 is mounted on the bottom of the service end of the Magnet Assembly. The Magnet Assembly is inside of the Helium Vessel. (These sensor diodes are identical to those found on the Coldhead Sleeve.)

NOTICE
The magnet temperature sensors are designed to be driven by a 10 microampere source. Some ohmmeters exceed this rating. Do NOT use any sensing or troubleshooting equipment which exceeds 10 microamperes. A voltmeter can also be used to troubleshoot the sensor circuit.
 
 

  1. Verify that any sensing or troubleshooting equipment used does not exceed 10 microamperes.

4.3  Measuring Magnet Temperature using an MTM-101 Magnet Temperature Meter

The MTM-101 Magnet Temperature Meter (5119507) is shown with its adapter cables for DVw magnets in Illustration 7.

Illustration 7:  MTM-101 Magnet Temperature Meter

NOTICE
Always keep a fresh set of batteries available for the MTM-101 meter, and ensure the meter is in calibration before using it.
 
 

  1. Plug the meter's circular DIN connector into the Diode connector on the Instrumentation Connector Box on the Magnet Assembly in conformance with Cryostat Temperature Check.

  1. To read the upper temperature diode, press the green button on the meter until the SI410D1 LED is lit, and then read the temperature (in Kelvin) on the meter.

  1. To read the lower temperature diode, press the green button on the meter until the SI410D2 LED is lit, and then read the temperature (in Kelvin) on the meter.

4.4  Cryostat Cooling and Cryogen Filling Requirements

  1. Measure and record the Diode Temperatures in conformance with Cryostat Temperature Check, Cryostat Temperature Check.

NOTICE
Do not proceed with Nitrogen Purge-Precool, Liquid Helium Fill, Preparations for Field Measurement, or initiate magnet ramping prior to establishing the Cryostat cooling and cryogen filling requirements in conformance with this section.
 
 

  1. Use Table 2, the temperature readout obtained in Cryostat Temperature Check, Cryostat Temperature Check, and the reading from the Magnet Monitor installed in Magnet Monitor Installation to determine the Cryostat cooling and cryogen filling procedures required.
