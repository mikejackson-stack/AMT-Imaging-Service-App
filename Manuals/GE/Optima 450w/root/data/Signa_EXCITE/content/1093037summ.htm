1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before AND WHILE PERFORMING ice removal:

  • MAKE SURE TO REVIEW AND FULLY UNDERSTAND ALL SUPERCONDUCTING MAGNET PORTIONS, AND

  • MAKE SURE TO FULLY COMPLY WITH ALL REQUIRED ITEMS FOR ICE REMOVAL IN THE MAGNET & CRYOGEN SERVICE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS SUBSECTION OF 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting ice removal:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
Gas cylinder rupture/damage or opening cylinder valve fast will release 2400 psi gas from cylinder.
Make sure gas cylinders are secure from falls and away from moving objects before using. Always open cylinder valve slowly.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
A magnet quench during Ice Removal could result in rapid expulsion of liquid helium through the Vertical Penetration.
Make sure magnet is ramped down to zero field before starting portions of the ice removal procedure.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air is generated during Ice Removal.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before starting ice removal.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn or Asphyxiation Hazard!
Internal cryostat pressure build-up to >5.25 psig will open the 5.25 psig relief valve.
Before removing either Ramp or Fill Port Caps or loosening any component resulting in the release of cryogen pressure:

  1. Observe the internal cryostat pressure gauge reading,

  2. Set magnet monitor pressure to 0.5 psig, and

  3. Vent cryostat down to 0.2 psig through Vent Valve V2.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Ice Removal.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing ice removal.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, Follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

NOTICE
The Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage. (See Illustration 1.)

Illustration 1:  DVw Service Platform with Side Shield

  • Maintain a positive pressure inside the Cryostat, and keep all valves closed in strict conformance with the Ice Removal procedure to minimize cryopumping of air.

  • Turn off the Cryocooler before depressurizing and opening the cryostat to prevent the migration of ice and moisture into the Recondenser.

  • Do NOT allow the Vertical Penetration to remain open during deicing procedures to prevent air and moisture from entering and cryopumping the walls. Cover the open vertical stack immediately with the Cover Plate.

 
 

NOTE:If the site has Magnet Monitor proprietary software, connect to the Magnet Monitor and turn off the alarm for vessel pressure. Turn the vessel pressure control to a high value of 0.5 psig and a low value of 0.4 psig.

3  Procedure

3.1   Vertical Penetration Deicing

NOTICE
Make sure the Vertical Penetration is clear of any obstructions before inserting/extracting any tools/apparatus to prevent possible magnet damage.

When removing ice in the Vertical Penetration, avoid contacting the Instrumentation Lead Connector with ice clearing tools. Contact could damage the lead or connector.

 
 

  1. Either turn Magnet Monitor power off, or disable the Magnet Monitor's Master Alarm and Vessel Pressure Control. (This will prevent false alarms and long-term heater operation.)

  1. Ramp down the magnet (if at field) in conformance with Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field.

  1. Turn off the Cryocooler to prevent ice migration into the Recondenser.

    1. Turn the Coldhead Drive Switch ON (up position), located on the Compressor's panel. (See Illustration 2.)

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the Drive Switch on the Compressor's front panel in the O (Off) position. (See Illustration 2.)

    1. Run the Coldhead for only 10 seconds with the Compressor turned off to prevent pressure buildup in the Coldhead, then turn OFF the Coldhead Drive Switch and the Compressor's Main Power Switch. (See Illustration 2.)

    WARNING
    Electrical Shock Hazard!
    Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
    Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow lockout/tagout procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

    1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Compressor. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Vent the magnet down to 0.2 psig by opening/closing Vent Valve V2. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Make sure magnet pressure is 0.2 psig, venting the magnet as required.

NOTICE
Perform Step 6 through Step 7 rapidly to prevent cryopumping of air into the Vertical Stack. Make sure Lexan Cover Plate 46-294765G1 is on the Service Platform and the holes are plugged before removing the Top Cover Plate Assembly.
 
 

  1. Loosen and remove the eight 1/4-20 retaining bolts securing the Top Cover Plate Assembly (see Illustration 3) and remove it.

  1. Immediately cover the Vertical Penetration with the Lexan Penetration Cover Plate 46-294765G1, and align the scribe mark toward the Vent Adapter.

  1. Secure the Lexan Penetration Cover Plate onto the Vertical Penetration with the eight 1/4-20 retaining bolts removed earlier.

  1. Attach Deicing Purge Tube 2347725 to the end of the Tygon tubing, and purge the tube at 4 psig.

  1. Remove the plugs on the Cover Plate, and insert the Deicing Purge Tube through one of the Ramp Lead holes. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove ice by directing an 8 to 10 psig flow of warm helium gas onto the Vertical Penetration and other affected areas while Vent Valve V2 is partially open.

  1. Shine the flashlight through the Lexan Penetration Cover Plate, and verify that all ice is removed inside.

  1. Remove any remaining ice on the inside of the Vertical Penetration. (Some bending of the Deicing Purge Tube may be required.)

  1. Shine a flashlight through the Lexan Penetration Cover Plate. When all ice is removed, close V2, and remove the Lexan Cover Plate.

  1. Temporarily disconnect the Instrumentation Lead Connector using the Connector Insertion/Removal Tool (46-281934P1).

  1. Direct an 8 to 10 psig flow of warm helium gas at the connector until all ice is removed, then reconnect the Instrumentation Lead Connector.

NOTICE
Perform all MRU checks in upon completion of Vertical Penetration ice removal to make sure no Instrumentation Lead Connector damage has occurred. Correct any damage found by reconnecting the Instrumentation Lead Connector or replacing the Instrumentation Lead Assembly.
 
 

  1. Check the Instrumentation Lead Connector by performing Magnet Rundown Unit (MRU) Checks.

  1. After completing the above step, remove LOTO, connect input power to the Compressor, and turn on the Cryocooler.

  1. Leak check the magnet in conformance with Leak check the magnet in conformance with Temperature, Pressure and Leak Troubleshooting. Repair any leaks found.

  1. Return the Magnet Monitor to normal operation by re-enabling the Magnet Monitor's Master Alarm and Vessel Pressure Control or by turning Magnet Monitor power on.

3.2   Recondenser Deicing

Recondenser Deicing, Recondenser Deicing, contains two procedures:

  • Level One Deicing, Level One Deicing

  • Level Four Deicing, Level Four Deicing

    • The Level Four Deicing procedure requires greater cost and down time than the Level One procedure. It requires a magnet ramp down warm up and a dry nitrogen purge. Always evaluate the magnet, start with the Level One procedure, and proceed to the Level Four procedure only if the Level One procedure fails to clear the ice.

3.2.1  Initial Magnet Evaluation

NOTICE
Check the flow through Vent Valve V2. If the flow from V2 is restricted, slightly open the Fill Port. If there is no flow from V2 or the Fill Port, immediately contact your MAC Team Representative before continuing.
 
 

  1. Evaluate the magnet's condition before taking any action involving changes to the magnet operating condition. Confirm that the magnet is not operating in Controlled Zero Boil-Off (C0BO) condition, where the heater maintains the magnet pressure at 4 psig ±0.1 psig, before continuing.

  1. Verify that the Magnet Monitor functions properly for C0BO operation. Correct any problems found, and recheck for C0BO operation per Step 1.

  1. Verify flow through the shield vent line either by frost on the 5.25 psig relief valve line at point “A" or by momentarily cracking the 1/2 in. NPT plug at point “B" (both shown in Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Determine if the second stage Coldhead temperature is ≤4.0K.

  1. If the shield vent line is blocked, remove and inspect the 5.25 psig relief valve. Remove any blockage and reinstall the valve.

  1. If the shield vent line is still blocked and/or the coldhead temperature is ≤4.0K, continue with Level One Deicing, Level One Deicing.

3.2.2  Level One Deicing

The Level One Deicing procedure denies cooling flow to the Recondenser and expands shield-trapped gas back through the Recondenser using the DVw De-ice Heater Kit (5342456).

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
A magnet quench during Ice Removal could result in rapid expulsion of liquid helium through the Vertical Penetration.
Make sure magnet is ramped down to zero field before starting portions of the ice removal procedure.

NOTICE
Make sure the Coldhead is off during Level One Deicing to prevent any re-icing of purged contaminants in the Recondenser.
 
 

  1. Verify that the helium level is <90% with ≥4.0 psig or <85% at 1 to 2 psig. (A helium level greater than this could result in an overfill condition and block the Recondenser cycle.)

  1. If the pressure is ≥4.0 psig, partially open Vent Valve V2 (shown in Illustration 3), and vent down to 4 psig. If the shield vent line is blocked, remove the 5.25 psig relief valve and leave the end open.

  1. Referring to Illustration 6, shut down the compressor to prevent pressure build-up in the Coldhead as follows:

    1. Turn ON (upward) the Coldhead Drive Switch on the Compressor's back panel.

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the Drive Switch on the Compressor's front panel in the 'O' (Off) position.

    WARNING
    Electrical Shock Hazard!
    Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.

    1. Run the Coldhead for the duration of the deicing procedure.

  1. Set up the De-ice cable (5340948) from the DVw De-icing Kit (5342456):

    1. Connect the 10-Pin female Amphenol connector to the cold sleeve instrumentation port at the magnet.

    1. (Optional) Connect the 10-Pin male Amphenol connector to either the MagMon3 interface cable, or a MTM-101 Handheld Magnet Temperature Meter (5119507) if you wish to continue monitoring the magnet.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect the dual banana jack to Loop 1 (Heater Out) port at the back panel of the Lake/shore 325 Controller (5341054). Polarity does not matter.

      (Step Details)

    1. Connect the two 6-Pin plugs to Input A and Input B at the back panel of the Lakeshore 325 Controller (5341054).

  1. The Lakeshore 325 Temperature Controller can be setup to Read Stage 1 and Stage 2 diodes from Input A and Input B respectively.

    Illustration 9:  Front Panel of Lakeshore 325 Controller

    1. Make sure Lakeshore 325 Temperature Controller is powered on and the de-icing cable is connected.

    1. Press the Input Setup button on the front panel.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Input Setup: Input A, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Type: Silicon Diode, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Curve 01: DT-470, then press Enter.

    1. Repeat the above step for Input B.

    1. The Input A and Input B diode readings should appear on the front panel display

  1. Set up the Lakeshore 325 Temperature Controller for open-loop operation:

    1. Press the Control Setup button on the front panel.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Control with: Input A, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select SP Units: Temp K, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Control Mode: Open, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Power up: Enable, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Heater out: Power, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select SetPoint ramp: Off, and then press Enter.

    1. Use the up and down arrow buttons to select Heater load: 50 ohm, and then press Enter.

  1. The full power of Loop 1 (Heater Out) is 12 Watts. Set Loop 1 power to 50%:

    1. Make sure Lakeshore 325 Temperature Controller is powered on and the de-icing cable is connected.

    1. Press the Manual Heater +/- button on the front panel.

      (Step Details)

    1. If “Manual Out” is not 50%, press the 5 and 0 buttons to get 50%, and then press Enter.

  1. Turn the Loop 1 (Heater Out) power on:

    1. Press the Heater Range button on the front panel.

      (Step Details)

    1. Press the Heater Range button again until the LED display shows Select for Loop 1, Heater Range: High.

    1. Press Enter to turn the power on. The supply is now outputting power to the heaters in the magnet.

      (Step Details)

  1. Do not allow the RuO B, Stage 1 temperature to exceed 150K. Verify by the diode reading from the Lakeshore 325 controller Input B or by direct RuO B measurement using the MTM-101 Magnet Temperature Meter. See Illustration 9.

  1. Gradually increase Loop 1 Heater Out power to 100% in increments of 10% every five minutes.

    1. Press the Manual Heater +/- button on the front panel.

    1. Increase the output by 10% by entering 60 (or the next increment), and then pressing Enter.

  1. Monitor the Coldhead RuOB temperature. When it stabilizes at an elevated temperature (>80K), check for helium gas flow through the 5.25 psig relief valve (indicating the blockage has cleared).

    (Step Details)

  1. After four hours, check for helium flow through the 5.25 psig line or valve, turn the Coldhead on again and reset the Magnet Monitor for C0BO operation (4.0 psig ±0.1 psig).

  1. Remove the De-ice cable, turn off power to the heater and reconnect the magnet instrumentation for normal operation.

  1. If the 5.25 psig safety valve was removed before deicing, reinstall the valve and check for leaks.

  1. Monitor the Recondenser - Coldhead temperature difference (ΔT) as well as Coldhead performance to verify ΔT is <0.2K and that the second stage Coldhead temperature is ≤4.3K.

    (Step Details)

3.2.3  Level Four Deicing

The Level Four Deicing procedure is a complete magnet warm up and dry purge. Try Level One Deicing before progressing to Level Four.

NOTICE
Make sure the Coldhead is off during Level Four Deicing to prevent any re-icing of purged contaminants in the Recondenser.
 
 

  1. If Level One Deicing, Level One Deicing, has failed to deice the magnet, ramp down the magnet in conformance with Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field.

  1. Warm and dry purge the magnet in conformance with Cryostat Warm-Up.
