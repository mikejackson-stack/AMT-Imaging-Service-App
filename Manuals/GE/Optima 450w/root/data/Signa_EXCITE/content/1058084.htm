8kW 3.0T MNS Amplifier Calibration

(Click here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

Follow this procedure after Installing the Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) to calibrate the 8kW AMT Amplifier for 3.0T.

3.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD !
FATAL ELECTRICAL VOLTAGES exist THROUGHOUT THE MNS CABINET.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THE MNS RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY!
POSSIBLE RF BURN WHEN DISCONNECTING HELIAX CABLES FROM THE RF AMPLIFIER.
ensure THAT THE SCAN DESKTOP ICON DISPLAYS THE “IDLE” MESSAGE AND THE MNS EXciter rf-enable SWITCH IS IN THE "DISABLE" (down) POSITION.

NOTICE
Possible equipment damage to RF amplifier connected with an improper load to its output. Do not operate the RF amplifier with an improper load connected to its output. Doing so may permanently damage the amplifier
 
 

4  Procedure

  1. Disable the T/R fault monitor on the driver module with the following steps:

    1. Ensure the system is idle and all coils have been removed from the magnet bore.

    1. Open the front door of the PEN Cabinet.

    1. On the front of the driver module, set the T/R switch to Disable. This will disable the T/R fault reporting (see Illustration 1).

      NOTE:HV and DD should remain set to Enable.

      Illustration 1:  Driver Module Switch

    1. Move the RF Enable S2 switch on MNS Exciter into disable (down) position (see Illustration 2).

      Illustration 2:  RF Enable Switch

  1. Use the digital multimeter to measure the resistance between the dummy load RF connector center pin and the shield. Confirm a reading of 50 ohm (+/- 5 ohm). Replace dummy load if out of range.

  1. Set up the Power Meter Method as shown in Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Calibrating MNS Amplifier - Power Meter Method

    1. Set up the RF coupler for the AMT 8kW calibration. Choose the correct elements based on the Quick Reference Card provided with the RF Power Measurement Kit (see Illustration 4).

      Illustration 4:  8kW RF Power Measurement Kit Quick Reference Card

    1. Insert the 50kW into the forward sensor port and ensure the arrow points from input to output (see Illustration 5).

    1. As a safety measure, place the 5kW or blank slug element into the reflected port and ensure the arrow points from output to input (if applies) (see Illustration 5).

    1. Lock these elements into place using the locking tab on each sensor (see Illustration 5).

      Illustration 5:  RF Coupler Set

      AWattmeter Sensor
      BForward Sensor Element
      CReflected Sensor Element
      DSensor Locking Tabs

  1. Move RF Enable S2 switch on MNS Exciter into enable (up) position (see Illustration 2).

  1. Set up the wattmeter to measure forward maximum power.

    1. Press the ON button on the wattmeter. (If coupler and sensor are not correctly attached to the amplifier, a “No Sensor” message will display)

    1. Select the up arrow button under scale.

    1. For the AMT amplifier calibration, type 50000 to set the range. Press the <Enter> button.

    1. Check the setting by selecting the up arrow under scale once again. Press <Enter> to exit this mode.

    1. Select the up arrow button under Fwd Units to make sure the scale reads “W” (or “kW” on some wattmeters). If the scale reads dBm, select the up arrow button under Fwd Units again to toggle the scale to “W” (or “kW”) on the TOP, forward power scale. Ignore the BOTTOM, reflected power scale value as it is not used during maximum power calibration.

    1. Press the <Shift> button.

    1. Select the up arrow button under Setup. Ensure it reads 43. If not, type the number 43 and press the <Enter> button.

      NOTE:This action display the sensor type configured in the wattmeter. This procedure uses sensor Type 43 which is optimized for peak power measurement.

      Illustration 6:  Wattmeter Display (Peak Power Measurement)

    1. Select the up arrow button under Type and change the unit of measurement to Peak. The meter will now be configured to read the RF peak power for the 8kW maximum output calibration

    1. Press the [Offset] button on the wattmeter. Enter in the 3.0T MNS 8kW offset value from the RF Power Measurement Kit Quick Reference Card (see Illustration 4).

  1. Connect MNS 31P T/R switch to LPCA.

  1. Landmark in center of head coil area and select [Advance to Scan].

  1. Setup the scan parameters following these steps:

    1. Click the [Scan New Pt] button.

      Illustration 7:  New Patient Icon

    1. Insert Patient ID: geservice , then press <Enter>.

    1. Insert Weight (lbs): 300, then press <Enter>.

    1. Click [Show All Protocols...] button.

      1. Click [Service] button.

      1. Click the Other tab.

      1. Scroll through protocols and highlight 49.2 apbcal. Select Series 2, Head and click the right arrow to select.

      1. Click [Accept].

    1. Click the [Start Exam] button.

    1. Click [Coil].

      1. Under Coil Type, select [Currently Connected].

      1. Select the TR Switch, MNS TR Configuration option.

      1. Click [Accept].

      1. Click [Save Rx].

    1. Select Research Options from the drop down menu next to the Scan button.

      1. Choose Setup Params .

      1. Set TG to 0

      1. Click [Done].

    1. From the drop down menu next to the Scan button, highlight Research Options and choose [Display CVs].

      1. In the CV name window, set value of calmode to 5, then press <Enter>.

      1. Set the value of the mns_xmit to 1 (for MNS), then press <Enter>.

      1. Set value of spec_nuc to match element number printed on TR Switch (such as 31 or 13). Press <Enter>.

      1. Click [Accept].

  1. Calibrate the amplifier with the following steps:

    1. Remove the front cover of the AMT amplifier (Note: There are 50+ screws. This step takes about eight minutes.). With the covers removed, notice the two holes for gross (6.0dB) and fine (0.5 dB) attenuation adjustments as pictured in Illustration 8 and Illustration 9.

      NOTE:It is possible that these two holes are covered with an external screw. If this is the case, simply remove the screw so that the amplifier looks like the one in Illustration 8.

      Illustration 8:  AMT Amplifier with Covers Removed.

      Indicators point to adjustment points.

      Illustration 9:  AMT Amplifier Detail of dB Adjustment

    1. Click on [Manual Prescan].

  1. In manual prescan window, increase TG to 50. If the RF amplifier faults, then reduce the TG to 0. Wait for the amplifier to recover, then press [Scan] button on SCIM.

  1. Increase the TG to 200 so that wattmeter reads 8kW + 200W/- 800W.

    NOTE:In the following steps, make attenuator adjustments slowly. Give wattmeter about five seconds between changes to settle. If amplifier faults, reverse the previous adjustments and select [Start Scan] on SCIM when it illuminates.

  1. If TG equals 200, but wattmeter reads less than 7.2kW, rotate 0.5dB adjustment control counterclockwise to achieve 8kW +200 / -800W.

  1. If output is still less than 7.2 kW and TG = 200:

    1. Set 0.5dB adjustment control for minimum wattmeter reading.

    1. Rotate 6.0dB adjustment control counter-clockwise by one step.

    1. Proceed to Step 12.

  1. If wattmeter reads 8kW +200 / -0W before TG reaches 200:

    1. Rotate 6.0dB adjustment control clockwise by one step.

    1. Proceed to Step 11.

  1. Select [Done] to end manual prescan.

5  Finalization

  1. Ensure that the scanner is “idle” and disable RF Enable switch on MNS Exciter (see Illustration 2).

  1. Disconnect the RF watt sensor from the RF output connection.

  1. Connect the original output cable to the MNS RF amplifier and tighten the connector.

  1. Perform remaining cleanup procedures.

  1. Turn on the RF Enable.

  1. Place T/R Switch on front of the driver module in the enable position (see Illustration 1).

  1. Reset the TPS to put the amplifier back into a known state.

  1. Perform a goodbye scan to ensure the system is functioning correctly.

  1. Perform the UPM - MNS Functional Check.

  1. Perform the Multi Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) Functional Check.
