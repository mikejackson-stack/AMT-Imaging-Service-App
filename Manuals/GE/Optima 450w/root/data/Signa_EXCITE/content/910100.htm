2  Overview

The Power, Gradient, RF Cabinet (PGR) Power Distribution Unit (PDU) and eXtreme Gradient Amplifier (XGA) reside in the PGR cabinet. The XGA is part of the eXtreme Gradient Driver subsystem (XGD) . Prior to servicing and/or maintenance work on the PGR PDU or any element of the XGD, the steps in this Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) procedure must be performed by a LOTO-authorized GE Field Engineer. Completing all the steps in this procedure ensures a safe environment and avoids equipment damage when working on these parts.

4  Procedure

4.1  Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy – five minutes.

4.2  Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnection(s)

  1. Two personal locks and tags per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

  1. Voltmeter to read voltage for verification that energy has dissipated in the PDU.

4.3  Applying LOTO

  1. To prepare for shutdown of equipment, notify affected personnel working in the area the LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the Operator Workspace, Shut down the Host PC.

  1. At the PGR cabinet, go to the front panel of the PGR PDU and press the POWER OFF button.

    Illustration 1:  Shut Down PGR PDU Power and LOTO

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU rotate the large main breaker counterclockwise to the Green O (OFF) position (see Illustration 1).

  1. At the front panel of the PGR PDU pull out the locking mechanism on the end of the main breaker to expose the hole where the Lock and Tag can be placed.

    Place the LOTO lock on the PGR PDU main breaker. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Locking the main breaker

  1. Locate and turn off the PGR PDU main breaker on the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) which feeds the PGR cabinet.

    Lock Out/Tag Out the PDU breaker on the MDP. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  PDU breaker on MDP

  1. Verify that all energy has been dissipated. Use a voltmeter to measure the voltage between each INPUT phase voltage test point and GND (Ground) on the PDU. See Illustration 4.

    NOTE:The test points are 100:1 ratio (208VAC = 2.08VAC, 480VAC = 4.80VAC). Therefore, a low voltage reading still indicates the presence of high voltage. Your voltage reading for each of the three phases must be 0VAC before proceeding.

    Input Voltage Range: 380 - 480VAC = 3.8 - 4.8VAC

    Output Voltage Range: 208VAC = 2.08 VAC

    Illustration 4:  PDU test points

4.4  Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment.

    Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the MDP.

  1. Switch on the MDP's PDU breaker.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PGR PDU.

  1. Rotate the larger main breaker clockwise to the RED I (ON) position.

    Illustration 5:  Power on the PGR PDU and remove LOTO

  1. Press the EMO RESET switch (see Illustration 5).

  1. Power up the Host PC.
