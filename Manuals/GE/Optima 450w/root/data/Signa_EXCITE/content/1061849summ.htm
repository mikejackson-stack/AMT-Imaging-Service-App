Network Switch Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous voltages Exist Within The System Cabinet When energized.
Turn off the Power to the Network switch with the circuit breaker located in the top area of the PGR Cabinet.

NOTICE
Perform LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics) before performing this procedure.
 
 

2  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Turn the power off to the network switch circuit breaker before unplugging (see Removal/Replacement of Network Switch, Step 2).

-

-

Turn off all ICNs from the Common Service Desktop. Go to Utilities >> Toolbox >> VRE >> Power Control. Click [Power Off] for all ICNs.

-

-

3  Procedure

3.1   Removal/Replacement of Network Switch

  1. Locate the network switch in right side of the PGR cabinet. The network switch is located underneath the ICNs (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Turn off the network switch circuit breaker (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the Ethernet cables from the front of the network switch, taking note of the order and placement of the terminal wires that they plugged into (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Using a Phillips screw driver, unscrew the four (4) screws on the front of the switch.

  1. Pull the network switch forward to remove it from the cabinet.

  1. Turn the power off with the switch located next to the power plug in the rear of the network switch.

  1. Remove the power plug from the outlet at the back of the switch.

    (Step Details)

3.2   Re-install and Power-up Procedure

  1. Plug in the network switch's power connection.

  1. Ensure that the power switch in the rear of the network switch is in the ON position.

  1. Carefully slide the network switch into the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Secure the switch into its original location by tightening its four (4) screws.

  1. Reconnect all Ethernet cables into the front of the network switch.

  1. Ensure the power light on the front of the network switch is on and that the lights for each Ethernet connection shows that the connection is active (see Illustration 3).

4  Finalization

  1. Reboot the host computer.

  1. Perform verification scan.
