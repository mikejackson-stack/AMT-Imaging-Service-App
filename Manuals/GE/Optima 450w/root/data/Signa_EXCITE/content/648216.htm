2  Overview

Follow this process to replace the cable assembly on the 3.0T HD Shoulder Coil by GE/USAI, catalog M3334TR.

4  Procedure

  1. Remove the cable from the coil.

    1. Remove the 9 screws holding the cover on the unit as shown in Illustration 1.

      Illustration 1:  Remove the 9 Screws

    1. Disconnect the RF cables and the DC wires from the feed boards as shown in Illustration 2.

      Illustration 2:  Disconnect The Cables

    1. Remove two screws on the clamp of strain relief as shown in Illustration 3.

      Illustration 3:  Strain Relief

    1. Remove the cable from the coil.

  1. Insert the new cable to the coil.

    1. Attach the cable with the cable clamp to the coil as shown in Illustration 4.

      Illustration 4:  Attach The Cable

    1. Connect the DC wires and RF cables to the board, see Illustration 5.

      NOTE: The cables are marked for proper placement.

      Illustration 5:  Connect Cables

    1. Replace the two covers and the 9 retaining screws, see Illustration 1.
