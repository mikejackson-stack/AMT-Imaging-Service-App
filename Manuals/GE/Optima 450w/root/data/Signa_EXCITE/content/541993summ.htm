HDx Service Platform 5155291 Set-Up

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

CAUTION
Potential Pinch Hazard
Fingers placed in the path of moving components while HDx Service Platform 5155291 is being assembled/adjusted can be pinched.
Do not place fingers in the path of any HDx Service Platform 5155291 components being assembled/adjusted.

CAUTION
Potential Scratches/Cuts
HDx Service Platform 5155291 may have edges sharp enough to scratch or cut skin.
Wear leather gloves while assembling, adjusting or disassembling HDx Service Platform 5155291 to protect against scratches/cuts and to keep your hands clean.

1  Procedure

  1. Position the ladder components in an open area away from the magnet about 6 feet (1800 mm) square.

  1. Lay the platform section on the floor with its underside facing up as in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert each ladder leg in the ladder sockets on the platform's underside as in Illustration 1.

  1. Securely latch both ladder latches of each ladder leg to the platform. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
Always extend and secure the outriggers fully out (as in Illustration 3) before using the HDx Service Platform at its 60 in. (1524 mm) platform height.
 
 

  1. Adjust both telescoping ladder legs to the platform height required. See Illustration 2. Pinning holes are available for 40 in., 50 in. and 60 in. (1016 mm, 1270 mm and 1524 mm) platform heights.

    1. Disengage each Telescoping Leg Locking Pin.

    1. Simultaneously slide both sides of each ladder section until the pinning hole for the platform height desired aligns with the Locking Pin.

    1. Securely engage all four Locking Pins in their pinning holes.

    1. If adjusting the platform to its 60 in. (1524 mm) height, fully extend and securely pin all four Ladder Outriggers. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Verify that all connections/adjustments are secure, then carefully flip the platform and legs upright.

CAUTION
Potential Back Strain
Poor posture when lifting can cause back strain/damage.
Keep the upper body directly over your legs and use the service ladder's lifting handles when lifting an end of the service ladder.

  1. Use the service platform's lifting handles to lift one ladder end high enough to engage the other ladder end's wheels. Move the platform into the magnet room before lowering the end lifted back to the floor.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Long Side Safety Rail into two Pipe Sleeves along the long edge of the platform away from the magnet as in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Short Side Safety Rail into two Pipe Sleeves along a short edge of the platform, typically at the service end of the magnet. See Illustration 4.

  1. Insert the Magnet Side Toeboard along the magnet side of the platform. See Illustration 4.

  1. Repeat Step 7 whenever relocating the assembled HDx Service Platform 5155291 inside the magnet room.

2  Finalization

  1. Disassemble HDx Service Platform 5155291 in reverse order to assembling it.

  1. Always return HDx Service Platform 5155291 to its designated storage location after usage.
