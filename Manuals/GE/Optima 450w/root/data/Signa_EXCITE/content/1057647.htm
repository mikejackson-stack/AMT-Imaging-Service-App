3  Procedure

3.1  Removing the Body Hybrid Splitter

  1. Lock out and tag out the PDM-RF power for the system (see LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Make sure that the scanner is idle. Select [End Exam] if the scanner is at any point in the scan process.

  1. Remove the rear cable cover. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Removing the Rear Covers

  1. Remove the right side cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the top right cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the right skirt cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the right pedestal cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove the right rear cover. See Illustration 1.

  1. Remove all cables connected to the body hybrid.

  1. Loosen the four (4) nuts that secure the 1.5T Body Hybrid to the magnet. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  1.5T Body Hybrid Module

  1. Remove the four (4) L-brackets from the old hybrid to be reused on the new module.

  1. While carrying the module out of the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

3.2  Installing the Body Hybrid Module

  1. Attach the L-brackets from the old module onto the new hybrid module.

  1. While carrying the module into the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. Secure the module to the magnet by tightening the four (4) nuts provided.

  1. Attach cables to the new hybrid module.

4  Finalization

  1. Replace All Covers Removed.

  1. Reapply power to the system, remove all LOTO equipment.

  1. Complete a Body and Head scan to makes sure the system is functional.
