3  Procedure

3.1  Removing the 3.0T RF Attenuator

  1. Lock out / Tag out the PDM-RF Power on PDU according to the procedure, LOTO for RF Subsystem and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Remove the side covers from the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Remove cables J12, J13 and J14 from the bottom of the 3.0T RF Attenuator.

  1. Remove the six (6) Allen screws from the rear of the 3.0T RF Attenuator. See Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Remove Allen Screws

3.2  Re-installing the 3.0T RF Attenuator

  1. Mount the 3.0T RF Attenuator to the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Tighten the six (6) Allen screws to secure it. See Illustration 1.

  1. Re-attach cables J12, J13 and J14 to the bottom of the 3.0T RF Attenuator.

4  Finalization

  1. Replace the side covers for the Rear Pedestal.

  1. Remove the Lock out / Tag out for the PDM - RF Power on PDU according to the procedure, LOTO for RF Subsystems and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics.

  1. Complete the UPM Quad calibration.

  1. Perform scan to check system performance.
