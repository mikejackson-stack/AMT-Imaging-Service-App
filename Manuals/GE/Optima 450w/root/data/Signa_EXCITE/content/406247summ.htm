1.5T HD Lower Leg Coil Setup for Coil SNR Test

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

The following coil configuration names must be installed to run this tool: HD LowerLeg_serv, HD_LowerLegLserv, and HD_LowerLegRserv.

-

-

2  Procedure

2.1   Unified Phantom Set Installation

2.1.1  Mode: Lower Leg LEFT

  1. Place the base of the coil on the patient table and position the left leg segments on the base as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the small cylindrical unified phantom in the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom in the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the cubicle unified phantom in the foot part of the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cover the phantoms with the anterior portion of the coil, as shown in Illustration 5, using the straps provided.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect both the cables of the preamp box to the coil, as shown in Illustration 6, and then connect the cables of preamp box to port A and port B of the system LPCA as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to the respective port of system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil on the marks provided, as shown in Illustration 8, and press [advance to scan].

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Test with the Unified Phantom Set.

  1. For Lower Leg Array left mode, the following phantom sets are listed from the dropdown menu as shown in Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select Unified Phantom Set – Dual connector – Left Mode from the dropdown menu.

  1. Proceed with the test.

2.1.2  Mode: Lower Leg RIGHT

  1. Place the base of the coil on the patient table and position the left leg segments on the base as shown in Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the small cylindrical unified phantom in the right leg portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 11.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the cubicle unified phantom in the foot part of the left leg portion of the coil as shown in Illustration 12.

    (Step Details)

  1. Cover the phantoms with the anterior portion of the coil, as shown in Illustration 13, using the straps provided.

    (Step Details)

  1. While covering the foot part, make sure that the arrow marks are aligned with each other as shown in Illustration 14.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect both the cables of the preamp box to the coil, as shown in Illustration 15, and then connect the cables of preamp box to port A and port B of the system LPCA as shown in Illustration 16.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to the respective ports of system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil on the marks provided, as shown in Illustration 17, and press [advance to scan].

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the Multi-Coil Quality Assurance (MCQA) Test with the Unified Phantom Set.

  1. For Lower Leg Array Right mode, the following phantom sets are listed from the dropdown menu as shown in Illustration 18.

    (Step Details)

  1. Select Unified Phantom Set – Dual connector – Right Mode from the dropdown menu.

  1. Proceed with the test.
