2  Overview

The Motor On actuation switch in the dock assembly should be adjusted so it is activated when the Table Up foot pedal is pressed approximately 3/4 of its total length of travel. This is factory adjusted; however, it is possible to adjust the position of the Motor On switch in the field to maintain the proper actuation level.

4  Procedure

4.1   Removing the Dock from Magnet Room

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, DISCONNECT POWER FROM THE PDU BEFORE YOU PERFORM THE FOLLOWING PROCEDURES.
PERFORM LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURE PER GE OSHA LOCKOUT / TAGOUT REQUIREMENTS LISTED IN THE FIELD SERVICE EHS MANUAL & TRAINING 2000 P/N 2135387-200.

  1. Perform Lock Out Tag Out. See LOTO for XFRD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics)

  1. Undock the Patient Table and remove it from the scan room.

  1. On the magnet, remove the right and left side skirt covers. For more information, see the Skirt Cover Removal and Install procedure.

  1. Disconnect all cable connections from the magnet to the rear of the dock.

  1. At rear of the dock, use a 3/4 inch non-ferrous wrench to remove four (4) nuts (2 on each side) from dock bracket bolts. See Illustration 1

    Illustration 1:  Dock Brackets

    NOTE:It is necessary to remove only the nuts from the rear of the dock that connects it to the dock brackets. You do not have to remove the dock brackets from the magnet (see Illustration 2), otherwise you will have to go through table leveling and alignment procedures during reassembly.

    Illustration 2:  Dock Brackets at rear of Dock

    NOTE:Anytime you remove the dock from the magnet, you face the possible task of having to check levelness and alignment of the dock and Patient Table when you reattach the dock to the magnet. As a helpful tip, before you move the dock from its position on the floor, use pieces of tape to mark points on the floor where the dock is to be replaced.

WARNING
back injury
DOCK IS HEAVY, WEIGHING MORE THAN 50 LBS.
TWO PEOPLE ARE NECESSARY TO MOVE DOCK OUT OF MAGNET ROOM TO PERFORM MAINTENANCE.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THIS PROCEDURE REQUIRES REMOVAL OF FERROUS MATERIAL IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO THE MAGNET
TWO FIELD ENGINEERS ARE REQUIRED AT ALL TIMES.

  1. Using two people, remove the dock from the magnet room.

    1. With two field engineers, one holding each side of the dock, slide the dock away from the magnet front to where the foot of the patient table would normally be located.

    1. When a safe distance away from the magnetic field (at least as far as the foot end of the patient table), two field engineers may slowly lift the dock from the floor and exit the room.

4.2  Adjusting Motor On Switch Actuation

  1. Using a 3/4 inch wrench, remove four bolts (2 on each side) to unfasten the dock cover from the dock base. See Illustration 1.

  1. While removing the dock cover disconnect the switch connector.

  1. Carefully remove the dock cover from the dock base and then turn the dock cover upside down.

    NOTE:Use care to ensure the top of the cover is not scratched. Lay down a cloth before setting the cover on the floor.

  1. Loosen two mounting screws securing Motor On switch to side of dock assembly.

  1. Adjust Motor On switch position so it is activated (clicks) when the Table Up foot pedal is pressed approximately 3/4 of its total length of travel.

  1. After adjustment is complete, tighten two mounting screws.

  1. Replace main dock cover by securing four mounting bolts.

WARNING
POSSIBLE PERSONAL INJURY!
THE DOCK CONTAINS FERROUS MATERIALS.
FROM THIS POINT OF THE PROCEDURE FORWARD, DOWNWARD PRESSURE MUST BE CONTINUOUSLY APPLIED TO THE DOCK SO AS TO PREVENT IT FROM BEING PULLED INTO THE MAGNET BORE.

  1. Move dock into magnet room, observing danger precautions noted above.

  1. Reattach dock to dock brackets. Hint: use tape markers on floor if available.

  1. Remove Lock Out Tag Out.

  1. Dock Patient Table.

  1. Ensure that dock Motor On switch operates properly when Table Up pedal is pressed.

  1. Verify that cradle travel into bore is still OK. If not, refer to Dock Adjustments and perform recentering procedure for the dock/table.

  1. Perform a “good-bye” or validation scan.
