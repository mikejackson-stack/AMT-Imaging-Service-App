• If pressure is above 1 psig, slowly open Dewar's Vent Valve and reduce Dewar pressure to 1 psig.

  • If Dewar pressure does not vent down to 1 psig, verify that the Dewar pressure relief valve is left in the open position. Contact the cryogen supplier immediately.

NOTE:The Pressure Relief Valve is normally open during shipping and storage to prevent excessive build up of pressure in the Dewar. Always reopen the Pressure Relief Valve after using a Dewar.

If 99.999% (five nines) Helium Gas is used, the purity of the gas remaining in the cylinder will degrade (to something less than 99.999%) as a result of this process.
