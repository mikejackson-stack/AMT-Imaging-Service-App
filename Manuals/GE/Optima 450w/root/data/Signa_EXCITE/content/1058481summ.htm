Hard Disk Drive Replacement for HP8400 PC

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages Exist Inside The Global operator console (Goc) when Power Is Applied.
Before Servicing Any Components Inside The Goc, Perform lock out/tag out.

1  Procedure

  1. Refer to LOTO GOC/Operator Workspace before beginning the replacement.

  1. Remove the two (2) screws that secure the left side panel. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by lifting up. Avoid straining the short ground wire, which can be disconnected at the middle junction if necessary, that connects the cover to the GOC chassis. (See Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:  Left Side Panel Ground Lead

  1. Lift up on the latch and remove the left side panel of the PC. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the power cable and the data cable from each of the hard disk drives. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove each of the three (3) drives by pinching the green restraining tabs together as shown in Illustration 5, then slowly pulling the drive out.

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm that the data cables from all three drives are connected to the correct SAS device locations as shown in Illustration 6 and Table 1.

    Illustration 6:  SAS Device Locations

    (Step Details)

  1. Install the replacement hard drive by reversing Step 5 through Step 7.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform LOTO to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Perform the applicable Loading Host System Software procedure to load software and applications onto the operating system.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
