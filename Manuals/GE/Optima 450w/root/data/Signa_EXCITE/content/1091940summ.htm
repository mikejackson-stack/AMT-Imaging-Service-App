Potential Fatal Injury!
before filling the magnet:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions, and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for Liquid Helium Fill in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before filling the magnet:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Liquid Helium Fill.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggle when filling the magnet.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
The Liquid Helium Fill procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on (or the hatch is opened if a mobile van) before filling the magnet.

WARNING
Explosion Hazard!
Liquid cryogens can liquefy atmospheric oxygen, producing a highly combustible oxygen liquid.
No smoking in magnet and cryogen storage rooms.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Never bring helium Dewars or gas cylinders that are made from ferromagnetic material into the magnet room. Make sure all equipment and tools used in the magnet room are nonferromagnetic.

WARNING
Potential High Pressure Gas Release
If a gas cylinder falls, the valve outlet could shear, releasing hazardous high pressure gas.
Secure the gas cylinder before removing the protective valve cap.

NOTICE
For the MR450w, the DVw Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage. (See Illustration 1.)

3  Procedure

3.1   Helium Fill Equipment

Helium fills should be performed as top fills using the indicated equipment unless it is the initial Dewar fill into a empty magnet. Top filling magnets with helium will result in lower cryostat pressures and higher transfer efficiencies. Select the proper cryostat and Dewar stinger lengths to be used during a Top or Bottom fill.

NOTE:A Teflon Tip is installed on each cryostat stinger and stinger extension to protect the threads. Cryostat Stinger Teflon Caps must be removed from threads prior to fill.

  1. Top Fill: Preferred fill method, and should be used if any liquid helium exists in the magnet. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
If bottom filling, make sure the 16 in. (406 mm) Cryostat Stinger Extension and Teflon Tip are firmly secured during installation to prevent them from loosening and falling into the magnet.
 
 

NOTE:For a low-ceiling option site, use two 8 in. (203 mm) stinger extensions instead of the 16 in. (406 mm) stinger extensions. Insert the 8 in. (203 mm) stinger extensions into the Magnet, and screw onto the 7 in. (178 mm) Cryostat Stinger Assembly (see Illustration 3) one at a time.

  1. Bottom Fill: Performed only when there is no liquid helium in the magnet such as after a quench. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

3.2   Preparation

Refer to Volumetric Conversion of Helium Level to determine the approximate quantity of liquid helium required to fill the cryostat. An empty and partially warmed cryostat will require an additional quantity of liquid helium to cool the cryostat down to the 4.3K temperature, where liquid helium will begin to collect in the cryostat.

  1. If there is helium in the magnet, proceed to Step 4.

  1. If there is no helium in the magnet, refer to Magnet Commissioning Checks to check shield temperatures.

  1. If temperature is >100K, refer to Magnet Commissioning Checks for cryostat cooling/filling requirements.

NOTICE
Make sure the Cryogen Level Meter was calibrated in conformance with the Magnet Monitor Manual. A calibrated Cryogen Level Meter is required to accurately determine helium fill level and efficiency.
 
 

  1. Check Magnet Monitor calibration. Read and record the liquid helium level in Helium Fill Data. Make sure the Magnet Monitor sample rate is set to 11. (Refer to the vendor's manual for Magnet Monitor calibration.)

  1. Obtain a full LHe Dewar. Check the Dewar's pressure gauge. (See Illustration 4.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Obtain one full aluminum 135 SCF cylinder of helium gas for every two 250 liter LHe Dewars required.

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
Gas cylinder rupture/damage or opening cylinder valve fast will release 2400 psi gas from cylinder.
Make sure gas cylinders are secure from falls and away from moving objects before using. Always open cylinder valve slowly.

WARNING
Potential High Pressure Gas Release!
If a gas cylinder falls, the valve outlet could shear, releasing hazardous high pressure gas.
Secure the gas cylinder before removing the protective valve cap.

  1. Connect a Helium Regulator and Hose Assembly (46-306734G1) to the Valve Outlet (CGA 580) on the GHe cylinder.

  1. Make sure the Regulator's adjusting handle is fully backed out, then slowly open the GHe cylinder's valve.

  1. Observe the Regulator's high pressure gauge. Make sure the indicated pressure is approximately 2000 psig, indicating a full cylinder.

  1. Record the Cryostat Pressure Gauge reading in Magnet Commissioning Log.

  1. Vent the magnet by temporarily opening Helium Vent Valve V2 (see Illustration 5) as required to obtain 1.0 to 1.5 psig pressure. Read and record the final pressure in the Cryostat Performance Log.

    (Step Details)

3.3   Liquid Helium Fill

  1. Verify that the Dewar's Liquid Helium Outlet Valve is in the closed position. (See Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the 1/2 in. cap and adapters, exposing the 5/8 in. locking collar.

  1. Loosen the 5/8 in. threaded brass locking collar.

  1. Verify that the Pressure Relief Valve is in the open position. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Make sure the Dewar Stinger Assembly's Valve is closed. (See Illustration 2 for Top Fills or Illustration 3 for Bottom Fills.)

  1. Insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly through the 5/8 in. locking collar until the Stinger Tip contacts the Helium Outlet Valve. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Open the Helium Outlet Valve.

  1. Slowly insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly into the Dewar until the Stinger Tip contacts liquid helium. (Contact is indicated by a pressure increase on the Dewar's pressure gauge and by expulsion of gas from the Pressure Relief Valve Port.)

  1. Continue to insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly at a rate that maintains a maximum 5 psig reading on the Dewar's pressure gauge.

  1. When the Dewar Stinger Assembly contacts the bottom of the Dewar, raise the Stinger Assembly 1 in. (25 mm), and securely tighten the 5/8 in. locking collar.

    (Step Details)

  1. When Dewar pressure stabilizes ≤5 psig, close the Pressure Relief Valve.

  1. Attach the selected Cryostat Stinger Assembly (46-294511P1 for 250 liter helium Dewars; 46-294511P2 for 500 liter helium Dewars) to the Helium Transfer Line.

  1. Install the threaded Coupling Nut 46-318619P1, Retainer Ring 46-260272P1, and O-Ring 46-260342P9 onto the Cryostat Stinger.

  1. Attach the opposite end of the Helium Transfer Line onto the Dewar Stinger Assembly.

WARNING
Potential Impact Injuries!
The unattached end of the gas line may exhibit whipping motion and collide into objects and personnel during gas line purge.
Firmly hold the unattached end during gas line purge.

NOTE:The next two steps will provide a helium rich environment for connecting the GHe line to the Helium Gas Pressurizing Port.

  1. Purge Helium Regulator and Hose Kit 46-306734G1 by alternately turning the regulator handle fully in and out 3 times. Upon completion of the purge, back the regulator out until a minimal flow is felt exiting the hose.

  1. Open the helium Dewar's Vent Valve to allow a small amount of gas flow.

  1. Attach the purged Helium Regulator and Hose Kit to the helium Dewar's GHe Pressurizing Port. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Back out the regulator handle all the way.

  1. Open Helium Vent Valve V2 (see Illustration 5) on the magnet. Turn the Magnet Monitor key switch to the Fill (horizontal) position. Vent the magnet cryostat pressure to <1.0 psig.

  1. Partially open the Dewar Stinger Assembly's valve and:

    1. Allow the liquid helium to purge and precool the Transfer Line Assembly until a liquid plume is observed exiting the assembly.

    1. Allow Dewar pressure to decrease no more than 3 psig before continuing.

NOTICE
If Bottom Filling and ceiling height prevents Cryostat Stinger insertion in Step 21:

  1. Install a Brass Blanking Cap on the Cryostat Stinger Assembly,

  2. Remove the Extension from the Cryostat Stinger,

  3. Place a brass alligator clip near the top end of the Cryostat Stinger,

  4. Partially insert the Extension into Fill Port V1, and

  5. Reattach the Extension.

 
 

  1. With the plume present, uncap Fill Port V1 (Illustration 5), fully insert the Cryostat Stinger Assembly, then securely tighten Fill Port V1's compression fitting.

  1. Fully open the Dewar Stinger Assembly Valve.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the Vent Valve at the Dewar's GHe Pressurizing Port. (Maximum transfer efficiency occurs when Dewar pressure is near 2 psig.)

  1. Verify that the GHe cylinder valve is fully open, and adjust the GHe cylinder regulator to obtain a Dewar pressure which is 1.5 psig above the cryostat pressure during the entire fill. (Do not set Dewar pressure above 3.5 psig.)

NOTICE
Normal cryostat pressure during helium fill is approximately 1 psig. Cryostat pressure will rise to just above 1 psig at the beginning of the fill, and then slowly drop back to around 1 psig. Make sure cryostat pressure never exceeds 1.5 psig during fill. If the cryostat pressure gauge reads >2.0 psig, stop the fill immediately and check fill equipment for loss of vacuum, high Dewar pressure, etc.

If frost is detected on the transfer line, stop the fill immediately (as if the Dewar is empty) to avoid a magnet quench.

 
 

NOTE:Helium Vent Port V2 should frost up, indicating there is no restriction in the venting circuit.

  1. Check the Magnet Monitor for an increase in the helium level. During the fill process monitor cryogen level readings once every minute for the first 5 minutes of the fill and once every 3 minutes thereafter.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Blowing warm helium gas into a magnet at field may cause the magnet to quench.
Do not allow an empty Dewar to blow warm helium gas into a ramped magnet.

NOTICE
If the Magnet Monitor's helium level reading is not increasing, check the magnet and fill equipment for frosting or blockage. If the Magnet Monitor reading is decreasing:

  1. Stop the fill immediately,

  2. Remove the Stinger from the magnet, and

  3. Contact Service.

 
 

  1. Monitor the Dewar transfer for one or more of the following to prevent blowing helium gas into the helium vessel:

    (Step Details)

  1. Record the information for each Dewar in Helium Fill Data or Liquid Helium Refill Log.

  1. When the cryostat is full (a Magnet Monitor reading of 97%), or when changing helium Dewars:

    1. Close the valve on the Dewar Stinger Assembly,

    1. Close the GHe cylinder valve,

    1. Close the Dewar's Vent Valve, and

    1. Open the Dewar's Pressure Relief Valve.

  1. Remove the Cryostat Stinger from Fill Port V1, and immediately replace the Fill Port Cap.

  1. If additional Dewars are required, change Helium Dewars in conformance with Changing Helium Dewars, Changing Helium Dewars, before continuing with the Liquid Helium Fill procedure.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten Fill Port V1's compression fitting to prevent a leak from occurring.

  1. Disconnect the Helium Transfer Line Assembly from the Helium Dewar Stinger Assembly.

  1. Make sure the helium Dewar is under 1 psig, then remove the Helium Dewar Stinger Assembly from the helium Dewar.

  1. Close the GHe cylinder valve.

  1. Disconnect the Helium Gas Line from the Dewar's Helium Gas Pressurizing Port.

  1. Back off the GHe cylinder Pressure Regulator's adjusting handle (CCW) until no resistance is felt.

  1. Verify the following Dewar configuration:

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the GHe cylinder Regulator from the helium gas cylinder and install the protective valve cap on the cylinder.

  1. Turn the Magnet Monitor key switch to the normal (vertical) position.

  1. Check and record the Magnet Monitor and cryostat pressure readings. Make sure the vessel pressure is increasing at a rate of 0.11 psig/hour.

3.4   Changing Helium Dewars

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Helium gas from the helium transfer line can enter the magnet while Dewars are being changed if the Cryostat Stinger is left in the Fill Port. This can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
Always remove the Cryostat Stinger from the Fill Port and start the Fill Procedure over again when changing helium Dewars while filling a ramped magnet.

  1. Close Helium Vent Valve V2 (Illustration 5) on the magnet.

  1. Remove the Stinger and cap the Fill Port V1.

  1. Obtain a full liquid helium Dewar. Check the Dewar pressure gauge. (See Illustration 6.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Observe the high pressure gauge on the GHe cylinder Regulator. Make sure the indicated pressure is >1000 psig, indicating sufficient gas volume for transferring a full 250 liter helium Dewar.

WARNING
Potential High Pressure Gas Release!
If a gas cylinder falls, the valve outlet could shear, releasing hazardous high pressure gas.
Secure the gas cylinder before removing the protective valve cap.

  1. If a new GHe aluminum cylinder is required in Step 4, connect a Helium Regulator and Hose Assembly (46-306734G1) to GHe cylinder's Valve Outlet (CGA 580).

  1. Make sure the Regulator's adjusting handle is fully backed out, then slowly open GHe cylinder valve.

  1. Refer to Helium Fill Equipment for appropriate Helium Fill equipment.

  1. Observe the cryostat pressure gauge. Temporarily open Vent Valve V2 as required to obtain a pressure of ≤0.5 psig.

  1. Verify that the Helium Outlet Valve is closed on the full Dewar.

  1. Loosen the 5/8 in. locking collar on the full Dewar.

  1. Remove the 1/2 in. cap and adapters, exposing the 5/8 in. brass locking collar.

  1. Verify that the Pressure Relief Valve is in the open position.

  1. Verify that the Dewar Stinger Assembly Valve is in the closed position, and disconnect the Helium Transfer Line from the Dewar Stinger Assembly.

  1. Remove the Dewar Stinger Assembly from the empty Dewar.

  1. Wipe off frost or moisture on the Dewar Stinger, and insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly through the 5/8 in. locking collar until the Stinger Tip contacts the Helium Outlet Valve in the full Dewar. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Open the Helium Outlet Valve.

  1. Slowly insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly into the Dewar until the Stinger Tip contacts liquid helium. (Contact is indicated by a pressure increase on the Dewar pressure gauge and by expulsion of gas from the Pressure Relief Valve Port.)

  1. Continue to insert the Dewar Stinger Assembly at a rate that maintains a maximum 5 psig reading on the Dewar pressure gauge.

  1. When the Dewar Stinger Assembly contacts the bottom of the Dewar, raise the Stinger Assembly 1 in. (25 mm) and securely tighten the threaded 5/8 in. locking collar.

    (Step Details)

  1. When Dewar pressure stabilizes at 5 psig, close the Pressure Relief Valve.

  1. Attach the Helium Transfer Line onto the Dewar Stinger Assembly.

WARNING
Potential Impact Injuries!
The unattached end of the gas line may exhibit whipping motion and collide into objects and personnel during gas line purge.
Firmly hold the unattached end during gas line purge.

  1. Disconnect the Helium Gas Line from the empty Dewar.

    1. Purge the GHe cylinder Regulator and Hose Assembly by alternately turning the Regulator's handle fully in and out three times.

    1. Upon completion of the purge, back the Regulator out until a minimal flow is felt exiting the hose.

  1. Slightly open the GHe Vent Valve to allow helium gas to flow from the Dewar.

  1. Attach the purged Gas Line Assembly to the Dewar's Helium Gas Pressurizing Port. Fully back out the Regulator's adjusting handle.

  1. Attach the Cryostat Stinger to the Transfer Line.

  1. Prepare the empty Dewar as follows:

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed with Changing Helium Gas Bottles.

3.5   Changing Helium Gas Bottles

Change the helium gas bottle when bottle pressure drops below 5 psig. Approximately one 235 SCF helium gas bottle is needed to pressurize a 500 liter liquid helium Dewar.

DANGER
Fatal Explosive Hazard!
Gas cylinder rupture/damage or opening cylinder valve fast will release 2400 psi gas from cylinder.
Make sure gas cylinders are secure from falls and away from moving objects before using. Always open cylinder valve slowly.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Helium gas cylinder (bottle) caps are ferrous and must be removed before bringing the cylinder into the magnet room of a ramped magnet.

  1. Close the Dewar vent valve and remove the gas hose.

  1. Close the main valve and regulator on the helium gas bottle.

  1. Bring a full helium gas bottle into the magnet room and reconnect the regulator and hoses.

  1. Purge the hose before reconnecting to the Dewar.
