1  Safety

CAUTION
System board or component damage could result.
The cooling fan is off only when the workstation is turned off or the power cable has been disconnected. The cooling fan is always on when the workstation is in “On” or “Standby” mode.
You must disconnect the power cord from the power source before opening the workstation.

CAUTION
Memory module damage could result during removal or installation.
If you do not unplug the power cord while installing memory, your memory modules might be damaged and the system will not recognize the memory changes.
Power off the workstation and unplug the power cord from the AC power outlet. Wait until the LED on the back of the power supply turns off before removing memory.

NOTICE
Turn off the workstation before disconnecting any cables.
 
 

NOTICE
TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO COMPONENTS WITHIN THE HSDA, OBSERVE THE FOLLOWING ESD PRECAUTIONS:

  • WORK ON A STATIC-FREE MAT.

  • WEAR A STATIC STRAP TO ENSURE THAT ANY ACCUMULATED ELECTROSTATIC CHARGE IS DISCHARGED FROM YOUR BODY TO GROUND.

  • CREATE A COMMON GROUND FOR THE EQUIPMENT YOU ARE WORKING ON BY CONNECTING THE STATIC-FREE MAT, STATIC STRAP AND PERIPHERAL UNITS TO THAT PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING ESD PROTECTION, REFER TO THE SAFETY CHAPTER IN THIS PUBLICATION

3  Procedure

3.1   Workstation Service Preparation

Perform the following steps before servicing the workstation.

  1. Remove/disengage any security devices that prohibit opening the workstation.

  1. Disconnect all peripheral device cables from the workstation.

  1. Perform the LOTO Global Operator Console / Operator Workspace procedure before beginning the replacement.

  1. Remove the two (2) screws that secure the left side panel. (See Illustration 1.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the left side panel of the GOC by lifting up. Avoid straining the short ground wire, which can be disconnected at the middle junction if necessary, that connects the cover to the GOC chassis. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift up on the latch and remove the left side panel of the PC. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lay the workstation on its side with the system board facing up.

  1. Remove System/Memory fan assembly as per the instructions below.

3.2   System/Memory Fan Assembly Removal

  1. Press down on the green release latch at the bottom of the system/memory fan assembly (Illustration 4) and rotate the memory fan holder up until it snaps into a vertical position.

    (Step Details)

  1. Disconnect the system and memory fan cables from the system board.

  1. Press down on the top of the system/memory fan assembly to release the retaining latch (Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the assembly inwards until the hinge tabs on the bottom of the assembly are released.

  1. Lift and remove the system/memory fan assembly from the chassis.

3.3   Memory Module Removal

  1. Gently push out and down on the socket levers on the slot that contains the suspected faulty memory module. (See Illustration 6 for slot locations.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the DIMM straight up and remove it from the unit (Illustration 7).

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure that the socket levers are still out and away from the memory socket.

NOTICE
DIMMs and the DIMM sockets are keyed for proper installation. Be sure these guides line up before pushing down in Step 4.
 
 

  1. Place the new memory module into the slot and push down firmly into the socket until the memory socket latches snap into place.

  1. Ensure that the module is fully inserted and properly seated in the socket.

3.4   System/Memory Fan Assembly Replacement

  1. Insert the hinge tabs on the bottom of the system/memory fan assembly into the slots in the chassis (Illustration 8).

    (Step Details)

  1. Rotate the top of the assembly up until the retaining latch snaps into the slot on the chassis (Illustration 9).

    (Step Details)

  1. Reconnect the system and memory fan cables to the system board.

  1. Rotate the memory fan holder down until it snaps into a horizontal position (Illustration 10).

    (Step Details)

  1. In DDR2 mode, dual-rank DIMMs are placed farther from the Memory Controller Hub (MCH) than single-rank DIMMs.

  1. If there is no way to obtain a valid memory configuration by disabling some of the plugged-in memory, the BIOS will halt with a diagnostics 2004 code for memory error (4 beeps/blinks).

  1. If the BIOS can find a valid memory configuration by disabling some of the plugged-in memory, it will do so and will report a warning during POST (“215-mismatched memory”).

    (Step Details)

4  Finalization

  1. Replace and secure all cables and covers.

  1. Perform the LOTO Global Operator Console / Operator Workspace procedure to restore power to the GOC and Operator Workspace.

  1. Run HP Linux PC Service Diagnostics using the HP Diagnostics Tool.

  1. Reboot, and ensure that no memory BIOS errors are observed during the boot process.

  1. Perform a scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
