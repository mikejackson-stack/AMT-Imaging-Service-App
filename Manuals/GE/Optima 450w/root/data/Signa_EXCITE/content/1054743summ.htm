3  Procedure

3.1   System Power Shutdown

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
LETHAL VOLTAGES ARE PRESENT WITHIN THE PDU EVEN IF ALL PDU BREAKERS ARE OFF.
Disconnect and Lockout and tagout power from the system at the facilities Main Disconnect.

NOTICE
Prior to powering down the entire Systems Cabinet, perform a system shutdown of the system software. The Volume Recon Engine (VRE) System which resides in the Systems Cabinet can become corrupted if it loses power while applications software is active.
 
 

  1. To prevent fatal electric shock, disconnect the power from the PDU before performing the following procedures.

  1. Notify ALL personnel that are working on the system that the system is being shut down.

  1. Shut down the system software before removing power.

  1. For PDU (in PGR Cabinet, with plastic safety cover):

    1. Open the front left door of the PGR cabinet.

    1. Open the plastic cover and flip the operator's workspace host circuit breakers to the OFF position. This allows control upon power up, when this procedure is completed.

    1. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT counter-clockwise to the green OFF (O) position.

    1. Perform the LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem which will disconnect the INPUT circuit breaker.

3.2   MNS Cabinet Setup

For reference, see Illustration 19 for the MNS cable map.

  1. Move the MNS cabinet door down approximately five holes using the adjustable hinges. All the cables, with the exception of the power cable (routed through the bottom of the cabinet), are routed up and out of the top of the MNS cabinet (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Perform the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. After installing the RF cable at connector J4 using the L-angle HN adaptor (46-221942P3), ensure that the cable connector HN and N adaptor threads are not loose.

3.3   Installing the RF Detector Boards and Cables

For the 8kW MNS installation there are supplemental circuit boards that need to be installed. These include:

  • 2 BB UPM RF detector boards (5250034 or equivalent).

  • 1 BB Amp I/F board (5250032).

  1. At the front of the PGR Cabinet, locate slots 19U and 19L of the UPM chassis (see Illustration 2). Remove covers for blank slots and insert one BB UPM RF detector board in each slot (see Illustration 2).

  1. Locate slot 17U/18U in the UPM Chassis. Remove the covers for the blank slots and insert the BB Amp I/F board (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate pre-installed white sub-D cable, part number 5166354, that is coiled and secured in right-side of cabinet. Uncoil and attach to BB AIF Board J3 in slots 17U, 18U.

  1. Remove power distribution module (PDM) located in the top right corner of the PGR cabinet.

    1. Remove all cables connected to the front and top of the PDM (see Illustration 3 and Illustration 4).

      (Step Details)

    1. From the top of the PGR cabinet, remove the outer edge screws surrounding the PDM securing this unit to the rails. Then, remove all the screws to the PDM door (see Illustration 5).

      (Step Details)

    1. Open the door. From inside the PDM, unscrew the two cables locks (block retaining nuts) from the rear of the PDM and disconnect the connections from the two terminal blocks. Once cables are disconnected, the PDM door should be closed.

      (Step Details)

    1. Slide the PDM out from the front of the PGR Cabinet. Set this to the side on a flat and safe surface.

  1. At the I/O panel, remove the blank plate from the J204 connection.

  1. Attach the cable 5271096 to J204 on the underside of the I/O panel (see Illustration 7). Run this cable along the side of the cabinet between the cabinet wall and the Recon components. Attach the other end of the cable to the BB AIF board to CAM SLOT 17/18FU J5(see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

NOTE:Before attaching the BNC cables (J200-203) to the I/O panel, connect the adapters (46-208990P1) to the BNC connectors (see Illustration 8).

Illustration 8:  BNC Connector and Adapter

  1. Attach the BNC cables to J200-203 connections on the underside of the I/O panel (see Illustration 7 and Illustration 9). Run these cables along the side of the cabinet between the cabinet wall and the Recon components. Attach the other ends to CAM Slot 19FU J4, CAM Slot 19FU J5, CAM Slot 19FL J4, and CAM Slot 19FL J5(see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. Add the 50 ohm terminators (2329754 or equivalent) to all empty J-numbers on the RF Detector boards (see Illustration 2).

  1. Tie-wrap the cables that run along the side of cabinet (see Illustration 10). Ensure that these cables are not in the way of other components that may require service.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once all cables are connected properly and tie-wrapped, slide the PDM into the PGR cabinet.

    1. From the top of the PGR cabinet, open the PDM door and lead the power cables through the openings at the rear of the PDM Illustration 11.

      (Step Details)

    1. From the inside of the PDM, connect the cables and screw the black retainer nuts to secure the cables (see Illustration 12).

      (Step Details)

    1. Close the PDM door. Attach the PDM to the rails with the surrounding screws; then, screw the PDM door closed.

    1. Connect all cables to the top and front of the PDM (see Illustration 3 and Illustration 4).

3.5   Installing the MNS Upconverter (5189655-2)

The MNS upconverter is installed on the rear pedestal of the magnet.

Illustration 15:  Upconverter Location

  1. Unscrew J8 connection from the re-route box with a flat-head screwdriver and set to the side. This will be connected to J4 on the upconverter. Carefully push wiring to the side and out of the way for upconverter installation.

  1. Attach one mounting bracket to the re-route box and the other mounting bracket directly to the MNS upconverter.

  1. Approach the opposite side of the rear pedestal from where the mounting bracket was attached and slide in the MNS upconverter. Fully attach the MNS upconverter to the re-route box.

  1. Locate and connect the cables indicated in Table 2 and Illustration 16.

3.6   Installing the LPCA Cover (5183855-2)

NOTE:The MNS T/R module should be removed from the system when spectroscopy is not in use. The presence of the module during a non-MNS scan may cause image quality degradation.

  1. With the LPCA cover off, place the two LPCA rails (p/n 5267384) on the top of the MNS LPCA cover so that the notches are facing up. When viewing from the patient-end, the right LPCA rail should have the notch farthest away and the left rail closest to.

  1. Attach the rails to the cover by screwing the screws in from the underside (screwheads should be hidden underneath the cover).

  1. Install the LPCA cover onto the system and secure with four screws.

3.7   Installing MNS T/R Switch on LPCA (2354050)

  1. Move the LPCA to a position for easy access – either to the front or the rear of the magnet.

  1. Slide the MNS T/R Switch onto the two rails on front of the LPCA (see Illustration 17).

    (Step Details)

  1. Attach the cables of the MNS T/R Switch to the MNS A-port adapter (see Illustration 17).

  1. Attach the A-port adapter to the A-port of the LPCA.

3.8   Installing the MNS TX Switch (2254635)

  1. Loosen the two thumbscrews to remove the support bracket from the SPW (see Illustration 20).

  1. Install the MNS TX switch to the support bracket and connect all cables as shown in Illustration 19, the DVMR MNS Cable Map. Refer to item C for the location of the MNS TX switch in Illustration 20.

    (Step Details)

  1. Reattach the support bracket onto the SPW.

  1. On the upper TX switch, remove the connection from J15 of the Magnet I/O and attach Y-cable to J123 on the upper TX switch and to J127 on the MNS TX switch (lower) (see Illustration 20). Connect the other end of the Y-cable to the J15 Magnet I/O connector (see Illustration 21).

    (Step Details)

3.9   Restoring System Power

  1. Notify all personnel that are working on the system that the system is being powered-up.

  1. Remove the LOTO for XRFD Amplifier and PEN Cabinet (Magnet Room Electronics).

NOTICE
Whenever the PEN cabinet LOTO is removed, the Exciter board requires 20 minutes of power-up time before scanning.
 
 

  1. Remove the LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Open the plastic door over the front of the PDU breakers and switch ON the appropriate sub-system breakers.

  1. Place any individual circuit breakers turned off to the ON position.

3.10   Installing Spectroscopy Software

  1. From the Common Service Desktop, click [Configuration], [Guided Install], and [FE mode](see Illustration 22).

    Illustration 22:  Common Service Desktop Path

  1. Start the Guided Install using the operator login.

  1. After the Guided Install window opens select [Option Key] tab.

  1. Install the MNS option key using eLicense (refer to Software Option Install with eLicense Generated Keys).

  1. Place rating plate on the rear of the GOC (if applicable).

  1. Reboot the computer. The installation is now complete.

3.11   Configuration

NOTE:The MNSpectro Option Key delivered with the Hydrogen Only Spectroscopy Option (PROBE/SV) must be previously installed.

  1. At the Common Service Desktop Manager, click on the [Configuration] tab.

  1. Select Guided Install, then FE Mode from the navigation sidebar to launch the Guided Install GUI (see Illustration 23).

  1. At the hardware configuration page, select AMT for Spectro RF Amp Type(see Illustration 23).

    (Step Details)

  1. Ensure all other 3.0T settings are correct for the system.

  1. Modify and save all changes. A reboot is required.

3.12   Verifying Configuration Settings

  1. At the Common Service Desktop Manager, click on the [Utilities] tab.

  1. Select the Config File Manager. Ensure the following parameters match the definitions set below (see Illustration 24):

    1. Spectroscopy RF Amplifier= yes to configure the Multi-Nuclear Spectro RF Amplifier (amp to ready).

    1. Broadband Transceiver = yes to configure the MNS BroadBand Transceiver (frequency change).

    1. Power Monitor Type = 4.

    1. 3T Spectro RF AMP Type = 0.

      (Step Details)

  1. Click [Save Changes] before exiting.

  1. Click [Quit] to exit the Config File Manager.

  1. Reboot host to save file changes.

  1. Perform Verification of SaveInfo Data.

4  Finalization

  1. Perform the TR DD System Path Check. This will ensure that the cables are connected properly and not accidentally swapped, primarily with the TX switch cables.

  1. Perform the 8kW 3.0T MNS Amplifier Calibration.

  1. Perform the Universal Power Monitor – MNS Setup and Calibration.

  1. Perform the UPM - MNS Functional Check.

  1. Perform the Multi Nuclear Spectroscopy (MNS) Functional Checks.
