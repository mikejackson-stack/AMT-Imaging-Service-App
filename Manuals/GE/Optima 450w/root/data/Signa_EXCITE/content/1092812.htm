3  Procedure

3.1  Using Portable RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219

NOTICE
RuO Temperature Monitor readings will increase slightly if the meter is cold. For best results, the meter should be at or near room temperature.
 
 

  1. Attach either cable of Portable RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219 to the CAL Receptacle located in the upper left side of the monitor shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  RuO Temperature Monitor (2171219) Connections

  1. Press the [Push to Read] button. The reading for the first cable should be 3.95K to 4.05K.

  1. Attach the Portable RuO Temperature Monitor's other cable to the CAL Receptacle.

  1. Press the [Push to Read] button. The reading for the second cable should be 3.95K to 4.05K.

WARNING
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Signa Magnet.
The RuO Temperature Monitor contains ferrous materials. Keep meter away from the magnet bore.

  1. Place the RuO Temperature Monitor on the left (service) side of the magnet, close enough to the Coldhead for the monitor cables to reach the Coldhead connection ports.

  1. Run the two cables up to the Coldhead box and attach the cables as shown in Illustration 1, connecting to the Coldhead and Recondenser Connection Ports shown in Illustration 2.

    NOTE:Make sure the cables are straight between the monitor and the magnet. Coiled cable can affect the accuracy of the readings.

    Illustration 2:  RuO Temperature Monitor 2171219 Connections at Coldhead

  1. Read the Recondenser temperature:

    1. Rotate the switch on the RuO Temperature Monitor to the Recondenser position.

    1. Depress the [Push to Read] button.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds, then record the reading.

    NOTE:Since the Recondensor reading will vary slightly, record the average of the high and low readings.

  1. Read the Coldhead temperature:

    1. Rotate the switch in the Coldhead position, and depress the [Push to Read] button.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds and record the reading.

    1. Wait a few minutes, then depress the [Push to Read] button again.

    1. Let the readings stabilize for 10 seconds.

    1. Average the first and second Coldhead readings.

    NOTE:If readings are not within the normal range, check battery output by connecting a DVM across the red and black battery terminals on the front panel of the RuO Temperature Monitor. Battery output should be above 8 VDC. When sampling, voltage will drop about 0.7 VDC.

3.2  Using a Digital Voltmeter (DVM)

  1. Connect a DVM to the appropriate Recondenser contacts shown in Illustration 3 and the appropriate Coldhead contacts shown in Illustration 4. RuO locations are shown in Illustration 5.

    NOTE:Use a DVM with 10 microamp or less current supply to minimize I2 R heating of the RuO.

    Illustration 3:  RuO Circuit and Pinouts, Recondenser Vacuum Sleeve

    Illustration 4:  RuO Circuit and Pinouts, Coldhead Transition Flange

    Illustration 5:  RuO Sensor/Diode Locations

  1. Check for isolation of contacts A, B, C and D to the cryostat ground.

  1. Check the resistance between contacts A and C (ohms A, C) = ______) and between contacts B and D (ohms (B, D) = ______). This is the resistance of the phosphor bronze wire loop to the RuO.

    NOTE:Phosphor bronze wire has a resistance of 1.08 ohm/foot at 300K and 0.87 ohm/foot at 4.2K. Wire loop resistances are generally in the range of 25 to 75 ohms. Low resistance readings (<20 ohms) may indicate a short in the wire loop or at the connector. Infinite resistance indicates an open circuit.

  1. Check resistance between A and B (ohms (A, B) = ______) and between C and D (ohms (C, D) = ______).

  1. Calculate RuO Resistance:

    RuO resistance should be 1,450 to 1,325 ohms when the liquid helium pressure is 3 psig or less.

    NOTE:Significant differences between readings in Step 3, Step 4 or out of the specified range in Step 5 indicate a resistance short or open problem. A fully open sensor will read 000K; a fully shorted sensor will read 300K on the portable temperature monitor.

3.3  Using the Handheld Magnet Temperature Meter MTM-101 (5119507)

When troubleshooting thermal issues on magnets, using the MTM-101 meter is mandatory. Measurements made with the MagMon3 are not accurate enough for this purpose. There are two points for temperature measurements (see Illustration 6):

  • Coldhead – This point allows the measurement of RuO A (also known as the second stage coldhead temperature).

  • Recondenser – This point allows the measurement of:

    • RuO B (also known as the recondenser temperature)

    • the shield temperature diode (also known as the first stage coldhead temperature)

    • the second stage coldhead temperature

      Illustration 6:  Connection Points for Temperature Measurements

  1. Connect the meter to a measurement point.

  1. Press the green button on the meter until the LED on the meter indicates that the meter is measuring the temperature you want to measure. Table 1 shows how the LED indicates which temperature is being measured.

    Table 1:  MTM-101 Indicator LEDs

    For this connector:This LED on the MTM-101 meter:Indicates the meter is measuring:
    ColdheadRuOSecond stage coldhead temperature (RuO A)
    RecondenserRuORecondenser temperature (RuO B)
    RecondenserSI-410D1First stage coldhead temperature
    RecondenserSI-410D2Second stage coldhead temperature

    For example, if you want to measure the first stage coldhead temperature, connect the meter to the recondenser measurement point, and press the green button twice. To measure the second stage coldhead temperature, press the green button a third time.
