• Listen for a whistling sound coming from the transfer line indicating the Dewar is empty. Depending on equipment and conditions, the whistle may not always be heard.

  • Monitor the Dewar pressure gauge and watch for a decrease in pressure (a decrease in cryostat pressure could also be caused by an empty gas cylinder).

  • Monitor the percent change on the Magnet Monitor. Stop the transfill when there is no positive (increasing) change in the meter reading.

  • Make sure the Dewar Stinger remains frost free during the transfill. Frost on the Stinger is one indicator for passing helium vapor. Stop transfill if frost is present.

NOTE:Multiple Dewars may be required to achieve a 97% fill of the magnet cryostat.
