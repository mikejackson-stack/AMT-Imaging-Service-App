8-Channel Body Array Anterior Handle Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Required Conditions

ConditionReferenceEffectivity

Broken Anterior Handle.

-

-

2  Procedure

  1. Place the anterior section of the coil foam side up on the work area. See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate and remove the 6 screws holding the smaller anterior cover in place. See Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate and remove the 2 screws holding the ends of the handle on the inside of the cover. See Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the covers to expose the ends of the handle. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Lift the ends of the handle off of the posts and pull the handle out of the slots in the cover.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the ends of the new handle in the slots of the cover, making sure it is orientated as shown.

  1. Place the covers over the ends of the handle and secure them in place with their screws.

  1. Place the cover back on the anterior section of the coil and secure it in place by retightening the 6 screws.

3  Finalization

  1. Perform Signa 1.5T HD 8 CH Body Array Coil Setup for SNR Test to ensure proper operation of the coil.
