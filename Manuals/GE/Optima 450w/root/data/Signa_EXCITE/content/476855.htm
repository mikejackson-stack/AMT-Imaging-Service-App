EFB & Unblank Signal Generator

1  Diagnostic Link

Diagnostics >> Peripherals >> Unblanking Functional Diagnostic

2  Purpose

UNBLANK, UNBLANK2, EFB and EFB2 are played out at different duty cycles so you can verify that these signals are being generated by the IRF, transferred through the IRF I/O board, and visible at the remote RF.

3  Components Tested

  • APS board

  • IRF board

  • IRF I/O

4  Requirements

  • APS board

  • IRF board

  • IRF I/O

  • Oscilloscope

5  Test Sequence

  1. Remove the cable from the Remote RF Front Panel's J16 serial connector.

  2. Click [Run].

  3. Probe each pin of the J16 connector with a short piece of wire such as a paper clip and connect the probe to the wire to get a reading (see Illustration 1).

Illustration 1:  Remote RF Front Panel, J16 Connector

6  Expected Results

Each pin should have a reading as noted below.

Table 1:  Remote RF Front Panel, J16 connector pin-out

Pin 1UNBLNK_PPin 6UNBLNK_N
Pin 2ENVFBK_PPin 7ENVFBK_N
Pin 3UNBLNK2_PPin 8UNBLNK2_N
Pin 4ENVFBK2_PPin 9ENVFBK2_N
Pin 5GND  

  • UNBLANK will be a 10µS pulse every 70µS.

  • ENVFBK will be a 20µS pulse every 70µS.

  • UNBLANK2 will be a 30µS pulse every 70µS.

  • ENVFBK2 will be a 40µS pulse every 70µS.

  • The xxx_P signals are inactive at 5 volts and active at 0 volts.

  • The xxx_N signals are inactive at 0 volts and active at 5 volts.
