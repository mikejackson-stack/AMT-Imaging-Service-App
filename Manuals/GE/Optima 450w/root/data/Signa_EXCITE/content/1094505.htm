2  Overview

Service Platform 5372674 provides a ~22.5 in. x 44 in. (~570 mm x 1120 mm) work surface that can hold 500 lb. (226 kg) at platform heights of 40 in., 50 in. or 60 in. (1016 mm, 1270 mm or 1524 mm). The overall width of the platform is 24.75 in. (627 mm). Adjustable outriggers in the ladder's base provide for better stability at higher platform heights. Each safety rail includes a 2.5 in. (63.5 mm) high toe board, and a 6 in. (152.4 mm) high toe board is provided for the platform side closest to the magnet. The magnet components will be protected when the side shield is installed on the platform.

NOTE:All service procedures must be performed from the floor or from the platform of the Service Platform.

3.2  Safety

CAUTION
Potential Pinch Hazard!
Fingers placed in the path of moving components while Service Platform 5372674 is being assembled/adjusted can be pinched.
Do not place fingers in the path of any Service Platform 5372674 components being assembled/adjusted.

CAUTION
Potential Scratches/Cuts!
Service Platform 5372674 may have edges sharp enough to scratch or cut skin.
Wear leather gloves while assembling, adjusting or disassembling Service Platform 5372674 to protect against scratches/cuts and keep your hands clean.

4  Procedure

  1. Position the ladder components in an open area away from the magnet about 6 feet (1800 mm) square.

  1. Lay the platform section on the floor with its underside facing up as in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Positioning Ladder Leg Sections to Platform Underside

  1. Insert each ladder leg in the ladder sockets on the platform's underside as shown in Illustration 1.

  1. Securely latch both ladder latches of each ladder leg to the platform. (See Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:  Ladder Leg Section Latches

NOTICE
Always extend and secure the outriggers fully out before using the Service Platform at its 60 in. (1524 mm) platform height.
 
 

  1. Adjust both telescoping ladder legs to the platform height required. Pinning holes are available for 40 in., 50 in. and 60 in. (1016 mm, 1270 mm and 1524 mm) platform heights.

    1. Disengage each Telescoping Leg Locking Pin.

    1. Simultaneously slide both sides of each ladder section until the pinning hole for the platform height desired aligns with the Locking Pin.

    1. Securely engage all four Locking Pins in their pinning holes.

    1. If adjusting the platform to its 60 in. (1524 mm) height, fully extend and securely pin all four Ladder Outriggers. (See Illustration 3.)

    NOTE:At the 60 in. (1524 mm) height, the ladder can be located to span over the magnet's Rear Pedestal or Patient Table for service operations not readily accessible from beside the magnet.

    Illustration 3:  Extending the Ladder Outriggers

  1. Verify that all connections/adjustments are secure, then carefully flip the platform and legs upright.

CAUTION
Potential Back Strain!
Poor posture when lifting can cause back strain/damage.
Keep the upper body directly over your legs and use the service ladder's lifting handles when lifting an end of the service ladder.

  1. Use the service platform's lifting handles to lift one ladder end high enough to engage the other ladder end's wheels. Move the platform into the magnet room before lowering the end lifted back to the floor.

    NOTE:Avoid pushing the service platform across the floor, which can mark or damage the floor.

  1. Insert the Long Side Safety Rail into two Pipe Sleeves along the long edge of the platform away from the magnet as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Assembled Service Platform

  1. Insert the Short Side Safety Rail into two Pipe Sleeves along a short edge of the platform, typically at the service end of the magnet. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Insert the Magnet Side Shield along the magnet side of the platform. (See Illustration 4.)

  1. Repeat Step 7 whenever relocating the assembled Service Platform 5372674 inside the magnet room.

5  Finalization

  1. Disassemble the Service Platform 5372674 in reverse order of assembling it.

  1. Always return the Service Platform 5372674 to its designated storage location after use.
