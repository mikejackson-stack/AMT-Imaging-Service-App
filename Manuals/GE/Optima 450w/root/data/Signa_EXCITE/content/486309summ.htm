ECG Leads Installation and Functional Checks

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   Test Functionality of ECG Leads

  1. If not already done, disconnect the ECG Cables from the PAC and from each other. (Illustration 1 shows a pin diagram of the ECG connection.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Check the impedance of the ECG Cables from each of the leads at point “a” to the other end of that lead at point “b.” The impedance should be 60k ohms ±5% tolerance. (See Illustration 2.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Check the impedance of the ECG Cables from each lead at point “b” to its respective connection at point “c.” The impedance should be 0 ohms. (See Illustration 3.)

    (Step Details)

1.2   Connect Leads and Plug ECG Leads to PAC

  1. Connect the white High Impedance MRI ECG Lead Wires to the grey ECG Patient Cable by matching the colors on the white cable to the colored dots on the grey cable.

  1. Plug the open end of the grey ECG Patient Cable into the ECG Port of the PAC. (See Illustration 3.)

2  Finalization

  1. Return the system to the patient scanning condition.

  1. Perform a check scan to ensure the system is operating properly.
