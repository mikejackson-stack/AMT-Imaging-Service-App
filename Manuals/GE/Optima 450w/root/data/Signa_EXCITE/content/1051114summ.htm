Laser Light Alignment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

1.1   System with In Room Monitor

If an In Room Monitor is installed, follow the steps in this section. If not, refer to System without In Room Monitor.

  1. Remove the laser light lens cover (top of magnet) by removing two (2) screws (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the Alignment button on the control panel to turn on the laser light.

WARNING
MagnetIC Field!
USE OF FERRO-MAGNETIC DEVICE MAY CAUSE INJURY OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.
When running a Plumb Line, a non-magnetic device or tool must be used.

  1. Run a plumb line, using a non-magnetic device, down from the Sagittal and Axial (top) laser lights. Verify that both the Sagittal and Axial alignment lights are aiming straight down and aligned with the string (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. If adjustments are needed, loosen the flat head screws and move the joysticks until the Axial and Sagittal laser lights are aligned properly. See Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the laser light lens cover.

1.2   System without In Room Monitor

If an In Room Monitor is not installed, follow the steps in this section.

  1. Remove the Lens cover for the laser lights.

  1. Turn the laser lights on.

    (Step Details)

WARNING
MagnetIC Field!
USE OF FERRO-MAGNETIC DEVICE MAY CAUSE INJURY OR EQUIPMENT DAMAGE.
When running a Plumb Line, a non-magnetic device or tool must be used.

  1. Run a plumb line, using a non-magnetic device and string down from the Sagittal and Axial (Top) laser light. Verify that both the Sagittal and Axial Alignment Lights are aiming straight down (see Illustration 6, Illustration 7 and Illustration 8).

    Illustration 6:  Alignment Light Adjustment

  1. When adjusting the Sagittal Alignment Light, the plumb line must run down the front of the Laser Light cover, as shown in the illustration below. The Sagittal Alignment light runs parallel to the magnet in the center of the bore.

    Illustration 7:  Sagittal Alignment Light

    (Step Details)

  1. When adjusting the Axial Alignment Light, the plumb line must run down the side of the Laser Light cover, as shown in the illustration below. The Axial light runs perpendicular to the magnet bore.

    Illustration 8:  Axial Alignment Light

    (Step Details)

  1. If adjustments are needed, remove the upper display by carefully prying the cover off. See Illustration 9 and Illustration 10.

    (Step Details)

  1. For dual laser light configuration, both joysticks will need adjustment. For the single laser light configuration, only adjust light on the left side.
