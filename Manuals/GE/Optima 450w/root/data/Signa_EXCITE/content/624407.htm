1.5T Cardiac Coil Setup for MCQA Test

2  Overview

This procedure prepares for the automated SNR test using the 1.5T HDx Cardiac Array by GE/USAI, catalog (M3335MD).

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Unified Phantom Setup

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Posterior of Coil on Table

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms over the posterior section in such a way that the phantom set is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 2 and Illustration 3.

    Illustration 2:  T/L Unified Phantom Centered on S/I Direction

    Illustration 3:  T/L Unified Phantom Centered on R/L Direction

  1. Place the anterior section on the phantom set such that the coil is well centered in both S/I and R/L directions with respect to the phantom set as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Coil Centered in Both S/I and R/L Directions

  1. Fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantom as shown in Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Fasten Velcro Straps

  1. Connect the coil to Port A of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil at the cross mark as shown in Illustration 6 and advance to scan.

    Illustration 6:  Landmark the Coil

  1. Continue on to the MCQA test .
