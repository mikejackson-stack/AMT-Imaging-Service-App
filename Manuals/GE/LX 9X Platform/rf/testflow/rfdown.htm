Safety Awareness (Based on Service Note)

Proper Way to Shut Down Erbtec High Field RF AMP Subsystem Prior to Repair or Adjustment

Erbtec Power Down Preparation:
  1. Remove the screws necessary to withdraw the amplifier unit from the cabinet enclosure.
  2. Attach a laptop or other terminal to the serial port using the serial cable that comes with the Amp, and command the amplifier to the "Off" mode. With the laptop running Windows 95/98:
  3. From Start button, select Find and Files or Folders.
  4. Enter the word and symbols: *terminal* in the "Named" dialog box and select Find Now.
  5. In the list that displays, select Terminal.exe.
  6. When the Terminal window displays, select Settings and Communications.
  7. Under Communications, ensure the following setup:
  • Baud rate: 9600
  • Data bits: 8
  • Stop bits: 1
  • Parity: none
  • Flow control: Xon/Xoff

    Attach the cable connector to the com port being used. With service laptop, this should be com2. (Com1 is used either by the external mouse or the modem card.)

Troubleshooting:

If unable to obtain control of the Amp:

  1. Make sure you have the right type of serial cable attached. Two CPU's attached to one another require a NULL modem cable (Xmit/Rcv lines are crossed)
  2. Make sure you have an active com port. (switch to the other port)
  3. Try 7 data bits 1 parity and 1 stop bit. (Older terminals/Laptops only)

Command RF Amp to OFF State:
  1. Command the Erbtec Microprocessor controller to internal control by typing: O0 (letter "O" and a zero) in the Terminal window. (This will disconnect the Amp from external computer control.)
  2. Turn OFF the Erbtec Amp breaker at the rear of the cabinet.
  3. Perform LOTO procedure at the Main disconnect, and remove and lock the incoming AC power cord in the cup.

    CAUTION: USE EXTREME CAUTION IN THE NEXT STEP. THERE STILL MAY BE HAZARDOUS VOLTAGES PRESENT!

  4. WAIT at least 10 minutes to ensure the HV capacitors have bled down to ground potential.

    WARNING: NEVER SHORT THE POWER SUPPLY TO GROUND. DOING SO MAY RESULT IN A DESTROYED POWER SUPPLY OR SEVERE SHOCK HAZARD!

  5. Remove the respective covers, and connect the negative lead of a DVM to the chassis; measure potential hazard points in the areas where you will be placing your hands. (At each point, you should measure ZERO volts. If you measure a potential anywhere above 20 volts, STOP. Something is not powered off correctly or has not bled down yet. Proceed ONLY when measured potentials are below 20 volts.)

  6. Attach a ground wire to the Amp frame and the other end of the ground wire to each power supply. (This requires two ground wires: one for the 3kv supply and one for the 6kv supply. These can be #14 gauge wire.)
The Erbtec Amplifier should now be safe to work on. Remember your ESD procedures if removing any of the TTL logic boards within the cabinet.

To Re-enable Erbtec RF Amplifier:
  1. Remove any ground wires that were attached.
  2. Replace all covers.
  3. Turn on the circuit breaker for the RF Amplifier, and remove LOTO equipment.
  4. Reconnect your laptop and enter the terminal program.
  5. Command the Erbtec Microprocessor Controller to internal control by typing: O1 (letter "O" and the number one) followed by O3 (letter "O" and the number three) in the Terminal window. (This will disconnect the Amp from external computer control.)
