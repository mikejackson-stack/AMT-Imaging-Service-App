Direction 15370, Rev 8

This is the latest update to Direction 15370, Multi-Nuclear Spectroscopy Subsystem. This update was changed to support the increased Field Site needs for 4.x - 5.3 Hardware (M1040FF) and 5.4 Hardware (M1040FK) 1.5T Spectroscopy. The remainder of the manual was updated to correct errors contained in previous revisions.

Revision 8 of this manual is a complete replacement for all existing lower Revision pages. This Direction will no longer be available in paper format beginning May 1998.
