Ellis & Watts Service Bulletins

Listed below are Service Bulletins issued by Ellis & Watts International concerning Ellis & Watts chillers and cooling subsystems.

6/25/01 SB-006 It has become necessary for the Service Provider to perform many tasks during the course our recent Start-up visits, that do not fall within the scope of work that we arranged with GE.
8/10/01 SB-007 It is often necessary for the Service Provider to perform many tasks during the course of a start-up visit, that do not fall within the scope of work that E & W arranged with GE. Servicing the customer is always our primary goal. Therefore, these," out of scope," efforts will likely continue.
10/02/01 SB-008 In accordance with GE specification, Engineering has changed the DOWFROST Glycol mixture to a 50/50 mix (instead of 35/65). It is also now yellow in color (instead of clear).
10/10/01 SB-009 The following issues pertain to LC20M, LC20M/RAC, LC20M/WC, LC20MT, LC20MT/O, LC18MT/WC, model chillers.
10/10/01 SB-010A The LC20MT/O model chillers in serial number range from 18067 to 18075 must be converted when started up in a 50 Hz environment. For 380 V/50 Hz applications, follow these instructions in addition to switching SW6.
