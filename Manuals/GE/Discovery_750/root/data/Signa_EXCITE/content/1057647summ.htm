Body Hybrid Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
Shock Hazard!
high bias voltages are present at the Body Hybrid during normal operation.
Remove power, lockout and tagout the PDM-RF power on pdu before accessing the Body Hybrid.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interacting with the Body Hybrid module can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the Body Hybrid module from the side of the magnet, always do so with two people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing the Body Hybrid Splitter

  1. Lock out and tag out the PDM-RF power for the system (see LOTO for RF Amplifier and PEN Cabinet Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Make sure the scanner is idle. Select [End Exam] at any point in the scan process.

  1. Remove the lower side cover from the magnet.

  1. Remove the upper magnet side cover to access the body hybrid module, as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove all cables connected to the body hybrid module.

  1. Remove the four (4) allen screws holding the 3.0T Body Hybrid Module to the magnet. See below.

    (Step Details)

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interacting with the Body Hybrid Module can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the Body Hybrid Module from the side of the magnet, always do so with two people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. While carrying the module out of the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

1.2   Installing the Body Hybrid Module

  1. While carrying the module into the magnet room, make sure to stay as far away from the magnet as possible.

CAUTION
Strong Magnetic Field!
The module has no ferrous material and will not be attracted to the magnet, but eddy currents set up by the strong magnetic field interacting with the Body Hybrid Module can make it very hard for one person to handle.
When removing and or replacing the Body Hybrid Module from the side of the magnet, always do so with two people, keeping the module as far away from the magnet as possible.

  1. Secure the module to the magnet by tightening the eight (8) allen screws provided.

  1. Attach cables to the new body hybrid module.

2  Finalization

  1. Replace all covers.

  1. Reapply power to the system and remove all LOTO equipment.

  1. Perform a TR Dynamic Disable Calibration.

  1. Complete a Body and Head scan to makes sure the system is functional.
