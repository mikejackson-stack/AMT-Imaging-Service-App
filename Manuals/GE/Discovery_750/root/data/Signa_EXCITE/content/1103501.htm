3  Procedure

  1. Turn off power to the PDU by following LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove the XPS 'Z' unit from the PGR cabinet. by following XPS Replacement.

    Illustration 1:   XPS 'Z'

  1. Unhook the power strip cable plug from the top of the PDU near the back. (See Illustration 2.)

    Illustration 2:  PDU Panel

  1. Remove the tie wraps.

  1. Note the power strip cable routing through the PGR cabinet. You will need to duplicate this routing when installing the new power strip. Pull out the power cable from the back of the PDU.

  1. Unhook the power strip and remove it from the PGR cabinet.

    Illustration 3:  Power Strip in Cabinet

  1. Install the new power strip and reroute the cable as per the original routing.

  1. Plug the end of the power strip cable into the outlet labeled “RECON” on top of the PDU near the back. (See Illustration 2.)

  1. Restore the cable wraps, as needed.

  1. Restore the XPS 'Z' unit into the PGR cabinet by following XPS Replacement.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from PGR PDU/Gradient subsystem.

  1. Perform TPS reset.

  1. Perform a verification scan.
