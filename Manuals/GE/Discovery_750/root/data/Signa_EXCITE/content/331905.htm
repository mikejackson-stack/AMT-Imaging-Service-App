2  Overview

It is important to identify and eliminate any leak at installation after the magnet has been converted to its operating condition.

NOTE:Cryostat pressure can increase up to 5 psig during operation, thereby increasing any leakages. A leak will slowly deplete the helium supply, possibly causing cryo-pumping or icing, and may interfere with Recondenser operation.

3.3  Safety

DANGER
Potential fatal injury!
before and while performing the System Leak Test procedure:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for the System Leak Test procedure in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential fatal injury!
before starting the System Leak Test procedure:
Have all Work Assistants or Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Potential Asphyxiation Hazard!
Cryogen exposure will be encountered on air and ocean-shipped magnets.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard!
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Never bring ferromagnetic material into the magnet room. Make sure all equipment and tools used in the magnet room are non-ferromagnetic.

4  Procedure

  1. Make sure all hospital personnel and contractors in the area know that the magnet is being commissioned.

  1. Secure the area from unauthorized personnel.

    1. Post signs outside the area alerting of magnet commissioning.

    1. Post Authorized Personnel Only 2289812 signs at the MR Suite entrance.

  1. Secure Magnet Room doors in an open position. Make sure there is ventilation through the magnet room flowing toward unoccupied areas.

  1. Allow the cryostat to build pressure prior to operating the cryocooler coldhead.

  1. When the cryostat pressure reaches 5 psig, check the following areas for leaks using a Snoop or leak detector liquid:

    • All plumbing joints

    • Vertical Turret and Instrumentation Lead cover

    • Ramp Lead and Fill Port caps

    • All 360 degrees around the Burst Disc to Plenum contact

    • Pressure gauge and relief valves

    • Shim Lead collar/cap

    NOTE:Commercial leak detector fluids or instruments available through HVAC distributors can be used to determine small leaks in the helium plumbing and venting.

  1. Repair any leaks found prior to commissioning the magnet.
