4  Environmental Requirements

Storage

This equipment must be stored under the following conditions:

  • Ambient temperature range of +10°C to +40°C

  • Relative humidity range of 10% to 90%, non-condensing

To store the coil, baseplate, riser, and phantom, a storage space greater than 43.9 cm (width) x 35.6 cm (length) x 20.3 cm (height) (17.3 in x 14 in x 8 in) is required.
