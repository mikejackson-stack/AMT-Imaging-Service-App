Illustration 3:  Gradient Terminals

NOTE:Red cable indicates positive (+) wires, black cables indicate negative (-) wires.

Two Wrench Technique: two wrenches are necessary when attaching the cables from the gradient input/output connectors on each side of the SPW. One wrench prevents the post from turning, while the other tightens the nut. If you try to tighten the nut with only one wrench, the post itself could turn and damage the gradient filter.

NOTE:Make sure to tighten each cable as you attach it, as you can not reach all cables after attaching them.
