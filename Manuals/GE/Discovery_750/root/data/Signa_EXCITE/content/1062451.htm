Gradient Filter Replacement

(Click here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable30 minsNot Applicable

2  Overview

The gradient filter attaches to the Secondary Pen Wall (SPW) from the equipment room. The thumbscrews on the filter attach to pem nuts on the SPW.

3.3  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
THE GRADIENT Amplifiers ACT AS CONSTANT LOAD SOURCES, AND WILL SEND maximum CURRENT TO ANY LOAD including any human who get across the outputs.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, ENSURE THAT POWER IS OFF TO the gradient subsystem before CONTINUING WITH THIS PROCEDURE.

WARNING
Potential Projectile Hazard
Ferromagnetic objects in a strong magnetic field can become dangerous projectiles.
Do not bring a Ferromagnetic tool into the magnet room.

4  Procedure

4.1  REMOVAL OF THE GRADIENT FILTER

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!!
THE GRADIENT Amplifiers ACT AS CONSTANT LOAD SOURCES, AND WILL SEND maximum CURRENT TO ANY LOAD including any human crosses the outputs.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, ENSURE THAT POWER IS OFF TO the gradient subsystem before CONTINUING WITH THIS PROCEDURE.

  1. Before proceeding, remove power from the PGR cabinet, See LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

  1. Remove the plastic gradient filter terminal covers from the scan room side of the gradient filter.

    Illustration 1:  XRMB Gradient Filter

    NOTE:Two Wrench Technique: two wrenches are necessary when removing the cables from the gradient input/output connectors on each side of the SPW. One wrench prevents the post from turning, while the other loosens the nut. If you try to loosen the nut with only one wrench, the post itself could turn and damage the gradient filter.

  1. Remove the cables attached to the terminals in the scan room.

  1. Repeat Steps 2 and 3 for equipment room.

  1. While in the equipment room at the Secondary PEN Wall (SPW), remove the screws securing the gradient filter assembly to the SPW Panel. See Illustration 2. Remove the gradient filter and set aside.

    Illustration 2:  Gradient Filter Thumbscrews

4.2  INSTALLING THE REPLACEMENT GRADIENT FILTER

  1. Lift the gradient filter onto the guide pins on the SPW and tighten the thumb screws. When done, use a screwdriver to tighten.

  1. Attach the cables to the gradient filter connections. Start with the GND, Z-axis, Y-axis, and X-axis cables and ground wires. Make sure to attach the correct axis cable to the correct gradient filter connection.

    Illustration 3:  Gradient Terminals

    NOTE:Red cable indicates positive (+) wires, black cables indicate negative (-) wires.

    Two Wrench Technique: two wrenches are necessary when attaching the cables from the gradient input/output connectors on each side of the SPW. One wrench prevents the post from turning, while the other tightens the nut. If you try to tighten the nut with only one wrench, the post itself could turn and damage the gradient filter.

    NOTE:Make sure to tighten each cable as you attach it, as you can not reach all cables after attaching them.

  1. Tighten nuts to a torque of 35 ft-lbs (67-74Nm) or after tightening, verify that nuts are not loose.

    NOTE:Final check is by hand. Make sure screw is fixed, nut is tight, and cable is not detached from terminal.

    Illustration 4:  Cables Connected

  1. Replace the plastic gradient filter terminal covers.

  1. Release the lock-out/tag-out from the PGR Cabinet.

5  Finalization

  1. Run one pass of head SPT Check and Troubleshooting For XRMB.

  1. Run one pass of EPI White Pixel Test - Full Mode.
