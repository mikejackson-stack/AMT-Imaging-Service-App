Digital Tuning Tests

By cycling through a series of Digital Tuning time constants (t) and gains (a), the exponential response of the ACGD System Ierror signal, is examined. Illustration 1 shows the Exponential Response of the Ierror signal with and without eddy current compensation.

Illustration 1:  EXPONENTIAL RESPONSE FOR EDDY CURRENT COMPENSATION

The overall time constant (t) is actually composed of the summation of seven time constants. Together, these time constants provide greater resolution and overall range. Likewise, the overall gain (a) is actually composed of the summation of seven gains. Together, these gains also provide greater resolution and range.
