• When raising the hoist's carabiner without an attached Coldhead/Carriage Bracket, apply a moderate downward force to the hoist's carabiner.

  • When lowering the hoist's carabiner without an attached Coldhead/Carriage Bracket, position the hoist's rope at a high angle to disengage the hoist's safety cleat, and apply moderate downward force to the hoist's carabiner.
