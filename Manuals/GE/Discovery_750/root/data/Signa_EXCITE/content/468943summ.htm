Lockout / Tagout Global Operator Console / Operator Workspace

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

  1. Visual inspection of LEDs on computer and monitor.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) used to ensure Disconection(s)

  1. 1 personal lock and tag per FE at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. At the operator’s workspace, bring down the software and power off the computer.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the OFF position:

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breaker switches, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE who will be working on the equipment.

  1. Test that power has dissipated for each item by trying to turn the host computer on. If it does not turn on, it is safe to work on the components in the operator’s workspace. If it does turn on, verify LOTO has been done properly

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the ON position:

    (Step Details)

  1. Power up the host computer.
