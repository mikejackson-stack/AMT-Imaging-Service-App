2  Overview

This procedure describes the replacement or installation process of the gradient busbar, which is the interface between the gradient coils and gradient cables.

4  Procedure

4.1  Gradient Busbar Removal

  1. Follow all safety lockout/tagout procedures (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove the screws and bolts that are securing the XRMB busbar terminal safety cover. Pull the safety cover away from the terminals to get access to the brass nuts and Nordlock washers that are securing the busbar lugs.

    Illustration 1:  XRMB Busbar Terminal Safety Cover

  1. Remove six (6) brass nuts from XRMB coil terminals (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Busbar/Coil Electrical Connection

  1. Remove five (5) screws that secure cable guard. Remove guard (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  Gradient Cable/Busbar Connection

  1. Remove the twelve (12) bolts holding gradient cables to busbar terminal (see Illustration 3).

  1. Remove four (4) nuts, washers, and rubber isolators that secure the busbar assembly (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  Busbar

4.2  Gradient Busbar Replacement

NOTICE
The copper leads of the busbar must be protected to avoid distortion. Do not lift the busbar assembly by the copper leads.
 
 

NOTICE
Busbar alignment to XRMB coil is critical for system performance and reliability.
 
 

  1. Align the six (6) busbar lugs with the XRMB coil terminal studs by smoothly pushing the lugs onto the terminal studs (see Illustration 5). Meanwhile, slide the busbar assembly onto the four (4) mounting support studs as shown in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 5:  Fitting Busbar Lugs onto Coil Studs

    Illustration 6:  Fitting Busbar onto Support Studs

  1. Push the busbar toward the magnet so that the lug surface is parallel to the surface of the coil terminal. To better see the lug/coil interface, remove the two M6x30 screws from the XRMB coil and pull the safety cover outward as shown in Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Safety Cover and Lug/Coil Interface

  1. Hold the busbar with one hand and inspect the gap as shown in Illustration 8.

    • Lug - Coil

    • Magnet mounting block - Busbar (upper)

    • Magnet mounting block - Busbar (Lower)

    Illustration 8:  Inspecting Busbar Alignment

  1. The distance between the busbar and magnet should be equal at upper and lower mounting points. If the difference between the upper and lower gap is greater than 3 mm, adjust the number of shims as show in Illustration 9.

    NOTE:One shim is 3mm thick and can be used as a feeler gauge.

    Illustration 9:  Adjusting the Number of Shims

  1. Once the busbar alignment is verified, place one (1) rubber isolator, one (1) G10 shim, and one (1) brass nut at each mounting stud (front side). The step of the rubber isolator faces inwards (see Illustration 10).

  1. Using the provided Feeler Gauge, gradually tighten the four (4) brass nuts until the height of the G10 Shim surface reaches 7.2 mm(see Illustration 10).

    Illustration 10:  Checking the G10 Shim Height Using the Feeler Gauge

  1. After tightening the four (4) brass nuts with proper torque, use the feeler gauge to check the gap between the busbar lugs and coil terminals as shown in Illustration 11. The gauge SHALL NOT be able to enter the gap between lugs and coil terminals.

    Illustration 11:  Checking Busbar Final Alignment Using the Feeler Gauge

WARNING
possible equipment failure
Improper alignment of the Busbar electrical cables can shorten its useful life.
Follow steps to assure proper gap is less than 1.6 mm thick.

  1. The feeler gauge is 1.6 mm thick. If it enters the gap, the busbar alignment is out-of-spec. Remove one (1) shim from each mounting point and repeat step 1 through 7.

NOTICE
The Nordlock washer consists of two (2) washers with teeth facing outwards. They SHALL NOT be taken apart.

The Nordlock washer SHALL NOT be reused.

 
 

  1. When the busbar alignment meets spec, secure busbar lugs to XRMB coil terminals using Nordlock washers and M10 brass nuts as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. At the upper interface of the busbar assembly, connect gradient power cables to busbar terminals using Nordlock washers and M10 brass nuts as shown Illustration 3.

  1. Re-install the safety cover on the busbar.

  1. Re-install the front piece of the gradient terminal safety cover.

  1. Complete procedure by following Finalization, Step 1 through Finalization, Step 6.

5  Finalization

  1. Tighten the two M10x14 SS bolts on the top mounting block to secure the busbar support bracket.

  1. Apply Loctite (Red, #271) to the two M6 mounting holes in XRMB coil and tighten the two M6x30 screws to secure the gradient terminal safety cover.

  1. Re-install the rear end bell.

  1. Re-position and re-install the rear pedestal to the magnet.

  1. Re-install the bridge.

  1. Perform a good-bye scan.
