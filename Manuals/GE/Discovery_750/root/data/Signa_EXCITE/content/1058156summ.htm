2  Procedure

2.1   Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle and connect the cable in Port A.

  1. Place phantoms on the coil as shown in Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Landmark the coil at the raised mark closest to the head section of the coil (see Illustration 2 and Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.

2.2   Unified Phantom Setup

NOTE:Connect the coil to the system as shown in Legacy Phantom Setup, Step 1.

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and latch it to the table as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms on the center of the TL section of the coil as shown in Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Align the phantoms to the center in S/I and R/L directions as shown Illustration 5 and Illustration 6.

    (Step Details)

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom on the CT section as shown in Illustration 7.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust the large cylindrical unified phantom such that it is in complete contact with the TL unified phantom as shown in Illustration 8.

    (Step Details)

  1. Adjust TL unified phantoms such that both the phantoms are in complete contact with each other as shown in Illustration 9.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil on the mark as shown in Illustration 10. Advance to scan.

    (Step Details)

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
