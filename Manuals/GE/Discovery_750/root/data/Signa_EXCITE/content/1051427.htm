2  Overview

This section covers required electrical connections that need to be made before performing the following procedures: Ramping Magnet to Field and Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field (respectively) and Shimming.

NOTE:Observe the following:

  • The Main and Superconducting Shim Power Supply input/output connections have the same P and J designations, used in the Connections for Ramping and Shimming procedure for the Service Power Supply units.

  • Make sure the Power Supply Cabinets are installed and checked in conformance with the supplier's manual, supplied with the unit, before making any power supply connections to the magnet. (See Vendor Manual Matrix for supplier manual numbers.)

  • Magnet System Interconnects shows the cable numbers, run numbers and interconnect pattern covered in this section.

Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing the Connections for Ramping and Shimming:

  • Read, understand and apply all safety requirements,

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions, and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items to make ramping and shimming Connections in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting the Connections for Ramping and Shimming:
Have all Work Assistants or Work Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!

Follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

4  Procedure

4.1  Superconducting Shim Coil Power Supply Connections to Magnet

NOTICE
Observe the following:

  • Do not connect input power to the Shim Coil Power Supply until it is verified that ALL current controls are set to zero (turned fully counterclockwise) and the supply is turned off.

  • Connecting the Shim Coil Harness to the magnet when the Shim Coil Power Supply is turned on may cause irreparable damage to the vapor-cooled shim leads.

 
 

  1. Engage the Shim Leads in conformance with Shim Lead Engagement and Disengagement.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loto procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Shim Coil Power Supply. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Connect electrical cabling to the Shim Coil Power Supply as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Shim Power Supply Connections

  1. Remove LOTO from the Shim Coil Power Supply.

4.2  Main Coil Power Supply Connections to Magnet

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized power supply can cause electrical shock.
Follow loto procedures while making electrical connections to any power supply.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn or Asphyxiation Hazard!
Internal cryostat pressure build-up to >5.25 psig will open 5.25 psig relief valve.
Before removing either Ramp or Fill Port Caps or loosening any component resulting in the release of cryogen pressure:

  1. Observe the internal cryostat pressure gauge reading,

  2. Set magnet monitor pressure to 0.5 psig, and

  3. Vent cryostat down to 0.2 psig through Vent Valve V2.

NOTE:If the site has Magnet Monitor proprietary software, connect to Magnet Monitor and turn off the alarm for vessel pressure. Turn the vessel pressure control to a high value of 0.5 psig and a low value of 0.4 psig.

  1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Main Coil Power Supply. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Connect electrical cabling to the Main Coil Power Supply as shown in Illustration 3 or Illustration 4. Either heater connector J5-1 or J5-2 can be used to turn on the heater(s). Do not connect two heater cables at the same time.

    NOTE:Clean all connections on the Main Leads and Main Lead Extensions to prevent high resistance contacts and minimize voltage drops. Brass wool or scouring pads can be used to clean connections.

    Make sure all nuts are tightened sufficiently to prevent a high resistance contact. Connect red cables to the positive (+) bus bar and black cables to the negative (-) bus bar. Two red and two black cables are connected in parallel to the bus bars for a 750 amp power supply (three red and three black cables for a 1,000 amp power supply). Make sure the cable lugs and/or exposed wire from the Ramp Cables are not touching the case of the Main Coil Power Supply.

    Illustration 3:  Magnet Power Supply Connections, 750 Amp Power Supply

    Illustration 4:  Magnet Power Supply Connections, 1,000 Amp Power Supply

NOTICE
The Main Lead Extensions used for the LCC magnet are the same as those used for other active shield magnets (SIV, SV and SX), but different than those used for non-active shield magnets (SII, SIII and Max). The LCC Main Lead Extensions have a helium flow hole seen from the Contact Band end of each Extension and are shorter than SIII Ramp Leads.

Make sure Main Lead Extensions 46-294204G1 are used for the Connections for the Ramping and Shimming procedure when the ceiling height is >103 inches (2616 mm). Use Flexible Main Lead Extensions 2287029 and the Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool (2276755) in low ceiling height conditions. (See Illustration 5.)

 
 

  1. Replace the Contact Bands on the Main Lead Extensions in conformance with Main Lead Extension Contact Band Replacement before starting the ramp procedure. Make sure the gas flow holes are not blocked.

    NOTE:Use a new set of Contact Bands for each ramp performed.

    Illustration 5:  Main Lead Extensions

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before performing the following step:
Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before performing the following step.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Improper engagement/disengagement of the Main Lead Extensions with the Main Lead Pins can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
Insert Main Lead Extensions one at a time; if the magnet is at field, allow them to cool sufficiently (a fog or water vapor forms around the Lead Extensions) before they contact the Main Lead Pins.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Touching both Main Lead Extensions at the same time, or allowing them to contact each other while the magnet is at field, will result in a rapid discharge through their contact points if the Switch Heater is activated or circuit resistance develops at that moment.
Do not Touch both Main Lead Extensions at the same time, and Do not allow the Main Lead Extensions to contact each other.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
while inserting or extracting the Main Lead Extensions:
Do not allow exposed body areas to come in contact with the cryostat or plumbing.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
contact with cold objects is possible during this Connections for Ramping and Shimming procedure.
Wear nonabsorbent leather gloves with no holes or tears during insertion or extraction of the Main Lead Extensions.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
miswiring can result in a magnet quench.
To prevent the possibility of miswiring and a resulting quench during future ramping of the magnet, record connection polarities in Magnet Ramping and Parking Current Log.

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Contact between the brass bolts securing the power cables to the Main Lead Extensions 46-294204G1 and the Fill Port (V1) Cap can quench the magnet.
Make sure the brass bolts are installed correctly.

  1. When using Fixed Main Lead Extensions 46-294204G1 only, connect the other end of the Main Power Cables to the Main Lead Extensions with the 1 in. (25.4 mm) brass bolts as shown in Illustration 6. Secure the bolts with the brass nuts and brass washers provided. Tighten the connections sufficiently to provide a good electrical connection.

    NOTE:Only use brass washers when connecting Main Leads to the Main Lead Extensions to keep voltage drop to a minimum.

    Main Lead connections for Flexible Main Lead Extensions 2287029 are made after lead insertion in Step 12.

    Illustration 6:  Hardware Mounting on Fixed Main Lead Extensions 46-294204G1

  1. Install the 10-32UNF gas flow hole screws into the gas flow holes on each Main Lead Extension. (See Illustration 6.)

  1. Open the vent valve V2 shown in Illustration 7, turn off the proprietary pressure control feature in Magnet Monitor if applicable, and vent to 0.2 psig. Close vent valve V2.

    Illustration 7:  Helium Vent Plumbing

NOTICE
Normal ramp polarity has positive (+) on the left and negative (-) on the right as viewed from the Coldhead side of the magnet.
 
 

  1. Remove the Positive (+) Ramp Port Cap located on the magnet's vertical stack shown in Illustration 8). Do not lose the gasket inside the cap.

    Illustration 8:  Ramp Lead Port Polarities

NOTICE
If using Flexible Main Lead Extensions 2287029, bend the lead extension to a 45° angle to clear the ceiling when inserting the lead in the following step. Allow the flexible lead to straighten out and pre-cool before full insertion. The lead must be in its straight position to prevent cooling exhaust helium from escaping the coils.
 
 

  1. Quickly insert the Positive (red cables attached) Main Lead Extension less than halfway into the open receptacle.

  1. Quickly screw the Main Lead Extension compression nut onto the ramp port to prevent icing of the threads.

  1. Loosely screw the compression nut onto the Ramp Lead Port. (See Illustration 8 for correct connection polarities.)

  1. Repeat Step 7 through Step 10 for the other Main Lead Extension.

  1. When using Flexible Main Lead Extensions 2287029 only, connect the other end of the Main Power Cables to the Main Lead Extensions with the 1 in. (25.4 mm) brass bolts as shown in Illustration 9. Secure the bolts with the brass nuts and brass washers provided. Tighten the connections sufficiently to provide a good electrical connection.

    NOTE:Only use brass washers when connecting Main Leads to the Main Lead Extensions to keep voltage drop to a minimum.

    Illustration 9:  Hardware Mounting on Flexible Main Lead Extensions 2287029

  1. Connect the Voltage Sense Leads to the Main Lead Extension's Voltage Sense Screw Lugs (shown in Illustration 5) to allow for correct placement of the Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool. Terminate the other end of the Voltage Sense Leads to a DVM or VOM placed near the Main Coil Power Supply.

  1. Remove the flow hole screws shown in Illustration 5.

WARNING
Potential Magnet Quench!
Improper engagement/disengagement of the Main Lead Extensions with the Main Lead Pins can cause a ramped magnet to quench.
if the magnet is at field, allow the Main Lead Extensions to cool sufficiently (a fog or water vapor forms around the Lead Extensions) before they contact the Main Lead Pins.

NOTICE
Make sure gas flow holes in the Main Lead Extension are not blocked and that helium gas is exiting the holes.
 
 

  1. When the Main Lead Extensions are sufficiently cooled, loosen the compression fittings and fully engage the Main Lead Extensions. Loosely screw the compression nut onto the Ramp Lead Ports.

    NOTE:The Main Lead Extensions will depress approximately 1 in. (25 mm) from the point of contact to the fully engaged position. A firm resistance will be felt when fully engaged. Do not rotate the Main Lead Extensions excessively when in the engaged position as internal contact wear could result.

    When using LCC300 Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool 5116737 orient the Main Lead Extensions 30° right and left of vertical. See Illustration 10.

CAUTION
Potential Magnet Quench!
Excess power cable motion during ramping may disrupt contact between the Main Lead Extensions and the Main Lead Contact Pins. This can cause the magnet to quench.
Secure the Main Power Cables using tie-wraps and the Main Lead Extensions using a Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool.

NOTICE
After installing the Main Leads, take the following steps to prevent power cable motion disrupting Main Lead Extension contact.
 
 

  1. After installing the Main Leads, secure the Main Power Cables using tie-wraps (field-supplied item). The cables should be secured to each other and to convenient fixed points, such as the shroud rails, to prevent their movement.

NOTE:Using a Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool is required to minimize motion of the Main Leads and aid in getting good contact resistance.

  1. Install a Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool (2193922 with Fixed Main Lead Extensions, 2276755 with Flexible Main Lead Extensions, or LCC300 Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool 5116737) when LHe top-off is planned immediately after magnet is parked during ramping up/down):

    1. Make sure the heads of the Main Lead Extensions are oriented to match the grooves for them in the under side of the hold-down tool being used. Refer to Illustration 10 for the steps that follow.

    1. Screw the mounting studs of the Hold-Down Tool into the mounting holes welded onto the Main Lead Base Plate.

      Illustration 10:  Hold-Down Tool Installation

    1. Install the Hold-Down Tool onto the mounting bolts. Make sure that the Main Leads fit into the slots on the plate.

    NOTICE
    If using LCC300 Ramp Lead Hold-Down Tool 5116737, make sure the copper tubes will deflect away from the Fill Port the helium gas escaping the Main Lead Extension flow holes.
     
     

    1. Tighten the nuts on top of the Hold-Down Tool to lock the tool firmly in place and prevent Main Lead motion. While tightening the nuts, adjust the leveling screw to keep the tool level.

    CAUTION
    Potential Magnet Quench!
    Blocked Main Lead Extension vent holes can allow the voltage between the Main Lead Extensions and the Main Lead Contact Pins to drop. This can cause the magnet to quench.
    Make sure the vent holes in the Main Lead Extensions are not blocked.

    1. Verify that the vent holes in the Main Lead Extensions are not blocked.
