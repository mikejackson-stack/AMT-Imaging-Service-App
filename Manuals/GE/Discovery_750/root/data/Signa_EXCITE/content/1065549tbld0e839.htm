2267910

Power Cabinet AUX Board is reporting a 48 V undervoltage/overvoltage

This error indicates that the Auxiliary Module is measuring an out-of-tolerance change of 48 V (48 V +/- 4%) during the diagnostic. This may indicate a malfunction of the PDU PS1 supply. A measurement failure in the Auxiliary Module (much less likely) could also cause this error.
