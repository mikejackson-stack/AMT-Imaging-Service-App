2  Overview

Procedure for replacement of the PAC assembly on a fixed site system.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

ItemQtyEffectivityPart#Manufacturer
Nonferrous 19mm hex head wrench1---

3.2  Safety

DANGER
Electrocution Hazard!
Dangerous Voltages are Present around the Magnet Enclosure and Rear Pedestal electronics.
Remove Power to The Magnet Enclosure and Lockout/Tagout.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removal of PAC

  1. Perform Lock-Out Tag-Out procedure (see LOTO for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. The PAC is located under the left side of the magnet enclosure.

    Illustration 1:  Location of PAC

  1. Remove the left skirt covers (see Skirt Cover Removal and Install) to expose the tray that holds the PAC and SRI (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  PAC Tray

  1. Disconnect the cables attached to the PAC.

  1. The tray is held in place by one hex head screw on each slide. Remove the two (2) screws and slide tray out for access to the PAC board (see Illustration 3).

    Illustration 3:  Screw Location

  1. Velcro holds the PAC to tray. Lift PAC off tray and remove.

4.2  Installation of New PAC

  1. Place the PAC in the proper location and install in the reverse order as it was removed.

  1. Reconnect the cables.

  1. Slide tray under magnet and reinstall the two (2) hex head screws.

  1. Replace enclosure trim panels.

  1. Remove Lockout / Tagout hardware.

  1. Restore power.

5  Finalization

  1. Setup a test scan and test system operation.
