Rear End Bell Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

  1. Follow all lockout/tagout procedures by turning off the applicable breaker in the Power Distribution Unit (PDU) (see Lockout/Tagout for RF and PEN Cabinets and Magnet Room Electronics).

  1. Remove the front portion of the split bridge (see Bridge and Longitudinal Drive Belt Replacement).

  1. Remove the rear cosmetic covers (see Rear Cosmetic Cover Removal and Installation).

  1. Remove the nuts and washers that secure the rear pedestal to the magnet (see Illustration 1) and position the rear pedestal away from the magnet.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the five (5) bolts which attach the rear end bell to the turtle bracket (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

NOTICE
There are several hoses and cables (including the fiber optic cable for patient bore lighting) hidden behind the rear end bell. Be careful when removing the end bell so as not to inadvertently tug on any cables or hoses.
 
 

  1. Remove the two RF Transmit cables from the RF Body Coil.

  1. Remove the rear end bell and the gasket ring.

  1. Reverse steps to install the rear end bell.
