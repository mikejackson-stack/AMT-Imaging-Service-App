Cradle Release From Carriage Adjustment

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. Unlatch LPCA from cradle and drive LPCA to rear of magnet.

  1. Remove LPCA cover by unscrewing four top screws.

    (Step Details)

  1. Dock patient transport, if necessary.

  1. Latch LPCA to cradle.

  1. Ensure that the LPCA meets the following criteria:

    1. LPCA latch hook must be low enough to positively engage cradle.

    1. LPCA latch hook must be high enough to be disengaged from cradle when cradle handle is twisted. Verify that the LPCA can be moved into the bore without moving the cradle.

  1. If adjustment is needed, see Section 3 in the Cradle Release Block Adjustment procedure.

  1. Replace LPCA cover.
