Lockout / Tagout for Pen Panel, Patient Blower, and Magnet Room

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCKOUT / TAGOUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

  1. Multimeter set to read voltage for verification of PHPS capacitor discharge.

  1. Visual inspection for all other equipment in magnet room and penetration panel.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) used to ensure Disconnections

  1. Personal lock and tag for each FE at the site working on the de-energized equipment.

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. When replacing the SRI only: press an <E-Stop>button either on the SCIM or on the front of the scanner.

  1. All items (including SRI): At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the OFF position:

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU covering the circuit breaker switches, apply a personal LOTO device for each FE who will be working on the equipment.

  1. Test that power has dissipated for each item by:

    1. Gradient Water Chiller – Verify all LED's and displays are off.

    1. Rear Pedestal, LPCA, Dock, SRI and Magnet Room Electronics: All items in the rear pedestal, LPCA, Dock and SRI fall into the category of low voltage low energy. Verify all LEDs and displays are off.

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the ON position:

    (Step Details)
