Universal Power Monitor - Head and Body Setup and Calibration

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable1 hour10 mins

2  Overview

This procedure sets up the Universal Power Monitor (UPM) for 3.0T LCC300 systems in both Head Mode and Body mode using the system and the Power Measurement Kit.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

3.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
Dangerous voltages throughout the cabinet.
TO PREVENT FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, MAKE SURE THE RF AMPLIFIER IS DISABLED WHEN CONFIGURING THE CALIBRATION TOOLS.

4  Procedure

4.1  Head Mode

4.1.1  Head Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the Transmit/Receive (TR) faults. Disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection in the PEN cabinet. (See Illustration 1.) Move Switches SW2 and SW3 to the TR DISABLE and DD Disable positions respectively.

    Illustration 1:  Driver Module Front Switches

  1. Move the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter in top of the Penetration Cabinet to the DISABLE (DOWN) position as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  RF ENB Switch

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Head Heliax cable from HEAD RF OUT / J3.

    1. Connect the 35 kW RF Dummy Load to HEAD RF OUT / J3. (See Illustration 3.)

    1. Plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    NOTE:Make sure the fan is running on the 35kW RF Dummy Load.

    Illustration 3:  Setup for 3.0T LCC Head Output Measurement

4.1.2  Setting Up Software Tool to Calibrate UPM - Head Forward Power

NOTICE
This tool must have the head coil installed in order to enable Coil ID.
 
 

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch on the front of DTX1 Exciter in top of the Penetration Cabinet by moving it to the ENABLE (UP) position.

  1. With head coil attached to the LPCA, landmark on the head coil and advance to scan.

  1. Start the Common Service Desktop (CSD) , then click the Calibration browser tab.

  1. Start the UPM Calibration Tool.

    • To start non-proprietary service tools:

      1. From the CSD, select the Calibration tab.

      2. From the Calibration menu, select [UPM Tool]

4.1.3  Head Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Before proceeding, disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2. Review the Safety section at the beginning of this procedure.

  1. On the front of the RF Amplifier, disconnect the RF Dummy Load from HEAD RF OUT / J3, and leave J3 open and unconnected.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Return to the Universal Power Monitor Tool user interface.

  1. For Nuclei, confirm [1H] is selected.

  1. For RF Chain, confirm [Head] is selected.

  1. For monitor Power Type, select [Reflected].

  1. Confirm [Calibrate] is selected.

  1. Select [Start] and validate the hardware setup, then select [Yes].

  1. Verify that status indicates a Pass or Fail within about five minutes. (Tool should notify user of current step in progress.) If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting .

  1. Quit the tool and do a TPS Reset.

  1. Proceed to the UPM- Body and Head Functional Check and perform the Head Functional Check procedure before going to Finalization.

4.2  Body Mode

4.2.1  Body Forward Power Calibration

  1. Inhibit the TR faults, and disable the Driver Module TR and DD fault detection. (See Illustration 7.) Ensure switches SW2 and SW3 are in the TR DISABLE and DD DISABLE positions respectively.

    Illustration 7:  Driver Module Switches

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the RF Amplifier to the Dummy Load:

    1. Remove the Body Heliax cable from BODY RF OUT / J4.

    1. Connect the 35 kW RF Dummy Load to BODY RF OUT / J4. (See Illustration 8.)

    1. Plug the Dummy Load power cable into a 110 VAC service outlet in the system cabinet.

    NOTE:Make sure the fan is running on the 35kW RF Dummy Load.

    Illustration 8:  Plug Dummy Load into J4, Body Out

4.2.3  Body Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Disconnect the RF Dummy Load from BODY RF OUT / J4 on the front of the RF Amplifier as shown in Illustration 8, and leave J4 open and unconnected.

    NOTE:The head cable may remain disconnected if an RF Head output calibration was just completed.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Return to the Universal Power Monitor Tool user interface.

  1. For Nuclei, confirm [1H] is selected.

  1. For RF Chain, confirm [Body] is selected.

  1. For monitor Power Type, select [Reflected].

  1. Confirm [Calibrate] is selected.

  1. Select [Start], validate the hardware setup, then select [Yes].

  1. Verify that the status indicates a PASS or FAIL within about three minutes. (Tool should notify user of current step in progress.) If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting.

4.2.4  Body Quadrature Hybrid Reflected Power Calibration

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Connect the System Body output cable to J4.

  1. Move the Driver Module SW2 TR ENABLE switch into the Enable (Up) position.

  1. Disconnect the body quadrature hybrid cables at J2 and J3 on the hybrid in the magnet room.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Return to the UPM Tool interface.

  1. For Nuclei, confirm [1H] is selected as shown in Illustration 12.

    Illustration 12:  UPM Tool Interface

  1. For RF Chain, select [Body] as shown in Illustration 12.

  1. For monitor Power Type, select [Hybrid Ref.] as shown in Illustration 12.

  1. Confirm that [Calibrate] is selected. (See Illustration 12.)

  1. Select [Start] and validate the hardware setup, then select [Yes].

  1. Check that the status indicates a PASS or FAIL within about five minutes. (Tool should notify user of current step in progress.) If failure occurs, refer to Universal Power Monitor (UPM) Troubleshooting.

5  Finalization

  1. Disable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the DOWN position as shown in Illustration 2.

  1. Remove the cables and power measurement equipment.

  1. Reattach the Head J3 cable and Body Hybrid J2 and J3 Heliax cables.

  1. Enable the RF ENB switch by moving it to the UP position.

  1. Confirm that switch SW2 is set to TR ENABLE and switch SW3 is set to DD ENABLE for TR drive fault detection. (See Illustration 1.)

  1. Upon completion of any UPM calibration, click [Reset TPS]. The system cannot scan until this step is complete.
