Side Cover Removal and Installation

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
Strong Magnetic Field!
Ferrous materials can become dangerous projectiles in the presence of the magnetic field Produced by the Magnet.
Do not Bring any ferromagnetic tools or equipment into the magnet room.

1  Procedure

1.1   Removing Left Side Covers

  1. Loosen the large screw on lower portion of the lower side cover by using a non-ferrous allen key and turning the screw counter-clockwise (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Grab sides of lower cover and lift upwards until the cover is released from the upper cover. Set lower cover aside (see Illustration 2).

    (Step Details)

  1. The upper cover is secured to the arc covers primarily by two round feet on the lower portion of the side cover. To remove the cover, grab the two handles on the upper cover firmly and push the cover up and then pull out away from the enclosure. This should release the cover’s feet and allow you to move it freely (see Illustration 4).

  1. Once the feet are released, use the arc cover as a guide rail, and allow the cover to slide down the side of the enclosure. Once the cover clears the fixtures on the upper portion of the magnet, the cover can either be safely set aside or it can be rested on the side of the magnet using the cover’s hooks that latch on to the arc covers (see Illustration 3).

    (Step Details)

1.2   Installing Left Side Covers

NOTE:To replace the left side covers, the top right side cover must be installed.

  1. Using the arc covers as guide rails, slide the side cover up to meet the right side cover at the top of the magnet (see Illustration 4).

    (Step Details)

  1. Hold the handles away from the enclosure to ensure that the alignment tabs get properly slotted under the right side’s slot (see Illustration 5).

    (Step Details)

  1. Set the round foot on the magnet side of the cover into the upper oval slots on the arc cover (see Illustration 7). Snap into place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Hold the sides of the Lower Cover and align the round feet on the magnet side of the Lower Cover with the arc cover’s oval slots (see Illustration 8). Snap into place.

    (Step Details)

  1. Latch the cover into place by turning the screw clockwise so the latch is secure (see Illustration 9).

    (Step Details)
