Hydraulic Filter Replacement

(Click here for Summary Version)

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
140 minutes

2  Overview

Perform the following procedure once each year to maintain smooth, responsive operation of the Patient Table hydraulics.

4  Procedure

4.1  Removing the Hydraulic Filter

  1. Undock the Patient Table and move it out of the magnet room.

  1. Raise the Patient Table to full up position.

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE PERFORMANCE.
ENSURE THAT THE LOCK SAfety BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

  1. Lower the Table Lock Safety Bar.

  1. Lower the table to ensure all of its weight is resting on the lock bar.

  1. Remove Lower Base Covers. Refer to the Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Cover Removal procedure.

  1. Remove Front Base Cover.

    Illustration 1:  Front Base Cover

  1. At the lower front of the table chassis, remove the Table Interface Panel screws on both sides. Cut tie-wraps holding hydraulic line to frame. See Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Lower Right Front Table Chassis

    NOTE:Removing the screws on the Table Interface Panel allows you to move the board back sufficiently to use a small standard head screwdriver to loosen the screws on the front cable connectors.

  1. Disconnect all front, right side cable connectors from the Table Interface Panel. Note all cable designations and positions for proper reinstallation.

  1. Place absorbent towels under hydraulic cylinder.

  1. Raise Patient Table (three (3) or four (4) pumps) until Lock Safety Bar is out of the way. This provides enough clearance to get a 13/16" wrench onto high pressure hose fitting.

    Illustration 3:  Hydraulic Filter Location

  1. Turn wrench only enough to loosen the hose fitting initially, but not to let any oil leak out. Then lower the table using the Down foot pedal until table is again supported by Lock Safety Bar.

  1. Remove high-pressure hose from adapter. A small amount of oil will spill out of hose during removal; raise hose as high as possible to limit oil spillage.

  1. Remove filter cap from filter using two ¾" wrenches.

    Illustration 4:  Remove Filter Element

  1. Remove dirty oil from top of filter.

  1. Remove gasket and filter element from filter body.

4.2  Replacing the Hydraulic Filter

  1. Clean outside threads (both ends) of filter body with a clean cloth.

  1. Clean interior threads of filter cap with a clean cloth.

  1. Install new filter element, hole end down, in filter body.

    NOTE:Equipment damage possibility. When reassembling filter cap, use care not to damage gasket; ensure that gasket is properly seated before tightening with wrench. If gasket is deformed, oil will leak out of this connection when reassembled.

  1. Install filter cap and new gasket onto filter body. Ensure that new gasket seats properly before tightening with wrench.

  1. Reconnect high-pressure hose to cylinder block. Tighten by hand, then raise the table with Up foot pedal to allow clearance for wrench; tighten hose fitting securely.

  1. Raise table with several pumps on the Up foot pedal and check for oil leaks. If leaks occur, tighten connections snugly, but do not over tighten; brass fittings will deform if over tightened.

  1. Proceed to Patient Table Hydraulics Bleeding.
