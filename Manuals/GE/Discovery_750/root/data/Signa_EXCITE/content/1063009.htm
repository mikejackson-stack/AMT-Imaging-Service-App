3.0T HD 8-Channel Foot/Ankle Coil FRUs and Replacements

1  Personnel Requirements

Required PersonsProcedure Timing
130 minutes

2  Procedure Overview

This is the replacement procedure for the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Foot Ankle Coil by Invivo, catalog M3340CB.

3  Preliminary Requirements

3.1  Tools and Test Equipment

Non-Magentic Service Tool Kit, Qty. 1

3.2  Replacement Parts

See Section 5 for lists of the field replaceable units (FRUs) and other accessories.

4  Procedure

NOTICE
Replacement procedures are to be performed by an authorized Service Engineer only.
 
 

4.1  Cable Continutiy Check

Cable continuity checks are found in 3T HD 8CH Foot Ankle Coil Troubleshooting.

4.2  Cable Replacement Procedure

  1. There are two coil connector mounting screws located at the end of the coil. Remove the two coil connector mounting screws as detailed in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Remove Mounting Screws

  2. Remove the coil connector by grasping the coil connector housing and pulling it away from the coil in the inferior direction (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Disconnect Coil Cable

4.3  Mechanical Hardware Check

  1. Exercise the latches on the baseplate by moving the blue latch handle back and forth. The latches must move smoothly and lock positively into place. The latches must not loosen or open during the course of an examination. The foot support and coil is capable of a 15 degree Plantar Flexion.

  2. Move the coil from side to side on the baseplate. The coil must move smoothly. Swivel the coil on the baseplate. The coil must move smoothly within a 10-15 degree rotation. Lock the coil into position on the baseplate. The coil must remain in place while locked down, and resume smooth movement when unlocked.
