Lockout / Tagout for Gradient Amplifier / PDU

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

DANGER
ELECTROCUTION HAZARD.
HIGH VOLTAGE PRESENT.
USE PROPER LOCK OUT / TAG OUT PROCEDURES BEFORE REPLACING THE COMPONENTS.

1  Procedure

1.1   Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

Time method for dissipation of stored energy – 5 minutes.

1.2   Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconection(s)

Three (3) personal locks and tags per Field Engineer (FE) at the site working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

1.3   Applying LOTO

  1. Prepare for shutdown of equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Shutdown the VREs. From the Common Service Browser, go to the Utilities tab, and then the VRE folder. Then click Soft Power Off.

  1. At the operator’s workspace, shutdown the workstation.

  1. Wait for the system to indicate on the monitor that it is safe to power off the computer before proceeding. It usually takes about 90 seconds before this message is seen.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, press the Power Off button (see Step 1 of Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the OFF position:

    (Step Details)

  1. Close and Lock Out/Tag Out the Gradient/PDU Service Disconnect Panel.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, rotate the large main breaker labeled INPUT counterclockwise to the green Green O (OFF) position (see Step 3 of Illustration 1).

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, press on the left-pointing arrow on the end of the main breaker handle to expose the location where the Lock and Tag can be placed (see Illustration 2). Lock and Tag out the PDU INPUT circuit breaker.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate and turn off the main power supply feeding the Gradient/PDU cabinet (in this document we will refer to this as the Service Disconnect Panel. It may also be referred to as the Main Disconnect Panel (MDP) .)

  1. Lock Out/Tag Out the main power supply.

  1. Verify that all energy has been dissipated. Measure the power at the front panel 420 and 208 test points of the PDU.

    (Step Details)

1.4   Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the Service Disconnect Panel.

  1. Power On the main power supply feeding the Gradient/PDU cabinet.

  1. Remove personal LOTO device(s) from the front panel of the PDU.

  1. Rotate the larger main breaker labeled INPUT clockwise to RED I (ON) position.

  1. At the front panel of the PDU, flip the circuit breaker switches for the following to the ON position:

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the EMO Reset switch.

  1. Bring the Signa software back up.

    (Step Details)
