2  Overview

Prior to performing servicing and/or maintenance work on the Heat Exchanger Cabinet (HEC), the steps in this Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedure must be performed by a LOTO-authorized GE Field Engineer (FE). Completing all steps in this procedure ensures a safe environment, and avoids equipment damage when working on these parts.

4  Procedure

4.1  Types of Equipment and/or Methods Selected to Dissipate or Isolate Stored Energy

None required.

4.2  Type(s) of Equipment and/or Method(s) Used to Ensure Disconnection(s)

A personal lock and tag and ball valve LOTO device at the site per FE working on the de-energized equipment at the time of LOTO.

4.3  Applying LOTO to the MDP

  1. Prepare for shutdown of the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO is being performed.

  1. Look at the variable speed Light Emitting Diodes (LEDs) located on the front of the HEC to visually confirm that power is on. (See Illustration 1.)

    Illustration 1:  Powered HEC Cabinet

NOTE:Powering off HEC without first powering down pumps from VFD can put the VFD at risk of damage.

  1. Toggle off the pumps and blower with the PLC Signal Box keypad as follows:

    • Blower: Press and hold the up-arrow ( ^ ), and press [F1]

    • Gradient Coil Pump: Press and hold the up arrow ( ^ ), and press [F2]

    • Power Electronics: Press and hold the up arrow ( ^ ), and press [F3]

  1. Turn off the circuit breaker on the HEC power box.

  1. At the front of the MDP, move the HEC circuit breaker to the OFF position and apply LOTO.

    Illustration 2:  HEC Circuit Breaker

NOTICE
Powering off the HEC circuit breaker will cause the Cryocooler Compressor to also lose power. Ensure that the HEC downtime is minimized to avoid boiling off cryogens.
 
 

  1. Look at the variable speed drive LEDs again to visually confirm to ensure that power is off before removing the front panel. (See Illustration 3.)

    Illustration 3:  HEC LEDs Powered Off

  1. Verify all energy is dissipated. Carefully remove the six screws securing the HEC power box front cover and remove the cover. (See Illustration 4.)

    Illustration 4:  Location of Screws on HEC Power Box

  1. Confirm that voltage is removed by testing the line-to-line voltage at the top and bottom of the terminal points. (See Illustration 5.)

    Illustration 5:  Terminal Points of HEC Power Box

  1. Replace the power box front cover.

4.4  Applying LOTO to HEC Shut-Off Valves

  1. Prepare for shut down. Check with the facility owner to determine the proper shutdown method for the chiller.

  1. Shut off both the facility Return and Supply Valves by turning the handle to a 90º angle.

  1. Apply LOTO device to both the Return and Supply Valves. (See Illustration 6 and Illustration 7.)

    Illustration 6:  LOTO Installed on Return Valve

    Illustration 7:  LOTO Installed on Supply Valve

  1. Drain fluid between the Return and Supply Lines. (See Coolant Draining.)

  1. Confirm there is no water draining from Quick Disconnect, and pressure in the line is relieved.

4.5  Removing LOTO

  1. Prepare for re-energizing the equipment. Notify affected personnel working in the area that LOTO will be removed.

  1. If LOTO device was installed, remove personal LOTO device from the HEC circuit breaker on the MDP.

  1. If LOTO device(s) were installed, remove personal LOTO device(s) from both Return and Supply Valves.

  1. If the MDP circuit breaker was turned off at the front panel of the MDP, switch ON the HEC circuit breaker.

  1. Toggle on the pumps and blower from the PLC Signal Box keypad as follows:

    • Power Electronics: Press and hold the up arrow ( ^ ), and press [F3]

    • Gradient Coil Pump: Press and hold the up arrow ( ^ ), and press [F2]

    • Blower: Press and hold the up-arrow ( ^ ), and press [F1]

  1. If return and supply valves were shut off, return the handles in line to the 0º angle.
