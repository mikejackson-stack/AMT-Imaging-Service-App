3.0T HD 8-Channel Torso Array Coil Setup for MCQA Test

2  Overview

This procedure will setup for the automated MCQA test using the 3.0T HD 8-Channel Torso Coil by GE/USAI catalog M3335LN.

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1:

    Illustration 1:  Connecting the Coil

  1. Place phantoms and phantoms positioner in the coil as shown in Illustration 2:

    Illustration 2:  Positioning Phantom and Positioner

  1. Establish an axial landmark on the line on top of the coil. Advance to scan. See Illustration 3.

    Illustration 3:  Landmarking Coil

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil

4.2  Unified Phantom Setup

NOTE:Connect the coil to the system as shown in Legacy Phantom Setup, Step 1.

  1. Place the posterior section of the coil on the table as shown in Illustration 4.

    Illustration 4:  Placing the Posterior Section of the Coil

  1. Place the positioner (2381683-4) of Torso into gaps present on the posterior section of the coil as shown in Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Placing the Torso Positioner into Gaps

  1. Place the TL Unified phantom as shown in Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Placing the TL Unified Phantom

  1. Place the TL unified phantom such that the phantom is well centered on both S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 7 and Illustration 8.

    Illustration 7:  Aligning Phantom in S/I

    Illustration 8:  Aligning Phantom in R/L

  1. Place the anterior section (Illustration 9) in such a way that the anterior of the coil is well set in to the positioner as shown in Illustration 10 and fasten the Velcro straps so that the coil firmly snugs the phantom (Illustration 11).

    Illustration 9:  Anterior Section

    Illustration 10:  Placing Anterior Section into Positioner

    Illustration 11:  Secure Coil in Phantom

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA. Landmark the coil on the cross mark as shown in Illustration 12. Advance to scan.

    Illustration 12:  Landmarking the Coil

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
