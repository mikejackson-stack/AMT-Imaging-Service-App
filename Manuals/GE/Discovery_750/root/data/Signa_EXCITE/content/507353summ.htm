Pinot Chassis / MGD Chassis Replacements

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Procedure

  1. The replacement procedures for the following PCBs are the same – the only difference is their positions in the PINOT Chassis (or the MGD Chassis). See Illustration 1.

    (Step Details)

  1. Open the rear door of the chassis and switch-off the PINOT / MGD Chassis as shown in Illustration 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Switch OFF the Power to the Chassis from the switch as shown in Illustration 3.

    (Step Details)

  1. Loosen the two-faceplate holding screws (one located at the top and bottom of the faceplate). These screws are captive to the faceplate, and do not need to be completely removed.

  1. Using your thumbs, push in the grey release tabs, and gently pull the extractor tabs located on the top and bottom of the circuit board. The lever action of the ejectors will disengage the circuit board connections from the chassis as shown in Illustration 4.

    (Step Details)

  1. Slide the circuit board all the way out of the card cage, being careful not to bend or bind the card in any way.

    (Step Details)

  1. If a returnable FRU, securely repack the defective Switch into its packaging material to send it back to its respective place of destination.

  1. Remove the packing material from the replacement board, and gently slide the circuit board along the slot guides into the chassis.

  1. Gently engage the circuit board connectors with the backplane. If it doesn’t feel like the circuit board is properly engaging the backplane, remove the board and check the connectors for bent or broken pins.

  1. Once the board ejectors contact the MGD chassis faceplate, gently push the ejectors in to the center of the board. This action will cause the ejectors to insert the board into the chassis. Do not attempt to use anything other than the ejectors to insert or remove the board from the chassis.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the faceplate holding screws to secure the circuit board to the card rack.

  1. Where applicable, re-attach the cables to the face of the board (s).

  1. Power “ON” the PINOT / MGD chassis as shown in Illustration 3.
