Spike Noise Check

NOTICE
Possible Equipment Damage! Remove the Head Coil from the Patient Table before performing this procedure.
 
 

1  Procedure

1.1   Hardware Preparation for Spike Noise Data Collection

  1. Connect the oscilloscope power plug to the service outlet or power strip inside the PGR cabinet to prevent ground loops that may cause higher floor noise.

  1. Connect a twinax cable to J13 of the Transient Detection Module (TDM) . Connect the other end of this cable to an open port on the Penetration (PEN) panel.

    (Step Details)

  1. With a coaxial cable connect the PEN panel port to channel 1 (CH1) on the oscilloscope (o-scope) .

  1. Connect another coaxial cable to J5 on the Exciter panel. Connect the other end of the coaxial cable to channel 2 (CH2) on the o-scope.

    (Step Details)

  1. Connect another coaxial cable to J11 on the Exciter panel. Connect the other end of the coaxial cable to the Trigger Input on the o-scope.

    (Step Details)

  1. Set up the oscilloscope as follows:

    1. Channel 1 - 100 mV/div, 1 mW input, AC coupled, 1 meg ohm impedance.

    1. Channel 2 - 5 V/div, 1 mW input, DC coupled, 1 meg ohm impedance.

    1. Time Base - 2 msec/div.

    1. Trigger - auto, external.

1.2   Spike Noise Scan Preparation

  1. At the operator workspace, select the Scan icon on the desktop control panel.

  1. If necessary, exit out of any previous exams by selecting [End Exam].

  1. Click [New Pt] and enter the following:

    1. Name: spike noise

    1. ID: geservice

    1. Weight (lb.): 111

  1. Remove all phantoms from the patient table. This is an empty bore test.

  1. Place the head coil without a phantom on the cradle. Landmark on the center of the head coil. At the keypad on the front magnet enclosure, press <LANDMARK> and <MOVE TO SCAN>.

  1. The following three sub-steps are proprietary and only available for GE use, or to sites with a valid Advanced Service Package Limited License. The non-proprietary protocol setup procedure is listed in Table 1.

    1. Set Patient Protocols to Service.

    1. In the Protocol field, select spike_noise. Make sure that the protocol matches the magnetic field strength of the system.

    1. Click [Accept] to load the protocol.

  1. Click [Start Exam] and then press [Move to Scan].

    (Step Details)

1.3   Sample Noise Data Collection and Analysis

  1. At the Operator Workspace, click [Manual Prescan] and set R1 = 11, R2 = 15, TG =0 and select [Scan TR].

  1. Go to the oscilloscope and adjust the scope trigger to display one cycle of the A/D window on the scope channel 2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the <PAUSE SCAN> button on the console keyboard. Press the FAN button on the magnet enclosure to turn off the patient comfort fan (the fan turns on automatically with scan software).

    (Step Details)

  1. The Mulit-Generational Data acquisition/Radio Frequency (MGD/RF) are now in the receive mode and any noise can be observed at the Transient Detect Module (TDM) Spike Detect port. The scope must be in auto trigger mode.

  1. Put the scope in continuous acquisition mode. The method for doing this varies with the type of scope. Clear the oscilloscope display and ensure the trigger is in the auto position.

  1. Observe the scope display for about two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) is typically 200 mV p–p. Check that there are no spikes >0.5 Nenv (where Nenv is the noise envelope peak to peak value) above the noise envelope.

    (Step Details)

  1. Press the FAN button on the magnet enclosure to turn on the patient comfort fan. Clear the oscilloscope display and ensure that the trigger is in the Auto position. Observe the oscilloscope display for about two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) should remain as described in Step 6 above.

  1. Clear the oscilloscope display and change the trigger from Auto to Normal.

  1. Press the FAN button on the magnet enclosure to turn off the patient comfort fan.

  1. Press the <START SCAN> button on the console keyboard to resume pre-scan. Click on [Scan T/R].

  1. Observe the scope display after two minutes. The noise envelope (channel 1) should be approximately 200 mV. Check that there are no spikes >0.5 Nenv (where Nenv is the noise envelope peak-to-peak value) above the noise envelope in the region of interest (except for maybe a beginning and ending transition spike). See Illustration 5.

    (Step Details)

  1. Click [Done].

  1. Click [New Series]. Set up scan prescription for series #2 scan per notes and Table 1, then repeat steps Sample Noise Data Collection and Analysis.

  1. Repeat the previous step until all head pre-scans (series #1 through #4) are complete.

2  Finalization

  1. When you’re done with the loopback testing then restore the cabling to the default clinical state.

  1. Perform a Check Scan to check the system operation.
