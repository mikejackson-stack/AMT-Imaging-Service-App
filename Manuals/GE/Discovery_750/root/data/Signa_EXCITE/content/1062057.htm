3  Procedure

  1. Turn off power to the PDU by following LOTO for the PGR PDU/Gradient Subsystem.

NOTE:The PDU must be powered down completely to prevent damage to system electronics.

  1. Disconnect the cables on the front of the auxiliary board (see Illustration 1).

    Illustration 1:  Auxiliary Board - Front Cables Disconnected

  1. Undo the four screws on the front shelf holding the auxiliary board (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  Shelf Screws

  1. Before pulling the shelf out, there are two cable ties in the front of the auxiliary board shelf (see Illustration 3). Cut these ties to free the cables. Then, carefully moves cables away from the shelf so they do not catch when the shelf is being pulled out.

    Illustration 3:  Cable Ties

  1. Slowly pull the auxiliary board and shelf out of the cabinet (see Illustration 4).

    Illustration 4:  Auxiliary Board - Slide Out

  1. Before removing the shelf from the cabinet, disconnect any cables from the rear of the auxiliary board.

  1. Pull the shelf out completely and set the top of the auxiliary board on a flat surface, exposing the bottom of the shelf.

  1. The auxiliary board is mounted onto the shelf with six studs. Detach the auxiliary board from the shelf by unscrewing the nuts (see Illustration 5). Set the nuts aside as you will need these to attach to the auxiliary board FRU.

    Illustration 5:  Underside of Shelf

  1. The auxiliary board FRU will have red safety caps on each of the studs. Remove the red safety caps before placing the auxiliary board on the shelf.

  1. Align the auxiliary board FRU studs with the openings in the shelf. Attach the auxiliary board FRU to the shelf by screwing the nuts onto the six studs.

  1. Plug in all of the rear auxiliary board cables onto the new auxiliary board.

  1. Align the shelf on the cabinet tracks and push the auxiliary board into the PGR Cabinet.

  1. Secure the shelf into the cabinet with the two screws on each side (see Illustration 2).

  1. Reconnect the cables to the front of the auxiliary board.

    Illustration 6:  Cable Tie Wrap Holders

  1. Place in the new tie wraps onto the front of the auxiliary board shelf. See Illustration 6.

  1. Place cables in the tie wraps and secure. Cut any access tie wrap material.

4  Finalization

  1. Remove LOTO from the PDU.

  1. Perform TPS reset and review error log for Auxiliary Board faults.

  1. Perform a verification scan with DQA Phantom.
