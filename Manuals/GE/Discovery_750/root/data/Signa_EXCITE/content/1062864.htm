3  Environmental Requirements

Storage

This equipment must be stored under the following conditions:

  • Temperature range of -40° to 70° C (-40° to 158°F )

  • Relative humidity range of 40 to 95%, non-condensing

  • Atmospheric pressure range of 525 to 1,013 hPa
