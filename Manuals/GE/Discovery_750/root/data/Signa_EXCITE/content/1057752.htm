Line Voltage Checks

During the setup, calibration, and functional checks of this system, monitor the power line voltages similar to what was done during the pre-installation site planning checks (refer to the Discovery MR750 3.0T Pre-Installation, Power Source Monitoring). In particular, look for variations in the day-to-day line voltage that do not meet equipment specifications. Look for any voltage disturbances that might correlate to system malfunctions or component failures.

Some recommended analyzers that are designed for unattended monitoring are:

  • Dranetz

    • Model 656A

    • Model 658

  • RPM

    • Model 1651

    • Model 1656

    • Model 1658

Ensure that the power meets the site planning requirements (refer to the Discovery MR750 3.0T Pre-Installation, Power Requirements).
