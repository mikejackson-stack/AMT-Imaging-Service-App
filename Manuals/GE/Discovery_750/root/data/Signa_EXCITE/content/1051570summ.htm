Sav-Con Connector Replacement

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

1  Safety

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before and while performing the Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure:

  • Make sure to review and fully understand all superconducting magnet portions and

  • Make sure to fully comply with all required items for Sav-Con Connector Replacement in the Magnet & Cryogen Service Safety Requirements subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements.

DANGER
Potential Fatal Injury!
before starting the Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure:
Have all Work Assistants or Observers comply with the Buddy System Requirements & Certification subsection of 2301164PRE, MR Magnet - Safety Requirements .

WARNING
Asphyxiation & Cold Burn Hazard!
A magnet quench during procedure could result in rapid expulsion of liquid helium through the Vertical Penetration.
Make sure magnet is ramped down to zero field before starting the Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure.

WARNING
Asphyxiation Hazard!
Procedure generates odorless, colorless helium gas that displaces oxygen in the air.
Make sure magnet room vent exhaust fan is on before starting the Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn or Asphyxiation Hazard!
Internal cryostat pressure build-up to >5.25 psig will open 5.25 psig relief valve.
Before removing either Ramp or Fill Port Caps or loosening any component resulting in the release of cryogen pressure:

  1. Observe the internal cryostat pressure gauge reading,

  2. Set Magnet Monitor pressure to 0.5 psig, and

  3. Vent cryostat down to 0.2 psig through Vent Valve V2.

WARNING
Potential Cold Burn Hazard!
Exposure to Cryogens and contact with cold objects is possible during Sav-Con Connector Replacement.
Wear protective clothing, nonabsorbent gloves and goggles when performing the Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure.

WARNING
Electrical Shock Hazard!
Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow OSHA Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

NOTICE
Removal and replacement of the Sav-Con Connector must be performed quickly to prevent condensation and icing in the Vertical Penetration and on electrical connectors. Read and understand this Sav-Con Connector Replacement procedure and have all required tools and equipment on hand, including Tool Kit 46-294743G1, the tools (shown in Illustration 4) helium gas, a heat gun, wrenches, a flashlight, Snoop, goggles and gloves before starting.
 
 

NOTICE
Service Platform with side shield must be installed when working on the magnet to protect side-mounted components from damage.

Illustration 1:  Service Platform with Side Shield Installed

 
 

NOTE:If the site has Magnet Monitor proprietary software, connect to the Magnet Monitor and turn off the alarm for vessel pressure. Then turn the vessel pressure control to a high value of 0.5 psig and a low value of 0.4 psig.

3  Procedure

3.1   Sav-Con Connector Removal

NOTICE
Make sure the Vertical Penetration is clear of any obstructions before inserting/extracting any tools/apparatus to prevent possible magnet damage.
 
 

  1. Ramp the magnet down to zero field in conformance with Magnet Rampdown to Zero Field.

  1. Shut down the compressor as follows:

    1. Turn ON (upward I position) the Coldhead Drive Switch on the Compressor's back panel. (See the illustration below.)

      (Step Details)

    1. Place the Drive Switch on the Compressor's front panel in the O (off) position. (See the previous illustration.)

    1. Run the Coldhead for only 10 seconds with the Compressor turned off to prevent pressure build-up in the Coldhead, then turn Off the Coldhead Drive Switch and the Compressor's Main Power Switch.

    WARNING
    Electrical Shock Hazard!
    Contact with connectors leading to an energized Compressor can cause electrical shock.
    Whenever the input power to the Compressor is disconnected, follow loto procedures to make sure power is not supplied to the Compressor.

    1. Disconnect and LOTO input power to the Compressor. Verify that no voltage is present by using a DVM or equivalent measuring device.

  1. Slowly open Vent Valve V2 (shown below), and vent the magnet until internal pressure drops to 0.2 psig on the Cryostat Pressure Gauge. Close Valve V2.

    (Step Details)

  1. Assemble the Sav-Con Connector Insertion/Removal Tool (46-294310G1) to the Lexan Tool Plate (46-294306G1) by removing the knob on the tool shaft, and inserting the shaft through the center hole in the Lexan plate. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Position the connector end of the Sav-Con Connector Insertion/Removal Tool toward the Lexan Tool Plate, and align the tool's keyway to the dimple shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Tighten the knurled nut to hold the tool in place, then reassemble the knob to the tool's shaft.

NOTICE
Immediately after the Shim Lead Assembly is removed, quickly place the assembled Sav-Con Tool and Plate (Illustration 5) onto the Vertical Penetration to prevent icing.

Always replace the Shim Lead O-Ring when performing Sav-Con Connector Replacement.

 
 

  1. Remove the Shim Lead Assembly and Instrumentation Lead Assembly in conformance with Shim Lead and Baffle Replacement and Instrumentation Lead Assembly Replacement, respectively. Store carefully.

  1. Immediately position the assembled Sav-Con Tool and Plate (Illustration 5) on top of the Vertical Penetration with arrow oriented as shown below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Allow the flow of helium gas, exiting through holes in the Lexan Tool Plate, to subside.

  1. Make sure a new Shim Lead O-Ring (46-281101P5) is in its groove on top of the Vertical Penetration, and firmly sandwiched between the Sav-Con Tool Assembly and the Vertical Penetration.

  1. Bolt the Sav-Con Tool Assembly's Lexan Plate in place using the bolts removed earlier.

  1. Shine a flashlight through the Lexan Tool Plate, and locate the Sav-Con Connector and its retaining screws. Check for any ice build-up.

    1. If ice builds up on the Sav-Con Connector or associated hardware during Sav-Con Connector Replacement, insert a helium gas tube through one of the holes in the Lexan Tool Plate to the iced area.

    1. Blow warm helium gas at 15 psig to remove the ice, then allow the flow of helium gas to subside.

  1. Locate the two Sav-Con Connector Retaining Screws directly below insertion holes in the Lexan Tool Plate. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Lower the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool (46-294292G1) through one of the two appropriate holes to contact the Allen head on either Sav-Con Retaining Screw.

  1. Push downward to engage the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool with a Sav-Con Retaining Screw and loosen each screw.

  1. Withdraw the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool.

  1. Loosen the knurled nut holding the Sav-Con Connector Insertion/Removal Tool.

  1. Push downward and carefully align the keyways of the two mating connectors, then seat the Sav-Con Connector Insertion/Removal Tool onto the Sav-Con Connector. (See the illustration below.)

    (Step Details)

  1. Insert the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool through the appropriate holes in the Lexan Tool Plate, and engage each screw on the Sav-Con Connector Tool.

  1. Carefully thread the Sav-Con Tool Screws into the threaded bolt heads of the Sav-Con Connector and tighten lightly.

  1. Remove the Screw Insertion/Removal Tools.

  1. Pull upward with the Sav-Con Tool to remove the Sav-Con Connector from the G10 Connector/Platform. Tighten the knurled nut to secure the tool.

  1. Remove the Lexan Tool Plate and Sav-Con Tool Assembly, and quickly replace with the Lexan Top Cover (46-294765G1 shown in Illustration 4).

3.2   Sav-Con Connector Replacement

NOTICE
Make sure the Vertical Penetration is clear of any obstructions before inserting/extracting any tools/apparatus to prevent possible magnet damage.
 
 

  1. Remove the old Sav-Con Connector from the tool, and insert a replacement Sav-Con Connector onto the Sav-Con Tool. Lightly tighten the tool's screws onto the Sav-Con threaded bolt-head screws.

  1. Remove the Lexan Top Cover from the Vertical Penetration, and immediately position the Sav-Con Tool Assembly (Illustration 8) over the Vertical Penetration. Orient the scribe mark on the Lexan Tool Plate towards the Vent Adapter.

  1. Repeat Sav-Con Connector Removal, Step 9 through Sav-Con Connector Removal, Step 12.

  1. Loosen the knurled nut and lower the Sav-Con Tool Assembly.

  1. Carefully engage the Sav-Con Connector into the mating G10 Connector/Platform.

    (Step Details)

  1. Locate the Sav-Con Connector Retaining Screws directly below insertion holes in the Lexan Tool Plate.

  1. Lower the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool to contact the Allen head of the Sav-Con Retaining Screws.

  1. Engage and hand tighten the Sav-Con Retaining Screws.

  1. Withdraw the Screw Insertion/Removal Tool.

  1. Unscrew the Sav-Con Tool Assembly's screws from the Sav-Con Connector. Disengage, pull the Sav-Con Tool Assembly upward, and secure it in place with the knurled nut.

  1. Remove the Sav-Con Tool Assembly from the Vertical Penetration, and replace with the Lexan Top Cover.

4  Finalization

  1. Check for and remove any ice before reinstalling the Shim Lead Assembly.

  1. Inspect the Shim Lead O-Ring (46-281101P6) at the top of the Vertical Penetration. Replace if nicked, scratched or damaged. Use vacuum grease when installing the O-ring.

  1. Reinstall the Shim Lead Assembly in conformance with Shim Lead and Baffle Replacement and the Shim Lead exhaust plumbing.

  1. Engage the Shim Lead in conformance with Shim Lead Engagement and Disengagement before reramping the magnet.

  1. Ramp the magnet in conformance with Ramping Magnet to Field.
