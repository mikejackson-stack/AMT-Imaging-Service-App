Z axis XPS is reporting a High Voltage I-Bus Over-current Fault

  1. Swap pair of black power cables between Z XPS and neighboring Y XGA and Z XGA and neighboring Y XPS.
    NOTE:Early sites don’t have black power cables that are long enough to swap. FMI 60769X will provide long length power cables to early sites that don’t have them.

  2. Repeat scan or run gradient static or fidelity diagnostic.
    NOTE:If the same Z axis XPS faults, the Z XPS is faulty. Replace the XPS. If Y axis XPS faults, the Z XGA is faulty. Replace the XGA.
