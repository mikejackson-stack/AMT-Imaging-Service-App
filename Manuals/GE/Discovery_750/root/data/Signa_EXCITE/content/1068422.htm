2.2  Safety

DANGER
FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK HAZARD!
TO PREVENT POSSIBLE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK, Shut down the Host computer and perform lockout/tagout (LOTO) BEFORE beginning THIS PROCEDURE.

3  Procedure

  1. Close the application software on the Host Computer. Refer to Lockout/Tagout of GOC to LOTO power sources.

  1. Remove the three screws at the rear of the GOC that secure the right side panel.

    Illustration 1:  Removing Screws

  1. Remove the right panel of the GOC, and disconnect the ground lead that connects the cover to the main chassis.

    NOTE:Avoid straining the short ground lead.

  1. Disconnect the power cord from the Ethernet Switch.

  1. Remove all cables connected to the Ethernet Switch, and make a note of labeling for reconnection.

    Illustration 2:  Removing Cables

  1. Loosen the strap that secures the Ethernet Switch (shown above) and carefully remove it.

  1. Install the replacement Ethernet Switch by reversing the above steps.

4  Finalization

  1. Restore power to the GOC and reboot the computer.

  1. Perform a test scan to ensure proper operation.
