4  Procedure

4.1  AMP Diagnostics

  1. The system status of the AMT amplifier is displayed on the front panel of the AMT. The locations of the front panel light emitting diode (LED) indicators supply are shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  AMT RF Amp

  1. The amplifier front panel indicators are:

    1. POWER: Indicates the presence of DC voltage in the control module. This indicator is lit when the circuit breaker is in the ON position.

    1. STANDBY: When lit (and the READY LED is unlit), the amplifier is in the standby state.

    1. READY: Indicates the amplifier is in an operational state.

    1. UNBLANK: The amplifier is receiving UNBLANK commands from J1 or J2.

    1. FAULT: Indicates the unit detects a fault. Refer to the status LEDs and/or the status code.

4.3  Problem / Solution Table

  1. In many cases, problems with the AMT display through fault codes. Six STATUS CODE LED indicators, labeled left-to-right 5 to 0, are on the front of the cabinet. These indicators (as pictured in Illustration 1) display status and fault codes.

  1. Within five seconds of power-up, the correct fault status displays. During this five second power-up interval, the fault status may not correctly display. All faults cause the system to shut down by going into the standby mode. In response to a fault, readback from the amplifier the controlling system may assert a Standby condition through the J1 connector. Therefore, the status LEDs mask out this condition during a fault and the only means of recovering from a fault condition is to RESET the system by cycling the AC power.

  1. Table 1 shows fault codes, a description of each fault, the recommended action and, if applicable, necessary replacement parts to overcome the fault that can be seen via the Fault Code LEDs. Table 2 shows a list of problems, probable causes and solution recommendations

    NOTE:Cycling power initiates the five minute warm-up timer. The amplifier remains in standby until this timer expires.

4.4  Troubleshooting for Low Gain

This section provides troubleshooting help to find a failed FRU that does not show an LED fault code. This type of failure produces system symptoms of low gain or no RF output. Follow this procedure only after completing the steps listed in Table 3 and Table 2.

NOTE:Use a 100 MHz scope (and 1.5T RF Power Measurement Kit) for this troubleshooting procedure.

NOTE:Do not use the 50-Ohm feed-through terminator in the oscilloscope. Use the 50-Ohm selectable termination from the kit.

  1. Remove the fan panel assembly by loosening all thumbscrews, disconnecting the cable at J17, and lifting the assembly up and out of the rack (refer to Illustration 2 for connector locations and labels).

  1. Defeat the kill switch interlock by pulling out the plunger on the switch in the upper left hand corner of the RF deck assembly (refer to Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  RF Deck Assembly – Rear View

  1. Disconnect the RF input cable from J3 on the RF controller module and attach to Channel 1 input of oscilloscope. Make sure the oscilloscope Channel 1 input is set to 1 megohm.

    NOTE:Triggering from the unblank signal delivers the best results.

  1. If you haven’t already done so, click [New Pt]

  1. Set the following parameters:.

    • Id: Type geservice then press <Enter>

    • Name: Type apm cal head then press <Enter>.

    • Weight (Lb): Type 300 then press <Enter>.

  1. Set Patient Protocols to Service.

  1. Click [Other] and a pop-up menu appears. Scroll through the options for MNS_31P_Setup. Single click on this option, then select RF cal series from the window on the right.

  1. Click [Save Series].

  1. Click [Prepare to Scan].

    In the next steps, change the CV values as indicated. See the User Interface Tutorial for specific help with User CVs. Select [Research Options] with the mouse cursor, and right click. Then select [Display CVs].

  1. Set value of CV ia_rf1 to 0.

  1. Set value of CV ia_rf2 to 32767.

  1. Click [Accept], then click [Download], then click [Done].

  1. Select [Spectro Prescan], set TG to 0, and click [Start]

  1. Gradually increase TG so the RF signal measures 502mVp-p, ± 6mVp-p (-2 ± 0.1 dBm). Record this value of TG: ____________________. Also record the voltage as measured at J3: ____________

  1. Decrease TG to 0 (zero) then select [Done].

  1. Reconnect the BNC coaxial cable to J3 RF IN located at the rear of the AMT RF Amplifier.

  1. Proceed with RF Troubleshooting, Table 4: (the following is an example procedure to be used for the connection points referred to in Table 4).

    1. Connect a 50Ohm terminated scope to the connection point.

    1. Click [Start]. Gradually increase TG to the value in Step 14.

    1. Measure the peak-to-peak voltage at connection point referenced in table.

    1. Record the voltage: _____________.

    1. Decrease TG to 0 (zero), then select [Done].

    1. Reconnect the cabling.

    1. Move to the next connection point to determine failure(s).
