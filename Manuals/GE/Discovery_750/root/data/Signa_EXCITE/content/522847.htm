Magnet Enclosure Display Test

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization
1Not Applicable10 minsNot Applicable

2  Overview

This test provides a quick method of testing the control panel display.

3  Procedure

  1. Return cradle to Home position.

  1. From the Common Service Desktop:

    1. Click [Diags].

    1. Click [Peripherals].

    1. Click [SRI Functional Tests].

    1. Select [Magnet Enclosure Display Test].

    1. Click [Run].

    Illustration 1:  Starting Magnet Enclosure Display Test

  1. Verify all LEDs turn on and that no LED remains on throughout the entirety the test (see Illustration 2).

    Illustration 2:  DISPLAY TEST INDICATORS

  1. Click [Stop Diags] to end the test. Click [Quit] to exit diagnostics.

4  Finalization

No finalization steps.
