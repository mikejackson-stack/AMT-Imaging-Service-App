1  Procedure

1.1   Troubleshooting the Receive Chain

1.1.1  Problem : No receive signal; no noise on the prescan screen.

  1. Ensure the proper center frequency is being used for either carbon or phosphorous nucleus. Check the switch and determine what type of switch it is (see Table 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. Confirm TPS resets without MNS receive errors.

    1. If TPS resets okay, check the connection between J4 and the LPCA. If the connection is working properly, connect the narrow band coil to the A-Port connector and perform a test scan. If the connection is not working properly, fix the connection.

      NOTE:Alternatively, if there is a MCRv Tool available, perform the Coil Datapath Diagnostics.

      1. If the test scan passes, inspect the MNS quick disconnect box for damage. Confirm with the customer that the coil is in working order or run the MNS Functional Checks procedure.

        (Step Details)

      1. If the test scan fails or the system cannot scan, inspect the A-Port connector, ODU egress connector for damage. Also check the RF Amplifier to the proper RF output power.

    1. If the TPS reset fails, check these connections at the MNS Upconverter and confirm each is in working order:

      (Step Details)

1.1.2  Problem: Multiple spurs in the MNS noise tests, spaced apart about 60 kHz apart.

  1. At the PEN wall, check all the cabling coming from the PHPS2. In particular check the quality of the contacts and shielding.

  1. Check the in-line filters (if any) and toroids (if any)

1.1.3  Problem: Single spur in the 13C MNS Noise test.

  1. Check that the magnet pressure transducer is properly shielded.

  1. Confirm that the SRI-4 firmware is at the latest revision. Check the SRI-4 cabling for good contact and shielding.

1.2   Troubleshooting the Transmit Chain

1.2.1  Problem: Transmit is malfunctioning.

  1. Scope the amplifier by connecting the transmit line to the 3.0T RF Power Measurement Kit to detect the RF power (see Illustration 1).

    (Step Details)

  1. If it is a TR Bias issue, there is a switch arrangement on the Second Penetration Panel (SPW) (see Illustration 2). Ensure that the switches are properly connected. Use a multimeter in the diode drop mode (1.5V one direction, 0.4V in the other direction) to ensure that each switch is properly connected.

    (Step Details)

1.2.2  Problem: There is no signal. Transmit signal is out of specification.

  1. Ensure that the amplitude of the RF transmit pulse at the input of the RF amplifier by disconnecting the cable at the RF IN port on the amplifier and connecting it to the 50 Ohm DC input of an oscilloscope, with a DC block.

  1. Run a test scan. The amplitude of the square pulse should be >0.2 V peak-to-peak at a TG setting of 200.

1.2.3  Problem: Reflected power on RF amplifier high (>50% of Forward power).

  1. Attach the dummy load to the RF output.

  1. If the problem is corrected, check the transmit chain connection and other components (T/R switch, coil).

1.2.4  Problem: UPM sense fault; UPM sense light and/or inhibit light constantly illuminated.

  1. Check connections to RF amplifier. Refer to System Block Diagrams.

  1. Systematically swap the circuit boards inside the ASC chassis nothing if the error follows one particular board.

  1. Swap the connections from the amplifier to the boards in the ASC to see if this indicates a problem coming from the amplifier.

  1. If there is an issue coming from one particular component (UPM RF detector boards, UPM processor boards, RF amplifier, 50 ohm terminators), investigate the issue to ensure that component is working properly with the system.

1.2.5  Problem: Receiving UPM errors at the operator workstation, “spectro amp not ready.”

  1. The 50 ohm terminators may not be connected. Check that the 50 ohm loads (BNC male snap-on) are applied to J1, J2, J3, and J6 on UPM RF detector boards 19U and 19L. Ensure UPM cables are connected to J4 and J5 on UPM RF detector boards 19U and 19L.

  1. If the connections are okay, ensure the UPM RF detector boards and 50 ohm terminators are working properly.
