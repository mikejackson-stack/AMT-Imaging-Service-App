HD 8-Channel CTL Coil Setup for Coil MCQA Test

(Click here for Summary Version)

Required PersonsPreliminary ReqsProcedureFinalization

2  Overview

This procedure will set up for the automated MCQA test using the 3.0T HD 8-Channel CTL Array by GE (M3335LM).

NOTE:Coils do not ship with phantoms. Phantoms come in a unified phantom set with the MR system.

4  Procedure

4.1  Legacy Phantom Setup

NOTE:If installing the coil for the first time in a system, please refer to Auto Coil Install.

  1. Position the coil in the cradle, connect the cable as shown in Illustration 1.

    Illustration 1:  Connecting the Coil

  1. Place phantoms on the coil as shown in Illustration 2.

    Illustration 2:  Positioning Phantom

  1. Landmark on the coil crosshairs as shown in Illustration 3 and Illustration 4.

    Illustration 3:  Landmarking Coil

    Illustration 4:  Landmarking Coil

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.

4.2  Unified Phantom Setup

NOTE:Connect the coil to the system as shown in Legacy Phantom Setup, Step 1.

  1. Place the coil on the patient table and latch it to the table as shown in Illustration 5.

    Illustration 5:  Positioning the Coil

  1. Place the TL unified phantoms on the center of the TL section of the coil as shown in the Illustration 6.

    Illustration 6:  Aligning Phantom in S/I

  1. Align the phantoms to the center in S/I and R/L directions as shown in Illustration 6 and Illustration 7.

    Illustration 7:  Aligning Phantom in R/L

  1. Place the large cylindrical unified phantom on the CT section as shown in Illustration 8.

    Illustration 8:  Positioning Phantom on CT Section

  1. Adjust the large cylindrical unified phantom such that it is in complete contact with the TL unified phantom as shown in Illustration 9.

    Illustration 9:  Large Cylindrical Phantom in Complete Contact with TL Unified Phantom

  1. Adjust TL unified phantoms such that both the phantoms are in complete contact with each other as shown in Illustration 10.

    Illustration 10:  TL Unified Phantoms in Complete Contact with Each Other

  1. Connect the coil to respective port of the system LPCA.

  1. Landmark the coil on the mark as shown in Illustration 11 and advance to scan.

    Illustration 11:  Landmarking the Coil

  1. Proceed to the MCQA procedure to test the coil.
