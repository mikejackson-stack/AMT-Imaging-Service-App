Braking System Replacements

(Click Here to Return to Detailed Procedure)

  Safety

WARNING
PERSONAL INJURY POSSIBILITY!
SEVERE PERSONAL INJURY CAN OCCUR IF TABLE IS ACCIDENTALLY LOWERED DURING MAINTENANCE.
WHEN PERFORMING MAINTENANCE ON THE TABLE IN ITS UP POSITION, ENSURE THAT THE LOCK BAR IS SAFELY IN PLACE.

1  Procedure

1.1   Shaft Linkage-Right Replacement

  1. Undock the table and remove it from the magnet room.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove both lower base side covers by removing the two screws that fasten each cover to the table assembly. Lift up and pull out the covers. Note that Velcro strips attach the lower base side covers to the bellows. See Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Side Cover Removal for more information.

  1. Remove both the front and rear base covers by removing the two front screws and the two screws that attach the bellows. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Raise the table to its maximum height.

  1. Lower the table Lock Safety Bar. Lower the table to ensure all weight is resting on the lock bar. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the screws that attach the shaft linkage-right to the link right front, located at the right, front side of the table. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the two screws that attach the link-right rear. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Remove the extension shaft from the shaft linkage right.

    1. Remove the shoulder screw, as shown below. Apply Loctite when reinstalling the shoulder screw.

      (Step Details)

    1. Turn the shaft linkage to access the extensions mounting screws, and remove the screws. Apply Loctite when reinstalling the mounting screws.

      (Step Details)

    1. Press the lower pedal to lower the table, and remove the shaft linkage through the rear of the table. See illustration below.

      (Step Details)

  1. To install the new parts, reverse Step 1 through Step 8.

1.2   Extension Shaft Replacement

  1. Undock the table and remove it from the magnet room.

  1. Remove both lower base side covers by removing the two screws that fasten each cover to the table assembly. Lift up and pull out the covers. Note that Velcro strips attach the lower base side covers to the bellows. See Patient Table Upper and Lower Base Side Cover Removal for more information.

  1. Remove both the front and rear base covers by removing the two front screws and the two screws that attach the bellows. See Illustration 2 and Illustration 4.

  1. The extension shaft is held to the link reversing bar by a shoulder bolt and to the shaft linkage by two screws. See Illustration 7and Illustration 8. Remove these bolts taking care not to damage the plastic washer between the extension shaft and the shaft linkage. See Illustration 7 and note the orientation of the extension shaft.

  1. To install the extension shaft, reverse the order of Step 1 through Step 4.

1.3   Replacing Bearing IGUS

  1. Perform Shaft Linkage-Right Replacement, Step 1 through Shaft Linkage-Right Replacement, Step 8.

  1. Both the front and rear bearing IGUS can be removed by hand and replaced as required. See illustration below.

    (Step Details)

  1. Once the bearing IGUS has been replaced, repeat Step 1 through Step 2 in reverse order to reassemble the table.

1.4   Link Reversing Replacement

  1. Undock the table and remove it from the magnet room.

  1. See Illustration 4 for the location of the shoulder screws. Shoulder screws are accessible from the bottom of the table.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace the link reversing bar and shoulder screws as required. Tighten the shoulder screws. The shoulder screw will allow some movement of the reversing bar when installed.

  1. Dock the table and verify proper operation from the operator's console.

1.5   Block Mounting Replacement

  1. Undock the table and remove it from the magnet room.

  1. See Illustration 4. The block assembly is attached to the shaft linkage-left using two screws and the link-left rear with four bolts. Remove the bolts as required. See the illustration below. Apply Loctite when reinstalling the bolts.

    (Step Details)

  1. Replace block mounting and install by reversing Step 1 and Step 2

1.6   Caster Pin Actuator Replacement

The pin actuator is a threaded pin that bolts into the caster arm.

  1. Loosen and remove the caster pin actuator.

  1. Replace the caster pin actuator. See the following illustration.

2  Finalization

  1. Verify that all four wheels lock and that only the left, front wheel engages the steer lock.

  1. Dock the table and verify proper operation from the operator's console.
