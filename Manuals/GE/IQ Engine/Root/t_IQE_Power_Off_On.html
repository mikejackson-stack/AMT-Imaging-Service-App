Reset/Power on/off

About this task

IQ Engine has 4 working status, they are offline, idle, processing and Error.
There is 3 functions on IQE window Home tab. They are:
  • Power On
  • Power Off
  • Reset
Topic ID: task_n23_gtk_21c

Power On

Procedure

Go to the IQE window, click Power On button.
Note IQE needs to be powered up manually in equipment room when IQE is set Disabled and IQE server in Power off status.
After power on, the IQ Engine shows in window.
Topic ID: task_wgl_2tk_21c

Power Off

Procedure

  1. Go to the IQE window, click Power off button and then click Confirm.
  2. After power off, IQE will show below.
Topic ID: task_lkq_2tk_21c

Reset

About this task

There is two situations and need do IQE Reset.
  1. If the task status continues to report red or does not update under the Home tab, perform an IQE reset, see Reset.
  2. After removing or adding option keys, perform an IQE reset, see Reset.

Procedure

  1. Go to the IQE window, click Reset button and then click Confirm.
  2. After Reset, IQE will restart.
